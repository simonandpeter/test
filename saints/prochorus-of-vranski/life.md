# Prochorus of Vranski

saint.gr's calendar for 15 Ιανουαρίου names a venerable Prochorus, and the page behind the line gives him one sentence: he came from Bulgaria and lived about the end of the tenth century and the beginning of the eleventh; he lived the ascetic life at the monastery the page calls Vranski in Bulgaria, near the river it spells Πσίνζα; and he fell asleep, venerably, in peace.

That is all. There is no year of birth or death, no account of his tonsure or his labours, no relics, and no hymn. Beside the line the page glosses his name — Prochorus, the first of any company.

The Ohrid Prologue keeps him on the Julian 19 October — not on the 15 January of the Greek calendar — and calls him by the river rather than by the monastery: Prochorus of Pčinja. It sets him among company the Greek page does not mention at all. He was the contemporary and the friend of [John of Rila](/saints/john-of-rila) and of [Gabriel of Lesnovo](/saints/gabriel-of-lesnovo).

God showed him the place of his struggle in answer to his prayer, the entry says, and the place was a grove beside the river Pčinja. There he struggled until his old age and his death. Only God who sees all things knows all his labours and the temptations he endured in the course of them — but his greatness, the book goes on, may be judged from his myrrh-streaming relics and from the countless wonderful healings which have not ceased even to this day; and from these the measure may be taken of the grace of God that was given him for his great labours. He fell asleep and passed to the heavenly kingdom, it ends, in the eleventh century, which lies inside the span this folder already recorded.
