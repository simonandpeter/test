# Erotiis

The Greek and Romanian calendars both keep Erotiis on 6 October and both say only that she was martyred by fire — for her unshaken faith in Christ, the Romanian page adds. The Greek entry’s verse calls her a virgin, burned who was already burning with the love of Christ. Neither page gives a year or a place.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 6 Οκτωβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/1026/saint.aspx); doxologia.ro — [the saint’s page](https://doxologia.ro/sfanta-mucenita-erotiida); read 17 September 2026.*
