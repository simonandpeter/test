# Nicholas the Bulgarian (17 May)

saint.gr's calendar for 17 May names him and gives him two sentences, the second of which says there is nothing more. The holy great martyr Nicholas came from Bulgaria and was martyred in the year 1555. We have no further details, the page says, about the saint's life or his martyrdom; its head line dates him only «(; - 1555)».

The Greek calendar keeps a second Nicholas on this same day, the neomartyr [Nicholas of Metsovo](/saints/nicholas-of-metsovo), who died at Trikala in 1617 and whose page runs to several thousand words; the two are not the same man, and nothing about this one is borrowed from that one. What is recorded here is a Bulgarian great martyr of 1555, which is the whole of the entry.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 17 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/17/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1624/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
