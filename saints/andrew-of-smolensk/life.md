# Andrew of Smolensk

The Ohrid Prologue keeps him on the Julian 27 October and gives him three sentences, which is the whole of what this folder has. Out of love for Christ he left behind the glory and the honour of this world, and hid himself in a monastery — the book does not say which one — where he lived in disguise and unrecognised, serving as the church servant, the sexton, for thirty years. A prince of Smolensk who spent three decades keeping a church nobody knew he had any claim on is the entire portrait the calendar draws.

He reposed in the Lord peacefully, it says, in 1390; and his relics, which work wonders, were found in 1540, a hundred and fifty years afterwards. The Prologue names no monastery, no city, no bishop and no companion, and it gives him no year of birth and no age at death, so none is recorded here.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 27. октобар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2025-11-09&prolog=1); read 6 October 2026.*
