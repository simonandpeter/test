# The Four Martyrs of Tyre

The Greek calendar keeps on 21 Ιανουαρίου four martyrs whose names it does not give, and the whole of the page behind that line is one sentence: «Οι Άγιοι Τέσσερις Μάρτυρες, μαρτύρησαν δια ξίφους στην Τύρο» — the holy Four Martyrs bore witness by the sword at Tyre. Above it the page sets an epigram that makes them speak with one voice: «Στερρῶν ἀθλητῶν τεσσάρων φωνὴ μία· Ἡμῖν τὸ θνῄσκειν ἐκ ξίφους εὐθυμία».

Because the source enumerates nobody, this is one entry and not four: there is no name to give a folder to. No year is printed, no emperor, no judge, and no hymn. This folder records the number, the city and the manner of their death, which is the whole of what the day claims.
