# Paphnutius of the Thebaid

The Ohrid Prologue keeps Paphnutius the Confessor, a bishop «Таиски» in the Egyptian Thebaid. He suffered much for the Orthodox faith: heretics put out one of his eyes and broke his left leg. At the First Ecumenical Council he took part in refuting the heresy of Arius with great force, and the emperor Constantine valued him so highly that he would often kiss the eye he had lost for the truth of Orthodoxy. At the same Council he rose decisively against the Western representatives who proposed that priests living in the world be forbidden marriage altogether; he himself was a virgin all his life. The Prologue gives no year.
