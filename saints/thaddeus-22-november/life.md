# Thaddeus (22 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 22 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιος Θαδδαίος», glosses the name as the brave praiser of God, and then gives one clause of narrative and nothing else: «Ἐν τροχῷ δεθεὶς καὶ κατὰ πρανοῦς ἀφεθεὶς τελειοῦται» — bound to a wheel and let go down a slope, he was made perfect. That clause, quoted in the older Greek, is the whole of the narrative the calendar has for him.

The couplet set over it says the same thing twice over, turning the wheel into the psalm's: the wheel spins Thaddeus down the slope, and the voice of thunder is in the wheel, as the psalm says. The page gives him no country, no city, no year, no emperor, no companions and no hymn, and prints no relics for him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 22 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/22/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3069/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
