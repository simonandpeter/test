# Thaumastos

saint.gr's whole account of him is one sentence: «Ο Όσιος Θαυμαστός έζησε κατά τον 6ο αιώνα μ.Χ. και κοιμήθηκε με ειρήνη» — the venerable Thaumastos lived in the sixth century and fell asleep in peace. The calendar's line for the day says even less than that: «Όσιος Θαυμαστός», the venerable Thaumastos, and no more.

The name itself means wonderful, or admirable, and the page treats it as his name rather than as a description; whether it was the name he was given or one he was called by, it does not say. There is no country, no monastery, no elder, no disciple, no wonder, no relic, no couplet and no hymn — one century and one verb, and this folder does not supply the rest.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/24/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1464/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
