# Aeros

saint.gr's calendar for 8 December keeps the holy Aeros the archbishop, and it begins by saying that he is unknown to the synaxaria. His memory, the page says, is recorded in the Jerusalem Kanonarion of the archimandrite Kallistos, in these words: memory of the archbishop Aeros. He belongs in any case, it adds, to the saints of the Sionitis church, that is of Jerusalem.

That is the whole of the page. It gives no century, no see beyond the rank, no country, no manner of death, no relic, no hymn and no synaxaristic couplet. The same calendar keeps a bishop of this name on 6 December, whose page the site does not vouch for and which guesses that he may be the same man as this one; that guess is the other page's and nothing is made of it here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3201/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
