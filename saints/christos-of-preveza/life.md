# Christos of Preveza

saint.gr says that although Christos was a sailor by trade, he was in no way estranged from a Christian bearing. When the ship he worked on came once from Crete into the harbour of Kos, he ran at once to the church, prayed, found a confessor and made his confession. Some days later, as he was going back to his ship, he met janissaries, who began to curse his faith and his holy baptism.

Christos was not afraid. He answered the Turks that Mohammed is the damned antichrist. Full of anger they rushed on him, and with savage blows told him to deny his faith and so save himself; and he answered by reciting the symbol of the faith. At that they took him outside the city and burnt him alive.

The unbelievers left his body unburied for a month. But the relic of the saint stayed unaltered, and on the first nights a sweet light out of heaven covered it; and in that time, the page says, it worked many wonders besides. All this happened on 5 August 1668.
