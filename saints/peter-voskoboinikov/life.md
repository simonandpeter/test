# Peter (Voskoboinikov)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him sixth of ten people on one line for 31 октября ст. ст. and gives him no life. His name stands there as «Петра», a forename in the genitive, under a rank word that serves seven of the ten and above one year that serves all ten. The name link behind it adds the surname the line leaves out and the grade the line gives only in the plural: «Петр (Воскобойников), протоиерей, сщмч.», an archpriest of that name, one of the new martyrs of the Russian church.

So the year is the whole of what is recorded of him, and the year is 1937. There is no life page behind his name, and his name page carries no text, no icon and no hymn — only his name and his days, of which the second is 26 января, the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. The surname returns no other folder in this corpus, which matters more for him than for most: the bare forename is the commonest of all the forms this calendar prints for its twentieth-century martyrs, and the surname on the link is what tells him from the others.

Two of the ten on this line have lives on the calendar, and both of those men were shot on 13 November 1937 in the Tver region; nothing on the page says that of him, and nothing is said for him here. The second Russian calendar, azbyka.ru, which has given this corpus full lives and archival references for others of these men, refused the request with a 403 on the day he was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 31 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261031.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13840.html); read 4 October 2026.*
