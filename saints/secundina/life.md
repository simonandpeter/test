# Secundina

saint.gr's calendar for 15 Ιανουαρίου names Secundina, and the page behind the line is one sentence long: «Η Αγία Σεκουνδίνη άθλησε επί αυτοκράτορα Δεκίου (249 - 251 μ.Χ.)» — she contended under the emperor Decius, whose reign the page dates 249 to 251.

That is the whole of the record, and it is a reign rather than a year. The page gives her no city, no judge, no account of how she was taken or what she suffered, no relics, and no hymn; it does not even gloss her Latin name, as it does for many of the names beside hers. The calendar keeps her on 15 January.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 15 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/15/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1285/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
