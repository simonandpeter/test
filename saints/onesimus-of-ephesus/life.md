# Onesimus of Ephesus

saint.gr keeps Onesimus, archbishop of Ephesus, on the first of December, and what it says of him is one sentence: he was archbishop of Ephesus and he died in peace — «Ο Άγιος Ονήσιμος ήταν Αρχιεπίσκοπος Εφέσου και απεβίωσε ειρηνικά». There is no century, no emperor, no council, no relic and no hymn.

It adds one thing about the name. In certain sources, it says, this Onesimus is reported as Onesiphoros, together with Solomon, who was also bishop of Ephesus — and that Solomon is the [Solomon of Ephesus](/saints/solomon-of-ephesus) whom the Romanian calendar keeps on the second of December. The page's own note elsewhere on the same day is that there were only two archbishops of Ephesus among these entries, Onesimus and Solochon or Solomon, which is as much as it will say about how they are to be counted.

His couplet is the only other thing on the page, and it plays on the name as the Greek dictionaries do: though the body of Onesimus went down into the earth, it wells up the profitable grace for those who need it. Nothing further is recorded here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3003/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
