# Sergius (Lyubomudrov)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him in one line with three other priests for 8 февраля ст. ст. — «Сщмчч. Симеона, Андрея, Сергия и Петра пресвитеров (1938).» — four hieromartyrs and presbyters who died in 1938. The name link behind his place in the line gives the calendar's own nominative, «Сергий (Любомудров), протоиерей», which adds both the surname the line leaves out and the rank of archpriest.

The calendar prints no life page for him, and none is given here: the line and the name link are the whole of what it records. The three priests named with him are not set down as his companions in anything but the day.

**The Russian calendar’s 9 апреля ст. ст. keeps the watchman of the church he served in**, and this folder gains its first `related` row from it. azbyka.ru’s life of [Gabriel (Fomin)](/saints/gabriel-fomin), read for the civil 22 April, says that after three years’ imprisonment and the confiscation of his house Gabriel settled in the village Сушково in the Коломенский district and began to help «служившему здесь в Казанском храме священнику Сергию Любомудрову» — the priest serving there in the Kazan church.

**The identification is secure and rests on one village and one church**: azbyka’s own life of this folder’s man, read the same day, has him ordained deacon in 1906 to the church of the Kazan icon in the village Сушково of the Луховицкий uyezd and priest to the same church in 1916, and says «Здесь он прослужил до ареста», he served there until his arrest, which came on 6 February 1938. The two pages name one surname, one village and one church, and the years overlap. That page is a full life, where days.pravoslavie.ru carries none; it is not written into this folder here, this reader’s range being the Julian 9 April, and the question of taking it is put to the author in the batch that wrote this row.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 8 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260208.html); read 3 October 2026; and azbyka.ru’s lives of the martyr Gabriel (Fomin) for the civil 22 April and of this folder’s own man for the civil 21 February, read in the in-app Browser pane — [the one](https://azbyka.ru/days/sv-gavriil-fomin) and [the other](https://azbyka.ru/days/sv-sergij-ljubomudrov), read 6 October 2026.*
