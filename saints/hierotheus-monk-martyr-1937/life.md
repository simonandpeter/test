# Hierotheus (Glazkov), Hieromonk, Monk-martyr (1937)

Hierotheus — Nikandr Arsenyevich Glazkov in the world — was born on 27 October 1889 in the village of Pankratovo in the Lezha district of the Vologda county of Vologda province, to a peasant family. He finished the course of the zemstvo primary school, and at nineteen entered the brotherhood of the Trinity monastery of Paul of Obnora in the Gryazovets district, where he bore the obediences of baker and sacristan; in 1919 he was tonsured with the name Hierotheus — hallowed by God — for the hieromartyr Hierotheus, bishop of Athens, and in 1920 was ordained hierodeacon. He took hard the ruin — the calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. After the monastery was closed he served as hieromonk in the Yaroslavl diocese; in 1930 he was arrested and sentenced to five years in the camps, and on his return went on preaching openly, denouncing the godless power. Arrested again in 1937 for counter-revolutionary agitation, he refused to testify against others; he was sentenced to be shot and executed on 4 September 1937. He was glorified in 2000, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 22 August (Julian).
