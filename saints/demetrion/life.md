# Demetrion

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives one sentence: the holy Demetrion was martyred, being put to death with arrows. Over it stands a couplet, and the couplet is the only other thing the page has of him — the arrows that wound the flesh mortally, Demetrion reckoned the arrows of children, «Βέλη τὰ τιτρώσκοντα σάρκα καιρίως, / Δημητρίων ἡγεῖτο βέλη νηπίων».

That is the whole of the record here. There is no country, no century, no emperor, no place of martyrdom, no relics and no hymn; the page adds only the meaning it gives for the name Demetrius, which is its habit with any name near a saint's, and not an account of this man. Nothing is claimed beyond the name, the rank, the manner of death and the day.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1517/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
