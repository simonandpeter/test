# The Six Martyrs of Egypt

saint.gr's calendar for 28 Φεβρουαρίου prints a company of six with no names at all. Its line reads «Άγιοι Έξι Μάρτυρες εξ Αιγύπτου», the holy six martyrs from Egypt, and the page standing under it says only that these holy martyrs were perfected by the sword in Egypt. Because the line names nobody, and the page names nobody either, the company is kept here as the one entry the calendar makes of it rather than as six folders.

Its couplet turns on the writing of the records: those who wrote the contests and the lives of the age wrote that six Egyptians died by the sword — «Ξίφει θανεῖν ἔγραψαν ἓξ Αἰγυπτίους, οἱ τοῦ χρόνου γράψαντες ἄθλους καὶ βίους». No year, no emperor, no city in Egypt, no relics and no hymn are printed for them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/28/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1929/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
