# Basil (Sokolov)

He is the fourth of the six presbyters the Сретенский monastery’s calendar names on one line for the Julian 16 November: «Сщмчч. Иоанна , Николая , Виктора , Василия , Макария и Михаила пресвитеров (1937).» That calendar prints no life page behind his name and his name page carries only its title and its days, so the life here is the one the Russian church calendar at azbyka.ru prints, which gives both a short and a full account of him. Basil Alexandrovich Sokolov was born on 5 May 1880 in the village of Chyorny Ruchey in the Vyshny Volochok district of the province of Tver, the son of a psalmist.

He finished a church school and from 1909 served as a psalmist in the province of Moscow. In 1925 he was ordained priest, and the page says of the diocese he then served that one database gives Moscow and Игумен Дамаскин gives Tver — a disagreement it names itself and does not settle, and neither does this folder. In 1930 he was arrested on a charge of anti-Soviet agitation and counter-revolutionary activity. What followed the page tells two ways: the short life has him sentenced to three years in a corrective labour camp, and the full life to eight years’ banishment to the Northern region, served in the Arkhangelsk country, where he was arrested again in exile on 17 November 1931 and sentenced on 16 March 1932 to three years in the camps.

The short life adds an arrest in 1935 for unpaid taxes and two years in a camp, which the full life does not mention. Both agree on the end. Coming back from imprisonment he served in the Transfiguration church in the village of Spas-Ulstim in the Udomlya district, where he helped to open the parish school that stood empty and took part in services held secretly in houses. On 20 November 1937 he was arrested, charged with a hostile attitude to the Soviet power and with anti-Soviet agitation aimed at wrecking the work of the collective farm. He did not admit his guilt.

On 27 November the troika of the УНКВД for the Kalinin region sentenced him to be shot, and he was shot on 29 November 1937 and buried in a common unmarked grave. He was rehabilitated in 1989 and glorified by the council of bishops of the Russian Orthodox Church in August 2000.
