# Parthenius Parthenniades

saint.gr says Parthenius Parthenniades was a member of the movement of the Kollyvades, and that when they had to leave the Holy Mountain he went to the monastery of the Annunciation on Ikaria. In 1798 he reached Patmos, and with the help of builders from Samos he built a small monastery in the area known today as Livadi Kalogiron, the Monks' Meadow. To finish the work he needed more resources, and for that reason he went to Thrace, to gather the money it wanted.

In Thrace he worked as a priest. At some point he advised a young Christian woman to avoid a relationship with a Muslim Turk; the man learned of it, and in his anger killed Parthenius, on the fifth of March 1805 — which is the day the Greek calendar keeps him on.

That is the whole of the notice. It gives no place and no year of birth, no family, no monastery of his tonsure, no city in Thrace, no name for the man who killed him, no manner of the killing, no relic, no distich and no hymn. What it sets beside his name is the synaxis of the saints of Patmos, and that is a company and not a person, so it is not kept here.
