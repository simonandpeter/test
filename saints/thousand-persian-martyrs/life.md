# The Thousand Persian Martyrs

The Сретенский monastery's calendar keeps a thousand Persian martyrs on the Julian 14 April, and names not one of them. Its whole line is «Мчч. 1000 Персидских и Азата скопца (341).» — the thousand martyrs of Persia and [Azat the Eunuch](/saints/azat-the-eunuch) — and the page it gives the company of its own carries nothing but the day. So this is one folder and not a thousand: the line enumerates nobody, and a company named only by its number is one entry here.

What the same calendar does give is the persecution they died in. The life it serves behind that line has the eunuch, a man of high place at the Persian court, executed together with the thousand for confessing Christ; and the second life the same index serves, of [Simeon of Persia](/saints/simeon-of-persia), the bishop of Seleucia-Ctesiphon, sets out what was happening. The king laid a heavy tax on the Christians, and when some refused to pay it, which was reported to him as a revolt, he opened a cruel persecution. The bishop would not bow to him and would not worship the sun, and was beheaded with his presbyters and with everyone the prison held.

That life says the executions went on through the whole of Pascha week, until 23 April, and that the sources it follows count a thousand martyrs, then another hundred, then another hundred and fifty. The thousand of this day's line are that first number. Of the year the calendar is at odds with itself — the line says 341 and both lives say 344 — so both readings are kept here as an interval and neither is chosen. After the eunuch's death, the first life adds, the king was so grieved that he forbade the killing of Christians by edict, and for a time the edict held.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 14 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260414.html), [the calendar's page for the company](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14680.html), which gives the day and nothing else, and the two lives behind the line, [of the eunuch](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life6386.htm) and [of the bishop's company](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life873.htm); read 7 October 2026.*
