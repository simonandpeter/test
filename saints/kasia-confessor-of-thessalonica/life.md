# Kasia of Thessalonica

saint.gr gives the four of them one sentence. The holy confessors [Agathon](/saints/agathon-confessor-of-thessalonica), [Eutychia](/saints/eutychia-confessor-of-thessalonica), Kasia and [Philippa](/saints/philippa-confessor-of-thessalonica) lived under the emperor Diocletian, whose reign it dates 284 to 305; they were arrested at Thessalonica in the year 304 and confessed Christ before the governor Dulcitius and the prefect Cassander. Because they were young they were not condemned to death but shut up in prison, and that is why the calendar calls them confessors rather than martyrs.

That is all the page prints of her, and hers is the one name of the four it does not stop to gloss. It gives her no parentage, no age, no length of imprisonment and no year of death, and neither couplet nor hymn.
