# Antiochus (24 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 24 December names the venerable Antiochus, and what it prints in place of a life is a guess, carefully marked as one. Nikodemos the Hagiorite supposes, the page says, that this venerable father is the one called Pandektos — that is, the author of the Pandektes — who lived in the middle of the seventh century, came from Galatia, and was a monk at the monastery of Saint Sabbas in Jerusalem.

That same man, it goes on, described the sack of Jerusalem by the Arabs and lamented the murder of the monks of the Lavra by the raiders. Then it closes with where to read further: a good study of Antiochus was written by the Archimandrite Kallistos in 1910, and by I. Phokylides in his work «Ἡ Ἱερὰ Λαύρα Σάββα τοῦ ἡγιασμένου». No year is set down for him here, because the century belongs to the identification and not to the calendar's own statement, and the page prints no hymn. It carries no asterisk and not the sentence by which saint.gr marks an entry it will not vouch for.
