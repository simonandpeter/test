# Zosimas the Hermit

Zosimas lived in the fourth century as a hermit among the wild beasts. Dometian, the governor of Cilicia, came on him while hunting, talking quietly and gently with the animals round him, which ran off when the hunters appeared. Asked who he was and why he lived in the desert, the old man said his name was Zosimas, that he was a Christian, and that he could not live in the city among the enemies of the Lord Jesus Christ, and so lived alone among the beasts. Dometian threatened to torture him before all the people at Nazareth until he renounced the Nazarene, and to every question about the sorcery by which he tamed the beasts Zosimas answered only that he was a Christian. At Nazareth he was hung head downwards with a great stone tied to his neck and torn with iron hooks, and his tormentors mocked him: if the beasts obey you, call one of them here, and then we will believe in your God. He prayed, and a huge lion ran in; everyone fled in fear, and the lion came up to the old man and held up the stone at his neck with its paw. The governor begged him to calm the beast and had him untied, to be taken to the emperor, but Zosimas died there and then.

The Ohrid Prologue, for the Serbian calendar, tells it with differences of its own. Its Dometian is a prince of Cilicia and himself a fierce persecutor of Christians; the old man says he has long lived with the beasts, who are better than the persecutors of Christians in the city; he is bound and sent ahead to Nazareth, beaten until he is covered in blood, and hoisted onto a tree with the stone at his neck, and the lion puts its own head under the stone to ease him. The frightened prince sets him free, and he gives up his spirit to the Lord soon afterwards. Its verses make him a man who fled early into the dry desert to talk with God, loved by the beasts and killed by men.

The Russian calendar keeps him on 19 September old style. Its name page lists this life under Zosimas of Cilicia beside a second life, of a venerable martyr Zosimas of Cilicia; that life was not read for this folder, and whether the two are one man is left open.
