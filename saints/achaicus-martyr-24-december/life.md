# Achaicus (24 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 24 December names Achaicus the martyr, and the whole of what it has to say of him is that he was martyred by the sword. Above that sentence stands a distich, which says that Achaicus, having cast out every grief of the soul, gave his head to the sword with all his joy. There the entry ends, and nothing else of him is printed.

The calendar gives him no year, no city, no emperor and no judge, prints no hymn, and marks the feast fixed. He is not the Achaicus the same calendar keeps on 15 June with Fortunatus and Stephanas, whom it ranks among the apostles and whom this corpus holds under that name; the two pages say nothing of each other, and two entries on one calendar are two commemorations. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence by which saint.gr marks an entry it will not vouch for.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/24/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3227/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
