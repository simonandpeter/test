# Urošica

The Ohrid Prologue gives him three sentences and they are the whole of what this folder has. He was the son of King Dragutin, it says — the king who ended his life as the monk [Theoctistus](/saints/theoctistus-brother-of-stephen-milutin), whom the same book and three other calendars keep on the Julian 30 October. He preserved purity and chastity even in marriage. And myrrh flowed from his grave.

That is all of it: three sentences, no year of birth, no year of death, no place, and no account of how he lived or died beyond the chastity and the myrrh. Nothing has been added to them here. The book heads him «кнез Српски», prince of the Serbs, and it is the only one of the four calendars this corpus reads that keeps him at all.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 11. новембар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2025-11-24&prolog=1); read 7 October 2026.*
