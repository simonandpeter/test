# Alexander, companion of Barbarus and Acoluthus

saint.gr's calendar for 14 May keeps three martyrs on one page with one notice between them: Alexander, [Barbarus](/saints/barbarus-companion-of-alexander) and [Acoluthus](/saints/acoluthus-the-martyr). These saints, it says, having lived a holy life and brought many idolaters to the Christian faith, were martyred by beheading; and their synaxis and feast is held in the church of Saint Irene, which stands near the sea.

That is the whole of the account. The couplet above it counts them — for the severed heads of three fellow contenders the Trinity sets three worthy crowns — and the page's name glosses explain only what the three names mean. No century, no country, no emperor, no judge, no relics and no hymn is printed, and none is set down here. This Alexander is not the soldier of the count Tiberianus whom the same day keeps on a page of his own.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 14 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/14/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4020/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
