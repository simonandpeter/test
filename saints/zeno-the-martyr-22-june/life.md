# Zeno (22 June)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 22 iunie reads «Sfântul Mucenic Zenon», and the page behind it carries the day, the epistle appointed for it and the gospel read with it, and nothing more — no country, no century, no manner of death, no year and no hymn. The corpus already keeps six other men of this name, and nothing on this page ties him to any of them.

The same day's calendar prints «Sfântul Mucenic Zina», whose page is equally bare and whose icon there is titled «Sfinții Mucenici Zinon și Zina; Sfântul Ierarh Eusebie» — the two martyrs together with the bishop of Samosata. That the calendar keeps them on one day is all that is set down here.

The readings appointed are 2 Timothy 2:1–10 and John 15:17–27 with 16:1–2.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 22 iunie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/22-iunie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-zenon); read 24 September 2026.*
