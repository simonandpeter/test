# Joseph of Astrakhan

The holy hieromartyr Joseph, first Metropolitan of Astrakhan, was born at Astrakhan in the year 1579, saint.gr says. He was tonsured a monk and became abbot of the monastery of the Holy Trinity at Astrakhan at the age of fifty-two. In 1656 he was elected Metropolitan of Astrakhan.

On the eleventh of May in the year 1672, during a rising of the inhabitants of the city, Saint Joseph was perfected as a martyr. The page does not say by what means. His martyrdom was written down in detail by two eyewitnesses, priests of the cathedral of Astrakhan, Father Cyril and Father Peter.

The priests took the honourable relic of the hieromartyr, clothed it in the vestments of a bishop and laid it in a tomb prepared for it. The next day, after the serving of the Panychis, the relic was carried to the chapel and remained unburied for nine days. The relics of the holy hierarch were then placed within a tomb, and they worked wonders for those who came to them with faith. Saint Joseph was numbered among the saints by the Synod of the Russian Orthodox Church in April 1918, and his relics, the page says, are in the cathedral of the Ascension at Astrakhan in Russia.
