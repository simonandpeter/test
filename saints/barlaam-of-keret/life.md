# Barlaam of Keret

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar for 6 ноября ст. ст. names him in four words and a century, «Прп. Варлаама Керетского (XVI).», and gives him a life page of three sentences. He lived in the sixteenth century and came from the Keret district of the Arkhangelsk province, on the Karelian shore of the White Sea. He was a priest of the Kola stockade, and afterwards took the monastic habit and lived as a monk in a hermitage near Keret, where he was buried.

The page adds one thing about how he is kept: among the people of the White Sea coast he is honoured as a protector against «водного потопления», drowning in water. The name link behind the line gives the calendar's own nominative with a second epithet and his grade, «Варлаам Керетский, Архангельский, иеромонах», and that grade is why this folder reads him as a hieromonk beside the «Прп.» of the line. Nothing else is recorded: no year, no parentage, no age, and no account of his death.

The day page carries a troparion and a kontakion under his name, and they are longer than his life. Both are kept here whole in the Church Slavonic the page prints, with the English rendered for this site. The troparion is the only place either of these pages describes what he did: that he crossed the open sea in a small boat against the fiercest storms, and that by his prayers the impassable sea road round the Holy Nose — Святой Нос, the cape on the Murman coast — was made safe for men to pass from the poisonous worm that infested it.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 6 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261106.html) and [his life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life6477.htm); read 4 October 2026.*
