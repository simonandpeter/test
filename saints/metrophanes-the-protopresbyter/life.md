# Metrophanes (12 February)

saint.gr's calendar for 12 Φεβρουαρίου names «Άγιος Μητροφάνης ο Ιερομάρτυρας εκ Ρωσίας», Metrophanes the hieromartyr from Russia, and heads his page «(; - 1931)». The whole of what it prints is one sentence: «Ο Άγιος Ιερομάρτυς Μητροφάνης ήταν πρωτοπρεσβύτερος και μαρτύρησε το έτος 1931 μ.Χ. Δεν έχουμε περισσότερες λεπτομέρειες για τον Βίο του Αγίου» — he was a protopresbyter and was martyred in the year 1931; we have no further details of the saint's life.

So the rank of protopresbyter, the country and the year are the whole of what the Greek calendar keeps of him — no city, no see, no parentage, no account of the martyrdom, no hymn, no relics and not even a distich or a gloss on the name. He is not Mitrophan of Voronezh, nor Mitrophan of Constantinople, both of whom this corpus keeps; the century alone separates him from either.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/12/index.aspx) and [his page](https://www.saint.gr/1231/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
