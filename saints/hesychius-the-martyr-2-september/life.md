# Hesychius (2 September)

Hesychius was martyred by hanging, bearing the noose quietly for the Master he longed for. That is the whole of the synaxarion’s notice for the martyr kept on 2 September, with its verse; the Synaxaristes have nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 2 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/921/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
