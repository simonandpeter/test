# The Five Martyrs in Nicomedia

saint.gr's calendar for 16 June names the Five Martyrs in Nicomedia and gives no life. All the page says of them is one sentence: the holy Five Martyrs in Nicomedia were martyred by the sword. Beside it stands a two-line epigram, and that is the only other thing printed — that a great five-towered stronghold of fellow contenders falls, shaken by the earthquake of the executioner's blade. The company is named by no member.

The page gives no names for the five, no country beyond Nicomedia, no year, no emperor and no hymn. Because it enumerates nobody it is one entry and one folder for the company rather than five, and nothing here identifies them with any of the many martyrs of Nicomedia the calendar keeps on other days.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 16 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/16/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1871/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
