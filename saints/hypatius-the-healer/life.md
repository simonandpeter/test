# Hypatius the Healer

saint.gr's calendar for 31 Μαρτίου names the venerable Hypatius the healer, and the page behind the line is four short sentences about how he lived. He lived keeping a strict fast. At night he made his prayers and slept little; he ate a little bread and drank only water. He gave himself wholly to the service of the sick, and he received from God the gift of healing.

That is all of it. The page gives no century, no country, no monastery, no bishop, no teacher, no relic and no hymn, and it tells not one of the healings it credits him with; this folder therefore records no date and no place. What the source keeps of him is a rule of life and a charism, and that is what is kept here.

One caution belongs on the folder, because the Greek calendar sets two men of this name on one day. The other is Hypatius, bishop of Gangra, who sat at the First Ecumenical Council and was stoned to death by Novatianists in 326; he has his own entry, his own page and his own hymns on saint.gr, and nothing of his is here. This Hypatius is identified by his epithet and by his nursing of the sick, and by nothing else.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 31 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/31/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2612/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
