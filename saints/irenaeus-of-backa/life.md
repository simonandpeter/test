# Irenaeus of Bačka

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and carries no life. Its line for the twenty-fourth of March, old style, reads «Свт. Иринея исп., еп. Бачского (1955) (Серб.).» — the hierarch Irenaeus the confessor, bishop of Bačka — and closes with the mark the calendar uses for a saint of the Serbian church. The name page behind «Иринея» is headed «Святитель Ириней, епископ Бачский, исповедник» and gives that one day and no other.

He is the only one of the nine persons this day names whose page does not list the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church beside its own day, which fits both the marker and the rank: a confessor of the Serbian church rather than one of the Russian new martyrs. Nothing else is said of him on either page — no year of birth, no place beyond the see, no account of what he confessed or how he died. The 1955 the line closes with is the whole of his chronology.

This calendar keeps another saint of the same bare name, a martyr of 258, on the thirteenth of August, old style, and the corpus holds him; the two share nothing but the name. The Serbian book has not been read for this bishop, and it is the one source that would say most about him.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 24 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260324.html); read 8 October 2026.*
