# Isaac

The Romanian calendar keeps the martyr Isaac on 22 September, beside the
martyr [Martin](/saints/martin-the-martyr-22-september), and prints no life
and no year for either. The Greek synaxarion, which keeps the two together
the same day, adds the one thing the tradition has held of them, that they
died by the sword, and the two couplets the Synaxarion gives each: for
Isaac, that what was wanting to the passion of Isaac the son of Sarah, who
was not slain, the new Isaac made up by being slain. Nothing more is known
of them — not the place, not the century — and this entry says so rather
than borrow another Isaac’s story.

*After the day’s line of the Romanian calendar — [doxologia.ro, 22 septembrie](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-isaac), read 23 August 2026 — and the Greek synaxarion’s page for the pair — [saint.gr](https://www.saint.gr/2484/saint.aspx), read 6 September 2026.*
