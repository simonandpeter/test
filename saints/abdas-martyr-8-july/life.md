# Abdas (8 July)

saint.gr's calendar for 8 Ιουλίου names him and gives no life. The whole of its account is one sentence, «Ο Άγιος Αβδάς (ή Αυδάς) μαρτύρησε δια ξίφους, μάλλον μαζί με τον Άγιο Προκόπιο (βλέπε 8 Ιουλίου)» — the holy Abdas, or Audas, was martyred by the sword, probably together with [Procopius](/saints/procopius-of-jerusalem), whom the same day keeps. The «μάλλον», probably, is the page's own: it does not say the two deaths were one.

The page names no country, no city, no century, no emperor and no judge, and gives no year in its text, though it heads him «(; - 303)». It prints no hymn under his line, only the couplet the synaxaria set over him: «Άπαξ εμαυτόν προς το πάσχειν θαρσύνας, / Xαράν το πάσχειν εκ ξίφους Aβδάς έχω» — having once made myself bold to suffer, I, Abdas, hold suffering by the sword to be a joy. That is everything the source says of him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2050/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
