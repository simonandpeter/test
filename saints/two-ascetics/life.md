# The Two Ascetics

saint.gr's calendar for 20 Ιουνίου names them and gives no life. The whole of its account of them is a single sentence, «Οι Άγιοι Δύο Ασκητές απεβίωσαν ειρηνικά στην έρημο» — the two holy ascetics reposed peacefully in the desert. The page gives no names for either of them, no place beyond the desert itself, no century, no year and no emperor, and no hymn is printed under their line.

Besides that sentence it prints only the two-line verse the synaxaria carry for the day: «Ἀνιπτόσαρκοι καὶ χαμαιεῦναι δύω, / Ψυχὰς πλύναντες, ὕψος ᾤκησαν πόλου» — two men unwashed in the flesh and sleeping on the ground, who, having washed their souls clean, came to dwell in the height of heaven. That is everything the source says of them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 20 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/20/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1945/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
