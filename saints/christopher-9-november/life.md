# Christopher (9 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 9 Νοεμβρίου keeps Christopher and Maura in one entry, and the whole of its notice is six words: «Οι Άγιοι Χριστόφορος και Μαύρα μαρτύρησαν δια ξίφους» — the holy Christopher and Maura were martyred by the sword. The page gives them no country, no century, no emperor, no trial, no burial, no year and no hymn, and says nothing of how the two were joined.

What it does print is the couplet of the synaxarion, which plays on her name as darkness and on his as the bearer of Christ: «Πλάνην ἀμαυροῖ Μαῦρα καρθεῖσα ξίφει· / Χριστοῦ δὲ τμηθεὶς φῶς ὁρᾷ Χριστοφόρος» — Maura, shorn by the sword, darkens error; and Christopher, cut down, sees the light of Christ. Kept with him is [Maura](/saints/maura-9-november).

*After saint.gr's calendar for 9 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/09/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2955/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
