# The Ten Martyrs of Phoenicia

saint.gr's calendar for 16 Μαρτίου keeps ten martyrs in Phoenicia whose names it does not have — «Άγιοι Δέκα μάρτυρες οι ἐν Φοινίκῃ» — and the page behind that line holds one sentence about them: that these ten holy martyrs were martyred by the sword. Beside it stand two lines of verse which say it again with a play on the words: «Χορὸν δέκανδρον Μαρτύρων διὰ ξίφους, / Ἀνδροκτόνοι κτείνουσι Μαρτυροκτόνοι» — a ten-man choir of martyrs is killed by the sword by man-slayers, martyr-slayers.

No name is given for any of the ten, no city of Phoenicia, no year, no emperor and no governor. Because the line enumerates nobody, the company is kept here as the one entry the calendar makes of it, and not as ten folders for men whose names the source does not have. What is recorded is the commemoration, the number, the country and the sword.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 16 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/16/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3900/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
