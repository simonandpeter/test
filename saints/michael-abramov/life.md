# Michael (Abramov)

He is the last of the six presbyters the Сретенский monastery’s calendar names on one line for the Julian 16 November: «Сщмчч. Иоанна , Николая , Виктора , Василия , Макария и Михаила пресвитеров (1937).» That calendar prints no life page behind his name, so the life here is the one the Russian church calendar at azbyka.ru prints. Michael Ivanovich Abramov was born on 1 November 1885 — by other documents the 3rd — in the village of Rogovaya in the Pronsk district of the province of Ryazan, into a peasant family. He entered the theological school at Skopin in 1897 and finished it in 1901, and went on to the Ryazan theological seminary.

Leaving the seminary in 1907 he was appointed psalmist at the church of the Protection of the Mother of God in the town of Pronsk and teacher of the law in its parish school, and in 1908, by the decree of the bishop of Ryazan and Zaraysk, to the church of the Entry in the village of Chulkovo, where he was ordained priest on 3 August. The clergy register of that church for 1912 gives him as twenty-six years old, with his wife Evdokia Andreevna, twenty-one, who had finished her course at the Ryazan diocesan school for women, and their son Vladimir, two years and three months old. Of him it says that his conduct was excellently good, that he was diligent and exact in his duty, that he gave the building up of the parish and the church his whole attention, and that he preached after the Liturgy on every Sunday and feast.

In 1912 he was moved to the church of St Nicholas in the village of Mostya in the Ryazhsk district, where he taught in the parish school as well; in March 1913 he was given the nabedrennik for diligent service, and in February 1914 he was moved to the diocese of Tula, to the village of Bogoslovo. In May 1916 the diocesan authority marked him with an award for particular labours in the circumstances of the war — he had set up a crèche for children. By the beginning of 1917 the register of that church gives him two sons, Vladimir, born in 1909, and Leonid, born on 8 August 1913.

After the revolution he was pressed as most priests were, by the loss of the vote and by taxes beyond bearing. In 1928 he was arrested and convicted under article 72 of the criminal code, and served a year in the house of correction at Tula, from 14 January 1929 to 14 January 1930. On his release the ruling bishop received him and gave him a new charge: from 28 December 1930 he served as priest, in the rank of archpriest, at the church of the Praise of the Most Holy Mother of God in the village of Gorodishche at the mouth of the Dubna. In 1934 came a second arrest and three years’ exile under article 58-10, and at the beginning of 1937 he came back from exile to that same church.

His case, number 6916, was opened by the Kimry district office of the НКВД for the Kalinin region, under the operational order of 30 July 1937 that required an investigation conducted quickly and in a simplified manner. It was begun on 13 November and finished on 21 November 1937. The warrant for the search and the arrest was taken out on the 13th and both were done the same day in the presence of witnesses; there was nothing to confiscate from him, and the officers took only his passport. He was brought to the district office that day. His first and last interrogation was on 14 November, his own birthday, and the page observes that at that point the investigation had no material at all about any anti-Soviet activity of his.

The material came afterwards: a certificate from the village soviet on 20 November and three witnesses for the prosecution questioned on the 19th and the 20th. On 21 November, his name day, the Synaxis of the Archangel Michael, the indictment was already signed. It charged him with counter-revolutionary agitation among the backward part of the collective farmers and the independent peasants, with spreading rumours of famine and of war against the Soviet Union, with urging men to leave the collective farm, with praising the enemies of the people who had been shot, with slandering the new Constitution — and it recorded that he did not admit the charge laid against him.

On 27 November the troika of the УНКВД for the Kalinin region resolved that he be shot. It was carried out in the internal prison of the НКВД of the Kalinin region on 29 November 1937, and he was buried in an unmarked grave. In 2000 he was glorified by the Russian Orthodox Church among the saints. The grade is the one place the two hosts read differently in form and the same in substance: the day’s line calls all six «пресвитеров» and azbyka heads its page «пресвитер», while the Сретенский calendar’s own name link reads «Михаил (Абрамов), протоиерей, сщмч.» and azbyka’s own text, with the indictment it quotes, has him an archpriest from 1930 on. Both bodies say archpriest, and that is what is written here.
