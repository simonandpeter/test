# Meletius of Stânișoara

All his care was to please God and to live in fasting and prayer and purity of body and soul — to keep his thoughts always cleansed, to cast down every high thing that rises against the knowledge of God, and to bring all his understanding captive to the obedience of Christ. So the Romanian life of Meletius, who advanced well in secular learning and surpassed many, and was persuaded — the life runs on past the point read here. The Romanian calendar keeps him on 3 September with [Neophytos](/saints/neophytos-of-stanisoara), the two hermits of the monastery of Stânișoara, with a common troparion and kontakion.

*After the Viețile Sfinților as printed by doxologia.ro — [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-cuvios-meletie-de-la-manastirea-stanisoara); read 23 August 2026.*
