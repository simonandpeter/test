# Raphael of Banat

Raphael was a Serb by birth and a monk of Hilandar on Athos. Hilandar then held several metochia in the Serbian lands, among them one in the territory of present-day Zrenjanin in the Banat, and the brotherhood sent the hieromonk Raphael there on some monastic obedience.

There he kept up the strict ascetic life he had led on the Mountain — not only in the obedience laid on him but in every God-pleasing labour of the monastic life, above all prayer and fasting. He lived in a small hut made of ordinary reeds, and in it he reposed in peace. His relics were shown to be wonderworking, and many people come to his grave. He lived in the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries.
