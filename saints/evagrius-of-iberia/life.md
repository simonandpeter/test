# Evagrius of Iberia

saint.gr says the venerable Evagrius came from Iberia and was the son of a priest. He was ordained reader by [Basil the Great](/saints/basil-the-great) and deacon by Basil's brother [Gregory](/saints/gregory-of-nyssa), bishop of Nyssa. God had endowed him with the gift of a formidable eloquence, and it was that gift, the page says, which led him into pride: the sin of self-regard brought him so far that he fell in love with a rich woman and came near to losing his chastity.

But he came to repentance and turned his steps towards Jerusalem, where he met the temptation of pride again. His spiritual counsellor there was [Melania the Roman](/saints/melania-the-roman), whom the page notes is honoured on 31 December, and who was living her ascetic life in a small and poor cell in the city. So Evagrius took refuge on a mountain of Nitria, where he lived the monastic polity with prayer, fasting and self-restraint. God did not abandon him, but counted him worthy of the gift of foresight; and he fell asleep in peace about the year 415.
