# Joseph of Lythrodontas

Joseph is one of six saints of Cyprus whom the Greek calendar keeps together on 6 October — [Hilarion the New](/saints/hilarion-the-new-of-cyprus), [John](/saints/john-companion-of-hilarion-the-new), Joseph “at Lythrodontas”, [Kalandios in Tamasia](/saints/kalandios-of-tamasia), [Cassian of Glyfa](/saints/cassian-of-glyphia) and [Cassian of Axylou](/saints/cassian-of-axylou) — and it says only that they were five of the three hundred martyrs and venerable fathers called the Alamanoi who bore witness in Cyprus; it names six and counts five. Its entry marks them as saints whose existence, details or feast day have not been cross-checked, and it gives no year, no place of death and nothing that tells the martyrs from the venerable fathers among them.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 6 Οκτωβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/1023/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
