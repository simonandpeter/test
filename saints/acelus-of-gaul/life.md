# Acelus

saint.gr names him in a single sentence and gives no life. The holy martyr Acelus, it says, was a subdeacon, and was martyred in Gaul in the year 303, under the emperor Diocletian, whose reign it gives as 284 to 305, together with the holy hieromartyr [Acacius, or Asius](/saints/acacius-of-gaul). That is the whole of the notice: no birthplace, no city in Gaul, no manner of death, no relic and no hymn.

His companion's page on the same day says as little, and says it without him: Acacius or Asius was a deacon and was martyred in Gaul in 303 under Diocletian. So the two notices between them give one martyrdom, two orders of the clergy and one year, and each is the other's only corroboration.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1446/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
