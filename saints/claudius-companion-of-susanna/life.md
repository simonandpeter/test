# Claudius, companion of Susanna

Claudius was a dignitary and kinsman of the emperor Diocletian, sent by him to the presbyter [Gabinius](/saints/gabinius-the-presbyter) to arrange the marriage of his daughter [Susanna](/saints/susanna-of-rome) to the co-ruler Maximian. After talking with the devout family he received baptism, and so did his brother [Maximus](/saints/maximus-companion-of-susanna), sent after him, his wife [Praepedigna](/saints/praepedigna-of-rome) and his sons [Alexander](/saints/alexander-companion-of-susanna) and [Cuphius](/saints/cuphius-of-rome). Learning that a whole family of his relatives had turned Christian, Diocletian sent them into exile; soon they were burned at Ostia near Rome and their ashes thrown into the sea. The Prologue tells the same, the executioners taking Claudius and Maximus with their households to Ostia.
