# Matronianus, brother of Hieron

saint.gr's calendar for 7 Νοεμβρίου keeps Matronianus and Antonius in an entry of their own, and the whole of its notice is one sentence: they were brothers of the holy martyr Hieron — the first of the thirty-three martyrs «ἐν Μελιτινῇ», whom the same day keeps — and they too were martyred by beheading in the city of Melitene.

The page of [the thirty-three](/saints/thirty-three-martyrs-of-melitene) tells the rest of what is said of them anywhere that day: Hieron was of Tyana in Cappadocia, his father died early, and their mother Stratonike brought up him and his two brothers, Matronianus and Antonius, alone. When the persecution began under Diocletian the eparch Agricolas arrested Hieron for preaching Christ to the labourers on Sundays and feasts; his two brothers were taken with him, and thirty more of his fellow-workers in the service of the Gospel. The names of the thirty-three the page enumerates do not include the brothers.

There is no year on their own page, no emperor, no manner of trial beyond the beheading, no distich and no hymn. The other brother kept with him is [Antonius](/saints/antonius-brother-of-hieron).

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2933/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
