# Artemidorus (26 October)

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Οκτωβρίου names Artemidorus and Basil together and gives them no life. One sentence is the whole of the narrative: «Οι Άγιοι Αρτεμίδωρος και Βασίλειος μαρτύρησαν διά ξίφους» — they were martyred by the sword. The couplet over the entry says that Artemidorus, whose sentence of death was the sword, had [Basil](/saints/basil-companion-of-artemidorus) for a fellow-contestant towards that sword.

The page gives them no country, no city, no year, no emperor and no trades; it prints no hymn and no relics. The day they stand on is the day of Demetrius of Thessalonica, and the page does not connect them with him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2651/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
