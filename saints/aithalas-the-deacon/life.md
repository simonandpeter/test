# Aithalas the Deacon

The martyr Aithalas, a deacon, was stoned to death for confessing the name of Christ in 380, by order of the Persian king Shapur II. That is the whole of the Sretensky calendar’s life; the Greek synaxarion keeps Aithalas with Ammoun on 2 September.

*After the life printed by the Sretensky calendar (days.pravoslavie.ru) — [the entry](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1427.htm); read 23 August 2026.*
