# Demetrius (Kiranov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him in one line for two men for 22 декабря ст. ст.: «Сщмчч. Димитрия и Феодора пресвитеров (1938).» The line declines the forename to «Димитрия» and gives no surname, no place, no year of birth and no word of narrative. The rank word «Сщмчч.» that governs both names and the year the line closes with, 1938, are the whole of what it says of him.

Behind that «Димитрия» stands the calendar's own name page 9310, which carries the fuller form «Димитрий (Киранов), протоиерей, сщмч.» — the surname the day's line leaves out, and an archpriest's grade. That page lists two days: this 22 декабря and «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. It carries no life, and neither does the day page.

So this folder says what the calendar says and no more: a Russian archpriest of that name, who died a martyr in 1938 and whom this calendar keeps on this day with one other priest.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 22 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261222.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9310.html), which carries no life; read 13 October 2026.*
