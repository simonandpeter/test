# Averky (Severovostokov)

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 17 июня ст. ст. prints him in one line of four words and a year: «Сщмч. Аверкия пресвитера (1918).». The line gives the forename in the genitive, the grade-class word «Сщмч.» and the rank «пресвитера», and no surname. The name link behind his place in it supplies the calendar’s own nominative with the surname, «Аверкий (Северовостоков), иерей, сщмч.», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from.

The name page carries nothing else. It heads «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 17 июня» and then stops: there is no life, no article, no place of service, no account of his death, and no icon. The calendar’s line and that heading are the whole of what this source gives him, and nothing is supplied for them here — not a diocese, not an age, and not the manner of his death in 1918.

The line’s «Сщмч.» makes him a hieromartyr and «пресвитера» a presbyter, which the name link repeats as «иерей». The second day the name page lists is the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which is a synaxis and not a second feast, so he renders on 17 июня ст. ст. only. The three martyrs the day’s first line names are of another century and another country, and he is given no relation to them.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 17 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260617.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9415.html); read 9 October 2026.*
