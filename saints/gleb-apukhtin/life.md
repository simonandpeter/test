# Gleb (Apukhtin)

The Russian calendar names the priest Gleb (Apukhtin) on 10 September of the old style, in the one line the day keeps for its seventeen new martyrs. The calendar links no written life, so this entry records the line and nothing more.

*After the Православный церковный календарь (days.pravoslavie.ru), [10 сентября ст. ст.](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260910.html); read 30 August 2026.*
