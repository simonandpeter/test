# Barlaam (Nikolsky)

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar for 6 ноября ст. ст. carries eleven names on one line and his is the seventh of them: «Сщмч. Никиты , еп. Орехово-Зуевского, Анатолия , Арсения , Николая , Николая , Константина пресвитеров., прмчч. Варлаама и Гавриила , Гавриила , прмцц. Нины и Серафимы (1937).» The line sorts the eleven into four classes and gives each class one rank word: «Сщмч.» for the bishop and the five presbyters, «прмчч.» for the abbot and the two hieromonks, «прмцц.» for the two nuns, and the single year 1937 for all of them. The name link behind his place in it supplies the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line leaves out, «Варлаам (Никольский), игумен, сщмч.», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from.

That is everything the calendar records of him. He has no life page, no icon, no hymn, no place, no age and no parish anywhere on this calendar, and the name page behind his place on the line carries its title, two days and no text at all. That page also lists «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which is a synaxis and not a second feast; this folder renders on 6 ноября ст. ст. only. Nothing further is written here, and in particular nothing is reasoned from the surname.

**The line and the name page do not give him the same class.** The line puts him among the «прмчч.», the venerable martyrs, with the two hieromonks after him; his own name page calls him «сщмч.», a hieromartyr. The name page is the reading about this man rather than about a group of three, so `hieromartyr` stands in his types with the «игумен» of the same page, abbot — and **no `office` is written**, because the grade is the thing in dispute. Both readings are recorded here and neither is chosen.

He is given no relation to the ten others the line names. They share a line, a year and the classes the line sorts them into, and **the one life page this entry has does not say that any of the other ten died with its own subject** or knew him; a shared day is not a relation here, and a line of men sharing a line, a grade and a year is not a company unless a page says they died together.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 6 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261106.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9187.html); read 4 October 2026.*
