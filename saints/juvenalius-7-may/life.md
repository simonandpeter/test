# Juvenalius (7 May)

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives one sentence: the holy martyr Juvenalius was martyred in the year 132 after Christ, in the reign of the emperor Hadrian, who held the empire from 117 to 138. The heading over it prints «Άγιος Ιουβενάλιος ο Μάρτυρας (; - 132)» — the year of his death, and a question mark where the year of his birth would stand.

That is the whole of the record here. There is no country, no city, no judge, no manner of death, no relics and no hymn, and the page prints no couplet for him as it does for most of the martyrs of this day. Nothing is claimed beyond the name, the rank, the year, the reign and the day.
