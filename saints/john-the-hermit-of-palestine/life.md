# John the Hermit of Palestine

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 19 июня ст. ст. names him in four words and a century, «Прп. Иоанна отшельника (VI).», and gives him a life page of its own. He laboured in Palestine, it says, and passed his days in fasting and prayer in a cave not far from Jerusalem. Owning nothing, he had only an image of the Most Holy Mother of God, and before it he always kept a lamp burning.

The old man went often to the holy places of Jerusalem and to the mountain of Sinai, and walked out to pray at the burial places of the martyrs and the ascetics. Going away he would leave the lamp lit before the image of the Queen of Heaven and ask a blessing on the road; and coming back after a month, or even after six, he found the lamp still burning and still full of oil. Once he had to go along a narrow path fenced on both sides, so that two walkers could not pass one another, and a lion came towards him; the beast stood up on its hind legs and left the way clear for him.

Once a monk came to his cave and, finding in it not even what is needed to live, asked the abba why he lived in such poverty. The old man answered that his cave held a spiritual wealth which is beyond price against the goods of the earth. He departed in the sixth century in great old age, the page says, and was numbered by the Church among the saints.

He is not the Cretan hermit of the ninety-nine ascetics whom the Greek calendar keeps in October and whom this corpus holds in his own folder: that man laboured on Crete, and the book here puts this one in a cave near Jerusalem and in the sixth century. The name link behind his place in the day line carries the string «Иоанн Отшельник. Палестинский, прп.», with the source’s own full stop in the middle of it, and the form stored in this folder is the one his life page’s title gives.
