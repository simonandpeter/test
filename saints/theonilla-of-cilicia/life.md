# Theonilla of Cilicia

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names her last in a company of four for 29 октября ст. ст. — «Мчч. Клавдия, Астерия, Неона и Феониллы (285)» — and the name link behind her forename adds the province the line leaves out: «Феонилла Киликийская, мц.», a martyress of Cilicia. No life page follows any of the four names. What her name page carries instead is two icons and the opening paragraph of an article, and that paragraph is the only narrative any page read here gives the four of them.

It makes them **four children of one house**. Their stepmother wished to be rid of them so as to have the family's wealth, and so told Lysias, the governor of Cilicia, that they were Christians. He questioned them and tried in vain to make them renounce Christ; and then, the article says, he decided to take their wealth himself, and for that reason put them with particular cruelty to tortures of several kinds. The teaser stops there, and the folder stops with it.

The other three of the company this corpus already held, from the Greek calendar's 30 October, which sets the martyrdom in the years of Diocletian and of the same Lysias, governor of Cilicia, and dates it about 288 where this line prints 285. [Claudius](/saints/claudius-of-laranda), [Asterius](/saints/asterius-of-laranda) and [Neon](/saints/neon-of-laranda) carry the Greek year; this folder carries only the Russian one, because the Russian page is the only one read for her. Two small differences in the names are worth writing down and are not resolved: the article heads the four «Святые мученики Астерий, Клавдий, Неон и Неонилла», spelling her name with an N where the calendar's own link spells it with the Ф this folder stores; and the other three carry the by-name of Laranda off the Greek page, where the Russian calls all four Cilician.

The second Russian calendar, azbyka.ru, which has given this corpus a full life for others whom the Сретенский calendar names in four words, answered 403 at the first request on the day she was read, having served three pages the day before; so it is a quota and the quota was spent.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 29 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261029.html) and [her name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/2726.html); read 4 October 2026.*
