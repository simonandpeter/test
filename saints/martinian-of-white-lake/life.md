# Martinian of White Lake

Martinian of White Lake was Michael in the world, and was born, saint.gr says, in the year 1370 in the village the page spells Berezniko. At thirteen he left his parents and went secretly to the venerable Cyril of White Lake, whom the calendar keeps on 9 June.

The young Martinian set himself with zeal to imitate his teacher, and gave him complete obedience. In the monastery he learned reading and writing, and with Cyril's blessing he took up the copying of books; after a time he was ordained deacon. Later, yielding to the insistent asking of the brethren of his monastery, he consented to become their abbot.

Martinian fell asleep at the age of eighty-five, and his relics, the page adds, were discovered in the year 1514, which the Greek calendar keeps on 7 October. The page's header dates him 1397 to 1483, which does not square with the 1370 its own text gives for his birth; both readings are the page's, and neither is corrected here. There is no hymn.
