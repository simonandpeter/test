# Martinian of White Lake

Martinian of White Lake was Michael in the world, and was born, saint.gr says, in the year 1370 in the village the page spells Berezniko. At thirteen he left his parents and went secretly to the venerable Cyril of White Lake, whom the calendar keeps on 9 June.

The young Martinian set himself with zeal to imitate his teacher, and gave him complete obedience. In the monastery he learned reading and writing, and with Cyril's blessing he took up the copying of books; after a time he was ordained deacon. Later, yielding to the insistent asking of the brethren of his monastery, he consented to become their abbot.

Martinian fell asleep at the age of eighty-five, and his relics, the page adds, were discovered in the year 1514, which the Greek calendar keeps on 7 October. The page's header dates him 1397 to 1483, which does not square with the 1370 its own text gives for his birth; both readings are the page's, and neither is corrected here. There is no hymn.

The Russian calendar keeps the finding of his relics on its own 7 October, «Обретение мощей прп. Мартиниана Белоезерского (1514)», and his repose on 12 January, which is the day the Greek keeps him on; its index names him «Мартиниан Белозерский, игумен». Its life agrees with the Greek that he was Michael in the world and born in 1370, and adds the village: Berezniki, not far from the Kirillov monastery. At thirteen he left his parents and came in secret to [Cyril of the White Lake](/saints/cyril-of-the-white-lake), of whom he had heard much as a great ascetic, and set himself to imitate his teacher, living under him in perfect obedience. In the monastery he learned his letters and, with Cyril's blessing, copied books; in time he was ordained hierodeacon and then hieromonk.

After Cyril's death in 1427 he withdrew for silence to an uninhabited island on Lake Vozhe, where a few monks gathered to him; he built them a church of the Transfiguration of the Lord and gave them the coenobitic rule. Yielding to the pressing requests of the brethren of the monastery of [Therapon](/saints/therapon-of-the-white-lake) he agreed to be its abbot and brought it to a flourishing state. He gave spiritual support to the grand prince Vasily Vasilyevich the Dark in his hard time, when his cousin Dmitry Shemyaka was claiming the Moscow throne unjustly, and the page calls him always a champion of truth and justice. Later, at the grand prince's insistence, he took on the government of the monastery of [Sergius of Radonezh](/saints/sergius-of-radonezh).

In 1455 he came back to the Ferapontov monastery. His last years were a heavy illness in which he could not walk and the brethren carried him into the church, and he fell asleep at the age of eighty-five. His relics were found in 1514, and that finding is what the calendar keeps on 7 October. Eighty-five years from a birth in 1370 is about 1455, the year he returned, and the Russian page names no year of death at all, where saint.gr's header prints 1483; the dates on this folder are the Greek page's and the disagreement is recorded on the Russian row rather than settled. The day also prints four hymns for him, a troparion and a kontakion and another of each.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1402/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026; and the Russian church calendar's [7 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261007.html) with [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/1490.html) and [the life it reaches](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life200.htm), read 4 October 2026.*
