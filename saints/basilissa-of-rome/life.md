# Basilissa of Rome, with Anastasia

Basilissa and [Anastasia](/saints/anastasia-of-rome-15-april) were of the great city of Rome, well-born and rich, and disciples of the holy Apostles. When the Apostles had been put to death the two women came by night, gathered up their holy relics and buried them; they were denounced for it to the emperor Nero, who put them in prison first of all.

When they were brought out and said that they remained in the confession of Christ, they were hung up, and their breasts and their hands and their feet and their tongues were cut off; last of all their heads were struck off with the sword.

That is the whole of the notice, which the Romanian calendar prints twice over, once under each woman's name. It carries no date but the name of Nero and no troparion. Two other women of this name are kept in this corpus — Basilissa of Nicomedia, the child martyred under Diocletian, and Basilissa of Sirmium — and neither is this Roman woman.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 15 aprilie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/15-aprilie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfintei-mucenite-vasilisa); read 20 September 2026.*
