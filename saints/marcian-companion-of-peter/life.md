# Marcian, companion of Peter

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαρτίου prints one line for a company of five and their companions: «Άγιοι Πέτρος, Μαρκιανός, Ιωάννης, Θέκλα, Κασσιανός οι Μάρτυρες και οι συν αυτών μαρτυρήσαντες» — the holy martyrs Peter, Marcian, John, Thekla and Cassian, and those who were martyred with them. Marcian is named second. The page behind the line says two things and stops.

The first is where they suffered: «Οι Άγιοι αυτοί Μάρτυρες Μαρτύρησαν στην Ρώμη» — these holy martyrs were martyred at Rome. The second is that nothing more is known: «Δεν έχουμε περισσότερες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο και το μαρτύριο των Αγίων». No year, no emperor, no judge, no manner of death, no relic, no hymn, and nothing that belongs to Marcian apart from the others.

He is recorded here as one of the five the source names, with [Peter](/saints/peter-companion-of-marcian), [John](/saints/john-companion-of-peter), [Thekla](/saints/thekla-companion-of-peter) and [Cassian](/saints/cassian-companion-of-peter). The unnamed companions of the line have no folder. The page's notes on the name — that it comes from Μάρκος, and that a Greek geographer and a Byzantine emperor bore it — are about the word and not about the martyr.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3869/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
