# Stephen (21 October)

saint.gr's calendar for 21 Οκτωβρίου names four martyrs in one line — Andrew, Stephen, [Paul](/saints/paul-companion-of-stephen) and [Peter](/saints/peter-companion-of-stephen) — and says that in the Synaxaristes they are called newly-appeared saints, and that they were martyred under Constantine Copronymus. Of the four, the page says, Andrew was a monk from Crete who rebuked Copronymus to his face, was beheaded, and was buried «in the Judgement»; and it sends the reader to 17 October for him.

The remaining three, it says, were most likely monks as well, and after they had defended the sacred canons of the Orthodox with vigour they were beheaded by Copronymus. That is the whole of their story on the page: no monastery, no city, no year inside the reign, and no hymn. The couplet over the entry is addressed to the Protomartyr Stephen and tells him he has three crown-bearers of his own, who contended together under one sword.

The Andrew of this line is the man the corpus already keeps as [Andrew the Monk-Martyr of Crete](/saints/andrew-the-monk-martyr-of-crete), whose day in both the Romanian and the Greek reckoning is 17 October.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 21 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/21/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2560/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
