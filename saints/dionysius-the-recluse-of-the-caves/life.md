# Dionysius the Recluse of the Caves

Dionysius is mentioned briefly, the Greek synaxarion says, in the Patericon of the Lavra of the Caves. At Pascha in 1463 he went to the place where the relics of the fathers of the Caves lay and cried out: holy fathers and brethren, today is a great day, Christ is risen; and he heard a voice like thunder from the fathers who had fallen asleep answer, truly he is risen. From then on he lived as a recluse for the rest of his life, and after many struggles fell asleep in peace. The miracle is recalled in the eighth ode of the canon to the saints of the Kyiv Caves. The Greek calendar keeps him on 3 October; whether the Russian calendar keeps him, and on which day, is for its own pages to say.

The Russian church calendar keeps him on the Julian 3 October and gives the fifteenth century where the Greek gives the one dated event of his life, the Pascha of 1463 he spent in the caves; the century contains the year. Its index of names carries more of him than the Greek does — «Дионисий Печерский, Щепа, иеромонах, затворник» — a byname, Shchepa, and a priesthood: he was a hieromonk as well as a recluse. A second day is listed there too, «28 августа (Печер.(Д))», which the abbreviation marks as one of the Caves synaxes.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 3 Οκτωβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2606/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026; the Russian church calendar keeps him on the Julian 3 October — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261003.html); read 4 October 2026.*
