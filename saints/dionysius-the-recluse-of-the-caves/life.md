# Dionysius the Recluse of the Caves

Dionysius is mentioned briefly, the Greek synaxarion says, in the Patericon of the Lavra of the Caves. At Pascha in 1463 he went to the place where the relics of the fathers of the Caves lay and cried out: holy fathers and brethren, today is a great day, Christ is risen; and he heard a voice like thunder from the fathers who had fallen asleep answer, truly he is risen. From then on he lived as a recluse for the rest of his life, and after many struggles fell asleep in peace. The miracle is recalled in the eighth ode of the canon to the saints of the Kyiv Caves. The Greek calendar keeps him on 3 October; whether the Russian calendar keeps him, and on which day, is for its own pages to say.

The Serbian church's Ohrid Prologue keeps him third of the three it enumerates on this day and tells the same Pascha of 1463, in the same words: he was going round the caves with cross and censer to incense the relics and the graves of the fathers who had fallen asleep, and being filled with the joy of the Resurrection he cried, holy fathers and brethren, Christ is risen — and at that moment a voice came out of the graves, strong as thunder: truly he is risen.

It adds his rank, which the Greek heading leaves out: «Преп. Дионисије Печерски Јеромонах и затворник», hieromonk and recluse. That is the one thing it carries that the other source does not, and the type is added on its word.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 3 Οκτωβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2606/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026; and the Serbian church's [Ohrid Prologue for the Julian 3 October](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-10-16&prolog=1), read 4 October 2026.*
