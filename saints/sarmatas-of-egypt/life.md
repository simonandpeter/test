# Sarmatas of Egypt

Sarmatas was an ascetic of the desert who died in peace. Nikodemos the Hagiorite, whom the synaxarion quotes, says the Evergetinos records of him that, fasting always and keeping himself in check, he so mastered sleep that if he said to it, go away from me, it went, and if he said, come, it came. The Paradise of the Fathers has a man sitting in stillness ask him: my thoughts tell me, go out and visit the brothers; and Sarmatas answered: do not listen to them — say, I listened to you the first time, now I cannot. Another said: my thoughts tell me, do not work, but eat, drink and sleep; and the elder answered him too.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 30 Αυγούστου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/845/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
