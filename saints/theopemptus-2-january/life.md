# Theopemptus (2 January)

saint.gr's calendar for 2 Ιανουαρίου names Theopemptus and prints one sentence of narrative about him: the holy Theopemptus departed this life in peace. Everything else on the page is about the name rather than the man — Θεόπεμπτος, from Θεοῦ and πέμπομαι, the one sent by God — and a two-line epigram, which says that fiery Minds came, God having sent them, to loose Theopemptus from the fetter of the flesh.

The source records no see, no place, no companion and no year, and none is added here. He is not the bishop of Nicomedia whom the same calendars keep on 5 January; this entry stands on its own day with its own single line.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 2 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/02/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/16/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
