# Mark (20 May)

saint.gr names him and gives him no life to speak of. Its whole account is three sentences: the venerable Mark was a monk; the place he came from and the time when he was active are unknown; and having lived in holiness, he fell asleep in peace. The page adds no country, no monastery, no teacher, no miracle and no relics, and prints no hymn to him. It is one of the barest entries on a day that otherwise runs to four hymns for a single martyr.

What stands beside those three sentences is the couplet of the synaxarion, which gives him a garden for a garden: «Δίδωσι Mάρκω της Eδέμ κήπον μέγαν, O κήπον ευρών εις ταφήν Xριστός πάλαι» — Christ, who once found a garden for his own burial, gives Mark the great garden of Eden. Nothing further about him is recorded here.
