# Alexander (Orlov) (14 April)

The Сретенский monastery's calendar gives him one line on the Julian 14 April and no life at all: «Св. Александра исп., пресвитера (1941).» — the holy Alexander the confessor, a presbyter, and the year 1941. The name link behind it carries more of his name than the line does, «Александр (Орлов), иерей, исп.», with a surname the line leaves out altogether and the grade of priest; the page behind that link gives his days of commemoration and nothing else.

That is the whole of what the source gives. No place, no year of birth, no parish, no arrest, no sentence and no word of his own: the bracketed year is the year of death, and the rank word «исп.», confessor, is the only thing said about how he ended. The calendar's confessors of these years are the ones who were not killed outright, and that distinction is the only reading taken from the word here; nothing else is filled in.

Two other priests of the same name and surname already stand in this corpus, both of 1937 and both on other days of this calendar, and this folder's name says which day it is kept on because that is the only thing that tells the three apart in the calendar's own words. His second day on the name page is 26 января, marked as the new martyrs', which is the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia — a commemoration of a whole company rather than a day of his own, so it is not recorded here as a feast.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 14 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260414.html), which gives him one line and no life, and [the calendar's page for his name](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9439.html); read 7 October 2026.*
