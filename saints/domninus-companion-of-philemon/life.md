# Domninus, companion of Philemon

saint.gr's calendar for 21 Μαρτίου names two men together, [Philemon](/saints/philemon-companion-of-domninus) and Domninus, and gives each of them a heading of his own. They came from Rome, the page says — though it adds that other sources give Thessalonica as the place they came from, and that they left it for Rome — and they lived in the years of the great and hard struggle of idolatry against Christianity.

With the flame of their zeal for Christ they went about various cities of Italy preaching the eternal word of the salvation of souls. They were arrested, and after they had been stripped and stretched out on the ground from four sides they were beaten without mercy and then imprisoned. But their devotion to the Christian faith went on whole and undiminished, and so, under the swords of the executioners, they were counted worthy of the martyr's crown.

That is the whole of what has been read. The page gives no emperor, no year, no city of their death and no relics, and prints no hymn under either name; the couplet over the two calls Domninus his friend. Nothing has been taken from any other source and nothing supplied from memory, and nothing on the page identifies him with the Domninus of Thessalonica the corpus keeps on 1 octombrie.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 21 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/21/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3334/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
