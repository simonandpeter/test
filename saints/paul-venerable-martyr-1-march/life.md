# Paul (1 March)

saint.gr names the venerable martyr Paul on this day and gives him one sentence, in which the site both says what it has and marks how far it will go: «Άγνωστο που και πότε μαρτύρησε, μάλλον όμως επί των εικονομάχων, αφού οι Συναξαριστές σημειώνουν ότι υπέρ των αγίων εικόνων δια του πυρός ετελειώθη» — it is unknown where and when he was martyred, but rather under the iconoclasts, since the synaxarists note that he was perfected by fire for the holy icons.

So two things are recorded and one is only guessed at. What the synaxarists give is the cause and the manner: he died by fire, and he died for the holy icons. The century the site puts him in it puts with the word «μάλλον», rather, reasoning back from that cause to the iconoclast persecutions; because the reasoning is the site's and it says so, no date is written into this folder at all.

There is no place, no emperor, no judge, no monastery, no relic, no distich and no hymn. Of the many Pauls the corpus keeps, none stands on this day and none is named on this page.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4092/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
