# Pausolypius, companion of Theodore

saint.gr gives him one sentence, shared with his companion. The holy martyr Pausolypius and the holy hieromartyr [Theodore](/saints/theodore-companion-of-pausolypius) the presbyter, it says, were made perfect by the sword in Thrace in the years of the emperor Hadrian, which it dates 117 to 138; and their synaxis used to be kept at the old people's home of Melobios. Nothing else is printed of him: not his station, not his country, not his age, and no year of his own.

His name means the ending of grief, and the page's couplet turns on it: «Παυσολυπίῳ καὶ Θεοδώρῳ θύτῃ, Δῶρόν τι παυσίλυπον ὄντως τὸ ξίφος» — to Pausolypius and to Theodore the sacrificer the sword was truly a gift that put an end to grief. The page prints no hymn for him.
