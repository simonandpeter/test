# Nicholas (Vasyukovich)

Nicholas Vasyukovich was born on 28 March 1882, the son of the priest of St Anna's church at Koski in the Minsk district. After church school he taught in the people's schools, then served as reader at the church of the Nativity of the Theotokos in Litvyany, and continued at the same church after his ordination deacon. When the Bolsheviks burned that church down in the night in 1930 he wept and said what he thought of a power in which such men held power; when the loan subscriptions were forced on the people in 1936 he refused the worthless tickets - "I will not sign; I have no reason to strengthen this regime."

He was arrested on 6 August 1937 and said little at his interrogation; unable to make him renounce his orders, they sentenced him to be shot, and he was killed in Minsk on 26 September 1937 - the very day the calendar now keeps him, with the twenty-two other new hieromartyrs of the Belarusian land whose common life the calendar links. They were glorified as local saints of the Minsk diocese in 1999, and for the whole Church among the New Martyrs and Confessors of Russia in 2000.

*After the Православный церковный календарь (days.pravoslavie.ru), [Новомученики, в земле Белорусской просиявшие](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4766.htm); read 30 August 2026.*
