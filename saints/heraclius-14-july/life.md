# Heraclius (14 July)

saint.gr's calendar for 14 July names «Άγιος Ηράκλειος» and tells one thing about him: he was beaten and put to death with clubs. The page adds the couplet of the Synaxarion, which plays on the name — Heraclius will soon boast of the beatings from clubs as Heracles boasted of his club. No year, no place, no judge and no emperor is given for him, and the page prints no hymn.

The notice sits under the site's note on the name itself, which remarks that Heraclius was also the name of the famous emperor of Byzantium who beat the Avars and the Persians and raised at Jerusalem, on 14 September 622, the Cross they had carried off. That is the site's observation about the name and not a statement about this martyr, and none of it is recorded here as his.
