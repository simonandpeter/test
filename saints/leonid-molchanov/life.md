# Leonid (Molchanov)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him in four words and gives no life. Its line for 31 октября ст. ст. reads «Прмч. Леонида (1918)» — a rank word, a forename in the genitive and a year — and the name link behind the line adds the surname the line leaves out and the office it does not: «Леонид (Молчанов), игумен, прмч.», an abbot of that name, one of the new martyrs of the Russian church. The abbreviation on the line is the one the calendar uses for a monastic put to death.

So the year is the whole of what is recorded, and the year is 1918, the first of the two deaths of these years that this day keeps. There is no life page behind the line, and the name page carries no text, no icon and no hymn — only his name and his days, of which the second is 26 января, the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, where he is commemorated with the rest of that company rather than alone.

The second Russian calendar, azbyka.ru, which has given this corpus a full life and its archival references for others of these men, refused the request with a 403 on the day he was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 31 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261031.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14342.html); read 4 October 2026.*
