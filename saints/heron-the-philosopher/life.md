# Heron the Philosopher

Heron was a Christian philosopher. That is nearly the whole of what the Ohrid Prologue records of him: Gregory the Theologian mentions him in his writings, and he reposed in peace.

The Prologue gives neither dates nor a place, and this entry does not supply them. The Serbian calendar keeps his memory on 10 August (Julian), in second place after Lawrence the Archdeacon and Sixtus of Rome.
