# Anthousa the New

Anthousa the New was martyred: they put a rag of haircloth on her and a great stone about her neck and threw her into a well. The synaxarion’s verse says as much — a cloak of felt, a clasp of stone, and with them you went down, Anthousa, into the deep well. Nothing more is recorded.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 27 August — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/909/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
