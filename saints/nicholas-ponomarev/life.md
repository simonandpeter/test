# Nicholas (Ponomarev)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Nicholas among the hieromartyrs on 28 July and gives no life. Its line is «Сщмч. Николая диакона (1918).» — the hieromartyr Nicholas the deacon, 1918 — and the calendar's own name page for him adds the surname and keeps the grade the line gives: «Николай (Пономарев), диакон, сщмч.», Nicholas Ponomarev, deacon.

That is the whole of the record. He served as a deacon and not as a priest; he died in 1918; and the calendar keeps him on this day among the new martyrs of Russia, between a bishop of the seventeenth century and a company of eight who died in 1937.

The day page is a long one, with twenty troparia and kontakia, fourteen of them for its icons, and none under his name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 28 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260728.html); read 13 October 2026.*
