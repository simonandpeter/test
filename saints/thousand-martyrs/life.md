# The Thousand Martyrs

The Greek synaxarion says only that the thousand martyrs were martyred by the sword; its verse calls them a phalanx a thousand strong of the beheaded, who won crowns beyond number. It names none of them and gives no date and no place.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 30 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/1016/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
