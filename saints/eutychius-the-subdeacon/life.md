# Eutychius the Subdeacon

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαρτίου names the holy martyr Eutychius the subdeacon, and the page behind the line is one sentence: he was a subdeacon in the church of the Alexandrians and a champion of the dogmas of the First Ecumenical Council at Nicaea; he was greatly honoured by [Athanasius the Great](/saints/athanasius-of-alexandria); and he was martyred by the Arian heretics and by the heretical bishop George, in the year 356.

That sentence places him exactly in the years when Athanasius was driven from Alexandria and George of Cappadocia was set in his place, and it is the whole of what the source says. The page gives no birthplace, no parents, no account of the arrest, no manner of death, no relic and no hymn, and it heads him «(; - 356)», leaving his year of birth a question mark.

The name it glosses only as a word, «ΕΥΤΥΧΙΟΣ (από το ευτυχής) = ο μακάριος», the blessed, the fortunate. The corpus keeps no rank slug for a subdeacon, so that office is recorded in this prose and not in the folder's fields; the epithet stays in his name because the calendar's own line carries it.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3870/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
