# Romanus of Macedonia

saint.gr keeps a bishop Romanus of Macedonia on 28 November, and the whole of what it prints about him is an account of where the memory is not. His commemoration, it says, is not mentioned by the Synaxarist of Saint Nikodemos, nor by that of Delehaye either: «Η μνήμη του δεν αναφέρεται από τον Συναξαριστή του Αγίου Νικόδημου, αλλά ούτε και απ' αυτόν του Delehaye». It is found in the Parisian Codex 1578, as bishop of Macedonia.

That is all. No country, no city, no century, no emperor, no manner of death and no burial; no distich, and no hymn. The page does not say which part of Macedonia, nor whether he was a martyr or died in peace, and nothing of the kind is written here. What the Greek calendar attests is that a bishop of that name in Macedonia is kept on this day on the authority of one manuscript.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/28/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3105/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
