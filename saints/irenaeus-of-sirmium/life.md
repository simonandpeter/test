# Irenaeus of Sirmium

Irenaeus was bishop of Sirmium, the capital of Pannonia — a pure and active pastor, the synaxarion says, full of sincere love for his flock and ready to give his life for it, after the Good Shepherd.

He was denounced to the governor of the city for drawing pagans to the religion of Jesus, and was arrested. When the governor Probus asked him whether the charge was true, he prayed in front of him instead of answering. The governor had him imprisoned and then scourged, and at last beheaded; his head was thrown into the river Sava. The synaxarion gives the year as 288.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 23 August — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/855/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
