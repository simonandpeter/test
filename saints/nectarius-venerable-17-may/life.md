# Nectarius (17 May)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 17 mai reads «Sfântul Cuvios Nectarie» — the holy venerable Nectarius — with no epithet, no monastery, no country and no century, and his own page on the site carries only that line, the date, and the day's scripture readings. There is no Viață tab and no Tropar tab; the page is a heading and nothing more.

The display name here carries the day, because the calendar gives nothing else to tell him by, and the corpus already keeps men of that name. He is not to be taken for Nectarios of Aegina, who is a twentieth-century Greek hierarch kept in November; the only thing said of this Nectarius is the rank word «Cuvios», that he was a monastic. No other calendar was checked for him.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 17 mai — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/17-mai) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-nectarie-0), which carries no life and no troparion; read 24 September 2026.*
