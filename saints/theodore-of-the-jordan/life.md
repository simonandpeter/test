# Theodore of the Jordan

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery gives him four sentences and no year. It says he lived in the sixth century; that in his youth he left the world, took the monastic habit and withdrew into the wilderness of the Jordan; and that he was counted worthy by God of the gift of working wonders. That is the whole of what the calendar knows of his life, and this folder adds nothing to it.

It tells one wonder. Travelling once by ship to Constantinople, he asked of the Lord that water drawn out of the sea to quench his fellow-travellers' thirst should become fresh, and it did. To those who thanked him for it he said that God had worked such a wonder out of pity for people tormented by thirst, and not for his own unworthy prayer — «а не по его недостойной молитве». The calendar names no monastery, no companion and no place but the Jordan and the city he was sailing to.
