# Sergius (Uvitsky)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him for 27 февраля ст. ст. in five words — «Сщмч. Сергия пресвитера (1932).» — a hieromartyr, a presbyter, who died in 1932. The name link behind the line carries the calendar's own nominative, with the surname the line leaves out and with the word for his order: «Сергий (Увицкий), иерей, сщмч.», a priest of that name, one of the new martyrs of the Russian church in the Soviet years.

The calendar prints no life page for him and no hymn under his name; the troparia it sets on this day belong to three of the older saints it heads. Nothing further is written here. No parish, no diocese, no place and no manner of death is recorded, because the calendar gives none of them, and nothing is reasoned out from the surname or from the year.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 27 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260227.html); read 3 October 2026.*
