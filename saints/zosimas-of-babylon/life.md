# Zosimas of Babylon

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery gives him a short reading and places him by stages rather than by years. He was born in Cilicia, in Asia Minor, and left the world while still young to settle on the mountain of Sinai; from there he went on to a more solitary place, in Libya. There, the account says, he once met an elder and ascetic who foretold to him that he would be a bishop in Babylon.

When he returned to Sinai the superior sent him on an errand to Alexandria, and the patriarch of Alexandria made him bishop in Babylon — the Egyptian Babylon, as the calendar's own line for the day says. He governed his flock there wisely into deep old age; and feeling death draw near he went back to Sinai, and there departed to God in peace, in the sixth century. The calendar gives no year, for his birth or his death, and names no other person in the whole of the reading.
