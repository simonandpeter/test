# Cyril Phileotes

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 2 decembrie reads «Sfântul Chiril Fileotul» — and the page that stands under his name carries neither a Viață tab nor a Tropar tab. The line gives no rank at all beyond «Sfântul», so this folder carries none: nothing is said there of whether he was monk, priest or layman, of where he lived or of when.

The epithet is kept in the English form the way the Romanian prints it, and nothing else about him is written here.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 2 December, and saint.gr gives the life the Romanian line had none of. He was born in 1015 in the village of Philea, in the province of Derkoi in Thrace; at holy baptism he was named Kyriakos, Kyrillos being afterwards his monastic name, and from a child he was distinguished in sacred letters.

In his twentieth year he married and had a child. But because he had a zeal for the monastic life, as everyone of that time had, he decided to leave his wife and his child and withdraw to a monastery, saying to his wife: either let us separate, or let us both go to a monastery. His wife accepted neither, and so Cyril stayed in his own house, living ascetically there. In 1060 he founded a monastery in that village, whose church Alexios Komnenos raised; and so Cyril lived in holiness and fell asleep in peace in 1110.
