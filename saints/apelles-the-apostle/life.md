# Apelles

Apelles was among the choicest members of the Church of Christ, working very fruitfully for the Gospel; divine zeal brought him as far as Rome, where he became a support of the faithful there, and there the apostle Paul came to know him, who later wrote in the letter to the Romans to greet Apelles, approved in Christ (Romans 16:10). He died as a good soldier of Christ at Smyrna, working to his last breath for the spread and confirmation of the Gospel. The Greek calendar keeps him on 10 September with [Luke](/saints/luke-apostle-of-laodicea) and [Clement](/saints/clement-apostle-of-sardis): the divine Luke with Apelles and Clement, the verse says, apostles with the apostles.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 10 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2377/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
