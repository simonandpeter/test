# Theodore of the Dardanelles

saint.gr says the new martyr Theodore was born in a village of the province of the Hellespont and the Troad which in Turkish was called Erenkioi, and that he was brought up by pious parents, George and Kyriake. At a young age he went to the city of Tanak Kale, the Dardanelles, where he learned and practised the craft of working sesame. Although he was barely twenty years old, the page says, God had endowed him with rich virtue.

That was noticed by a certain rich Turk of the city, who wanted to make him a Muslim and to make him his son-in-law and his heir, and who used many crafty means for it. Theodore rejected every one of his proposals. Then the Turk slandered him to the judge of the city, saying that Theodore had supposedly declared, after an illness of his, that he would become a Mohammedan if he got well. The judge asked Theodore whether all this was true.

Then the martyr answered: «Εγώ είμαι χριστιανός απ' τους γονείς μου και χριστιανός θέλω να πεθάνω. Τον Ιησού μου δεν αρνούμαι και τη μιαρή θρησκεία σας απεχθάνομαι» — I am a Christian from my parents and a Christian I wish to die; my Jesus I do not deny, and your defiled religion I abhor. At once they seized him, and he underwent many and dreadful tortures; and at last, on 2 August 1690, at three in the afternoon, they beheaded him.

In 1922, the page ends, leaving the homes of their fathers, the late priest Father Konstantinos Oikonomou with some of his countrymen carried the holy skull to Nikaia, and it is kept in the church of Hosia Xene there. The page prints six megalynaria to him and no apolytikion and no kontakion, so this folder records no hymn of his.
