# Peter (Lyubimov)

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him second of five presbyters on the one line it gives to fourteen people of 1938 for the Julian 1 March. Peter Pavlovich Lyubimov was born on 6 January 1867 in the village of Svitino in the Podolsk district of the province of Moscow, the son of the psalmist Pavel Petrovich Lyubimov. He finished the Pererva theological school in 1882 and the Moscow theological seminary in 1888, then taught until 1893 at the parish school at Vanilovo in the Bronnitsy district and until 1900 at the parish school in the village of Vertkovo in the same district.

In 1900 he became psalmist at the church of Saint Nicholas in Plotniki on the Arbat in Moscow, and on 26 September 1903 he was ordained priest and appointed rector of the Dormition church in the village of Kishkino in the Bronnitsy district. From 1903 to 1919 he taught religion in the Kishkino village school. In 1908, the old church being very decayed, he asked the bishop of Dmitrov for leave to build a new one of stone; the priest and his parishioners had it built, and it was consecrated in 1912. In 1920 he was given the pectoral cross, and afterwards raised to the rank of archpriest and given the mitre.

Through the 1920s and into the 1930s the authorities tried again and again to close the church at Kishkino by means of taxes, raising them higher and higher, and he managed to pay the sums demanded on time. In 1936 the priest serving the neighbouring village of Martynovskoye was arrested, and the warden of that church, [Nadezhda (Abbakumova)](/saints/nadezhda-abbakumova), began inviting him to serve there; from that time he had two parishes to look after, and she helped him keep her church from being closed.

The two of them were arrested together on 2 March 1938 and held in the prison at Kashira. The investigator asked him whether he knew her, and what his connection with her was, and he answered that he knew her and that the connection was the work of the church, since she was its warden; asked whether there had been talk in her lodging of a war coming soon, he said there had been no talk of war with her at all; and asked whether he admitted the charge of anti-Soviet agitation and counter-revolutionary activity, he said that he did not. On 9 March 1938 the troika of the НКВД sentenced him to be shot, and he was shot on 14 March 1938 and buried in a common unmarked grave at the Butovo range outside Moscow.

azbyka.ru keeps him on 14 марта and on two moveable days besides — the synaxis of the new martyrs who suffered at Butovo and the synaxis of the saints of Moscow — and neither is a fixed day nor an offset from Pascha this corpus can hold, so neither is recorded as a feast. On the grade the two hosts differ and the reading is the calendar’s own index: the day’s line calls the five of them «пресвитеров» together and azbyka.ru heads him «пресвитер», while the name link behind his name reads «протоиерей», archpriest, which is what the office here records and what his own life says he was raised to in 1920.
