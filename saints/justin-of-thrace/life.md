# Justin of Thrace

saint.gr's calendar for 15 November names «Άγιοι Ιουστίνος και Θεοδώρα οι ευσεβείς βασιλείς», Justin and Theodora the pious sovereigns, and then says, in its own words, that here things are a little confused: other sources put the memory of Justinian and Theodora on this day, and others that of Justin and Euphemia. The page does not resolve which woman belongs beside him, and this folder therefore records only the man it does describe.

As far as Justin is concerned, it says, he came from Thrace, and before anything else was a herdsman of sheep and swine. Afterwards he became a soldier, then a count, and at the last a king, in 518. He had for wife one Lupicia, whom he made Augusta and renamed Euphemia; and when that wife of his died he took another, named Theodora.

Justin, though unlettered, gathered up the state and set it in order, and was a very pious Christian king, helping the Church in every way he could. He reigned nine years and thirty-three days. The verse over the entry says that the Master of all called Justin the sovereign, with Theodora, to the heavens.
