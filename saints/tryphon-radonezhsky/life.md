# Tryphon (Radonezhsky)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 14 февраля ст. ст. in a line of four words: «Сщмч. Трифона диакона (1938).» The rank word is «Сщмч.», hieromartyr; «диакона» gives him as a deacon, which is the one thing the calendar says about his service; and the year the line prints for his death is 1938. The name link behind the line carries the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line leaves out, «Трифон (Радонежский), диакон».

The calendar prints no life page under his name and no hymn under it either; the troparia on the day page are for other saints it heads. Nothing further is written here. The line, the rank word, the order, the name the link behind it carries and the year 1938 are the whole of what was read, and in particular he is not identified with any saint of the same name whom the calendar keeps on another day.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 14 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260214.html); read 3 October 2026.*
