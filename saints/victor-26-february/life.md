# Victor (26 February)

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Φεβρουαρίου names him and gives no life. Its line reads «Άγιος Βίκτωρ», and the page standing under it says only that the holy Victor was martyred by the sword and that we have no other details of his life. Its couplet leaves him lying stretched on the ground without his head on his shoulders: «Bίκτωρ επ' ώμοις την κεφαλήν ουκ έχων, Kείται προ της γης εκταθείς μέγας μέγα».

Above that the page prints only its standing gloss on the name — that Victor is the Latin victor, the conqueror, and that two Byzantine iconographers bore it — which belongs to the name and not to this saint. No year, no place, no emperor, no relics and no hymn are given for him, and the calendar leaves him without an epithet to tell him apart from the other martyrs of the name; what is recorded here is the commemoration saint.gr keeps on this day, and the day is what the display name carries.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3651/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
