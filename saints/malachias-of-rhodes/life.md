# Malachias of Rhodes

Malachias was the son of a priest from Rhodes, the Greek synaxarion says. Once he went to Jerusalem, and there the Turks slandered him, saying he had cursed Muhammad. He was arrested at once and brought before the authorities, who pressed him to deny his faith. He showed great courage, which roused their anger: they flogged him, pierced his ankles and tied him behind a wild horse, and after a series of dreadful tortures he was led out of the city, impaled and burned over a fire. So he gave up his soul on 29 September 1500.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 29 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2404/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
