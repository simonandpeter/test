# Basil (Pokrovsky, 13 December)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him among the hieromartyrs on 13 декабря and gives no life. Its line is «Сщмчч. Емилиана, Василия пресвитеров (1941).» — the hieromartyr presbyters Emilian and Basil, 1941 — and the calendar’s own name page adds the surname the line leaves out: «Василий (Покровский), иерей, сщмч.»

That is the whole of the record. He was a priest; he died in 1941; and the calendar keeps him on this day beside [Emilian (Kireev)](/saints/emilian-kireev), the other presbyter of his line. The same calendar keeps another priest of the same name and the same surname on 3 ноября ст. ст., who died in 1937 and is a different man; this folder carries the day in its name so that the two are not confused. The day page carries troparia and kontakia for five of the memories it heads, and none under his name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 13 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261213.html); read 13 October 2026.*
