# Peter of Gortyna

saint.gr's calendar for 14 July names Peter, bishop of Gortyna in Crete, the new hieromartyr. Its page puts him at the beginning of the fourth century, in the last great persecutions under Diocletian, and reasons his years from the men on either side of him in the see: he must have been the successor of the hieromartyr [Cyril](/saints/cyril-of-gortyna) and the predecessor of Paul, that is from 304 to 311 or 312, when the persecutions end — because by 312 Paul is already bishop of Gortyna.

Of his life the page says there is nothing else. He was martyred «τοὺς πόδας ἐκκοπείς», his feet cut off, and the couplet set over his notice turns on that: Peter walked without stumbling, and they penalised him by taking his feet away. The service composed for him has never been published, and the page says it offers no further testimony either.

The rest of the page is about his byname. A great deal was written about why he is called the new hieromartyr, and the page calls those explanations unfounded and the cause of confusion. On the view of Archimandrite Chrysostomos Papadakis, protosyncellus of the metropolis of Gortyna and Arkadia — the view church tradition carried into all the menologia, synaxaria and menaia — the byname is there for a plain reason: he and Cyril were both hieromartyrs and both bishops of the same see, and the two had to be distinguished.

Full services for him do exist. His first church was built at the end of the 1990s, on the proposal of the same archimandrite, in the parish of Paranymphoi in that metropolis, and was consecrated by the late Metropolitan Cyril Kypriotakis.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 14 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/14/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/703/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
