# George of Maleon

George lived in the ninth century. His parents wanted to marry him, and he refused and went to the monastery on Mount Maleon in the Peloponnese, where many disciples gathered around him. He had the gift of seeing what was to come, and he foretold his own death three years before it happened. In the service sung at his commemoration he is called an earthly angel and a worker of miracles.

That is the whole of the life doxologia prints: three sentences, a century and a mountain, and no year, no emperor, no account of how he governed his disciples or how his end came. The page does carry a troparion for him, in the eighth tone.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and fills in the shape of the life. saint.gr says he loved the Lord with his whole soul from a young age, and that when his parents wanted to marry him off against his will he became a monk instead and gave himself with all his strength to every kind of ascetic labour — fasting, hardness of living, prayer and the study of the divine Scriptures. Many who ran to him were enlightened and turned back to Christ by repentance; but because his visitors were so many that they left him no quiet in which to pray, he withdrew to Mount Malaio and kept stillness there. Even there a crowd of monks gathered to him, and he guided them by prayer and asceticism.

He went so far forward in virtue, the page says, that he became famous and admired among the rulers and even among the emperors, to whom he had written many considerable letters of counsel on various matters. He foretold the end of his earthly life three years before it came. So, after a short illness, he gathered the monks of Mount Malaio, gave them divine counsels, and gave up his righteous soul to the God he had loved from infancy.

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 4 апреля ст. ст. — «Прп. Георгия , иже в Малеи (V-VI).» — and its life is three sentences. He lived in the fifth and sixth centuries, it says; he was a monk and laboured in a house on mount Малое in the Peloponnese, and died there. And it adds one thing the other two books do not: that in the service he is blessed as an earthly angel and a wonderworker.

That century is the disagreement this day turned up. The Romanian and Greek pages this folder was written from put him in the ninth century; this one puts him three or four hundred years earlier, and the line itself carries the mark «(V-VI)». The folder’s date is widened to hold both readings and chooses neither. No `locations` is recorded: the mountain and the peninsula are named and no coordinate is given. The day page prints no hymn under his name.
