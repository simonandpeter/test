# George of Maleon

George lived in the ninth century. His parents wanted to marry him, and he refused and went to the monastery on Mount Maleon in the Peloponnese, where many disciples gathered around him. He had the gift of seeing what was to come, and he foretold his own death three years before it happened. In the service sung at his commemoration he is called an earthly angel and a worker of miracles.

That is the whole of the life doxologia prints: three sentences, a century and a mountain, and no year, no emperor, no account of how he governed his disciples or how his end came. The page does carry a troparion for him, in the eighth tone.
