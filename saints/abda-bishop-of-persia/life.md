# Abda (Abdias)

Abda — Abdias or Abidas, as the calendars variously spell him — suffered in
Persia in the reign of the shah Yazdegerd I for refusing to renounce Christ
and worship the sun and fire instead. He died after many tortures, and the
life the Orthodox Church in America prints for the day says that to his last
breath he gave thanks to God for letting his chosen one die for his holy name.

The Russian calendar keeps him on 5 September (Julian), a martyr in Persia of
the fifth century, and prints no life of its own. The Greek synaxarion keeps
Abdaios, bishop of Persia, on the same day with a verse — the martyr Abdaios
bears the thorns of the rods, honouring the Master crowned with thorns — and
sends the reader on to 31 March, where his memory is repeated as Saint Abdas.
