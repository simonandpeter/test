# Clement (23 February)

saint.gr names the holy martyr Clement on this day and gives him one sentence, and the life says exactly that: «Ο Άγιος Μάρτυς Κλήμης μαρτύρησε διά ξίφους. Δεν έχουμε περισσότερες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου» — the holy martyr Clement was martyred by the sword, and we have no further details of the saint's life. No year, no country, no emperor, no judge, no relic and no hymn stand on the page.

The one thing the site adds is the distich over him, which turns on the name: Clement, the branch of the noetic vine, cut through, pours out blood like some new sweet wine — κλῆμα, a vine shoot, against Κλήμης. That is a play on words and not a fact about him, and nothing else is written here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 23 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/23/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3688/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
