# John of Valaam

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life: his page carries no Viață tab and no Tropar tab, only a few lines of its own. Saint John was born on 14 February 1873 in the village of Gubka. In June 1910 he was tonsured a monk at the Monastery of Valaam, near Saint Petersburg. With his name is bound the monastic community of Petsamo, which in 1940 joined the brotherhood of the New Valaam.

The page gives his civil name as Ivan Alekseyevich Alekseyev, and says that his numbering among the saints, under the name John of Valaam, was approved by the Synod of the Ecumenical Patriarchate in November 2018, and that the proclamation of the canonisation took place between 31 May and 2 June 2019 at the Monastery of the New Valaam in Finland, where his relics are today. He passed into eternity on 5 June 1958.

Beyond that the page prints only a set of sayings under the heading «Texte de sfânt» and the day's Epistle and Gospel; it gives no narrative of his life, no place of birth beyond the village named, and no hymn.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 5 iunie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/5-iunie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-ioan-de-valaam); read 24 September 2026.*
