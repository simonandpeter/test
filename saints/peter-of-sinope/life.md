# Peter of Sinope

saint.gr's calendar for 30 Ιουνίου names him from Sinope on the Black Sea and gives him a single sentence of life: «Ο Άγιος Πέτρος από τη Σινώπη μαρτύρησε αφού τον θανάτωσαν συρόμενο επάνω σε αιχμηρές πέτρες» — the holy Peter of Sinope was martyred after they put him to death by dragging him over sharp stones. The page gives no reign, no judge and no year, so none is recorded here.

All it adds is the distich the synaxaria set over his memory, which turns on his name and the stones that killed him: «Έχουσιν αιδείσθαί σε και θείοι νόες, / Aιδοίε Πέτρε, δόντα τας σάρκας πέτραις» — even the divine minds must reverence you, reverend Peter, who gave your flesh to the rocks.
