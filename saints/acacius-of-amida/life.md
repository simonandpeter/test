# Acacius of Amida

saint.gr's whole account of him is one sentence: «Ο Όσιος Ακάκιος εξαγόρασε 7.000 χριστιανούς αιχμαλώτους, αφού εκποίησε εκκλησιαστικά σκεύη, κατά τον Βυζαντινοπερσικό πόλεμο (421 - 422 μ.Χ.)» — the venerable Acacius bought the freedom of seven thousand Christian captives, having sold off the church's sacred vessels, during the Byzantine-Persian war of 421 to 422. The day's line adds the see: «επίσκοπος Αμίδης», bishop of Amida.

That is all the calendar has. It glosses the name — Ακάκιος, from the privative alpha and κακός, the man without malice and so the virtuous man — and stops there. It gives no birth year and no year of his death, names no emperor and no Persian king, says nothing of what became of him after the ransom, lists no relic, and prints neither couplet nor hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 9 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/09/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/213/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
