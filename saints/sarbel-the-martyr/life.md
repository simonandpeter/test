# Sarbel

Sarbel — Zarbel in some texts — was martyred by stoning; the verse says that refusing to revere a profane cult he was stoned by the hands of profane men. That is the whole of the Greek notice for 4 September; the Russian calendar keeps Sarbel on 5 September “by the Greek calendar”.

The Russian calendar keeps him on 5 September (Julian) with the mark “Gr. Cal”, from the Greek calendar, and prints no life.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 4 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2346/saint.aspx); After the day’s line of the Sretensky calendar (days.pravoslavie.ru), 5 September (Julian) — [the page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260905.html), which prints no life; read 23 August 2026.*
