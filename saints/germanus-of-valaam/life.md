# Germanus of Valaam

saint.gr's calendar for 28 Ιουνίου keeps the two of them together as wonderworkers of Valaam: «Όσιοι Σέργιος και Γερμανός οι θαυματουργοί οι εν Βαλάμη». What the page says of them is four short lines. [Sergius](/saints/sergius-of-valaam) and Germanus settled on the island of Valaam in 1329, and tried to spread Christianity there under difficult conditions; they fell asleep about 1353; and there are no other particulars of the venerable ones' lives to be had — «Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο των Οσίων».

The page's two headings do not quite agree with that. Sergius is headed «Οσιος Σεργιος ο Θαυματουργος ο Εν Βαλαμη (; - 1353)» and Germanus «Οσιος Γερμανος ο Θαυματουργος ο Εν Βαλαμη (; - 1553)», two hundred years apart, where the narrative puts the falling asleep of both about 1353. The year in the narrative is the one recorded here for each of them, because it is the one the page states of the two together and the one the settlement in 1329 agrees with.

The page records that the holy relics of Sergius are in the monastery that bears their name, and it links a translation of the relics of the two as a separate commemoration. It names no country for either man, no monastery of their tonsure and no manner of their preaching, and it prints no hymn. The epithet «ο Θαυματουργός», the wonderworker, is the only thing it gives them besides the island and the year.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/28/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1920/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
