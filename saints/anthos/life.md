# Anthos

saint.gr's calendar for 12 December names the venerable Anthos and gives him one line of life: he reposed in peace. No country, no century, no monastery, no relic and no hymn is printed, and he is joined to no other name on the day. What the page adds is the couplet set over his name, which reads the name itself through a verse of the psalms.

Saying fittingly the word of the prophet David, it runs, the divine Anthos — the flower — flowed away as the flower of the field. That is the whole of the entry, and nothing further is claimed here.
