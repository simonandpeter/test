# Simeon (Krechkov)

days.pravoslavie.ru names him last of seven on one line for 12 ноября ст. ст. and alone of the seven as a deacon; the name page behind his place in the line, 9861, gives him as «Симеон (Кречков), диакон, сщмч.» and carries no text. The life is on azbyka.ru, and it is not his alone: it is headed for the three clergy of one parish together, «СВЯЩЕННОМУЧЕНИКИ ИОАНН КЕСАРИЙСКИЙ, ПЕТР КОСМИНКОВ, ПРЕСВИТЕРЫ И СИМЕОН КРЕЧКОВ, ДИАКОН».

He was born in 1876 in the village of Понизовье in the Vereya district of the Moscow province, the son of a psalmist, Nikolai Krechkov. He was schooled at the Pererva theological school and then at the Moscow theological seminary, which he finished in 1900. He worked first as a teacher, and in 1903 was ordained deacon to the church of the Vladimir icon of the Mother of God in the village of Быково, where he served until his arrest in 1937.

In the persecution of the Russian Orthodox Church, the page says, it happened that the militant godless arrested not only the priest but the whole clergy and the most active lay members of the church council, as happened at Быково in the Ramenskoye district. Arrested there were the priests [John (Kesariysky)](/saints/john-kesariysky) and [Peter (Kosminkov)](/saints/peter-kosminkov), the deacon Simeon, the warden of the church [Eudocia (Safronova)](/saints/eudocia-safronova), and the council members «Петр Пискарев-Молоков» and «Александр, Екатерина и Дмитрий Чиликины». They were taken over three days, from 1 to 3 November 1937, and shut up in the Taganka prison in Moscow.

His own interrogation was four questions and four refusals. Told that he was arrested for counter-revolutionary activity and required to give evidence about it, he said that he had carried on none and carried on none now, and so could give no evidence. Told that he was a member of a counter-revolutionary grouping in the village and asked to name all its members, he said he belonged to no such grouping and knew of none. Asked who had organised it, he said he knew neither of the organisation nor of its organisers. Asked where the illegal meetings of the churchmen's grouping had been held, he answered that he knew nothing of meetings or of any grouping — and the page adds that the deacon would not slander himself or others.

On 15 November 1937 one NKVD troika sentenced the two priests and the deacon to be shot, two of the council members to ten years in a labour camp, and the warden and two others to eight. The three clergy were shot on 16 November 1937 and buried in one unmarked common grave on the Butovo range near Moscow. One arrest, one village, one troika decision and one grave are what the relations written here rest on: this is a company and not a line, and the rows run to the two priests and to the warden, who was sent to the Taishet camp and died in prison on 25 August 1938.

The day he was shot is not the day he is kept on. Both calendars commemorate him on 25 November, which is 12 ноября ст. ст.; he was shot on 16 November, which is 3 ноября ст. ст., and that is the day the calendar keeps the two priests and the warden on. The service azbyka prints with the life is headed «Ме́сяца ноября́ в 3-й день» and «Новому́чеников Бы́ковских», and its text was approved by the Holy Synod of the Russian Orthodox Church on 14 May 2018, журнал № 37; its troparion is in this folder, and it names the three clergy and the warden together. Nothing here chooses between the two days: the row stands on the day the calendar's line gives him. The day page itself carries no hymn under any of the seven names on that line.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 12 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261112.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9861.html) — and the life and the service printed by the Russian church calendar at azbyka.ru, [the entry](https://azbyka.ru/days/sv-simeon-krechkov); both read 5 October 2026.*
