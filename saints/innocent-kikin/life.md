# Innocent (Kikin)

The Russian calendar's 13 October names him in six words, and shares them with another man: «Сщмчч. Иннокентия и Николая пресвитеров (1937)» — Innocent and Nicholas, hieromartyrs, presbyters, 1937. «Сщмч.» is the mark of a priest put to death for the faith. The site's index of names supplies the surname the day line withholds and the grade it generalises, heading his page «Иннокентий (Кикин), иерей, сщмч.» — a priest of the simple grade, where the man beside him was an archpriest.

That is the whole record. 1937 is the worst year of the Soviet campaign against the Church, and neither page gives a birth, a diocese, a parish, a charge or a place of death. His index entry keeps him here and on 26 January with the whole company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and carries no life. The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian saints are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026 even with a browser user-agent, so the line stands as the line.

[Nicholas (Ermolov)](/saints/nicholas-ermolov) is the other half of it, and the two have the same two days and the same silence.

*After the Russian church calendar's [13 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261013.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13827.html); read 4 October 2026.*
