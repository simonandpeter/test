# Justin (17 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 17 November names «Όσιος Ιουστίνος», and its whole notice of him is one sentence: the venerable Justin died in peace. The calendar gives him no country, no century, no monastery, no teacher, no companion and no hymn, and it is one of three entries on this one day whose whole content is that a man died in peace. The corpus holds other saints of the name — a philosopher, a modern Serbian theologian, a companion of Chariton — and none of them is this man.

The only other thing on the page is the two-line verse over the entry, and it is worth having because it is all the character the source allows him: an ascetic and sleepless eye closes — Justin, the wonder of those who have practised asceticism. From that much the calendar's own words make him a monastic and an ascetic, and this folder goes no further than they do.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 17 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/17/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3030/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
