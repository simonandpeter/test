# Herodion of Iloezersk

He was a disciple of [Cornelius of Komel](/saints/cornelius-of-komel), whom the page dates to 19 May 1537, and when his teacher died he left for Lake Iloozero, twenty kilometres from Belozersk, and set a cell for himself on a peninsula there. He built a church in honour of the Nativity of the Mother of God and so began the Iloezersk Ozad hermitage.

The page's account of his life is four facts: he fasted strictly, he spent his nights at prayer, he received communion every Saturday, and an angel told him his end was near. He died in the schema on 28 September 1541 and was buried in the chapel he had built.

The first icon of him was painted after he appeared to a man named Sophonias — who had prodded at the grave with his staff without reverence and was struck blind, and saw again when he prayed to the saint. A short life was written by Archimandrite Mitrophan of the Belozersk monastery, who in 1653, by the blessing of Metropolitan Nikon of Novgorod — afterwards patriarch — examined the wonders worked at the relics; the keeping of his memory was settled then. Where the chapel had stood a church of the Nativity of the Mother of God was raised, with a side chapel in his own name.

*After the Russian church calendar's [28 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260928.html) and [its life of him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1621.htm); read 4 October 2026.*
