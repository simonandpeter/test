# Kyria, companion of Doule

saint.gr's calendar names her and gives no life. Its whole account of the two women is one clause — «Οι Αγίες Κυρία και Δούλη μαρτύρησαν διά ξίφους», the holy Kyria and Doule were martyred by the sword. The page gives them no century, no country, no city, no emperor and no judge; it gives them no years, names no relics and prints no hymn under either name.

What it prints beside the line is a couplet which turns on the two names, since in Greek they are the words for a mistress and for her serving girl: «Ἐχρῆν ἕπεσθαι κυρίᾳ τὴν δουλίδα, / Ἐκ γῆς ἰούσῃ πρὸς Θεὸν διὰ ξίφους» — the servant girl had to follow her mistress, who was going from earth to God by the sword. The page does not say that the two women stood to each other as their names do, and this folder does not say it either. [Doule](/saints/doule-companion-of-kyria) is the other of the pair.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/05/index.aspx) and [the life of the two](https://www.saint.gr/3977/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
