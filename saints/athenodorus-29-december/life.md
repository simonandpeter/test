# Athenodorus (29 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 29 December keeps the venerable Athenodorus, and gives almost nothing of him. Its whole account is one sentence: «Ο Όσιος Αθηνόδωρος ήταν ασκητής, (άγνωστο που) και απεβίωσε ειρηνικά» — the venerable Athenodorus was an ascetic, it is unknown where, and he died in peace. There is no century, no country, no monastery, no deed and no hymn.

The one other thing the page says is which book keeps him and in what company: in Codex 1578 of Paris, it says, Athenodorus is accompanied by other venerable fathers as well, Babylas and [Benjamin](/saints/benjamin-29-december) — and that Benjamin is the one this same calendar keeps on this same day. Its couplet offers him the only gift the page has to give: «Αθηνοδώρω δώρον εξ εμού λόγος, / Θείω γαρ ανδρί και νεκρώ πρέπει λόγος» — my gift to Athenodorus is a word, for a word befits a divine man even when he is dead.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 29 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/29/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1062/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
