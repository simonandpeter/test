# Paul of Taganrog

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him in one line for 10 марта ст. ст., «Прп. Павла Таганрогского (1879)», and that line with his name page is the whole of what the calendar gives: a venerable of Taganrog, on the Sea of Azov, who died in 1879, and whom the calendar also keeps on 7 июня for the day of his glorification. There is no life page for him on either, and nothing here is filled in from elsewhere.

The name page does print one sentence of his, under his picture, and it is the only thing the calendar reports him as having said: «Всегда молиться Богу. Искать утешение и наслаждение в молитве» — to pray to God always, and to seek consolation and delight in prayer; so, it says, did the blessed elder Paul of Taganrog instruct his spiritual children. The page calls him an elder and the day's line calls him venerable, and it prints no hymn for him.

The same calendar's day page for 7 июня names him too, and names him for a different reason: «Прав. Павла Таганрогского (прославление 1999).» That is his glorification, the year the Russian church numbered him among the saints, and not a second day of his memory; the line grades him прав., righteous, where the 10 марта line grades him прп., venerable. The 7 июня page prints no hymn under his name either, and the one hymn it does print belongs to a martyr of Ancyra.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 10 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260310.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14807.html), which carries no life; read 7 October 2026; and the same calendar's day page for 7 июня ст. ст., which carries his glorification — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260607.html), read 8 October 2026.*
