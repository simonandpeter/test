# The New Martyrs and Confessors of Radonezh

This is a synaxis and not a man, and the calendar names nobody in it. The Сретенский monastery's calendar for 27 ноября ст. ст. prints «Собор новомучеников и исповедников Радонежских» — the assembly of the new martyrs and confessors of Radonezh — with no year after it, no list of members, and no life page; the name page behind it carries the heading and this one day and nothing else at all.

So this folder is the commemoration and not a roll. It records that the Russian church keeps, on this day, the clergy and monastics of Radonezh who suffered in the Soviet years, and it names none of them, because the page read names none of them. Radonezh is the country of the Trinity-Sergius Lavra north of Moscow, and the same day's calendar prints, in a separate clause of its own, twenty-three men shot in 1937 of whom a good many were of that house; each of those twenty-three has his own name page and his own folder, and this entry is not a second record of them but the general commemoration beside them.

A synaxis that enumerates its members is one folder for each member, and a synaxis that names nobody is one entry — which is what this is.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 27 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261127.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14690.html), which carries no life; read 7 October 2026.*
