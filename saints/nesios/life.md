# Nesios

saint.gr's calendar for 27 Φεβρουαρίου names him and gives one sentence. Its line reads «Άγιος Νήσιος», and the page standing under it says that the holy martyr Nesios — or Nisios, for it allows both spellings in the one sentence — was martyred after being flogged with whips made of ox sinew. That is the whole of what is printed of him: a name in two forms and a manner of death.

Its couplet keeps to the sinews: Nesios bears his suffering under the ox sinews, for being strung with sinews he has forgetfulness of the pains — «Νεύροις βοείοις Νήσιος πάσχων φέρει· Νευρούμενος γὰρ τῶν πόνων λήθην ἔχει». No year, no place, no emperor, no relics and no hymn are given for him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 27 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/27/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3593/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
