# Euthymius of Sardis

Euthymius lived in the days of the emperors Constantine and Irene. He shone first as a star in the monastic life, and was then made a hierarch; and at the Seventh Council at Nicaea he crushed the heretics with power. When Nikephoros came up to the imperial throne and began to live lawlessly, the saint rebuked him, and the emperor banished him to the West together with other Orthodox bishops; and from then until his suffering as a confessor, for twenty-nine years, the blessed one did not see his metropolis again.

When the emperors changed and Leo, who bore the name of a beast, came to the throne, the saint was called back from banishment, and the emperor asked him whether he venerated the holy icons; and the saint, being full of zeal, laid the emperor under anathema. At this the emperor in his anger banished him again, to Ason. After Leo had been killed the new emperor called him back from Ason and required him to renounce the holy icons.

And Euthymius, in his zeal for Christ, said with a loud voice: «If anyone does not venerate our Lord Jesus Christ painted in an icon, let him be anathema». For this the saint was beaten, banished and shut in prison; and having been cruelly beaten with ox sinews he lived a little longer and gave up his soul into the hands of the Lord.
