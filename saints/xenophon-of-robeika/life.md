# Xenophon of Robeika

The Russian calendar keeps him on this day as «Прп. Ксенофонта Робейского (1262)», and the life behind that line is five sentences long. He was a disciple of the venerable Barlaam of Khutyn, who died in 1192 and whose memory the same calendar keeps on 6 November, and he was superior of the Khutyn monastery after the abbot Isidore, who died in 1243. Leaving that office he founded a monastery of the Trinity on the bank of the river Robeika, not far from Novgorod, and there he blessedly reposed on 28 June 1262.

That is the whole of what the source says of him: a teacher, a monastery he governed, a monastery he founded, a river and a date. The calendar's own list of his days gives both 26 January and 28 June, and the day page that names him is the first of the two, so that is the day his row records; the second is the day of his repose, which his life prints and which this note keeps. His troparion on the day calls him a fair growth of the wilderness and a praise and a stronghold to Great Novgorod.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 26 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260126.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1410.htm); read 3 October 2026.*
