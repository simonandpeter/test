# Albeus (Ailbe) of Ireland

The Greek synaxarion keeps the Irish saint Albeus — Ailbe — on 12 September and says it has no details of his life. This entry records the line and nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 12 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2417/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
