# Cumein of Iona

saint.gr names him on this day and gives him one sentence, and the life says exactly that: «Ο Άγιος Cumein ήταν ηγούμενος στο μοναστήρι της Iona» — the holy Cumein was abbot at the monastery of Iona. The page prints the name in Latin letters, without a Greek rendering, and adds no distich, no etymology, no relic and no hymn.

It gives no year of birth or of repose, no country beyond the island the monastery stands on, nothing of how he came there and nothing of what he did as abbot. Nothing more is written here than the site prints, and the site prints one line.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/24/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4216/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
