# Diomedes

Diomedes and [Laurence](/saints/laurence-the-martyr) were martyred together: bound to a plane tree and shot with arrows until they died. The synaxarion’s verse makes a play of it — to two athletes tied to a plane, the drawn bows are the empty rustling of planes. The Romanian calendar has them on the same day with the same line.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 28 August — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2209/saint.aspx). The Romanian entry is from doxologia.ro — [the page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-diomid-0); read 23 August 2026.*
