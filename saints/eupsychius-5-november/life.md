# Eupsychius (5 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 5 Νοεμβρίου keeps Eupsychius and Carterius in one entry, and its account of them is a single sentence. They lived in the years of the emperor Maximian, when the governor of Palestine was Urbanus, beside whose name the page sets the year 298. They were martyred, it says, after their genitals were cut off, so that they died of bleeding that could not be stopped. The page gives them no city, no trial, no burial, no year of their own and no hymn.

The couplet of the synaxarion names the other of the two and plays on his name as endurance: «Αἰδοῖα Καρτέριος ἐκτετμημένος, / Σὺν Εὐψυχίῳ καρτεροψύχως φέρει» — Carterius, cut in his private parts, bears it stout-souled together with Eupsychius. The man kept with him is [Carterius](/saints/carterius-5-november).

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2893/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
