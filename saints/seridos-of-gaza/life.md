# Seridos of Gaza

Seridos is remembered as the founder of the famous monastery near Gaza in Palestine — the community in which Barsanuphius, John, abba Dorotheos, Dositheos and others lived their ascetic lives.

He ended his earthly life in the sixth century, the Prologue says, and passed to the eternal joy of his Lord. It records nothing else of him.
