# Seridos of Gaza

Seridos is remembered as the founder of the famous monastery near Gaza in Palestine — the community in which Barsanuphius, John, abba Dorotheos, Dositheos and others lived their ascetic lives.

He ended his earthly life in the sixth century, the Prologue says, and passed to the eternal joy of his Lord. It records nothing else of him.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same menologion day and names the house he governed. saint.gr calls him a great ascetic and says he was abbot of the great cenobium that lay near Gaza, where [Barsanuphius](/saints/barsanuphius-the-great) with his disciple [John](/saints/john-the-prophet-of-gaza) shone by their spiritual labour, and the great abba [Dorotheos](/saints/dorotheus-of-gaza) as well; and that the venerable Seridos died in peace. The couplet printed above it says only «Όπου συ και Σέριδος ω Θεού Λόγε, / Δόξαν θεωρών, ην Πατήρ δέδωκέ σοι» — where you are, Word of God, there is Seridos also, beholding the glory the Father has given you. The page gives no year and no hymn.
