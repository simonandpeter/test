# Theodore (Bakkalinsky)

Theodore was born on 8 February 1880 in the village of Zarudye in the Oratov district of the province of Kiev, the son of a man who served the church. He finished four classes of the Uman church school, and from 1901 to 1903 served in the army as a clerk at an artillery store in Chita. Demobilised, he became psalmist in the church where his father served, and soon after the revolution he was ordained priest.

In 1935 he and his wife moved to the Tver region and he took the church at Stepurino in the Staritsa district. His service there was short. On 14 November 1937 the authorities arrested him and put him in the prison at Rzhev, and the interrogations began at once.

He denied everything the investigator put to him: that he had systematically carried on counter-revolutionary agitation, that he had slanderously discredited the Soviet government and spread rumours of war, that he had mocked the constitution of the USSR by the church fence in July, that he had wandered round the collective farms extorting food. «Нет, виновным себя не признаю, вторично заявляю: контрреволюционную агитацию я не проводил.» He was sentenced on 25 November 1937 and shot two days later, on 27 November. He was canonised for the whole church in August 2000.

The day's line names twenty-seven new martyrs of 1937 together — twenty-three presbyters, a deacon, a hieromonk, a lay martyr and a woman — and the calendar carries a life page for ten of them, of whom he is one. The men of that line were tried separately, in different districts and different prisons, and no one of these lives names another of them, so no relation is recorded between them here.
