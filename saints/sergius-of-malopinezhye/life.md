# Sergius of Malopinezhye

The Охридски пролог gives him four sentences and they are the whole of what this folder holds. He was a Russian parish priest. As a priest he lived and served in a manner pleasing to God for twenty-two full years in the province of Vologda. He reposed peacefully in the Lord on the sixteenth of November 1585, in the ninety-second year of his life.

That is all of it. The book gives him no birthplace, no monastery, no account of his service beyond its length, no companion and no wonder; and it gives no year of birth, so none is written here, although the age it does give would put one about 1493. Of the four calendars this corpus reads he is kept by this one alone, and the Russian calendar — the one most likely to keep a Russian priest — has not been looked at for him.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 16. новембар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2025-11-29&prolog=1); read 7 October 2026.*
