# Job of Moscow

saint.gr's calendar for 19 June names Job, metropolitan of Moscow, and its page calls him the first patriarch of Moscow. He was born about 1530 at Staritsa, the page says, and fell asleep in 1607; and then it adds that we have no other details of the saint's life. Under the heading it notes that the relics of the saint are in the cathedral church of the Dormition of the Mother of God in the Kremlin.

The day's line gives him the rank of metropolitan and the page's own sentence gives him that of patriarch, and both are recorded here as the source has them. The page also links a separate commemoration, the translation of the relics of Saint Job metropolitan of Moscow, which belongs to another day and is not this entry. Beyond the two years, the birthplace and the relics there is nothing in it: no epigram, no hymn and no account of his patriarchate.

**The Russian church calendar keeps him on the Julian 5 October with the synaxis of the hierarchs of Moscow**, seventeen names in one line under one polyeleos service, marked «(Моск. Свт.)» and not expanded: Peter, Theognostus, Alexis, Cyprian, Photius, Jonah, Gerontius, Joasaph, Macarius, Philip, Job, Hermogenes, Tikhon, Peter, Philaret, Innocent and Macarius — two and a half centuries of metropolitans, then the patriarchs, then the metropolitans of Moscow and Kolomna, ending with a man shot in 1937. He is the eleventh of them and the first patriarch on the line. His index entry carries three further days — 19 June for his repose, 5 April for the translation of his relics, and «26 января (интронизация)», his enthronement, which for the first patriarch of Moscow is the feast one would expect it to keep.
