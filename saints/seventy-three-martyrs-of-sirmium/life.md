# The Seventy-three Martyrs of Sirmium

saint.gr's entry for the Seventy-three Martyrs is a single sentence, and the life says exactly that: the holy Seventy-three Martyrs were martyred in the city of Sirmium, under Diocletian, whose reign the page gives as 284 to 305, in the year 303. The page names none of them. It gives no manner of death, no judge, no relic and no hymn, and it prints no distich over them.

Because the seventy-three are not enumerated anywhere on the page, they are kept here as one entry and not as seventy-three folders; a number is what the calendar prints, and a number is what this folder holds. Sirmium in Pannonia is where the site puts them, and nothing further about the place is claimed here.
