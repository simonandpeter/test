# Theoklitos

Theoklitos is unknown to the Synaxaristes. The Patmos Codex 266 has him thus: on the same day, of the venerable Theoklitos — he was from Constantinople and held the rank of magistros; he left the world and went up to Mount Olympus, and there, having renounced all and practised asceticism for very many years, he fell asleep.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 27 August — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/906/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
