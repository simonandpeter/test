# Donnan of Eigg

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives one sentence of a life. Saint Donnan was a monk, it says, and was martyred together with his company in the year 618 on the little island of Eigg in Scotland. That is the whole of it: no monastery, no parentage, no manner of death, no number for the company and not one of their names, and no year of birth.

The page keeps his name in Latin letters throughout, which the rest of this calendar rarely does, and gives no Greek form of it. It prints neither couplet nor hymn, links him to the synaxis of the saints who shone in Scotland, and credits the Archdiocese of Thyateira and Great Britain as its source.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 17 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/17/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4213/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
