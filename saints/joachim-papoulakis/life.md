# Joachim Papoulakis of Ithaca

Joachim Papoulakis was born in 1786 in the hamlet of Kalyvia on Ithaca, in Greece, of devout parents, Angelos and Agnes. His mother died when he was very young and his father married again; the stepmother was jealous of the child John — John was the saint's baptismal name — and it is well known, the life says, that she tormented him. That made him learn patience early, and hide from her for hours together, reading the Scriptures and praying in a little chapel of Saint Spyridon near their house.

Later, following his father's example, the young John became a seaman. On one of his voyages he visited Mount Athos, and there, kindled with zeal for holy things, he stayed at the monastery of Vatopedi and became a monk, receiving the name Joachim.

In 1827 he came back to Ithaca and served in the church there until the end of his life. He went to the Lord in peace on 2 March 1868. He was canonized in 1998, and works many miracles to this day, as he did while he was still on earth. doxologia prints no hymn for him.
