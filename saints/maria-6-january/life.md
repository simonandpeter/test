# Maria (6 January)

saint.gr keeps on the sixth of January a company of martyrs of 1919, and its line for the day names them as far as it can: «Άγιος Ανδρέας, οι δυο θυγατέρες του, και οι νεομάρτυρες γυναίκες Λυδία, Δομνίκη και Μαρία» — the holy [Andrew](/saints/andrew-6-january), his two daughters, and the new-martyr women [Lydia](/saints/lydia-6-january), [Domnica](/saints/domnica-6-january) and Maria. The notice behind the line gives four of those names again over its heading, each with the year 1919, and then one sentence: «Οι Άγιοι αυτοί Μάρτυρες, μαρτύρησαν το έτος 1919. Δεν έχουμε περισσότερες λεπτομέρειες για τον Βίο των Μαρτύρων.»

That is all the page has: these holy martyrs were martyred in the year 1919, and we have no further details of their lives. It gives no country, no city, no manner of death and no account of who killed them. The two daughters of Andrew are counted but never named, so they have no folder here; the four the page does name have one each, with nothing in it the page does not say.

Of Maria the calendar says only her name, the year, the word it puts over it — «Αγια Μαρια η Νεομαρτυς», Maria the new-martyr — and two etymologies of the name, the embittered one who was raised up and glorified, or else the rising star of dawn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1082/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
