# The Two Women Martyrs

The Greek synaxarion says of the two women only that they were probably nuns and were martyred in a way that is not known. Its verse speaks of a pair of women nurtured in the virtues, adorned by being cut down with the sword of contest, and gives no names and no date.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 30 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/1019/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
