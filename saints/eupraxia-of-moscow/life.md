# Eupraxia of Moscow

The Russian church calendar names her and gives no life. Its list for 3 мая ст. ст. reads «Прпп. Иулиании (1393) и Евпраксии (1394) Московских» — the venerable Juliana and Eupraxia of Moscow, with a year for each — and the name page behind the second of those names carries the heading «Евпраксия Московская», the two days on which she is kept, and nothing further except a link to the Conception convent in Moscow, «Зачатьевский ставропигиальный женский монастырь». The day page prints no troparion or kontakion under her name.

So what the source says of her is this: that she was a woman under monastic vows, that she is kept together with [Juliana of Moscow](/saints/juliana-of-moscow), that she died in 1394, a year after her, that her memory falls on 3 мая and again on 24 августа as Moscow keeps it, and that the house her name is attached to is that convent. It gives her no birthplace, no family, no office and no account of her labours, and none has been supplied here.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 3 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260503.html) and [her name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14457.htm), which gives no life; read 8 October 2026.*
