# Dionysius, the other companion of Quadratus of Corinth

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery prints the name Дионисий twice in one line for 10 марта ст. ст., among the twenty-two it names in the company of [Quadratus of Corinth](/saints/quadratus-of-corinth), and it gives each of the two a name page of its own. The second it titles «Дионисий Коринфский (другой), мч.» — the Corinthian Dionysius, the other one — and that parenthesis is the whole of what the calendar says to tell him from his namesake. This folder is that second man.

What the day's life page says of him is one clause. The friends and followers who came out to the mountains for the hermit physician's teaching were seized with him, tried under the commander Jason in the persecution the page sets in the reign of Decius, and beheaded; and then, it says, the rest of the disciples also suffered for Christ, and of these the other Dionysius was stabbed to death with a knife. The page gives no year for him beyond the 258 of its own line and the 249 to 251 of the reign in its text, and it prints no hymn.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 10 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260310.html) and [the life of the company](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life604.htm); read 7 October 2026.*
