# John Karhapää

Johannes Vasilinpoika Karhapää, whom the Romanian calendar names Saint John of Sonkajanranta, is one of the first Finnish martyrs. He was born on 13 July 1884 in Finland. In his native village he founded an Orthodox school, which was running from 1908 with the blessing of Archbishop Sergius of Finland.

He was put to death as a martyr at only thirty-three years of age, on 8 April 1918, shot by a firing squad near the town of Joensuu. His wife managed to recover his body some months afterwards, and buried it in the Kokonniemi cemetery at Ilomantsi.

The Holy Synod of the Ecumenical Patriarchate approved the Autonomous Orthodox Church of Finland's proposal for his canonisation in November 2018, and the canonisation was proclaimed in 2019 in the church of Saint Anne, after the Liturgy served by Archbishop Leo of Karelia and All Finland. doxologia prints no hymn for him, and keeps him on 8 martie although the life on the same site gives 8 April 1918 as the day he died.
