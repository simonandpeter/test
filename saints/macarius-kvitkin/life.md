# Macarius (Kvitkin)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and carries no life. Its line for the twenty-third of March, old style, reads «Сщмч. Макария пресвитера (1931).», and the name page behind «Макария» is headed «Макарий (Квиткин), протоиерей, сщмч.», which gives the surname the line leaves out. That page lists the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church on «26 января» and this twenty-third of March.

Nothing else is said of him on either page: no place, no diocese, no parish, no year of birth and no account of how he died. The 1931 the line closes with is the whole of his chronology. The second source for the Russian new martyrs, azbyka.ru, was not read for him.

His is the one of the nine name pages that carries anything besides a name and a day: it gives the address of a parish church dedicated to him, of the diocese of Orenburg, in the village of Alexandrovka Pervaya — which says where he is remembered and still says nothing of his life.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 23 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260323.html); read 8 October 2026.*
