# Proba, daughter of Gaius

saint.gr says that Eustochius the presbyter, Gaius — or Gaianus — his nephew, and Gaius's children Lollia — or Loulo — Proba and Urbanus — or Urbasius — came from the city of the Ousades, and that they lived in the days of the emperors Maximian and Agrippa, in the year 300. Eustochius was at first a priest of the idols; but when he saw the manly spirit of the Christians in the face of the torments, he came to Eudoxius the bishop of Antioch, was baptised a Christian and was ordained presbyter.

Afterwards he went to Lystra in Lycaonia, where he found his kinsman Gaius with his three children, Lollia, Proba and Urbanus, and baptised them Christians. When he was arrested by the pagans he confessed before the governor that he was a Christian, and they hung him upon a piece of wood and tore his flesh from him.

Then he was taken, with Gaius and Gaius's children, to Agrippinus — or, as others have it, Agrippa — the governor of Ancyra, where all of them refused to deny Christ. They were all tortured with frightful and barbarous torments, and because they stayed firm in their faith they were beheaded at the last, and so went up crowned to heaven. The page gives each of the five the year 300 and no year of birth, prints no hymn under their line, and carries a couplet for each of them out of the synaxaria.

The day's line names all five of them — [Eustochius](/saints/eustochius-the-presbyter), [Gaius, nephew of Eustochius](/saints/gaius-nephew-of-eustochius), and his children [Lollia, daughter of Gaius](/saints/lollia-daughter-of-gaius), Proba, daughter of Gaius and [Urbanus, son of Gaius](/saints/urbanus-son-of-gaius) — so the entry is one folder for each, and nothing in it is a heading over people it does not name.

The Ohrid Prologue keeps the company on the Julian 23 June, the same menologion day the Greek calendar keeps them on, under a heading that names only [Eustochius](/saints/eustochius-the-presbyter) and [Gaius, nephew of Eustochius](/saints/gaius-nephew-of-eustochius) and then says, and others with them. The Prologue names her in its body and not in its heading, as the first of the three children baptised with Gaius. This book calls Gaius the kinsman of Eustochius and not his nephew, and it does not say the three children were his; it has them baptised **together with** him, after Eustochius had brought his kinsfolk over to Christianity by degrees.

All these, it ends, and still others besides them, were brought before the court, tortured and beheaded for the faith in Christ the Lord at Lystra, and their souls passed over into the immortal kingdom of Christ. Where the Greek account above has the company taken on from Lystra to the governor of Ancyra and beheaded before him, this one has them die at Lystra and names no governor. It gives no year, dating the persecution only by the emperor Maximian, so the year recorded on this folder is still the Greek page’s. The other two of the three are [Lollia, daughter of Gaius](/saints/lollia-daughter-of-gaius) and [Urbanus, son of Gaius](/saints/urbanus-son-of-gaius), and the ending this book gives the first two of the three names is masculine, where the Greek page above makes Lollia and Proba women.
