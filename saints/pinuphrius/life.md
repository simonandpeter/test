# Pinuphrius

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 27 noiembrie reads «Sfântul Cuvios Pinufrie» — the venerable Pinuphrius — and the page it links to carries only that name, the date, and the readings appointed for the day: the Apostle from Galatians 5:22–26 and 6:1–2, on the fruit of the Spirit, and the Gospel from Matthew 11:27–30, *Come to me, all you who labour and are heavy laden.*

There is no Viață tab and no Tropar tab on that page, so no story, no century, no country and no hymn are recorded here. What the Romanian calendar attests is that he is kept as a venerable father on this day; anything more would have to come from a source this folder has not read.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 27 November, and saint.gr does give a life where doxologia.ro gave none. It says he was a great ascetic in the Egyptian desert, a priest and the abbot of a community near the city of Panepho; that because he would not have honours he left his own monastery, took lay clothing, and took refuge in the monastery of the Tabennesiotes, where he served like the lowest of the servants; that after three years one of his own disciples recognised him and he was brought back; and that he left it again for the same reason, went to Palestine, returned once more to his monastery, and there lived in full humility and died in peace. Sayings of his, it adds, are preserved in the Evergetinos. It gives him no year and prints no hymn.

The Охридски пролог gives him four short sentences. He was a contemporary of [John Cassian](/saints/john-cassian), whom it names with his day, 29 February, and a great Egyptian ascetic. He lived in the fourth century, and he practised in one place after another, always fleeing the praise of men.

He had many disciples, the entry closes, who laboured to imitate the lofty example of their teacher. The century is the first year of any kind this folder has held: the Romanian and Greek calendars it was otherwise built on give him none, and it is recorded here as the span of a life and not as a year of death, because that is what the book says of it.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 27 noiembrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/27-noiembrie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-pinufrie); read 25 September 2026; and after saint.gr's calendar for 27 Νοεμβρίου — [the Greek day](https://www.saint.gr/11/27/index.aspx) and [its life](https://www.saint.gr/2730/saint.aspx), read 2 October 2026; and the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 27. новембар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2025-12-10&prolog=1), read 8 October 2026.*
