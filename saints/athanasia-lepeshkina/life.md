# Athanasia (Lepeshkina)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Athanasia Lepeshkina on 29 апреля and gives no life of her. Its line for the day is the whole of what it says: «Прмц. Афанасии (1932).» — the venerable-martyr Athanasia, and the year 1932 — with a link to her name and none to any reading. So what the calendar tells is that she was counted among the venerable-martyrs and that she died in 1932.

Her own name page on that site, 15089, carries nothing but the heading «Афанасия (Лепешкина), игумения, прмц.», which gives the surname the day's line leaves out and the grade «игумения», an abbess, and the three days «26 января (Новомуч.) , 29 апреля , 24 августа (Моск.)». There is no life page for her anywhere on the site, no icon and no article. No page names the house she governed, or a diocese, or any place at all — which for an abbess is a conspicuous silence, and it is left as a silence here rather than filled in.

That she was an abbess is recorded in this folder as a type and not as an office, because the four languages this site reads in have no word prepared for it and a reader may not invent four. The day page prints four hymns, two for the nine martyrs of Cyzicus and two for the wonderworker of Ostrog, and none under her name. The 26 января commemoration of the new martyrs of Russia and the Moscow synaxis of 24 августа were not read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 29 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260429.html) and [her name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/15089.html); read 8 October 2026.*
