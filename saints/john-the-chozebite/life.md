# John the Chozebite

John, the Greek synaxarion says, at first fell into the error of the Monophysites: when, under the empress Pulcheria, the Fourth Ecumenical Council at Chalcedon condemned the heretical teaching of Eutyches, he would not accept its dogmatic judgement. Yet he was not moved by pride, for his rich and devout parents at Thebes in Egypt had brought him up in Christian learning. He went to Jerusalem to venerate the Lord’s tomb and receive communion there, and before the day came Orthodox clergy tried to free him of his error, but still he wavered; on the eve of the day he was to commune he heard a voice in a dream saying that those who separate themselves from the truth of the Orthodox Church are unworthy to commune, and he was convinced for good.

Returning, he became a monk at the place called Choziba, which the entry puts in Egypt, and grew so in the virtues of Orthodoxy that God gave him to heal the possessed by his prayer. He was made bishop of Caesarea but went back to his hermitage, and died there, having helped countless souls with his Orthodox teaching. The Greek calendar keeps him on 3 October; the entry gives no year.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 3 Οκτωβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2586/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
