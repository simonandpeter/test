# Eutychius (Didenko)

**A widower who went to the monastery, fought a wolf with a pitchfork, and was everybody’s favourite for his simplicity.** The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him twelfth of the sixteen it closes with 1937, among the five it calls «прмчч.», and carries no life for him; the life here is the article azbyka.ru prints for the civil 5 декабря out of fond.ru, which it hangs behind all five pages of this company. He was born in 1870 in the village of Николаевское in the Курья volost of the Змеиногорск district of the province of Томск, son of a peasant, Иван Диденко, and named Емельян in baptism. He was cousin to [Laurence (Levchenko)](/saints/laurence-levchenko), a hieromonk of the same house whom the corpus already keeps on 12 октября; he married and was widowed, and then went to a monastery. He was some time in the Kyiv Caves Lavra, and on 10 November 1901 entered the skete of John the Forerunner at Оптина; on 20 November 1901 he was clothed in the undercassock, and on 4 June 1905 tonsured to the rassophore. His obedience was the work of the monastery farm. He was a small man and the article says they all loved him «за простоту и доброту», for his simplicity and his kindness.

**He was tonsured to the mantia on the skete’s patronal feast, by the hand of [Barsanuphius of Optina](/saints/barsanuphius-of-optina).** On 24 June 1911 the liturgy for the feast of the Nativity of John the Forerunner was served in council, the superior of the skete leading the service and the moleben, and on that day the novice Емельян was tonsured to the mantia with the name Евтихий. At the vigil of Palm Sunday, 14 April 1917, there happened to him what the article says can be read as a foretoken of all that came afterwards: he was then at his obedience on the monastery farm, and a wolf got into the yard after it was locked and raised an uproar. He came out with a pitchfork at the cause of it, but the starving beast was not frightened, went for him, and there was a fierce struggle between them. The wolf ran in the end, but he was badly torn and had to be sent urgently to hospital in Moscow.

**After the house was closed he lodged first with the cell-attendants of an elder and then with the two monks he was shot with.** The godless shut the monastery in 1923, and he lived at first in Козельск together with Пётр (Швырев) and the hierodeacon the corpus keeps as [Sebastian of Karaganda](/saints/sebastian-of-karaganda), both cell-attendants of the elder Нектарий; in the thirties he settled in one house with the Optina monks Авенир (Синицын) and Савва (Суслов). He earned his bread by helping about the house for whoever needed it and by serving in the church.

**He was questioned twice and both times about one priest.** The investigator told him the investigation had it that he was closely tied to a former dean then under arrest for counter-revolutionary activity, and demanded truthful testimony; he answered that he knew the man well, had been to his house and met him in church, «он был хорошим человеком, защищал монахов» — he was a good man, he defended the monks. On 16 November the investigator questioned him again, putting it that having no settled occupation he had carried on counter-revolutionary work in close connection with that priest; he answered that he had often met him in church, where the man served the church service, «но никакой работы против советской власти я не проводил». Of what he said at the first interrogation the article keeps this: that when there was a monastery life was better and all the monks gathered in church for the services, and that to the last the monks all kept the monastic way of life.

**Five men of one house, one lodging and one case.** The article is headed for all of them — «Преподобномученики Евтихий (Диденко), Авенир (Синицын), Савва (Суслов), Марк (Махров) и мученик Борис Козлов» — and begins with what 1937 was: the last period of the destruction of the Russian Church by a government of militant godless men, which reached the monks of Оптина still at liberty, living after the closing of their house in the town of Козельск, the nearest town to the monastery they loved. Three of them — Евтихий, Авенир and Савва — lodged in one house, each in his own cell with his own icons; Марк lodged elsewhere in the town and Борис at the town hospital where he worked. All four monks were arrested on 30 October 1937 and shut in the prison at Сухиничи; Борис had been taken two days earlier, on 28 October.

**The evidence against them is their neighbour and their landlady, and it is candid.** On 4 November the standing witnesses were questioned. The neighbour said the landlady kept the monks «на квартире специально, потому что они молились Богу», that they paid twelve roubles between them for the lodging while he, a working man, paid eighteen alone, that the four of them «ничем полезным не занимались, ходили по домам, распространяли церковный дурман», went to the church in a group and lived in a group in one lodging each with his own cell fitted out with icons — and that in October, when a pre-election meeting was held in the school building, the monks were invited and all of them flatly refused to go, which told on the other citizens. The landlady said they lived in her house, each in his own cell, and «монашествовали»; that one of them had argued in her presence «что раньше в монастыре жилось лучше, чем теперь при советской власти»; and that another had said «теперь всех сажают в тюрьму, потому что будут выборы советов и уже арестованы попы».

**The troika sentenced all five on 21 November 1937, and they were shot a fortnight later.** The article closes with the one sentence that makes this day their own: it pleased the Lord that on the very day of the feast of the Entry of the Most Holy Theotokos, 4 December, they should spend the day in prayer, in prison but still on earth, and that they were shot «на следующий день, 5 декабря 1937 года» — on the next day, 5 December 1937 — and buried in a common unmarked grave. 5 December 1937 is the Julian 22 ноября, the day both calendars keep them on; the ten other names of the same line were shot on 4 December, which is the 21st.
