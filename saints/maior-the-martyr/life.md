# Maior

Maior lived in the days of the emperors Maximian and Diocletian, and was a soldier in the company called that of the Moors. While he was in the city of Gaza he was denounced before the governor there as a Christian. Coming to the judgement seat and confessing Christ his God, he was beaten without mercy for seven days, and so cruelly that thirty-six soldiers took turns at the beating; the blood ran from his body like a stream and reddened the ground where he stood.

So, bravely suffering torments as great as these, the martyr of Christ passed — in David's words, as the life puts it — out of the order of soldiers on earth into the order of soldiers in heaven, and gave up his soul into the hands of God. Doxologia names the two emperors but prints no year for him, and its page for him carries no troparion.
