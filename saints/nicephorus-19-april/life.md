# Nicephorus (19 April)

The calendar names him in four words, «Прп. Никифора игумена» — the venerable Nicephorus, an abbot — and gives him a life page of four short paragraphs. He was born at Constantinople into a rich and noble family, and his parents, whom the page names Андрей and Феодора, brought him up in the Christian faith. When they had died the young man gave away the whole of his inheritance to the poor and went over to Chalcedon.

The strict life of the monks of the house of «святого Андрея» there pleased him, and he stayed among the brethren of that monastery. From the beginning he showed an uncommon zeal in prayer and in labour, and such strength of endurance in the ascetic life that the abbot soon sent him out as a preacher of the faith of Christ to what the page calls «Финикийский остров», the Phoenician island, and set him as abbot of a monastery in honour of the Most Holy Theotokos.

He stayed on that island thirty-three years and brought many pagans to Christ, and in the place of the heathen shrines, the page says, churches of God were built there. When he felt his end drawing near he had himself carried aboard a ship and said to the shipmaster: be at peace, for I am departing to the Lord, and carry my body to Chalcedon, to the house of «святого Андрея». With those words he died. The ship came safely to Chalcedon, and the brethren of that house buried the body of the holy ascetic with honour.

No year appears anywhere in the account — not of his birth, not of his repose, and not so much as a century — so nothing of the kind is recorded here. His name page lists this one day and gives the one life. The day page prints no hymn under his name: the three hymns it carries are all of the blessed eldress whose line stands second on the page, and the epithet «Катавадский» that his name link gives him is explained on neither page.
