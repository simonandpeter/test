# Gaius

Gaius was bishop of Rome from 283 to 296 and the uncle of the virgin martyr [Susanna](/saints/susanna-of-rome), whose father was his brother, the presbyter [Gabinius](/saints/gabinius-the-presbyter); the family was related to the emperor Diocletian. When Susanna refused the emperor’s co-ruler and was beheaded, the empress buried her in secret and Gaius consecrated the room where she was killed as a church and celebrated there. Her father soon died a martyr, and in 296, the life says, Gaius himself. The Prologue has him and Gabinius of imperial stock and kinsmen of Diocletian.

The Greek calendar keeps him on 22 April, where the Russian keeps him on 11 August of the old style, and saint.gr's notice adds his country and the year of his election. Gaius came from Dalmatia, it says, and was elected bishop of Rome in the year 283. According to certain researchers — the page gives it as their opinion and not as its own — he was a kinsman of the emperor Diocletian and the brother of the martyr Gabinus, whom it keeps on 19 February. He suffered many persecutions and ill-usage in the years of Diocletian, and for a time was obliged to take refuge in a cave far from Rome. He fell asleep in peace, the page says, in the year 296 — although its own heading calls him hieromartyr, a contradiction the page does not resolve. It prints no hymn.
