# The Seven Virgins of Gaza

Seven virgins of Gaza who were martyred by the sword; the synaxarion records nothing more, and its verse says the seven beheaded virgins honour him who honoured the world with seven days.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 31 Αυγούστου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/861/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
