# Basil (6 July)

saint.gr's calendar for 6 Ιουλίου names him and gives no life. The whole of its account is one sentence, «Ο Άγιος Βασίλειος μαρτύρησε στη Σκυθούπολη μαζί με άλλους 69 χριστιανούς (βλέπε 28 Ιουνίου)» — the holy Basil was martyred at Scythopolis together with sixty-nine other Christians, and see 28 June. The page names no century, no year, no emperor and no judge, gives no manner of death and names none of the sixty-nine, and it prints no hymn and no synaxarion couplet under his line.

Besides that sentence it carries only a note on the name itself, which explains it from βασιλεύς and remarks that many kings, patriarchs, bishops and emperors bore it, chiefly Basil II the Bulgar-slayer; that remark is not about him. The cross-reference to 28 June is the site pointing at its own page for that day, which this reader did not open, and so the day recorded here is the day page quoted above. That is everything the source says of him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/673/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
