# James of Rostov

saint.gr keeps him on 27 November and gives him seven words of life. Its head line reads «Άγιος Ιάκωβος ο θαυματουργός Επίσκοπος Ροστοβίας (; - 1392)» — James the wonderworker, bishop of Rostov — and the page says of him only that he was bishop of Rostov, that he fell asleep in the year 1392, and «Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου»: we have no further details of the saint's life.

So nothing else is recorded here. The page gives him no birth, no country, no account of his episcopate and no miracle, though it calls him wonderworker; it prints neither apolytikion nor kontakion, and no distich. The only other thing on it is a link to the synaxis of all the saints who shone in Rostov and Yaroslavl, which is a feast and not a part of his life.

What the Greek calendar attests is that a wonderworking bishop of Rostov of that name is kept on this day and that he died in 1392. Anything more would have to come from a Russian source this folder has not read.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 27 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/27/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2733/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
