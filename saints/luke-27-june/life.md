# Luke (27 June)

saint.gr's calendar for 27 Ιουνίου names him and gives almost no life. Its line reads «Όσιος Λουκάς ο ερημίτης», and the page behind that line carries one sentence — «Ο Όσιος Λουκάς ο ερημίτης απεβίωσε ειρηνικά», the venerable Luke the hermit died in peace. No country is named, no century, no desert or monastery, no year and no relics, and the page prints no hymn.

What stands above the sentence is a couplet of the synaxarion: «Παρήλθε Λουκάς, αλλ’ εμοί τούτον λόγοι, Kαι των εφεξής σαλπίσουσι τω βίω» — Luke has passed away, but words will trumpet him to me and to the life of those who come after. The page glosses his name from the Latin for light, as the enlightened one, him who shines with beauty. That is the whole of what is printed, and the day in the name here is the day the calendar keeps him on and not an epithet of his own.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 27 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/27/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/634/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
