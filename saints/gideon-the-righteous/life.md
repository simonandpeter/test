# Gideon

The Romanian calendar keeps the righteous Gideon of the Old Testament on 26 September, and its life prints one line for him: he is the one who saw the Nativity of Christ before it came to pass, through the dew upon the fleece. This entry records that line and nothing more; the scriptural narrative is the Book of Judges.

*After doxologia.ro, [Viaţa Dreptului Ghedeon](https://doxologia.ro/viata-dreptului-ghedeon); read 30 August 2026.*
