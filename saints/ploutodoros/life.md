# Ploutodoros

saint.gr's calendar for 28 December keeps the holy Ploutodoros, and says at once that the synaxarists do not know him: «Ο Άγιος Πλουτόδωρος είναι άγνωστος στους Συναξαριστές.» What the Church has of him is one line in one manuscript. His memory, the page says, is written in Patmos Codex 256 in these words: «τη αύτη ήμερα άθλησις του αγίου μάρτυρος Πλουτοδώρου» — on the same day, the contest of the holy martyr Ploutodoros.

That is the whole of the entry. It gives him no century, no country, no judge and no manner of death, and the page prints neither a hymn nor a distich for him; the codex's one word, άθλησις, the contest, is what makes him a martyr, and the day it stands on is why this calendar keeps him here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/28/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/548/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
