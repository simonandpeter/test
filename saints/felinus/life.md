# Felinus

saint.gr's calendar for 1 Ιουνίου names him with his companion and gives two sentences. The holy martyrs Felinus and [Gratinianus](/saints/gratinianus), it says, were soldiers of the Roman army, and they were martyred at Perugia in Italy in the year 250, under the emperor Decius, whose reign the page dates 249 to 251.

Their holy relics, it adds, were translated in the year 979 to the city of Arona near Milan. That is the whole of the account: the page gives neither man a birthplace, an age, a rank in the army or a manner of death, and it names no judge. It prints no hymn and no epigram for them.

Because the calendar gives each of the two his own heading, each has his own folder here, and both carry this same account. This one is Felinus, of whom nothing is recorded apart from what he shares with his companion.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4395/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
