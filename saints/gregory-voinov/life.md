# Gregory (Voinov)

The Сретенский monastery's calendar names him second in its clause of fifteen for 25 ноября ст. ст. and carries no life for him; azbyka.ru prints one out of fond.ru. Григорий Александрович Воинов was born on 26 January 1875 at Теплый Стан in the province of Moscow, the son of a psalm-reader. He finished the Vifany seminary in 1898, took a post teaching in the parish school of the church of the Kazan icon by the Kaluga gate, married, and in time had six children. On 27 June 1900 he was ordained priest to the Trinity church of his own village, where he served until 1931.

In 1922, during the seizure of church valuables, he objected to the taking of a silver cross given to the church in the seventeenth century. The commission's man reported him for agitation the same day, writing that the priest had said in front of everyone that the gold and silver were being melted down into cigarette cases, and asked that measures be taken. He was arrested on 6 May and questioned on the sixteenth, and told the investigator that the cross weighed about half a pound, was three hundred years old, would be melted into a cigarette case in America in any case, and was a thing of great antiquity to them — and that he had carried on no agitation at all.

The revolutionary tribunal tried him in November and December 1922. He did not admit guilt, said he had never received the Patriarch's appeal and so had not read it out, and asked the tribunal to send him back to his family after eight months in prison. He was sentenced to three years, held on the Lubyanka and then in the Lefortovo and Taganka prisons, and released after seven months. He was given a pectoral cross in 1924, raised archpriest in 1928, and moved in 1931 to the church of the Archangel Michael at Кубинка.

He was arrested on 27 November 1937 and held in the Taganka prison. The charge was that on 25 October he had come to a meeting at the «Кубинский обувщик» stadium to nominate candidates for the Supreme Soviet, and when the head of the NKVD was proposed had waved his hand and said: «Все равно наших людей не наметят» — our people will not be put up anyway, the Soviet power puts up its own, there is nothing here for me — and then grumbled something and walked off. Questioned in the night of 29 November he said he kept company with nobody but his brother Peter, a priest; and he did not admit guilt. The troika sentenced him on 3 December, and he was shot on 8 December 1937 and buried in an unmarked common grave on the Butovo range outside Moscow.
