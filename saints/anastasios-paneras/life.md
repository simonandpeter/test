# Anastasios Paneras

saint.gr says that these victorious new martyrs of Christ were young men of Lesbos: Anastasios, twenty years old, came from Asomatoi, and [Demetrios](/saints/demetrios-begiazis), eighteen years old, from Agiasos on the island. Both of them suffered for the holy faith of Christ at Kasaba in Asia Minor, in the year 1816 or 1819 — the page gives the two years and chooses neither.

At Kasaba, it says, the two followed the trade of basket-makers, and probably they were even relatives. But along with the practice of their trade they preached the Gospel to the Christians who were living under the Turkish yoke, and for that reason the Turks arrested them, imprisoned them and tortured them horribly. Because they went on confessing and preaching their faith, they were hanged beneath a plane tree.

A magnificent church was raised at Agiasos in the name of both saints, the page ends, and it is there that their memory is kept on 11 August. The page gives no year of birth for either of them, no account of the trial, and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/11/index.aspx) and [the life of the two](https://www.saint.gr/778/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
