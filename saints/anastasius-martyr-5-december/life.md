# Anastasius (5 December)

Anastasius, the martyr of Christ, once saw how the holy martyrs were questioned and judged by the pagans and how their limbs were cut off for the confession of Christ; and because he was a Christian, and knew the good things God has prepared for martyrs, he desired to contend by the same road for Christ. The life doxologia prints gives no year, no city and no persecutor's name, and none is written here.

One day his heart grew hot in him, and he made the sign of the cross and went into the stadium. Standing in the middle of the gathered crowd he cried out with a loud voice: "I was found by those who did not seek me; I was made manifest to those who did not enquire after me." Every eye turned to him, and he said: "Listen, all you servants of the devil, listen. I am a Christian and I believe in my Lord Jesus Christ; and your idols and those who worship them I anathematise and hate."

The crowd was struck dumb at his courage. But at once they stripped him and scourged him bitterly, saying: "This is for your shamelessness and your insolence. But if you honour Christ and will not deny him, your head will be cut off and you will be food for the fishes, the creeping things and the birds." He did not deny him. Then the executioners seized him and cut off his head, and carrying it out a little way from the shore in a ship, threw it into the sea.
