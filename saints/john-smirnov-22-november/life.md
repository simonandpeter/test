# John (Smirnov) (22 November)

**The calendar calls him a deacon and his own life has him ordained priest ten years before he died.** The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him tenth of the sixteen it closes with 1937, and names his grade — «и Иоанна диакона» — and carries no life for him; the life here is what azbyka.ru prints for the civil 5 декабря out of pstgu.ru, under a slug of its own, `sv-ioann-smirnov-tretij`. Иван Яковлевич Смирнов was born on 12 November 1872 in the village of Пахомово in the Плещеево volost of the province of Tver, son of a corn merchant. Of his life before the revolution little is known; only that from 1895 to 1899 he served in the Tsar’s army.

**He was ordained deacon in 1922 by [Athanasius (Sakharov) of Kovrov](/saints/athanasius-sakharov-of-kovrov) and spent the next fourteen years being arrested in his circle.** He began to serve in the Данилов monastery in Moscow. In 1927 he was arrested and accused of «антисоветской пропаганде, пропаганде среди церковников мысли об избрании патриархом антисоветского деятеля митрополита Кирилла» — of propagating among churchmen the idea of electing as patriarch the metropolitan the corpus keeps as [Cyril of Kazan](/saints/cyril-of-kazan) — and sentenced to three years of exile in Siberia. In 1930, the term over, he was given a «минус» and settled at Владимир, serving in the Николо-Галейская church.

**The arrest of 1931 collapsed for want of evidence, and somewhere about then he became a priest.** On 3 November 1931 he was arrested again, charged with «участие в к/р группе церковников, близких еп. Афанасию (Сахарову), а/с сборища, помощь заключенным и ссыльным» — belonging to a counter-revolutionary group of churchmen close to the bishop, anti-Soviet gatherings, help to prisoners and exiles. After four months under investigation he was let out, the evidence being evidently too thin; and it was about that time, the article says, that he was ordained priest, going on to serve in the same church at Владимир. **The calendar’s word for him is nevertheless «диакон», and the `office` here follows the calendar, which is the source of record; the ordination is in the life.**

**The last arrest was in the bishop’s case, and the last but one took him to the Kazakh steppe.** On 26 April 1936 he was arrested again, tried in one case with the bishop who had ordained him, and sentenced to exile in Kazakhstan; in the village of Марьевка in the Тонкерейский district of the North Kazakhstan region he worked at a butter factory. On 24 November 1937 he was arrested in exile: «Находясь в ссылке в Казахстане, поддерживал связи с ссыльным епископатом, занимался контрреволюционной агитацией», ran the charge. Required to tell of his counter-revolutionary agitation he answered: «Контрреволюционной агитации я не проводил. Существовал на средства родственников – получал переводы» — he had carried on no agitation, and had lived on money his relatives sent him. The troika of the NKVD for North Kazakhstan condemned him to the highest measure of punishment, and on 4 December 1937 he was shot; his place of burial is unknown. That day was the Julian 21 ноября, the day before the one he is kept on.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 22 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261122.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9638.html), which carries no life; and the article azbyka.ru prints for the civil 5 декабря — [the entry](https://azbyka.ru/days/sv-ioann-smirnov-tretij); read 6 October 2026.*
