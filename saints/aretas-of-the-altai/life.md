# Aretas of the Altai

saint.gr names him and says in so many words that it has nothing to tell. Its line for 15 Μαΐου reads «Άγιος Αρέθας των Αλταΐων», Aretas of the Altai; its head line gives him one date, «(; - 1910)», with the year of his birth left open; and the whole of the body under it is the sentence «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ρώσου Αγίου» — we have no details of the Russian saint's life.

So nothing is set down here beyond that: no birthplace, no parents, no office, no monastery, no manner of death, no wonder, no hymn and no relics. The page sends its reader on to the synaxis of all the saints who shone in Siberia, which names nobody in particular, and to a page of the Orthodox Church in America. He is not the great-martyr Aretas of Nagran, whom the corpus keeps in October; he is a Russian of the Altai whom the Greek calendar keeps on this day and for whom it prints one sentence of honest ignorance.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 15 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/15/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4243/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
