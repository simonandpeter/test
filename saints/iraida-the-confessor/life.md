# Iraida

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Iraida among the confessors on 25 July and gives no life. Its line is «Св. Ираиды исп (1967).» — the holy confessor Iraida, 1967 — printed just so, with the abbreviation's point missing, and the calendar's own name page for her says only «Ираида, исповедница», Iraida, confessor.

There is nothing more: no surname, no place, no monastery, no rank in the church, which is rare among this calendar's modern entries and is worth saying rather than filling in. She is a confessor and not a martyr, and the calendar gives the year 1967 and no other.

The calendar keeps one other Russian form of this name, on another day and for a virgin martyr of the early fourth century, and the two have nothing in common but the name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 25 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260725.html); read 13 October 2026.*
