# Hyacinth, son of Theoclitus

doxologia.ro's calendar for 18 iulie gives Hyacinth a single sentence and no more: he was the son of the right-believing Theoclitus and Theopila, and he received a martyr's death, being pierced with a reed.

That is the whole of it. The page names no place, no emperor and no judge, gives no year, and prints no troparion for him; his parents are named there but the calendar does not keep them on this day. Nothing has been supplied from elsewhere.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 18 iulie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/18-iulie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-mucenic-iachint-0); read 24 September 2026.*
