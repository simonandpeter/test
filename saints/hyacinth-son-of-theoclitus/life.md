# Hyacinth, son of Theoclitus

doxologia.ro's calendar for 18 iulie gives Hyacinth a single sentence and no more: he was the son of the right-believing Theoclitus and Theopila, and he received a martyr's death, being pierced with a reed.

That is the whole of it. The page names no place, no emperor and no judge, gives no year, and prints no troparion for him; his parents are named there but the calendar does not keep them on this day. Nothing has been supplied from elsewhere.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 18 July, and saint.gr gives the names and the place. His parents were the devout Theocletus and Theonilla; his homeland was Amastris on the Euxine Sea, whose bishop in those years was Heraclides. While still a small child he showed the piety of a grown spiritual man, and at three years old he raised a dead child by calling on the name of Christ; so he grew in the stature of virtue as well as of body, and worked many wonders.

Then he saw the idolaters bowing down before a fruit tree, and out of holy indignation he went and cut it down. For that he was seized and brought before the governor Kastrinsios, who beat him without mercy and tore out his teeth; after which they dragged him outside the city with ropes and wounded him with sharpened reeds, and then threw him into prison, where he gave up his holy soul. The page gives no year for any of it and prints no hymn.
