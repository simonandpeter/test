# The Twelve Maidens

saint.gr's calendar for 31 Οκτωβρίου names «Άγιες Δώδεκα κόρες», and the page behind that line says one thing only: the holy Twelve Maidens were martyred, after they had been hung up in the middle of a colonnade. There is no city, no persecutor, no year, and not one of the twelve names. The calendar keeps them as a company, and that is the whole of what it tells.

The page sets an epigram over them which makes a figure of the manner of their death: the beam in the middle of the colonnade was a sundial, and it held the twelve maidens out as the twelve hours. That is the only other thing printed under the entry.

Because the twelve are named nowhere, this is one folder and not twelve, in the shape the corpus already uses for a company it cannot name. No hymn is printed. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands, bare as it is.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 31 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/31/index.aspx) and [their notice](https://www.saint.gr/1042/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
