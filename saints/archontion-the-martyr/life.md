# Archontion

Archontion was martyred by hunger — starving, the verse says, and so wearing down the ruler of this world, the devouring beast. That is the whole of the synaxarion’s notice for the martyr kept on 3 September, with its verse.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 3 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/935/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
