# The Twelve Soldier Martyrs (19 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 19 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιοι Δώδεκα Μάρτυρες Στρατιώτες» — twelve soldier martyrs — and gives them one sentence: they were martyred by the sword, perhaps in the time of [the hundred and fifty soldier martyrs](/saints/hundred-and-fifty-soldier-martyrs) whom the same calendar keeps on the same day. The «ίσως», the perhaps, is the page's own hedge and not a reader's. It names not one of the twelve.

The couplet set over the notice puts the words in their mouths, a dozen of the army telling the King of all that today they enlist with him by the sword. Beside them the day prints [Azes](/saints/azes-19-november), through whom the hundred and fifty believed, and [the wife and daughter](/saints/wife-and-daughter-of-aquilinus) of the eparch Aquilinus who condemned him.

The page gives them no country, no city, no year, no emperor and no hymn, and prints no relics for them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 19 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/19/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2906/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
