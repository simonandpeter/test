# Victorinus (Dobronravov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him third among the hieromartyrs of its last line for 15 декабря and gives him no life. The line is «Сщмчч. Александра и Василия, Викторина пресвитеров (1937).» — the hieromartyrs Alexander and Basil and Victorinus, presbyters, 1937 — and the calendar’s own name page adds the surname the line leaves out: «Викторин (Добронравов), иерей, сщмч.»

That is the whole of the record for him. Two readings hang behind the line, and both of them belong to the other two priests named in it and say nothing of a third man. He was a priest; he died in 1937, in the years the Russian church counts its new martyrs; and nothing is said here of how, or where, or at whose hands, because the source does not say it. The day page carries eight hymns for the other memories it heads, and none under this line’s name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 15 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261215.html); read 13 October 2026.*
