# Basil the Macedonian, Emperor

The memory of Basil is not in the Synaxaristes; it appears only in Gedeon’s Byzantine Heortologion (p. 161). As founder and restorer of many churches and monasteries, Basil (867–886) found a place in certain Menologia among the saints, for his piety and for enriching the capital with sacred buildings to the glory of God.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 29 August — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2327/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
