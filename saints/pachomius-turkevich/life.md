# Pachomius (Turkevich)

He was Pavel Akimovich Turkevich in the world, born in 1864 in the small town of Milyanovichi in the Kovel district of Volhynia province, in a blacksmith's family. He laboured in the Zlatoustov monastery in Moscow until it was closed in 1923. In the late twenties and early thirties, being by then an old man, he lived in Moscow with his spiritual children. He was an abbot, and that is the whole of what the life says about his life.

It gives him one answer. The investigator asked him what his attitude to the Soviet power was, and he said: I consider the Soviet power the power of antichrist, which has been sent by God to the people as a punishment. He was arrested on 29 September 1937 with [Demetrius (Dobroserdov)](/saints/demetrius-dobroserdov), archbishop of Mozhaysk, [Ambrose (Astakhov)](/saints/ambrose-astakhov) and six others.

The NKVD troika sentenced the company to be shot on 17 October 1937, and on 21 October he was shot at the Butovo range outside Moscow and buried in a common unmarked grave. He was numbered among the new martyrs and confessors of Russia for church-wide veneration at the Jubilee Council of Bishops in August 2000. The site's day line and his own index entry disagree about his rank, venerable-martyr against hieromartyr, and the disagreement is recorded on this folder rather than settled.
