# Capito (24 July)

saint.gr's whole notice of him is one sentence: the holy Capito was martyred by the sword. The day's line names him bare, «Άγιος Καπίτων», and the page gives no country, no city, no year, no emperor and no judge, prints no life beyond that sentence and no hymn. Its heading gives the feast as fixed, kept on 24 July of every year, and the entry carries no asterisk.

Above the notice the page sets a distich, and that is the only other thing it prints of him: «Ψυχής μεν όμμα παντεπόπτη Δεσπότη, Tείνει Kαπίτων τον τράχηλον δε ξίφει» — Capito stretches out the eye of his soul towards the all-seeing Master, and his neck towards the sword. The same Greek calendar keeps a Capito on 12 August, paired with another martyr, and a bishop of Cherson of that name on 7 March; both of those the corpus already holds, and this bare martyr of 24 July is a third entry.
