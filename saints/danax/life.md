# Danax

Danax lived in the second century at Avlona in Illyricum — the Albania of today, doxologia.ro's life says — and was reader in the church there. Once, when pagans burst into the church, he took up the vessels and the vestments to save them from falling into profane hands, and kept them in a fortified place about five miles from the town, towards the sea. That is the whole of what the page tells of his life before his arrest.

The unbelievers caught him and held him, and pressed him by every means to sacrifice to the god Dionysus. He withstood the pressure and stood firm in his faith in Christ, and they gave order that his head be struck off; his body was thrown into the sea. So, the life ends, the humble reader Danax received the crown of martyrdom. The page is a translation from saint.gr and gives no year beyond the century.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 16 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/16-ianuarie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-mucenic-danact-citetul); read 19 September 2026.*
