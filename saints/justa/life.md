# Justa

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Απριλίου names her and gives no life. Its whole notice of her is one sentence — «Η Οσία Ιούστα, αφού ασκήτεψε θεοφιλώς, κοιμήθηκε με ειρήνη» — that the venerable Justa, having lived the ascetic life in a way pleasing to God, fell asleep in peace. Nothing else is printed: no century, no country, no monastery, no kindred, and nothing at all about what her asceticism consisted in. The page adds only a gloss on the name, that Ιούστος comes from the Latin Justus and means the just.

Her couplet is the one thing the page says about her at any length: «Τρόπον σελήνης πλησιφαοῦς, Ἰούσταν, / Λάμψασαν ἔργοις, μνήματος κρύπτει νέφος» — the cloud of the tomb hides Justa, who shone by her works after the manner of the moon at the full. The feast type the page gives is the plain fixed one, «Σταθερή. Εορτάζει στις 26 Απριλίου εκάστου έτους», and that day is the only firm thing the source has to give.
