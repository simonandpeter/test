# Urbicius of Meung

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives him two sentences. The line for 3 June reads «Όσιος Ουρβίκιος», the venerable Urbicius, with no epithet at all, and the page behind it says this: the venerable Urbicius lived in the sixth century after Christ and practised asceticism together with the venerable [Liphardus](/saints/liphardus-of-orleans), whom the same day keeps, and whom he succeeded in the abbacy of the monastery of Meung; and having practised asceticism in a manner pleasing to God, the venerable Urbicius fell asleep in peace.

So what is recorded of him is a century, a companion whose life is the one the page tells, a house he inherited the charge of, and a death in peace with no year against it. The page gives him no birthplace, no office before the abbacy and no wonder; it prints neither verse nor hymn. The name of the monastery stands in this folder's slug because the day's line gives nothing else to tell him apart by.
