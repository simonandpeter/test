# Michael (1 May)

saint.gr names him and all but says it knows nothing. Michael, it prints, is unknown to the synaxarists and to the Menaia; a service dedicated to him exists, under 1 May, in Parisinus 1574, folio 153a, in which his ascetic virtues are hymned and the wondrous grace that welled up from his tomb. That is the whole of the notice, and the whole of what the Greek calendar records of him on this day.

So there is no century, no country, no monastery, no year of death, no account of the wonders and no relic — only a man who lived as an ascetic, a grave that healed, and a service copied into one Paris manuscript that has kept his day. The page prints no hymn from it.
