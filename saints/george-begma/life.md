# George (Begma)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him with five other men and gives no life. Its ninth entry for 10 июля ст. ст. reads «Сщмчч. Александра , Василия , Петра и Стефана пресвитеров, Георгия и Нестора диаконов (1918).» — the hieromartyrs Alexander, Basil, Peter and Stephen, presbyters, and George and Nestor, deacons, of the year 1918 — and behind each of the six names stands a link to a name page and to no reading at all.

His page, 13747, prints «Георгий (Бегма), диакон, сщмч.», a deacon and a hieromartyr, and beneath it «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 10 июля». That is the whole of it: a clergyman of that surname who died in 1918, the first year of the persecution, kept on this day, which is his own, and on the Julian 26 January with the synaxis of the New Martyrs and Confessors of Russia.

Where he served, how he was taken and how he died the calendar does not say, and nothing here is supplied from anywhere else. The other five of the line are [Alexander](/saints/alexander-popov), [Basil](/saints/basil-pobedonostsev), [Peter](/saints/peter-zefirov), [Stephen](/saints/stephen-lukanin) and [Nestor](/saints/nestor-gudzovsky), and nothing beyond their sharing the line and the year is said to join them.

The day page carries eleven hymn headings — three for the forty-five martyrs of Nicopolis, three for Anthony of the Caves, two for the Konevskaya icon and three for the laying up of the Lord's robe — and none under any of the six names. azbyka.ru, which might hold an account of him, was not read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 10 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260710.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13747.html), which name him and give no life; read 9 October 2026.*
