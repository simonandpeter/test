# Maximus (30 April)

saint.gr's calendar for 30 Απριλίου names him and gives no life. Its whole notice of him is one sentence — «Ο Άγιος Μάξιμος μαρτύρησε αφού του διαπέρασαν την κοιλιά με ξίφος» — that Maximus was martyred after they ran a sword through his belly. Nothing else is printed: no century, no country, no emperor and no judge, and the day's line gives him no epithet of any kind, only the name.

The gloss the page prints above that sentence, under the heading ΜΑΞΙΜΟΣ, is about the name and not the saint: it offers a philosopher from Tyre of the second century after Christ and a philosopher of the fourth century before it. His couplet plays on the letter xi in the middle of his name and the sword, ξίφος, in the middle of his belly. The feast type the page gives is the plain fixed one, «Σταθερή. Εορτάζει στις 30 Απριλίου εκάστου έτους».

The Russian calendar keeps him on 30 апреля and its line for him is «Мч. Максима .» — the martyr Maximus, and no year, where every other line on that day carries one in brackets. Its page under his name adds a single sentence: «Святой мученик Максим пострадал за веру во Христа и был прободен мечом в чрево», he suffered for the faith in Christ and was run through the belly with a sword. That is the whole of what this source says of him, and it is one detail more than the Greek calendar gives. No name form was stored for him here, because the only name the calendar prints is the bare forename, which eight folders of this corpus would then share.
