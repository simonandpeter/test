# Bithonius, companion of Elpidephorus

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 3 апреля ст. ст. in a line that names four men at once: «Мчч. Елпидифора , Дия , Вифония и Галика (III).» The life behind that line is titled for the four of them together — «Мученики Елпидифор, Дий, Вифоний и Галик» — and it is four sentences long. It says that the holy martyrs suffered for confessing the faith in Jesus Christ, and then gives each of them his own death; his is that he was drowned in the sea.

That is the whole of what the calendar has about him, and this folder says so rather than filling it out. It names no country and no city, no emperor and no judge, nothing of his birth, his trade or his age, and places him only by the «(III)» that closes the line, the third century. The name link behind the line carries his own page, number 429, and the four men have four different pages under the one line. The day page prints no hymn for any of the four; the only saint’s kontakion on it stands under another of the day’s names.

What the same sentence says of the others is in their own folders: [Elpidephorus](/saints/elpidephorus-the-martyr) was beheaded with the sword; [Dius](/saints/dius-companion-of-elpidephorus) was stoned; [Galicus](/saints/galicus-companion-of-elpidephorus) was given to the beasts.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 3 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260403.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life771.htm); read 5 October 2026.*
