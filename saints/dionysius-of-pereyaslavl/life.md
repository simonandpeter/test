# Dionysius of Pereyaslavl

saint.gr gives him one sentence. The venerable Dionysius lived in Russia and practised asceticism as a monk and a recluse in the monastery of Saint Nicholas at Pereyaslavl-Zalessky, it says, and he fell asleep in peace in the year 1645. That is the whole of the notice: it names no parents, no birthplace, no abbot, no year of his entry into the monastery and no wonder of his, and it gives the year of his death only.

The page prints no couplet and no hymn, and the day's own line names him bare, «Όσιος Διονύσιος του Περεγιασλάβλ», with the place and nothing further.
