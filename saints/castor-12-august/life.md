# Castor (12 August)

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for the day is «Όσιος Κάστωρ» and the whole of its notice is five words: «Ο Όσιος Κάστωρ απεβίωσε ειρηνικά.» The venerable Castor departed in peace. There is no year, no country, no monastery, no account of his death beyond that it was peaceful, and no hymn.

What the page does print is the distich the Greek books give him, and it is a small image of exactly that quiet end: «Θεία θρυαλλίς σβέννυται Κάστωρ μέγας, / Λείψαντος εἰς φῶς ὡς ἑλαίου τοῦ βίου» — Castor the great is quenched like a divine wick, his life having failed for the light as oil fails. The rest of the page is a gloss on the name, which it reads as the ornament, with the remark that a mythical hero and a rhetorician of Rhodes bore it.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/788/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
