# Boros

saint.gr names him and says in as many words that it cannot tell us who he was. Its whole entry is two sentences: the holy Boros is unknown to the synaxarists, and his memory is mentioned, together with an Isidore and a Theodore, in the Jerusalem Kanonarion, in the edition of the archimandrite Kallistos, at page ninety-three. That is the only witness the page offers for him, and it offers it plainly, with an editor and a page number.

There is nothing else. The page gives him no country, no century, no rank, no manner of death and no relics, prints no synaxarion couplet and no hymn, and does not say which Isidore and which Theodore stand beside him in that book. He is kept on 21 Μαΐου, and what is kept is a name.
