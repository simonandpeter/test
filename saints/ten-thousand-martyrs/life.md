# The Ten Thousand Martyrs

saint.gr's calendar for 18 Μαρτίου keeps a company it calls the Myriad Martyrs, and glosses the number on their own page: «Οι Άγιοι Μύριοι (10.000) Μάρτυρες». Of them it says two things and no more — that it is unknown where and when they were martyred, and that perhaps they were perfected by the sword at Nicomedia in the year 300. The «perhaps» is the page's own, and no year and no place have been written into this folder from a sentence that offers them as a guess.

The entry names no member of the company and enumerates none, so it is kept here as one entry rather than as ten thousand. Over it saint.gr prints a couplet: ten thousand men, having had their necks cut through, went away to where the minds of ten thousand eyes are. That is the whole of what the page prints; there is no hymn, no relic and no persecutor by name.

The corpus keeps a company of twenty thousand burned at Nicomedia on 28 December, and the number, the day and the manner of death are all different here; nothing on this page identifies the one with the other, and the only thing joining them is the guess the page itself marks as a guess.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/18/index.aspx) and [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2944/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
