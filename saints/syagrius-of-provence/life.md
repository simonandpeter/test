# Syagrius of Provence

saint.gr says he lived in the eighth century and came from Gaul, and gives him two sentences and nothing more. He became a monk on the island of Lérins and later founded a monastery in the region of Provence; and he fell asleep in peace in the year 787, in the city of Nice in Gaul.

The page gives him no birth year, no family, no name for his monastery and no account of his ascetic life beyond the naming of the two places, and it prints no hymn to him. Nothing further about him is recorded here.
