# The 3,628 Martyrs of Nicomedia

The three thousand six hundred and twenty-eight martyrs of Nicomedia suffered under Diocletian and Maximian. They were Christians come from Alexandria, who had believed in Christ after the killing of Peter, archbishop of Alexandria; taking their wives and children with them they came to Nicomedia and gave themselves up to torture of their own will, crying, we are Christians. Diocletian tried at first to persuade them to deny Christ, and seeing them unbending ordered them all beheaded and their bodies thrown into a mountain ravine; many years later their relics were discovered by various signs of grace. The Greek synaxarion keeps them on 4 September.
