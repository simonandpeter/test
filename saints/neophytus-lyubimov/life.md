# Neophytus (Lyubimov)

The Russian calendar names him in a line of four and gives no life at all. Its 17 October old style has «Сщмчч. Неофита и Анатолия пресвитеров, прмчч. Иакинфа и Каллиста (1918)» — the hieromartyrs Neophytus and [Anatolius](/saints/anatolius-ivanovsky), presbyters, and the monk-martyrs [Hyacinth](/saints/hyacinth-pitatelev) and [Callistus](/saints/callistus-oparin), 1918 — and because the line names all four of them, each has a folder of his own. The year is the line's and covers the four.

His page in the calendar's index of names adds his surname and a rank the day line does not give him: «Неофит (Любимов), протоиерей, сщмч.», Neophytus Lyubimov, archpriest and hieromartyr, where the day line has only presbyter. It also gives the second day he is kept on, 26 January, among the new martyrs and confessors of Russia; and then it stops. There is no life link on either page.

So what is known here is a priest of the Russian church, by his own index page an archpriest, killed in 1918, the first year of the persecution, and remembered by name on the Julian 17 October with three others who died in the same year. Where he served, how he died and whether the four died together the calendar does not say, and nothing is supplied for it.

azbyka.ru is the source that carries lives for the new martyrs of the twentieth century, and it would not be read: asked for its page for this run of days, it answered 403 to the fetcher this corpus uses, on 28 October 2026. The refusal is recorded because it is the reason this page is short rather than a judgement about the man.

*After the Russian church calendar of the Sretensky monastery (days.pravoslavie.ru) for 17 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261017.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9361.html), which is all there is; read 30 October 2026. The same menologion day was read in the other three calendars and does not keep him — [saint.gr](https://www.saint.gr/10/17/index.aspx), [doxologia.ro](https://doxologia.ro/17-octombrie) and the [Ohrid Prologue](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-10-30&prolog=1).*
