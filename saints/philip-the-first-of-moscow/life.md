# Philip the First of Moscow

saint.gr says he lived in the fifteenth century and was bishop of Suzdal from 1455. He was chosen metropolitan of Moscow in 1464 by Theognostus, metropolitan of Moscow, and by the grand prince Ivan III Vasilyevich, whose reign the page dates 1462 to 1505. saint.gr gives him no year of birth and tells nothing of his life before the see of Suzdal.

The new metropolitan began his work at once, the page says, and started the raising of the Uspensky cathedral. He took careful thought for the education of the clergy and for the cultivating of a priestly character in them. He fell ill on 4 April 1473 and reposed in peace; after his repose his ascetic life was uncovered, for he had worn heavy chains upon him for his discipline. The Greek calendar tells him from the later metropolitan Philip by the numeral it puts on his name.
