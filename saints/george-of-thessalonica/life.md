# George of Thessalonica

Almost nothing is known of him, and doxologia's page says so. He is remembered in the Synodikon of the metropolis of Thessalonica, where he stands after Niketas, whom the page dates 1020, and before bishop Theophanes, whom it dates 1031 to 1038; that place in the list is the whole of the evidence for when he lived, and the page draws no year from it and neither does this folder.

Oral tradition, the page adds, reports him as having come from the Vatopedi cell. His icon is kept in the nave of the Vatopedi monastery and is dated 1312, and there, it says, the natural features of his face must be preserved. His commemoration is unknown to the synaxaria; he is honoured together with the saints of Vatopedi on 10 July.

The page names the book it is taken from: Moses the Athonite, The Saints of the Holy Mountain, Mygdonia, Karyes, 2008, pages 171 to 172. It gives no account of his episcopate, no wonders, no year of death and no place of burial, and this folder adds nothing to it.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 10 July, and saint.gr says where the memory comes from and how little else there is. The archbishop George of Thessalonica is named in the Synodikon of Thessalonica, in the twenty-ninth place — an important text, the page says, which was purged from time to time of men who had strayed from dogmatic exactness — and the position of his name in it fixes his episcopate in the third decade of the eleventh century. His portrait in the sanctuary of the monastery of Vatopedi is, it says, probably unique: he is shown in a bust in the space of the prothesis, on the north wall above the window of the lavabo, as a bishop in a light-coloured phelonion and omophorion, beardless, blessing with his right hand and holding a closed Gospel in his left, with the inscription Ο ΑΓΙΟΣ ΓΕΩΡΓΙΟΣ ΘΕΣΣΑΛΟΝΙΚΗΣ. A tradition of Vatopedi holds that he came from a cell outside the monastery, which the page says would account for his being painted in its katholikon. His memory is unknown to the synaxaria; he is honoured with the Vatopedi saints on 10 July, and keeps feast as well at the synaxis of all the saints of Thessalonica.
