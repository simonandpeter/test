# George of Thessalonica

Almost nothing is known of him, and doxologia's page says so. He is remembered in the Synodikon of the metropolis of Thessalonica, where he stands after Niketas, whom the page dates 1020, and before bishop Theophanes, whom it dates 1031 to 1038; that place in the list is the whole of the evidence for when he lived, and the page draws no year from it and neither does this folder.

Oral tradition, the page adds, reports him as having come from the Vatopedi cell. His icon is kept in the nave of the Vatopedi monastery and is dated 1312, and there, it says, the natural features of his face must be preserved. His commemoration is unknown to the synaxaria; he is honoured together with the saints of Vatopedi on 10 July.

The page names the book it is taken from: Moses the Athonite, The Saints of the Holy Mountain, Mygdonia, Karyes, 2008, pages 171 to 172. It gives no account of his episcopate, no wonders, no year of death and no place of burial, and this folder adds nothing to it.
