# Athanasius the Wonderworker

saint.gr says that from a young age Athanasius gave himself with his whole soul to the service of the Gospel, preaching it and doing it, so that God gave him the power to heal sicknesses of body and of spirit. His prayers gave health to many, and for that reason many ran to him or called him to their houses.

After a time he wanted to live among the men of the monastic life. So he went to a monastery near the river Sagaris whose abbot was a former senator; but worldly passions had got into that house, and at the abbot's request Athanasius agreed to work for the correction of the unruly monks. His efforts had an astonishing success: his warm word, his blameless example, his brotherly entreaties and his all-night supplications to God brought about what was hoped for, and compunction and holiness entered souls that had been rough and hard before.

Afterwards Athanasius went to the monastery of Trajan, where he stayed as a priest. There he made religious books, which he sold, spending the money on the poor. He died peacefully in deep old age. The page gives no year for any of this, and no country; it prints a couplet that says immortality's crown belongs to Athanasius, with holiness and the wonders themselves.
