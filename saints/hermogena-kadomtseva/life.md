# Hermogena (Kadomtseva)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names her and gives no life. Its line for 28 мая ст. ст. reads «Прмц. Гермогены (1942).» — one name, a rank and the year 1942 — and the name carries a link to a name page and none to a reading. The name page behind this one prints the family name and the degree, «Гермогена (Кадомцева), инокиня, прмц.», and beyond the title one line, «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 28 мая», and nothing further.

So the calendar sets down a member of the Russian church who died in 1942, with a degree and a family name, and no more than that. The second of those two days is not a feast of her own: it is the general commemoration of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which this calendar prints beside the proper day of each of them, and it is the one mark on her page that places her among that company. She is kept here on the day the calendar gives her for her own, and the synaxis is recorded on her attestation.

Nothing else about her was found. No folder in this corpus held the name before.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 28 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260528.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14461.html), which name her and give no life; read 8 October 2026.*
