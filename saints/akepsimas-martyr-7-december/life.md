# Akepsimas (7 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 7 December keeps three martyrs in one entry and names all three: [Isidore](/saints/isidore-martyr-7-december), Akepsimas and [Leo](/saints/leo-martyr-7-december). The whole of the life it gives them is one sentence — that the holy Isidore, Akepsimas and Leo were martyred by fire. No country, no reign, no persecutor and no year is printed for any of them.

The couplet the page sets over the entry is addressed to Isidore and speaks of the other two only as his fellow contestants in the fire, so nothing is said of Akepsimas apart from his name and the shared sentence. The Greek calendar keeps another Akepsimas on 29 January, whom the corpus already holds; that is a separate entry and this one is recorded as its own.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3186/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
