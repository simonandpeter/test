# Alexander (Pozdeevsky)

The Russian calendar's 12 October names him in six words: «Сщмч. Александра пресвитера (1940)» — Alexander, hieromartyr, presbyter, 1940. «Сщмч.» is the mark of a priest put to death for the faith, and «пресвитер» is the simple priesthood rather than the archpriesthood. The site's index of names supplies the surname the day line withholds and says the grade a second way, heading his page «Александр (Поздеевский), иерей, сщмч.»

That is the whole record. Neither page gives a birth, a diocese, a parish, a charge or a place of death, and 1940 is late enough in the campaign against the clergy that the men taken in it were often tried for a second or a third time. His index entry keeps him here and on 26 January with the whole company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and carries no life. The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian saints are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026 even with a browser user-agent, so the line stands as the line.
