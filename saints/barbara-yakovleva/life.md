# Barbara (Yakovleva)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps her with the grand duchess she served, in one line for 5 июля ст. ст.: «Прмцц. вел. кн. Елисаветы и инокини Варвары (1918).» — the venerable martyrs, the grand duchess Elizabeth and the nun Barbara. Her own reading says that where she came from and out of what sort of household nobody knows: «Откуда, и из какой среды пришла в обитель сестра Варвара — нам неизвестно.» It gives her surname, Яковлева, and says that those close to the house called her Варя.

She was a cross-sister of the Martha and Mary house of mercy in Moscow and one of the first to follow its foundress into it, the cell-attendant of the superior and one of the sisters closest to her; and she was not lifted up by it, the reading says, but stayed gentle and within reach of everybody. Of her own will she went after her superior into suffering and death, fulfilling the Lord’s word that there is no greater love than to lay down one’s life for one’s friends.

In 1918 the prisoners were brought to Alapaevsk in Siberia. At Yekaterinburg the two sisters who had come with [Elizabeth of Russia](/saints/elizabeth-of-russia) were set free, but Barbara made them keep her with the grand duchess, and when she was threatened she answered that she was ready to share her superior’s lot; being the elder of the two, it was she who was sent back. On the night of 5 (18) июля 1918 the prisoners were driven out towards the village of Sinyachikha and thrown down the shaft of an abandoned mine, beaten with rifle butts as they went.

The longer reading adds that months afterwards, when the bodies were brought up out of the shaft, the fingers of the two women and of one of the princes were set for the sign of the cross; and that in 1920, as the White army fell back, the coffins were carried to Jerusalem, where their relics now rest, the reading says, «в храме равноапостольной Марии Магдалины у подножия Елеонской горы», at the foot of the Mount of Olives. Her own reading closes by saying that she accomplished her struggle in her thirty-fifth year, and no year of birth is recorded here from it.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 5 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260705.html) and the two readings behind [its index of her lives](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/id2426.htm); read 9 October 2026.*
