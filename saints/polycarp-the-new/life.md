# Polycarp the New

saint.gr's calendar for 7 Ιουλίου names him and gives no life. The whole of its account is one sentence, «Ο Άγιος Πολύκαρπος ο νέος μαρτύρησε αφού τον θανάτωσαν με μαχαίρι» — the holy Polycarp the New was martyred after they put him to death with a knife. The page names no country, no city, no century, no year, no emperor and no judge, and it prints no hymn under his line.

Besides that sentence it carries only the couplet the synaxaria print for him: «Oύτως όναιο σων σφαγεύ θελημάτων, / Σφάττειν τάχει με τον Πολύκαρπον θέλων» — so may you have the good of your own wishes, butcher, since you wish to butcher me, Polycarp, in haste. The epithet «ο νέος», the New, is the only thing besides his day and his death that the source gives him. That is everything it says of him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/689/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
