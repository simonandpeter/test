# Vladislav of Serbia

Vladislav was king of Serbia, the son of King [Stefan](/saints/stefan-the-first-crowned). The Russian life says he reigned seven years and was known for his kindness and mercy to the sick, to pilgrims and to the poor, and that he built the monastery at Mileševa, where he died in 1239 and was buried; the Russian calendar’s own line for the day dates him after 1264, and the two are left standing side by side.

The Ohrid Prologue, for the Serbian calendar, says he built the monastery of Mileševa and brought to it from Trnovo the relics of [Saint Sava](/saints/sava-of-serbia); that he was marked out by an uncommon mercy to the wretched; and that on the coins of his time he signed himself “servant of Christ Vladislav”.
