# Maximus, brother of Dometius

Maximus and his brother [Dometius](/saints/dometius-brother-of-maximus) were, saint.gr says, shoots of a lordly root, brought up with every ease and honour that their father's imperial station gave them; the page does not name the father. Their striking looks and the comfort written on their faces bore no witness to the humble and struggling mind of their hearts. Out of love for Christ they came one day to Abba [Macarius](/saints/macarius-the-egyptian) asking to be received as his disciples and fellow ascetics, and the practised eye of the teacher of the desert was deceived: he thought that young men so well reared would not bear the desert's hard trial, yet he did not send them away, and showed them a place to build a small cell of stone.

For three whole years they struggled beyond what is human and came to a very high measure of virtue, giving Abba Macarius no sign of life; they went out only to church, to communicate, and were silent there. At last Macarius came to visit them. They greeted him in silence, set a little plain food and three rusks before him after the ninth hour, and asked him by a sign whether he would go or stay the night. At the morning prayer he saw, at every verse of the psalter they said, a lamp of fire come out of their mouths and reach to heaven, and he asked them to pray for him.

The elder brother had come to perfection, the page says, while the younger was still under assault from the evil one and guarded by an angel. A few days later the elder fell asleep, and three days after him the younger. Macarius thereafter set them before the other ascetics as patterns, and showed the place, saying: come and see the place of the witness of the young men who were sanctified by unceasing prayer and by silence. The two are much honoured in the desert of Nitria, and the monastery raised where they laboured is called by the Egyptians today «Μονή Baramous», the monastery of the Romans. saint.gr's day line names Maximus first of the two.
