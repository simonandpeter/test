# Theodore of Rostov and Suzdal

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives him three sentences, of which one says there is nothing more. The line for 8 June reads «Όσιος Θεόδωρος Επίσκοπος Ροστώφ και Σούζνταλ Ρωσίας», the venerable Theodore, bishop of Rostov and Suzdal in Russia, and the page behind it says this: the venerable Theodore was a Russian bishop; he died in 1023; we have no other details of his life.

What the page does have is his relic. Under the heading of holy relics it says that parts of the saint's incorrupt relic are in the church of the Panagia of Kazan at Suzdal in Russia, and that an incorrupt finger of his is in the church of Saints Constantine and Helen at Suzdal. So what is recorded of him is two sees, a year, a country, and two churches that keep pieces of him — and no birth, no consecration, no work and no hymn. The Russian form of his name is not on the page and is not invented here.
