# Philotheus, founder of Philotheou

doxologia.ro's calendar keeps Philotheus on 24 ianuarie, and what it prints of him is three sentences. He was, it says, true to the name he bore — Philotheos, the lover of God — and he was the first founder of the monastery of Philotheou. As a father he left his holy icon behind him, in place of his relics, for the consolation of those who came after; and that icon, as a hallowed treasure, is venerated by the monks of Philotheou.

That is all the page gives. It tells nothing of where he came from, nothing of how the house was founded or under whom, and nothing of his death; the only date on the page is in the heading of its own article about him, which calls him Philotheos the founder, of the end of the tenth century. There is no troparion and no kontakion for him there.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 24 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/24-ianuarie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/cuviosul-filotheos-ctitorul), which carries no separate life; read 19 September 2026.*
