# Procopius of Sázava

Procopius was born in Bohemia, in the village of Chotouň, the Russian calendar says, and as a priest laboured much at planting Christianity in the Czech land. On the river Sázava he founded a monastery dedicated to St John the Forerunner, and he died there in 1053. That is all its life prints; the day’s line calls him abbot of Sázava.

The Ohrid Prologue keeps him on the Julian 1 April, which is not the day the Russian calendar keeps him on, and tells the same life with three things in it that the Sretensky account does not have. He was born at Chotouň in Bohemia, it says, of parents of note; he became a priest, and withdrew into a mountain to live after the pattern of the desert fathers of the East. The duke Ulrich came upon him there by chance, and helped him to found the monastery of St John the Forerunner by the river Sázava. This holy man reposed, the entry ends, in the year 1053 — the same year the Russian life gives, so nothing in this folder's date was altered. The duke is named here as the Prologue names him and is not identified with anybody; he was not read as any folder in this corpus.
