# Basil (Nadezhdin)

Basil Nadezhdin was born on 12 January 1895 at Moscow, in the family of Fyodor Alexeyevich Nadezhdin, a servant of the Palace Administration, and his wife Sofia Pavlovna. He was the fifth of six children, three sons and three daughters. Many of the Nadezhdins had served in church as psalmists, and Fyodor Alexeyevich's own father had been a deacon.

Family tradition, the Russian life says, remembered that the boy Basil once walked an old priest home, and that the priest blessed him with an icon and foretold that he too would become a priest. His parents were his example of faith and piety from his early years and brought their children up in the Christian faith; in old age his father wrote that he had heard the saying, children are the blessing of God, and looked back on his life by it.

The life's long middle account of his schooling, his ordination, his parish and the imprisonment in which he died was not read in this pass, and nothing is set down here in its place. What the end of the page says is that at the jubilee Council of Bishops in 2000 the priest Basil Nadezhdin was numbered among the New Martyrs and Confessors of Russia, and that his memory is kept on the day of his repose, 19 February — which is the civil date of the 6 февраля on which the calendar's day page prints him. The page also prints a troparion and a kontakion for him, both in the second tone, which are copied into this folder.
