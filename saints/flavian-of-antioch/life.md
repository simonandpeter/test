# Flavian of Antioch

The Ohrid Prologue keeps him on the Julian 20 July, in one entry with [Elias of Jerusalem](/saints/elias-of-jerusalem), and gives the two of them five sentences between them. They were great zealots for the faith, it says, and defenders of Orthodoxy. Both died in exile, where the heretical emperor Anastasius had banished them. And they saw exactly, beforehand, the death of the emperor Anastasius and their own.

For at the same time, from places far apart, the two of them wrote to one another: the emperor Anastasius has died today, let us go with him to the judgement of God. And after two days both the saints came to their end, in the year 518.

This is all the entry says of him. It gives him no birth, no country, no account of his patriarchate and no word of the exile beyond the fact of it; the see in his heading is the whole of what tells him from the other men of his name, and the emperor who banished him is named as a heretic and is no saint.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 20. јул ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-08-02&prolog=1); read 6 October 2026.*
