# Basil (Sokolov, 20 March)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and carries no life. Its line for the twentieth of March, old style, reads «Сщмч. Василия диакона (1938).» — the hieromartyr Basil, deacon — and the name page behind that «Василия» is headed «Василий (Соколов), протодиакон, сщмч.», giving both the surname the line leaves out and a grade the line reduces: he was a protodeacon. That page lists two days, the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church on «26 января» and this twentieth of March.

The surname is among the commonest in Russia and this calendar keeps three men of this name and surname, each on a page and a day of its own. The other two are a priest it keeps on the sixteenth of November, old style, and an archpriest on the twenty-seventh, both of whom it puts in 1937. This man is a protodeacon of 1938. Three name pages, three days, three grades and two years are the whole of what tells them apart, and nothing read puts any two of the three in one sentence.

Nothing else is said of him on either page: no place, no diocese, no parish, no year of birth and no account of how he died. The 1938 the line closes with is the whole of his chronology. The second source for the Russian new martyrs, azbyka.ru, was not read for him.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 20 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260320.html); read 8 October 2026.*
