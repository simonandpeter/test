# Hanani

The prophet whom the Romanian calendar names Anania lived in the days of the king of Judah, about the year 955 before Christ. At first King Asa, with God's help, threw back in victory the invasion of the king of Syria against him; but later, turning aside from his hope in the Lord, Asa made an agreement with the king of Syria and sent him gold and silver, that he might be his support in the war against the kingdom of Israel.

Then the prophet came before King Asa and rebuked him sharply for his unfaith, as it is written in the second book of Paralipomena — the passage doxologia.ro cites, 2 Chronicles 16, 7–10, where he is Hanani the seer. The king was angry and ordered the prophet imprisoned. At the last, the prophet passed to the Lord in peace.

doxologia.ro carries no fuller life for him than this, and no troparion.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and tells the same episode with the chapter and verse attached. saint.gr sets him in the years of the king of Judah, about 955 before Christ: king Asa had at first, with God's help, beaten back victoriously the invasion of the king of Syria, but afterwards made a treaty with that same king of Syria and sent him gold and silver to help him in the war against the kingdom of Israel. It was then that the prophet Hanani came before Asa and rebuked him in the sharpest terms — the page points to 2 Chronicles 16:7-10 — and Asa in his anger imprisoned the prophet. In the end, the page says, he died peacefully. Its couplet is on the threefold gift of prophecy: «Τριὰς τρίπους σός, Ἀνανὶ Θεοπρόπε, Δι' ἧς τὸ μέλλον προὔλεγες πρὸ τοῦ τέλους» — the Trinity is your tripod, Hanani, seer of God, by which you foretold what was coming before the end.
