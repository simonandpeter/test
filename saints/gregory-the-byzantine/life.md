# Gregory the Byzantine

He is named third in a line of five and a great many unnamed. The Сретенский monastery's calendar for 28 ноября ст. ст. prints «Мчч. Стефана , Василия , Григория , другого Григория , Иоанна и иных многих (VIII).» — martyrs Stephen, Basil, Gregory, another Gregory, John and many others, of the eighth century — and the name page behind his place gives him the by-name the line omits: «Григорий Византийский, мч.».

The one life the calendar carries for the five is two sentences long and is the whole of the record: they suffered for the honouring of the holy icons together with [Stephen the New](/saints/stephen-the-new), with whom they were shut up in the prison, and after his martyr's end they were put to death. Nothing is said of their homes, their trades, their ages or the manner of their killing, and the calendar numbers the others of the company without naming one of them.

The Roman numeral the line closes with, VIII, is the whole of the dating; the year of the confessor they were imprisoned with, which this calendar gives as 767, is his and is not written here as theirs.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 28 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261128.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life3244.htm); read 7 October 2026.*
