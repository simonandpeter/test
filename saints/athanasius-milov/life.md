# Athanasius (Milov)

**A three-class village school was his whole education, and the one charge against him was that he had been nominated for the Supreme Soviet.** The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him ninth of the sixteen it closes with 1937 and gives him his grade where it has governed the eight before him with one word — «Афанасия пресвитера» — and carries no life for him; the life here is what azbyka.ru prints for the civil 5 декабря out of pstgu.ru. Афанасий Дмитриевич Милов was born in 1877 in the village of Солдатское in the Нижний Ломов district of the province of Пенза, and finished the three-class school of his own village. Until 1930 he served as priest in the village of Щучье, in what is now the Мамлютский district of the North Kazakhstan region.

**He kept up with the one church in Петропавловск that had not gone over to the Renovationists**, the Никольская church, whose clergy, the article says, «сохранившим верность Православию во время распространения обновленчества» kept their faithfulness to Orthodoxy while the schism spread — the same church that [Basil (Bova)](/saints/basil-bova), named three places before him on the same line of the same calendar, attended in those years. He lived at Кузнецк, moved in 1932 to the village of Прудки, and then returned to Щучье.

**In 1937 the parish council put him up for the Supreme Soviet and the collective farmers rejected him; that nomination became the charge.** He was arrested on 28 November 1937, accused of counter-revolutionary activity expressed in an intention «бойкотировать выборы в Верховный Совет», to boycott the elections to the Supreme Soviet. He did not admit himself guilty. The troika of the regional NKVD administration for North Kazakhstan sentenced him on 2 December 1937, and the article says he was shot by that sentence without saying on what day; his place of burial is unknown. He was glorified by the Council of Bishops of the Russian Church in 2000.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 22 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261122.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9479.html), which carries no life; and the article azbyka.ru prints for the civil 5 декабря — [the entry](https://azbyka.ru/days/sv-afanasij-milov); read 6 October 2026.*
