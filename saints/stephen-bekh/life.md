# Stephen (Bekh)

saint.gr says the holy hieromartyr Stephen, Valerij Stepanovich Bekh in the world, was born in the year 1872 at Zhitomir, near the country of Vologda as the page puts it. He followed courses at the seminary of Saint Petersburg, and in 1903 entered the theological academy of Moscow; on 20 December of the same year he was tonsured a monk and took the name Stephen. When he finished his studies he was appointed head of the theological school of Solikamsk, and later of those of Mingrelia and of Bezhetsk.

On 8 October 1914 he gave up the direction of the theological school and presented himself to the army, in order to serve the religious needs of the soldiers. From the end of 1915 until 1918 he directed the theological school of Kargopol and served as archimandrite in the Lavra of Saint Alexander Nevsky.

On 9 October 1921 he was elected bishop of Izhevsk, which in church order belonged to the jurisdiction of Sarapul. His missionary work led to his arrest: in 1924 he was shut in the Taganka prison at Moscow, and from 1924 to 1926 in the country of Solovki, to be set free a little afterwards. In 1929 he was arrested again by the authorities and confined to the village of Pomozdino. On 7 September 1932 they shut him in prison once more, and there, after hardships and torments, in the year 1933, he gave up his spirit to the Lord.

The page gives no day for that death, no place for the last prison, and no grave; it lists no relic and prints neither couplet nor hymn.
