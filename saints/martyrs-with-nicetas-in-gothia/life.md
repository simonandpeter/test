# The Martyrs with Nicetas in Gothia

These holy martyrs were martyred with [Nicetas the Goth](/saints/nicetas-the-goth); a multitude of the devout contend in Gothia, the verse says, having cast the barbarian mind underfoot. That is the whole of the Greek synaxarion’s notice for 15 September; this entry records it and nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 15 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2432/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
