# Severus, companion of Peter and Leucius

saint.gr's calendar for 11 Ιανουαρίου names three martyrs in one line, «Άγιοι Πέτρος, Σεβέριος και Λεύκιος οι Μάρτυρες», and the page behind the line has one sentence for all three: «Οι Άγιοι Πέτρος, Σεβέριος και Λεύκιος, μαρτύρησαν στην Αλεξάνδρεια» — [Peter](/saints/peter-companion-of-leucius), Severus and [Leucius](/saints/leucius-companion-of-peter) were martyred at Alexandria. Severus stands second of the three, and the Greek spells his name Σεβέριος.

That is all the calendar has. It names no emperor and no governor, gives no year, says nothing of how the three died or what they were charged with, and prints no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/11/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1093/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
