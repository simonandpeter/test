# John (Kalinin)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names John among the confessors on 24 July and gives no life. Its line is «Свв. Николая (1942) и Иоанна (1951) испп., пресвитеров.» — the holy confessors and presbyters Nicholas, 1942, and John, 1951 — and the calendar's own name page for him adds both a surname and a place: «Иоанн (Калинин, Оленевский), иерей, исп.» — John Kalinin, priest, with a second parenthesised word, «Оленевский», which this folder stores as part of his Russian name and does not translate, nothing read saying what place it names.

He stands second on the line, after [Nicholas (Pongelsky)](/saints/nicholas-pongelsky), the priest with whom he shares the entry; the two died nine years apart and the calendar keeps them together. Here line and name page agree about the grade, which they do not for his companion: he is a confessor and a priest in both.

That is the whole of the record. He was a priest; he died in 1951; the calendar keeps him on this day. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 24 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260724.html); read 13 October 2026.*
