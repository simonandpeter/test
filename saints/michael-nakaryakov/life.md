# Michael (Nakaryakov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Michael among the hieromartyrs on 22 July and gives no life. Its line is «Сщмч. Михаила пресвитера (1918).» — the hieromartyr Michael the presbyter, 1918 — and the calendar's own name page for him adds the surname the line leaves out: «Михаил (Накаряков), иерей, сщмч.»

That is the whole of the record. He was a priest; he died in 1918; the calendar keeps him on this day among the new martyrs of Russia. The day page carries troparia and kontakia for three of the memories it heads, and none under his name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 22 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260722.html); read 10 October 2026.*
