# John Koulakiotis

The new martyr John came of Koulakia, the town saint.gr identifies with today's Chalastra, also called Pyrgos, and he was a schoolmaster. For a time he lived on the Holy Mountain, though the page is careful to say it is not made clear whether he had taken the monastic habit or was a layman; he had a disciple there called Argyres.

He was at Thessalonica when, in the course of a banquet with Turks, they claimed that he had supposedly told them he wished to become a Mohammedan himself. John denied the claim flatly, and for that he was taken to the bazaar — probably the central market of the city, the page says — and put to death there without a trial, by hanging, on a Tuesday at four in the morning, in the year 1776. After the hanging the new martyr's body was thrown into the sea.

A service in his honour was composed by Gerasimos Mikragiannanites and another by Archimandrite Joel Frangakos, metropolitan of Edessa. The page itself prints no hymn.
