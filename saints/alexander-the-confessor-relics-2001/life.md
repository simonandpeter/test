# Alexander the Confessor (relics found 2001)

The Russian calendar keeps on 5 September (Julian) the finding in 2001 of the relics of the venerable Alexander the confessor, and prints no life under the line; this entry records the line and nothing more, and says nothing of who he was beyond what it says.

*After the day’s line of the Sretensky calendar (days.pravoslavie.ru), 5 September (Julian) — [the page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260905.html), which prints no life; read 23 August 2026.*
