# Alexander the Confessor (relics found 2001)

Alexander — George Andreyevich Urodov in the world — was born in 1882 in a
peasant family at Nevezhkino in the Chembar district of Penza province,
finished the zemstvo school and was made a singer of his parish church. In
1901, on a journey to Sarov, he stopped at the Sanaksar monastery and
resolved there to give his life to the monastic labour; he was tonsured on
30 August 1911 with the name Alexander, ordained hierodeacon soon after at
Tambov and hieromonk on 22 July 1913, and on 20 July 1915 the brethren chose
him superior of Sanaksar and Archbishop Cyril (Smirnov) made him igumen. In
the revolution and the red terror part of the brethren, led by a hieromonk
Vladimir with the Bolsheviks behind him, rose in the monastery at the end of
February 1918 and deposed the igumen; by order of the military-revolutionary
tribunal he was arrested on 27 March 1918, admitted nothing at the
interrogation, and was banished from the monastery. On 3 April 1922 the
Synod made him archimandrite and superior of the Seven Lakes hermitage in
the Kazan diocese; there too the authorities turned on him, prosecuting him
the same year over the seizure of church valuables, and in spite of
everything he became that year a leading fighter against Renovationism in
the Kazan country, the hermitage under him its centre. On 31 October 1928 he
was arrested and given three years in the camps and three of exile in the
Urals; after the exile he was in the Vyatka diocese, and on 5 August 1935
Bishop Demetrius (Pospelov) of Yaransk sent him to Sobolevo, now in the
Sanchursk district of Kirov region, where he laboured in the Lord for
twenty-six years and ended his days. For a long time he was one of the
“non-commemorators”; in 1955 he recognised the election of Patriarch Alexy
(Simansky) and rejoined the Patriarchal Church, writing to Archbishop
Benjamin (Tikhonitsky) of Kirov to ask a monastic burial after his death,
and on 14 August 1961, in deep old age, he went to the Lord. His relics rest
now at Sanaksar; their finding is kept on 18 September, the day this entry
stands under, and he is kept also on 27 August and in the synaxes of the new
martyrs of Solovki and of the Kazan and Vyatka saints.
