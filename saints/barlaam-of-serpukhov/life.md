# Barlaam of Serpukhov

The Russian church calendar names him and gives no life. Its list for 5 мая ст. ст. reads «Прп. Варлаама Серпуховского (1377)» — the venerable Barlaam of Serpukhov, with the year of his death — and the name page behind that line carries the same words, the day, and a heading for the pictures of him, and nothing else at all.

So what the source says of him is this: that he was a man under monastic vows, that his name is joined to the town of Serpukhov, and that he died in 1377. It gives no birthplace, no family, no monastery, no office and no account of his labours, and the day page prints no troparion or kontakion under his name. None of it has been supplied here, and the calendar does not call him abbot or hieromonk, so nothing has been read out of the epithet either.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 5 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260505.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/10997.htm), which gives no life; read 8 October 2026.*
