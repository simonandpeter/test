# Andrew (Argunov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Andrew among the martyrs on 23 July and gives no life. Its line is «Сщмч. Михаила пресвитера и мч. Андрея (1938).» — the hieromartyr Michael the presbyter and the martyr Andrew, 1938 — and the calendar's own name page for him adds the surname and keeps the grade the line gives: «Андрей (Аргунов), мч.», Andrew Argunov, martyr.

He stands second on the line, after [Michael (Troitsky)](/saints/michael-troitsky), the priest with whom he shares the entry and the year. The calendar gives him no priestly grade and no office of any kind, so none is written here; the surname is not on the line either, but on his own numbered name page.

That is the whole of the record. He died in 1938; the calendar keeps him on this day among the new martyrs of Russia. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 23 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260723.html); read 13 October 2026.*
