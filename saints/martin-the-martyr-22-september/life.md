# Martin

The Romanian calendar keeps the martyr Martin on 22 September, beside the
martyr [Isaac](/saints/isaac-the-martyr-22-september), and prints no life
and no year for either. The Greek synaxarion, which keeps the two together
the same day, adds the one thing the tradition has held of them, that they
died by the sword, and gives Martin his couplet: with Christ breathing
courage into him from above, Martin hastened to breathe his last under the
blade. Nothing more is known of them — not the place, not the century — and
this entry says so rather than borrow another Martin’s story.

*After the day’s line of the Romanian calendar — [doxologia.ro, 22 septembrie](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-martin), read 23 August 2026 — and the Greek synaxarion’s page for the pair — [saint.gr](https://www.saint.gr/2484/saint.aspx), read 6 September 2026.*
