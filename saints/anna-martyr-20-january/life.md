# Anna (20 January)

The Greek calendar names her on 20 Ιανουαρίου with two words, «Αγία Άννα», and the whole of the page behind that line is one sentence: «Η Αγία Άννα μαρτύρησε στην Ρώμη» — Saint Anna bore witness at Rome. Above it the page sets an epigram: «Άννα ποθούσα τον νοητόν νυμφίον, Αυτώ προσήλθε διά του μαρτυρίου» — Anna, longing for the bridegroom perceived by the mind, came to him through her witness.

That is all. No year, no emperor, no judge, no manner of death and no hymn is printed, and the day gives her no epithet and no kin. Several Annas stand in the corpus already on other days; the page says nothing that would join this one to any of them, so nothing is carried over, and the folder records the city and the fact of her witness and no more.
