# Peter Abesalamites

The glorious martyr Peter was very strong, saint.gr says, in body and in soul and in faith alike, and he confessed Christ with great boldness while he was still young. The ruler of Eleutheropolis could not change his mind by flattery, nor frighten him by threats, and so cast him into the fire; and there this brave contender for godliness received the crown of martyrdom.

That is the whole of the page. It gives no year, no emperor, and no account of what brought him before the ruler. The epithet the calendar gives him, «ο Αβεσαλαμίτης», it does not explain. Over him it prints the couplet «Ἐπανθρακωθεὶς καρδίαν θείῳ πόθῳ, / Ἐπ' ἀνθράκων ἥδιστα Πέτρος ἐκπνέει» — his heart made coals by divine longing, Peter breathes out his life most sweetly upon the coals. There is no hymn.

The Russian calendar keeps him on 13 января ст. ст. and gives him a short notice of its own. He came, it says, from the village of Aneia in Palestine; in the persecution he was seized and brought before the governor of Palestine, and the judge and the people urged him in vain to honour the pagan gods and so escape his torments. «Then shall I truly have mercy on myself, when I do not depart from the Gospel and do not sacrifice to idols», the saint answered — and he was burnt on a pyre.

The same page adds a warning about its own tradition: in some menologia his memory is set down twice, on 12 января as the martyr Peter Abesalamites and on 13 января as [Peter of Aneia](/saints/peter-of-aneia), «так как ошибочно полагают, что это — разные лица» — because they mistakenly suppose these to be different persons. The Russian calendar's own name for him joins the two, «Петр Авессаломит (Анийский)», and its bracket dates him 309–310.

Петра Анийского (309-310)» and names him «Петр Авессаломит (Анийский)».*
