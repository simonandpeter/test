# Philologus of Sinope

Philologus is the last of the five apostles of the Seventy whom saint.gr's calendar keeps together on 5 Νοεμβρίου, and like three of the others he is named out of the Apostle Paul's letter to the Romans. The page calls the five of them true shepherds of the rational flock of the Church. Of Philologus it says one thing only: he was appointed bishop of Sinope by the Apostle Andrew. There is no year, no account of his death, and no hymn for the entry.

The Greek books give him a couplet of his own, which turns on his name as a lover of the word: «Φιλόλογος φιλῶν σὲ τὸν Θεὸν Λόγον, / Πληροῖ λόγους σούς, καὶ θανὼν σύνεστί σοι» — Philologus, loving you the God and Word, fulfils your words, and in dying is with you. Those kept with him are [Hermas](/saints/hermas-5-november), [Patrobas](/saints/patrobas), [Linus](/saints/linus-of-rome) and [Gaius](/saints/gaius-of-ephesus).

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2889/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
