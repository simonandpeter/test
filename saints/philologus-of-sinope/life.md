# Philologus of Sinope

Philologus is the last of the five apostles of the Seventy whom saint.gr's calendar keeps together on 5 Νοεμβρίου, and like three of the others he is named out of the Apostle Paul's letter to the Romans. The page calls the five of them true shepherds of the rational flock of the Church. Of Philologus it says one thing only: he was appointed bishop of Sinope by the Apostle Andrew. There is no year, no account of his death, and no hymn for the entry.

The Greek books give him a couplet of his own, which turns on his name as a lover of the word: «Φιλόλογος φιλῶν σὲ τὸν Θεὸν Λόγον, / Πληροῖ λόγους σούς, καὶ θανὼν σύνεστί σοι» — Philologus, loving you the God and Word, fulfils your words, and in dying is with you. Those kept with him are [Hermas](/saints/hermas-5-november), [Patrobas](/saints/patrobas), [Linus](/saints/linus-of-rome) and [Gaius](/saints/gaius-of-ephesus).

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar for 5 ноября ст. ст. keeps him as one of five named on a single line: «Апп. от 70-ти Патрова , Ерма , Лина , Гаия , Филолога (I).». The line gives the five forenames in the genitive, the class «Апп. от 70-ти» for all five together, and the century, and the name link behind his place in it supplies the calendar's own nominative with the see, «Святитель Филолог, епископ Синопский, апостол от 70-ти». That page carries the title and two days and **no life link at all**, so the line and the link are the whole of what this calendar has of him, and the «(I)» at the line's end is the first date this folder has carried. The name page also lists «4 января (70 ап.)», the synaxis of the Seventy Apostles, which is a synaxis and not a second feast; this folder renders on 5 ноября ст. ст. only.

The see the link gives, Sinope, is the one already in this folder's `office`, and the form «Филолог» is the only one of its shape in this corpus. The calendar prints no account of his preaching or his death and no hymn under his name.
