# Christopher the Roman

Christopher, a Roman, lived in the sixth century and was tonsured at the monastery of Theodosius in Palestine, near Jerusalem; the stories abba Theodoulos told of him are handed down in the Spiritual Meadow of John and Sophronius. Once he went up to Jerusalem to venerate the Holy Sepulchre and the life-giving Cross, and at the gate of the church saw a monk who could not move from the spot, with two ravens flying before his face; Christopher understood that they were demons keeping the man out of the church, and asked why he stood in the gateway and did not go in. Forgive me, father, the brother said, two thoughts are fighting in me — one says go and venerate the precious Cross, the other says no, go and do your business first, you can come and venerate another time. Christopher took him by the hand and led him into the church, and the ravens vanished. The Prologue adds that in a vision he saw the lamps of the labouring monks burning and those of the idle ones dark.
