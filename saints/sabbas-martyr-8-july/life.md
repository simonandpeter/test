# Sabbas (8 July)

saint.gr's calendar for 8 Ιουλίου names him and gives no life. The whole of its account is one sentence, «Ο Άγιος Σάββας μαρτύρησε δια ξίφους, μάλλον μαζί με τον Άγιο Προκόπιο (βλέπε 8 Ιουλίου)» — the holy Sabbas was martyred by the sword, probably together with [Procopius](/saints/procopius-of-jerusalem), whom the same day keeps as great martyr. The «μάλλον», probably, is the page's own, and it does not say the two deaths were one.

The page names no country, no city, no century, no emperor and no judge, gives no year in its text, though it heads him «(; - 303)», and unlike most of its martyrs prints not even a synaxarion couplet for him, nor any hymn under his line. That is everything the source says of him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2051/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
