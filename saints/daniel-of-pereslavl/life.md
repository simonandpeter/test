# Daniel of Pereslavl

saint.gr heads him «Οσιος Δανιηλ ο Θαυματουργος (; - 1540)», Daniel the Wonderworker. He was Demetrius in the world, and was born between the years 1459 and 1460 at the place the page writes Περεγιασλάβλ - Ζελέσκιυ in Russia, of devout and God-loving parents, Constantine and Theodosia; his mother became a nun later with the name Thekla. From a young age he was taught the things of the monastic state under Jonah, the abbot of the Nikitsky monastery of that town.

He became a monk in the house the page names «μονή του Οσίου Πανφουτίου (Μπορόβσκι)», and his spiritual life was guided by [Leucius of Volokolamsk](/saints/leucius-of-volokolamsk), whom saint.gr keeps on this same 7 April. When he returned to the place of his birth he gave himself to the poor and to the burial of the homeless, and then founded a monastery beside the burial ground of the town, and fell asleep in peace in the year 1540.

That is all the page tells. It adds that the Church keeps his memory also on 28 July and on 30 December, and that a portion of his relic is in «Λαύρα Αγίου Αλεξάνδρου Νέβσκι Αγίας Πετρουπόλεως». It gives him no office in the monastery he founded, tells no wonder of his, names no relic beyond that portion, and prints no couplet and no hymn; the epithet ο θαυματουργός, the wonderworker, is all the day's line says beyond his name.

The Ohrid Prologue keeps him on the same day and gives him four sentences, of which the first is the one saint.gr does not have. He had as his particular ascetic labour the care of the dead: whenever he heard that somebody had been found frozen to death, or put to death in some other way, he would hurry to bury him decently and to pray to God for him. He reposed in peace in 1540, the year this folder already holds, and his relics rest whole — where the Greek page knew only of a portion of them, in the Saint Petersburg lavra it names.

**The Russian church calendar keeps him on the Julian 7 April**, the same number the Greek and the Serbian keep, and its line is «Прп. Даниила Переяславского (1540).» with the year this folder already holds, so `dates` are untouched. The name link, page 582, gives «Преподобный Даниил Переяславский», and the index behind it is an index with two readings on it, both followed here. **What the two readings give this folder is everything except the years**: saint.gr had «Όσιος Δανιήλ ο θαυματουργός» and the Ohrid Prologue the Serbian form of his name, and neither told his life.

In the world he was Димитрий, born about 1460 at Переславль-Залесский to devout parents, whom the second reading names «Константина и Феодосии», she called «Фекла» in monasticism. From his early years he showed his love of the ascetic life and imitated the labours of «св. Симеона Столпника», whose day the page gives as 1 September. The boy was given to be brought up in the Никитский monastery, to his kinsman the abbot «Иона», and there he came to love the monastic life and resolved to be a monk himself.

Afraid that his parents would stop him, he went away secretly with his brother «Герасимом» to the monastery of «преподобного Пафнутия Боровского», and there, having taken the tonsure, he lived ten years under the direction of the experienced elder [Leucius](/saints/leucius-of-volokolamsk), whose day the second reading gives as 17 August. Having gained experience in the spiritual life he went back to Pereslavl, to the Горицкий monastery, and there received the priesthood. His strict and God-pleasing life and his unsleeping labours drew the attention of everybody, and many began to come to him for confession and for spiritual counsel; nobody, the page says, went away from him uncomforted.

**The particular ascetic shape his love of his neighbour took was the care of the dead poor** — the destitute, the homeless and those with no kin. If he heard of a man killed by robbers, or drowned, or frozen on the road, with nobody to bury him, he did everything he could to find the body, carried it in his own arms to the «скудельница», the burying-place of the homeless, buried it, and afterwards commemorated it at the divine liturgy. On the site of that burying-place he built a church in honour of All Saints, so that prayer for the repose of Christians who had died unknown should be offered in it.

Several monks built themselves cells around it and so formed a small monastery, of which he became superior in 1525. One of the chief commandments the new superior laid down was that every wanderer and every poor and destitute person should be received. He brought the brethren to their senses and set them on the way of truth not by force but by meekness and love, giving them all an example of a pure life and a deep humility.

Many wonders were worked at his prayers: he turned water into a healing kvass, healed the brethren of their illnesses, and delivered them from dangers. In a famine, when little bread was left in the monastery granary, he gave it to a destitute widow with children — and from then on, in reward for his mercy, the flour in the granary did not fail for the whole of the famine. In his own lifetime his standing was such that at his asking the grand prince «Василий III» released men condemned to death, and twice asked him to be godfather at the baptism of his children.

Foreseeing the approach of his end he took the great schema, and the blessed elder departed **in the eighty-first year of his life, on 7 April 1540** — which is this calendar’s own day, and the only entry of this day whose feast the page dates to the very number. His incorrupt relics were found in 1625. The calendar’s own list of his days is «7 апреля» and «30 декабря (обретение мощей)», and the second reading adds «28 июля»; one row per church is the schema, so the April day is the one he renders on and the other two are recorded here.
