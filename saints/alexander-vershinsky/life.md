# Alexander (Vershinsky)

The Сретенский monastery's calendar names him tenth in its clause of fifteen for 25 ноября ст. ст. and carries no life for him; azbyka.ru prints one out of fond.ru, and it is one life for three men — this priest and the two laymen of his parish council, [Paul (Kuzovkov)](/saints/paul-kuzovkov) and [Nicholas (Kopninsky)](/saints/nicholas-kopninsky). He was born on 6 March 1873 at Кунганово in the Старица district of the province of Tver, the son of a deacon, Андрей Вершинский, who greatly honoured [John of Kronstadt](/saints/john-of-kronstadt) and travelled in February 1906 to a church at Упировичи expressly to serve beside him.

He finished the Tver seminary in 1897 and became psalm-reader of the Boris-and-Gleb cathedral at Старица, and married Еликонида, daughter of a priest of Torzhok. He was ordained deacon on 30 January 1900 and priest a week later. When his father-in-law died at the end of 1901 he was moved to that priest's own Ильинская church in Torzhok, where he served until the militant atheists closed it in 1927, and the care of his father-in-law's large family fell on him. He and his wife had two daughters and a son. He was given a pectoral cross in 1920 and raised archpriest in 1922.

When the persecution came he did not doubt the road he had taken. In 1923 the clergy and faithful elected him first dean of their district and the diocesan bishop confirmed him, and he was made dean of the Boris-and-Gleb monastery in Torzhok the same year; awards followed in 1924 and 1925. After the Ильинская church was closed, the archbishop of Tver, [Thaddeus of Tver](/saints/thaddeus-of-tver), blessed the community to move into the Transfiguration cathedral of Torzhok and appointed him its rector. He served there until that cathedral was closed in 1931, then at the Николо-Пустынская church, and was given the mitre in 1932. In March 1937 the renovationists took his church with the authorities' help and he was left without a place; he was invited at once to the Smolensk church at Ивантеевка in the province of Moscow, and served there to the day of his arrest.

On 24 October a member of the school administration at Ивантеевка wrote a denunciation reporting what the deacon of the church had told him: that the priest preached on feast days, that the preaching was monarchist in character, that he had been warned more than once and took no notice, and that there was a suspicion he belonged to some party. The deacon repeated it to the NKVD on 10 November, adding that Vershinsky had great authority, which he supposed he had won among the women by his sermons, and that he kept solemn services on all the old feasts. The three men of the parish were arrested, and the priest and the warden were sentenced to death by the troika on 1 December 1937 while the third man got eight years. He was shot on 8 December 1937 and buried in an unmarked common grave on the Butovo range outside Moscow.
