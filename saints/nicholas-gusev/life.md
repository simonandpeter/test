# Nicholas (Gusev)

He is the last of five named in one line of the Russian calendar for 26 September, and one of the two in it who were not priests: «Сщмч. Афанасия, Александра и Димитрия пресвитеров, мчч. Иоанна и Николая (1937)» — three presbyters and then «мчч. Иоанна и Николая», martyrs without an office. The calendar's index of names gives him as «Николай (Гусев), мч.» and keeps him here and on 26 January with the new martyrs and confessors of Russia.

That is everything the source holds. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which refused this reading on 4 October 2026. The four he is named with are [Athanasius (Dokukin)](/saints/athanasius-dokukin), [Alexander (Levitsky)](/saints/alexander-levitsky), [Demetrius (Rozanov)](/saints/demetrius-rozanov) and [John (Zolotov)](/saints/john-zolotov).

*After the Russian church calendar's [26 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260926.html) and its [index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13805.html); read 4 October 2026.*
