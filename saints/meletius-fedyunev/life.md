# Meletius (Fedyunev)

Meletius — Michael Mikhailovich Fedyunev in the world — was born in 1871 in
the village of Zanulye in the Ust-Sysolsk district of Vologda province, now
in the Priluzye district of Komi, and became a hieromonk of the
Trinity-Stephen monastery at Ulyanovo in the same district. In the summer of
1918 four hundred poods of “surplus grain” were taken from the monastery, in
the autumn it was closed, and through September and October the Red
detachments plundered its goods and food and made a state farm of the place;
Meletius and the other monks went on living there. On 18 September 1919 he
was arrested by the North Dvina Cheka with the igumen Ambrose (Morozov) and
four other monks, charged with concealing five hundred poods of grain, sent
to the North Dvina concentration camp, and soon released on a written
undertaking to live at Lalsk until the case was tried; in November the
Whites took the monastery, and the monks, then at the John the Forerunner
monastery at Veliky Ustyug, were arrested again on 17 November and sentenced
to the camp until the end of the civil war, and were freed either soon after
or, by other accounts, in May 1920. He settled at Kuzhba in the Ust-Kulom
district of the Komi region, where he did every office that was needed but
did not serve in the church. In 1929 he was arrested for illegal currency
dealings and concealing church valuables, a charge so absurd that it never
came to trial, and the case was revived in 1932 and dropped again. He was
arrested for the last time on 2 September 1937; on 21 September the troika
of the Komi NKVD sentenced him to death, and on 23 September 1937 he was
shot. He is kept on 10 September of the old style, 23 September.
