# Vladimir (Chetverin)

Vladimir Chetverin was born in 1874 in the village of Barakhman-Gart in the Ardatov district of Simbirsk province to a simple, devout family. From youth he loved the teacher’s road and wished with all his heart to bring people the light of the faith of Christ; he was first a teacher of a school of letters, then seven years a teacher of the parish school at Kutyevo in the Karsun district, and at twenty-nine, burning with love for the Lord and wishing to give all his strength to his service, petitioned the Simbirsk examining commission for ordination. Noting the humility and firm faith of the young ascetic the diocesan authorities made him psalmist, first at Yulevo in the Karsun district, then at Bolshaya Ryazan, Vyrypaevka and Sursky Ostrog — the calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. Ordained priest at thirty-six, he served in the villages of Syryatino and Barakhman-Gart; in the years of persecution he refused to hand over the parish registers and the church’s property, was arrested on 19 August 1918, tortured and shot on 2 September, and his relics have remained incorrupt. The Russian calendar keeps him on 20 August (Julian).
