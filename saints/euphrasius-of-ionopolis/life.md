# Euphrasius of Ionopolis

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its page for him carries the feast line 17 Iulie and nothing else — no Viață tab and no Tropar tab — so all that is recorded here is what the calendar's own line for the day says: «Sfântul Ierarh Eufrasie, Episcopul Ienopolei», the holy hierarch Euphrasius, bishop of Ionopolis.

That line gives his rank and his see and nothing more. No year, no account of his life and no hymn were found on the Romanian pages read for this day, and nothing has been supplied from elsewhere. The English form of the see here follows the Romanian «Ienopolei»; the Russian and Serbian calendars have not been checked for him.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 17 July, and saint.gr is nearly as brief as doxologia: «Ο Άγιος Ευφράσιος ήταν επίσκοπος Ιωνοπόλεως και απεβίωσε ειρηνικά» — he was bishop of Ionopolis and he died peacefully. That last clause is the one thing the Romanian page did not say; it means he was not a martyr. Above the notice the Greek calendar sets the Synaxarion's couplet for him, «Eυφράνας Eυφράσιε τοις έργοις Λόγον, / Xαίρων πορεύου την προς ουρανόν τρίβον» — having gladdened the Word by your works, Euphrasius, go rejoicing on the path towards heaven, a play on his name and the verb to gladden. No year, no birthplace and no account of his years in the see is given, and there is no hymn.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 17 iulie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/17-iulie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-ierarh-eufrasie-episcopul-ienopolei), which prints only the day; read 24 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 17 Ιουλίου — [the Greek day](https://www.saint.gr/07/17/index.aspx) and [the Greek life](https://www.saint.gr/720/saint.aspx), read 1 October 2026.*
