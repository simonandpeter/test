# Euphrasius of Ionopolis

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its page for him carries the feast line 17 Iulie and nothing else — no Viață tab and no Tropar tab — so all that is recorded here is what the calendar's own line for the day says: «Sfântul Ierarh Eufrasie, Episcopul Ienopolei», the holy hierarch Euphrasius, bishop of Ionopolis.

That line gives his rank and his see and nothing more. No year, no account of his life and no hymn were found on the Romanian pages read for this day, and nothing has been supplied from elsewhere. The English form of the see here follows the Romanian «Ienopolei»; the other calendars have not been checked for him.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 17 iulie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/17-iulie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-ierarh-eufrasie-episcopul-ienopolei), which prints only the day; read 24 September 2026.*
