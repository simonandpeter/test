# The Two Martyrs of Tyre

saint.gr's calendar names them and gives no life. The whole of its notice is one sentence: «Οι Άγιοι Δύο Μάρτυρες από την Τύρο, μαρτύρησαν αφού θανατώθηκαν συρόμενοι κατά γης.» The two holy martyrs from Tyre suffered martyrdom after being put to death by being dragged along the ground. The page names neither of the two, and gives no year and no emperor.

Above that sentence it prints the distich the Greek books give them, which turns their death into the figure of a plough: «Ζεύγος τρίβει γην, ου ρυμός Θεού φόβος, / Ζεύγλη δε πίστις, και τροχοί προθυμία» — a yoked pair wears the earth, whose pole is the fear of God, whose yoke-strap is faith and whose wheels are eagerness. It prints no hymn of any kind.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2238/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
