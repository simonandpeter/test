# Demetrius (Shishokin)

The Russian calendar gives him a line of his own on 27 September and no life: «Сщмч. Димитрия пресвитера (1918)» — Demetrius the hieromartyr, presbyter, 1918. Its index of names adds the surname, «Димитрий (Шишокин), иерей, сщмч.», a priest, and keeps him here and on 26 January with the whole company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia.

1918 makes him one of the first of them: the year the killing of clergy in Russia began in earnest, before there were camps or quotas and when a priest was shot where he was found. The calendar says nothing about where that was. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which refused this reading on 4 October 2026 — a 403 to the fetcher's user-agent and to a full browser one alike — so the line stands as the line.

*After the Russian church calendar's [27 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260927.html) and its [index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9564.html); read 4 October 2026.*
