# Anna (Stolyarova)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery closes its list for 10 декабря with her, and gives no life. Its line is «Прп. Анны исп (1958).» — the venerable Anna the confessor, 1958 — and the calendar’s own name page adds both the grade and the surname the line leaves out: «Анна (Столярова), схимонахиня, исповедница», Anna the schemanun.

That is the whole of the record. She had taken the great habit, which is what схимонахиня says; the word the calendar uses of her end is исповедница, a confessor, one who suffered for the faith and was not killed for it; and she died in 1958, which is the latest year on this whole day and more than twenty years after the worst of the persecution. The calendar keeps her on this day alone, her line joined to no one else’s.

Two women of her forename stand on this one day of the calendar, a decade apart, and only the surnames on the name pages tell them apart. The day page carries eleven hymns and not one is under her name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 10 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261210.html); read 13 October 2026.*
