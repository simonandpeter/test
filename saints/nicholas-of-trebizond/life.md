# Nicholas of Trebizond

The hieromartyr Nicholas came of Trebizond and lived in the town of Katigogi in Pontus, in the province of Amaseia and Amisos. From a small age, saint.gr says, he longed to give himself to God, but his brothers would not let him; and under their pressure, so that he should not become a monk, he married. His wife died soon afterwards, and one of his children with her, while a kinsman took the other into his keeping. The saint then gave himself to study and to prayer.

He came to Constantinople, where he learned the physician's art and healed many who were sick. A little later he was tonsured a monk and was ordained bishop in the region of Amisos. God, who saw his spiritual struggle, counted him worthy of the gift of foresight as well.

He was arrested by the Turks and, after many torments, gave up his holy soul to God in the year 1920, at the age of sixty-six. The distich saint.gr prints over him says that Nicholas shepherded his flock well while he lived, and that he would not abandon it in its hardships.
