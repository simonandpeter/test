# Antony

doxologia.ro's calendar for 1 martie names Antony and gives no life for him: his page carries no Viață tab and no Tropar tab, only a single sentence of summary. That sentence says that the saints [Marcellus](/saints/marcellus-the-martyr-1-march) and Antony, kept on 1 March, were martyred after being thrown into the fire — «Sfinții Marcel și Antonie prăznuiți pe 1 martie au fost martirizați după ce au fost aruncați în foc».

That is the whole of the source. It names no country and no city, no emperor and no year, and doxologia prints no hymn for him.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, pairs him with [Marcellus](/saints/marcellus-the-martyr-1-march) on one page and adds the one thing doxologia did not give — the manner of their death. saint.gr says in a single sentence that the holy Marcellus and Antony were martyred after they had been thrown into the fire, and prints a distich over the pair of them: the furnace is a crucible for two contenders, who shine in it brighter than gold. It gives no year, no country, no emperor, no judge and no relic, and it prints no hymn; the two names stand together on that page and are told in no other terms than each other's.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 1 martie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/1-martie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-antonie); read 19 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 1 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3776/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026.*
