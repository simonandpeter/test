# Antony

doxologia.ro's calendar for 1 martie names Antony and gives no life for him: his page carries no Viață tab and no Tropar tab, only a single sentence of summary. That sentence says that the saints [Marcellus](/saints/marcellus-the-martyr-1-march) and Antony, kept on 1 March, were martyred after being thrown into the fire — «Sfinții Marcel și Antonie prăznuiți pe 1 martie au fost martirizați după ce au fost aruncați în foc».

That is the whole of the source. It names no country and no city, no emperor and no year, and doxologia prints no hymn for him.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 1 martie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/1-martie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-antonie); read 19 September 2026.*
