# Moses, disciple of Polychronius

saint.gr keeps four ascetics together on this day and gives them one page, and says at the end whose book it is drawing on: Theodoret of Cyrrhus wrote their lives in his Philotheos Historia. The venerable [Zebinas](/saints/zebinas) set his hermitage on a mountain and lived there as an ascetic in prayer and fasting. [Polychronius](/saints/polychronius-disciple-of-zebinas) came to him, and imitated his venerable teacher in unceasing prayer and in self-restraint.

The venerable [Damian](/saints/damian-disciple-of-polychronius) and Moses were disciples of Polychronius. The synaxarion relates, the page says, that Moses stayed on in his elder's cell, while Damian went off to the small town of Niara, where near the threshing floors he found a deserted little house and lived in it as a hermit. Our venerable and God-bearing fathers fell asleep in peace.

The page prints two distichs, one for Zebinas alone — the divine Zebinas reaches a divine end, the life he had among men having come to its end — and one for the other three: Polychronius and two fellow ascetics, the three of them together filling out the times of their life. There is no year on the page for any of them, and no hymn.

The Russian calendar keeps the eight Syrian hermits together on this day, and this Moses is the seventh of them, «Моисей другой» — the other Moses. He lived with [Polychronius](/saints/polychronius-disciple-of-zebinas) as his disciple and imitated him in everything, showing the same pattern of a hard ascetic life. The older account the same page quotes knows him as one of two men who were persuaded to serve the old man in his weakness and who grew in virtue until they matched their teacher in wisdom; Moses stayed with him to the end, caring for the elder as for a father and a lord, and reflecting in himself the rays of his holy soul.
