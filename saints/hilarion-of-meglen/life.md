# Hilarion of Meglen

The Ohrid Prologue of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović keeps him on the Julian 21 October and gives him a life that begins before he did. He came of notable and devout parents, and his mother was barren and prayed to God a long time to give her a child; and after her prayer the most holy Theotokos appeared to her and comforted her with these words: do not grieve, you will bear a son, and he will turn many to the light of truth.

When Hilarion was three years old, the entry says, a song was heard ceaselessly out of his mouth: Holy, holy, holy, Lord of Sabaoth. He was well schooled, was tonsured a monk in his eighteenth year, and founded a monastery that followed the typikon of Saint Pachomius. In 1134 Eustathius, archbishop of Trnovo, consecrated him bishop of Meglen.

For almost the whole of his life after that he waged a great struggle against the Bogomils and the Armenian heretics. By his spiritual learning and his incomparable holiness, the book says, he put them all to shame, and he brought many of them to Orthodoxy. He fell asleep in peace and passed to the kingdom of his Lord in 1164.
