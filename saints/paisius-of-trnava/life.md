# Paisius of Trnava

The Ohrid Prologue gives him four lines under a heading he shares with one other man, and this folder gives no more. Paisius was abbot of the monastery of Trnava near Čačak, it says, and [Avacum](/saints/avacum-the-deacon) was his fellow-brother and his deacon. Both of them, as Christians, were impaled on the stake by the Turks, at Belgrade on the Kalemegdan, on 17 December 1814 — the same day of the same month that this calendar keeps them on.

That is the whole of what the book says of him. It names no year of his birth, nothing of his monastery beyond where it stood, nothing of how he came to be its abbot, and nothing of what was asked of him before he died. The rest of the entry belongs to his deacon: that he sang as he carried his own stake through the streets of Belgrade, and what he answered his mother when she begged him weeping to turn Turk and save his life. The book prints no hymn for either of them, and this folder holds none.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 17. децембар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2025-12-30&prolog=1); read 7 October 2026.*
