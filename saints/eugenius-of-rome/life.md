# Eugenius of Rome

saint.gr says that Saint Eugenius, bishop of Rome, was born at Rome and was the son of Rouphinianos. He was elected bishop of Rome by the clergy and the people on 10 August 654, and succeeded Pope Martin I, whom the emperor Constans II had arrested in 653 for refusing to sign a text forbidding men to speak of one or of two wills in Christ, and had exiled, sick and bedridden, to Cherson, where he died.

When an exposition of faith was sent from Constantinople by Patriarch Pyrrhos, in which the acceptance of three wills in Christ was recommended in place of one or of two, Eugenius was ready to accept it; but the people and the clergy of Rome resisted and forbade him the divine Liturgy. So he did not agree with the text of the letter, and he kept a firm stand against the heresy of Monothelitism, with the result that the emperor was enraged against him.

That did not dismay the saint, who contended for the piety of the fathers and for the teaching of the Fourth Ecumenical Council. Saint Eugenius was distinguished for the holiness of his life, for his gentleness and for his love of men, and he fell asleep in peace in the year 657. The page prints no hymn for him and no verse.
