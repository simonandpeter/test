# Theosebia

saint.gr's calendar for 10 Ιανουαρίου keeps Theosebia the deaconess on the same day as [Gregory of Nyssa](/saints/gregory-of-nyssa), and what it prints of her is chiefly the question of who she was to him. She lived in the fourth century. Some historians, the page says, hold that she was Gregory's wife, and that she died quite young; but [Cyril of Jerusalem](/saints/cyril-of-jerusalem), in a letter to Gregory, speaks of her as his sister. The page leaves the two readings standing side by side and does not settle them.

What it does say can be said for certain is how [Gregory the Theologian](/saints/gregory-the-theologian) spoke of her: «όντως ιερά, το της Εκκλησίας καύχημα, το του Χριστού καλλώπισμα, το της καθ' ημάς γενεάς όφελος, την γυναικών παρησσίαν και των μεγάλων μυστηρίων αξίαν» — truly holy, the boast of the Church, the adornment of Christ, the profit of our generation, the boldness of women, and worthy of the great mysteries.

The page gives her no year of birth or death, no place, and no hymn.
