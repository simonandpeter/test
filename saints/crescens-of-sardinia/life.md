# Crescens of Sardinia

saint.gr's calendar for 31 Μαΐου names him and gives one sentence. The holy martyr Crescens, it says, came from the city of Sarrari in Sardinia, and he was martyred in the year 130, in the years of the emperor Hadrian, whose reign the page dates 117 to 138.

That is the whole of the source. It gives him no age, no trade and no rank in the Church; it names no judge, no companion and no manner of death; it records no wonder, and prints neither hymn nor epigram. What it does give is a named birthplace, a named emperor and a year, which is more than most of this day's entries carry.
