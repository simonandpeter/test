# Domnina (5 January)

saint.gr's calendar names her and gives no life. Its whole notice is one sentence: «Η Οσία Δομνίνα (ή Δόμνα), έζησε κατά Θεόν και κοιμήθηκε εν ειρήνη» — the venerable Domnina, or Domna, lived according to God and fell asleep in peace. The page prints beside it a two-line verse saying that the hardship of the flesh below wins her the inheritance above, and a note that the name Domna comes from the Latin dominus and means the lady, the mistress. No year, no place and no other notice of her is given there.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/577/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
