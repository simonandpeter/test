# Themistocles of Myra

doxologia.ro gives him five sentences and no more. Themistocles lived in the days of the emperor Decius, at Myra in Lycia, and was a shepherd of sheep. When the governor Asclepius raised a persecution against the Christians and was hunting for Saint Dioscorides, Themistocles, who had hidden him, said that he did not know where he was — and then gave himself up instead, confessing that he was a Christian.

Brought before the governor Asclepius he preached Christ. Speaking boldly, he was hung on a piece of wood and beaten about the belly until his belly was torn open, and was then dragged over sharpened iron spikes, and so gave up his spirit into the hands of God. The page gives no year, no parentage and no burial. The Dioscorides he hid is named and nothing more is said of him; the corpus keeps a martyr «Dioscor» on 11 May of whom it knows only the name, and whether the two are one man is not settled here.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and puts a year to his death. saint.gr has Themistocles from Myra in Lycia, living in the time of the emperor Decius, a shepherd by trade. Asklepios, the governor of Lycia, had begun a persecution of the Christians, and what he wanted was to seize the holy martyr Dioskourides; Dioskourides took refuge on the mountain where Themistocles was pasturing his sheep, and the governor's men, going up after him, met Themistocles instead. Asked about his faith he did not hesitate a moment to confess the one and only God, and the soldiers who heard him arrested him and brought him before Asklepios. For all the threats put to him the saint stayed unshaken, which enraged the governor, and he ordered him tortured; and in the course of the torture, in the year 251, Themistocles gave up his spirit.
