# Akepsimas (11 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 11 December keeps two martyrs in one entry and names both: [Peter](/saints/peter-the-ascetic) the ascetic and Akepsimas. The whole of what the page has to say of them is where it has them from and where they died: they are recorded in the Synaxaristes of Delehaye and in the Parisian Codex 1578 as having been martyred in Persia.

No century, no king, no city, no manner of death, no relic, no hymn and no couplet is printed for either of them, and Akepsimas is given neither office nor rank beyond the word holy. The Greek calendar keeps other men of this name on other days, and the same 11 December keeps an Akepsees in another entry; nothing here is joined to any of them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/11/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3170/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
