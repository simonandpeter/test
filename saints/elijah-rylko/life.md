# Elijah (Rylko)

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar for 9 ноября ст. ст. carries eleven names on one line and he is the eighth of them: «Сщмчч. Парфения , еп. Ананьевского, Константина , Димитрия , Нестора , Феодора , Константина , Виктора , Илии , Павла пресвитеров, Иосифа диакона и прмч. Алексия (1937).» One year stands for all eleven, «Сщмчч.» heads the ten clergy and «прмч.» the monk who closes the line, and the grades it prints sort them into a bishop, eight presbyters, a deacon and a hieromonk. The name link behind his place supplies the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line leaves out, «Илия (Рылько), протоиерей, сщмч.», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from.

**days.pravoslavie.ru gives this company nothing but that line.** Each of the eleven name pages carries a title, a list of days and no text at all, and not one of them has a `/Life/` link of any kind; the day page's hymns are the two martyrs' of 284–305, the venerable Matrona's, the Mother of God's before the Скоропослушница icon and the wonderworker of Aegina's, and nothing is sung under any of these eleven names. The life below is azbyka.ru's, read in the in-app browser because that host answers no fetcher, and the paragraphs are this corpus's own.

He was born in 1881 in the village of Rydomlya in the Orsha country of the Mogilev province, the son of a peasant, and finished a teachers' seminary. From 1906 to 1913 he served as psalmist in the village of Khlebnoye. He received the priesthood in 1914 and served in turn at Usokhskaya Buda, at Khlebnoye again and at Blagovichi. In 1934 he moved to the Moscow region and was appointed to the church of St Nicholas at Nikolskoye-Gagarino in the Novo-Petrovsky district, a stone church built in 1783 in a village that had taken its owner's name.

There he served the last years of his life, and in 1936 he was raised archpriest. On 23 September 1937 he was appointed to the church of the Prophet Elijah at the graveyard of Ilyinskoye in the Solnechnogorsk district. On 30 October 1937 he was arrested and shut in the Taganka prison in Moscow. Asked with whom he had been on good terms, he named two men, both by then arrested for anti-Soviet agitation, and said that they had visited one another and talked in private of the collective farms and of the church, and of nothing else.

He was told he had been arrested as an active member of a counter-revolutionary group and that the investigation required his evidence, and he answered that he had belonged to no such group and carried on no agitation. The chairman of the collective farm was questioned and said he had heard the priest say that the constitution writes one thing and the thing itself is another: that it promises freedom of religion and the Soviet power squeezes priests with taxes to make an end of religion altogether.

A second false witness said that the priest drew children into the church, his own five-year-old son among them, gave him «святым медом» and some sort of gingerbread, and taught him to cross himself and to bow.

On 19 November 1937 a troika of the NKVD sentenced him to be shot. He was shot on 21 November 1937 at the Butovo range outside Moscow and buried in an unmarked common grave. **The calendar keeps him on 9 ноября ст. ст., which is the civil 22 November, and his life has him shot on 21 November 1937** — the day before, 8 ноября old style. The difference is one day and not the thirteen of the style conversion, so it is neither a second feast nor an arithmetic slip that could be read away: five of the eleven died on the 22nd and six on the 21st, and the calendar holds all eleven on the one day. The attestation's day is the day page that listed him, as the brief requires, and the death above is the date his own life gives.

**He is given no relation to the ten others the line names.** Four of them — the company of Semipalatinsk — are tied to one another by their own pages and to nobody else; of the six that are not, five lives end at Butovo on 21 November 1937 and no one of them names another, under sentences passed by troikas sitting on different days over different cases. A shared line, a shared class, a shared year and even a shared grave are not a company unless a page sentences the men together, and none of these pages does.
