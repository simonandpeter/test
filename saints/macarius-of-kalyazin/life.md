# Macarius of Kalyazin

saint.gr's calendar for 17 Μαρτίου keeps a Russian abbot, Macarius of the monastery of Kalyazin, and heads his page «(1402 - 1483)». In the world he was called Matthew, and he was born in the year 1402 in the village of Kozino in Russia, near the town of Kashin; his father, Basil Koza, was a soldier in the service of Prince Basil II the Dark.

At his parents' insistence, and against his own inclination to the monastic life, he married Helen Yakhontova. Within the next three years his parents and his wife died, and so he entered the monastery of St Nicholas of Klobukov, where he was tonsured a monk. With his abbot's blessing he left that house and lived as an ascetic in a desert place near two lakes in the region of Kashin.

Later he became abbot of the monastery of Kalyazin, and there he carried his spiritual struggle on. He was a simple man, the page says, and would often withdraw into the wilderness to live in stillness; in the forest he talked with the wild beasts, and they took their food from the saint's hand. Macarius fell asleep in peace in the year 1483.

That is the whole of what saint.gr prints. It gives no account of the founding of the Kalyazin house, names no relics beyond setting the finding of them beside him as its own commemoration, and prints neither a couplet nor a hymn under his name; nothing has been added here from any other source or from memory.
