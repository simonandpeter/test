# The Three Martyrs in Melitene

saint.gr's whole entry for them is one sentence: the three holy martyrs bore their witness after being dragged to death over sharp stones. Above it stands the synaxarion's distich, which says that the three, dying as they were dragged along, will give a voice out of the midst of the stones, as the Scripture has it. The city is in the calendar's line, «οι εν Μελιτινή», and not in the notice.

The page names none of the three, and gives them no year, no century, no emperor and no hymn. What the corpus holds of them is a number, a city and a manner of death.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 21 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/21/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2020/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
