# Huldah, mother of Selim

saint.gr keeps the prophetess on 10 April under the name Ολδά, and gives a second form of it, Όλδα, in the body of its page. It calls her the mother of Selim, the son of Thekoue, the son of Araas, and says she is spoken of in the fourth book of Kings in the Old Testament. She dwelt at Jerusalem in the days of king Josiah, and the page's whole account of her is the answer she gave to the king's messengers, which it prints in full.

Thus says the Lord, the God of Israel, she told them: say to the man who sent you to me, thus says the Lord — behold, I will bring a great destruction upon this place and upon those who dwell in it, applying to them all the punishments of this book which the king of the kingdom of Israel has read. And this will come to pass because the Israelites abandoned me, offering incense to strange gods, and so forced me to be angry at these works of their hands, and made my anger burn against this place and not be quenched.

That is all the calendar has of her. It gives no year, names neither her husband nor her own father, tells nothing else of her life or of her end, and prints no hymn. Its couplet says that Huldah, the pythoness full of the divine Spirit, gave up a spirit that saw what was to come.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/10/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1305/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
