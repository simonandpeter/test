# John (Kuminov)

The calendar keeps him with [Michael (Pyataev)](/saints/michael-pyataev) in one line for 15 февраля ст. ст. — «Сщмчч. Михаила и Иоанна пресвитеров (1930).» — and the life page it carries is the life of the two of them together, headed «Священномученики иереи Михаил Пятаев и Иоанн Куминов». Both took holy orders in the years of the persecution of the church, the page says, undeterred by the cruelty they had watched the godless authorities use on believers.

What the page then tells is Father Michael's story, and of Father John it says only the thing the two shared and the thing that made them martyrs: by sentence of a GPU troika the two priests were shot together on 15 (28 n. st.) February 1930 at Kainsk, now Kuybyshev in the Novosibirsk region. The name link behind the day's line carries the calendar's own nominative with his surname, «Иоанн (Куминов), иерей».

Nothing further is written here. The two of them were canonised as locally venerated saints of the Omsk diocese in 1999, and numbered among the New Martyrs and Confessors of Russia by the Jubilee Council of Bishops in August 2000, for veneration throughout the church. The calendar prints no hymn under either name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 15 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260215.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4856.htm); read 3 October 2026.*
