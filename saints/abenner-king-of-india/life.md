# Abenner, father of Joasaph

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him third on one line for the Julian 19 November, «Прпп. Варлаама и Иоасафа, царевича Индийского, и отца его Авенира царя (IV).», and the name link behind his place in it, name page 14, gives «Авенир Индийский, царь» — a king, and no abbreviation of sanctity at all, where the two named before him carry one. Its life page, titled for the three of them, makes him king in India, an idolater and a cruel persecutor of the Christians, in a country which had once received the Christian faith through the preaching of the apostle Thomas.

For a long while he had no children. At last a son was born to him and was named Joasaph, and at the birth the wisest of the royal star-readers foretold that the prince would accept the Christian faith his father persecuted. Wishing to prevent what had been foretold, the king had a separate palace built for the prince and gave orders that he should not hear one word about Christ or his teaching. The prince, grown to youth, asked leave to drive beyond the palace, and saw then that there are sufferings, and sicknesses, and old age, and death.

When his son had been baptised the king tried to turn him from Christianity. On the advice of one of his nobles he arranged a disputation about the faith between Christians and heathen, at which a magician and sorcerer named Nachor appeared in the hermit’s likeness and was to own himself beaten and so turn the prince from the faith. The prince learned of the deceit in a dream and threatened Nachor with a cruel death if he were beaten; and the frightened Nachor not only defeated the heathen but came himself to believe in Christ, repented, was baptised and withdrew into the desert. The king tried other means and the prince overcame every temptation.

Then, again on his nobles’ advice, the king gave his son half the kingdom. [Joasaph, son of Abenner](/saints/joasaph-of-india), become king, restored Christianity in his country, built the churches up again and at the last turned his father to Christ; and soon after his baptism the king Abenner departed. The line gives the century, «(IV)», and no year, and this folder records nothing more: the page gives him no length of reign, no age and no place with a coordinate. It gives him no rank of sanctity either, so the only `types` here is the kingship the line itself prints.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 19 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261119.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life2561.htm); read 5 October 2026.*
