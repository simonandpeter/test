# Charissa, companion of Leonides

saint.gr keeps her on 16 April with [Leonides](/saints/leonides-of-epidaurus), whom it calls the great champion of Epidaurus, and with six other women. They lived in the years of the king Decius, which the page dates 249 to 251, and were of the Peloponnese; they were arrested in the days of the Lord's Resurrection at Troezenia and brought before Venustus, the governor of Corinth. The martyrs would not move from their faith, and the governor ordered Leonides torn and all of them thrown into the sea.

On the road they sang psalms, and then the women were bound with stones and cast into the water with him, one day before holy Pascha, that is on Great Saturday. Her own couplet plays on her name, which is the word for grace: «Θάλασσαν ἡ Χάρισσα φρίττειν οὐκ ἔχω, Ἥτις θάλασσαν προξενεῖ μοι χαρίτων» — I, Charissa, cannot shudder at a sea which procures for me a sea of graces. Nothing else is printed of her: no age, no family, and no year of her own.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 16 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/16/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/278/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
