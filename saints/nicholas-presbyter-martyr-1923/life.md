# Nicholas (Sushchevsky)

The archpriest Nicholas Sushchevsky served at the church of the village of Georgievka in the Pishpek district of the Semirechye region. In 1923 he was shot by the godless power not far from the town of Taldy-Kurgan. He was glorified among the saints at the jubilee Council of Bishops of the Russian Church in 2000, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 3 September (Julian) among the martyrs of Semirechye. That is the whole of the calendar’s notice; this entry records it and nothing more.
