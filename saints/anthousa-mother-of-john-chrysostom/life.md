# Anthousa, mother of John Chrysostom

doxologia.ro's calendar names her and gives no life. The entry for 13 noiembrie reads «Sfânta Antuza, mama Sfântului Ierarh Ioan Gură de Aur» — Anthousa, the mother of [John Chrysostom](/saints/john-chrysostom), Archbishop of Constantinople — and the page behind that line carries no Viață tab and no Tropar tab, only the day's Epistle and Gospel, icons, photographs and articles. A name, a kinship and a day is the whole of what this source has for her.

The calendar gives her no century, no place, no rank and no manner of death; the one thing it says of her is whose mother she was, and it keeps her on the same day as her son. Even the articles it links beside her name are about the mothers of the Three Hierarchs generally, and are not a life. This folder records what the Romanian calendar keeps on 13 noiembrie and stops there.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 13 noiembrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/13-noiembrie) and [her page](https://doxologia.ro/sfanta-antuza-mama-sfantului-ierarh-ioan-gura-de-aur); read 25 September 2026.*
