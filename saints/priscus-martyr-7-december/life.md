# Priscus (7 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 7 December keeps three martyrs in one entry and names all three: Priscus, [Martin](/saints/martin-martyr-7-december) and [Nicholas](/saints/nicholas-martyr-7-december). Of Priscus it says only that he died a martyr's death from the withholding of food. No country, no reign, no persecutor and no year is printed for him or for the other two.

The page gives each man his own couplet, and his turns on the hunger he died of: Priscus having died for want of earthly bread, Christ will feed him with the bread of the angels. The festival of all three, the page adds, is kept near the wall of the Blachernae. The Greek calendar keeps another Priscus on 21 September, whom the corpus already holds, and nothing here joins this one to him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3196/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
