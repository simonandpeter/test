# Callistus II of Constantinople

saint.gr's calendar for 22 Νοεμβρίου names «Όσιος Κάλλιστος ο Β', Πατριάρχης Κωνσταντινούπολης», glosses the name as the most beautiful and the most glorious, and gives him three sentences. He went up to the patriarchal throne in the year 1397. He was distinguished for a living piety and for his love of men.

But he left the patriarchate, because those years were very troubled ones, and withdrew to a monastery, where he died practising the ascetic life. The page gives him no country, no year of birth or of death, no relics and no hymn.
