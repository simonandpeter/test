# Nestor, father of Conon

saint.gr keeps Nestor on this day for his son's sake, and gives him three sentences. He was the father of [Conon of Isauria](/saints/conon-of-isauria), whom the same day keeps; his wife was Nada; and at first the two of them were idolaters. But by the grace of God their son Conon led them to the Christian faith and baptised them; and Nestor was martyred, receiving the crown of his Lord's glory.

That is the whole of the notice. It gives him no city and no year of his own — the village of Badine in Isauria and the end of the first century stand on Conon's page and are not repeated on his — no emperor, no judge, no manner of death, no burial and no relic. It prints no distich over him and no hymn, and the etymology block above his name is about Nestor son of Neleus, king of Pylos, which is a note on the word and not a statement about him.

The corpus keeps four other men of this name — of Thessalonica, of Maghid, one on the twenty-first of September and one on the second of March — and none of them stands on this day or is named on this page.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3843/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
