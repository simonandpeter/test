# Anthimus the Presbyter

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 7 iunie reads «Sfântul Cuvios Antim, preotul» — the venerable Anthimus, the priest — and his page on the site is the barest of the day: no Viață tab, no Tropar tab, no icons, no articles, nothing but the Epistle and Gospel appointed for the day.

Those readings are the ones the calendar gives for a monastic saint: the fruit of the Spirit from Galatians, and the Lord's "take my yoke upon you" from Matthew. They tell nothing about this man. So what is recorded here is what the line records: his name, that he was reckoned among the venerable, that he was a priest, and that his day is 7 June. No country, no century, no year and no hymn.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 7 iunie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/7-iunie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-antim-preotul); read 24 September 2026.*
