# Diokles

saint.gr calls him the venerable abba Diokles, and gives his life in two sentences and a saying. He studied grammar and philosophy, and at twenty-eight years old he became an anchorite, living inside a cave. Heracleides, whom the page names as the writer of the Lausiac History, met him there, and among other things asked him: how is it possible, father, for the human mind to be always among divine thoughts?

The venerable one answered: as a man accustoms himself, whether with good thoughts or with evil reasonings, so he also lives his life. That is the whole of what the page prints. It gives him no year and no century, no place beyond the cave, and no hymn; what stands above the life is the site's own gloss on the name Diokles, from Zeus and glory, and a list of five pagan bearers of it, which is not part of his life at all.
