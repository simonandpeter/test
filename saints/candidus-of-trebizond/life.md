# Candidus of Trebizond

Candidus suffered at Trebizond with three others whom doxologia.ro keeps with him on 21 ianuarie, and the life the site prints is one life for the four. Three were taken first — [Valerian](/saints/valerian-of-trebizond), Candidus and [Aquila](/saints/aquila-of-trebizond) — in the mountains above Trebizond, in the reign of Diocletian and Maximian, by a man the page names Lysias. The persecution pressing hard, they had left houses and goods and gone out into the hills, preferring, says the life, the company of wild beasts to that of men who hated God.

They were taken in the land of Lazica, in a small fortress the page calls Până, and were sent into a narrow prison there. After a time they were brought to Trebizond and set before Lysias; questioned about the faith of Christ and pressed to sacrifice, they refused. They were stripped and beaten with sinews, hung up and torn with iron claws, and burned with lighted torches; and the life says that the tormentors fell to the ground like dead men, so that Lysias took fright and sent the three back to prison.

Not many days later [Eugene](/saints/eugene-of-trebizond) was seized as well. All four were thrown at the last into a burning furnace, came out of it unhurt, and were put to death by the sword.

The Greek calendar keeps the four together on the same day and gives him a year the Romanian page did not: the heading for him on their joint page reads «Αγιος Κανδιδος ο Εκ Τραπεζουντας (; - 292)». saint.gr says that a portion of his holy relic is at the monastery of Panagia Soumela of Vermio, names the four patrons of Trebizond, in Turkey, and of Nea Trapezounta in Pieria, and says of their end that they remained unshaken and victorious in their faith through the torments. It prints no hymn for them.

Евгения, Кандида, Валериана и Акилы (III-IV)» and names each of them Trapezuntine.*
