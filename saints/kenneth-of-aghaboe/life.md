# Kenneth of Aghaboe

saint.gr's calendar for 11 Οκτωβρίου names «Άγιος Kenneth», writing the name in Latin letters as the English write it, and heads him «(525 - 599)». According to a legend — «σύμφωνα με έναν μύθο», the page says, and the qualification is its own — he was born in 525 at Glengiven in northern Ireland. He became a monk at Llancarfan in Wales and founded the monastery the page spells Agahanoe.

He may perhaps also be the founder of the monastery of Kilkenny in Ireland, the page goes on, and it says he fell asleep in peace in 599 at Aghaboe in Ireland. That is the whole of the record: no parents, no teacher, no office, nothing of what he did in either country, and no hymn. The only thing the page sets beside him is the synaxis of the saints who shone in Scotland, whose page it links as a neighbour. The Greek form of his name is not printed, so no Greek name form is recorded for this folder. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands.
