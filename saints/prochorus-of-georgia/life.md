# Prochorus of Georgia

saint.gr's calendar for 12 Φεβρουαρίου names the venerable Prochorus from Georgia, and the whole of what its page for him says is one sentence: «Ο Όσιος Πρόχορος έζησε κατά τον 11ο αιώνα μ.Χ. και ασκήτεψε στη Λαύρα του Αγίου Σάββα και στη μονή του Τιμίου Σταυρού στα Ιεροσόλυμα, στην οποία υπήρχαν πολλοί Γεωργιανοί, ενώ διετέλεσε και ηγούμενος αυτής. Κοιμήθηκε οσίως με ειρήνη το έτος 1066 μ.Χ.»

He lived in the eleventh century, that is, and practised ascesis in the Lavra of Saint Sabbas and in the monastery of the Holy Cross at Jerusalem, in which there were many Georgians, and of which he also served as abbot; and he fell asleep in holiness and peace in the year 1066. Nothing else is printed: no parentage, no account of his abbacy, no hymn, no relics and not even a distich, and the page adds only its gloss on the name, that Πρόχορος is the first of any company.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/12/index.aspx) and [his page](https://www.saint.gr/1237/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
