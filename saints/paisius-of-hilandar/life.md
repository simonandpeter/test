# Paisius of Hilandar

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 19 июня ст. ст. names him «Прп. Паисия Хилендарского, Болгарского (XVIII) (Болг.).», marking the commemoration a Bulgarian one, and gives him a life page of its own. He was born in 1722 at Банско of a devout family, it says. One of his brothers, Лаврентий, was abbot of the monastery of Хилендар, and another was known as a generous benefactor of churches and monasteries, while he himself was under obedience in the monastery of Рыла.

In 1745, at twenty-three, he went over to his brother at Хилендар on the Holy Mountain and was tonsured there. On Athos he grew in the spirit, studied the Scriptures deeply, and was counted worthy of holy orders. In 1762 he wrote the «История славяноболгарская», a book which the page says strengthened the Christian faith and awoke the national consciousness of an enslaved people; in the dark of a foreign yoke he lit again the lamp of Orthodoxy that had once been lit by the equals of the apostles «Кирилл и Мефодий».

The time and the place of his repose are still unknown, the page says in terms, and this folder records no year for it: the day line gives only the eighteenth century. On 26 June 1962 the Holy Synod of the Bulgarian Church, under its patriarch and with all its metropolitans taking part, ruled that he be honoured among the venerable and that his memory be kept on 19 июня — the day, as the page puts it, on which the pan-Orthodox menologion keeps the memory of «преподобный Паисий Великий», the Egyptian ascetic of his own name whose day this is. That is why he stands on this day of the Russian calendar and not on another.

The state university at Plovdiv bears his name, the page adds, and so do many institutes and schools in other Bulgarian towns and villages, which it offers as evidence of how deeply the Bulgarian people honour him. The day page prints a troparion and a kontakion for him, both proper, and the kontakion makes the little book of his history the weapon with which he entered the battle.

He is not the Serbian abbot of Trnava whom the Ohrid Prologue keeps in December and whom this corpus already holds: that man was impaled near Čačak in 1814, and this one was a monk of Hilandar and a writer, and the calendar marks him Bulgarian.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 19 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260619.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1350.htm); read 9 October 2026.*
