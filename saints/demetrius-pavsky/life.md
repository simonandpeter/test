# Demetrius (Pavsky)

The calendar names him in four words — a priest Demetrius, shot in 1937 — and the reading behind the name link is one of twenty-three lives printed together under the heading of the new martyrs of the Minsk diocese. It says he was born in 1874 into a hereditary priestly family living in the province of Tver. He finished the Kazan Theological Academy in 1903, took holy orders, and served as inspector of the parish schools of the Minsk diocese.

In 1911 he was appointed rector of the Minsk cathedral of the Holy Apostles Peter and Paul and taught at the same time in the Minsk Theological Seminary. After the revolution he became rector of the church of the Holy Spirit at Ostroshitsky Gorodok in the Minsk district. He was first arrested in 1931, for refusing to give up his priesthood; after the camp he moved to the province of Tver and served as rector of the church of the village of Ulyanovo in the Pogorelsky district.

In June 1937, in a season of hard drought, he served a service of supplication, and rain came soon after. The faithful said it in tears: «Помолились и Господь дал дождя» — we prayed, and the Lord gave rain. The authorities answered quickly, and he was arrested in July. He was questioned once and held himself very calmly; seeing how the questions were put he refused to give evidence, admitted no guilt and named nobody. He was shot on 14 August, new style, 1937.

The reading closes with the two acts of glorification it reports for the whole company: a local veneration granted by the Synod of the Belarusian Exarchate in 1999, and a general one at the Jubilee Council of the Russian Church in August 2000.
