# Deiniol

saint.gr's calendar for 23 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιος Deiniol», printing the name in Latin letters inside its Greek line and giving no Greek spelling of it, and then says the whole of what it has in a single sentence: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέριες για τον βίο του Ουαλού Αγίου» — we have no details of the life of the Welsh saint. The misspelling of λεπτομέρειες is the page's own.

That one word, Welsh, is everything the calendar says about where he came from. It gives him no century, no year, no see, no monastery, no companions, no relics and no hymn, and it sets no couplet over him. This entry records the line and the sentence and nothing more, because there is nothing more on the page to record.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 23 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/23/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3086/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
