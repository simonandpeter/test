# Kevin of Ireland

saint.gr says that Saint Kevin came of a noble family and was educated among distinguished monks of Ireland. When he came of age he founded a monastery in the region of the Two Lakes. So great was the fame of the holiness of his life that many monks gathered around him and that region grew into a town; and having been ordained bishop he built his cathedral church beside the monastery.

Saint Kevin fell asleep in peace in the year 618, at the age of a hundred and twenty. That is the whole of the page's account of him: a family, an education, a foundation, a town that grew out of it, a church, an episcopate named in one word and two round numbers. It says nothing of a rule, of a disciple or of a wonder, and prints no hymn and no verse. Of his company the same calendar keeps one man on this day, the venerable [Cronan](/saints/cronan-of-ireland), whose own page says he belonged to Kevin's following.
