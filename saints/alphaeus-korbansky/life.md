# Alphaeus (Korbansky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Alphaeus among the hieromartyrs on 24 July and gives no life. Its line is «Сщмч. Алфея диакона (1937).» — the hieromartyr Alphaeus the deacon, 1937 — and the calendar's own name page for him adds the surname and keeps the grade the line gives: «Алфей (Корбанский), диакон, сщмч.», Alphaeus Korbansky, deacon.

That is the whole of the record. He served as a deacon and not as a priest, which is unusual enough among this calendar's twentieth-century entries to be worth saying; he died in 1937; and the calendar keeps him on this day among the new martyrs of Russia.

The day page carries ten troparia and kontakia for other memories it heads, and none under his name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 24 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260724.html); read 13 October 2026.*
