# Simon (Shleev) of Ufa

He was the first bishop the Russian Church gave to the edinovertsy, the communities keeping the old rite in union with it, and the reading calls him that throughout: «единоверческий епископ Уфимский». He was born in 1873 in the province of Simbirsk and was called Simeon Ivanovich Shleev in the world. After the Nizhny Novgorod seminary he entered the Kazan Theological Academy as an external student and finished it in 1899 with the degree of candidate of theology.

On 17 January 1900 he was ordained priest to the old-rite church of Kazan, and from 7 February 1905 he was priest of the old-rite church of Saint Nicholas in Saint Petersburg, of which he became rector on 22 November 1907. On 16 June 1918 he was consecrated by the patriarch, in the monastery the reading names «Александро-Невская Лавра», to the newly founded old-rite see as bishop of Okhta. From 1920 he was the old-rite bishop of Ufa, and in February 1921 he was appointed to the see at Ufa as its ruling bishop.

He was killed on his way back to his residence from the cathedral on 5 August, old style, 1921 — 18 August by the new. The reading closes with his glorification among the New Martyrs and Confessors of Russia at the Jubilee Council of August 2000, and lists his days as 26 января among the new martyrs, 8 июня among the saints of Petersburg, and this one.
