# Helen (26 May)

doxologia.ro's calendar names her and gives no life. The line for 26 mai reads «Sfânta Muceniță Elena» — the holy martyr Helen — with no epithet, no city, no country and no century, and her own page on the site carries only that line, as heading and as title. There is no Viață tab and no Tropar tab; the page is a name and nothing more.

The display name here carries the day for that reason. She is not the empress Helen, the mother of Constantine, whom the same calendar keeps on 21 mai and whom it calls «Împărăteasă» and not «Muceniță»; the two are kept on different days and given different ranks, and nothing here joins them. This folder asserts one thing only: that the Romanian church keeps a woman martyr named Helen on 26 May. No year is claimed and no other calendar has been checked.

The Greek calendar keeps her on the same day and gives her a family. saint.gr's whole account is one sentence: «Στους Συναξαριστές αναφέρεται ότι ήταν αδελφή του Αγίου Αβερκίου… και μαρτύρησε δια λιθοβολισμού» — in the synaxarists it is recorded that she was the sister of [Abercius](/saints/abercius-martyr-26-may), kept on this same day, and that she was martyred by stoning. Since the same page makes Abercius a son of the Apostle [Alphaeus](/saints/alphaeus-the-apostle), who is kept on the day as well, she is his daughter on the same reckoning. Beside the sentence the page sets a couplet that turns the stones into jewellery: the stones were your ornament, Helen martyr, and by them you were shown comely to the Bridegroom. No year, no place and no hymn is given her.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 26 mai — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/26-mai) and [her page](https://doxologia.ro/sfanta-mucenita-elena), which carries no life and no troparion; read 24 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3939/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026.*
