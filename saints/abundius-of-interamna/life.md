# Abundius of Interamna

Abundius was the son of the prefect of Rome, and he came to Christ through a healing. Saint [Valentine of Interamna](/saints/valentine-of-interamna), bishop of Interamna in Umbria, had from God the gift of healing sicknesses by prayer in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ, and he cured the bent son of a teacher named Craton after a night of prayer. Word of it went through the city, and many turned to the faith; Abundius was among them, and having been baptised he confessed himself a Christian openly before all.

That was a bold thing while the old gods still ruled the world and Christians were hunted, and his father's anger, with that of the other magistrates, fell on the bishop who had taught the young men. Valentine was pressed to deny Christ and worship the idols, and after many torments he was shut in prison and then brought out and beheaded. His three disciples [Proculus](/saints/proculus-of-interamna), [Ephebus](/saints/ephebus-of-interamna) and [Apollonius](/saints/apollonius-of-interamna) carried his body to Interamna and buried it with honour.

The three kept their days at the tomb in prayer, and were seized for drawing believers and pagans to the faith and beheaded in prison by night. Abundius, hearing that his friends were held, hurried to them and found them already dead; he grieved greatly, took their bodies and buried them beside the tomb of the bishop. The calendar calls him righteous and not a martyr, and prints no hymn for the company and no word of how he died.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 30 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260730.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4353.htm); read 13 October 2026.*
