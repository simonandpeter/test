# Julian of Cenomania

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps on 13 July a bishop of the apostolic age. Its reading says Julian was made bishop by the apostle Peter, and adds an opinion it does not endorse — that he is one person with the Simon called the leper of Mark 14:3, who received the name Julian at his baptism. Peter sent him to preach the Gospel in Gaul.

He came, the reading says, to Cenomania, which it glosses as the region of the river Po in the north of what is now Italy, and settled outside the city — probably Cremona, it says — in a small hut, and began to preach among the pagans. They heard him at first with mistrust, but great wonders went with the preaching and he healed many diseases by prayer, so that crowds came to him and he healed their souls as well as their bodies.

To give his visitors water he prayed, thrust his staff into the ground, and drew a spring out of a dry place, and that wonder turned many to Christ. The local prince wished to see him; at the gate of the prince's house he gave sight to a blind man, and the prince, hearing of it, fell at his feet and asked for baptism. Julian catechised him and his household, set them a three-day fast, and baptised them, and most of the prince's subjects followed. The prince gave his own house for a church and endowed it.

He cared for the teaching of his flock and went on healing, and the reading says that out of pity for bereaved parents he asked God by his prayer to give their dead children back. He kept his throne a long time and died a very old man, having preached Christ to the end and put down idolatry in the Cenomanian country altogether.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 13 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260713.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4230.htm); read 9 October 2026.*
