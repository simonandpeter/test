# Amonathas

saint.gr's calendar for 12 December names the venerable Amonathas and gives him one line of life: he reposed in peace. No country, no century, no monastery and no relic is printed, and he is joined to no other name on the day. What the page adds is the couplet set over his name, and it puts the words in his own mouth as he dies.

I hate the things of the earth; receive me, angels — so, it says, the divine Amonathas speaks as he departs. That is the whole of the entry, and nothing further is claimed here. The corpus holds no other folder of this name against which he would have to be told apart.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3245/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
