# Cleonicus, disciple of Athenogenes

saint.gr's calendar for 16 July keeps Athenogenes, bishop of Pedachthoe, together with ten of his disciples, and Cleonicus is one of the ten the page names. Its account is short. [Athenogenes](/saints/athenogenes-of-pedachthoe) came from Sebastia in Cappadocia; his learning, his warm faith and his brave works of charity made him bishop of Pedachthoe, and as bishop he took particular care to train capable helpers, so that the work of the Gospel should go on after him.

Those helpers are the ten. When the persecution under Diocletian came, the page says, Athenogenes with ten disciples of his — and it lists them one by one, Cleonicus among them — was arrested by the governor Philimarchos, and after they had all confessed Christ they were beheaded. The page heads each of the eleven «(; - 311)», and of Cleonicus in particular it says nothing further: no birthplace of his own, no rank, no age, and no word of anything he said or did.

What the Greek calendar records of him, then, is his name, his master, his company and his death. The two apolytikia the page prints both address the bishop, and speak of the disciples only as the all-venerable decad of his students, so no hymn is taken for Cleonicus himself.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 16 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/16/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2098/saint.aspx), which the eleven share; read 1 October 2026.*
