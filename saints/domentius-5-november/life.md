# Domentius (5 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 5 Νοεμβρίου names Domentius and gives no life. Its entry for the day is «Άγιοι Δομέντιος και Παύλος ο επίσκοπος», and the page behind it says of the pair only this: «Άγνωστοι στους Συναξαριστές. Αναφέρονται στο Ιεροσολυμιτικό Κανονάριο εκδ. Καλλίστου Αρχιμανδρίτου σελ. 117, χωρίς βιογραφικά στοιχεία» — they are unknown to the synaxarists, and are mentioned in the Jerusalem Kanonarion, in Archimandrite Kallistos's edition at page 117, with no biographical details.

So the whole of what is recorded of Domentius is his name, his day, and the one book that carries him. The page gives him no century, no country, no rank, no manner of death, no year, no couplet and no hymn. The other man of the entry is [Paul](/saints/paul-5-november), whom the day's line calls a bishop and of whom the page says exactly as little.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2898/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
