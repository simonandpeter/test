# Basil (Izmailov)

He was born on 4 June 1885 at Vyshny Volochok in the Tver province, in the family of a man in holy orders. He finished the theological academy and taught in the Minsk theological seminary. In 1927 he was appointed rector of the cathedral of the Resurrection in the town of Borisov, where he served only a few months before he was arrested.

Charged with counter-revolutionary activity, he was sentenced to three years in the camp at Solovki, and he died there on 22 February 1930. The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 9 февраля ст. ст. with the line «Сщмч. Василия пресвитера (1930).», and its notice of the twenty-three hieromartyrs of Minsk is where the rest of this is read.

That notice spells his surname «Измаилов» where the name link behind the day's line has «Измайлов»; the calendar gives both, and the difference is the calendar's own. The day page's hymns carry nothing under his name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 9 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260209.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4766.htm); read 3 October 2026.*
