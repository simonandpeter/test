# Joseph of Kantara

The thirteen fathers of the monastery of Kantara in Cyprus are kept together on 19 Μαΐου, and saint.gr gives them one narrative under thirteen separate head names. They shone by their life, it says, on the island of Cyprus in the thirteenth century, in the monastery of Kantara. Then the Latins took the island, and did everything they could to bring the Church under the authority of the Pope.

That attempt began in 1220, at a council held in the city of Limassol, where representatives of the Latin clergy and of the ruling class took part and resolved that oppressive measures should be imposed for the complete prevailing of the Latin Church. So, about 1228, the conquerors brought these fathers before their court, where with flatteries and with threats they tried to change their mind.

The saints stayed firm in the Orthodox confession and declared it with courage in front of the Latins. Then, having got nothing from them, their captors threw them bound into prison for three years and tortured them hard; and because they still got nothing, they burned them alive on 19 May 1231. saint.gr prints no hymn for the company and names no place where they were laid.

Of Joseph of Kantara himself saint.gr gives no separate account. His name stands in the day's line and again at the head of the page, «Αγιος Ιωσηφ Οσιομαρτυρας Και Ομολογητης Της Μονης Κανταρας Κυπρου (; - 1231)», and everything else recorded here he shares with the other twelve fathers of his house.
