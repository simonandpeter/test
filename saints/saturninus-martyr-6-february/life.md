# Saturninus (6 February)

saint.gr names him and gives one sentence of life. Its calendar for 6 February prints «Άγιοι Θεόφιλος, Σατουρνίνος και Ρεβοκάτος», and the page behind that line says that [Theophilus](/saints/theophilus-martyr-6-february), Saturninus and [Revocatus](/saints/revocatus) were martyred in Spain in the reign of the emperor Decius, which the page dates 249 to 251. That is all: no city, no trial, no manner of death, no hymn, no verses.

The corpus already holds a Saturninus, one of the Ten Martyrs of Crete, and that is a different man on a different day. It is also worth noticing that Saturninus and Revocatus are the names of two of the companions of Perpetua and Felicity at Carthage, whom this corpus keeps; the Greek line puts these three in Spain two generations later, and no folder is held for the Carthaginian pair, so nothing here is doubled.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3616/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
