# Gerasimus (Sukhov)

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him nineteenth of twenty-four and gives no life; the life here is the one azbyka.ru prints for the civil 2 декабря, out of pstgu.ru, and it is short. Gerasim Fedotovich Sukhov was born in 1866 in the Chembar district of the province of Penza, in a peasant family. He was tonsured a monk and raised hieromonk, and the page says of his arrest in 1936 only that in the years of the great persecution the man who had laid his whole life on the altar of the service of Christ could not but suffer.

At seventy he was charged with belonging to a counter-revolutionary group and sentenced to five years of exile in Kazakhstan, and found himself in the village of Borodulikha in the Semipalatinsk region together with a number of other clergy. On 22 November 1937 he was arrested in exile by the Bel-Agach district branch of the NKVD on the same ground as the archpriest Demetrius Kuklin, and when the case of Kuklin and others was made up the charges were put as «проведение религиозных обрядов и нелегальных сборищ» and the spreading of agitation against the collective-farm order. On 27 November 1937 he was sentenced to the highest measure of punishment, and on 2 December he was shot at Semipalatinsk.
