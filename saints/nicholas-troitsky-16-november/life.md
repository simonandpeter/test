# Nicholas (Troitsky) (16 November)

He is the second of the six presbyters the Сретенский monastery’s calendar names on one line for the Julian 16 November: «Сщмчч. Иоанна , Николая , Виктора , Василия , Макария и Михаила пресвитеров (1937).» He was born on 23 July 1887 in the village of Pokrov in the province of Tver, where his father, the archpriest John Troitsky, served in the church. When he had finished the theological seminary he was ordained priest to that same church of the village of Pokrov, not far from the town of Udomlya, and served there until the persecution of the end of the twenties and the beginning of the thirties.

In 1930 he was arrested because he could not pay the tax laid on him, and sentenced to two years in a corrective labour camp and five years’ exile. When he came back to Tver from the camp in 1932 the holy archbishop Thaddeus blessed him to serve in the church of the village of Vereskunovo in the Udomlya district, and there he served until 1937. The НКВД arrested him on 20 November 1937, he was shut in the prison of the town of Bezhetsk, and the investigator questioned him the same day.

The questions turned on a christening. He agreed that he had baptised the child of a villager in the summer of 1936 and had been asked in afterwards; he did not remember what he had talked about with the man’s brother. Told that he had held anti-Soviet conversations and spoken of a war to come and the perishing of the godless, he said his whole talk had been to persuade the man of the need to believe in God, and that he had said God is long-suffering but a moment would come when He would begin to deal with the godless. Asked about the watchman’s hut on the feast of the Protection, he said he had told the collective farmers that God was beginning to punish people for what they did: the water was gone and the wells were dry, and the people might suffer thirst because of the godless, and then those who had fallen away would repent.

The investigator put it to him that such talk was meant to turn the collective farmers against the Soviet power and demanded a sincere confession; he answered that he had had no such aim. Told there was evidence he had agitated against the candidates put up for the Supreme Soviet, saying that whoever they had in mind would be elected and nobody would ask the people, he said there had been no such conversation. He would not admit the charge. On 27 November the troika of the НКВД sentenced him to be shot, and he was shot on 29 November 1937. He was numbered among the New Martyrs and Confessors of Russia at the jubilee council of bishops of August 2000, for the veneration of the whole church.

He is not the Nicholas (Troitsky) this corpus already keeps. That folder was written off the same calendar’s line for 7 ноября ст. ст., where fifteen names stand together, and the name link behind his place in it is name page 9734; the link behind this line is name page 9735, and the two pages carry the same string, «Николай (Троицкий), иерей, сщмч.», for two different men on two different days. The 7 November folder has no life page at all, while this one has life4871, which gives a birth in 1887, a father, two parishes and the interrogation above. Nothing identifies them, and the calendar separates them by day and by dossier.
