# Livia

doxologia.ro's calendar names her and gives no life. The line for 25 iunie reads «Sfânta Muceniță Livia», and her page carries the day, an icon under her name, some articles and the readings appointed for the day, and nothing else — no country, no century, no manner of death, no year and no hymn.

One thing on the same day's calendar stands near her name without being joined to it. In the life of [Fevronia](/saints/fevronia-of-sivapol), which doxologia prints for this day, the abbess Bryene strengthens her disciple by recalling earlier martyrs, and among them «Livia și Leonida, cele două surori» — Livia and Leonida, two sisters, of whom Livia was beheaded with the sword and Leonida thrown into the fire, and both entered the chamber of the heavenly Bridegroom.

Whether the Livia of the calendar's line is that Livia the source does not say, and it is not said here. The name, the rank of martyr and the day are what the calendar gives, and that is what is recorded.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 25 iunie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/25-iunie) and [her page](https://doxologia.ro/sfanta-mucenita-livia); read 24 September 2026.*
