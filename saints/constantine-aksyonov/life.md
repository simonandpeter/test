# Constantine (Aksyonov)

His line follows the pair of 1918 in the same paragraph of the Russian 9 October and is four words long: «Сщмч. Константина пресвитера (1937)» — Constantine, presbyter, hieromartyr, 1937. He is the second Constantine the paragraph names and nineteen years later than the first, and the only thing that separates two otherwise identical lines is what the site's index of names adds: his own page is headed «Константин (Аксёнов), иерей, сщмч.» where the other is [Constantine (Sukhov)](/saints/constantine-sukhov).

1937 is the worst year of the terror and the calendar says nothing at all of how it reached him: no birth, no diocese, no parish, no charge, no place of death. His index entry keeps him here and on 26 January with the whole company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and carries no life. The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian saints are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026 even with a browser user-agent, so the line stands as the line: a forename, a surname spelled with the ё, the simple priesthood, and the year.

*After the Russian church calendar's [9 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261009.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9656.html); read 4 October 2026.*
