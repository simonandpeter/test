# Oropsis

saint.gr keeps three men together on 22 August and tells one story for all of them. [Irenaeus](/saints/irenaeus-the-martyr-22-august), it says, was distinguished among the priests of the Church of Christ, and he did not only support the faithful by his teaching and his example but spread his spiritual nets among the unbelieving as well, and brought many of them into the ark of salvation, that is, into the Church of Christ. For that activity he was arrested and underwent dreadful torments.

The other two, [Or](/saints/or-the-martyr-22-august) and Oropsis, had the courage to make the sharpest remonstrance to the eparch, who was a spectator of the tortures. Then they met the same fate: they were tortured along with the priest, and all three were afterwards thrown into the fire.

But suddenly, the page says, a violent rain put the fire out, and the three martyrs came out of the furnace untouched. The eparch, panic-stricken, gave the order and they were beheaded. The page's one distich is for the three together — «Ξίφει τριὰς τμηθεῖσα, τριστάτας πλάνης, Βυθῷ καλύπτει τῶν ἑαυτῆς αἱμάτων» — a triad cut down by the sword covers the captains of error in the depth of its own blood. It gives no year, no city, no name for the eparch, and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 22 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/22/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/830/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
