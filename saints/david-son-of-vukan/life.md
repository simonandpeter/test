# David, son of Vukan

David, first Prince Dimitrije, was the son of Vukan, the brother of [Stefan](/saints/stefan-the-first-crowned). The Ohrid Prologue, for the Serbian calendar, keeps him on 24 September old style and says only that he built a monastery on the Lim, where he himself became a monk. It gives no year.
