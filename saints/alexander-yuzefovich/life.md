# Alexander (Yuzefovich)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him among the hieromartyrs on 13 декабря and gives no life. Its line is «Сщмч. Александра пресвитера и мч. Иоанна (1920).» — the hieromartyr Alexander the presbyter and the martyr John, 1920 — and the calendar’s own name page adds the surname the line leaves out: «Александр (Юзефович), иерей, сщмч.»

That is the whole of the record. He was a priest; he died in 1920; and the calendar keeps him on this day beside [John (Menkov)](/saints/john-menkov), the layman its line joins to him. The day page carries troparia and kontakia for five of the memories it heads, and none under his name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 13 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261213.html); read 13 October 2026.*
