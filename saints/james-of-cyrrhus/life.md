# James of Cyrrhus

It is Theodoret of Cyrrhus who tells of James, saint.gr says, and he knew him personally. James came from the city of Cyrrhus and at first practised asceticism inside a very narrow cell. Afterwards he went up a mountain near the same city, where he walked the ascetic road with severity.

In the name of owning nothing he never built himself a hut. He prayed in the open air, standing, with iron weights on him, and his fasting was of the strictest. So, having contended well, he gave up his soul to God in peace.

That is the whole of the page: a witness who knew him, a city, a mountain, a discipline, and a peaceful end. It gives no year and no century, prints no hymn and no verses, and does not say which of the many saints of this name the Greek calendar means beyond what the reference to Theodoret fixes.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3605/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
