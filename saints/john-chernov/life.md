# John (Chernov)

His line in the calendar is three words long, and it is the whole of what is recorded here. The Сретенский monastery’s calendar for 28 марта ст. ст. prints «Мч. Иоанна (1939)» — the martyr John, 1939 — and the name page behind that forename gives the surname the line leaves out and nothing besides: «Иоанн (Чернов), мч.» The calendar gives him no monastic grade and no rank in the clergy, which is how it prints a layman rather than a monk or a priest.

So nothing is said here of where he was born, what he did, when or by whom he was arrested, or where he died. There is no life page for him on the day and none on his name page, and 1939 is the only year the calendar gives. He is the third of three men this day of the calendar prints with a year of the nineteen-thirties and no life, and the only one of the three the calendar does not give a rank in the clergy.

His name page lists one other day, the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church. One row per church is the schema, so that day is recorded on his Russian row and he does not render on it.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 28 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260328.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13710.html); read 8 October 2026.*
