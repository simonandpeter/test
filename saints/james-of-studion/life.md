# James of Studion

The Ohrid Prologue's entry for the Julian 24 March is the whole of what this folder records, and it is five sentences long. James suffered for the icons under the emperor Leo the Armenian. He was a monk and a brother of the monastery of Studion. And when the great [Theodore the Studite](/saints/theodore-the-studite) was in exile, they threw this James into great torments, to make him renounce the veneration of the icons; but he stayed steadfast and faithful to Orthodoxy to the end.

Beaten and worn out, they brought him back to the monastery when the wicked emperor Leo had come to his own bad end. In the monastery he died of his heavy beatings, and passed over among the citizens of heaven. The entry gives no year of any kind, so this folder holds none; and it names no city, no judge and no place of his exile beyond the monastery he came back to. The Prologue prints no hymn for him, or for any saint of the day.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 24. март ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-04-06&prolog=1); read 4 October 2026.*
