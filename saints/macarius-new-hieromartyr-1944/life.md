# Macarius (1 April)

saint.gr's whole notice for him is one sentence: the holy hieromartyr Macarius was martyred in Russia in the year 1944. That is all the Greek calendar says. There is no name in the world, no see and no monastery, no city, no manner of death, no persecutor named, no relic, no hymn and not even the distich the site usually prints — only a rank, a country, and a year late enough that a fuller record certainly exists somewhere this reading did not go.

The day's line calls him ο Νέος, the new, which is how the Greek calendar marks the martyrs of the modern persecutions off from the ancient ones of the same name; and this day keeps an ancient Macarius too, the confessor and abbot of Pelekete, who belongs to the ninth century and the struggle over the icons. The two are a thousand years apart and are two commemorations on one day.

What this folder records, then, is that the Greek church keeps on the first of April a priest named Macarius killed in Russia in 1944. The Russian calendar has not been read for him, and reading it is what would give him a surname and a place.
