# Bartholomew (Ratnykh)

The Russian calendar's line is all there is. It names two together on this day, «Сщмч. Владимира пресвитера и прмч. Варфоломея (1938)» — the hieromartyr Vladimir, presbyter, and the venerable-martyr Bartholomew, 1938. The name behind his half of it reads «Варфоломей (Ратных), иеромонах, сщмч.», Bartholomew Ratnykh, hieromonk, hieromartyr — and so the line and the name link give him two different ranks, прмч. in the one and сщмч. in the other.

There is no life page behind either name and no hymn for them on the day. Nothing here says where they served, whether they were taken together or where they died; the year 1938 is the calendar's own and the only date it prints. He is kept with [Vladimir](/saints/vladimir-pishchulin), whom both the line and the name link call a priest.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 28 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260128.html); read 3 October 2026.*
