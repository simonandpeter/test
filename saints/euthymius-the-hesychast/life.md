# Euthymius the Hesychast of the Kyiv Caves

The Greek calendar keeps him on 20 Ιανουαρίου with [Laurence the Recluse](/saints/laurence-the-recluse), and the page behind the line says of both that they lived in a manner pleasing to God, in ascetic labour and prayer, in the Lavra of Kyiv. Of Euthymius it says that he practised the ascetic life in the fourteenth century in the monastery of [Saint Theodosius](/saints/theodosius-of-the-kyiv-caves) of the Great Lavra of Kyiv, and that he had taken a vow of silence and opened his mouth only to chant.

That is the whole of what the page gives him: a century, a house, and the manner of his silence. No year of birth, no year of repose, no miracle and no hymn is printed for him. He is not the Laurence of Chernigov whom the Romanian calendar keeps on this same day, nor is his companion here; the Chernigov elder is another man of a much later age, and nothing is carried between the folders.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 20 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/20/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2607/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 20 января ст. ст. — [the Russian day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260120.html), read 3 October 2026, which keeps him as «Прп. Евфимия схимника Печерского (XIV)».*
