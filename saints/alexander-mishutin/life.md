# Alexander (Mishutin)

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him fifth of twenty-four and gives no life; the life here is the one azbyka.ru prints for the civil 2 декабря, out of pstgu.ru and the Orthodox Encyclopaedia. Alexander Lavrentyevich Mishutin was born in 1885 in the town of Ryazhsk in the province of Ryazan. He finished the Ryazan teachers’ seminary, was in holy orders from 1916, and lived at Moscow. On 19 February 1935 he was sent into exile in Kazakhstan for five years, and lived more than two of them at the Maysky mine in the Beskaragay district of the Pavlodar region.

He was arrested on 25 November 1937 with the other clergy exiled to that mine, and the page lists them: the archimandrites Gregory (Rebeza) and Benjamin (Zykov), the archpriests John Malinovsky, Constantine Mikhailovsky and Alexander Serebrov, the priests Ignatius Teslin, Simeon Krivosheev, John Piramidin, John Florovsky and Nicholas Amasiysky, and the layman Valentine Kornienko. He was charged with counter-revolutionary agitation and sentenced to be shot, and the sentence was carried out on 2 December 1937. The Jubilee Council of Bishops of 2000 glorified him among the new martyrs.
