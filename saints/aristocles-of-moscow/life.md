# Aristocles, Elder of Moscow

The Russian calendar keeps on 24 August (Julian) the venerable Aristocles, elder of Moscow, who died in 1918, and prints no life under the line — the same day it keeps the Synaxis of the saints of Moscow, with its troparion that says the prophecy of the hierarch Peter is fulfilled before our eyes. This entry records the line and nothing more.

*After the day’s line of the Sretensky calendar (days.pravoslavie.ru), 24 August (Julian) — [the page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260824.html), which prints no life; read 23 August 2026.*
