# The Six Hermit Martyrs

saint.gr's calendar for 19 October keeps «Άγιοι Έξι Οσιομάρτυρες οι ερημίτες», the six holy venerable-martyrs, the hermits, and its whole notice of them is one sentence: the six were martyred by the sword together with Saint [Varus](/saints/varus-the-martyr), and it sends the reader to him on the same day. Not one of the six is named, here or there, and the verse over their entry says only that six men who had lived as ascetics die together as contestants by the sword.

What story they have is in his page. Varus was a soldier of Tyana set to guard the prisons in the persecutions under Diocletian, a Christian whom nobody suspected, and he used that to bring food to the prisoners and to strengthen them. Once six faithful and venerable ascetics were brought in; there had been a seventh with them, who died on the road of old age and hardship. These prisoners worked so powerfully on Varus by their faces, their words and their ways that he wanted to die with them, and when the judge asked where their seventh companion was, he cried out, here am I.

Varus died under very savage torture without changing his faith, having asked the six to pray that God would give him strength to bear it. The next day the heads of the ascetics too were cut off, and in the night Christian hands reverently buried the seven martyrs of the faith together. The calendar gives the six no names, no country and no year, and prints no hymn for them; so they stand here as one folder for the whole company, because the source keeps them as a number beside a man it does name.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 19 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/19/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2768/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
