# Gerasimus of Boltinsk

Gerasimus of Boltinsk was Gregory in the world, and was born in 1490 at Pereslavl-Zalessky in Russia. From childhood he was marked, saint.gr says, by his love of the services and his piety; he took Daniel of Pereyaslavl for his model and went early to the monastic life, and after a short probation was tonsured and given the name Gerasimus. The new monk fulfilled the virtues of fasting and prayer with great zeal, and soon became known in Moscow for his spirituality and the severity of his life, and for that reason was called to Moscow, where he met the tsar.

His great reputation in the country was a weight on him, for he wanted quiet and ascetic labour. So after twenty-six years under Daniel's guidance he took his elder's blessing for the hesychastic life, and settled near the town of Dorogobuzh in the land of Smolensk, in a wild forest where only snakes and wild beasts lived. Robbers often attacked him, and he bore it calmly and quietly, praying for men who were far from the way of God. Because of a particular vision he went to the hill of Boldina, where an enormous oak stood by a spring; the natives beat him with clubs and wanted to drown him, and then took fright and handed him to the governor of Dorogobuzh, who threw him in prison for being homeless. Gerasimus endured the humiliation patiently, kept silent and prayed.

While he was there an imperial envoy came from Moscow to the governor, and seeing Gerasimus he bowed to him and asked his blessing, for he had seen him once before with Saint Daniel in the tsar's presence. The governor was terrified, begged the monk's forgiveness at once, and promised to build him a dwelling to keep him safe from robbers. From then on Gerasimus began to receive men who wanted the monastic life, and asked leave of Moscow to build a monastery; and in 1530 he raised a church dedicated to the Holy Trinity and built cells for the monks.

Besides the house at Boldina he founded another monastery in honour of John the Forerunner in the town of Vyazma, and later, in the forest of Vrianka on the river Zhizdra, a monastery dedicated to the Entry of the Mother of God, over which Peter Korostelev, a disciple of his, became abbot. Several ascetics were under his guidance: the abbot Antony, who later became bishop of Vologda, and Arkady, who lived as a hermit and was buried at the monastery of Boldina. Before his death Gerasimus called all the abbots and monks of the houses he had founded, told them about his life and gave them his last spiritual charges, which were written down in the book «Life» that Saint Antony wrote at the elders' demand. He fell asleep in peace in 1554.

The Russian church calendar keeps him on the same day, and it is the source that names the hill and the four houses. He was born in 1490 at Pereslavl-Zalessky and called Григорий. He loved to go to church from childhood, and at thirteen he entered the monastery of «преподобный Даниил Переяславский», who tonsured him before long with the name Герасим; he kept fasting and prayer zealously and served the poorer brethren with his trade of shoemaker. His reputation as a strict ascetic reached Moscow and he was summoned there with his teacher and met the tsar, which weighed on him.

After twenty-six years under that teacher he took his blessing for the hermit's life and settled in the Smolensk land not far from the town of Dorogobuzh, in wild forest full of snakes and beasts. Robbers set upon him many times and he bore the injuries meekly and prayed for those who did them. By a particular vision he moved to the hill of Болдино, where a great oak stood by a spring. The people of the place beat him with sticks and meant to drown him, then took fright and handed him over to the governor of Dorogobuzh, who had him put in prison as a vagrant; and he bore the mockery in silence and prayer until a messenger from the tsar arrived, recognised him from Moscow, bowed to him and asked his blessing, at which the frightened governor begged his pardon and promised to shield him.

From then he began to receive those who wanted the monastic labour, and having asked leave in Moscow to found a house he raised a church in honour of the Holy Trinity in 1530 and built cells for the brethren gathering to him. Besides Болдино he founded a monastery of John the Forerunner at Vyazma in 1553, a house of the Entry of the Mother of God into the Temple in the forest of Bryansk on the river Жиздра, and nine years before his death another near Dorogobuzh in the name of the Nativity of the Mother of God. Before the end he called together the abbots and monks of the houses he had founded, told them his life and gave them his last instruction; that spoken account was taken into the life which «святитель Антоний Вологодский», a disciple of his, composed by decision of the elders of the council. He fell asleep on 1 May 1554, in the sixty-fifth year of his life.
