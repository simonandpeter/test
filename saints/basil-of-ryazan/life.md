# Basil of Ryazan

saint.gr says he lived in Russia between the thirteenth and the fourteenth century, and that for his virtues he was elected bishop of Ryazan and Murom. The one deed it keeps of him is a crossing: he it was who, sailing miraculously over water, carried the wonderworking icon of the Mother of God of Murom to the city of Ryazan. He fell asleep in holiness and in peace.

That is all the page prints. It gives no year of birth, no year of death and no account of the crossing beyond the one clause, so nothing further is recorded here. It does add that the Church keeps his memory on 10 June as well, and that the finding of his relics is kept as its own commemoration; neither of those pages has been read.
