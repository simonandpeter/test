# Basil of Ryazan

saint.gr says he lived in Russia between the thirteenth and the fourteenth century, and that for his virtues he was elected bishop of Ryazan and Murom. The one deed it keeps of him is a crossing: he it was who, sailing miraculously over water, carried the wonderworking icon of the Mother of God of Murom to the city of Ryazan. He fell asleep in holiness and in peace.

That is all the page prints. It gives no year of birth, no year of death and no account of the crossing beyond the one clause, so nothing further is recorded here. It does add that the Church keeps his memory on 10 June as well, and that the finding of his relics is kept as its own commemoration; neither of those pages has been read.

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 3 июля ст. ст. — «Свт. Василия , еп. Рязанского (1295).» — the same menologion day as the Greek page this folder was made from, and its name page widens the see to bishop of Ryazan and Murom and sets him on two days, this one and the finding of his relics on 10 июня. The year it prints, 1295, falls inside the interval this folder already holds and was not written into it, because the life the calendar links was not opened.

Under his name the day page prints a troparion and a kontakion, and both tell the same story: that he was first honoured with the episcopate in Murom, that he was driven out of it unjustly, that he went up on his mantle as on a light ship and travelled over the water beyond nature, as one without a body, guided by the providence of the Most High and the protection of the Mother of God, until he reached the city of Ryazan, where he took the throne and shepherded the flock given him of God.
