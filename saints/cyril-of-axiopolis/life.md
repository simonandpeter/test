# Cyril of Axiopolis

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 26 April reads «Sfinții Mucenici Chiril, Chindeu și Tasie din Axiopolis (Cernavodă)» — the holy martyrs Cyril, Quindeus and Dasius of Axiopolis — and the page that stands under that line carries icons, the epistle and the gospel appointed for the day, and links to articles, but no account of any of the three. It prints no year, no emperor, no manner of death and no place beyond the one the line itself names.

That one place the calendar glosses for its readers: Axiopolis, and in brackets Cernavodă. He is kept with [Quindeus](/saints/quindeus-of-axiopolis) and [Dasius](/saints/dasius-of-axiopolis), and the headings above the page's own articles spell the third name «Dasie» where the calendar line spells it «Tasie». Nothing else on the page tells the three of them apart.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 26 aprilie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/26-aprilie) and [the page for the three martyrs](https://doxologia.ro/sfintii-mucenici-chiril-chindeu-tasie-din-axiopolis-cernavoda); read 20 September 2026.*
