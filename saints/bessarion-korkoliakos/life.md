# Bessarion Korkoliakos

Bessarion was born at Petalidi in Messenia in 1908 and was given the name Andrew at his baptism. At eighteen he went to Kalamata, where he met men of the spiritual life and decided to enter the clergy; he was tonsured a monk with the name Bessarion, and was afterwards ordained deacon and priest and given the rank of archimandrite. In 1935 he went to Karditsa at the invitation of the metropolitan there, and took part in the charitable work of the Sotiria hospital.

During the German occupation, the page says, he helped many of his countrymen and by his own interventions saved children whom the Germans had seized. From 1955 he lived the ascetic life at the monastery of Agathon in Phthiotis, and gave himself to the spiritual renewal of that house. Every Monday and Tuesday he went to the hospitals of Lamia to visit the sick, to console them and to hear their confessions; and doxologia.ro says that by his charismatic person, his love of people and his plain way of being he comforted souls in grief.

He fell asleep in the Lord on 22 January 1991, of a pulmonary oedema, at the Sotiria hospital in Athens. His relics were found incorrupt on 3 March 2006.
