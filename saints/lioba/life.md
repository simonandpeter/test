# Lioba

saint.gr's calendar for 28 Οκτωβρίου names «Αγία Lioba», writing her in Latin letters in the day's line and as «Λαϊόβα» in the notice itself. She was the niece of [Boniface, Apostle of Germany](/saints/boniface-apostle-of-germany), the page says, whom the same calendar keeps on 5 June; and she went to Germany together with thirty more British nuns, sent by the princess Telta, abbess of the monastery of Wimborne, to help in the spreading of the Christian faith among the German tribes.

Of what she did there, where she settled and how she died the page says nothing: only that the venerable Lioba fell asleep in 782, which is the year its heading gives for her as well. It names no place of hers in Germany, records no relics and prints no hymn. The entry carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for it as it stands.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/28/index.aspx) and [her notice](https://www.saint.gr/2832/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
