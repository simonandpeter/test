# Theodore of Alexandria, martyr

Theodore was seized by the pagans of Alexandria because he preached Christ with particular boldness; they crowned him with a crown of thorns, struck him in the face and led him chained round the streets of the city, and after other tortures he was beheaded at the ruler’s order and so received the unfading crown of eternal glory — his head cut off by the sword, the verse plays, he shared in God’s gifts as his name says. Eustratiades notes in his Hagiologion that he is wrongly carried as bishop of Alexandria and was a simple God-fearing Christian; indeed in the list of the patriarchs the first Theodore is Theodore I the Scribe (608–610), kept on 3 December. The Greek calendar keeps him on 12 September.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 12 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2411/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
