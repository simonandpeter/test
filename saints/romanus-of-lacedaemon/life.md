# Romanus of Lacedaemon

saint.gr says the holy hieromartyr Romanus came from Dominitza — or Diminitza, the page gives both spellings — in Lacedaemon, and that he was a hieromonk at Constantinople. Refusing to give way to the pressure the Turks put on him to renounce his faith, and rebuking the error of the unbelieving with boldness of speech, he was martyred by the sword in the year 1695.

That is the whole of the notice. The page's two-line verse says that Romanus, having set out from earth by the sword, dwells there where the companies of the martyrs are. saint.gr sends the reader on to two synaxes he belongs to — of the saints who shone in Laconia, and of the new martyrs who bore witness after the fall of Constantinople — and prints no hymn for him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1077/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
