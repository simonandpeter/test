# Meletius the New of Myoupolis

Meletius was born in 1035 in the village of Moutalaske in Cappadocia to very virtuous parents, John and Sophia, who brought him up in their faith. He left his father’s house with a longing for the monastic life and went to Constantinople, later to Thessalonica, and then to Thebes, to the monastery of St George (now the parish church); then to the monastery of Sagmata, and later to Cithaeron, where he raised a monastery. The synaxarion’s life runs on past the point read here; its verses call him a tree of fruit-bearing virtues, meditating on the laws of the Lord.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 1 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/887/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
