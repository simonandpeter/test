# Vsevolod (Potyominsky)

Vsevolod Ioannovich Potyominsky was born on 30 March 1870 in the town of Nikolsk in Vologda province, the son of the sexton of the Annunciation church at Lyamen, John Potyominsky, and finished three classes of the Vologda seminary. From 1890 he was deacon of the church at Simonovolomskaya in the Ustyug district and from 1900 of the church of the Entry in the Nikolsk district, teaching from 1891 to 1900 in the school of the village of Simonovolomskaya Pustyn; in 1903 Alexis (Sobolev), bishop of Vologda, ordained him priest to the church of St Nicholas at Semukovo, where he was also catechist of the people’s school, and from 1910 — the calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. A priest in the churches of the Komi country, he was persecuted repeatedly — deprived of the vote, evicted from his house, tried three times, in 1928, 1934 and 1936; arrested in 1937 on a charge of counter-revolutionary activity and agitation against the Soviet power, he did not admit guilt, was sentenced by the troika to be shot and executed on 19 September 1937 near Syktyvkar. He was numbered among the saints in 2001, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 6 September (Julian).
