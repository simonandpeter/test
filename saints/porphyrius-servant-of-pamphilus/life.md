# Porphyrius, servant of Pamphilus

saint.gr's calendar for 16 February prints one line for the whole company, «Άγιος Πάμφιλος και οι συν αυτώ Μάρτυρες» — Pamphilus and the martyrs with him — and the page behind it names the twelve of them one by one: [Pamphilus](/saints/pamphilus-of-caesarea), Daniel, Elias, Isaiah, Theodulus, Jeremiah, Julian, [Valens](/saints/valens-the-deacon), [Paul](/saints/paul-of-jamnia), Porphyrius, Samuel and [Seleucus](/saints/seleucus-of-cappadocia). They were martyred under the emperor Diocletian, whose reign the page gives as 284 to 305; they came from various places, but the love and the faith of Christ joined them. Working at Caesarea in Palestine they confessed Christ before the eparch Firmilianus, who spent every effort on persuading them to deny their faith in Christ, and they stayed fixed immovably in the piety of their fathers; so Firmilianus gave the order that they should be put to death after being tortured first.

Porphyrius is the one of the twelve the page gives a station to: he was [Pamphilus](/saints/pamphilus-of-caesarea)'s servant, and he was seized at the hour when he was searching for his master's body. He was burned alive together with the martyr [Julian](/saints/julian-companion-of-pamphilus), and over the two the page prints one couplet: two divine martyrs leapt towards the fire, the two of them feeding one fire of divine longing. The name it glosses as the royal one, the magnificent; and beside that it prints, as it does for several of this company, a note about a namesake who is not a saint at all — the philosopher of Tyre who was Plotinus's pupil. No homeland, no age and no hymn are given him.

The Serbian church keeps him on the same day, in one entry for the whole company: the Ohrid Prologue of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović heads it „Светих дванаест мученика.“ — the holy twelve martyrs who suffered in the time of the emperor Diocletian — and then names all twelve in its body. He suffered with them: a youth who went looking for their bodies to bury them, and who was burned in the fire for it.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 16 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/16/index.aspx) and [the company's life](https://www.saint.gr/3724/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026; and the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 16. фебруар ст. ст. — [the Serbian day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-03-01&prolog=1), read 4 October 2026.*
