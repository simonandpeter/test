# Eustathius II

Eustathius II succeeded Jakov on the archiepiscopal throne and governed the Serbian Church from 1292 to 1309 — a strict ascetic and a zealous pastor, the calendar says.

His years were the years of the medieval Serbian state’s sudden rise under King Milutin: Skopje taken, and the lands north of Ohrid, Štip and Prilep given as the dowry of the emperor Andronikos II’s daughter Simonida, married to Milutin at Thessalonica in 1299. The Church grew by three dioceses in his time — Zvečan, Lim and Skopje — and he restored the monastery of Žiča, which had been laid waste in a Cuman raid. He reposed in peace on 16 August 1309 and was venerated as a saint from the moment of his death; the Church keeps his memory on 16 August.
