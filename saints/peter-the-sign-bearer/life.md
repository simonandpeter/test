# Peter the Sign-bearer

The Greek calendar keeps on 3 January a Peter called the Sign-bearer, at Saint Zacharias of Atroa, and saint.gr is unusually frank about how little stands behind the line. He was an ascetic in the eastern parts of the Propontis, where Atroa lay. There is no notice for him in the Menaia at all, the page says: the calendar keeps a name, an epithet and a place, and no narrative.

It then reports and rejects one identification. Nicodemus the Hagiorite conjectured that this is Peter, bishop of Argos, whom the calendars keep on 3 May, but the page calls that unlikely, because the sources nowhere witness that Peter of Argos lived as a monk at Atroa. The epigram it prints says only that another Peter was seen, a chosen Peter, working countless signs as the first one did. Who he was is left open here as the source leaves it open.
