# Gervasius, companion of Leo

saint.gr names the holy Gervasius with [Leo](/saints/leo-companion-of-gervasius) on this day, calls the two of them in its day's line «οι αυτάδελφοι», own brothers, and gives them two sentences between them. The first is all that is said of Gervasius: the holy Gervasius and Leo were martyred in the second century after Christ. The second belongs to his brother, and says that the holy Leo was apostle of the Basques.

So of this man the page records the name, the kinship, the century and the fact of martyrdom, and nothing else. There is no country, no city, no emperor, no judge, no manner of death, no relic, no distich and no hymn; and the site does not say whether Gervasius shared his brother's preaching among the Basques or only his death.

The corpus keeps a Gervasius of Milan, and he is a different man: saint.gr itself keeps that one on the fourteenth of October, in the company of Nazarius, Protasius and Celsius, which is the same day the Romanian calendar gives him. Two entries on one calendar are two commemorations, and nothing on either page connects the two.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3785/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
