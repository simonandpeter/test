# Pelagia (Testova)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps her on 21 октября ст. ст. in a line of three words, «Прмц. Пелагии (1944).» The rank word is «Прмц.», a venerable-martyr, which the calendar uses of a woman under monastic vows who was killed for the faith; so she was a nun, and the title on the name link behind the line adds the surname and nothing else: «Пелагия (Тестова), прмц.»

1944 is late. The day's other twentieth-century lines all end in 1937, and she outlived them by seven years, which is to say she died in the camps or in prison during the war rather than in the Terror; but the calendar does not say that, and it is not written here as though it did.

Nothing further is recorded — not a birth, not a monastery, not a charge, not a place of death — and the calendar carries no life page under her name and no hymn. The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 21 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261021.html); read 4 October 2026.*
