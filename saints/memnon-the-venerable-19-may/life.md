# Memnon (19 May)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 19 mai reads «Sfântul Cuvios Memnon», and the page that line links to carries no Viață tab and no Tropar tab — only the name, the day, and the epistle and gospel appointed for it. What the calendar says of him is the single word Cuvios, venerable, which is the Romanian rank for a monastic saint. It gives no country, no century, no monastery and no companions.

This corpus already keeps a Memnon the Centurion, a martyr of the twentieth of August in the Russian calendar. He is a soldier and a martyr; the rank this line gives its Memnon is monastic, and nothing on either page joins the two. They are therefore kept apart, and the date is carried in this one's name so that a reader can tell which is meant.
