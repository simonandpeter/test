# Memnon (19 May)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 19 mai reads «Sfântul Cuvios Memnon», and the page that line links to carries no Viață tab and no Tropar tab — only the name, the day, and the epistle and gospel appointed for it. What the calendar says of him is the single word Cuvios, venerable, which is the Romanian rank for a monastic saint. It gives no country, no century, no monastery and no companions.

This corpus already keeps a Memnon the Centurion, a martyr of the twentieth of August in the Russian calendar. He is a soldier and a martyr; the rank this line gives its Memnon is monastic, and nothing on either page joins the two. They are therefore kept apart, and the date is carried in this one's name so that a reader can tell which is meant.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and adds no life either. saint.gr's page for 19 Μαΐου gives him the epithet ο Θαυματουργός, the wonderworker, a couplet — «Ὑπνοῖ τι μικρόν, ἁρπαγὴν τὴν ἐσχάτην, Τὴν εἰς ἀπαντὴν τοῦ Θεοῦ, Μέμνων μένων», he sleeps a little while, Memnon abiding, waiting for the last catching up, the going out to meet God — and then sends the reader elsewhere: «Βλέπε βιογραφία του στις 28 Απριλίου». It prints no hymn. Whether the Memnon of that April page is this one or the other Memnon this corpus keeps is a question neither calendar answers on the day that was read, and it is left open.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 19 mai — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/19-mai) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-memnon), which prints no life; read 20 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 19 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/19/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1632/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026.*
