# John (Rumyantsev)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him in a line of four and gives no life. Its line for 31 июля ст. ст. reads «Cщмч. Иоанна пресвитера, св. Константина исп., пресвитера, мц. Анны и св. Елисаветы исп (после 1937).» — a presbyter who died a hieromartyr, with [Constantine (Razumov)](/saints/constantine-razumov) a confessor and presbyter, [Anna (Serova)](/saints/anna-serova) a martyr and [Elizabeth (Rumyantseva)](/saints/elizabeth-rumyantseva) a confessor. Behind the line the calendar's own name page gives his name in full as «Иоанн (Румянцев), иерей, сщмч.»

The line's first word is printed «Cщмч.», with a Latin C in place of the Cyrillic С; that is the page's own spelling and it is what split the entry in two for the reader's extractor. The year is given as «после 1937», after 1937, which is a lower bound and not a year of death.

Nothing else is printed. The calendar names no parish, no diocese, no place and no manner of death for any of the four, and carries no life page and no hymn for them. Two of the four share the surname Rumyantsev with the martyr of 1928 whom the previous line names, and the calendar says nothing of any kinship between them.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 31 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260731.html); read 13 October 2026.*
