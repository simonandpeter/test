# Milica of Serbia

Milica was the wife of the holy prince [Lazar of Serbia](/saints/lazar-of-serbia), who fell in the great battle of Kosovo in 1389 defending the holy Church against the Muslims, and she ruled the people until her son [Stefan the Tall](/saints/stefan-the-tall) was grown. When he left the country for a time in 1402 she governed the state again, monastic though she then was.

The two readings the calendar prints do not agree on where she was tonsured. The first says that in 1393 she entered the Županjevac monastery near Kruševac with the name Eugenia; the second says that she founded the convent of Ljubostinja and was tonsured there with that same name. Both call her Eugenia and neither says more of the other house.

She cared for widows, taking into her monastery many wives whose soldier husbands had fallen in the field, and she helped to build and maintained many monasteries and churches. At the end of her life she took the great schema with the name Euphrosyne. She died in her monastery as a schema-nun on 11 November 1405, and after her death, the first reading says, she worked many wonders, her myrrh-streaming relics healing many of the sick.
