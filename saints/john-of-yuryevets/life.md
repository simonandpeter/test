# John of Yuryevets

saint.gr's calendar for 14 Μαρτίου names him «Άγιος Ιωάννης του Γιούρεβιτς», and the page behind that line holds one sentence about him: that the holy John of Yuryevets came from Russia and was a fool for Christ. Everything else on the page is the note this calendar prints wherever the name John occurs — that it is Hebrew and means the grace and favour of God, and that it was borne by the Forerunner, by the Evangelist, by Chrysostom and by very many other saints, kings and hierarchs.

So no year is given, no century, no town of his beyond the one in his name, no account of his folly for Christ's sake, no relic and no hymn. What is recorded here is the commemoration, the country, and the rank — that hardest of the ranks of holiness, which the Greek calls σαλός and which the corpus calls fool-for-Christ.
