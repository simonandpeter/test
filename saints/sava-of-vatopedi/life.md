# Sava of Vatopedi

Sava lived as a monk at the monastery of Vatopedi on the Holy Mountain. He was a hierodeacon, and his obedience was at the altar; and when the barbarian raids came, this devout and wonderful server took the icon of the Mother of God called Vimatarissa — which means, the one of the sanctuary — and the Honourable Cross, and hid them in the well inside the holy altar. He himself was taken prisoner and carried off to Crete.

After he had been set free he sent word to the other fathers to go and fetch the icon and the cross, and they came upon a wonder: the icon of the Mother of God was standing upright above the water, and the lamp before it was burning. Of the rest of his life doxologia quotes its source in a single clause — that living afterwards a most wonderful life, he departed to the Lord in peace.

The page gives no year for any of this, no year of his death and no account of his burial, and it names the book it is drawn from: Moses the Athonite, The Saints of the Holy Mountain, Mygdonia, Karyes, 2008, page 151. He is honoured with the saints of Vatopedi on 10 July, and remembered also in the service of the Vimatarissa icon, kept on the Tuesday of Bright Week.
