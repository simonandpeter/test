# Florian of Noricum

Florian was born in the city of Ems in Austria, saint.gr says, and he was a senior officer of the Roman army, serving in the city of Noricum in Austria in the years of the emperor Diocletian, whose reign it gives as 284 to 305. He confessed his faith in Christ before the governor Aquilinus and was tortured hard; and at the last they tied a stone to his neck and threw him into the river Ems, where he was perfected as a martyr in the year 303.

He is one of the patron saints of Austria, the page adds, and that is the whole of the notice. It gives no year of birth, no account of what was said at the trial or what the tortures were, no relic, no hymn, and not even the couplet this site usually prints. The one thing it repeats is the Ems: the town he came from and the water he died in bear the same name on this page, and Noricum, where he served, is the province the Romans made of that country.
