# Iador

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him in a single line for 4 февраля ст. ст. — «Мч. Иадора (III).» — a martyr of the third century. The name link behind the line gives the calendar's own nominative, «Иадор, мч.», and adds nothing: no surname, no place, no year inside the century. There is no life page for him on the site, and none is given here; the line, with the rank and the century, is the whole of what the calendar records.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 4 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260204.html); read 3 October 2026.*
