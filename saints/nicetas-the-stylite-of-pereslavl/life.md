# Nicetas the Stylite of Pereslavl

saint.gr says the holy venerable-martyr Nicetas lived in the twelfth century in the city of Pereslavl in Russia. He worked as a tax-collector for the prince Dolgoruky and exacted from the taxpayers enormous sums which they were obliged to give for the building of the city and of a church; and so many years passed. But the merciful God, who wills that all should be brought to repentance and be saved, did not abandon Nicetas, and prepared the road of his return.

One day when Nicetas went into the church he heard the words of the Prophet Isaiah: «Λούσασθε καὶ καθαροὶ γίνεσθε, ἀφέλετε τὰς πονηρίας ἀπὸ τῶν ψυχῶν ὑμῶν ἀπέναντι τῶν ὀφθαλμῶν μου, παύσασθε ἀπὸ τῶν πονηριῶν ὑμῶν, μάθετε καλὸν ποιεῖν, ἐκζητήσατε κρίσιν, ρύσασθε ἀδικούμενον, κρίνατε ὀρφανῷ καὶ δικαιώσατε χήραν» — wash and be clean, put away the wickedness from your souls before my eyes, cease from your wickednesses, learn to do good, seek out judgement, deliver the wronged, judge for the orphan and do justice for the widow. His sleeping conscience woke and he was brought to repentance. He ran at once to the monastery of Pereslavl-Zalessky, which was dedicated to Saint Nicetas, fell at the feet of the spiritual father and confessed his sins; and the old confessor, to test whether the man confessing was sincere, set him a penance.

What the page records after his death is the history of the visible signs of his martyrdom and his asceticism. Robbers took the chains he had worn, thinking them precious, and when they had got away and understood that the chains were made of iron they threw them into the river Volga. But God willed to honour those signs too: one night the devout old monk Symeon, who was practising asceticism at the monastery of the Apostles Peter and Paul of Yaroslavl, saw three shafts of light above the river. The fathers of the monastery ran at once to see what was happening, and saw with awe the chains of the venerable Nicetas floating; and they took them up with reverence and carried them to his tomb. When, between the years 1420 and 1425, they opened the venerable one's tomb, they found his holy relics incorrupt.
