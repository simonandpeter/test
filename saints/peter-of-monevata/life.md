# Peter of Monevata

saint.gr names him and gives no life. Its calendar for 7 February prints «Όσιος Πέτρος "ὁ ἐν Μονεβάτοις ἀγωνισάμενος"» — the venerable Peter who did his contending at Monevata — and the page behind that line holds a single sentence about him: that the venerable Peter was a monk and fell asleep in peace. There is no century here, no country, nothing of where Monevata was or what stood there, and no hymn.

Over that sentence the page prints a couplet and the reading of his name. Peter, having accomplished the greatest contest, has received in the heavens an all-honoured crown; and the name itself, from πέτρα, a rock, it glosses as the one who is steady as a rock. That, with the day and the epithet the calendar gives him, is the whole of what the Greek church's synaxaristes keeps of him here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3620/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
