# Sozon of Cyprus, the shepherd boy

Sozon is a local saint of Paphos and a martyr of the Church of Cyprus unknown to the synaxarists, mentioned by the medieval chronicler Leontios Machairas: Saint Sozon at Plakountoudi, a shepherd boy, whom the Saracens chased when they burned the icon of the Theotokos at the monastery — and the icon was printed on the stones and is there to this day; he was holding the milking-pail with the milk, and stumbled, and the pail broke and the milk spilled, and it shows to this day; and he went into the cave with the other children, and they lit a fire and burned them; and they built a church and laid the holy relics there, and they heal every sickness. By Machairas, then, when the Saracens came to Cyprus in the seventh century they burned the icon of the Panagia at the monastery — probably the nearby Chrysorrogiatissa, or the monastery of the Priests — and the form of the Panagia was miraculously printed on the stones there, and remained until Machairas’s own time, the fourteenth century — the synaxarion’s notice runs on past the point read here.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 7 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/4338/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
