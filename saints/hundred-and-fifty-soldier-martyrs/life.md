# The Hundred and Fifty Soldier Martyrs

saint.gr's calendar for 19 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιοι Εκατόν πενήντα Μάρτυρες Στρατιώτες» — a hundred and fifty soldier martyrs — and the whole of its notice is a single sentence: they believed in Christ through [Azes](/saints/azes-19-november), the saint the same calendar keeps on the same day, and they were martyred by the sword. The page names not one of the hundred and fifty, and so this is one entry and not a hundred and fifty.

The couplet set over the notice counts them in threes, asking Christ to honour with a threefold fifty of crowns the three fifties that were cut down. Beside them the day prints [twelve soldier martyrs](/saints/twelve-soldier-martyrs-19-november) more, of perhaps the same time, and [the wife and daughter](/saints/wife-and-daughter-of-aquilinus) of the eparch who condemned Azes.

The page gives them no country, no city, no year, no emperor and no hymn, and prints no relics for them.

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar keeps them on one line for the Julian 19 November, «Мч. Азы и с ним 150-ти воинов (284-305).», and its life page has them suffer in Isauria in Asia Minor under the emperor Diocletian, whose reign it gives as those same years. The hundred and fifty were the soldiers sent to fetch Aza, and he turned them to Christianity on the road.

They received holy baptism from water that flowed out wonderfully at his prayer; and he persuaded them to keep the commandment of obedience to the authorities and to bring him to the eparch Aquilinus. They confessed the Christian faith before Aquilinus together with him, and for that all of them were beheaded. With them the eparch put to death his own wife and daughter, who had come to believe on seeing the saint’s steadfastness under his torments. The line counts the soldiers and names none of them, and neither does the life page, so they stand here as the one company the Greek calendar also keeps.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 19 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/19/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2905/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 19 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261119.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life2564.htm); read 5 October 2026.*
