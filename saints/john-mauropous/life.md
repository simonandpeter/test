# John Mauropous

John, called Mauropous, bishop of Euchaita, lived in the eleventh century and was, the Greek calendar says, an outstanding poet and a most polished writer; he died in peace after living more than a hundred years. None of the synaxaria mention him: his memory is kept only in the Palatine codex 138, with his service, composed by his nephew Theodore, koitonites and imperial notary.

doxologia.ro, which calls him metropolitan of Euchaita and prints no life, says that he was one of the most important Byzantine intellectuals of the eleventh century — a teacher in Constantinople, a theologian, poet, hymnographer and orator, and the mentor and model of Michael Psellos among others — and that his name is linked with the keeping, from his time, of the feast of the Three Hierarchs on 30 January.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 5 Οκτωβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2671/saint.aspx); doxologia.ro — [the saint’s page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-ioan-mavropous-mitropolitul-evhaitelor); read 17 September 2026.*
