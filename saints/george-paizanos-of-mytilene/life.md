# George Paizanos of Mytilene

The new martyr George, called Paizanos, was born — by the tradition that holds, saint.gr is careful to say — in the village of Plagia in the district of Plomari on the island of Lesbos. He was a tailor by trade. He was martyred at Constantinople in the year 1693, because he confessed his faith in Christ and refused to change his religion. The page gives no more of his life than that, and leaves the year of his birth a question mark.

A portion of his relic is kept in the monastery of Leimonos on Lesbos. The distich over him asks who this George is now, and answers that he is a new martyr: «Ω της ανδρίας!» — what manliness.
