# Triantafyllos of Zagora

saint.gr says that the glorious new martyr of Christ Triantafyllos was born at Zagora of Magnesia in 1663, and that from a young age he worked as a sailor on one of the most celebrated of the Zagorian ships. In the summer of 1680 his vessel tied up in the harbour of Constantinople, and there Triantafyllos went about visiting the churches and the holy springs of the City.

For some reason unknown to us, the page says, he fell into a quarrel with some Turks, who then slandered him with the claim that he had wanted to become a Mohammedan. Triantafyllos denied the slander, and standing firm and unshaken in his faith he received the crown of martyrdom on 8 August 1680 in the Hippodrome of Constantinople, at the age of eighteen.

The page adds that his memory is honoured on that day both at Zagora and at Alykes of Volos, and that a portion of his relic is treasured at the monastery of Saint Nicholas on the island of Andros, together with his icon.
