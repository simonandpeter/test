# Anthony of Krasnokholmsk

The line on the Greek day page for 17 Ιανουαρίου reads simply «Όσιος Αντώνιος εκ Ρωσίας», Anthony out of Russia, and the page behind it holds a single sentence of life: that Anthony of Krasnokholmsk lived about the fifteenth century, that he practised the ascetic life in the monastery of Saint Nicholas in the region of Tver in Russia, and that he fell asleep in peace. The rank the calendar gives him is *Όσιος*, which is the Greek for venerable, and it is the whole of what the day claims about his manner of life.

No year of birth, no year of death, no abbacy and no miracle is printed for him here, and no hymn. This folder records the Greek calendar's line and the one sentence behind it; anything fuller would have to come from a source that has not been read.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 17 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/17/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1121/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
