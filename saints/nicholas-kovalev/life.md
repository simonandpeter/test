# Nicholas (Kovalev)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him among the hieromartyrs on 14 декабря and gives no life. Its line is «Сщмч. Николая пресвитера (1937).» — the hieromartyr Nicholas the presbyter, 1937 — and the calendar’s own name page adds the surname the line leaves out: «Николай (Ковалев), иерей, сщмч.»

That is the whole of the record. He was a priest; he died in 1937, in the years the Russian church counts its new martyrs; and the calendar keeps him on this day alone, his line joined to no one else’s. The day page carries one kontakion, for the martyrs of a company three centuries older than he, and nothing under his name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 14 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261214.html); read 13 October 2026.*
