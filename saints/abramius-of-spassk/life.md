# Abramius of Spassk

saint.gr gives the venerable Abramius one sentence and a half, and the life says exactly that: the venerable Abramius of Spassk lived and was an ascetic in Russia in the sixteenth century, at the monastery of Christ the Saviour of Koryazhem; and he fell asleep in a holy manner, in peace. The day's own line calls him simply «εκ Ρωσίας», the one out of Russia, and the epithet in the display name is the page's own word for him.

Nothing else stands on the page. There is no place of birth and no year for it, no year of repose, no elder, no office in the house, no foundation, no relic, and the site does not even print over this name the etymology block it sets over almost every other entry of the day. It prints no distich and no hymn.

The corpus keeps an Abramius of Arbela, a bishop of the Persian martyrs, and he stands on another day and is not named on this page.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 2 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/02/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3893/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
