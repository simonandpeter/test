# Nicephorus (23 October)

saint.gr's calendar for 23 Οκτωβρίου names a venerable Nicephorus and gives him no life beyond a citation. For this venerable one, the page says, the Synaxaristes record that «τὴν ἐν τῷ Χαρσιανῷ συστησάμενος Μονήν, ἐν εἰρήνῃ τελειοῦται» — having established the monastery in Charsianon, he was perfected in peace; and then, in its own words, «Δεν έχουμε περισσότερες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του», we have no further details of his life.

The couplet over the entry plays on his name, saying that Nicephorus, having raised a sacred monastery, hastened to be a bearer of victory in his manner of life as well. The page gives no century, no year, no companions, no relics and no hymn, and it names no office in the house he founded.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 23 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/23/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2790/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
