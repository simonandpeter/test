# Domnina, daughter of Nero

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names her once, in a single line of eleven persons for the twentieth of March, and the reading behind that line is the whole of what it says of her. She was a daughter of the emperor Nero. When he had the confessors of Christ brought to Rome for judgement, and their hands broken on an anvil, he sent [Photini the Samaritan](/saints/photini-the-samaritan) and five of her sisters into the imperial palace to be held under this daughter's watch.

Instead of keeping them she was won. The reading says that the Samaritan woman turned her to Christ, and all her serving-women with her, and that they received holy baptism; the sorcerer who was sent in with a poisoned drink to kill the confessors was turned the same way. Three years passed before the emperor sent to the prison again.

The calendar's rank word for her is мученица, a martyr, and the reading it carries does not say how she died. It ends with the sisters beheaded, one of them torn apart between two bent trees, and the Samaritan woman drawn out of a well, kept twenty days in prison and thrown back into it to give up her spirit. The line closes «(ок. 66)». Her own name page lists this day and no other, and neither page says another word about her.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 20 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260320.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life680.htm); read 8 October 2026.*
