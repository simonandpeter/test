# Tarasius (7 June)

saint.gr's calendar for 7 Ιουνίου names two martyrs on one line and on one page, Tarasius and John, and gives them a single sentence: «Οι Άγιοι Ταράσιος και Ιωάννης μαρτύρησαν δια ξίφους» — the holy Tarasius and John were martyred by the sword. That is the whole of the life; the page adds no year, no city, no emperor and no judge.

The couplet it prints above the sentence says the same thing and adds only their steadiness: they cut John down with Tarasius, and the two were not thrown into confusion by the cutting sword. Nothing else is kept of either of them, and no hymn is printed. The other of the two is [John](/saints/john-companion-of-tarasius).

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/499/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
