# Boniface the Merciful

The Охридски пролог keeps him on 19. децембар ст. ст. as „Свети Бонифатије Милостиви, епископ ферентијски.“ — Boniface the Merciful, bishop of Ferentino — and gives him three sentences, which are the whole of what is read here. From childhood, it says, he was uncommonly merciful, so much so that his own mother reproached him for it; and the book leaves her unnamed and says nothing of what she said.

But by prayer he received from the Lord a hundredfold, and that one clause is all the book offers of how the giving away was answered. He ended peacefully in Italy in the sixth century. No year, no city beyond the see in his heading, no wonder, no companion and no relic: where this source gives a line, the folder says so and says what the line says, and nothing is added to it here.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 19. децембар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-01-01&prolog=1); read 8 October 2026.*
