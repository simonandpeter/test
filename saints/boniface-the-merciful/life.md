# Boniface the Merciful

The Охридски пролог keeps him on 19. децембар ст. ст. as „Свети Бонифатије Милостиви, епископ ферентијски.“ — Boniface the Merciful, bishop of Ferentino — and gives him three sentences, which are the whole of what is read here. From childhood, it says, he was uncommonly merciful, so much so that his own mother reproached him for it; and the book leaves her unnamed and says nothing of what she said.

But by prayer he received from the Lord a hundredfold, and that one clause is all the book offers of how the giving away was answered. He ended peacefully in Italy in the sixth century. No year, no city beyond the see in his heading, no wonder, no companion and no relic: where this source gives a line, the folder says so and says what the line says, and nothing is added to it here.

The Russian calendar keeps him on 19 декабря ст. ст., the same menologion day as the other calendars here, and its line is «Свт. Вонифатия Милостивого, еп. Ферентийского (VI).» — the hierarch Boniface the Merciful, bishop of Ferentino, sixth century. That is the century already recorded above out of the Serbian book, and the two agree; the see is named in the line and not on the calendar’s own name page. The same day’s first line keeps a martyr of the same forename, the one who suffered at Tarsus in 290, and the calendar gives the two men separate lines and separate name pages, as this corpus gives them separate folders; they are two men and no relation between them is claimed here. The day page’s three hymns all belong to that other Boniface.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 19. децембар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-01-01&prolog=1); read 8 October 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 19 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261219.html), read 13 October 2026.*
