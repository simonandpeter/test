# Isidore of Antioch

saint.gr's calendar for 2 Ιανουαρίου names Isidore the hieromartyr and gives him a single sentence: he lived in the fourth century, was bishop of Antioch in Syria, and suffered a martyr's death at the hands of the Arian heretics. The page adds nothing else — no persecutor by name, no manner of death, no year, no companions.

What it does print beside the line is a gloss on the name, Ἰσίδωρος read as the equal gift. The one fact the calendar insists on is the one recorded here: that a bishop of Antioch was killed for the faith by fellow Christians who had gone over to Arius, in the century when that quarrel was at its worst.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 2 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/02/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1272/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
