# John (Tarasov)

The Сретенский monastery's calendar names him third in its clause of fifteen for 25 ноября ст. ст. and carries no life for him; azbyka.ru prints one out of fond.ru. Иван Яковлевич Тарасов was born on 18 February 1867 at Бородино in the Клин district of the province of Moscow, a peasant's son. He finished the town elementary school and from the age of ten worked for well-off traders in Клин, then in a prosecutor's office, and from 1895 until the revolution for a succession of large merchants. After it he took a clerk's post in the people's commissariat of transport, and left it in 1921 for illness and age.

From that time he gave his life to the Church. He became a psalm-reader at the Moscow church of the Life-giving Trinity in Listy and was ordained deacon there in 1923, and priest in 1924 to a church in the province of Tula. From 1925 he served in the province of Moscow, and was moved again and again: Обухово, Майданово, Голенищево, Воронино, Фроловское, and in August 1937 the church of the Nativity of the Theotokos at Вырки in the Орехово-Зуево district. He was raised archpriest in 1934. **Those frequent moves, the page says, spared him arrests he would certainly have suffered had he stayed in one place** — but the time came when the persecutors meant no one to slip through.

On 22 October 1937 the chairman of a collective farm was questioned and testified that Tarasov was hostile to the Soviet power and had said to him in September that the farm's affairs were plainly going badly, that the farmers had become not people but shadows, worn out and exhausted by work they could not bear, and would not live long at such a life. He was arrested on 26 October, imprisoned at Орехово-Зуево and questioned at once; he rejected the witnesses' statements out of hand. The investigation ran about a month, and on 16 November he was questioned again in the hope that the prison would change his position. He confirmed what he had said before and answered: «Контрреволюционной деятельностью я не занимался – виновным себя не признаю.» The troika sentenced him on 29 November, and he was shot on 8 December 1937 and buried in an unmarked common grave on the Butovo range outside Moscow.
