# Gregory of Alexandria

Gregory loved Christ from his young years and adorned himself with all the virtues that are according to God. When the most holy pope of Alexandria died, he was ordained archbishop of Alexandria by the bishops gathered together and by the Christ-loving people, and he became, the Romanian life says, a chosen teacher of Orthodoxy: gentle, humble, merciful, a father to orphans, a support to widows, a guide to the straying, a physician to the sick, a comfort to the grieving; and it could be seen that all rejoiced in him.

But the devil, the worker of scandal, entered into the emperor of that time, Leo the fighter against the icons, and breathed foul counsels into him, so that he sent to bring the saint bound from Alexandria to Constantinople. When Gregory stood before him he rebuked the emperor in front of the whole senate, calling him a heretic, godless and unbelieving. The tyrant, not bearing the reproach, had his body torn with wounds, beating him with ox sinews; and the saint, suffering the beating with thanksgiving, said to those standing by that for the holy icon of his Christ he was ready to suffer cutting over every part of his body.

Hearing this, the emperor ordered him sent into exile, and there, after some years, he gave up his soul into the hands of God. That is the whole of what doxologia.ro prints of him: it names no year, names the persecuting emperor only as Leo who fought the icons, and gives him no hymn — the page carries no Tropar tab.
