# Filotimia of Râmeț, mother of Dometie

Filița — Filofteia Manolache — bore twelve children to her husband Ion, and they brought them up, doxologia says, in the fear of God and in obedience and respect towards their elders, with a sense of responsibility, of duty and of honest work. One of the twelve was [Dometie the Merciful](/saints/dometie-of-ramet), afterwards the confessor of Râmeț. In her old age she withdrew to that monastery, and it was her own son who tonsured her, giving her the name Filotimia.

Shortly before she died, in 1989, she told the disciple who kept her cell that Saint Dometie had appeared to her and told her she was going to the Lord. She made herself ready, confessed, was at the Liturgy and communicated; then she went back to her cell and told her disciple that Dometie thanked her for the care she had taken of him and would remember her always for that sacrifice. She took her leave and departed in peace, and she is buried at Râmeț, where she had lived her last years.

Of this nun, the page adds, Saint Porphyrios of Kavsokalyvia told the former abbess Ierusalima Ghibu that after her falling asleep she rose to heaven like a cherub. The Holy Synod of the Romanian Orthodox Church canonised her on 1 July 2025 under the title «Sfânta Cuvioasă Filotimia de la Râmeț, mama Sfântului Cuvios Dometie cel Milostiv», and appointed her feast on 6 iulie.
