# Theotimus, executioner converted by Hermione

Theotimus — Timothy in some lists — and
[Theodulus](/saints/theodulus-the-executioner) were the two servants sent
to carry out the sentence on
[Hermione](/saints/hermione-daughter-of-philip), the daughter of the
apostle Philip, when the emperor, defeated by the cauldron and the red-hot
vessel that had not harmed her and by the idols that fell at her prayer,
ordered her led out of Ephesus and beheaded. In their hurry they would not
give her time to pray, and their hands withered. Then they believed in
Christ, fell at her feet, and begged her to pray that the Lord would heal
them and take them to himself before her own death — and so it was: by her
prayer they died first, and she was beheaded after them and buried at
Ephesus. The Greek synaxarion keeps the two on 4 September in a line, the
executioners who believed through Hermione and died in peace, with a verse
— Theotimus died with Theodulus, giving the Lord a servant’s honour with
him; the Orthodox Church in America tells the rest in its life of her.

[the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2343/saint.aspx); and the life of Hermione
printed by the Orthodox Church in America for 4 September —
