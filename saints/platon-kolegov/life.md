# Platon (Kolegov)

The calendar's line is all there is of him. For 2 августа ст. ст. it prints «Прмч. Платона (1937).» — the venerable-martyr Platon, 1937 — and behind the forename the name link gives the surname and his standing, so that the calendar's own fullest reading of him is Платон (Колегов), иеромонах. There is no life page, no monastery, no place of arrest and no manner of death.

The two halves of the entry give him two different martyr grades, and both are the source's: the day line has «Прмч.», the venerable-martyr's grade, which is the grade of a monastic, while the name link has «сщмч.», the hieromartyr's, which is a priest's. A hieromonk is both a monk and a priest, so the two readings are the same man seen from his two orders, and this folder keeps both rather than choosing.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 2 августа ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260802.html); read 13 October 2026.*
