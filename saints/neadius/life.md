# Neadius

saint.gr says that Saint Nicodemus does not mention this venerable one in his Synaxaristes, but that a complete service for him survives in the Paris Codex 1575, folio 846, a poem of Joseph the Hymnographer; and it is from that service, the page says, that what follows is learnt.

From it we learn that Neadius was a bishop — of where, the page adds in a parenthesis, is unknown — and that he was a man gentle and without malice, a fighter against the heretics and a servant of those who suffered. Because of his great virtues God had given him the gift of working wonders, and so, with all of this, he became a pillar of Orthodoxy and an example of faith.

That is the whole of the entry. The page gives him no century and no year, no country, no see, no companions, no relics; it prints none of the service it names, so no hymn is recorded here; and it does not say what wonders he worked or which heretics he fought.
