# Dubritius

saint.gr's calendar for 14 November names «Άγιος Dubritius», printing the name in Latin letters and giving no Greek form of it, and its whole notice is one sentence: we have no details of the Welsh saint's life. That is the entire source. The calendar does not say when he lived, what rank he held in the Church, where in Wales he belonged or how he died, and it prints no hymn for him.

What it does do is keep him, and say honestly that it knows nothing more. So this folder records a commemoration and not a life: a saint of Wales whom the Greek calendar keeps on 14 November under the name Dubritius, with no detail attached, and nothing is imported here from any other book to fill the gap the source leaves open.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 14 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/14/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/172/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
