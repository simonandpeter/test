# Victorinus of Pettau

The Ohrid Prologue gives him five sentences and they are the whole of what this folder has. Many hold, it says, that he was a Slovene by origin. [Jerome](/saints/jerome-of-stridon) singles him out as a man learned and pious. He knew Greek better than he knew Latin. He wrote commentaries on several books of the Old and the New Testament. And he suffered for the faith of Christ about the year 303.

That is all. The book names no city but the see in its own heading, no emperor, no manner of death and no age; it gives him no company and places him in no persecution by name. Where it says «many hold» about his origin it is reporting an opinion and not settling one, and this life says the same rather than turning the opinion into a birthplace.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 2. новембар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2025-11-15&prolog=1); read 7 October 2026.*
