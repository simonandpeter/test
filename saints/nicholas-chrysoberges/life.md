# Nicholas II Chrysoberges

saint.gr's calendar for 16 December keeps Saint Nicholas II Chrysoberges, patriarch of Constantinople, and corrects his surname as it names him: Nicholas the Second, the Chrysoberges — and not Grammatikos, as Saint Nicodemus records him. He succeeded on the patriarchal throne the patriarch Antony the Third, who had resigned; the page gives the years 984 to 995 in that same sentence, in a place where they could belong either to his own patriarchate or to his predecessor's.

What the page says of his patriarchate is one thing, and it is the thing it thinks worth saying: that in those years the Byzantine mission was organised for the spreading of Christianity in Russia, in the year 988, and that the Church there was placed under the administrative jurisdiction of the Ecumenical Patriarchate. Its heading gives 995 as the year of his death and no year for his birth, and it prints no hymn for him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 16 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/16/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3273/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
