# Barlaam the Anchorite

saint.gr's calendar for 18 July names him and gives no life. Its line reads «Όσιος Βαρλαάμ ο αναχωρητής κοντά στην Αντιόχεια» — the venerable Barlaam, the anchorite near Antioch — and the page behind the line says one sentence and stops: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Οσίου», we have no details of the life of this venerable one. There is no year, no hymn, no account of his withdrawal and nothing of his end.

So the folder records what the line records, which is three things and no more: that he was Ὅσιος rather than a martyr, that he lived as an anchorite, and that his place was somewhere near Antioch. The calendar does not say when, or how near, or under whom, and nothing has been brought in from elsewhere to say it for him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2153/saint.aspx), which prints only that one sentence; read 1 October 2026.*
