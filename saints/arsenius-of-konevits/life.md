# Arsenius of Konevits

saint.gr's calendar for 12 Ιουνίου names him and gives no life. Its whole notice is one sentence: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ρώσου Οσίου Αρσενίου» — we have no details of the life of the Russian venerable Arsenius. What the day's line and the head of the page add is the rest of what the site knows: that he is called the wonderworker, that he is named for Konevits, and that he died in 1447.

The page prints no synaxarion verses for him and no hymn, and it says nothing of where he was born, of what house he founded or entered, or of the wonders for which he is called a wonderworker. It carries the site's gloss on his name — Arsenios, the manly, the brave — and two pictures of him, and that is all.
