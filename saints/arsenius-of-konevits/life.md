# Arsenius of Konevits

saint.gr's calendar for 12 Ιουνίου names him and gives no life. Its whole notice is one sentence: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ρώσου Οσίου Αρσενίου» — we have no details of the life of the Russian venerable Arsenius. What the day's line and the head of the page add is the rest of what the site knows: that he is called the wonderworker, that he is named for Konevits, and that he died in 1447.

The page prints no synaxarion verses for him and no hymn, and it says nothing of where he was born, of what house he founded or entered, or of the wonders for which he is called a wonderworker. It carries the site's gloss on his name — Arsenios, the manly, the brave — and two pictures of him, and that is all.

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on the same day, and where the Greek page had a name and a gloss on it this one has a life. He was born at Veliky Novgorod and was a craftsman, a worker in copper. In 1373 he entered the Lisitsky monastery outside the city and was tonsured there with the name he is known by, and he lived eleven years in that house, passing through its various obediences, before he left for the Holy Mountain of Athos in search of a harder labour.

He spent three years in one of the Athonite houses, making copper vessels for the monks there and giving much of his time to prayer. When the time came to go back to Russia its abbot blessed him with an icon of the Most Holy Theotokos, the icon afterwards called the Konevskaya, and handed him a rule for a house of the common life. He laboured next on Valaam, and there he asked the Lord again and again to show him where a new monastery ought to stand.

A storm took him, while he was at sea, to the island of Konevets in Lake Ladoga. There he set up a cross and stayed, and in 1393 he built a chapel; after five years in that skete he turned it, in 1398 and with the blessing of «Новгородского архиепископа Иоанна», into a monastery of the common life, with a church in honour of the Nativity of the Most Holy Theotokos. Later, under «архиепископе Симеоне», he went a second time to the Holy Mountain, to ask the prayers and the blessing of its fathers for his own house.

While he was away the brethren, left without their abbot, came to suffer want and were for scattering. An elder «Иоанн» who lived nearby prayed for them on the summit of the island, and the Mother of God consoled him in a dream — «Скажи сетующим братиям, что Арсений скоро доставит им всякое продовольствие» — and he did return soon after with everything that was needed. In 1421 the flooding of Ladoga drove the brethren to a new place on the same island, and the house flourished again under him.

«Особое усердие проявил святитель Евфимий II»: in 1446 that archbishop came to the monastery and, besides generous gifts, made him a present of his own klobuk. So, «евангельски подвизаясь», he «возшел к горнему торжеству» on 12 June 1447, in the arms of the brethren who loved him, and was buried in the monastery church. He is honoured as a protector of seafarers: he once saved an elder «Моисей» and the fishermen with him, caught in a storm, covering them with his mantle so that the boat came safely to shore. His own life was written in the sixteenth century by «Коневским игуменом Варлаамом» and printed in 1850 together with his service and a word of praise.
