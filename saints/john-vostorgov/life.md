# John Vostorgov, Archpriest, Hieromartyr (1918)

John Vostorgov was born on 20 January 1864 in the stanitsa of Novoalexandrovskaya in Stavropol province, a priest’s son whose father died early. After the Stavropol seminary in 1887 he taught for two years and was ordained in 1889; his first parish, the Kuban village of Kirpilskoye, was mostly Old Believers, and at his own expense he set up a parish school, preached at every chance and opened a temperance society, so that within a year more than a hundred of the village’s Old Believers were reunited with the Church. From September 1890 he taught religion at the Stavropol boys’ gymnasium, then was moved to the Tiflis diocese as missionary of the Georgian Exarchate, learned the Syro-Chaldean language of the Nestorians and went to Persia to organise their reunion with Orthodoxy — three bishops were reunited. He came home in the disorders of 1905 and chose the road of struggle and confession, founding with like-minded patriots the Union of the Russian People and publishing its papers — the Sretensky calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. Arrested in Moscow on 20 May 1918 with Bishop [Ephrem](/saints/ephrem-of-selenginsk) of Selenginsk, who was staying in his flat, he was held in the Butyrka and shot with him on 23 August 1918.
