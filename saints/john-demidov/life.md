# John (Demidov)

The calendar’s own line is the whole of what this folder records of him, and the line names seven men at once. The Сретенский monastery’s calendar for 7 декабря ст. ст. prints them together under one year: «Сщмчч. Сергия, Михаила и Сергия пресвитеров, Никифора диакона и прмчч. Галактиона и Гурия, мч. Иоанна (1937).» Three presbyters, a deacon, two monastics and a layman, and 1937 is the only year it gives any of them.

His own place in that line is the last name in the line and its only layman, under the bare rank «мч.», a martyr. The name page behind it gives the surname the line leaves out, and it gives him no grade in orders at all: «Иоанн (Демидов), мч.». It lists one other day for him, «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church; one row per church is this corpus’s schema, so that day is recorded on his feast and he does not render on it.

Behind that page there is no life, and no article stands under his name. Nothing is said here of where he was born, where he served, when or by whom he was arrested, or how and where he died in 1937, because no page read says any of it.

The six other men of his line are recorded in this folder’s `related`, in both directions: the calendar commemorates the seven as one company under one year, which is more than a shared day. None of the six is named in this life, because nothing read tells one of them from another. The day page carries nine hymns, and all nine stand under the names of the older saints of the same day.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 7 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261207.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14368.html); read 13 October 2026.*
