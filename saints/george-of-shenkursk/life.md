# George of Shenkursk

saint.gr's whole account of him is three sentences. George of Shenkursk was a contemporary, it says, of Barlaam of Vazhsk and Shenkursk, whom the Greek calendar honours on 19 June. He practised asceticism in a way pleasing to God; and according to the tradition he fell asleep in peace on the feast day of his own patron, the great martyr [George the Trophy-bearer](/saints/george-the-trophy-bearer) — which is why the calendar keeps his memory on this day and not on another.

The third sentence is about his icon rather than his life: «Ο Όσιος απεικονίζεται με κουρελιασμένα ενδύματα, ανυπόδητος και με τα χέρια υψωμένα σε προσευχή» — he is depicted in ragged clothes, barefoot, with his hands raised in prayer. That is all. No year, no parentage, no monastery, no elder, no wonder and no relic, and the page prints no hymn; and the Barlaam it names for a contemporary is not among the five of that name this corpus keeps, so he could not be linked.
