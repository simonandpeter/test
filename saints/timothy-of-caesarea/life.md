# Timothy of Caesarea

saint.gr's calendar for 27 Φεβρουαρίου names him with the place that distinguishes him — its line reads «Όσιος Τιμόθεος ὁ ἐν Καισαρείᾳ», the venerable Timothy in Caesarea — and the page standing under it gives one sentence: the venerable Timothy lived and practised his asceticism at Caesarea and fell asleep in peace. There is no more of him than that.

Its couplet honours him as God's friend and his end as very much to be honoured: «Tον Tιμόθεον ως Θεού τιμώ φίλον, Oυ και τελευτή σφόδρα μοι τιμητέα». No year, no house, no office, no relics and no hymn are given; and what stands above the couplet is only saint.gr's standing gloss on the name Timothy and on the Athenians of that name, which belongs to the name and not to this saint.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 27 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/27/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3595/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
