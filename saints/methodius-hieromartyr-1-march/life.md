# Methodius (1 March)

saint.gr names the hieromartyr Methodius on this day and gives him one sentence, and the life says exactly that: «Ο Άγιος Ιερομάρτυς Μεθόδιος μαρτύρησε το έτος 1920 μ.Χ. Δεν έχουμε περισσότερες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου» — the holy hieromartyr Methodius was martyred in the year 1920, and we have no further details of the saint's life. The day's own line adds the two words «εκ Ρωσίας», out of Russia.

So three things stand recorded: the name, the country, and the year. There is no surname and no patronymic, no city, no diocese or parish, no account of the arrest or the death, no relic, no distich and no hymn; and the page gives no year for his birth.

The corpus keeps a Methodius (Ivanov), a monk of the new martyrs killed in 1937, and four older men of the name — of Constantinople, of Moravia, of Patara. None of them is on this day, none is named on this page, and the year and the rank here belong to neither of the two moderns.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3787/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
