# Simeon of Dajbabe

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him in one line for 19 марта ст. ст.: «Прп. Симеона Дайбабского (1941) (Серб.).» The rank «Прп.» makes him venerable, a monastic; the by-name is the Russian calendar's spelling of Dajbabe, the monastery in Montenegro; the year is 1941; and the bracket «(Серб.)» is the calendar's own mark for a saint of the Serbian church whom the Russian books keep. There is no narrative, no year of birth and no word about how he died.

Behind the line stands the calendar's own name page 14691, «Симеон Дайбабский, прп.», which adds nothing to the name and carries no life. It lists one further day, «30 августа (Серб.)», marked the same way; his row here stands on the 19 марта this day page printed him on, and the August day is recorded on the row.

He belongs by birth to the Serbian calendar and not to this one, so this folder is deliberately thin: the Serbian source was not read, the Serbian attestation stays `undocumented` and says so, and azbyka.ru was not read either. What the Russian calendar prints — a Serbian monastic of Dajbabe who died in 1941 and is kept on this day — is the whole of what this folder says.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 19 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260319.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14691.html), which carries no life; read 8 October 2026.*
