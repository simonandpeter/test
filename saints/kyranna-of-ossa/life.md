# Kyranna of Ossa

saint.gr's calendar keeps the new martyr Kyranna on the eighth of January, gives the year 1751 over its notice, and then declines to tell her life on that page: «Για τον βίο της Αγίας Κυράννας βλέπε στις 28 Φεβρουαρίου» — for the life of Saint Kyranna see 28 February. That other day's page was not read here, so nothing of her story is told: what is recorded is the day this calendar keeps her on and what this page prints.

What it prints besides the year is a two-line verse and three hymns. The verse says that Kyranna, shown to be the mistress of sufferings and torments, departed to the Lord of lords. The apolytikion calls her the offspring and divine shoot of Ossa, a virgin-martyr and bride of Christ who contended in these latter years; the kontakion calls her the fair virgin bride of Christ; and a kathisma, not copied here, has the place that bore her running to her intercession. The English form of her name given here is taken from the page's own Ossa.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1932/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
