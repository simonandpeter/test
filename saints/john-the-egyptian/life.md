# John the Egyptian

John was a great Egyptian confessor. Maximian could not bear the boldness with
which he confessed Christ and ordered him beheaded together with forty other Christians, in the
year 295. The Greek calendar keeps him on 20 September.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 20 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/979/saint.aspx); read 26 August 2026.*
