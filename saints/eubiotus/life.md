# Eubiotus

saint.gr's calendar for 18 December keeps Saint Eubiotus, an unconquerable and daring soldier of the Christian faith, who lived, it says, in the third century after Christ. He was born in the village of Ptoketo — or Poketos, as the page also gives it — in the province of Opsikion, and was marked out for his courage and his warm faith.

In the persecution against the Church under Diocletian he was put to many torments and imprisonments, but divine grace protected him and saved him. He was stoned, beaten with rods, scourged and his flesh was torn, and for all that he survived, and brought many onto the road of salvation. Later the triumph of Constantine the Great found him in solitary confinement with heavy chains; he was set free, and glorified God for the victory he had given the Church. Saint Eubiotus died, the page ends, in the year 320.
