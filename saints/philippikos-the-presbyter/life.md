# Philippikos

saint.gr's calendar for 24 Ιανουαρίου names the venerable Philippikos the presbyter, and the page behind that line gives one sentence of life: «Ο Όσιος Φιλιππικός απεβίωσε ειρηνικά» — the venerable Philippikos died peacefully. Nothing else is recorded of him: no country, no monastery, no year, no hymn and no relics. The one other thing the page prints is the distich that stands over him in the synaxarion.

«Καθώς περ ίπποις τοις πόνοις κεχρημένος, Χαίρων ανέπτη Φιλιππικός προς πόλον» — using his labours as a man uses horses, Philippikos flew up rejoicing to heaven. The couplet is a play on the horse that stands inside his name, and it is the only description of him the calendar has. That he is called Όσιος and πρεσβύτερος is the whole of his rank.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/24/index.aspx) and [his page](https://www.saint.gr/3521/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
