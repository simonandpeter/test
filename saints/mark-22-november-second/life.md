# Mark (22 November, the second of the name)

saint.gr's calendar for 22 Νοεμβρίου names three men in one line, two of them of the same name: «Άγιοι Μάρκος, Στέφανος και Μάρκος». They were martyred in the years of the emperor Diocletian and of the governor Magus, the page says, in the year 290; they came from Antioch of Pisidia, and because they confessed Christ as true God they were beheaded. That is the whole of the notice.

The couplet set over it counts them the same way: cut down by the sword, Stephen, with two Marks, you receive with them many crowns. The page's heading carries «(; - 290)» against each of the three names and gives none of them a year of birth; it adds no see, no rank and no relics, and prints no hymn.

This folder is the second Mark of the line, whom the page distinguishes from the first only by naming the name twice, beside [Mark](/saints/mark-22-november) and [Stephen](/saints/stephen-22-november).

*After saint.gr's calendar for 22 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/22/index.aspx) and [the life the three share](https://www.saint.gr/3061/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
