# Athanasius of Corinth

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 4 mai reads «Sfântul Ierarh Atanasie, Episcopul Corintului» — Athanasius the hierarch, bishop of Corinth — and the page behind it carries only the day, the epistle from Hebrews appointed for a bishop and the gospel of the good shepherd read with it. His see is the one thing the calendar tells us about him beyond the name.

No century, no country beyond Corinth, no manner of death and no hymn is printed there, and none is set down here.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and under the same see, and saint.gr's notice is one sentence longer than doxologia's line. Athanasius, having lived in piety, died peacefully in the days of the reign of Basil and Constantine, in the year 937; and its couplet says only that it is fitting to offer praise to Athanasius, for he chose a virtue worthy of praise. That year is the one thing the Greek adds, and it does not sit with the reign the same sentence names: the emperors Basil II and Constantine VIII reigned from 976. Both are printed on the page, the year twice, and neither is corrected here. Of his country, his birth, his election, his years in Corinth and his burial the Greek says as little as the Romanian.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 4 mai — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/4-mai) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-ierarh-atanasie-episcopul-corintului); read 20 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 4 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/04/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/374/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026.*
