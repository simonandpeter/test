# John (Selmanov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names three Johns in a company of eight on 28 July and gives no life for any of them. The entry is «Прмч. Василия , прмцц. Анастасии и Елены , мчч. Арефы , Иоанна , Иоанна , Иоанна и мц. Мавры (1937).» — a hieromonk, two nuns, four laymen of whom three bear one name, and a laywoman, all with the single year 1937.

The line gives the forename three times and no surname whatever. What makes them three men rather than one repeated is that the calendar hangs three different numbered name pages on the three words, and the three pages carry three surnames. His is «Иоанн (Сельманов), мч.», John Selmanov, martyr, and he is the last of the three as the line runs.

The calendar gives him no rank in the church at all, so none is written here. He died in 1937 with the seven others of this entry, and the calendar keeps them together on this day among the new martyrs of Russia. The company is recorded here as `related` on all eight folders. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 28 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260728.html); read 13 October 2026.*
