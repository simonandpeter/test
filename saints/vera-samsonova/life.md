# Vera (Samsonova)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names a martyr Vera on 1 июня and gives no life of her. Its line for the day is the whole of what it says: «Сщмч. Василия пресвитера, мц. Веры (1940).» — a hieromartyr Basil, a presbyter, and the martyr Vera, with one year, 1940, given to them both. So what the calendar tells is that she was counted among the martyrs and that she died in 1940.

Her own name page on that site, 10060, carries nothing but the heading «Вера (Самсонова), ктитор, мц.» and the two days «26 января (Новомуч.) , 1 июня». The heading gives two things the day's line does not: the surname, and the word ктитор — the church warden, the layperson who kept a parish's fabric and its accounts. The calendar grades her мученица and gives her no monastic or clerical standing, and nothing was added to that. There is no life page for her on the site, no icon, and no page names a parish, a diocese or a place.

The day page prints eight hymns and none under her name. The 26 января commemoration of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia was not read, and nothing of it is in this folder.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 1 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260601.html) and [her name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/10060.html); read 8 October 2026.*
