# Nicholas, son of Philosophos Ornatsky

saint.gr keeps him on 31 Μαΐου with his father and his brother, and the life behind the line is the father's. [Philosophos Ornatsky](/saints/philosophos-ornatsky), it says, finished the theological academy of Saint Petersburg in 1885, married Helen Zaozerkov, was ordained presbyter, and built up an enormous work of charity and mission; through the first world war he stood beside the wounded soldiers and their families.

Of Nicholas the page says one thing: that he was his son, and that he served with senior rank in the ninth battalion of the Russian army. His brother [Boris](/saints/boris-son-of-philosophos) had been appointed head of the twenty-third artillery brigade and fought bravely on the Austro-Hungarian front.

After the revolution of 1917 the father was arrested, on 9 August 1918, together with his sons, by men of the state security, who took them to the prisons of Kronstadt; and there the three of them were executed by shooting, and so gave the witness of their faith. The page gives Nicholas no year of birth, no trial and no further word, and prints no hymn for him.
