# Thessalonike (19 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 19 December keeps a company of which it names two: Eutychius, Thessalonike, two hundred men and seventy women. The whole of what its page says of their story is four words — they were all martyred by the sword. It gives no year and no reign, names no judge and no city, tells nothing of their lives before that sentence and lists no relic of any of them.

What the page carries besides is two epigrams, and both of them only count and play on the names: that together with Eutychius, Thessalonike too found victory happily by the sword; and that the sword took away ten score of men and seven times ten women. The other named one of the two here is [Eutychius](/saints/eutychius-19-december); the two hundred and the seventy the page does not name, and no folder is made for them. It prints no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 19 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/19/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3303/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
