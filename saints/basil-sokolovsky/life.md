# Basil Sokolovsky

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps him with his two brothers. Its ninth entry for 6 июля ст. ст. reads «Прпп. Тихона , Василия и Никона Соколовских (XVI).» — the venerable Tikhon, Basil and Nikon of Sokolovo, of the sixteenth century — and one reading of three sentences serves all three.

«Братья по плоти, жили и подвизались в VI веке.» — brothers in the flesh, who lived and strove as ascetics. On an ancient icon in the church of the village of Sokolovo, in the Zvenigorod district of the Moscow governorate, the three of them are shown in the garb of the great schema; and they are buried in the Sokolov grove. That is the whole of the account, and the calendar names neither a monastery nor a founder nor a single year.

The two halves of the source do not agree on when they lived: the day's own line says the sixteenth century and the reading says the sixth. The sixteenth is what this folder records, because it is the line the attestation quotes, and the reading's century is set down here and used for nothing. His brothers are [Tikhon](/saints/tikhon-sokolovsky) and [Nikon](/saints/nikon-sokolovsky), and his name page 388 gives «День памяти: 6 июля», one day and no other, and links to this reading alone.
