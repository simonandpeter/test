# Diodorus of Laodicea

Diodorus was one of the three martyrs Diodorus, Diomedes and Didymus, all of Laodicea in Syria, who were seized by the ruler there and, because they confessed bravely that they were Christians, were scourged to death; giving their flesh to the whips, the verse says, they scourged error. The Greek calendar keeps them on 11 September.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 11 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/999/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
