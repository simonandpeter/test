# Theodore the Byzantine

Doxologia's page for Theodore the Byzantine carries no life tab and no troparion tab, only a single notice under his name. It says that he confessed Christ in the city of Mytilene, on the island of Lesbos in Greece, at the age of twenty-one, on 17 February in the year 1795, after he had been tortured by the Turks; and that his relics lie for veneration in the cathedral at Mytilene.

The rest of the page is photographs — of those relics, of the metropolitan cathedral at Mytilene, and of the treasures of the monastery of Dionysiou on Athos. There is no account of where he came from, of who took him, or of what he was asked, and this entry adds nothing to the notice.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 17 februarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/17-februarie) and [the page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-teodor-bizantinul); read 19 September 2026.*
