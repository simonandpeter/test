# Pimen (Myasnikov) of Ugresha

Pimen — Peter Dmitrievich Myasnikov in the world — was born on 10 August 1810 in the city of Vologda; his parents Demetrius Afanasievich and Avdotya Petrovna were of the merchant estate. At ten or eleven he took to reading the Bible; his father was churchwarden of his parish, and Peter was often in church, and at seventeen he got the book The Spiritual Alphabet and, reading it, was finally confirmed in the thought of renouncing the world. In June 1832 he entered the Novoezersk monastery of St Cyril; in 1833, a novice, he moved to the Optina hermitage, where he became cell-attendant to the hieromonk Hilarius, and in 1834 — the calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. He followed Hilarius to the Nikolo-Ugreshsky monastery, where he was tonsured in 1838 and ordained; in 1853 he was appointed its superior and in 1858 raised to archimandrite, and in twenty-seven years of government built five churches and brought the monastery to spiritual flower. He died on 17 August 1880, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 17 August (Julian).
