# Dunstan of Canterbury

Dunstan was born in 909 at Glastonbury in England, of noble and well-to-do parents, and saint.gr counts it his good fortune that his teachers were distinguished monks from Ireland who were living in the town at the time. He was tonsured a monk and was ordained deacon and then presbyter by Alphege, bishop of Winchester. He was first elected bishop of Winchester himself, and afterwards, in 961, archbishop of Canterbury.

In that see he imposed a great discipline, and brought back seemliness and reverence among the clergy and the monks. He imposed discipline on the laity as well, and saint.gr singles out the law of the family as the part of their life he pressed hardest. The page explains why the work was needed: in the years that followed the Danish invasions there had been a great loosening of manners. Dunstan fell asleep in peace in 988.
