# The Syrian Martyrs (11 March)

saint.gr's calendar for 11 Μαρτίου keeps a memory of the Syrian holy martyrs — «Μνήμη των Σύρων Αγίων Μαρτύρων» — and the page behind that line holds two sentences. The first is that these holy martyrs were martyred in the fourth century. The second is the page's own admission: «Δεν έχουμε περισσότερες λεπτομέρειες για το μαρτύριο των Αγίων», we have no further details of the martyrdom of the saints.

So no name is given for any of them, and no number, no city of Syria, no persecutor and no manner of death; and because the line enumerates nobody, the company is kept here as the one entry the calendar makes of it rather than as folders for men whose names the source does not have. What is recorded is the commemoration, the country and the century.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/11/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3820/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
