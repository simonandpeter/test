# Tanco of Verden

saint.gr names him and gives him two sentences. Its calendar for 16 February prints «Άγιος Tanco Επίσκοπος Βέρντεν», with the name in Latin letters, and the page behind that line says that Saint Tanco — or Tancho, or Tatta; it allows all three — came from Scotland and worked as a missionary in Germany. He was chosen bishop of the city of Verden, and he was martyred in the year 815.

That is the whole of the entry. The page gives no year of birth, and its own heading leaves that date a question mark; it gives no account of the mission, no manner of death, no relics, no couplet and no hymn. It is the only line on this Greek day that prints its saint's name in the Latin alphabet, and the Greek form «Τάνκο» appears once, in a parenthesis on the life page.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 16 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/16/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3729/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
