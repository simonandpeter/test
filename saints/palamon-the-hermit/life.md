# Palamon the Hermit

doxologia.ro's calendar names him on 12 august and gives no full life — his page opens no Viață tab — but it prints one paragraph about him, and this is what that paragraph says. Palamon was a great Egyptian hermit, known for the hardness of his life and for an unshakable abstinence: he ate only bread and water, and never tasted oil or wine. He was a severe father, but full of discernment, and received those who wanted the monastic life with care, testing their patience and the honesty of their wish to serve Christ.

So he received Pachomius the Great, whom the same calendar keeps on 15 mai: seeing him settled in his purpose and humble, he clothed him in the monastic schema and formed him in fasting, prayer, work and almsgiving. Their life was full of bodily and spiritual labour, and Palamon guided his disciple with a father's attention, keeping him from pride and teaching him humility. After a life of hard struggle, weakened by fasting and toil, Palamon departed to the Lord, and was buried by his beloved disciple, leaving him the spirit of prayer and the monastic rule for his inheritance.

Nothing further is set down here because nothing further was read: the page gives him no century, no year and no place beyond Egypt, and the Greek, Russian and Serbian calendars were not consulted for him.
