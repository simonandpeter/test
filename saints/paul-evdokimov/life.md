# Paul (Evdokimov)

**The shortest life of the eighteen, and it says so itself.** The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him fourth of the sixteen it closes with 1937, among the «пресвитеров», and carries no life for him; azbyka.ru prints for the civil 5 декабря an entry out of pstgu.ru of two short paragraphs, and the first of them says «Об этом священномученике известно мало» — little is known of this hieromartyr. Павел Васильевич Евдокимов was born on 17 August 1877 in the village of Очаково in the Зубополянский district of the province of Тамбов. He served in one of the churches of the town of Вышний Волочёк, and it was in that town that he was arrested, on 25 November 1937.

**Nine days from arrest to execution.** On 2 December 1937 the troika of the NKVD administration for the Калинин region charged him with «контрреволюционной деятельности» and sentenced him to the highest measure of punishment; on 4 December he was shot. That day was in 1937 the Julian 21 ноября, the feast of the Entry of the Theotokos, and the day before the one the calendars keep him on. Of his parents, his schooling, his ordination, the name of his church, his interrogation and his burial the sources read say nothing at all, and nothing is added here.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 22 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261122.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9747.html); and the entry azbyka.ru prints for the civil 5 декабря — [the page](https://azbyka.ru/days/sv-pavel-evdokimov); read 6 October 2026.*
