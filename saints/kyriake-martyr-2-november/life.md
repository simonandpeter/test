# Kyriake (2 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 2 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιες Κυριακή, Δομνίνα και Δόμνα», and the page behind that line says one thing only: the holy Kyriake, [Domnina](/saints/domnina-martyr-2-november) and [Domna](/saints/domna-martyr-2-november) were martyred by the sword. There is no city, no persecutor, no year, nothing of who the three women were to each other, and nothing of their relics.

The page sets one epigram over them, and it repeats the names and the manner of the death and adds only an adjective: Kyriake, Domnina and revered Domna, the three of them, found a revered end by the sword. Each of the three has a heading of her own on the page, which is why there are three folders here and not one.

This folder takes its name from the day, because the calendar gives it nothing else to take a name from. No hymn is printed. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands, bare as it is.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 2 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/02/index.aspx) and [the notice of the three](https://www.saint.gr/1050/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
