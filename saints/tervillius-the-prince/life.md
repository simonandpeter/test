# Tervillius, son of Joel of Wales

saint.gr's whole account of him is two sentences: «Ο Άγιος Τερβίλλιος γεννήθηκε το έτος 566 μ.Χ. στην Ουαλία και ήταν υιός του βασιλέως Ιωήλ του Β'. Ακολούθησε τον ερημικό βίο στην περιοχή του Λάντερφελ και κοιμήθηκε με ειρήνη το έτος 660 μ.Χ.» — Tervillius was born in the year 566 in Wales and was the son of king Joel II; he followed the hermit's life in the country of Llanderfel and fell asleep in peace in the year 660.

That is all. The day's line adds only the word πρίγκιπας, prince. The page tells no incident of the ninety-four years it gives him, names no teacher, no monastery and no bishop, names no relics, glosses his name nowhere, and prints no couplet and no hymn. It writes the place as Λάντερφελ and does not say where in Wales it lies.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1826/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
