# Demetrius (Rozhdestvensky)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him in one line with one other for 6 февраля ст. ст. — «Сщмч. Димитрия пресвитера и мч. Анатолия (1921).» — a hieromartyr and an archpriest who died in 1921. The name link behind the line gives the calendar's own nominative, «Димитрий (Рождественский), протоиерей», which adds the surname the line leaves out. The martyr named beside him on the line carries the same surname, and the calendar says nothing about how the two were related; nothing is asserted here. The calendar prints no life page for him, and none is given here: that is what it records of him, and the whole of it.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 6 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260206.html); read 3 October 2026.*
