# Susanna, disciple of Pancratius

saint.gr's calendar for 7 Ιουνίου names two women on one line and on one page, Aisia and Susanna, and gives them a single sentence on the authority of Nikodemos the Hagiorite: «Κατά τον Άγιο Νικόδημο οι Αγίες Αισία (ή Εσία ή Ευσέβεια) και Σωσάννα ήταν μαθήτριες του Παγκρατίου, επισκόπου Ταυρομενίου και η Σύναξή τους γίνεται στο ναό του Βασιλίσκου» — according to Saint Nikodemos the holy Aisia (or Esia, or Eusebia) and Susanna were disciples of [Pancratius](/saints/pancratius-of-tauromenium), bishop of Tauromenium, and their synaxis is held in the church of Basiliskos.

That is the whole of it. The page gives no century, no country, no manner of death and no relics, and the only rank it puts on either of them is «Αγία». The couplet it prints above the sentence says that both maidens, keeping their virginity, entered the flame as though it were a maidens' chamber — which is the synaxaria's comment and not a statement of how they died; nothing is concluded from it here. The other of the two is [Aisia](/saints/aisia-disciple-of-pancratius).

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/502/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
