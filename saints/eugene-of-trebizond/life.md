# Eugene of Trebizond

Eugene is the last of four men taken at Trebizond in the reign of Diocletian and Maximian, and doxologia.ro prints one life for all four on 21 ianuarie. [Valerian](/saints/valerian-of-trebizond), [Candidus](/saints/candidus-of-trebizond) and [Aquila](/saints/aquila-of-trebizond) were caught first, in the mountains, and were already in prison at Trebizond when Eugene was seized, not many days after them, and beaten hard for confessing Jesus Christ.

The life says that when Lysias went into the idols' temple Eugene was brought in with him, and that at his prayer the idols fell and were broken small as dust. At the tyrant's order the servants bound him hand and foot with rope, stretched him on the ground and beat him with thick staves; then they hung him up naked, tore his body with iron claws, burned him with torches and poured sharp vinegar mixed with salt into his wounds.

After that all four were thrown into a burning furnace. They came out of it without hurt, and were killed by the sword; so, the page says, they finished their confession.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 21 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/21-ianuarie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-mucenic-evghenie); read 19 September 2026.*
