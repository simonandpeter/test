# Peter (Ignatov)

The Russian church calendar's last line for 1 November is the whole of what is recorded of him here: «Мч. Петра (1941)» — the martyr Peter, and the year. Its index of names gives the surname the line does not, «Петр (Игнатов), мч.», and lists this day and the 26 January synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia against his name.

There is no life page behind that name, and the name page carries nothing but the two days. The calendar gives him no grade and no office, which among the twentieth-century entries of this day marks him out as a layman, and 1941 is the latest death the day lists. So this folder records the year, the day the Church keeps him on and nothing else; azbyka.ru, which has carried long lives for others of these years, is a quota this corpus has spent and was not asked again on 4 October 2026.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 1 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261101.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13842.html); read 4 October 2026.*
