# Matthew, companion of Justus

The holy [Justus](/saints/justus-companion-of-matthew) and Matthew, saint.gr says, together with [Theophilus](/saints/theophilus-companion-of-trophimus) and [Trophimus](/saints/trophimus-companion-of-theophilus) — and the page sends its reader to its own entry for those two, on this same 21 July — were martyred under Diocletian. Because they would not deny Christ they were stoned, they were wounded, they were thrown into the fire, and at the last they were beheaded.

That is the whole of the page's notice for the two of them, and it is the same notice for both: the Greek calendar names them in one line and tells their passion in one sentence. The synaxarion's distich above it says that two men, giving up their necks to the sword, received with a common end a common crown. He is not the evangelist of the same name, and the page gives him no byname, no country and no year — only the emperor under whom he died, and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 21 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/21/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2021/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
