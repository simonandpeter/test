# Cyriacus the Commoner of Alexandria

Cyriacus was one of the Christians of Alexandria, hunted in the persecution of Decius in 250; seized with many others and tortured in various ways, he was at last beheaded — a commoner, the verse plays, but by the sword a fellow of the chorus of martyrs. The Greek synaxarion keeps the martyrs of Alexandria under Decius in separate entries on 6 September; the Russian calendar counts them as the eleven with the presbyter Faustus and the deacon Abibus.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 6 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2366/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
