# Micah of Radonezh

saint.gr keeps him on this day as one of the first disciples of [Sergius of Radonezh](/saints/sergius-of-radonezh), and says that he lived together with him in the same cell. Under that guidance, the page says, he reached a great degree of spiritual perfection; and for the meekness of his soul and the striving of his heart the Most Holy Theotokos appeared to him too, and not to his elder only.

The page tells the visit. Once, when Sergius had finished the morning rule of prayer and sat down a little while to rest, he said suddenly to his disciple: «Να είσαι έτοιμος, παιδί μου, θα έχουμε μια θαυμάσια επίσκεψη» — be ready, my child, we shall have a wonderful visit. He had barely whispered the words when a voice was heard: the All-Pure is drawing near. A light shone there stronger than the sun, and Micah fell to the ground in fear and lay as though he were dead.

When Sergius lifted his disciple up, Micah asked him: tell me, father, what is the reason for this wonderful vision? He could not get a word out for fear, and it was Sergius who explained to him the appearing of the Most Holy Theotokos and her protection. He reposed in peace in 1385, and his relics are kept in a crypt in the Lavra of the Holy Trinity of Saint Sergius at Moscow. On 10 December 1734 a church was consecrated over his grave, dedicated to that appearing of the Theotokos and to the memory of the Apostles Peter and John the Theologian.

The Russian church calendar keeps him on 6 мая ст. ст. and gives him two short lives. Both make him a disciple and the cell-attendant of [Sergius of Radonezh](/saints/sergius-of-radonezh), one of his first disciples, living in the same cell with him; and both say that labouring under that great elder in simplicity of heart he reached such a height of purity that he alone of all Sergius's fellow-labourers was counted worthy to be present when the Mother of God visited the man she had chosen.

Every day, the fuller of the two says, Sergius sang the akathist to the Mother of God before her icon when his evening rule in the cell was done. One night, after an earnest prayer for his monastery, his soul felt a heavenly visitation drawing near, and he said to his disciple: «Бодрствуй, чадо: мы будем в сей час иметь чудесное посещение». He had hardly said it when a voice was heard — «Се, Пречистая грядет!» The elder rose and went quickly out into the passage, where a light brighter than the sun shone round him and he saw the most blessed Virgin, and with her the apostles «Пётр» and «Иоанн Богослов». She promised not to depart from that place and always to cover it.

The elder trembled with fear and joy; and when he came to himself he saw his disciple lying on the floor as though dead, for the master could see the Queen of Heaven and hear her voice, while the disciple, struck with awe, could not see all of it and saw only the heavenly light. When «Михей» came to his senses he fell at his teacher's feet and asked what that wondrous vision had been, saying that his soul had all but parted from his body; and Sergius, who could not yet speak for the stirring of his own soul and whose face was in flower with a heavenly gladness, answered only: «Подожди, чадо, и моя душа трепещет от этого видения». When at last the elder was somewhat quieted he sent his disciple to call two reverent men of the brotherhood, Исаак the silent and Симон the ecclesiarch, and all of them together sang a service of supplication to the Mother of God.

The gentle disciple of a gentle elder fell asleep while his abba was still living, on 6 May 1385. His relics rest under a bushel in the Trinity-Sergius Lavra, beneath a small church built over the place of his burial and consecrated on 10 December 1734 in honour of the appearing of the Mother of God, with the apostles beside her, to his teacher — the thing he himself had been counted worthy to witness.
