# Neophytus (7 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 7 December names Neophytus and gives him one line of life: the holy Neophytus was martyred by drowning in the sea. No country, no reign, no persecutor and no year is printed, and he is joined to no other name on the day. The couplet set over him is the rest of what the page has, and it turns on the water he died in.

Neophytus having died in the midst of the waters, it says, lives now beside the outflowings of mystical waters. That is the whole of the entry, and the gloss the site prints under the name — the newly initiated, the one born again — is its standing note on the name itself and not on the saint.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3184/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
