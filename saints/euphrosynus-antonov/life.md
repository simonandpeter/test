# Euphrosynus (Antonov)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him ninth of ten people on one line for 31 октября ст. ст. and gives him no life. His name stands there as «Евфросина», a forename in the genitive, under the second of the line's three rank words — the one the calendar uses for a monastic put to death, which governs his name and the one before it — and above a year that serves all ten. The name link behind it adds the surname the line leaves out and the grade: «Евфросин (Антонов), иеромонах, прмч.», a hieromonk of that name, one of the new martyrs of the Russian church.

So the year is the whole of what is recorded of him, and the year is 1937. There is no life page behind his name, and his name page carries no text, no icon and no hymn — only his name and his days, of which the second is 26 января, the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. The surname returns no other folder in this corpus, and the other bearers of his forename here are of other centuries altogether.

The other monastic on this line does have a life, and his name link calls him a hieromartyr where the line calls him a venerable-martyr; this one's link and the line agree. Both of the two men on the line with lives were shot on 13 November 1937 in the Tver region, and nothing on the page says that of him. The second Russian calendar, azbyka.ru, which has given this corpus full lives and archival references for others of these men, refused the request with a 403 on the day he was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 31 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261031.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14343.html); read 4 October 2026.*
