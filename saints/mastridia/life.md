# Mastridia

Mastridia lived at Jerusalem and kept a strict ascetic rule, and the whole of what is recorded of her here is three sentences in the Ohrid Prologue, where she stands third under the Julian 7 February. A young man set his eyes on her and began to trouble her; and so that both she and he should be saved from sin, she took a few soaked beans in a basket and went away from the city into the desert.

There she passed seventeen years, and for the whole of that time, by the power of God, neither did the beans run out nor did her garment wear old. She fell asleep in peace about the year 580. Nothing else of her is written: not her family, not where she came from before Jerusalem, not who the young man was and not what became of him. The rank the Prologue gives her is Преподобна, venerable, and it gives her no office.
