# Schinon

saint.gr's calendar for 23 December names Schinon the martyr, and the whole of what it has to say of him is one short sentence: he was martyred by the sword. Above the sentence stands a distich which addresses him by name and says no more than the same thing — that the writer would not forget him either, nor him who did not escape notice, since his head was struck off. There the entry ends.

The calendar gives him no year, no city, no emperor and no judge, prints no hymn for him, and marks the feast fixed: «Τύπος εορτής: Σταθερή. Εορτάζει στις 23 Δεκεμβρίου εκάστου έτους». The name is a rare one, and no folder this corpus already holds reads like it. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence by which saint.gr marks an entry whose existence, biography or date it will not vouch for.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 23 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/23/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3309/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
