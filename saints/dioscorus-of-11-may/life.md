# Dioscorus

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 11 mai reads «Sfântul Mucenic Dioscor», and the page kept under his name carries no Viață tab and no Tropar tab — only the epistle and gospel appointed for the day, Colossians 3:12–16 and Luke 21:12–19, which are the readings of a martyr. The calendar gives no country, no century and no manner of death, and this page says no more than the calendar says.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and gives him a city and a trial. The holy martyr Dioscorus — or Dioscorides — the New came of Smyrna, saint.gr says, and in that same city he was martyred for Christ. The fury of the idolaters against the Christians only kindled his zeal for the holy faith the more. He was denounced, then, as a Christian; and he confessed that he was one and that he would remain unmoved in that holy confession. With the idea that a heavy imprisonment would tame his spirit, they threw him into the most wretched and dark of prisons. But because even then he went on declaring that to his last breath he would stay faithful to his Jesus, they put him to death by beheading. The page gives no year, no emperor and no judge, and prints no hymn; the couplet over him plays on the old myth of the Dioscuri living on alternate days, and says that Dioscorus, being cut down, has to live for ever.
