# Theodotus (3 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 3 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιοι Δάσιος, Σέβηρος, Άνδρωνας, Θεόδοτος και Θεοδότη», and the page behind that line says one thing only: the holy [Dasius](/saints/dasius-3-november), [Severus](/saints/severus-3-november), [Andron](/saints/andron), Theodotus and [Theodote](/saints/theodote-3-november) were martyred by the sword. There is no city, no persecutor, no year, nothing of who the five were to one another, and nothing of their relics.

The page's epigram adds the one distinction it makes among them, which is of sex and not of name: let the woman too be a fellow martyr to the men, cut down along with the four who were cut. So Theodote is the woman of the company and Theodotus one of its four men, and the page keeps the two names apart in two headings of its own.

This folder takes its name from the day, because the calendar gives it nothing else, and because the corpus already keeps more than a dozen men of this name on other days. No hymn is printed. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου».

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/03/index.aspx) and [the notice of the five](https://www.saint.gr/2874/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
