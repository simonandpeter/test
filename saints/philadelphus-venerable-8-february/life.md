# Philadelphus (8 February)

saint.gr names him with one companion and gives the pair a single sentence. Its calendar for 8 February prints «Όσιοι Φιλάδελφος και Πολύκαρπος», and the page behind that line says that the venerable fathers Philadelphus and [Polycarp](/saints/polycarp-venerable-8-february) were ascetic monks and fell asleep in peace. There is no monastery here, no wilderness, no country, no century, no relics and no hymn.

Over that sentence the page prints a couplet on the two of them, and it turns on the friendship the first name already carries: the end of life was indeed a friend, it says, both to Philadelphus and to Polycarp. Φιλάδελφος it glosses as the man who is a friend to his brothers and loves them.

The same page records that their memory is repeated on 25 October. That day's page was not read, and the day kept here is the day page that was.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/532/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
