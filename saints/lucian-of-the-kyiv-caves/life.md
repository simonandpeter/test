# Lucian of the Kyiv Caves

saint.gr's calendar for 15 October keeps «Άγιος Λουκιανός ο ιερομάρτυρας Πρεσβύτερος των Σπηλαίων του Κιέβου» — Lucian the hieromartyr, presbyter of the Caves of Kyiv — and then says that we have no details of the saint's life. That sentence is the whole of the page.

So what the calendar holds of him is the line: two ranks, hieromartyr and presbyter, and the house he belonged to, the Caves of Kyiv. It gives no year and no century, no account of his priesthood and none of his martyrdom, and prints neither a verse nor a hymn for him. The same day's other Lucian, [Lucian of Antioch](/saints/lucian-of-antioch), is a separate entry with a page and a life of its own, and is a different man.

The Russian calendar keeps him on the same menologion day, and adds one thing to the line and nothing else. Its entry is «Сщмч. Лукиана Печерского (1243)» — Lucian of the Caves, hieromartyr, 1243 — and that year is the only year any of the four calendars gives him. Where almost every other name on the day carries a link to a life, his carries none, and his page in the calendar's own index of names prints his name, his rank and the one day and stops there. So the Russian has no account of him either.

The Romanian and Serbian calendars were read for the same day and neither keeps him. doxologia.ro's 15 octombrie and the Ohrid Prologue for that Julian day both have the other Lucian, the presbyter of Antioch, and he is a different man.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 15 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/15/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2600/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026; and the Russian church calendar of the Sretensky monastery (days.pravoslavie.ru) for 15 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261015.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/1385.html), read 28 October 2026.*
