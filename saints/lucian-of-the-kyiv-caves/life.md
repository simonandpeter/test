# Lucian of the Kyiv Caves

saint.gr's calendar for 15 October keeps «Άγιος Λουκιανός ο ιερομάρτυρας Πρεσβύτερος των Σπηλαίων του Κιέβου» — Lucian the hieromartyr, presbyter of the Caves of Kyiv — and then says that we have no details of the saint's life. That sentence is the whole of the page.

So what the calendar holds of him is the line: two ranks, hieromartyr and presbyter, and the house he belonged to, the Caves of Kyiv. It gives no year and no century, no account of his priesthood and none of his martyrdom, and prints neither a verse nor a hymn for him. The same day's other Lucian, [Lucian of Antioch](/saints/lucian-of-antioch), is a separate entry with a page and a life of its own, and is a different man.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 15 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/15/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2600/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
