# Roman (Marchenko), Presbyter, Hieromartyr (1929)

The priest Roman Marchenko was rector of the Protection church of the stanitsa of Nadezhdinskaya in the Verny district of the Semirechye region, today the town of Issyk. The closing of the church in the autumn of 1929 turned into a great tragedy: the Cossacks of the stanitsa actively resisted the sacrilegious acts of the godless authorities, mounted militia were brought into the stanitsa to put them down, several dozen parishioners were arrested, and the priest was killed for defending the church. He was glorified among the saints at the jubilee Council of Bishops of 2000, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 3 September (Julian) among the martyrs of Semirechye.
