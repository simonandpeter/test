# Alto of Altomünster

Alto was a Scottish monk who, travelling in Germany, became known for many wonders and founded, with King Pepin’s leave, the monastery of Altmünster in Bavaria in the middle of the eighth century. Among a barbarous nation then living in ignorance, vice and superstition, the gentleness and devotion of this saint drew many to the true faith, and his monastic life showed the power of divine grace, raising men fallen into weakness and corruption to the greatest heights of holiness. His honour in Germany falls on 9 February, the day he is believed to have reposed, but the British calendars, which the Greek synaxarion follows, keep him on 5 September.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 5 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2356/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
