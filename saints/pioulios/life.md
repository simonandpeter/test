# Pioulios

saint.gr names him and gives him one clause. Its calendar for 18 February prints «Άγιος Πιούλιος», bare, and the page behind that line says only this: that Saint Pioulios was martyred by the sword. There is no homeland here, no city, no emperor, no century, no relics, no reading of the name and no hymn.

What the page does print over that clause is a couplet, and the couplet is the whole of his character as the Greek calendar has kept it. Pioulios said he expected every suffering; but having only been beheaded, he has suffered no loss. That, and the day, and the rank the line gives him, is what this folder holds.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3738/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
