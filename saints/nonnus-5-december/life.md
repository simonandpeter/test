# Nonnus (5 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 5 December names Nonnus and gives him one line of life: the venerable Nonnus reposed in peace. No country, no reign and no year is printed. The couplet over his name is the rest of what the page has: Nonnus having fled the fetter of the adversary, they fetter him, lying dead, with the burial bands. The gloss the site sets under the name belongs to the fifth-century epic poet of the Dionysiaca and not to this saint.

The same day's calendar keeps the venerable [Gratus](/saints/gratus-5-december), whose page says that in certain synaxaria he is mentioned as bishop of Ephesus and mentioned together with this Nonnus. The page of Nonnus itself says nothing of Ephesus and nothing of Gratus, so the connection is recorded as the one page's and not as both.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3159/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
