# The Martyrs of the Pantokrator Monastery at Daou Pendeli

The Russian church calendar names them and gives no life. Its line for the day reads «Прмчч. многих священной обители Пантократора, в Дау Пендели (Аттика) пострадавших (ок. 1680).» — of the many venerable martyrs of the holy monastery of the Pantokrator at Daou Pendeli in Attica, who suffered about the year 1680. That is the whole of it. The entry the line links is not a life but an index page, which repeats the heading, prints the one day, and carries nothing else.

So the line names not one of them, gives no number beyond «многих», many, and says nothing of the manner of their death, of who killed them, or of what became of the house they died in. By the ruling this corpus follows for a company whose members a source does not enumerate, they are kept here as one entry and not as a folder for each; the number of folders is the number of names the source prints, and it prints none.

The only year is the one in the line's own brackets, «ок. 1680», about 1680, and it is recorded as that and nothing narrower. The day page carries three hymns, and not one of them is under this company's name. The Greek calendar this corpus reads keeps the same menologion day, and its page for it does not name them, so nothing has been added to this folder from that side; no other Greek day has been searched for them.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 10 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260410.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14999.html); read 7 October 2026.*
