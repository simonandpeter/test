# Nepotian, nephew of Heliodorus

saint.gr names him and gives him a sentence and a half. Nepotian lived in the fourth century, its page says, and was ordained presbyter by his uncle, Saint Heliodorus, bishop of Altinum in Italy, whose day it gives as 3 July; and he fell asleep in peace in the year 395. To that it adds one thing more, and it is the only thing that puts him in the wider record: [Jerome](/saints/jerome-of-stridon) dedicated a treatise of his to the priestly life of Nepotian.

There is nothing else on the page: no year of birth, no birthplace, no church or town where he served, no wonder, no relic and no hymn. Not even a couplet, which this site usually prints. The uncle who ordained him is the one thing that places him, and the treatise is the one thing that survives of what he was like.
