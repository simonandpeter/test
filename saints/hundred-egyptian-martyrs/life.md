# The Hundred Egyptian Martyrs

These are the martyrs who were martyred with [Peleus](/saints/peleus-bishop-and-hieromartyr) and Nilus — a tenfold ten of Egyptian martyrs, the verse says, have their heads cut off with one eagerness. That is the whole of the Greek synaxarion’s notice for 17 September; this entry records it and nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 17 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2460/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
