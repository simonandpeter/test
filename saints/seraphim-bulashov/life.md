# Seraphim (Bulashov)

The Russian calendar's line is all there is. It names three together on this day and gives no year at all: «Прмч. Серафима, прмц. Евдокии и Екатерины» — the venerable-martyr Seraphim and the venerable-martyrs Eudocia and Catherine. The name behind his part of it reads «Серафим (Булашов), игумен, прмч.», Seraphim Bulashov, abbot, venerable-martyr — his surname, his rank in a monastery and the manner of his end.

Behind that line there is no life page for any of the three, and the day prints no hymn for them. Nothing here says where they lived, what house they belonged to, when they were killed or by whom: the calendar gives the names, the ranks and nothing further. He is kept with [Eudocia](/saints/eudocia-kuzminova), whom the calendar calls a novice, and [Catherine](/saints/catherine-cherkasova), whose line gives her no office at all.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 23 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260123.html); read 3 October 2026.*
