# Barlaam of Sikisk

The Greek calendar keeps Barlaam, a hermit, on 5 October and says only that it has no details of the life of this Russian saint; its heading gives his death as 1846, and the page points to the synaxis of all the saints of Siberia. “Sikisk” is the Greek page’s own rendering of his place, and which Russian saint it means is left for the Russian calendar to show.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 5 Οκτωβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/4245/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
