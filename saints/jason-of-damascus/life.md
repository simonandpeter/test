# Jason of Damascus

saint.gr's calendar for 12 October names him and gives no life. Its whole notice is one sentence: «Ο Άγιος Ιάσων ήταν επίσκοπος Δαμασκού και απεβίωσε ειρηνικά» — Saint Jason was bishop of Damascus and died peacefully. That is the see and the manner of his end, and the page adds nothing further: no year, no birthplace, no account of his episcopate and no hymn.

Beside it stand the two things the site prints under every name — a two-line verse, which says that Jason by his sufferings overthrows the strongholds and raised towers of the virtues before his end, and a note on the name, that Ιάσων is from ίασις, healing, and means the healer.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2721/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
