# Eumenius of Murmansk

saint.gr's calendar names him with two companions and gives the three of them one sentence between them. The line for 4 June reads «Όσιοι Ελεάζαρος, Ναζάριος και Ευμένιος οι εν Μουρμάνσκ της Ρωσίας οι Θαυματουργοί», and the page behind it says this: the venerable Eleazar, Nazarius and Eumenius lived in Russia in the fifteenth century after Christ and practised asceticism in a manner pleasing to God in the monastery of Saint John in the region of Murmansk, or Murom, near Olonets; they were counted worthy of the gift of working wonders; and they fell asleep in holiness and in peace.

The page adds one thing more: that the Church honours their memory also on the day of the feast of all the saints of Novgorod, on the fourth Sunday after Pentecost. That second commemoration is reckoned from Pascha and falls on no fixed date, so this folder keeps the 4 June of the day page and records the other in a note. His companions are [Eleazar](/saints/eleazar-of-murmansk) and [Nazarius](/saints/nazarius-of-murmansk), and the three folders carry the same sentence because the source gives them nothing apart; what wonders they worked, and whether in life or after death, the page does not say.
