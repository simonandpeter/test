# Alexander (Aksyonov)

Alexander Aksyonov — Petrovich or Pavlovich, the records differ — was born
in 1886 in the village of Rychkovo in the Nikolsk district of Vologda
province, now in the Vokhma district of Kostroma region, finished the
Vologda seminary, married, and from 1917 served as priest of the church of
St Nicholas at Chernovsko-Nikolayevskoye in the Nizhny Novgorod country of
the Vyatka diocese. In 1929 he was arrested for anti-Soviet agitation and
exiled three years to the north, to the Narym region, and afterwards given
ten years in the camps for keeping sermons of counter-revolutionary content.
In 1937 he was in the Karaganda camp in Kazakhstan, where the priest
[Stephen Kostogryz](/saints/stephen-kostogryz) of the Odessa region was also
held; one group case was opened on the two of them, for systematic
counter-revolutionary agitation among the prisoners, and they were sentenced
to death and shot together in the first Volkovo section of the Karlag on 26
September 1937. He is kept on 13 September of the old style, 26 September,
and in the synaxis of the Vyatka saints.
