# Macarius of Kyiv

Macarius, saint.gr says, was abbot of the monastery of the Holy Trinity at Vilna. In 1495, after the death of Jonah, metropolitan of Kyiv, whose years the page gives as 1488 to 1494, Macarius was elected metropolitan of Kyiv in his place. He was martyred in the year 1497, in the village of Strigolovo on the river Vzhizha, when the Tatars came into Russia.

His incorrupt relics, the page says twice, are kept in the cathedral church of Saint Vladimir in Kyiv. That is the whole of the notice: no year of birth, no account of the martyrdom beyond the Tatar raid, no place of origin, and no hymn.
