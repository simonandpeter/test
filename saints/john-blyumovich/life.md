# John (Blyumovich)

His line in the calendar is four words long, and it is the whole of what is recorded here. The Сретенский monastery’s calendar for 31 марта ст. ст. prints «Сщмч. Иоанна пресвитера (1938)» — the hieromartyr John, a presbyter, 1938 — and the name page behind that forename gives the surname the line leaves out and nothing besides: «Иоанн (Блюмович), иерей, сщмч.» The two agree about him in every word they share: a priest, a hieromartyr, and no more.

So nothing is said here of where he was born, where he served, when or by whom he was arrested, or where he died. There is no life page for him on the day page and none on his name page, and 1938 is the only year the calendar gives. He is the only twentieth-century name this day of the calendar prints, and the last of the Russian March; the eight entries above him on the page run from a bishop of the First Council to an abbot of the Glinsk hermitage who died in 1841.

His name page lists one other day, the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church. One row per church is the schema, so that day is recorded on his Russian row and he does not render on it.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 31 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260331.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9596.html); read 8 October 2026.*
