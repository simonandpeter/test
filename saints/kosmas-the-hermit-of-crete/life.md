# Kosmas the Hermit and Confessor

Kosmas, a great ascetic figure of seventh-century Crete, is honoured above all at the monastery of Koudoumas, in a cave church, on 2 September. He was born in the second half of the sixth century somewhere in Crete, received enough education in his youth to engage with and oppose the heresy of the Monothelites, and probably first lived as a monk in some cenobitic house; his resistance to Monothelitism likely belongs to that time, and the pressure and reactions of the Monothelite bishops drove him to leave his monastery — the synaxarion’s life runs on past the point read here.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 2 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/58/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
