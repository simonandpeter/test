# Thekla (Makusheva)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names her among the saints on 10 декабря and gives no life. Its line is «Св. Феклы исп (1954).» — the holy Thekla the confessor, 1954 — and the calendar’s own name page adds the surname the line leaves out: «Фекла (Макушева), исп.»

That is the whole of the record. The word the calendar uses of her is исповедница, a confessor: one who suffered for the faith and was not killed for it. She died in 1954, which is the latest year on this whole day, and the calendar keeps her on it alone, her line joined to no one else’s. The day page carries eleven hymns and not one is under her name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 10 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261210.html); read 13 October 2026.*
