# Euodus

Euodus belongs to the chorus of the seventy apostles and became bishop of great Antioch after the apostle Peter; having become a loud-voiced herald of the Gospel and shone in all the virtues, he died in peace. Running the straight road of the word, the verse says, he taught all to run it. The Greek calendar keeps him on 7 September with [Onesiphorus](/saints/onesiphorus-of-colophon).
