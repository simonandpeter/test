# Ibistion

Ibistion died in peace. That is the whole of the synaxarion’s notice, with its verse: washed with the hyssop of tears, Ibistion, you go cheerful to the tearless country.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 26 August — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/902/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
