# Sophronius of Cyprus

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 8 decembrie reads «Sfântul Ierarh Sofronie, Episcopul Ciprului», and his own page on that site carries the name, the see and the date and nothing else: no life, no troparion, no kontakion, no century, no account of how he came to the episcopate or what he did in it. Two things the line does assert: that he was a hierarch, which is what «Ierarh» says, and that his see was Cyprus.

What the page prints beside the name are the readings appointed for his commemoration: the epistle Hebrews 7:26-28 and 8:1-2, that such a high priest became us, holy, harmless, undefiled, separate from sinners and made higher than the heavens; and the Gospel John 10:9-16, the Lord as the door and the good shepherd who lays down his life for the sheep. Those are the readings the Romanian calendar gives a hierarch.

Nothing further is set down here because nothing further was read. Another calendar may well carry his life; none was consulted for this page, and the three other churches' rows say so.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 8 December, and names the man he followed in the see. saint.gr has Sophronius come of Cyprus, the son of devout Christian parents, and marked out for his great learning and his quickness of mind; he became so devout and so virtuous, it says, that God counted him worthy of many gifts, and in particular of working wonders.

After the death of Damianos, bishop of Cyprus, Sophronius was made archbishop of Cyprus by the unanimous judgement of clergy and people; and after his consecration he took great care for the poor, for orphans, for the crippled and for the old. Having so run his life in a way pleasing to God, the page ends, he reposed in peace. The couplets set over his name say that Sophronius, having quenched every fleshly passion, flew away from the clay of the flesh itself, and that closing his eyes below he opened them above upon the contemplation of God.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 8 decembrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/8-decembrie) and [his page there](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-ierarh-sofronie-episcopul-ciprului); read 25 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 8 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3202/saint.aspx), read 2 October 2026.*
