# Jerusalem of Beroea

Jerusalem came from Alexandria. After her husband’s death she travelled through many places preaching Christ, reaching Rome, and then went to Beroea, where she became a nun, bringing monasticism to the region, and by her mission led many pagans to the Christian faith; denounced to the duke of Thessalonica, Kindianos, who came to Beroea himself to interrogate her, she was tortured for her steadfastness, and her children were put to death cruelly — one dragged behind horses, the second beaten to death, the third made to wear — the synaxarion’s life runs on past the point read here. The Greek calendar keeps her with her sons [Kegouros](/saints/kegouros-son-of-jerusalem), [Secundinus](/saints/secundinus-son-of-jerusalem) and [Secundus](/saints/secundus-son-of-jerusalem).

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 4 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2339/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
