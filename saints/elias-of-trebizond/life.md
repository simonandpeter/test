# Elias of Trebizond

saint.gr says the new martyr Elias came from Kryoneri near Trebizond and was the son of a priest named Constantine. He was seized and tortured at the Mole of Trebizond, the place the page calls Moum Hane, in the year 1749, and was hanged at the last; and the Christians took up his holy body and buried it at the monastery of Theoskepastos.

That is the whole of the entry. The page gives him no year of birth — its heading leaves the first of his two dates a question mark — no trade, no account of what he was accused of, and it prints neither couplet nor hymn under his name; what stands above the entry is only its standing gloss on the name Elias, that it is the Hebrew for the man who has divine power.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 27 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/27/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3596/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
