# George of Chios

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιος Γεώργιος ο Νεομάρτυρας από τη Χίο» and tells a hard story. He was born on Chios; his father was called Paraskevas and his mother Angerou. At eighteen months, orphaned of his mother, he was handed over by his father to be brought up by his stepmother; and as a child his parents gave him to a joiner named Vissetzis to learn the trade.

When he came once with his master to Psara, to work the templon of the church of Saint Nicholas, George went off with some other young men to Kavala. There he was caught stealing from a garden and handed to the judge; and to escape the punishment he accepted Islam, was circumcised, and was named Ahmet.

At ten years old he came back to Chios weeping and confessing Christ, and his father, to keep him safe, moved him to an estate of his at Kydonies. Later, at twenty-two, he was betrothed; and his fiancée's brother, who had money quarrels with him, denounced him to the Turkish governor as a man who had become a Muslim and gone back to Christianity.

He was tortured hard, and after he had communicated of the immaculate mysteries inside the prison, on the morning of 26 November 1807 they cut off his head — in a martyr's fashion, the page says, little by little. So he received the crown of martyrdom from Christ the giver of the contest. The couplet set over the line has the knife that procured his end procure his crown as well.
