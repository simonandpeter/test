# Gordian (9 May)

The holy martyrs [Epimachus](/saints/epimachus-9-may) and Gordian came of Rome, saint.gr says, and contended under the emperor Decius, who reigned from 249 to 251. They were arrested, and the magistrate pressed them to deny Christ and sacrifice to the idols; and because they stood unshaken in their faith they were cruelly tortured and afterwards beheaded, and so took the crowns of the contest.

Their holy relics were laid in one and the same grave, and a considerable part of them is kept in the Benedictine abbey at Kempten in Bavaria, in Germany. Their synaxis, the page says, is held in the church of the holy martyr Stratonikos, and their memory is repeated on the thirty-first of October. The couplet set over Gordian says only that he paid no heed to the sword, taking heart for his soul in the crown: «Oυ δε προσείχε Γορδιανός τω ξίφει, / Ψυχήν εαυτού θαρσοποιών τω στέφει». The page gives him no year of his own, no father, no office and no hymn.
