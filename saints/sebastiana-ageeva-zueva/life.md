# Sebastiana (Ageeva-Zueva)

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 28 июня ст. ст. prints three new martyrs on one line of its own and her is the second of them, «Прмц. Севастианы (1938).». The line gives the forename in the genitive, a grade-class word, no rank word at all and a year, and it gives no surname. The name link behind the forename supplies the calendar’s own nominative with the surname and the grade, «Севастиана (Агеева-Зуева), прмц.», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from; the parentheses are the calendar’s own.

The name page carries nothing else: it heads «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 28 июня» and stops, with no life, no place, no house or diocese and no account of the death. Nothing is supplied for any of it here, and no year of birth is recorded because the calendar gives none. The year in the line, 1938, is the year of death recorded here. The line’s «Прмц.» is read as a venerable-martyr.

The second day the name page lists is «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which is a synaxis and not a second feast, so the folder renders on 28 июня ст. ст. only. The surname stands in the display name because the calendar gives her no epithet and nothing else to tell her by. The day page prints fifteen hymns and not one of them is under this name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 28 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260628.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14280.html); read 9 October 2026.*
