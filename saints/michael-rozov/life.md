# Michael (Rozov)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him in a single line for 8 января ст. ст. — «Св. Михаила исп., пресвитера (1941).» — a confessor and a presbyter who died in 1941. The line calls him a confessor and not a martyr, which is the calendar's own distinction: one who suffered for the faith and did not die under the sentence. The name link behind the line gives its nominative, «Михаил (Розов), иерей», which adds the surname the line leaves out.

The calendar prints no life page for him, and none is given here: the line and the name link are the whole of what it records.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 8 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260108.html); read 3 October 2026.*
