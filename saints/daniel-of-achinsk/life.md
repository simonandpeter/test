# Daniel of Achinsk

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life. «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ρώσου Αγίου» — we have no details of the life of this Russian saint, the page says, and that is the whole of its notice. Its heading sets him down as «Αγιος Δανιηλ Του Ασινσκ (; - 1843)», giving 1843 as the year of his death and no year for his birth, and it keeps him on 15 April as a fixed feast.

The page adds only a link to the synaxis of all the saints of Siberia, which is the one thing that places him anywhere. It prints no couplet, no hymn, and no rank beyond the word saint, so nothing here says whether he was a monk, a priest or a layman.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 15 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/15/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4247/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
