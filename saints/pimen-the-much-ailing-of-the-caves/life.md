# Pimen the Much-Ailing of the Caves

saint.gr's calendar for 7 August names the venerable Pimen, the one in the cave, the much-suffering, and sets his death in its own head line as 1110. Of the life behind that year it says one sentence and no more: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον Βίο του Οσίου παρά μόνο ότι ήταν Ρώσος μοναχός που κοιμήθηκε το 1110 μ.Χ.» — we have no details of the venerable one's life, except that he was a Russian monk who fell asleep in the year 1110.

The epithets are the whole of his portrait on this page: the cave he lived in, and the long suffering his name there carries. The same day's listing holds a second Russian Pimen, the faster, whom the page dates to the fourteenth century and keeps as an entry of his own; the two are not one man. saint.gr prints no hymn to either of them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/754/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
