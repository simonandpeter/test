# Theophanes of Naousa

Theophanes was born at Ioannina and as a young man left everything for the Holy Mountain, where he was tonsured at the monastery of Docheiariou; in fasting, prayer, vigil and the denial of every needless thing he was a pattern to all the monks, and in time was chosen abbot. Later, over some disagreement with the monks, he left the Mountain and came with his sister’s son to Veria in Macedonia, where he founded a monastery in honour of the Mother of God; when it flourished in the spiritual life he left his nephew to govern it and went to Naousa, where he founded another, to the holy Archangels. He died in peace in the fifteenth century, and his wonderworking relics lie at Naousa to this day, the Prologue says, and show forth the great power of God.

The Romanian calendar keeps him on 19 August, and doxologia.ro prints the Ohrid Prologue's notice of him in Romanian. It says he was born at Ioannina, left his father's house while still young and went to the Holy Mountain, where he was tonsured a monk in the monastery of Docheiariou; that he became an example to all by the holiness of his life, in vigils, fasting, prayer and every labour, and was for that chosen abbot by the whole community with one heart and one mouth. When trouble arose in the monastery through the devil's tempting he left the Mountain with his nephew and went to Veria in Macedonia, where they settled in a monastery dedicated to the Mother of God; and when that community was established in holy living he left it in his nephew's charge and withdrew into the wilderness of Mount Naousa, where he founded a community in the name of the Holy Archangels. He fell asleep in peace in the fifteenth century, and his wonderworking relics rest on Mount Naousa.
