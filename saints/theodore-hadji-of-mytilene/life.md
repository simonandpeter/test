# Theodore the Hadji of Mytilene

In 1785 Theodore of Mytilene, from Pyrgi Thermis on Lesbos, was hanged by the Turks at Mytilene for his faith in Christ; his life stands under 30 January. The Turks threw his body into the sea, but after a few days the sea cast it ashore and the Christians buried it in the chapel of John the Forerunner at the place called Mothonas, where it stayed until 4 September 1967, when his holy relics were found — the synaxarion prints an extract from the record of the finding. The Greek calendar keeps the finding on 4 September.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 4 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2351/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
