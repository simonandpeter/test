# Irenaeus (22 August)

Doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 22 august reads «Sfântul Mucenic Irineu» — the holy martyr Irenaeus — and the page that line links to carries neither a Viață tab nor a Tropar tab. No see, no country, no persecution and no year is printed beside the name.

The corpus already holds three other men of this name — Irenaeus of Lyon, Irenaeus of Rome and Irenaeus of Sirmium — each on his own day and out of his own source, and no claim is made here that this is any of them. Doxologia's own life of Agathonicus and his company, printed for four of the other names on this day, does not name him either. He is entered on the Romanian line alone.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 22 august — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/22-august) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-irineu), which is empty of both life and hymn; read 24 September 2026.*
