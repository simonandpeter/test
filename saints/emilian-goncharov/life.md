# Emilian (Goncharov)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him fifth of six priests of 1937 on 23 октября ст. ст. The line declines his name and gives it without a surname; the title on the name link behind his place supplies both the surname and the grade, «Емилиан (Гончаров), иерей, сщмч.». His is the only forename on the line that is not shared with another of the six or common in the corpus, so his place on it is not in doubt.

The line calls the six of them пресвитеров and the link behind his place says иерей, which is the same grade, so this folder's office and its types say the same thing. Nothing on either page says where he was born or served, what he was charged with, or whether he died with the one of the six whose life the calendar does carry.

The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line. The day page's five troparia and kontakia all belong to the three older entries it heads, and none of them to this company of 1937.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 23 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261023.html); read 4 October 2026.*
