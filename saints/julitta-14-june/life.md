# Julitta (14 June)

saint.gr's calendar for 14 Ιουνίου names her and gives no life. Its whole notice is one sentence: «Η Οσία Ιουλίττα απεβίωσε ειρηνικά. Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο της» — the venerable Julitta died peacefully, and we have no further details of her life. There is no century on the page, no place, no monastery and no hymn.

What it does print is the couplet of the synaxarion: «Tην Iουλίτταν εξάγει του σαρκίου, / O σαρξ δι’ ημάς του Θεού φανείς Λόγος» — the Word of God, who for our sake appeared as flesh, leads Julitta out of the flesh. The Greek calendar keeps two other women of this name on other days, both of them martyrs; this one it calls venerable, and gives her nothing else.
