# Epitacius of Tui

saint.gr says that the holy Epitacius and the holy Basil lived in the first century, and gives each of them a clause. Of him it says: «Ο Άγιος Επιτάκιος θεωρείται ως ο πρώτος Επίσκοπος της πόλεως Τούι της Ισπανικής Γαλικίας» — he is reckoned the first bishop of the city of Tui in Spanish Galicia. Of his fellow it says that he is named in episcopal lists as the second bishop of Braga in Portugal, in the year 60.

The page adds one sentence for the two of them together: «Οι Άγιοι κοιμήθηκαν με ειρήνη» — the saints fell asleep in peace. It gives him no birth, no death year, no companions beyond [Basil](/saints/basil-of-braga), who is named with him in the day's line, and no hymn. Nothing further about him is recorded here.
