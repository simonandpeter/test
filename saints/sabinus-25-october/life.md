# Sabinus (25 October)

saint.gr's calendar for 25 Οκτωβρίου names Sabinus and gives him no life. One sentence is the whole of the narrative: «Ο Άγιος Σαβίνος μαρτύρησε διά πυρός» — he was martyred by fire. The couplet over it tells him to enter the fire and dance there, martyr Sabinus, a divine dance that brings a divine crown.

The page gives him no country, no city, no year, no emperor and no companions; it prints no hymn and no relics. The corpus's other Sabinus folders — of Cyprus, of Hermopolis and the servant of Chryse — are other men on other days.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 25 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/25/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2803/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
