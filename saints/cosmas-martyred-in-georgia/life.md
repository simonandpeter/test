# Cosmas martyred in Georgia

saint.gr's calendar for 17 October keeps «Άγιος Κοσμάς “ὁ ἐν Γεωργίᾳ”», and its whole notice is one sentence, which is as much a warning as an entry: Saint Cosmas the one in Georgia is unknown to the synaxarists, and is mentioned in the Jerusalem Kanonarion in these words — the memory of Cosmas who was martyred in Georgia — for which it refers the reader to Archimandrite Kallistos's edition of that Kanonarion, page 114.

So the calendar gives him a day, a country and a death, and no more: no year, no city within Georgia, no persecutor, no rank beyond the word saint, and no hymn or verse. It does not say he was a Georgian, only that he was martyred there. The same day's other Cosmas, [Cosmas of Arabia](/saints/cosmas-of-arabia), is a separate entry with a page and an account of its own, and is a different man.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 17 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/17/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2543/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
