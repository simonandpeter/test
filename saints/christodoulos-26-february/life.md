# Christodoulos (26 February)

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Φεβρουαρίου names him and gives no life. Its line reads «Άγιος Χριστόδουλος», and the page standing under it says only that the holy Christodoulos was martyred by the sword and that we have no other details of his life. Its couplet makes the name do the work: «Kλήσις διδάσκει τίς συ, και δούλος τίνος, Σπάθη δε Xριστόδουλε, ποίον σοι τέλος» — your calling teaches who you are and whose servant you are, and the sword, Christodoulos, what your end was.

Above that the page prints only its standing gloss on the name, that it is Χριστός and δούλος together, the man given over to Christ, which belongs to the name and not to this saint. No year, no place, no emperor, no relics and no hymn are given for him, and the calendar leaves him without an epithet; what is recorded here is the commemoration saint.gr keeps on this day, and the day is what the display name carries.

The Russian calendar names him with his fellow on the same day and adds the little that saint.gr's page withholds. Its line reads «Мч. Севастиана и Христодула (ок. 66) (Gr. Cal).», marked as a reading taken over from the Greek calendar, and its life is two sentences: the holy martyrs [Sebastian](/saints/sebastian-of-cartagena) and Christodoulos died by the sword under Nero, whose reign it gives as 54–68, and they belonged to the company of the holy martyr [Photini](/saints/photini-the-samaritan), whose memory it keeps on 20 марта. That company is the whole of what is known of him, and the manner of his death — the sword — is what the Greek page gives too.
