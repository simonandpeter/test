# Charis

doxologia.ro's calendar for 28 ianuarie names her and gives no life. The page it links under her name carries one sentence and no more: «Sfintei Muceniţe Haris, i s-au tăiat picioarele şi astfel s-a săvârşit» — the holy martyr Charis had her feet cut off, and so was made perfect. That single line is the whole of the Romanian entry.

It names no persecutor, no place, no year and no companion, and nothing has been supplied here from anywhere else. The name is printed «Haris»; the English form on this page is the reader's choice, and the calendar's own form is kept in the name list.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 28 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/28-ianuarie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfintei-mucenite-haris); read 19 September 2026.*
