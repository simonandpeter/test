# Demetrius (Voskresensky)

The Russian church calendar names him and gives no life. Its list for 9 мая ст. ст. reads «Сщмч. Димитрия пресвитера (1938)» — the hieromartyr Demetrius, a presbyter, with the year of his death — and the name page behind that line carries one thing more than the day gives: his surname. It heads him «Димитрий (Воскресенский), протоиерей, сщмч.», then prints his days, «26 января (Новомуч.), 9 мая», and nothing else at all.

So what the source says of him is this: that his family name was Воскресенский, that he was a priest, that he died in 1938, and that he is numbered among the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church, whose synaxis is the January day. There is no birth year, no place, no diocese, no parish, no account of his arrest or of how he died, and the day page prints no troparion or kontakion under his name.

The two pages differ in one particular, and it is recorded here rather than resolved. The day line calls him «пресвитера», a presbyter; the name page calls him «протоиерей», an archpriest — a grade above. Both readings are kept, and the second is the reason this folder carries no office at all: the word the fuller page uses has no English form in this site’s language packs, and putting the lesser word in its place would quietly throw away what the source says.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 9 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260509.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14459.html), which gives no life; read 8 October 2026.*
