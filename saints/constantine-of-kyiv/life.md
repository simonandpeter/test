# Constantine of Kyiv

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him on 5 июня and gives no life of him. Its line for the day says this much: «Свт. Константина , митр. Киевского и всея России (1159).» — the hierarch Constantine, metropolitan of Kyiv and all Russia, and the year 1159. His name page, 1274, repeats the heading, lists that one day, offers prayers, and carries no reading of his life at all.

What the day page does carry is his service, and the two hymns say more of him than the line does. The troparion calls him a successor of the apostles and a sharer of the hierarchs' throne, and shepherd and teacher of all Russia. The kontakion calls him a star of Russia «от Киева возсиявшую», shining out from Kyiv, which «Чернигов блаженною кончиною своею озарившую» — lit Chernigov by its blessed end. That is a hymn's statement and not a biography, and it is the only thing printed anywhere on the day that places him.

Two things this folder does not have, and both are deliberate. There is no Russian name form: the calendar gives him the bare «Константин» and nothing else, everything beside it being the rank or the see, and that one word already stands for five other men in this corpus whose dossiers nobody has yet set side by side. And there is no office, because the four languages this site reads in have no settled form of his see; it is written here instead.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 5 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260605.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/1274.html); read 8 October 2026.*
