# John the Despot

The Охридски пролог names him in its heading for 10. децембар ст. ст. — „Преподобна Ангелина и свети Јован деспот.“ — and then tells the day as his mother’s life, so that everything the book says of him it says inside her story. He was one of the two sons of [Angelina of Serbia](/saints/angelina-of-serbia) and of Стефан, despot of the Serbs, and she brought him and his brother Максим up, the book says, in a true Christian spirit.

It calls the two brothers «посвећених синова», her consecrated sons, and says that her wonderworking relics rest together with those of her righteous husband and of her sons in the monastery of Крушедол — if the Turks have not destroyed them. The heading’s one word for him is деспот, the rank his father held before him. The book gives him no year of birth, no year of death and no deed of his own, and none is supplied here.

The entry closes with the book’s own cross-reference, «в. 30. јул», see 30 July. That page was not read for this folder and nothing above rests on it.

days.pravoslavie.ru keeps him on the same menologion day as the Ohrid Prologue and gives the fuller line of the two: «Блж. Иоанна (1503) и родителей его блжж. Стефана (1446) и Ангелины, Бранковичей, правителей Сербских.» — the blessed John and his parents the blessed Stephen and Angelina, Brankovići, rulers of Serbia. That parenthesis is the first year any calendar in this folder has given him.

The reading behind the line is of his father’s house rather than of him. [Stephen Branković](/saints/stephen-brankovic) was the middle son of the man then ruling Serbia, gentle in temper and excellently read in Holy Scripture; after the Turks took the country in 1457 he went to their capital after his sister, who had been given to the sultan, and then stood up to defend Serbia when he learnt that the monastery of Mileševa had been burnt. He married Angelina, the daughter of an Albanian prince, and with her and three children had to hide in Albania and then in Italy, where he died. Angelina carried his incorrupt remains to Kupinovo.

The ruler of Serbia at the end of the fifteenth century, the page goes on, was their son, the blessed John; and the incorrupt relics of the righteous John and of his parents were afterwards glorified by many signs. The day page carries a troparion and a kontakion under his name and they are here.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 10. децембар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2025-12-23&prolog=1); read 8 October 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 10 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261210.html) and [the life of him and his parents](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life3098.htm), read 13 October 2026.*
