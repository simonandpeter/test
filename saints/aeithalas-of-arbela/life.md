# Aeithalas of Arbela

saint.gr's calendar for 11 December keeps two martyrs in one entry and names both: Aeithalas and [Akepsees](/saints/akepsees-of-arbela). They came from the city of Arbela in Persia, the page says, and Aeithalas was a bishop and Akepsees a deacon. Their warm and living faith drove them to preach the Gospel daily and to draw souls to Christ the Saviour.

They were denounced to the Persian king; and having stayed unshaken in the Christian faith, after dreadful tortures they were beheaded. The page then adds, out of the Synaxaristes of Saint Nicodemus the Hagiorite, that Aeithalas had first been a priest of the idols, and was catechised in the Christian faith by the bishop of some city who came and was healed of a haemorrhage.

The couplet set over the entry gives the two men a line each other: seeing me suffering by the sword, Akepsees says, be jealous, Aeithalas — I was jealous of you. The page names neither the king nor the year.

days.pravoslavie.ru keeps the two of them on the same menologion day and prints one short reading for the pair, which is the first account either folder has had, and the only place either of them is given a grade. They were Persians, it says. Akepsees was a pagan priest in the city of Arbela; having been healed of a sickness by the prayers of a Christian bishop, he turned to the faith of Christ, confessed it boldly, and was thrown into prison for it. Soon a deacon of the church of Arbela, the holy Aeithalas, was shut in the same prison. The martyrs were brought before the governor, confessed their faith again in front of him, and were beheaded. The Russian line is the only one of the two calendars that gives a year: «(354)».

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/11/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3166/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 11 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261211.html) and [the life of the pair](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life3103.htm), read 13 October 2026.*
