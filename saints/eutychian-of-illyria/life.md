# Eutychian of Illyria

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him among four for 22 декабря ст. ст., «Мчч. Хрисогона, Феодотии, Евода, Евтихиана и иных (ок. 304)», and his name page gives him as «Евтихиан Иллирийский, мч.» — Eutychian of Illyria. The calendar's life for the company brings him in at its end, among the condemned with whom [Anastasia, pupil of Chrysogonus](/saints/anastasia-pupil-of-chrysogonus) was ordered to be drowned, and says he was himself hunted for the Christian faith.

When the ship had put out to sea the soldiers bored holes in it and went over into a boat, leaving the prisoners to sink. The life says that Theodota appeared to them and brought the ship to shore, and that the hundred and twenty who came out of it alive believed in Christ and were baptised by Anastasia and by him. They were all seized soon after and given over to a martyr's death. The calendar sets the company at about 304 and his name page lists this day and no other.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 22 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261222.html) and [the life of the company](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life3165.htm); read 13 October 2026.*
