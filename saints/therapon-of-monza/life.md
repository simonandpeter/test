# Therapon of Monza

saint.gr's calendar for 12 December names the venerable Therapon of Monza and then says in one sentence that it has no details of the Russian monk's life, only that he fell asleep in the year 1597. The page's own head says the same, with a question mark where the year of birth would be. No parentage, no monastery, no manner of death, no relic, no hymn and no synaxaristic couplet is printed.

What the entry gives is the name, the place — which the page writes in Latin letters as Monza beside its Greek — the rank word venerable, and the one year. It is written down here as that, and nothing is added from the other men of this name whom the same calendar keeps on other days.

The Russian church calendar keeps him on 27 мая ст. ст. and gives him two readings, where the source this folder was built from said it had no details of his life at all. Nothing has come down of his origin and his early years. He began his labours at Moscow — the fuller reading adds, with its own «вероятно», that he probably played the fool for Christ there, imitating the blessed Василий of Moscow — and then went on to the Воздвиженский monastery at Kostroma, where he was tonsured and lived thirteen years. He was famous for wonders in his own lifetime.

Once he appeared in a dream to two monks of the Pavlo-Obnorsky monastery, Пафнутий and Андриан, and bade them found a house on the bank of the river Monza, near Soligalich. Fleeing the praise of men he left the Kostroma house and lived for a time as a hermit. When the monastery on the Monza had been built by the elder Андриан, he glorified it with two wonders: appearing in a dream to a fisherman of Soligalich and to a smith of Buy, whose sons were ill, he foretold that their children would be healed in the newly founded monastery of the Annunciation on the Monza — and both the young men, brought there, were healed. The second reading tells it the other way about, making the two monks come to the Monza by his own blessing and settle twenty-five versts from Galich, and him a monk of the house of Адриан; and it adds that every day, with the superior’s blessing, he went away into the forest thicket and prayed there, and that in the nights he read and copied books of profit to the soul.

After a time he came himself to the house of the Annunciation and asked its abbot, the elder Андриан, to be his spiritual father; and at the confession Андриан was astonished at the purity of his soul and the strength of his faith. The two years he spent in the Monza house were a time of great humility, meekness and ascetic labour. The house was always dear to him: he helped its building and its moving to a new place by wonders, defended it from ill-wishers and found it the means of its keeping. Knowing the day of his end beforehand, he took leave of the brethren and died in peace on 12 декабря 1597; before his death he asked earnestly that the holy water of Theophany be given him when he was already dead, and when his will was carried out he swallowed it.

The chronicle of the monastery records many healings at his tomb. Once he saved boats from being lost in the rapids of the river Kostroma, right opposite the monastery, and some saw a comely elder sitting down on the stern of each vessel and steering it through the dangerous place; and appearing in a dream to the abbot he foretold the famine of 1601 and bade him lay up more bread. Twenty years after his death his incorrupt relics were found, with even the vestments whole, and the house on the Monza was named after him the Ferapontova.
