# Paul (Berezin)

Paul Berezin was born in 1866 in the village of Makovnitsy in Kashin district of Tver province, the son of the priest Michael Berezin. He finished seminary, studied in the faculty of history and philology at the University of Warsaw, and taught the Law of God at the Novotorzhok teachers’ seminary; at thirty-seven he was ordained priest, and his parishioners remembered him as an outstanding preacher who preached at every service. He was widowed early.

In the 1920s he served at the church of the Ascension in the village of Kotovo in Molokovo district, and lent religious books to children who asked for them, which the school’s headmistress took from them. In September 1929 he was arrested on the testimony of teachers and village Komsomol members that he had given children the Law of God and Sacred History to read and had told women at confession to keep their daughters from village plays; he denied any agitation, and on 13 January 1930 the OGPU’s Special Council sentenced him to three years’ exile in the Northern region, and he was sent to Mezen in Arkhangelsk region.

His petition for release was granted, and on 18 July 1931 he left Arkhangelsk and returned to serve at the same church in Kotovo, living alone; his son Dmitry, a student at Leningrad University, had all but lost his faith, and wrote to his father in the spring of 1937 about the Bolsheviks’ ideals, while still sending him money. He was arrested on 27 July 1937 and taken to the prison at Krasny Kholm; the charge was that on his return he had gone on with anti-Soviet agitation before the faithful. On 3 October the NKVD troika sentenced him to be shot, and he was shot a few days later, on 7 October 1937. He was glorified by the Jubilee Council of Bishops in August 2000. The long record of the 1929 case, the witnesses’ testimony against the peasants tried with him, was read only in outline.

The Russian calendar keeps him on 24 September old style on one line with [Andrew (Bystrov)](/saints/andrew-bystrov), [Vitaly (Kokorev)](/saints/vitaly-kokorev), [Basil (Vinogradov)](/saints/basil-vinogradov), [Sergius (Mikhailov)](/saints/sergius-mikhailov) and [Spyridon (Savelyev)](/saints/spyridon-savelyev), all shot on 7 October 1937 after sentences of 3 October.
