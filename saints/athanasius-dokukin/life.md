# Athanasius (Dokukin)

He is one of five named together in a single line of the Russian calendar for 26 September: «Сщмч. Афанасия, Александра и Димитрия пресвитеров, мчч. Иоанна и Николая (1937)» — three priests and two laymen, all of 1937. The calendar's index of names gives him as «Афанасий (Докукин), иерей, сщмч.», a priest, and keeps him on this day and on 26 January with the whole company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia.

That is everything the source holds. It prints no place, no see and no account of his death, and the long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which refused this reading on 4 October 2026 — a 403 to the fetcher's user-agent and to a full browser one alike. The four he is named with are [Alexander (Levitsky)](/saints/alexander-levitsky), [Demetrius (Rozanov)](/saints/demetrius-rozanov), [John (Zolotov)](/saints/john-zolotov) and [Nicholas (Gusev)](/saints/nicholas-gusev).
