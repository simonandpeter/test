# Quadratus of Nicomedia

The Ohrid Prologue says this Quadratus was a nobleman and a rich man, and at the same time a convinced and baptised Christian. In the time of the persecution, when Valerian shut many Christians up in prison, Quadratus bribed the jailers and so got in to them, bringing the prisoners provisions of various kinds and strengthening them in the faith.

When they were brought before the judge and asked by him for their names, their country and their estate, they kept silence. Then Quadratus appeared from behind them and cried out with the whole of his voice that they were Christians by name, servants of Jesus Christ the Lord by rank and by thanksgiving, and that their city and their fatherland was heaven. After that declaration he too was put on trial, and after long and heavy torments he was beheaded with the rest.

That is the whole of the entry. It gives no year and no century — only the persecution of Valerian — names no judge, does not say how many the prisoners were or what became of their bodies, and prints no hymn.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 10. март ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-03-23&prolog=1); read 4 October 2026.*
