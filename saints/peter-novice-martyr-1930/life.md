# Peter (Tupitsyn)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him on 14 января ст. ст. in one line with five companions and gives no life for any of the six. The whole of its account is «Прмчч. Раифских: Антония, Варлаама, Иова, Иосифа, Сергия и Петра (1930)» — the venerable martyrs of Raifa: Anthony, Barlaam, Job, Joseph, Sergius and Peter, 1930 — and behind that line the calendar's own name links carry the surnames and the ranks the line leaves out.

His is «Петр (Тупицын), послушник, прмч.»: Peter Tupitsyn, and the calendar makes him a послушник, a novice, where it makes the other five hieromonks. They are [Anthony (Chirkov)](/saints/anthony-hieromonk-martyr-1930), [Barlaam (Pokhilyuk)](/saints/barlaam-hieromonk-martyr-1930), [Job (Protopopov)](/saints/job-hieromonk-martyr-1930), [Joseph (Gavrilov)](/saints/joseph-hieromonk-martyr-1930) and [Sergius (Guskov)](/saints/sergius-hieromonk-martyr-1930). «Раифских» is their house, the Raifa hermitage of the Mother of God near Kazan.

The calendar gives no place of death and no manner of it, and the 1930 in its bracket is the whole of what it dates the six of them by. The day page prints no hymn under their names. That is everything the source says of him.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 14 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260114.html); read 3 October 2026. The calendar prints no life page for him.*
