# Athanasius of Sparte in Attaleia

saint.gr's calendar for 29 Οκτωβρίου names «Άγιος Αθανάσιος ο Ιερομάρτυρας «ἐκ Σπάρτης Ἀτταλίας»», and dates him «(; - 1653)». According to the New Martyrology, the page says, this martyr suffered at Moudania on 29 October 1653, and a canon for him was written by Meletios Syrigos. That is the whole of what it tells of him: no parents, no birthplace beyond the epithet, no account of his arrest and nothing of how he died.

The page then does an unusual thing: it argues with another writer about who he is. Otto Meinardus, it says, gives 7 January as this new martyr's day of memory, and confuses the events of his life with those of [Athanasius of Attaleia](/saints/athanasius-of-attaleia), who suffered in 1700. The corpus keeps that other man with a Greek feast on 7 January and a beheading at Smyrna in 1700. So the two are two men on saint.gr's own insistence, and this folder is the one of 1653 at Moudania.

The page prints no hymn. It carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches both for the entry and for the distinction it draws.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 29 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/29/index.aspx) and [his notice](https://www.saint.gr/2842/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
