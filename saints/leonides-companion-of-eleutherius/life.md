# Leonides, companion of Eleutherius

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives almost no life. He is the second of the two men in the day's line, «Άγιοι Ελευθέριος, Λεωνίδης και τα Άγια Νήπια» — [Eleutherius](/saints/eleutherius-martyr-8-august), Leonides and [the Holy Infants](/saints/holy-infants-with-eleutherius-and-leonides) — and all the page says of their story is two sentences: «Μαρτύρησαν όλοι δια πυρός. Η σύναξη τους γίνεται στην Αγία Ειρήνη στις Ιουστινιαναίς.» They all suffered martyrdom by fire, and their synaxis is kept at the church of Saint Irene in Justinianae.

The distich the page sets above those sentences speaks of two contenders and not of three parties: «Ἀμφοῖν ἀθλητῶν, οὐ θυόντων τῇ πλάνῃ, / Ποινὴν κατακρίνουσιν οἱ πλάνοι φλόγα» — of both contenders, who would not sacrifice to the error, the deceivers decreed the flame as punishment. saint.gr glosses the name Leonides as the lion's child, and it gives no year, no country and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2236/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
