# Cyril of Kazan

saint.gr's calendar for 26 January names Cyril, metropolitan of Kazan, and says in as many words that it has no life to give. The whole of the page's account of him is this: that he bore witness as a martyr in the year 1937, and that «Δεν έχουμε περισσότερες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου» — we have no further details of the saint's life.

So what is recorded here is what the Greek calendar records: the day, the name, the see in his title, the year in which he was killed, and the page's own admission that it knows no more. It gives no year of birth, says nothing of where or how he died, and prints no troparion and no kontakion. The Russian calendar, which keeps the new martyrs of that decade with their own dossiers, is where this entry could be finished, and it has not been read.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1341/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
