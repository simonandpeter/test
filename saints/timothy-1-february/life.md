# Timothy (1 February)

saint.gr's calendar for 1 February names Timothy the confessor and says in as many words that it has no life to give. The whole of what the page says of him is this: that he was a confessor of our faith and fell asleep in peace, and that «Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του» — we have no other details of his life.

So what is recorded here is the day, the name, the rank of confessor, and the page's own admission that it knows no more. There is no homeland, no century, no year of birth or of death, nothing about what he confessed or under whom, and no apolytikion and no kontakion. The corpus already keeps nine other men of the name, and this is none of them; the epithet in brackets is the day, because the source gives nothing else to tell him apart by.

The couplet above the sentence is a play on the name: «Τιμόθεος μετέσχε τιμῆς τῆς ἄνω, / Μὴ τι προκρίνας τῆς Θεοῦ τιμῆς κάτω» — Timothy shared in the honour above, having preferred nothing here below to the honour of God.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/558/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
