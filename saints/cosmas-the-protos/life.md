# Cosmas the Protos of Athos

Cosmas was Protos of the Holy Community of Athos at the time when the supporters of union with the Roman Catholics, following the church policy of the emperor Michael VIII Palaiologos and of the patriarch John Bekkos, pressed the monks of the Holy Mountain to support that union. Like Saint [Euthymius and the twelve monks of Vatopedi](/saints/euthymius-of-vatopedi), and many others, especially of the monasteries of Iviron and Zographou, who suffered persecution and a martyr's death for their opposition to the union, Cosmas was put to death by hanging.

His martyrdom was confirmed by the Holy Community's exhumation of his remains in the year 1981, in the place where tradition held the martyrdom to have happened and where an unsleeping lamp was kept burning. His memory is kept on 5 December. The page gives no year for his birth or for his death, and none is written here.
