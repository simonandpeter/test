# Euphrasia of the Thebaid

saint.gr's calendar for 13 Μαρτίου names her «Οσία Ευφρασία», the venerable Euphrasia, and the page behind that line is one sentence long: that the venerable Euphrasia lived as an ascetic in the Thebaid and fell asleep in peace. There is nothing else on it — no year, no parents, no monastery or laura of hers, no relic, no hymn, and not even a verse, which most of this calendar's entries have.

So what is recorded here is the commemoration, the country of her ascesis and a peaceful end. The corpus keeps two other women of this name, Euphrasia of Ancyra and Euphrasia of Nicomedia, and both of them are martyrs on other days; this one the Greek calendar sets on 13 March and calls venerable, which is the rank of the desert and not of the arena.
