# Philoumenos (29 November)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 29 noiembrie reads «Sfântul Mucenic Filumen» — the holy martyr Philoumenos — and the page that stands under his name has no Viață tab at all. He is not the hieromartyr [Philoumenos of Jacob's Well](/saints/philoumenos-of-jacobs-well), who is named by his own epithet on the same day's list and has a life and a troparion of his own.

The one other thing the site prints under his name is a recording, titled «Troparul Sfinților Mucenici Paramon și Filumen (în limba greacă)» — the troparion of the holy martyrs [Paramon](/saints/paramon) and Philoumenos, in Greek. The page carries no text of that hymn, so no hymn is entered here, and nothing is said of his country, his century or the manner of his death, because the page says nothing of them.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 29 November, and saint.gr gives him a country, a trade, a magistrate and a year. He came of Lycaonia, it says, and he was a merchant in grain. In the year 270 he was denounced as a Christian to Felix, the governor of Ankara; he was arrested and brought before him, but he was not frightened by the threats he met and stayed faithful to the one true faith of Christ. They put him to dreadful tortures, piercing his limbs through with nails, and in that martyr's way he gave up his spirit to the Lord.

His couplet turns his name over as the apolytikion does: they pierce the feet of Philoumenos with nails — of Philoumenos, who loves Christ and is loved by God. The hymn printed on his page is the one that names him beside Paramon, with whom the day keeps him.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 29 noiembrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/29-noiembrie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-filumen); read 25 September 2026; and after saint.gr's calendar for 29 Νοεμβρίου — [the Greek day](https://www.saint.gr/11/29/index.aspx) and [its life](https://www.saint.gr/3120/saint.aspx), read 2 October 2026.*
