# Alexander (Belyakov)

The priest Alexander Nikolayevich Belyakov was born in 1890 in Nizhny Novgorod. He was arrested on 6 August 1937, during the mass repressions against the clergy of Nizhny Novgorod, and charged with belonging to a “counter-revolutionary church group”. He did not admit guilt and refused to give evidence against others, and he was shot on the sentence of 21 August 1937 — the same day, azbyka.ru’s short life says. The Council of Bishops of the Russian Orthodox Church glorified him in 2000. azbyka.ru’s life is drawn from the Orthodox Encyclopedia.

The Russian calendar keeps him on 21 September old style on one line with the priests [Valens (Nikolsky)](/saints/valens-nikolsky), [John (Lazarev)](/saints/john-lazarev), [Andrew (Benediktov)](/saints/andrew-benediktov), [Peter (Sakharovsky)](/saints/peter-sakharovsky) and [John (Nikolsky)](/saints/john-nikolsky), of 1937. azbyka.ru prints a seventh priest, Basil Nosov, on its line for the day, whom the Sretensky day does not name; it keeps him also with the saints of Nizhny Novgorod, and prints no hymns for him.

*After the life printed by the Russian church calendar at azbyka.ru — [the entry](https://azbyka.ru/days/sv-aleksandr-beljakov); the Sretensky calendar (days.pravoslavie.ru), 21 сентября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260921.html); read 17 September 2026.*
