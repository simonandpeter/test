# Malchus

saint.gr's calendar for 24 Νοεμβρίου names «Όσιος Μάλχος» and then says in one sentence that there is nothing more: «Ο Όσιος Μάλχος απεβίωσε ειρηνικά. Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Οσίου» — the venerable Malchus died in peace, and we have no other details of his life. Even the rank word in his line, «Όσιος», venerable, is the only thing in the entry that says what kind of life it was.

What the page does print is the couplet set over the line, and it is the only picture of him there is: the soul-leading minds came flying down together, Malchus, to carry your soul away to the Lord. It gives him no country, no city, no monastery, no century, no year, no companions, no relics and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/24/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2998/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
