# Peter (Belyaev)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names a priest Peter on 4 июня and gives no life of him. Its line for the day is the whole of what it says: «Сщмч. Петра пресвитера (1918).» — the hieromartyr Peter, a presbyter, and the year 1918. So what the calendar tells is that he was a priest, that he was counted among the hieromartyrs, and that he died in 1918.

His own name page on that site, 13722, carries nothing but the heading «Петр (Беляев), иерей, сщмч.», which gives the surname the day's line leaves out and grades him иерей, a priest, and the two days «26 января (Новомуч.) , 4 июня». There is no life page for him on the site, no icon and no article, and no page names a parish, a diocese or a place of any kind.

The day page prints six hymns, for an abbot, a patriarch and two sisters of Bethany, and none under his name. The 26 января commemoration of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia was not read, and nothing of it is in this folder.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 4 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260604.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13722.html); read 8 October 2026.*
