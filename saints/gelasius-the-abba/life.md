# Gelasius the Abba

saint.gr's calendar for 31 December keeps the venerable Gelasius, and its whole account of him is one sentence: «Ο Όσιος Γελάσιος ήταν Αβάς της ερήμου, του οποίου διήγημα περί κλοπής κάποιου βιβλίου βρίσκεται στον Ευεργετινό. Απεβίωσε ειρηνικά» — the venerable Gelasius was an abba of the desert, a story about whom, concerning the theft of a certain book, is in the Evergetinos; he died in peace.

The page does not tell that story, and gives him no century, no country, no desert by name and no hymn. Its couplet plays on his name and says the same thing about him as the sentence does: «O Γελάσιος άχρι και τέλους βίου, / Tον άξιον γέλωτος ην γελών βίον» — Gelasius, right to the end of his life, was laughing at the life that deserves laughter.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 31 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/31/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1506/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
