# Christopher of Adrianople

saint.gr says he came from Adrianople in eastern Thrace and was called Christodoulos in the world. While he was young he was made a Muslim by a trick, by an Armenian who had himself renounced the faith; but he felt the wrong of it, repented and made his confession. At nineteen he came to the monastery of Dionysiou, where he lived four years in obedience and compunction, and there he was given the name Christopher.

With the blessing of his elder Stephen he went back to Adrianople, and there, before the judge, he renounced Mohammedanism and confessed his Christian faith with courage. So on 16 April 1818, the page says, on the Tuesday of Bright Week and at the eighth hour, he was beheaded singing «Χριστός Ανέστη», Christ is risen. The page prints neither couplet nor hymn for him, and it links him to the synaxis of the new martyrs who suffered after the fall of Constantinople.
