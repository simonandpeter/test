# Ardalion (Ponomarev)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Ardalion among the venerable martyrs on 16 July and gives no life. Its line is «Прмч. Ардалиона (1938).» — the venerable martyr Ardalion, 1938 — and the calendar's own name page for him adds the surname and the grade: «Ардалион (Пономарев), архимандрит, прмч.»

That is the whole of the record. He was an archimandrite; he died in 1938, at the height of the terror; the calendar keeps him on this day among the new martyrs of Russia. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 16 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260716.html); read 9 October 2026.*
