# Michael the Presbyter, Hieromartyr (1937, 22 August)

Michael Lyubertsev was born on 21 October 1883 in the town of Ostashkov, the son of a tailor. He finished the church school and from 1901 to 1907 taught at the fourth parish school of his town; choosing a wholly churchly path, he became psalmist at the Transfiguration church of Ostashkov in 1907, and in 1912 was ordained deacon to a church of Staritsa, where he served until 1918, when, returning to Ostashkov, he served at the Znamensky monastery. The Bolsheviks’ assault on the Nilov hermitage, the seizure of church valuables from the churches of Ostashkov — all this passed before him; the Sretensky calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. He was shot in 1937, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 22 August (Julian) with the bishops John and Alexius and their company.
