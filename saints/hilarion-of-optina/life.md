# Hilarion of Optina

Hilarion of Optina was born on 8 September 1805 and fell asleep on 18 September 1873; the Greek synaxarion says it has no further details of his life. The Greek and Romanian calendars keep him on 18 September, the Romanian with the prayers of the elders of Optina and no life; this entry records the lines and nothing more.
