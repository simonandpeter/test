# Hilarion of Optina

Rodion Ponomarev was born on the night of Pascha, 8 to 9 April 1805, at
Kluch, the third son of a tailor, and named for Herodion of the Seventy; he
kept 8 April as his birthday all his life. (The Greek entry prints
8 September for the birth; the Orthodox Church in America’s life is followed
here.) A quiet, clumsy child mocked by other children and treated roughly at
home, he grew inward and thoughtful. He learned his father’s trade, spent a
hard year in Moscow at it, was twice engaged and twice not married, and at
Saratov, a town full of sectarians, fell in with men who argued them back
to the church — which ended in a trial and four years under police watch.
Scripture and the Fathers rekindled an old wish to be a monk, and after
visiting Sarov, Suzdal, Rostov, Tikhvin, Moscow and Pochaev he came in 1839,
at thirty-four, to the gate of Optina.

There the retired igumen Barlaam of Valaam taught him the Jesus Prayer, and
when Macarius became superior of the skete that December Rodion was made his
cell-attendant, and stayed so until the elder’s death in 1860 — confessing
to Macarius, opening his thoughts daily to Leonid, and between times
gardening, baking and keeping bees. Tonsured Hilarion, he was named by
Macarius as one of the two who would succeed him, with Ambrose, and after
1860 he was superior of the skete and confessor of the monastery, hearing
every monk five times a year, then the nuns, then whoever came, and refusing
no one. He gave little of his own opinion, quoting the Fathers or what
Macarius had said, and people found that he practised what he told them.
Two years of illness he bore without asking to be cured, only for patience;
in his last month he could not lie down for the water in his lungs and sat
before a portrait of Macarius, communicating daily. Early on 18 September
1873 he heard the morning rule, received communion, and five hours later
died with his prayer rope in his hands. He was buried beside Macarius. The
Optina elders were glorified for the whole church in August 2000.

The Greek and Romanian calendars keep him on 18 September, the Romanian
with the prayers of the elders of Optina and no life.
