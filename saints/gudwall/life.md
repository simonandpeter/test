# Gudwall

saint.gr's calendar names him and then says, in its only sentence, that it has nothing to tell: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου Gudwall (Γκουντγουώλ)» — we have no details of the life of Saint Gudwall. The day's line adds one word in brackets, «(Ουαλός)», Welsh, and that is the whole of what the Greek calendar keeps of him.

The name is printed in Latin letters on both the day page and the life page, with the Greek rendering «Γκουντγουώλ» in brackets beside it. No century, no see, no monastery, no manner of death and no relics are given, and no hymn is printed; nothing is supplied here, not even a rank.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/484/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
