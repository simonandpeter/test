# Philip of Irap

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on the Julian 14 November and prints a short life of him. He was Theophilus in the world, an orphan who did not remember his parents; at twelve he settled near the monastery of Komel and lived on what he was given. [Cornelius](/saints/cornelius-of-komel), whose memory the calendar notes on 19 мая, received the devout boy into the house, and three years later tonsured him a monk with the name Philip. Meek, humble and hard-working, he was granted the priesthood at the brethren's petition.

A reaching after greater labours moved him away to the country of the White Lake. There, under the protection of prince Andrei Sheleshpansky, who set aside for him land by the river Irap some forty-five versts from Cherepovets, he put up a chapel in the name of the Holy Trinity and built himself a cell. Word of the hermit spread through the whole district and monks began to gather about him, and before long a church of the Holy and Life-giving Trinity stood where the chapel had been. He spent fifteen years in that wilderness and died at the age of forty-five.

His relics were laid under the floor of the Trinity church. Over his tomb hung an icon which a monk named Theodosius painted from a vision, and soon after his death the Krasnoborsk Philippov monastery grew up on the place of his labour. The keeping of his feast was established at the end of the sixteenth century, and the manuscript service to him belongs to the same years. The day page prints a troparion and a kontakion for him, both here; the calendar gives no year of birth, and the forty-five years are not turned into one. No place is recorded in this folder either, the page giving the river and the distance from Cherepovets and no more.
