# Timothy of Konstamonitou

saint.gr numbers him with the monks [Benedict](/saints/benedict-of-konstamonitou), [Synesius](/saints/synesius-of-triglia) and [Paul](/saints/paul-of-ioannina) of the holy monastery of Konstamonitou who were martyred on 12 June, and says plainly that the information it has of him is exceedingly scanty. Its source, which it names, is the monk Dositheos of Konstamonitou's «Νέον Υπόμνημα των νεοφανών Ιερομαρτύρων και Οσιομαρτύρων».

He came, the page says on Dositheos's own wording, from the villages of Veroia — from the district that fell ecclesiastically under the jurisdiction of the metropolitan of Veroia. We know, it goes on, that when Timothy was young he married; and that after his wife's death he came to the Holy Mountain and to the cells of Karyes. He chose the monastery of Konstamonitou, where the coenobitic life of the brotherhood was hard, and there at last he was tonsured a monk and lived in stillness for a considerable time.

When he was seized with the other fathers of the monastery and taken by force to Thessalonica, where he was imprisoned, he was the eldest of them, over sixty years of age. He was tortured, and was martyred under Rubut Pasha, probably in 1822; and so, the page ends, he was counted worthy of the crown of martyrdom. It prints no synaxarion verses for him and no hymn.
