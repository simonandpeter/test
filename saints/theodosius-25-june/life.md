# Theodosius (25 June)

saint.gr's calendar for 25 Ιουνίου names him and gives one sentence of life. Its line reads «Άγιος Θεοδόσιος ο μάρτυρας», and the page behind it says that this saint was martyred in 1419, and that there are no other particulars of his life to be had: «Ο Άγιος Θεοδόσιος μαρτύρησε το 1419 μ.Χ. Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου».

The heading over that sentence reads «Αγιος Θεοδοσιος ο Μαρτυρας (; - 1419)», so the year of death is the one thing the page is sure of and the year of birth is left as a question mark. No country is named, no persecutor, no manner of death and no relics, and the page prints no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 25 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/25/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1984/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
