# Victor (Kiranov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him in a single line for 17 марта ст. ст.: «Сщмч. Виктора пресвитера (1942).» The line declines the forename to «Виктора», gives no surname, no place, no year of birth and no word of narrative; the year it closes with, 1942, is the only year it gives, and the rank word «Сщмч.» that governs the name is the only rank.

Behind that «Виктора» stands the calendar's own name page 9290, which carries the fuller form «Виктор (Киранов), протоиерей, сщмч.» — the surname the day's line leaves out, and an archpriest's grade, a step above the «пресвитера» of the line. That page lists two days: this 17 марта and «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. It carries no life, and neither does the day page.

His is one of the later years this calendar's March gives, 1942, in the middle of the war. azbyka.ru, which has lives for some of the martyrs of these years, was not read for him. So what the calendar prints is the whole of what this folder says: an archpriest of the Russian church of that name, who died a martyr in 1942 and whom this calendar keeps on this day.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 17 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260317.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9290.html), which carries no life; read 7 October 2026.*
