# George of Nicomedia

saint.gr's calendar for 29 December keeps the venerable George, bishop of Nicomedia, a maker of sung canons and troparia. He lived, the page says, in the noisy age that was a great one for Orthodoxy, the time of [Photius](/saints/photius-of-constantinople), patriarch of Constantinople, which it dates 857 to 891, and he kept up a correspondence with him.

What it records of him is his work. He composed two encomia for the feast of the Entry of the Theotokos and set its service to music; he set to music the forefeast canon of the Annunciation, and other canons to the Theotokos besides; and he wrote festal orations, among them ones for the Entry, for the conception of Saint Anna, and on the words «Ειστήκεισαν παρά τω Σταυρώ του Ιησού» — they stood by the Cross of Jesus.

The page then warns the reader about him. Elements of his biography, it says, are confused with those of [George, bishop of Amastris](/saints/george-of-amastris), chiefly as regards the composition of the sung canons — and perhaps, it adds, the opposite happens. It gives no year of his birth or of his death, and no hymn of his own.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 29 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/29/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1061/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
