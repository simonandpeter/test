# Barlaam of Tobolsk

saint.gr's calendar for 27 December keeps Barlaam, archbishop of Tobolsk, and gives no life of him. Its whole account of him is one sentence: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ρώσου Αγίου» — we have no details about the life of this Russian saint. What the page does carry is his name and his rank in its own heading, «Άγιος Βαρλαάμ Αρχιεπίσκοπος Τομπόλσκ», and in that heading's bracket the year of his death, «(; - 1802)», with no year of birth beside it.

It adds two things and no more. It sends the reader on to the synaxis of all the saints of Siberia, «Σύναξη πάντων των εν Σιβηρία Αγίων», among whom it keeps him; and it names its own source for the entry, the Orthodox Church in America's Feasts and Saints. No hymn is printed for him, and no place but the see itself.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 27 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/27/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4244/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
