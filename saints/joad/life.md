# Joad

This saint, whom the lion struck so that he died, when he had rebuked Jeroboam over the heifers, was of the tribe of Judah. God had commanded him to go to Jeroboam and rebuke him, and to eat no bread and drink no water there, but to return from that place quickly. Finding Jeroboam offering sacrifice, he called him and said: «Thus says the Lord: behold, a son is born to the house of Judah, and his name is Josiah, and he shall sacrifice upon you the priests of these high places.»

Jeroboam stretched out his hand to seize him, and his hand withered; but when the prophet prayed, the hand was healed as it had been before. And as he was returning he was deceived by a false prophet, whose name was Emvis, and ate bread with him, breaking the commandment of the Lord. For that disobedience God allowed him to be killed by a lion — but not to be eaten by it; and when he was dead he was buried at Bethel, beside the man who had deceived him.

doxologia.ro gives him no year, and no troparion, and this is the whole of the life it prints.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and under the other of his two names. saint.gr heads him «Προφήτης Ιωήλ» and explains at once: Joel according to 2 Chronicles 9:29, Joad according to some synaxaristae. He came of Judah, and he was sent by God to king Jeroboam to rebuke him for the heifers; when Jeroboam stretched out his hand to seize the prophet the hand withered, and it was restored again by the prophet's prayer. But because the prophet had God's command not to eat and not to drink, and he ate, having been led astray by the false prophet Enbe, he was torn to pieces for his disobedience by a lion — which did not, however, eat his body — and he was buried near the tomb of the false prophet Enbe who had misled him. The page's couplet says the same in two lines: «Λέων πατάσσει σε Προφῆτα Κυρίου, Ὡς παραβάντα τὸν λόγον τοῦ Κυρίου» — a lion strikes you down, prophet of the Lord, for having transgressed the word of the Lord. It ends with a caution worth keeping: the prophet Joel whose memory is kept on 19 October is another man.
