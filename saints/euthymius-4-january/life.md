# Euthymius (4 January)

saint.gr's notice for him is one sentence, and it is about a building rather than a man: in the synaxarists, it says, it is recorded only that the venerable Euthymius — his church, or his tomb — lies near the church of Saint Mocius, whom the page dates to 11 May. Nothing else is said of him: no country, no century, no manner of life, no year.

The page's two-line verse plays on the name, which it glosses as the always cheerful one: cheerful Euthymius, having laboured in ascetic toil, came cheerful to the dwellings of cheerfulness. No hymn is printed and nothing is supplied here.
