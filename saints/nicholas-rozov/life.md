# Nicholas (Rozov)

The Russian calendar prints one line for him and no life: «Св. Николая исп., пресвитера (1941)» — Nicholas the confessor, presbyter, 1941. Its own index of names adds the surname and the rank, «Николай (Розов), протоиерей, исп.», an archpriest, and lists him twice: here on 25 September, and on 26 January with the whole company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia.

That is everything the source holds. He was a parish priest who did not deny the faith and died in 1941, in the middle of the years when that was the usual reason for a Russian priest's name to be written down; neither page says where he served, what was done to him, or where he died. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which refused this reading on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line.

*After the Russian church calendar's [25 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260925.html) and its [index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9379.html); read 4 October 2026.*
