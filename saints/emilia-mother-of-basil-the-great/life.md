# Emilia, mother of Basil the Great

Doxologia.ro's calendar for 1 ianuarie names Emilia beside her son, and the page it gives her is short. It says she is written into the assembly of the saints who shone by a life brought to its perfection, and that she is honoured as a pattern of the Christian mother, full of love and of an unshaken faith. It says too that by God's mercy she carries the name „mamă de sfinți”, mother of saints, because five of her ten children were raised to the honour of sanctity.

The same page adds one thing about her temper rather than her household. In all her troubles, and in her many straits, it says, Emilia showed herself a brave woman and a true heroine, and grieving mothers should follow her so as not to despair in their own trouble but to fight always with God's help, and so overcome their grief. Beyond that doxologia.ro gives her no life. It names no year, no place and no death; it keeps her on 1 January and again on 8 May.

Her son [Basil the Great](/saints/basil-the-great) stands above her on the same day's list, and the line the calendar prints for her names her by him: «Sfânta Emilia, mama Sfântului Ierarh Vasile cel Mare».

The Serbian church keeps her on the Julian 8 May, which is the second of the two days doxologia prints her on, and the Ohrid Prologue of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović gives her the five sentences that are the whole of her life in this book. She was the mother of [Basil the Great](/saints/basil-the-great). In her youth she wished to remain a virgin her whole life long, but was forced into marriage. She bore nine children, and so breathed the spirit of Christ into them that five of the nine became saints of the Church — her son Basil and four others, whom this book names and whom this corpus keeps under their own days.

In her old age she built a monastery, where she lived with her daughter [Macrina](/saints/macrina-sister-of-basil-the-great), and where she fell asleep in the Lord **on the eighth of May in the year 375**. That year is the first this folder has carried; doxologia gave her no year, no place and no death at all.

**The two books do not agree about how many children there were**, and neither reading was chosen: this one says nine, where doxologia's page says that five of her **ten** were raised to the honour of sanctity. Both stand as they were printed.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 1 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/1-ianuarie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/sfanta-emilia-mama-sfantului-ierarh-vasile-cel-mare); read 19 September 2026; and the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 8. мај ст. ст. — [the Serbian day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-05-21&prolog=1), read 5 October 2026.*
