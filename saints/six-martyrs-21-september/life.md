# The Six Martyrs (21 September)

Six martyrs, shield-bearers of the emperor Maximian, died by the sword. That is the whole of the Greek notice, with the year 298; its verse sets the head God bowed upon the Cross beside the six heads bowed to the sword.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 21 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/1005/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
