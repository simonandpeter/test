# Leucius of Volokolamsk

saint.gr's line for the day calls him «Όσιος Λεύκιος εκ Ρωσίας», the venerable Leucius from Russia, and its heading prints «(; - 1492)». Its whole account is one sentence: he was the founder and abbot of the monastery of the Dormition of the Mother of God at Volokolamsk in Russia, near the river Ruza, and he fell asleep in holiness and in peace in the year 1492. The page glosses the name from the Latin lux, lucis, light — ο φωτεινός, ο λαμπρός, the shining one, the bright one.

The other thing the calendar says of him is on somebody else's page. [Daniel of Pereslavl](/saints/daniel-of-pereslavl), whom saint.gr keeps on this same 7 April, is said there to have had his spiritual life guided by «ο Άγιος Λεύκιος του Βολοκολάμσκ που τιμάται την ίδια ημέρα» — Leucius of Volokolamsk, honoured on the same day. Nothing else is told: no birth year, no country he came from, no teacher of his own, no relic, no couplet and no hymn.
