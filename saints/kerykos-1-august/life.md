# Kerykos (1 August)

saint.gr's calendar for 1 Αυγούστου names him and gives no life. Its whole notice of him is one sentence: «Ο Άγιος Κήρυκος μαρτύρησε δια αποκεφαλισμού» — Saint Kerykos was martyred by beheading. There is no country, no century, no emperor, no judge and no hymn. What the Greek calendar keeps of him is therefore a name, a day and a manner of death, and nothing else at all.

Besides that the page prints the gloss on the name — ΚΗΡΥΚΟΣ, from κήρυξ, the herald — and the couplet of the synaxarion, which is the only other thing it says of him: «Τοῦ δημίου φήσαντος· Οὐ τμηθῇς θύων. / Κλίνας κάραν Κήρυκος, εἶπεν· Οὐ θύω» — when the executioner said, you shall not be cut down if you sacrifice, Kerykos bent his head and said, I do not sacrifice.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2184/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
