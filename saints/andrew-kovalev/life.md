# Andrew (Kovalev)

Andrew Kovalev was born on 12 August 1871 in the stanitsa of Korenovskaya in
the Kuban. He served at the church of Peter and Paul at Platnirovskaya, the
Ascension church at Kushchevskaya in the Kuban region, the church of Cosmas
and Damian at Blagodarnoye and the Kazan church at Udelnoye in Stavropol
province, taught in the parish schools and colleges, had a saving influence
on people of other faiths, and brought a family of five into Orthodoxy from
the schism of the Austrian hierarchy; after 1917, as the working churches
dwindled catastrophically, he was moved to Krasnodar. He was married and had
two children. He was arrested on 21 September 1921 on the charge of setting
fire to the candle works and agitating against the Soviet power; the
investigation could not prove it, and the sentence read: taking into account
the alarming moment in connexion with the bands’ attack on the stanitsa of
Dinskaya and their supposed intention to attack Krasnodar, and to maintain
the general political situation in the town, as an element hostile to the
Soviet power — to be shot. It was carried out at one in the morning of 28
September 1921, and [Gregory Konokotin](/saints/gregory-konokotin), [Gregory
Troitsky](/saints/gregory-troitsky) and [John
Yakovlev](/saints/john-yakovlev) died the same night. He is kept on 15
September of the old style, 28 September, and in the synaxis of the saints
of the Kuban metropolia.
