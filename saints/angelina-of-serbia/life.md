# Angelina of Serbia

The Охридски пролог gives her in one paragraph, and the greater part of it is her family. She was the daughter of Ђорђе Скендербег, prince of the Albanians, and the wife of Стефан, despot of the Serbs, who was the son of Ђурађ. She bore her husband’s banishment with him and shared every bitterness of his life, in Serbia and in Albania and in Italy alike, and none of the three is a place the book sets her down in for long.

She brought up her two sons, Максим and [John](/saints/john-the-despot), in a true Christian spirit. After her husband’s death she was tonsured and gave herself wholly to prayer, to works of mercy, and to the building and the repairing of churches. A faithful wife, an excellent mother and a perfect Christian, she earned in truth the name „Мајка Ангелина“ — Mother Angelina — by which the people call her still, and the book says so in the present tense.

Her wonderworking relics rest together with those of her righteous husband and of her consecrated sons in the monastery of Крушедол — and the book adds, in a parenthesis of its own, «уколико нису од Турака уништене», if the Turks have not destroyed them. She reposed at the beginning of the sixteenth century and passed into the kingdom that does not die. The entry closes with the book’s own cross-reference, «в. 30. јул», see 30 July; that page was not read here, and nothing above rests on it.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 10. децембар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2025-12-23&prolog=1); read 8 October 2026.*
