# Menas of Polotsk

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 20 июня ст. ст. names him «Свт. Мины , еп. Полоцкого (1116).» and gives him a life page of five sentences, which is the whole of what this folder holds. He laboured a long while in the Kiev Caves monastery, it says, and on 13 December 1105 he was consecrated bishop of Polotsk.

Because he had been under obedience in that house before he was raised to the episcopate, his name entered the service of the holy fathers of the Kiev Caves, and a mention of him is kept in the Kiev Caves Paterikon. He is known, the page says, as one of the first Russian chief shepherds to carry on the spreading of the faith of Christ after the baptism of the land.

That is all. There is no birthplace, no account of his years as bishop, no manner of death and no year of death on the life page: the 1116 in this folder is the year the day line prints beside his name, and the only date the life page gives is the day of his consecration. The corpus holds eight other folders of this name and not one of them is a bishop of Polotsk. His page lists two further days, both 8 июня and one of them marked Belarusian, which the 20 июня day page does not repeat; he renders on this day alone.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 20 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260620.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1362.htm); read 9 October 2026.*
