# Nicholas (Pongelsky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Nicholas among the confessors on 24 July and gives no life. Its line is «Свв. Николая (1942) и Иоанна (1951) испп., пресвитеров.» — the holy confessors and presbyters Nicholas, 1942, and John, 1951 — and the calendar's own name page for him adds the surname: «Николай (Понгельский), иерей, сщмч.», Nicholas Pongelsky, priest.

He stands first on the line, before [John (Kalinin)](/saints/john-kalinin), the priest with whom he shares the entry; the two died nine years apart and the calendar keeps them together. The surnames are not on the line at all, but on the two numbered name pages, which is how it is known that they are two men and not one.

The line and his own name page do not agree about what kind of saint he is: the line calls him a confessor, his name page a hieromartyr. This folder writes the line's reading and records the other, and nothing here resolves it. He was a priest; he died in 1942. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 24 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260724.html); read 13 October 2026.*
