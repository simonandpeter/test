# Castulus (24 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 24 December names Castulus the martyr, and says first where his memory comes from: he is unknown to the Synaxaristes of Saint Nikodemos and to the printed Menaia, and is mentioned in the Parisian Codex 1621 with a brief biographical note. According to that note, the page says, this saint was martyred in the years of the king Licinius, whose reign it dates 307 to 323, having been denounced to him as a Christian.

When they had seized him they hung him up and flayed the skin off him. After that they handed him over to the governor Zelikinthios; and because this one too failed to make the martyr change his faith, he tortured him hard and in the end beheaded him. That is the whole of the entry: no city, no year of birth, and no hymn. He is not the Castulus the same calendar keeps on 12 January with Zoticus, Rogatus and Modestus, whose name it writes Καστούλιος. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence by which saint.gr marks an entry it will not vouch for.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/24/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3232/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
