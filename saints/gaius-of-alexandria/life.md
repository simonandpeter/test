# Gaius of Alexandria

saint.gr's calendar for 28 Φεβρουαρίου names four martyrs in one line — «Άγιοι Καιράλιος, Πουπούλιος, Γάιος και Σεραπίων οι Μάρτυρες» — and the page standing under it gives one sentence: «Οι Άγιοι αυτοί Μάρτυρες, μαρτύρησαν στην Αλεξάνδρεια» — these holy martyrs were martyred at Alexandria. Because the line names each of the four, each is kept here: Gaius, with [Cerealis](/saints/cerealis-of-alexandria), [Pupulus](/saints/pupulus-of-alexandria) and [Serapion](/saints/serapion-of-alexandria).

That is the whole of the entry. The page prints the four names again above the sentence, each with its own rank word, and gives them no account of their suffering, no manner of death, no governor, no relics, no couplet and no hymn; Alexandria is the only thing in it besides the names. The epithet in this folder's name is that place, because the calendar gives these men nothing else to tell them apart from the others of their names.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/28/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1937/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
