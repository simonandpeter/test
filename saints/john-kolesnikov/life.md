# John (Kolesnikov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 4 апреля ст. ст. in four words, «Мч. Иоанна (1943).», and the name link behind the line, page 14242, gives his surname: «Иоанн (Колесников), мч.» The calendar has no life for him and azbyka.ru has one. He is a layman, and the only one on this day.

He was born in 1877 in the village Крылатка of the Глазовская volost, Можайский uyezd, Moscow province, the son of the peasant Михаил Колесников. In 1910 Иван Михайлович set up a wheelwright’s workshop and worked in it with his own sons, taking on nobody from outside; it lasted until 1930. He had been brought up in piety from his youth and sang in the choir of the church of the Nativity of the Mother of God in the village Поречье, and in 1934 he was elected a member of the parish council and chairman of its auditing commission.

In 1937 the priest of Поречье was arrested and the parishioners were left without services. The parish resolved to send him to Moscow, to the patriarchate, to ask for a priest; he came home with one, and to the satisfaction of all the faithful the services began again. It did not last long. On 5 February 1938 the priest and the deacon were arrested, and on 1 April he was arrested too, with the nuns who helped in the church. He was shut in the Mozhaisk prison and questioned the same day.

Two false witnesses had been found for the case — one of whom, azbyka says, was regularly used for such cases, while the investigator talked the other into it. Told that witnesses had established he belonged to a group of churchmen and spread slander against the party and the government, he answered: «В группировке церковников я не состоял и контрреволюционной клеветы не распространял.» Brought face to face with them, he said that he had never met the one in a way that would let them talk at all, and that the other was telling an obvious untruth, and he denied the whole of the testimony.

On 7 June 1938 an NKVD troika gave him ten years in a corrective labour camp and he was sent to Magadan; on 4 April 1939 he was moved to a camp in the Kemerovo region. He sent the authorities a complaint that he had been convicted wrongly on slander, the case was looked at again, and in January 1940 the prosecutor proposed to drop it for want of proof; in May 1940 the Moscow regional NKVD asked for his ten years to be cut to three. Nothing came of either.

He died on 17 April 1943 in the second camp point of the Taiga section of Siblag in the Kemerovo region, and was buried in the camp cemetery in grave number Е-31. 17 April 1943 is the civil date of the Julian 4 April, so the calendar keeps him on the day he died; the line closes «(1943)». azbyka lists three further days beside this one, the moveable synaxis of the New Martyrs on 7 February, the moveable synaxis of the Kuzbass saints on 29 August and the moveable synaxis of the Moscow saints on 5 September; all three are synaxes and none is written into the feast. Neither book prints a hymn under his name.
