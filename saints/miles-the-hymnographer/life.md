# Miles the Hymnographer

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life. Its whole account of him is one sentence — «Ο Όσιος Μήλης (ή Μήλος ή Μαλλός) απεβίωσε ειρηνικά.» — the venerable Miles, or Milos, or Mallos, died in peace. The day's listing calls him ο Υμνωδός, the hymn-writer, and the couplet printed above that sentence says it is only right to hymn in words Miles the hymn-maker, now that he has gone out of this life: «Eκστάντα Mήλην υμνοποιόν εκ βίου, / Yμνείν λόγοις δίκαιον ως εμός λόγος».

That is all there is. The page gives no century, no country, no monastery and no see; it names no hymn of his that has come down, and it prints neither apolytikion nor kontakion for him. The three forms of the name stand in its own parenthesis and are kept here as it has them, without one being preferred to the others.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1529/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
