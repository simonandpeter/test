# Euthymius (Goryachev), Archpriest, Hieromartyr (1937)

Euthymius Nikitich Goryachev was born in the village of Nikolskoye-Barnuki in the Gorodishche district of Penza province on 19 January 1884 to a poor peasant family. He was four when his father died, and soon his mother died too; the only boy, he was left in his sisters’ care, and his uncle Alexis Goryachev and his wife Alexandra, his godmother, who had four sons of their own, gladly took him into their house — a God-fearing family, and Alexandra, even among her Orthodox neighbours, marked by deep religion and judgement, so that the local priest would send parishioners in family difficulties to her — the calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. He became a priest and served in the villages of the Krasnoyarsk country; in the years of persecution he stood firm against the Renovationists, for which he was arrested repeatedly and spent three years in a camp, and after his church was closed secretly celebrated the services at home. In 1936 he was arrested again for anti-Soviet agitation and sent to the Karaganda camps, where he was shot on 15 September 1937; he was numbered among the saints in 2000, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 2 September (Julian).
