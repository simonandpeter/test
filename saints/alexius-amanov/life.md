# Alexius (Amanov)

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him fifth of the fifteen and gives no life; the life here is azbyka.ru’s for the civil 3 декабря, out of fond.ru. He was born on 9 February 1870 in the town of Касимов in the province of Ryazan, son of a psalm-reader, Иоанн Аманов, three of whose sons became priests. He studied two years at the Ryazan seminary, and after his marriage to Серафима Андреевна Анохина he was ordained priest.

From 1914 to 1927 he served in the village of Балыки in the Pochep district of the Bryansk region, and at the very beginning of the twenties a thing happened there. Several horsemen rode into his yard and came into the house, and one of them told him in conversation not to go anywhere, that they would be back in an hour and would shoot him publicly; then they rode off. He went out into the garden and began to pray. Word of it ran round the village and the people began to gather at the priest’s house to defend him, which they managed to do.

In 1927 he transferred to the Moscow diocese and, because his wife’s sisters lived at Зарайск, asked to serve in that town; he was made rector of the Nikolsky church of the village of Маслово, where he served until 1930, then on 1 December 1930 moved to the Saviour church of Зарайск, on 24 November 1931 was raised archpriest, and in 1935 was made rector of the Elias church of the same town, where he served until his arrest. His wife was the choir director. The Elias church was small and stood on the edge of the town, its income was slight, and with God’s help the taxes the godless power laid on it were paid on time; the priest’s grandchildren often went round the church with a plate collecting the alms of the worshippers.

At times the family went hungry. The elder grandchildren, Василий at ten and Зоя at seven, wandered the thawed potato fields in spring looking in the wet clinging earth for last year’s rotten potatoes, and ate with great pleasure the pancakes their grandmother made of what they brought. In winter there was not enough hay to feed the goat, and the two went with sacks to the market square on market day to gather what they could; seeing them off, he warned them: «Из-под морды лошади сено не сметь брать, иначе хозяин ударит вас кнутом» — do not dare take hay from under a horse’s muzzle, or the owner will strike you with his whip.

He had no horse and went on foot to every service in any weather, and in Paschal week spent several days visiting the parishioners’ houses. At Балыки a young colt had struck him on the knee with its forehoof and broken the joint, and in the early thirties he fell ill with glaucoma and could see almost nothing with his left eye. In 1930 his household was registered as a kulak one; several men came to the house at night with a paper, and began carrying the things out, and when his granddaughter woke at the noise and her grandmother asked them to leave the little feather mattress the child was lying on, one of the women pulled it out from under her and carried it away. People came often to ask his advice in one affair or another — how to draw up a letter to a court, how to act, whom to turn to — and he helped as he could and did not leave them in trouble, and the people respected him much for it.

In the middle of November 1937 the investigator of the Zaraysk section of the NKVD questioned two people who agreed to bear false witness against him; one of them had him saying that the arrests had come back, that the communists were afraid of not being elected under the new constitution and laid the whole blame on the clergy, and that «все равно, сколько они ни арестовывай, нас много». He was arrested at his house on 24 November 1937, and taking leave of his wife said: «Спасибо тебе, верной ты была мне женой! Пойду я по стопам Спасителя» — thank you, you were a faithful wife to me; I shall go in the Saviour’s footsteps. He was questioned the same day and answered each of the four charges «нет, я это отрицаю».

From his arrest and through the whole short investigation he was held in the prison of the Kolomna district and then moved to the Taganka prison in Moscow. On 29 November 1937 the troika of the NKVD sentenced him to be shot, and he was shot on 3 December 1937 at the Butovo range near Moscow and buried in a common unmarked grave. **A few hours before his death the photographer of the Taganka prison photographed him**, so that among the many shootings being carried out in those days the executioner should not make a mistake.
