# Romanus (16 March)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 16 martie reads «Sfântul Mucenic Roman», and the page it links to carries neither a Viață tab nor a Tropar tab: there is the heading, the day, and nothing further. What the line says is that he was a martyr, and that the Romanian church keeps him on this day; that is the whole of what has been read here.

No year, no country, no account of his suffering and no hymn has been taken from anywhere else, and none has been supplied from memory. Several martyrs of this name are kept on other days and in other calendars, and nothing on this page identifies him with any of them. If a life for him is printed elsewhere, it has not been read, and this page will say only what doxologia says until it has been.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and adds two things to the bare Romanian line. The first is an epithet: it names him «Άγιος Ρωμανός ο ἐν τῷ Παρίῳ», Romanus in Parion. The second is the manner of his death, in one sentence — «Ο Άγιος Ρωμανός μαρτύρησε δια ξίφους», he was martyred by the sword. Beside it the page keeps two lines of verse which say that his love for the Saviour showed itself in his contests, as he rushed towards the sword. It gives no year, no persecutor and no hymn.
