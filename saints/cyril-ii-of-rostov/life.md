# Cyril II of Rostov

Cyril shepherded the diocese of Rostov in a God-loving way, saint.gr says, in the years 1231 to 1262. He was elected bishop while he was abbot of the monastery of the Nativity of the Mother of God at Volodymyr. He was marked out for his ascetic life and for the gift of preaching, and crowds of people came running from Rostov and the districts around it to hear him speak and to be helped in their souls; and the page records one convert by name, the prince Peter Ordynsk, who was baptised a Christian through him.

He also wrote theological works, and the page names two subjects of them: the fear of God, and the power and providence of God. He fell asleep in peace in 1262. The page gives him no birthplace, no year of birth and no relics, and prints neither a synaxarion couplet nor a hymn.
