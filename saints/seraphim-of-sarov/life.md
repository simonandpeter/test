# Seraphim of Sarov

Seraphim rose over the Russian land, as the life doxologia.ro prints has it, on 19 July 1759, in the years when the spirit of the so-called Enlightenment was moving through Europe and Russia. He was the son of devout merchants of the city of Kursk, grew up in humility and in love for the Church, and while still a child was healed in a wonderful way by the mercy of the Mother of God. At seventeen he left the world with his mother's blessing and entered the monastery of Sarov.

There he became quickly a model of obedience, taking on gladly even the most wearying work for the good of the brethren, fasting to break the body's promptings, and holding his mind on God day and night with the Prayer of Jesus. When he fell gravely ill he refused the doctors and asked for the one remedy proper to those who have left everything, Communion; and as he was thought to be dying the Most Holy Mother appeared to him in a great light with the Apostles Peter and John the Theologian, pointed to the young novice and said: „Acesta este din neamul nostru!” — this one is of our kindred. He recovered, and built an infirmary on the place of the apparition.

After eight years as a brother in the monastery he was tonsured with the name Seraphim, which the life glosses as the burning one, and ordained deacon; he would spend the whole night in prayer before serving the Liturgy. Not long after his ordination, and after his spiritual father's death, he was allowed to withdraw into the depth of the forest six or seven kilometres from the monastery, where he made himself a wooden hut with a small garden on a hill that he called the Holy Mountain, thinking of Athos.

From 1807 to 1810 his infirmity brought him to the struggle of silence in complete solitude; in 1826 the Mother of God told him the time had come to leave it, and he began to give others the fruit of what he had learned. He was given the gift of prophecy, which out of humility he hid. At seventy, on 1 January 1833, after receiving Communion he bowed before every icon in the church and lit a candle before each, and blessed the brethren, saying: „Lucrați pentru mântuirea voastră; vegheați! Cununile vă sunt pregătite”. That same night, kneeling in his cell and singing the hymns of the Resurrection, he gave up his spirit. He was canonised on 19 July 1903.

The Romanian calendar keeps a second day for him on 19 iulie, the finding of his relics; its page for that day prints no life, only the troparion recorded here.
