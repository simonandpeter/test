# Bassus, companion of Eusebius

Bassus suffered with [Eusebius](/saints/eusebius-companion-of-bassus) in the days of the emperor Diocletian. The two are given separate lines in doxologia.ro's calendar for 20 ianuarie and one life between them: they were men of great wealth and members of the senate, who believed in Christ and were baptised when they saw the bishop Theopemptus bearing torments and working miracles by the power of Christ. The page names no city and gives no year.

Brought before the emperor, they had their belts of office stripped from them first, and then were given every kind of torment unto death. Bassus was put into a pit up to the waist, his hands were cut off, and his whole body was butchered until he gave up his spirit; Eusebius was hanged head downwards and cut in pieces with an axe; a third of their number, Eutychius, was stretched on four stakes until he was torn apart. That is the whole of what the Romanian page prints of him.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, first of the four, with [Eusebius](/saints/eusebius-companion-of-bassus), [Eutychius](/saints/eutychius-companion-of-bassus) and [Basilides](/saints/basilides-companion-of-bassus). saint.gr gives each of the four an epigram and one sentence on the manner of his death. Of Bassus it says that the hands of the executioners cut off his hands, and the epigram turns on it: «Χεῖρας Βάσσου τέμνουσι χεῖρες δημίων, Χεῖρες βέβηλοι, χεῖρας ἡγιασμένας» — the hands of executioners cut Bassus's hands, profane hands cutting hands made holy. Of the others it says that Eusebius was hung by the head and his body cut in pieces with axes, that Eutychius was set between four stakes and parted, and that Basilides had his belly torn open; and so, it ends, these holy martyrs received the crown of witness.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 20 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/20-ianuarie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-mucenic-vas); read 19 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 20 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/20/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3323/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 20 января ст. ст. — [the Russian day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260120.html), read 3 October 2026, which keeps the four of them as «Мчч. Васса, Евсевия, Евтихия и Василида (303)» and names each of them Nicomedian.*
