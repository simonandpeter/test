# Antiochus the Physician

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its page for him carries the feast line 16 Iulie and nothing else — no Viață tab and no Tropar tab — so all that is recorded here is what the calendar's own line for the day says: «Sfântul Mucenic Antioh, doctorul din Antiohia», the holy martyr Antiochus, the physician of Antioch.

That line gives his trade and his city and nothing more. No year, no emperor, no account of his passion and no hymn were found on the Romanian pages read for this day, and nothing has been supplied from elsewhere. The Russian and Serbian calendars have not been checked for him.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 16 July, and saint.gr gives him the life doxologia withheld. It makes him the brother of [Plato](/saints/plato-of-ancyra), whom it keeps on 18 November, and says he came from Sebastia in Cappadocia and was a physician — which is not quite the Romanian line, that called him the physician of Antioch, and the two readings are left standing side by side.

He went from town to town and village to village, healing the sick not in body only but in soul. The governor Hadrian arrested him and put him through a series of dreadful torments: he was hung on a frame and his sides torn, left in the fire, thrown into a cauldron of boiling oil, and then set before wild beasts. From all of it he came out unharmed by the grace of God; he broke the idols by his prayer, and received the crown of martyrdom by beheading. The executioner, [Cyriacus](/saints/cyriacus-the-executioner), saw milk run from his neck instead of blood, confessed Christ there and then, and was beheaded on the spot.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 16 iulie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/16-iulie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-antioh-doctorul-din-antiohia), which prints only the day; read 24 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 16 Ιουλίου — [the Greek day](https://www.saint.gr/07/16/index.aspx) and [the Greek life](https://www.saint.gr/2100/saint.aspx), read 1 October 2026.*
