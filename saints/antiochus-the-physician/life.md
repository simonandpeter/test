# Antiochus the Physician

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its page for him carries the feast line 16 Iulie and nothing else — no Viață tab and no Tropar tab — so all that is recorded here is what the calendar's own line for the day says: «Sfântul Mucenic Antioh, doctorul din Antiohia», the holy martyr Antiochus, the physician of Antioch.

That line gives his trade and his city and nothing more. No year, no emperor, no account of his passion and no hymn were found on the Romanian pages read for this day, and nothing has been supplied from elsewhere. The other calendars have not been checked for him.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 16 iulie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/16-iulie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-antioh-doctorul-din-antiohia), which prints only the day; read 24 September 2026.*
