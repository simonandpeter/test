# Bassa of Tyre

The Greek calendar knows Bassa from a single line of the Sirmond synaxarion, as Delehaye edited it: on the same day, the holy martyr Bassa of Tyre. It records no other detail of her life.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 21 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/1004/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
