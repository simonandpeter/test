# The Nine Martyrs of Georgia

saint.gr's entry for the Nine Martyrs of Georgia is a single sentence, and the life says exactly what it says: these nine holy martyrs were martyred in the village of Marabda in eastern Georgia, in the region of Kakheti, in the year 1625.

Because the page names none of the nine, they are kept here as one entry and not as nine folders: a number is what the calendar prints, and a number is what this folder holds. There is no manner of death, no persecutor, no army and no occasion; the site does not say whether they died together or separately, whether they were clergy, monks or laymen, or what became of their bodies. It names no relic, prints no distich and gives no hymn, and it does not even carry over them the etymology block it sets over almost every named entry of this day.

Marabda, the page's Μαραμπντά, is the village it puts them in, and the region it names beside it is Kakheti; nothing further about either place is claimed here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/03/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1734/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
