# Zacharias of Prusa

saint.gr says he was born at Prusa of devout parents and became a priest. One day he got drunk, and under the power of the drink he denied Christ, in 1801. When he came to himself out of that delusion he presented himself before the judge, threw to the ground the turban he was wearing, and confessed his faith in Christ; and the judge ordered him shut up in prison at once. There he prayed without ceasing, asking for strength and help from on high.

When they brought him before the judge and the aghas the hieromartyr stood unshaken in his faith with a spiritual courage, and for that they threw him back into prison, where they tortured him without pity, beat him, and put a red-hot copper cap on his head. After those torments they pierced the nails of his feet and hands with sharpened reeds, and tore the nails out.

He was led before the judge again, confessed his faith with great boldness and reproached the religion of Mohammed. When the sentence of beheading had been given he was shut up in prison once more, in case the condemnation and the fear of death should frighten him into denying his faith; and when he was brought to the tribunal a third time he declared with courage that his faith would not be moved. So he was beheaded in 1802, at the age of thirty-eight, and received the crown of martyrdom, confessing our Lord Jesus Christ who redeemed the world upon the Cross.
