# Valeria (23 July)

doxologia.ro's calendar names her and gives no life. Its line for 23 iulie is «Sfânta Muceniţă Valeria» — the holy martyr Valeria — and her own page on the site carries the day, the epistle and the gospel appointed for it and nothing else: no Viață tab, no troparion, no country, no century and no account of her martyrdom. What the calendar says of her is that she is a martyr and that the Romanian church keeps her memory on the twenty-third of July.

Nothing further is set down here, because nothing further was read. Where a fuller synaxarion is opened one day, this folder is the place for it.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 23 iulie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/23-iulie) and [her page](https://doxologia.ro/sfanta-mucenita-valeria), which prints no life; read 24 September 2026.*
