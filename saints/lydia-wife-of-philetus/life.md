# Lydia, wife of Philetus

Lydia suffered and received the martyr's crown together with her husband, [Philetus](/saints/philetus-the-senator) of the senate, and with their four sons: Macedon, Theoprepius, Cronides the commentarisius and Amphilochius the duke. This was in the time of the emperor Hadrian, and it came upon them for their faith in Christ and because they worshipped God without ceasing.

They were tied to a pillar and beaten with swords and then thrown into prison — Lydia and the first three sons joining Philetus on the road to it, after he had been brought before the emperor alone. Afterwards they were put to terrible torments, being thrown into great vessels of boiling oil mixed with resin; but by the power of God they were left unharmed. Seeing this, the emperor was ashamed; and the saints, after some time which they spent in prayer, departed this life.

doxologia.ro's calendar gives them no year, and no troparion, and says only that they suffered under Hadrian.
