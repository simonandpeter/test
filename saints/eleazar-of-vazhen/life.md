# Eleazar of Vazhen

saint.gr's calendar for 17 May names him and gives him two sentences. The holy martyr Eleazar, or Lazarus, lived in the eighteenth century in Russia and came from the village of Vazhen. He was martyred for the faith of Christ near the region of Olonets.

That is the whole of the entry. It gives no year within the century, no parents, no trade, no judge, no manner of death, no relics and no hymn, and it does not say which of the two names was his own and which the other tradition's. The Greek calendar keeps a Russian martyr of that village on 17 May, and this folder carries the line and the two sentences behind it.
