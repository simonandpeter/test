# Vladimir (Alekseev)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him among the hieromartyrs on 16 декабря and gives no life. Its line is «Сщмч. Владимира (1918).» — the hieromartyr Vladimir, 1918 — and the calendar’s own name page adds the surname and the grade the line leaves out: «Владимир (Алексеев), иерей, сщмч.»

That is the whole of the record. He was a priest; he died in 1918, the first year of the persecution the Russian church counts its new martyrs from; and the calendar keeps him on this day on a line of his own, joined to nobody. The seven hieromartyrs the next line of the same day names died nineteen years later, and nothing read here connects him to them. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 16 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261216.html); read 13 October 2026.*
