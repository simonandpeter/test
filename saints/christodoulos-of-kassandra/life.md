# Christodoulos of Kassandra

saint.gr says the new martyr Christodoulos was born in a village of Kassandra called Valta. At a young age he came to Thessalonica, where he followed the trade of a tailor. When he learned that a certain Bulgarian Christian was preparing to deny Christ, he got himself a small cross and went to the coffee-house where the renegade's circumcision was to take place.

He cut through the crowd of janissaries, and while the drums were beating he came up to the man who was about to deny Christ, held out the cross and said to him: brother, what has happened to you? Here is our faith, here is Christ, who was crucified for the love of us; why do you leave Christ your saviour and become a Turk? The Turks were enraged, and they rushed on Christodoulos and arrested him and brought him with blows before the judge, where they demanded his death.

When the judge urged him to deny Christ, the martyr answered boldly: and you, leave Mohammedanism and become a Christian. Having proved his faith unshaken in that way, he was handed over to the executioners, who hanged him near the church of Saint Menas in Thessalonica. His body, with the cross nailed to its back, was left hanging naked for two days; afterwards the Christians bought it for six hundred piastres and buried it with honours. Above the notice the page sets a distich: «O Xριστόδουλος σταυρόν αίρων αγχόνην, Στερρώς υπήλθε δούλος ως Xριστού μέγας» — lifting up the cross, Christodoulos went steadfastly under the noose, as a great servant of Christ.
