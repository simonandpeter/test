# Mammilus

saint.gr keeps a company of martyrs of Africa on this day and gives the whole company one sentence: these holy martyrs, who are mentioned in the Roman Martyrology, were martyred at an unknown time in Africa. That is the entire notice. There is no city, no persecutor, no manner of death, no relic, no hymn and not even the distich the site usually prints, and the year is not merely missing but expressly unknown.

The day's line names nine of them — Felicity, Herenia, Rogatus, Felix, Rogatus, Beatus, Urbanus, Silvanus and Mammilus — and the page's own heading repeats the nine one by one, giving Felicity and Herenia the feminine form of the rank and the other seven the masculine. Two of the nine bear the same name, Rogatus, and nothing on the page tells those two apart, so neither of them is recorded here; the rest are, one folder each, which is what an enumerated company gets.

Of Mammilus himself the page says nothing beyond his name, his rank as martyr, and his place in this company. No other calendar this corpus reads has yet been checked for him, and he is the only saint of this name it keeps.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1248/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
