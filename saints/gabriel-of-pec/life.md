# Gabriel of Peć

saint.gr's calendar for 13 December keeps the hieromartyr Gabriel, archbishop of Peć in Serbia. With the permission of the Turks he had gone to Wallachia and to Russia to gather money for the needs of the Serbian Orthodox Church. While he was away he was accused by his own deputy in the archbishopric, a Bulgarian named Maximos, of plotting against the Turkish power; and when Gabriel came back from Russia he found Maximos on the archiepiscopal throne.

Gabriel at once moved to have him put out. Maximos, however, went to Prousa, where the Sultan and the Vizier were, and repeated his slanders against Gabriel. The Vizier then summoned Gabriel from Serbia to Prousa. There, having questioned him, he was persuaded that the charge was a slander — but he required the martyr to deny Christ, and offered him honours and offices for doing it. The hierarch threw back the proposals and stayed unshaken in the Christian faith.

So they tortured him harshly, and he received the crown of martyrdom by hanging, on 13 December 1659. The page adds, in its own last sentence, that Otto Meinardus places the martyrdom of this new hieromartyr on 13 October 1681 instead; it does not choose between the two, and neither does this entry.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 13 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/13/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3254/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
