# Pharmouthius the Anchorite

saint.gr's whole account of him is one sentence: «Ο Όσιος Φαρμούθιος απεβίωσε ειρηνικά» — the venerable Pharmouthius departed in peace. The day's line adds one word to it, ο Αναχωρητής, the anchorite, and the page's heading gives him no years at all.

What is left is the couplet the page prints over the entry, which plays his name against the calendar: Pharmouthius went out from the things here in the month Pharmouthi — plainly, it says, in April. That is the Egyptian month whose name he bears, and it is the only thing on the page that points to where he lived.

Nothing else is told. The page gives no century, no desert and no monastery, names no teacher and no disciple, lists no relic, and prints no hymn. The corpus holds no other man of this name, so nothing here had to be told apart from anybody.
