# Joachim of Osogovo

Joachim was one of the four great hermits of the Bulgarian lands, the
Russian church calendar says, whose labours stirred hundreds and thousands
to the ascetic life. He lived as a hermit in the second half of the eleventh
century on Mount Osogovo, at a place called Sarandapor, in a cave, known to
no one; only just before his death two hunters came on him by chance, and he
blessed them for a good hunt. The time of his death he revealed himself in a
vision after it — “before the great darkness”, an eclipse, “by eight years”
— which puts it, by that reckoning, about 1115.

Later another ascetic, Theodore from Ovče Polje, to whom Joachim appeared in
a dream, built a church on the spot. Many miracles have been worked from his
relics through the centuries, the Prologue says, and are worked today. The
Serbian calendar keeps him on 16 August of the old style, 29 August; the
Russian church calendar prints his page the same day and notes that he is
not in the present Russian menologion.

The Romanian calendar keeps him on 16 August too. Doxologia.ro's life calls him one of the greatest hermits of Bulgaria and a disciple of [John of Rila](/saints/john-of-rila), says he struggled in a cave on the steep bank of the river Kriva in the Osogovo mountain, from which his name comes, and that he fell asleep in the Lord in 1105 and was buried by two hunters in the cave where he had lived; fifty years later the founder of the monastery, father Teofan, laid his relics in what is now the monastery of Saint Joachim of Osogovo in Macedonia. The Ohrid Prologue puts his struggle in the second half of the eleventh century and doxologia.ro gives 1105; both readings are left standing.
