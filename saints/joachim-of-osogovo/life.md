# Joachim of Osogovo

Joachim lived as a hermit in the second half of the eleventh century on Mount Osogovo, at a place called Sarandapor, in a cave.

Later another ascetic, Theodore from Ovče Polje, to whom Joachim appeared in a dream, built a church on the spot. Many miracles have been worked from his relics through the centuries, the Prologue says, and are worked today.
