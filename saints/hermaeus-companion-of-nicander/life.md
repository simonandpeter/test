# Hermaeus, companion of Nicander

Hermaeus was enlightened in the faith and set to the sacred work by the [Apostle Titus](/saints/titus-the-apostle), and doxologia.ro's calendar names him a priest. Turning many Greeks from their error and bringing them to Christ, he was seized by the pagans and brought for questioning before Livanius the count, who pressed him to deny Christ; and when he saw that he could not, he ordered him tied to swift horses, and the horses driven, so that the servant of Christ should be dragged.

He was dragged a long while, and the ground was reddened with his blood, for his body was torn as it struck the stones and the wood; and he was ready to die, had the Lord not strengthened him in such torments. Barely alive, he was thrown into prison and tormented there with hunger and thirst, and the Lord fed him with heavenly bread and healed his wounds. Brought out to be questioned again after a time and refusing to obey, he was hung on a tree, raked with iron claws and burnt with torches.

After that he was thrown into a furnace of fire and stayed unharmed, for an angel of the Lord came to him and cooled the furnace and kept him from the fire. Hearing this, the governor ordered iron nails driven into his head, his heart and his belly, and had a pit dug for him; and while he still breathed they dragged him to it and covered him with earth. So he was made an end of by a hard and violent death, and now lives with the Lord.

doxologia.ro prints this same narrative, word for word, under the name of [Nicander](/saints/nicander-of-myra), Bishop of Myra, whom the Apostle Titus also enlightened and who was brought before the same count; the Romanian calendar keeps the two together on 4 November, and the troparion the site prints is printed for both of them at once. The page gives no year and names no place.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 4 noiembrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/4-noiembrie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-ermeu-preotul); read 25 September 2026.*
