# Herodion

Herodion, called also Rodion, was of Tarsus in Cilicia by birth and is numbered among the seventy apostles. He was a kinsman of the apostle Paul, as the letter to the Romans says, and bishop in Neopatras, where he suffered much at the hands of the Jews — but not before he had turned many of the Greeks to Christ by his holy teaching.

Hating him for it, they fell on him with the servants of the idols and tormented him hard: some beat him, some broke his mouth with stones, others struck him over the head with pieces of wood, and afterwards he was struck with a knife and left as dead, bathed in blood. Thinking him dead, they left him and went away; but Herodion rose up and went on helping the apostles, and being kept alive by the grace of God he preached the word of the Lord afterwards at Rome together with the apostle Peter. It is of him that Paul wrote: greet Herodion, my kinsman.

By the witness of Metaphrastes, he was beheaded at Rome together with the apostle Olympas while the two were helping the apostle Peter, on the same day on which Peter himself was crucified with a multitude of the faithful. His commemoration is kept on 4 January with the seventy apostles, on 28 March, on 8 April and on 10 November; the Romanian calendar prints him on 8 aprilie, with the apostles [Agabus](/saints/agabus-the-apostle), [Rufus](/saints/rufus-apostle-of-thebes), [Phlegon](/saints/phlegon-of-marathon), [Asyncritus](/saints/asyncritus-the-apostle) and [Hermes](/saints/hermes-apostle-of-dalmatia).

The page adds a caution of its own. The Hermeneia of Dionysius of Fourna, in the Bucharest edition of 2000, names two apostles of this name, Herodion and Rodion, and says that one is to be painted as an old man and the other as a young one, holding a rolled scroll, the sign of apostleship; the page does not settle whether they are two men or one, and neither does this entry.

The Greek calendar keeps him on 28 March, one of the four days his own Romanian page names, and tells the end at greater length. saint.gr places him in the circle of the Seventy Apostles of the Lord; after the Ascension, it says, he gave himself to the spreading of the Gospel and was a fellow worker of the Twelve and of the apostle Peter in particular. After the martyrdom of [Andrew the First-called](/saints/andrew-the-first-called), Herodion became bishop in the church of Patras, and in that new place showed all the virtues that adorned him. For his Christian work he was seized by Jews and by idolaters; they beat him savagely and stoned him, and at the last butchered him in the crudest way, so that he sealed his faith in his Saviour and Redeemer by a martyr's death. The page adds that his memory is repeated on 10 November, and prints an apolytikion for him which calls him the luminary and shepherd not of Patras but of Hypata — a disagreement with its own prose that it does not explain and that is not mended here.

The Ohrid Prologue keeps the six together on this day and gives each of them a few lines. All were of the Seventy, it says, and the apostle Paul mentions them all in his epistles. Herodion was Paul's kinsman — greet Herodion my kinsman, Paul writes to the Romans — and he suffered much from the Jews as bishop of Neopatras: they beat him over the head with staves, struck him on the mouth with stones and stabbed him with knives, and when they left him for dead he rose up and went on serving the apostles. At Rome he helped the apostle Peter, and he was beheaded, with many other Christians, on the very day on which Peter was crucified. The see this entry gives him is Neopatras, which is the Romanian reading and not the Patras of the Greek page's prose or the Hypata of its own hymn; it names no companion with him at the beheading. It closes of all six that, like bees of Christ, they carried the honey of the Gospel about into various countries, suffering much for the love of Christ, and that all of them passed over into the eternal kingdom of Christ the beloved.

The Russian calendar keeps him on 10 ноября ст. ст. as well, in a line of six of the Seventy, and gives him two lives that do not agree about his see. The life of the six says he left an episcopal chair «в Патрах», at Patras, to go to Rome; the longer life of his own company says the two chief apostles made him bishop of «города Патары», the city of Patara. Patras in Achaia and Patara in Lycia are two different cities, so both readings are set down here and neither is chosen, and this folder's `office` stays the bare Bishop it already was.

That longer life adds the beating with staves and the stoning by idolaters and Jews together, the knife-thrust that felled him, and his being raised up unharmed once the murderers had gone; then long years more in the company of the chief apostle, and the sword at the same hour as that apostle's cross, which the page dates about 67. His name page lists three days: the synaxis of the Seventy on 4 января, 8 апреля — the day the other three calendars here keep him on — and this one.

**This day’s Russian line is the 8 апреля his name page already names above, and it brings no second row.** The line is «Апп. от 70 Иродиона, Агава, Асинкрита, Руфа, Флегонта, Ерма и иже с ними (I).», he stands first in it, and the calendar’s one `russian` row for this folder is the 10 ноября one, which is the day of his own company; a church keeps one row here, and a second feast on the same calendar is not one. The life behind this day’s line is the life of the six already read for the November row, so nothing in it is new — the Patara of the two chief apostles, the staves and the stones and the knife, the raising up unharmed, the sword at the hour of the chief apostle’s cross.

**What the day does bring is a hymn the folder did not have.** Under «Молитвы» the 8 апреля column prints a kontakion of the apostles in the second tone, «Явистеся Христовы ученицы и апостоли всечестнии», which names all six of the company in order and names him first, «Иродионе славне», glorious Herodion. It is not one of the three hymns this folder already carries from 10 ноября: those are the troparion and the two kontakia the November column prints for a different company of six, and this one is proper to this day and to these six. It is filed under 8 апреля on his own name page, which is how the calendar itself keeps the two days’ hymns apart.
