# Theodora (12 January)

The Ohrid Prologue keeps a venerable mother Theodora on this day and gives her four sentences. She was a nun of Alexandria and a teacher of nuns: the entry calls her „Славна монахиња и наставница монахиња из Александрије“ — a glorious nun, and an instructress of nuns, out of Alexandria. Of her parents, her house, her monastery and her years in it the Prologue says nothing at all.

What it does keep is one saying of hers, and it is the whole of what the book wants remembered: „Као што је дрвећу потребна зима и снег, да би доносило рода, тако су муке и напасти потребне животу нашем“ — as trees need the winter and the snow if they are to bear fruit, so hardships and trials are needed by our life. The entry closes by saying that she reposed in peace at the beginning of the fifth century, and it gives no year.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 12. јануар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-01-25&prolog=1); read 3 October 2026.*
