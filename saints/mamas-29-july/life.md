# Mamas (29 July)

saint.gr's calendar for 29 Ιουλίου names him and gives almost nothing. Its whole notice of him is one sentence: «Ο Άγιος Μάμας μαρτύρησε δια πνιγμού, αφού τον έριξαν μέσα στη θάλασσα» — Saint Mamas was martyred by drowning, after they had thrown him into the sea. There is no country, no century, no emperor, no judge, no name of a companion and no hymn.

What the page prints besides is the gloss on the name — ΜΑΜΑΣ, ο στοργικός, the affectionate one — and the couplet of the synaxarion, which makes the manner of his death the whole of the praise: «Ως οία μύστης των αλιέων Mάμας, / Tολμά θαλάσσης εξερευνάν τα βάθη» — Mamas, like an initiate of the fishermen, dares to search out the depths of the sea. This folder stands for the Greek line of 29 July alone, and is marked by its day because the calendar gives him nothing else to be known by.
