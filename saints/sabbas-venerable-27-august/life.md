# Sabbas (27 August)

The Russian calendar keeps on 27 August (Julian) the venerable Sabbas,
without a date and without a life under the line. The fuller calendar at
azbyka.ru names him Sabbas of Venefaly and says only that the place and the
time of his life are unknown; the Greek calendar keeps a Sabbas of Venetala
on 25 August, and the two names look like one place — but neither calendar
says so, and nothing here asserts it. This entry records the two lines and
nothing more.

*After the day’s line of the Sretensky calendar (days.pravoslavie.ru), 27 August (Julian) — [the page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260827.html), which prints no life, read 23 August 2026; and the Russian church calendar at azbyka.ru — [the entry](https://azbyka.ru/days/sv-savva-venefalskij), read 6 September 2026.*
