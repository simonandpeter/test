# Sabbas (27 August)

The Russian calendar keeps on 27 August (Julian) the venerable Sabbas, without a date and without a life under the line; the Greek calendar keeps a Sabbas of Venetala on 25 August, who may or may not be the same man — nothing here asserts it. This entry records the line and nothing more.

*After the day’s line of the Sretensky calendar (days.pravoslavie.ru), 27 August (Julian) — [the page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260827.html), which prints no life; read 23 August 2026.*
