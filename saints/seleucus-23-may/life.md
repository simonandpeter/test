# Seleucus (23 May)

saint.gr names him and gives no life. Its whole account of him is one sentence: «Ο Άγιος Σέλευκος μαρτύρησε αφού τον θανάτωσαν με πριόνισμα» — the holy Seleucus was martyred after they put him to death by sawing. The page gives no year, no emperor, no place and no companions, and it prints no hymn to him.

What stands beside that sentence is the couplet the synaxarion sets over his memory: Seleucus bears the sawing without a groan, and so leaves behind this life that is full of groaning. The page notes only that the name is probably from σέλας and λευκός, the bright or the glorious. Nothing further about him is recorded here.
