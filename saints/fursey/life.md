# Fursey

saint.gr's calendar for 16 Ιανουαρίου names «Όσιος Φουρσάς», and the page behind that name says in one sentence that it has nothing further to give: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Οσίου» — we have no details of the venerable one's life. That single line is the whole of the source. The page gives him the rank Όσιος, venerable, and no century, no country, no place of asceticism, no verses and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 16 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/16/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/300/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
