# Artemius the Sinaite

Artemius was a brother of the monastery of Sinai, and served at the monastery's dependency at Aghia Triada of Herakleion in Crete. He was a member of the company of the hieromartyr Neophytus the Sinaite, whom the Greek calendar honours on 20 December, and he was martyred in the year 1822.

Those three sentences are the whole of saint.gr's page for him. It does not say where he was born, how he died, or at whose hands; it prints no hymn and no verses; and it does not say whether the corpus's other saints of the name — the great-martyr Artemius among them — have anything to do with him, which they do not appear to.
