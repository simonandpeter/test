# Alexander (Krutitsky)

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 18 июня ст. ст. names four presbyters on one line, «Сщмчч. Василия , Александра , Василия и Сергия пресвитеров (1938).», and he is the second of them. The line gives the forenames in the genitive, one grade-class word and one rank word for the four together, and one year, and no surname to any of them. The name link behind his place in it supplies the calendar’s own nominative with the surname and the grade, «Александр (Крутицкий), иерей, сщмч.», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from.

His name page carries no life. Under a heading «Статья на Православие.Ru» it offers a link to a site essay on the new martyrs of the Щелково country, with one general sentence of its opening — that the more the Christians were driven, the more of them there became — and nothing about this priest in particular. That essay is an article on the site and not the calendar’s own life page, and it was not opened. No place of service, no age and no manner of death is supplied for him here.

The line’s «Сщмчч.» makes him a hieromartyr and «пресвитеров» a presbyter, and the name link repeats the rank as «иерей». The second day his name page lists is the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which is a synaxis and not a second feast, so he renders on 18 июня ст. ст. only. He is given no relation to the three others on the line: they share a line, a rank word and a year, and the calendar prints nothing that says they died in one place or knew one another.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 18 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260618.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9428.html); read 9 October 2026.*
