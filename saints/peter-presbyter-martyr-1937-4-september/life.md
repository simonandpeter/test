# Peter (Lebedinsky)

Peter Ivanovich Lebedinsky was born in 1881, a priest’s son, and served as priest in the village of Sorochki in the Gagino district of the Gorky region. In 1935 he was arrested, charged with preaching a sermon and sentenced to two years’ exile; on 8 September 1937 he was arrested again at Sorochki, charged this time with having organised on 6 July 1937 a solemn service that took the peasants from work on the collective farm, and given a death sentence. On 17 September 1937 he died a martyr’s death by shooting, with the priests John Romashkin, Nicholas Khvoshchev and Alexander Nikolsky and the laymen of the district; he was numbered among the new martyrs in August 2000, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 4 September (Julian).
