# Theophilus (6 February)

saint.gr names him and gives one sentence of life. Its calendar for 6 February prints «Άγιοι Θεόφιλος, Σατουρνίνος και Ρεβοκάτος», and the page behind the line says that Theophilus, [Saturninus](/saints/saturninus-martyr-6-february) and [Revocatus](/saints/revocatus) were martyred in Spain in the reign of the emperor Decius, which it dates 249 to 251. There is no city here, no account of the trial, no manner of death, no hymn and no verses.

Two things are worth setting beside it. The same Greek day keeps, on another line, a second Theophilus, a martyr at Caesarea with Dorothea, whom saint.gr itself marks as uncorroborated; this is not that man, and the corpus takes neither of them as the other. And the names Saturninus and Revocatus together are the names of two companions of Perpetua and Felicity at Carthage; but the Greek line puts these three in Spain under Decius, two generations later, and the corpus holds no folder for the Carthaginian pair.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3616/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
