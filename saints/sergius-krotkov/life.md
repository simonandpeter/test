# Sergius (Krotkov)

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 18 июня ст. ст. names four presbyters on one line, «Сщмчч. Василия , Александра , Василия и Сергия пресвитеров (1938).», and he is the fourth of them. He is also the only one of the four the calendar gives a life page of its own, and it is a full one. It says he was born in 1876 in the village of Подлесная слобода in the Луховицкий district of the province of Ryazan, the son of a priest, Михаил Кротков; that his father died when the boy was three; and that the relations who raised him afterwards were well off, but that he remembered those years as heavy and without joy.

As an orphan he was sent to the Ryazan theological seminary at the public charge. Out of the seminary he was appointed to teach the Law of God in a parish school; and having married Мария, daughter of the archpriest Палладий Орлов who served in the town of Луховицы in the Ryazan diocese, he was appointed to his father-in-law’s place and ordained priest in 1903.

When the First World War began he was sent as regimental priest to the 139th Моршанский regiment on the Austrian front, and the page says he went through the whole war with the unit in its forward positions. For that service he was given the order of Anna of the third degree and the cross of St George and was raised to archpriest. He stayed with the regiment until the Bolshevik overturn of 1917, and when it was disbanded he went to the province of Voronezh as rector of the church at Валуйчики. Before he left the front «епископ Кременецкий Дионисий (Валединский)» offered to settle him in western Europe rather than let him return to a Russia then beginning to burn, and he refused.

From 1922 to 1930 he was rector of the Nicholas church at Царево in the Пушкинский district of the Moscow region. The page keeps the small things of those years. Firewood was not to be had, so he would go down to the river Талица, dive into the cold water and fish out a heavy wet log, saying as he dived «Благодать-то, какая!» — what grace this is. He took nothing from those who could not pay, gave away his last money, and met believer and unbeliever with the same evenness. The page also tells how a priest of a neighbouring village worked to get his place and got it.

In 1930 he was moved to the church of the Protection at Покровское by Царицыно, now inside Moscow, and kept his family in the house at Царево, so that he travelled to his church by rail through Софрино, Москва and Царицыно and then on foot. He rode the open platform of a little goods train in a worn ear-flapped cap with a reed bag held against his chest, and once, when some bold young men jumped up on the platform and one of them said «Смотрите, поп сидит» — look, there sits a priest — he only looked at them, and they went off abashed. He served there more than seven years.

At the end of 1937 the authorities decided to close the church. In February 1938 the NKVD called him in, warned him of it, and hinted that his own position was now dangerous. He did not leave off serving: «прихожане придут молиться, а я окажусь дезертиром, предавшим Бога и паству» — the parishioners will come to pray, and I shall turn out a deserter who has betrayed God and his flock. On 2 March 1938, having served the liturgy, he came out of the church to a cab that took him to the district NKVD and then to the Таганская prison.

Under questioning, which the page prints as question and answer, he said that his whole counter-revolution consisted in the church service; that he had been raised in the faith from childhood and it had entered his flesh and blood, so that he was an opponent of the party and the government and of their measures; that he had not carried counter-revolution openly among the people, but had spoken among some of the faithful of the hardness of life and of the taxes laid on him; and that he had served at Покровское unregistered because he did not live in that district. On 14 June 1938 a troika of the NKVD sentenced him to be shot. He was shot on 1 July 1938 and buried in an unmarked common grave at the Бутово range outside Moscow.

He was numbered among the New Martyrs and Confessors of Russia at the jubilee council of bishops in August 2000. The second day his page lists is the synaxis of those new martyrs on 26 января, which is a synaxis and not a second feast, so he renders on 18 июня ст. ст. only. The day page prints hymns for the martyrs its first line heads, for the canonarch of the Caves and for the Боголюбская icon, and none under his name.
