# Nathanael

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 27 noiembrie reads «Sfântul Cuvios Natanail» — the venerable Nathanael — and the page it links to carries only that name, the date, and the readings appointed for the day: the Apostle from Galatians 5:22–26 and 6:1–2, on the fruit of the Spirit, and the Gospel from Matthew 11:27–30, *Come to me, all you who labour and are heavy laden.*

There is no Viață tab and no Tropar tab on that page, so no story, no century, no country and no hymn are recorded here. What the Romanian calendar attests is that he is kept as a venerable father on this day; anything more would have to come from a source this folder has not read.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 27 noiembrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/27-noiembrie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-natanail); read 25 September 2026.*
