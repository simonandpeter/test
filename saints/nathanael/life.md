# Nathanael

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 27 noiembrie reads «Sfântul Cuvios Natanail» — the venerable Nathanael — and the page it links to carries only that name, the date, and the readings appointed for the day: the Apostle from Galatians 5:22–26 and 6:1–2, on the fruit of the Spirit, and the Gospel from Matthew 11:27–30, *Come to me, all you who labour and are heavy laden.*

There is no Viață tab and no Tropar tab on that page, so no story, no century, no country and no hymn are recorded here. What the Romanian calendar attests is that he is kept as a venerable father on this day; anything more would have to come from a source this folder has not read.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 27 November, and saint.gr says a little more than the Romanian calendar did. He was an ascetic, it says, whom Palladius treats in the Lausiac History, telling the strange things of his life: that he lived thirty-seven years inside his cell without once coming out, for all the traps the demon laid to drag him from his hut, and that he died in peace. His distich puts the words in his own mouth as he was about to breathe his last — «Ἀφεῖσα κόσμον, πρὸς Θεὸν ψυχὴ τρέχε», having left the world, soul, run to God. The page gives him no century and no country, and prints no hymn.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 27 noiembrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/27-noiembrie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-natanail); read 25 September 2026; and after saint.gr's calendar for 27 Νοεμβρίου — [the Greek day](https://www.saint.gr/11/27/index.aspx) and [its life](https://www.saint.gr/2731/saint.aspx), read 2 October 2026.*
