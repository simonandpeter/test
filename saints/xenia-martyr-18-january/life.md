# Xenia (18 January)

The Greek calendar names her on 18 Ιανουαρίου with two words, «Αγία Ξένη», and the page behind that line is one sentence long and says so itself: she bore witness by fire, and there are no other details of the saint's life — «Η Αγία Ξένη μαρτύρησε διά πυρός. Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο της Αγίας.» Above it stands an epigram: «Ὁ Χριστὸς ἦλθεν πῦρ βαλεῖν εἰς τὴν γῆν πάλαι, Ξένη τρέφουσα καρτερεῖ τὸ πῦρ ξένως».

The page gives her no year, no city, no emperor and no judge, and it prints no hymn for her. She is not the Xenia the same calendar keeps on 24 January, who was a deaconess and fell asleep in peace; this folder is for the martyr of 18 January and nothing about the other is carried over to her.
