# Donagos of Libya

saint.gr's calendar for 28 Ιουνίου names him and gives him one sentence. Its line reads «Άγιος Δονάγος επίσκοπος Λιβύης», and the page behind that line says only that this holy bishop of Libya was martyred by fire. No century is named, no persecutor, no city of his see beyond the province, no year and no relics, and the page prints no hymn.

Above the sentence stands a couplet of the synaxarion addressed to him, which answers the manner of his death with the Gospel: «Στέξον το πυρ Δόναγε. Tης γαρ σης κάρας, Ώς φησι Xριστός, ουκ ολείται θριξ όλως» — bear the fire, Donagos; for of your head, as Christ says, not a hair shall perish at all. The page does not gloss the name, as it does for most, and there is nothing else on it.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/28/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1916/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
