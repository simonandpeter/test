# Macrina the Elder

Macrina was the grandmother of [Basil the Great](/saints/basil-the-great). Doxologia prints her life from the Ohrid Prologue of Saint Nikolai Velimirović, and it is short. She was famous, it says, for her great wisdom, faith and piety, and she was a disciple of Saint Gregory the Wonderworker of Neocaesarea.

In the reign of Diocletian she left her house, and she and her husband Basil went and hid in wildernesses and in forests. Their properties were confiscated by the pagan authority, and neither of them regretted anything. Stripped of everything except their love for God, they made themselves a home out of the wilderness of the forest and lived there seven years; by God's providence goats came down to them from the mountain and gave them their daily food.

The Prologue says that they both fell asleep in peace in the fourth century, after passing through great sufferings for their faith. It gives no closer year, and no place beyond the forest and Neocaesarea, so nothing further is recorded here.

The Serbian calendar keeps her on the same day, and the Охридски пролог has now been read at first hand rather than through the Romanian page that prints it. The two agree in every clause and in their order, so nothing above is changed by it. The Serbian text says of her only that she was wonderful in mind and in piety, where the Romanian has great wisdom, faith and piety; and it says that the goats came down from the mountain and gave themselves, where the Romanian says they gave them their daily food. There is no year in it beyond the fourth century, and no place beyond the forest.
