# Theodosius, companion of Paisius of Neamț

The Greek calendar prints him on 12 Ιανουαρίου as «Όσιος Θεοδόσιος εκ Ρωσίας», the venerable Theodosius from Russia, and the page behind the line gives him two sentences. He was born in Russia and was a fellow ascetic of [Paisius Velichkovsky](/saints/paisius-of-neamt), whose memory, the page notes, the Church honours on 15 November. He fell asleep in peace in the year 1802.

Nothing more is printed for him: no monastery, no office, no year of birth, no account of the years he shared with Paisius, and no hymn. The page's header gives only the year of his death.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1400/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
