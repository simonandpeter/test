# Heraclius, companion of Alexander

saint.gr's calendar for 22 Οκτωβρίου names six martyrs in one line and tells a single story for them. [Alexander](/saints/alexander-22-october) was a bishop who preached the Gospel with great zeal to crowds of idolaters, and drew a multitude of them to the faith; the ruler of the place, enraged, had him seized and tortured to force him to sacrifice to the idols, and he was not persuaded.

A soldier named Heraclius, seeing Alexander's endurance under that torture, believed in Christ. He was himself put to many tortures and at the last beheaded. The page says nothing else of him: no city, no legion, no year, no emperor and no family, and it gives him no couplet of his own.

Alexander was healed by the grace of God of the wounds of his torture and worked a wonder, by which four women — [Theodota](/saints/theodota-of-adrianople), [Glyceria](/saints/glyceria-of-adrianople), [Anna](/saints/anna-of-adrianople) and [Elizabeth](/saints/elizabeth-of-adrianople) — were drawn to the faith; they confessed before the ruler and were beheaded, and after all of them Alexander was beheaded too. The page prints no hymn for the company.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 22 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/22/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2778/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
