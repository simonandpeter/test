# Basil (Krymkin)

Basil Ivanovich Krymkin was born in 1901 in the village of Protasyevo in the Ranenburg district of Ryazan province, to the peasants Ivan Alexeyevich and Martha Leontyevna Krymkin. At eight he went to the Trinity monastery a verst and a half from the town of Lebedyan in Tambov province, finished its parish school, and remained a novice there until 1928. When the godless authorities closed the monastery he went home and served as psalm-reader in his village church, and in 1930 he was ordained priest and served in the Trinity church at Ostafyevo in the Podolsk district of Moscow province.

He was arrested on 4 December 1937, questioned the same day, and with that the investigation was closed. Told that he had spread counter-revolutionary agitation and rumours of the Soviet power’s early fall among his parishioners, he answered that he had done neither, and he did not admit guilt. On 7 December 1937 an NKVD troika sentenced him to ten years in a corrective labour camp, and he died in a camp in Kuibyshev province on 4 October 1942.

The Russian calendar keeps him on 21 September old style. azbyka.ru’s life is Abbot Damascene (Orlovsky)’s, from the supplementary volume of his lives of the new martyrs and confessors of the Moscow diocese, and it prints no hymns for him.
