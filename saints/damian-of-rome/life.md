# Damian of Rome

Damian and his brother [Cosmas](/saints/cosmas-of-rome) were born of one father and one mother in old Rome and brought up in the Christian faith. They learned the craft of medicine, and whatever suffering people or beasts they laid their hands upon were at once made perfectly whole, for the grace of the one God helped them in everything.

They took no payment for their healing and were called the physicians without fee; the only payment they asked was that those they healed should believe in Christ. They sold the property they had inherited from their forefathers and shared it out among the poor and the needy, fed the hungry and clothed the naked, and going through the towns and villages about Rome they healed the sick and turned many from idol-worship to Christ.

They lived apart in a village within the borders of Rome, where their parents' property lay. Slandered to the emperor Carinus by certain of their own servants, they refused to hide when the faithful begged them to, and went out to the soldiers of their own will. At Rome the emperor was healed by them and gave thanks to God before all the people, honoured the two physicians and let them go in peace; and they returned to their village and to their old round of the towns and villages.

The envious one, unable to destroy them by his first device, found another. A famous physician of those parts, the man from whom they had first learned their craft, could not bear their good name. He called them to him deceitfully and led them far up a mountain as though to gather herbs, setting each to gather apart; then he fell upon the one and killed him with stones, and killed the other in the same way, and hid the two bodies by a watercourse. So the two unmercenaries received the martyr's crown. The page names the emperor Carinus and gives no year, so none is recorded here.
