# Ia of Persia

Ia came from the fortress called Bizala. She was taken captive with nine thousand Christians and led to Persia, where the Persian king Sapor handed her to the wise men of Persia to persuade her to deny Christ; when they failed she was imprisoned, brought out after fifteen months, harshly tortured and at last beheaded — cut from the earth of the valleys by the sword, the verse says, and brought to the Saviour like a sweet-smelling violet. Though the verses differ, the synaxarion notes, she may be the same as the Ias kept on 11 September and 4 August.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 10 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2376/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
