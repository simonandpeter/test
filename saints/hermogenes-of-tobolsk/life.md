# Hermogenes

Hermogenes — George Efremovich Dolganov in the world — bishop of Tobolsk and Siberia, was born on 25 April 1858 in the family of an Edinoverie priest of the Kherson diocese who later became a monk. He finished the full course of the law faculty at Novorossiysk, and there too took courses in mathematics and in history and philology; then he entered the St Petersburg Academy, where he was tonsured with the name Hermogenes, and on 15 March 1892 became a hieromonk. The Sretensky calendar’s life — which joins him with the priest Peter Karelin, killed with him — runs on past the point read here, to his episcopate and his death in 1918; the Russian calendar keeps on 20 August (Julian) the finding of his relics in 2005.
