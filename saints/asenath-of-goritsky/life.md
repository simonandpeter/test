# Asenath of Goritsky

saint.gr's whole account of her is one sentence: «Η Αγία Ασινέθ καταγόταν από την Ρωσία και ήταν δια Χριστόν σαλή» — Asenath came from Russia and was a fool for Christ's sake. The day's line adds the one word that distinguishes her, του Γκορίτσκυ, of Goritsky, which the body of the page does not repeat and does not explain.

There is nothing else. The page gives her no century and no emperor or tsar, no monastery, no manner of life beyond the folly itself, no death and no relic, and it prints neither couplet nor hymn. The corpus holds no other saint of this name, so nothing here had to be told apart from anybody.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 19 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/19/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/299/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
