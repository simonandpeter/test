# Vukašin of Klepci

Vukašin Mandrapa of Klepci was a Serbian Orthodox Christian from Herzegovina, martyred by the fascists during the Second World War for refusing to acknowledge the local Nazi leader Ante Pavelić. Little is known of his life, doxologia says; what is known of him is bound up with the event of his martyrdom, which came to light in 1946. He was born in the village of Klepci in Herzegovina at the beginning of the twentieth century.

At the beginning of the war, members of the Croatian fascist Ustaša arrested him and took him, with other Serbs of that region, to the well-known concentration camp at Jasenovac, near the town of Jasenovac in Croatia, which the Ustaša had set up during Ante Pavelić's rule in Croatia between 1941 and 1945. The page notes that the number of the camp's victims has been estimated at between eighty thousand and eight hundred thousand.

He was cruelly tortured in the camp, and kept the peace of Christ in his soul to his last breath. He was entered as a martyr on the list of saints of the Serbian Orthodox Church in 1998, and his day of commemoration is 16 May.
