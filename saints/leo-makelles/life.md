# Leo Makelles

The Greek calendar names him on 20 Ιανουαρίου as Leo Makelles, the Great, the Thracian. saint.gr says he came of Thrace and reigned from about 457 to 474, succeeding [Marcian](/saints/marcian-the-emperor) on the throne of Byzantium; Leo I was proclaimed emperor on 7 February 457 at the Hebdomon, in the presence of the Patriarch. Over him the page sets a distich: «Άνακτα πιστόν τον Λέοντα τον μέγαν, Έστεψε Χριστός ουρανοκράτωρ Άναξ» — Christ, the King who holds heaven, crowned Leo the Great, the faithful sovereign.

His measures, the page says, looked to the defence of Orthodoxy and the unity of the empire, and reached the offices of state and of the courts, and by tradition he raised the church of the Life-giving Spring. He died of sickness on 18 January 474 — which is not the day the calendar keeps him on, and this folder says so rather than moving him. The page prints no hymn for him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 20 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/20/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3327/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
