# Dionysius of Milan

Dionysius lived in the fourth century and was bishop of Milan in Italy. In 355, saint.gr says, he was exiled to Cappadocia by the Arian-minded emperor Constantius, whose reign the page gives as 337 to 361, and the reason it gives is his stout stand against the heretics. He fell asleep there in 359, four years into the exile and a long way from his see.

That is the whole of the entry. The page names no birthplace, no parents, no writings and no relics; it does not say whether his body came back to Milan; and it prints neither a synaxarion couplet nor a hymn to him. What it records is a see, a year of exile, a cause and a year of death.
