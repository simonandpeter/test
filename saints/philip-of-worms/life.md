# Philip of Worms

saint.gr names him and gives him two sentences. Philip was an Anglo-Saxon by origin, its page says, and he lived as a hermit in the eighth century in the region of the city of Worms in Germany; and he fell asleep in peace in the year 770. That is the whole of the notice.

Nothing else stands: no year of birth, no name in his own tongue, no reason given for his coming from England to the Rhine, no monastery, no disciple, no wonder, no relic and no hymn. The day's listing calls him «ο εν Βόρμς», the one at Worms, and that place is the only thing that distinguishes him from the other Philips of the calendar.
