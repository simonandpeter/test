# Gerasimus of Astrakhan

saint.gr's calendar for 24 Ιουνίου names him and gives no life. Its line reads «Άγιος Γεράσιμος Επίσκοπος του Αστραχάν», and the page behind that line says in a single sentence that there are no details to be had: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ρώσου Αγίου» — we have no particulars of the life of the Russian saint. Beyond that sentence the page carries only its heading, the day, a gloss on the name and two links.

The heading reads «Αγιος Γερασιμος Επισκοπος Του Αστραχαν (; - 1880)», so the year of death is 1880 and the year of birth is left as a question mark. Of the see the page says nothing but its name. The gloss it gives the name is «ΓΕΡΑΣΙΜΟΣ (εκ του γέρας) = ο θριαμβευτής» — from the word for a prize of honour, the victor — and the two links it offers are a synaxis of all the saints of Siberia and an English feasts-and-saints page. It prints no hymn, so none is recorded here, and it lists no relics.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/24/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4249/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
