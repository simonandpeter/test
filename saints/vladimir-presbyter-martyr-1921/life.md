# Vladimir (Sadovsky)

The priest Vladimir Sadovsky served until 1912 at the church of the village of Lugovskoye in the Kopal district, and in 1912 was moved to the stanitsa of Zaitsevskaya (Chilik) in the Verny district of the Semirechye region. He was shot in 1921 by a sentence confirmed by the regional revolutionary tribunal, founded on the charge of agitation against the Soviet power, keeping arms and failing to produce documents. He was glorified among the saints by the jubilee Council of Bishops of 2000, as the Orthodox Encyclopaedia records, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 3 September (Julian) among the martyrs of Semirechye.
