# Leo, companion of Gervasius

saint.gr names the holy Leo with [Gervasius](/saints/gervasius-companion-of-leo) on this day, calls the two of them in its day's line «οι αυτάδελφοι», own brothers, and gives them two sentences between them. The first says that the holy Gervasius and Leo were martyred in the second century after Christ. The second is the only thing said of Leo alone, and it is a title rather than a story: «Ο Άγιος Λέων ήταν Απόστολος των Βάσκων» — the holy Leo was apostle of the Basques.

That title is kept here as the page's own word for him and is not taken as the rank apostle, which the corpus keeps for the twelve and the seventy. Beyond it there is nothing: no country, no city, no emperor, no judge, no manner of death, no year of birth, no relic, no distich and no hymn, and the page does not say where among the Basques he preached or how his mission ended.

The corpus keeps a dozen other men of this name — the Great, of Catania, of Nicaea, of Optina, and the rest — and none of them stands on this day or is named on this page.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3785/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
