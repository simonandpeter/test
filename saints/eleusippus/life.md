# Eleusippus

Eleusippus was the second of three brothers born at a single birth in the city doxologia.ro's life calls Lingonia, in Gaul, and brought up by their grandmother [Neonilla](/saints/neonilla). They had been schooled in the liberal arts and served idols after their fathers' custom until Benignus, a presbyter sent into Gaul by Polycarp of Smyrna, was received in their grandmother's house and taught them the faith with her.

On the night before, the life says, Eleusippus had seen one seated on a throne in heaven, shining with a glory that darkened his eyes, so that fear fell on him; and the bright face called him and said that he should not be afraid, for he would be counted worthy to receive the crown of victory. His brothers [Speusippus](/saints/speusippus) and [Meleusippus](/saints/meleusippus) had seen the like, and Benignus baptised the three of them together.

When they had broken the idol of Nemesis and twelve more in their own house, the city rose against them, and one of the pagan chiefs, Quadratus, struck Speusippus and Eleusippus across the face. Eleusippus answered that the more torments they made ready, the more they would strengthen the brothers in the divine gift. The three were hung from a beam and stretched until their joints parted, then cast into a great fire and walked in it unharmed; afterwards they knelt, prayed, and gave up their souls together, and were buried at a village called Urvat two stadia from the city.
