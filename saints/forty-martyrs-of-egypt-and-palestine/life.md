# The Forty Martyrs of Egypt and Palestine

saint.gr's calendar for 14 October keeps «Άγιοι Σαράντα Μάρτυρες από την Αίγυπτο και Παλαιστίνη», and its whole notice is one sentence: the holy forty martyrs from Egypt and Palestine were martyred by the sword, together with Saint [Silvanus](/saints/silvanus-of-gaza), and it sends the reader to him on the same day. His own page closes with the same thing from the other side: with Saint Silvanus, it says, forty other martyrs were beheaded too, some of them from Egypt and some from Palestine.

The calendar names not one of the forty. It gives no year, no city, no governor, no rank for any of them and no hymn; the two-line verse over the company says only that a double score of martyrs bore the sword, whom Egypt and Palestine brought forth. Because nobody in it is named, it stands here as one folder for the company and not as forty — the Church keeps them as a number, and that is what the source holds.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 14 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/14/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2748/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
