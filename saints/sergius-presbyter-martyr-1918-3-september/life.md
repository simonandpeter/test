# Sergius (Fenomenov)

The archpriest Sergius — Sergei Vasilievich Fenomenov — served at the
Fortress church of the town of Ust-Kamenogorsk and was army chaplain of
the garrison quartered in the fortress. He is listed in the rolls of the
50th Belostok infantry regiment at Sevastopol for 1905, so that it may be
supposed he served throughout as a military chaplain or with the
disciplinary battalions; his last post was the church of the
Transfiguration of the Ust-Kamenogorsk disciplinary company, which stood
in the disused fortress and took up a room for three hundred on the second
floor of the stone barracks, today the brothers’ block of the Holy Trinity
monastery of Ust-Kamenogorsk. In 1918, when power in Ust-Kamenogorsk was
seized for a time by the Bolsheviks, he was shot by them before the eyes
of his family — his wife and three sons — on the Commandant’s Island
opposite the fortress, where he lived with his family; a guard was set
over the martyr’s body and let no one near, and after three days it was
taken away secretly from the family in an unknown direction, and his
burial place remains unknown. He was rehabilitated under the law of the
Republic of Kazakhstan of 14 April 1993, and numbered among the new
martyrs and confessors of Russia by a resolution of the Holy Synod of 27
December 2000; the Russian calendar keeps him on 3 September (Julian).
