# Sergius (Fenomenov)

The archpriest Sergius — Sergei Vasilievich Fenomenov — served at the Fortress church of the town of Ust-Kamenogorsk and was army chaplain of the garrison quartered in the fortress. In 1918, when power in Ust-Kamenogorsk was seized for a time by the Bolsheviks, he was shot by them before the eyes of his family — his wife and three sons — on the Commandant’s Island opposite the fortress, where he lived with his family; a guard was set over the martyr’s body and let no one near, and after three days it was taken away secretly from the family in an unknown direction, and his burial place remains unknown — the calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. The Russian calendar keeps him on 3 September (Julian).
