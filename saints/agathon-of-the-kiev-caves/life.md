# Agathon of the Kiev Caves

saint.gr says Agathon lived in Russia in the fourteenth century and practised his asceticism in the monastery of the Great Lavra of Kiev, where God counted him worthy of the gift of prophecy and gave him the power to work wonders. He fell asleep in peace; and the page adds what is unusual in so short an entry, that the canonical ecclesiastical act of his proclamation as a saint was made in the year 1643 by [Peter Mohyla](/saints/peter-mohyla), metropolitan of Russia.

That is the whole of the entry. It gives him no year of birth and no year of death, names no relics, and prints neither couplet nor hymn under his name. What stands above it is saint.gr's standing gloss on the name Agathon — that it comes from ἀγαθός, the very good in soul and in character, and that an Athenian tragic poet of the name was the friend of Socrates, Plato and Euripides — which belongs to the name and not to this saint.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 20 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/20/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2602/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
