# Ananias (9 June)

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 9 June reads «Άγιος Ανανίας», Saint Ananias, and the page behind it says only this: Saint Ananias bore witness by the sword. So what is recorded here is a name, the rank of a martyr and the manner of his death, and nothing else at all: not a century, not a country, not the name of whoever condemned him.

The couplet the page sets above that sentence is the only other thing it gives him, and it turns on the same weapon twice: Ananias the martyr had his tongue for a sword against error, and a sword is what kills him. There is no century here, no emperor, no city, no accusation and no burial; there is no troparion. The heading above the entry glosses the name out of the Hebrew, as the man on whom the Lord had mercy, and that is the page's own furniture and not a word about this martyr.
