# Theodore

The Greek synaxarion keeps on 19 September the holy prince Theodore and his sons [David](/saints/david-son-of-prince-theodore) and [Constantine](/saints/constantine-son-of-prince-theodore), and says it has no details of the lives of these Russian saints; the Russian calendar keeps them the same day of its own reckoning, outside the weeks read here. This entry records the line and nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 19 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/3846/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
