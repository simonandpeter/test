# The Three Martyrs of Galatia

saint.gr's calendar for 28 Ιουνίου keeps three men from Galatia whose names it does not give. Its line reads «Άγιοι Τρεις Μάρτυρες από τη Γαλατία», and the page behind that line says only that they were martyred by the sword. No century is named, no persecutor, no city of Galatia, no year and no relics, and the page prints no hymn for them.

The couplet of the synaxarion above it speaks in their own voice: «Tρεις άνδρες ημείς, αλλά συμπνοίας λόγω, Ως είς ιδού χωρούμεν οι τρεις προς ξίφος» — we are three men, but by reason of being of one breath, see, we three go to the sword as one. Because the source names none of the three, they are kept here as one entry and not three, and nothing is supplied that the page does not print.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/28/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1918/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
