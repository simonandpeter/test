# The Father and Son (13 February)

saint.gr's calendar for 13 Φεβρουαρίου keeps two martyrs it calls nothing but father and son. Its page for them heads them «Αγιος Πατηρ» and «Αγιος Υιος», as though each had a name, and then gives one sentence: «Οι Άγιοι αυτοί μαρτύρησαν με σταυρικό θάνατο. Δεν έχουμε περισσότερες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο τους» — these saints were martyred by death on a cross; we have no further details of their life.

Because neither is named — and because the kinship is the only thing the calendar has of either — this folder is one entry for the two of them. Nothing else is printed: no place, no year, no emperor, no hymn and no relics. The distich over them sets the manner of death against the reason for it: «Πατὴρ σὺν Υἱῷ σταυρικὸν πάσχει πάθος, Ὑπὲρ Πατρός, τοῦ δόντος Υἱὸν εἰς πάθος» — a father with a son suffers the passion of the cross, for the Father who gave his Son to the passion.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 13 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/13/index.aspx) and [the page for the two of them](https://www.saint.gr/711/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
