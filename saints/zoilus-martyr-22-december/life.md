# Zoilus (22 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 22 December keeps Zoilus, and its whole account of him is one sentence. He suffered a martyr's death under Diocletian when, together with the holy women [Agape](/saints/agape-of-aquileia), [Irene](/saints/irene-of-aquileia) and [Chionia](/saints/chionia-of-aquileia), whom the page refers to 16 April, he gathered up out of the waters of the lake the relic of [Chrysogonus](/saints/chrysogonus), whom it refers to this same day and whom another page of the same calendar has beheaded in a desert place.

The page adds that certain synaxaria name beside Zoilus the memory of a certain Saint Eutychianos, and it says nothing further of that man; no folder is proposed for him here, and none of the three Eutychians this corpus already holds is claimed to be he. Above the sentence stands a distich, that Zoilus, forsaking a life that flows away, found an abiding life in the place of the living. The calendar gives him no year of his own, no city, and no hymn, and marks the feast fixed.

The Greek keeps two other men of this name on two other days — an ascetic with Zeno on 3 March, and Zoilus the Roman on 13 April, who was hung upon a piece of wood — and nothing on any of the three pages joins any two of them, so this folder carries its day in its name. The page itself carries no asterisk and not the sentence by which saint.gr marks an entry it will not vouch for.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 22 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/22/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3236/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
