# Eutasia

doxologia.ro's calendar names her and gives almost nothing more. Her entry in the Lives of the Saints on that site is a single sentence, printed twice and standing alone: «Sfânta Muceniţă Eutasia cu sabia a fost tăiată şi aşa s-a săvârşit» — the holy martyr Eutasia was cut down with the sword, and so she was made perfect. That is the whole of it. The page gives no city and no year, names no emperor and no persecutor, and says nothing of what she was accused of or what she answered.

The calendar keeps her on 12 January, where the line reads «Sfânta Muceniță Eutasia», and it prints no troparion and no kontakion for her. «Muceniță» is the Romanian word for a woman who died for the faith, and the sword is the one thing the sentence records of how she died. Nothing further is claimed for her here, because nothing further was read.

The Greek calendar keeps her on the same day and is as bare as the Romanian one, and says the same thing. saint.gr spells her Ευθασία and gives her one sentence — «Η Αγία Ευθασία μαρτύρησε διά ξίφους, προσφέρουσα στον Κύριο την τιμία αυτής κεφαλή», she was martyred by the sword, offering to the Lord her honourable head — with a couplet above it that says it again: that Euthasia offers her own head, taken by the sword, to God the Saviour of the world. There is no city, no year, no emperor and no hymn on the Greek page either.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 12 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/12-ianuarie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfintei-mucenite-eutasia); read 19 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 12 Ιανουαρίου — [the Greek day](https://www.saint.gr/01/12/index.aspx) and [the Greek life](https://www.saint.gr/1395/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026.*
