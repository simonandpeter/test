# Nicholas (Troitsky)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him for 25 февраля ст. ст. in five words — «Сщмч. Николая пресвитера (1945).» — a hieromartyr, a presbyter, who died in 1945. The name link behind the line carries the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line leaves out, and gives him a rank the line does not: «Николай (Троицкий), протоиерей, сщмч.», an archpriest of that name, one of the new martyrs of the Russian church in the Soviet years.

The year is the latest on this day's list and the last year of the war; the calendar says nothing of where he served or how he died, and prints no life page for him and no hymn under his name. Nothing further is written here, and nothing is reasoned out from the surname or from the year.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 25 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260225.html); read 3 October 2026.*
