# Potamon of Heraclea

saint.gr's calendar for 18 May names him and gives him four sentences. The holy hieromartyr Potamon was bishop of Heraclea in Egypt and a warm defender of the Orthodox faith and of [Athanasius the Great](/saints/athanasius-of-alexandria). In the year 310 he was arrested and imprisoned in the mines. He took part in the First Ecumenical Council and in the synod of Tyre in 335. He was martyred in the year 340, by the heretical followers of Arius.

The page says nothing else: no birthplace, no parents, no length of episcopate, no manner of death, no relics and no hymn. What it gives is a bishop who survived the mines under the pagan persecution and was killed thirty years later by Christians of another party, and who stood beside Athanasius at the two assemblies that decided the Arian question; that is what this folder records.
