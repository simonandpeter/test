# Honorius

The Greek synaxarion keeps Honorius, and says only that it has no details of the life of this English saint. It gives no date, no see and no place.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 30 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/1013/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
