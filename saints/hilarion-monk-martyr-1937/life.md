# Hilarion (Tsurikov), Hieromonk of Athos, Monk-martyr (1937)

Hilarion — Rodion Fyodorovich Tsurikov in the world — was born on 8 April 1856 in the village of Krivopolyany in the Ostrogozhsk district of Voronezh province, to a peasant family deeply religious — both his sisters became nuns — and he went to Athos, to the monastery of St Panteleimon, and was tonsured there with the name Hilarion. In 1913 he was taken off Athos in the affair of the Name-worshippers and tried, as he wrote himself in a questionnaire, by the tribunal of a court-martial and sentenced to death for the Name of God, freed for want of proof; coming to Moscow he entered the brotherhood of the Moscow Protection monastery, and after 1924 moved to the farmstead of Solyony, where he lived with the Athonite hieromonk [John (Laba)](/saints/john-monk-martyr-1937) — the calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. For confessing the faith he was arrested repeatedly and exiled; after his last arrest in 1937 he was charged with founding a secret monastery and counter-revolutionary activity, did not admit guilt, and was shot on 4 September 1937. He was numbered among the saints in 2000, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 22 August (Julian).
