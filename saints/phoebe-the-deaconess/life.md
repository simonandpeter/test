# Phoebe the Deaconess

Phoebe the deaconess is the Phoebe whom the apostle Paul commends in the Epistle to the Romans (16:1–2) — our sister, a deaconess of the church at Cenchreae, to be received in the Lord as befits the saints and helped in whatever she needs, for she has been a helper of many and of Paul himself. That is all the Sretensky calendar says. The Greek synaxarion notes that her memory stands for this day only in the Small Euchologion of the Apostoliki Diakonia (1956) and nowhere else, and takes her for the Phoebe of the epistle: a choice soul with many spiritual interests, so that when Paul visited Cenchreae — the entry runs on past the point read here.
