# Gerontius

Doxologia.ro's calendar for 1 aprilie names him and gives no life. The line on the day is «Sfântul Mucenic Gherontie» — the holy martyr Gerontius — and his own page on the site carries no life tab and no troparion, only a single sentence: «Sfântul Gherontie a suferit moarte mucenicească prin sabie», he suffered a martyr's death by the sword.

That is the whole of what the source says. It names no country and no city, no year and no emperor, no persecutor and no companions, and nothing of how he came to be a Christian or what was asked of him. The appointed readings the page sets beside his name are the common ones for a martyr, from the second letter to Timothy and from the farewell discourse in John.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and adds one thing the Romanian did not: a companion. saint.gr's whole notice is a single sentence — the holy Gerontius and [Basilides](/saints/basilides-companion-of-gerontius), or as others have it Basiliades, were martyred by the sword — and the distich over them says that Gerontius, by the sword together with Basilides, came to share in the Word who is King of all. There is still no year, no city, no persecutor and no relic, and the Greek page prints no hymn for him either.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 1 aprilie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/1-aprilie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-gherontie); read 19 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 1 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/158/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026.*
