# Constantine (Sukhov)

The Russian calendar's 9 October turns, in its third paragraph, from the apostles and the fathers to three priests of the Soviet years, and the first line names two of them together: «Сщмчч. Константина и Петра пресвитеров (1918)» — Constantine and Peter, hieromartyrs, presbyters, 1918. «Сщмч.» is the mark of a priest killed for the faith. The site's index of names supplies the surnames the day line leaves out, heading his own page «Константин (Сухов), иерей, сщмч.» — «иерей» being the simple priesthood.

1918 is the first year of the campaign against the clergy, and the calendar does not say what happened to him in it; it says nothing about him at all beyond the name, the rank, the office and the year. His index entry keeps him here and on 26 January with the whole company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and carries no life. The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian saints are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026 even with a browser user-agent, so the line stands as the line. [Peter (Vyatkin)](/saints/peter-vyatkin) is the other half of it.
