# Parthenius III

saint.gr's calendar for 24 Μαρτίου keeps a patriarch of Constantinople hanged by the Porte. This new hieromartyr came from Mytilene, the page says, and was the son of God-fearing parents; Parthenius gave himself to study and became one of the learned men of the seventeenth century. He was ordained priest, and later became a hierarch and shepherd of Chios, and at the end of 1656 patriarch of Constantinople, successor of the patriarch Parthenius II.

As metropolitan and as patriarch alike, the page says, he showed himself a reverent man, upright, learned and a zealot for good things and for piety, and as patriarch he did much for Greek education in particular. But a letter of his fell into the hands of the Sublime Porte, which held its contents to be revolutionary, and the sultan Ibrahim I ordered the patriarch Parthenius put to death. So he was arrested, and they pressed him to accept Islam and save himself; but Parthenius stayed unmoved in his faith.

Then, on Lazarus Saturday, 24 March 1657, they hanged him at the latticed gate Parmak Kapi. By order of the Grand Vizier Mehmet Köprülü the body was left hanging three days and was then thrown into the sea; later the Christians took it up and buried it on one of the islands «τη λεγομένη των Πριγκίπων», the one called the Princes'. The martyrdom of this saint, the page adds, was written by Meletius Syrigos, and its couplet says that a double crown was woven for Parthenius, as a priest and as an athlete of the Lord.
