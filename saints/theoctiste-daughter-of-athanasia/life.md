# Theoctiste, daughter of Athanasia

Theoctiste was the second of the three daughters of the widow [Athanasia](/saints/athanasia-the-widow) in the order saint.gr names them. She was arrested with her mother and with her sisters [Theodote](/saints/theodote-daughter-of-athanasia) and [Eudoxia](/saints/eudoxia-daughter-of-athanasia) in the persecution of Diocletian, and the four were brought before the governor and declared their faith in God with boldness.

What the page records of her is what she shared with them. The governor tried to bend her mother's courage by showing her her daughters and laying their guilt upon her; Athanasia turned to the three and told them that beauty of body lasts a little while, while the beauty of the soul is kept undying; and the daughters answered that they felt great joy, because they were to leave this vain world along with her for the love of Christ and never be parted from her. Then the governor ordered them put to many hard tortures, and so they bore witness, in the same year in which Cyrus and John were beheaded, 292.

saint.gr gives her no year of birth, no age and no separate manner of death, and prints no apolytikion and no kontakion for the four women; the hymns on that page are the unmercenaries'. Their life and martyrdom, it says, was written by Sophronius the Sophist, and their synaxis was kept in the martyrion raised in their honour in the quarter of Phorakion.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 31 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/31/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3576/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
