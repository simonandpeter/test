# George (Kossov)

George Alekseevich Kossov, a true confessor of the faith of Christ, was born on 4 April 1855 in the village of Androsovo in the Dmitrovsk district of Oryol province, in the middle of the nineteenth century, when Russians, especially in the villages, kept firmly and sacredly all the traditions of the nation’s piety. His parents were very devout — his father a priest, his mother raising the children — and named their firstborn for George the Victorious, and the boy’s heart took in many stories of wonders, healings and rescues; they strove to give him a good education — the calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. He served at Spas-Chekryak in Oryol province, where with the blessing of Ambrose of Optina he built a stone church and founded an orphanage, schools and a hospital, and became famous for the gifts of clairvoyance, healing and spiritual guidance; arrested more than once by the Soviet power, he stayed faithful to his pastoral service, and died in 1928. He was canonised in 2000, when his relics were found, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 26 August (Julian).
