# Timothy the Stylite

saint.gr's notice for him is one sentence: the venerable Timothy lived in a way that loved God, and fell asleep in peace in the year 872. Everything else the calendar gives is in the day's own line, which calls him «ο Στυλίτης», the stylite — so the pillar is the whole of what is known of how he lived.

Beyond that the page prints a gloss on the name, that Timothy is the one who honours God, the devout, and a list of three bearers of it out of Greek antiquity who are not saints. There is no birthplace, no pillar named, no hymn, and nothing is supplied here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 4 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/04/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1279/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
