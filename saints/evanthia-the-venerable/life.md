# Evanthia

Evanthia died in peace. That is the whole of the synaxarion’s notice, with its verse on the fair flowering of her name — blossoming in fragrant virtues.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 1 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/892/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
