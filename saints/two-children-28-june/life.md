# The Two Children

saint.gr's calendar for 28 Ιουνίου keeps two children whose names it does not give. Its line reads «Άγια Δύο Παιδιά», the holy two children, and the page behind that line says only that they were martyred by death on a cross — «Τα Άγια Δύο Παιδιά μαρτύρησαν δια σταυρικού θανάτου». No country is named, no century, no persecutor, no year and no relics, and the page prints no hymn.

The couplet of the synaxarion above it says where the memory of them comes from: «Eν τοις χρόνου πίναξι και Παίδας δύω, Σταυρουμένους γράφουσι παίδες ζωγράφων» — on the tablets of time the sons of the painters write two children also, being crucified. Because the source names neither of them, they are kept here as one entry and not two, and nothing is supplied that the page does not print.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/28/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1914/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
