# Anastasius of Paramythia

saint.gr's calendar for 18 November keeps «Άγιος Αναστάσιος ο Νεομάρτυρας από την Παραμυθιά της Ηπείρου», and dates his death 1750. The holy Anastasius came from Paramythia in Epirus. At harvest time he was out in the fields with his sister and with other Christians, and there he came to blows with some Turks who attacked his sister with evil intent.

Stung by that fight, the Turks slandered Anastasius to the pasha, claiming that he had given his word to change his faith. The pasha arrested him and pressed him to apostatise. To the pasha's offers Anastasius answered: he never gave such a word — a Christian I was born, a Christian I shall die, with the help of my Christ; and as for the goods you promise me, I care nothing at all for them, for I have many eternal goods, which are in the heavens and bear no comparison with the present ones.

By those words, the page says, Anastasius succeeded in making a Christian of the pasha's son, who was called Musa; some reports, it adds, say that he was renamed Demetrius and that he too was martyred for Christ. The saint himself, after being tortured in prison in the most dreadful way, was at the last beheaded outside Paramythia, near a monastery, on 18 November 1750; and the monks of that monastery buried his holy relic with honours.
