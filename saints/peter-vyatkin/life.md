# Peter (Vyatkin)

He is the second of the two priests the Russian calendar's 9 October names on one line: «Сщмчч. Константина и Петра пресвитеров (1918)» — Constantine and Peter, hieromartyrs, presbyters, 1918. The day line gives only the forenames, and the site's index of names is what tells the two apart: his own page is headed «Петр (Вяткин), иерей, сщмч.», Peter Vyatkin, a priest of the simple grade and a hieromartyr, which is to say a priest killed for the faith.

How the calendar keeps the two is itself the whole of what it records about their deaths: one year, one rank, one office, one day. It does not say whether they died together, or where, or on what charge. His index entry keeps him here and on 26 January with the whole company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and carries no life. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026 even with a browser user-agent, so the line stands as the line, and [Constantine (Sukhov)](/saints/constantine-sukhov) is its first half.

*After the Russian church calendar's [9 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261009.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9758.html); read 4 October 2026.*
