# Theodore of Pentapolis

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαρτίου names four men on one line — «Άγιοι Θεόδωρος ο Ιερομάρτυρας, Ειρηναίος ο Διάκονος, Σεραπίων και Αμμώνιος οι αναγνώστες», the holy Theodore the hieromartyr, Irenaeus the deacon, and Serapion and Ammonius the readers — and the page behind that line is one sentence long: «Ο Άγιος Ιερομάρτυς Θεόδωρος μαρτύρησε το έτος 304 μ.Χ. στην Πεντάπολη της Λιβύης μετά του Διακόνου του Ειρηναίου και των αναγνωστών Σεραπίωνος και Αμμωνίου.»

That is: the holy hieromartyr Theodore was martyred in the year 304 in the Pentapolis of Libya, together with his deacon [Irenaeus](/saints/irenaeus-of-pentapolis) and the readers [Serapion](/saints/serapion-of-pentapolis) and [Ammonius](/saints/ammonius-of-pentapolis). The sentence makes him the head of the group and the other three his clergy, which is all the source says about the order among them.

The page gives no city within the Pentapolis, no see for Theodore, no emperor, no judge, no manner of death, no relic and no hymn. It heads all four «(; - 304)», so not one of them has a year of birth. What else is on the page is a note about the name Θεόδωρος, the gift of God, and a list of the architects, philosophers and physicians who bore it; none of that is about him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3868/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
