# Anastasia of Rome, with Basilissa

Anastasia and [Basilissa](/saints/basilissa-of-rome) were of the great city of Rome, doxologia's notice says: well-born and rich, and disciples of the holy Apostles. After the Apostles' end the two women gathered up their holy relics by night and buried them, and for that they were denounced to the emperor Nero, who first put them in prison.

Brought out again and saying that they stood by their confession of Christ, they were hung up, and their breasts and their hands and their feet and their tongues were cut off; and at the last their heads were cut off with the sword.

That is the whole of what the Romanian calendar's life prints for her — four sentences shared with her companion. It gives no year beyond the name of Nero, no parentage, no burial place and no hymn; the page carries no troparion.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 15 aprilie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/15-aprilie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfintei-mucenite-anastasia-0); read 20 September 2026.*
