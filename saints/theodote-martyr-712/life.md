# Theodote (3 July)

saint.gr's calendar for 3 Ιουλίου names her and gives her a year and no more. Its whole account is «Η Άγια Θεοδότη μαρτύρησε το 712 μ.Χ. Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο της» — the holy Theodote was martyred in the year 712, and we have no further details of her life. No place, no emperor, no manner of death and no companion is printed, so none is recorded here.

The year is what keeps her apart from the Theodote the same day's calendar numbers among the six taken under Trajan, whom saint.gr heads «(; - 98)». These are two entries on one day of one calendar, and the corpus's rule is that two entries are two commemorations; the six hundred years between the two years the page prints is the plainest confirmation of it that a reader could ask for.
