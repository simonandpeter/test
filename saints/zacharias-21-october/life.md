# Zacharias (21 October)

saint.gr's calendar for 21 Οκτωβρίου names Zacharias and gives him no life. The page's one sentence of narrative is the whole of it: «Ο Άγιος Ζαχαρίας μαρτύρησε διά πνιγμού μέσα στη θάλασσα» — he was martyred by drowning in the sea. The couplet set over the entry takes up the same image from the psalms, saying that having passed through the paths of the seas he reaches the paths of heaven.

The page then does something it rarely does, and argues the identity itself: the Zacharias kept today, it says, is not the same as the one of 22 October, because the two have different couplets and because one is entitled a venerable-martyr and the other a martyr. It gives him no country, no year, no companions and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 21 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/21/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2559/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
