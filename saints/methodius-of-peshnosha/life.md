# Methodius of Peshnosha

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives him two sentences. The line for 4 June reads «Όσιος Μεθόδιος ο εκ Ρωσίας», the venerable Methodius of Russia, and the page behind it says this: the venerable Methodius lived in the fourteenth century after Christ in Russia and was abbot of the monastery of Peshnosha, which was near the town of Dmitrov in the province of Moscow; he practised asceticism in a manner pleasing to God and fell asleep in peace, in the year 1392.

So what is recorded of him is a country, a century, a monastery with a place beside it, an abbacy, and a year. The page says nothing of who tonsured him, nothing of whether he founded the house or inherited it, nothing of a disciple or a wonder or a relic, and it prints neither verse nor hymn. The Russian form of his name is not on the page and is not invented here.
