# Abramius of Arbela

doxologia.ro gives him four short paragraphs, and this is all of them. Abramius, bishop of Arbela, suffered martyrdom in the persecution the Christians endured under the emperor Shapur II. When he was told to renounce Christ and worship the sun, he answered: "How senseless it is to persecute Christ and to worship the creatures! Is the sun not a thing made by my God?"

After that he suffered terrible beatings and was tortured, and all the while he prayed, saying the words the Saviour himself had said: "Lord, lay not this sin to their charge, for they know not what they do." Immediately afterwards he was beheaded with the sword in the city of Felman. The page closes with a prayer, and gives no year for his birth, none for his death, and no other circumstance of his life.
