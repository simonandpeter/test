# Abramius of Arbela

doxologia.ro gives him four short paragraphs, and this is all of them. Abramius, bishop of Arbela, suffered martyrdom in the persecution the Christians endured under the emperor Shapur II. When he was told to renounce Christ and worship the sun, he answered: "How senseless it is to persecute Christ and to worship the creatures! Is the sun not a thing made by my God?"

After that he suffered terrible beatings and was tortured, and all the while he prayed, saying the words the Saviour himself had said: "Lord, lay not this sin to their charge, for they know not what they do." Immediately afterwards he was beheaded with the sword in the city of Felman. The page closes with a prayer, and gives no year for his birth, none for his death, and no other circumstance of his life.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and gives him a century the Romanian page did not: the fifth, under king Shapur. It locates the see for its reader — the ancient Arbela of Assyria in Mesopotamia, some ninety kilometres south-east of Mosul near the Iraqi-Persian border, «Τώρα ανήκει στο Ιράκ και ονομάζεται Ερμπίλ», now in Iraq and called Erbil. And it names his arrester and his words. In the fifth year of the persecution in Persia he was taken by the king's chief magus, Adelphoras, who pressed him with threats and promises to deny Christ and sacrifice to the idols; and the saint answered him: wretch and miserable man, are you not afraid, urging me to do what must not be done? Do you think it natural that I should deny the Creator and worship his creature and handiwork? They beat him with knotted rods, and while they struck he prayed, «Κύριε, μην τους λογαριάσεις αυτή την αμαρτία, δεν ξέρουν τι κάνουν» — Lord, do not reckon them this sin, they do not know what they are doing — and at every torture called on Christ to help his servant. At that the chief magus ordered him beheaded with the sword.
