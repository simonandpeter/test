# Marinos Vaanes

saint.gr begins with what is not known: the memory of the venerable Marinos Vaanes is unknown to the synaxarists, and is written down by Gedeon in the Byzantine Eortologion, at page 110. Everything the page then says about him it says on that one authority, and it says it in four sentences.

There it is recorded that he was born at Constantinople of notable parents, the droungarios Nikephoros and Maria; that he was brought up in piety at Bizye in Thrace; and that he was tonsured a monk by his brother Symeon, who was coming down from Kyminas. He was devout, merciful and most given to asceticism. Having lived in a holy manner, he died in peace, and his honourable relics were laid in the monastery of the Theotokos of Korone.

The page puts no year to any of that — not to his birth, not to his tonsure, not to his death — and the corpus adds none. It prints no hymn for him either.
