# Kyntion of Seleucia

saint.gr's calendar for 15 November keeps «Όσιος Κυντίων επίσκοπος Σελευκείας», and its whole notice of him is three sentences. The venerable Kyntion — or Kyntirion, or Kyntianos, or Kyntirianos, for the page offers all four spellings — bishop of Seleucia, was one of the three hundred and eighteen Fathers of the First Ecumenical Council at Nicaea in Bithynia. He was ascetic, the page says, but also a wonderworker. He died in peace.

That is the whole of the source. It gives no year of his own, no account of his see, no miracle of his in particular and no hymn; the verse over the entry says only that Kyntion the sacrificer increased the gift he was given, and so heard, well done, good servant — and what more is there than that? The council is the one thing that places him, and this folder records it in prose and infers no date from it.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 15 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/15/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2915/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
