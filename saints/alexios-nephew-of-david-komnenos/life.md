# Alexios, nephew of David the Great Komnenos

saint.gr's calendar for 1 Νοεμβρίου keeps five of one household: «Άγιος Δαβίδ ο Μέγας Κομνηνός ο νεομάρτυρας και η συνοδεία του Βασίλειος, Γεώργιος, Μανουήλ οι υιοί του και Αλέξιος ο ανεψιός του». Alexios was the nephew of [David the Great Komnenos](/saints/david-the-great-komnenos), the last emperor of Trebizond, and his heir: the page calls him «τον ανεψιό του και διάδοχο Αλέξιο τον Ε», Alexios the Fifth.

After Mehmet the Conqueror took Trebizond on 15 August 1461, the page says, the emperor was held hostage with his three children and with this nephew, and moved to Adrianople. On 26 March 1463 he was seized there and shut in chains in the Tower, as the synaxarial note the page quotes from a gospel-book of the monastery of the Theotokos on Halki records.

The same note gives the end: on the first of November, a Sunday, at the fourth hour of the night, he was finished by the sword together with his three sons and his nephew, in the year 1463, at Constantinople. Brought before Mehmet, the emperor had been offered his life for his faith, or death for himself and his whole family, and had chosen the second — and so the heir of Trebizond died with the three sons, [Basil](/saints/basil-son-of-david-komnenos), [George](/saints/george-son-of-david-komnenos) and [Manuel](/saints/manuel-son-of-david-komnenos). No hymn is printed, and the page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου».

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/01/index.aspx) and [the life of the five](https://www.saint.gr/4333/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
