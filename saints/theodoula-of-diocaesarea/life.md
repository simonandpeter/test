# Theodoula of Diocaesarea

saint.gr's calendar for 18 Ιανουαρίου keeps Theodoula together with those who came to believe through her — Evagrius, Macarius, Helladius the commentaresios, and Boethus. Theodoula lived in the years of Diocletian, whom the page dates 284 to 305, and she was from Diocaesarea of Cilicia. When the prefect Pelagius pressed her with violence to deny Christ, she set against him that she was a Christian and would remain one.

Pelagius then put her to tortures, over which her woman's nature rose by the power of divine grace; and such was the impression made by her most modest and heroic bearing that the prefect's own men, who had been among her tormentors before, declared to him that they acknowledged Jesus Christ as their God. So he threw her into the furnace, together with Evagrius and Macarius. Two others, Helladius and Boethus, were beheaded. The page's headings give the year of all five as 298.

Theodoula is the first of the five, and the other four are named on the day only as those who believed through her: [Evagrius](/saints/evagrius-companion-of-theodoula), [Macarius](/saints/macarius-companion-of-theodoula), [Helladius](/saints/helladius-the-commentaresios) and [Boethus](/saints/boethus-companion-of-theodoula). The epigram set over her on the page reads «Σύνδουλον ουκ έφριττε πυρ Θεοδούλη, Θεώ τιθείσα δουλικώς τας ελπίδας».

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1126/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
