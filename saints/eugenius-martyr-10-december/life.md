# Eugenius (10 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 10 December names Eugenius and gives him one line of life: the holy Eugenius was martyred after they beat him with sticks until he died. The page prints no country, no reign, no persecutor and no year, and it joins him to no other name on the day. What it adds is the couplet set over his name, and the couplet plays four times on the one word for wood.

As though he were himself put together out of wood, it says, Eugenius suffering by woods was wood towards woods. That is the whole of the entry. The corpus keeps several other men of this name on other days, and nothing here joins this one to any of them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/10/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3219/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
