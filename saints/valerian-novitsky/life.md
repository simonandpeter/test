# Valerian (Novitsky)

He was born in June 1897 into a priest's family and studied at the Minsk theological seminary. When it was closed in 1918 he entered the law faculty of the Belarusian University, but he did not study there long: in 1923 he took holy orders and was appointed rector of the church of the Holy Trinity at Telyadovichi in the Kopyl district, where his father had served before him. He served zealously, saying that the faith had to be saved, and he rebuked the peasants who went to the so-called anti-religious circles, where mocking plays were put on.

He was arrested in January 1930 and shut up in the prison at Slutsk. There he was offered his freedom in exchange for renouncing his orders, and he refused; his wife held him to that refusal. On 23 February 1930 he was sentenced to be shot. They took him out to the Timkovichi wood and put the offer to him again, and when he refused a second time they ordered him to dig his grave and then shot him.

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 10 февраля ст. ст. in one line with another priest shot the same day.
