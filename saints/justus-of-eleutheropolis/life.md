# Justus of Eleutheropolis

saint.gr's calendar for 30 Οκτωβρίου keeps «Άγιοι Τέρτιος, Μάρκος, Ιούστος και Αρτεμάς οι Απόστολοι από τους Εβδομήκοντα» — four of the Seventy in one entry, each with a see of his own. Of this one the page says: «Ο Ιούστος (ή Ιησούς) έγινε επίσκοπος Ελευθερούπολης» — Justus, or Jesus, became bishop of Eleutheropolis; and by his words and his wonders he drew the unbelievers of that place to the truth of the Gospel.

The page gives him two names and treats them as one man's: the day's line calls him Ιούστος, the notice offers Ιησούς beside it, and the epigram over him is headed «Eις τον Iησούν» and plays on the second of them — having found Jesus, Jesus found salvation, and was shown a saviour to others, true to his name.

That is all of it. There is no year, no birthplace, no account of his death, no relics and no hymn. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands.

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him with three other apostles of the Seventy on the Julian 30 October, and it gives him two more names, a parentage and a death the Greek page had not. Its own page for him is headed «Святой Варсава (Иосиф, Иуст), апостол от 70-ти»: he was the son of [Joseph the Betrothed](/saints/joseph-the-betrothed), and one of the two disciples of Christ put forward for the apostleship in place of Judas Iscariot — and he was not the one chosen, because the lot fell on [Matthias](/saints/matthias-the-apostle), as Acts 1.23-26 tells it.

Afterwards, both of its pages say, he was a bishop and ended his life a martyr at Eleutheropolis. The see is the one the Greek page gave this folder, and it is how the two entries are read as one man; the martyrdom is new, since saint.gr gave no account of his death at all. The day's line closes the four of them with «(I)», the first century, and no page read for him gives a year.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 30 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/30/index.aspx) and [the notice of the four](https://www.saint.gr/2847/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 30 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261030.html), [the life of the four](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4615.htm) and [his own life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life6486.htm), read 4 October 2026.*
