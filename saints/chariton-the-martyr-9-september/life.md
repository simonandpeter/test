# Chariton (9 September)

Chariton was martyred by the sword, and his synaxis is celebrated “in the Deuteron”. That is the whole of the Greek synaxarion’s notice for 9 September; its verse says, much grace to you, martyr of Christ Chariton, your neck cut off for the grace of Christ.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 9 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/990/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
