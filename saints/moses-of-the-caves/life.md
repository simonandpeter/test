# Moses of the Caves

saint.gr's whole notice of him is three short sentences, and this is all of them. The venerable Moses lived as an ascetic in the Lavra of the Caves. He wore chains and a heavy cross. God counted him worthy of the gift of working wonders.

The day's line calls him «ο θαυματουργός», the wonderworker, and the page glosses the name Moses as a Hebrew word meaning the one saved out of the waters. Beyond that it gives no century, no country, no parents, no account of his repose, no relics, no distich and no hymn. Its heading gives the feast as fixed, kept on 28 July of every year, and the entry carries no asterisk. This folder says no more than the page does.
