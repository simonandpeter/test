# Anthony of Tobolsk

saint.gr's calendar for 27 Μαρτίου names Anthony, metropolitan of Tobolsk, and heads him «(; - 1740)». Then it says plainly that it knows no more: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ρώσου Αγίου» — we have no details of the life of the Russian saint. So the record is a name, a see at the far end of Siberia, a year of death in the page's heading, and nothing else: no birthplace, no monastic house, no year of consecration, no work among the peoples of the Ob, no relic and no hymn.

The one further thing the page does is set him among his own: it links the entry to the Synaxis of all the Saints of Siberia, the common feast under which the metropolitans of Tobolsk are kept together. That is a pointer and not a fact about him, and no part of the Synaxis's own contents is imported here.

The note the page attaches to the name — «ΑΝΤΩΝΙΟΣ (αντί + ωνούμαι) = ο πλειοδότης σε προσφορά αξίας», the one who bids highest — is about the word. A Russian source would give this metropolitan a life; none was read for this folder, and the year 1740 stands on the page's heading alone.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 27 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/27/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4242/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
