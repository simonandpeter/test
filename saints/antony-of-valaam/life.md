# Antony of Valaam

saint.gr's calendar names him Antony of the monastery of Valaam and gives him two sentences: «Ο Άγιος Αντώνιος της Μονής Βαλαάμ ήταν δια Χριστόν σαλός. Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου.» — the holy Antony of the monastery of Valaam was a fool for Christ; we have no other details of the saint's life.

That is the whole of what the Greek calendar keeps of him: the monastery, the manner of his ascetic life, and the admission that nothing further is known. The page gives no century, no year, no name in the world, no account of his repose and no relics, and it prints no hymn and not even a couplet.
