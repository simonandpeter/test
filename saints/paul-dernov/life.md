# Paul (Dernov)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 14 февраля ст. ст. in a line that names a priest and his three sons together and gives the year all four died: «Сщмч. Павла Дернова пресвитера и сыновей его мчч. Бориса, Григория и Симеона (1918).» The rank word over him is «Сщмч.», hieromartyr, and «пресвитера» gives him as a presbyter; the sons are marked off with their own rank word, «мчч.», martyrs. The line is one of the few here that prints the surname itself, Дернов, and the name link behind it adds that he was an archpriest.

The sons the line names after him are [Boris (Dernov)](/saints/boris-dernov), [Gregory (Dernov)](/saints/gregory-dernov) and [Simeon (Dernov)](/saints/simeon-dernov), and the calendar says of them only that they were his sons and that they died in the same year as their father.

The calendar prints no life page for any of the four and no hymn under any of their names; the troparia on the day page are for other saints it heads. Nothing further is written here, and in particular he is not identified with any saint of the same name whom the calendar keeps on another day. The line, its two rank words, the surname and the year 1918 are the whole of what was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 14 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260214.html); read 3 October 2026.*
