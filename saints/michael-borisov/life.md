# Michael (Borisov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and gives no life. Its line for 22 мая ст. ст. reads «Сщмч. Михаила пресвитера (1942).» — the hieromartyr Michael, presbyter, of the year 1942 — and the line carries a link to his name and none to a reading. The name page behind it adds his family name, Борисов, and writes his grade as «протоиерей», archpriest, where the day line had said presbyter. Beyond the title it carries one line, «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 22 мая», and nothing further.

So the calendar sets down a priest of the Russian church who died in 1942 and no more than that. The second of those two days is not a feast of his own: it is the general commemoration of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which this calendar prints beside the proper day of each of them, and it is the one mark on his page that places him among that company rather than among the martyrs of an older persecution. He is kept here on the day the calendar gives him for his own, and the synaxis is recorded on his attestation.

The same calendar keeps another presbyter of the same year, and of the same forename, four days earlier in its month; the two have different name pages and different surnames, and nothing read joins them. Nothing else about this one was found. No folder in this corpus held the name before, and the one other source likely to carry an account of a parish priest of 1942 — azbyka.ru — was not read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 22 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260522.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/11135.html), which name him and give no life; read 8 October 2026.*
