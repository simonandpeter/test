# Paul of Ioannina

saint.gr says he was born at Ioannina about the end of the eighteenth century, and that his name in the world was Peter. He was left fatherless while still small, and his mother sent him to the Greek school of Balanos to be taught sacred letters by Anastasios Balanos, called Kamikaris. He loved the services and took his part in the life of the church and in the holy offices at the church of Saint John of Bounila, or of the Panagia Perivleptos.

Through the lady Vasiliki, a favourite of Ali Pasha, Peter came to know the fathers of the monastery of Konstamonitou on the Holy Mountain — the abbot Chrysanthos and the hieromartyr [Benedict](/saints/benedict-of-konstamonitou), whom saint.gr keeps on this same day — who had come to Ioannina on their monastery's business. When they went back to the Holy Mountain the saint went with them, and after a probation he was tonsured a monk at Konstamonitou and received the name Paul.

He was seized together with other monks of his house during the Revolution of 1821, by Amblut Rubut Pasha, when Alexandros Ypsilantis urged Emmanouil Papas, the leader of Macedonia, to begin the rising from the Holy Mountain; and he was taken to Thessalonica. There, after frightful and barbarous tortures, he came to a martyr's end, in the year 1824.

The page keeps him with three other monks of Konstamonitou on this day — [Synesius](/saints/synesius-of-triglia), Benedict and [Timothy](/saints/timothy-of-konstamonitou) — and prints for him no synaxarion verses and no hymn.
