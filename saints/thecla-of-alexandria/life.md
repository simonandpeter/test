# Thecla of Alexandria

Thecla and [Andropelagia](/saints/andropelagia-of-alexandria) were virgin
sisters, seized at Alexandria in Egypt by the governor Valerian, who put
them to harsh tortures and at last beheaded them; the verse says
Andropelagia was as manly as Thecla towards the cutting off of her head.
The synaxarion adds that the name Andropelagia is probably a fusion of two
names, Andrew or Andronicus and Pelagia, since the Patmos codex 266
records the memory of the virgin sisters Pelagia and Thecla and, lower
down, of Andronicus the soldier; some synaxaria keep the memory of these
saints also on 3 November and on the Sunday of All Saints. The Greek
synaxarion keeps the martyrs of Alexandria under Decius in separate
entries on 6 September; the Russian calendar counts them as the eleven
with the presbyter Faustus and the deacon Abibus.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 6 Σεπτεμβρίου —
[the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2364/saint.aspx), read 5 September 2026 — this
time whole; the earlier reading had stopped partway.*
