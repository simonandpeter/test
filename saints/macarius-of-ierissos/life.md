# Macarius of Ierissos

saint.gr says that Macarius lived in the years of the emperors [Constantine the Great](/saints/constantine-the-great), whose reign it dates 324 to 337, Theodosius, 379 to 395, and Arcadius, 395 to 408. He was, in all probability, the first Bishop of Ierissos in Chalcidice, and he is held to be the founder of the church of Saint Stephen at the monastery of Konstamonitou on the Holy Mountain.

In the first years of his episcopal ministry, the page says, he met Constantine the Great, who was seeking to build the New Rome near the Acanthian isthmus, which lies close to Ierissos and to the Holy Mountain. By the wisdom of his words he persuaded the emperor not to go on with that plan, and so preserved the quiet of the region, and the Holy Mountain in particular. Under Julian the Apostate he was persecuted and took refuge on Athos, and he is reckoned the second founder of the monastery of Konstamonitou.

He fell asleep in peace, holily, under the reign of Arcadius. The page gives him no year of birth and no year of death, and prints no hymn for him.
