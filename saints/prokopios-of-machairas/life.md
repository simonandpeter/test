# Prokopios of Machairas

saint.gr's calendar for 13 December keeps four founders of the monastery of the Panagia of Machairas in Cyprus together, and Prokopios is the third of them. The page says that when the venerable [Neophytos](/saints/neophytos-of-machairas) fell asleep, another aged monk, Prokopios, came to [Ignatios](/saints/ignatios-of-machairas) — the man who with Neophytos had found the icon of the Panagia the Hagiosoritissa in a cave on the mountainside where the monastery now stands.

The two fathers decided, once the brotherhood had grown numerous, to raise a monastery that would be run on the coenobitic pattern the great monastic centres of the time followed; and for that they went to Constantinople, where they met the emperor Manuel Komnenos, whose reign the page dates 1143 to 1180, and set their wish before him. The emperor, who loved Christ, granted them not only the money they needed but gave the new house the mountain it was to be built on together with the country round about it.

He gave it the stavropegion as well, and the privilege of being free and untaxed by the state and by private persons; the page adds that these privileges were later confirmed by the emperors Isaac and Alexios of the house of Angelos. The monastery stands at the eastern end of the Troodos range near the peak of Kionia, at a height of 870 metres, and keeps its own feast at the Entry of the Theotokos, on 21 November. Its first enthroned abbot was [Neilos](/saints/neilos-of-machairas). For Prokopios himself the page gives no year of birth and no year of death.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 13 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/13/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3252/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
