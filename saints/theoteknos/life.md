# Theoteknos

saint.gr's calendar for 10 December names Theoteknos and gives him one line of life: the holy Theoteknos was martyred by the sword. The page prints no country, no reign, no persecutor and no year, and it joins him to no other name on the day. What it adds is the couplet set over his name, which reads his name as a description of his death.

Theoteknos, as a child of God, dies readily by the sword for the Father's sake. That is the whole of the entry, and nothing is claimed here beyond it. The corpus holds no other folder of this name against which he would have to be told apart.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/10/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3217/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
