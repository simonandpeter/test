# Photius, companion of Cyril

saint.gr names the holy Photius with [Cyril](/saints/cyril-companion-of-photius) on this day, and the whole of what it has for either of them is one hedged sentence: «Οι Άγιοι Φώτιος και Κύριλλος μάλλον άνηκαν στους 152 Μάρτυρες, που μαρτύρησαν μαζί με τον Άγιο Αρχέλαο» — the holy Photius and Cyril probably belonged to the hundred and fifty-two martyrs who were martyred with St [Archelaus](/saints/archelaus-with-152-martyrs). That «μάλλον», probably, is the site's own guess about their company, and it is recorded here rather than acted on.

So two things stand: the name, and a pairing with one other name on the same day. There is no country, no city, no century, no year, no emperor, no judge, no manner of death, no relic, no distich and no hymn; the sword and the number belong to the page about Archelaus, and whether these two were among that number the site will not say.

What the page does print over the name is its usual etymology block, which is a note on the word and not a fact about the man. Nothing else is written here than the site prints, and the site prints one sentence.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3840/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
