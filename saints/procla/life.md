# Procla, wife of Pilate

saint.gr's calendar for 27 Οκτωβρίου names Procla, the wife of Pilate, and gives her two sentences and a couplet. While her husband did not take the responsibility of freeing Christ, the page says, for fear of the Jews, his wife Procla came after Pilate's dreadful death to the Christian faith; and having lived in goodness and piety she gave up her spirit in peace.

The couplet above tells her that the Master now has her standing beside him, he who once stood before Pilate her husband. The page gives her no year, no place, no family beyond her husband and no hymn, and it does not repeat the dream the Gospel of Matthew gives her.
