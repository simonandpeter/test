# Mnason of Cyprus

saint.gr's calendar for 18 October names «Άγιος Μνάσων επίσκοπος Κύπρου», Mnason bishop of Cyprus, and its whole notice of him is one sentence: the holy Mnason, bishop of Cyprus, was martyred by the sword. The calendar gives him no year, no city of his own and no hymn, and the verse over his entry says only that Mnason, yielding like a tower and bowing his head, awaits a fall from the sword before he falls to the ground.

The page then adds a conjecture of its own, and it is worth repeating exactly: it is possible, it says, that he is the same saint who is kept on 19 October as Mnason the ancient disciple, although that one fell asleep in peace. The two notices disagree about how the man died, and the calendar prints them as two entries on two days; so this folder keeps the bishop of Cyprus whom the 18 October line names, and the identification the page suggests is left where the page leaves it, as a possibility.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2763/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
