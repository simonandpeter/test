# Marina of Raithou

saint.gr's calendar for 21 Οκτωβρίου names [Plantinos](/saints/plantinos), [Irene](/saints/irene-companion-of-plantinos) and Marina together, and for a life it gives a citation rather than a story. The memory of Plantinos, the page says, is recorded in the Jerusalem Kanonarion at page 115, accompanied there by that of Saint Irene and of Saint Marina of Raithou. That is the whole of the page's own authority for the three of them.

For Marina the page quotes a sticheron out of the office of [Philotheus the Athonite](/saints/philotheus-of-dionysiou), composed by the monk Gerasimos Mikragiannanites, which is sung on this same day. It calls her an all-modest virgin brought today into the life above, who threw down the assaults of the demons, endured sufferings for Christ's sake, destroyed the crooked dragon utterly and kept her lamp unquenched; and it says that her honoured coffin of relics lies in a reliquary at the dependency where the Sinaite fathers now dwell, at the monastery of the Most Holy Theotokos beside the sea, where God-loving lay people also live.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 21 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/21/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2554/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
