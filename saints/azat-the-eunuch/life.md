# Azat the Eunuch

saint.gr gives him four sentences. He was a eunuch of the Persian king whom the page calls Savorios, rich, and much loved by the king for his devotion to him. He confessed Christ and suffered a martyr's death, together with a thousand other martyrs. But the king repented so far of the death of his faithful and beloved eunuch, the page says, that he gave orders for the persecution of the Christians to stop.

That is the whole of the notice. It names no city, no judge, no manner of death and no year, and it prints neither couplet nor hymn. The same calendar keeps on this same day the bishop Simeon of Persia and the 1150 killed with him under the king it names in the same two ways, Savorios and Shapur II; whether Azat's thousand are those 1150 the page nowhere says.

**The Russian calendar keeps him too, on its own 14 апреля and again on the Greek calendar's 17 апреля, and it is the first source read for this folder to put a year on him.** Its line is «Мчч. 1000 Персидских и Азата скопца (341).», and the life behind it is the same account saint.gr gives in different words: he was a eunuch who held a high place at the royal court and had the particular love and respect of the Persian king — the king this calendar calls Sapor, where the Greek page has Savorios — and for boldly confessing the faith of Christ he was executed together with a thousand martyrs. The king was greatly grieved by his death, and after it issued an edict forbidding Christians to be put to death, which held good for a time.

The years do not agree with each other. The line prints 341 and the life prints 344, and the second life the same index serves — of [Simeon of Persia](/saints/simeon-of-persia), the bishop of Seleucia-Ctesiphon, and the company killed with him — also puts the tax edict and the persecution in 344, has the bishop beheaded on 13 April of that year, and says the executions went on through the whole of Pascha week until 23 April. That second life names this eunuch among the sufferers and reports that a thousand martyrs suffered, then another hundred, then another hundred and fifty. The [Thousand Persian Martyrs](/saints/thousand-persian-martyrs) of this day's line have a folder of their own, and the two readings of the year are held here as an interval with neither chosen.

The man kept on this day is not the eunuch Azat of 20 ноября ст. ст., whom the same calendar names in the company of the bishop Nirsa and whose own folder stores a different Russian form; another company, another day, and nothing read links the two.
