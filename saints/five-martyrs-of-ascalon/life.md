# The Five Martyrs of Ascalon

saint.gr's calendar for 31 Μαΐου names them and gives one sentence. These five holy martyrs, it says, were perfected in martyrdom after being dragged along the ground until they died. The place is in the calendar's own line and not in the sentence: εν Ασκαλώνι, at Ascalon on the coast of Palestine.

Not one of the five is named, and the page does not say under what emperor or what governor they suffered, nor in what year, nor whether they were soldiers or citizens. The distich printed over them calls them men — garments were spread before the Lord once, it says, and now a strange spreading of five dragged men — and that is the only further word about them. Because the calendar names no member of the company, this is one folder for the five and not five folders.
