# Callistratus of Timișeni

He came from Burdujeni, in Suceava, and was tonsured a monk at the hermitage of Sihăstria Neamțului in 1925. In 1931 he received ordination to the priesthood and served at the monastery of his tonsure for twelve years. In 1942 he was sent as a missionary priest into the country of the Banat, to bring back to life the Orthodox monasticism of that part of Romania; and in 1945 he founded the women's monastery of Timișeni, while at the same time caring for the spiritual guidance of those living in the monasteries of Săraca, Partoș and Vasiova.

Little by little he became the most sought-after spiritual father of the Banat, both for the genuineness of his service at the altar and, above all, for the comfort he gave to souls; and he was sought out, the page says, for the gift of healing and of driving unclean spirits out of people. A headline the site keeps beside the life names him as Protosinghel Calistrat Bobu and repeats that he had the gift of healing and of casting out unclean spirits.

For thirty-three years he was a torch always alight for the monks and the faithful of the Banat. He passed to the Lord on the day of Pascha in the year 1975, and was buried beside the church of the monastery of Vasiova. This, in full, is what doxologia.ro prints about him; the page the site calls his troparion is an audio recording and prints no hymn text.
