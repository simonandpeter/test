# Manuel (21 July)

saint.gr's calendar for 21 Ιουλίου names the venerable Manuel, emperor of Constantinople, and its page says of him that by the angelic habit — that is, the monastic one — he was renamed Matthew, in 1425. Then it says plainly what the books do and do not hold: his memory is unknown to the Synaxaristae.

An Akolouthia was composed for him by somebody unknown, which Lambros published in the Neos Hellenomnemon, volume 14 of 1917, pages 318 to 341, together with two canons, but without a synaxarion note. That is the whole of the page. It prints no life, no hymn, no year of his birth and none of his death, and it does not say where or when he died or what he had been as emperor.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 21 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/21/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2030/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
