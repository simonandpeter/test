# Matrona of Anemnyasevo

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Matrona among the blessed on 16 July, grades her a confessor, and gives no life: its line is «Блж. Матроны Анемнясевской, исп (1936).» and its name page adds only the surname, «Матрона (Белякова) Анемнясевская, исп.»

What the calendar does print for her is two hymns, and they are where the little that is recorded of her stands. The troparion calls her a blessed eldress who shone in the land of Ryazan like a flash of heavenly fire, and asks through her intercession for patience in sicknesses, troubles and sorrows. The kontakion says more: that she found strength in weakness and incorruptible eyes in blindness, that she remained upon a bed of sickness and yet was borne everywhere in spirit like a bird, that she was an infant in body, and that she was a mother to the sorrowing and the storm-tossed. Its heading gives her a second place beside Anemnyasevo — Kasimov.

She died in 1936. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read: the calendar does not say how she died, and it calls her a confessor rather than a martyr.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 16 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260716.html); read 9 October 2026.*
