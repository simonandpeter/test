# Seraphim (Shchelokov)

The Russian calendar's 8 October names a company of eleven on one line: «Сщмчч. Ионы , еп. Велижского, прмч. Серафима , сщмчч. Петра , Василия , Павла , Петра , Владимира пресвитеров, мчч. Виктора , Иоанна , Николая и мц. Елисаветы (1937)». Seraphim is in it, and the site's index of names gives the surname: «Серафим (Щелоков), архимандрит, прмч.». He is the one «прмч.» of the line, a venerable-martyr: a monastic killed for the faith rather than a priest of the parishes.

The day line sets him alone between the bishop and the five priests, and his index entry adds the office the line leaves out: archimandrite. Nothing else is printed — no year of birth, no house, no charge, no place of death.

The site carries a life for one of the eleven, the bishop [Jonah (Lazarev)](/saints/jonah-lazarev), and for the other ten it carries none. His index entry keeps him here and on 26 January with the whole company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and the year 1937 at the end of the line is the only date on either page. The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian saints are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026 even with a browser user-agent, so the line stands as the line.

*After the Russian church calendar's [8 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261008.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13818.html); read 4 October 2026.*
