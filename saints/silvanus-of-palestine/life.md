# Silvanus of Palestine

The Greek calendar names him on 18 Ιανουαρίου as «Όσιος Σιλβανός ο εν Παλαιστίνη ασκήσας» — the venerable Silvanus who practised the ascetic life in Palestine — and the page behind that line says in one sentence that there is no more: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Οσίου.» The line itself is therefore the whole of the account: a name, the rank *Όσιος*, and the country in which he laboured.

No century is given, no monastery, no teacher and no year of repose, and no hymn is printed for him. The calendar keeps several men of this name on other days; this folder is for the one the Greek day of 18 January names, and nothing from the others is carried over to him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1295/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
