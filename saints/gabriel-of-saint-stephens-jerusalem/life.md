# Gabriel of Saint Stephen's in Jerusalem

saint.gr's calendar for 26 January names Gabriel and gives one sentence of life: that he was a monk and abbot of the monastery of Saint Stephen in Jerusalem, and that he fell asleep in holiness and in peace. Nothing else about the man is on the page — no homeland, no parentage, no year of birth or of death, no length of his abbacy, and no troparion or kontakion.

What else the page carries is the etymology it gives for the name Gabriel and a sentence about the archangel who bears it, which are notes on the name and not on this man. Above the sentence stands a couplet that plays on the two Gabriels: «Σὺν τῷ Γαβριὴλ τῷ Νόων πρωτοστάτῃ, / Καὶ Γαβριὴλ ἵστησι Χριστὸν τὸν νέον» — along with the Gabriel who leads the ranks of the minds, Christ sets up this newer Gabriel too.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1170/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
