# John (Uspensky)

The Russian calendar's line is all there is of him. It names him on this day with one other, «Сщмчч. Иоанна и Евфимия пресвитеров (1938)» — the hieromartyrs John and Euthymius, presbyters, 1938 — and the name behind his half of the line reads «Иоанн (Успенский), иерей, сщмч.», John Uspensky, priest, hieromartyr. That is his surname, his rank and the year of his death, and the calendar gives nothing else.

There is no life page behind his name, where there is one behind [Euthymius](/saints/euthymius-tikhonravov)'s, and no hymn for him on the day. Nothing here says where he served, where he was arrested or where he was shot, and the year 1938 is the calendar's own and the only date it prints. He is one of the new martyrs of Russia, kept with the priest the same line names beside him.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 22 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260122.html); read 3 October 2026.*
