# James

Yakov Ivanovich Gortinsky was born in 1899. He lived in the settlement of Gnilitsy in the Avtozavod district of the city of Gorky and worked as a mechanic at the hospital. In August 1937 he was arrested, together with the nun [Anna Ezhova](/saints/anna-nun-martyr-1937), for taking part in a church counter-revolutionary group; he admitted no guilt. On 21 August 1937 the special troika of the Gorky NKVD sentenced him to be shot; he was executed on 29 August and buried in an unknown grave. He was glorified at the Jubilee Council of 2000. The Russian calendar keeps him on 16 August (Julian) with the presbyter [Alexander](/saints/alexander-presbyter-martyr-1937) and the nun Anna; the Sretensky calendar prints no life for him, and this one follows azbyka.ru, from the life compiled by Igumen Damaskin (Orlovsky).

*After the life printed by the Russian church calendar at azbyka.ru — [the entry](https://azbyka.ru/days/sv-iakov-gortinskij); read 23 August 2026.*
