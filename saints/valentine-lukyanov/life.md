# Valentine (Lukyanov)

The Russian calendar names him and nothing more. Its line for 19 мая ст. ст. reads «Прмч. Валентина (1940)» — the venerable-martyr Valentine, with the year — and the name link behind it gives what the line leaves out: «Валентин (Лукьянов), иеромонах, прмч.», the surname and the grade of hieromonk. That page carries a heading and two days and not a sentence of narrative: no monastery, no birthplace, no year of birth, nothing of his arrest or of his death. The first of its two days, 26 января, is the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, a company's feast and not a day of his own.

So this folder asserts what the calendar asserts: that the Russian church keeps a hieromonk of this name and surname as a venerable-martyr on this day, and that he died in 1940. He is the last by year of the twentieth-century entries the day holds, standing after a priest of 1919, another of 1937 and the fourteen of 1938. What a second source would add is everything, and azbyka.ru was not read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 19 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260519.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14259.html), which carries no life; read 8 October 2026.*
