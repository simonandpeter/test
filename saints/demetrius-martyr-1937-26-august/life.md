# Demetrius (Morozov)

Demetrius Mikhailovich Morozov was born in 1875 in the village of Melnikovo in the Yaransk district of Vyatka province, a peasant, and served in the army from 1911 to 1914. On 27 November 1936 he was arrested on a charge of taking part in a counter-revolutionary group of churchmen, and on 27 March 1937 the special board of the NKVD sentenced him to five years in the camps; he was held in the prisons of Yaransk and Kirov and later of Petropavlovsk in northern Kazakhstan, and on 16 May 1937 moved to the Ortau section of the Karaganda camp. On 7 August 1937 a report on Demetrius Morozov and [Peter Bordan](/saints/peter-martyr-1937-26-august) reached the camp’s commissioner: the prisoners Morozov and Bordan do no work, but systematically carry on anti-Soviet agitation; they hold that the Soviet power is of Antichrist, built on violence, and that one must serve only God — the calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. Charged with anti-Soviet agitation, he was shot on 3 September 1937 with his like-minded companion Peter Bordan; he was numbered among the saints in 2000, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 26 August (Julian).
