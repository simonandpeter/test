# Basil the Great

Basil was born in 330 at Caesarea, the administrative centre of Cappadocia, into a family both illustrious and zealous for the faith — his grandparents on his father's side had hidden seven years in the forests of Pontus during Diocletian's persecution, and his father, also Basil, was a lawyer and a renowned rhetorician of that city.

He was given the fullest education his age could offer, and his contemporaries did not think him the property of one place or one generation: he belongs, one of them said, not to the Church of Caesarea alone, nor merely to his own time, nor was he of benefit only to his own kinsmen, but to all lands and cities, and to all people he brought and still brings benefit.

He died in 379. The liturgy that bears his name is served on his feast, and the Russian calendar keeps him on the day of the Circumcision of the Lord.
