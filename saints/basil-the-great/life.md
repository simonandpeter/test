# Basil the Great

Basil was born in 330 at Caesarea, the administrative centre of Cappadocia, into a family both illustrious and zealous for the faith — his grandparents on his father's side had hidden seven years in the forests of Pontus during Diocletian's persecution, and his father, also Basil, was a lawyer and a renowned rhetorician of that city.

He was given the fullest education his age could offer, and his contemporaries did not think him the property of one place or one generation: he belongs, one of them said, not to the Church of Caesarea alone, nor merely to his own time, nor was he of benefit only to his own kinsmen, but to all lands and cities, and to all people he brought and still brings benefit.

He died in 379. The liturgy that bears his name is served on his feast, and the Russian calendar keeps him on the day of the Circumcision of the Lord.

The Romanian calendar keeps him on 1 January as well, at the head of the day and marked with its cross, and it keeps his mother beside him: [Emilia](/saints/emilia-mother-of-basil-the-great) is named on the same page as «mama Sfântului Ierarh Vasile cel Mare».

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same first of January, and saint.gr's page adds the shape of his household. He was born in 329, by other reckonings 330, at Neocaesarea of Pontus in the village of Annisa, and grew up at Caesarea in Cappadocia; of the nine children three became bishops — Basil at Caesarea, Gregory at Nyssa and Peter at Sebasteia — one, Naucratius, became a monk, and the eldest of the family, Macrina, became a nun. The page records that the Liturgy he wrote is served ten times in the year, the first of them on the day of his memory.

The Russian calendar keeps him again on 30 января ст. ст., in the synaxis its own heading names outright: «Собор Вселенских учителей и святителей Василия Великого, Григория Богослова и Иоанна Златоустого», the Synaxis of the Ecumenical Teachers and Hierarchs. The heading enumerates the three and nobody else, and the day prints a troparion and a kontakion over them together, headed «святителей» and calling them of one mind with the apostles and teachers of the whole world. The row for his own feast stands; this day is a note on it.
