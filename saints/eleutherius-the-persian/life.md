# Eleutherius the Persian

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 13 April reads «Sfântul Mucenic Elefterie Persul» — Eleutherius the Persian, with the rank of a martyr — and his own page on the site prints the date, 13 Aprilie, and then only the day's Epistle and Gospel, icons and articles: no Viață tab and no Tropar tab.

So this folder holds a name, a nation, a rank and a day, and nothing further. No century is given, no place in Persia, no king under whom he suffered and no manner of death. Another Eleutherius kept in this corpus, the disciple of Dionysius the Areopagite, is a different man on a different day and is not to be read into this one.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 13 aprilie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/13-aprilie) and [the saint's page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-elefterie-persul); read 20 September 2026.*
