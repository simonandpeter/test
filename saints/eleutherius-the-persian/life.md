# Eleutherius the Persian

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 13 April reads «Sfântul Mucenic Elefterie Persul» — Eleutherius the Persian, with the rank of a martyr — and his own page on the site prints the date, 13 Aprilie, and then only the day's Epistle and Gospel, icons and articles: no Viață tab and no Tropar tab.

So this folder holds a name, a nation, a rank and a day, and nothing further. No century is given, no place in Persia, no king under whom he suffered and no manner of death. Another Eleutherius kept in this corpus, the disciple of Dionysius the Areopagite, is a different man on a different day and is not to be read into this one.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and names his teacher. saint.gr says this saint was a Christian from Persia who went to the bishop Symeon to be taught the word of the truth more perfectly; and that on his way home, on the road, he taught the things of the economy of Christ to those who came to him, and so managed to bring many unbelievers to the Christian faith and to baptise them. The fire-worshipping Persians denounced him to the king, who arrested him; and when the king could not turn him himself, he handed him over to the chief magi to persuade him to deny Christ. When they too could not manage it, after many torments, they beheaded him at last, and so he took the unfading crown of martyrdom. The page's couplet plays on his name: Eleutherius was not enslaved to error, but stood free before the sword's mouth.
