# Luke the New

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 11 decembrie reads «Sfântul Cuvios Luca cel Nou», and his own page on that site carries the name, the date, an icon and a list of articles, and no life: no country, no century, no monastery, no account of what he did or when. Two things the line does assert, and they are all this entry rests on: that he was «Cuvios», the Romanian word for a monastic saint, and that he is called «cel Nou», the New — the epithet that distinguishes him from the older saints of his name.

What the page prints beside the name are the readings appointed for his commemoration: the epistle Galatians 5:22-26 and 6:1-2 on the fruit of the Spirit and the bearing of one another's burdens, and the Gospel Matthew 11:27-30, the Lord's easy yoke and light burden. Those are the readings the Romanian calendar gives a monastic saint, and they are the whole of what stands under his name.

Nothing further is set down here because nothing further was read. Another calendar will carry his life; none was consulted for this page, and the three other churches' rows say so.
