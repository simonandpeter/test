# Dionysius of Vatopedi

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and gives no life. Its line for 31 июля ст. ст. reads «Прмч. Дионисия Ватопедского (1822) (Конст.).» — the venerable martyr Dionysius of Vatopedi, who died in 1822 — and the parenthesis «(Конст.)» marks the entry as one the calendar carries from the Patriarchate of Constantinople rather than from its own Russian reckoning.

Nothing else is printed. There is no life page, no year of birth, no place of birth and no manner of death, and the day page carries no hymn under his name. The monastery of Vatopedi on the Holy Mountain, the rank of a monk who died a martyr, and the year 1822 are the whole of what the calendar keeps of him.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 31 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260731.html); read 13 October 2026.*
