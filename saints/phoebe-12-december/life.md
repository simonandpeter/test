# Phoebe (12 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 12 December keeps two women in one entry and names both: [Euphemiane](/saints/euphemiane) and Phoebe. Of Phoebe the page says only that she too is an unknown saint, and that her memory stands with Euphemiane's in the Jerusalem Kanonarion at page 120. It gives her no country, no century, no office, no manner of death, no relic and no couplet.

The Greek calendar keeps the deaconess Phoebe whom Paul commends in the letter to the Romans on 3 September, and the corpus already holds her; this page makes no claim of that kind for this woman — it says expressly that she is unknown — so the two are kept apart, and what is written down here is the name, the pairing and the book.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3242/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
