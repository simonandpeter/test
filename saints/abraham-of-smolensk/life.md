# Abraham of Smolensk

Abraham of Smolensk, a preacher of repentance and of the coming Last Judgement, was born in the middle of the twelfth century at Smolensk to rich parents who had had twelve daughters before him and prayed to God for a son. From childhood he grew in the fear of God, went often to church and had the means to learn from books; his parents hoped their only son would marry and carry on the noble line, but he sought another life, and when they died he gave all he had to monasteries, churches and the poor and went about the city in rags, praying God to show him the way of salvation. He was tonsured at the monastery of the Mother of God five versts from Smolensk, at Selishche, and passed through its obediences — the Sretensky calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. The Prologue, for the Serbian calendar, adds that he later founded the monastery of the Holy Cross near Smolensk, bore many temptations from demons and men with patience and thanksgiving, brought down rain by his prayer in a great drought, and after fifty years in the monastic habit reposed in peace around 1220.
