# Phileortos

Phileortos was martyred by the sword. That is the whole of the synaxarion’s notice, with a verse that plays on his name — lover of the feast — and writes down his end by the sword.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 31 Αυγούστου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/863/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
