# Aretios

saint.gr's calendar names him with a companion and gives the two of them one sentence between them. The line for 4 June reads «Άγιοι Αρέτιος και Δακιανός οι Μάρτυρες», the holy martyrs Aretios and Dacianus, and the page behind it says only this: the holy martyrs Aretios and Dacianus bore witness at Rome.

So what is recorded of him is a name, a rank, a companion and a city. The page gives no year, no emperor, no office, no accusation, no manner of death and no burial; it prints neither verse nor hymn, and it does not say which of the two was the elder or whether they suffered together. His companion is [Dacianus](/saints/dacianus-martyr-at-rome), and the two folders carry the same sentence because the source gives them nothing apart.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 4 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/04/index.aspx) and [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/4418/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
