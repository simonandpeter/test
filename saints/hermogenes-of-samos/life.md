# Hermogenes of Samos

Hermogenes, a hieromartyr and wonderworker, is unknown to the synaxaria but known in Cyprus as a local saint. He was bishop of Samos, and his holy relics reached Cyprus in a wondrous way, by sea. The Greek calendar has no details of his life, and gives no year; his apolytikion calls him a branch of Phoenicia, says his coffin crossed the sea as though he were alive and came to rest at the town of Episkopi, and his kontakion calls him the boast of the Samians and the glory of the Cypriots.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 5 Οκτωβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2670/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
