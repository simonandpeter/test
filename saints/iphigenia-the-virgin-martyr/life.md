# Iphigenia

saint.gr's calendar for 16 November keeps «Αγία Ιφιγένεια η Παρθενομάρτυς», Iphigenia the virgin martyr, and everything it has to say about her is where it found her. The memory of Saint Iphigenia, it says, is mentioned in one line in the «Μικρόν Ευχολόγιον ή Αγιασματάριον», in the Apostoliki Diakonia edition of 1956, with no further information; and nowhere else is her memory mentioned at all.

So the calendar gives her no century, no country, no account of her martyrdom and no hymn, and it does not quote the one line it has found. What it does is keep her, and say plainly how slender its warrant is. This folder records exactly that and no more: a virgin martyr whom the Greek calendar commemorates on 16 November on the authority of a single line in one modern service book, and about whom its compilers could find nothing else.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 16 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/16/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2496/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
