# Desiderius of Vienne

saint.gr says he lived in the sixth and seventh centuries and was born in the city of Autun in Gaul. He studied at Vienne, where he was ordained deacon and presbyter, and later was elected its bishop. He worked with a godly zeal and reproached the rulers of his time for the dissolute manner of their life and for their injustice; and for that they denounced him to the bishop of Rome, [Gregory the Dialogist](/saints/gregory-the-dialogist), who however recognised the saint's innocence.

He also reproached Thierry II, king of Burgundy, whose life was immoral, and for that he was sent into exile. He returned to his bishopric after a few years, but was murdered in the year 608 by the king's order. The page gives him no birth year and prints no hymn to him.
