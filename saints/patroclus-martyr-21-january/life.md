# Patroclus (21 January)

The Greek calendar names him on 21 Ιανουαρίου with two words, «Άγιος Πάτροκλος», and the page behind that line says two things. The first is a caveat the site prints over the entry itself, marked with an asterisk: «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου, ή τα βιογραφικά στοιχεία του ή η ημερομηνία εορτής του» — the existence of the saint, or his biographical data, or the date of his feast, is not corroborated. The second is the entry: the holy martyr Patroclus bore witness under the emperor Aurelian, whose reign the page dates 270 to 275, and there are no further details of his life.

That caveat is part of the source and is recorded here with the rest of it, rather than quietly dropped. No city, no judge, no manner of death and no hymn is printed. He is not the Patroclus of Troyes whom the corpus keeps on another day; the page says nothing that would join them, so nothing is carried over.
