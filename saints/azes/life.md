# Azes

saint.gr's calendar for 21 Οκτωβρίου names Azes and gives him no life. The page's one sentence of narrative is the whole of it: «Ο Άγιος Άζης μαρτύρησε διά πυρός» — he was martyred by fire. The couplet set over it plays on his name, saying that Azes lived even though he died into the flame, a true life that knows no end.

The page gives him no country, no city, no year, no emperor, no companions and no hymn, and it prints no relics for him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 21 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/21/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2555/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
