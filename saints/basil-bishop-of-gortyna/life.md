# Basil

Basil, bishop of Gortyna, succeeded Eumenius, kept on 18 September. In
680–681 he sat at the Sixth Ecumenical Council in the Trullan hall with the
bishops John of Lampe and Gregory of Kantanos, the council that condemned
Monothelitism and its defenders, and its acts show his theological training
and his intelligence: a speech of his to the emperor Constantine IV
Pogonatus proving the heresy’s error, and his dialogue with the heretical
monk Stephen, the disciple of the heretical patriarch Macarius of Antioch
and counted a great theologian, among other passages that show how forceful
a presence he was. He signed not only as bishop of Gortyna, and so
archbishop of Crete, but as legate of the whole synod of the apostolic
throne of elder Rome. He sat again at the Quinisext Council of 691–692 with
the bishops Nicetas of Kydonia, Sisinnius of Chersonesus and Theopemptus of
Kisamos, the council that completed the work of the Fifth and Sixth by
laying down a hundred and two canons, and signed as bishop of Gortyna and
as holding the place of the whole synod of the holy church of Rome. In 2006
the metropolitan of Gortyna and Arcadia, Makarios, consecrated a chapel in
his name near the St Nicholas dependency of the monastery of Koudoumas. The
Greek calendar keeps him on 16 September.
