# Macarius of Alexandria

Macarius of Alexandria was priest of the monastic settlements called the Cells. doxologia.ro's life says he showed complete stillness and patience and did great things and miracles, and that [Anthony the Great](/saints/anthony-the-great) marvelled at his works and said to him: «See, the Holy Spirit has rested upon thee, and thou shalt be from now on the heir of my good works.» The page gives no year for his birth or his death, and none is supplied here.

When he heard that somebody had done some notable spiritual work he was filled with zeal and did it himself. Hearing that the monks of Tabennisi ate through the whole Great Fast food not touched by fire, he resolved to eat nothing cooked for seven years, only raw greens and soaked pulse; he fought sleep, and for twenty days and nights did not go under a roof. Troubled once by the demon of fornication he went off into a far desert and stood naked there six months, where mosquitoes as big as wasps stung him until his whole body was blistered, and when he came back to his cell he was known only by his voice, his body having changed to look like a leper's. Digging a well, he was touched on the hand by a venomous snake, and caught it and killed it, saying: «Not being sent by God, how didst thou dare to touch me?»

Once, sitting in his yard and telling others what was useful, a hyena came in and laid her blind cub at his feet; he spat on its eyes and made it see, and she took it and went out, and next morning hurried back with a sheepskin for him — to which he said, «I do not take unjust things», and she bowed her head and left the yard. The life adds that he was small, thin and beardless, with only a few hairs on his lip and at the point of his chin, because from the hardness of his labours the hair never grew; and that living so in the desert and coming to a great age he passed to the Lord.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 19 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/19-ianuarie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-cuvios-macarie-alexandrinul); read 19 September 2026.*
