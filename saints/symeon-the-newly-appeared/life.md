# Symeon the Newly-appeared of Thessalonica

saint.gr's whole notice of him is two sentences, and this is all of them. The venerable Symeon was born at Thessalonica in 1042, in the years of the empress Zoe. He was ordained at Thessalonica, and then departed for the Thebaid of Egypt, where he lived inside a cave until the end of his life. The day's line calls him «ο Νεοφανής», the newly-appeared, and a Thessalonian, and the page sets his birth year and a question mark where his death year would stand.

The page gives no year of his death, no name for the cave or for its place beyond the Thebaid, no order into which he was ordained, and no account of what he did there; it prints no hymn, no distich and no notice of relics. Its heading gives the feast as fixed, kept on 24 July of every year, and the entry carries no asterisk.
