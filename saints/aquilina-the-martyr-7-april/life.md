# Aquilina (7 April)

Doxologia.ro's calendar for 7 aprilie names her and gives no life. The line is «Sfânta Muceniță Achilina», and her own page on the site carries no life tab and no troparion — only the name, the date, and the readings appointed for her day, the labourers together with Christ from the second letter to the Corinthians and the Canaanite woman from Matthew. No country, no city, no year, no emperor and no manner of death is given.

She is not Aquilina of Zagliveri, the new-martyr near Thessalonica whom the corpus keeps on 27 septembrie with a life of her own. She is also not named as the mother of Calliopius, who stands on the same Romanian day: that mother is called Theoclia in the life doxologia prints for him, and the calendar keeps the two women on separate lines. Who she is beyond a martyr's name on this day, the source does not say.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 7 aprilie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/7-aprilie) and [her page](https://doxologia.ro/sfanta-mucenita-achilina); read 19 September 2026.*
