# John (Piramidin)

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him eleventh of twenty-four and gives no life; the life here is the one azbyka.ru prints for the civil 2 декабря, out of pstgu.ru. Ivan Petrovich Piramidin was born in 1877 in the village of Ivanisovo in the Pereyaslavl district of the province of Vladimir, in a priest’s family. Following the family way he finished the seminary, and in 1904 was ordained priest to the church of the village of Gruzdevo in the Ivanovo industrial region. He was arrested on 21 October 1930.

The indictment, which azbyka prints with its own spelling kept, charged that as priest of Gruzdevo he had by systematic anti-Soviet talk brought the district’s farming campaigns down; that in May 1930, talking with a group of peasants in the church watch-house, he had thanked them for not going into the collective farm and called on them to go on refusing; that going round the village soviet with a moleben at the Protection he had told the peasants the Soviet power was robbing them with taxes; and that he had threatened at a general meeting that the church would be closed if they did not pay its levy.

The one protocol of his questioning runs a few lines, and he wrote in it: «Виновным в предъявленном мне обвинении я себя не признаю. С крестьянами в сторожке говорил, но против власти ничего не говорил. Серебро не успел сдать». On 27 November 1930 he was sentenced to three years of corrective labour camp with confiscation. He was arrested again on 21 March 1937, sentenced to five years of exile in Kazakhstan, and came among the exiled clergy at the mine of the settlement of Mayskoye. He was arrested in the case of the archimandrite Gregory (Rebeza) on 25 November 1937; the troika ruled on 1 December, and he was shot at two in the morning on 2 December. Where he is buried is not known.
