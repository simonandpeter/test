# Theodore of Ancyra

saint.gr's calendar for 3 Νοεμβρίου names «Όσιος Θεόδωρος ο Ομολογητής επίσκοπος Άγκυρας», and then hedges almost everything it says about him. Its whole notice is one sentence: the venerable Theodore was probably a champion of the holy icons, and perhaps gave his blood as well for their right place in Orthodoxy.

So the see is firm and the rest is not. There is no year on the page, no account of his election, nothing of the emperor he stood against, no city but Ancyra, no relics and no hymn. This folder therefore calls him a confessor, which is the calendar's own word in the day's line, and does not call him a martyr, which the page itself only guesses at.

The epigram set above the notice is a pun on the name and says no more than the line does: you are a gift given to the city of Ancyra, Theodore, from the God of all. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands, hedged as it is.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/03/index.aspx) and [his notice](https://www.saint.gr/2873/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
