# Gabriel of Georgia

saint.gr's calendar for 25 January names Gabriel of Georgia and dates him 1825 to 1896. His name in the world, the page says, was Gerasimos, and he was born on 15 November 1825 in the village of Vakhvi in Georgia. He studied at the church seminary of Tiflis and at the theological academy of Saint Petersburg, and went on with his studies in psychology, theology and philosophy.

He was elected a bishop in the Church of Georgia, and fell asleep in peace in the year 1896. saint.gr names no see for him, gives no account of his episcopate and none of his writing, and prints no troparion and no kontakion. What else stands on the page is the etymology of the name Gabriel and a sentence about the archangel who bears it, which are notes on the name and not on this man.
