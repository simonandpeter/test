# Joannicius of Devič

Joannicius was a Serb of the region of Zeta, which is the Montenegro of today, saint.gr says, and he lived in the thirteenth century. From a young age he had a great love for Christ, and while still young he decided to leave the life of the world and his father's house and begin the monastic life, for which he had a great zeal.

He lived first as a monk on a mountain called Crna Reka, the Black River, where another well-known ascetic had been a monk before him, the venerable Petar Koriški. But when he became known among people for the greatness of his asceticism he went away to Drenica and hid himself in the thick forest, where he passed his whole life in asceticism and prayer.

According to tradition, the page goes on, the despot of Serbia Đurađ Branković brought his sick daughter to the saint, and Joannicius healed her; and in thanks the despot built a monastery in that region, which is known today as the monastery of Devič. In that monastery, which lies in the region of Kosovo, the holy relics of the venerable father are kept.

The two halves of that account do not agree about when he lived: Branković ruled in the fifteenth century, and the page's own opening sentence puts Joannicius in the thirteenth. It does not notice the difficulty or resolve it, and no year is recorded in this folder on either account. It prints no hymn for him.
