# Erconwald of London

saint.gr's calendar for 30 Απριλίου names him and says outright that it has nothing more. Its whole notice of him is two sentences — «Ο Άγιος Erconwald (Ερκονγουάλδος) κοιμήθηκε το έτος 693 μ.Χ. Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του» — that Erconwald fell asleep in the year 693, and that we have no other details of his life. The day's line adds the see: bishop of London.

Nothing else is printed: no year of birth, no kindred, no monastery, no act of his episcopate and no manner of his death. The page gives the Archdiocese of Thyateira and Great Britain as where the entry comes from, and it spells his name in Latin letters, with a Greek form only in brackets. The feast type is the plain fixed one, «Σταθερή. Εορτάζει στις 30 Απριλίου εκάστου έτους».

*After saint.gr's calendar for 30 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/30/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3995/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
