# John, son of Cleopatra

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 19 октября ст. ст. in the second half of his mother's line, «Блж. Клеопатры (327) и сына ее Иоанна (320)», and the name link behind that half reads «Иоанн Палестинский, воин» — John of Palestine, the soldier. The year the line gives for his death is 320, seven years before the year it gives for hers. What the calendar tells of him it tells in her life, which it prints under her name and not under his.

He was [Cleopatra](/saints/cleopatra)'s only son and she loved him, and the life says he had been given an honourable officer's post. He died suddenly, to his mother's great grief. She had carried the relics of the martyr [Varus](/saints/varus-the-martyr) home to her own village of Edra near Mount Tabor in Palestine and had built a church over them, and it was at those relics that she wept bitterly and prayed that her son be raised.

Varus and her son appeared to her in her sleep, both of them shining in bright garments with crowns on their heads, and from that she understood that the Lord had received her son into the heavenly army, and was comforted. The calendar gives him no birth year, no place where he served and nothing about his death beyond its suddenness, and in particular it does not call him a martyr; the rank word at the head of the line, «Блж.», stands over his mother.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 19 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261019.html) and [his mother's life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1774.htm); read 4 October 2026.*
