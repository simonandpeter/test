# Eutropia of Alexandria

saint.gr's calendar for 30 Οκτωβρίου names «Αγία Ευτροπία», and gives her a short notice that is almost all her own speech. She was from Alexandria in Egypt, the page says, and the prefect Appellianos tried in vain to stir her soul towards the impulses of a life of comforts, delights and pleasures, which he promised her if she would deny Christ.

Delight, pleasure and comfort for me, Eutropia said, is to live by the Christian commandments: what draws us is self-restraint, frugality, hardships and griefs borne for other people, and the greatest of pleasures is death for Christ. And she proved it by deeds as well, the page goes on. Neither prison, nor iron claws, nor lighted torches frightened her soul or embittered it; her words went on brave and heroic, and her head was dyed with her own blood. That head, which fell in such a way, was more honourable than any head adorned with diamonds and royal crowns.

The page gives her no year and no relics, and prints no hymn. It carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands.
