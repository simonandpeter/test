# Anastasius the Deacon of the Kyiv Caves

The Greek calendar names him on 22 Ιανουαρίου as «Όσιος Αναστάσιος ο Διάκονος», the venerable Anastasius the deacon, and the whole of the page behind that line is one sentence: «Ο Όσιος Αναστάσιος ασκήτεψε στη Μεγάλη Λαύρα του Κιέβου τον 12ο αιώνα μ.Χ.» — he practised the ascetic life in the Great Lavra of Kyiv in the twelfth century. The rest of the page is a note on the name, which it derives from the resurrection.

So the day claims a rank, a house and a century. No year of birth or of repose is given, no miracle, no word of his and no hymn. The same calendar keeps several other men of this name on other days, including two disciples of Maximus the Confessor; nothing on this page joins him to any of them, so nothing is carried over.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 22 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/22/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1145/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
