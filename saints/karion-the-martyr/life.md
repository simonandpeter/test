# Karion

saint.gr's calendar for 1 February names Karion and gives one line of life and one line of disclaimer. Karion bore witness, it says, after they cut out his tongue; and «Δεν έχουμε περισσότερες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου» — we have no further details of the saint's life.

So one fact about him is recorded, which is the one the source records: the manner of his death. There is no homeland, no century, no year, no reign, no persecutor, no place, and no apolytikion and no kontakion.

The couplet above it turns the cutting into his victory: «Φωνῆς στερήσει γλωττότμητος Καρίων, / Γλωσσῶν πλάνων ἔπαυσεν ὑθλοφωνίαν» — Karion, his tongue cut out, by the loss of his voice silenced the empty noise of deceiving tongues.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/560/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
