# Nicholas (Benevolsky)

The Russian church calendar names him and gives no life. Its list for 3 мая ст. ст. reads «Сщмч. Николая пресвитера (1941)» — the hieromartyr Nicholas the presbyter, with the year of his death — and the name page behind that line gives his surname, which the line itself leaves out: «Николай (Беневольский), иерей, сщмч.» It lists his days as 3 мая and 26 января, the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church, and carries nothing else at all.

So what the source says of him is this: that he was a priest, that he is honoured as a hieromartyr, and that he died in 1941, one of the last years in which the Russian church's new martyrs fell. It gives no diocese, no parish, no place, no date of birth, no account of his arrest and no manner of death, and the day page prints no troparion or kontakion under his name. None of it has been supplied here.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 3 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260503.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9701.htm), which gives no life; read 8 October 2026.*
