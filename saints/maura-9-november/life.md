# Maura (9 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 9 Νοεμβρίου keeps Maura with Christopher, and its whole notice of the two is one short sentence: «Οι Άγιοι Χριστόφορος και Μαύρα μαρτύρησαν δια ξίφους» — the holy Christopher and Maura were martyred by the sword. Nothing is said of where they lived or when, who tried them, what they were charged with, or whether they were kin, companions or married; and the page prints no hymn for them.

The couplet of the synaxarion is the rest of what is there, and it turns on the two names: «Πλάνην ἀμαυροῖ Μαῦρα καρθεῖσα ξίφει· / Χριστοῦ δὲ τμηθεὶς φῶς ὁρᾷ Χριστοφόρος» — Maura, shorn by the sword, darkens error; and Christopher, cut down, sees the light of Christ. Kept with her is [Christopher](/saints/christopher-9-november).

*After saint.gr's calendar for 9 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/09/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2955/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
