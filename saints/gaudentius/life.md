# Gaudentius

saint.gr's calendar for 31 December keeps the holy Gaudentius, and gives no life of him: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον Βίο του Αγίου» — we have no details about the saint's life. There is no century, no country, no rank, no office, no manner of death, no year and no hymn, and the line itself gives nothing but the name and the word Άγιος.

The couplet set over the entry is the only thing that says anything about him, and what it says it says twice over: «Aθλητικής άπειρος ου πάντη πάλης, / Kαν ουκ επ’ αυτή Γαυδέντιος εκπνέη» — not altogether untried in the athlete's wrestling, although Gaudentius does not breathe his last in it. The calendar will neither call him a martyr nor take the contest from him, and this folder follows it in that.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 31 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/31/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1512/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
