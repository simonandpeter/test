# Telesphorus of Rome

The holy Telesphorus came out of Greece, saint.gr says, and reached Rome at the beginning of the second century. He was elected bishop of Rome in the year 125, succeeding bishop Sixtus I. As bishop of Rome he fought the various heresies of the Gnostics, and he brought many liturgical ordinances of the Church of the East into the West.

He was martyred in the year 136. That is the whole of the page, and it prints no hymn; the year of his birth stands on it as a question mark. The distich over him has Telesphorus bear fruit by the cutting, having brought his work to its end like a noble seed — a play on the name, from τέλος and φέρω, the man who brings every work to completion.
