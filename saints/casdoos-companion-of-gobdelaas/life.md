# Casdoos, companion of Gobdelaas

Gobdelaas was the son of King Sapor, the Greek synaxarion says, and became a Christian through one of his father’s officers, [Dadas](/saints/dadas-companion-of-gobdelaas). When Sapor learned of it he had them both arrested, and when they refused to sacrifice to the gods he had Dadas cut to pieces alive, and his son [Gobdelaas](/saints/gobdelaas-son-of-sapor) tortured: he flayed his head, cut off his limbs and pierced his body with reeds. Casdoos, or Casdius, was a kinsman of the king, and for becoming a Christian was flayed alive. [Casdoa](/saints/casdoa-sister-of-gobdelaas) was the king’s daughter and the sister of Gobdelaas: she visited her brother in prison and he made her a Christian, and when it became known the king arrested her and, unable to change her faith, tortured her cruelly to death. The verses add that Gobdelaas was pierced with reeds for honouring Christ, who was struck with a reed; that Dadas was cut limb from limb by the sword; and that Casdoos was put to the sword and Casdoa crushed with wood. The entry gives no date.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 29 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2403/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
