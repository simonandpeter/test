# Hormisdas the Persian

The Ohrid Prologue of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović gives him the last entry of its Julian 8 August, and it is the whole of what is written here. He was a magnate at the court of the Persian king Yazdegerd. Because he would not renounce Christ, the king stripped him of his rank and of his estate and sent him to look after cattle. The king hoped that Hormisdas would soon be sorry for the rank and the estate and would bow down to the idols; but the king was deceived.

Hormisdas looked after the cattle peacefully and held to his faith. So the king put him to fierce torments, by which he succeeded only in wearing out the body of Christ’s martyr and not at all in changing his spirit. At last Hormisdas was killed, in 418, immediately after the passion of Saint [Abdas](/saints/abda-bishop-of-persia) the bishop, whom the entry sends the reader to find on 31 March — which is the day this corpus keeps him on, out of this same book. He suffered on earth, it ends, and was glorified in heaven.
