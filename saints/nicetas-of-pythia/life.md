# Nicetas of Pythia

The page says at once what it does not have. Nicetas, it begins, is unknown to the Synaxarists — «Ο Όσιος Νικήτας είναι άγνωστος στους Συναξαριστές» — and it is the Byzantine Calendar of Manuel Gedeon that remembers him, as a venerable ascetic «εν τοις Πυθίοις», at the Pythia, which the page glosses as the place now called Kouri, before the iconoclasms. He fell asleep in holiness and in peace.

That is the whole of it: one named modern source standing behind him, a place, a rough position in time given as a term rather than a century, and a peaceful death. There is no hymn, no verses, no homeland and no year; and the corpus records it exactly as the page does, including the fact that the synaxaria do not carry him.
