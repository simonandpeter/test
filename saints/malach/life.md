# Malach

saint.gr's calendar for 20 Νοεμβρίου prints ten names in one line: «Αγίες παρθένες Θέκλα, Βαουθά, Δενάχις, Τεντούς, Μάμα, Μαλοχία, Άννα, Νάνα, Άστη και Μαλάχ». They were ascetic Christian women of Persia, the page says. The king of the Persians ordered them to worship the fire, and because they would not accept it they were all cut to pieces by the swords of the soldiers.

The page spells the third of them «Δενάχις (ή Δινάχ)», and its couplet names only the first three — Thecla, Bauthas, Denachis, virgins; the contest of the sword showed them virgin athletes. Its heading blocks gloss two of the ten names in the way that site glosses any name, by listing famous bearers: under ΘΕΚΛΑ it prints «Κόρη του Αυτοκράτορα Θεόφιλου», the daughter of the emperor Theophilos, and under ΑΝΝΑ the Hebrew Hannah. Neither gloss is a statement about the woman in this line, and nothing of either is recorded here.

The page gives them no city, no year, no king by name and no hymn, and prints no relics for them.

This folder is Malach, named last in the line, beside [Thecla](/saints/thecla-20-november), [Bauthas](/saints/bauthas), [Denachis](/saints/denachis), [Tentous](/saints/tentous), [Mama](/saints/mama), [Malochia](/saints/malochia), [Anna](/saints/anna-20-november), [Nana](/saints/nana-20-november), [Aste](/saints/aste).
