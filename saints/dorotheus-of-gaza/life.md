# Dorotheus of Gaza

doxologia.ro's calendar names him on 13 august and prints three sentences. Our venerable and God-bearing father Dorotheus of Gaza lived the ascetic life first in the community of Abba [Seridos](/saints/seridos-of-gaza), near Gaza; afterwards, when Abba [John](/saints/john-the-prophet-of-gaza), who was called the Prophet, had died and the perfect silence of the great [Barsanuphius](/saints/barsanuphius-the-great) was complete, he founded his own monastery; and he ended his days in peace.

There, the page says, his disciples gathered the Spiritual Instructions, which became in time one of the fundamental works for Orthodox monastic life. His writings are part of the Philokalia, and they are the one concrete witness left of him across the centuries.

Nothing further is set down here because nothing further was read: the page gives him no year, and the Greek, Russian and Serbian calendars were not consulted for him.
