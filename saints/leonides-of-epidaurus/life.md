# Leonides of Epidaurus

saint.gr calls him the great champion of Epidaurus, the name his apolytikion gives him, and keeps him on 16 April with seven women: [Charissa](/saints/charissa-companion-of-leonides), [Nike](/saints/nike-companion-of-leonides), [Galene](/saints/galene-companion-of-leonides), [Kallida](/saints/kallida-companion-of-leonides), [Nounechia](/saints/nounechia-companion-of-leonides), [Basilissa](/saints/basilissa-companion-of-leonides) and [Theodora](/saints/theodora-companion-of-leonides). They lived in the years of the king Decius, which the page dates 249 to 251, and they were of the Peloponnese.

They were arrested in the days of the Lord's Resurrection at Troezenia in the Peloponnese and brought before Venustus, the governor of Corinth. The martyrs stayed unmoved in their faith, and the governor gave orders that Leonides be torn, and that he and all the women with him be thrown into the sea. On the road to the martyrdom they sang psalms together; then the women were bound with stones and cast into the sea, and Leonides with them. So they received the unfading crowns, one day before holy Pascha, that is on Great Saturday.

The page's couplet for him has the sea for a bosom: «Κόλποις θαλάσσης ἐκδοθεὶς Λεωνίδης, Φθάνει κολυμβῶν Ἀβραὰμ κόλπων ἄχρι» — given over to the bosom of the sea, Leonides swims as far as the bosom of Abraham. In 1833, it adds, the priest Nikolaos Natsoulis built a church to his honour and glory; and in 1916, after dreams that would not stop, pious people of Epidaurus were called to find a hidden treasure under the foundations of the ruined church of the Mother of God. They dug where they had been shown and found first a holy icon of the Panagia, and then, seventy centimetres down, seven skeletons which proved to be women's; and a stone slab, moved aside, uncovered a man's skeleton that bore the marks of strangling, while a fragrance rose out of the grave.
