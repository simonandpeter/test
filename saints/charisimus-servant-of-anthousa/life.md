# Charisimus, servant of Anthousa

Charisimus, a servant of [Anthousa](/saints/anthousa-of-seleucia) of Seleucia, was baptised with her by [Athanasius](/saints/athanasius-of-tarsus), bishop of Tarsus, and died for Christ — beheaded with the bishop, the Prologue says, under Valerian, about 257; the Russian calendar prints 270–275. That is all either source says of him.

*After the life printed by the Sretensky calendar (days.pravoslavie.ru) — [the entry](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4495.htm); The Ohrid Prologue for 4 September 2026 (22. август ст. ст.), as printed by the Православни подсетник (pravoslavno.rs), tells it for the Serbian calendar — [the day’s page](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-09-04&prolog=1); read 23 August 2026.*
