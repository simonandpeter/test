# Eulogius the Hospitaller

Eulogius lived in the parts of the Thebaid in Egypt, and his trade was that of a quarryman, a cutter of stone. He was called the Hospitaller, saint.gr says, because for his whole life his chief care and his whole enjoyment was to receive men in his own house and to give every help to the poor and to travellers. For all the weight and the labour of his work, in the evening, the moment he was let off, he would run to the market place, carrying a little lantern in winter, and look for strangers, so as to give them shelter and every other hospitality.

Once, the page says, he gave hospitality to Abba Daniel and his disciple, when the two of them came down into the city and were left without bread and without a roof. Eulogius lived more than a hundred years, doing good to the men around him, and died in peace. No year is printed for any of it, and no place beyond the Thebaid. His couplet says: let Abraham, who entertained God, entertain Eulogius, the entertainer of strangers.
