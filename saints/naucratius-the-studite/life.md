# Naucratius the Studite

saint.gr gives him three sentences. The venerable Naucratius lived in the ninth century, it says, and was a warm defender of the veneration of the holy icons; for that he suffered persecutions and exiles under the emperor Leo V, whose reign it dates 813 to 820. When the emperor Theophilos died, whose reign it dates 829 to 842, he returned to the monastery of the Studion, and in the year 842 was elected its abbot by the monks assembled there.

He fell asleep in peace in the year 847, and his body was buried as a confessor's in the monastery of the Studion, where his synaxis also used to be kept. The page says nothing of his birth, his parentage, his tonsure or where the exiles took him, and it prints neither couplet nor hymn.
