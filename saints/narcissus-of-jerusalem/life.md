# Narcissus of Jerusalem

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 7 august reads «Sfântul Sfinţit Mucenic Narcis, Patriarhul Ierusalimului» — the holy hieromartyr Narcissus, Patriarch of Jerusalem — and the page behind that line repeats the name and the day and prints nothing else: no narrative, no troparion, no years, no place beyond the see in his title.

So what is recorded here is what the source records: that the Romanian church keeps on 7 August a bishop of Jerusalem named Narcissus, and calls him a hieromartyr. Neither his century nor the manner of his death is stated on the page, and neither is supplied here. The Greek and Russian calendars have not been read for him.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 7 august — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/7-august) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-sfintit-mucenic-narcis-patriarhul-ierusalimului); read 24 September 2026.*
