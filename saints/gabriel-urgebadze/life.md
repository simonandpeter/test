# Gabriel Urgebadze

Gabriel Urgebadze was born in Tbilisi, the capital of Georgia, on 26 August 1929, and was given the name Goderdzi at his baptism, the youngest of the three children of Vasili and Varvara. When he was nearly two his father, an official of the Communist Party, was killed in mysterious circumstances, in 1931; from then on the boy was called by the pet name Vasiko.

Learning about our Lord Jesus Christ, little Vasiko built himself a cell of earth in his parents' garden and lived in it as ascetically as he could. Afterwards he turned the cell into a little church, which the communist authorities pulled down several times. The little church had seven domes painted blue, and its walls were covered entirely with the icons he had gathered as a child, for he had the habit of rescuing any little icon the atheists threw out on the rubbish tip. The church is still standing in Tbilisi today and can be visited.

After his compulsory military service in the Soviet army he decided to join the monastic life, and at his tonsure in 1955 he received the name Gabriel. He then became a hieromonk at the monastery of Betania. He became famous at the great comradely festival of 1 May 1965, when he pulled down a banner showing Lenin during a parade in the centre of Tbilisi; he was arrested, tried, held to be psychotic, and shut up in a hospital for mental illness for seven months.

After 1989 he gathered a community of nuns and withdrew to the monastery of Samtavro near Mtskheta, where he fell asleep on 2 November 1995 and was buried in the monastery courtyard. After his burial very many wonders happened at his grave, healings above all. He had the gift of foresight. He was canonised by the Synod of the Church of Georgia on 20 December 2012, under the name "Saint Gabriel the Confessor, the Fool for Christ", and on 22 February 2014 his holy relics were found and moved into the church of the Transfiguration.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 2 November, and says why that is the day. saint.gr's whole notice for him is the act of canonisation: on 20 December 2012, by decision of the Synod of the Apostolic Autocephalous Orthodox Church of Georgia, the archimandrite Gabriel Urgebadze was proclaimed a saint under this name — the venerable father Gabriel, the Confessor and Fool for Christ — and his memory was to be honoured on 2 November. The page's heading dates him 1929 to 1995, and its title for him, «ο Ομολογητής - Σαλός», keeps both of the things the Georgian decision named: the confession and the folly for Christ. It tells nothing else of his life and prints no hymn.

The Russian calendar keeps him on 20 октября ст. ст., which is the same civil day the Greek and the Romanian keep him on, and marks the line «(Груз.)», Georgian, as it does for the saints it takes from the Georgian church's calendar. Its rank word for him is «Прп.», venerable, where the Greek line calls him both confessor and fool and the Romanian venerable confessor; its line gives 1995 for the year, as the other two do. It names him from the monastery at Samtavro, «Гавриила Самтаврийского», and the title on its name link adds the surname, «Гавриил (Ургебадзе) Самтаврийский». The calendar carries no life page under his name and no hymn.
