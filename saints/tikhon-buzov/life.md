# Tikhon Buzov

The calendar's own line is the whole of what is recorded of him here. The Сретенский monastery's calendar for 26 ноября ст. ст. closes a clause of ten priests with him, «Сщмчч. Иоанна , Георгия , Назария , Василия , Василия , Илии , Василия , Даниила , Михаила , Николая пресвитеров, прмч. Тихона (1937).», and the name page behind his name gives the surname bare, without the parentheses the ten carry: «Тихон Бузов, архимандрит, прмч.» — an archimandrite and a venerable-martyr, which is the monastic grade of the word the line uses for him.

So nothing is said here of where he was born, which house he governed, when or by whom he was arrested, or where he died: no page read says any of it. The calendar carries no life page behind his name page and none on the day itself, and the day page's troparia all stand under other names. The year 1937 is the year the day line closes the clause with, and it is given for all eleven men at once.

His name page gives him one day only, 26 ноября, and does not list him among the new martyrs commemorated on 26 января, which the ten priests of his own line all carry. The calendar's rank word for him is «прмч.», a venerable-martyr, and not «новомч.», so neither type is written here from the word alone.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 26 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261126.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13874.html); read 7 October 2026.*
