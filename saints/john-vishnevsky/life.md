# John (Vishnevsky)

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for the Julian 19 November reads «Сщмч. Иоанна пресвитера (1920).», and the name link behind it, name page 13885, gives «Иоанн (Вишневский), иерей, сщмч.» — a title, a list of days and no text. The life here is the one the Russian church calendar at azbyka.ru prints for the civil 2 декабря, out of orthodox.etel.ru. Ivan Vsevolodovich Vishnevsky was born on 6 October 1888 in the family of a priest of the village of Fedkovka, in the Shuralinskaya volost of the province of Ekaterinburg.

He finished the ecclesiastical school and one class of the seminary. Until 1914 he served in the church of the Verkh-Neyvinsk works in the Ekaterinburg district and taught in the local school. In the first Russian revolution he was under the watch of the tsar’s police for his left convictions, but was not arrested. Before the October revolution he married Evdokia Elpidiforovna, a village schoolmistress. He was ordained deacon in 1917 and served at the church of the Nevyansk works, and in November 1919 he was ordained priest and sent to Fedkovka.

In the village he was much respected, and people came to him often for advice in a hard pass of their lives. Even the assistant of the plenipotentiary of the provincial Cheka allowed that «священник Вишневский хорошо образован, разбирается в текущих событиях». That standing got in the way of the local communists’ political-enlightenment work, and reports began to go to the head of the militia: that when the villagers were offered a lecture they went off home as from the plague and preferred to listen to their own priest, who had, the report said, caught the people of Fedkovka in his web and laughed at the communists almost openly.

In July 1920 the local authorities put on a lecture in the library, «Религия и коммунизм», to clear the mind of the masses of religious fog; the communist librarian found a suitable article and began to read it out. The priest came into the library, said «Бросьте вы эту ерунду читать» — leave off reading that nonsense — pulled the book out of his hands and tried to tear it up, and the lecture broke up; the people went home content. He spoiled such meetings more than once, and once brought an accordion to a lecture and drowned the lecturer out with it. The communists asked the authorities again and again to have the priest taken out of the village.

He was arrested in October 1920 and charged with discrediting the Soviet power and anti-Soviet propaganda among the dark masses. He rejected every charge and put his arrest down to a personal score being settled with him by the communist schoolmaster; under questioning he called himself a supporter of the Soviet power and recalled that in 1908 he had been prosecuted by the old government for his political convictions. The investigator wrote back with indignation that a revolutionary of 1907 and 1908 with a good education ought to know that religion only darkens the heads of the masses, and proposed that he be isolated in a concentration camp until the civil war ended.

The case went to Ekaterinburg, and the collegium of the provincial Cheka resolved instead to subject him to the highest measure of punishment. He was shot on 2 December 1920 — the civil day on which the Julian 19 November of that year fell, so that the day of his death and his feast are one day. The page gives him no place with a coordinate and this folder carries none.
