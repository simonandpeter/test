# Pherbinus, with Theonas and Symeon

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life. Its whole account of the three is one sentence — «Οι Όσιοι Θεωνάς. Συμεών και Φερβίνος απεβίωσαν ειρηνικά», the venerable Theonas, Symeon and Pherbinus died peacefully. The page gives no country, no century, no monastery and no incident for any of the three; it gives them no years, names no relics and prints no hymn.

What it does print is a couplet for the three together: «Ψυχὰς δέχου τρεῖς οὐ κεκηλιδωμένας, / Ψυχῶν ἀκηλίδωτε τῶν θείων τόπε» — receive three souls that are not stained, O unstained place of souls divine. Beyond the fact of a peaceful end, that is the whole of what the source says about them, and what it says is that the three are counted together. [Theonas](/saints/theonas-with-symeon-and-pherbinus) and [Symeon](/saints/symeon-with-theonas-and-pherbinus) are the other two.

Pherbinus stands last of the three in the page's own order, and his is the only one of the three names that the page glosses nowhere and spells only once.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 4 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/04/index.aspx) and [the life of the three](https://www.saint.gr/180/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
