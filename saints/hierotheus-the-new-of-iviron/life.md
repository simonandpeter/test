# Hierotheus the New of Iviron

According to Nikodemos of the Holy Mountain, Hierotheus was born in 1686 at Kalamata in the Peloponnese to rich and devout parents, Demos and Asimina; he lived in ascesis at the monastery of Iviron on Athos, worked many wonders and died in peace in 1745. Eustratiades, though, writes in his Hagiologion that Nikodemos placed this saint in the Church’s hagiologion having found a fanciful life of him, accompanied by wonders, written by a fanatical friend of Hierotheus. The Greek calendar keeps him on 13 September with megalynaria, and the Romanian calendar keeps him the same day, with his cell on the island of Skopelos.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 13 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2428/saint.aspx); The Romanian life is at doxologia.ro — [the page](https://doxologia.ro/viata-preacuviosului-ierotei-cel-tanar-de-la-manastirea-iviron); read 23 August 2026.*
