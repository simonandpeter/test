# Philotheus of Antioch

saint.gr's calendar for 12 Ιανουαρίου names a martyr Philotheus, and the page behind the line gives him two sentences: «Ο Άγιος Φιλόθεος μαρτύρησε στην Αντιόχεια τον 4ο αιωνα μ.Χ. Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου» — he was martyred at Antioch in the fourth century after Christ, and we have no other details of his life.

So the record is a city, a century and a day. The page adds, as it often does, the sense of the name — φίλος and Θεός, the friend of God, the one who loves God, the devout man — and nothing about the emperor under whom he died, the manner of his death, or a shrine. There is no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1407/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
