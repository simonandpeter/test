# Anastasius the Wonderworker in Cyprus

The memory of Anastasius is mentioned in no Synaxaristes. He was a monk in Cyprus with three hundred others — the Alamanoi saints — in the reigns of Alexius and John Comnenus, in the twelfth century, and died in peace. That is the whole of the Greek notice for 17 September; this entry records it and nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 17 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2464/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
