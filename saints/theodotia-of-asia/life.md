# Theodotia of Asia

The Russian church calendar names her on 1 November beside her two sons, as «матери их прп. Феодотии» — their mother, the venerable Theodotia — and its index of names titles her «Феодотия Азийская», Theodotia of Asia, with this day against her and no text. Everything else the calendar records of her stands in the long life it serves for [Cosmas](/saints/cosmas-of-asia) and [Damian](/saints/damian-of-asia): that their father was a Greek and a pagan, and their mother a Christian named Theodotia.

She lost her husband while the boys were still young, and the life reads even that as mercy, because it left her free to bring them up. She resolved to remain a widow for the rest of her life, gave up what the world offered, kept the Christian law with zeal and cared for nothing but to please the Lord. The life calls her a true widow in the apostle's own words — she that is a widow indeed, and desolate, trusts in God and continues in prayers night and day.

From their infancy she worked to put the fear of God and the love of virtue into her children, and as soon as they were old enough she gave them to a God-fearing man to be taught letters, where Scripture was the chief study and where, moved by love for suffering people, the two of them learned medicine as well. For this, the life says, the holy Church numbered her among the saints, named her venerable, and keeps her memory together with her children.

No year of her own is printed anywhere that was read. The day's line closes the whole entry with the third century, while the life refuses to date the family at all and says only that they lived no later than the fourth.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 1 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261101.html), [her name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/2716.html) and [the life of her sons](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life6686.htm); read 4 October 2026.*
