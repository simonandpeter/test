# Onesiphorus, Apostle of the Seventy, Bishop of Colophon

Onesiphorus was a Christian householder of the church of Ephesus, whom the apostle Paul mentions in the second letter to Timothy (1:16–18); he became bishop of Colophon in Asia Minor and distinguished himself in the teaching of the Gospel, which he defended bravely to the shedding of his blood. True to his name, the verse says, he hastened to bring profit to all by preaching the Word. The Greek calendar keeps him on 7 September with [Euodus](/saints/euodus-of-antioch), and sends the reader also to the notice of Thecla on 24 September.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 7 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/946/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
