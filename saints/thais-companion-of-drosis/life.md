# Thais, companion of Drosis

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names her in one line with five others for the twenty-second of March, old style: «Мц. Дросиды, дщери царя Траяна, и с нею дев Аглаиды, Аполлинарии, Дарии, Мамфусы, Таисии (ок. 104-117).» — the martyr [Drosis, daughter of Trajan](/saints/drosis-daughter-of-trajan), and with her, as the fifth of the five virgins, Thais, together with [Aglaida](/saints/aglaida-companion-of-drosis), [Apollinaria](/saints/apollinaria-companion-of-drosis), [Daria](/saints/daria-companion-of-drosis) and [Mamphusa](/saints/mamphusa-companion-of-drosis). Her own name page, 13983, is headed «Таисия, мц.», gives this one day and nothing else, and carries no life.

What the calendar tells of her it tells in the life it hangs under the princess’s name. It puts the five of them in the reign of Trajan, who revived the old law against secret societies in the year 99 and made a law against the Christians in 104, after which the persecution ran to the end of his reign. The bodies of the killed were left unburied, to frighten the rest; and these five women took the burying of them upon themselves, carrying the bodies away in secret, anointing them with sweet spices, winding them in linen and putting them in the ground.

The emperor’s daughter, a Christian in secret and not yet baptised, asked the virgins to take her with them when they went out to bury. On the counsel of Adrian, a courtier of the palace and the princess’s betrothed, a watch was set over the dead, and on the first night all six of them were taken. Recognising his own daughter among the prisoners, Trajan had her kept apart, in the hope that she would think better of it.

The five virgins were sentenced to be burnt in a copper-smelting furnace, and they took that death bravely. Out of the copper mixed with their ashes tripods were beaten for the emperor’s new bath, and while those tripods stood in it nobody could use it; and when they were melted down instead into five statues of naked girls and set up before the door of the bath for mockery, Trajan dreamt of five pure lambs feeding in paradise, and of the shepherd who told him that the Good and Merciful Shepherd had taken from him the women whose likeness he meant to shame.

Of Thais by herself the calendar says nothing beyond her name, the rank word and this day. She is the last of the five it lists. The same calendar keeps the penitent of Egypt under this name on the eighth of October, old style, and the corpus holds a third woman whom the Serbian book spells Тајса on the tenth of May; all three are separate.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 22 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260322.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life690.htm); read 8 October 2026.*
