# The Twenty-seven Martyrs

saint.gr names the company and no member of it. Its line for 3 Μαΐου reads «Άγιοι Είκοσιεπτά Μάρτυρες», the holy Twenty-seven Martyrs, and the whole of the notice behind it is one sentence: these holy martyrs were martyred by fire. There is no place, no year, no emperor, no persecution named, no account of the trial, no relic and no hymn, and not one of the twenty-seven is called by name anywhere on the page.

What it does print is a couplet, and that too counts rather than names: «Bληθείσιν εις πυρ ανδράσι τρίς εννέα, / Ίαμα Σώτερ ση το της Γραφής δρόσος» — for men thrice nine cast into fire, O Saviour, the dew of the Scripture was Thy healing. Three times nine is twenty-seven, and the dew is the dew that spared the Three Youths in the furnace of Babylon; so the one thing the service says about them is the shape of their death.
