# Nicholas of Georgia

saint.gr names him and gives him one sentence and a pair of years. Its calendar for 18 February prints «Άγιος Νικόλαος Πατριάρχης Γεωργίας», and the page behind that line says that Saint Nicholas held the high priesthood in the years 1584 to 1591 as Patriarch of the Orthodox Church of Georgia, and that we have no further details of the saint's life.

So the years this folder records are the years of his patriarchate and not of his life: the page gives no birth, no death, no place within Georgia, no relics, no couplet and no hymn. What the Greek calendar keeps of him is the day, the see, the rank and those seven years.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3741/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
