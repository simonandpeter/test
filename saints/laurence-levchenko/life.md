# Laurence (Levchenko)

«Прмч. Лаврентия (1937)» is the whole of him on the Russian calendar's 12 October — a rank, a forename and a year. «Прмч.» stands for преподобномученик, a venerable martyr: a monk put to death for the faith, as against the priest of the next line and the confessor of the line before. The site's index of names adds the surname and the same rank again, heading his page «Лаврентий (Левченко), прмч.», and adds nothing else at all.

1937 is the worst year of the Soviet campaign against the Church, and neither page says which monastery he came out of, where he was taken, or by what court. The index keeps him here and on 26 January with the whole company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and carries no life. The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian saints are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026 even with a browser user-agent, so the line stands as the line. He is not Laurence of Chernigov, who died in 1950 and has a life.

*After the Russian church calendar's [12 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261012.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14430.html); read 4 October 2026.*
