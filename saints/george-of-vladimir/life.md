# George of Vladimir

George — saint.gr gives the family name in brackets, «(Βσεβολόντοβιτς)» — was born in Russia in 1189, son of the great prince Vsevolod. He succeeded his brother Constantine and became great ruler of Vladimir and Suzdalia shortly before the battle of the Kalka, in which the Mongols of Batu Khan destroyed the Russian army. His reign ran out in civil bloodshed and struggles, and in wars against the Mongols, who had invaded Russia and sacked Moscow, Suzdalia and Vladimir; in 1223, the page says, the Mongol armies came into the country of Russia, beat the divided Russian rulers, and went back to Asia.

He was killed in the battle he joined with the Mongols at the river Sit. The page dates that to 4 March 1238, which is not the day it keeps him on; the calendar's day is 4 February, and only the year is taken here. Bishop Cyril buried his body in the cathedral of Rostov and two years later carried it, with reverence and ceremony, to the church of the Dormition of the Mother of God at Vladimir.

The Church declared him a saint in 1645, the page ends. It prints no hymn for him and no verses.
