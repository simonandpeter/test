# Porphyrius Mindrinos

Porphyrius Mindrinos came of Gonia on Thera, and saint.gr first names him as the virtuous hieromonk living in the monastery of the Prophet Elijah who wanted a life in the desert. The one place of quiet on the island he could think of was the remote and sheer ground beyond Ancient Thera. He confided the thought to the abbot of his monastery, Gerasimos Mavrommates, who refused to let him go, because the house was short of men.

Then the fame of the elder [Agapius](/saints/agapius-metaxas), who had come to Santorini from the Holy Mountain in 1822 and was practising asceticism in a cave at Monolithos, reached the monastery of the Prophet Elijah as well. The moment came when Porphyrius met him and in the end followed him. The two took refuge in the Kollyvades monastery of the Prophet Elijah on Hydra, where Porphyrius was initiated into the Kollyvadic spirit, and they returned together to the hermitage at Monolithos.

After Agapius fell asleep in 1844, Porphyrius was ordered to serve in the historic church of the Holy Cross at Perissa. In 1845 the archbishop of the Cyclades Daniel, an opponent of the Kollyvadic revival, came to Thera, and learning of the struggle of the Kollyvades — and in particular that they did not bow to the new western religious notions of the foreign-driven Bavarian power — he demanded that the confessor Porphyrius renounce it. Porphyrius would not give way.

Daniel then laid a report before the Holy Synod of the Kingdom of Greece, which the page describes as weakened and held in the gears of the secular power. Porphyrius was tried in his absence, without a defence, and condemned to exile in the monastery of the Evangelistria on Skiathos, where he fell asleep on 26 March 1852. He is honoured together with Agapius on 18 August.
