# Michael of Edessa

The Russian church calendar keeps him on 23 мая ст. ст. and gives him two readings, one of a single sentence and one a full account. He lived in the ninth century, came of the city of Edessa in Mesopotamia, of Christian parents, and was a zealous disciple of «святого Феодора Эдесского». Having given away to the poor what was left him at his parents’ death, he set out for Jerusalem to venerate the Holy Places; and Jerusalem at that time was held by the Muslims. He stayed in Palestine and settled in the monastery of St Sabbas.

Once he was sent out from the house to Jerusalem to sell the monks’ work. In the market a eunuch of the Muslim queen Сеида, seeing that the work was beautiful and well made, brought him with him to the queen. The young monk pleased her, and she tried to draw him into sin; and when her designs came to nothing she had him beaten with staves in her anger and then accused before the king as an enemy of the religion of Mahomet. The king questioned him and began to urge him to accept it, and he answered: «Прошу тебя – или отпусти меня в обитель к моему наставнику, или крестись в нашу христианскую веру, или отсеки мне голову, и я тогда отойду ко Христу Богу моему» — I beg thee, either let me go back to the house, to my teacher, or be baptised into our Christian faith, or strike off my head, and then I shall go to Christ my God.

The king ordered a cup of deadly poison given him, and having drunk it he was unharmed; and after that the king commanded his head struck off. His end came at Jerusalem, but the monks of the house of St Sabbas carried his body to their own Lavra and buried it there with honour. At the beginning of the twelfth century his relics were seen there by Даниил, abbot of the Kyiv Caves monastery, who was travelling through the Holy Places. The calendar gives him no year, only the century, and keeps him on two days, this one and 29 июля.
