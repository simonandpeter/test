# Papylinus

saint.gr names him and gives one sentence: «Ο Άγιος Παπυλίνος τελειώθηκε διά ξίφους» — Saint Papylinus was perfected by the sword. Above it stands the synaxarion couplet, which plays on his name and says that Papylinus, finding his own breaking by the sword, broke with it the gates of delusion and its gods together.

That is the whole of the entry. The page gives him no century and no year, no country and no city, no emperor and no judge, no office, no companions, no relics and no hymn; and because it does not, neither does this folder. What is recorded is that the Greek calendar keeps a martyr named Papylinus on 16 May and says he died by the sword.
