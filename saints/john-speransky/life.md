# John (Speransky)

John was born on 1 January 1864, the son of a priest of his own name — at Желовижи in the Moscow province by the calendar's life page, at Желовиж in the Kaluga district of the Kaluga province by azbyka's, and the two pages do not agree. He finished the Kaluga seminary, was ordained priest, and served in the churches of the Kaluga diocese. For his service he was made archpriest and given the mitre and several orders. He was held in great regard by the faithful of Kaluga, and when the godless came to power and the civil war began he was arrested at once and held in prison as a hostage. During the seizure of the churches' valuables in 1922 he was arrested again and given a year.

In 1937 they arrested him a third time, as rector of the city church he then served. The investigator's questions and his answers are printed, and the whole of the case is in them. Asked to give evidence about the counter-revolutionary activity he had carried on as rector, he said he had carried on none. Asked what his relations had been with the archbishop [Augustine (Belyaev)](/saints/augustine-belyaev), he said that he had regarded him as the bearer of the highest priestly rank — and then the investigators pressed him for false witness against the archbishop, which he flatly refused to give.

At one of the last interrogations he was told that the investigation had established beyond doubt that he belonged to a counter-revolutionary monarchist organisation and had worked in it on the minds of believers. He answered that he had belonged to no such organisation and did not know that one existed, and that if he were confronted with his accusers he would deny it to their faces as well.

On 19 November 1937 an NKVD troika sentenced him with the archbishop, the archimandrite [Joannicius (Dmitriev)](/saints/joannicius-dmitriev), the psalmists [Alexis (Gorbachev)](/saints/alexis-gorbachev) and [Apollon (Babichev)](/saints/apollon-babichev), and [Michael (Arefyev)](/saints/michael-arefyev) of a church council, to be shot; they were shot on 23 November 1937 and buried in one unmarked grave. 10 ноября ст. ст. is that civil day.

**The two hosts swap him with the other John of this day.** The Sretensky line names him among the presbyters who died with the archbishop of Odessa; azbyka names him here, with the archbishop of Kaluga — and the Sretensky calendar's own life page for him, quoted above, puts him in the Kaluga sentence by name. The attestation quotes the line that listed him, because that is the rule, and the folder follows the pages. He was glorified in August 2000 by the jubilee council of bishops.
