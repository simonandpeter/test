# Nestor of Maghid

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 28 februarie reads «Sfântul Sfințit Mucenic Nestor, Episcop de Maghid» — the holy hieromartyr Nestor, bishop of Maghid — and the page that line points to carries neither a Viață tab nor a Tropar tab, so that the rank, the name and the see are the whole of what this source records of him.

Where Maghid was, when he lived and how he died are not on the page, and are not written here. The form «Maghid» is the calendar's own spelling and has not been matched against any other calendar.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and gives the officials who killed him. saint.gr says Nestor came from Perge in Pamphylia in Asia Minor and spread the Christian faith warmly, and was so bold in the work that he went on doing it fearlessly even after the edicts against the Christians; that Eirenarchos, the governor of Perge, arrested him and sent him to Poplios, the eparch of Pamphylia, who tried in vain to draw him into denying Christ and, when he had lost every hope of it, ordered him crucified; and that Nestor endured his martyrdom with much patience and hymned Christ from the cross while the pains pierced him. The Patmos Codex 266, the page adds, calls him a bishop, which is the rank this folder already carries.

On the year the page does not agree with itself, and no date is recorded here on its account: its heading prints «(; - 250)», while its prose puts the arrest after «τα διατάγματα του αυτοκράτορα Διοκλητιανού κατά των χριστιανών», the edicts of Diocletian, who reigned from 284. Its couplet says, in the prophet's words, that Nestor stretched out on the wood had neither form nor beauty.

The Serbian church keeps him on the same day, and the Ohrid Prologue of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović gives him four sentences: he was marked out by his great gentleness, and in the time of Decius was brought to trial and fiercely tortured for Christ. Before his death he saw a sacrificial lamb in a vision, which he read as the sign of his own sacrifice soon to come.

He was tortured by an eparch named Publius, the Prologue says, and at the last crucified on a cross at Perge, in 250.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 28 februarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/28-februarie) and [his entry](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-sfintit-mucenic-nestor-episcop-de-maghid); read 19 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 28 Φεβρουαρίου — [the Greek day](https://www.saint.gr/02/28/index.aspx) and [the Greek life](https://www.saint.gr/1927/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026; and the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 28. фебруар ст. ст. — [the Serbian day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-03-13&prolog=1), read 4 October 2026.*
