# Nestor of Maghid

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 28 februarie reads «Sfântul Sfințit Mucenic Nestor, Episcop de Maghid» — the holy hieromartyr Nestor, bishop of Maghid — and the page that line points to carries neither a Viață tab nor a Tropar tab, so that the rank, the name and the see are the whole of what this source records of him.

Where Maghid was, when he lived and how he died are not on the page, and are not written here. The form «Maghid» is the calendar's own spelling and has not been matched against any other calendar.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 28 februarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/28-februarie) and [his entry](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-sfintit-mucenic-nestor-episcop-de-maghid); read 19 September 2026.*
