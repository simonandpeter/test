# Hermogenes, companion of Mamas

saint.gr's calendar for 24 Ιανουαρίου names Hermogenes and [Mamas](/saints/mamas-companion-of-hermogenes) together — «Άγιοι Ερμογένης και Μάμας» — and the page behind that line says plainly: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο των Αγίων», we have no details of the saints' life. What the page does print is the distich that stands over the two of them in the synaxarion.

«Eρμογένης Μάμας τε του Xριστού φίλοι, Θανείν δι' αυτόν ουδόλως εδειλίων» — Hermogenes and Mamas, friends of Christ, were not in the least afraid to die for him. That couplet is the whole of what is recorded. It gives them no city, no year and no emperor; it is also the only word for how they died, and the only reason this folder calls them martyrs.

saint.gr prints no hymn for them and names no relics, and it adds to the names only its usual gloss: that Ερμογένης is Ερμής with γένος, one descended from Hermes, and that Μάμας means the affectionate one.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/24/index.aspx) and [the page for the two of them](https://www.saint.gr/3519/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
