# Anatoly

Anatoly Andreevich Barashkov was born in 1870 in the village of Gridino in
the Ferapontovo volost of the Kirillov district, to a peasant family, and
was a peasant himself. Arrested at Kirillov in September 1918, in the
Kirillov prison he was with [Barsanuphius
(Lebedev)](/saints/barsanuphius-of-kirillov), bishop of Kirillov, brought in
on 14 September, who told the prisoners that he did not fear a violent death
but did not dare think the Lord would find him worthy of a martyr’s end, and
with the abbess [Seraphima (Sulimova)](/saints/seraphima-abbess-martyr-1918)
of the Ferapontov convent. On Sunday 15 September 1918, about five in the
morning, the bishop, the abbess and four laymen — Nicholas Burlakov, Anatoly
Barashkov, Michael Trubnikov and Philip Maryshev — were led out of the
prison toward Goritsy by a squad of twenty executioners. The early Liturgy
was beginning in the monastery, and the bishop’s request to go in and
communicate was refused; passing the metochion of the Filippo-Irapsky
hermitage he wished to cross himself before the icon of Philip over its door
and a guard struck his hand with a rifle-butt, and when he quickened his
pace they mocked him: do not hurry, you will get to the kingdom of heaven in
time. They went by the old road of Cyril’s day along the shore of Lake
Siverskoye, the bishop first in his klobuk with his staff, the abbess almost
beside him, the laymen behind — he firm in the knowledge that the decisive
hour had come, the hour of release from earthly bonds to be with Christ, she
not yet believing they would be killed without trial and thinking they were
being taken to Goritsy for the steamer; a prayerful quiet and the peace of
Christ came down on their souls, until at the verst-post that read five
versts to Goritsy and two to Kirillov the executioners turned them off the
road to the right, and there was no more doubt. Here is our Golgotha, said
the bishop; the abbess swayed and he held her up — mother, take heart; you
are a person of the Church; we must go to death without fear, as to a
wedding feast, with gladness; the time will come when we shall be envied —
and her weakness passed; one of the condemned began to speak sharply of
those who had condemned them, and the bishop stopped him: after the
Saviour’s example we must forgive everyone everything; we must pass into the
other life at peace with all. They were stood facing Zolotukha hill with
their backs to the monastery, the bishop between Seraphima on his right and
Trubnikov on his left. Every evening after compline the abbess had bowed to
the ground before her sisters and said, forgive me, accursed; now she turned
to the killers and said quietly, forgive me, accursed — they took it that
she called them accursed, and fired and killed her; then five volleys one
after another, and all were dead but the bishop, who stood praying with his
hands raised to heaven, reading the prayer for the departing, and when he
had said amen heard an executioner shout, put your hands down! — I have
finished, he said; you finish too — and turned to the monastery, blessed it,
and lowered his hands, and a shot at point-blank range felled him. Merchants
were made to dig the grave; the bodies were laid in with great care, the
bishop nearest the monastery, then Burlakov, then the abbess, with Barashkov
and Maryshev at their feet. Hearing the shots at dawn, many ran to the
place, and the Red Guards they met mocked them: run, run! yours has risen!
in three years he will turn up as relics! Twice the brethren got leave to
dig up the bodies at night and twice were stopped, once by Red Guards firing
in the air — we will shoot all the Volkovs; what is the executive committee
to us! — and once by a written ban; on the evening of 17 September, behind
closed gates, the retired bishop Misail (Krylov) sang the funeral for the
murdered in their absence, and Bishop Alexis (Simansky) of Tikhvin ordered
the bishop’s name into every synodicon of the Novgorod diocese. The
Cherepovets chairman’s answer to the diocese’s envoy was: allow you to carry
the body to the monastery? never! you will want to make relics of him next!
The place was honoured for many years by the Orthodox who came to pray
there; in the 1960s the authorities, seeing that the veneration did not
wane, destroyed every sign of the grave and built farm buildings on it, and
on 17 December 1998 a cross was raised on the place of execution. With the
bishop, the abbess, the priest [John
Ivanov](/saints/john-presbyter-martyr-1918-2-september), shot four days
later, and the martyrs [John
Ivanov](/saints/john-presbyter-martyr-1918-2-september), [Seraphima
(Sulimova)](/saints/seraphima-abbess-martyr-1918), [Nicholas
Burlakov](/saints/nicholas-martyr-1918-2-september), [Michael
Trubnikov](/saints/michael-martyr-1918-2-september), [Philip
Maryshev](/saints/philip-martyr-1918) he was numbered among the new martyrs
and confessors of Russia by the jubilee Council of Bishops of August 2000,
for veneration by the whole church; the Russian calendar keeps the seven
together on 2 September of the old style, 15 September. The life is by
Igumen Damascene (Orlovsky).
