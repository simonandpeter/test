# Zosimas (24 January)

The venerable Zosimas whom the Greek calendar keeps on 24 January is, as saint.gr says at the outset, other than the Abba Zosimas who found Mary of Egypt and is kept on 4 April. What this Zosimas was distinguished for is a method he first applied to himself and afterwards taught to others, against anger: that when he grew angry he should keep absolute silence.

So his anger died of atrophy, the page says, without ever sending out flames, and his rage subsided easily, as the sea goes quiet the moment a passing gust of wind is over. That is the whole of his life as saint.gr has it; no country, no monastery, no year, no hymn and no relics are printed. The distich over him asks what nobody can answer: «Τὶς τοὺς μακρούς σου Ζωσιμᾶ φράσει πόνους; Καὶ τὶς θανόντος τῶν πόνων σου τὰ στέφη;» — who will tell your long labours, Zosimas, and who, now that you are dead, the crowns of those labours?

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/24/index.aspx) and [his page](https://www.saint.gr/3523/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
