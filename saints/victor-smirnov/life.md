# Victor (Smirnov)

The Сретенский monastery's calendar names him twelfth in its clause of fifteen for 25 ноября ст. ст. and carries no life for him; azbyka.ru prints one out of fond.ru. Виктор Павлович Смирнов was born on 20 October 1875 at Ильинское in the Звенигород district of the province of Moscow, the son of a psalm-reader. He finished the Звенигород church school in 1891 and the Vifany seminary in 1898, and went as a teacher into one of the parish schools of the Можайск district.

On 7 November 1902 he was ordained priest to the church of Elijah at Сандыри in the Коломна district. In April 1930 he was moved to the church of the Nativity of Christ at the Хотяиново churchyard, and in March 1934 to the Trinity church of Протопопово, which is now part of the town of Коломна. For his long and diligent service he was raised archpriest in 1927, given a jewelled cross in 1930 and the palitsa in 1936. In 1930 he was sentenced to three months of forced labour for not paying a tax.

He was arrested on 27 November 1937 and shut in one of the prisons of Коломна. The next day the investigator called two witnesses who knew him well, and both signed statements slandering him with active anti-Soviet activity. The page prints his own answers. Asked whether he admitted guilt, he said: «Контрреволюционной деятельности я не проводил, виновным себя в этом не признаю.» Told that the investigation had material that he had voiced anti-Soviet sentiments and that truthful evidence was required of him, he said he had never carried on counter-revolutionary anti-Soviet activity. The investigator asked whether he knew the witnesses, read him their evidence and demanded that he admit his guilt; he refused flatly. Asked once more whether he confirmed their evidence, he answered: «Показания свидетелей я отрицаю и виновным себя в проведении контрреволюционной деятельности не признаю.»

The investigation was closed that same day and he was moved to the Taganka prison in Moscow. On 1 December 1937 the troika of the NKVD sentenced him to be shot, and he was shot on 8 December 1937 on the Butovo range outside Moscow and buried in an unmarked common grave.
