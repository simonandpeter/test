# Vladimir (Kholodkovsky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and gives no life. Its line for 31 июля ст. ст. reads «Сщмч. Владимира пресвитера (1937).» — the hieromartyr Vladimir, a presbyter, who died in 1937 — and behind that line the calendar's own name page gives the name in full as «Владимир (Холодковский), иерей, сщмч.», with the surname the day line leaves out.

Nothing else is printed. The calendar names no parish, no diocese, no place and no manner of death, and it carries no life page and no hymn for him. The rank of a parish priest, the surname and the year 1937 are the whole of what it keeps.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 31 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260731.html); read 13 October 2026.*
