# Basil, companion of Euphrasius

saint.gr's calendar for 14 Μαρτίου names him with his companion, «Άγιοι Βασίλειος και Ευφράσιος οι Μάρτυρες», and the page behind that line holds one sentence about the two of them: that the holy martyrs Basil and Euphrasius were martyred together with other Christians at Thessalonica in the first Christian times. That is all: no year, no emperor, no governor, no manner of death, no relic and no hymn, and no name for any of the others who died with them.

So what is recorded here is the commemoration, the city and the company. [Euphrasius](/saints/euphrasius-companion-of-basil) is kept with him in the same line, and the others the page mentions are left unnamed, as the source leaves them. The rest of what the page prints is a note on the name Basil and on the emperors and patriarchs who have borne it.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 14 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/14/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1390/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
