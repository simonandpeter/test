# The Eight Martyrs of Nicaea

saint.gr's calendar for 12 Ιανουαρίου keeps eight martyrs together and names not one of them: «Άγιοι Οκτώ Μάρτυρες από την Νίκαια». The page behind the line has one sentence for all eight — «Οι Άγιοι Οκτώ Μάρτυρες κατάγονταν από την Νίκαια της Βιθυνίας και μαρτύρησαν διά ξίφους» — they came from Nicaea in Bithynia, and they were martyred by the sword.

The couplet printed over them plays on the number: a choir eight in number dies by the sword, wishing to find the life of the eighth age. That is all. No names, no emperor, no governor, no year, and no hymn. Because the calendar enumerates nobody, this is one entry and not eight.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1394/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
