# Paraskevas of Trebizond

saint.gr gives the new martyr Paraskevas three sentences, and they are all there is. He came of Trebizond and was one of the notables of the city — «ένας από τους προύχοντες της πόλεως» — and he was martyred there in the year 1659 by hanging. His holy relic was buried in the church of St Gregory of Nyssa, and from there it was translated to the monastery of Theoskepastos of Trebizond.

The page gives no year for his birth, no family, no trade beyond the standing the word προύχων implies, and nothing at all about what brought him before a judge or who the judge was. It prints no distich over him and no hymn. What it does set beside his name is the synaxis of the holy new martyrs who suffered after the taking of Constantinople, and that is a company and not a person, so it is not kept here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3782/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
