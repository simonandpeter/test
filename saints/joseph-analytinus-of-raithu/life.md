# Joseph Analytinus of Raithu

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 14 января ст. ст. as «Прп. Иосифа Аналитина Раифского (IV)», and the life it gives him is four lines long. He was a strict ascetic, it says, and reached such a height in the spiritual life that a flame would overshadow him while he prayed. He foretold the hour of his own death to a disciple — «Предсказав время своей кончины ученику своему Геласию» — and died in peace in the fourth century.

The one thing the page sets him beside is the slaughter at the holy mountain: he died, it says, before the killing of [the fathers of Sinai](/saints/fathers-slain-at-sinai), whom the same day keeps. The calendar names no monastery, no persecutor and no year of its own beyond the century, and it prints no hymn under his name. The epithet «Аналитин» it gives him it does not explain.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 14 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260114.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life217.htm); read 3 October 2026.*
