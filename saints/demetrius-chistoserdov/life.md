# Demetrius (Chistoserdov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Demetrius among the hieromartyrs on 26 December and gives no life. Its line is «Сщмчч. Александра и Димитрия пресвитеров (1918).» — the hieromartyrs Alexander and Demetrius, presbyters, 1918 — and the calendar’s own name page for this memory carries the fuller form the line leaves out: «Димитрий (Чистосердов), протоиерей, сщмч.»

That is the whole of the record. He was an archpriest; the year of death the line gives is 1918; the calendar keeps the memory on this day, the second day of the Nativity. The line names two men and both are in this draft, the two name pages behind it giving the surnames the line omits. The day page carries no troparion and no kontakion under this name; the three it prints belong to the day’s own feasts and to one of its icons. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 26 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261226.html); read 13 October 2026.*
