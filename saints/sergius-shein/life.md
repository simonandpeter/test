# Sergius (Shein)

Vasily Pavlovich Shein was born in 1866 in the village of Kolpna in the Novosil uezd of the Tula province. He finished the school of jurisprudence in 1893, sat as a member of the Fourth State Duma, and belonged to the secretariat of the Council of the Orthodox Russian Church of 1917 and 1918. He was deputy chairman of the board of the Society of United Orthodox Parishes of Petrograd, and in religion he was Sergius, an archimandrite.

He was tried with [Benjamin of Petrograd](/saints/benjamin-of-petrograd) in the case opened on 10 June 1922, after the metropolitan's arrest on 29 May over the seizure of church valuables, with eighty-six others in the dock. The tribunal gave sentence on 5 July, and in the night of 12 to 13 August 1922 he was shot on the edge of Petrograd with the metropolitan and with the two laymen of the parish society, [Yuri (Novitsky)](/saints/yuri-novitsky) and [John (Kovsharov)](/saints/john-kovsharov).

That is all the calendar's lives say of him: a lawyer who sat in the Duma and in the Council's secretariat, a monk and an archimandrite, and the deputy chairman of the society whose chairman died beside him. A cross stands over the symbolic grave of the Russian new martyrs in the brotherhood cemetery of the Lavra in Petrograd, and the day page prints no hymn under his name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 31 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260731.html) and [the life the four share](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life356.htm); read 13 October 2026.*
