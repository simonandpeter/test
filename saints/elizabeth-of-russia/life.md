# Elizabeth of Russia

doxologia.ro's calendar names her and gives no life. Its page for her carries the feast line 18 Iulie and no Viață tab, so all that is recorded here is what the calendar's own line for the day says: «Sfânta Cuvioasă Muceniță Elisabeta, ducesa Rusiei», the holy venerable martyr Elizabeth, duchess of Russia.

The page's only other tab is a video of a troparion sung for her, which prints no hymn text — only the invocation under the recording, «Sfântă Cuvioasă Elisabeta, roagă-te lui Dumnezeu pentru noi!» — so no hymn is recorded here. No year, no place beyond Russia and no account of her life were found on the Romanian pages read for this day, and nothing has been supplied from elsewhere. The other calendars have not been checked for her.

The Russian calendar is the one that gives her rank in full: «прмц. вел. кн. Елисаветы», the venerable-martyr grand duchess, where the Romanian has «Sfânta Cuvioasă Muceniță Elisabeta, ducesa Rusiei». Its index of names heads her «Святая преподобномученица великая княгиня Елисавета» and keeps her on five days — this one for the finding of her relics, 5 July for her repose, 26 January with the new martyrs, 8 June with the Petersburg saints and 24 August with the Moscow ones. The «(1918)» printed against this day is the year she died, not the year she was found.

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery prints her repose and her sister’s in one line on 5 июля ст. ст. — «Прмцц. вел. кн. Елисаветы и инокини Варвары (1918).» — and the cross-sister named beside her there is now in this corpus as [Barbara (Yakovleva)](/saints/barbara-yakovleva). The readings that calendar links say that at Yekaterinburg the two sisters who had come with her were set free, that Barbara made them keep her, and that when the prisoners were thrown down the mine shaft near Sinyachikha the two women were brought up months later with their fingers set for the sign of the cross. This folder’s russian row was NOT moved: it keeps the Julian 28 сентября, the finding of her relics, and its note already records this 5 июля as her repose.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 18 iulie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/18-iulie) and [her page](https://doxologia.ro/sfanta-cuvioasa-mucenita-elisabeta-ducesa-rusiei), which prints only the day; read 24 September 2026; and the Russian church calendar's [28 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260928.html), read 4 October 2026; and after the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 5 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260705.html), read 9 October 2026.*
