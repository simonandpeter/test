# Michael, companion of Arsenius

saint.gr names the two of them together and gives them two sentences. Michael and [Arsenius](/saints/arsenius-of-georgia) came of Georgia and lived in the ninth century, it says, and they lived as ascetics in a monastery of Mount Olympus in Bithynia of Asia Minor. That is the whole of the notice: no year of birth, no year of death, no name for the monastery, no account of what either of them did or said, no wonder, no relic and no hymn.

So what stands of him is an origin, a century, a mountain, and the fellow he is remembered with. Olympus of Bithynia was one of the great monastic mountains of the Byzantine east, and it is the only place the page puts them in.
