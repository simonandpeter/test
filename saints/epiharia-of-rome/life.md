# Epiharia

Epiharia - or Epiharis - the martyr of Christ, lived at Rome in the days of the emperor Diocletian and was seized by the eparch Chesarie. Confessing the name of Christ with boldness, she was hung up, her body was torn, and she was tormented in many ways; she received her end by the sword, and so gave her blessed and honoured soul to God. The Romanian calendar keeps her on this day.
