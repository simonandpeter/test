# Epiharia

Epiharia — or Epicharis — lived at Rome in the reign of Diocletian (284–305)
and was seized by the eparch, whom the Romanian life calls Caesarius. For
her firm confession of faith in Christ the Saviour she was hung up and her
body torn with iron hooks, and then she was beaten with hammers of lead; she
prayed, and an angel of God struck her torturers down. Then she was
beheaded, and so gave her blessed and honoured soul to God. The Russian
calendar keeps her on 27 September of the old style, 10 October, and the
Romanian calendar on 27 September.

*After the life printed by the Russian church calendar at azbyka.ru — [the entry](https://azbyka.ru/days/sv-epiharija-rimskaja), read 6 September 2026; the Romanian life at doxologia.ro — [Viața Sfintei Mucenițe Epiharia](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfintei-mucenite-epiharia), read 30 August 2026, was all this entry had until then.*
