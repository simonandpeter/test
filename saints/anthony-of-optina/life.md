# Anthony of Optina

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 7 august reads «Sfântul Antonie de la Optina», and the page that line points to carries the same name and the same day and nothing else of him: no narrative, no troparion, no year, no place beyond the monastery in his name. What the page does put under his name is one sentence, set there as a saying of his — Lord, help me to see my own sins; Lord, give me patience, generosity and gentleness — and links out to the akathist of the Elders of Optina, to the daily prayer of those elders, and to photographs of the monastery.

So what this entry records is that the Romanian calendar keeps, on 7 August, one of the elders of the monastery of Optina in Russia, and that the source consulted says no more than that. The Russian calendar has not been read for him, and until it is, neither his years nor which of the Optina fathers of that name he is can be set down here.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 7 august — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/7-august) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-antonie-de-la-optina); read 24 September 2026.*
