# Symeon of Verkhoturye

saint.gr's calendar for 18 December keeps Saint Symeon of Verkhoturye, and says of him only that it has nothing of his life: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ρώσου Αγίου» — we have no details of the life of the Russian saint. The heading above that sentence gives his years as 1607 to 1642, and that heading is the only place a year appears at all.

So the whole of the Greek entry is four things: the fixed day, the place Verkhoturye, the word that he is a Russian saint, and the two years. The page gives him no rank beyond saint, names no monastery and no office, tells nothing of how he lived or died, lists no relic, prints no hymn, and sends the reader on only to the synaxis of all the saints of Siberia, which was not read for this folder.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4256/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
