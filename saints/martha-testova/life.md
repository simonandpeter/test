# Martha (Testova)

The Сретенский monastery's calendar prints her last of the entries it puts on the Julian 13 April, and the whole of the entry is one line: «Прмц. Марфы (1941).» — the venerable-martyr Martha, and the year 1941. There is no life under her name on the day page and none behind it. The name link carries a fuller form than the line does, «Марфа (Тестова), прмц.», with a surname the line leaves out altogether, and the page behind that link gives her two days of commemoration and almost nothing besides.

That is the whole of what the source gives. No place of birth, no year of birth, no house she was clothed in, no arrest, no sentence, no word of her own and nothing of the years between: the bracketed year is the year of death, and the rank word is the only thing said about her state in life. It is written here as the calendar has it rather than filled out from elsewhere, and a reader who wants more of her will have to go to a source this folder has not read.

Her second day on that page is 26 января, marked as the new martyrs', which is the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia — a commemoration of a whole company rather than a second day of her own, so it is not recorded here as a feast. The page also names a parish site for her, a women's monastery in the Vladivostok diocese, and links an article about the sisters of a convent and the clergy around it; neither is a life, and nothing has been taken from either.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 13 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260413.html), which gives her one line and no life, and [the calendar's page for her name](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13714.html); read 7 October 2026.*
