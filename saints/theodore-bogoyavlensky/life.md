# Theodore (Bogoyavlensky)

The Russian calendar's line is all there is of him. It names him alone on this day, «Св. Феодора исп., пресвитера (1933)» — the holy Theodore the confessor, presbyter, 1933 — and the name behind that line reads «Феодор (Богоявленский), иерей, исповедник», Theodore Bogoyavlensky, priest, confessor. That is his surname, his rank and the year of his death.

There is no life page behind his name and no hymn for him on the day. The calendar calls him a confessor and not a martyr, which in its own vocabulary distinguishes those who suffered and survived their confession from those who were killed for it; it does not say where he served, where he was held or how he died. The year 1933 is the calendar's own and the only date it prints.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 28 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260128.html); read 3 October 2026.*
