# Nicholas (Tokhtuev)

The Russian church calendar names him and gives no life. Its list for 4 мая ст. ст. reads «Сщмч. Николая диакона (1943)» — the hieromartyr Nicholas the deacon, with the year of his death — and the name page behind that line gives the surname the line itself leaves out and raises his grade: «Николай (Тохтуев), протодиакон, сщмч.», a protodeacon. It lists his days as 4 мая and 26 января, the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church.

So what the source says of him is this: that he was a deacon, that he is honoured as a hieromartyr, and that he died in 1943, which is later than almost any year in the long roll of the Russian new martyrs. It gives no diocese, no parish, no date of birth and no manner of death, and the day page prints no troparion or kontakion under his name.

His name page carries one thing more, which is unusual for an entry of this kind: a link to an article on Православие.Ru. That article was not read, and nothing has been taken from it; a folder is worth what its citations are worth, and the three places its standfirst names are not a claim this life is entitled to make.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 4 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260504.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14254.htm), which gives no life; read 8 October 2026.*
