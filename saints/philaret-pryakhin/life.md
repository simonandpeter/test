# Philaret (Pryakhin)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him for 22 февраля ст. ст. in four words — «Прмч. Филарета (1942).» — a venerable-martyr, which is its word for a monastic who died a martyr's death, in 1942. The name link behind the line carries the calendar's own nominative with the family name the line leaves out and with his office: «Филарет (Пряхин), игумен, прмч.», the abbot of a monastery, one of the new martyrs of the Russian church in the Soviet years.

The calendar prints no life page for him and no hymn under his name; the only hymns it sets on this day belong to a finding of relics with which it heads the day. It names no monastery, no place and no manner of death, and nothing further is written here — nothing is reasoned out from the family name, from the office or from the year.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 22 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260222.html); read 3 October 2026.*
