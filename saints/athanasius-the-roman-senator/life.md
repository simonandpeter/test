# Athanasius the Roman Senator

saint.gr's calendar for 18 July names him and gives no life. Its line reads «Άγιος Αθανάσιος ο Ρωμαίος Συγκλητικός, ο εν Κλύσματι» — Athanasius the Roman senator, the one at Klysma — and the page behind the line says one sentence and stops: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου», we have no details of the life of this saint. There is no year, no account of how he came from the senate to sanctity, and no hymn.

So what this folder records is the calendar's own line: a Roman of senatorial rank, remembered at a place the page calls Klysma, kept by the Greek church on 18 July. The page says nothing about Klysma — not where it is, not what he did there, not whether he died there — and nothing has been supplied here from anywhere else to make the line longer than the source made it.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2152/saint.aspx), which prints only that one sentence; read 1 October 2026.*
