# Archil II of Iberia

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 21 июня ст. ст. names two Georgian kings of different centuries on one line, «Мчч. Арчила II, царя Иверского (744), и Луарсаба II, царя Карталинского (1622) (Груз.).», marks the entry «(Груз.)» for a Georgian commemoration, and gives each of them a life page of his own. He belonged to the Khosroid dynasty, his page says, and was a direct descendant of the king who first believed, whom it dates to 342.

In his reign Georgia was laid waste by an invader whom the Georgian people called the Deaf for his unrelenting cruelty. The position was hopeless, and the king together with his brother, the ruler of western Georgia, asked the intercession of the Most Holy Mother of God with tears; and she showed her mercy, for in the battle at the rivers Абаша and Цхенисцхали the Georgians overcame forces far greater than their own.

After that victory he set about restoring the kingdom. He rebuilt the town of Нухпатис, repaired the ruined churches at Мцхета, and helped many of the mountain tribes to receive Christianity. But a new Arab invasion came soon, an unlooked-for incursion by «Джиджум-Асим»; and the king, who had been paying his tribute punctually and had not expected the attack, judged it best, in order to spare the country another sack and the Islam being pressed on it, to go to the invader himself, acknowledge Georgia a vassal and ask for peace.

Putting all his hope in God’s mercy and prepared to lay down his life for the faith and for his people, he came into the Arab camp. He was received hospitably and promised protection, but pressed to accept Islam; and the page quotes the «Летопись Грузии», the Georgian chronicle, for his answer — that it should never be that he leave Christ, the true God, who for our salvation took on human flesh, and that if he obeyed he would die an eternal death, while if his firmness brought him to death he would rise as his Lord rose and come to Him.

At those words he was bound and taken to prison, and neither tortures nor persuasions nor promises could make him an apostate. On 20 March 744 he was put to death by beheading. Georgian Christians carried the body away secretly to a place called Эрцо and buried it in Kakheti, in the church of Ноткори which the king had built himself.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 21 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260621.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1369.htm); read 9 October 2026.*
