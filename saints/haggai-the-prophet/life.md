# Haggai

doxologia.ro prints a short notice of him, and this is the whole of it. Haggai was of the tribe of Levi, born in Babylon in the time of the captivity of the Jews. While still young he came up from Babylon to Jerusalem, and prophesied together with the prophet Zacharias for thirty-six years, four hundred and seventy years before the incarnation of the Lord Christ. He prophesied of the people's return from the captivity, and saw in part the building of the temple renewed by Zerubbabel after the return from the captivity of Babylon.

Then he reposed in peace and was buried with honour near the graves of the priests, since he too was of the priestly family. The page adds a description of him: he was bald and old, with a round beard, honoured and renowned for virtue, and loved and honoured by all as a great and glorious prophet. It closes by saying what his name means, that it is interpreted as feast, or as the one who keeps the feast.
