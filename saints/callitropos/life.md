# Callitropos, sister of Epiphanius of Cyprus

saint.gr's calendar for 12 May names her and gives almost no life. Its page for her is a single sentence: the venerable Callitropos was the sister of saint [Epiphanius](/saints/epiphanius-of-cyprus), bishop of Constantia in Cyprus, and we have no further details about her life. The Greek church keeps the two of them on the same day.

Her brother's page, read the same day, is what fills the outline in. Their parents were a very poor family of Jewish farmers at the village of Vesandouke near Eleutheropolis in Palestine, and Callitropos was their only other child. When Epiphanius, orphaned at ten, was drawn to Christ by the monks Lucian and Hilarion, he waited seven days after his own baptism, settled his sister in a women's monastery, and went off to the desert of Palestine.
