# Nicholas (Kuligin)

He is the third of the three presbyters the Russian calendar keeps in one line on 1 October, with a layman beside them and 1937 printed once for all four. The day line gives him as «Николая»; the site's index of names heads his page «Николай (Кулигин), протоиерей, сщмч.», an archpriest. One thing on that page is his alone of the four: it lists three days of memory where the others have two.

The other three carry this day and «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. His page carries a third, «8 июня (Петерб.)», an abbreviation the calendar uses and does not expand, and it is written down here as it was read rather than guessed at. His fellows on the line are [Alexander (Agafonnikov)](/saints/alexander-agafonnikov), [George (Arkhangelsky)](/saints/george-arkhangelsky) and [John (Artemov)](/saints/john-artemov). The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line.

*After the Russian church calendar's [1 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261001.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13813.html); read 4 October 2026.*
