# Zeno the Faster of Kiev

saint.gr's calendar for 30 January names Zeno the Faster of Kiev and gives him two sentences. He lived in the fourteenth century, it says, and practised his asceticism in the caves of the Great Lavra of Kiev in Russia; and he fell asleep in peace.

That is all. The epithet the faster is the page's own and is the only thing it says about how he lived; there is no year of birth, no year of death, no birthplace, no account of his tonsure, no wonders, and no apolytikion and no kontakion. The corpus keeps a dozen other men of the name Zeno, and none of them is this one.

The Russian calendar, which keeps the fathers of the Kiev Caves with their own accounts, is where this entry could be finished, and it has not been read for this folder.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 30 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/30/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2610/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
