# Peter the Gaoler

Peter was the gaoler set over the prison in which the priest [Ananias](/saints/ananias-26-january) was shut up, in Phoenicia under the governor Maximus, in the years saint.gr dates 295. The page says nothing of him before that: no homeland, no parentage, no year of birth, and no age.

What it does say is what he saw and what he did. Ananias, after the scourging and the heated spits and the vinegar and salt on his wounds, was fed in prison by God for whole days together; and that wonder drew Peter to faith in Christ, and with him seven [soldiers](/saints/seven-soldiers-26-january). The governor drowned them all in the sea together with Ananias, and so, the page says, they received the crowns of the contest from the Lord who gives them. It prints for them neither troparion nor kontakion.

The Russian calendar keeps him on the same menologion day, in the same line and with the year 295, and it says his office in two words where the Greek calendar said it in one: темничный страж, the prison guard. Its name link places him Финикийский, in Phoenicia. No hymn is printed for the line.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1172/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 26 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260126.html), read 3 October 2026.*
