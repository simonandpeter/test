# Carion

saint.gr's calendar for 24 Νοεμβρίου names «Όσιος Καρίων» and places him by his son: he is the father in the flesh and in the spirit of the venerable [Zacharias](/saints/zacharias-son-of-carion), whom the same calendar keeps on 24 March. For the rest the page prints one saying of his, which it credits to the Euergetinos, page 468, and the statement that he died in peace.

The saying is about living with a child: a man who stays with a child, if he is not strong, comes down and is harmed; and if he is strong, then although he does not come down — that is, is not harmed — still he makes no progress to a higher measure of virtue. The couplet set over the line says the same thing from the other side: having put everything far from the head of his soul, Carion contended with a sober mind right to the end.

The page gives him no country, no city, no monastery, no century, no year, no relics and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/24/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2997/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
