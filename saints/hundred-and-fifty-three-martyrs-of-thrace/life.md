# The Hundred and Fifty-three Martyrs of Thrace

saint.gr's whole notice of them is one sentence: these holy ones were martyred by drowning in the sea. The day's line gives their number and their country — «Άγιοι Εκατό πενήντα τρείς Μάρτυρες από τη Θράκη», a hundred and fifty-three martyrs out of Thrace — and the page names not one of them, gives no year, no emperor, no judge and no coast, and prints no hymn. Its heading gives the feast as fixed, kept on 27 July of every year.

Above the notice the page sets a distich, and it turns the number into the one in the Gospel: «Θαλασσοάθλους ισαρίθμους Xριστέ μου, Άγρα θαλάσσης Tιβεριάδος, δέχου» — receive, my Christ, these who contended in the sea, equal in number to the catch of the sea of Tiberias. That is the whole of what the Greek calendar prints of them, and this folder says no more.
