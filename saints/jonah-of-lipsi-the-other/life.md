# Jonah of Lipsi (the other)

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 29 июня ст. ст. names five venerable-martyrs of Lipsi on one line, «Прмчч. Неофита, Ионы, Неофита, Ионы и Парфения Липсийских.», and gives each of the five his own name link. The line gives no year at all — not a century, not a reign — and no place beyond the island in the epithet, and no folder here records one.

Each of the five name pages carries the name, the grade and «День памяти: 29 июня», and nothing else: no life, no account of the martyrdom, no monastery. Nothing is supplied for any of it. The line grades the five together «Прмчч.», venerable-martyrs, which is the grade recorded here; each name link grades the man alone «мч.», martyr, and that reading is recorded in this folder’s row and not in its `types`.

**Two of the five are called Neophytus and two are called Jonah, and the calendar tells them apart with the word «(другой)», the other, inside the name link of one of each pair.** The marker falls on the FIRST Neophytus of the line (name page 14285) and on the SECOND Jonah (name page 14287), which is the source’s own asymmetry and is left as it stands. The marker is an editorial note and not part of anybody’s name, so the Russian name form stored on both folders of a pair is the same, and a fold key naming the two holders is written for each pair in the same commit.

The day page carries two hymns, both of them of the chief apostles whose feast this day is, and none under any of these five names.

This folder is the fourth of the five as the line orders them, and its name page is 14287, which heads it «Иона Липсийский, (другой), мч.». That page and the day’s one line are the whole of the source for him.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 29 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260629.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14287.html); read 9 October 2026.*
