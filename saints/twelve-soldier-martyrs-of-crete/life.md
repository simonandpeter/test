# The Twelve Soldier Martyrs of Crete

saint.gr's calendar names them and gives almost no life. Its line for the day is «Άγιοι Δώδεκα Στρατιώτες Μάρτυρες από την Κρήτη», the twelve holy soldier martyrs from Crete, and the whole of its notice is one sentence: «Οι Άγιοι Δώδεκα Στρατιώτες Μάρτυρες από την Κρήτη μαρτύρησαν δια ξίφους.» They suffered martyrdom by the sword. The page names none of the twelve, and gives no year, no emperor and no commander.

Above that sentence it prints the distich the Greek books give them, which makes their number the whole of the praise: «Aριθμός αθλεί Mαρτύρων διά ξίφους, / Xριστού μαθηταίς ισάριθμος εγκρίτοις» — a number of martyrs contends by the sword, equal in number to the chosen disciples of Christ.

And then, in a parenthesis of its own beneath the verse, the page qualifies its own heading: «(Ή, εκ Kρήτης, παρ’ άλλοις.)» — or, from Crete, in others. It reads as a note that other books place the twelve where this one does not, without printing the other place; so Crete is recorded here because the day's own line says Crete, and the doubt is recorded with it.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/789/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
