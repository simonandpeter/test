# Anicetus of Rome

saint.gr says the holy hieromartyr Anicetus came from the city of Emesa in Syria and was bishop of Rome in the middle of the second century, which it dates 155 to 166. To him came [Polycarp](/saints/polycarp-of-smyrna), bishop of Smyrna, over the fixing of the time of the Pascha. As is well known, the page explains, the churches of Asia Minor kept Pascha on the fourteenth of the month Nisan, on whatever day of the week it fell; the other churches did not keep Pascha at all, but were content with the weekly Sunday celebration of the Resurrection, laying the greater stress on the first Sunday after the full moon of the spring equinox.

Because the church of Rome held a strict line against the Asians over that difference, the page says, Polycarp was obliged to go to Rome to settle the question with the bishop Anicetus, and some lesser matters with it. Anicetus was martyred in the year 166, under the emperor Marcus Aurelius, whose reign it dates 161 to 180. The page says nothing of the manner of his death, nothing of his family, and nothing of what was agreed at Rome; it prints neither couplet nor hymn.
