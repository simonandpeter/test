# Acoluthus, companion of Alexander and Barbarus

saint.gr's calendar for 14 May keeps three martyrs on one page with one notice between them: [Alexander](/saints/alexander-companion-of-barbarus), [Barbarus](/saints/barbarus-companion-of-alexander) and Acoluthus. These saints, it says, having lived a holy life and brought many idolaters to the Christian faith, were martyred by beheading; and their synaxis and feast is held in the church of Saint Irene, which stands near the sea.

That is the whole of the account, and the couplet above it counts the three — for the severed heads of three fellow contenders the Trinity sets three worthy crowns. His name, the page's gloss says, means the companion, the one who follows, the next. No century, no country, no emperor, no judge, no relics and no hymn is printed, and none is set down here.
