# Ebba the Elder

Ebba the Elder was the sister of Oswald and Oswiu, kings of Northumbria. The Greek synaxarion, which keeps her on 25 August among the saints of Britain, has no other details of her life, and none are supplied here.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 25 August — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/3357/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
