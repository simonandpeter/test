# Jonah of Odessa

Jonah Moiseevich Atamansky was born at Odessa on 14 September 1855. His father was called Moses and was a deacon, and his mother Glykeria; but they died early and left him an orphan while he was small, and he used to pass his time, saint.gr says, day and night, praying at his parents' graves. His uncle took the guardianship of the poor child, and Jonah advanced in virtue, in piety and in learning, with a divine zeal kindling in his heart. So in 1884 he was ordained deacon and in 1886 presbyter.

He served the Church through the Russo-Japanese war, through 1905, through the revolution of 1917 and through the years of the Church's persecution, and went on with his ministry without fear, in faith towards God and love towards the people; and God counted him worthy of the gift of wonderworking, so that many came to him for his blessing and for healing of their bodily and spiritual sicknesses. He fell asleep in peace in 1924. The canonical act of his glorification was made in 1995, and in 1996 his holy relics were translated to the church of the Dormition of the Mother of God at Odessa, where they are kept.
