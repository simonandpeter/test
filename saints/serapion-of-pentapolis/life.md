# Serapion of Pentapolis

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαρτίου names him third of four on one line — «Άγιοι Θεόδωρος ο Ιερομάρτυρας, Ειρηναίος ο Διάκονος, Σεραπίων και Αμμώνιος οι αναγνώστες» — and the page behind it has one sentence for the whole company: the holy hieromartyr Theodore was martyred in the year 304 in the Pentapolis of Libya, together with his deacon Irenaeus and the readers Serapion and Ammonius. Serapion was therefore a reader, one of the two whom the sentence sets after the deacon.

He suffered with [Theodore](/saints/theodore-of-pentapolis), with the deacon [Irenaeus](/saints/irenaeus-of-pentapolis) and with his fellow reader [Ammonius](/saints/ammonius-of-pentapolis), in the Pentapolis of Libya, the five cities of Cyrenaica; the page names no city among the five and gives no see for their bishop.

It heads him «(; - 304)», so there is no year of birth. It records no emperor, no judge, no manner of death, no relic, no hymn and no word of his own. The corpus has no rank slug for a reader, so that office is recorded here in the prose only.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3868/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
