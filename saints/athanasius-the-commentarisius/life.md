# Athanasius the Commentarisius

Athanasius was the commentarisius — the officer who kept the prison register — when [Zosimas of Cilicia](/saints/zosimas-the-hermit), a monk who had lived in the desert among the wild beasts, was taken by Domitian the governor and tortured for Christ. While Zosimas was under torture a lion ran out of the desert to the spectacle and spoke with a human voice, putting fear on everyone who stood there.

Seeing it, Athanasius knew the power of Christ, the one true God, and believed in him. The two of them then made their end in the mountains, where a rock split open and they went into it and gave up their souls to the Lord. Doxologia.ro prints this same short account on both men's pages; the calendar's own line for the day is only «Sfântul Mucenic Atanasie», and the page gives no year and no place but Cilicia and the mountains.

The Russian calendar keeps him on 4 января old style and names him on one line with the man whose prisoner he had charge of — «Прмч. Зосимы, пустынника Киликийского и мч. Афанасия комментарисия (III-IV)» — and it files the whole account under the other's name. [Zosimas](/saints/zosimas-the-hermit) came out of Cilicia and lived as a hermit; in the persecution under Diocletian, who reigned from 284 to 305, he was seized and cruelly tortured for the faith, and by the power of God he was kept unhurt. Seeing that wonder of God, the keeper of the prison, Athanasius by name, believed in Christ and was baptised. The two were let go, and they went away into the wilderness, where they lived out their struggle in a cleft of the mountain until their end.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 4 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/4-ianuarie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-mucenic-atanasie); read 19 September 2026; and after the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 4 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260104.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life150.htm) it files under Zosimas's name, read 3 October 2026.*
