# Athanasius the Commentarisius

Athanasius was the commentarisius — the officer who kept the prison register — when [Zosimas of Cilicia](/saints/zosimas-the-hermit), a monk who had lived in the desert among the wild beasts, was taken by Domitian the governor and tortured for Christ. While Zosimas was under torture a lion ran out of the desert to the spectacle and spoke with a human voice, putting fear on everyone who stood there.

Seeing it, Athanasius knew the power of Christ, the one true God, and believed in him. The two of them then made their end in the mountains, where a rock split open and they went into it and gave up their souls to the Lord. Doxologia.ro prints this same short account on both men's pages; the calendar's own line for the day is only «Sfântul Mucenic Atanasie», and the page gives no year and no place but Cilicia and the mountains.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 4 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/4-ianuarie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-mucenic-atanasie); read 19 September 2026.*
