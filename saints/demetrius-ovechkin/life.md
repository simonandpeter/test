# Demetrius (Ovechkin)

The Russian church calendar's line for 1 November is the whole of what is recorded of him here: «Сщмчч. Александра и Димитрия пресвитеров, мц. Елисаветы (1937)» — the hieromartyrs Alexander and Demetrius, presbyters, and the martyr Elizabeth, with the year 1937 closing the line for all three. Its index of names gives his surname and his grade, «Димитрий (Овечкин), иерей, сщмч.», a priest, and lists this day and the 26 January synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia against his name.

There is no life page behind that name, and the name page itself carries nothing but the two days. So this folder records the year of his death, his grade, the day the Church keeps him on and nothing else; azbyka.ru, which has carried long lives for others of these years, is a quota this corpus has spent and was not asked again on 4 October 2026.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 1 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261101.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9561.html); read 4 October 2026.*
