# Agathodorus (2 February)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its page for him prints the line «Sfântul Mucenic Agatodor» over the date, 2 Februarie, and over the readings appointed for a martyr — the Apostle from the second letter to Timothy and the Gospel from John — and nothing besides: no homeland, no century, no account of his suffering, and no troparion. The rank the calendar gives him is mucenic, martyr.

He is kept on the day of the Meeting of the Lord, together with two other martyrs the same calendar names and leaves equally unwritten, Gavriil and Iordan. Whether he is one of the martyrs of that name whom other calendars keep on other days is a question this page does not answer, and no other calendar was read for him.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, and where doxologia left him unwritten saint.gr gives him a life of three sentences. It says he was martyred at Tyana in Cappadocia — the town the page calls Νίδρα or Νίγδελι today — and that they first pulled out his teeth, then cut out his tongue, then took off his skin with a razor; his faith in Christ stayed whole and unshaken through all of it, and put his savage torturers to shame. At the last he died when they drove red-hot spits through his brain, out of which, the page says, his zeal for godliness and his love for Christ came away hotter still. The Greek gives no century and no year, and prints no hymn; over him it sets a couplet offering him a gift of words, to him who was given to God through blood. The question the Romanian entry left open — which Agathodorus this is — the Greek page answers this far and no further.
