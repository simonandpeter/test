# Elappas

saint.gr's calendar for 16 June names Elappas, and the page's whole content is one sentence about its sources rather than about the man: Saint Elappas is mentioned epigrammatically in the Small Euchologion or Hagiasmatarion, in the 1959 edition of the Apostoliki Diakonia, without other information, and nowhere else is his memory mentioned. So the calendar keeps the name and says plainly that it knows nothing further.

There is no rank beyond «Άγιος», no country, no year, no manner of death, no epigram and no hymn, and this life claims none. saint.gr does not put its own mark of a doubtful entry on the page — the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου» is not there — so the name is recorded as the calendar has it, with the one book it rests on named, and nothing is added to it.
