# Sergius the Confessor

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 13 mai reads «Sfântul Cuvios Serghie Mărturisitorul», and the page kept under his name carries no Viață tab and no Tropar tab — only the epistle and gospel appointed for the day, Ephesians 6:10–17 and Luke 12:8–12, which are the readings of a confessor. The calendar gives no country, no century, and no account of what he confessed or before whom; this page says no more than the calendar says.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 13 mai — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/13-mai) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-serghie-marturisitorul); read 20 September 2026.*
