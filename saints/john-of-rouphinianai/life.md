# John of Rouphinianai

saint.gr's calendar for 16 Μαρτίου names him «Όσιος Ιωάννης ο ἐν Ρουφιαναῖς», the venerable John in Rouphinianai, and the page behind that line holds one sentence about him: that the venerable John died peacefully. Beside it stand two lines of verse which make of that the whole of his praise — «Τιμῶμεν, Ἰωάννη, σὴν ἐκδημίαν, / Ἐνδημίαν δὲ πρὸς Θεὸν μᾶλλον φάναι» — we honour your departure, John, or rather, we should say, your coming home to God.

Everything else on the page is the note this calendar prints wherever the name John occurs. So no year is given, no century, no monastery, no office, no relic and no hymn; and the one thing that places him is the epithet of the line itself, Rouphinianai, the suburb across the water from Constantinople whose monastery the Greek calendar names him from. What is recorded here is the commemoration, the place, and a peaceful end.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 16 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/16/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3896/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
