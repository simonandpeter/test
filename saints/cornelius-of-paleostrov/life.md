# Cornelius of Paleostrov

Cornelius of Paleostrov was born in the city of Pskov in Russia and lived, saint.gr says, in the fourteenth and fifteenth centuries. He practised his ascesis on the island of Pali in Lake Onega, where he founded a monastic community and raised a church in honour of the Nativity of the Mother of God. For a greater ascesis he went on to live inside a cave near the monastery, where he prayed without ceasing, and there he fell asleep in peace in 1420.

That is the whole of what the page holds about him: no parentage, no teacher, no relics and no hymn. saint.gr prints a second Cornelius on the same day, of Komel and of the next century, on a page of his own.
