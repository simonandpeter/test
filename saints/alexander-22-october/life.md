# Alexander (22 October)

saint.gr's calendar for 22 Οκτωβρίου names six martyrs in one line and tells a single story for them. Alexander was a bishop, the page says, and preached the Gospel with great zeal to crowds of idolaters; his preaching drew a multitude of them to the Christian faith, and that enraged the ruler of the place, who ordered him seized and put to torture so as to force him to sacrifice to the idols. He was not persuaded.

A soldier named [Heraclius](/saints/heraclius-companion-of-alexander), seeing Alexander's endurance, believed in Christ; he was put to many tortures and at the last beheaded. Alexander himself was healed by the grace of God of the wounds of his torture, and worked a wonder besides, and so drew to the faith of Christ four women — [Theodota](/saints/theodota-of-adrianople), [Glyceria](/saints/glyceria-of-adrianople), [Anna](/saints/anna-of-adrianople) and [Elizabeth](/saints/elizabeth-of-adrianople).

These women confessed their faith before the ruler and were led for it under the executioner's sword; and after all of them Alexander too was beheaded with the sword. The page sets a couplet over each group: his own says that the venerable Alexander, his neck cut through, is a martyr now and not only a priest of Christ. It prints no hymn for any of the six.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 22 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/22/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2778/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
