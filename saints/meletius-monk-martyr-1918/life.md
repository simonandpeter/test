# Meletius (Golokolosov)

Meletius (Golokolosov) was born in 1870, of the Cossacks of the Don Host. In 1898 he entered the Glinsk hermitage as a novice and was tonsured there on 27 August 1902; on 25 August 1905 at his petition he was moved to the Issyk-Kul monastery of the Turkestan diocese, ordained hierodeacon on 30 April 1906 and hieromonk on 30 November of the same year, and on 22 December 1907, a hieromonk of the Issyk-Kul Holy Trinity monastery — the calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. After the ruin of the Issyk-Kul monastery he served in the Kopal district of the Semirechye region, and died in 1918 in the fratricidal strife. In August 2000 at the jubilee Council of Bishops he was glorified among the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 3 September (Julian).
