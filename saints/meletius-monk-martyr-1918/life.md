# Meletius (Golokolosov)

Meletius (Golokolosov) was born in 1870, of the Cossacks of the Don Host.
In 1898 he entered the Glinsk hermitage as a novice and was tonsured
there on 27 August 1902; on 25 August 1905 at his petition he was moved to
the Issyk-Kul monastery of the Turkestan diocese, ordained hierodeacon on
30 April 1906 and hieromonk on 30 November of the same year, and on 22
December 1907, a hieromonk of the Issyk-Kul Holy Trinity monastery, was
given the nabedrennik. On 2 May 1908 he was sent to fill the parish
priest’s place in the town of Przhevalsk, and afterwards was often away
on assignment, doing a pastor’s duties in small village parishes and
military garrisons to which, for their poverty and other hardships, a
married priest could not be appointed; on 1 April 1913 the Holy Synod
gave him the pectoral cross. After the ruin of the Issyk-Kul monastery in
the Kirghiz rising of 1916 he served in the Kopal district of the
Semirechye region, and there died in 1918 in the fratricidal strife. In
August 2000 the jubilee Council of Bishops glorified him among the new
martyrs and confessors of Russia for veneration by the whole church; the
Russian calendar keeps him on 3 September (Julian) and in the synaxis of
the saints of Central Asia.
