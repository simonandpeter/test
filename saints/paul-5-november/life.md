# Paul (5 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 5 Νοεμβρίου names Paul the bishop and gives no life. Its entry for the day is «Άγιοι Δομέντιος και Παύλος ο επίσκοπος», and the whole of the page behind it is one notice on the pair: «Άγνωστοι στους Συναξαριστές. Αναφέρονται στο Ιεροσολυμιτικό Κανονάριο εκδ. Καλλίστου Αρχιμανδρίτου σελ. 117, χωρίς βιογραφικά στοιχεία» — unknown to the synaxarists, mentioned in the Jerusalem Kanonarion, Archimandrite Kallistos's edition at page 117, with no biographical details.

The rank is the whole of what is stated of him beyond the name and the day: a bishop, and the page names no see for him, no century, no country, no manner of death and no year. It prints neither a couplet nor a hymn. The other man of the entry is [Domentius](/saints/domentius-5-november), of whom it says exactly as little.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2898/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
