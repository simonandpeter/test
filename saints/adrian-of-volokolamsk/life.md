# Adrian of Volokolamsk

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life beyond one sentence shared with his companion. The venerable [Zosimas](/saints/zosimas-of-volokolamsk) and Adrian of Volokolamsk, founders of the monastery of Sestrin at the mouth of the river Sestra, lived in the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries. They fell asleep in peace, and their holy relics were laid in the church of the Dormition of the Mother of God of that monastery.

The page spells his name Ανδριανός, with the nu, and that is the form kept here; the English Adrian is the form a reader would look for. Beyond the founding, the river and the two centuries there is nothing: no year of birth or death, no birthplace, no tonsure, no office in the house and no hymn. Which of the two was the elder, and which brought the other, the page does not say.
