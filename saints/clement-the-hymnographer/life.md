# Clement the Studite

Clement was a Studite monk and a disciple of Theodore the Studite, saint.gr says, and after his teacher's death he took his place in the abbacy of the monastery. He was a fervent adherent of the veneration of the holy icons, and he died of hardships in exile as a confessor of the faith. And it is of course well known, the page adds, that he was a Byzantine hymnographer of the ninth century, a poet of canons.

That is the whole of it: no year of birth, no year of death, no place of exile named and no canon of his identified. His couplet turns on the songs: «Τέρψας ὁ Κλήμης γηγενεῖς ᾠδαῖς κάτω, / Ἀπῆλθε τέρψων, ὥσπερ οἶμαι, καὶ νόας» — Clement, having delighted the earth-born below with his odes, departed, I suppose, to delight the intelligences too. The one saint the page names is [Theodore the Studite](/saints/theodore-the-studite).
