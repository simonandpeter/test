# John of Bithynia

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 13 июня ст. ст. in one line with his mother — «Прп. Анны (826) и сына ее Иоанна (IX).» — and the name link behind «Иоанна», name page 966, gives the calendar's own nominative, «Иоанн Вифинский, прп.», a venerable father of Bithynia, with this day and no other beside it. The line names him, and that is why he is kept here in a folder of his own.

The life the day page sends the reader to is one reading for the mother and the son together, headed «Преподобная Анна и сын ее святой Иоанн». It says that they lived in Byzantium; that [his mother](/saints/anna-renamed-euphemianos) was the daughter of a deacon of the church of the Blachernae at Constantinople; and that after her husband's death she put on men's clothing and, under the name Евфимиан, went with her son to struggle in one of the Bithynian houses near Olympus. She fell asleep at Constantinople in the year 826.

That is the whole of what this calendar records of him: a son who went with his mother into a men's monastery in Bithynia and whom the Church keeps beside her. The day line gives him the ninth century and no year of his own, and no year is written here. The other reading the calendar keeps for his mother, headed «Преподобная Анна Вифинская» and «Дни памяти: 13 июня , 29 октября», adds that she was renowned for the gift of wonders in her own lifetime, and does not name him. The day page prints no hymn for either of them.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 13 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260613.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1297.htm); read 9 October 2026.*
