# Nilus of Stolobnoe

saint.gr's calendar for 6 December names the venerable Nilus, the New and wonderworker, «ὁ ἐν τῇ νήσῳ Στολοβνόε ἐν τῇ λίμνῃ Σεληγέρα» — he who is on the island of Stolobnoe in the lake Seliger — and then says in one sentence that it has no details of the Russian venerable's life. That is the whole of the page: no century, no parentage, no manner of death, no relic, no hymn, and no synaxaristic couplet.

What the entry says is in the line itself, and it is set down here as the line has it: a Russian ascetic whom the Greek calendar places on the island of Stolobnoe in lake Seliger, calls the New, and calls a wonderworker. Nothing further is claimed of him here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3139/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
