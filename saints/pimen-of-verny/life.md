# Pimen

Pimen — Peter Zakharyevich Belolikov in the world — bishop of Semirechye and Verny, was born on 5 November 1879 in the village of Vasilyevskoye in the Cherepovets district of Novgorod province, into the large and devout family of the priest Zachary Ivanovich and his wife Maria, daughter of the priest John Ornatsky; the well-known Petersburg priests Philosoph and John Ornatsky were his cousins, and through Father John he stood in family connection with John of Kronstadt, in whose circle, living a vivid church life, the future hieromartyr was formed. The Sretensky calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. The Russian calendar keeps him on 3 September (Julian) with the presbyters Sergius, Basil, Philip and Vladimir and the monk-martyr Meletius, killed in 1918.
