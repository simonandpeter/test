# Damascene of Gabrovo

Damascene came from the village of Gabrovo, in the province of Tarnovo in Bulgaria. He came to the Holy Mountain and was a monk in the Serbian monastery of Hilandar, and there he was made hieromonk. Later the fathers of the monastery sent him to the town of Svishtov in Bulgaria, where the monastery held a dependency, meaning to have him back at Hilandar once his errand in Bulgaria was done.

The errand was to recover what a Turk owed the monastery for that dependency. Instead the man slandered the martyr, saying that he had had dealings with a Mohammedan woman; and having found his opening, he seized the property of the dependency and dragged Damascene himself violently before the judge. The judge objected, but the false witnesses standing by carried the day and obtained the sentence of death by hanging.

The Turks pressed him hard to embrace Islam and save his life, and he stayed firm in his faith. So on 16 January 1771 he prayed, and then received a martyr's death on the gallows. saint.gr prints no hymn for him. Over him it sets a couplet that reads the slander as the gift: the martyrdom is bestowed on you, athlete of the Lord, by way of a calumny.
