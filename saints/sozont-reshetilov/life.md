# Sozont (Reshetilov)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him last of six priests of 1937 on 23 октября ст. ст. The line declines his name and gives it without a surname; the title on the name link behind his place supplies both the surname and the grade, «Созонт (Решетилов), иерей, сщмч.». His is the name the word «пресвитеров» stands after, which is what fixes the grade of the five before him as well as his own, and the link behind his place says иерей, the same thing.

The calendar carries a life for one of the six and his is not it. Nothing on either page says where he was born or served, what he was charged with, or whether he died with the one whose life it does carry. The forename is uncommon, and the corpus holds no other man of it with this surname.

The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line. The day page's five troparia and kontakia all belong to the three older entries it heads, and none of them to this company of 1937.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 23 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261023.html); read 4 October 2026.*
