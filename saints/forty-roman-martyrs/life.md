# The Forty Roman Martyrs

saint.gr's calendar for 16 June names the Forty Roman Martyrs and gives no life. All the page says of them is one sentence: the holy Forty Roman Martyrs were martyred by fire. Beside it stands a two-line epigram which counts the number out in fives instead of naming it — fire burns five times five brave men, the page glossing that as twenty, and five times five times five and five more, glossed again as twenty — and that is the only other thing printed about them.

The page gives no names for the forty, no year, no emperor, no place beyond Rome and no hymn. Because it enumerates nobody it is one entry and one folder for the company, and they are not the Forty Martyrs of Sebaste, whom the calendar keeps on 9 March and names one by one, nor any other company of forty.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 16 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/16/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1870/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
