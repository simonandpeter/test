# Alvianus of Aneia

He was bishop of the city of Aneia in the province of Asia, and he suffered for Christ about the year 304, in the persecution of the Christians under the emperor Diocletian and his co-ruler Maximian. He was ordered to sacrifice to the idols on pain of death; he confessed his faith in Christ firmly and refused to serve them. They tortured him with red-hot iron and beat him without mercy, and he remained unshaken.

A disciple of his was tortured along with him, and he too stayed faithful to the Lord Jesus Christ. Both of the holy martyrs were sentenced to death and thrown into a red-hot furnace, and in it they died and received the martyrs' crowns. The calendar's own line gives the companions in the plural, «и учеников его», and the life in the singular, and neither names any of them; the day page carries no hymn under his name.
