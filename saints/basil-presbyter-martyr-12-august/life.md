# Basil the Presbyter, Hieromartyr

Basil Ivanovich Infantyev was born in 1853 and was the priest of the church in the village of Mekhonskoye in the Shadrinsk district. On 12 August 1918 — 25 August by the civil calendar — he was killed in the village of Taushkan during the mass repressions against the clergy in the Perm region. The Holy Synod added him to the Synaxis of the New Martyrs and Confessors of Russia on 17 July 2002. The Sretensky calendar prints no life for him and no year; this one follows azbyka.ru, which cites the Orthodox Encyclopaedia. The Russian calendar keeps him on 12 August (Julian) with [Alexius](/saints/alexius-presbyter-martyr-12-august) the presbyter.
