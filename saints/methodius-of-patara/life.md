# Methodius of Patara

Methodius gave himself to God from his youth, doxologia's life says, and showed himself a clean vessel and a dwelling of the Holy Spirit. By the divine election he was counted worthy of the rank of a bishop and took the pastorate of the reasonable sheep of the city of Patara, which is in the parts of Lycia. There he shepherded his flock well and in the love of God, enlightening the fullness of the Church with gentle words.

In that time the heresy of Origen had risen and was deceiving many. Seeing it grow strong, the hierarch of Christ stood up against it like a chosen shepherd and burned it with his words as with a divine fire, undoing all the darkness and fog of the heresy by his most wise teaching and by the divine gift. His God-inspired words shone out over the whole world like lightning, and the report of him went out into all the earth like the sound of a trumpet.

The unseen enemy could not bear his boldness or his great labours for the Church of Christ and for the right faith, and armed against him the enemies that are seen, his own servants, the Greeks who worshipped idols, urging them to kill by torments the servant of God, who before his martyrdom had already clothed himself in the life-giving mortification of the body. So the pagans took the hierarch of Christ and cut off his head with the sword, and he passed to the better life: he who had served the Lamb of God was himself brought as a living sacrifice, slain like a lamb.

For this he was adorned with a double crown, being a brave defender of the right faith: he fought against the heresies, and before the Greeks he confessed Christ with boldness to the shedding of his blood and to a martyr's end. The life places his suffering in the reign of Aurelian and prints no year, so none is recorded here.
