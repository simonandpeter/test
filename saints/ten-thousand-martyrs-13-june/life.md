# The Ten Thousand Martyrs (13 June)

saint.gr's calendar for 13 Ιουνίου names them and gives no life. Its whole notice is one sentence, and half of that is a gloss on the word: «Οι Άγιοι Μύριοι (δηλαδή δέκα χιλιάδες) Μάρτυρες μαρτύρησαν δια ξίφους» — the holy Myrioi, that is to say ten thousand, martyrs were martyred by the sword. No name, no place, no emperor, no judge and no year is given for any of them, and the page prints no hymn.

What it does print is the couplet of the synaxarion, which turns the number itself into the praise: «Nικά πεσούσα μυριάς διά ξίφους, / Πίπτουσα και γαρ η Θεού νικά φάλαγξ» — the ten thousand conquer in falling by the sword, for God's phalanx conquers even as it falls. Because the entry names nobody, it is kept here as one entry.
