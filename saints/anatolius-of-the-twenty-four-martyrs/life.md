# Anatolias, one of the Twenty-four Martyrs

saint.gr's calendar for 6 Ιουλίου keeps a company it calls the Twenty-four Martyrs, and its page names fifteen of them. They were among the many who were martyred in the year 301 by beheading, it says, together with [Lucia](/saints/lucia-of-rome-companion-of-rix) the virgin-martyr and [Rix](/saints/rix-the-vicar) the vicar, who suffered in Campania of Italy. Lucia had been seized by Rix, who pressed her to sacrifice to the idols and to deny Christ; instead she brought him to the faith of Christ, and he left wife, children, wealth and all worldly glory behind and went away with her to Campania to be martyred there.

In that city both of them were arrested, and before the governor they confessed Christ with courage and were beheaded; and with them, the page says, many other holy martyrs were beheaded as well. These are the names it gives: Anatolias, Antoninus, Lycias, Neas, Serinus, Diodorus, Dion, Apollonius, Apamus, Pappianus, Cottyius, Oronus, Papicus, Satyrus and Victor — and then «και άλλοι εννέα», nine others, whom it does not name.

That is everything the source says of them. It gives no country for any one of them apart from the Campania where they died, no rank, no age and no separate manner of death, and it prints no hymn under their line — only the synaxarion couplet: «Eικάς τετάρτη Mαρτύρων έργον ξίφους, / Ώφθη υπέρ σού, Xριστέ δόξα Mαρτύρων» — a four-and-twentieth of martyrs was the sword's work, and it was seen for you, O Christ, the glory of the martyrs.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/683/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
