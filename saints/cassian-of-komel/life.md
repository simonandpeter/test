# Cassian of Komel

saint.gr says that Cassian of Komel was a disciple of Saint Cornelius of Komel and kept his ascesis in the monastery of Komel. Every day he laboured to imitate his spiritual teacher in everything, and he followed his monastic rule faithfully. He counselled the monks to pass their lives in the fear of God, to pray unceasingly, to occupy themselves with the cultivation of their inner world and to put off all their worldly thoughts, to be sober in mind and watchful in soul.

The page says he fell asleep in peace in 1537, and its head line gives the same year with his year of birth left open. On the same day, and on the same page, it keeps [Laurence of Komel](/saints/laurence-of-komel), who was a disciple of the same teacher and became abbot of that monastery in 1538.

Nothing else is printed for Cassian: no birthplace, no parents, no year of entering the monastery, no office, no wonder told out, no relics and no hymn; and nothing else is set down here.
