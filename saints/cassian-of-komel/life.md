# Cassian of Komel

saint.gr says that Cassian of Komel was a disciple of Saint Cornelius of Komel and kept his ascesis in the monastery of Komel. Every day he laboured to imitate his spiritual teacher in everything, and he followed his monastic rule faithfully. He counselled the monks to pass their lives in the fear of God, to pray unceasingly, to occupy themselves with the cultivation of their inner world and to put off all their worldly thoughts, to be sober in mind and watchful in soul.

The page says he fell asleep in peace in 1537, and its head line gives the same year with his year of birth left open. On the same day, and on the same page, it keeps [Laurence of Komel](/saints/laurence-of-komel), who was a disciple of the same teacher and became abbot of that monastery in 1538.

Nothing else is printed for Cassian: no birthplace, no parents, no year of entering the monastery, no office, no wonder told out, no relics and no hymn; and nothing else is set down here.

The Russian calendar's two readings of him are nearly the same words and both are about his teacher. He was a disciple of Корнилий Комельский, and he governed the house at Komel during the time his teacher spent away at lake Sura. Chosen abbot by the brethren with that teacher's blessing and consent, he tried to copy him in everything and kept his rule strictly; and following his directions he taught the monks to abide in prayer with the fear of God, to attend to the inner work and drive off every earthly thought, to be sober in mind, watchful in soul and contrite in heart — which both readings cite from the first chapter of the rule on good order in church and on common prayer. When his teacher came back to the monastery he met him with joy and gave up the abbacy, wishing to remain as before in obedience to the old man. He died in 1537, the first reading adding that he went before his teacher and finished his course earlier than he did.
