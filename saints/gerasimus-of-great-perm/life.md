# Gerasimus of Great Perm

saint.gr's calendar for 29 January keeps three Russian hierarchs together and gives them one sentence between them: that Gerasimus, who fell asleep in 1441, [Pitirim](/saints/pitirim-of-perm), who fell asleep in 1455, and [Jonas](/saints/jonas-of-great-perm), who fell asleep in 1471, came from Russia, lived in the fifteenth century, all three were bishops of Great Perm, and all three fell asleep in holiness and in peace.

Of Gerasimus in particular the page records nothing more: no year of birth, no birthplace, no length of his episcopate, no manner of his death, and no apolytikion and no kontakion. It does say where his relics are: in the church of the Annunciation of the Theotokos at Ust-Vym in the region of Vologda in Russia, where it puts the relics of the other two as well.

The Russian calendar, which keeps these three bishops of Perm with their own accounts, is where this entry could be finished, and it has not been read for this folder.

The Russian calendar keeps him on the same menologion day as «Свт. Герасима Великопермского, Устьвымского (ок. 1449)», and it gives him the service that was missing. His troparion says what his episcopate was for — «землю Зырянскую озарил еси», thou didst enlighten the land of the Zyryans, and brought many of its people to Christ — and his kontakion has the Russian Church honouring him as a most great priest.

The same calendar keeps him again on 29 января ст. ст., first of the three bishops of Great Perm whom one line holds — «Свтт. Герасима (ок. 1441-1467), Питирима (1455) и Ионы (1470)» — where the 24 января line that his row records names him alone. The joint day gives the three of them four hymns of their own, three troparia and a kontakion, which are added here; the second day is a note and not a second feast.
