# Rufinus (7 May)

saint.gr's whole account of the two of them is one sentence: the holy Rufinus and [Satorninus](/saints/satorninus-7-may) were martyred by the sword at Caesarea, of Cappadocia, at the time when the proconsul of Nicomedia Perinios was bringing the martyr [Codratus](/saints/codratus-of-nicomedia) there — and it sends the reader to that man's page on the same day.

The couplet over them says only how they died: the sword dared the cutting of two fellow-contenders, for they had been tempered in the faith of the Lord, «Δυοῖν συνάθλων πρὸς τομὸν τόλμη ξίφος. / Πίστει γὰρ ἐστομοῦντο τῇ τοῦ Κυρίου». The page gives them no year, no judge of their own, no country of birth, no relics and no hymn; and it does not say whether they were of Codratus's company or men whom his passing merely dated. Nothing is claimed here beyond the names, the rank, the sword, the city and the day.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/07/index.aspx) and [the life of the pair](https://www.saint.gr/4004/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
