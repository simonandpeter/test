# Theodore (Malyarovsky)

Theodore Malyarovsky was born in 1885 in the village of Novoorskoye in the Orenburg region. Of him it is known that he was a priest and in the thirties lived in the village of Grodekovo in the Mirzoyan district of the South Kazakhstan region; on 12 June 1937 he was arrested by the Mirzoyan district NKVD, defined in the criminal case as a priest of the Tikhonite orientation and charged with carrying on counter-revolutionary Trotskyist-fascist propaganda. At the trial he did not admit guilt; on 31 August 1937 the military tribunal of the NKVD for the South Kazakhstan region sentenced him to the highest measure, and on 4 September 1937 he was shot. At the jubilee Council of Bishops of 2000 he was numbered among the saints, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 22 August (Julian).
