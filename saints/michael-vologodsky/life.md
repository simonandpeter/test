# Michael (Vologodsky)

His line on the Russian 1 October reads «Сщмч. Михаила пресвитера (1920)» — Michael the hieromartyr, presbyter, 1920 — and the site's index of names heads his page «Михаил (Вологодский), иерей, сщмч.», which gives the surname and keeps him a priest rather than an archpriest. The surname is a place-name, Vologda's, as a great many Russian clerical surnames are, and the calendar does not say whether he served anywhere near it.

The year sets him slightly apart from the rest of the day. Two of the three new-martyr lines here belong to the two great waves, 1918 and 1937; 1920 belongs to the civil war between them. The index keeps him on this day and on 26 January with the company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and prints nothing further. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line.

*After the Russian church calendar's [1 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261001.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9679.html); read 4 October 2026.*
