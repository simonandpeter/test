# Timothy of Euripos

saint.gr says that the venerable Timothy was born at the village of Kalamos in Attica in the year 1510. His father was a priest and taught him his first Christian letters, and from childhood Timothy was marked out for the purity of his character and his reverence towards things divine. He studied the sacred letters at Athens with the help of the then bishop of Oropos. After his studies he returned to Oropos, to his protector the bishop, who ordained him deacon and then priest; and when that bishop died, Timothy took his place.

So great was Timothy's spiritual work among the people of God, the page goes on, that he was later elected archbishop of Euripos, which is Chalkis. But the difficulties of that time obliged him in 1575 to take refuge in his own country, Kalamos in Attica. From there he withdrew to the mountain of Penteli, where in 1578 he built the holy monastery of the Dormition of the Theotokos, which later took the name of this venerable father.

In 1590 he went to the island of Kea, which is also called Tzia, and there on 16 August he gave up his spirit in peace. His holy skull, the page ends, is preserved to this day in the monastery of Penteli, along with a part of his relics. Beyond that the Greek calendar gives him no hymn, and this folder records none.
