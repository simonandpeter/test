# Paul of Simonov

saint.gr's line for him is «Όσιος Παύλος εκ Ρωσίας», Paul out of Russia, and the page behind it is one sentence long: Paul, disciple of St Paisius — the page gives the family name in brackets, «(Βελιτσκόφσκυ)», the elder the corpus keeps as [Paisius of Neamț](/saints/paisius-of-neamt) — lived the ascetic life in the monastery of Simonov in Russia, and fell asleep in peace in the year 1825.

The discipleship and the monastery are what the page offers to tell him apart from the other Pauls; it gives him no year of birth, no account of his asceticism, no writings, and no hymn or verses. On the page's own arithmetic he outlived his elder by well over thirty years.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/03/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1199/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
