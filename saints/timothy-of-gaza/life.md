# Timothy of Gaza

Timothy, [Agapius](/saints/agapius-of-gaza) and [Thecla](/saints/thecla-of-gaza) suffered in 304. Timothy was born at Gaza in Palestinian Caesarea; he studied the Scriptures and, receiving a special gift of speech, became a teacher of the Christian faith. In the persecution under Diocletian and Maximian he was brought before the governor Urbanus, declared himself a Christian without fear and preached of the love of the Lord Jesus Christ for men and of his coming into the world for their salvation; he was cruelly tortured, and when they saw him unbending, burned. In the same city and the same year Agapius and Thecla suffered for Christ, given to the beasts. The Prologue tells it the same way for the Serbian calendar: Timothy burned, Agapius and Thecla thrown to the beasts, under Diocletian.

The Romanian calendar keeps the three of them on 19 August, and doxologia.ro prints one short notice under all three names. It says the glorious martyr Timothy was of Palestine by birth and, being perfect in life and in word, was made a teacher of the right faith; that he was seized and stood at Gaza before Urban, and said that he was on the Christians' side; that he was badly beaten and tortured in every way, and, since he would not turn at all, was put into the fire, where he received his end. In the same city [Agapius](/saints/agapius-of-gaza) and [Thecla](/saints/thecla-of-gaza) of Byzia, holding to the faith of Christ, were given to the beasts and took the crown of martyrdom through them.
