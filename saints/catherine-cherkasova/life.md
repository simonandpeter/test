# Catherine (Cherkasova)

The Russian calendar's line is all there is. It names three together on this day and gives no year at all: «Прмч. Серафима, прмц. Евдокии и Екатерины» — the venerable-martyr Seraphim and the venerable-martyrs Eudocia and Catherine. The name behind her part of it reads «Екатерина (Черкасова), прмц.», Catherine Cherkasova, venerable-martyr — her surname and the manner of her end, and no office or house beside it.

Behind that line there is no life page for any of the three, and the day prints no hymn for them. Nothing here says where they lived, what house they belonged to, when they were killed or by whom: the calendar gives the names, the ranks and nothing further. She is kept with [Seraphim](/saints/seraphim-bulashov), whom the calendar calls an abbot, and [Eudocia](/saints/eudocia-kuzminova), whom it calls a novice.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 23 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260123.html); read 3 October 2026.*
