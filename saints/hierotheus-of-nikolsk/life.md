# Hierotheus of Nikolsk

saint.gr says the hieromartyr Hierotheus the New, bishop of Nikolsk, was born about 1891. He arrived at Nikolsk as bishop on Palm Sunday of 1923, and there served the Church with godly zeal and self-denial. In 1927 he was transferred to the see of Veliky Ustyug, near the region of Vologda.

In that period he refused to sign the declaration of Metropolitan Sergius on the subjection of the Church to the power of the state. For that reason the men of the secret police, in the time of the Soviet regime, summoned him for interrogation several times. In the end they arrested him while he had gone to serve the liturgy in a small town of his province, and the people wept and cried out, because they felt that they were losing their spiritual father.

The steamer soon reached the city of Ustyug. Because he was ill, they took him to the hospital, and there Hierotheus fell asleep in holiness in the year 1928. But his memory did not disappear, the page says: he became the salt of the piety and the hope of the Russian people. The calendar calls him hieromartyr, although what it narrates is an arrest and a death in a hospital bed, and it prints no hymn for him.
