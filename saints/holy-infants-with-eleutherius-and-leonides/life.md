# The Holy Infants, with Eleutherius and Leonides

saint.gr's calendar names them and gives no life. They are the third party of the day's one line, «Άγιοι Ελευθέριος, Λεωνίδης και τα Άγια Νήπια» — [Eleutherius](/saints/eleutherius-martyr-8-august), [Leonides](/saints/leonides-companion-of-eleutherius) and the Holy Infants — and the whole of what the page says of their story is two sentences: «Μαρτύρησαν όλοι δια πυρός. Η σύναξη τους γίνεται στην Αγία Ειρήνη στις Ιουστινιαναίς.» They all suffered martyrdom by fire, and their synaxis is kept at the church of Saint Irene in Justinianae.

The page prints no number for them, no name of any one of them, no year, no country and no hymn. Its distich speaks of two contenders only, «Ἀμφοῖν ἀθλητῶν, οὐ θυόντων τῇ πλάνῃ» — of both contenders, who would not sacrifice to the error — so even the verse the Greek books give the day leaves the infants unnamed and uncounted, and they are kept here as the one company the source makes of them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2236/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
