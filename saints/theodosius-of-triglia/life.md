# Theodosius of Triglia

saint.gr's calendar for 1 Ιανουαρίου keeps Theodosius, abbot of Triglia, and its page opens by saying plainly that we do not know many things about his life. What it does record is that he was hegumen of one of the four known monasteries at Triglia — the monastery of Medikion, the monastery of Bathys Ryax, the monastery of Saint Stephen and the monastery of Saint John — and it does not say which of the four was his.

The page adds that his memory is mentioned only by [Saint Nicodemus](/saints/nicodemus-the-hagiorite) in his Synaxarist, and prints a two-line epigram: Theodosius, shown great in virtues, was judged fit to rule over a venerable monastery as well. No birthplace, no year and no account of his death is given on the page, and none is supplied here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/6/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
