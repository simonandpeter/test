# Sisinius

Sisinius and [Atticus](/saints/atticus-the-martyr) were martyred by the sword; perhaps, the synaxarion says, they were arrested together with [Adrian](/saints/adrian-of-nicomedia) in Nicomedia, whose memory is the same day. Their verse: two athletes run the course together, running the stadium of the sword light-footed. Nothing more is recorded.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 26 August — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/903/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
