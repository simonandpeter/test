# Adrian (the second of the day)

Adrian lived in the years of Licinius (313). He came from Rome, the son of Probus, who the synaxarion says ruled Rome in 276, and lived in Byzantium with his brother Dometius, later bishop of Byzantium after Titus. Longing to suffer for Christ he went to Nicomedia and rebuked Licinius for troubling the Roman army in vain on the pretext of persecuting Christians; Licinius tortured him in various ways and at last beheaded him.

Dometius took his relic and buried it at Argyropolis near Byzantium, where the relics of [Adrian and Natalia](/saints/adrian-of-nicomedia) also lay, with those of the apostle Stachys, first bishop of Byzantium after Andrew the First-called.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 26 August — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/901/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
