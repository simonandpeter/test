# Agapius, son of Eustathius

Agapius was a son of [Eustathius the Great
Martyr](/saints/eustathius-the-great-martyr) and
[Theopiste](/saints/theopiste-wife-of-eustathius), baptised with his parents and his brother
[Theopistus](/saints/theopistus-son-of-eustathius). He was separated from his father on the road
into exile and found again years afterwards, and under Hadrian was tortured with the rest of the
household for refusing the sacrifices. The Greek calendar keeps him with them on 20 September.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 20 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/985/saint.aspx); read 26 August 2026.*
