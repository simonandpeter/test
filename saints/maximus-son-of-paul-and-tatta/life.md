# Maximus, son of Paul and Tatta

[Paul](/saints/paul-of-damascus) and [Tatta](/saints/tatta-of-damascus) and their children [Sabinian](/saints/sabinian-son-of-paul-and-tatta), Maximus, [Rufus](/saints/rufus-son-of-paul-and-tatta) and [Eugene](/saints/eugene-son-of-paul-and-tatta) came from Damascus. Because they were Christians they were arrested and tortured horribly until they died. That is the whole of the Greek notice; its verse has the father Paul bear witness with his children, and Tatta wisely take her place with them. It gives no date.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 25 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2514/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
