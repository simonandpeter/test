# Nicostratus

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 9 noiembrie reads «Sfântul Mucenic Nicostrat», and the page it points to has no Viață tab: it carries the name, the day, and a Tropar — a common of the martyrs, sung to no one of them by name. His is a name the corpus has not held before.

The hymn page is headed «Troparul Sfinţilor Mucenici Claudiu, Castor, Sempronian şi Nicostrat; al Sfinţilor Mucenici Onisifor şi Porfirie», which sets him in one company of four with [Claudius](/saints/claudius-martyr-9-november), [Castor](/saints/castor-martyr-9-november) and [Sempronian](/saints/sempronian), and distinguishes that company from the two martyrs kept the same day.

That grouping is the whole of what the calendar gives beyond the name: no country, no century, no year, no emperor, no governor and no manner of death.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 9 noiembrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/9-noiembrie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-nicostrat); read 25 September 2026.*
