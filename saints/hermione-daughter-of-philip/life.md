# Hermione, daughter of the Apostle Philip

Hermione was a daughter of the apostle Philip — one of his four daughters who, the Greek synaxarion says, had the prophetic gift (Acts 21:8–9) and gave herself to the apostolic work. Wishing to see John the Theologian she set out with her sister Eutychis for Asia to find him, and on the way learned of his death; going on, the sisters met Paul’s disciple [Petronius](/saints/petronius-of-ephesus) and became his disciples, imitating him in everything. Hermione, skilled in medicine, helped many Christians and healed sicknesses by the power of Christ. At that time the emperor Trajan (98–117) was marching against the Persians and passed with his army through the place where she lived; told that she was a Christian — and having heard much of her gift of prophecy, the Greek says — he had her brought to prophesy for him. The Sretensky calendar’s life runs on past the point read here; the calendars give her death about 117. The Greek synaxarion keeps with her [Theotimus and Theodulus](/saints/theotimus-the-executioner), the executioners who believed through her.
