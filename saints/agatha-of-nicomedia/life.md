# Agatha of Nicomedia

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Agatha among the martyrs of Nicomedia on 28 December, inside a line that enumerates thirteen names beside [The Twenty Thousand Martyrs of Nicomedia](/saints/twenty-thousand-martyrs-of-nicomedia) themselves. The reading behind that line, life 3189, makes her the abbess of the women’s monastery to which [Domna of Nicomedia](/saints/domna-of-nicomedia) came when she had feigned madness to be sent out of the emperor’s palace.

The reading says the abbess clothed Domna in man’s dress, cut her hair and let her go from the monastery — and that when Maximian’s soldiers came looking for the runaway priestess they ruined the house, threw the sisters into prison and gave them over to torture and insult, and that not one of them was defiled. Of that house the reading names the abbess Agatha and [Theophila of Nicomedia](/saints/theophila-of-nicomedia), who was sent to a brothel and brought out of it.

When about twenty thousand Christians had gathered in the church of Nicomedia and refused to sacrifice, the herald’s threat was carried out and the church was burnt with the people in it. The reading says the abbess Agatha was among them, and Theophila with her. The day line closes «(302)» for the whole company; the reading gives no year of its own, only the reign of Maximian. The day page prints two hymns, a troparion and a kontakion of the martyrs, and both are the company’s own; nothing stands under this name alone.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 28 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261228.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life3189.htm); read 13 October 2026.*
