# Timothy, companion of Diogenes

saint.gr's calendar names him with his companion and gives no life beyond a sentence: «Οι Άγιοι Μάρτυρες Τιμόθεος και Διογένης μαρτύρησαν στη Μακεδονία, πιθανώς το έτος 345 μ.Χ., θανατούμενοι από αιρετικούς Αρειανούς» — the holy martyrs Timothy and [Diogenes](/saints/diogenes-companion-of-timothy) were martyred in Macedonia, probably in the year 345, being put to death by heretical Arians.

That is all the page says of either of them. It names no city in Macedonia, no bishop and no judge, tells nothing of the manner of their death, names no relics and prints no couplet and no hymn. The two notes above their names on the page are the site's standing etymologies of the names Τιμόθεος and Διογένης, with their lists of ancient generals, sculptors and philosophers, and have nothing to do with these two men.

The page's own «πιθανώς», probably, hangs over the year, and its heading nonetheless prints it flat as «(; - 345)»; the year is recorded here with that qualification in its note.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/06/index.aspx) and [the life of the two](https://www.saint.gr/1827/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
