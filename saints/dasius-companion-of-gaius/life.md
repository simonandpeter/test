# Dasius, companion of Gaius

saint.gr's calendar for 21 Οκτωβρίου names three martyrs in one line and tells a single story for them. [Gaius](/saints/gaius-21-october), Dasius and [Zoticus](/saints/zoticus-companion-of-gaius) were at Nicomedia, the page says, and inspired by divine zeal they went and threw down the idols of the Greeks inside their temples. Afterwards they gave themselves up of their own accord, and endured fearful tortures.

They were hung upon wooden frames and their flesh was rubbed with cloths woven of goat's hair; then stones were tied to their necks and they were thrown into the sea, and so all three received a martyr's end. The couplet set over the entry turns that death about: three men who died in salt water, it says, now drink the sweet torrent of delight above.

That is all the page prints of them. It gives no year, no emperor, no trades and no families, and it prints no hymn for them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 21 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/21/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2562/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
