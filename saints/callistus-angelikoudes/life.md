# Callistus Angelikoudes

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 20 iunie reads «Sfântul Calist Angelicoudes», and the page that line points to carries no biography at all: no country, no century, no monastery, no manner of death, no year, and no hymn. What it carries instead of a life is his own writing, set out under the titles it bears there.

The page lists texts by him and articles about him by their headings — «Lucrarea isihiei», «Ce este isihia?», «Care este începutul liniștii și care sfârșitul?», «Ia aminte la tine însuți cu priveghere și rugăciune», «Omul trebuie să trăiască pururea cu luare aminte la gânduri» — so the one thing the calendar says of him beyond the name is that he wrote on stillness and on the watch a man keeps over his own thoughts.

The epistle appointed for the day is Galatians 5:22–26 and 6:1–2, on the fruit of the Spirit, and the gospel is Matthew 11:27–30. Nothing further is set down here, because nothing further was read.
