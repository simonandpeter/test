# Jonah (29 March)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 29 March is «Sfântul Mucenic Iona», and its page for him carries no Viață: only the service texts, which it prints for him and for [Barachisius](/saints/barachisius) together — a canon, a troparion and a kontakion «al Sfinţilor Mucenici Iona şi Varahisie şi al celor împreună cu dânşii», to the holy martyrs Jonah and Barachisius and those with them.

So the calendar keeps the two of them on the same day, with companions it does not number or name, and neither hymn says anything particular of either man: the troparion is the common of martyrs, and the kontakion calls them enlightened by the true Grace and setting the ends of the earth aright. That is the whole of what the Romanian source prints, and this entry records it and nothing more.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and puts names to almost everyone in the story. saint.gr dates the martyrdom to about the year 330, when Shapur was king of the Persians and Constantine the Great of the Byzantines. Jonah and [Barachisius](/saints/barachisius) were ascetics; they left the monastery where they lived as monks and went to a small town called Marbiabokh, or Marmiabokh, where they visited nine martyrs being held in the prison — [Zanithas](/saints/zanithas-companion-of-jonah), [Lazarus](/saints/lazarus-companion-of-jonah), [Marouthas](/saints/marouthas-companion-of-jonah), [Narses](/saints/narses-companion-of-jonah), [Elias](/saints/elias-companion-of-jonah), [Mares](/saints/mares-companion-of-jonah), [Abibus](/saints/abibus-companion-of-jonah), [Simiathes](/saints/simiathes-companion-of-jonah) and [Sabbas](/saints/sabbas-companion-of-jonah) — and encouraged them for their martyrdom. The two were seized at once and brought before three Persian magistrates, Masdrath, Siro and Marmises, who advised them to deny Christ and worship the fire, the water and the sun. They would not; they were bound and beaten without pity, dragged over rough rods and left out in the frost all night. Then Jonah's fingers and toes were cut off, he was bound tight to a tree, sawn through the middle and thrown into a pit. The relics of all of them were bought by a devout Christian named Audeisotes for five hundred miliarisia, Persian coins, and reverently buried. The page adds that the nine were perfected on 27 March and Jonah and Barachisius on 29 March, and it names Pharasa in Cappadocia as the place whose patron he is. Its couplet plays on the prophet: «Ἔχεις Ἰωνᾶν καὶ σύ, γῆ, πάντως μέγαν, Κατ' οὐδὲν ἐνδέοντα τοῦ θαλαττίου» — you too, O earth, have a Jonah, and a great one, in nothing falling short of the Jonah of the sea.
