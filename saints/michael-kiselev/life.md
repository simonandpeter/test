# Michael (Kiselev)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him in one line for 21 декабря ст. ст.: «Сщмч. Михаила пресвитера (1918).» The line declines the forename to «Михаила» and gives no surname, no place, no year of birth and no word of narrative. The rank word «Сщмч.» that governs the name and the year it closes with, 1918, are the whole of what it says of him.

Behind that «Михаила» stands the calendar's own name page 9357, which carries the fuller form «Михаил (Киселев), протоиерей, сщмч.» — the surname the day's line leaves out, and an archpriest's grade. That page lists two days: this 21 декабря and «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. It carries no life, and neither does the day page. So this folder says what the calendar says and no more: a Russian archpriest of that name, who died a martyr in 1918 and whom this calendar keeps on this day.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 21 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261221.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9357.html), which carries no life; read 13 October 2026.*
