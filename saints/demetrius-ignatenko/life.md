# Demetrius (Ignatenko)

Demetrius Theophotovich Ignatenko was born on 17 July 1872 in the village of
Agotmany in the Melitopol district, now in Zaporozhye region, a priest’s
son, finished the Kiev theological academy in 1898 and was ordained priest.
Until 1920 he taught the law of God at a boys’ gymnasium; after it he was a
parish priest and head of the diocesan chancery. In December 1922 he stood
before the proletarian court beside his own bishop, the future hieromartyr
Nicodemus (Krotkov), charged with refusing to give up the liturgical vessels
to desecration and with two “illegal” meetings, one condemning the
Renovationists of the Tauric diocese and the other concerned with the
consecration of Sergius (Zverev) as bishop of Melitopol; the trial ran from
5 November to 1 December, he admitted nothing, and was given three years in
the camps. Having served them he came to Simferopol, was charged again with
anti-Soviet agitation and banished from Ukraine, and came home to preach and
defend the Church as before — an ascetic life, constant prayer, burning
faith and undivided trust in God’s providence gave him a particular boldness
in rebuking the enemies of Orthodoxy. Arrested again in 1926, he was exiled
by the OGPU troika for three years to the Mari region, and afterwards
settled at Melitopol and in time received the parish of Kruchi in Zaporozhye
region. Early in July 1935 he was arrested once more, on the charge usual
for the time of anti-Soviet activity, his sermons being the offence, since
they contradicted the accepted ideology; at the interrogations he hid
neither his views nor his convictions, and said that in matters of religion
he had differences of principle with the Soviet power. He was held in the
Simferopol prison, sixty-three years old and worn by the years since the
revolution; as the investigation drew to its end his body was spent and his
soul unshaken in the confession of the faith, and on 9 September he was
moved to the prison hospital, where on the feast of the Exaltation of the
Cross, 27 September 1935, he died, having carried his cross to the end. He
is kept on 15 September of the old style, 28 September, and in the synaxes
of the new martyrs of the Crimean metropolia and of the Crimean saints.
