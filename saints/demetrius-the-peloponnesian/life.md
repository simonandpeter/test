# Demetrius the Peloponnesian

Demetrius was born in the village of Ligoudista in Arcadia, and came while still young, together with his brother, to Tripoli, where he worked alongside some builders. Because they tormented him he left their company and went into the house of a Turkish barber, who contrived his conversion to Islam and gave him the name Mehmet. Later Demetrius left Tripoli, repented of his apostasy, and came to Argos.

From Argos, for greater safety, he went on to Smyrna, and from there to the monastery of the Forerunner near Kydonies, where he found a devout spiritual father, made his confession, and asked for his counsel. At that man's urging he came to Chios, and there he stayed a good while with another renowned spiritual father, in prayer and in repentance.

Prepared now for martyrdom, he went back to Argos, where he stayed hidden and under the hand of the priest Antonios Sakelarios, and from there he reached Tripoli. He presented himself before the Turkish governor, declared that he believed in Christ, and said that he was ready to pour out his blood for Him. The tortures that followed were dreadful, saint.gr says, but Demetrius stayed unmoved in his faith, and so on 14 April 1803 they beheaded him at Tripoli.

His holy relic was saved by the Christians, in the church of the monastery of St Nicholas of Varsi, and in the central market of Tripoli a small church is honoured in this new martyr's name.
