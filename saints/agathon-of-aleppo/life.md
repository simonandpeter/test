# Agathon of Aleppo

saint.gr's calendar for 10 Μαρτίου names him «Όσιος Αγάθων», the venerable Agathon, with no epithet and no year, and the page behind that line holds one sentence about him: that the venerable Agathon lived as an ascetic at the monastery of St Symeon, near Aleppo in Syria, and fell asleep in peace. Everything else on the page is a note on the name itself, from ἀγαθός, very good in soul and in character, and on the Athenian tragic poet who bore it.

So what is recorded here is the commemoration, the monastery and the country, and a peaceful end. The page gives him no year, no office, no relic and no hymn, and tells nothing of his ascesis beyond the fact of it. The corpus keeps two other men of the name — the ascetic of the Gerontikon, whom the same site keeps on 8 January without a place, and a bishop of Rome on the Romanian 20 February — and this entry is neither of them: it is the one the Greek calendar sets on this day and puts in Syria.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/10/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1259/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
