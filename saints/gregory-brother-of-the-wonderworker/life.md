# Gregory, brother of Gregory the Wonderworker

saint.gr's calendar for 7 Νοεμβρίου keeps a Gregory of whom it says one thing only: «Το μόνο που γνωρίζουμε για τον Άγιο Γρηγόριο είναι ότι ήταν αδελφός του Γρηγορίου του Θαυματουργού» — the only thing we know about Saint Gregory is that he was the brother of [Gregory the Wonderworker](/saints/gregory-the-wonderworker-of-neocaesarea), whom the Greek calendar keeps on 17 November.

The page gives him no country, no century, no rank, no monastery, no manner of death, no year and no hymn. What it prints beside the sentence is his couplet, which turns on the brotherhood and on the name the two shared: «Τῷ θαυματουργοῦ συγγόνῳ Γρηγορίῳ, / Ἐπωνύμως ζήσαντι διπλοῦν τὸ κλέος» — for Gregory, the kinsman of the wonderworker, who lived up to his name, the glory is double.

The same day keeps [Athenodorus](/saints/athenodorus), of whom the page says that some sources make him the Wonderworker's brother instead, and calls that improbable on the evidence of this Gregory's distich.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2937/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
