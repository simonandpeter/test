# Sisinnius of Cyzicus

saint.gr's calendar for 23 Νοεμβρίου names «Όσιος Σισίνιος ο Ομολογητής» and gives him a short life. He was distinguished in the persecution against the Christians when Diocletian was emperor, in 300 by the page's reckoning. He came from Cyzicus and preached the Christian truth with courage and daring, and for that work he suffered many imprisonments and merciless beatings.

When Constantine the Great became emperor, Sisinnius was set free and went on with his pious struggle, well-pleasing to God. But when the heresy of Arius broke out he took part in the fight as a champion of the Orthodox doctrine; and so, contending bravely, he finished a life devoted with his whole soul to Christ. The couplet set over the line turns on the word for force: having endured many violences first in his life, Sisinnius passed out of the violence of life.

The page gives him no year of birth or of death, no see, no relics and no hymn; the two emperors and the heresy are the whole of its dating.
