# Nicholas of Valaam

saint.gr's calendar names him in one sentence and gives no more: the venerable Nicholas lived his ascetic life in the monastery of Valaam in Finland, and fell asleep in peace in the year 1824. The page adds only a note on the name, that Nicholas is the victor out of the people, the victory of the people. It prints no hymn, no place of birth and nothing further about him, and nothing is supplied here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1079/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
