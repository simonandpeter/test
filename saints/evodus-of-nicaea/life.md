# Evodus of Nicaea

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him among four for 22 декабря ст. ст., «Мчч. Хрисогона, Феодотии, Евода, Евтихиана и иных (ок. 304)», and his name page gives him as «Евод Никейский, мч.» — Evodus of Nicaea. The calendar's life for the company makes him the eldest of the three young sons of [Theodota, with her three sons](/saints/theodota-with-her-three-sons), a widow of Nicaea in Bithynia who brought her boys up in the faith.

When the household was brought before the governor of the province and the judge began to threaten his mother with torments, the boy answered that Christians do not fear torments but fear to be forsaken by God. He was beaten savagely in front of her until the blood ran, and she prayed that the Lord would strengthen her son in his sufferings and rejoiced that he was counted worthy of a martyr's end for the truth. The judge then condemned the mother and her children to the fire, and the calendar's other reading says the four were thrown into a heated furnace.

The same calendar keeps him with [Anastasia, pupil of Chrysogonus](/saints/anastasia-pupil-of-chrysogonus), whose friend his mother was, and sets the year of the company at about 304. His name page lists this day and no other.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 22 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261222.html), [the life of the company](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life3165.htm) and [his mother's life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4344.htm); read 13 October 2026.*
