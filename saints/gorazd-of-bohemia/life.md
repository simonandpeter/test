# Gorazd, Bishop of Bohemia and Moravia-Silesia, Hieromartyr (1942)

The Russian calendar keeps on 22 August (Julian) the hieromartyr Gorazd, bishop of Bohemia and Moravia-Silesia, killed in 1942, marked as a saint of the Serbian church — which ordained him — and prints for him a troparion that calls him the worthiest successor of his teacher Methodius, first hierarch of the Church of Moravia; the Serbian calendar keeps him the same day as the hieromartyr Gorazd the Czech. Neither prints a life under the line, and this entry records the lines and the troparion’s words and nothing more.

*After the day’s line of the Sretensky calendar (days.pravoslavie.ru), 22 August (Julian) — [the page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260822.html), which prints no life; After the Православни подсетник’s calendar line for the day (pravoslavno.rs) — [the month](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=kalendar&godina=2026&mesec=09), which prints no life; read 23 August 2026.*
