# Lollianus

saint.gr's calendar for 15 July names «Άγιος Λολλιανός» and tells one thing about him: he was put to death by executioners with kicks. The page carries no year, no place, no emperor, no judge, no hymn and no account of anything he said or did, and nothing has been supplied from elsewhere to fill the gaps.

What it does print is the couplet of the Synaxarion set over his notice, and it turns on the same detail: «Ποσὶ προθύμοις ἔδραμε τρίβον τέλους. / Ὁ Λολλιανός, οὗ ποσίκρουστον τέλους» — with willing feet he ran the path of his end, Lollianus, whose end was dealt by feet. That play on the manner of his death is the whole of what the source gives, and this folder keeps to it.
