# John III Scholasticus

saint.gr says John came from the village of Serimion near Antioch, studied law at Antioch and practised afterwards as an advocate — which is what the calendar's «ο από Σχολαστικών» means, the one who came from among the advocates. He gave all of it up to become a monk, and was sent to Constantinople as the representative of the patriarch of Antioch.

On 15 April 565 he was elected patriarch of Constantinople, and governed the Church in the love of God for twelve years and more. At his urging, the page says, the emperor Justin II, who reigned from 565 to 578, sent the abba Photinus to Egypt to bring peace to the churches there that the heresies had disturbed. He fell asleep in peace in the year 577. The page prints a couplet on the advocate who died to Christ and now keeps holiday from his long labours, and no hymn; what it gives no year for is his birth.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 21 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/21/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3744/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
