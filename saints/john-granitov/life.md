# John (Granitov)

The Russian calendar's line is all there is. It names three men and counts five more on this day, «Сщмчч. Иоанна и Леонтия пресвитеров, Константина диакона и с ними 5 мучеников (1920)» — the hieromartyrs John and Leontius, presbyters, Constantine the deacon, and with them five martyrs. The name behind his part of it reads «Иоанн (Гранитов), протоиерей, сщмч.», John Granitov, archpriest, hieromartyr, which gives him the senior of the two priestly ranks on the line.

There is no life page behind any of the names and no hymn for them on the day. The five the line counts it does not name, and they have no folder here. Nothing says where any of them served or where they died; the year 1920 is the calendar's own and the only date it prints. He is kept with [Leontius](/saints/leontius-klimenko), whom the calendar calls a priest, and [Constantine](/saints/constantine-zverev), whom it calls a deacon.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 29 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260129.html); read 3 October 2026.*
