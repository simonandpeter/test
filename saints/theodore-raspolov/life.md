# Theodore (Raspolov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him with one other man and gives no life. Its fifth entry for 8 июля ст. ст. reads «Сщмчч. Феодора и Николая пресвитеров (1918).» — the hieromartyrs Theodore and Nicholas, presbyters, of the year 1918 — and behind each name stands a link to a name page and to no reading at all.

His page, 13744, prints «Феодор (Располов), иерей, сщмч.», a priest and hieromartyr, and beneath it «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 8 июля». That is the whole of what the calendar says: a parish priest of that surname who died in 1918, the first year of the persecution, kept on this day, which is his own, and on the Julian 26 January with the synaxis of the New Martyrs and Confessors of Russia. He is named in one breath with [Nicholas (Bryantsev)](/saints/nicholas-bryantsev), the other priest of the same line and the same year, and nothing beyond their sharing the line and the year is said to join them.

Where he served, how he was taken and how he died the calendar does not say, and nothing here is supplied from anywhere else. The day page carries fifteen hymn headings, every one of them for a Procopius of the day or for an icon, and none under his name. azbyka.ru, which might hold an account of him, was not read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 8 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260708.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13744.html), which name him and give no life; read 9 October 2026.*
