# Jeremiah the Damascene

saint.gr's calendar for 29 Μαΐου names him and gives two sentences. The venerable Jeremiah, it says, has been numbered among the saints of the Church of Antioch; and, having lived the ascetic life in a manner pleasing to God, he fell asleep in peace. The epithet the calendar gives him is ο Δαμασκηνός, the Damascene.

That is the whole of the source. It states no century and no year, so this folder enters none; it names no monastery, no teacher and no disciple; it records no wonder and prints no hymn. What it does state is a church that counts him its own, and a death in peace rather than by martyrdom.
