# Silouan of the Kyiv Caves

saint.gr's calendar for 10 Ιουνίου names him and gives no life. Its whole notice is one sentence: «Ο Όσιος Σιλουανός ο της Λαύρας Κιέβου ήταν Ρώσος και έζησε στα τέλη του 13ου και αρχές του 14ου αιώνα μ.Χ. Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του» — that the venerable Silouan of the Lavra of Kyiv was a Russian and lived at the end of the thirteenth and the beginning of the fourteenth century, and that we have no further details of his life.

So what the Greek calendar keeps of him is a name, a house and a span: the great monastery of the Caves at Kyiv, and the turn of the thirteenth century into the fourteenth. The page gives him no office, no place of birth, no manner of death and no hymn, and prints no synaxarion verses for him. It also carries the site's gloss on the name, from silva, a wood — the child of the forest.
