# Silouan of the Kyiv Caves

saint.gr's calendar for 10 Ιουνίου names him and gives no life. Its whole notice is one sentence: «Ο Όσιος Σιλουανός ο της Λαύρας Κιέβου ήταν Ρώσος και έζησε στα τέλη του 13ου και αρχές του 14ου αιώνα μ.Χ. Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του» — that the venerable Silouan of the Lavra of Kyiv was a Russian and lived at the end of the thirteenth and the beginning of the fourteenth century, and that we have no further details of his life.

So what the Greek calendar keeps of him is a name, a house and a span: the great monastery of the Caves at Kyiv, and the turn of the thirteenth century into the fourteenth. The page gives him no office, no place of birth, no manner of death and no hymn, and prints no synaxarion verses for him. It also carries the site's gloss on the name, from silva, a wood — the child of the forest.

The Russian calendar keeps him on the Julian 10 June, which is the menologion day the Greek page kept him on, and its line is as short: «Прп. Силуана , схимника Печерского (XIII-XIV).» What it has and the Greek page had not is his service. The day page prints a troparion and a kontakion over him, both headed for the schemamonk Silouan of the Caves «в Дальних пещерах», in the Far Caves, and they are copied here with their English typed. The troparion gives the great abstinence and the measureless fasting for which he received the grace of wonderworking and calls him the adornment of those who fast; the kontakion has him put his body to death in wakeful and sober prayer and bring many to Christ by teaching them the same virtues. His name page adds two days this reader did not open, 10 июля and the 28 августа synaxis of the fathers of the Far Caves.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/10/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/538/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 10 июня ст. ст. — [the Russian day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260610.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/1988.html), read 8 October 2026, whose two further days and two Russian readings of his life were not opened.*
