# Claudia, mother of Eugenia

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names her last of four for 24 декабря ст. ст., «Прмц. Евгении и с нею мчч. Прота, Иакинфа и Клавдии (ок. 262)», and her name page gives her as «Клавдия Римская, мц.» The life page the line links makes her the wife of [Philip, father of Eugenia](/saints/philip-father-of-eugenia), whom the emperor sent to Alexandria as governor of Egypt, and the mother of [Eugenia](/saints/eugenia).

When her daughter's secret came out at the trial her family rejoiced to have found the one they had long mourned, and in time all of them were baptised. Her husband was put out of the governorship on a pagan's denunciation, was chosen bishop by the Christians of Alexandria, and died of wounds given him by hired men while he was praying alone. Widowed, Claudia took her daughter and her servants to her estate in the country near Rome.

There, the page says, she built a hospice for strangers and served the widows, while her daughter brought many maidens to Christ; and when Gallienus began the persecution again, many Christians found refuge with the two of them. She received the martyr's crown soon after her daughter did, and her daughter had told her beforehand the day of her death.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 24 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261224.html) and [the life of the company](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life3172.htm); read 13 October 2026.*
