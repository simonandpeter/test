# Syncletica of Nagran

She and her two daughters suffered under the Jewish king of Arabia who set out to destroy the Christians of his country, in the sixth century. The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery, which keeps her on 24 октября ст. ст., says she came of a family of rank, was widowed while still young, and gave herself to bringing her daughters up as Christians while living a chaste and virtuous life herself.

When the persecution began the king called her before him with her daughters and tried to persuade her to leave her «безумие», her madness, promising her a place in his wife's retinue for it. She answered: «Как не боишься ты, царь, злословить Того, Кто дал тебе царскую корону и жизнь?» — how is it that you are not afraid to speak evil of Him who gave you your crown and your life?

He ordered that she and her daughters be led about the city like criminals. The women of the place wept at the shame put upon her, and she told them that this disgrace was dearer to her than any honour on earth. Brought before him again, she was told that if she wished to stay alive she must deny Christ, and answered: «Если я отрекусь, кто тогда избавит меня от вечной смерти?» — if I deny Him, who then will deliver me from everlasting death? In his anger he ordered her daughters killed first and their mother given their blood to drink, and then her own head struck off.

The calendar names the daughters nowhere, in the line or in the life, and gives them no ages and no words of their own, so this day's company of three is one folder: that is docs/CORPUS.md's rule for a company that enumerates nobody, and the shortfall is deliberate and recorded. The governor of the same city, with the company of more than four thousand the calendar keeps with him, stands on this same day as his own entry.
