# Aphrodisius (24 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 24 December names the venerable Aphrodisius, and what it says of him it says with a hedge of its own: he was probably an ascetic of the desert, who died in peace. That single sentence, with the word «μάλλον» standing in it, is the whole of the entry, and no year, no city, no monastery and no hymn is given with it.

Because the page itself will not say more than probably, nothing more is claimed here: the rank venerable is the calendar's own, and the desert is not written into this folder as a fact. He is not the Aphrodisius whom the same calendar keeps on 21 June as a martyr, nor either of the two others of the name this corpus holds; the pages say nothing of one another, and two entries on one calendar are two commemorations. Above the sentence the site prints the note it gives every Aphrodisius, on the goddess the name is drawn from, which is furniture and not a reading. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence by which saint.gr marks an entry it will not vouch for.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/24/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3230/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
