# John, companion of James the Presbyter

saint.gr's calendar for 1 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιοι Ιωάννης ο Επίσκοπος και Ιάκωβος ο Πρεσβύτερος» — a bishop and a presbyter, each with a heading of his own on the page and neither with a see or a city. These hieromartyrs lived in the years of Shapur, king of the Persians, the notice says, which it dates 332; and they taught the word of the true faith to the devout and managed to draw many to it.

They were arrested by Shapur, and after being put first to hard torments they were beheaded at the end, and so received the unfading crown of martyrdom. That is the whole of it: no birthplace, no account of the see this John held, nothing of where they were buried and no relics.

The page sets one epigram over the pair, and it makes them both priests of Christ: along with [James](/saints/james-companion-of-john-the-bishop), the sacrificer of Christ, John the sacrificer of Christ bore the sword. No hymn is printed. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/01/index.aspx) and [the notice of the two](https://www.saint.gr/2862/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
