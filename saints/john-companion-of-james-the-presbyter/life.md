# John, companion of James the Presbyter

saint.gr's calendar for 1 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιοι Ιωάννης ο Επίσκοπος και Ιάκωβος ο Πρεσβύτερος» — a bishop and a presbyter, each with a heading of his own on the page and neither with a see or a city. These hieromartyrs lived in the years of Shapur, king of the Persians, the notice says, which it dates 332; and they taught the word of the true faith to the devout and managed to draw many to it.

They were arrested by Shapur, and after being put first to hard torments they were beheaded at the end, and so received the unfading crown of martyrdom. That is the whole of it: no birthplace, no account of the see this John held, nothing of where they were buried and no relics.

The page sets one epigram over the pair, and it makes them both priests of Christ: along with [James](/saints/james-companion-of-john-the-bishop), the sacrificer of Christ, John the sacrificer of Christ bore the sword. No hymn is printed. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands.

The Russian church calendar keeps the same pair on the same day, and its line is the Greek pairing with a later year: «Сщмчч. Иоанна епископа и Иакова пресвитера, в Персии пострадавших (ок. 345)» — the hieromartyrs John the bishop and James the presbyter, who suffered in Persia, about 345. Its index of names gives him a see the Greek page does not, «Иоанн Бет-Селевкийский (Персидский)», John of Bet-Seleucia, the Persian, and lists two days against him: this one and 20 ноября.

That second day is why this folder takes the day and the name and no story. The life the calendar hangs behind his name is not of this pair at all but of a company of Persian bishops and martyrs killed in the persecution under Shapur II, in which a bishop John is stoned — and that company is the one the Greek calendar keeps on 20 November, where this corpus already holds a separate John. The calendar therefore folds two commemorations the corpus keeps apart. Deciding that two entries are one man is not a reader's to decide, so nothing here is folded and the second day is recorded unread.

What the Russian does settle is that the year is two readings and not one. The Greek page set the pair in the reign of Shapur and printed 332; this line prints about 345. The folder's interval now holds both and chooses neither.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/01/index.aspx) and [the notice of the two](https://www.saint.gr/2862/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 1 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261101.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/961.html), read 4 October 2026.*
