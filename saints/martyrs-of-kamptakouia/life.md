# The Martyrs of Kamptakouia in Georgia

saint.gr says these holy martyrs were martyred in Georgia in one of the exterminating raids of the Mongol horde led by Tamerlane, in the year 1386, at the monastery of Kamptakouia. Tamerlane's army pushed into the city of Kartli, in the central region of Georgia, plundered the whole country and destroyed the monastery of Kamptakouia; and the inhabitants of the neighbouring villages had shut themselves inside it.

After the plundering of the monastery, the page says, the warlord Tamerlane gathered all the monks together and, wishing to humiliate and degrade them, forced them to sing and to dance; and the monks cried out, weeping: «Συμφορά μας, συμφορά μας» — woe to us, woe to us.

Tamerlane's soldiers then led them into the cathedral church of the Most Holy Mother of God, which was full of captive Christians. They heaped firewood around the church and set it alight. So, the page ends, the holy confessors were martyred and received the crown of martyrdom from the Lord of glory.

It names none of them and numbers none of them — neither the monks, nor the captives inside the church, nor the villagers who had taken refuge there — so the calendar's one line is kept here as one entry. The page gives no relic, no couplet and no hymn.
