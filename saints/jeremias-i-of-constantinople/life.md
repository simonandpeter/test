# Jeremias I of Constantinople

Jeremias I was Ecumenical Patriarch from 1522 to 1546, with one interruption in the year 1526. He came from Zitsa in Epirus, and was elected metropolitan of Sofia some time before 1513. saint.gr says he had a limited education, but was very popular and had great administrative ability; the Holy Synod elected him patriarch on 31 December 1522, with the backing of the archon Constantine Kounoupis.

He died in Wallachia, on 13 January 1546, which is the day the calendar keeps him on. On 10 January 2023 the Holy and Sacred Synod of the Ecumenical Patriarchate decided to enter Patriarch Jeremias I in the calendar of saints of the Orthodox Church, and it is by that decision that he is here at all.

The page's account of his patriarchate between those two dates is longer than what is set down above, and this life does not attempt it; what is written here is what was read. The calendar prints no hymn for him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 13 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/13/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4486/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
