# Diogenes (5 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 5 December names Diogenes and gives him one line of life: the holy Diogenes was martyred by stoning. Nothing else about him is printed — no country, no reign, no persecutor and no year. What the page adds is the couplet the synaxaria set over his name, which says that the martyr Diogenes is clothed, rejoicing, by deceiving men in a tunic of stone.

The hymns on the page say the same thing at greater length and add no fact to it: that by divine strength he endured the hurling of the stones, that through manly contests in the stadium he put on splendidly a garment of stone, and that he confessed Jesus in the stadium and bore the stones with a steadfast soul as dew sent down from heaven for the refreshing of the souls of the faithful. A megalynarion printed there calls him of one zeal with Stephen, and the page links a full service to the glorious martyr Diogenes kept at the monastery of Saint Raphael at Ano Souli of Marathon.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3157/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
