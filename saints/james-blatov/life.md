# James (Blatov)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery closes its line of ten people for 31 октября ст. ст. with him, and gives him no life. His name stands last, as «Иакова», under the third of the line's three rank words — «мч.», martyr, which governs him and nobody else, where seven of the ten stand under the word for a hieromartyr and two under the word for a monastic put to death. **So he is the one layman on the line, and that rank word is the only way the calendar says it.** The name link behind his name adds the surname the line leaves out and nothing further: «Иаков (Блатов), мч.»

The year is the whole of what is recorded of him, and the year is 1937. There is no life page behind his name, and his name page carries no text, no icon and no hymn — only his name and his days, of which the second is 26 января, the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. Because the link gives him no grade and no trade, this folder records no office and no state of life, and does not reason from the company he is named with to what he might have been.

Two of the ten on this line have lives on the calendar, and both of those men were shot on 13 November 1937 in the Tver region; nothing on the page says that of him. The second Russian calendar, azbyka.ru, which has given this corpus full lives and archival references for others of these men, refused the request with a 403 on the day he was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 31 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261031.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9971.html); read 4 October 2026.*
