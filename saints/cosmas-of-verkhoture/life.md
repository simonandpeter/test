# Cosmas of Verkhoture

saint.gr's calendar for 1 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιος Κοσμάς του Βερχοτούρε» and dates him «(; - 1706)», and the page behind that line says one thing only: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ρώσου Αγίου» — we have no details of the Russian saint's life. So the Greek calendar keeps him on this day, gives him a Russian town and a year of death, and tells nothing else at all.

There is no birthplace, no monastery, no account of what he did or how he died, no relics and no hymn; and the page gives him no rank either, which is why this folder prints him with none of its own. The Russian calendar, which would be the one to say more, has not been read here, and its row on this folder stays undocumented.

The entry carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands, bare as it is.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/01/index.aspx) and [his notice](https://www.saint.gr/4246/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
