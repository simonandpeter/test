# Erastus

Erastus is one of the seventy apostles. doxologia.ro's notice of him is two sentences long: that he is of the Seventy and that the Apostle [Paul](/saints/paul-the-apostle) mentions him in the Epistle to the Romans; and that he was first steward of the holy Church of Jerusalem, and afterwards, being bishop of Paneas, went over to the Lord.

That is the whole of it. The page gives no year, no manner of death and nothing of his work in either place; and the hymn it prints for him is a common of the apostles, headed «Troparul Sfinților Apostoli Olimp, Rodion, Sosipatru, Erast, Terțiu, Cvart» for the six of them together and naming none of them in its text.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 10 November, in one entry with four others of the Seventy. saint.gr says all five belonged to the Lord's seventy apostles, whose synaxis it puts on 4 January; that all are named in Romans 16; and that most of them were bishops who kept excellently the command of 1 Peter 5, to shepherd the flock of God willingly and not for gain, becoming patterns for it rather than lording it over it. Of Erastus it says that he governed the bishopric of Neas in the same way as Sosipater governed Iconium — the page's spelling of the see, which is not the folder's. His distich makes a lover of him: «Ἐρῶν Ἔραστος μανικῶς τοῦ Κυρίου, / Σύνεστιν αὐτῷ καὶ χαρὰν χαίρει ξένην» — Erastus, loving the Lord to madness, is with him and rejoices with a strange joy.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 10 noiembrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/10-noiembrie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-apostol-erast); read 25 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 10 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/10/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2963/saint.aspx), read 1 October 2026.*
