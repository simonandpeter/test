# Sophronius (19 February)

saint.gr names him and then says, in one sentence, both that it knows nothing and where the little it has comes from. Its calendar for 19 February prints «Όσιος Σωφρόνιος», bare — no epithet, no see, no city — and the page behind that line answers: there is no information about the life of the venerable one; his memory is mentioned by the Paris Codex 1578 and by Delehaye, where he is called bishop.

So the rank this folder carries is a mention in a manuscript and a Bollandist's note, and it is recorded as that and not as a life. There is no century here, no country, no manner of death, no relics, no couplet and no hymn; and the corpus keeps several other Sophronii, none of them on this day and none of them named on this page.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 19 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/19/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3756/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
