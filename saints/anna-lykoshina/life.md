# Anna (Lykoshina)

The Russian calendar gives her a line of her own on 28 September and no life: «Мц. Анны (1925)» — Anna the martyr, 1925. Its index of names adds the surname, «Анна (Лыкошина), мц.», and keeps her on three days: this one, 26 January with the whole company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and 8 June with the saints of Petersburg.

That last is the only thing in the source that places her, and it places her loosely. She is a layman — the calendar gives her no rank beyond martyr and no office at all — and 1925 is early, the years of the trials of the Petersburg clergy rather than of the camps. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which refused this reading on 4 October 2026 — a 403 to the fetcher's user-agent and to a full browser one alike — so the line stands as the line.
