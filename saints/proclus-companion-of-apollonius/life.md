# Proclus, companion of Apollonius

saint.gr keeps three martyrs together on this day — Proclus, Apollonius and Ephebius — and gives them one page and one sentence. The holy martyrs were arrested when they went to bury the holy relic of the hieromartyr [Valentine of Rome](/saints/valentine-of-rome), whom the page tells the reader to look for on the same day, and they were martyred at Rome. That is the whole of what the site prints of them: no year, no order of their deaths, no hymn.

The page's headings for the three are uneven. Over Proclus and over Apollonius it prints the etymology of the name and a note on the pagan Greek who bore it — the last Neoplatonist philosopher for Proclus, the epic poet of the Argonautica and the mathematician of Alexandria for Apollonius — and over Ephebius it prints nothing at all. None of that is about the martyrs.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 14 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/14/index.aspx) and [the page for the three of them](https://www.saint.gr/3710/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
