# Neophytus (Osipov)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him last of fifteen on 21 октября ст. ст., the second of the two the line marks off with «прмчч.», venerable-martyrs, after eleven priests and two deacons of 1937. The line gives him as «Неофита» and no more; the title on the name link behind his place adds the surname and a rank the line does not print, «Неофит (Осипов), архимандрит, прмч.» — an archimandrite, and so the senior monastic of the company.

Ten of the fifteen have life pages on this calendar and he is not one of them: the name link behind his place carries no life, and the day page says nothing of him beyond his rank, his name and the company's year. The ten whose lives are given were all sentenced by the same NKVD troika on 1 November 1937 and shot on 3 November, which is 21 October old style, and that is what the day keeps them on; nothing on the page says whether this archimandrite died with them or where.

Nothing further is written here — not a birth, not a parish, not a charge, not a place of death. The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line. The day page's troparia and kontakia all belong to other saints it heads and none to this company of 1937.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 21 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261021.html); read 4 October 2026.*
