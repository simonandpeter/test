# Hierotheus of Hungary

saint.gr's whole account of Hierotheus is two sentences. He was the first Orthodox bishop of Hungary, it says, and he was sent from Constantinople at the beginning of the eleventh century; and the declaration of his sanctity was made in the summer of the year 2000 by the Ecumenical Patriarchate. The page gives no year of birth, no year of death, no see city and no account of his work.

What it does carry is his apolytikion, and the hymn says rather more than the notice does. It has him receiving the zeal of the apostles in his soul and standing in Hungary as an angel of peace, and lighting that country up towards the knowledge of God by the lightnings of his teachings — «ἐν τῇ Οὑγγαρίᾳ ἐπέστης, τῆς εἰρήνης ὡς ἄγγελος, και ηὔγασας πρός γνῶσιν θεϊκήν, αὐτήν τῶν διδαχῶν σου ἀστραπαῖς».

The page keeps him beside [Stephen](/saints/stephen-of-hungary), the first king of Hungary, who is kept on the same day and whose sanctity the Ecumenical Patriarchate declared in the same summer, and it links both of them to the Patriarchate's Exarchate of Hungary.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 20 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/20/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2308/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
