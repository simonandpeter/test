# Oceanus

Oceanus was martyred by fire or by the sword; that is the whole of the Greek synaxarion’s notice for 12 September. Think of Oceanus as a bright sun, its verse says, bathed in the pyre as in the Ocean. This entry records it and nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 12 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2415/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
