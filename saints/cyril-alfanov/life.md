# Cyril Alfanov

saint.gr names five men together for this day and tells one story for all of them. [Isaac](/saints/isaac-alfanov), [Clement](/saints/clement-alfanov), Cyril, [Nicetas](/saints/nicetas-alfanov) and [Nicephorus](/saints/nicephorus-alfanov) Alfanov were brothers, its page says, and they lived in the fourteenth century at Novgorod. In 1389 they founded the monastery of Sokolnitski and lived there as ascetics in a way pleasing to God, and they fell asleep in peace.

Because a fire destroyed the monastery of Sokolnitski, their holy relics were moved to the monastery of Antoniev in 1775. Their memory, the page adds, is kept also on 17 June — a second fixed day, which the Greek church does for a number of its saints and which this corpus records as a note rather than as a second commemoration.

That is the whole of it, and it is the whole of it for each of the five: the page gives them one paragraph between them, a head line each, and no separate word about any one of them. There is no year of birth or of death, no account of the founding beyond its date, no wonder, no name for their father, and no hymn. The surname Alfanov appears only once, in the body, and only in Greek letters.

The Russian church calendar keeps the five brothers on the same day and gives them one life, which is where the house and the trouble come from. They lived in the fourteenth century at Novgorod, led a righteous life and founded the Sokolnitsky monastery; the chronicles, the life says, record that «на Сокольей горе поставиша церковь древяну святого Николу и монастырь устроиша» in 1389 — that on the Falcon hill they set up a wooden church of St Nicholas and ordered a monastery.

The brothers were kin to a man the chronicles know, «Яков Анфал» or «Алфан», who fled to the Dvina to escape being pursued for his dealings with Moscow; and for that kinship, the life says, the righteous men came into troubles, and were cleansed for a blessed eternity by the heavy sorrows of suffering they had not deserved. It is the whole of what the source gives as the matter of their ascesis, and the second of the two life pages repeats it in the same words.

The life adds that the «Сказание» of the holy brothers describes the wonders that came from their relics after their death, that their memory is appointed on 4 мая and 17 июня, and that after a fire had destroyed the Sokolnitsky house their relics were carried to the Antoniev monastery on 4 May 1775. The day page carries four hymns for the five of them, two troparia and two kontakia, each naming all five by name, and prints one of each pair a second time under a shorter heading.
