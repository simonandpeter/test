# Castinus of Byzantium

Castinus — or Kestinos, as saint.gr also gives the name — came from Rome, held the rank of a senator, and was at first a worshipper of idols. He was drawn to Christianity by Cyrillianus, bishop of Argyroupolis, to whom he had gone for healing. At once Castinus distributed what he owned among the poor and gave himself wholly to the service of the Church.

He served as bishop of Argyroupolis, and then for seven years as bishop of Byzantium, from 230 to 237. Until his time, the page says, the cathedral church stood by the sea at what is now Galata; Castinus was the first to build a church within Byzantium itself, in the name of Saint Euphemia. Having ordained Titus as his successor, he fell asleep in peace.

saint.gr adds a note of its own, that Nikephoros Kallistos writes his name as Konstantinos. The page gives no year for his birth and none for his death, and prints no troparion and no kontakion for him.
