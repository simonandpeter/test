# Gennadius (Letyuk)

The calendar’s own line is the whole of what this folder records of him. The Сретенский monastery’s calendar for 5 декабря ст. ст. names him in the day’s last clause, standing between the archpriest of 1932 and the confessor of 1950: «Прмч. Геннадия (1941).» The rank is «прмч.», a venerable-martyr — a monastic put to death — and 1941 is the only year the calendar gives him.

The name page behind his place gives the surname the line leaves out together with his monastic grade: «Геннадий (Летюк), иеромонах, сщмч.», a hieromonk. It also gives him a different rank word from the day line’s, «сщмч.», a hieromartyr: two readings of one death, a monk in orders put to death, and this folder follows the day line and records the other here rather than choosing between them.

That page lists one other day for him, «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church; one row per church is this corpus’s schema, so that day is recorded on his feast and he does not render on it. Behind the page there is no life, and no article stands under his name.

So nothing is said here of where he was born, which house he was professed in, when or by whom he was arrested, or how and where he died in 1941, because no page read says any of it. The day page carries six hymns, and all six stand under the names of the ancient saints of the same day.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 5 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261205.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9205.html); read 13 October 2026.*
