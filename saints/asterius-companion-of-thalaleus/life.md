# Asterius, companion of Thalaleus

Alexander and Asterius were two of the soldiers set to torture the physician-martyr Thalaleus at Anazarbus in Cilicia, and saint.gr's whole account of them is the sentence in which they change sides: they were ordered to pierce Thalaleus's ankles, they came through him to believe in Christ, they confessed him, and they were beheaded. Thalaleus's own page tells the same thing from his side — that his patience under that horrible torture was so great that two of the soldiers who were torturing him, by name Alexander and Asterius, believed.

The page gives the two of them no country, no years and no burial place, and sets neither of them in a family. The governor who gave the order is Tiberian, and the emperor whose years Thalaleus's page names is Numerian, 283 to 284; but nothing on their own page repeats either date, so no years are recorded for them here.
