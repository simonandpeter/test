# Apollonius (23 July)

saint.gr's calendar for 23 Ιουλίου names him a hieromartyr and bishop of Rome, and his own page then takes the second half of that back. Saint Apollonius bore witness, it says, after they killed him with shot arrows and then threw him into the fire. But there was never a bishop of Rome of that name; and the Paris Codex 223 Coislin calls this saint simply a hieromartyr.

That is the whole of the page. It gives no year, no century, no city and no emperor, and prints no hymn. What the corpus therefore holds is a name, a day, a rank one manuscript gives him, a manner of death, and the calendar's own correction of its own line.
