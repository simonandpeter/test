# Alexis (Stavrovsky)

The Russian calendar's 1 October is the Protection of the Mother of God, and after the feast and the ancient saints of the day it prints three lines of new martyrs. His is the first of them and the shortest thing on the page: «Сщмч. Алексия пресвитера (1918)» — Alexis the hieromartyr, presbyter, 1918. The site's own index of names gives the surname and raises the rank, heading his page «Алексий (Ставровский), протоиерей, сщмч.», an archpriest.

That index keeps him here and on 26 January with the whole company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and carries no life. Where he served, what he was charged with and where he was killed the calendar does not say, and 1918 is all the chronology there is. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026 even with a browser user-agent, so the line stands as the line.
