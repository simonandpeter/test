# Hilarion (Pisarets)

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him thirteenth of the fifteen, last of the three it calls «прмчч.», and gives no life; the life here is azbyka.ru’s for the civil 3 декабря, out of fond.ru. He was born in 1871 in the village of Собичево in the Glukhov district of the province of Chernigov, son of a peasant, Павел Писарец. In 1893 he entered the Глинская пустынь in the province of Kursk as a novice and stayed there until 1914; there he was tonsured a monk.

In 1914 he was ordained hieromonk, and afterwards served in Moscow. In 1927 the people of the village of Рождество in the Naro-Fominsk district of the Moscow region, who had then no priest, began asking for him; he agreed, and was sent by the church authority to serve in that church.

On 15 October 1937 the chairman of the village council sent a statement to the NKVD reporting that at the feast of the Protection «по домам с молебнами ходил рождественский поп, не имея на это разрешения» — the priest of Рождество had gone round the houses with services of supplication without permission — adding that there had been breaches before, that he had collected the signatures of the faithful, and asking that appropriate measures be taken.

He was arrested on 26 November 1937 and held in the Taganka prison in Moscow, and at the investigation rejected categorically every charge laid against him. On 29 November the troika of the NKVD sentenced him to be shot; he was shot on 3 December 1937 and buried in a common unmarked grave at the Butovo range near Moscow.
