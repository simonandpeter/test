# David, with Minas and John

saint.gr's whole account of the three is one sentence: «Οι Άγιοι Οσιομάρτυρες Μηνάς, Δαβίδ και Ιωάννης ήταν μοναχοί και τελειώθηκαν τοξευόμενοι» — the holy venerable-martyrs [Minas](/saints/minas-with-david-and-john), David and [John](/saints/john-with-minas-and-david) were monks and were made perfect by being shot with arrows. The day's line adds nothing but the rank word, οι Οσιομάρτυρες.

Above it the page prints a couplet which is the only other thing said of them: three servants of God, abbas, slayers of the passions, are killed with arrows by slayers of men. Under David's name it sets a gloss on the name — a Hebrew word, it says, meaning the beloved, the one loving and loved — and that gloss belongs to the name and not to the man.

Nothing else is told. The page gives no century, no country, no monastery, no persecutor and no year; it does not say whether the three were of one house or only of one death, and it lists no relic and prints no hymn. The eleven other Davids the corpus keeps are told from this one by the company and by nothing else, because nothing else is on the page.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/239/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
