# Photus, son of Terence

Photus was one of the seven sons of the martyrs [Terence](/saints/terence-husband-of-neonilla) and [Neonilla](/saints/neonilla-wife-of-terence), whom doxologia.ro's life for 28 October names one by one: Sarvil, Fota, Teodul, Vila, Ierax, Nitul and Eunichie. His brothers were [Sarbelus](/saints/sarbelus-son-of-terence), [Theodulus](/saints/theodulus-son-of-terence), [Vilas](/saints/vilas-son-of-terence), [Hierax](/saints/hierax-son-of-terence), [Nitus](/saints/nitus-son-of-terence) and [Eunicius](/saints/eunicius-son-of-terence). Their father was a right-believing Christian and married Neonilla, who held the same faith, in lawful marriage, and the two brought up all seven sons in the right faith. The life tells the family's passion as one story and says nothing of the sons apart from it.

All of them were taken by the pagans, parents and children alike, and brought before a lawless court, where they confessed Christ and reviled the idols. For this they were tortured beyond measure and then hung up; their wounds were sprinkled with vinegar and burned with fire, and while they were being tortured the saints prayed quietly for one another and comforted one another.

God did not overlook their prayers, but sent his holy angels, who loosed them from their bonds and healed their wounds. The pagans, seeing them loosed and whole, were terrified, and gave them to the beasts to be eaten; but they suffered no harm, because at God's command the beasts changed their cruelty for the gentleness of sheep. Then they were thrown into a cauldron of boiling pitch, and at once the fire went out and the pitch grew cold.

Seeing that the tortures would not touch them, the pagans cut off their heads with the sword, and so they came to their end. Photus was beheaded with his father, his mother and his six brothers, and the Romanian calendar keeps them all on 28 October.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 28 octombrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/28-octombrie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-terentie-cu-cei-7-fii-ai-sai); read 24 September 2026.*
