# Nicholas (Khvoshchev)

Nicholas Alexandrovich Khvoshchev was born on 3 December 1883 in the village of Kalapino in the Lukoyanov district of Nizhny Novgorod province, into the large family of the priest Alexander Khvoshchev. He studied at the Nizhny Novgorod seminary but left after the fourth year; on 25 August 1905 he was made psalmist of the Epiphany cathedral of the district town of Knyaginino, where he met and married Alexandra Yakovlevna Smorchkova, adopted daughter of a local townsman. In the autumn of 1907 he passed the examination for ordination and was appointed to the priest’s place at the Protection church of the village of Isupovo in the Sergach district, today the Gagino district, and on 24 October 1907 was — the calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. He served at Isupovo from 1907; arrested in 1937 on a charge of counter-revolutionary activity for resisting the radio installation and defending the church from closure, he did not admit guilt at interrogation; the troika of the NKVD sentenced him to be shot, and he was executed on 1 November 1937. He was numbered among the saints at the council of 2000, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 4 September (Julian) with the priests and laymen of the Gagino district.
