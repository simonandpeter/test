# Paul (6 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 6 Νοεμβρίου keeps Paul the fool for Christ's sake, and says in so many words how little it has of him: «Δεν γνωρίζουμε πως απεβίωσε. Ξέρουμε μόνο ότι ήταν από την Κόρινθο και ότι έγινε σημειοφόρος» — we do not know how he died; we know only that he was from Corinth and that he became a bearer of signs. The epithet in the day's line, «ὁ διὰ Χριστὸν σαλός», is the whole of what is said of his manner of life.

There is no century on the page, no emperor, no monastery, no companions, no year and no hymn. What it does print is the couplet of the synaxarion, which turns on the folly he put on: «Ὑποκριθεὶς ὁ Παῦλος ἐν γῇ μωρίαν, / Χορῷ θανὼν σύνεστι τῶν θεοφρόνων» — Paul, having feigned folly on earth, in dying is numbered with the choir of the godly-minded.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2926/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
