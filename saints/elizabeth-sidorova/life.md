# Elizabeth (Sidorova)

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar for 7 ноября ст. ст. carries fifteen names on one line and hers is the fifteenth and last of them: «Сщмч. Кирилла , митр. Казанского, Михаила , Александра , Александра , Михаила , Александра , Николая , Алексия , Павла , Василия , Павлина пресвитеров, Иоанна и Вениамина диаконов, мч. Николая , мц. Елисаветы (1937).» The line sorts the fifteen into four classes with one year for all of them: «Сщмч.» heads the metropolitan, the eleven priests and the two deacons, «пресвитеров» marks the eleven, «диаконов» the two, and «мч.» and «мц.» the layman and the woman who close it. The name link behind her place in it supplies the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line leaves out, «Елисавета (Сидорова), мц.», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from.

That is everything the calendar records of her. She has no life page, no icon, no hymn, no age and no parish of her own anywhere on this calendar, and the name page behind her place on the line carries its title, two days and no text at all. That page also lists «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which is a synaxis and not a second feast; this folder renders on 7 ноября ст. ст. only. Nothing further is written here, and in particular nothing is reasoned from the surname.

**The calendar gives her no office either, and she is the only woman on the line.** Its class for her is «мц.», the feminine of the layman's class, and her name page says «Елисавета (Сидорова), мц.» and nothing more: no grade, no house, no age, no place. Whether she was a nun, a reader's widow or a parishioner the calendar does not say, and nothing is reasoned from the surname.

She is given no relation to the fourteen others the line names, and the reason is in the one life the line has. Its heading claims a company — «Убиение святителя Кирилла, митрополита Казанского и ихже с ним», the slaying of the metropolitan of Kazan and those with him — but that life says the metropolitan was shot in the Fox ravine outside Chimkent in southern Kazakhstan, together with two men who are not on this line at all and have no folder in this corpus. Nothing on the day page or on any name page read says that the other fourteen died with him or with one another. A shared line, a shared class and a shared year are not a company unless a page says they died together, so no `related` row is written here in either direction.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 7 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261107.html) and [her name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/10075.html); read 4 October 2026.*
