# Theodore (Gusev)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him on 19 января ст. ст. and gives no life. The whole of its account is one line, «Мч. Феодора (1940)» — the martyr Theodore, 1940 — and behind that line the calendar's own name link carries the surname the line leaves out, «Феодор (Гусев), мч.»: Theodore Gusev.

It gives him no rank in the clergy. «Мч.» and not «Сщмч.» is the calendar's own reading: a martyr, a layman, and not a priest, which is unusual on a day whose other two twentieth-century names are both presbyters. No birthplace, no diocese, no parish, no place of death and no manner of it is named.

The 1940 in its bracket is the whole of what it dates him by, and it is the year of his death, the latest year on this day's list. He stands on this day among the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. The day page carries troparia for a dozen of the saints it heads, and none under his name. That is everything the source says of him.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 19 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260119.html); read 3 October 2026. The calendar prints no life page for him.*
