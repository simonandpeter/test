# Papyrinus, companion of Pachomius

Papyrinus is the second of the two martyrs the Greek calendar keeps in one line on 13 Ιανουαρίου, «Άγιοι Παχώμιος και Παπυρίνος», and the page behind the line has one sentence for the pair of them: they were martyred when their persecutors drowned them in a river, and there are no other details of their lives.

The couplet printed above that sentence names him first — such an end did Papyrinus find as thou, [Pachomius](/saints/pachomius-companion-of-papyrinus), being cast into the water — and is the only other thing the page says about him. There is no river named, no persecutor, no emperor, no year, and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 13 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/13/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1414/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
