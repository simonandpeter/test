# Nicholas (Dobronravov)

Nicholas Pavlovich Dobronravov was born on 21 November 1861 at Игнатовка in the Дмитровский district of the province of Moscow, in a priest's family. He finished the Moscow theological seminary in 1881 and the Moscow theological academy in 1885, and taught theology and Scripture at the Вифанская seminary. He married, was ordained priest, served the church of the Alexandrovsky military college and taught the law of God in three Moscow gymnasia. After the revolution of 1917 closed the college he was moved to the church of All Saints на Кулишках, and he was one of the most active members of the council of 1917 and 1918.

The first arrest came in the summer of 1918. On 19 August men of the Cheka came to All Saints to search it, found the church locked, and demanded the keys of him as its rector; he answered that a search of a church needed the chairman of the parish council present, and was taken to the Cheka prison on the Lubyanka for the answer. He was made bishop afterwards, and archbishop of Vladimir and Suzdal, and the rest of his life ran between Moscow, prison and exile.

The last interrogation is printed in full, and what it wanted from him was other men: who he had met, when, and where. He named three — a bishop who had been his assistant in the Vladimir diocese, a bishop he had come to know in exile, and a metropolitan of his own mind who had visited him in Moscow to talk over how the Church was now to be governed — and said of all three only that they had visited him. The charge put to him was membership of a counter-revolutionary monarchist organisation of churchmen. He denied it twice in the words the record keeps: «Нет, это я отрицаю».

An NKVD troika sentenced him to be shot on 7 December 1937. He was shot on 10 December 1937 — which is this day, 27 ноября by the old reckoning — and buried in an unmarked common grave at the Бутово range outside Moscow. He was numbered among the new martyrs and confessors of Russia for church-wide veneration by the jubilee council of bishops in August 2000.

His name page lists one other day, «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church. One row per church is the schema, so that day is recorded here and he does not render on it.
