# Serapion of Rome

Serapion was one of three Roman martyrs — Hermas, Serapion and Polyaenus — who suffered for Christ in the second century. Thrown into prison, and confessing their faith firmly at their interrogation and refusing to sacrifice to the idols, they were dragged through narrow and impassable places, and striking against the rocks and ledges they died and received the heavenly crowns. That is all the life says.
