# Anastasia of Uglich

saint.gr's whole account of her is three sentences: «Η Οσία Αναστασία έζησε κατά τον 17ο αιώνα μ.Χ. στη Ρωσία. Έγινε μοναχή και αναδείχθηκε ηγουμένη της μονής του Ούγκλιχ. Κοιμήθηκε οσίως με ειρήνη» — the venerable Anastasia lived in the seventeenth century in Russia; she became a nun and was made abbess of the monastery of Uglich; and she fell asleep in holiness and in peace. The day's line says the same and adds nothing: «Οσία Αναστασία ηγουμένη του Ούγκλιχ».

That is all. The page glosses the name in its masculine form, Αναστάσιος, from ἀνάστασις: the everlasting one, as the risen Jesus is. It gives no year of birth and none of death, does not say what the monastery at Uglich was called or to whom it was dedicated, names no bishop, no sister and no successor, tells no incident of her rule, lists no relic, and prints neither couplet nor hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/10/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1308/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
