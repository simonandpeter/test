# Barlaam of the Kyiv Caves

saint.gr's calendar for 19 Νοεμβρίου names «Όσιος Βαρλαάμ ηγούμενος του Σπηλαίου» — Barlaam, abbot of the Caves — heads his page «(; - 1065)», giving the year of his death and no year of birth, and then says outright that it has nothing more: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ρώσου Αγίου», we have no details of the life of the Russian saint. That one word, Russian, is the whole of what it says about where he came from.

The only other thing it prints is his relics: «Το Λείψανο του Οσίου βρίσκεται αδιάφθορο στη Λαύρα των Σπηλαίων του Κιέβου» — the relic of the venerable one lies incorrupt in the Lavra of the Caves at Kyiv, which is the house the office in his title belongs to. The page gives him no birth year, no manner of death, no companions and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 19 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/19/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2911/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
