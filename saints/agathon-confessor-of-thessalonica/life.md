# Agathon of Thessalonica

saint.gr gives the four of them one sentence. The holy confessors Agathon, [Eutychia](/saints/eutychia-confessor-of-thessalonica), [Kasia](/saints/kasia-confessor-of-thessalonica) and [Philippa](/saints/philippa-confessor-of-thessalonica) lived under the emperor Diocletian, whose reign it dates 284 to 305; they were arrested at Thessalonica in the year 304 and confessed Christ before the governor Dulcitius and the prefect Cassander. On account of the youth of their years they were not condemned to death but shut up in prison, and it is for that the calendar calls them confessors and not martyrs.

That is the whole of the notice. The page gives no parentage, no age, no length of imprisonment and no year of death, and it prints neither couplet nor hymn. The governor it names is the same Dulcitius who on this day and in this year tried the three sisters Agape, Chionia and Irene at Thessalonica.
