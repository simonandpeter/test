# Theodore of Vršac

saint.gr says at the outset that there is not sufficient hagiological information about the holy hieromartyr Theodore, bishop of Vršac in the country of northern Serbia. He lived in the sixteenth century, and in 1593 he is recorded as bishop and as the spiritual leader of his people, who were then fighting against the Turks.

To save his flock from slaughter, the page says, he led them towards Hungary; and after a peace had been signed with the Turks he returned again to Vršac, where he was arrested, tortured without mercy and flayed alive. So the holy hieromartyr Theodore, by his martyrdom, entered into the joy of his Lord and into the kingdom of God.

That is the whole of the entry. The page gives him no year of birth and no year of death, no parents, no birthplace, no monastery, no relics and no hymn, and nothing beyond it is set down here.
