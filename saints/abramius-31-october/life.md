# Abramius (31 October)

saint.gr's calendar for 31 Οκτωβρίου keeps nine men in one line and then admits that it knows nothing about them. The line is «Άγιοι Στέφανος, Bαρνάβας, Tρόφιμος, Δορυμέδων, Kοσμάς, Δαμιανός, Σάββας, Bάσης, Αβράμιος «και της συνοδίας αυτών»» — and with their company — and the whole of the notice behind it is «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο των Αγίων», we have no details of the saints' lives. Each of the nine carries his own heading on the page, and this folder is one of them.

The only thing printed beyond the names is the epigram: a nine of contestants, together with a multitude of fellow contestants, shared equally in the crowns in heaven. So there is no city, no persecution, no year, no manner of death and no relics; and the company called their «συνοδία» is neither named nor numbered, so it is one entry here and not a set of folders.

This folder takes its name from the day, which in his case is doing real work: the Greek calendar keeps a bishop of Ephesus of this name on 28 October and an archimandrite of Rostov of this name on 29 October, and this man is neither of them. No hymn is printed. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου».

*After saint.gr's calendar for 31 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/31/index.aspx) and [the notice of the nine](https://www.saint.gr/1044/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
