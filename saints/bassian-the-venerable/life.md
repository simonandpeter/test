# Bassian

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 10 octombrie reads «Sfântul Cuvios Vasian», and his own page on the site carries no Viață tab and no Tropar tab: it prints the name, the date, and the epistle and gospel appointed for the day, and nothing else about the man. So all that can be said here is what the calendar says: that the Romanian church keeps on the tenth of October a monastic saint whom it calls Vasian, and that it sets beside him the reading from Galatians on the fruit of the Spirit and the Lord's words in Matthew about the easy yoke, which is the common of a venerable father.

Nothing is written here about where he lived, or when, or what he did, because the source read for this folder says none of it. Other calendars may; they have not been read.

The Greek calendar keeps a Bassian on the same civil day and, unlike doxologia, gives him a life. saint.gr says he came from Syria and lived in the years when Marcian was emperor, about 450. From his childhood years he was marked out for his Christian principles, and he was shown to be a worthy expounder of the Christian truths and of the Gospel. He went to Constantinople and continued his teaching there to great effect, and not only men followed him but women also; one of his many women disciples was Saint Matrona, whose feast the page gives as 9 Νοεμβρίου. Bassian died at a great age, and he preached the word of the Lord to his last moments. Marcian built a church in his name, probably after the saint's death, which survives to this day. The Greek entry is taken here as the same man on the strength of the shared day, name and rank, and nothing more: doxologia's page gave nothing that could be compared with it.
