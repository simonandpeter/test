# John (Kesariysky)

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar for 3 ноября ст. ст. carries twelve names on one line and his is the eighth of them: «Сщмчч. Василия, Василия, Александра, Владимира, Сергия, Николая, Викентия, Иоанна, Петра, Александра, Павла, Космы пресвитеров (1937).» The line gives the forenames in the genitive, the grade-class «пресвитеров» for all twelve together, and one year, 1937, and it gives no surname to any of them. The name link behind his place in it supplies the calendar's own nominative with the surname, «Иоанн (Кесарийский), иерей, сщмч.», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from.

That is everything the calendar records of him. He has no life page, no icon, no hymn, no place, no age and no parish anywhere on this calendar, and the name page behind his place on the line carries two days and no text at all. The name page behind his place on the line also lists «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. That is a synaxis and not a second feast, and he does not render on it. Nothing further is written here, and in particular nothing is reasoned from the surname.

The line names the class and the link names the grade, and both are kept: the line's «Сщмчч.» and «пресвитеров» make him a hieromartyr and a presbyter, and the link's «иерей» is recorded as his office, Priest. He is given no relation to the eleven others on the line. They share a line, a grade-class and a year, and the calendar prints nothing at all that says they died in one place or knew one another; a shared day is not a relation here.

The Russian church calendar at azbyka.ru prints what days.pravoslavie.ru does not: a life for the three clergy of his parish together, headed «СВЯЩЕННОМУЧЕНИКИ ИОАНН КЕСАРИЙСКИЙ, ПЕТР КОСМИНКОВ, ПРЕСВИТЕРЫ И СИМЕОН КРЕЧКОВ, ДИАКОН». He was born on 30 January 1878 in the village of Одишево in the Kineshma district of the Kostroma province, the son of a priest, Nikanor Kesariysky. He finished a theological seminary and, in 1903, the Moscow theological academy; he was ordained priest in 1904 and served in the churches of the Moscow diocese, latterly in the church of the Vladimir icon of the Mother of God in the village of Быково, where he was rector.

In the persecution it happened, the page says, that the godless arrested not only the priest but the whole clergy and the most active lay members of the church council, as at Быково in the Ramenskoye district. Taken there over three days, from 1 to 3 November 1937, and shut up in the Taganka prison in Moscow, were himself, the second priest [Peter (Kosminkov)](/saints/peter-kosminkov), the deacon [Simeon (Krechkov)](/saints/simeon-krechkov), the warden of the church [Eudocia (Safronova)](/saints/eudocia-safronova), and the council members «Петр Пискарев-Молоков» and «Александр, Екатерина и Дмитрий Чиликины».

His questioning began on the day of the arrest. Asked whom he knew, he answered that he knew the priest and the deacon who served with him, the warden and some members of the council, and that if he met and talked with them it was on church matters only. The interrogator was not satisfied and went on the next day: told that he was one of the organisers of a church group at Быково — meaning that he was the rector — and asked to name its members, he said that he knew nothing of it and was no organiser; asked where the illegal meetings had been held, he said he knew nothing, having been at none.

He was then required to admit that he had discussed an act of terror against the chairman of the collective farm, and he rejected the charge. Told that he had spread slander about the Soviet power and the collective farms, he answered that he had never uttered or spread counter-revolutionary slander, and that he might have been misunderstood when once over tea in the church watchman's lodge he retold a case that had been printed in the newspaper, of a chairman of a district executive committee who came to a collective farm, went into the stable, saw a worn horse-collar and put it on a farm worker's neck, saying «Вы раньше ходили в хомуте и теперь будете ходить в хомуте» — you went in a collar before and you will go in a collar now. He had told it, he said, as a thing that had happened, with no slander of the farms in it.

Required at last to give evidence on the counter-revolutionary activity of each member of the group, he said he knew of none and knew no members, and the questioning ended.

On 15 November 1937 one NKVD troika sentenced the two priests and the deacon to be shot, two of the council members to ten years in a labour camp, and the warden and two others to eight. The three clergy were shot on 16 November 1937 and buried in one unmarked common grave on the Butovo range near Moscow. One arrest, one village, one troika decision and one grave are what the relations now written here rest on — a company, and not the line of twelve the calendar prints him in; his relations are to the two who were shot with him and to the warden, and to none of the other eleven names on the line.

The service azbyka prints with the life is headed «Ме́сяца ноября́ в 3-й день» and «Новому́чеников Бы́ковских», its text approved by the Holy Synod of the Russian Orthodox Church on 14 May 2018, журнал № 37; it names the three clergy and the warden together, and its troparion is now in this folder with the English typed here. The deacon is the one of the four whom the calendar does not keep on this day: both calendars keep him on 12 ноября ст. ст., the civil 25 November, which is not the day any of the three were shot.
