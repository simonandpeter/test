# Meletius, founder of the monastery of Sergius

The memory of the venerable Meletius is mentioned only in the Sinai Typikon no. 1094, folio 35: on the fifteenth of September our venerable father Meletius, founder of the monastery of Sergius, fell asleep (Dmitrievsky, Typika II, p. 30). That is the whole of the Greek notice for 15 September; this entry records it and nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 15 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2439/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
