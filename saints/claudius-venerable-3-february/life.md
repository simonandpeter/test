# Claudius (3 February)

saint.gr names him and gives no life. Its calendar for 3 February prints «Όσιος Κλαύδιος», and the page behind the line says two things and stops: that he departed in peace, and that no further details of the venerable one's life are held — «Ο Όσιος Κλαύδιος απεβίωσε ειρηνικά. Δεν έχουμε περισσότερες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Οσίου». The rank it gives him is Όσιος, venerable, and it gives no homeland, no century, no monastery and no hymn.

Over him it sets one couplet, which says that Claudius rejoiced at finding the end of his life as a man might rejoice, in the words of the psalm, at finding much spoil. Which Claudius of the several the Church keeps this is, the page does not say, and no other calendar was read for him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/03/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1193/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
