# Longinus the Stylite

saint.gr's calendar for 10 Οκτωβρίου names Longinus the Stylite in one entry with [Theophilus](/saints/theophilus-of-selention) the Confessor, and gives him three lines at the end of their shared page. He was, it says, the helper of Saint Theophilus; and when the two of them went together to Nicaea, in the reign of Leo the Isaurian who made war on the holy icons, Longinus too was subjected to many torments.

In one of them, the page says, they laid him on his back and put on his head the holy icons he had with him, and burned them. That is the whole of what saint.gr records of him: no birthplace, no pillar, no year, no end, and no hymn. The epithet «ο Στυλίτης», the stylite, is the page's own and is all it says of how he lived. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/10/index.aspx) and [the life he shares with Theophilus](https://www.saint.gr/2708/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
