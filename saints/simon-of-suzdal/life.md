# Simon of Suzdal

saint.gr names him and gives no life. Its whole account of him is one sentence: «Ο Άγιος Σίμων, Επίσκοπος Σουζδαλίας, έζησε κατά τον 12ο αιώνα μ.Χ. και κοιμήθηκε με ειρήνη» — the holy Simon, bishop of Suzdal, lived in the twelfth century and fell asleep in peace.

The page gives no year of his birth or of his repose, no birthplace, no account of his episcopate and no companions, and it prints no hymn to him. Beyond the see it names and the century it names, nothing further about him is recorded here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 23 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/23/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4053/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
