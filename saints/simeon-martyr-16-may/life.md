# Simeon (16 May)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 16 mai reads «Sfântul Mucenic Simeon» — the holy martyr Simeon — with no epithet, no see, no country and no century, and his own page on the site carries that same line as heading and title and nothing else: no Viață tab, no Tropar tab, nothing that would tell him from any other martyr of the name.

The display name here carries the day for that reason, because the calendar gives nothing else to tell him by, and the corpus keeps several men called Simeon or Symeon. This folder asserts one thing only: that the Romanian church keeps a martyr named Simeon on 16 May. Whether he is the same as any Simeon kept elsewhere is an open question that the page read here cannot settle, and no other calendar was checked.

The Russian calendar names him first of three and gives the three a notice of five sentences, printed at the foot of another company's page. They were Christians, it says, and lived in the third century in Persia under king Шапур, a cruel persecutor of Christians. They were pressed to renounce Christ and to turn to the superstition of the fire-worshippers, and refused, answering the pagans: we will not renounce the Creator of creation, and we will not bow to the sun and to the fire. They were cruelly tortured, then thrown into prison, where they were given no food for seven days; and at the last they were beheaded with the sword.

Two things in that notice do not sit together, and both are left standing here. The calendar's own day line prints the fourth century against the third its text gives; and the king it names reigned across both, by the dating the Greek book gives him. The two men the Russian names with him are the two this folder was already linked to through the Greek entry for 16 Μαΐου, and the Russian keeps the three of them two days later in the menologion.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 16 mai — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/16-mai) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-simeon), which carries no life and no troparion; read 24 September 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 18 мая ст. ст. — [the Russian day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260518.html) and [the Russian notice of the three](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1095.htm), read 8 October 2026.*
