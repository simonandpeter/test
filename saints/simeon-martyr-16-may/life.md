# Simeon (16 May)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 16 mai reads «Sfântul Mucenic Simeon» — the holy martyr Simeon — with no epithet, no see, no country and no century, and his own page on the site carries that same line as heading and title and nothing else: no Viață tab, no Tropar tab, nothing that would tell him from any other martyr of the name.

The display name here carries the day for that reason, because the calendar gives nothing else to tell him by, and the corpus keeps several men called Simeon or Symeon. This folder asserts one thing only: that the Romanian church keeps a martyr named Simeon on 16 May. Whether he is the same as any Simeon kept elsewhere is an open question that the page read here cannot settle, and no other calendar was checked.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 16 mai — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/16-mai) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-simeon), which carries no life and no troparion; read 24 September 2026.*
