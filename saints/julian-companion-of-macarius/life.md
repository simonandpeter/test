# Julian, companion of Macarius

saint.gr's calendar for 30 Οκτωβρίου keeps «Άγιοι Αλέξανδρος, Κρονίων, Ιουλιανός, Μακάριος και άλλοι Δεκατρείς Μάρτυρες» — four named men and thirteen more. All of them were martyred in the years of the emperor Decius, in the year 250, at Alexandria, the page says, and it gives them no other circumstance: no trial, no governor, and nothing of who any of them was before.

The first two, [Alexander](/saints/alexander-companion-of-cronion) and [Cronion](/saints/cronion-companion-of-alexander), were put to death after hard torments, having boiling lime poured over them. Julian and [Macarius](/saints/macarius-companion-of-julian) came next: they too were tortured inhumanly, and at the end they were beheaded. Of the other thirteen martyrs, some were burned alive and some beheaded.

The epigram the page sets over this pair says that Julian and Macarius found by the sword the most blessed life. The thirteen are named nowhere, so they are one entry here and not thirteen folders. saint.gr prints no hymn for the company, and carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου».

*After saint.gr's calendar for 30 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/30/index.aspx) and [the notice of the company](https://www.saint.gr/2854/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
