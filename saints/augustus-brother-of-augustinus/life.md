# Augustus, brother of Augustinus

saint.gr's calendar names three men in one line and gives them one sentence. The holy hieromartyr [Flavius](/saints/flavius-of-nicomedia) was Bishop of Nicomedia, it says, and was martyred in the reign of the emperor Diocletian, who held the empire from 284 to 305, together with the brothers Augustus and [Augustinus](/saints/augustinus-brother-of-augustus).

That is the whole of the page, and it is all that is known here of him: a name, a rank, a brother whose name is a syllable from his own, a bishop in whose company he died, a reign, and the day. The page's word for the two of them, «των αυταδέλφων», makes them brothers by both parents, and the display name keeps that because nothing else tells the two apart. There is no city, no judge, no manner of death, no relics and no hymn, and the page prints no couplet for them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/07/index.aspx) and [the life of the three](https://www.saint.gr/4011/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
