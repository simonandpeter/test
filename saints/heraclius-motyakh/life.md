# Heraclius (Motyakh)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 28 мая ст. ст. — «Прп. Ираклия , исп. (1936).» — and gives him his family name and his degree on its name page, «Ираклий (Мотях), схимонах, исповедник, прп.», a schemamonk and a confessor. The reading the line links is not his own but a company’s: it is headed for the venerable-martyrs of Verny, that is of Alma-Ata, and names seven, Серафим, Феогност, Пахомий, Анатолий, Ираклий, Виктор and Евдокия. The page says plainly that after nearly a century it is no longer possible to establish the biographical facts of the Kazakhstan ascetics with any certainty.

What it tells is the house they belonged to. To spread the Orthodox faith in the Turkestan country and to teach the nomads of Asia, the bishop of the Turkestan see, Александр (Кульчицкий), meant to light a missionary star on the shores of Lake Issyk-Kul; in 1882 he blessed the chosen place and set up a wooden cross where the men’s Holy Trinity missionary monastery was to stand. Its first inhabitants laboured at the walls and the buildings and the farm, bearing the ill will of the wandering tribes, which turned often enough into open attack on the novices and the trampling of the monastery pastures. Eleven monks came from the Transkuban hermitage in 1886, and in 1894 eight more set out from Valaam under the abbot Севастиан, carrying the spirit of that house with them; and early in the twentieth century several monks were invited from the Glinsk hermitage in the diocese of Kursk, among them Серафим, Феогност and Анатолий. It is of that arrival that the page names him: at the Holy Trinity monastery the Glinsk monks «сблизились с монахами Пахомием и Ираклием», became close to the monks Пахомий and Ираклий — and that one clause is the whole of what the calendar says of him by name.

After the Bolsheviks came to power at Verny in March 1918 and the bishop Пимен was shot, the monks went up into the mountains and made a skete eight versts from the city, on the Mohnataya hill in the Medeo gorge, with a church dug into the mountainside; later they gave that skete to nuns and sought a more solitary place, and found it on the mountain Kyzyl-Zhar, where they set up cells and dug caves and had an altar. The calendar gives him the year 1936 and the grade of confessor, and no year of birth and no account of his death; the company it names him in is kept on another day, which was not read here.
