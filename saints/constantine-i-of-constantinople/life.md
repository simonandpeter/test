# Constantine I of Constantinople

saint.gr's notice on him is three sentences. It says that Constantine the First succeeded John the Fifth on the patriarchal throne of Constantinople, and that he was an opponent of the heresy of Monothelitism — the teaching of a single will in Christ, which the sixth Ecumenical Council met to condemn in those very years.

Before his election, the page says, he served as deacon and as skeuophylax, the keeper of the vessels, of the Great Church of Christ. And it cites Ephraim, who names him as a visitor of Thrace and of Macedonia and describes him as an important man and a glory of right-minded piety: «χαρακτηρίζεται σπουδαίος ανήρ και ευσεβοφρόνων κλέος».

That is all saint.gr gives. It prints no year for his birth, none for his death, and no hymn; the only figures on the page, «(668 - 674 μ.Χ.)», stand in the sentence beside the name of the predecessor he succeeded, and the page does not say whose years they are.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 9 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/09/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2296/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
