# Andron

saint.gr's calendar for 3 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιοι Δάσιος, Σέβηρος, Άνδρωνας, Θεόδοτος και Θεοδότη», and the page behind that line says one thing only: the holy [Dasius](/saints/dasius-3-november), [Severus](/saints/severus-3-november), Andron, [Theodotus](/saints/theodotus-3-november) and [Theodote](/saints/theodote-3-november) were martyred by the sword. There is no city, no persecutor, no year, nothing of who the five were to one another, and nothing of their relics.

The page's epigram adds the one distinction it makes among them, which is of sex and not of name: let the woman too be a fellow martyr to the men, cut down along with the four who were cut. Each of the five has a heading of his or her own on the page, which is why there are five folders here and not one.

Of the five, his is the name the corpus has never held before, and the page writes it «Άνδρωνας» and not Andronicus, which is a different name and a different set of men. No hymn is printed. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου».

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/03/index.aspx) and [the notice of the five](https://www.saint.gr/2874/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
