# Arsenius of the Kyiv Caves

The venerable Arsenius, a lover of work, lived in the fourteenth century, saint.gr says. He practised asceticism in the monastery of the Caves of Kyiv, which was dedicated to the Dormition of the Mother of God, and he was marked out by his love of hard labour. He took no rest at all and prayed without ceasing, eating only a very little at the setting of the sun. For his humility and for that love of work, the page says, Christ gave him the gift of working wonders.

Arsenius fell asleep in peace. The page gives no year for his birth and none for his death, names no abbot under whom he lived and no disciple, says nothing of where he was buried, and prints neither apolytikion nor kontakion for him. It is the epithet «ο εν τη Λαύρα του Κιέβου ασκήσας» in the day's own line that tells him apart from the three other Arsenii the Greek calendar keeps on this same eighth of May.
