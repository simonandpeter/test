# Joasaph of Alaska

Joasaph, whose surname the page gives in brackets as Bolotov, was born in Russia in the eighteenth century. Out of love for God he became a priest and worked as a missionary in Alaska. He reached the island of Kodiak on 24 September 1794, and there, for all the enormous and unimaginable difficulties, he served the preaching of the Gospel with all his strength.

In July of the year 1796 the Synod of the Russian Church declared Alaska a diocese of Irkutsk in Siberia, and Joasaph was elected its bishop. On a missionary journey his ship was wrecked in the frozen waters of the ocean, carrying Joasaph to the bottom together with the whole crew. The page gives no year for the wreck, none for his birth, and no hymn for him; the calendar's own epithet for him is Enlightener of Alaska.
