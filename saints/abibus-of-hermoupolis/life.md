# Abibus of Hermoupolis

saint.gr's calendar for 13 Μαρτίου names him «Άγιος Αβίβος από την Ερμούπολη», and the page behind that line holds one sentence about him: that the holy Abibus was from Hermoupolis of Egypt and received the crown of martyrdom after they had bound a great stone to his body and thrown him into the river. Beside it stand two lines of verse which say the same: «Βληθεὶς Ἄβιβος εἰς ποταμὸν σὺν λίθῳ, / Ἐκπλεῖ ποταμὸν συρφετώδη τοῦ βίου» — cast into a river with a stone, Abibus sails out of the muddy river of this life.

The page then adds a guess of its own in a bracket: perhaps he is the same person as the holy Sabinus the Egyptian, whom it keeps on 16 March and drowns in the same way. That perhaps is left as the source's own; the Greek calendar prints two entries on two days, and this folder is the one it prints here. Beyond the city, the stone and the river there is no year, no persecutor, no relic and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 13 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/13/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/37/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
