# Germanus of Sagmata

saint.gr's calendar for 26 January names Germanus of Sagmata and gives a short notice. It says that he lived in the dark years of the servitude, 1480 to 1540, and that he is one more ascetic figure who lights the Church of Boeotia like a bright star. His biographer, the page says, calls him «πνευματοφόρο», spirit-bearing, since his person shone with the spiritual gifts of the Holy Spirit, so that he reached heights of moral and spiritual perfection and became a teacher and a guide to his fellow ascetics.

That is the whole of the life the page prints: no birthplace, no parentage, no separate year of birth or of death, and no account of his repose. It prints an apolytikion for him in the fourth tone, and it keeps him among the saints of Boeotia. The kontakion of the day belongs to him jointly with [Clement of Mount Sagmation](/saints/clement-of-mount-sagmation), whom the same calendar keeps on this day, and stands on Clement's page rather than on his.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4071/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
