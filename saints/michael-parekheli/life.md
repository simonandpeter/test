# Michael Parekheli

saint.gr's calendar names him Michael out of Georgia, and the page behind the line gives him the surname Parekheli and two sentences: «Ο Όσιος Μιχαήλ Παρεκχέλι έζησε και ασκήτεψε κατά τον 13ο και 14ο αιώνα μ.Χ. στη Γεωργία. Κοιμήθηκε οσίως με ειρήνη.» — the venerable Michael Parekheli lived and practised the ascetic life in Georgia in the thirteenth and fourteenth centuries, and reposed in peace.

The page names no monastery and no town, gives no year within those two centuries, and prints no hymn. It glosses the name itself, from the Hebrew, as the one who is like God, and says nothing else of him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 27 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/27/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1791/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
