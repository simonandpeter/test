# Victor (Usov)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him in a single line for 8 января ст. ст. — «Сщмч. Виктора пресвитера (1937).» — a hieromartyr and a priest who died in 1937. The name link behind the line gives the calendar's own nominative, «Виктор (Усов), иерей», which adds the surname the line leaves out. The calendar prints no life page for him, and none is given here: the line and the name link are the whole of what it records.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 8 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260108.html); read 3 October 2026.*
