# Pambo the Recluse

saint.gr's calendar for 18 July keeps two men called Pambo, and this is the second of them, «Όσιος Παμβώ ο Έγκλειστος», Pambo the recluse. Its page for him is four sentences long. It calls him a confessor of the faith; it says he was taken captive by the Tatars and for many years suffered dreadful torments to make him deny the Christian faith; that afterwards, as though by a miracle, he was carried out of his captivity back into his own cell; and that he died in 1241.

Nothing else is given. There is no birthplace, no year of birth, no monastery named, no account of how he came to be a recluse or of what he did after his return, and no hymn. He is not the other Pambo of this day, [Pamvo of Nitria](/saints/pamvo-of-nitria), whose page the same calendar prints a few lines above this one and whose life ran a thousand years earlier in the Egyptian desert; the Greek church keeps both on 18 July, and this folder is for the later man.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2621/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
