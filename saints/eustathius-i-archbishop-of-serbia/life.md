# Eustathius I

saint.gr's notice for him is one sentence: Saint Eustathius I was Archbishop of Serbia, and he fell asleep in peace in the year 1286. The day's line writes the see «Αρχιεπίσκοπος Ζερβών» and the notice behind it «Αρχιεπίσκοπος Σερβίας»; both spellings are the page's, and the see recorded here is the one the notice gives.

Beyond that the page prints only a note on the name, that Eustathius means the man steady in a decision, the undisturbed, the settled character, and a list of three other bearers of it who are not saints. There is no birthplace, no account of his years in the see, no hymn and no manner of death, and nothing is supplied here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 4 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/04/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/473/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
