# Taurion (Tolokontsev)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and gives no life. Its line for 25 мая ст. ст. reads «Прмч. Тавриона (1939).» — the venerable-martyr Taurion, of the year 1939 — and the line carries a link to his name and none to a reading. The name page behind it adds his family name, Толоконцев, and his degree, «инок», monk, and nothing else: its one line is «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 25 мая».

So the calendar sets down a Russian monk who died in 1939, and no more than that. The second of those two days is not a feast of his own: it is the general commemoration of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which this calendar prints beside the proper day of each of them, and it is the one mark on his page that places him among that company. He is kept here on the day the calendar gives him for his own, and the synaxis is recorded on his attestation.

This corpus already keeps a saint of the same bare name, a martyr of Amphipolis in Macedonia whom all four calendars hold on the Julian 7 November, and the two are not the same man: different century, different country, different company, and nothing on either page joins them. It is for that reason, and not for the sake of a longer label, that the surname the calendar prints is part of this folder’s name. Nothing else about him was found, and azbyka.ru, which is where an account of him would be, was not read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 25 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260525.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9881.html), which name him and give no life; read 8 October 2026.*
