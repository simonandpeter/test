# Nicholas of Spetses

Nicholas was the old captain of the boat, and saint.gr's line calls him «ο συνοδίτης αυτών», their travelling companion, rather than their kin. In 1822 he sailed from Spetses for Constantinople with the brothers [Stamatios](/saints/stamatios-of-spetses) and [John](/saints/john-of-spetses) and four other trading sailors; a heavy sea put them aground opposite Chios at Çeşme on the Asia Minor coast, where the Christian they hired to find timber for the damaged boat betrayed them to the agha.

The soldiers killed two of the seven and two escaped by sea. The Ottomans took the two brothers and Nicholas, and the pasha, after questioning them, ordered the brothers imprisoned and Nicholas beheaded on the plain outside the castle. The Turks urged him to change his faith and save his life, and he answered with courage that he would not deny it; and so, confessing Christ, he received the martyr's crown when they struck off his head.

The page's own head prints the years 1800 and 1822 for him as it does for the younger brother, which does not agree with its narrative, where he is the old captain; no year of birth is taken from it here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/03/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1192/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
