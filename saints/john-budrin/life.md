# John (Budrin)

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 21 июня ст. ст. prints him in the line «Сщмч. Иоанна пресвитера (1918).», and the name link behind his place in it supplies the calendar’s own nominative with the surname, «Иоанн (Будрин), иерей, сщмч.» (name page 13729). That is where the name form in this folder is read from; the line gives the forename in the genitive and no surname at all.

His name page carries nothing else. It heads «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 21 июня» and then stops: no life, no article, no place of service, no account of his death in 1918. The calendar’s line and that heading are the whole of what this source gives him, and nothing is supplied for them here — not a diocese, not an age, and not the manner of his death. He is the only one of this day’s twentieth-century names whom the calendar dates to 1918.

The line’s «Сщмч.» makes him a hieromartyr and «пресвитера» a presbyter, and the name link repeats the rank as «иерей». The second day his name page lists is the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which is a synaxis and not a second feast, so he renders on 21 июня ст. ст. only. The day page prints six hymns, none of them under his name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 21 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260621.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13729.html); read 9 October 2026.*
