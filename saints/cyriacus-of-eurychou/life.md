# Cyriacus of Eurychou

saint.gr says it is not known when the venerable Cyriacus, the ascetic of Eurychou, lived, and that what it knows of him comes from his service: that from infancy «ἐγένου τοῦ Κυρίου ἐραστὴς», he became a lover of the Lord. He loved God and followed him; he left the world and became a citizen of the desert and a lover of stillness.

Crowds of the faithful used to visit him at his hermitage, to see him, to take his counsel and to receive his blessing; and the venerable one received them all with love and with patience, taught them and comforted them, and counselled them to leave off the hatreds and the malice they held against one another and to repent.

The Triune God endowed him with the gift of working wonders. «Φέρων τὸν σταυρὸν ἐπ' ὤμων σου… καὶ πάθη θανατώσας τὰ τοῦ σώματος συντόνοις ἀγρυπνίαις καὶ δεήσεσι χάριν ἀπείληφας, Ὅσιε, τοῦ θεραπεύειν νοσήματα», writes his hymnographer — bearing the cross on your shoulders and putting to death the passions of the body by strenuous vigils and supplications, you received the grace, venerable one, of healing sicknesses. Daily in his cell, along with the teaching he offered, he healed the sick and the suffering. So, having lived as an angel in soul and as a wonderworker, the venerable Cyriacus fell asleep in peace.
