# Pothinus of Lyon

Pothinus was bishop of Lugdunum — Lyon, in Gaul — before [Irenaeus](/saints/irenaeus-of-lyon). He lived, the synaxarion says, probably in the early part of the second century, and died a martyr for Christ in its second half.

That is all the Greek entry records; the Romanian Lives of Irenaeus add only that Irenaeus took the see after the end of the holy martyr Pothinus.

The Ohrid Prologue keeps him on 23 August (Julian) for the Serbian calendar: sent from Asia Minor by Polycarp to preach in Gaul, he became the first bishop of Lyon and brought many pagans to Christianity; in the persecution of 177 he too was brought to trial — carried in, being ninety years old. Who is the God of the Christians, the proconsul asked him; you will know, if you are made worthy, the old man answered. The pagans fell on him with sticks and stones and beat him without mercy, and thrown into prison he died of the beating after two days.
