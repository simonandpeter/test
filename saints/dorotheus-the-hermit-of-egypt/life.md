# Dorotheus the Hermit of Egypt

Dorotheus the hermit in Egypt lived in the fourth century; the Greek synaxarion says it has no further details of his life. The Greek calendar keeps him on 16 September, and this entry records the line and nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 16 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2448/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
