# Andrew of Suzdal

saint.gr says that Saint Andrew lived in the fourteenth century after Christ in Russia and was the son of the prince Constantine Vasilyevich. After his father's death, in 1355, he received from the khan Khanibek the rule of Suzdal, of Nizhny Novgorod and of Gorodets. According to the tradition, he gave up the throne in 1359 in favour of his younger brother Demetrius.

Having lived in piety, Saint Andrew fell asleep in peace in 1365. That is the whole of the page's account: three dates, a father's name, a brother's name, three principalities and an abdication. It says nothing of how he lived after he laid the rule down, nothing of a monastery, and nothing of his burial; and it prints no hymn for him. The Russian form of his name is not on the page, so it is not stored here, and the Russian calendar's own reckoning of his day has not been checked.
