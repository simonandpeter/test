# Eugene (24 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 24 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιος Ευγένιος», glosses the name as the man of good birth and good feelings, and then gives the manner of his death and nothing else: «Ο Άγιος Ευγένιος μαρτύρησε, αφού τον έκτισαν ζωντανό, μέσα στην τρύπα ενός τείχους» — he was martyred after they walled him up alive inside the hole of a wall.

The couplet set over the line is addressed to that wall: in vain, hole, do you bear the mud like bolts, for to Eugene you become a path to God. The page gives him no country, no city, no century, no year, no emperor, no companions, no relics and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/24/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2993/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
