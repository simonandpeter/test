# Babylas (28 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 28 December keeps the venerable Babylas, and gives no life of him. Its whole account is two sentences: «Ο Όσιος Βαβύλας απεβίωσε ειρηνικά. Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του» — the venerable Babylas died in peace; we have no other details about his life. It gives no year, no country, no monastery and no hymn, and it calls him Όσιος, venerable, and not a martyr.

The one other thing it keeps of him is the couplet set over the entry: «Άπαν Bαβύλας υλικόν ρίψας βάρος, / Άυλος εις άυλον ήκει χωρίον» — Babylas, having cast off every material weight, came immaterial to an immaterial place. That is the ascetic's end stated in verse, and it adds no fact to the two sentences above it.
