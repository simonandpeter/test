# George of Alikianos

George was born on 24 May 1846 in the village of Alikianos, in the province of Kydonia in Crete. His father was a priest and was called Nicholas Divolis — the page allows the form Devolis as well — and his mother was named Catherine. Of his childhood and his schooling saint.gr says nothing; what its page keeps of him is his birth, his priest's household, and the rising that took him twenty years later.

In the Cretan revolution of 1866 he was seized by the Turks together with other insurgents, and was pressed in every way to change his faith so as to save his life. For all the threats and all the tortures, the page says, the martyr confessed Christ with a spiritual manliness; and so he received the crown of martyrdom, when they cut off his honourable head, in the year 1867.

The page adds one thing more, about his service rather than his life: the office for this saint was written by the hymnographer [Gerasimos of Little St Anne's](/saints/gerasimos-of-little-st-annes). It prints no troparion under his name, names no relics, and gives no couplet.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3617/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
