# Zosimas of Kumurdo

saint.gr names him and gives almost no life. Zosimas lived in the fourteenth and fifteenth centuries in Georgia, its page says, and was bishop of the city of Kumurdo, and he fell asleep in holiness and peace. That is the whole of the notice: no year of birth, no year of death, no place he came from, nothing of how he was made bishop or what he did in the see, no relic and no hymn.

The day's listing prints him as «Άγιος Ζωσιμάς εκ Γεωργίας», Zosimas of Georgia, and the see comes from the notice itself. The two centuries are the page's own and it names no year inside them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1452/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
