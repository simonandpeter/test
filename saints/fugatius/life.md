# Fugatius

Fugatius and Damian lived in the second century and were monks at Rome, and saint.gr's whole account of them is that they were sent by Eleutherius, bishop of Rome, to England, to spread the Gospel, and that they fell asleep in peace. Two sentences, and the page carries nothing else: no birthplace, no year, no account of the mission, no see, no relics and no burial place.

They are the earlier of this day's two Western missions to Britain: saint.gr keeps Augustine of Canterbury and his forty monks on the same 26 Μαΐου, four hundred years later, and the page for them tells the whole story of the landing on Thanet, where this page gives a sender and a destination and nothing more. It prints neither a synaxarion couplet nor a hymn for either of these two.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3945/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
