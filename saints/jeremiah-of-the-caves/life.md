# Jeremiah of the Caves

Jeremiah is one of three monks of the Kyiv Caves whom the Serbian church's Ohrid Prologue keeps under a single heading on this day, with [Damian](/saints/damian-the-healer) and [Matthew](/saints/matthew-of-the-caves): «Печерски прозорљивци и чудотворци», seers and wonderworkers of the Caves, all three of the eleventh century. That one sentence is the whole of what it says of him, and it says no more of the other two.

The Russian church calendar keeps the same three on the same day and adds only a year each — «Прпп. Дамиана, пресвитера, целебника (1071), Иеремии (ок. 1070) и Матфея (ок. 1085) прозорливых, Печерских» — which puts Jeremiah at about 1070 and calls all three prescient. Neither page read for this folder tells anything else about him: not where he came from, not what he foresaw, not how he died.

So this entry says what the two lines say and stops. The Russian calendar has a life page of its own for the fathers of the Caves which was not read here, and whoever opens it will have more of him than this.

*After the Serbian church's [Ohrid Prologue for the Julian 5 October](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-10-18&prolog=1) and the Russian church calendar's [5 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261005.html); read 4 October 2026.*
