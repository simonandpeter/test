# Jeremiah of the Caves

He had from the Lord, his life says, the gift of seeing the future and of seeing the moral state of a man's soul, and he died in deep old age about 1070. That is the whole of what is recorded of him, and it is recorded in good company: the lives of [Damian the Healer](/saints/damian-the-healer), of Jeremiah and of [Matthew of the Caves](/saints/matthew-of-the-caves) were written by [Nestor the Chronicler](/saints/nestor-the-chronicler), who is kept on 27 October.

The Russian calendar keeps the three of them in one line on 5 October — a presbyter and healer and two clairvoyants of the Kyiv Caves — and his own index entry adds the one word the day line shares out among them, «Прозорливый», the clairvoyant. It keeps him also on 28 September with the synaxis the abbreviation «Печер.(Б)» marks, the saints of the Near Caves.

The three have a troparion of their own on the page, which names them together as a threefold dwelling of the Trinity and asks them to heal the sick and foretell what is to come. This batch did not take it: a hymn here needs either a Hapgood citation or a rendering made beside it, and neither was done.

*After the Russian church calendar's [5 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261005.html), [the life it carries for the three of them](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1674.htm) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/915.html); read 4 October 2026.*
