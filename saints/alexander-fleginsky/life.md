# Alexander (Fleginsky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and carries no life. Its line for the twenty-fourth of March, old style, puts two priests of two different years in one printed sentence and leaves out the full stop between them: «Сщмч. Александра пресвитера (1918) Сщмч. Владимира пресвитера (1920).». The name page behind the first «Александра» is headed «Александр (Флегинский), сщмч.», which gives the surname the line leaves out and, unlike the line, no grade.

That page lists the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church on «26 января» and this twenty-fourth of March. Nothing else is said of him on either page: no place, no diocese, no parish, no year of birth and no account of how he died. The 1918 the first half of the line closes with is the whole of his chronology, and it makes him one of the earliest of the new martyrs this corpus holds.

The second source for the Russian new martyrs, azbyka.ru, was not read for him.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 24 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260324.html); read 8 October 2026.*
