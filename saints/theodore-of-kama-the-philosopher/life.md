# Theodore of Kama the Philosopher

saint.gr's whole account of him is one sentence: «Ο Άγιος Μάρτυς Θεόδωρος της Κάμα, ο επονομαζόμενος Φιλόσοφος, μαρτύρησε το έτος 1323 μ.Χ. από τους Μογγόλους» — the holy martyr Theodore of Kama, surnamed the Philosopher, was martyred in the year 1323 by the Mongols. The calendar's line for the day says the same and adds nothing, and the page's heading repeats the year: «Αγιος Θεοδωρος Της Καμα ο Φιλοσοφος (; - 1323)».

There is nothing else at all. The page does not say where Kama is, or why he was called the Philosopher, or what he wrote or taught, or how he was put to death, or who took him; it names no companion, no relic and no wonder, and it prints neither couplet nor hymn. The two things it gives him besides his name are the year and the hand that killed him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 21 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/21/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1987/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
