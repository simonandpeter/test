# Sophronius of Irkutsk

Sophronius, whose name in the world saint.gr gives as Stephen Kristalevsky, was born on 25 December 1703 in Ukraine, near the district of Chernigov, of devout parents. From his childhood, the page says, he loved the Church and the monastic life; he developed a considerable missionary work, and he was elected bishop of the city of Irkutsk. He fell asleep in peace in the year 1771, on the second day of Pascha.

What the page tells at length is what happened to his body. While the decision of the Holy Synod about the burial of the holy relic was awaited, the remains lay unburied for six months without suffering the least change; and that, together with the reputation of his strict ascetic life, drew crowds of the faithful, who venerated the relic as the body of a saint of God. The relics were saved, the page says, by a wonder from the fire that utterly destroyed the cathedral of Irkutsk on 18 April 1917, and they work a multitude of miracles.

The proclamation of his canonisation was made by the Russian Church on 23 April 1918. The page links him to the Synaxis of all the Saints of Siberia, and prints no hymn. Of his missionary work among the peoples of eastern Siberia it says only that it was considerable; that, and everything else a Russian source would carry, is another reader's to bring, since none was read for this folder.
