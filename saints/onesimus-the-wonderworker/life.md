# Onesimus the Wonderworker

saint.gr's calendar for 14 July names Onesimus the Wonderworker, and the page behind the line opens by setting him apart: this is not the apostle [Onesimus](/saints/onesimus-the-apostle), the disciple of Paul. He lived in the years of the emperor Diocletian, came from the small town of Karyne in the territory of Caesarea in Palestine, and was brought up by devout parents. From early on he was marked out for his zeal for God, for his study of the holy Scriptures, and for his compassion towards those who suffered.

Later he went to Ephesus and became a monk there. After the persecution under Diocletian the monks of the monastery where he was were scattered, and he himself returned to his own country. His parents rejoiced greatly at his coming back and grieved at the same time, because they had gone blind and could not see the face of the child they loved. Through his fervent prayer, one morning God gave the two of them their sight again, and all together they glorified their Benefactor.

Afterwards Onesimus went to Magnesia, where he founded a monastic brotherhood; and there, contending for the faith of Christ, he died in deep old age. The page gives no year for any of it. The emperor's name is the only mark of time it prints, and since it prints no years for that reign either, no date is recorded here.
