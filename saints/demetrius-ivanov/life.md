# Demetrius (Ivanov)

saint.gr's calendar for 22 Μαρτίου names a hieromartyr Demetrius, and gives him one sentence: the holy hieromartyr Demetrius (Ivanov) was the father of the venerable-martyr [Sophia](/saints/sophia-of-kyiv), abbess in Kyiv, and was martyred in 1934, without our having more details of the saint's martyrdom. The page heads him «(; - 1934)», so the year of his birth is a question mark there too.

That is the whole of what has been read. saint.gr gives him no birthplace, no parish or see, no account of his arrest or of the manner of his death, and no persecutor; it names no relics and prints neither a couplet nor a hymn under his name. Nothing has been added here from any other source and nothing from memory. What the entry does carry is the kinship, and the day's other entry — his daughter's — repeats it from her side, which is why each of these two folders names the other.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 22 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/22/index.aspx) and [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/71/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
