# Memnon of Ephesus

saint.gr's calendar for 16 December keeps Saint Memnon, archbishop of Ephesus, and the whole of its page is one sentence with a correction after it: Saint Memnon, archbishop of Ephesus, died in peace; and certain synaxaristes, it adds, wrongly record him as bishop of Jerusalem. That is all. The page gives no year of birth and none of death, names no place but the see, lists no relic and prints no hymn for him.

The same day's calendar keeps [Symeon](/saints/symeon-of-antioch), archbishop of Antioch, whose own page says that his memory is recorded in Delehaye's synaxarion together with this Memnon's — and calls him there bishop rather than archbishop of Ephesus. That pairing in the one manuscript tradition is the only other thing saint.gr offers towards placing either man.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 16 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/16/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3278/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
