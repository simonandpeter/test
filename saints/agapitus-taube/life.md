# Agapitus (Taube)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and gives no life. Its line for 5 июля ст. ст. reads «Прп. Агапита исп (1936).» — the venerable Agapitus, confessor, of the year 1936, with the word for confessor clipped short and left without its full stop — and the line carries a link to his name and none to a reading. The name page behind it, 14468, adds his family name, Таубе, and his grade in the house, «инок», a monk; beyond the title it prints one line, «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 5 июля», and nothing else at all.

So the calendar sets down a Russian monk who died in 1936 and whom it calls a confessor and not a martyr; and that distinction is kept in this folder, which carries no rank of martyrdom although the second of his two days is the general commemoration of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. That day was not read. Nothing else about him was found: the day page carries twelve hymns under other names and none under his, and azbyka.ru, which is where an account of a monk of the 1930s would be, was not read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 5 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260705.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14468.html), which name him and give no life; read 9 October 2026.*
