# Stephen the Newly Shining

saint.gr's calendar for 9 December keeps the venerable Stephen the newly shining, he who lies in the church of Saint Antipas. He was born at Constantinople, the page says, and brought up in piety by his parents Zacharias and Theophano; and he was a fervent contender for Orthodoxy during the persecutions under the emperor Theophilos the iconoclast.

In the years of the empress Theodora the patriarch Methodios ordained Stephen presbyter and enrolled him in the clergy of the archbishopric of Constantinople. When his father died he inherited a great fortune, and spent it on works of mercy. He lived at the church of Saint Antipas, into which crowds of people of every class used to come to hear the divine word from him. He died, the page ends, when he had completed the seventy-third year of his age.

The page's head gives his years as 838 to 912, which agrees with that seventy-third year and with the reigns it names; its own first sentence calls him a man of the eighth century, which does not. The couplet set over his name turns on it: you led the crown out of the earth and out of life, and you, Word, crowned him with the crown of glory.
