# Daniel (Kushnir)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Daniel among the martyrs on 29 July and gives no life. Its line is «Мч. Даниила Черкасского (1766).» — the martyr Daniel of Cherkasy, 1766 — and the calendar's own name page for him adds the surname: «Даниил (Кушнир) Черкасский, мч.», Daniel Kushnir of Cherkasy.

That is the whole of the record. The calendar gives him no rank in the church at all, so none is written here, and 1766 puts him two centuries before the twentieth-century company this day is otherwise full of.

The day page carries seven troparia and kontakia for the memories it heads, and none under his name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 29 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260729.html); read 13 October 2026.*
