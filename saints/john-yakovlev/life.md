# John (Yakovlev)

The Russian calendar names the priest John (Yakovlev) on 15 September of the old style, in the one line the day keeps for its four priests of 1921. The calendar links no written life, so this entry records the line and nothing more.

*After the Православный церковный календарь (days.pravoslavie.ru), [15 сентября ст. ст.](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260915.html); read 30 August 2026.*
