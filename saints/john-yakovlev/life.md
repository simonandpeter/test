# John (Yakovlev)

John Yakovlev was born on 11 November 1866 in a peasant family at
Novoyegorlykskoye in Stavropol province, and served at St Nicholas at Yeisk,
the church of St John the Theologian at Novonikolayevskaya, the Daniel
church at Stavropol and the Demetrius church at Yekaterinodar, taught the
law of God at the Yekaterinodar diocesan girls’ school, and sat for the
clergy in the Yekaterinodar town duma. He was married and had four children.
He was arrested on the night of 16 September 1921 for corresponding with
exiled priests, and the whole parish of the Demetrius church rose in his
defence, writing that having lived beside him more than twenty years they
knew him closely and well as a man who always treated people with love, a
spiritual pastor on the road of good, conscience and honour, who sowed what
was good and reasonable and useful instruction in the spirit of
Christianity; but on 23 September 1921 the troika of the Kuban-Black Sea
Cheka sentenced him to death, and on 28 September 1921 he was shot, the same
night as [Andrew Kovalev](/saints/andrew-kovalev), [Gregory
Konokotin](/saints/gregory-konokotin) and [Gregory
Troitsky](/saints/gregory-troitsky), and buried at Krasnodar in an unknown
grave. He is kept on 15 September of the old style, 28 September, and in the
synaxis of the saints of the Kuban metropolia.
