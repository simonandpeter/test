# Maria (Rykova)

Maria was born in 1892 in Moscow, and little is known of her life beyond
that she resolved to give it to God and became a nun. On 23 June 1937 she
was arrested in the group case of Archbishop Alexis (Orlov) and others in
the South Kazakhstan region, charged together with the nun Evdokia
Perevoznikova with belonging to a counter-revolutionary centre of churchmen;
at her interrogation she said, I belonged to no counter-revolutionary
organisation and did no counter-revolutionary work. In August 1937 the
troika of the South Kazakhstan NKVD sentenced her to death, and on 27
September 1937 she was shot in the Lisya Balka, the Fox Gully, near
Chimkent, the same day and place as [Lyudmila
Petrova](/saints/lyudmila-petrova). She is kept on 15 September of the old
style, 28 September.
