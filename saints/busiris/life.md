# Busiris

The Greek calendar names Busiris in one entry with the brothers [Eusebius](/saints/eusebius-of-gaza), [Nestabus](/saints/nestabus-of-gaza) and [Zeno](/saints/zeno-of-gaza) of Gaza, and with [Nestor](/saints/nestor-21-september), and says nothing more of him: its notice tells only of the three brothers, martyred under Julian the Apostate.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 21 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/1008/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
