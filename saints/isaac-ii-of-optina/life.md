# Isaac II of Optina

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps the hieromartyr Isaac of Optina on 26 December, the second day of the Nativity, and its line is «Прмч. Исаакия II, Оптинского (1938).» The reading behind it, life 4637, is titled «Преподобный Исаакий, Оптинский, священномученик» and names him in the world Ivan Nikolaevich Bobrikov, born in 1865 in the village of Ostrov in the Maloarkhangelsk district of the Oryol province, to a peasant family, and schooled in the village.

He came to the Optina hermitage at nineteen, in 1884, and stayed four decades. He was received into the brotherhood on 17 December 1897, clothed in the mantle with the name Isaac on 7 June 1898, and ordained hierodeacon that October. The reading calls him the last archimandrite of Optina, and the calendar’s name page for him — «Исаакий (Бобриков) Оптинский, архимандрит, сщмч.» — gives him that rank.

A hundred people were arrested in 1937 and shot, and twenty more on 16 December of that year. The reading says they were kept standing and sleepless under relieving interrogators, and that he answered shortly: «В состав подпольного монастыря я не входил...» The indictment was handed down on 25 December 1937 and the sentence of shooting on 30 December. It was carried out on «26 декабря/8 января 1938 года», the reading’s own double date, and the dead were buried secretly in woodland at the 162nd kilometre of the Simferopol road. The day page carries no troparion and no kontakion under this name; the three it prints belong to the day’s own feasts and to one of its icons.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 26 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261226.html); read 13 October 2026.*
