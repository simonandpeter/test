# Alexander of Rome

The holy hieromartyr Alexander lived in the second century and was elected bishop of Rome in the year 105. saint.gr begins his entry by correcting the name it has inherited: the Synaxarist of St Nikodemos and the Menaia call him Alexandrion, but no pope of Rome ever bore such a name, so, the page reasons, this is pope Alexander I. The historian Eusebius, reworking a notice of [Irenaeus](/saints/irenaeus-of-lyon), bishop of Lyon, makes him fifth in order among the bishops of Rome.

He was martyred, the page says, under the emperor Hadrian — which does not agree with the years 105 to 115 it puts in brackets round the pope it identifies him with, and the two readings are left here as the page leaves them. With him on the same day the calendar keeps [Eventius](/saints/eventius-companion-of-theodulus) and [Theodulus](/saints/theodulus-companion-of-eventius), presbyters of the Church of Rome, who its page for them says were martyred under Hadrian together with the bishop of Rome Alexander.

Of the martyrdom itself there is nothing: no charge, no judge, no manner of death, no relic and no hymn. The two lines the page keeps for him play on the rejected name — «Ῥώμης παλαιᾶς Ἀλεξανδρίων Πάπας, / Ἐδὲμ κατοικεῖ τὴν παλαιὰν Πατρίδα» — the pope Alexandrion of old Rome now dwells in Eden, the old homeland.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 16 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/16/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3902/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
