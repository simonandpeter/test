# Boris (Dernov)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 14 февраля ст. ст. in a line that names a priest and his three sons together and gives the year all four died: «Сщмч. Павла Дернова пресвитера и сыновей его мчч. Бориса, Григория и Симеона (1918).» He is one of the three sons, given in it as «Борис». The sons carry their own rank word, «мчч.», martyrs, over against the «Сщмч.», hieromartyr, that stands over their father, and the line says of them nothing but that they were his sons.

The father is [Paul (Dernov)](/saints/paul-dernov), an archpriest, and his brothers in the line are [Gregory (Dernov)](/saints/gregory-dernov) and [Simeon (Dernov)](/saints/simeon-dernov). The name link behind the line carries the calendar's own nominative with the surname, «Борис (Дернов), мч.».

The calendar prints no life page for any of the four and no hymn under any of their names; the troparia on the day page are for other saints it heads. Nothing further is written here, and in particular he is not identified with any saint of the same name whom the calendar keeps on another day. The line, its rank words, the surname and the year 1918 are the whole of what was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 14 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260214.html); read 3 October 2026.*
