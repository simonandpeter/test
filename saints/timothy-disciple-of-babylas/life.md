# Timothy, disciple of Babylas

Timothy is named on doxologia.ro's calendar for 24 ianuarie in his own line, and the life the site gives under that line is the life of his teacher: «Sfântul Mucenic Vavila din Sicilia, este pomenit împreună cu ucenicii săi, Timotei şi Agapie» — Babylas is remembered together with his disciples Timothy and Agapius. Of Timothy by himself the page says nothing beyond that he was one of the two disciples, and everything else it says of him it says of the three together.

[Babylas](/saints/babylas-of-sicily) was born at Antioch, gave away his inheritance when his parents died, and withdrew to the mountain, where Timothy and [Agapius](/saints/agapius-disciple-of-babylas) lived in stillness with him. When Babylas had been made a priest and the pagans began looking for a way to hand him over, the three went together to Sicily, where they brought many to the knowledge of God.

They were taken together by the magistrate, and together confessed Christ to be the true God. They were beaten with staves until their bodies bled, then carried about the city and struck in various cruel ways, to frighten the cities of Sicily and to satisfy their tormentors. The next day the three were cut down with the swords and thrown into a fire, which did not harm their bodies; and Christians took them up and buried them worthily on the island.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, with [Babylas](/saints/babylas-of-sicily) and [Agapius](/saints/agapius-disciple-of-babylas), on one page. saint.gr has Babylas withdraw to a hesychastērion with his two disciples after his parents' death, and it is on a missionary journey of his to Sicily that the governor there, having tried in vain to pull the saint and his disciples from the faith, put them to fearful tortures which proved unable to bend their fighting spirit or their faith, and at last slaughtered them with knives and threw their bodies into the fire. The distich the page prints for the two disciples together says of them only that they were plainly imitators of their Teacher: «Τον Aγάπιον συνάμα Τιμοθέω, Σαφώς μιμητάς οίδα του Διδασκάλου».

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 24 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/24-ianuarie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-mucenic-timotei), which is the life of Babylas and his two disciples; read 19 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 24 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/24/index.aspx) and [the life of the three of them](https://www.saint.gr/3516/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026.*
