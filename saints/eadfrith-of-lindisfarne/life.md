# Eadfrith of Lindisfarne

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives him two sentences. The line for 4 June reads «Άγιος Έντφριθος Επίσκοπος Λιντισφέϊρν ο εξ Ιρλανδίας», Saint Eadfrith, bishop of Lindisfarne, of Ireland, and the page behind it says this: Saint Eadfrith lived in the seventh and the eighth centuries after Christ; he was elected bishop of the city of Lindisfarne in the year 698; and he fell asleep in peace in the year 721.

So what is recorded of him is a see, two centuries, two years and a death in peace. The page says nothing of his birth or his schooling, nothing of the monastery of Lindisfarne, nothing of a predecessor or a successor, and nothing of any book or work of his; it prints neither verse nor hymn. The day's line calls him a man out of Ireland, and the entry does not repeat it or explain it; both readings are left as the source has them.
