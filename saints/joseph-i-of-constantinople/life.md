# Joseph I of Constantinople

saint.gr's calendar for 30 Οκτωβρίου keeps «Άγιοι Κλεόπας ο Απόστολος και Ιωσήφ Πατριάρχης Κωνσταντινούπολης», one line for two men who have nothing to do with one another, and the second of them is this Joseph, whom the page dates «(; - 1283)». He lived in the thirteenth century, it says. He became a hieromonk after the death of his wife, and he succeeded Germanus III on the patriarchal throne of Constantinople in 1268.

In 1275 he submitted his resignation, because the emperor Michael had signed at Lyon in France, without the will of clergy or people, the union of the Eastern Church with the papal one. On 31 October 1282 he returned to the throne, and made many laborious journeys to restore the Orthodox order of the Church. But in March 1283, worn out by those labours, he gave up his spirit to God.

The page gives him no birthplace and no year of birth, records no relics, and prints no hymn; the epigram over his name says only that Joseph, having shone greatly among patriarchs, has heaven for his tent with the patriarchs. It carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου».

*After saint.gr's calendar for 30 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/30/index.aspx) and [the notice he shares with Cleopas](https://www.saint.gr/2852/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
