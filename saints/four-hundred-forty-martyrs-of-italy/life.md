# The Four Hundred and Forty Martyrs of Italy

The Ohrid Prologue keeps four hundred and forty martyrs on this day and gives not one of them a name. It says that they were killed by the Lombards in Italy about the year 579, and that [Gregory the Dialogist](/saints/gregory-the-dialogist) writes about them. They did not all die in one place: in one place forty of them were beheaded, and in another the remaining four hundred, and the reason laid against both companies was the same, that they would not eat of what had been sacrificed to the idols.

Against the four hundred there was a second charge as well, which the Prologue states and does not explain further: they would not, after the custom of the pagan Lombards, dance around a goat's head that the heathen had offered in sacrifice to demons. That is the whole of the entry. It names no bishop, no judge and no city, gives no account of any single one of the four hundred and forty, and prints no hymn.

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar keeps the company on the Julian 2 March, the same day the Охридски пролог gives them, and is the second source read for them: «Мчч. 440 Италийских (ок. 579).» Its whole entry is two sentences. The Italian martyrs, four hundred and forty men, refused to take part in the service of idols and were cut down by the Lombards, a Germanic tribe, in the year 579. The calendar heads its own page «День празднования: 2 марта», a day of celebration rather than a day of memory, which is the form it uses for a company and for a feast.

The second sentence is the one thing it adds to what this folder carried, and it is a question as much as a gain. Among those who perished for the faith, it says, the names of the presbyter Sanctulus and the hermit Hospitius are known. Neither is identified here with any folder of this corpus: a name and a century are not an identification, the calendar gives neither man a see, a year or a place, and it prints no page for either. The day prints no hymn under the company’s name, and the year it gives, about 579, is the year the folder already records off the Охридски пролог.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 2. март ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-03-15&prolog=1); read 4 October 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 2 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260302.html) and [the entry](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life548.htm), read 5 October 2026.*
