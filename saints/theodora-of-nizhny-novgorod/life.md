# Theodora of Nizhny Novgorod

saint.gr gives her one sentence. The venerable Theodora, called Vassa or Vasilissa in the world, was princess of Nizhny Novgorod, which belonged to the district of the principality of Suzdalia; out of love for God she became a nun, and she fell asleep in peace in the year 1347. That is the whole of the notice, and the page's heading repeats the year beside her name.

It names no parents and no husband, no monastery and no monastic name beyond Theodora, and it says nothing of the years between her tonsure and her death. The page prints neither couplet nor hymn, and she is not the Theodora the same calendar keeps on this day among the women drowned with Leonides.
