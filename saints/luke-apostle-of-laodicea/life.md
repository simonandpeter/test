# Luke

Luke of the Seventy lived in the apostolic years and was made bishop of
Laodicea in Syria, where he ended his life spreading the gospel and caring
for his flock as a faithful servant of Christ. Which Luke he is, the
traditions do not agree on. The Greek sources say plainly that he is another
than the Evangelist — άλλος του Ευαγγελιστή Λουκά — and the Greek calendar
keeps him so on 10 September with [Apelles](/saints/apelles-the-apostle) and
[Clement](/saints/clement-apostle-of-sardis); Demetrius of Rostov and the
Slavic tradition after him take him for the Evangelist himself, who was also
of the Seventy. This entry follows the Greek calendar it was read from, and
records the disagreement rather than settling it. The three are kept
together again on 22 April, and the Russian calendar keeps them on the same
day of its own reckoning.
