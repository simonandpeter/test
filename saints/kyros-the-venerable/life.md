# Kyros

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 9 June reads «Όσιος Κύρος», the venerable Kyros, and the page behind it says only this: the venerable Kyros died peacefully. So what is recorded here is a name, the rank of a venerable — that is, a monastic — and a death that was not a martyrdom.

The couplet the page sets above that sentence is the only other thing it gives him, and it is about the image of God in a man: not having defiled the spiritual image, Kyros, you stood before God, in whose image you are. There is no century here, no country, no monastery, no companion, no wonder and no relic; there is no troparion. The heading above the entry glosses the name as kin to the word for lord, and names Cyrus the king of Persia of the sixth century before Christ, and that is the page's own furniture and not a word about this monk.
