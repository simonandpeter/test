# Minas, with David and John

saint.gr's whole account of the three is one sentence: «Οι Άγιοι Οσιομάρτυρες Μηνάς, Δαβίδ και Ιωάννης ήταν μοναχοί και τελειώθηκαν τοξευόμενοι» — the holy venerable-martyrs Minas, [David](/saints/david-with-minas-and-john) and [John](/saints/john-with-minas-and-david) were monks and were made perfect by being shot with arrows. The day's line adds nothing but the rank word, οι Οσιομάρτυρες.

Above it the page prints a couplet which is the only other thing said of them: three servants of God, abbas, slayers of the passions, are killed with arrows by slayers of men. Under each of the three names it sets a gloss on the name — Μηνάς from μήνη, the moon, the one bright in the dark as the moon is; Δαβίδ a Hebrew word, the beloved, loving and loved; Ἰωάννης the grace and favour of God towards men — and those glosses belong to the names and not to the men.

Nothing else is told. The page gives no century, no country, no monastery, no persecutor and no year; it does not say whether the three were of one house or only of one death, and it lists no relic and prints no hymn. Because the line names the three and enumerates them, this is one folder for each, and each says only this much.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/239/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
