# Euthymius of Jerusalem

saint.gr's calendar for 12 May names him and gives him no life. Its page says only this: saint Euthymius, Patriarch of Jerusalem, is not recorded by the compilers of the synaxaria or in the Menaia, and his memory is met with in the Hierosolymitan Kanonarion. The calendar line adds nothing beyond the see.

So nothing here dates him, names his century, or says how his patriarchate ended. The Greek calendar keeps a patriarch of Jerusalem of this name on 12 May, and one manuscript book of the Jerusalem church is the whole of its warrant; this folder carries that and no more.
