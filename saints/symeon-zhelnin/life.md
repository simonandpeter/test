# Symeon (Zhelnin)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him on 5 января old style and prints one line only: «Прп. Симеона Псково-Печерского (1960)» — the venerable Symeon of the Pskov Caves, 1960. The name link behind that line carries the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line itself leaves out, «Симеон (Желнин) Псково-Печерский, прп.»; and there the entry ends, for the calendar holds no life page for him and the day's hymns are the eve of Theophany's, not his.

So what is set down here is what the calendar sets down. A monk named Symeon Zhelnin of the Pskov Caves monastery is kept on this day as a venerable father, and the year given for his death is 1960, which places him among the elders of the Soviet century rather than among the ancients the rest of this day holds. Of his birth, his years in the monastery and his end the calendar says nothing here, and nothing has been supplied from elsewhere.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 5 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260105.html); read 3 October 2026.*
