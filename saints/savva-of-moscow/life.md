# Savva of Moscow

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 13 июня ст. ст. in one line with his teacher — «Прпп. Андроника (1395), Саввы (XV), иконописцев Московских.» — two venerable fathers called icon painters of Moscow, with the fifteenth century set against his name and no year. The name link behind «Саввы», name page 1896, gives «Савва Московский, прп.» and heads itself «День памяти: 13 июня» and no other day.

Its life page for him is short. It is headed «Преподобный Савва Московский», with «Преподобные Андроник и Савва Московские» standing over the text, and it makes him the disciple of [Andronicus](/saints/andronicus-of-moscow). Shining with many virtues, and honoured for them not by the common people only but by princes, he was set as abbot of the Спасо-Андроников monastery after his teacher's death. Of his many disciples a good number were made abbots of other monasteries and some became bishops. He went to the Lord in peace about the year 1410.

After his death, the page says, many who came with faith to his holy tomb received healing of sicknesses of soul and of body. One of his own disciples, the priest-monk Ефрем, was troubled by a demon of unchastity; he came with faith to the tomb and called on him in his prayers, and was soon healed. The calendar records nothing else of him, and nothing else is written here.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 13 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260613.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life6815.htm); read 9 October 2026.*
