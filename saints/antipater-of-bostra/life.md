# Antipater of Bostra

saint.gr says plainly that the synaxaria hold no biographical material for him, and that what it knows it knows from elsewhere — without naming where. From that elsewhere it gives three facts and no more: that he lived in the second half of the fifth century; that he wrote a work against the doctrines of Origen, which was lost and of which only a few fragments survive; and that two homilies of his are extant, on the Annunciation and on John the Baptist. He died in peace.

The see is the day's line rather than the notice: «Άγιος Αντίπατρος επίσκοπος Βόστρων», bishop of Bostra. The page names no place of birth, no council he sat at and no year, and prints no hymn for him; what it does print above the notice is the couplet of the synaxarion, «Tον Aντίπατρον πλούσιον Σώτερ δέχη, / Tων αρετών φέροντα κορβανάν μέγαν» — receive Antipater rich, O Saviour, bringing a great treasury of the virtues.
