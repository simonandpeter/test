# Matthew (Solovyov)

He is the second of the two laymen the Russian calendar's 30 September line calls «мчч.», martyrs with no rank in the Church and no office, after six presbyters, a protodeacon and a schemanun, and before the one laywoman; 1937 is printed once for all eleven. The day line gives him as «Матфея» and the site's index of names heads his page «Матфей (Соловьев), мч.». The other layman is [Alexis (Serebrennikov)](/saints/alexis-serebrennikov).

One of the six presbyters on the same line carries the same surname, [Peter (Solovyov)](/saints/peter-solovyov). The calendar does not say whether the two were related, and this folder does not guess. The index keeps him here and on 26 January with the company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and prints no birth, no trade, no charge and no place of death. The long lives are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line.

*After the Russian church calendar's [30 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260930.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13811.html); read 4 October 2026.*
