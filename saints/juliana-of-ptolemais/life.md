# Juliana of Ptolemais

Juliana and her brother [Paul](/saints/paul-of-ptolemais) were put to death under the emperor Aurelian (270–275) in the Phoenician city of Ptolemais. Paul was seized for making the sign of the cross as the emperor entered the city, and confessed Christ at his trial; Juliana, seeing her brother tortured, accused the emperor before everyone of injustice and cruelty, and was given over to the same torments. They were beaten, torn with iron hooks and burned on red-hot grills, and their endurance could not be broken; three of the soldiers who tortured them believed in Christ and died with them.
