# Theodore of Sanaksar

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 19 февраля ст. ст. in a line of four words: «Прп. Феодора Санаксарского (1791).» The rank word is «Прп.», venerable, which the calendar uses of a monastic, and the epithet is the house he is named for, Sanaksar; the year the line prints for his death is 1791. The name link behind the line repeats exactly that and adds nothing: «Феодор Санаксарский, прп.», with no surname and no second name.

The calendar prints no life page under his name and no hymn under it either: the two hymns the day page carries are the apostle Archippus's. Nothing further is written here — not where he was born, not what he was in the monastery, not how he came to be named for it. The line, the rank word, the epithet and the year 1791 are the whole of what was read, and in particular he is not identified with any saint of the same name whom the calendar keeps on another day.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 19 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260219.html); read 3 October 2026.*
