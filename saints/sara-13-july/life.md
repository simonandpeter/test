# Sara (13 July)

doxologia.ro's calendar names her and gives no life. Its line for 13 iulie reads «Sfânta Cuvioasă Sara», and the page the calendar links under that name carries the name again and nothing more: no life, no troparion, no year, no country, no monastery. What the Romanian calendar prints of her on this day is that she is a venerable mother and that she is called Sara, and this folder says no more than that.
