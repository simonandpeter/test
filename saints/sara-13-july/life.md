# Sara (13 July)

doxologia.ro's calendar names her and gives no life. Its line for 13 iulie reads «Sfânta Cuvioasă Sara», and the page the calendar links under that name carries the name again and nothing more: no life, no troparion, no year, no country, no monastery. What the Romanian calendar prints of her on this day is that she is a venerable mother and that she is called Sara, and this folder says no more than that.

The Greek calendar keeps her on the same day, 13 July, and gives her the sayings the Romanian line did not. saint.gr quotes Nicodemus the Hagiorite, who takes her from the Paradise of the Fathers: she said, if I were to pray God that all men should be satisfied in me, I should find myself repenting at every man's door; rather I shall pray that my heart be pure with all. Two great elders and anchorites once came to her from the parts of the mountain of Pelusium, saying to one another on the way, let us humble this old woman, and then said to her: see that your mind is not lifted up and you say, look, the anchorites are coming to me, a woman. And she answered them: by nature I am a woman, but not by my mind. Another time some of the Sketiotes came, and she set before them certain dishes that had been brought to her; they left the good food and ate the poor, and she said to them, truly you are Sketiotes. So great a contender was this blessed woman, the page says, that she sat sixty years above a river and never once bent down to look at it. The venerable Sara fell asleep in peace. Above the notice the page sets a distich: «Kόλπους υποσχών την νέαν Σάρραν δέχου, Ω της παλαιάς Aβραάμ Σάρρας άνερ» — opening your bosom, receive the new Sara, O Abraham, husband of the Sara of old.

The Serbian church keeps her on the same 13 July, and the Ohrid Prologue of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović is the one calendar of the three that gives her anything beyond a rank and a name. It heads her the Egyptian, and says that as a virgin she went out to the ascetic life and struggled for sixty years on the bank of the river Nile, not far from Alexandria; that by her own example she drew many women to the monastic life; and that she reposed in the Lord in 370. That year is the first date of any kind this folder has carried. The sixty years are a span and not a date, and nothing is reckoned back from 370 by them.
