# Achilles the Deacon of the Caves

saint.gr's notice for him is two sentences. The venerable Achilles lived as a monk in the fourteenth century in the Lavra of Kyiv; and he was ranked among the saints, together with other saints of the Lavra of the Caves of Kyiv, in the period after the Mongol invasion, by the Metropolitan of Kyiv and Galicia Saint Peter Mohyla, in the year 1643.

That is all. The day's line calls him the deacon of the Lavra of Kyiv, which is the only office the calendar gives him. Beyond that the page prints two etymologies of the name Achilles and two bearers of it out of Greek antiquity who are not saints. No year of his death, no birthplace and no hymn is printed, and nothing is supplied here.

The Russian calendar keeps him on 4 января old style and tells what his abstinence was. He struggled in the Kyiv Caves monastery and surpassed many ascetics in it: he ate nothing sweet and nothing cooked, and his ordinary food was one prosphoron in the week. He spent a long time in enclosure. Those who wish to be rid of gluttony and to learn abstinence have recourse to his intercession, and his relics rest in the Far Caves.

The day's kontakion says the same thing in the Church's own words — that he had a great abstinence truly, and showed much fasting, for scarcely once in the week would he eat, and that one small prosphoron, and wore himself out besides with standing all night at vigil. His troparion has him kiss the ascetic life and take great labours on himself from his youth, and then be brought to the step of the deaconate, in which he served blamelessly in the church of the Mother of God.
