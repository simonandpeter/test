# Baruch (21 October)

saint.gr's calendar for 21 Οκτωβρίου names Baruch and gives him no life. One sentence is the whole of the narrative — «Ο Όσιος Βαρούχ απεβίωσε ειρηνικά», he died in peace — and the couplet over it says that the divine Baruch, having left behind the weight of the body, pitched his tent there in a place of green pasture. The page glosses the name itself as the Hebrew for blessed.

It gives him no country, no monastery, no century, no companions and no hymn. He is entitled Όσιος, a venerable one, and not a martyr or a prophet.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 21 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/21/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2556/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
