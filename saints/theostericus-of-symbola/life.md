# Theostericus of Symbola

saint.gr's calendar for 10 Νοεμβρίου keeps the venerable Theostericus «ὁ ἐν Συμβόλοις», he of Symbola, and gives him two sentences. He lived in the time of the iconoclasts and contended for the holy icons; and he died in peace. The page adds that his memory is kept on 17 February as well, and that is the whole of the notice: no monastery named beyond the epithet, no emperor, no exile, no writings, no year and no hymn.

What stands beside it is the couplet of the synaxarion, which turns on his name as one set firm by God: «Θεοστήρικτος τὸν Θεὸν στήσας βάσιν, / Ὄντως θεοστήρικτος ἔργοις ὡράθη» — Theostericus, having set God for his foundation, was seen to be truly God-established in his works.

The Russian calendar keeps him on the same day and that is the whole of what it has: «Прп. Феостирикта , иже в Символех.» — four words, the rank, the name in the genitive and the place, with no year and no life page behind the name. What the name page adds is the name itself: it heads him «Феостирикт (Стирикт) Олимпийский (в Символах), прп.», giving a short form of the name, the mountain of Olympus beside the Symbola the day's line names, and three icons. No day but this one is listed.

A third calendar was asked for this folder, because it is one that nothing else can be added to: azbyka.ru, which five readers before this one had left unasked, answered once on 4 October 2026 and had no more than the others. Its line for 23 ноября is the same line in the same words, «прп. Феостирикта (Стирикта), иже в Символех», with the short form of the name in brackets as the Russian calendar's name page also gives it; there is no life page behind it, no year, and no hymn under his name among the many that day prints. Three pages have now been read for him — the Greek calendar's, the Russian calendar's and this one — and not one of them tells his story.
