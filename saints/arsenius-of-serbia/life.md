# Arsenius of Serbia

saint.gr's calendar for 28 Οκτωβρίου names «Άγιος Αρσένιος Αρχιεπίσκοπος Σερβίας», and the page behind that line says one thing only: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου» — we have no details of the saint's life. So the Greek calendar keeps an archbishop of Serbia on this day and tells nothing of him but his see and his rank, and nothing else on the page expands either.

There is no year on it, no birthplace, no account of his election, his rule or his death, and no hymn. The entry carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands, bare as it is. The Serbian calendar, which would be the one to say more, has not been read here, and its row on this folder stays undocumented.

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on the same day, names him by his numeral — «Свт. Арсения I, архиеп. Сербского (1266)» — and adds the year the Greek page did not. Its two short lives agree that he spent the greater part of his life as a monk in the monastery of Žiča, that he was consecrated archbishop of Serbia in 1233 for the strictness of his ascetic life, and that he died in peace in 1266 with his relics resting in the Peć house he founded. They do not agree how long he governed: one says thirty years of wise rule, the other thirty-three, and they also spell the monastery and his resting place differently. Nothing is chosen between them. His days there are two, this one and the Serbian 30 August, which is the synaxis of the Serbian saints.
