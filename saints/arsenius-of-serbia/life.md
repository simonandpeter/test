# Arsenius of Serbia

saint.gr's calendar for 28 Οκτωβρίου names «Άγιος Αρσένιος Αρχιεπίσκοπος Σερβίας», and the page behind that line says one thing only: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου» — we have no details of the saint's life. So the Greek calendar keeps an archbishop of Serbia on this day and tells nothing of him but his see and his rank, and nothing else on the page expands either.

There is no year on it, no birthplace, no account of his election, his rule or his death, and no hymn. The entry carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands, bare as it is. The Serbian calendar, which would be the one to say more, has not been read here, and its row on this folder stays undocumented.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/28/index.aspx) and [his notice](https://www.saint.gr/2828/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
