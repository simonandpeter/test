# Appion

saint.gr's whole notice of him is one sentence: the holy Appion was martyred by the sword. The day's line names him bare, «Άγιος Αππίων», and the page gives no country, no city, no year, no emperor and no judge, prints no life beyond that sentence and no hymn. Its heading gives the feast as fixed, kept on 26 July of every year, and the entry carries no asterisk.

Above the notice the page sets a distich, and that is the only other thing it prints of him: «Tον σον ξίφει τράχηλον ατρέπτως κλίνας, Tο της τελευτής Aππίων πίνεις πόμα» — bending your neck to the sword without flinching, Appion, you drink the draught of death. The page does not even gloss his name, as it does for most of the saints of this day.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2164/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
