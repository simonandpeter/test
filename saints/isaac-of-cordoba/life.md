# Isaac of Cordoba

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives him one sentence of life. The line for 3 June reads «Άγιος Ισαάκ ο εξ Ισπανίας», Saint Isaac of Spain, and the page behind it calls him a venerable martyr and says this: he lived in the ninth century after Christ and was born in the city of Cordoba in Spain, in the year 825; he was a notary, and later became a monk in a monastery near his birthplace; and he bore witness for the faith of Christ, in the year 852, at the hands of the Mohammedans.

So what is recorded of him is a city, two years, a profession he left, a monastery the page does not name, and a martyrdom whose manner it does not give. It says nothing of what brought him before his judges, nothing of a companion, and nothing of where he was buried; it prints neither verse nor hymn. The heading above the entry is a note on the name, that in Hebrew the word Isaac sounds like the word he laughed, and it is the page's own furniture.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/03/index.aspx) and [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/4417/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
