# Nicanor (Morozkin)

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 18 июня ст. ст. prints him in three words and a year: «Прмч. Никанора (1938).». The line gives the forename in the genitive and the class word «Прмч.», a venerable-martyr, and no surname. The name link behind his place in it supplies the calendar’s own nominative with the surname and the monastic rank, «Никанор (Морозкин), архимандрит, прмч.», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from.

The name page carries nothing else. It heads «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 26 апреля (Бут.) , 18 июня» and then stops: no life, no article, no monastery, no account of his death in 1938. The calendar’s line and that heading are the whole of what this source gives him, and nothing is supplied for them here — not a house, not an age, and not the manner of his death.

Of the three days his name page lists, two are synaxes: the new martyrs and confessors of Russia on 26 января, and the Бутово synaxis on 26 апреля, which the calendar marks «(Бут.)». Neither is a second commemoration of the man, and he renders on 18 июня ст. ст. only. The day page prints hymns for the martyrs its first line heads, for the canonarch of the Caves and for the Боголюбская icon, and none under his name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 18 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260618.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14274.html); read 9 October 2026.*
