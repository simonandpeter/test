# Pachomius, companion of Papyrinus

saint.gr's calendar for 13 Ιανουαρίου keeps two martyrs in one line, «Άγιοι Παχώμιος και Παπυρίνος», and the page behind the line gives them one sentence between them: «Οι δύο αυτοί Άγιοι μαρτύρησαν, όταν επνίγησαν από τους διώκτες τους σε ποτάμι» — these two saints were martyred when they were drowned in a river by their persecutors. It adds that there are no other details of their lives.

The couplet above it puts [Papyrinus](/saints/papyrinus-companion-of-pachomius) and Pachomius to the same end: such an end did Papyrinus find as thou, Pachomius, being cast into the water. The calendar names no river, no persecutor, no emperor and no year, and prints no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 13 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/13/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1414/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
