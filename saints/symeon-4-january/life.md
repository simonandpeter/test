# Symeon (4 January)

saint.gr says the venerable Symeon — the page gives a surname in Greek letters, Μολγιούμποφ — was born in the city it writes Τομπόλσκ in Russia. His love for the monastic life took his steps to the monastery of the holy Boris and Gleb in the city of Rostov Veliky. In 1672 he became hegumen of a monastery of Novgorod, and in 1674 of the monastery of the Saviour at Moscow. On 9 April 1676 he was ordained bishop of the sees the page writes Σμολένσκ and Ντορογκομπούζ. Marked out for the holiness of his life, he fell asleep in peace in 1699.

The Russian forms of his surname and of those places are not invented here, because saint.gr prints none; that is also why the English form of his name carries the day instead of the see. The page counts him in the synaxis of all the saints of Siberia and prints no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 4 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/04/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1278/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
