# Silvanus, companion of Bianor

The Romanian calendar gives Silvanus a line of his own on 10 iulie and sends it to a notice of four lines about two men. All that notice says of him is the last clause of it: that [Bianor](/saints/bianor-of-pisidia), who was from Pisidia in Asia Minor, was brought before Severian, governor of Euphratensis, confessed his faith in the Lord Jesus Christ with great courage, was put to many torments, and in the end received a martyr's death together with the martyr Silvanus.

The page gives him no country, no year, no manner of death and no separate account at all; he is named only as the man who died with Bianor. This folder exists because the calendar names him, and says no more of him than the calendar does.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 10 iulie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/10-iulie) and [the notice of the two martyrs](https://doxologia.ro/sfintii-mucenici-vianor-siluan); read 24 September 2026.*
