# Silvanus, companion of Bianor

The Romanian calendar gives Silvanus a line of his own on 10 iulie and sends it to a notice of four lines about two men. All that notice says of him is the last clause of it: that [Bianor](/saints/bianor-of-pisidia), who was from Pisidia in Asia Minor, was brought before Severian, governor of Euphratensis, confessed his faith in the Lord Jesus Christ with great courage, was put to many torments, and in the end received a martyr's death together with the martyr Silvanus.

The page gives him no country, no year, no manner of death and no separate account at all; he is named only as the man who died with Bianor. This folder exists because the calendar names him, and says no more of him than the calendar does.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 10 July, and names the two together as the Romanian one does. saint.gr says [Bianor](/saints/bianor-of-pisidia) came from the province of Pisidia and was arrested for confessing Christ by the governor of Euphratesia, which it says is now called Atzar and lies in Syria; that they hung him up and beat him without pity with wooden swords, and then burned him with red-hot iron pellets, so that he lost his teeth and his ears. Silvanus, who was standing by and watching that torture and that courage, believed and confessed Christ in front of all the idolaters, and on the spot they cut out his tongue and then his head. Bianor was blinded in the right eye, flayed on the scalp and beheaded last of all, and so the two received the crown of the contest together. The page gives no country for Silvanus, no year, and prints no hymn.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 10 iulie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/10-iulie) and [the notice of the two martyrs](https://doxologia.ro/sfintii-mucenici-vianor-siluan); read 24 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 10 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/10/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/699/saint.aspx), read 1 October 2026.*
