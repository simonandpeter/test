# Benjamin (Famintsev)

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him fourth of five presbyters on the one line it gives to fourteen people of 1938 for the Julian 1 March. Benjamin Ivanovich Famintsev was born on 19 January 1873 in the town of Kolomna in the province of Moscow, the son of the priest John Famintsev and of his wife Maria. He was educated at the Moscow theological seminary, which he finished in 1893, and in 1894 he went as psalmist to a church in the town of Klin.

In 1901 he was ordained deacon to a church in the village of Karpovo in the Bogorodsk district, and in 1906 priest to the Trinity church in the village of Troitskoye in the Bronnitsy district. From 1912 to 1917 he served in the church of the Transfiguration at Gari in the Dmitrov district; in 1918 he was moved to the church of the Exaltation of the Cross in Kolomna; and from 1919 he served in the church of the Nativity of the Mother of God in the village of Meshcherino in the Kolomna district. He was made archpriest in 1925 and given a cross with ornaments in 1931. His son Seraphim, ill from birth, died in 1934 aged twenty-five; his daughter lived apart and his wife had long been dead, and he lived alone.

In 1936 the Meshcherino village soviet forbade him to go round the houses of his parishioners with prayers. A certificate from the local clinic that there was no epidemic in the village had been needed for it, and from that year the clinic refused to give the church council one; without it the soviet would give no leave. The parish council asked that if they might not go with icons and crosses for people to venerate, they might go at least with a collecting box which nobody venerates, so that the parishioners could put in what they wished for the upkeep of the church after the priest had greeted them on the feast. That too was refused, on the ground of an epidemic of scarlet fever, when it was known that a few people had sore throats.

On 7 March 1936 the parish council petitioned the central authorities, writing that the income within the church was far too small because people working in the collective farms had not always time to come to it, so that the main income was from the services asked for — christenings and burials — of which there were not many either, and from going round the parish on the feasts. They had no answer. On 26 November 1937 the district newspaper «Вперед» printed a piece saying that the Meshcherino priest tried by every means to make friends with the collective farmers, and that whatever he read them he twisted to his own advantage.

He went to the head of the local post office to ask who had written it, so as to have it out with the man and ask on what facts it rested, and was told that this was a secret; and he waved his hand and said in his vexation that the Soviet power had nothing left to write about but the gathering of such complaints, and went out. That was one of the three charges. The others were a conversation at a funeral in which he was said to have praised Mussolini, which he did not remember, and the schoolmaster Chekalin, who had come up to him on the riverbank in the summer of 1937 and asked him whether there was a God, and talked with him two hours about the life of Christ, and asked for a Gospel, and after that began going to church.

He was arrested on 27 February 1938, held in the prison at Kashira and questioned on 2 March; he denied the charge. The investigation was closed on 6 March and on the 9th the troika of the НКВД sentenced him to be shot. He was shot on 14 March 1938 at the Butovo range outside Moscow and buried in a common unmarked grave. azbyka.ru keeps him on 14 марта and on two moveable days besides, the synaxis of the new martyrs of Butovo and the synaxis of the saints of Moscow, and neither is a day this corpus can hold.

On the grade the two hosts read differently and the reading is the calendar’s own index. The day’s line calls the five of them «пресвитеров» together and azbyka.ru heads him «пресвитер», while the name link behind his name reads «протоиерей», archpriest, which is what the office here records and what his own life says he was raised to in 1925.
