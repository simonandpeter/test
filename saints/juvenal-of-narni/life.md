# Juvenal of Narni

saint.gr names him in one sentence and gives no life. Juvenal was ordained bishop of Narnia by Damasus, bishop of Rome, whose years the page gives as 366 to 384; and having drawn many of the heathen to the faith of Christ, he fell asleep in peace in the year 377. That is the whole of the notice.

There is nothing else: no birthplace, no year of birth, no length of his episcopate, no account of a single conversion or of a wonder, no relic and no hymn. The day's listing spells his see Ναρνί, the modern Narni in Umbria, and the notice spells it Ναρνίας, the ancient Narnia; nothing beyond the name of the town is said about the place.
