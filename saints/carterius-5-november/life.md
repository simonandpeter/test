# Carterius (5 November)

Carterius is kept with Eupsychius in one entry of saint.gr's calendar for 5 Νοεμβρίου, and the page's whole account of the pair is one sentence. They lived in the years of the emperor Maximian, when the governor of Palestine was Urbanus — the page sets the year 298 against the governor's name — and they were martyred after their genitals were cut off, dying of bleeding that could not be stopped. There is no city, no trial, no burial, no year of their own and no hymn.

The couplet of the synaxarion is given to him and reads his name as endurance: «Αἰδοῖα Καρτέριος ἐκτετμημένος, / Σὺν Εὐψυχίῳ καρτεροψύχως φέρει» — Carterius, cut in his private parts, bears it stout-souled together with Eupsychius. The man kept with him is [Eupsychius](/saints/eupsychius-5-november).

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2893/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
