# Olympios the Eparch

saint.gr's calendar for 3 August names Saint Olympios the eparch, martyr, and gives him a single sentence. He was, the page says, a Byzantine lord in the reign of the emperor Heraclius, «επί της Βασιλείας του αυτοκράτορα Ηρακλείου (610 - 641 μ.Χ.)», who was put to death in prison by Chosroes, king of the Persians, for his confession of the Christian faith. The page glosses his name from the word for shining: the one who shines, the blessed one.

That is the whole of the entry. It gives him no city, no companions, no manner of his imprisonment and no year of his own beyond the reign it names, and it prints no hymn to him. Where the source has one line, the life says exactly that line.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/03/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2206/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
