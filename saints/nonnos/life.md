# Nonnos

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives him two sentences: «Η μνήμη του Αγίου Νόννου αναφέρεται στο Ιεροσολυμιτικό Κανονάριο σελ. 95. Γιορτάζεται από την Εκκλησία των Ιεροσολύμων στον ναό, που βρίσκεται στο ορός των Ελαίων.» — the memory of Saint Nonnos is mentioned in the Jerusalem Kanonarion, page 95; he is celebrated by the Church of Jerusalem in the church that stands on the Mount of Olives.

That is the whole of it, and it is a notice about a book and a church rather than a life. The page gives no century, no country, no rank and no manner of death, so nothing is said here of what he was. Its only other remark is a note on the name, that a Nonnos was the epic poet of the fifth century who wrote the Dionysiaca — which is the page's own gloss on the word and not a claim about this man. No hymn is printed for him.
