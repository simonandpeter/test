# Daniel of Shuzhgora

Daniel was born in the Moscow lands in the sixteenth century and laboured in the north of Rus’. He took monastic vows in the Komel monastery, founded by Cornelius of Komel in 1498, and then left it to go on alone as an ascetic in an empty, wooded place in the Beloozero country, on a hill called Shuzhgora. There he founded his own monastery, dedicated to the Transfiguration of the Lord, and he was buried in its church of the Transfiguration. In 1764 the monastery was turned into a parish.

A second, shorter life on the same calendar says the same and no more: that he came from the Moscow borders, was tonsured in the monastery of Cornelius, went out to the hill of Shuzhgora to found his own, and lived in the sixteenth century. The Russian calendar keeps him on 21 September old style and prints no hymn for him.

*After the lives printed by the Sretensky calendar (days.pravoslavie.ru), 21 сентября ст. ст. — [the first](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1575.htm) and [the second](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life6551.htm), with [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260921.html); read 17 September 2026.*
