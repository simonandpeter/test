# Sylvester, companion of Sophronius

saint.gr names the holy Sylvester with [Sophronius](/saints/sophronius-companion-of-sylvester) on this day and gives the pair of them one sentence, and the life says exactly that: «Οι Άγιοι Σιλβέστρος και Σωφρόνιος μαρτύρησαν δια ξίφους» — the holy Sylvester and Sophronius were martyred by the sword. No year, no country, no emperor, no judge and no relic stand on the page, and it prints no hymn.

The one thing the site adds is the distich over them, which names both and says only that the sword was easier to bear than the alternative: not enduring, Sylvester, to practise error, with Sophronius you endured the cutting. That is a turn of phrase about the choice and not a fact about either man.

The corpus keeps other men of this name, and none of them is on this day and none is named on this page; on the same Greek day, on a page of its own, stands a [Patriarch Sylvester](/saints/sylvester-the-patriarch) whose see the site says is not known, and nothing on either page connects the two.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3777/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
