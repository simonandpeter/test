# Zosimus the Soldier

saint.gr's calendar for 19 June names Zosimus and heads his page «(; - 98)». He belonged to the military ranks, the page says, and came from Apollonias of Sozopolis, when the king of Rome was Trajan, whose years it gives as 89 to 117. He received the Christian teaching, was baptised, and became one of the most fervent of Christians.

But in the year 98 Domitian, the eparch at Antioch of Pisidia and a hard enemy of the Church, ordered his death, having seen that no threat availed to drag him back to the worship of the idols. First they beat him and tore his sides, which they then burned with red-hot iron; and when these too brought no result, they beheaded him, and so he received the immortal crown of martyrdom.

The page prints an apolytikion and a kontakion that name him, a megalynarion that calls him a shining star risen out of Apollonias, and a kathisma; and its note on the name, that Zosimus comes from the verb to live and means the one full of life, is followed by a line saying that the name belonged to a Byzantine writer of the fifth century — a remark about the name and not about this saint, which is not taken into this life.
