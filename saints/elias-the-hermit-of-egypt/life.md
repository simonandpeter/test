# Elias of Egypt (8 January)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 8 января ст. ст. with one line, «Прп. Илии Египетского (IV).», and the page behind it holds a single sentence. Having taken the monastic habit, he struggled for seventy-five years on a desert mountain in a cave of rock, and he reposed in the fourth century, an old man of a hundred and ten years. That one sentence is the whole of the life the calendar prints.

Nothing else is set down there: no place within Egypt, no teacher, no monastery, no account of what those seventy-five years held. The name link behind the day's line gives the calendar's own nominative, «Илия Египетский, пустынник» — Elias of Egypt, a hermit — and the day page's hymns carry nothing under his name. Nothing further is written here because nothing further was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 8 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260108.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life175.htm); read 3 October 2026.*
