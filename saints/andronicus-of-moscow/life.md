# Andronicus of Moscow

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 13 июня ст. ст. in one line with his disciple — «Прпп. Андроника (1395), Саввы (XV), иконописцев Московских.» — two venerable fathers called icon painters of Moscow, with the year 1395 set against his own name. The name link behind «Андроника», name page 195, gives the calendar's own nominative, «Андроник Московский, игумен», an abbot, and heads itself «День памяти: 13 июня» and no other day.

The calendar prints no life page for him. What it does print under his name is hymns, and they are the only connected words it gives. His own troparion, «Ин тропарь преподобного Андроника Московского» of the first tone, says that he loved stillness from his youth, that he came to the hill of Маковец, and that he set up a monastery of the common life in the earthly city of Moscow — «обитель общаго жития устроил еси в земнем граде Москве».

The troparion he shares with [Savva](/saints/savva-of-moscow) adds that both of them from their youth settled «в пустыни ... Сергия великаго» and followed that manner of life, growing the fruits of humility and obedience, and that they were counted worthy to be «строители и первоначальники обетныя обители святителя Алексия». These are the hymns' words and not a life, and nothing is built on them here beyond what they say.

The life page the calendar gives for his disciple is the only prose on this day that touches either of them, and it opens by naming him «Ученик преподобного Андроника Московского» and saying that after his teacher's death he was set as abbot of the Спасо-Андроников monastery. That is the whole of what this calendar records of the teacher besides the year against his name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 13 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260613.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/195.html); read 9 October 2026.*
