# Gregory of Chryse Petra

saint.gr's calendar for 24 Νοεμβρίου names «Όσιος Γρηγόριος» and gives him one sentence: «Ο Όσιος Γρηγόριος ήταν από τα μέρη της Ανατολής και ήλθε και ασκήτευσε στη Χρυσή Πέτρα. Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Οσίου» — he was from the parts of the East, and he came and practised his asceticism at Chryse Petra, the Golden Rock, and we have no other details of his life.

The couplet set over the line says the same two things, that he grew out of Asia for a homeland and shone in the virtues after the manner of the sun. The page gives him no city, no monastery, no century, no year, no companions, no relics and no hymn, and it does not say where the Golden Rock is.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/24/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2990/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
