# The Twenty-eight Martyrs

saint.gr's calendar for 3 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιοι Εικοσιοκτώ Μάρτυρες», and the page behind that line says one thing only: the holy Twenty-eight Martyrs were martyred by fire. There is no city, no persecutor, no year, and not one of the twenty-eight names. The calendar keeps them as a company, and that is the whole of what it tells.

The epigram over them does the counting in two halves, and the site glosses both: they burn five twice and two twice — that is, fourteen — men, and with them they burn five twice and two twice again, fourteen more. So the twenty-eight of the heading are two fourteens on the page's own reckoning, and the page says nothing about what divided them.

Because the company is named nowhere, this is one folder and not twenty-eight, in the shape the corpus already uses for a company it cannot name. No hymn is printed. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands, bare as it is.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/03/index.aspx) and [their notice](https://www.saint.gr/2877/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
