# Basil (Kondratiev)

Basil Emelyanovich Kondratiev was born on 1 March 1887 in the village of Ginutyevo — now Genutyevo, in the Sergiev Posad district — in the Alexandrov district of Vladimir province. In the 1920s and early 1930s he lived at Zagorsk, now Sergiev Posad, and traded, and from 1926 to 1935 he was churchwarden of the church where Archimandrite [Maurice (Poletaev)](/saints/maurice-poletaev) served. On 21 October 1935 he was arrested for voicing “counter-revolutionary views” on the Soviet power’s policy and for anti-Soviet agitation, and held in the Butyrka prison, and he did not admit guilt.

On 8 January 1936 the NKVD’s special council sentenced him to three years of corrective labour, which he served in the Karaganda camp as a carter. In 1937 a new case was opened against him, charging that he gave his “flat” for church services; he did not admit guilt, and confirmed only that he had an icon. He was shot on 4 October 1937 on the decision of the Karaganda NKVD troika of 28 September, and buried in an unmarked common grave. The Jubilee Council of Bishops of 2000 glorified him.

The Russian calendar keeps him on 21 September old style with Maurice, of 1937, and azbyka.ru keeps him also with the new martyrs and confessors of Radonezh. It prints for the two the same troparion and kontakion as on Maurice’s page, given here with the site’s English. Its life is Abbot Damascene (Orlovsky)’s, from the state archive’s file and the Karaganda security service’s.
