# Theodore of Novgorod

The Greek calendar names him on 19 Ιανουαρίου as Theodore of Novgorod, and the page behind the line is one sentence long. It calls him a fool for Christ — «διά Χριστόν Σαλός» — and says that from a small child he was dedicated to God, and that he fell asleep in peace in the year 1392 in Russia. The page's own heading repeats that year and leaves the year of his birth blank.

So the day claims of him a city, a manner of life, and a year of repose. Nothing of what he did in Novgorod is printed, no miracle, no word of his, and no hymn. This folder records that much, and anything fuller would have to come from a source that has not been read here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 19 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/19/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1132/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
