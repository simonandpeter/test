# John (Smirnov) (5 August)

The calendar's line is all there is of him. For 5 августа ст. ст. it prints «Сщмч. Иоанна диакона (1939).» — the hieromartyr John the deacon, 1939 — and behind the forename the name link gives his surname and his order. There is no life page, no diocese, no parish, no place and no manner of death.

The corpus already holds three other deacons and priests of exactly this name and surname, on three other days of this calendar, and nothing read links any of the four. He is kept here under his day for that reason, and what the calendar records of him is a name, an order, a day and the year 1939, which is the latest year any entry of this day carries.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 5 августа ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260805.html); read 13 October 2026.*
