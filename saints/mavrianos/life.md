# Mavrianos

saint.gr's calendar for 27 Οκτωβρίου names Mavrianos and [Valentine](/saints/valentine-companion-of-mavrianos) together, and for a life it gives a citation rather than a story. Their memory, the page says, is honoured today according to the Jerusalem Kanonarion at page 116, without any other information about their lives. That is the whole of the page's own authority for the two of them, and the whole of the entry.

There is no century, no country, no city, no manner of death and no companions besides each other; the page prints no couplet for them, no relics and no hymn. It stands them on the same day as Nestor of Thessalonica and does not connect them with him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 27 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/27/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2665/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
