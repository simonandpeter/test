# Olga (Evdokimova)

The Russian calendar's line is all there is of her. It names her alone on this day, «Мц. Ольги (1938)» — the martyr Olga, 1938 — and the name behind that line reads «Ольга (Евдокимова), мц.», Olga Evdokimova, martyr. That is her surname, her rank and the year of her death, and the calendar gives nothing else.

There is no life page behind her name and no hymn for her on the day. The calendar gives her no monastic rank and no office, which is how it prints a lay woman killed for the faith. Nothing here says where she was born, where she lived or where she died; the year 1938 is the calendar's own and the only date it prints.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 28 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260128.html); read 3 October 2026.*
