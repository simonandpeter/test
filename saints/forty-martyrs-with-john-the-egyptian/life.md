# The Forty Martyrs with John the Egyptian

Forty Christians were beheaded with [John the Egyptian](/saints/john-the-egyptian)
by order of Maximian, in the year 295, and received the crown of martyrdom with him. The Greek
calendar keeps them with him on 20 September, and gives no names for them.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 20 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/979/saint.aspx); read 26 August 2026.*
