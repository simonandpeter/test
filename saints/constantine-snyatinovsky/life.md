# Constantine (Snyatinovsky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and carries no life. Its line for the twenty-third of March, old style, reads «Сщмч. Константина пресвитера (1918).», and the name page behind «Константина» is headed «Константин (Снятиновский), иерей, сщмч.», which gives the surname the line leaves out. That page lists the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church on «26 января» and this twenty-third of March.

Nothing else is said of him on either page: no place, no diocese, no parish, no year of birth and no account of how he died. The 1918 the line closes with is the whole of his chronology. The second source for the Russian new martyrs, azbyka.ru, was not read for him.

He is the earliest of the day’s nine new martyrs by thirteen years, and the only one of them the calendar puts in the first year of the persecution.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 23 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260323.html); read 8 October 2026.*
