# Anysia (Maslanova)

The calendar's own line is the whole of what is recorded here. The Сретенский monastery's calendar for 28 ноября ст. ст. names two priests, a deacon, two monastics and a laywoman in one clause closing on one year: «Сщмчч. Алексия , Алексия пресвитеров, Василия диакона, прмч. Рафаила , Викентия и мц. Анисии (1937).»

She stands sixth in it. The name page behind that place gives the surname the line leaves out together with the grade: «Анисия (Масланова), мц.» — a martyr, with no monastic grade and no office. The calendar carries no life page behind that name page and none on the day itself, and the day page's troparia all stand under other names.

So nothing is said here of where she was born, what she did, when or by whom she was arrested, or where she died: no page read says any of it. The year 1937 is the year the line closes on, and it is given for all six at once.

Her name page lists one other day, «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church. One row per church is the schema, so that day is recorded here and she does not render on it.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 28 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261128.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/10049.html); read 7 October 2026.*
