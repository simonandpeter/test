# Peter (Bogorodsky)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery gives him the last line of 24 октября ст. ст., and the whole of it is «Сщмч. Петра пресвитера (1938).» The line declines his name and gives it without a surname; the title on the name link behind it supplies both the surname and the grade, «Петр (Богородский), иерей, сщмч.».

His line is his own and not part of the two priests of 1937 above it: a year later, and a rank word in the singular. The line calls him пресвитера and the link иерей, which is the same grade, so this folder's office and its types say the same thing. His name page carries the day and the synaxis of the new martyrs and nothing else, and nothing on either page says where he was born or served, what he was charged with or when in 1938 he died.

His is the day's one real risk of a fold, and it does not arise. Eleven folders in this corpus store a bare «Петр» as their whole Russian name, because the calendar prints an unidentified twentieth-century martyr by his forename alone; the link behind this line gives the surname as well, so the fuller form is what this folder keeps and none of the eleven shares it.

The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line. The day page's thirteen troparia and kontakia belong to the four older entries it heads and to the icon it keeps, and none of them to him.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 24 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261024.html); read 4 October 2026.*
