# The Two Priests of Carthage

saint.gr's calendar for 7 December keeps two priests whom it counts and does not name. They were martyred at Carthage, the page says, in the years of the emperor Zeno, which it dates 474 to 476, and when the ruler there was Honorichos the Arian. Having arrested them, their persecutors put them to a martyr's death by sawing them in two.

The couplet set over the entry says that the two priests chose rather to be sawn through, and eagerly, than to abandon their own worship. That is the whole of the page: no names, no origin, no age, no relic and no hymn. The other entries of the same day call the persecutors at Carthage Monophysites rather than Arians, and that difference is the source's own and is left as it stands.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3191/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
