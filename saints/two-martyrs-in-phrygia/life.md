# The Two Martyrs in Phrygia

saint.gr's calendar for 26 January keeps two martyrs in Phrygia and names neither of them. The whole of what it prints about them is one sentence: that the two holy martyrs bore witness in Phrygia after tortures and savage beatings. There is no year, no reign, no city, no word about who they were or what they did before, and no troparion and no kontakion.

What the page adds is a couplet and a gloss on it: «Βάκλοις ἀθληταὶ τραυματισθέντες δύω, / Στεφθέντες εὗρον τὴν συνούλωσιν τάχει» — two contestants, wounded with the baklа, were crowned and quickly found their healing. The note explains the word: baklа are the sticks with which drums are struck. That is the one detail the source gives about how they suffered, and it is set down here as the page has it.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1169/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
