# Stratonicus

The Greek synaxarion says only that Stratonicus was martyred by the sword. Its verse plays on his name, the victor over the army: by his end by the sword he conquers alone the whole army of the demons. It gives no date and no place.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 30 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/1018/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
