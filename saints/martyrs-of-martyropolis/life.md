# The Martyrs in Martyropolis

saint.gr keeps them on the same line as the bishop who gathered them: «Όσιος Μαρουθάς ο επίσκοπος συν των Αγίων Μαρτύρων εν Μαρτυροπόλει» — the venerable [Marouthas](/saints/marouthas-of-martyropolis) the bishop, together with the holy martyrs in Martyropolis. What the page says of them it says through him: they are the martyrs who suffered in Persia, whose holy relics Marouthas asked of the Persian king Shapur the Third and was granted, and for whom he then built a city and named it after them — Martyropolis, the city of the martyrs, today Miefercin, in Greater Armenia by the river Nymphaeus — and in it he laid up their relics.

The page names not one of them and gives no number, no reign and no manner of death, so they are kept here as one entry. What it gives them of their own is a couplet, and the couplet is about their city's name: the calling is a work, it says, for the city named from martyrs, since many in it were martyred by the sword.

Their relics being brought to Martyropolis is a translation and not their martyrdom, and the page gives the martyrdom no date; so this folder carries none.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 16 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/16/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3725/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
