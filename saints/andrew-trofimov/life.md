# Andrew (Trofimov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and gives no life. Its line for 29 мая ст. ст. reads «Сщмч. Иоанна диакона и мч. Андрея (1938).» — the hieromartyr John, deacon, and the martyr Andrew, of the year 1938 — and each name carries a link to a name page and neither to a reading. The page behind his adds his family name, Трофимов, and nothing else: its one line is «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 29 мая».

He is given no church rank anywhere, which is what marks him out from the man the line names before him: a layman killed in 1938. The second of his two days is not a feast of his own but the general commemoration of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which this calendar prints beside the proper day of each of them.

The line names the two of them together and gives them one year, and that is the whole of what joins them on the page: there is no «with him», no place and no story, and no folder in this corpus has been made to say that they died together. Nothing else about him was found, and azbyka.ru, which is where an account of him would be, was not read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 29 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260529.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14462.html), which name him and give no life; read 8 October 2026.*
