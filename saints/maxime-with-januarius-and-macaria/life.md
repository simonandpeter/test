# Maxime, with Januarius and Macaria

saint.gr's whole account of her is one sentence, and it is about three people at once: «Οι Άγιοι Μάρτυρες Ιανουάριος, Μαξίμη και Μακαρία μαρτύρησαν στην Αφρική» — the holy martyrs [Januarius](/saints/januarius-with-maxime-and-macaria), Maxime and [Macaria](/saints/macaria-with-januarius-and-maxime) were martyred in Africa. The page heads her «Αγια Μαξιμη η Μαρτυρας» and glosses the name in its masculine form, Μάξιμος, from the Latin maximus, the greatest.

That is all. The page names no city in Africa, no emperor and no governor, gives no year, says nothing of how she died, and does not say whether the three were kin, or servants of one household, or only fellow sufferers. It lists no relic and prints neither couplet nor hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1843/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
