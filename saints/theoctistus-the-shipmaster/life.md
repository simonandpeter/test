# Theoctistus the Shipmaster

Theoctistus the shipmaster was seized in the persecution of Decius by the governor Valerian at Alexandria, harshly tortured and beheaded — his head cut off, the verse says, he steers the ship of his soul to the pole. The Greek synaxarion keeps the martyrs of Alexandria under Decius in separate entries on 6 September; the Russian calendar counts them as the eleven with the presbyter Faustus and the deacon Abibus.
