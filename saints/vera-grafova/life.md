# Vera (Grafova)

The calendar’s own line is the whole of what is recorded of her here. The Сретенский monastery’s calendar for 2 декабря ст. ст. names her second in a clause of two: «Сщмч. Димитрия пресвитера и прп. Веры исповедницы (1932).» She is given the grades «прп.» and «исповедница» — venerable and confessor — and not a martyr’s grade, which is why this folder records no martyrdom; the year beside her name is the year of her death and the line does not say she was killed.

The name page behind her place gives the surname the line leaves out and repeats the two grades: «Вера (Графова), прп., исповедница». There is no life behind it, and the day page carries no hymn under her name. The line pairs her with [Demetrius (Blagoveshchensky)](/saints/demetrius-blagoveshchensky), and nothing read makes the two of them companions beyond the clause and the shared year.

So nothing is said here of where she was born, in what house or under what rule she lived, what she confessed or where, or how and where she died. This calendar gives her the surname, the grade, the day and the year, and that is all.

Her name page lists one other day, «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church. One row per church is the schema, so that day is recorded here and she does not render on it.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 2 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261202.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14441.html); read 7 October 2026.*
