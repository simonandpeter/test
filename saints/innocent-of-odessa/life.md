# Innocent of Odessa

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 25 mai reads «Sfântul Ierarh Inochentie al Odessei» — the holy hierarch Innocent of Odessa — and his own page on the site carries that line as heading and title, the feast date, and a tab of icons, but no Viață and no Tropar. Nothing on the page says when he lived, what see he held besides the city the name gives him, or how he died.

What the calendar's words say is held here and nothing more: that the Romanian church keeps a hierarch called Innocent of Odessa on 25 May. The rank word «Ierarh» is the whole of what is said about his office. The corpus keeps another Innocent, the hierarch of Moscow, who is a different man on a different day and has not been identified with this one. No year is claimed here, and no other calendar has been checked.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 25 mai — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/25-mai) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-ierarh-inochentie-al-odessei), which carries no life and no troparion; read 24 September 2026.*
