# Hilarion (Gromov)

The Russian calendar names him with a schemanun in one line for 28 September: «Прмч. Илариона, прмц. Михаилы (1937)» — Hilarion the venerable-martyr and Michaela the venerable-martyr, 1937. The two prefixes matter: «прмч.» and «прмц.» mark monastics killed for the faith, not clergy and not laymen, and the calendar's index of names makes him «Иларион (Громов), иеромонах, прмч.», a hieromonk.

That the calendar keeps the two of them in one line is all it says about how they were connected, and it is enough to put [Michaela (Ivanova)](/saints/michaela-ivanova) on this page. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which refused this reading on 4 October 2026, so a monk and a nun of 1937 have a line between them and nothing else.

*After the Russian church calendar's [28 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260928.html) and its [index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13807.html); read 4 October 2026.*
