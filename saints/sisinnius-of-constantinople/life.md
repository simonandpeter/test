# Sisinnius of Constantinople

saint.gr's calendar for 11 Οκτωβρίου keeps three patriarchs of Constantinople in one entry, and Sisinnius is the third of them. He took the patriarchal throne of Constantinople at the beginning of 426, the page says, succeeding the patriarch Atticus; and before his election he had been carrying out the duties of a presbyter at Elaia. He was famous for his virtues and for his tireless efforts in the care of the poor.

His ordination and installation were carried out by a synod convoked by Theodosius II. As patriarch, the page says, Sisinnius I continued his works of charity and was shown to be a tender father of the poorer classes. He was patriarch one year and ten months, and fell asleep in peace at the age of eighty-seven; the head of the page gives his death as 427. Its distich reads «Ἀσινὲς ἦραν πνεῦμα τὸ Σισινίου, / Οὐ συνιασθὲν μηχαναῖς τῶν δαιμόνων» — they took up the spirit of Sisinnius unharmed, not hurt by the contrivances of the demons.

The two kept with him are [Nectarius](/saints/nectarius-of-constantinople) and his brother [Arsacius](/saints/arsacius-of-constantinople), each with his own paragraph on the same page. saint.gr prints no hymn under the entry. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/11/index.aspx) and [the life the three patriarchs share](https://www.saint.gr/2086/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
