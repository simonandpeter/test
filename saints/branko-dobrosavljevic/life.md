# Branko (Dobrosavljević)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and gives no life. Its line for 24 апреля ст. ст. reads «Сщмч. Бранко пресвитера (1941).» — the hieromartyr Branko, presbyter, of the year 1941 — and the line carries a link to his name and none to a reading. The name page behind it prints his family name, Добросавлевич, and writes his grade out as «иерей», priest, beside the abbreviation «сщмч.», hieromartyr; it gives the one day, 24 апреля, and nothing else at all.

So the calendar sets down a Serbian priest killed in 1941 and no more than that. The rank of new martyr was deliberately not written into this folder. The Russian calendar marks its own twentieth-century martyrs by giving them a second day, the general commemoration of the new martyrs and confessors on 26 января, and his page carries no such day: he belongs to another local church’s roll of 1941, and the Russian keeps him without claiming him for that synaxis.

The English name this folder goes by renders the surname in the Serbian spelling it belongs to rather than transliterating the Russian letters back. That is a choice and it is recorded as one. The Ohrid Prologue, which is where his own church’s account of him would be, was not read, and the Serbian row of his attestation says so.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 24 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260424.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14508.html), which name him and give no life; read 8 October 2026.*
