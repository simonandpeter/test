# Eugenia (Domozhirova)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names her on 5 января old style and prints one line only: «Мц. Евгении (1933)» — the martyr Eugenia, 1933. The name link behind that line carries the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line itself omits, «Евгения (Доможирова), мц.»; and there the entry ends, for the calendar holds no life page for her and prints no hymn under her name.

So what is set down here is what the calendar sets down. A woman named Eugenia Domozhirova is kept on this day as a martyr, and the year given for her death is 1933. The calendar gives her no rank in the Church and no monastic name, so she is recorded simply as a martyr; of her birth, her home, her arrest and her end it says nothing, and nothing has been supplied here from any other source.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 5 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260105.html); read 3 October 2026.*
