# Ephraim of Russia

The Greek synaxarion keeps the venerable Ephraim from Russia, the wonderworker, on 26 September, and says that it has no details of the Russian saint’s life. Its heading gives his years as 1412–1492. That is all it prints.

The Russian calendar names him properly, where the Greek had only «ο εκ Ρωσίας» — «Прп. Ефрема Перекомского, Новгородского (1492)», Ephraim of Perekom, of Novgorod, with the year the Greek heading also gives. Its life page fills in the rest. He was born on 20 September 1412 at Kashin and baptised Eustathius, the son of Stephen and Anna, who lived near the Kashin convent of the Dormition; drawn to solitude, he left home young for the Trinity monastery at Kalyazin, and when his parents came to fetch him he talked them into taking the monastic habit themselves, which the page says they did, ending their lives as solitaries.

After three years there a revelation moved him to the monastery of [Sabbas of Vishera](/saints/sabbas-of-vishera), where he was tonsured Ephraim in 1437, and a second sent him out: with Sabbas's blessing he went in 1450 to Lake Ilmen, to the mouth of the river Verenda, and set a cell on the bank of the Chernaya. An elder named Thomas came with two monks, then others, and at their asking he was ordained at Novgorod by [Euthymius of Novgorod](/saints/euthymius-of-novgorod), whom the page dates «(+ 1458; память 11 марта)». He built a church of the Theophany on the island at the Verenda's mouth, and dug a channel through to Lake Ilmen to bring the house its water — which is where the name Perekopskaya, then Perekomskaya, comes from. A stone church of St Nicholas followed, finished in 1466 with masons sent by the Grand Prince at his request. He died on 26 September 1492 and was buried in it. In 1509 the floods drove the monastery to move, and the page says Ephraim appeared to the abbot Roman and named the place: Klinkovo. His relics were carried there on 16 May 1545, and the council of 1549 settled his commemoration.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 26 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2257/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026; and the Russian church calendar's [26 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260926.html), read 4 October 2026.*
