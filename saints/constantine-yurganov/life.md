# Constantine (Yurganov)

The Russian church calendar's line for 2 November is the whole of what is recorded of him here: «Сщмчч. Константина и Анании пресвитеров (1918)» — the hieromartyrs Constantine and Ananias, presbyters, with the year 1918 closing the line for both. Its index of names gives the surname and the grade the line withholds, «Константин (Юрганов), иерей, сщмч.», a priest, and lists this day and the 26 January synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia against his name.

There is no life page behind that name, and the name page itself carries nothing but the two days. So this folder records the year of his death, his grade, the day the Church keeps him on and nothing else. azbyka.ru, which has carried long lives for others of these years, is a quota this corpus has spent and was not asked again on 4 October 2026.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 2 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261102.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9664.html); read 4 October 2026.*
