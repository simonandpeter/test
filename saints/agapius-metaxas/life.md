# Agapius Metaxas

The founders of the sheer hermitage at the ends of the Mesa Vouno, between the bays of Perissa and Kamari on Thera, saint.gr says, are the Kollyvades elders Agapius and [Porphyrius](/saints/porphyrius-mindrinos). Agapius Metaxas came of Constantinople, and in 1822 he arrived on Santorini from the Holy Mountain in rags, and became the evangelist of the island by his pure and guileless preaching.

At first he practised his asceticism in a cave at Monolithos, and the hermitage there turned at once into a spiritual spring for those who thirsted for the true Word and for prayer. His fame reached the monastery of the Prophet Elijah, where Porphyrius Mindrinos was then living and wanting a desert life of his own; the two met, and in the end Porphyrius followed him.

Later the two took refuge in the Kollyvades monastery of the Prophet Elijah on Hydra, where Porphyrius was initiated into the Kollyvadic spirit, and from there, with the blessing of its abbot, they returned to Thera and the hermitage of Monolithos. Afterwards they moved to Gonia and served their offices in the church of the Transfiguration, and in those years they began to build their trackless hermitage above the little bay of Kioni, turning the one at Gonia into a monastic training ground for women.

Agapius fell asleep in the year 1844 and rests in that hermitage. The people charged the iconographer Merkourios Sigalas with making two icons of him, one set on his grave and the other in the church of Saint Nicholas on the shore at Kamari. Three years after his repose his body was taken up by the representative of the monastery of the Prophet Elijah on Hydra, the elder Kyprianos Ntakoutros, who came to Thera for the purpose and carried the relics to the monastery of his repentance. The page ends by saying that the two Kollyvades are honoured together on 18 August.
