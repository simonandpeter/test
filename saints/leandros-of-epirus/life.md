# Leandros of Epirus

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 2 June reads «Άγιος Λέανδρος ο μάρτυρας ο Ηπειρώτης», Saint Leandros the martyr, the Epirote, and the page behind it says of him only this: we have no details of the life of the saint. So what is recorded here is a name, a rank and a country — that he was a martyr and that he came from Epirus — and nothing else at all.

The page's heading for the name is a lexicon entry rather than a life: it derives Λέανδρος from λαός and ανήρ, the brave man of the people, the young hero, and then names the Leander of the old story, the youth from Abydos who drowned crossing the Hellespont by night to reach Hero at Sestos. That is the page's own furniture and has nothing to do with the martyr. There is no year, no reign, no persecutor and no manner of death; there is no troparion. He is not to be confused with Leander of Seville, whom this corpus keeps already.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 2 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/02/index.aspx) and [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/447/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
