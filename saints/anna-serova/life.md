# Anna (Serova)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names her in a line of four and gives no life. Its line for 31 июля ст. ст. reads «Cщмч. Иоанна пресвитера, св. Константина исп., пресвитера, мц. Анны и св. Елисаветы исп (после 1937).» — with [John (Rumyantsev)](/saints/john-rumyantsev) a presbyter who died a hieromartyr, [Constantine (Razumov)](/saints/constantine-razumov) a confessor and presbyter, and [Elizabeth (Rumyantseva)](/saints/elizabeth-rumyantseva) a confessor. Behind the line the calendar's own name page gives her name in full as «Анна (Серова), мц.»

The calendar calls her a martyr, and gives her no rank in the Church and no monastic name, so she is one of the laywomen of those years. The year is given as «после 1937», after 1937, which is a lower bound and not a year of death.

Nothing else is printed. The calendar names no place, no parish and no manner of death for any of the four, and carries no life page and no hymn for them. The forename, the surname and that bound are the whole of what it keeps of her.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 31 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260731.html); read 13 October 2026.*
