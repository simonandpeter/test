# Basil (Smolensky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and gives no life. Its line for 30 мая ст. ст. reads «Сщмч. Василия пресвитера (1942).» — the hieromartyr Basil, presbyter, of the year 1942 — and the line carries a link to his name and none to a reading. The name page behind it adds his family name, Смоленский, and writes his grade as «протоиерей», archpriest, where the day line had said presbyter. Beyond the title it carries one line, «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 30 мая», and nothing further.

So the calendar sets down an archpriest of the Russian church killed in 1942, and no more than that. The second of those two days is not a feast of his own: it is the general commemoration of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which this calendar prints beside the proper day of each of them, and because both his pages call him a martyr that marker is the ground on which the rank of new martyr stands in this folder.

The surname is a family name and not a place, though the letters are also the city’s: no folder in this corpus carries it as a surname, and the three that carry the letters at all carry them as Smolensk. Nothing else about him was found, and azbyka.ru, which is where an account of a parish priest of 1942 would be, was not read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 30 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260530.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14268.html), which name him and give no life; read 8 October 2026.*
