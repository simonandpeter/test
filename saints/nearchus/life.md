# Nearchus

saint.gr says one thing about Nearchus and then says that it is guessing about the rest. The one thing is that «Ο Άγιος Μάρτυς Νέαρχος τελειώθηκε διά πυρός» — the holy martyr Nearchus was perfected by fire. The guess is this: «Εικάζεται ότι ήταν φίλος του Αγίου Μάρτυρα Πολυεύκτου», it is surmised that he was a friend of the martyr [Polyeuctus](/saints/polyeuctus-of-melitene), whom the calendar keeps on 9 January.

And the page draws out the consequence as a condition, not a fact: «Εάν θεωρηθεί ότι αυτό είναι αληθές, τότε ο Άγιος Νέαρχος πρέπει να μαρτύρησε κατά τους χρόνους των αυτοκρατόρων Δεκίου (249 - 251 μ.Χ.) και Ουαλεριανού (251 - 259 μ.Χ.)» — if that be held true, then Nearchus must have been martyred in the years of the emperors Decius and Valerian. Because the years rest on the surmise, no years are recorded in this folder.

Its couplet is the last thing the page gives him, and it turns on the fire: of the burning divine longing towards thee, O Saviour, Nearchus said, not even fire shall part me. Of his country, his rank, his judge and his relics the page says nothing at all, and it prints no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 22 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/22/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1351/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
