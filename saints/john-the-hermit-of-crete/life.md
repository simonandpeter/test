# John the Hermit of Crete

saint.gr's calendar for 7 Οκτωβρίου keeps one entry for ninety-nine ascetics of Crete: «Όσιος Ιωάννης ο Ερημίτης και οι συν αυτώ Eνενήντα Oκτώ Θεοφόροι Πατέρες που ασκήτευσαν στην Κρήτη» — John the Hermit, and with him the ninety-eight God-bearing Fathers who lived the ascetic life in Crete. The page says that only Saint Nicodemus mentions these ascetics, in his synaxaristes, and that a service for them was published at Herakleion in 1879.

Following that service, it says, all ninety-nine fell asleep in peace at the same time. They came from various parts of the Mediterranean basin and lived as ascetics in Crete. The chief of them was called John, and he is honoured locally in Crete. The ninety-eight are named nowhere on the page, so they stand here as the company they are called and not as ninety-eight folders; this one is the one man the page names.

saint.gr gives no year for John and none for the company, and no place in Crete more exact than the island. It prints one apolytikion, in the fourth tone, which names no person at all but sings the whole numerous choir. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/07/index.aspx) and [the life John and the ninety-eight share](https://www.saint.gr/2698/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
