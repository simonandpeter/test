# Alexander (Smirnov)

He is named on 25 September with another priest, and the line is about their bones rather than their deaths: «Обретение мощей сщмчч. Александра и Феодора пресвитеров (1985)» — the finding of the relics of the hieromartyrs Alexander and Theodore, presbyters, in 1985. The calendar's index of names gives his surname, «Александр (Смирнов), иерей, сщмч.», a priest, and marks this day as the finding; it keeps him also on 1 November and on 26 January with the new martyrs and confessors of Russia.

That is the whole of what this day says. It prints no year for his birth, and 1985 belongs to the finding and not to him. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which refused this reading on 4 October 2026.

The other day his name page lists, the Julian 1 November, was read afterwards, and it is the day of the death rather than of the bones. Its line is «Сщмчч. Александра и Феодора пресвитеров (1918)» — the hieromartyrs Alexander and Theodore, presbyters, 1918 — the same pair under the same two grades, with the year the September line withholds. So this folder now carries a death year, and nothing else: there is no life page behind either day, and no year of birth anywhere. One row per church is the schema, so the row stays on the day it was written for and 1 November is recorded in its note.
