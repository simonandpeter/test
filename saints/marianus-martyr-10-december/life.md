# Marianus (10 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 10 December names Marianus and gives him one line of life: the holy Marianus was martyred by stoning. The page prints no country, no reign, no persecutor and no year, and it joins him to no other name on the day. What it adds is the couplet set over his name, which turns the stone that broke him into the crown he got.

Marianus, his head crushed with a stone, is bound about with it spiritually by a divine crown. That is the whole of the entry. The corpus keeps two other men of this name on other days, and nothing here joins this one to either of them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/10/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3218/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
