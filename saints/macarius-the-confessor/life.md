# Macarius the Confessor

Doxologia's calendar names Macarius for 19 February and gives no life. His page carries the line «Sfântul Cuvios Mărturisitor Macarie», the date, and the readings appointed for his day — the Apostle from Galatians on the fruit of the Spirit, and the Gospel from Matthew, Come unto me, all ye that labour — and there is no life tab, no troparion tab and no notice of any kind.

What the line itself says is that he is kept as a venerable one and as a confessor: a monk, that is, who suffered for the faith and was not killed for it. Where he lived, under whom he confessed and what was done to him, this source does not print, and this entry does not supply it. Doxologia keeps another confessor, Eugenius, on the same day and prints as little for him.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 19 februarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/19-februarie) and [the page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-marturisitor-macarie); read 19 September 2026.*
