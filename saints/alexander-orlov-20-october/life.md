# Alexander (Orlov) (20 October)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 20 октября ст. ст. in a line that names eleven people together: «Сщмчч. Зосимы, Иоанна, Иоанна, Иоанна, Николая, Леонида, Иоанна и Александра пресвитеров, Михаила и Петра диаконов и мч. Павла (1937).» He stands eighth in it, as «Александра», under the line's own rank words — «Сщмчч.», hieromartyrs, over the eight presbyters and the two deacons, and «мч.», martyr, over the layman at the end. The title on the name link behind his place carries the surname the line leaves out, and his grade with it: «Александр (Орлов), иерей, сщмч.» — a priest, иерей.

Four of the eight presbyters in the line are Johns and the surname on the name link is the only thing that tells them apart; the line itself prints «Иоанна» three times running and once more after Leonid. The calendar carries no life page under his name, and the four hymns the day page prints are for two other saints it heads, neither of them in this line.

The corpus already holds an «Александр (Орлов)» killed in the same year and kept on 30 сентября ст. ст., and the two are not the same man. That line's name link reads «Александр (Орлов), протоиерей, сщмч.» and points to index page 14482, where this one points to 9438 — an archpriest against a priest, two commemorations on one calendar, which by this corpus's settled reading is two men. The day in this folder's display name is what keeps them apart; the Russian form the calendar gives is the same for both, and that is recorded as a deliberate fold rather than as a duplicate.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 20 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261020.html); read 4 October 2026.*
