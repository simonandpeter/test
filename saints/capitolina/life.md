# Capitolina

saint.gr's calendar for 27 Οκτωβρίου names Capitolina and Erotiis together and tells one story for them. They lived in the time of the emperor Diocletian, the page says, when Zilikinthios was governor in Cappadocia, about the year 289. Capitolina was of noble birth and rich, but gave no weight at all to earthly riches: she shared out her whole property among the poor and set her slaves free, and then presented herself before the governor Zilikinthios and confessed her faith in Christ. He ordered her shut in the prison, and the next day she was beheaded.

[Erotiis](/saints/erotiis-companion-of-capitolina), who had been Capitolina's slave, snatched up stones and threw them at the governor; and he, enraged, ordered his guards to beat her mercilessly with rods. But by the grace of Christ, the page says, she stayed unharmed, so that the governor then ordered her beheaded with the sword; and thus both of them found a martyr's death. The couplet above the entry says that they kill a slave and a mistress by the sword, both of them slaves of the Trinity, the true Mistress.

The Russian calendar keeps the two of them on the same day, in a shorter telling that differs from the Greek one at three points and is left to differ. Of a rich and noble family, she gave her whole property away to the poor and set her slaves free; and then, this page says, she was seized **as a Christian** and shut in prison, where the Greek page has her come before the governor of her own accord. For refusing to sacrifice to the idols she was beheaded. The governor is not named here and the province is in her name instead, «Капитолина Каппадокийская».

The third difference is the year, and it is not settled here. This page closes «Святые мученицы пострадали в 304 г.»; saint.gr dates them to about 289, from the reign of Diocletian and the governor it names. Both readings are now in the date, and neither is chosen. The name page adds one thing more, the teaser of an article it links on Православие.Ru, which reports her answer when the governor wanted to spare so well-born a woman a public torture: that for her family there was no greater honour than to bring up martyrs, preachers of the faith and teachers in divine wisdom. It is a teaser and not the calendar, and the article itself was not followed.
