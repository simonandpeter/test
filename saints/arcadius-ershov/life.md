# Arcadius (Ershov)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 21 октября ст. ст. as the second of five, in the line «Сщмчч. Павлина , архиеп. Могилевского, Аркадия , еп. Екатеринбургского, и с ними Анатолия и Никандра пресвитеров и мч. Киприана (1937)». The line gives him his see and the rank word «Сщмчч.», hieromartyrs, and the title on the name link behind his place adds the surname: «Святитель Аркадий (Ершов), епископ Екатеринбургский, сщмч.»

The see is the one thing the calendar says about him beyond the name, the rank and the year, and it is a diocese and not a commemoration, so it is recorded as his office. The company he is in is headed by an archbishop, [Paulinus (Kroshechkin)](/saints/paulinus-kroshechkin), who was shot that day with a group of clergy in the Kemerovo camp; the calendar carries a life for him and for none of the other four, and says nothing about where the five died or whether they died together.

Nothing further is written here — not a birth, not an earlier see, not a charge, not a place of death. The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 21 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261021.html); read 4 October 2026.*
