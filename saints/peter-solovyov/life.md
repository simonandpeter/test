# Peter (Solovyov)

The day line on the Russian 30 September names two Peters among its six presbyters, and they are two men rather than one printed twice: the site's index of names gives them separate pages and separate surnames, 9390 for Pushkinsky and 9391 for Soloviev. This is the second of them, headed «Петр (Соловьев), протоиерей, сщмч.» — Peter the hieromartyr, archpriest — and the first is [Peter (Pushkinsky)](/saints/peter-pushkinsky).

A third Soloviev is on the same page, [Matthew (Solovyov)](/saints/matthew-solovyov), one of the three lay martyrs of the eleven; the calendar says nothing about whether the two were related and this folder does not guess. Beyond the surname the index prints only the other day it keeps him on, «26 января (Новомуч.)», with the whole company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. No birth, no parish, no charge, no place of death. The long lives are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line.

*After the Russian church calendar's [30 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260930.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9391.html); read 4 October 2026.*
