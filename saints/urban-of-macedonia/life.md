# Urban of Macedonia

doxologia.ro's calendar names Urban among the apostles it keeps on 31 October, and the life it prints for him is two sentences. He was set as bishop in Macedonia by the holy Apostle Andrew; and preaching Christ and destroying idols he raised the Jews and the Greeks against himself, and being killed by them he wove himself a martyr's crown.

That is all the page says. It gives no year, no city in Macedonia, no name for those who killed him and no manner of death beyond the word itself. doxologia.ro prints no separate troparion for him. The hymn on his page is the one it gives to all six apostles it names on this day — Apelles, Stachys, Ampliatus, Urban, Aristobulus and Narcissus together — a short common of apostles in the third tone, asking them to entreat the merciful God for the forgiveness of our offences.
