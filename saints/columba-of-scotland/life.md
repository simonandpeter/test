# Columba, enlightener of Scotland

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives him one sentence. The line for 9 June reads «Άγιος Columba φωτιστής της Σκωτίας», Saint Columba, enlightener of Scotland, and the page behind it says this and no more: Saint Columba was born on 7 December 521 in Ireland and fell asleep on 9 June 597 in Scotland.

So what is recorded of him is two dates, two countries and the title the day's line gives him, that he was the enlightener of the Scots — and the day he is kept on is the day of his death. The page says nothing of Iona, nothing of a monastery or a rule or a disciple, nothing of how the enlightening was done, and it prints neither verse nor hymn. It does not even give the name in Greek letters, which is why this folder stores it as the page has it, in Latin ones.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 9 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/09/index.aspx) and [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/1842/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
