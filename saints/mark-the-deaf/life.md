# Mark the Deaf

saint.gr's calendar for 2 Ιανουαρίου names Mark the Deaf and gives him one sentence: the venerable Mark the deaf was an ascetic, and having lived a holy life he departed in peace. That is the whole of the account, and the page offers nothing beyond it — no country, no monastery, no teacher, no year.

What it does print is a two-line epigram that plays on his deafness: Mark did not hear the words of earth, and before he left the earth he was already carrying his ears away from it. The reading the page gives his name is the Latin marceo, to wither, taken to mean the man indifferent to earthly things.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 2 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/02/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/21/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
