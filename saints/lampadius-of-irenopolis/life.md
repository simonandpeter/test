# Lampadius of Irenopolis

Doxologia's whole life of Lampadius is a single sentence, and it says this: that this saint gave himself from infancy to the life of a hermit, and subduing the body to the spirit by the restraint of his desires and by frequent prayer, he shone like the sun and gave light to those who were darkened by the deceits of the demons.

He worked many wonders both in his life and after he had departed to the Lord, giving healing to those who came in faith to the cave in which he had struggled, near Irenopolis in Isauria, where his holy relics were also laid.

That is the whole of what the page gives. It names no parents, no year of birth and no year of death, and none of those is recorded here.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 5 iulie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/5-iulie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-cuvios-lampadie); read 24 September 2026.*
