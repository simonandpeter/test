# Lampadius of Irenopolis

Doxologia's whole life of Lampadius is a single sentence, and it says this: that this saint gave himself from infancy to the life of a hermit, and subduing the body to the spirit by the restraint of his desires and by frequent prayer, he shone like the sun and gave light to those who were darkened by the deceits of the demons.

He worked many wonders both in his life and after he had departed to the Lord, giving healing to those who came in faith to the cave in which he had struggled, near Irenopolis in Isauria, where his holy relics were also laid.

That is the whole of what the page gives. It names no parents, no year of birth and no year of death, and none of those is recorded here.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 5 July, under the bare name Λαμπαδός and without the place the Romanian calendar gives him. saint.gr says that even while he was still in the world he shone with the Christian virtues, and was marked out for his chastity, his goodness, his bridling of his tongue, his patience, his long-suffering, his meekness and his great love for the poor and the suffering. Afterwards he took to the ascetic life, and was one of those ascetics who, by their holy life and the regard their virtue earns, become most useful to the world as well: the many who came to his hermitage he led to the right spiritual balms, and they found again the moral health that the passions and the pleasures of the flesh had dug away.
