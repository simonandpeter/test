# Nicetas the Patrician

saint.gr's calendar for 13 October keeps «Όσιος Νικήτας ο Ομολογητής και Πατρίκιος», and gives him a whole career. He came from Paphlagonia and was born in 763 to devout parents, Gregory and Anna, who brought him up according to the commands of the Gospel. A kinswoman of his had a particular love for him: the empress Irene, the page says, though others say Theodora. He was taken into the palace and rose by his virtue and his ability to the rank of patrician and general of Sicily.

Having an inclination to the quiet life, however, he became a monk. For that he was persecuted by the iconoclast emperors Leo and Theophilus and exiled many times; and after many hardships, at the age of seventy-five, he died peacefully in the year 838. The calendar prints no hymn for him, and its two-line verse says that Nicetas, having borne afflictions for the sake of the divine icons, rejoices with the joy of the bodiless powers.
