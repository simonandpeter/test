# Gerontius of Milan

saint.gr's calendar names him and then gives two sentences and stops. Saint Gerontius lived in the fifth century after Christ in Italy, the page says; he was elected Bishop of Milan, and he reposed in peace in the year 470. The heading over those lines prints «Άγιος Γερόντιος Επίσκοπος Μιλάνου (; - 470)» — the year of his death and a question mark where the year of his birth would stand.

That is the whole of the record here. There is no account of his election or of his episcopate, no city of birth, no relics named, no hymn printed and no second source pointed to; and the page does not say whether the fifth century and the year 470 come from one witness or two.
