# Valerian of Auxerre

saint.gr's calendar for 13 May names him and gives him two sentences. Saint Valerian was bishop of Auxerre in France, and he contended for the Orthodox faith against the impious Arian heretics. He fell asleep in peace in the year 350; the page's head line gives his dates as an unknown birth and that death.

Nothing else is printed: no birthplace, no parents, no length of episcopate, no council he sat at, no relics and no hymn. The Greek calendar keeps a Gallic bishop of this name on 13 May, with three other bishops of the Latin West on the same day, and this folder carries the calendar's line and the two sentences behind it.
