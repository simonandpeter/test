# John, son of Metrophanes Ji-Sung

saint.gr names him among the Chinese martyrs of 1900 as the younger son of father [Metrophanes Ji-Sung](/saints/metrophanes-of-peking), the first Chinese priest of the Russian mission at Peking, and of [Tatiana](/saints/tatiana-of-peking) the priest's wife. It tells his age twice and differently — a boy of eight in one paragraph, of seven in the next — and gives him no year of birth.

What it does give at length is his answer. In the persecution the Boxers loosed on the Christians of Peking on the night after 10 June 1900, the boy John suffered the amputation of both his hands and the cutting open of his breast; and when his executioners asked whether he was in pain, he answered them smiling: «Δεν είναι δύσκολο να υποφέρει κάνεις για τον Χριστό» — it is not hard to suffer for Christ. The Boxers beheaded him and burnt his remains on a festal pyre. In another paragraph the page says that they cut off the toes, the nose and the ears of this other son, aged seven, at the very hour of his father's martyrdom, and that he felt no pain at all, and that when his torturers called him a son of demons he answered: «Είμαι ένα πιστός του Θεού και όχι μαθητής δαιμόνων!» — I am a believer in God and no disciple of demons.

His father was stabbed in the breast in his own courtyard, where some seventy Christians had taken refuge after the mission buildings were burnt; his mother and his brother [Isaiah](/saints/isaiah-of-peking) were beheaded, and [two hundred and twenty-two](/saints/two-hundred-twenty-two-chinese-martyrs) more received the crown with them.
