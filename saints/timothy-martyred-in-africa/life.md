# Timothy, martyred in Africa

Timothy, Polios and Eutychius are kept together on 21 Μαΐου, and saint.gr's whole account of the three of them is one sentence: «Οι Άγιοι Ιερομάρτυρες Τιμόθεος, Πόλιος και Ευτύχιος ήταν διάκονοι της Εκκλησίας και μαρτύρησαν στην Αφρική σε άγνωστο χρόνο» — the holy hieromartyrs Timothy, Polios and Eutychius were deacons of the Church and were martyred in Africa at an unknown time.

That is all. The page gives them no city, no bishop, no emperor, no persecutor and no manner of death, and it says of the time itself that it is unknown, so there is no year here to record. It prints no synaxarion couplet and no hymn, and carries no asterisk against any of the three.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 21 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/21/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1708/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
