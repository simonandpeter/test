# Joasaph (Skripitsyn)

He stands eighth in the Russian calendar's synaxis of the hierarchs of Moscow, between Gerontius and the first of the line’s two Macarii, and the calendar gives him his name, his surname, his rank and his see, and then stops. Its index of names heads him «Святитель Иоасаф (Скрипицын), митрополит Московский и всея Руси» — hierarch Joasaph Skripitsyn, metropolitan of Moscow and all Russia — and keeps him on three days: this synaxis, 27 July, and «6 июля (Радонеж.)», which the abbreviation marks as a Radonezh commemoration and which the page does not expand.

This folder therefore has no year at all, and that is a fact about the source rather than about the man. Alone of the seventeen on the line he has no life on the site: the thirteen life links the day prints reach the other names and not his. Where the calendar is silent nothing is supplied, so he stands Undated, with a see and a surname and the company he is kept in.

*After the Russian church calendar's [5 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261005.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/11767.html); read 4 October 2026.*
