# Boniface (Vinogradsky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names the venerable Boniface on 27 December and gives no life. Its line is «Прп. Вонифатия Феофановского, Киевского (1871).» — the venerable Boniface of Theophania, of Kyiv, 1871 — and the calendar’s own name page for him carries the surname and the rank the line leaves out: «Вонифатий (Виноградский) Феофановский, Киевский, игумен, прп.»

That is the whole of the record. He was an abbot; the hermitage the calendar names him for is Theophania, outside Kyiv; the year of death the line gives is 1871. The day page carries four hymns — three for the protomartyr Stephen and one for the venerable Theodore — and none under this name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 27 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261227.html); read 13 October 2026.*
