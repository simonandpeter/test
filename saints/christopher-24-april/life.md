# Christopher (24 April)

Christopher is one of eight martyrs saint.gr prints together on 24 April, and the whole of what it says of them it says of all eight at once. They were martyred in the year 303, it says, when they came to the Christian faith through the wonders which the great martyr [George](/saints/george-the-trophy-bearer) performed — George whose own feast the calendar had kept the day before.

Having confessed Christ they were arrested and held in prison. Afterwards, by order of Diocletian, whose reign the page dates 284 to 305, they were hung up and their flesh was scraped, and at the last they were beheaded, all of them taking so the unfading crown of martyrdom.

That is the whole account. The page heads Christopher «(; - 303)» like the rest and gives him no country, no city, no rank and no relic; its couplet is a piece of arithmetic on their number and nothing about them, and it prints no hymn. The seven others, as the calendar's line names them, are [Danabos](/saints/danabos), [Demetrius](/saints/demetrius-24-april), [Eusebius](/saints/eusebius-24-april), [Leontius](/saints/leontius-24-april), [Longinus](/saints/longinus-24-april), [Nestabus](/saints/nestabus-24-april) and [Neon](/saints/neon-24-april).
