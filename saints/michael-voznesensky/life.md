# Michael (Voznesensky)

The Russian calendar keeps him in one line with thirteen others on this day and he stands last in it, and alone of the fourteen he is given a rank of his own: «...Павла , Павла , Георгия пресвитеров и мч. Михаила (1938)». The word «пресвитеров» closes the run of twelve forenames before him; he is introduced after it as the martyr Michael, and his name page repeats it, «Михаил (Вознесенский), мч.», which gives the surname the line leaves out. He is therefore the one layman of the company, and this folder carries no clerical rank and no office.

He is also the second Michael of the line. The first stands inside the run the word «пресвитеров» covers and is a priest of another surname; the two are told apart by the name links and by the grade. The same day keeps a priest of his own surname, Матфий (Вознесенский), in a separate entry and of another year, and nothing read links the two.

His page carries a heading and two days and not a sentence of narrative, and the day page hangs no life on the line; the second day, 26 января, is the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, a company's feast and not a day of his own. So this folder says what the calendar says: a layman of this name and surname, kept as a martyr on this day with [Antony (Pankeyev)](/saints/antony-pankeyev), bishop of Belgorod, and twelve priests, dead in 1938. What a second source would add is everything, and azbyka.ru was not read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 19 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260519.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9997.html), which carries no life; read 8 October 2026.*
