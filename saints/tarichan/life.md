# Tarichan, brother of David

saint.gr's calendar for 18 May keeps two brothers on one page and gives them one sentence between them. The holy martyrs [David](/saints/david-brother-of-tarichan) and Tarichan were brothers, and they were martyred in the year 693, in the years of the Persian king Abdul, because they refused to change their faith and embrace Islam.

That is the whole of the entry, and Tarichan has nothing of his own in it beyond the name and the head line's «(; - 693)». The page gives no country, no parents, no judge, no manner of death, no relics and no hymn. What is recorded here is what the Greek calendar keeps on 18 May and no more than that.
