# Sergius

Doxologia.ro's calendar for 2 ianuarie names him and gives almost no life. The page headed «Viaţa Sfântului Mucenic Serghie» carries a single sentence, and that sentence is the whole of it: «Sfântului Mucenic Serghie s-a săvârşit, fiind tăiat cu sabia» — the holy martyr Sergius made his end, being cut down with the sword.

No country is named, no year, no emperor and no companions; the calendar's own line says only «Sfântul Mucenic Serghie». What is recorded here is a name, a rank and a manner of death, and nothing has been added to them. He is not the Sergius kept with Bacchus, whose troparion the page links to among its related articles; that is a link the site makes and not a statement about this man.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 2 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/2-ianuarie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-mucenic-serghie); read 19 September 2026.*
