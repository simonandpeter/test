# Fortunatus (1 June)

saint.gr's calendar for 1 Ιουνίου names him and gives three sentences. The holy Fortunatus, it says, was a presbyter in the town of Territa near Spoleto in Italy. He was marked out for his love of his fellow men and for the gift of wonderworking, which the Lord gave him. He fell asleep in peace in the year 400.

That is the whole of the source. It names no wonder of his, no bishop over him and no church of his; it gives him no birthplace and no year of birth, and it prints neither hymn nor epigram. The same calendar keeps another Fortunatus, a martyr, on 30 August, and the two are kept apart here: one dies by violence and the other in peace, and two entries on one calendar are two commemorations.
