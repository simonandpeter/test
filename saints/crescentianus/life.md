# Crescentianus

saint.gr's calendar for 1 Ιουνίου names him and gives one sentence. The holy martyr Crescentianus, it says, was a soldier, and he was martyred in the year 287, under the emperor Diocletian, whose reign the page dates 284 to 305, in the city of Saldo in Italy, near Castello.

That is the whole of the source. It gives him no birthplace, no age, no rank in the army, no judge and no manner of death; it names no companion, records no wonder, and prints neither hymn nor epigram. The two place-names it does give are not put on the map here, because the spellings do not settle which town in Italy is meant, and no point beats a wrong one.
