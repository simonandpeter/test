# Eusebius, companion of Bassus

Eusebius was one of a group of martyrs who lived in the days of the emperor Diocletian. doxologia.ro's life says they were men of great wealth and members of the senate, and that they believed in Christ and received baptism when they saw the bishop Theopemptus bearing torments and working miracles by the power of Christ. The page tells their passion in a few lines and gives no year, no city and nothing of their lives before the day they were brought in.

For their confession they were taken before the emperor. Their belts of office were stripped from them first, and then they were given every kind of torment unto death. [Bassus](/saints/bassus-companion-of-eusebius) was put into a pit up to the waist, his hands were cut off, and his whole body was butchered until he gave up his spirit; Eusebius was hanged head downwards and cut in pieces with an axe; and a third of their number, Eutychius, was stretched on four stakes until he was torn apart. That is the whole of what the Romanian page prints of him.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 20 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/20-ianuarie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-mucenic-eusebiu); read 19 September 2026.*
