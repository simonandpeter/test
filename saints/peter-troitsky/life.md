# Peter (Troitsky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Peter among the hieromartyrs on 15 July and gives no life. Its line is «Сщмч. Петра диакона (1938).» — the hieromartyr Peter the deacon, 1938 — and the calendar's own name page for him adds only the surname and the grade: «Петр (Троицкий), диакон, сщмч.»

That is the whole of the record. He was a deacon; he died in 1938, at the height of the terror; the calendar keeps him on this day among the new martyrs of Russia. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read, and the surname is not a biography.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 15 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260715.html); read 9 October 2026.*
