# Anastasius the Elder, disciple of Maximus

Anastasius, the elder of the two of that name among the disciples of [Maximus the
Confessor](/saints/maximus-the-confessor), was exiled to Thrace by the emperor Constans II and
suffered much there. Because both Anastasii held to the Orthodox mind they were brought to
Constantinople, condemned by a synod and handed to the prefect for punishment; he cut out their
tongues and cut off their right hands, and their teacher Maximus with them. This Anastasius died
in exile in Lazica twenty years afterwards. The Greek calendar keeps him on 20 September.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 20 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/982/saint.aspx); read 26 August 2026.*
