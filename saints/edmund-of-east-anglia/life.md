# Edmund of East Anglia

saint.gr's calendar for 20 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιος Edmund ο μάρτυρας» — printing the name in Latin letters inside its Greek line — and heads his page 841 to 869. He was a king and martyr of East Anglia, it says. He was born in 841 and succeeded his father Ethelweard on the throne at the age of only fourteen.

Little is known of his adolescence. What is certain in any case is that his upbringing was Christian; indeed he is said to have studied the scriptures deeply and to have learnt the Psalms by heart.

According to his biographer, Abbo of Fleury, when the Danes invaded his territory in 869 Edmund chose not to strike at them. He was taken all the same, and carried to Hoxne in Suffolk. There they beat him savagely and then bound him to a tree; the pagan Danes tried to force him to deny his faith, and hearing instead his appeals to Christ they set on him with arrows. His martyrdom ended with his beheading, on 20 November 869.
