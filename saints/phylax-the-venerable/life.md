# Phylax

Phylax is unknown to the Synaxaristes. He is named in the Jerusalem Codex 1096 (folio 123) in one line with the day’s patriarchs: the memory of the venerable patriarchs Alexander, John and Paul the New, and of the venerable Phylax. Nothing else is recorded.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 30 Αυγούστου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/840/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
