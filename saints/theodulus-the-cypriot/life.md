# Theodulus the Cypriot

Theodulus was a Cypriot ascetic of an unknown period, saint.gr says, and that is how it introduces him: «Κύπριος ασκητής άγνωστης εποχής». Because of the greatness of his asceticism he was counted worthy of a clear-seeing spirit; and he played the part of the madman, the salos, and reproved people for the lawless things they were doing.

So, having lived in holiness, he died in peace; and his relic, the page adds, heals many sicknesses miraculously. That is the whole of the account. It gives him no century, no monastery, no town in Cyprus, no single reproof or healing told, and no couplet beyond the one set over the entry, which puts him in the vineyard: Cyprus's vine-shoot Theodulus, having blossomed and made a cluster, came to the vintage. The page prints no hymn for him and reproduces one modern icon, credited to its painter.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/03/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/252/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
