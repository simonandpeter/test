# Nicholas (Vinogradov)

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar for 4 ноября ст. ст. names him on a line of its own among the day's last four: «Св. Николая исп., пресвитера (1931).» The line gives the forename in the genitive and no surname at all. The name link behind it supplies the calendar's own nominative with the surname, «Николай (Виноградов), иерей, исповедник», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from.

That is everything the calendar records of him. He has no life page, no icon, no hymn, no place, no age and no parish anywhere on this calendar, and the name page behind the line carries two days and no text at all. The name page also lists «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which is a synaxis and not a second feast; this folder renders on 4 ноября ст. ст. only. Nothing further is written here, and in particular nothing is reasoned from the surname.

The line's «исп.» and «пресвитера» make him a confessor and a presbyter, and the link's «иерей» is recorded as his office, Priest. The rank word on the line is «Св.» and not «Сщмч.»: this calendar does not call him a martyr, and nothing here says he was killed. 1931 is the year the line prints at its end and the only thing on the page that places him in a decade.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 4 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261104.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13844.html); read 4 October 2026.*
