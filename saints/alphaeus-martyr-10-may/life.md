# Alphaeus (10 May)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 10 mai reads «Sfântul Mucenic Alfeu», and the page it links for him carries neither a Viață tab nor a Tropar tab — only the feast line "10 Mai" and the readings appointed for the day. No country is named, no century is given, and nothing is told of what he did or how he ended.

The corpus already keeps an Alphaeus, the brother of Alexander and Zosimas, from the Greek synaxarion for 28 September; nothing read here connects the two, and the folder stands on the calendar's line alone.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and names his brothers and his judges. The holy martyrs Alpheus, [Cyprinus](/saints/cyprian-martyr-10-may) and [Philadelphus](/saints/philadelphus-martyr-10-may) were brothers, saint.gr says, sons of the ruler of the prefects Vitalius, and they came of the country of the Vascones in southern Italy. Having been taught the Orthodox faith and baptised by a certain venerable man named Onesimus, they preached Christ. They were denounced for that God-loving work of theirs and were seized by one Anengelion under the emperor Decius, who reigned from 249 to 251, and sent bound to Rome. From there, after the first examination, they were carried to Puteoli to the governor Diomedes, and he referred them to Tertyllus, the ruler of Sicily, who, when he could not change their minds, condemned them to death. Alpheus they beheaded, after first cutting out his tongue; Philadelphus they burned upon a red-hot gridiron; and Cyprinus they fried in an enormous pan. Their own page adds nothing of relics or burial, and the page of [Erasmus](/saints/erasmus-10-may) and [Onesimus](/saints/onesimus-10-may), who are kept on this same day, says that those two were martyred with fourteen others together with these three brothers. The couplet over Alpheus says that though his tongue was taken away, he glorifies the Word with the tongue of his soul: «Aλφειός ει και γλώτταν εξαφηρέθη, / Tη ψυχική γλώττη σε δοξάζει Λόγε».

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 10 mai — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/10-mai) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-alfeu), which prints no life; read 24 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 10 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/10/index.aspx) and [the life of the three](https://www.saint.gr/1540/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026.*
