# Domna of Tomsk

saint.gr's calendar for 16 December keeps Saint Domna of Tomsk, the fool for Christ, and says of her only that it has nothing of her life: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο της Ρωσίδας Αγίας» — we have no details of the life of the Russian saint. The heading above that sentence gives the year of her death as 1872 and no year of birth, and that heading is the only place a year appears at all.

So the whole of the Greek entry is four things: that she is kept on 16 December every year, that she was of Tomsk, that she was a fool for Christ's sake, and that she died in 1872. The page names no monastery and no church, lists no relic, prints no hymn, and sends the reader on to the synaxis of all the saints of Siberia and to an English calendar, neither of which was read for this folder.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 16 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/16/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4248/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
