# Alexius (Zinoviev), Presbyter, Hieromartyr (1937)

Alexius was born on 1 March 1879 to the priest John Zinoviev. From 1898 to 1904 he studied at the Tula seminary, after which he was ordained priest for the church of the village of Voskresenskoye in the Chern district of Tula province; in 1914 he was moved to a church in the town of Efremov, and from 1917 served at the church of the village of Storozha in Moscow province, where in thirteen years the Orthodox came to love the kind and zealous pastor. When in 1930, in another wave of persecution, he was arrested on the charge of supposedly setting up anti-Soviet groups in the village, the parishioners rose as one in his defence — almost the whole village came to the village soviet and demanded the release of the innocent pastor; the authorities did not free him but took him under guard to the prison at Efremov, where he was held about five weeks, then released after all, and he returned and served in the church as before. In the early thirties, with collectivisation, the ruin of the peasant farms, the forced founding of collective farms and the taking of all the peasants’ grain, the peasants began to hide it so as not to starve; the plan by which they were to hand grain to the state exceeded — the calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. He was shot in 1937, among the martyrs of Butovo; the Russian calendar keeps him on 3 September (Julian).
