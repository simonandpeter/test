# Anthion

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Ιουνίου names him and gives almost no life. Its line reads «Όσιος Ανθίων», and the page behind that line carries a single sentence — «Ο Όσιος Ανθίων απεβίωσε ειρηνικά», the venerable Anthion died in peace — with a couplet of the synaxarion standing above it. No country is named, no century, no monastery, no office and no year, and the page prints no hymn.

The couplet plays on his name, which is built on the word for a flower: «Δένδρον τι θείον καλλίφυλλον Aνθίων, Φυλλορροεί δε τη ρύσει του σαρκίου» — Anthion, a sort of divine tree with lovely leaves, sheds his leaves in the flowing away of the little body. The page does not even gloss the name, as it does for most of the saints it keeps, and there is nothing else on it at all.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/628/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
