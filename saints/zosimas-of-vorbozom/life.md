# Zosimas of Vorbozom

saint.gr says Zosimas of Vorbozom was born about the end of the fifteenth century in Russia. He practised asceticism in a way pleasing to God at the monastery of Komel and on the island of Vorbozom, near the White Lake, where he founded a monastic brotherhood and raised a church dedicated to the Annunciation of the Mother of God. He fell asleep in peace in the year 1550.

That is the whole of what the page says. It gives him no place of birth, no family, no year of birth, tells no incident of his life and names no relics, and it prints no couplet and no hymn under his name. The day's line calls him only «εκ Ρωσίας», out of Russia, and it is the life page behind the line that names the island he is called after.

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 4 апреля ст. ст. — «Прп. Зосимы Ворбозомского (ок. 1550).» — and the name page behind the line, number 848, is an index holding two readings of him. Between them they give the house and the place, which the Greek calendar this folder was written from did not: he founded a monastery of the Annunciation of the Most Holy Mother of God on the island in lake Ворбозомское, twenty-three versts south of Belozersk, and governed it for many years in great labours.

The fuller of the two sets the house in the fifteenth century and gives the reason: in 1501 its abbot was a disciple of his, «игумен Иона», so the monastery was already standing. It was one of the many small desert houses that made up the monastic «Заволжье» about the Kirillo-Belozersk monastery. And it adds the one thing he left behind in writing: a teaching, and a letter to his spiritual daughter «Анастасия».

The two readings date his death differently in form and not in substance — «скончался около 1550 г.» in the shorter, «в первой половине XVI века» in the fuller — and the day’s own line gives «ок. 1550». The folder’s flat 1550 came from the Greek page and is left as it stands, with the «ок.» recorded here. The calendar lists 7 November beside this day; the day page for 4 April is what listed him, so the November day is written here and not in the feast. The day page prints no hymn under his name.
