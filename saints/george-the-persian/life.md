# George the Persian

saint.gr's calendar names him and then says plainly that it knows no more: «Ο Άγιος Οσιομάρτυς Γεώργιος μαρτύρησε το έτος 615 μ.Χ. Δεν έχουμε περισσότερες λεπτομέρειες για τον Βίο του Οσίου.» — the holy venerable-martyr George was martyred in the year 615, and we have no further details of his life. The day's line calls him «ο Πέρσης», the Persian, and that with the year and the word venerable-martyr is the whole of the notice. The page adds a note on the name, that George is the tiller of the earth, and prints no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1081/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
