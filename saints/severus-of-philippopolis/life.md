# Severus of Philippopolis

Severus, [Memnon](/saints/memnon-the-centurion) and thirty-seven martyrs with them suffered at Philippopolis in Thrace under Diocletian. For his firm and fearless confession of faith in Christ Severus was raked with iron hooks; then red-hot rings were put on the fingers of his hands and a red-hot iron belt about his waist, and after these torments he was beheaded. When the governor learned that Severus had turned the centurion Memnon to Christ, he ordered him tortured too: Memnon was stretched and three strips cut from the skin of his back, and with him suffered thirty-seven more. All of them had their hands and feet cut off and were thrown into a burning furnace, in 304.
