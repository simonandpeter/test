# Arcadius of Cyprus

saint.gr's entry for this Arcadius is two sentences, and the first of them is a citation rather than a life: he is counted by Leontios Machairas among the saints of the Church of Cyprus. The second is his whole biography: he served as Archbishop of Cyprus, and he fell asleep in peace.

So three things stand recorded and no more — the name, the see, and a peaceful end — on the authority of the fifteenth-century Cypriot chronicler the page names. There is no century here, no year of birth or of repose, no city, no predecessor or successor on the throne, no council he sat in, no act of his episcopate, no relic, no distich and no hymn.

The same Greek day keeps a venerable [Arcadius](/saints/arcadius-teacher-of-julian), an ascetic of Cyprus under Constantine the Great and the teacher of Julian and Euboulos, on a page of its own; and the corpus keeps [Arcadius the Wonderworker](/saints/arcadius-of-arsinoe), bishop of Arsinoe in Cyprus, whom saint.gr keeps on the twenty-ninth of August. Two entries on one calendar are two commemorations, so these are kept apart; but nothing on any of the three pages tells them apart in terms of one another, and nothing is decided here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3858/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
