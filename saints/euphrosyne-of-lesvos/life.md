# Euphrosyne of Lesvos

saint.gr's calendar names her with her abbess and gives her no life of her own. The venerable [Olympia](/saints/olympia-of-lesvos) and the venerable Euphrosyne lived in the thirteenth century, the page says, and gave up their souls by a martyr's death on the eleventh of May in the year 1235. All that it tells of Euphrosyne herself is her rank in the house and the manner of her killing: she was a γερόντισσα, an old nun, of the monastery of Karyai at Thermi on Lesvos, the monastery that is now Saint Raphael's.

On that eleventh of May pirates came to Mytilene and went up to the monastery. They scattered the thirty nuns, and those who could not get away in time they abused; the abbess and the old Euphrosyne they tortured terribly. Euphrosyne they hung on a tree and burned. Olympia they burned over her whole body, and in her grave, when the relics of the saints of Thermi were found and their story became known by divine revelations, the twenty nails with which they had nailed her were found too.

The page gives Euphrosyne no year of birth, no family, no birthplace, no name in the world and no relics of her own, and the two hymns it prints name Olympia and not her. None of the thirty nuns is named.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/11/index.aspx) and [the life of the two](https://www.saint.gr/400/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
