# The Forty Thousand Martyrs

saint.gr's calendar for 28 Φεβρουαρίου prints a company with no names and no place. Its line reads «Άγιοι Τεσσαράκοντα Χιλιάδες Μάρτυρες», the holy forty thousand martyrs, and the whole of the page standing under it is one sentence: these holy martyrs were burnt by the Mameluke Turks in the thirteenth century. Because the line names nobody and the page names nobody, the company is kept here as the one entry the calendar makes of it.

Nothing else at all is printed: no city, no year within the century, no name of any one of them, no relics, no couplet and no hymn. The number is the calendar's own, and the century is the only date this folder can carry.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/28/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1935/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
