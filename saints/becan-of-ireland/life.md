# Becan of Ireland

saint.gr says Becan lived in the sixth century and was a scion of the royal family of Ireland. When he was building his church he stayed many times on his knees; and while his hands worked, his lips murmured prayers and the tears ran from his eyes without stopping. He is counted, the page says, among the twelve missionaries of Ireland. Becan fell asleep in peace.

That is the whole of it. The page gives him no year of birth and no year of death, names no place in Ireland, no see and no monastery, names no relics, tells nothing of the twelve he is counted among, and prints no couplet and no hymn. It gives him no rank word either, beyond calling him holy, so no rank is recorded here.
