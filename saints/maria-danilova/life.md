# Maria (Danilova)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names the martyr Maria on 30 December and gives no life. Its line is «Мц. Марии (1946).» — the martyr Maria, 1946 — and the calendar’s own name page for her carries the surname the line leaves out: «Мария (Данилова), мц.»

That is the whole of the record. The calendar gives her no grade and no office, only the rank of martyr; the year of death the line gives is 1946, which is later than any other twentieth-century memory in this stretch of the calendar; it keeps her on this day, inside the afterfeast of the Nativity. The day page prints four hymns — two for the hierarch Macarius, one for the hieromartyr Zoticus and one for the martyr Anysia — and none under this name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 30 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261230.html); read 13 October 2026.*
