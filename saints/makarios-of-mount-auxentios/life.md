# Makarios of Mount Auxentios

saint.gr's calendar for 17 Αυγούστου names him and then names its own source for him, which is the whole of what it has: his memory, it says, is mentioned in the Byzantine Eortologion of Gedeon, at page 158. It adds two facts and no more. He was martyred for the holy icons in the years of the emperor Copronymus, that is Constantine V, in the year 768; and he was a monk on the mountain of Auxentios, across the water from Constantinople.

There is no account of the martyrdom, no manner of death, no birthplace and no hymn. What the two sentences do give is the one thing that tells him apart from the many venerable Makarioi of the calendar: the cause he died for and the year he died in. This folder stands for that line of the Greek 17 August and records nothing the page does not print.
