# Basil (Militsyn)

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 25 июня ст. ст. prints two lines of the same words a few entries apart, «Сщмч. Василия пресвитера (1918).» and «Сщмч. Василия пресвитера (1940).», and this folder is the first of them. The line gives one grade-class word, one rank word, the forename in the genitive and a year, and it gives no surname. The name link behind the forename supplies the calendar’s own nominative with the surname and the grade, «Василий (Милицын), иерей, сщмч.», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from.

His name page carries nothing else. It heads «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 25 июня» and stops there: no life, no place of service, no diocese and no account of his death. Nothing is supplied for any of it here, and no year of birth is recorded, the calendar giving none. The line’s «Сщмч.» makes him a hieromartyr and «пресвитера» a presbyter, and the name link repeats the rank as «иерей», a priest.

The second day the name page lists is the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which is a synaxis and not a second feast, so he renders on 25 июня ст. ст. only. The surname is in the display name because the calendar gives him no epithet and no see to tell him by, and because the day prints the other presbyter’s line in the same words. The day page prints no hymn under either name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 25 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260625.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13735.html); read 9 October 2026.*
