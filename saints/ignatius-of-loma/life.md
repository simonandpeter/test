# Ignatius of Loma

saint.gr's calendar for 28 December keeps the holy Ignatius of Loma, and gives no life of him: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου» — we have no details about the life of the saint. What the page does carry is his name and his place in its own heading, «Άγιος Ιγνάτιος της Λόμα», and in that heading's bracket the year of his death, «(; - 1591)», with no year of birth beside it and no rank of any kind.

The one other thing it says of him is where it keeps him: it sends the reader on to the synaxis of all the saints who shone in Rostov and Yaroslavl, «Σύναξη πάντων των εν Ροστώβ και Γιαροσλάβλ διαλαμψάντων Αγίων». No hymn is printed, no distich, and no place but Loma itself.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/28/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4036/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
