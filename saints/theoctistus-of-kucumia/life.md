# Theoctistus of Kucumia

Doxologia.ro's calendar for 4 ianuarie names him, and the page it heads «Viaţa Sfântului Cuvios Teoctist, egumenul de la Kucumia Siciliei» carries no life at all. What it carries instead is a note about who he is not. In the Prologue, it says, another Theoctistus was put in this man's place: the hermit honoured on the third day of September, who was with Saint Euthymius the Great in Palestine, out of the new Menaion.

The synaxarion of the months, the note goes on, shows that on this day it is «Teoctist cel din Sicilia, iar nu cel din Palestina» who is honoured — the Sicilian and not the Palestinian. So the Romanian calendar keeps an abbot of Kucumia in Sicily on 4 January and gives him a name, a rank, a monastery and an island, and nothing else: no year, no parents, no account of his life or his death. He is expressly not [Theoctistus of Palestine](/saints/theoctistus-of-palestine), with whom the page says the Prologue confused him.

The Russian calendar keeps him on 4 января old style and gives in three sentences what the year it prints beside him stands for. He founded the monastery at the town of Kucumion on the island of Sicily and became its abbot. Greek monks who had fled the persecution of the iconoclasts lived in that monastery of his. He fell asleep, the calendar says, in the year 800.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 4 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/4-ianuarie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-cuvios-teoctist-egumenul-de-la-kucumia-siciliei); read 19 September 2026; and after the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 4 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260104.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life148.htm), read 3 October 2026.*
