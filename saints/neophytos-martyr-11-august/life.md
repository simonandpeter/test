# Neophytos (11 August)

saint.gr's calendar names him in a company of six and gives almost no life. Its line for the day is «Άγιοι Νεόφυτος, Ζήνων, Γάιος, Μάρκος, Μακάριος και Γαϊανός» — Neophytos with [Zenon](/saints/zenon-martyr-11-august), [Gaius](/saints/gaius-martyr-11-august), [Mark](/saints/mark-martyr-11-august), [Macarius](/saints/macarius-martyr-11-august) and [Gaianos](/saints/gaianos-martyr-11-august). The whole of its notice is one sentence and a quotation, and the sentence says only that the six suffered martyrdom by fire.

The quotation gives the place of their commemoration: «Τελείται δε η αυτών Σύναξις εν τω εξαέρω οίκω των Αγίων και ενδόξων Αναργύρων Κοσμά και Δαμιανού εν της Δαρείου» — their synaxis is celebrated in the airy house of the holy and glorious unmercenaries Cosmas and Damian at Dareiou. The page then prints two distichs and divides the six between them.

The first is headed «Eις τον Nεόφυτον, Ζήνωνα, και Γάιον» and reads «Nεοφύτω Ζήνωνι και τω Γαΐω, / Στρωμνή τις η κάμινος, ης κείνται μέσον» — for Neophytos, Zenon and Gaius the furnace in whose midst they lie is a kind of bed. The second, «Eις τον Mάρκον, Mακάριον, και Γαϊανόν», calls three martyrs burned alive a strange fragrance of incense to the Trinity.

Of Neophytos apart from his companions the page gives nothing at all: no year, no country, no hymn, only a gloss on the name, which it derives from νέος and φύομαι and reads as the newly initiated, the man born again. The corpus keeps another martyr of this name on 5 Μαΐου, where saint.gr's own line is «Άγιοι Νεόφυτος, Γάιος και Γαϊανός οι Μάρτυρες»; two entries on one calendar are two commemorations, so that is another man, and this folder is not his.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/11/index.aspx) and [the life of the six](https://www.saint.gr/782/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
