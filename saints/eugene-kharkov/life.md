# Eugene (Kharkov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him third of thirteen in one line on 10 декабря. The line is «Сщмчч. Анатолия, Александра, Евгения, Константина, Николая пресвитеров, и с ними мчч. Петра, Евсевия, Михаила, Дорофея, Лаврентия, Григория и мцц. Александры и Татианы (1937).» — five hieromartyr presbyters, and with them six men martyrs and two women martyrs, all of 1937 — and the calendar’s own name page adds the surname the line leaves out: «Евгений (Харьков), протоиерей, сщмч.»

That is the whole of the record for him. He was an archpriest, which is what the name page says where the line says only presbyter; he died in 1937; and the line sets him third among thirteen, of whom one, the second presbyter, has a life page. From that page it can be read that twenty-three people were shot on this day in the Ryazan region, so the thirteen of this line were part of a larger company; of this man it says nothing.

The day page carries eleven hymns, for a company of martyrs of the fourth century and for three of the saints it heads, and not one is under any of the thirteen names. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 10 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261210.html); read 13 October 2026.*
