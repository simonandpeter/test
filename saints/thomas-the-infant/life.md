# Thomas the infant

saint.gr's calendar for 1 Νοεμβρίου keeps seven martyrs in one line: «Άγιοι Καισάριος, Δάσιος, και άλλοι πέντε Μάρτυρες, Σάββας, Σαβινιανός, Αγρίππας, Αδριανός και Θωμάς το νήπιο». Each of the seven has a heading of his own on the page and each is called ο Μάρτυρας; and of all seven only this one carries a word that says anything about the person, «το νήπιο», the infant. Beyond the names the whole account is two sentences long.

They were arrested at Damascus, the page says, and punished with various torments so that they should deny Christ; but because they did not deny their Christian faith, all of them received the crown of martyrdom by beheading. There is no year, no emperor and no governor named, and nothing of any one of them apart from the rest — and nothing of how old the infant was or whose child he was.

The page sets a single epigram over the company, and it names only [Caesarius](/saints/caesarius-of-damascus): with six fellow contenders, friends of the truth, the friends of falsehood cut Caesarius down. The other five are [Dasius](/saints/dasius-companion-of-caesarius), [Sabbas](/saints/sabbas-companion-of-caesarius), [Sabinianus](/saints/sabinianus-companion-of-caesarius), [Agrippa](/saints/agrippa-companion-of-caesarius) and [Adrian](/saints/adrian-companion-of-caesarius). No hymn is printed, and the page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου».

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/01/index.aspx) and [the notice of the seven](https://www.saint.gr/2861/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
