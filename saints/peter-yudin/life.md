# Peter (Yudin)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and gives no life. Its line for 28 мая ст. ст. reads «Прмч. Макария , Дионисия , сщмч. Николая диакона, мчч. Игнатия и Петра (1931).» — five people, each with a rank and all with the one year 1931 — and each forename carries a link to a name and none to a reading. The name page behind this one prints the family name and the degree, «Петр (Юдин), мч.», and beyond the title one line, «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 28 мая», and nothing further.

So the calendar sets down a member of the Russian church who died in 1931, with a degree and a family name, and no more than that. The second of those two days is not a feast of his own: it is the general commemoration of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which this calendar prints beside the proper day of each of them, and it is the one mark on his page that places him among that company. He is kept here on the day the calendar gives him for his own, and the synaxis is recorded on his attestation.

The line names five people together and gives them one year, and that is the whole of what joins them on the page: it has no «with him» and no head, and nothing read says that any two of them knew each other or died in one place. No folder in this corpus has been made to say that they did.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 28 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260528.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14267.html), which name him and give no life; read 8 October 2026.*
