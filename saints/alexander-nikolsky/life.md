# Alexander (Nikolsky)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 19 октября ст. ст. in a line that names two priests of 1937 together, «Сщмчч. Александра и Сергия пресвитеров (1937)», and he stands first in it, as «Александра». The rank word over the pair is «Сщмчч.», hieromartyrs. The name link behind his half of the line carries the surname the line itself leaves out, and his grade with it: «Александр (Никольский), иерей, сщмч.» — a priest, иерей, rather than an archpriest.

The other of the pair is [Sergius (Pokrovsky)](/saints/sergius-pokrovsky), an archpriest of the same year, and nothing on the page says the two served together or died together; they are a pair because the line prints them as one. The calendar carries no life page under either name, and the hymns on the day page are for other saints it heads.

Nothing further is written here — not a birth, not a diocese, not a parish, not a charge, not a place of death. The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line. In particular he is not identified with any saint of the same name, or of the same surname, whom the calendar keeps on another day.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 19 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261019.html); read 4 October 2026.*
