# Pamphilus

The martyrs Pamphilus and [Capito](/saints/capito-the-martyr) were beheaded by the sword at a place called Olivria, near Constantinople. That is the whole of the life the Sretensky calendar prints for them, and this entry adds nothing to it.

The Romanian calendar keeps him on 12 august and prints one sentence of him: «Sfântul Mucenic Pamfil a fost decapitat cu sabia în regiunea Oliurea, lângă Constantinopol» — he was beheaded with the sword in the region of Oliurea, near Constantinople. That is the whole of what the page says, and it is the only place a place is named for him.

The Greek calendar keeps the two of them on the same day, 12 August, and adds nothing to the life. Its line is «Άγιοι Πάμφιλος και Καπίτων», and the one page behind it gives a head line to each of them and then a single sentence: «Οι Άγιοι Πάμφιλος και Καπίτων μαρτύρησαν δια ξίφους.» The holy Pamphilus and Capito suffered martyrdom by the sword. No year, no place, no hymn.

The page's one addition is the distich the Greek books give the pair: «Οἱ κείμενοι γῇ, καὶ λύθρῳ πεφυρμένοι, / Ξίφος μετῆλθον, Πάμφιλος καὶ Καπίτων» — lying on the ground and soaked in gore, Pamphilus and Capito passed over by the sword.

*After the life printed by the Sretensky calendar (days.pravoslavie.ru) — [the entry](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4427.htm); read 23 August 2026; the Romanian calendar keeps him on 12 august, doxologia.ro — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/12-august) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-pamfil-0), read 24 September 2026; saint.gr's calendar for 12 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/12/index.aspx) and [the life of the pair](https://www.saint.gr/787/saint.aspx), read 1 October 2026.*
