# Peter the Hesychast

saint.gr's calendar for 25 Νοεμβρίου names «Όσιος Πέτρος ο Ησυχαστής» — Peter the Hesychast, the man of stillness — and gives him a short life. He came from the parts of the Euxine Pontus, and from a child he was marked out by his fervent piety and his perfect devotion to the goods of the spirit. At the age of seven he went to Galatia, and afterwards to Palestine, where his faith was warmed still more, and he ended at Antioch, practising his asceticism in the mountains about that city, which so many great athletes and teachers of Christianity had made glorious.

His spiritual fruitfulness was not slow to spread, and crowds of people came to him and carried away valuable counsel and comfort for the wounds of the soul that are hard to heal. For his great virtue he received from God the gift of authority over the demons, and so he freed from demons a man called Daniel, whom he then catechised and made his fellow ascetic.

He died in peace at the age of ninety-five, or ninety-nine by other accounts, which is as far as the page will go; and the couplet set over the line plays on his epithet, that having crossed the sea of this life in stillness he arrives, Peter, at a still harbour. The page gives him no year, no monastery, no relics and no hymn.
