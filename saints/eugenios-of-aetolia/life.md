# Eugenios of Aetolia

saint.gr says the venerable Eugenios Ioannoulios, or Giannoulis, the Aetolian, was born in 1595, or by its own second reading 1596, at Mega Dendro of Apokouro, of poor parents. Young, he came to the monastery of the Panagia of Vlochos at Agrinio. He was ordained deacon in 1616 at the monastery of Tatarna, and stayed a short while at Xeropotamou on the Holy Mountain. In 1619 he was ordained presbyter at the monastery of Sinai by [Cyril Loukaris](/saints/cyril-loukaris), then patriarch of Alexandria and afterwards Ecumenical Patriarch.

With that patriarch, the page says, he lived through many hardships for his adherence to Orthodoxy and for his unsubdued spirit; he was even deposed by the Turk-loving and Latin-minded patriarch Cyril Kontaris, until in 1639 the patriarch Parthenios restored him with full honour. From 1639 to 1640 he directed the school of Arta. Later he went to Karpenisi, where he raised a larger church of the Holy Trinity and, in its precinct, a school of higher letters which became for Eurytania a centre of spiritual rebirth, and out of which came many notable men, bishops and patriarchs among them. He founded schools too at the monastery of Saint Paraskevi at Vragianna and at Aitoliko.

He fell asleep praying, in 1682, at the monastery of Vrangianna, surrounded by his beloved pupils, and left a considerable written work behind him; his biographer was his faithful pupil the hieromonk Anastasios Gordios. The Orthodox Church numbered him among the saints on 1 July 1982. From the whole of his work, the page says, his great love towards God and towards men is plain: he was a tireless spiritual labourer in hard hours of the nation, an enlightened guide and a virtuous teacher, and his rhetoric, his learning, his boundless memory, his courage and his will he put wholly to the raising up of his enslaved brothers. Two chapels of his stand at Karpenisi.
