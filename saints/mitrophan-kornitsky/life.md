# Mitrophan (Kornitsky)

The Сретенский monastery's calendar names him last of the five in «Сщмчч. Александра , Алексия , Иоанна , Корнилия и Митрофана пресвитеров (1937)» and carries no life for him; azbyka.ru prints one out of pstgu.ru. Митрофан Феодосьевич Корницкий was born in 1873 in the little town of Троянов in the province of Житомир. His priestly service began at Осники in the province of Чернигов, then at the village of Янковцы, then at Клишки; he served longest at Медведово, from 1915 to 1929, and was raised archpriest towards the end of that time.

In 1929 that church was closed, its building handed over to some «культурному» institution and afterwards pulled down. He was moved to the village of Логоватое in the Брянск region and served there a year, until that church too was closed; he was then appointed to Ярцево, and when the church there was closed was moved to the Михайловская church of the town of Стародуб, where he was rector until October 1937.

He was arrested on 20 October 1937, held first in the prison at Стародуб and then moved to Клинцы. His relatives did not know where he had been taken or why, and at the district NKVD office his wife Надежда Ивановна was told that he had been sent into exile. In fact on 22 November 1937 a troika of the NKVD administration for the Орёл region sentenced him to be shot, and he was shot at Клинцы on 7 December 1937. She died in 1955 knowing nothing of it, and the children learned of it only in 1989.

The page slips once, and it is worth saying so: in the sentence that records the sentence it writes «приговорила отца Михаила к расстрелу», Михаила for Митрофана, while the name is Митрофан everywhere else on it and on the calendar's own name page. Nothing else on the page disagrees with itself.
