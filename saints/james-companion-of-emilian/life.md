# James, companion of Emilian

saint.gr keeps three martyrs together on this day and gives them a single sentence: the holy [Emilian the Roman](/saints/emilian-the-roman), James and [Marianus](/saints/marianus-companion-of-emilian) were martyred at Rome in the year 259. That is the whole notice, and the page's head repeats the year over each of the three as «(; - 259)». There is no manner of death, no persecutor, no rank, no relic, no hymn and not even the distich the site usually prints.

So three things stand recorded here and no more: the name, the two men he died with, and the year, which falls in the persecution of Valerian that the page does not name. The only other thing under his name on that page is a gloss on the name itself — that James is a Hebrew word meaning the victor — which is an etymology and not a fact about the man.

The corpus keeps a good many men of this name, on other days and in other calendars, and nothing here tells him apart from them except his company and his year. He is recorded because the Greek calendar names him with Emilian and Marianus at Rome in 259, and for nothing else.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3876/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
