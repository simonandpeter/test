# Athanasius (Zhulanov)

The calendar's line is all there is of him. For 2 августа ст. ст. it prints «Мч. Афанасия (1918).» — the martyr Athanasius, 1918 — and behind the forename the name link gives his surname, so that the calendar's own fullest form of him is Афанасий Жуланов. There is no life page, no place, no trade, no manner of death and no second date; the rank it gives him is the martyr's grade without a priestly one, so what the source records of him is a layman killed in the first year of the persecution.

This folder therefore says only what the calendar says, which is a name, a day and a year. Nothing further about him has been read, and nothing about him should be read out of this folder.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 2 августа ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260802.html); read 13 October 2026.*
