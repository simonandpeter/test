# Erasmus (10 May)

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life. Its whole account of him is one sentence: the holy martyrs Erasmus and [Onesimus](/saints/onesimus-10-may) were martyred together with fourteen other martyrs, under the emperor Decius, who reigned from 249 to 251, along with the holy martyrs [Alpheus](/saints/alphaeus-martyr-10-may), [Cyprinus](/saints/cyprian-martyr-10-may) and [Philadelphus](/saints/philadelphus-martyr-10-may).

That is all of it. The page names no country, no judge, no accusation and no manner of death for him; it records no relics and no burial place, and prints neither apolytikion nor kontakion. The fourteen who died with them are counted and not named, so they stay in the calendar's line and are no folders here. The page's note on the name says only that Έρασμος comes from ἐρῶ and means the lovable.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/10/index.aspx) and [the life of the two](https://www.saint.gr/1541/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
