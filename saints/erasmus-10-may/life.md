# Erasmus (10 May)

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life. Its whole account of him is one sentence: the holy martyrs Erasmus and [Onesimus](/saints/onesimus-10-may) were martyred together with fourteen other martyrs, under the emperor Decius, who reigned from 249 to 251, along with the holy martyrs [Alpheus](/saints/alphaeus-martyr-10-may), [Cyprinus](/saints/cyprian-martyr-10-may) and [Philadelphus](/saints/philadelphus-martyr-10-may).

That is all of it. The page names no country, no judge, no accusation and no manner of death for him; it records no relics and no burial place, and prints neither apolytikion nor kontakion. The fourteen who died with them are counted and not named, so they stay in the calendar's line and are no folders here. The page's note on the name says only that Έρασμος comes from ἐρῶ and means the lovable.

The Russian church calendar keeps him on 10 мая ст. ст. in one line with four companions, «Мчч. Алфия, Филадельфа, Киприана, Онисима, Еразма и иных (251)», and gives the five of them a single life. It makes them men of Italy of the third century. Three of the five, Алфий, Филадельф and Киприан, were brothers and the sons of Вителий, the governor of Italy, baptised by the presbyter Онисим; and when Christians were ordered sought out and given over to torture, those three were sent to Rome with Онисим, with Еразм and with fourteen other Christians.

At Rome Онисим died of a heavy stone laid upon his breast, and Еразм was beheaded there with the fourteen. The three brothers were sent on from Rome to Sicily and suffered at Месополь in the territory of Леонтины, and the life dates that to 251 and the reign of Декий, having opened by setting the persecution under Ликиний; their relics were found at Леонтины in 1517. The calendar’s name link gives him Rome for his epithet and nothing else, and it names these five of the company and leaves fourteen unnamed.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/10/index.aspx) and [the life of the two](https://www.saint.gr/1541/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026; and after the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 10 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260510.html) and [the life the five share](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1031.htm), read 8 October 2026.*
