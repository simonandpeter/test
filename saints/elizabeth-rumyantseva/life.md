# Elizabeth (Rumyantseva)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names her last in a line of four and gives no life. Its line for 31 июля ст. ст. reads «Cщмч. Иоанна пресвитера, св. Константина исп., пресвитера, мц. Анны и св. Елисаветы исп (после 1937).» — with [John (Rumyantsev)](/saints/john-rumyantsev) a presbyter who died a hieromartyr, [Constantine (Razumov)](/saints/constantine-razumov) a confessor and presbyter, and [Anna (Serova)](/saints/anna-serova) a martyr. Behind the line the calendar's own name page gives her name in full as «Елисавета (Румянцева), исп.»

The calendar calls her a confessor, prints the rank abbreviation «исп» without its stop, and gives her no rank in the Church and no monastic name, so she is one of the laywomen who were held and not killed, as far as the line goes. The year is given as «после 1937», after 1937, which is a lower bound and not a year of death.

Nothing else is printed. The calendar names no place, no parish and no end for any of the four, and carries no life page and no hymn for them. She shares her surname with the presbyter who heads the line and with the martyr of 1928 whom the previous line names, and the calendar says nothing of any kinship.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 31 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260731.html); read 13 October 2026.*
