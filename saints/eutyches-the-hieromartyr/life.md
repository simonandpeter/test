# Eutyches, disciple of John the Theologian

Eutyches was a disciple of John the Evangelist — the synaxarion opens with John’s test of a true spirit, that every spirit which confesses Jesus Christ come in the flesh is of God, and says that Eutyches proved it by his life.

He preached the Gospel bravely, pulled down many idol temples, endured beatings and spent many years in chains. He was thrown into fire, then to hungry beasts; one of the beasts spoke with a human voice, and he came through the rest of the torments unharmed, so that all were astonished. Because of these wonders they let him go free to his own country, Sebaste, where he gave up his spirit to the Lord in peace. His memory is kept again on 30 May.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 24 August — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/423/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
