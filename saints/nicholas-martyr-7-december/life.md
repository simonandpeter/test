# Nicholas (7 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 7 December keeps three martyrs in one entry and names all three: [Priscus](/saints/priscus-martyr-7-december), [Martin](/saints/martin-martyr-7-december) and Nicholas. Of Nicholas it says only that he was martyred by fire. No country, no reign, no persecutor and no year is printed for him or for the other two.

The page gives each man his own couplet, and his names the decree he refused: a decree went out to sacrifice to the demons of error, and not sacrificing to them, Nicholas, you are yourself sacrificed in the fire. The festival of all three, the page adds, is kept near the wall of the Blachernae. The calendar keeps many other men of this name on other days, and nothing here joins this one to any of them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3196/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
