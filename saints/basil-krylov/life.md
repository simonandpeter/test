# Basil (Krylov)

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar for 6 ноября ст. ст. names him alone on the last line of the day, and in a different year from the eleven above him: «Сщмч. Василия пресвитера (1938).» — the rank word «Сщмч.», the forename in the genitive, the grade «пресвитера», and 1938. The name link behind the forename supplies the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line leaves out, «Василий (Крылов), иерей, сщмч.», and that form is what this folder stores.

That is everything the calendar records of him. He has no life page, no icon, no hymn, no place, no age and no parish anywhere on this calendar, and the name page behind the line carries its title, two days and no text at all. That page also lists «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which is a synaxis and not a second feast; this folder renders on 6 ноября ст. ст. only. Nothing further is written here, and in particular nothing is reasoned from the surname.

The line's «Сщмч.» and «пресвитера» make him a hieromartyr and a presbyter, and the link's «иерей» is recorded as his office, Priest. No relation is written between him and the eleven of 1937 the day page names before him: a different line, a different year, and nothing on either page that puts them in one place. The corpus holds no other folder whose surname is «Крылов».
