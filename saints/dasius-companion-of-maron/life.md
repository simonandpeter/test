# Dasius, companion of Maron

saint.gr's calendar for 18 July names two men on one line, «Άγιοι Δάσιος και Μάρων», and the page behind it gives them one sentence between them: Dasius and [Maron](/saints/maron-companion-of-dasius) were martyred by the sword. There is no year, no city, no emperor, no judge, nothing of their lives before it, and no hymn for either of them.

The only other thing the page prints is the Synaxarion's couplet, which divides the one death between the two: «H της κεφαλής εκτομή του Δασίου, / Tέλους ομοίου σοι Mάρων δαδουχία» — the cutting off of Dasius's head is the torch that lights you, Maron, to a like end. So Dasius went first and Maron after him, and that order, said in verse and nowhere in prose, is the whole of what distinguishes them here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2150/saint.aspx), which the two share; read 1 October 2026.*
