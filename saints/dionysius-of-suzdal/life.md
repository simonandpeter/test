# Dionysius of Suzdal

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Ιουνίου names him and gives him no life on this day. Its line reads «Άγιος Διονύσιος Αρχιεπίσκοπος Σουζδαλίας», the page behind that line is headed «Αγιος Διονυσιος Αρχιεπισκοπος Σουζδαλιας (; - 1385)», and the whole of its text is a pointer to another day: «Για τον Άγιο Διονύσιο βλέπε στις 15 Οκτωβρίου» — for Saint Dionysius see at 15 October.

So the Greek calendar keeps him twice over, on 26 June and on 15 October, and holds whatever life it has on the later of the two days. What is recorded here is the day page that was read, which is this one; the pointer is set down in the attestation's note and the October page has not been opened. The year of death, 1385, is the only other thing the page prints, with the year of birth left as a question mark, and it glosses the name itself as meaning one of divine origin. There is no hymn, no see beyond its name, and no narrative of any kind.

The second of saint.gr's two days for him, 15 Οκτωβρίου, has since been read, and it is the one that holds the life. The line there is «Άγιος Διονύσιος Αρχιεπίσκοπος Σουζδαλίας», the page behind it is headed «Αγιος Διονυσιος Αρχιεπισκοπος Σουζδαλιας (; - 1385)» as the June one is, and its whole text is a single sentence: that Saint Dionysius was a monk in the monastery of the Caves of Nizhny Novgorod and afterwards archbishop of Suzdal, until his death on 15 October 1385. That is three things the June page does not have — the monastery, the order of his life, and the day of his death — and the day of his death is the second of the two days the calendar keeps him on. The attestation row still records the June page, because that is the day this folder was written from and one row is what the schema holds; the October page is cited below beside it.
