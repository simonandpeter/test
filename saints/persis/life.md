# Persis

The Greek synaxarion knows Persis, or Persida, only from older books: Delehaye’s Synaxarion records her on 23 and 24 September, and the Paris codex 3041 on 24 September, with a distich — Persa, who went to sea for a little while, has put in at the great harbour of heaven. It says nothing more of her, and gives no date.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 24 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2492/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
