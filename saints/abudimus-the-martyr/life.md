# Abudimus

doxologia.ro's calendar for 16 iulie gives Abudimus two sentences and no more. It dates his death 303 to 305 and says he received the crown of martyrdom in the time of the emperor Diocletian, whose reign the page dates 284 to 305. He was seized and pressed to sacrifice to the idols, and because he refused he was put to countless torments and at the last died a martyr's death by the sword.

The page names no place and no judge, and prints no troparion for him. The English form of his name here is a chosen one; doxologia prints only the Romanian «Avudin».

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 16 iulie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/16-iulie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-mucenic-avudin); read 24 September 2026.*
