# Abudimus

doxologia.ro's calendar for 16 iulie gives Abudimus two sentences and no more. It dates his death 303 to 305 and says he received the crown of martyrdom in the time of the emperor Diocletian, whose reign the page dates 284 to 305. He was seized and pressed to sacrifice to the idols, and because he refused he was put to countless torments and at the last died a martyr's death by the sword.

The page names no place and no judge, and prints no troparion for him. The English form of his name here is a chosen one; doxologia prints only the Romanian «Avudin».

The Greek calendar keeps him a day earlier, on 15 July, and saint.gr tells more of him than the Romanian line did. It calls him a courageous soldier of Christ, says he came from the island of Tenedos, and counts him among the first whole-burnt offerings of the faith in the persecution against the Church under Diocletian. Because he would not sacrifice to the idols they bound him and beat him hard; but Abudimus had one answer always in his mouth — «Είμαι Χριστιανός», I am a Christian. Then they tore his sides with iron claws, and because he went on confessing Christ they put him to death by beheading. The page gives no year for any of it.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 16 iulie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/16-iulie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-mucenic-avudin); read 24 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 15 Ιουλίου — [the Greek day](https://www.saint.gr/07/15/index.aspx) and [the Greek life](https://www.saint.gr/2176/saint.aspx), read 1 October 2026.*
