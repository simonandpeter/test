# Maximus of Ungrovlachia

Maximus was born in Serbia, the son of the ruler of the Serbs, Saint Stephen Branković the Blind, whom the Greek calendar honours on 10 December, and of Saint Angelina, whom it honours on 30 July. His name in the world was George. The young prince left the glory of the world and became a monk at the monastery of Manasija in central Serbia, and because of the Turkish raids he took refuge in Romania, where he was ordained Bishop of Ungrovlachia.

When he had come to a great age he withdrew to the monastery of Krušedol in Serbia, and there, after a long illness, he fell asleep in the year 1546. saint.gr says that his relic lies incorrupt at the monastery of Kupinovo in Serbia. The page gives him no year of birth, no year for his consecration, and prints no hymn for him.

The Ohrid Prologue keeps him on this day and adds three things saint.gr does not. It names his parents as the Serbian despot Stefan the Blind and the despotess Angelina, and his tonsure as being at the monastery of Manasija; it says that as archbishop he reconciled two warring voivodes and so prevented a war between them — „Измирио завађене војводе Радула и Богдана и спречио рат међу њима“; and it says that the monastery of Krušedol, to which he withdrew in his later years, was one he himself raised. On the relics the two calendars disagree: the Prologue says his incorrupt and wonderworking relics lie at Krušedol to this day, where saint.gr puts them at Kupinovo. It gives his repose as 18 January 1546, the day it keeps him on.
