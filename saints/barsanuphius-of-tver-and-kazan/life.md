# Barsanuphius of Tver and Kazan

saint.gr says Barsanuphius was Basil in the world, and was born in 1495 at Serpukhov in Russia of a priestly family. While he was still young he was taken captive by the Tatars of the Crimea; and receiving that as the will of God he submitted humbly to his masters and carried out willingly every work they set him. After three years, though, his father managed to buy him back and to take him home.

He became a monk in 1515 in the monastery of the Saviour, of Saint Andronicus, at Moscow, and served as hierodeacon in the diocese of Tver. His gifts and his God-loving life led Macarius, metropolitan of Moscow, to place him in the monastery of Saint Nicholas of Peshnosha, near Moscow; and later he went to Kazan and founded a monastery dedicated to the Transfiguration of the Saviour. While he was at Kazan, the page says, the archimandrite Barsanuphius helped Saint Gurias, whom it keeps on 15 Δεκεμβρίου, in the spreading of the Christian faith among the Muslims and the idolaters, and his knowledge of the Tatar tongue proved very useful to that mission.

In the year 1567 he was elected bishop of Tver and Kazan. When he had come to a great age he went back to Kazan and to the monastery of the Transfiguration which he himself had founded; there he received the Great Angelic Habit and fell asleep in peace, in the year 1576.

The relics of Gurias and of Barsanuphius were discovered on 4 October 1596 and placed in reliquaries in the chapel of the church, according to the directions of the patriarch Job; and on 20 June 1630 they were carried from the monastery of the Transfiguration to the cathedral church of the Annunciation of the Mother of God. Neither of those days is his feast, and the page keeps him on 11 April.

**The Russian church calendar keeps him on the Julian 4 October as the finding of his relics**, in one line with Gurias of Kazan: «Обретение мощей свтт. Гурия, архиеп. Казанского, и Варсонофия, еп. Тверского (1595)». The Greek keeps him on 11 April instead, for his repose. The Russian names him only for Tver where the Greek names him for Tver and Kazan both, and the relic finding is the one line on the whole day with a rank above the plain one.
