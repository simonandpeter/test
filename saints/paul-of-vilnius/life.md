# Paul of Vilnius

saint.gr names him in one sentence and gives no life. The holy martyr Paul lived in the seventeenth century, its page says, and was martyred at Vilnius of Russia in the period of the monarchy of the Lithuanians. That is the whole of the notice: no year, no trade, no account of the trial or of the death, no relic and no hymn.

The one sentence does not hold together geographically, and it is printed here as it stands rather than corrected: Vilnius is called Russian and the martyrdom is placed under the Lithuanian monarchy in the same breath. What the day's listing adds is only the origin, «εκ Ρωσίας», out of Russia.
