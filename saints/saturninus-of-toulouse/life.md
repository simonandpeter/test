# Saturninus of Toulouse

The calendar names him, gives him his see, calls him its first bishop, and stops. The Сретенский monastery's calendar for 29 ноября ст. ст. prints «Сщмч. Сатурнина , первого епископа Тулузского (III).» — the hieromartyr Saturninus, first bishop of Toulouse, of the third century — and the name page behind that name repeats the see twice and adds nothing: no life page, no hymn, no place of death, no year.

So three facts are the record: that he was a hieromartyr, that he was the first bishop of Toulouse, and that it was in the third century. Nothing is said here of where he came from, who sent him, how he died or where he was buried, because no page read says any of it. The corpus keeps eight other men of the name on other days, and not one of them is of Toulouse.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 29 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261129.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14896.html), which carries no life; read 7 October 2026.*
