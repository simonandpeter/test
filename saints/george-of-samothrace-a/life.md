# George of Samothrace (first of the two)

saint.gr keeps five new martyrs together on this day, and refers their life to another day than this one: «Βλέπε βιογραφικό τους σημείωμα την Κυριακή του Θωμά» — see their biographical note on Thomas Sunday. What the 6 April page itself gives is the names, the year and the place: George, [Manuel](/saints/manuel-of-samothrace), [Theodore](/saints/theodore-of-samothrace), [a second George](/saints/george-of-samothrace-b) and [Michael](/saints/michael-of-cyprus-of-samothrace), each headed «(; - 1835)». The day's line calls all five from Samothrace; the page's own list makes Michael from Cyprus.

Its couplet praises the island rather than telling the story: «Χορὸν τὸν πεντάριθμον νέων Μαρτύρων, / Σαμοθράκη γεραίρε νῦν ἐπαξίως» — Samothrace, honour worthily now the choir of five new martyrs.

The hymns say a little more. The apolytikion calls them lamps of Samothrace and the adornment of Makri, shown to be new martyrs indeed, who contended steadfastly for Christ and undid the error of the enemy, and it names them as Manuel with Theodore and Michael «καὶ οἱ διττοὶ Γεώργιοι», and the two Georges. The kontakion says they preached Christ our God with a bright voice to most savage barbarians. The megalynarion calls the five bright sons of the island of Samothrace and of the country of Makri, its great glory and its guards, and physicians and saviours of the sick.

This folder is for the first of the two Georges. The page distinguishes the two only by their place in its list and in the day's line, and nothing here goes beyond that.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/06/index.aspx) and [the page of the five](https://www.saint.gr/192/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
