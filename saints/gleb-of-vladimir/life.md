# Gleb of Vladimir

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 20 июня ст. ст. names him «Блгв. кн. Глеба Владимирского (сына св. Андрея Боголюбского) (1175).», and gives him a life page of its own. In holy baptism he was George, it says, and he was the youngest son of the prince of Vladimir whose name the day line puts in that parenthesis. Under devout parents he grew up deeply believing, and from the age of twelve he led a solitary spiritual life; his parents did not hinder him in it and even helped him to it.

He particularly loved the reading of the holy books, the page says, honoured the clergy and was merciful to everybody, and young as he was he chose for himself the labour of strict fasting and of vigil in prayer. He died at nineteen. The day line gives the year as 1175 and the life page as 1174, and this folder records both and prefers neither.

His incorrupt relics were kept and were glorified by wonders. In 1238, when the Tatars under Batu came against the Russian land, they set fire to the cathedral at Vladimir, and in that fire the bishop, the grand princess and a great number of the townspeople who had shut themselves into the cathedral church were burned; the fire did not so much as touch his tomb. In July 1410 the Tatars fell on Vladimir again, and, plundering the cathedral treasury and supposing that treasure was hidden in the tomb, they tried to break it open; as soon as they touched the stone coffin a flame came out of it, and they left the city in fear.

By the prince’s prayers, the page adds, the city was saved from the Polish and Lithuanian invaders in 1613. His festival was appointed in 1702, and in the same year a service was written for him and somewhat later a life; his relics rest in the cathedral of the Dormition at Vladimir, and in 1774 the southern chapel of that cathedral was consecrated in his name. He is honoured as the particular patron and defender of the city.

The day page carries five hymns besides the two it prints under his name — three for his father, who is not an entry of this day, and two for the translation of another hierarch’s relics — and the two that are his are a troparion and a kontakion, both of which name him and the city.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 20 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260620.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1358.htm); read 9 October 2026.*
