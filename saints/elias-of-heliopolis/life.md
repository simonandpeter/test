# Elias of Heliopolis

saint.gr's calendar for 1 February names Elias the great-martyr and gives him a single sentence: that he bore witness at Heliopolis in Lebanon in the year 799. That is the whole of the page.

So three things are recorded, because three things are given: the rank the Greek calendar puts on him, the city, and the year. There is no year of birth, nothing of his family or trade, nothing of the trial or of the manner of his death, no place of burial, and no apolytikion and no kontakion. The epithet in the English name here is the city the page names, which is the only thing it says about where he was.

The Greek calendar keeps another Elias on the thirty-first of January, the venerable-martyr Ardounis of Kalamata, and he is not this man.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1346/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
