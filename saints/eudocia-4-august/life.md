# Eudocia (4 August)

Her reading is one paragraph and the calendar gives her no epithet at all, so she is kept here under her day. She was a Roman woman of rank, living in the fourth century, and she was taken captive with nine thousand Christians by the armies of the Persian king Shapur. In captivity she preached among the Persian women and turned many of them to Christ, and for that she was put to long and cruel torments and beheaded, about the years 362 to 364.

The page behind her name link carries two readings and the second is of another woman of the same nine thousand, a martyr taken to the Persian city of Bizada, pressed by the chief of the Persian magi to deny Christ, tortured, imprisoned, tortured again and beheaded. That second woman is kept by this calendar on 11 сентября and the corpus already holds her folder from that day; nothing of hers is claimed here, and the two are not one. The source lists both these days, 4 августа and 11 сентября, in the head of each reading.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 4 августа ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260804.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4376.htm); read 13 October 2026.*
