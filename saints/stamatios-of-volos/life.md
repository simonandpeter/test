# Stamatios of Volos

Stamatios was a peasant from the country around Volos in Thessaly. When an inhuman agha collecting the imperial taxes abused the people, he went with a few companions to Constantinople to complain to the vizier.

His sharp accusations against the agha offended the sultan’s grandees, and they had him arrested. First they tried flattery to make him a Turk, promising wealth, glory and honour; the martyr cried out: “My wealth, glory and honour is my Christ!” He was tortured and at last beheaded in front of Hagia Sophia in 1680. So, the Prologue says, this soldier of Christ was crowned with the martyr’s wreath.
