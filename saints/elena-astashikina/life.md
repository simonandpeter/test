# Elena (Astashikina)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Elena third in a company of eight on 28 July and gives no life for any of them. The entry is «Прмч. Василия , прмцц. Анастасии и Елены , мчч. Арефы , Иоанна , Иоанна , Иоанна и мц. Мавры (1937).» — a hieromonk, two nuns, four laymen of whom three bear one name, and a laywoman, all with the single year 1937 — and each of the eight has her own numbered name page, which is the whole of what tells them apart.

Her own name page is «Елена (Асташикина), инокиня, прмц.», Elena Astashikina, nun. Line and name page agree about her: a nun and a venerable-martyr. She died in 1937 with the seven others of this entry, and the calendar keeps them together on this day among the new martyrs of Russia.

The company is recorded here as `related` on all eight folders. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 28 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260728.html); read 13 October 2026.*
