# George (Arkhangelsky)

He is the second of the three presbyters the Russian calendar keeps in one line on 1 October with a layman beside them, all four under the year 1937. The day line prints «Георгия» and leaves the rank to the plural «пресвитеров» that follows the three names; the site's index of names heads his page «Георгий (Архангельский), иерей, сщмч.», which makes him a priest where it makes [Alexander (Agafonnikov)](/saints/alexander-agafonnikov) and [Nicholas (Kuligin)](/saints/nicholas-kuligin) archpriests.

His surname, like Michael (Vologodsky)'s on the same page, is one of the place-name surnames the Russian clergy carried, Arkhangelsk's. The index keeps him here and on 26 January with the company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and prints no birth, no diocese, no parish, no charge and no place of death. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line.

*After the Russian church calendar's [1 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261001.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9549.html); read 4 October 2026.*
