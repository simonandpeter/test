# Moses of Optina

Moses, schema-archimandrite and superior of the Optina hermitage, was born on 15 January 1782 in the town of Borisoglebsk in the province of Yaroslavl, and received the name Timothy at holy baptism. He was the first child of the pious merchant Ivan Grigorievich Putilov, and, brought up both in his father's house and in the church of God, he became the spiritual guide of his own brothers. Coming of age and seeing the vanity of earthly life, drawn by the spirit of the desert, Timothy went with his brother Jonah to visit a number of monasteries and well-known elders; those frequent living conversations had their blessed effect, and the fire of the monastic struggle settled in them unnoticed.

The brothers made their beginning at the Sarov hermitage, where they found the great elder [Seraphim of Sarov](/saints/seraphim-of-sarov). Three years later, "by ways unknown to him", the novice Timothy came among the forest monks of Roslavl, and lived ten years among those dwellers in the wilderness, receiving the tonsure there with the name Moses. But a city set on a hilltop cannot be hidden, and Moses, together with his brother the venerable Anthony, was called to a new labour.

He was called to Optina, at first to create and order the skete of Saint John the Forerunner, and in a short time became superior of the hermitage; from that time began the monastery's spiritual growth and its renown, and under him the house was transfigured. His repose came on 16 June 1862, in the eighty-first year of his life.

The page says he had not left the monastery even after his death, and records what followed. A young woman of the house, Nadezhda, slipped and fell into a vessel of boiling water and was badly scalded; she lived two days, and died having received all the Mysteries. Before her death a woman herding the cows saw Moses in a light sleep, in his mantle and with his staff, going towards the church where Nadezhda had been laid; taking his blessing she asked him where he was hurrying, and he answered, "I am going to escort Nadezhda." Later his relics were found incorrupt: at the burial of the venerable Anthony, the schemamonk Nestor and the abbot Mark opened the vault and found Moses's coffin whole despite the damp of the earth, and when they opened it, the elder's body had not decayed.
