# Eusebius of Corfu

The Greek calendar keeps four men of Corfu together on 29 Απριλίου — Eusebius, Zeno, Vitalius and Neon — and saint.gr's whole notice of them is one paragraph. These saints, it says, were of the inhabitants of Corfu, the first who were drawn by the preaching of the holy apostles [Jason](/saints/jason-of-tarsus) and [Sosipater](/saints/sosipater-of-iconium), and they became fervent fellow workers in their apostolic labour. The ruler Kerkyllinos hunted them down, and having seized them he stripped them and scourged them hard.

Because they refused to sacrifice to the idols he shut them in prison; and afterwards, having lit a great fire, he threw them into it, and so they were counted worthy of the immortal crown of martyrdom. The page gives no year, no country of origin and no kindred for any of the four. Its couplets take them in three groups: to Eusebius, that the fire is what will scour the filth away and leave him refined gold of Christ; to Zeno and Vitalius, «Μὴ δειλιάσῃς, Βιτάλιε, πρὸς φλόγα, / Ἡγήσομαι γάρ, καὶ προεισέλθω Ζήνων» — be not afraid of the flame, Vitalius, for I, Zeno, will lead the way and go in first; and to Neon, that by being burned, and refusing to serve the gods of the Greeks, he pulled the faith of the Greeks down.

saint.gr heads him «Άγιος Ευσέβιος», and the other three of the company are [Zeno](/saints/zeno-of-corfu), [Vitalius](/saints/vitalius-of-corfu) and [Neon](/saints/neon-of-corfu).

The Russian calendar's own reading of the island names him among the four who were burned, and says why. After the ruler's daughter «Керкира» had been hanged on a tree, choked with bitter smoke and shot through with arrows for confessing Christ, her father resolved to put every Christian on Corfu to death; and «Мученики Зинон, Евсевий, Неон и Виталий, просвещенные апостолами Иасоном и Сосипатром, были сожжены» — the four, enlightened by the apostles, were burned. The calendar sets the apostles' arrival on the island in 63 and heads the twelve martyrs «ок. 63». A separate two-sentence page behind the name link of the last of the four adds only that they suffered together and that the burning came after torture, «по истязании были сожжены».

*After saint.gr's calendar for 29 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/29/index.aspx) and [the entry for the four](https://www.saint.gr/368/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 28 апреля ст. ст. — [the Russian day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260428.html) and [the life of the company](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life946.htm), read 8 October 2026.*
