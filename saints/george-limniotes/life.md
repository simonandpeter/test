# George Limniotes

George loved the monastic life from childhood, and when he came of age he became a monk and lived the ascetic life on the Olympus of Bithynia.

In the years of the iconoclast emperor Leo the Isaurian (716) he contended for Orthodoxy and rebuked the impious emperor sharply; for this he suffered many torments — at the age of ninety-five. At the end they cut off his nose and burned his head, and so he gave up his spirit to God. The synaxarion’s verse says he bore the severed nose as the mark of his contest.

The Russian and Serbian calendars keep him on 24 August (Julian): a monk of the Olympus monastery near Constantinople in the eighth century, he suffered for the veneration of icons under the iconoclast Leo the Isaurian — his head was burned and his nose cut off — and died about 716, a great ascetic of Olympus, the Prologue says, ninety-five years old.
