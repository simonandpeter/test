# Sophronius of Bulgaria

saint.gr says he was Stephen in the world, came of Bulgaria, and lived in the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries. He was presbyter of the church community of Penkyovtsi, near Sofia, and because of the Turkish raids he took refuge in Wallachia, at the court of the voivode Radul. The page gives no year for the flight and none for his birth.

Afterwards he practised the ascetic life in the love of God in a monastery of the district of Ruschuk — perhaps, the page says, in the one founded by the patriarch of Tarnovo [Joachim](/saints/joachim-of-tarnovo), whom it marks as kept on 18 January — and he was marked out for his love of man and his almsgiving. He reposed in peace. The page gives no year of birth, no year of death and no relics, and prints no hymn.
