# Stephen (Yermolin), Presbyter, Hieromartyr (1937)

Stephen Vasilievich Yermolin was born on 22 May 1891 in the village of Chasovo to a peasant family, busy from small in the peasant holding, and finished the primary school; by unconfirmed reports he fought in the First World War, and in 1919–1922 served in the Red Army as a platoon commander and assistant company commander, returning after demobilisation to peasant work. A deeply believing man, he resolved to give himself to the service of the Lord and in 1926 was ordained; in the mid-thirties he was priest of the Exaltation church at Nizhny Konets in Syktyvkar, once attached to the Trinity cathedral of Ust-Sysolsk, and in 1936 on an anonymous denunciation was prosecuted — the calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. Arrested in 1937 in the fabricated case of the Sacred Brotherhood and charged with counter-revolutionary activity, he was sentenced to be shot and executed on 15 September 1937; he was numbered among the saints in 2001, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 2 September (Julian) with Bishop [Herman](/saints/herman-of-vyazniki) and the martyr [Paul Yelkin](/saints/paul-martyr-1937-2-september).
