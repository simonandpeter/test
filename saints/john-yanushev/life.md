# John (Yanushev)

The Сретенский monastery's calendar names him eleventh in its clause of fifteen for 25 ноября ст. ст. and carries no life for him; azbyka.ru prints one out of fond.ru. Иван Васильевич Янушев was born in Moscow on 6 May 1877, the son of a deacon. He finished the Moscow seminary in 1897 and the Kazan theological academy in 1902, became psalm-reader of the Archangel cathedral in the Kremlin in 1903, and on 18 May 1908 was appointed priest of the Spassky cathedral of Moscow.

On 22 September 1917 he was moved to the Annunciation cathedral of the Kremlin and made acting sacellarius of it. When the Soviet power closed all the Kremlin cathedrals and monasteries he was appointed rector of the church of [Spyridon of Trimythous](/saints/spyridon-of-trimythous) on Spiridonovka, and served there until it too was closed and pulled down; from 1 August 1930 he served at the church of the Nativity of Christ in Палаши. He was raised archpriest in 1920 and given the palitsa in 1924 and a jewelled cross in 1927, and in 1935 was appointed to the Dormition church at Вешняки.

He was arrested on 16 November 1937 and held in the Taganka prison, and the questioning began on 23 November. He said he had not served in the tsar's army; that he had been tried in 1937 for baptising a child without its parents' consent and sentenced to a year of forced labour, and that the appeal bench had acquitted him; and that he did not admit being a counter-revolutionary, had carried on no counter-revolutionary activity, and was not guilty of the charge. One of the false witnesses testified that in September he had spoken to her near the station about the constitution, and had said that it was written in fine phrases, that the communists could only write fine declarations and shout on every corner about universal elections and freedom of religion while the same communists closed churches and would not let parents baptise their children, and that soon the end would come for the persecutors and their constitution.

The troika sentenced him on 1 December 1937. He was shot on 8 December 1937 on the Butovo range outside Moscow and buried in an unmarked common grave. On the day of the shooting the photographer of the Taganka prison photographed him, so that in the mass executions then being carried out the executioner should not make a mistake.
