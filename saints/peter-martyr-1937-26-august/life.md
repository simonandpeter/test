# Peter (Bordan), Martyr (1937)

Peter Bordan was a prisoner of the Karaganda camp with [Demetrius Morozov](/saints/demetrius-martyr-1937-26-august), a peasant of Vyatka province sentenced in 1937 for taking part in a counter-revolutionary group of churchmen. On 7 August 1937 a report on the two reached the camp’s commissioner: the prisoners Morozov and Bordan do no work, but systematically carry on anti-Soviet agitation; they hold that the Soviet power is of Antichrist, built on violence, and that one must serve only God. Charged with anti-Soviet agitation in the camp, both were shot on 3 September 1937; the Russian calendar keeps them together on 26 August (Julian), and the calendar at azbyka.ru tells Peter’s story in Demetrius’s life.

*After the life printed by the Russian church calendar at azbyka.ru — [the entry](https://azbyka.ru/days/sv-dimitrij-morozov); read 23 August 2026.*
