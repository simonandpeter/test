# Gabriel (Vsevolod Mstislavich)

saint.gr's calendar for 11 Φεβρουαρίου names Saint Gabriel the king, and his page gives him his civil name and almost nothing else: «Ο Άγιος Γαβριήλ (Βσεβολόδος Μστισλάβιτς), υιός του μεγάλου Ρώσου ηγεμόνος, επί πολλά έτη καταπολέμησε, ως Πρίγκιπας του Νόβγκοροντ, τις διαμάχες μεταξύ των διαφόρων Ρώσων ηγεμόνων» — Gabriel, Vsevolod Mstislavich, son of the great Russian prince, fought for many years, as prince of Novgorod, against the quarrels among the various Russian princes.

After his repose in the year 1138, the page goes on, the Russian Church proclaimed him a saint and a wonderworker in the year 1549. That is the whole of it: a parentage without a name, a see, a lifetime of work against the feuds of princes, a year of death and a year of glorification four centuries later. No hymn, no relics and no distich are printed for him, and the day's line and his own page disagree about whether to call him king or prince.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/11/index.aspx) and [his page](https://www.saint.gr/3643/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 11 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260211.html), read 3 October 2026.*
