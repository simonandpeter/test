# Alexandra (Chervyakova)

She is the one monastic among the eleven the Russian calendar keeps in a single line on 30 September, standing between a protodeacon and three lay martyrs, with 1937 given once for the whole company. The day line marks her «прмц.» — venerable-martyr, the rank given to a monastic who dies a martyr — and the site's index of names heads her page «Александра (Червякова), схимонахиня, прмц.», which makes her a schemanun, a nun of the great schema, the strictest of the monastic degrees.

That is the whole of it. The index keeps her here and on 26 January with the company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and prints no birth, no monastery, no charge and no place of death — not even which house tonsured her, which for a schemanun is the fact one would most want. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line.

*After the Russian church calendar's [30 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260930.html) and [its index entry for her](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13810.html); read 4 October 2026.*
