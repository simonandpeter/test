# Alexander (Shklyaev)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him second of four in one line on 10 декабря and gives none of the four a life. The line is «Сщмчч. Иакова и Александра пресвитеров, сщмч. Евграфа пресвитера и сына его мч. Михаила (1918).» — the hieromartyrs James and Alexander, presbyters, the hieromartyr Eugraphus the presbyter and his son the martyr Michael, 1918 — and the calendar’s own name pages add the surnames the line leaves out, his being «Александр (Шкляев), протоиерей, сщмч.»

That is the whole of the record. He was an archpriest, which is what the name page says where the line says only presbyter; he died in 1918, the first year of the persecution in which the Russian church counts its new martyrs; and he is set down with three others, of whom two bear one surname and are named father and son. What joined him to them the calendar does not say, and nothing is supplied here.

The day page carries eleven hymns, for a company of martyrs of the fourth century and for three of the saints it heads, and not one of them is under any of these four names. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 10 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261210.html); read 13 October 2026.*
