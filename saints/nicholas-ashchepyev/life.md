# Nicholas (Ashchepyev)

Nicholas — the igumen Nicholas (Ashchepyev) — was an igumen, shot on 20
September 1937, 7 September of the old style, in the Karaganda camp in
Kazakhstan together with Metropolitan [Eugene
(Zernov)](/saints/eugene-zernov-metropolitan-of-gorky) of Nizhny Novgorod
and with the igumen Eugene (Vyzhva), the hieromonk Pachomius (Ionov) and the
priest Stephen Kreydich, by sentence of the troika of the Karaganda NKVD
passed that September. The Russian church calendar at azbyka.ru keeps the
five under one life, which is the metropolitan’s — his seminary rectorship
at Irkutsk, the Amur see, the arrest of 1923 that a whole town rose against,
the Solovki epistle of 1926 he helped to write, his last see at Gorky and
his arrest after Pascha of 1935 for riding home through the May Day crowds
in his klobuk, since, he said, it is God one must fear — and says of the
four who died with him only that they died with him; nothing more of his own
life is printed there, and nothing more is set down here. All five were
numbered among the new martyrs and confessors of Russia by the Jubilee
Council of Bishops in August 2000 for veneration by the whole church. The
Russian calendar keeps him on 7 September of the old style, 20 September,
and in the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Kazakhstan.

*After the joint life printed by the Russian church calendar at azbyka.ru — [the entry](https://azbyka.ru/days/sv-evgenij-vyzhva), read 6 September 2026; until then this entry had only the Sretensky calendar’s line.*
