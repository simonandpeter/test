# Gregory (Vorobyov)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him last of three on 22 октября ст. ст., in a short line of three men of 1937 — «Сщмчч. Николая , Николая пресвитера и прмч. Григория (1937).» — that comes just after a longer company headed by an archbishop. The line declines his name and gives it without a surname; the title on the name link behind his place supplies both the surname and the grade, «Григорий (Воробьев), иеромонах, сщмч.». The line sets him apart from the two priests with «прмч.», venerable-martyr, which is what his types record, and the link behind his place says сщмч., hieromartyr, and adds the rank of hieromonk, which the line does not print. Where the two disagree the line's word is kept and the link's is written down here.

The calendar carries no life page for any of the three, and the day page says nothing of him beyond his rank, his name and the year. Nothing on it says where he was born or served, what he was charged with, or where he died.

The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line. The day page's eight troparia and kontakia all belong to the two ancient entries it heads and to the icon it keeps, and none of them to this company of 1937.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 22 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261022.html); read 4 October 2026.*
