# Ahaz

saint.gr's whole notice for him is one sentence: the righteous Ahaz died peacefully. Everything else the page has to say about him is in the distich it prints above, and that distich is what identifies him: Ahaz stands near to God; Ahaz, who said, I will not tempt my God. The words are from the seventh chapter of Isaiah, where the king of Judah answers the prophet's offer of a sign, and it is on the strength of that verse that the Greek calendar keeps him among the righteous of the old covenant.

There is no year on the page, no place, no kin, no deed, no relic and no hymn. His day is a fixed one, the first of April, and the page says so plainly; the rank the calendar gives him is δίκαιος, righteous, which is the word it uses for the just of Israel rather than for a martyr or a monastic.

This corpus keeps nobody else of the name. The folder is here because the Greek calendar prints him, and it says no more about him than the Greek calendar does.
