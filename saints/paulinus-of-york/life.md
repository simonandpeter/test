# Paulinus of York

saint.gr's calendar for 10 Οκτωβρίου names «Άγιος Paulinus Αρχιεπίσκοπος Υόρκης», writing the name in Latin letters as the English write it and glossing it in Greek as Παυλίνος. Its page gives two sentences and a date and nothing else: that Paulinus was the first Archbishop of York, and that he fell asleep at Rochester in Kent in the year 644. The head of the page writes his dates as «(; - 644)», with the year of his birth unknown.

That is the whole of the record. The page says nothing of where he came from, of the mission that brought him to Northumbria, of whom he baptised, or of why he ended his life in Kent rather than in his own see; and it prints no hymn for him. The only saints it sets beside him are Hilda and the synaxis of the saints who shone in Scotland, whose pages it links as neighbours without saying anything that joins them to him. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/10/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2689/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
