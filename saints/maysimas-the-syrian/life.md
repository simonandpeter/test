# Maysimas the Syrian

The venerable Maysimas's grammatical learning, saint.gr says, was moderate; but he was marked out for the great virtue of his life. His outward appearance was rather ugly, and yet, while he dressed himself in old clothes, his works of charity were rich and past counting. His cell stayed open always to the poor and to strangers, and being supplied with wheat and oil he shared them out to as many of them as had need.

When he learned once that the lord of their town was oppressing the farmers, he did not hesitate to present himself before that proud and brightly dressed egotist in his own poor clothes, and to give him lessons in justice and nobility. With such holy occupations, the page says, he finished his life pleasing to God. The distich over him plays on the tongues he spoke: «Γλώσσαις λαλῶν πρὶν Μαϋσιμᾶς τῶν Σύρων, Γλώσσαις, λαλεῖ νῦν, Ἀγγέλων πρὸς Ἀγγέλους».

No year of his birth or of his repose is printed, and no hymn. The page closes by noting that his memory is repeated on 13 February, under the name Maÿoumas or Maïoumas; that day's page has not been read here, and the folder keeps the one day with a note.

The Russian calendar keeps him on the same menologion day, «Прп. Мавсимы Сирина (IV)», putting him in the fourth century, and its name link calls him иерей, a priest, which the Greek calendar's line did not. It prints no hymn for him.
