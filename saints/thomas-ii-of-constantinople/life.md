# Thomas the Second, the New

saint.gr's calendar for 15 November keeps «Άγιος Θωμάς ο Β' ο νέος, Πατριάρχης Κωνσταντινούπολης» — Thomas the second, the new, patriarch of Constantinople — and dates his death 669. He was a deacon and chartophylax of Hagia Sophia, the page says on the word of Nikephoros Kallistos; he was elected patriarch of Constantinople in 667, or by other accounts in 665, and succeeded Peter.

He remained on the throne until 669, governed it in a way pleasing to God, and died in peace; for which the page cites the Paris codex 259. That is the whole of his life as the calendar has it: no birth, no city, no act of his episcopate, and no hymn.

The page adds one thing that is worth repeating as it stands. Some synaxarists, it says, place his memory also on 14 or on 16 November. The day recorded here is the day page this folder was read from, the fifteenth, and the two other days are left as the source leaves them, a disagreement among the books rather than a second commemoration this folder can hold.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 15 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/15/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/168/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
