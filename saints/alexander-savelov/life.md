# Alexander (Savelov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him first among the hieromartyrs of its third line for 17 декабря and gives no life. The line is «Сщмчч. Александра и Николая и Сергия пресвитеров (1918).» — the hieromartyr presbyters Alexander and Nicholas and Sergius, 1918 — and the calendar’s own name page adds the surname and the grade the line leaves out: «Александр (Савелов), иерей, сщмч.»

That is the whole of the record. He was a priest; he died in 1918, the first year of the persecution the Russian church counts its new martyrs from; and the calendar keeps him on this day beside [Nicholas (Beptyukov)](/saints/nicholas-beptyukov) and [Sergius (Florinsky)](/saints/sergius-florinsky), the two archpriests its line joins to him. No life page stands behind the line for any of the three. The day page carries three hymns, and all of them belong to the prophet of the day’s first line and to the three youths with him.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 17 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261217.html); read 13 October 2026.*
