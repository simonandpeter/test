# Photini the Cypriot

saint.gr begins by saying what it does not know: when exactly the venerable Photini lived, and what her origin was, we do not know. Tradition tells us, it goes on, that she was born at Rizokarpaso of simple but devout parents. From a little girl Photou stood out among the girls of her age for her goodness, her bearing, her readiness to serve others, her virtue; her large bright eyes mirrored the wealth of her heart and scattered trust and joy everywhere. At the school of her village she learned her first letters, and when she had learned to read she began to learn by heart various psalms and hymns of the Church.

When she reached the age for marriage the saint left her house and went to a cave to live as an ascetic; and in that cave, the page says, the pure and heroic girl passed the whole of her life — a life of patient endurance and prayer, of self-restraint and of entire dedication to the heavenly Bridegroom Christ. Her holy relic, which was buried and then found in the cave around the years 1718 to 1732 with the carved inscription above it «Φωτεινή Παρθένος Νύμφη Χριστού», continues today to offer healing to the sick, sight to the blind, and to those in pain rest of soul and joy.

The hermitage of Saint Photini still exists, at the village of Agios Andronikos. One goes in by a narrow entrance and down a stair made of local stones, of twenty-three steps; the cave is like a catacomb, like those the first Christians used, and at the back of it is the holy water. The sick take of that water and wash their sick limbs to be healed, and the saint is believed especially to heal diseases of the eyes. In the black years of the Turkish slavery, the page adds, the cave was used as a church as well. A portion of her relic is at the monastery of Kykkos in Cyprus.
