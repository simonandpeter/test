# Apollonius (6 July)

saint.gr's calendar for 6 Ιουλίου names him and gives no life. The whole of its account is one sentence, «Ο Άγιος Απολλώνιος μαρτύρησε ως εξής: αφού τον έβαλαν μέσα σ' ένα πλοίο κατόπιν στ' ανοιχτά της θάλασσας, έβαλαν φωτιά στο πλοίο με αποτέλεσμα να βρει τραγικό θάνατο» — he was martyred thus: they put him inside a ship, took it out into the open sea, and then set the ship on fire, and so he met a terrible death. The page names no country, no city, no century, no year, no emperor and no judge, and it prints no hymn under his line.

Besides that sentence it carries only the couplet the synaxaria print for him: «Aπολλώνιος αμφιδεξίως έχει, / Aθλείν προς άμφω, προς θάλασσαν και φλόγα» — Apollonius is ambidextrous, able to contend against both at once, against the sea and against the flame. A note on the name itself remarks on the epic poet of Rhodes and the mathematician of Alexandria who bore it; neither is a statement about him. That is everything the source says of him.
