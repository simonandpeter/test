# Arcadius of Vyazniki

saint.gr's calendar for 26 January names Arcadius and gives two sentences. It calls him Arcadius of Vyaznikovsky, says that he lived in the sixteenth century in Russia, that he was a monk at the monastery of Saint Gerasimus in the town of Vyazniki in Russia, and that he fell asleep in peace in the year 1592. The page's heading gives no year of birth.

That is the whole of it: no parentage, no account of his tonsure, no office, no wonders, and no troparion or kontakion. The same calendar keeps on this day another Arcadius, the son of Xenophon of Constantinople, and the two are different men on the page as they are here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1343/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
