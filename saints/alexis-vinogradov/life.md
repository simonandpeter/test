# Alexis (Vinogradov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him in a single line for 15 марта ст. ст.: «Сщмчч. Алексия пресвитера (1938).» The rank abbreviation there is a plural governing one name, which is the page's own inconsistency; the line declines the forename to «Алексия», gives no surname, no place, no year of birth and no word of narrative, and the year it closes with, 1938, is the only year it gives.

Behind that «Алексия» stands the calendar's own name page 9271, which carries the fuller form «Алексий (Виноградов), протоиерей, сщмч.» — the surname the day's line leaves out, and an archpriest's grade, a step above the «пресвитера» of the line. That page lists two days: this 15 марта and «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. It carries no life, and neither does the day page.

azbyka.ru, which has lives for some of the martyrs of these years, was not read for him. So what the calendar prints is the whole of what this folder says, and it says that rather than fill the silence: an archpriest of the Russian church of that name, who died a martyr in 1938 and whom this calendar keeps on this day.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 15 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260315.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9271.html), which carries no life; read 7 October 2026.*
