# Silvanus of Tabennisi

saint.gr names him and gives two sentences and no more. It says that Silvanus lived in the fourth century and kept his ascesis at Tabennisi in the Upper Thebaid, near Saint [Pachomius the Great](/saints/pachomius-the-great), whom the same calendar keeps on this same day; and that he was distinguished for the gift of tears, for the gift of prophecy and for unceasing prayer. It says that he fell asleep in peace.

Nothing further is printed there: no birthplace, no parents, no year of his death, no wonder told out, no hymn and no relics. The corpus keeps eight other men of this name — an apostle, six martyrs and one more — and none of them is an ascetic of Tabennisi; this is a ninth, and what his page does not say is not set down for him here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 15 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/15/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1604/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
