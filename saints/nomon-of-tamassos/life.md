# Nomon of Tamassos

saint.gr's calendar for 11 December keeps the holy Nomon, who lived as an ascetic in the wider district of Tamassos in Cyprus, and it says at once that there is not much about him. His simplicity, his fatherly counsels and his asceticism drew people in pain, who were thirsty for a little rest from their daily lives, as a magnet draws; but that forced the saint to keep moving, because what he longed for was the calm and the quiet in which he could reach the purpose of his asceticism, which was nothing other than his union with God.

Because of that moving about, the page says, there are many caves and hermitages which it seems the saint visited. One of them is a cave near the small village of Anagyia — a name it reads as a compound of the adverb for above and the noun for a rise of ground — where there are also two churches, an older one east of the village and a newer one to the west, dedicated to the Cypriot saints Nomon and Vechianos, whom the same calendar keeps on this same day.

The apolytikion the page prints calls the two of them the glory of the people of Tamassos and the ornament of the Cypriots, and says that by fasting they wore down the unreasoning imaginings of the flesh and received from heaven the grace of healings. Nothing more of his story is printed: no century, no parentage, no manner of death and no relic.
