# Colman of Terryglass

saint.gr's calendar for 13 December keeps Saint Colman of Terryglass, and its page is one line long. The line says that he was a wonderworker, that he lived as a hermit, and that he was the founder of three monasteries; the heading above it gives his years as 490 to 552. The page prints nothing more of his life than that, names no country, and prints no hymn for him.

Where the three monasteries stood, and which of them is the Terryglass he is named from, saint.gr does not say, and it names no source for the two years in its heading. The whole of the entry is the one line quoted in the attestation.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 13 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/13/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2479/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
