# Seraphim (Vasilenko)

He is the one deacon among the eleven the Russian calendar keeps in a single line on 30 September, standing after six presbyters and before a schemanun and three lay martyrs, with 1937 given once for all of them. The two readings of him on the one site do not agree about his rank: the day line has «Серафима диакона», a deacon, and the index of names heads his page «Серафим (Василенко), протодиакон, сщмч.», a protodeacon. His folder follows the index and records the difference rather than deciding it away.

That index keeps him here and on 26 January with the whole company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and prints nothing else about him: no birth, no diocese, no church he served, no charge, no place of death. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026 even with a browser user-agent, so the line stands as the line.
