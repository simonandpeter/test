# Pardus

The Ohrid Prologue gives him two sentences and nothing else, and this folder gives nothing else either. In his youth, it says, he was a carter — a man who drove a waggon for hire; but on account of one unintentional sin he left the world and withdrew into the desert to the ascetic struggle. He lived in Palestine in the sixth century. The book names no monastery, no teacher and no companion; it does not say what the sin was, nor how long he lived in the desert, nor how or when he died.

The two words the heading sets about his name are its own ranks and not his story. «Преподобни» is the grade this corpus writes as venerable, and «Отшелник» is one who has gone apart, a hermit; what is left when both are taken off is the bare forename, and that is the only name the book gives him. It prints no hymn for him, as it prints none for anybody, and this folder holds none.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 15. децембар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2025-12-28&prolog=1); read 7 October 2026.*
