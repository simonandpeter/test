# Nicostratus the Tribune

saint.gr's calendar for 8 Ιουλίου keeps the two tribunes in one line, «Άγιοι Αντίοχος και Νικόστρατος οι Τριβούνιοι», and its page gives them one sentence between them: «Οι Άγιοι Αντίοχος και Νικόστρατος μαρτύρησαν δια ξίφους, μαζί με τον Άγιο Προκόπιο (βλέπε 8 Ιουλίου)» — they were martyred by the sword together with [Procopius](/saints/procopius-of-jerusalem), whom the same day keeps as great martyr. Their rank, tribune, is the only office the source gives them.

The page names no city, no age and no judge of their own, and gives no year in its text, though it heads each of them «(; - 303)». It prints no hymn under their line, only the couplet the synaxaria set over them: «O Nικόστρατος την τομήν οίσω λέγει, / Ως Aντίοχος. Mη γαρ ου καμοί κάρα;» — Nicostratus says, I shall bear the beheading, as Antiochus did; for have I too not a head? That is everything the source says of the two of them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2049/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
