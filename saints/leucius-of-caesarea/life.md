# Leucius of Caesarea

Leucius suffered with [Thyrsus](/saints/thyrsus-of-caesarea) and Callinicus under Decius (249–251) at Caesarea in Bithynia. He reproached the governor Cumbricius for his unjust persecution of the Christians, and after torture was beheaded. Thyrsus came through his torments unharmed and died in peace, and the pagan priest Callinicus, won by Thyrsus’s courage, confessed Christ and was beheaded too. The Russian calendar keeps Thyrsus, Leucius and [Coronatus](/saints/coronatus-of-caesarea) “and their company” on 17 August (Julian).
