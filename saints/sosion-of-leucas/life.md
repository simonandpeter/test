# Sosion of Leucas

saint.gr's calendar for 14 July names Sosion as the first bishop of Leucas, and gives him no life. He stands at the end of the day's line — «Άγιοι Ακύλας, Παύλος, Ηρωδίων οι Απόστολοι και Άγιος Σωσίων, πρώτος Επίσκοπος Λευκάδος» — and the page behind that line belongs to the apostle [Aquila](/saints/aquila), which says only that on this same day the local church of Leucas keeps together the apostles [Paul](/saints/paul-the-apostle) and [Herodion](/saints/herodion-the-apostle) and Saint Sosion, the island's first bishop.

No year is given for him, no birthplace, nothing of what he did and nothing of how he died. What the page does carry is the apolytikion of the Leucadian saints, which names him: it says that the choirs of Leucas left behind the error that knows not God through the teaching of Herodion and the exhortation of Aquila, and that Sosion, the first bishop, is to be honoured in hymns beside the apostles, as one who walked in the words of Paul. That line of a hymn is the whole of what the source gives him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 14 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/14/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/708/saint.aspx), which is the apostle Aquila's and names Sosion in passing; read 1 October 2026.*
