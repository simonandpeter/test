# Sais

saint.gr's calendar names the holy martyr Sais and gives him a single sentence. He ended his life in martyrdom, it says, having been thrown into the sea, and so escaped the drowning depths of the error of the idols. The page's two-line verse turns on the same figure: «Ἦλθον θαλάσσῃ εἰς βάθη, λέγει Σάϊς, Φυγὼν τὰ ποτίζοντα τῆς πλάνης βάθη.» There is no year, no place, and no other notice of him there.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/579/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
