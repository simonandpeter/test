# Pachomius (6 May)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 6 mai reads «Sfântul Cuvios Pahomie» — Pachomius the venerable — with no epithet, no monastery and no country, and his page opens no Viață tab: it carries the day and the readings appointed for a monastic saint, and no text of its own.

The rank is therefore all that is recorded of him here, and nothing further is claimed. The same day prints three other bare names, «Sfântul Cuvios Mamant», «Sfântul Mucenic Varvar» and a «Sfântul Cuvios Ilarion».

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and is the first source here to say anything of him beyond the name. saint.gr prints one line and one page for three men — «Όσιοι Μάμας, Παχώμιος και Ιλαρίων» — and one sentence of story for all of them. The venerable [Mamas](/saints/mamas-6-may), Pachomius and [Hilarion](/saints/hilarion-6-may) lived their ascetic life in a desert place, it says, and passed their monastic days in fasting, in vigils and in works of charity; and having lived in concord of soul and in a conscience set on the good, they fell asleep in holiness and in peace. Their synaxis used to be kept «εν Οχείαις», at Ocheiai. The couplet over them counts the three as one departure, Mamas setting out with Hilarion and Pachomius. There is still no year, no country, no monastery and no hymn.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 6 mai — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/6-mai) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-pahomie); read 20 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 6 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/06/index.aspx) and [the life of the three](https://www.saint.gr/1516/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026.*
