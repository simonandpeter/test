# The Two Maidens (15 September)

The two holy maidens were martyred by the sword; that is the whole of the Greek synaxarion’s notice for 15 September. Having one mind and one eagerness, its verse says, the two maidens bow their necks to the sword. This entry records it and nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 15 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2437/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
