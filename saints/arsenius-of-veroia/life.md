# Arsenius of Veroia

saint.gr says the holy hieromartyr Arsenius of Veroia lived in the fourteenth into the fifteenth century. At the siege of the city of Veroia he was seized while serving the Liturgy in the Old Metropolis, which the page says stands to this day, and he was either slaughtered or hanged. Its couplet says the same in two lines: being the great shepherd of Veroia, Arsenius died willingly for his flock.

His memory fell into forgetfulness in the hard years of the Turkish rule, the page goes on, until Georgios Chionidis, the historian of Veroia, found at the Meteora a pamphlet with a service to him. Before that the saint had been honoured with no name at all, or, by concession, with the name Kallinikos, at the wish of the metropolitan of Veroia Kallinikos Charalampakis. The important thing the service records is that he was buried in the church of the great-martyr, and it is conjectured that his holy relic lies in the church of the great-martyr Saint Demetrius in the quarter of the new metropolitan church. His memory is kept in the metropolitan church of Veroia on 28 May.
