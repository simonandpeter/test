# Menas (Shelayev)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him last of seven on 22 октября ст. ст., in a line that names an archbishop, four priests and two archimandrites of 1937. The line declines his name and gives it without a surname; the title on the name link behind his place supplies both the surname and the grade, «Мина (Шелаев), архимандрит, сщмч.». The line marks him and the sixth off with «прмчч.», venerable-martyrs, which is what his types record; the name link says сщмч., hieromartyr, and adds the rank of archimandrite, which the line does not print. Where the two disagree the line's word is kept and the link's is written down here.

The company is headed by an archbishop of Uglich who was shot in a Siberian camp that day, and the calendar carries a life for him and for none of the others on the line. Nothing on the page says where this one was born or served, what he was charged with, or whether he died with the man at the head of the line or anywhere near him.

The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line. The day page's eight troparia and kontakia all belong to the two ancient entries it heads and to the icon it keeps, and none of them to this company of 1937.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 22 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261022.html); read 4 October 2026.*
