# Nymphas

saint.gr's calendar for 28 Φεβρουαρίου names two apostles in one line — «Άγιοι Νυμφάς και Εύβουλος οι Απόστολοι» — and the page standing under it says that they were disciples of the Apostle [Paul](/saints/paul-the-apostle), who mentions each of them in his letters: Nymphas, who was living at Colossae or at Laodicea, in the letter to the Colossians, at the fifteenth verse of the fourth chapter; and Euboulos, who was with Paul, in the second letter to Timothy, at the twenty-first verse of the fourth chapter. Both of them, it ends, died peacefully.

That is the whole of the entry: two names, two verses and a peaceful end. The couplet over the pair calls them a planting of Christ, two apostles of Christ, and says that Euboulos and Nymphas are together with Christ. No year, no see, no relics and no hymn are printed for either of them, and because the line names each, each is kept here: Nymphas with [Euboulos](/saints/euboulos).

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/28/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1930/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
