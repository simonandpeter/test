# Athanasius of Methone

saint.gr's calendar for 10 December keeps the holy Athanasius, bishop of Methone, who was born at Catane in Sicily, the page says, probably between the years 814 and 818. About the year 827 his parents came over to Old Patras in the Peloponnese because of the Arabs who were ravaging Sicily; and it was the great age, the page adds, when a coeval of his who would become the great and wise hierarch of Caesarea was still living in Patras.

While he was still small in years Athanasius asked to become, and became, a monk in one of the monasteries of the district. He stripped for the monastic contests like a man and pressed to win the prize of the calling above, and for that the metropolitan of Patras of the time made him abbot of the monastery, in spite of his continual refusals.

His place as abbot did not cut short his virtuous life but showed that he could serve the Church in a higher office too, and so the bishop of Patras ordained him bishop of Methone, which then belonged to that metropolis. As bishop in little Methone he worked with a wonderful zeal to answer all the material and spiritual needs of his flock, while he was himself a perfect pattern of evangelical poverty and of all the Christian virtues.

When the hour of his departure to God drew near he called his disciples and counselled them always to practise the virtue of love and to keep unceasingly the memory of the Lord's second coming. After his blessed falling asleep, about the year 880, his grave became a spring of wonders, and crowds came running to it to receive their healing.
