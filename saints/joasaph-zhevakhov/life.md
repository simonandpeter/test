# Joasaph (Zhevakhov)

**A prince of an old house, a descendant of a bishop of Belgorod, and the only bishop on this day.** The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him first of the sixteen it closes with 1937 — «Сщмч. Иоасафа, еп. Могилевского» — and carries no life for him; the life here is what azbyka.ru prints for the civil 5 декабря out of pstgu.ru. He was born in 1874 in the province of Полтава, Владимир Давидович Жевахов in the world, of a princely family, and the article makes him a descendant of the eighteenth-century bishop of Белгород who bore the name he took in religion. He finished a university course and was in state service until 1917. He was a deeply believing man and a friend of the writer Сергей Нилус.

**The cave monastery he paid to dig out is the one he was tonsured in.** The Зверинецкий skete at Kyiv was founded in 1912 on his own initiative, and the excavations of old Kyiv that found a twelfth-century cave monastery in the Kyiv «зверинец» were paid for out of his money; the skete was raised on that same spot and he became its honorary trustee. In 1924 he was arrested in Kyiv and spent seven months in prison. Freed, he went to the skete he had founded and was tonsured a monk there with the name Иоасаф.

**Consecrated bishop in 1926, he was arrested in the same year.** At Nizhny Novgorod the metropolitan Сергий (Страгородский) of Nizhny Novgorod consecrated the hieromonk Иоасаф to the episcopate, and he began his archpastoral service as a vicar of the diocese of Курск. That same year he was arrested and sent to the Solovki camp of special appointment, where he was three years, and then served as long again in exile in the Нарымский country. From 1934 he held the see of Могилёв, but after two years was retired and settled at Белгород.

**He was shot at Kursk on the day the sentence was passed.** At Белгород he was arrested for the last time and sentenced to be shot, and azbyka.ru closes: «Приговор был приведен в исполнение в день вынесения, 4 декабря 1937 года, в Курске» — the sentence was carried out the day it was given, 4 December 1937, at Kursk. That day was the Julian 21 ноября; the calendars keep him on the 22nd. Of all the clergy the Сретенский monastery names on this day he is the only bishop, and the only one whose two sources give him a different list of other commemorations.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 22 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261122.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13865.html); and the entry azbyka.ru prints for the civil 5 декабря — [the page](https://azbyka.ru/days/sv-ioasaf-zhevahov); read 6 October 2026.*
