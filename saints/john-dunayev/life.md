# John (Dunayev)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him for 26 февраля ст. ст. in one line held with another man — «Сщмчч. Иоанна, еп. Рыльского, и Иоанна пресвитера (1938).» — two hieromartyrs of the same forename who died in 1938, a bishop and a priest. He is the second of them, the presbyter, and the name link behind him carries the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line leaves out: «Иоанн (Дунаев), иерей, сщмч.».

Two entries on one line are two men, and they are kept apart here by what the calendar itself gives: the first has a see and this one has none. It prints no life page for him and no hymn under his name, and nothing further is written here — no parish, no place and no manner of death — because the calendar gives none of them, and nothing is reasoned out from the surname or from the year.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 26 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260226.html); read 3 October 2026.*
