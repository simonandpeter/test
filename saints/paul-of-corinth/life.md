# Paul of Corinth

Paul came of Constantinople, and saint.gr says he was born of parents devout and dear to God, who themselves afterwards followed the monastic life. He was the brother of St Peter, bishop of Argos, whose memory the page puts on 3 May, and the two of them practised ascesis together at Corinth. Later Paul was elected bishop of that city, and he fell asleep in peace. The page gives no year for his birth, his election or his repose.

That is the whole of the notice, and the brotherhood is what it dwells on. The couplet it prints turns on it: «Φύσει ἀδελφὸς ὢν ὁ Παῦλος τοῦ Πέτρου, Πόλει σὺν αὐτῷ ἐγκατοικεῖ τῇ ἄνω» — Paul, being by nature the brother of Peter, dwells together with him in the city above. Of his episcopate the page records nothing: no council, no work, no dispute, no relic, no successor and no hymn.

The note it attaches to the name — that Παῦλος is Latin paulus, the very small, and that besides the chief of the apostles many hierarchs and rulers have borne it — is about the word and not about him. His brother has no folder in this corpus yet, so he is named here without a link.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 27 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/27/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1263/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
