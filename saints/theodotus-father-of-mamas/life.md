# Theodotus, father of Mamas

Theodotus and his wife [Rufina](/saints/rufina-mother-of-mamas), devout and noble Christians of Paphlagonia — of a great patrician family, the Romanian life says, honoured, rich and bright in the faith — could no longer hide their faith in Christ and confessed it before all; they were seized by the pagans and imprisoned at Caesarea in Cappadocia. Knowing his bodily weakness, Theodotus prayed that the Lord would take him before the tortures, and died in prison; Rufina died soon after, giving birth to their son [Mamas](/saints/mamas-of-caesarea), whom she commended to God. The Russian calendar keeps the parents with the son on 2 September (Julian).
