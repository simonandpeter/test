# Dula of Nicomedia

The Ohrid Prologue's entry for the Julian 25 March is the whole of what this folder records, and what it says of Dula is one sentence long. She was a servant. She suffered for the Lord alone, apart from the two women the Prologue heads with her, who were beheaded together after their imprisonment and torments; and she came to her end in the city of Nicomedia. The entry closes by saying that God transplanted three white roses, watered with a martyr's blood, into his heavenly garden.

That is all there is. The Prologue gives her no year, no reign, no persecutor and no manner of death, and names nobody she suffered with; the Greek calendar's own line for this menologion day names the other two women and not her, and nothing else has been read for her. So this folder records that one sentence and no more. The Prologue prints no hymn for her, or for any saint of the day.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 25. март ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-04-07&prolog=1); read 4 October 2026.*
