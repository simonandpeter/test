# Firmus (1 June)

Doxologia's life for Firmus is four lines, and this is all of it. He was in the days of Maximian, it says, and for his faith in Christ he was taken and brought before Magon the governor; and not bending to offer sacrifice to the idols he was stripped and beaten with sinews, then hung up and tormented in many ways, and at the last they cut off his head, and so he received the crown of his contest.

The page gives him no country, no office and no year: it names the emperor and the governor and nothing else, so nothing else is recorded here. He is entered under the day, apart from the martyrs of Justin's company whom the same calendar prints for 1 June, because the life doxologia gives him is a separate one and names none of them.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 1 iunie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/1-iunie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-mucenic-firm); read 24 September 2026.*
