# Laurence of Cyprus

saint.gr's calendar for 12 Μαρτίου names him simply «Άγιος Λαυρέντιος», and the page behind that line holds one sentence about him: that the holy Laurence belongs to the choir of the three hundred Martyrs and Venerables of Cyprus, those called the Alamanoi. To it the page adds, in its own words, that we have no other details of the life of the saint; and the rest of what it prints is a note on the name, from λαύρα, the fiery one.

So no year is given, no city of Cyprus, no manner of death, no relic and no hymn — and not even which of the two ranks in that choir was his, since the three hundred are counted as martyrs and venerables together. What is recorded here is the commemoration, the island, and his membership of that company; the corpus's other Greek Laurence, on 9 January, is a martyr whom the same site calls unknown to the Synaxarists, kept on another day and with no Cyprus about him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3829/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
