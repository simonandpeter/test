# Phaedrus

saint.gr keeps a martyr Phaedrus on 29 November, and what it says of him is one sentence: he was put to death as a martyr after they had poured boiling resin over him — «Ο Άγιος Φαίδρος θανατώθηκε μαρτυρικά, αφού έχυσαν επάνω του καυτή ρητίνη». No country, no century, no emperor, no judge and no burial; and no hymn.

What it prints besides is his couplet, which says that his was the one death of its kind: many men contend against other things, but Phaedrus we have known alone, contending stoutly against the seething of resin. Two modern icons of him are reproduced on the page and credited to the iconographers who painted them. Nothing else is recorded here, because nothing else is there.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 29 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/29/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3111/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
