# Menes, companion of Menaeus

saint.gr's calendar for 1 Αυγούστου keeps a company of martyrs «ἐν τῷ Βιγλεντίῳ, πλησίον τοῦ χαλκοῦ Τετραπύλου» — at the Biglention, near the bronze Tetrapylon — and names two of them, Μήνης and Μηναίος, leaving the others simply as holy martyrs, with no count and no names. Of their life it says one sentence and stops: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο των Αγίων», we have no details of the life of the saints.

What the page prints besides is the couplet of the synaxarion, which plays the two names against the moon: «Mήνης κατοικεί συγκάτοικον νυν έχων, / Mηναίον ένθα ουδαμώς μήνη πέλει» — Menes dwells, having now Menaeus for a housemate, where there is no moon at all. There is no country, no century, no emperor, no manner of death and no hymn. His companion [Menaeus, companion of Menes](/saints/menaeus-companion-of-menes) has a folder from the same line; the unnamed rest the page gives neither a number nor a name, so they are left here as the calendar leaves them, with no folder of their own.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2187/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
