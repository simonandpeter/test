# Eumenius of Koudoumas

saint.gr says that Eumenius was born in 1846 at Pitsidia of Pyrgiotissa in Crete, the younger son of Chariton and Maria Charitaki and the brother of [Parthenius](/saints/parthenius-of-koudoumas), and that in the world he was Emmanuel. Unlike his elder brother he was taught his letters by a schoolmaster of the village. The two set out in 1858 for the monastery of Odigitria, and after seven years of probation he was tonsured in 1865 with the name Methodius; when the abbot Gerasimus later tonsured both into the great schema at the cave church of Martsalo, he was named Eumenius.

In 1868 Meletius, bishop of Petra, ordained him deacon at Odigitria, and in 1870 the bishop of Arcadia ordained him presbyter; beside him throughout was the monk Parthenius, his abba and the support of his life. The two left Odigitria in 1874 after a new abbot turned against the crowds who came to them, wandered four years, and settled in the caves of the Asterousia mountains at Koudoumas, where they founded the monastery of the Mother of God. It is from a letter of his own, written to Basil, bishop of Arcadia, on 8 October 1915, that the page quotes the state of the place when they began: «...ἱδρύσαμε ἐκ βάθρων τὴν Μονὴν, μὴ ἔχων τότε εἰμὴ ὀλίγον τεῖχος παλαιὸν ἐν τὴ ἐκκλησία...».

He was the monastery's spiritual father, appointed to the office by Meletius, metropolitan of Crete. Keeping the sacred canons and in full awareness of what spiritual fatherhood is, the page says, he gave rest to thousands of souls by his counsel; and as a good teacher of souls he prepared many young men who came to him for the priesthood, of whom he required two things, the fear of God and purity. When he was to give testimony for a man he first fasted, kept vigil and prayed for three days and nights, and then set the candidate before the icon of Christ and called him to confess his sins, that he might enter the priesthood clean.

After Parthenius's repose he was much fought and much worn down, by brethren of the monastery and by others. His own great gift to it, besides his bearing inside it, was the rule he drew up: until then the monastery had none and went by what Parthenius had laid down, and his rule governed its administration, the brothers' dealings with one another, and the matters of daily life there. It is his frequent correspondence with his spiritual child Basil, bishop of Arcadia, that preserves the whole course of the founding and the working of Koudoumas, and the strong and marvellous presence of God and of the Mother of God in it.

Worn out by much ascesis, by hearing confessions and by the governing of the monastery, and within the obedience and humility he loved, he fell asleep on 12 September 1920. The page says the two elders imposed themselves on the conscience of the faithful as saints of the Church; that the first church in their honour was consecrated at Koudoumas by Cyril, metropolitan of Gortyna and Arcadia, on 10 July 1983, which is the day their memory was fixed on; and that in 2007 the Ecumenical Patriarchate formally entered the two venerable fathers in the calendar of saints, on the recommendation of Makarios, metropolitan of Gortyna and Arcadia, through the Holy Provincial Synod of the Church of Crete. His relics are at the monastery of Koudoumas.
