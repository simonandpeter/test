# Theoktiste of Voronezh

saint.gr's entry for the new martyr Theoktiste is a single sentence, and the life says exactly that: she came of Russia, and she was a fool for Christ's sake, and she was martyred in the year 1936. The day's line adds the place her name is kept with, «του Βορονέζ», of Voronezh, and the page's heading adds only that the year of her birth is unknown, printing her as «(; - 1936)». The body gives her a patronymic in brackets, Mikhailovna.

Nothing else is there. The page names no town, no monastery, no convent, no charge laid against her, no court and no manner of death; it records no relic and prints no hymn. She stands in this corpus on the strength of that one line of the Greek calendar, and the line is what the folder says.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 22 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/22/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3672/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
