# Paraskeva (Kochneva)

Her line in the calendar is three words long, and it is the whole of what is recorded here. The Сретенский monastery’s calendar for 26 марта ст. ст. prints «Мц. Параскевы (1939)» — the martyr Paraskeva, 1939 — and the name page behind that forename gives her the surname the line leaves out and nothing besides: «Параскева (Кочнева), мц.» The calendar gives her no monastic grade and no office, which is how it prints a laywoman rather than a nun.

So nothing is said here of where she was born, what she did, when or by whom she was arrested, or where she died. There is no life page for her on the day and none on her name page, and 1939 is the only year the calendar gives. The other two days of this calendar’s March that print a woman martyr of the twentieth century give no more than this one does.

Her name page lists one other day, «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church. One row per church is the schema, so that day is recorded on her Russian row and she does not render on it.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 26 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260326.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14240.html); read 8 October 2026.*
