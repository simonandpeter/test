# The Fifteen Martyrs

The Greek synaxarion keeps fifteen martyrs on 27 September and tells only how they died: the pagans put them aboard a ship, and when it had put out to sea they holed it, so that all fifteen were drowned. Its verse has the fleet of thrice-five champions sail down to the very bottom of the sea. It names none of them and gives no date.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 27 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2523/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
