# Theolepte

saint.gr's calendar for 1 Νοεμβρίου names «Αγία Θεολήπτη», and then says something it says of almost nobody: that she is completely unknown to the synaxaria. Everything the page has about her comes from one manuscript. She is spoken of in Paris Codex 259, folio 2a, where there is also a sticheron for her; and from that sticheron, the page says, we gather that she was tortured by the tyrant, was thrown into prison, and in all probability died a martyr.

So there is no year, no city, no persecutor's name, no account of her arrest, no relics, and nothing of her family. The rank recorded in this folder is the page's own «κατά πάσα πιθανότητα» and not a statement; the sticheron itself is not printed here, so there is no hymn to copy either.

What the page does not do is disown her: it carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», which it does carry on another of this day's entries. So the site vouches for the entry as it stands, thin as it is, and names its one source.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/01/index.aspx) and [her notice](https://www.saint.gr/2865/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
