# Iasimos

saint.gr's line for him is «Όσιος Ιάσιμος ο Θαυματουργός», and the page behind it is two sentences long: that holy God gave Iasimos the gift of working wonders, and that he healed many for the glory of God; and that he fell asleep in peace. There is no homeland here, no century, no monastery, and no hymn.

The one thing the page adds is a couplet, and it plays on his name, which is the Greek word for curable: even Iasimos's dumb dust alone deals out to the sick the healing grace. The corpus holds no other saint of this name against which to read the line.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 4 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/04/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1373/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
