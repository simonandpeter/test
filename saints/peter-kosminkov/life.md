# Peter (Kosminkov)

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar for 3 ноября ст. ст. carries twelve names on one line and his is the ninth of them: «Сщмчч. Василия, Василия, Александра, Владимира, Сергия, Николая, Викентия, Иоанна, Петра, Александра, Павла, Космы пресвитеров (1937).» The line gives the forenames in the genitive, the grade-class «пресвитеров» for all twelve together, and one year, 1937, and it gives no surname to any of them. The name link behind his place in it supplies the calendar's own nominative with the surname, «Петр (Косминков), иерей, сщмч.», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from.

That is everything the calendar records of him. He has no life page, no icon, no hymn, no place, no age and no parish anywhere on this calendar, and the name page behind his place on the line carries two days and no text at all. The name page behind his place on the line also lists «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. That is a synaxis and not a second feast, and he does not render on it. Nothing further is written here, and in particular nothing is reasoned from the surname.

The line names the class and the link names the grade, and both are kept: the line's «Сщмчч.» and «пресвитеров» make him a hieromartyr and a presbyter, and the link's «иерей» is recorded as his office, Priest. He is given no relation to the eleven others on the line. They share a line, a grade-class and a year, and the calendar prints nothing at all that says they died in one place or knew one another; a shared day is not a relation here.

The Russian church calendar at azbyka.ru prints what days.pravoslavie.ru does not: a life for the three clergy of his parish together, headed «СВЯЩЕННОМУЧЕНИКИ ИОАНН КЕСАРИЙСКИЙ, ПЕТР КОСМИНКОВ, ПРЕСВИТЕРЫ И СИМЕОН КРЕЧКОВ, ДИАКОН». He was born on 4 January 1887 in the village of Пахрино in the Podolsk district of the Moscow province, in the family of Vasily Petrovich Kosminkov, who served as psalmist in the Trinity church of that village. He finished the Pererva theological school in 1901 and the Moscow theological seminary in 1907 and was sent to work as a teacher; in 1910 he was ordained priest and served in the churches of the Moscow diocese, and from 1918 in the church of the Vladimir icon of the Mother of God in the village of Быково.

In the persecution it happened, the page says, that the godless arrested not only the priest but the whole clergy and the most active lay members of the church council, as at Быково in the Ramenskoye district. Taken there over three days, from 1 to 3 November 1937, and shut up in the Taganka prison in Moscow, were himself, the rector [John (Kesariysky)](/saints/john-kesariysky), the deacon [Simeon (Krechkov)](/saints/simeon-krechkov), the warden of the church [Eudocia (Safronova)](/saints/eudocia-safronova), and the council members «Петр Пискарев-Молоков» and «Александр, Екатерина и Дмитрий Чиликины».

Told that he was arrested for counter-revolutionary activity and required to give evidence, he answered that he had never carried on such activity and could not have. Told that he was one of the organisers of a counter-revolutionary group of churchmen and asked to name its members, he said that he was no organiser and knew no members. Asked where the illegal meetings of the group had been held, he answered that there had never been any and that he knew nothing of such a group existing — but that the members of the church council did meet, and met mainly in the church. Asked what questions were discussed at the illegal meeting, he said that the meetings of the active churchmen held in the church were always legal and that the questions discussed at them were only of practical church business.

Told that the investigation knew an act of terror against the chairman of the collective farm had been discussed at an illegal meeting, and asked whether he confirmed it, he said he did not. Required to give evidence on the counter-revolutionary activity of each member of the group of churchmen, he answered that he had already said he knew nothing of such a group existing, and therefore knew nothing of the activity of any member of it.

On 15 November 1937 one NKVD troika sentenced the two priests and the deacon to be shot, two of the council members to ten years in a labour camp, and the warden and two others to eight. The three clergy were shot on 16 November 1937 and buried in one unmarked common grave on the Butovo range near Moscow. One arrest, one village, one troika decision and one grave are what the relations now written here rest on — a company, and not the line of twelve the calendar prints him in; his relations are to the two who were shot with him and to the warden, and to none of the other eleven names on the line.

The service azbyka prints with the life is headed «Ме́сяца ноября́ в 3-й день» and «Новому́чеников Бы́ковских», its text approved by the Holy Synod of the Russian Orthodox Church on 14 May 2018, журнал № 37; it names the three clergy and the warden together, and its troparion is now in this folder with the English typed here. The deacon is the one of the four whom the calendar does not keep on this day: both calendars keep him on 12 ноября ст. ст., the civil 25 November, which is not the day any of the three were shot.
