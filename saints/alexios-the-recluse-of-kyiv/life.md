# Alexios the Recluse of Kyiv

Alexios lived in Russia in the thirteenth century, saint.gr says, and was a monk as a recluse — enclosed — in the Great Lavra of Kyiv; and he fell asleep in peace. Then the page says the thing that governs everything else about him: «Ο βίος του Αγίου δεν καταγράφηκε», the saint's life was never written down. What it has of him instead is the history of his bones.

His holy relics were found again in the year 1675, thanks to a landslide that followed an earthquake which struck Kyiv that year; and afterwards they were laid beside those of [Sabbas of the Kyiv Caves](/saints/sabbas-of-the-kyiv-caves), who is kept on this same day. His memory, the page adds, is also commemorated on 28 September, at the common feast of the holy monks of the Lavra of the near caves, and in the second week of Lent, at the common commemoration of the wonderworking Fathers of the Caves of Kyiv.

Of his country within Russia, his parentage, his name in the world, his elder, the length of his enclosure and the year of his death the page says nothing at all, and it prints no couplet and no hymn.

The Russian calendar gives this folder its century, its caves and the reason its day is its day, and very little else: the two readings its index links say the same thing in two lengths. He struggled in the Near Caves of the Kyiv Caves monastery in the thirteenth century, and his relics were found after the year 1675. Of his life nothing else is printed at all — no birthplace, no parents, no year of repose and no wonder.

Why he is kept on 24 April the page states outright: «его мощи почивают рядом с мощами преподобного Саввы Печерского» — his relics rest beside the relics of [Sabbas of the Kyiv Caves](/saints/sabbas-of-the-kyiv-caves), and that saint’s own page says in its turn that his day is this day because he shares a name with the martyr Савва Стратилат, whom this same day of the calendar heads. So the day this folder renders on stands at two removes from anything that happened to him: it is the day of the neighbour of his relics, which is the day of the namesake of that neighbour. He is kept besides with the synaxis of the venerable fathers of the Near Caves on 28 September and with the synaxis of all the Kyiv Caves wonderworkers on the second Sunday of Great Lent, the first a synaxis and not a feast of his own and the second moveable and impossible for this corpus to hold. The two hymns the day prints are addressed to him and his neighbour together.
