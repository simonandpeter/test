# Alexios the Recluse of Kyiv

Alexios lived in Russia in the thirteenth century, saint.gr says, and was a monk as a recluse — enclosed — in the Great Lavra of Kyiv; and he fell asleep in peace. Then the page says the thing that governs everything else about him: «Ο βίος του Αγίου δεν καταγράφηκε», the saint's life was never written down. What it has of him instead is the history of his bones.

His holy relics were found again in the year 1675, thanks to a landslide that followed an earthquake which struck Kyiv that year; and afterwards they were laid beside those of [Sabbas of the Kyiv Caves](/saints/sabbas-of-the-kyiv-caves), who is kept on this same day. His memory, the page adds, is also commemorated on 28 September, at the common feast of the holy monks of the Lavra of the near caves, and in the second week of Lent, at the common commemoration of the wonderworking Fathers of the Caves of Kyiv.

Of his country within Russia, his parentage, his name in the world, his elder, the length of his enclosure and the year of his death the page says nothing at all, and it prints no couplet and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/24/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1466/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
