# Helladius (9 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 9 Νοεμβρίου names the venerable Helladius and gives him one sentence: «Ο Όσιος Ελλάδιος απεβίωσε ειρηνικά» — the venerable Helladius departed in peace. That is the whole of the notice. There is no country on the page, no century, no monastery, no teacher, no disciples, no year and no hymn, and nothing at all of what he did beyond the manner of his end.

What stands beside it is the couplet of the synaxarion, which makes a painted panel of him for the living: «Εἰ καὶ μετέστης, Ἑλλάδιε, τοῦ βίου, / Πίναξ ἔμεινας ἀρετῶν τοῖς ἐν βίῳ» — though you have passed out of this life, Helladius, you have remained a tablet of the virtues for those still in it.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 9 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/09/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2958/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
