# Theophilus of Libya

saint.gr keeps two martyrs together on the eighth of January, a deacon and a layman, and gives them one short notice. These holy martyrs were from Libya, it says. They were arrested because they confessed Christ, and were brought before the proconsul and ruler of Libya; and because they persisted in the faith of Christ their flesh was torn to pieces, and so they gave up their souls into the hands of God and went up victorious to the heavens.

Of Theophilus in particular the page says only that he was the deacon of the pair, and glosses his name as the one God loves. Its two-line verse has fire carried on the backs of the two men, overshadowed by the back of God. No year, no city and no hymn is printed, and nothing is supplied here; [Helladius](/saints/helladius-the-layman), the layman who suffered with him, is kept on the same day.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/122/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 8 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260108.html), read 3 October 2026.*
