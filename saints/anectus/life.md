# Anectus

Anectus lived in the days of Diocletian and was arrested because he was a Christian. Because he would not sacrifice to the idols he was bound to four oxen and stretched out, then beaten with rods, mutilated and pierced with spears, and at the last they cut off his head.

That is the whole of the entry doxologia prints for him — three lines and no more. It gives no country, no city, no judge's name, no year, no place of burial and no hymn, and nothing more is set down here than the calendar sets down. It names Diocletian, and prints no year, so no date is recorded.
