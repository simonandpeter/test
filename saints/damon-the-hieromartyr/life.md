# Damon

Damon the hieromartyr is unknown to the Synaxaristes and the Menaia; his memory stands in Sinai Codex 631, which also carries two stichera to him. Nothing else is recorded.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 28 August — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2213/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
