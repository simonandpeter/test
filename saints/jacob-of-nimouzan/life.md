# Jacob of Nimouzan

saint.gr's calendar for 27 Φεβρουαρίου names two ascetics in one line — «Όσιοι Ασκληπιός και Ιάκωβος» — and says that their life was written by Theodoret of Cyrrhus in his Philotheos Historia. Of Jacob it gives one sentence: after many years of asceticism and of the hermit's life, at a very great age, he shut himself in a cell near the city of Nimouzan, seeing nobody and nothing.

Of [Asklepios](/saints/asklepios-disciple-of-polychronius), whom the line names beside him, it gives a sentence of his own, and it closes for the two of them together: so having lived, they fell asleep in holiness and in peace. Nothing else is printed here — no birthplace, no year, no relics, no hymn — and the couplet over the pair says that two righteous men stood together before the Master who judges all the earth righteously. Because the line names each of them, each is kept here; the place the page calls «Νιμουζάν» it does not explain, and the form of it in this folder's name is no more than a transliteration of what the page prints.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 27 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/27/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3594/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
