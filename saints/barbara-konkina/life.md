# Barbara (Konkina)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names her and carries no life. Its line for the twenty-third of March, old style, reads «Сщмч. Василия , Стефана пресвитеров, прмч. Илии , прмц. Анастасии , Варвары , мч. Алексия (1938).», and the name page behind «Варвары» is headed «Варвара (Конкина), прмц.», which gives the surname the line leaves out. That page lists the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church on «26 января» and this twenty-third of March.

Nothing else is said of her on either page: no place, no diocese, no parish, no year of birth and no account of how she died. The 1938 the line closes with is the whole of her chronology. The second source for the Russian new martyrs, azbyka.ru, was not read for her.

The line gives her no surname and the name page does; «прмц.», venerable-martyr, is the whole of her rank and the calendar names no house she belonged to.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 23 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260323.html); read 8 October 2026.*
