# Andrew of Raphailovo

saint.gr's calendar for 14 Μαρτίου names him «Όσιος Ανδρέας εκ Ρωσίας», the venerable Andrew out of Russia, and heads him «(1746 - 1820)». The page says that he was born in the year 1746 in Russia; that he lived as an ascetic in the monastery of the Holy Trinity of Raphailovo, near the diocese of Tobolsk, of which he was also abbot; and that later he continued his God-pleasing life in the monastery of St Symeon at Moscow.

He fell asleep in peace in the year 1820. That is the whole of the page: no parents, no name in the world, no account of his ascesis or of his governing of the house at Raphailovo, no relic and no hymn. What is recorded here is the commemoration, the two monasteries, and the two years the page prints, and the English name takes its epithet from the first of those houses.
