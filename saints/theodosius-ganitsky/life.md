# Theodosius (Ganitsky)

His line in the calendar is six words and a year. The Сретенский monastery’s calendar for 20 апреля ст. ст. prints «Свт. Феодосия исп., еп. Коломенского (1937)» — a hierarch of that name, a confessor, bishop of Kolomna, 1937 — and the name page behind the forename gives the surname the line leaves out and writes the rank out in full: «Святитель Феодосий (Ганицкий), епископ Коломенский, исповедник».

That is the whole of it. There is no life page for him on the day page and none on his name page, and 1937 is the only year either of them gives. Nothing is said of where he was born, when he was consecrated, when or by whom he was arrested, or where and how he died. The see and the word confessor are what his ranks here rest on, and they are set down among those ranks rather than written out as a biography he has not been given.

His name page lists one other day, «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church, and that day page was not read. One row per church is the schema, so the day is recorded on his Russian row and he does not render on it. The day page carries six hymns under four other names on the day and none under his.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 20 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260420.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14247.html); read 8 October 2026.*
