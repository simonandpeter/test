# Philemon, disciple of Passarion

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life. The whole of what it says about him is one clause at the end of another man's account: the venerable [Agapius](/saints/agapius-disciple-of-passarion) and Philemon, who are carried along with him, were disciples of the venerable [Passarion](/saints/passarion-the-presbyter) and his fellow-ascetics; and the memory of all three is recorded in the Jerusalem Kanonarion, at page ninety of Archimandrite Kallistos's edition.

The page gives him no year, no birthplace, no monastery, no account of his asceticism, no relics and no burial place, and prints neither apolytikion nor kontakion for him. Of the name itself it notes only that Φιλήμων comes from φίλημα and means the one who loves and is loved. Several other Philemons stand in this corpus, an apostle among them, and none of them is this man.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/10/index.aspx) and [the life of the three](https://www.saint.gr/1544/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
