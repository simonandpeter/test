# Mark, companion of Soterichus

doxologia.ro's calendar names Mark for 24 octombrie and gives no life. The Viață tab of his page carries one sentence, and it is the same sentence that stands on the pages of the two men he is named with: «Sfinţii Marcu, Sotirih şi Valentin, pe pământ fiind târâţi, aşa s-au săvârşit» — Saints Mark, Soterichus and Valentine, being dragged along the ground, so ended their lives.

That is everything. The page does not say where they suffered, or when, or under whom, or what they were dragged behind, and it gives them no rank beyond the word martyr in the calendar's line. His companions are [Soterichus](/saints/soterichus-companion-of-mark) and [Valentine](/saints/valentine-companion-of-mark).

His page carries no Tropar tab, and doxologia prints no hymn for him. Of the three, only the page of [Valentine](/saints/valentine-companion-of-mark) has one, and what it serves is the troparion of Saint Aretas and those with him.

The Greek calendar keeps the three on the same day, 24 October, and saint.gr says where they came from and where they went. These saints were from Asia, it says; they were seized by idolaters because they were Christians and dragged over sharp stones until they died. Their holy relics were afterwards carried from Asia to Thasos, where they remain to this day. The couplet over the entry says that the earth has the three fellow-contestants for a monument, crying out their renowned sufferings. The Greek line names them in the order Mark, [Soterichus](/saints/soterichus-companion-of-mark), [Valentine](/saints/valentine-companion-of-mark).
