# The Nine Martyrs (12 March)

saint.gr's calendar for 12 Μαρτίου keeps nine martyrs whose names it does not have — «Άγιοι Εννέα Μάρτυρες» — and the page behind that line holds one sentence about them: that the holy nine martyrs were martyred by fire. Beside it stand two lines of verse which say the same thing in another way: «Πρὸς τὴν κάμινον θαρσύνει τοὺς ἐννέα, / Θείου πόθου κάμινος ἐκκεκαυμένη» — a kindled furnace of divine longing gives the nine courage towards the furnace.

The page then adds a guess of its own, in a bracket: perhaps, «Ίσως», they are the same as those who were martyred together with the holy bishop Audas in Persia, whose memory it keeps on 31 March. That is the source's own perhaps and it is left as one: nothing is joined to anything here on the strength of it. What is recorded is the commemoration, the number, and the fire; no name, no place, no year and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3827/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
