# Procopius (25 June)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 25 iunie reads «Sfântul Mucenic Procopie», and the page it points to carries the day, an icon under his own name, some articles, and the epistle and gospel appointed for the day — and no biography whatever: no country, no century, no manner of death, no year and no hymn.

The calendar keeps another and much better known Procopius, the great-martyr, in July; nothing on this page ties the man named here to him, and nothing here makes that claim. The day he is kept on and the rank of martyr are all the source gives.

The readings appointed are 2 Timothy 2:1–10 and John 15:17–27 with 16:1–2, the apostle's charge to endure hardship and the Lord's word to His disciples that the world will hate them.

The Greek calendar keeps a Procopius on the same day, and unlike doxologia.ro it tells a story. saint.gr's entry for 25 Ιουνίου is «Άγιος Προκόπιος που μαρτύρησε στη Σμύρνη», headed «(; - 1810)»: a new martyr from a village near Varna, of devout parents, who went at twenty to the Holy Mountain and became a monk in the Skete of the Honourable Forerunner, under an elder called Dionysius, and was marked there for his monastic virtues. Later he left the monastic life, reached Smyrna and, in despair, became a Muslim. Remorse for so heavy a slip brought him in tears of repentance to a confessor; he prayed before the icon of the Mother of God and went to the city's judge, where he threw off the Turkish turban he was wearing, put on the monastic cap, rebuked the Muslim religion boldly and confessed Christ. When the Turks saw his mind was not to be moved they led him with mockery to the place of execution, but seeing the joy with which he went the executioners took fright and refused; and someone who had denied Christ, called in for the purpose, beheaded him on Saturday 25 June 1810. Whether the man the Romanian calendar names on this day is this man the Romanian source does not say, and the Greek page is the only witness to any of these particulars.
