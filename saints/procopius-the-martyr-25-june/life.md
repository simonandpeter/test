# Procopius (25 June)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 25 iunie reads «Sfântul Mucenic Procopie», and the page it points to carries the day, an icon under his own name, some articles, and the epistle and gospel appointed for the day — and no biography whatever: no country, no century, no manner of death, no year and no hymn.

The calendar keeps another and much better known Procopius, the great-martyr, in July; nothing on this page ties the man named here to him, and nothing here makes that claim. The day he is kept on and the rank of martyr are all the source gives.

The readings appointed are 2 Timothy 2:1–10 and John 15:17–27 with 16:1–2, the apostle's charge to endure hardship and the Lord's word to His disciples that the world will hate them.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 25 iunie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/25-iunie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-procopie); read 24 September 2026.*
