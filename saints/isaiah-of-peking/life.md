# Isaiah, son of Metrophanes Ji-Sung

saint.gr names him among the Chinese martyrs of 1900 as the son of father [Metrophanes Ji-Sung](/saints/metrophanes-of-peking), the first Chinese priest of the Russian mission at Peking, and of [Tatiana](/saints/tatiana-of-peking) the priest's wife. The head of the page brackets him «(1877 - 1900)», and the account says he was twenty-three years old when he died. Of his life before that it says nothing at all.

In 1900 the secret society of the «Boxers», with the backing of the dowager empress, let loose a persecution against the Christians of China, whom they blamed for all the country's calamities. On 10 June proclamations were posted in the streets of Peking calling on the idolaters to slaughter them; the next night the Boxers went through the city with torches, taking the Orthodox in their houses and torturing them to make them deny Christ. When the buildings of the Russian mission had been burnt, some seventy Christians took refuge in the priest's house, and the greater part of them were slaughtered there. The Boxers stabbed father Metrophanes in the breast in his own courtyard; Tatiana they beheaded, and Isaiah with her.

[Maria](/saints/maria-of-peking), his betrothed, a girl of nineteen, had come to father Metrophanes's house with the wish to die together with the family of the man she was to marry, and she was killed there; his brother [John](/saints/john-of-peking) was mutilated at the hour of their father's death. The page counts [two hundred and twenty-two](/saints/two-hundred-twenty-two-chinese-martyrs) more who received the crown with them.
