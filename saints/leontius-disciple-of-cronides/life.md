# Leontius, disciple of Cronides

Leontius was one of the martyrs Cronides, Leontius and Serapion, who took the crown of martyr’s glory in the fourth century under Licinius. Cronides, a deacon at Alexandria, was very learned, a gifted teacher of great virtue and zeal after the pattern of the protomartyr Stephen; Leontius and Serapion were his distinguished pupils, ready like their teacher to suffer everything for Christ — and so it happened: one day, as they were making a brilliant defence of the Christian faith before the pagans, these in their irritation abused them savagely with stones and clubs, then bound their hands and threw them into the sea, where they gave up their holy souls — the sea, the verse says, was their contest and their tomb. The Greek calendar keeps the three on 13 September.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 13 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2422/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
