# Sabbas of Venetala

Sabbas is unknown to the Synaxaristes and the Menaia. His memory survives in Sinai Codex 631 (tenth–eleventh century), which for this day lists Titus, the deposition of the relic of Bartholomew at Lipari, and the holy martyr Sabas of Venethala. That line is the whole of the record; the Orthodox Church in America, which keeps him on 27 August as Savva of Benephali, prints no information either.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 25 August — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2645/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
