# Faustus

doxologia.ro's calendar for 16 iulie gives Faustus two sentences and no more. It places him in the third century and says that he suffered in the time of the emperor Decius, whose reign the page dates 249 to 251, and that the holy martyr was tortured for five days before he received the crown of martyrdom.

The page names no place, no judge and no manner of death, gives no year of its own, and prints no troparion for him. He is not the Faustus the Romanian calendar keeps on 31 august among the company of Andrew the Stratelates; that one it places under Maximian.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 16 July, and saint.gr says a little more. It places him in the years of Decius, the savage persecutor of the Christians, whose reign it dates 249 to 251, and says that in the midst of hard persecutions, confiscations, exiles, imprisonments and deaths by torture, Faustus — a daring and brave soul — came forward preaching Christ. His persecutors arrested him in 250 and condemned him to crucifixion. He bore that dreadful punishment with admirable endurance; his martyrdom lasted five days, and then, blessing and thanking God, he gave up his spirit. The page heads him «(; - 250)» and gives no year of birth, no place and no judge.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 16 iulie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/16-iulie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-mucenic-faust); read 24 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 16 Ιουλίου — [the Greek day](https://www.saint.gr/07/16/index.aspx) and [the Greek life](https://www.saint.gr/2099/saint.aspx), read 1 October 2026.*
