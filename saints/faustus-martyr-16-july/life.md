# Faustus

doxologia.ro's calendar for 16 iulie gives Faustus two sentences and no more. It places him in the third century and says that he suffered in the time of the emperor Decius, whose reign the page dates 249 to 251, and that the holy martyr was tortured for five days before he received the crown of martyrdom.

The page names no place, no judge and no manner of death, gives no year of its own, and prints no troparion for him. He is not the Faustus the Romanian calendar keeps on 31 august among the company of Andrew the Stratelates; that one it places under Maximian.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 16 iulie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/16-iulie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-mucenic-faust); read 24 September 2026.*
