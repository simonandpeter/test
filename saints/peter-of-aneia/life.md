# Peter of Aneia

saint.gr's calendar for 14 October keeps «Άγιος Πέτρος ο Αυσελάμου», and gives him a short notice with a place and a year in it. He came from Ania — or Anea — of Eleutheropolis, the page says, and was marked out both for his strong body and for the manliness of his soul.

Having suffered much for his faith in Christ, in the end, in the year 292, in the reign of Diocletian and after many tortures, he was burnt alive by the idolaters, and so received the incorruptible crown of martyrdom. That is the whole of the account: no persecutor is named, no rank is given him, and no hymn is printed. The two-line verse set over his name says that Peter, going under the fire in divine zeal, puts out the fire of godless error.
