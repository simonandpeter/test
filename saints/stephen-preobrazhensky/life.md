# Stephen (Preobrazhensky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and carries no life. Its line for the twenty-third of March, old style, reads «Сщмч. Василия , Стефана пресвитеров, прмч. Илии , прмц. Анастасии , Варвары , мч. Алексия (1938).», and the name page behind «Стефана» is headed «Стефан (Преображенский), иерей, сщмч.», which gives the surname the line leaves out. That page lists the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church on «26 января» and this twenty-third of March.

Nothing else is said of him on either page: no place, no diocese, no parish, no year of birth and no account of how he died. The 1938 the line closes with is the whole of his chronology. The second source for the Russian new martyrs, azbyka.ru, was not read for him.

His name page is the only one of the nine to give a third day as well: «7 июня (Иван.)», the synaxis of the saints of Ivanovo, which places him in that region and is the nearest thing to a biography the calendar gives him.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 23 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260323.html); read 8 October 2026.*
