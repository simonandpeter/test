# Julian (18 May)

saint.gr's calendar for 18 May names him and gives him one sentence: the holy Julian — or Julius, the page allows — was perfected when he was tortured and dragged through the thorns of a bramble. Nothing else at all is printed: no country, no century, no emperor, no judge, no relics and no hymn.

The couplet above that sentence is the only other thing the page has of him, and it reads the manner of his death as an image: Julian found the bramble his contest, honouring the incarnation of God, of which the bramble is the type — the bush that burned and was not consumed. This folder records what the Greek calendar keeps on 18 May and adds nothing to it.
