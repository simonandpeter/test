# Arcadius the Emperor

None of the Synaxaristes names the emperor Arcadius (395–408) a saint. His memory is found only in the Kanonarion of Jerusalem (p. 105 in Archimandrite Kallistos’s edition), together with St Martyrius; perhaps, the synaxarion says, the Church of Jerusalem had some weighty reason of its own to count him among its saints.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 27 August — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/907/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
