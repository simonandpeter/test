# Probus (19 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 19 December keeps three martyrs together, Elias, Probus — whom it also calls Promus — and Ares, and says that they came of Egypt. Inspired by their warm faith, the page says, they went about to various cities and served the Christians who were being persecuted; and at Ascalon they were arrested, held fast to the confession of Jesus Christ, and were condemned to death by the judge Firmilianus.

Elias and Probus were beheaded, and Ares gave up his holy soul in the middle of the flames. That is the whole of what the page gives for the three of them; the other two of the company here are [Elias](/saints/elias-19-december), [Ares](/saints/ares-19-december). It names Egypt and Ascalon and nothing else, lists no relic and prints no hymn, and the only year anywhere on the page is in the head of Ares's name-block, which gives 308 for him alone and nothing for his companions.

The Russian calendar keeps him on 19 декабря ст. ст., the same menologion day as the Greek calendar here, and names him second of three in one line: «Мчч. Илии, Прова и Ариса, египтян (308).» — the martyrs Elias, Probus and Ares, Egyptians, 308. The year is the first this folder has had, and it is the same the Greek page gave to the third of the three alone; and the calendar calls all three Egyptians, which no other source here does, its own name page for him reading «Пров Египтянин». The reading the entry links stands behind all three men at once and was not opened.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 19 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/19/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3305/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 19 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261219.html), read 13 October 2026.*
