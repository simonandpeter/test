# Asterius of Laranda

saint.gr's calendar for 30 Οκτωβρίου keeps «Άγιοι Αστέριος, Κλαύδιος, Νέων και Νεονίλλη τα αδέλφια» — four brothers and sisters by blood. They were of the city of Laranda, the page says, Isaurians by race, and they lived in the years of Diocletian and of Lysias, governor of Cilicia, about the year 288.

When their father died they fell into the clutches of a wicked stepmother who wanted to seize the property he had left them. The four would not let her take it; instead they used that money, with every readiness, to help the poor. To be revenged on them the stepmother denounced the four to Lysias, the governor of Cilicia, as Christians. They were arrested at once and cruelly tortured, but their minds did not change.

So all four were beheaded, and their bodies were thrown into the ravines for the birds of prey; but their souls, the page says, flew in glory before the throne of God, waiting for the great day of recompense. The epigram over the three brothers calls [Claudius](/saints/claudius-of-laranda), Asterius and [Neon](/saints/neon-of-laranda) new stars in the contest of the sword, and a second epigram has their sister stretched on wood. saint.gr prints no hymn, and carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου».

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps the company on a day of its own, the Julian 29 October, and prints a fourth name the Greek page did not: «Мчч. Клавдия, Астерия, Неона и Феониллы (285)», so that [Theonilla](/saints/theonilla-of-cilicia) joins the three. It gives no life page for any of them. The one piece of narrative on it is the opening paragraph of an article carried on her name page, and it says something no page in this folder said before: that the four were **children of one house**. Their stepmother wished to be rid of them so as to have the family's wealth, and told Lysias, the governor of Cilicia, that they were Christians; he questioned them, tried in vain to make them renounce Christ, and then — the article says — decided to take the wealth himself, and for that put them with particular cruelty to tortures of several kinds. It is the same Lysias the Greek page names, which is how the two commemorations are read as one; and the Russian line dates the martyrdom 285 where the Greek dates it about 288, so the folder's interval now holds both.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 30 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/30/index.aspx) and [the notice of the four](https://www.saint.gr/2848/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 29 октября ст. ст., which keeps the company on its own day and adds a fourth name — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261029.html) and [the fourth one's name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/2726.html), read 4 October 2026.*
