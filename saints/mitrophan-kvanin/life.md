# Mitrophan (Kvanin)

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him twentieth of twenty-four, the last of the venerable-martyrs, and gives no life; the life here is the one azbyka.ru prints for the civil 2 декабря, out of pstgu.ru. It opens by calling him the brother of two bishops. Mikhail Andreyevich Kvanin was born in 1875 in the village of Gubkino in the Maloarkhangelsk district of the province of Oryol; the Kvanin brothers came of a peasant family and he had his education in the village school. He began as a novice of the Holy Spirit monastery at Vilna. Where he was tonsured is not truly known, the page says, but at the Nicholas monastery at Samara, where he was from 1914, he is already down as a monk and by some accounts a hieromonk.

From 1917 he helped at the bishop’s house at Taganrog, from 1920 he was a server of the church of the village of Bolokhovo in the province of Tula, and in 1923 he moved to Moscow and served at the Holy Spirit church of the Danilov cemetery; at some point he was raised archimandrite. On 23 February 1933 he was arrested with the group of priests who served that church, all of whom went into one case and were given different sentences, and on 15 March a special conference of the collegium of the OGPU sentenced him to three years of banishment to the Northern krai. He settled at Kargopol, the page says, or possibly at the settlement of Kargapolye in the Kurgan region.

When the term ran out in 1936 he went back to Moscow and was arrested there. The new sentence was three years again, and this time he was required to go to Kazakhstan. He lived about a year in the village of Borodulikha in the Semipalatinsk region and bore the hardship with the other clergy at prayer, and the clergy serving out their exile at Borodulikha were to go through the group case of the archpriest Dmitry Kuklin. He was arrested on 22 November 1937, and on the 27th of the same month the charge followed — that he had undermined the collective-farm order, agitated for the registering of a group of believers and carried on propaganda for the keeping of the fasts — and with it the final sentence. On 2 December 1937, having never admitted any guilt, he was shot at Semipalatinsk; where he is buried is not known.
