# Rostislav of Kyiv and Smolensk

saint.gr's calendar for 14 Μαρτίου names him «Άγιος Ροστισλάβος ο Πρίγκιπας Κιέβου και Σμολένσκ», and heads him «(; - 1176)». The page behind that line holds one sentence about him: that the holy Rostislav Mstislavovich, prince of Kyiv and of Smolensk, lived in the twelfth century in Russia and fell asleep in peace in the year 1176. The patronymic is the only thing it adds to the line.

There is nothing else on the page: no parents beyond that patronymic, no account of his rule, no church or monastery of his, no relic and no hymn, and not even a note on the name, which this calendar usually gives. What is recorded here is the commemoration, the two principalities, the century, and the year of his peaceful repose.
