# Epipodius of Lyons

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and gives no life. Its line for 22 апреля ст. ст. reads «Мч. Епиподия Лионского (ок. 177).» — the martyr Epipodius of Lyons, about the year 177 — and that is the whole of what the calendar says of him. The line carries a link to his name and none to a reading; the name page behind it prints the title «Елиподий Лионский, мч.» and the one line «День памяти: 22 апреля», and nothing else at all.

So the calendar gives three things about him and no more. «Мч.» is мученик, martyr, which is the one rank written into this folder. «Лионского» sets him at Lyons in Gaul, and the name page repeats the epithet in the nominative. «(ок. 177)» is about the year 177, recorded here as the year of his death, since martyrdom is the only thing the line says happened to him; no year of birth appears on either page and none was inferred from the other.

The two pages spell his forename differently, and neither spelling was altered to agree with the other. The day’s line declines it «Епиподия», from a nominative «Епиподий»; the name page’s own title reads «Елиподий». The name link is where the calendar’s nominative is read from, so «Елиподий Лионский» is the form this folder stores, with the day line’s spelling recorded beside it. No other calendar was read for him, and the Romanian, Greek and Serbian rows of his attestation name the day that was not checked.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 22 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260422.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14894.html), which name him and give no life; read 8 October 2026.*
