# Marcellus (18 July)

saint.gr's calendar for 18 July names «Άγιος Μάρκελλος» and tells one thing about him: he was laid out on a red-hot bed, and so was roasted alive. That is the page's whole notice, one sentence long. There is no year, no city, no emperor, no judge, nothing of what he said or did before it, and no hymn.

Above the notice the calendar sets the Synaxarion's couplet, which turns the bed into a figure: «Aνακλιθείς Mάρκελλος εις πυρός κλίνην, / Ύπνωσεν ύπνον τον μακάριον πάνυ» — laid down on a bed of fire, Marcellus slept a sleep altogether blessed. The verse and the sentence say the same thing twice, and between them they are everything the Greek calendar records of him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2149/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
