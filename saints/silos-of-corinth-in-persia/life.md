# Silos of Corinth in Persia

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Νοεμβρίου names «Όσιος Σίλος επίσκοπος Κορίνθου της Περσίας» — Silos, bishop of Corinth in Persia — and then says the whole of what it has in one sentence: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον Βίο του Αγίου», we have no details of the life of the saint. The see is everything the calendar gives, and what place it means the page does not explain.

Besides the line there is only the couplet set over it: having departed from the earth and from earthly things together, father, you now dwell in heaven, blessed Silos. The page gives him no country, no century, no year, no companions, no relics and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3092/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
