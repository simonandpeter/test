# Melania (8 June)

saint.gr's calendar names her and gives no life. The line for 8 June reads «Οσία Μελάνεια», the venerable Melania, and the page behind it says only this: the venerable Melania died peacefully; we have no other details of her life. So what is recorded here is a name, the rank of a venerable — that is, a monastic — and a death that was not a martyrdom.

The couplet the page sets above that sentence is the only other thing it gives her, and it plays on her name, which is the Greek for black: Melania, having turned away every black spirit, goes white in spirit towards God the Word. There is no century here, no country, no monastery, no companion and no wonder, and there is no hymn. She is not to be confused with Melania the Roman, whom this corpus keeps already and whom the calendars keep on another day; what the two have in common is a name and a rank, and this page offers nothing that would make them one woman.
