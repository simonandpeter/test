# Theodore of Pavia

Theodore was bishop of Pavia in Italy. saint.gr says that from the day of his election, in 743, he suffered much for the Orthodox faith, and that he was exiled by the Lombards. He fell asleep in peace in 778 — a bishop, on the page's own reckoning, for thirty-five years, of which it records only the suffering and the exile.

That is the whole of the entry: three sentences and the gloss the site gives his name, «το δώρο του Θεού», the gift of God. The page names no birthplace, no parents, no place of exile and no relics, and prints neither a synaxarion couplet nor a hymn to him.
