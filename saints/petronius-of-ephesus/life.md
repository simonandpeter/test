# Petronius, disciple of John the Theologian

Petronius was most likely the disciple of John the Theologian whom [Hermione](/saints/hermione-daughter-of-philip) met at Ephesus when she came with her sister Eutychis looking for the apostle, and who confirmed her in the work of the Gospel; he died in peace, the Greek synaxarion says — Christ was the rock to Petronius, its verse plays, standing above the violence of temptations. The Romanian calendar keeps him the same day as a martyr, with the same story and a peaceful end.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 4 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2344/saint.aspx); The Romanian life is at doxologia.ro — [the page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-petroniu); read 23 August 2026.*
