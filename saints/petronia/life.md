# Petronia

The Greek synaxarion says only that Petronia was martyred by the sword; its verse has her bow her head to the sword and fix the eye of her soul on the divine rock, playing on her name. It adds that some synaxaria keep here the memory of the venerable Anastasia the ascetic. It gives no date. The Romanian calendar keeps her on the same day and prints no life.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 29 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2401/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026; the Romanian entry is at doxologia.ro — [the page](https://doxologia.ro/sfanta-mucenita-petronia).*
