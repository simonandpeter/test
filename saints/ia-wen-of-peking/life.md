# Ia Wen of Peking

saint.gr names her among the Chinese martyrs of 1900, in the day's line as «Αγία Ία (ή Ίγια) η διδασκάλισσα» and in the account as Ia Wen, a teacher in the Russian mission at Peking. Of her the page says only what it says of her death: that she was tortured twice, and that she confessed Christ with great gladness. The head of the page brackets her «(; - 1900)» and gives her no year of birth and no place.

The persecution in which she died was loosed by the secret society of the «Boxers», with the backing of the dowager empress, upon the Christians of China, whom they blamed for all the country's calamities. On 10 June 1900 proclamations were posted in the streets of Peking calling on the idolaters to slaughter them and threatening any who dared hide them; the next night the Boxers went through every quarter of the city with lighted torches, seizing the Orthodox in their houses and torturing them to make them deny Christ. Their houses were burnt, and they were led out to the idol-temples of the Boxers and disembowelled, beheaded, or burnt.

In the same paragraph the page names Paul Wan, an Orthodox catechist who died with the prayer on his lips, and then Ia Wen, and then the boy John, the son of the priest [Metrophanes Ji-Sung](/saints/metrophanes-of-peking). Of the thousand souls of the Russian mission at Peking three hundred perished in those days, and [two hundred and twenty-two](/saints/two-hundred-twenty-two-chinese-martyrs) of them received the unfading crown of martyrdom.
