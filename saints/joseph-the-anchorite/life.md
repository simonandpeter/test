# Joseph the Anchorite

saint.gr's calendar for 17 June names Joseph the Anchorite and gives him three sentences. He was famous, the page says, for his self-restraint and his humility of mind; and in order to avoid the praises of the world he would withdraw with his disciples and pray and chant. In such a spirit of virtue he reposed in peace. The page adds that it is the Evergetinos that writes of him.

Beside that it prints a two-line epigram, which calls him a divine swan at his end, dying with a song as swans are said to die, and the note it gives on the name Joseph itself, that it is a Hebrew word for the one whom God has made perfect. There is no country, no monastery, no year, no name of a disciple and no hymn, and this life adds none.
