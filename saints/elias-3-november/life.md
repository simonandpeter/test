# Elias (3 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 3 Νοεμβρίου names «Όσιος Ηλίας», and the page behind that line is four words long: «Ο Όσιος Ηλίας απεβίωσε ειρηνικά» — the venerable Elias died peacefully. There is no city, no monastery, no century, no account of anything he did, no relics and no hymn. The Greek calendar keeps him, calls him venerable, says he died in peace, and stops.

The only other thing printed under his name is the epigram, which leans on the prophet he is named after rather than on him: having gone up into the chariot of the virtues, Elias came to the vault of heaven, like that other Elias. The page does not say he was a monk, where he lived, or whom he was taught by.

This folder takes its name from the day, because the calendar gives it nothing else to take a name from, and because the corpus already keeps fifteen men of this name on other days. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands, bare as it is.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/03/index.aspx) and [his notice](https://www.saint.gr/2875/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
