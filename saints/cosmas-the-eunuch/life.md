# Cosmas the Eunuch

The Ohrid Prologue of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović gives him the second entry of its Julian 3 August, and it is the whole of what is written here. Cosmas was a monk of the lavra of Pharan, very learned in the Holy Scriptures. He valued the works of Athanasius the Great so highly that he said to his own disciple: when you hear any word out of the books of Athanasius, and have no paper, write it on your garment.

In his old age he came to Antioch, to the patriarch Gregory, who died in 584, and there he ended his life. The patriarch gave orders that his body should be buried in the monastery of the Patriarchate. One man came often to the grave, honouring the saint and praying there to God; and when he was asked why he did it, he told them that he had lain paralysed for twelve years and that Cosmas had healed him.

The entry gives no year for anything of his own. The 584 it prints belongs to the patriarch he came to, not to him, and nothing is recorded from it here.
