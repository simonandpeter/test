# Valentine (Kornienko)

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him twenty-first of twenty-four and first of the four martyrs, who are laymen, and gives no life; the life here is the one azbyka.ru prints for the civil 2 декабря, out of pstgu.ru and the Orthodox Encyclopaedia. Valentin Alexandrovich Kornienko was born in 1873 in the town of Bobrinets in the Elisavetgrad district of the province of Kherson. He was first arrested in 1921 for acting as a go-between for speculators and sentenced to ten years of camps, but there is no record that the sentence was carried out. In 1930 and 1931 he was living in Moscow and sat on a church council.

On 18 April 1937, charged with counter-revolutionary activity, he was exiled to Kazakhstan for five years, and lived at the settlement of Mayskoye in the Beskaragay district of the Pavlodar region, where eight of the priests of this one day line were living too. His second arrest came in exile on 25 November 1937. At his questioning he rejected the charge of counter-revolutionary work and gave no evidence against anybody else. By the sentence of the troika of the East Kazakhstan region of 1 December 1937 he was shot, and he was buried in a common unmarked grave. He was glorified by the Jubilee Council of Bishops of 2000.
