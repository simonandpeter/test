# Straton of Nicomedia

Straton was one of four martyrs of Nicomedia — Straton, Philip, Eutychian and Cyprian — who suffered around 303. Going to the public spectacles, they taught the people to abandon the worship of idols and turned many pagans to Christ; the governor, noticing that the shows had begun to empty, summoned them, and when they confessed their faith in Christ firmly he had them thrown to the beasts. The beasts did not touch them; then they were tortured and thrown into the fire.

The Romanian calendar keeps him on 17 August. Doxologia.ro prints for him the same short notice it prints for his companion Cyprian: that he suffered in the persecution of the emperor Diocletian (284–305), that he was seized while preaching Christ and shut up with wild beasts and came out unharmed, and that in the end he was burned alive in the city of Nicomedia.

The Greek calendar keeps the four of them on the same menologion day, in one line, and tells how they came to be arrested. saint.gr says they were inhabitants of Nicomedia, and that they went every day to the amphitheatre and there taught and catechised idolaters systematically. The result was that many idolaters believed in Christ and the amphitheatre was almost emptied of spectators of unseemly shows. The ruler of Nicomedia noticed the thinning of the audience, learned the cause of it, and ordered the four of them arrested at once.

They did not hesitate, even before him, to confess Christ and the works they were doing for love of him. Enraged, the ruler had them tortured hard, and afterwards they were thrown alive into the fire, and so all of them gloriously received the crown of martyrdom. The page sets a distich of the synaxarion over each of the four, and prints no hymn. His own runs «Ἔθεντὸ με βδέλυγμα, φησὶν ὁ Στράτων, / Ἄνδρες βδελυκτοί, καὶ πυρὶ κτείνουσί με» — they have made me an abomination, says Straton, abominable men, and they kill me with fire. He is named on the page beside [Philip](/saints/philip-of-nicomedia), [Eutychian](/saints/eutychian-of-nicomedia), [Cyprian](/saints/cyprian-of-nicomedia).
