# The Seventeen Monks in India

The calendar counts them and does not name them. The Сретенский monastery's calendar for 27 ноября ст. ст. prints «Прмчч. 17-ти монахов в Индии (IV).» — seventeen monks in India, venerable-martyrs, of the fourth century — and the name page behind that line heads itself «Преподобномученики 17 монахов в Индии» and carries this one day and nothing else. There is no life page for them on the site.

So the whole of the record is four facts: that they were seventeen, that they were monks, that they were killed in India, and that it was in the fourth century. Nothing is said here of who they were, of what house they belonged to, of who killed them or why, because no page read says any of it. A heading that enumerates its members is one folder for each member; this one enumerates nobody, so it is one entry, and it says so.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 27 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261127.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/6936.html), which carries no life; read 7 October 2026.*
