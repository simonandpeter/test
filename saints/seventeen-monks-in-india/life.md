# The Seventeen Monks in India

The calendar counts them and does not name them. The Сретенский monastery's calendar for 27 ноября ст. ст. prints «Прмчч. 17-ти монахов в Индии (IV).» — seventeen monks in India, venerable-martyrs, of the fourth century — and the name page behind that line heads itself «Преподобномученики 17 монахов в Индии» and carries this one day and nothing else. There is no life page for them on the site.

So the whole of the record is four facts: that they were seventeen, that they were monks, that they were killed in India, and that it was in the fourth century. Nothing is said here of who they were, of what house they belonged to, of who killed them or why, because no page read says any of it. A heading that enumerates its members is one folder for each member; this one enumerates nobody, so it is one entry, and it says so.

The Охридски пролог gives them one short paragraph and it is the whole of their dossier here. They were Christian monks, it says, who suffered under the Indian king [Abenner](/saints/abenner-king-of-india). Enraged at the elder [Barlaam of India](/saints/barlaam-of-india) for having baptised his son [Joasaph](/saints/joasaph-of-india), the king sent parties out after Barlaam; and the parties never caught Barlaam, but took these seventeen other monks instead and brought them before the king.

He condemned them to death. Their eyes were put out first, then their tongues were cut out, then their legs and their arms were broken, and then they were beheaded with the sword. But by the blood of these knights of Christ, the entry closes, the faith of Christ was established the more firmly in the Indian kingdom. The book names not one of the seventeen, so this remains one folder and not seventeen, and it gives them no year: the fourth century this folder holds is the Russian calendar’s and is unchanged.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 27 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261127.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/6936.html), which carries no life; read 7 October 2026; and the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 27. новембар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2025-12-10&prolog=1), read 8 October 2026.*
