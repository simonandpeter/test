# Nektarios of Aegina

The Greek and Romanian calendars keep on 3 September the translation of the relics of Nektarios of Aegina, which took place in 1953; his feast and his life stand under 9 November, the Greek synaxarion says, and that is all its entry for this day records.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 3 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/930/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
