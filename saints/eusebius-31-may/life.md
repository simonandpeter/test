# Eusebius (31 May)

Doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 31 mai reads «Sfântul Mucenic Eusebiu», the holy martyr Eusebius, and the page the calendar links for him carries no Viață tab and no Tropar tab. It prints his name, the date, and the readings appointed — the epistle from 2 Timothy 2, be strong in the grace that is in Christ Jesus, and the gospel from John 15 and 16, if the world hates you, know that it hated me before it hated you. Nothing else about him is on the page.

The rank in the line is the whole of what is stated: a man who died a martyr, kept on 31 May. He is entered under the day because the source gives him no place, no companions and no century, and no year is recorded here for the same reason.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and gives what the Romanian line lacked. The holy martyrs Eusebius and Charalampus, saint.gr says, came from Nicomedia, and it is unknown when they were martyred. Because they were Christians they were arrested, and refusing to sacrifice to the idols they suffered a martyr's death upon the pyre together with the holy martyrs Romanus, Teletius — or Meletius — and Christina, whom the same calendar keeps on 30 May. The distich printed over the two of them says that two contenders, burnt with the fire of zeal, are not made dizzy at all by the fire that burns.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 31 mai — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/31-mai) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-eusebiu-0); read 24 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 31 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/31/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1487/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026.*
