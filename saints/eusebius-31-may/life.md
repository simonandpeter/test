# Eusebius (31 May)

Doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 31 mai reads «Sfântul Mucenic Eusebiu», the holy martyr Eusebius, and the page the calendar links for him carries no Viață tab and no Tropar tab. It prints his name, the date, and the readings appointed — the epistle from 2 Timothy 2, be strong in the grace that is in Christ Jesus, and the gospel from John 15 and 16, if the world hates you, know that it hated me before it hated you. Nothing else about him is on the page.

The rank in the line is the whole of what is stated: a man who died a martyr, kept on 31 May. He is entered under the day because the source gives him no place, no companions and no century, and no year is recorded here for the same reason.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 31 mai — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/31-mai) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-eusebiu-0); read 24 September 2026.*
