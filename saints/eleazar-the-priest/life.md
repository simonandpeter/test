# Eleazar the Priest

Eleazar and [Phinehas](/saints/phinehas-the-priest) were priests of the Hebrews: Eleazar the third son of Aaron, overseer of the Tabernacle (Numbers 4:16), and Phinehas his son, Aaron’s grandson. The Prologue, for the Serbian calendar, calls Eleazar the second high priest of Israel, Moses’ helper in numbering the people and Joshua’s in dividing the promised land among the twelve tribes; he kept the Ark of the Covenant faithfully at Shiloh and died in peace.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 2 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/915/saint.aspx); The Ohrid Prologue for 15 September 2026 (2. септембар ст. ст.), as printed by the Православни подсетник (pravoslavno.rs), tells it for the Serbian calendar — [the day’s page](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-09-15&prolog=1); read 23 August 2026.*
