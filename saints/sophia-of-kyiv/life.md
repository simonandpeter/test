# Sophia of Kyiv

saint.gr's calendar for 22 Μαρτίου names a venerable-martyr Sophia, and gives her one sentence: the holy venerable-martyr Sophia was the daughter of St [Demetrius](/saints/demetrius-ivanov) (Ivanov) and abbess in a monastery of Kyiv, and was martyred in the year 1941; we have no more details of the saint's life and martyrdom. The page heads her «(; - 1941)», so the year of her birth is a question mark there too.

That is the whole of what has been read. saint.gr does not say which monastery of Kyiv she governed, gives no account of her arrest or of the manner of her death and no persecutor, names no relics and prints neither a couplet nor a hymn under her name. Nothing has been added here from any other source and nothing from memory, and nothing on the page identifies her with any of the other Sophias the calendars keep.

What the entry does carry is the kinship. The day's other entry is her father's, and it names her from his side as she is named from hers, which is why each of these two folders names the other.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 22 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/22/index.aspx) and [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/72/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
