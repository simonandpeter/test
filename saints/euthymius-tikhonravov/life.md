# Euthymius (Tikhonravov)

The Russian calendar keeps him on this day as one of two priests shot in 1938, «Сщмчч. Иоанна и Евфимия пресвитеров (1938)», and behind that line it carries his life in full. Euthymius Sergeyevich Tikhonravov was born on 17 April 1881 at Shuya in the province of Vladimir; by some accounts his father Sergei was a priest. He finished the parish school, and in 1904 he married Tatiana Alexandrovna Speranskaya, daughter of the psalm-reader of the Transfiguration church at the village of Doronino, and moved there to live.

From then on he served as sexton in that church and, like many church servants of the time, earned his bread by peasant work. Three children were born to them. When the godless power came in 1917 the local authorities at Doronino threatened the priest serving there with the confiscation of everything he had if he did not stop; he had a large family and for their sake he left the church. The bishop sent another, and he too was driven out by threats.

Rather than leave the parish without a pastor, Euthymius Sergeyevich asked the bishop to ordain him. He had been weak in health from birth and relatives tried to talk him out of it — «Такое время, все отказываются, прячутся, а ты хочешь рукополагаться!», such a time, everyone is refusing and hiding, and you want to be ordained — but he held to it, and in 1933 he was ordained to the Transfiguration church at Doronino. His wife baked the prosphora at home until she fell gravely ill in 1935, went blind and died that same year; their younger daughter Antonina took over the baking and the heating of the stoves.

In 1935 he could not pay the taxes laid on him. His property was confiscated and the church closed, and the godless began to carry the icons out and destroy them; the building served afterwards as a grain store, a stable and a fertiliser shed, and was all but ruined. He stayed on in the village a while, serving what the faithful asked of him, then moved to Ivanovo, took work as a watchman at a medical institution, and went on serving as before while renting a corner of a room in a workers' settlement.

When it became known there that he was a priest he was denounced for living in the house illegally. He was arrested on 1 January 1938 and held in the prison at Ivanovo. He denied every charge: «Не признаю себя виновным», I do not admit myself guilty, since I conducted no anti-Soviet agitation and belonged to no underground organisation. False witnesses were produced, the first of them his landlady. On 3 February 1938 an NKVD troika sentenced him to be shot; the next day he was questioned again, denied the charges again, and that same day, 4 February 1938, he was shot at Ivanovo and buried in an unmarked grave.

He was numbered among the New Martyrs and Confessors of Russia at the Jubilee Council of Bishops in August 2000, for veneration throughout the church. The calendar's own list of his days gives 22 January and 26 January, the second being the general commemoration of the new martyrs; he is kept here on the day whose line names him with [John](/saints/john-uspensky).
