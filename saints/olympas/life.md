# Olympas

Olympas is one of the seventy apostles. doxologia.ro's notice of him is two sentences long: that he is of the Seventy, and that Olympas and [Herodion](/saints/herodion-the-apostle), following the Apostle [Peter](/saints/peter-the-apostle), were beheaded together by Nero in the city of Rome.

That is the whole of it. The page gives no country of his own, no year, and nothing of his work before Rome; and the hymn it prints for him is a common of the apostles, headed «Troparul Sfinților Apostoli Olimp, Rodion, Sosipatru, Erast, Terțiu, Cvart» for the six of them together and naming none of them in its text.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 10 noiembrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/10-noiembrie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-apostol-olimp); read 25 September 2026.*
