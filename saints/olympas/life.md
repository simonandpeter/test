# Olympas

Olympas is one of the seventy apostles. doxologia.ro's notice of him is two sentences long: that he is of the Seventy, and that Olympas and [Herodion](/saints/herodion-the-apostle), following the Apostle [Peter](/saints/peter-the-apostle), were beheaded together by Nero in the city of Rome.

That is the whole of it. The page gives no country of his own, no year, and nothing of his work before Rome; and the hymn it prints for him is a common of the apostles, headed «Troparul Sfinților Apostoli Olimp, Rodion, Sosipatru, Erast, Terțiu, Cvart» for the six of them together and naming none of them in its text.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 10 November, in one entry with four others of the Seventy. saint.gr says all five were of the Lord's seventy apostles, whose synaxis it puts on 4 January, that all of them are named in the sixteenth chapter of the Apostle Paul's letter to the Romans, and that most of them were bishops of the Church of Christ and became excellent keepers of the command of 1 Peter 5 — to shepherd the flock of God that is in your charge, not by constraint but willingly, not for shameful gain but with zeal, not lording it over those allotted to you but becoming patterns for the flock. Of Olympas it says one thing: that he and Herodion died a martyr's death under Nero. His own distich turns on his name and the mountain in it: «Λάμψας Ὀλυμπὰς ἡλίου λαμπροῦ δίκην, / Ὄλυμπον οἰκεῖ σὺν Ἀποστόλοις ἅμα» — having shone like the bright sun, Olympas dwells on Olympus, that is to say in heaven, together with the apostles.

The Russian calendar keeps him on the same day, in one line of six of the Seventy, and gives him two lives. The shorter one, his own page, has him a companion of the chief apostle at Rome, remembered in the greetings of Romans 16, and put to death by the sword in that city under the emperor Нерон on the very day the two great apostles suffered, which the page dates «+ 67 г.» — the first year this folder has carried.

The longer page, which tells the six together, says the same of his end in other words: that he and the other companion of that apostle were beheaded «в тот же день и час», at the same day and hour as the crucifixion at Rome. The name page adds the synaxis of the Seventy on 4 января and nothing else.

The Ohrid Prologue keeps six of the Seventy under one heading on the Julian 10 November and gives each of them a see or a death in a single clause. His is a death: he and [Herodion](/saints/herodion-the-apostle) were followers of the apostle [Peter](/saints/peter-the-apostle), and when Peter suffered they suffered with him, beheaded by the command of Nero. It closes on all six together — they fought the spiritual war, were victors, and were crowned with the crowns of glory.

The entry gives no year, so the 67 this folder already holds stands as it was; and it says of the last three names in the heading that the book keeps them on other days of its own as well, which it prints: 8 April, 28 April and 30 October.
