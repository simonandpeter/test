# Athenodorus

saint.gr's calendar for 7 Νοεμβρίου names Athenodorus and says it has no details of his life. What it adds is an argument rather than a fact: some hagiological sources, it reports, make him the brother of [Gregory the Wonderworker](/saints/gregory-the-wonderworker-of-neocaesarea) — and not the [Gregory](/saints/gregory-brother-of-the-wonderworker) who is kept the same day — which the page calls improbable, as the distich given to that Gregory witnesses.

So nothing of his own is recorded: no country, no century, no rank, no manner of death, no year and no hymn. The page prints his couplet, which asks for him from the other side: «Ἐκ γῆς καλοῦσιν Ἀθηνόδωρον Νόες, / Πρὸς τὰς νοητὰς δωρεὰς τοῦ Κυρίου» — the Minds call Athenodorus from the earth, to the spiritual gifts of the Lord. The gloss on his name reads it as the gift of Athena, the most wise.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2936/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
