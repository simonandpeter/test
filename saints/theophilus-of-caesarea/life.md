# Theophilus of Caesarea

saint.gr gives the hieromartyr Theophilus three sentences, and the life says exactly what they say. He was bishop of Caesarea in Palestine. He took part in a synod convened in Palestine to settle the day on which Pascha should be kept. And he was martyred under the emperor Commodus, whose reign the page gives as 180 to 192.

That is all the notice holds. There is no place of birth and no year for it, no family, no predecessor or successor in the see, nothing of what he said or held at that synod, no judge, no manner of death, no burial, no relic, no distich and no hymn. The page does not even say whether his martyrdom and the synod stood in any relation to one another.

The corpus keeps eight other men of this name — of Ephesus, the confessor, the myrrh-streamer, the fool for Christ of Kyiv, two deacons, a companion of Trophimus, and one of the Forty of Sebaste — and none of them stands on this day or is named on this page.
