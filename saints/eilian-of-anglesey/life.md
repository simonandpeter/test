# Eilian of Anglesey

saint.gr's calendar for 12 Ιανουαρίου names «Άγιος Εϊλιανός εκ Ρώμης», Eilian from Rome, and gives him a single sentence: he came from Rome and lived about the sixth century after Christ; he came to Britain, where he lived the ascetic life in a God-pleasing manner in the region north of Anglesey; and he fell asleep, venerably, in peace.

That is the whole of the page. It gives no year within the century, no monastery and no companion, says nothing of why he left Rome or how he reached the island, and prints no hymn. The Greek writes his name Εϊλιανός.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1406/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
