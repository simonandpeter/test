# Theophanes the Merciful of Gaza

Theophanes the Merciful came from Gaza, the Greek synaxarion says. He was very kind and given to almsgiving, gave all his property to the poor and lived in want himself. Towards the end of his life he fell ill with dropsy, which caused him great pain: his body began to swell, to rot and to give off a stench, and he bore this trial too with endurance and thanked God. A violent storm was raging while he was dying, and his wife feared she would not be able to bury him properly; but he comforted her and foretold that the merciful God, who knew the hour of his death, would stop the storm, and when he gave up his soul to the Lord a calm came. After his death his body, cleansed of its sores and corruption, began to pour out myrrh, as a proof of his holiness. The entry gives no date.

The Russian calendar keeps him on the same 29 September, as «Прп. Феофана Милостивого», and alone among the names on that page gives him no year and no century. Its life of him is short and is almost all about what he lost. He lived at Gaza — the page calls it a Syrian city — and was so kind and so open-handed that he took in travellers, helped the poor and the sick, and spent the whole of his estate on his neighbours until he had nothing. He did not grieve for the property, the page says, but a harder trial followed: his health went, and his body began to swell and to rot and to stink, and he bore that too, thanking God for everything.

When he was dying a terrible storm broke, and his wife grieved that she would not even be able to bury him properly. He comforted her: «Не плачь, жена, до сих пор продолжалось испытание, но вот наступает помилование от Милосердного Бога, ибо в час моей кончины прекратится, по воле Божией, буря». And so it happened — the moment he gave up his soul the air went still. After his death the body was cleansed altogether of its sores, and gave off a fragrance, and myrrh that healed flowed from it.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 29 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/4336/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026; and the Russian church calendar's [29 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260929.html), read 4 October 2026.*
