# Evangelus of Tomis

Doxologia's notice of him is short, and this folder holds no more than it says. Evangelus was a disciple of the apostle Andrew, and is the first bishop of the see of Tomis — Constanța, in the region the page calls Dacia Pontica, Scythia Minor or Dobrogea — for whom there is documentary attestation. He shepherded the faithful at the mouths of the Danube towards the end of the third century, and converted many of the pagans of that country to Christianity.

He is named in the account of the martyrdom of Epictetus and Astion, whose day is 8 July, where he is described as the founder of the Church in that region: it was Evangelus who baptised the parents of those two saints, after the priest Bonosus had brought them to the faith. He is believed to have been martyred in the persecution of Diocletian, who reigned from 284 to 305. The page gives no place and no year for his death.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 7 July, under the bare name Ευάγγελος and without the see the Romanian calendar gives him. saint.gr's whole account of him is one sentence — «Ο Άγιος Ευάγγελος μαρτύρησε δια ξίφους», the holy Evangelus was martyred by the sword — and besides it the page carries only the couplet the synaxaria print for him: «Eυάγγελος το θείον εκ ξίφους τέλος, / Eυαγγελισμόν είχε του θείου στέφους» — Evangelus held his divine end by the sword to be the good tidings of the divine crown, a play on his name. The page names no country, no city, no century, no year, no emperor and no judge, and it prints no hymn under his line.
