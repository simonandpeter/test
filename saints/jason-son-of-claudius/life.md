# Jason, son of Claudius

saint.gr's calendar for 19 Μαρτίου names four of one household — [Claudius](/saints/claudius-husband-of-hilaria) the tribune, his wife [Hilaria](/saints/hilaria-wife-of-claudius) and their children Jason and [Maurus](/saints/maurus-son-of-claudius) — and its life page gives each of them a heading of his own. Claudius held the office of tribune in the years of Numerian, and the eparch Celarinus handed [Chrysanthus](/saints/chrysanthus) and [Daria](/saints/daria) over to him to be punished.

When Claudius saw that the holy pair came unharmed out of the tortures, he believed in Christ and was baptised together with his wife and his two sons; the soldiers of his guard did the same. Then a great block of stone was tied to Claudius and he was thrown into the sea, and the two boys were beheaded along with their soldiers. Their mother took up their relics and buried them, and it was at their grave that she was seized and died herself.

That is the whole of what saint.gr says of Jason. It gives him no age, no separate interrogation and no words of his own, and its couplet speaks of the brothers together: with his own brother Maurus his head is cut off, Jason having been of one mind with him in brotherly fashion. No hymn stands under his name, and nothing here has been taken from any other source.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 19 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/19/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3919/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
