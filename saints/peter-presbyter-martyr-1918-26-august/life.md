# Peter (Iovlev)

Peter Ivanovich Ievlev — Iovlev in some sources, though every number of the
Ekaterinburg diocesan gazette writes Ievlev — was born in 1875 and educated
at the Ekaterinburg town school. He married Elizabeth Evgenievna, before her
marriage a teacher at the girls’ gymnasium, and they had five children.
Ordained deacon in 1896, he had a gift for practical management, and in
October 1901 was invited to be steward of the Ekaterinburg diocesan girls’
school, the largest and best-appointed in the diocese and often visited by
distinguished guests: the steward saw to all its material needs and chose
and oversaw its household staff, and he did it so well that he was made
steward of the bishop’s house as well. On 28 October 1905 he was appointed
deacon of the Catherine cathedral in Ekaterinburg and released from the
school, keeping the bishop’s house, so that he could give more time to the
services and to prayer; in September 1907 he was ordained priest for the
chapel of the Cross in the bishop’s house, and in March 1908 was moved to
the Transfiguration cathedral of the Nevyansk works in the Ekaterinburg
district. In the middle of June 1918 a rising against the Bolsheviks broke
out in the town, one of the largest in the Urals, organised by the soldiers
of the fourth rear motor workshop, which had repaired vehicles for the
northern front of the war at Luga near Petrograd and was evacuated to
Nevyansk after the peace of Brest; on 12 June they arrested the executive
committee of the local soviet, killed two of its staff and set up their own
military headquarters, and the people of the villages round and of the
Neyvo-Rudyanka and Verkh-Neyvinsk works joined them. Regular units of the
Red Army were sent against them, the rising was soon crushed, and terror
began in Nevyansk and its neighbourhood: more than three hundred people were
shot in the punitive operation, and since the clergy of the district were
blamed for starting the rising, the priests were killed first, and the
Transfiguration cathedral where he served was robbed and profaned. Father
Peter was shot in his own house in Nevyansk on 26 August 1918 of the old
style, 8 September. On 17 July 2002 the Holy Synod numbered him among the
new martyrs and confessors of Russia for veneration by the whole church. The
Russian calendar keeps him on 26 August of the old style, 8 September, in
the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church, and in
the synaxis of the Ekaterinburg saints.
