# Basil (Kadomsky)

saint.gr names him and gives him two sentences. Basil, in the world Vasily Petrovich Kadomsky, was born between the years 1775 and 1780 in the region of Ryazan in Russia; he followed the hard ascetic road of folly for Christ, and he fell asleep in peace in the year 1848. That is the whole of the notice.

There is nothing else: no town, no monastery, no account of a single thing he said or did in the seventy years between, no wonder, no relic and no hymn. The five-year spread on his birth is the page's own, and it gives no day for his death, only the year and the 2 May on which the Greek calendar keeps him.
