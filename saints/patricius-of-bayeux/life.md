# Patricius of Bayeux

saint.gr says he lived in the fifth century and gives him two sentences and nothing more. He was elected fourth bishop of the city of Bayeux in Normandy in Gaul, and he fell asleep in peace in the year 469; the page glosses the name itself as «ο ευγενής», the noble one.

It gives him no birth year, no birthplace, no family, no account of his episcopate beyond the see and the place in its succession, and no hymn. Nothing further about him is recorded here.
