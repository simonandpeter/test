# Helladius the Commentaresios

saint.gr's calendar for 18 Ιανουαρίου keeps Helladius among those who came to believe through Theodoula — with Evagrius, Macarius, and Boethus. Theodoula lived in the years of Diocletian, whom the page dates 284 to 305, and she was from Diocaesarea of Cilicia. When the prefect Pelagius pressed her with violence to deny Christ, she set against him that she was a Christian and would remain one.

Pelagius then put her to tortures, over which her woman's nature rose by the power of divine grace; and such was the impression made by her most modest and heroic bearing that the prefect's own men, who had been among her tormentors before, declared to him that they acknowledged Jesus Christ as their God. So he threw her into the furnace, together with Evagrius and Macarius. Two others, Helladius and Boethus, were beheaded. The page's headings give the year of all five as 298.

Helladius is named on the day with a title and not with a homeland: «Ελλάδιος ο Κομενταρήσιος», the commentaresios, which the page sets down without explaining it. He believed through [Theodoula](/saints/theodoula-of-diocaesarea) and was beheaded with [Boethus](/saints/boethus-companion-of-theodoula), and the epigram over the two of them reads «Θείον Βοηθόν συν Κομενταρησίω, Δόξης κατηξίωσε Χριστού, το ξίφος». Nothing else of him is printed.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1126/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
