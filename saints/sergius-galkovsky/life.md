# Sergius (Galkovsky)

The calendar’s own line is the whole of what this folder records of him. The Сретенский monastery’s calendar for 7 декабря ст. ст. keeps five saints of the twentieth century among the older ones, in four lines and under four years, and his is «Прмч. Сергия (1917).» He is the whole of its own line, and the earliest death the day carries, under the rank «прмч.», a venerable-martyr — a monastic put to death, and 1917 is the only year the calendar gives him.

The name page behind his place in the line gives the surname the line leaves out together with his monastic grade, a hieromonk. Its heading is «Сергий (Гальковский), иеромонах, сщмч.» and that heading is the whole of what it says of him. His name page lists one other day, «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church. One row per church is the schema, so that day is recorded here and he does not render on it.

Behind that page there is no life, and no article stands under his name. Nothing is said here of where he was born, where he served, when or by whom he was arrested, or how and where he died in 1917, because no page read says any of it.

No other man stands in his line, so this folder has no `related` row from the calendar: the four other twentieth-century saints of this day belong to three other lines and three other years, and sharing a day is not a relation. The day page carries nine hymns, and all nine stand under the names of the older saints of the same day.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 7 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261207.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9244.html); read 13 October 2026.*
