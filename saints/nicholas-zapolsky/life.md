# Nicholas (Zapolsky)

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 14 июня ст. ст. carries five names on one line and his is the fifth of them: «Сщмч. Иосифа и Димитрия пресвитеров (1918) Сщмчч. Александра , Павла пресвитеров и Николая диакона (1938).» The line gives the forenames in the genitive, two grade-class words for the five together, and two years — 1918 for the first two names and 1938 for the last three — and it gives no surname to any of them. The name link behind his place in it supplies the calendar’s own nominative with the surname, «Николай (Запольский), диакон, сщмч.», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from.

His name page is the only one of the five that carries any prose, and it carries one paragraph under the heading «Статья на Православие.Ru». It says that the mass arrests and destruction of clergy and church servants began in 1937; that in February 1938 the investigators questioned the witnesses kept on duty for the purpose, who signed false testimony; and that on the strength of it the deacon Nicholas was arrested on 21 March 1938 and shut up in one of the prisons of Moscow. The paragraph stops there. It does not say where he served, how old he was, or how or exactly when he died, and nothing here is supplied for it.

The line’s «Сщмчч.» and «диакона» make him a hieromartyr and a deacon, and the name link repeats the grade as «диакон». The second day his name page lists is «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which is a synaxis and not a second feast, and he does not render on it. He is given no relation to the four others on the line: they share a line and a year, and the calendar prints nothing at all that says they died in one place or knew one another.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 14 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260614.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/11400.html); read 9 October 2026.*
