# Leo the Archimandrite

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 30 decembrie reads «Sfântul Cuvios Leon, arhimandritul», and the page that line links carries neither a life nor a troparion nor any other text about him: no country, no century, no monastery and no deed.

So the two words the calendar gives him are all there is. Cuvios is the rank this calendar uses for a monastic saint, and arhimandrit is the office of a monastery's superior; and it is by that name and that office that he is kept on the last day but one of the Romanian year.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 30 December, and knows no more of him than the Romanian one did: «Ο Όσιος Λέων ο Αρχιμανδρίτης απεβίωσε ειρηνικά. Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Οσίου» — the venerable Leo the archimandrite died in peace; we have no other details about his life. The same rank, the same day, the same silence, in another calendar's words.

What that page adds is the couplet set over the entry, which is a play on his name: «Pευστού μεταστάς Xριστέ Λέων εκ βίου, / Σκύμνον λέοντος εξ Iούδα σε βλέπει» — Leo, having passed over from this fleeting life, sees you, Christ, the lion's whelp out of Judah. It prints no hymn and no year.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 30 decembrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/30-decembrie) and [his own page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-leon-arhimandritul), which carries no life; read 28 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 30 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/30/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1071/saint.aspx), read 2 October 2026.*
