# Salonas the Roman

saint.gr names him and gives no life. Its whole account of him is one sentence: «Ο Άγιος Σαλωνάς μαρτύρησε δια ξίφους» — the holy Salonas was martyred by the sword. The day's line adds the one further word it has for him, «ο Ρωμαίος», the Roman, and the page gives no year, no emperor, no place and no companions.

What stands beside that sentence is the couplet the synaxarion sets over his memory, which turns his refusal into the offering: not obeying the command to pour libations to the idols, Salonas is cut down, and so becomes himself a libation poured out to God. The page carries no more than this, and prints no hymn to him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 23 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/23/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4047/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
