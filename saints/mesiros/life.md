# Mesiros

saint.gr names three martyrs together for this day and gives one sentence for all of them: the holy martyrs Danax, Mesiros and [Therinos](/saints/therinos) — or Therianos, as the page allows in the same breath — were martyred by the sword, and their memory used to be kept at their martyrion at the Deuteron in Constantinople.

The couplet over them is about the beheading of two of the three and puts Mesiros between them: both contenders having had their heads struck off, Mesiros lies in the middle without a head, «Ἀμφοῖν ἀθλητῶν ἐκκεκομμένων κάρας, / Κεῖται Μέσιρος ἐν μέσῳ κάρας δίχα». That is the whole of the page. There is no country, no century, no emperor, no persecution named and no hymn, and nothing is claimed for him here beyond the name, the rank, the sword and the day.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/06/index.aspx) and [the life of the three](https://www.saint.gr/1518/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
