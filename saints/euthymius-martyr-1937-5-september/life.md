# Euthymius (Kochev)

Euthymius Petrovich Kochev was born in 1867 in the village of Vilgort in the Ust-Sysolsk district of Vologda province, today Vylgort in the Syktyvdin district of Komi, to a Komi peasant family; finishing the three-year village school he knew Russian well and wrote correctly, and made birch-bark baskets and worked as a joiner and a carter. Despite the persecution of believers he took an active part in the church life of Vilgort and made large gifts to the church of the Meeting of the Lord; in 1936 he was chosen churchwarden, and in June 1937, when the authorities resolved to close the church, went from yard to yard collecting signatures in its defence. On 13 September officers of the NKVD came to Vilgort, and he refused to hand them the keys of the church and tried to gather the people to prevent the inventory of the church’s property — the calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. Arrested, he rejected at interrogation the charge of counter-revolutionary activity; the troika sentenced him to be shot, and he was executed on 18 September 1937. He was glorified in 2001, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 5 September (Julian).
