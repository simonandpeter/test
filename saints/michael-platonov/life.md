# Michael (Platonov)

He is named with his bishop in one line of the Russian calendar for 27 September: «Сщмчч. Германа, епископа Вольского, и Михаила пресвитера (1919)». Its index of names gives him as «Михаил (Платонов), протоиерей, сщмч.», an archpriest, and keeps him here and on 26 January with the new martyrs and confessors of Russia.

That the calendar keeps the two of them in one line is the only thing it says about how they were connected, and it is enough to put [Germanus (Kosolapov)](/saints/germanus-kosolapov) on this page. Everything else — the parish, the charge, the manner of his death — the source does not print. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which refused this reading on 4 October 2026.

*After the Russian church calendar's [27 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260927.html) and its [index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14429.html); read 4 October 2026.*
