# Stephen of Tomsk

The Greek calendar keeps him on 30 Ιουνίου and says in as many words that it does not know him. saint.gr's heading is «Άγιος Στέφανος του Τόμσκ (; - 1876)» — Stephen of Tomsk, died 1876 — and the whole of the page beneath it is one sentence: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ρώσου Αγίου», we have no details of the life of the Russian saint. The year, the Siberian city and the word «Ρώσου» are all that this source gives.

The page gives him no rank — not monk, not priest, not martyr — so none is recorded here, and no century is guessed around the single year it prints. It links his memory to the synaxis of all the saints of Siberia, which is a feast and not a kinship. His own Russian calendar has not been read, and whatever life he has is there.
