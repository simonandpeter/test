# Diogenes, companion of Timothy

saint.gr's calendar names him with his companion and gives no life beyond a sentence: «Οι Άγιοι Μάρτυρες Τιμόθεος και Διογένης μαρτύρησαν στη Μακεδονία, πιθανώς το έτος 345 μ.Χ., θανατούμενοι από αιρετικούς Αρειανούς» — the holy martyrs [Timothy](/saints/timothy-companion-of-diogenes) and Diogenes were martyred in Macedonia, probably in the year 345, being put to death by heretical Arians.

That is all the page says of either of them. It names no city in Macedonia, no bishop and no judge, tells nothing of the manner of their death, names no relics and prints no couplet and no hymn. The note above his name on the page is the site's standing etymology of the name Διογένης, with its list of the Cynic philosopher, a tragic poet, a sculptor and Diogenes Laertius, and has nothing to do with this man.

The page's own «πιθανώς», probably, hangs over the year, and its heading nonetheless prints it flat as «(; - 345)»; the year is recorded here with that qualification in its note.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/06/index.aspx) and [the life of the two](https://www.saint.gr/1827/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
