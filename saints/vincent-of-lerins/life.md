# Vincent of Lérins

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and gives no life. Its line for 24 мая ст. ст. reads «Прп. Викентия Леринского (до 450).» — the venerable Vincent of Lérins, dead before 450 — and the line carries a link to his name and none to a reading. The name page behind it prints the title «Викентий Леринский, прп.» and the one line «День памяти: 24 мая», and nothing further: no surname, no see, no second day, no sentence of narrative.

So the calendar sets down a monk of the island house of Lérins, in Gaul, who died at some time before the year 450, and no more than that. It does not say that he wrote, and nothing on either page names a book or a controversy. The upper bound is the only year it gives, and the interval in this folder is therefore open at the beginning of the century and closed at the year the line prints.

No folder in this corpus held the name before: the four Vincents it keeps are a martyr of Spain, a martyr of 11 December and two twentieth-century Russians. The sources that would give him a life — azbyka.ru above all, and the Latin tradition his own church keeps him in — were not read.
