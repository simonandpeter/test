# Helladius, companion of Theophilus of Libya

saint.gr keeps two martyrs together on the eighth of January, a deacon and a layman, and gives them one short notice. These holy martyrs were from Libya, it says. They were arrested because they confessed Christ, and were brought before the proconsul and ruler of Libya; and because they persisted in the faith of Christ their flesh was torn to pieces, and so they gave up their souls into the hands of God and went up victorious to the heavens.

Of Helladius in particular the page says only what the day's line says, that he was «ο λαϊκός», the layman, beside [Theophilus](/saints/theophilus-the-deacon-of-libya) the deacon. No year, no city and no hymn is printed for either of them, and nothing is supplied here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/122/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
