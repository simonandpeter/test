# Peter (Kravets)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 20 октября ст. ст. in a line that names eleven people together: «Сщмчч. Зосимы, Иоанна, Иоанна, Иоанна, Николая, Леонида, Иоанна и Александра пресвитеров, Михаила и Петра диаконов и мч. Павла (1937).» He stands tenth in it, as «Петра», under the line's own rank words — «Сщмчч.», hieromartyrs, over the eight presbyters and the two deacons, and «мч.», martyr, over the layman at the end. The title on the name link behind his place carries the surname the line leaves out, and his grade with it: «Петр (Кравец), диакон, сщмч.» — a deacon, диакон.

Four of the eight presbyters in the line are Johns and the surname on the name link is the only thing that tells them apart; the line itself prints «Иоанна» three times running and once more after Leonid. The calendar carries no life page under his name, and the four hymns the day page prints are for two other saints it heads, neither of them in this line.

Nothing further is written here — not a birth, not a diocese, not a parish, not a charge, not a place of death. The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line. In particular he is not identified with any saint of the same name whom the calendar keeps on another day.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 20 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261020.html); read 4 October 2026.*
