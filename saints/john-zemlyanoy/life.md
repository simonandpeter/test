# John (Zemlyanoy)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him second among the hieromartyrs of its last line for 17 декабря and gives no life. The line is «Сщмчч. Петра и Иоанна пресвитеров (1937).» — the hieromartyr presbyters Peter and John, 1937 — and the calendar’s own name page adds the surname the line leaves out: «Иоанн (Земляной), иерей, сщмч.»

That is the whole of the record. He was a priest; he died in 1937, in the years the Russian church counts its new martyrs; and the calendar keeps him on this day beside [Peter (Pokrovsky)](/saints/peter-pokrovsky), the other presbyter of his line. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read: there is no life page behind the line, and the day page’s three hymns all belong to the prophet of its first line and to the three youths with him.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 17 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261217.html); read 13 October 2026.*
