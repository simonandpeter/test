# Alexis (Smirnov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him in a single line for 9 марта ст. ст., one line for nine persons: «Сщмчч. Михаила, Алексия, Димитрия, Сергия, Сергия пресвитеров и Николая диакона, прмч. Иоасафа и прмцц. Наталии и Александры (1938).» The line declines the forename to «Алексия» and gives no surname, no place, no year of birth and no word of narrative; the year it closes with, 1938, is the only year it gives, and the rank word «Сщмчч.» that governs his name is the only rank.

Behind that «Алексия» stands the calendar's own name page 10792, which carries the fuller form «Алексий (Смирнов), иерей, сщмч.» — the surname the day's line leaves out, and a priest's grade. That page lists two days: this 9 марта and «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. It carries no life, and neither does the day page; azbyka.ru, which has lives for some of the martyrs of these years, was not read for him. So what the calendar prints is the whole of what this folder says, and it says it rather than fill the silence: a priest of the Russian church of that name, who died a martyr in 1938 and whom this calendar keeps on this day.

This folder is **not** `alexis-smirnov`, and the two are as hard to tell apart as two entries in this corpus get. That folder's man stands behind the «Алексия» of the same calendar's line for 15 февраля ст. ст., which also closes with 1938, and the calendar titles him with the very same words, «Алексий (Смирнов), иерей, сщмч.» — the same forename, the same surname, the same rank and the same year. What separates them is that the calendar keeps two name pages, 13673 for the 15 февраля man and 10792 for this one, and each page lists its own single day beside the new martyrs' synaxis: 15 февраля on the one and 9 марта on the other. Two name pages are two men by this source's own reckoning, and neither page gives a year of birth, a place or a word of life by which to check it further.

*After the Православный церковный календарь, Сретенский монастырь of the Сретенский monastery for 9 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260309.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/10792.html), which carries no life; read 7 October 2026.*
