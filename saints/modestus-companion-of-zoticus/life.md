# Modestus, companion of Zoticus

Modestus is the third of the four martyrs the Greek calendar names in one line on 12 Ιανουαρίου: «Άγιοι Ζωτικός, Ρογάτος, Μοδεστός και Καστούλιος οι Μάρτυρες». The page behind the line gives all four one sentence — [Zoticus](/saints/zoticus-companion-of-rogatus), [Rogatus](/saints/rogatus-companion-of-zoticus), Modestus and [Castulus](/saints/castulus-companion-of-zoticus) were soldiers, and they were martyred in Africa — and then says it has no further details of their lives.

Beside the line the page glosses his name from the Latin modestus: the self-controlled, the sober-minded. That gloss and the four bare facts are the whole entry; there is no emperor, no city, no year, and no hymn.
