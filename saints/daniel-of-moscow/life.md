# Daniel of Moscow

The right-believing prince Daniel of Moscow, son of
[Alexander Nevsky](/saints/alexander-nevsky), died on 4 March 1303, and that
day is his chief memory. On 30 August 1652 his relics were found incorrupt,
and the Russian calendar keeps the finding on this day; they were moved
later to the church of the Fathers of the Seventh Ecumenical Council, and
rest now in the Danilov monastery at Moscow, where the Orthodox Church in
America’s notice says many of the faithful are healed at his shrine. The
Sretensky calendar’s life for the day says only the finding, and refers the
rest to 4 March.

*After the life printed by the Sretensky calendar (days.pravoslavie.ru) —
[the entry](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4549.htm); and the notice
printed by the Orthodox Church in America for 30 August —
[the day’s lives](https://www.oca.org/saints/all-lives/2026/08/30); read 5 September 2026.*
