# Alexander (Malinovsky)

Alexander Malinovsky (1892–1918) was born on 12 April 1892 in the town of Petrovsk in Saratov province to the priest John Malinovsky; his grandfather too was a cleric. He was educated at the town’s two-class school and, when the family moved to Volsk, at the church school, then entered the Perm seminary, where he met and married Vera, daughter of the priest Demetrius Zatoplyaev, and two sons were born to them, Michael and Valerian. In 1913 he finished the seminary in the first class and was ordained priest; in 1917 he was serving in the village of Verkh-Suksunskoye, where he spoke openly against the persecution of the Church. On 2 September 1918 he was arrested for a sermon in defence of the faith, savagely tortured and shot with the priest Leo Ershov at Krasnoufimsk; in 2002 his relics were found and identified — the marks of torture and a bullet wound remained on the body. He was canonised in 2000, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 20 August (Julian).
