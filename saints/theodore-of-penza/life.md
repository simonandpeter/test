# Theodore (Smirnov)

Theodore — Vladimir Alekseevich Smirnov in the world — was born on 17 January 1891 in the village of Kozlovka, today in the Lopatino district of the Penza region, a psalmist’s son. He finished the Saratov seminary and, in 1916, the Kazan Academy as a candidate of theology; from 1916 to 1918 he taught history and other subjects in the secondary schools of Volsk in Saratov province, in 1918–1919 worked in various offices at Saratov, in 1919–1920 served in the Red Army, in the Don North-Caucasian division, as instructor of the school and political departments, and in 1921 worked as instructor — the calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. He was arrested and exiled repeatedly; in 1935 he was consecrated bishop of Penza, but already in 1936 was arrested on the standard charge of counter-revolutionary activity with the priests of the town, among them [Basil Smirnov](/saints/basil-presbyter-martyr-1937-22-august) and [Gabriel Arkhangelsky](/saints/gabriel-presbyter-martyr-1937); despite his firmness at interrogation he was shot with them on 4 September 1937. He was numbered among the saints in 2000, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 22 August (Julian).
