# Elias of Calabria

Elias was a Greek by descent and abbot of the monastery of Melicucca in Calabria, in southern Italy. In the time of the iconoclasm in the East many Eastern monks fled with their icons to Calabria, and in time monastic life spread there widely; the Calabrian monks, the Prologue says, were marked by great learning and great strictness of life, and for a while there were so many Orthodox monasteries and monks in Calabria that men compared it to ancient Egypt. Orthodox Calabria afterwards came for centuries under the archbishops of Ohrid. Elias reposed in Thessalonica in 903.
