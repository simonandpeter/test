# Elias of Calabria

Elias was a Greek by descent and abbot of the monastery of Melicucca in Calabria, in southern Italy. In the time of the iconoclasm in the East many Eastern monks fled with their icons to Calabria, and in time monastic life spread there widely; the Calabrian monks, the Prologue says, were marked by great learning and great strictness of life, and for a while there were so many Orthodox monasteries and monks in Calabria that men compared it to ancient Egypt. Orthodox Calabria afterwards came for centuries under the archbishops of Ohrid. Elias reposed in Thessalonica in 903.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and gives the reason for the day. saint.gr says the venerable Elias the New was a founder of a monastery and an anchorite; that he came of Sicily and foretold the fall of Thessalonica; that he travelled in the Peloponnese and to Constantinople, where he fell ill; and that on his return towards Thessalonica he lodged in the church of the Holy Apostles and of Saint Demetrius. He died on 17 August 903, the page says, which is why his memory is kept on that day. Its head line gives his years as 823 to 903, where this folder had only the year of his repose; the figure is recorded here and the folder's dates are left as they stand. The page prints no hymn to him.
