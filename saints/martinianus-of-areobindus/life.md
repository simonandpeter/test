# Martinianus of the Areobindus church

saint.gr's calendar for 18 May names him and gives him one sentence, which begins by saying what is not known: the time in which the venerable Martinianus lived is unknown. He practised asceticism in the church of the Mother of God of the Areobindus quarter, and he fell asleep in peace.

That is the whole of the entry. It gives no country, no parents, no century, no manner of life beyond the word ascetic, no relics and no hymn. The couplet printed above it says only what his death was: Martinianus put off the things below, and has now put on the breadths of heaven. The Greek church keeps him on this day by the church he prayed in, and this folder does the same.
