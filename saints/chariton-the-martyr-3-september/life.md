# Chariton (3 September)

Chariton was martyred thrown into a pit of boiling lime, finding the unquenchable light of the undefiled place. That is the whole of the synaxarion’s notice for the martyr kept on 3 September, with its verse.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 3 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/936/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
