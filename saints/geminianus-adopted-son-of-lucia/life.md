# Geminianus, adopted son of Lucia

[Lucia](/saints/lucia-the-widow-of-rome) was a rich Roman woman in the time
of the emperors Diocletian (284–304) and Maximian (286–305). After
thirty-six years of widowhood, at seventy-five, she was betrayed to
Diocletian by her pagan son Eutropius as believing in Christ; she was
seized and shut in prison, and there suffered harsh tortures, but by God’s
grace remained unharmed and went about the city, so shaming the pettiness
of her torturers. Geminianus saw it, believed in Christ and presented
himself with Lucia before the king, confessing Christ; Lucia adopted
Geminianus and baptised him, and when they were rid of their torturers they
left for Tauromenium in Sicily — but there, because of persecution, Lucia
took to the mountains, where she died in peace, and Geminianus was seized
and beheaded. The synaxarion’s verses say Lucia, Christ’s servant, passed
peacefully to a place of peace, and the martyr Geminianus, full of courage,
bore bravely the honour of the sword. The Greek calendar keeps them on
17 September.
