# Hymenaeus

saint.gr's whole notice of him is one sentence: the holy Hymenaeus was martyred by the sword. The day's line names him bare, «Άγιος Υμέναιος», and the page gives no country, no city, no year, no emperor and no judge, prints no life beyond that sentence and no hymn. Its heading gives the feast as fixed, kept on 24 July of every year, and the entry carries no asterisk.

Above the notice the page sets a distich, which plays on his name, and that is the only other thing it prints of him: «Έχαιρεν Yμέναιος ήκων προς ξίφος, Ώσπερ προς υμέναιον ήκων νυμφίος» — Hymenaeus rejoiced as he went to the sword, as a bridegroom goes to his wedding song. The corpus keeps no other saint of the name, and the Greek calendar prints him beside two other martyrs of this day whose notices are just as short.
