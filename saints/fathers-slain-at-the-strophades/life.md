# The Fathers slain at the Strophades

The martyrs «slain at the Strophades» suffered in 1530, or in 1537, the Greek synaxarion says. What is known of them comes from their service, a work of Pachomios Rousanos published as a service sung to the venerable fathers slain at the Strophades and to all who met a like end. The historian Panagiotis Chiotis, of Banato, also published a short chronicle note kept in the monks’ hands, by which the monastery stood in its state until 29 July 1537, when the Turkish fleet passed Zakynthos and did no harm, but on its way back burned the Strophades and laid them waste. The entry names none of the fathers. The Greek calendar keeps them with an apolytikion and a kontakion.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 29 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2405/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
