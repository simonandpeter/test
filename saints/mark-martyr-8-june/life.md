# Mark (8 June)

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 8 June reads «Άγιος Μάρκος», Saint Mark, and the page behind it says only this: Saint Mark bore witness by the sword; we have no other details of his life. So what is recorded here is a name, the rank of a martyr and the manner of his death.

The couplet the page sets above that sentence is the only other thing it gives him: Mark, cut down, burns incense to the Lord, sending up a vapour out of his boiling blood. There is no year here, no emperor, no city, no accusation and no burial; there is no hymn. The heading above the entry is a note on the name, from the Latin marceo, to wither, and read there as meaning the man indifferent to the material things of earth — which is the page's own furniture and not a word about this martyr.
