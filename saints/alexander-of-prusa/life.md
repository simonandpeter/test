# Alexander of Prusa

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 9 June reads «Άγιος Αλέξανδρος επίσκοπος Προύσας», Saint Alexander, bishop of Prusa, and the page behind it says only this: Saint Alexander, bishop of Prusa, bore witness by the sword; there are no other biographical data of his, only an iconography of him. So what is recorded here is a name, a see in Bithynia, the rank of a hieromartyr and the manner of his death.

The couplet the page sets above that sentence is the only other thing it gives him: an all-becoming ornament of priesthood is your bright contest of the sword, Alexander. There is no year here, no emperor, no predecessor in the see and no account of what he was accused of; there is no troparion. The heading above the entry is a long note on the name and its famous bearers, ending with Alexander of Macedon, and it is the page's own furniture.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 9 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/09/index.aspx) and [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/1835/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
