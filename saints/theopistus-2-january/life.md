# Theopistus (2 January)

The Greek calendar keeps Theopistus on 2 January and prints one sentence about him: the holy Theopistus was martyred by stoning. The page reads his name as the man faithful to God, and gives a two-line epigram addressed to him — the crowd of the faithless, out of their ill counsel, kills you with stones, faithful Theopistus.

That is the whole of what this source holds. It names no place, no persecutor, no companion and no year, and none is added here. He is not the Theopistus who is kept with Eustathius and his household on 20 September; this is a separate entry on a separate day, and the calendar gives it a death of its own.
