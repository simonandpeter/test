# Nicetas the Chartularius

Nicetas lived in Constantinople and held the post of chartularius - a keeper of records. The calendar calls him the hidden one: living in the world, in the noise of the city, he reached perfection by secret labours of devotion, and nobody knew it while he lived.

The life the Russian calendar keeps tells how it came out. A priest and a deacon named Sozont had quarrelled; the priest died before they were reconciled, and the deacon's conscience gave him no rest. An experienced elder gave him a letter and told him to hand it to the first man he met at midnight by the doors of Hagia Sophia. That man was Nicetas the chartularius. He read the letter, wept, and said that what was asked of him was beyond his strength - but that by the prayers of the elder who had sent Sozont he would attempt it. He bowed before the church doors and they opened to him of themselves; he prayed, and the deacon was shown his answer, and the hidden servant's holiness was hidden no longer.

*After the Православный церковный календарь (days.pravoslavie.ru), [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1494.htm); read 30 August 2026.*
