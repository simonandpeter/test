# Tiridates

The Охридски пролог gives him six short sentences and they are the whole of this folder. He was a contemporary of Diocletian, and at first he persecuted the Christians horribly. But the judgement of God overtook him: he went mad and became like a beast, as Nebuchadnezzar had once become.

Of that madness he was healed by a wonder, by [Gregory the Illuminator](/saints/gregory-the-illuminator), whom the book names with his own day, 30 September — the enlightener of the Armenians, whose company of virgin martyrs belongs to this same persecution. From that time until his death Tiridates passed his life in repentance and in piety, and he ended peacefully in the fourth century.

That century is the only date the book gives him. It names no year of birth and no year of death, no city, and no place where he reigned beyond the nation in his title; it does not say how long he reigned, what he did after his repentance, or where he was buried. Nothing has been added here, and no other calendar was read for him, so this folder claims nothing either way about the Russian, the Romanian or the Greek church.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 29. новембар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2025-12-12&prolog=1); read 8 October 2026.*
