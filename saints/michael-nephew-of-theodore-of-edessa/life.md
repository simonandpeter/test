# Michael, nephew of Theodore of Edessa

saint.gr prints no life for this Michael, only a notice. His memory, it says, is mentioned in the Jerusalem codex 1096, folio 115. He was the nephew of [Theodore of Edessa](/saints/theodore-the-sabbaite-of-edessa) — the page sends its reader to its own entry for Theodore, on this same 19 July — and he was a monk of the Lavra of Saint Sabbas. That is the whole of it: the page gives him no year, no century, no place beyond the Lavra, and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 19 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/19/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/723/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
