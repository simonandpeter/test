# Alexander (Uksusov)

**A collective-farm bookkeeper who was for some years the chairman of his church community, and who died in a camp within three months of a ten-year sentence.** The Сретенский monastery’s calendar closes the day with «Сщмч. Елеазара пресвитера и мч. Александра (1937).» and carries no life for either; the life here is the short notice azbyka.ru prints for the civil 6 декабря out of the Yaroslavl diocese’s own site, and it is the whole of what the sources read say of him. Александр Александрович Уксусов was born in 1874 in a pious peasant family — the notice gives no place — and having finished school worked on the land as his parents had; in Soviet times he was a bookkeeper in the collective farms of the Некоузский district.

**He was arrested as a member of a church insurgent group and gave nobody away.** Being a deeply believing man he filled for several years the duties of chairman of the church community in the village of Марьино. In September 1937 he was arrested and accused as a participant in a church insurgent group «в нелегальных сборищах, повстанческой и пораженческой агитации, клевете на политику партии и вовлечении колхозников в религиозную общину» — of illegal gatherings, insurgent and defeatist agitation, slander of the party’s policy, and drawing collective farmers into a religious community. Under questioning he denied that the church meetings were anti-Soviet in character and spoke against nobody.

**He was sentenced to ten years and lived not three months of them.** The notice closes that he «закончил свой скорбный мученический путь 6 декабря 1937 г. в Кулойлаге» — that he finished his sorrowful martyr’s road on 6 December 1937 in the Кулой camp, which is the civil day of this Julian 23 November and the same day as [Eleazar (Spiridonov)](/saints/eleazar-spiridonov), whom the calendar names beside him and who died the same day in the mines of the Kolyma. Of his parents, his wife if he had one, his interrogators and his burial place nothing is said.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 23 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261123.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9931.html); and the entry azbyka.ru prints for the civil 6 декабря — [the page](https://azbyka.ru/days/sv-aleksandr-uksusov); read 7 October 2026.*
