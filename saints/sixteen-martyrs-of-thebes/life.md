# The Sixteen Martyrs of Thebes

Sixteen martyrs of Thebes who died by the sword. The synaxarion records nothing more; its verse plays on the number — sixteen men beheaded, four times four.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 30 Αυγούστου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/843/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
