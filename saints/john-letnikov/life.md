# John (Letnikov)

The Russian calendar gives him five words and a year: «Св. Иоанна исп (1930)» — Saint John, confessor, 1930. «Исп.» is the mark of one who suffered for the faith and was not killed for it, and it is the only thing either of the calendar's pages says about how he suffered. The site's index of names supplies the surname the day line leaves out, heading his page «Иоанн (Летников), исповедник», and stops there.

1930 is well inside the campaign against the Church, and the calendar does not say what that campaign did to him: no birth, no province, no trade, no office, no charge, no place of death. His index entry keeps him here and on 26 January with the whole company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and carries no life. The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian saints are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026 even with a browser user-agent, so the line stands as the line.

[Laurence (Levchenko)](/saints/laurence-levchenko) and [Alexander (Pozdeevsky)](/saints/alexander-pozdeevsky) stand beside him in the same paragraph of the same day under the same silence, and [Nicholas](/saints/nicholas-of-alma-ata), who outlived all three, closes it.

*After the Russian church calendar's [12 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261012.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13826.html); read 4 October 2026.*
