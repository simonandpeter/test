# Theodore, companion of Pausolypius

saint.gr gives the pair one sentence. The holy hieromartyr Theodore and the holy martyr [Pausolypius](/saints/pausolypius-companion-of-theodore), it says, were made perfect by the sword in Thrace in the years of the emperor Hadrian, which it dates 117 to 138; and their synaxis used to be kept at the old people's home of Melobios — «Η Σύναξη αυτών ετελείτο στο γηροκομείο του Μελοβίου». That is the whole of the notice: no trial, no interrogation, no place in Thrace named, and no year for either man.

Its couplet plays on the other man's name, which means the ending of grief: «Παυσολυπίῳ καὶ Θεοδώρῳ θύτῃ, Δῶρόν τι παυσίλυπον ὄντως τὸ ξίφος» — to Pausolypius and to Theodore the sacrificer the sword was truly a gift that put an end to grief. Theodore the page's line calls a presbyter and its prose a hieromartyr, and it prints no hymn for either of them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 15 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/15/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1316/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
