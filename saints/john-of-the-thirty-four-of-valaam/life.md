# John, one of the thirty-four of Valaam

The Valaam monastery stood near the border between the lands of Great Novgorod and Sweden, and in the course of its long history the Swedes laid it waste more than once. Under king Gustav Vasa, whose years the calendar gives as 1523 to 1560, the Reformation was carried through in Sweden; and in the reign of his son John III a troop of newly converted Lutherans — who, in words the calendar quotes from a Russian bishop of the nineteenth century, «were still burning with a fanatical attachment to their newly born faith» — crossed the ice from the mainland to the island in pursuit of Orthodox Karelians and fell on the monastery.

On 20 февраля 1578, the calendar says, eighteen elders of the house and sixteen novices were killed as martyrs for their firmness in the Orthodox faith. Their names were entered in a synodikon with the note «побиты от Немец на Валааме старцев и слуг» — beaten down by the Germans at Valaam, elders and servants — which afterwards came to light in the Vasilyevsky monastery. The list it prints names thirty-four men, nineteen of them before the word «послушники» and fifteen after it, where the sentence above it counts eighteen and sixteen; the thirty-four is what both agree on. The attacks of the Swedes went on afterwards, and there is no stone on Valaam, the page says, that is not sealed with the blood of ascetics.

In the nineteenth century one of the Valaam monks, near the hermitage of abbot Nazarius, was counted worthy of a vision of black-robed men he did not know: «they were walking in two rows out of a green grove flooded with sunlight, and singing the funeral prayers in the old chant. They walked with their hands folded on their breasts, and their faces were full of light and their eyes of an unspeakable meekness. Only when the procession came close to the monk did he see that all of them were spattered with blood and covered with wounds. Where they had passed, the grass was not trodden down. They vanished as they had appeared, into the green thicket, and the quiet echoes of the funeral chant hung a long while in the air.»

With the blessing of abbot Damascene the divine Liturgy «for their eternal rest» was sung at Valaam every year on the day of the thirty-four monks' martyrdom, 20 февраля — 4 March by the new style — and a general requiem with it. They were numbered among the saints of the Russian Orthodox Church for veneration throughout the church at the Jubilee Council of Bishops in August 2000.

This folder is for the John whom the synodikon names eleventh of the thirty-four, and whom it calls «инок», a monk of the house. The list names more than one man of this forename, and this is the one in that place in it; the calendar prints no further word to tell them apart.
