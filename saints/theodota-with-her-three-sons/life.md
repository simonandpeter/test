# Theodota, with her three sons

This saint was of the city of Cilicia, a woman of right belief, who laboured at the commandments of God and taught her children the knowledge of God; she lived in the time when the holy martyr Anastasia was living too. The ruler Leucadius pressed her to take him for a husband, and she would not at all.

For that she was sent to Nicetius, the ruler of Bithynia, who ordered that she be put, with her three children, into a furnace of fire; and there she received the crown of martyrdom. Their synaxis is kept in the place of their martyrdom, which is at Cambo.

That is the whole of what the Romanian calendar prints of her. It does not name her three sons, nor give their ages, nor set down any year; so the children are left here as the page leaves them, counted and unnamed, and they have no folders of their own.

The Greek calendar keeps her on the same day, 29 July. saint.gr says Theodota came from Nicaea in Bithynia — where the Romanian account says Cilicia — and that from a little girl she kept the commandments of God in her life with great consistency, something she tried to pass on to her children as well. Because of her Christian faith she was seized by the ruler Leucadius, who had asked her in marriage and had been refused, and he handed her over to Nicetius, the ruler of Bithynia; and he, hard and malicious as he was, threw Theodota and her three children into a white-hot furnace, where they gave up their souls to God and took the crown of eternal life.

The page names none of the three children either, and gives no year. It adds one thing the Romanian calendar does not: that the memory of Saint Theodota and her three children is kept again on 22 December, with similar biographical details.
