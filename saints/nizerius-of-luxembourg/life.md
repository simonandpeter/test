# Nizerius of Luxembourg

saint.gr's calendar for 5 December names Nizerius and gives him two sentences, which are the whole of what it has: the holy Nizerius was active in Luxembourg, and we have no other details of the saint's life. The page prints no century, no reign, no manner of death, no relics and none of the synaxaristic couplets it sets over most of its entries, and it gives him no rank beyond «Άγιος».

Luxembourg is the only place the entry names, and the page offers nothing by which to date him or to say what he was. What is written down here is that line and no more.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3163/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
