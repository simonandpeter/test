# Anastasius the Younger, disciple of Maximus

Anastasius, the younger of the two of that name among the disciples of [Maximus
the Confessor](/saints/maximus-the-confessor), was exiled to Thrace with the other Anastasius,
brought back to Constantinople, condemned by a synod, and mutilated with his teacher — tongue and
right hand. He died in a fort in Thrace, silent, and the Greek notice calls him for that a
thundering herald of the Orthodox truth. The Greek calendar keeps him on 20 September.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 20 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/982/saint.aspx); read 26 August 2026.*
