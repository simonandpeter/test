# Agapitus the Deacon

Agapitus was one of the two deacons — the other was [Felicissimus](/saints/felicissimus-the-deacon) — imprisoned with Pope [Sixtus](/saints/sixtus-ii-of-rome) in the persecution of Valerian, and beheaded with him on 6 August 258, before the soldier Romanus and the archdeacon [Lawrence](/saints/lawrence-of-rome). The life printed for the whole company says nothing of him on his own, and the Prologue only names him.
