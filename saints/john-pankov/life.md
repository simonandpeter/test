# John (Pankov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names the priest John Pankov on 26 апреля and gives no life of him. Its line for the day reads «Сщмч. Иоанна пресвитера и чад его мчч. Николая и Петра (1918).» — the hieromartyr John the presbyter, and his children the martyrs Nicholas and Peter. So what the calendar says of him is that he was a priest, that he suffered in 1918, and that he suffered together with his two sons, [Nicholas (Pankov)](/saints/nicholas-pankov) and [Peter (Pankov)](/saints/peter-pankov).

His own name page on that site, 9624, carries nothing but the heading «Иоанн (Панков), иерей, сщмч.», which gives the surname the day's line leaves out and writes the grade «иерей», priest, beside «сщмч.», and the two days «26 января (Новомуч.) , 26 апреля». There is no life page for him anywhere on the site, no icon and no article. The day page prints five hymns, for the three ancient saints it heads, and none under any of the three names of this family. The 26 января day page was not read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 26 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260426.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9624.html); read 8 October 2026.*
