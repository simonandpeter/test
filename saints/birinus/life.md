# Birinus

saint.gr keeps a saint Birinus on the third of December, printing the name in Latin letters inside a Greek line and marking him «(Άγγλος)», English. The whole of its entry for him is one sentence: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Άγγλου Αγίου Birinus» — we have no details of the life of the English saint Birinus. There is no «Άγιοι που εορτάζουν» notice behind it beyond the name, no couplet and no picture but one.

Two things are therefore attested and no more: that the Greek calendar keeps him on this day, and that he is English. The page gives him no century, no see, no monastery, no rank, no relic and no hymn, and it does not identify him with any particular Birinus of the English calendars. Anything further would have to come from an English source this folder has not read.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/03/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/245/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
