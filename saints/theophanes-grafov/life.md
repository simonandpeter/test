# Theophanes (Grafov)

**The third name on the line, and the only one of the day’s four whose service the Church has since written.** days.pravoslavie.ru heads him on name page 13694 and carries no life; the life here is azbyka.ru’s, out of игумен Дамаскин (Орловский)’s «Жития новомучеников и исповедников Российских ХХ века. Март». He was born on 6 January 1874 in the слобода Никитская of the Переславский уезд in the Vladimir country, son of a peasant, and was christened Феодосий.

In 1902 he entered the Борисоглебский monastery at Ростов as a novice; in 1907 he was tonsured with the name Феофан, and in 1908 ordained hierodeacon. The monastery was closed in 1923, and the monks, wanting to keep their own house, agreed to go on as an agricultural artel; in 1929 the last monasteries in the country were closed and theirs among them. He moved to Сергиев Посад and was taken on to serve at the Успенский church, where he served until 21 October 1935.

On that day he was arrested with a group of the clergy living in the Посад, all of them charged with counter-revolutionary and anti-Soviet activity. The investigator put it to him that he had spread false rumours among the believers of a persecution for religious conviction. «С таким вопросом ко мне никто не обращался», he answered — nobody had put such a question to him, and he had said nothing to anybody about a persecution of believers in the USSR; asked what he answered those who came to him about the closing of churches, he said again that nobody came. He admitted nothing: «против власти я нигде не говорил».

On 8 January 1936 a Special Conference at the NKVD gave him three years’ exile in the Northern krai, and he was sent first to Архангельск and then to Каргополь. In the autumn of 1937 an officer of the NKVD wrote out a warrant for his arrest on informers’ words: that he had spread provocative talk of hunger in the USSR, of peasants starving wholesale in the villages and dying of it, of life in the collective farms being impossible, of the Bolsheviks feeding the people on promises for twenty years and giving them nothing. Calling him «отъявленным контрреволюционером» and «фашистским выродком», the officer proposed that he be held in the first category, as a clergyman and a candidate for shooting, and arrested at once. He was arrested on 25 September 1937 and shut in the prison at Каргополь.

An informer among the clergy exiled there was questioned the same day, and said that Графов could not stomach the collective farms and called them an antichrist’s kingdom; that in the spring of 1937 he had said the Soviet power had no bread because nobody would work in the farms, and who would, when the best grain went to the state and the farm workers were left the poorest sorts and little of those. Then the deacon himself was questioned. «Расскажите о вашей практической контрреволюционной деятельности и ваших соучастниках!» the investigator demanded. «Контрреволюционной деятельностью я не занимался и соучастников не имею», he answered.

On 15 October 1937 a troika of the NKVD gave him ten years in a corrective labour camp. **He died in the camp at Каргополь on 18 March 1938 and was buried in an unknown grave.** The service to him was approved by the Holy Synod on 25 August 2022, and azbyka.ru prints it whole; its troparion and kontakion are entered in this folder with the accents that page gives them, and they are the only hymns any of the day’s four twentieth-century names has. Its stichera keep the house he was tonsured in and the country he was driven to, and say of the grave «телом в безвестней могиле посеявся, душею в Царствии Небеснем процвел еси» — sown in body in an unmarked grave, he flowered in soul in the Kingdom of Heaven.
