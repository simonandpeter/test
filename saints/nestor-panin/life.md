Ананьевского, Константина , Димитрия , Нестора , Феодора , Константина , Виктора , Илии , Павла пресвитеров, Иосифа диакона и прмч. Алексия (1937).» One year stands for all eleven, «Сщмчч.» heads the ten clergy and «прмч.» the monk who closes the line, and the grades it prints sort them into a bishop, eight presbyters, a deacon and a hieromonk. The name link behind his place supplies the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line leaves out, «Нестор (Панин), протоиерей, сщмч.», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from.

**days.pravoslavie.ru gives this company nothing but that line.** Each of the eleven name pages carries a title, a list of days and no text at all, and not one of them has a `/Life/` link of any kind; the day page's hymns are the two martyrs' of 284–305, the venerable Matrona's, the Mother of God's before the Скоропослушница icon and the wonderworker of Aegina's, and nothing is sung under any of these eleven names. The life below is azbyka.ru's, read in the in-app browser because that host answers no fetcher, and the paragraphs are this corpus's own.

Нестор Прокофьевич Панин was born in 1881 in the city of Semipalatinsk. Choosing the way of a pastor, he finished the Tomsk theological seminary and was ordained to the cathedral of the Resurrection in his own city, and for long and diligent service he was raised archpriest. He was arrested on 28 August 1937, on the feast of the Dormition. The charge of the NKVD was that he was «член контрреволюционной шпионской организации церковников», a member of a counter-revolutionary spy organisation of churchmen.

He did not admit the charge, and he refused to give evidence that would have put the blame on anybody else. On 19 November a troika of the NKVD sentenced him to be shot, and at two in the morning on 22 November he was shot. He was numbered among the saints by the Council of Bishops of the Russian Orthodox Church in the year 2000. 9 ноября ст. ст. is the civil 22 November, so the calendar keeps him on the very day he was shot.

**These four are a company and the pages say so in as many words.** Черепанов's life names the collective case he shared with [Nestor (Panin)](/saints/nestor-panin), [Theodore (Chichkanov)](/saints/theodore-chichkanov) and [Victor (Klimov)](/saints/victor-klimov); Климов's life says he was shot «вместе со священномучениками прот. Нестором Паниным, прот. Феодором Чичкановым и свящ. Константином Черепановым» on the strength of one decision of the troika of 19 November 1937; and Чичканов's life prints the decision itself, naming six men sentenced as members of one fabricated organisation and carrying the sentence out on 22 November 1937 at Semipalatinsk. One case, one sentence, one day, one city: `related` rows are written between all four, both ways.
