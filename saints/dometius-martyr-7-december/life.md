# Dometius (7 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 7 December names Dometius and gives him one line of life: the holy Dometius was martyred by the sword. The page prints no country, no reign, no persecutor and no year, and it joins him to no other name on the day. What it adds is the couplet set over him, and the couplet is an exchange.

Give, Dometius, martyr of Christ, and receive, it says: give your head, and receive a great crown. That is the whole of the entry. The corpus keeps several other men of this name on other days of the year, and nothing here joins this one to any of them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3185/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
