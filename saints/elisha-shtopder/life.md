# Elisha (Shtopder)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Elisha seventh in a company of eight on 7 August and gives no life for him. He is the one the entry itself sets apart as a deacon: «Сщмчч. Александра, Петра, Михаила, Иоанна, Димитрия и Алексия пресвитеров, Елисея диакона и прмч. Афанасия (1937).» — six presbyters, a deacon, and a monastic. The calendar's own name page adds the surname: «Елисей (Штопьдер), диакон, сщмч.», Elisha Shtopder, deacon.

That is the whole of the record. He was a deacon; he died in 1937; the calendar keeps him on this day, the day after the Transfiguration, among the new martyrs of Russia. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 7 августа ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260807.html); read 13 October 2026.*
