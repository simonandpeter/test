# John (Artemov)

He is the one layman on the Russian calendar's last line for 1 October, and the line's own grammar is what says so: «Сщмчч. Александра, Георгия, Николая пресвитеров, мч. Иоанна (1937)» puts three presbyters under «сщмчч.», hieromartyrs, and then changes to «мч.» for him — a martyr with no rank in the Church and no office. The year 1937 is printed once, after his name, and covers all four.

The site's index of names heads his page «Иоанн (Артемов), мч.» and keeps him here and on 26 January with the company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. It prints no birth, no trade, no parish he belonged to, no charge and no place of death. His fellows on the line are [Alexander (Agafonnikov)](/saints/alexander-agafonnikov), [George (Arkhangelsky)](/saints/george-arkhangelsky) and [Nicholas (Kuligin)](/saints/nicholas-kuligin). The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line.

*After the Russian church calendar's [1 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261001.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14098.html); read 4 October 2026.*
