# Paraskeve, sister of Photini

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Φεβρουαρίου prints «Αγία Παρασκευή» as a line of its own, and the page standing under it gives one sentence: «Η Αγία Παρασκευή ήταν η τέταρτη αδελφή της Αγίας Φωτεινής και μαρτύρησε δια ξίφους» — Paraskeve was the fourth sister of St Photini, and she was martyred by the sword. The fuller story is on the page of [Photini the Samaritan](/saints/photini-the-samaritan), which says that her sons and her five sisters were martyred together with her: she had been baptised among the first women of Samaria after her meeting with Christ at Jacob's well, had preached the Gospel in Africa and at Rome, and was put to death at Rome by the emperor Nero.

The rest of the household the calendar prints beside her on this day is [Anatole](/saints/anatole-sister-of-photini), [Photo](/saints/photo-sister-of-photini), [Photis](/saints/photis-sister-of-photini), [Kyriake](/saints/kyriake-sister-of-photini), [Photinos](/saints/photinos-son-of-photini) and [Joses](/saints/joses-son-of-photini). This page gives Paraskeve no birthplace, no year, no relics and no hymn of her own — the second apolytikion on Photini's page names all eight of them together — and over the one sentence it prints a couplet, «Ἡ Παρασκευὴ πρὸς ξίφος τὸν αὐχένα, Ἕτοιμον εἶχε καὶ παρεσκευασμένον»: Paraskeve held her neck ready and prepared for the sword, which is her own name over again.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3656/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
