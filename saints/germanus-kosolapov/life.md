# Germanus (Kosolapov)

The Russian calendar names him with his own priest in one line for 27 September: «Сщмчч. Германа, епископа Вольского, и Михаила пресвитера (1919)» — Germanus, bishop of Volsk, and Michael the presbyter, 1919. Its index of names gives him as «Святитель Герман (Косолапов), епископ Вольский, сщмч.», which is the one piece of substance the source adds: he was a bishop, of Volsk on the Volga, and he died with [Michael (Platonov)](/saints/michael-platonov) in the year the civil war was at its worst there.

That is the whole of it. The calendar prints no birth year, no account of the arrest and no place of death. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which refused this reading on 4 October 2026, so a bishop of a see gets three facts and an admission.

*After the Russian church calendar's [27 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260927.html) and its [index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14428.html); read 4 October 2026.*
