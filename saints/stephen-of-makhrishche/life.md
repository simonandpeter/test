# Stephen of Makhrishche

saint.gr's calendar for 14 July names Stephen, abbot of the monastery of Makhrishche, and gives his year of repose and nothing else. The heading over his notice reads «Οσιος Στεφανος Ηγουμενος Της Μονης Μαχριστσκ (; - 1406)», with a question mark where a year of birth would be; and of the man himself the page declines to say anything: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ρώσσου Αγίου» — we have no details of the life of this Russian saint.

So this folder holds what saint.gr holds, and no more: a Russian abbot, the house he governed, a year of death, and the Greek calendar's keeping of him on 14 July. The page prints no hymn, no place beyond the monastery's name, and no account of his life, his labours or his end. It writes the monastery in Greek letters as «Μάχριστσκ»; the English form used here is the usual rendering of its Russian name, and no other calendar has been read for him.
