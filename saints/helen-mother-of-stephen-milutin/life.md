# Helen, mother of Stephen Milutin

saint.gr's calendar for 30 Οκτωβρίου keeps three of one family together: «Όσιοι Στέφανος Μιλιούτιν, Θεόκτιστος ο αδελφός του και Ελένη η μητέρα τους». Helen is the mother, and the page's heading for her gives her no other name than that: «Οσια Ελενη η Μητερα Των Οσιων Στεφανου Μιλιουτιν Και Θεοκτιστου (; - 1306)». Her two sons, [Stephen Milutin](/saints/stephen-milutin) and [Theoctistus](/saints/theoctistus-brother-of-stephen-milutin), both ruled Serbia and are kept with her on this day.

Of her own life the notice says two things: that she lived in a way pleasing to God, and that she worked hard for the raising of new monasteries. Then it says that she fell asleep in 1306. There is no birthplace, no year of birth, no account of her marriage, no title and no relics; the page does not even call her a queen.

It prints no hymn for any of the three. It carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands, short as it is.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 30 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/30/index.aspx) and [the notice of the three](https://www.saint.gr/2850/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
