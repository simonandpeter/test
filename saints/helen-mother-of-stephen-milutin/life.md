# Helen, mother of Stephen Milutin

saint.gr's calendar for 30 Οκτωβρίου keeps three of one family together: «Όσιοι Στέφανος Μιλιούτιν, Θεόκτιστος ο αδελφός του και Ελένη η μητέρα τους». Helen is the mother, and the page's heading for her gives her no other name than that: «Οσια Ελενη η Μητερα Των Οσιων Στεφανου Μιλιουτιν Και Θεοκτιστου (; - 1306)». Her two sons, [Stephen Milutin](/saints/stephen-milutin) and [Theoctistus](/saints/theoctistus-brother-of-stephen-milutin), both ruled Serbia and are kept with her on this day.

Of her own life the notice says two things: that she lived in a way pleasing to God, and that she worked hard for the raising of new monasteries. Then it says that she fell asleep in 1306. There is no birthplace, no year of birth, no account of her marriage, no title and no relics; the page does not even call her a queen.

It prints no hymn for any of the three. It carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands, short as it is.

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps her with her two sons on one line and one page, and what it gives her is a list of works. She was the godly mother of her holy sons, and after her husband's death she gave the whole of her life to things pleasing to God: she built houses for villagers who had fallen into poverty, and she set up monastic houses for those who wished to live in purity and virginity.

Near the town of Spič she raised the Reča monastery and endowed it with lands of its own. Before her own end she received the monastic habit, and she went to the Lord on 8 February 1306 — the Greek page had the year, and the Russian adds the day. Her name link calls her a queen as well as venerable, which no page read for her had said. The calendar prints no hymn under her name, though it prints a troparion and a kontakion for each of her sons on the same day.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 30 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/30/index.aspx) and [the notice of the three](https://www.saint.gr/2850/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 30 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261030.html) and [the life of the three](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4618.htm), read 4 October 2026.*
