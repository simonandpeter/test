# Loukia, companion of Kyprilla

saint.gr's calendar for 4 Ιουλίου keeps three women of Cyrene in one line — Kyprilla, Aroa and Loukia — and its life page tells each of them apart. Of Loukia it says only what she did and how she died. [Kyprilla](/saints/kyprilla-of-cyrene), it says, came from the same homeland as [Theodore](/saints/theodore-of-cyrene), bishop of Cyrene, and was a widow of twenty-eight years who went to the saint in prison to be healed of dreadful headaches.

From that time Kyprilla stayed there together with Loukia and [Aroa](/saints/aroa-companion-of-kyprilla), and the three of them served the saint. After Theodore's martyric end Kyprilla too was denounced to the governor, and because they could not make her sacrifice to the idols they hung her on a piece of wood and tore her flesh, and so she gave up her blessed soul. Her honourable relic Loukia and Aroa received and buried with honours.

Later, the page says, the governor Dignianus put both of those two women to death as well; it names no manner of death for them and gives no year. That is all it says of Loukia. It prints no hymn for her, only the synaxarion couplet she shares with Aroa: «Έδειξαν ημίν Aρόα και Λουκία, / Ως και κόραι σθένουσι καρτερείν ξίφος» — Aroa and Loukia showed us that girls too have the strength to endure the sword.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 4 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/04/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1990/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
