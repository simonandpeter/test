# Nomon

saint.gr's calendar for 18 December keeps the venerable Nomon, and what its page says is where the name was found and what it does not know. The memory of the venerable Nomon, it says, is mentioned in passing in the Little Euchologion or Hagiasmatarion published by the Apostolic Diakonia in 1956, without any other information; and it adds, in its own voice and marked as a guess, that he is probably a saint of the Church of Cyprus.

That is the whole of the entry. The page gives him no year of birth and none of death, no monastery and no office, no manner of life and no manner of death, no relic, and no hymn; and because it says outright that it has no information, no rank is recorded for him here either, not even the one the calendar line's own word would give.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3297/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
