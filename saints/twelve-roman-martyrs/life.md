# The Twelve Roman Martyrs

saint.gr's calendar for 31 Ιουλίου keeps a company of twelve martyrs of Rome and names none of them. Its line is «Άγιοι Δώδεκα Μάρτυρες οι Ρωμαίοι» — the holy Twelve Martyrs, the Romans — and the page behind it is one sentence: «Οι Άγιοι Δώδεκα Μάρτυρες οι Ρωμαίοι μαρτύρησαν δια ξίφους», they were martyred by the sword.

Besides that the page prints the couplet of the synaxarion, which is the only other thing it says of them: «Pώμης παλαιάς δώδεκα βλαστούς Λόγε, / Tραχηλοτμήτους Mάρτυρας δέξαι νέους» — receive, O Word, twelve shoots of old Rome, new martyrs with their necks cut through. There is no century, no emperor, no judge, no place of their suffering, no hymn and no name for any of the twelve, so they are left here as the calendar leaves them, counted and unnamed, with no folders of their own.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 31 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/31/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2132/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
