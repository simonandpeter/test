# Vladimir (Volkov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him for 12 марта ст. ст. in the line «Сщмч. Иоанна, Константина пресвитеров, прмч. Владимира (1938).» The line declines the forename to «Владимира» and gives no surname, no place, no year of birth and no word of narrative; the year it closes with, 1938, is the only year it gives.

Behind that «Владимира» stands the calendar's own name page 10802, which carries the fuller form «Владимир (Волков), архимандрит, сщмч.» — the surname the day's line leaves out, and an archimandrite's grade — and lists two days, this 12 марта and «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. It carries no life, and neither does the day page; azbyka.ru was not read for him. So what the calendar prints is the whole of what this folder says, and it says that rather than fill the silence.

**The two halves of the calendar give him two different ranks and this folder carries both.** The day's line heads him «прмч.», a venerable-martyr, which is the grade of a monastic put to death; his name page 10802 titles him «сщмч.», a hieromartyr, which is the grade of an ordained man put to death. An archimandrite is both a monk and a priest, so neither reading contradicts the other and neither is dropped; the folder holds the two and the office the name page gives.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 12 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260312.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/10802.html), which carries no life; read 7 October 2026.*
