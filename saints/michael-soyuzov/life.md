# Michael (Soyuzov)

The Russian calendar's 6 October is the apostle Thomas's day, and after him it prints two priests of the Soviet years. The first is this one, and the line is six words: «Св. Михаила пресвитера, исп (1922)» — holy Michael, presbyter, confessor, 1922. «Исп.» is the mark of a confessor, so he did not die for the faith by violence; «св.» rather than «сщмч.» says the same thing again. The site's index of names gives the surname and heads his page «Михаил (Союзов), исп.», dropping the office the day line supplies.

1922 is the year of the campaign to seize the church valuables and of the trials that went with it, and the calendar does not say whether that is what he confessed through; it says nothing about him at all beyond the name, the rank and the year. His index entry keeps him here and on 26 January with the whole company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and carries no life. The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian saints are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026 even with a browser user-agent, so the line stands as the line. [John (Rybin)](/saints/john-rybin), fifteen years later on the same day, is the other.

*After the Russian church calendar's [6 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261006.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/15073.html); read 4 October 2026.*
