# Melchion of Antioch

saint.gr's calendar for 28 Οκτωβρίου keeps Melchion, presbyter of Antioch, together with [Firmilian of Caesarea in Cappadocia](/saints/firmilian-of-caesarea-in-cappadocia), and prints one short notice for the two of them. Both of these holy men contended against the heretics, the page says; and the epigram set above the notice has them die in peace: «Εἰρηνικῶς θνῄσκουσιν εἰρήνης τέκνα, Φιρμιλιανὸς καὶ σὺν αὐτῷ Μελχίων» — peaceably they die, the children of peace, Firmilian and Melchion with him.

Everything else on the page belongs to his companion: that [Basil the Great](/saints/basil-the-great) speaks of Firmilian with affection, and that a synod Firmilian convoked at Iconium decided that the Novatians, and heretics generally who wished to come over to the true Church, were to be baptised. Of Melchion himself saint.gr gives no year, no birthplace and no account beyond the struggle against heresy and the city whose presbyter he was.

The page prints no hymn for either man. It carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/28/index.aspx) and [the notice he shares with Firmilian](https://www.saint.gr/2822/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
