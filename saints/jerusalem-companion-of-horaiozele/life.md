# Jerusalem, companion of Horaiozele

saint.gr says the holy Jerusalem was the friend of Saint [Horaiozele](/saints/horaiozele-of-byzantium) and shared her house. She had a warm piety of her own and other virtues besides, and she helped her in spreading the Gospel in the city of Byzantium; and so she too was arrested, together with the woman who contended beside her.

The judge tried to draw her over by proposing to marry her to a most handsome pagan officer. But the faithful virgin rejected it, and so she was beheaded, and took the unfading crown of martyrdom. Above the notice the page sets a distich that plays on her name: «Tομήν κεφαλής Iερουσαλήμ φέρει, Kαι την άνω νυν Iερουσαλήμ βλέπει» — Jerusalem bears the cutting of her head, and now she looks upon the Jerusalem above.

That is the whole of what the Greek page prints of her. It gives no parents, no year, no name for the judge, and no hymn. It adds only that the saint's head is kept at the Metropolis of Beroea.
