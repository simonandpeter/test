# Alcibiades of Lyons

saint.gr's account of him is two sentences long, and one of them is a doubt. The holy martyr Alcibiades lived in the second century after Christ in Gaul; because he was a Christian he was arrested and was perfected in martyrdom, in the year 177, under the emperor Marcus Aurelius, who reigned from 161 to 180, in the city of Lyons.

Then the page raises a question about him which it does not answer: the holy Alcibiades of Lugdunum is perhaps the same as the Alcibiades of 16 August, who bore witness by fire. What the manner of his own death was, the page does not say; what he did before his arrest, it does not say either. The same calendar keeps on this day a company of martyrs of Lyons of the same year and the same reign — Photinos the bishop, Sanctius the deacon and those with them — but it gives Alcibiades a separate entry and a separate page, and does not number him among them.
