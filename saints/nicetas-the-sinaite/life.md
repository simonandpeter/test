# Nicetas the Sinaite

saint.gr's calendar for 12 May names him and gives no life: «Δεν έχουμε πληροφορίες για τον βίο του Οσίου Νικήτα του Σιναΐτου» — we have no information about the life of the venerable Nicetas the Sinaite. That sentence is the whole of the page, and the calendar line adds only the rank word Όσιος and the epithet ο Σιναΐτης, of Sinai.

The Greek church therefore keeps on this day a monk of Sinai whose name it remembers and whose story it does not. This folder says so, and infers nothing further from the epithet.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1559/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
