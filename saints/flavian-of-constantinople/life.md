# Flavian

Flavian was patriarch of Constantinople after Proclus, and a contemporary of Leo, pope of Rome. Like Leo he fought with resolve against the heresy of Eutyches and Dioscorus; but he did not live to see the triumph of Orthodoxy at the Fourth Ecumenical Council, held at Chalcedon in 451. Before that council, at the second council of Ephesus in 449 — the one history calls the Robber Council — he was savagely beaten and trampled underfoot, and three days later he died of it.

That is the whole of what doxologia's page prints for him, and it ends by calling him a great soldier of Christ the Emperor, a brave defender and a confessor of Orthodoxy. The page carries no troparion for him.
