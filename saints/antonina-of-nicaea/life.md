# Antonina of Nicaea

Antonina suffered at Nicaea in a persecution under the emperor Maximian, whom doxologia dates 284–305. She was horribly tortured and then thrown into prison, and Maximian could not bring her to renounce Christ and bow down to the idols. While the torments were going on, the life says, the angels of the Lord defended the martyr and put fear into the men who were killing her.

Even when they stretched her on a bed made red-hot with fire she was left unhurt by the power of the Lord. At the last, after a great many torments, they sewed her into a sack and drowned her in a lake. doxologia's page has no more than this to say, and prints no hymn for her.
