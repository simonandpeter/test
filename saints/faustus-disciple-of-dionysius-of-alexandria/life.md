# Faustus, disciple of Dionysius of Alexandria

Faustus was one of the eight disciples of [Dionysius of Alexandria](/saints/dionysius-of-alexandria), bishop of that city, who were martyred with him under Valerian in 254, the Greek synaxarion says: disciples in every way worthy of their teacher in the fervour of their piety and the firmness of their faith, who were bound with him and shut in a narrow place, harshly tortured there, and so gave up their souls to God. The entry names six of the eight — Gaius, Peter, Paul, Eusebius and Chaeremon beside Faustus — and gives none of them a story of his own.

The Greek calendar keeps Faustus with Dionysius and the others on 3 October. The entry says his memory is repeated on 4 October with Gaius, Eusebius and Chaeremon. The Greek synaxarion also keeps, on 6 September, a presbyter Faustus of Alexandria beheaded under Decius; nothing on this page says the two are one man, and they are kept apart.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 3 Οκτωβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2582/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
