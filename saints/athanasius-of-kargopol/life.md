# Athanasius of Kargopol

The Greek calendar names him on 18 Ιανουαρίου as the venerable Athanasius who was a fool for Christ, and the page behind that line gives him a surname the day does not: «ο Όσιος Αθανάσιος του Ναβολόσκυϊ». It says that he came from the region of Kargopol in Russia and lived between the sixteenth and the seventeenth century in the town of Vaga, and that he was a fool for Christ, living out the life of the Gospel by the rejection of those human things which to others look like good achievements.

That is the whole of the page. It gives no year of birth and none of death, no monastery, no miracle and no hymn. The English form used here keeps the region the page names; the surname it prints is Greek script for a Russian form, and no Russian spelling is set down in this folder because none was read.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1130/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
