# Sergius (Pravdolyubov)

The calendar’s own line is the whole of what this folder records of him, and he is the last saint of the day and the latest death on it. The Сретенский monastery’s calendar for 5 декабря ст. ст. closes the day with him: «Св. Сергия исп., пресвитера (1950).» He is not called a martyr. The line gives him «Св.», holy, the grade «исп.», a confessor, and «пресвитера», a presbyter, so this folder records a confessor and no martyrdom, and 1950 is the year of his death and not of a killing.

The name page behind his place gives the surname the line leaves out together with his grade in orders: «Сергий (Правдолюбов), иерей, исп.», a priest. It lists one other day for him, «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church; one row per church is this corpus’s schema, so that day is recorded on his feast and he does not render on it.

Behind that page the calendar prints no life. Three articles on the parent site stand linked under his name instead — a finding of relics, a recollection, and one titled his life, the last of them signed by a priest of the same surname — and none of the three was read for this folder: they are the parent site’s own writing and not the calendar, and they are named here as a source available and not used.

So nothing is said here of where he was born, where he served, when or by whom he was arrested, or where he died in 1950. The day page carries six hymns, and all six stand under the names of the ancient saints of the same day.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 5 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261205.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9789.html); read 13 October 2026.*
