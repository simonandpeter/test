# Liphardus of Orleans

saint.gr says that the venerable Liphardus lived in the sixth century after Christ and came from Orleans. He studied the science of law, and his zeal for spiritual things led him to the study of the divine word and to a share in the mysteries of the Church. At the age of forty he followed the monastic life and was ordained deacon. Together with the venerable [Urbicius](/saints/urbicius-of-meung), whom the same day keeps, they lived a life of asceticism that was very austere and hard.

For his great virtue he was ordained presbyter and became abbot of the monastery of Meung, which he had founded together with his fellow monk. Having lived in a manner pleasing to God, the venerable Liphardus fell asleep in peace, in the year 550. That is the whole of the page's account: a city, a profession given up, an age, two ordinations, a companion, a house and a year. It prints no verse for him and no hymn.
