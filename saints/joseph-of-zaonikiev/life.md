# Joseph of Zaonikiev

Joseph, in the world Hilarion, was a pious peasant of the village of Obukhovo in the Kubena district of Vologda province. For a long time he suffered from a disease of the eyes and prayed earnestly for help to the Lord, the Most Holy Theotokos and the saints, above all the unmercenaries Cosmas and Damian. His prayer was heard: in 1588, at a revelation of Saint Cosmas, he found in a marshy place in the forest an icon of the Mother of God, and was healed by it. In thanks he cleared the forest where the icon had appeared, built a chapel and set the icon in it, and settled beside it, taking the monastic habit with the name Joseph.

With the blessing of Anthony, bishop of Vologda, the Zaonikiev monastery grew up where he laboured, named after Anika, a robber who had once lived in that forest. When the community grew and the monks were many, Anthony was chosen abbot on Joseph’s advice; Joseph himself would not take the headship out of humility, hid his strict labours from others and took up foolishness for Christ’s sake, standing at night in prayer in his chapel and walking barefoot in hard frost. He died on 21 September 1612, at the age of 83, and was buried in the monastery he had founded.

The Russian calendar keeps him on 21 September old style and prints two troparia and two kontakia for him, which speak of his darkened eyes, his hair shirt and chains of hair, and the icon given into his arms from the air through the holy unmercenaries.
