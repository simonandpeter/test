# Sylvester of the Kyiv Caves

The Greek calendar keeps Sylvester the Russian on 2 January, and saint.gr's whole account of him is two sentences. He practised the ascetic life in the Lavra of Kyiv in the twelfth century, and he served as hegumen of the monastery of Vydubychi in Kyiv. He was the man who continued the work of the venerable Nestor the Chronicler, whom the same calendar keeps on 27 October, and he fell asleep in peace.

Nothing else is printed there — no birthplace, no year of birth or death, no account of what he wrote. The page names the Orthodox Church in America's calendar among its sources. What is recorded here is what the Greek calendar records: a monk of the Caves, an abbot at Vydubychi, and the hand that carried on a chronicle.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 2 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/02/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/22/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
