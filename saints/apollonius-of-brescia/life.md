# Apollonius of Brescia

saint.gr's calendar for 7 Ιουλίου names him and gives almost no life. The whole of its account is one sentence, «Ο Άγιος Ιερομάρτυρας Απολλώνιος ήταν Επίσκοπος της Βρεσκίας στην Ιταλία και μαρτύρησε τον 2ο αιώνα μ.Χ.» — the holy hieromartyr Apollonius was bishop of Brescia in Italy and was martyred in the second century. That is the see, the country and the century, and the page gives nothing else: no year, no emperor, no judge, no manner of death and no account of his episcopate.

It prints no hymn under his line and, unlike most of its martyrs, no synaxarion couplet for him either. The only other matter on the page is a note on the name itself, which remarks on the epic poet of Rhodes and the mathematician of Alexandria who bore it; neither is a statement about him. That is everything the source says.
