# Anthousa (27 July)

doxologia.ro's calendar names her and gives no life. Its line for 27 iulie is «Sfânta Cuvioasă Antuza» — the venerable Anthousa — and her own page on the site prints only the day and the epistle and gospel read on it. There is no Viață tab, no troparion, no city and no century.

The corpus already keeps two women of this name, and neither of them is claimed here: Anthousa of Seleucia, a martyr, whom the Russian and Serbian calendars keep on 22 August, and Anthousa the New, whom the Russian and Greek calendars keep on 27 August. The Romanian line gives a venerable, not a martyr, on a July day, and there is nothing on the page by which to say she is either of them; so this folder stands for that line alone and is marked by its day.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 27 iulie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/27-iulie) and [her page](https://doxologia.ro/sfanta-cuvioasa-antuza), which prints no life; read 24 September 2026.*
