# Basil (Amenitsky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Basil last among the memories of 7 August and gives no life. Its line is «Сщмч. Василия пресвитера (1938).» — the hieromartyr Basil the presbyter, 1938 — and the calendar's own name page for him adds the surname and raises the grade the line gives: «Василий (Аменицкий), протоиерей, сщмч.», Basil Amenitsky, archpriest.

That is the whole of the record. He was an archpriest; he died in 1938; the calendar keeps him on this day, a year apart from the company of eight whose entry stands just before his and who died in 1937. The day page carries troparia and kontakia for six of the memories it heads, and none under his name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 7 августа ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260807.html); read 13 October 2026.*
