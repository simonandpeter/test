# Priscus

Priscus was martyred by fire — or, some synaxaria say, beheaded. That is the whole of the Greek notice; its verse says that, carrying living water in his heart, he did not fear the burning of his flesh.

The Russian calendar keeps a martyr Priscus on 21 September old style on one line with [Eusebius](/saints/eusebius-of-phoenicia), in the fourth century, and prints no life for either; its line gives only the two names in the genitive, so no Russian form is written here. This folder takes that Priscus to be the Greek page’s on the day, the name and the rank alone, which is a thin call: neither page says more that could confirm or refuse it.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 21 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/1006/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026; the Sretensky calendar (days.pravoslavie.ru), 21 сентября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260921.html), read 17 September 2026.*
