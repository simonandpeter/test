# Macarius, Bishop of Vyazma, Hieromartyr (1918)

Macarius (Gnevushev), bishop of Orel and Sevsk, was born in 1858; he had a higher theological education and served as a priest, and became known as a spiritual and national figure while diocesan missionary of Kyiv, so that when he was widowed he was offered the episcopate — consecrated bishop of Vyazma in January 1917 — and settled in the ancient monastery of the Holy Spirit at Vyazma. From May of that year, at the demand of the revolutionaries, he was retired as “not answering to the spirit of the times”; in the summer of 1918 a Bolshevik detachment came to the monastery and arrested him — the Sretensky calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. The Russian calendar keeps him on 22 August (Julian) with the presbyters John and Alexius, killed in 1918.
