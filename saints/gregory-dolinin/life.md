# Gregory (Dolinin)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him third of four on 15 ноября ст. ст., in a line of two presbyters and two deacons who died in 1937: «Сщмчч. Николая и Петра пресвитеров, Григория и Никиты диаконов (1937).» The line declines his name to «Григория» and gives it without a surname; the title on the name link behind his place, name page 9847, supplies both the surname and the grade: «Григорий (Долинин), диакон, сщмч.».

The calendar carries no life page for any of the four, and the day page says nothing more of him than the rank, the name and the company's year — no birth, no diocese, no parish, no charge, no place and no day of death. The day's troparia and kontakia all belong to other saints it heads and none to this company of 1937. azbyka.ru, which prints the long lives of the twentieth-century Russian new martyrs, was not opened for him, and nothing here is read from the surname.

The four share a date, a year and a line, and that is proximity and not a company: nothing on the page says they were tried together or died together, so no relation is recorded between them here.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 15 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261115.html); read 5 October 2026.*
