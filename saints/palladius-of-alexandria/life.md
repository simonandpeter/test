# Palladius of Alexandria

The calendar names him, dates him by centuries, and prints him a hymn, and that is the whole of what it has. The Сретенский monastery's calendar for 27 ноября ст. ст. reads «Прп. Палладия Александрийского (VI-VII).» — the venerable Palladius of Alexandria, of the sixth and seventh centuries — and the name page behind that name carries a title, the one day, an icon and a troparion. There is no life page for him on the site at all, so nothing is said here of his parents, his monastery, his teachers or his death.

What the hymn says of him is what a common of a venerable father says of any of them: that he lived the monastic life in a way well-pleasing to God, that he sought God by prayer and fasting, that he became a clean vessel of the Spirit, and that healing is worked through him. It is sung in the first tone, and it is printed on the day page under his name and nobody else's.

The corpus keeps two other men of the name who are not this one: a bishop of Helenopolis who wrote a famous book of the Egyptian ascetics, and a bishop in Ireland. Neither shares this one's day or his centuries, and nothing read links any two of the three.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 27 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261127.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/1714.html), which carries no life; read 7 October 2026.*
