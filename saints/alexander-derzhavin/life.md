# Alexander (Derzhavin)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him for 12 марта ст. ст. in the line «Св. Александра исп. пресвитера (1933).» The line declines the forename to «Александра» and gives no surname, no place, no year of birth and no word of narrative; the year it closes with, 1933, is the only year it gives.

Behind that «Александра» stands the calendar's own name page 13701, which carries the fuller form «Александр (Державин), иерей, исповедник» — the surname the day's line leaves out, and a priest's grade — and lists two days, this 12 марта and «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. It carries no life, and neither does the day page; azbyka.ru was not read for him. So what the calendar prints is the whole of what this folder says, and it says that rather than fill the silence.

The line heads him «Св.» and calls him a confessor and a presbyter, and this calendar marks its martyred priests «Сщмч.» — so `hieromartyr` and `new-martyr` are both absent from this folder, and `confessor` is what the source gives.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 12 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260312.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13701.html), which carries no life; read 7 October 2026.*
