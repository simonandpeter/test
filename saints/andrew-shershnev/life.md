# Andrew (Shershnev)

The Сретенский monastery's calendar names him thirteenth in its clause of fifteen for 25 ноября ст. ст. and carries no life for him; azbyka.ru prints one out of fond.ru, and it is a schoolmaster's life for most of its length. Андрей Владимирович Шершнев was born in 1869 at Нижнее Маслово in the Зарайск district of the province of Ryazan, the son of a peasant. In 1882 he went to Petersburg and worked as a waiter in an inn, studying at the same time by correspondence at a teachers' seminary and reading hard on his own account.

In 1890 he went to the province of Kiev and was taken on as a teacher in a literacy school; in eight years he worked in four schools and went on educating himself, and from 1898 to 1921 he taught in three schools of his own Зарайск district. In the early twenties he fell ill and lived as his son's dependent. He went to the church of the Nativity of Christ at Пронюхлово, and for a time, as a lover of church singing, he did the duty of psalm-reader there.

In 1931 he was ordained priest to the Annunciation church at Степановское in the province of Moscow, and was moved in 1932 to the Trinity church of Троицкие-Озерки, in 1933 to the Entry church of Чанки, and in 1935 back to Троицкие-Озерки. In the summer of 1937 two peasants put in statements to the NKVD slandering him with anti-Soviet activity, which they said he carried on while going round the houses of the faithful to serve prayers. At the NKVD's demand the village soviet drew up a certificate on him which said: «Шершнев будет мешать на селе и мы считаем, что его нужно изжить как класс» — he will be in the way in the village and we consider that he should be done away with as a class. Five false witnesses were questioned and gave evidence against him.

He was arrested on 27 November 1937, shut in the Коломна prison and then moved to the Taganka prison in Moscow. At the questioning the investigator read him the false evidence and demanded that he confirm it, and to every threat the priest answered that he did not confirm it and did not admit himself guilty of anything. On 1 December 1937 the troika of the NKVD sentenced him to be shot, and he was shot on 8 December 1937 on the Butovo range outside Moscow and buried in an unmarked common grave.
