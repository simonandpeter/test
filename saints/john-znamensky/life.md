# John (Znamensky)

The Russian calendar prints four words for him and no life: «Сщмч. Иоанна пресвитера (1923)» — the hieromartyr John, presbyter, 1923 — and its index of names adds the surname, «Иоанн (Знаменский), иерей». He was born on 8 March 1868 at Vladykino in the St Petersburg district, the son of a priest, Basil Znamensky. He finished the Belozersk church school of the Novgorod diocese in 1882, and in 1887 was appointed psalmist to the Trinity church in the village of Varnakushki in the same diocese.

In 1898 he moved to the diocese of Turkestan and was posted to the church of the Protecting Veil at Pokrovka in the Przhevalsk district, and in 1902 to the church of St George at Tokmak in the Pishpek district. In 1903 he was ordained priest to the church at Georgievka in that district and appointed teacher of the law of God in its parish school. A year later he was moved to the church of the Archangel Michael in the Cossack settlement of Golubevskaya in the Jarkent district and made teacher in the settlement's parish school, and in 1906 he was moved back to Georgievka. The whole of his priesthood was spent in the Russian church of Central Asia, and the calendar of that region keeps him with its own saints.

He was shot by the godless Bolsheviks in 1923 in the town of Taldy-Kurgan and buried in an unmarked grave. **Neither of the two pages read for him gives a day for the execution.** The one 8 March in his dossier is the day he was born, fifty-five years before, and there is nothing on either page to say whether that is the reason the calendar keeps him on the Julian 8 March or a coincidence; so nothing is claimed about it here. The same calendar also names him on 26 января with the whole company of the new martyrs, and azbyka.ru adds the synaxis of the saints of Vologda and the synaxis of the saints of the Central Asian metropolitan district.

Март», Tver 2006, page 91; read 7 October 2026.*
