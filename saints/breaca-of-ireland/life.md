# Breaca of Ireland

saint.gr says that the venerable Breaca came from Ireland; that about the year 460 she followed [Patrick](/saints/patrick-of-ireland) on his mission, whom the same calendar keeps on 17 March; and that she worked also in the parts of the river Hayle in Cornwall. God gave her the gift of working wonders, and she fell asleep in peace.

That is the whole of the page's account of her: a country, a year, a famous companion, a river in another country, a gift named in one word and a death in peace. It gives her no monastery, no foundation, no disciple and no relic; there is no year of birth and none of death, and there is no hymn and no verse. What wonders she worked, and whether they were in her life or after it, the page does not say.
