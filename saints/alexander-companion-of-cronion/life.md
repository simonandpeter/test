# Alexander, companion of Cronion

saint.gr's calendar for 30 Οκτωβρίου keeps «Άγιοι Αλέξανδρος, Κρονίων, Ιουλιανός, Μακάριος και άλλοι Δεκατρείς Μάρτυρες» — four named men and thirteen more. All of them were martyred in the years of the emperor Decius, in the year 250, at Alexandria, the page says, and it gives them no other circumstance: no trial, no governor, and nothing of who any of them was before.

Alexander and [Cronion](/saints/cronion-companion-of-alexander) were the first two: they were put to death after hard torments, having boiling lime poured over them. The next two, [Julian](/saints/julian-companion-of-macarius) and [Macarius](/saints/macarius-companion-of-julian), were tortured just as inhumanly and beheaded at the end. Of the other thirteen martyrs, some were burned alive and some beheaded.

The page sets an epigram over each pair and one over the thirteen; that last one says that neither fire kindled nor sword sharpened will part twice five and three men from the Word. The thirteen are named nowhere on the page, so they are one entry here and not thirteen folders. saint.gr prints no hymn for the company, and carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου».

*After saint.gr's calendar for 30 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/30/index.aspx) and [the notice of the company](https://www.saint.gr/2854/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
