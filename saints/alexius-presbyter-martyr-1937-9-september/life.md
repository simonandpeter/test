# Alexius (Uspensky)

Alexius Uspensky was born on 22 September 1879 in the village of Sholy in the Novgorod province, the son of a church reader who died when the boy was a year and a half old. He finished seminary, was ordained priest, and served in a series of parishes; the persecutions of the late twenties found him at a church of the Tver diocese. The authorities used their ordinary instrument: a ruinous tax on the priest, and arrest when he could not pay it.

He returned from imprisonment to serve again, and was arrested again in the terror of 1937, and shot. The Russian calendar keeps him on 9 September of the old style, in one line with the hieromartyrs Zachariah, archbishop of Voronezh, the priests Sergius and Joseph, the deacon Demetrius, and the martyr Basil (Shikalov), whose life is written together with his.

*After the Православный церковный календарь (days.pravoslavie.ru), [the common life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4743.htm); read 30 August 2026.*
