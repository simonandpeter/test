# Vassian of Uglich

saint.gr's calendar for 12 Φεβρουαρίου names Saint Vassian of Uglich, and the whole of what its page for him says is three sentences: «Ο Άγιος Βασιανός καταγόταν από το χωριό Ροζαλώφ της Ρωσίας της περιοχής του Κέσοβ. Σε ηλικία 33 ετών εγκαταβίωσε σε μοναστήρι και ακολούθησε την ασκητική οδό. Κοιμήθηκε με ειρήνη το έτος 1509 μ.Χ.» — he came from the village of Rozalovo in the Kesov district of Russia; at the age of thirty-three he settled in a monastery and followed the ascetic road; and he fell asleep in peace in the year 1509.

So a village, a district, an age at entry, an unnamed house and a year are the whole of what the Greek calendar keeps of him — the age being the one detail the page thought worth having, and the monastery not even named. No parentage, no office, no hymn, no relics and no distich are printed, and the page gives no gloss on the name either.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/12/index.aspx) and [his page](https://www.saint.gr/1235/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
