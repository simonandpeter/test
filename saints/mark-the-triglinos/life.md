# Mark the Triglinos

saint.gr's calendar for 24 Νοεμβρίου names «Όσιος Μάρκος ο Τριγλινός» and gives him one hedged sentence: «Ο Όσιος Μάρκος ήταν μάλλον ήταν από την Τρίγλια της Βυθινίας και ασκήτευε σε μια από τις εκεί τέσσερις μεγάλες Μονές» — he was probably from Trigleia of Bithynia and practised his asceticism in one of the four great monasteries there. The «μάλλον», the probably, is the page's own, and so is the doubled verb.

Which of the four monasteries it was the page does not say, and it says nothing else of him at all. The couplet set over the line is all the rest: Mark, having attended unswervingly to God the Word, having plainly departed and being with him, sees him. The page gives him no century, no year, no companions, no relics and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/24/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2988/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
