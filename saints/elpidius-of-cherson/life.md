# Elpidius of Cherson

Elpidius is one of the bishops of Cherson whom the Romanian calendar keeps together on the seventh of March, and the life doxologia prints for the seven of them gives him a few lines. After [Basileus](/saints/basileus-of-cherson), the first of the bishops there, had been killed by a mob at Cherson, a disciple of his spent a night with his ship on the coast of the Hellespont and found three bishops preaching Christ in that place: [Eugene](/saints/eugene-of-cherson), Elpidius and [Agathodorus](/saints/agathodorus-of-cherson).

The three came with him to Cherson to carry on the work. The same enmity that had armed the people against Basileus was turned against them: the crowd gathered, took the holy bishops, dragged them bound along the road, and beat them with sticks and with stones until they died.

The page gives no year, and says nothing of where Elpidius came from or where he was buried. The troparion doxologia prints for the bishops of Cherson names Ephraim, Basileus, Eugene, Agathodorus and Capito, and not him, so no hymn is recorded here.
