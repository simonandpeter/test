# Alexis (Molchanov)

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar for 7 ноября ст. ст. carries fifteen names on one line and his is the eighth of them: «Сщмч. Кирилла , митр. Казанского, Михаила , Александра , Александра , Михаила , Александра , Николая , Алексия , Павла , Василия , Павлина пресвитеров, Иоанна и Вениамина диаконов, мч. Николая , мц. Елисаветы (1937).» The line sorts the fifteen into four classes with one year for all of them: «Сщмч.» heads the metropolitan, the eleven priests and the two deacons, «пресвитеров» marks the eleven, «диаконов» the two, and «мч.» and «мц.» the layman and the woman who close it. The name link behind his place in it supplies the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line leaves out, «Алексий (Молчанов), иерей, сщмч.», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from.

That is very nearly everything the calendar records of him. He has no life page, no icon, no hymn, no place, no age and no parish anywhere on this calendar, and the name page behind his place on the line carries its title, two days and no text at all. That page also lists «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which is a synaxis and not a second feast; this folder renders on 7 ноября ст. ст. only. Nothing further is written here, and in particular nothing is reasoned from the surname.

The line names the class and the link names the grade, and both are kept: the line's «Сщмч.» and «пресвитеров» make him a hieromartyr and a presbyter, and the link's «иерей» is recorded as his office, Priest.

**That article has now been read**, and it is the only thing anywhere that tells his story. It is «Житие для семейного чтения», a life for family reading, signed Марина Бригатова, and it is a devotional retelling with a child's-eye detail the calendar has none of; so what is set down here is what it states as fact, and nothing of its manner. It makes him a Kostroma man, the youngest child of a large family at the village of Нежитино, where his father Андрей Федорович was the psalmist of a stone church of the Resurrection above the river Унжа. He was at the spiritual school in the town of Макарьев when his father died; he had to leave it, come home and take his father's place, and on 6 June 1896, at eighteen, he was made a reader with the right to wear the stichar.

He married the daughter of a priest of the village of Пучеж, and the article names five children, two of them born in 1913 and 1916 and the youngest about 1921. In 1917 he took orders — the article's own reading of that choice is that he was not afraid of what an unbelieving state could do to a priest and trusted himself wholly to God — and in 1918 he moved with his family to the Perm country, to the old town of Нытва on the Kama. The way home was long, his wife caught a chill in a cold train and died of it within weeks, and the youngest child was two years old. By the 1930s he was serving at the church of the Protection in the village of Саурово, in the Lyskovo district of the Nizhny Novgorod country.

In February 1936 he was arrested on a denunciation for holding back small change, and released on payment of a fine. In the spring of 1937, his health failing, he moved to a daughter at the village of Валки, and there the Lyskovo NKVD arrested him and held him three months in the Gorky prison. He was interrogated several times; the investigator pressed him to renounce his faith and did not get it, and he admitted no guilt and signed no false witness against anyone. On 11 November 1937 an NKVD troika sentenced him to be shot, and he was shot on **20 November 1937** — which is the civil date of the 7 ноября ст. ст. this calendar keeps him on, the one thing in the article that can be checked against the calendar and the thing it agrees with. The article says his memory is kept on that day because it is the day his earthly life ended; that he was numbered among the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church at the Jubilee Council of 2000; and that the Gospel book that was his is still kept in the church at Валки, where there are people alive who saw him.

He is given no relation to the fourteen others the line names, and the reason is in the one life the line has. Its heading claims a company — «Убиение святителя Кирилла, митрополита Казанского и ихже с ним», the slaying of the metropolitan of Kazan and those with him — but that life says the metropolitan was shot in the Fox ravine outside Chimkent in southern Kazakhstan, together with two men who are not on this line at all and have no folder in this corpus. Nothing on the day page or on any name page read says that the other fourteen died with him or with one another. A shared line, a shared class and a shared year are not a company unless a page says they died together, so no `related` row is written here in either direction.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 7 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261107.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9462.html); read 4 October 2026; and the article that name page links under his own name, Марина Бригатова's [Священномученик Алексий Молчанов](https://pravoslavie.ru/157396.html), a life for family reading, read 4 October 2026.*
