# Theophilus the New

Theophilus lived in the years of the emperors Constantine and Irene, which saint.gr gives as 780 to 795. He was born and brought up at Constantinople, became a general, and was posted to the theme of the Kibyrrhaiots, with its seat at Kibyra in Asia Minor.

In a sea battle with the Saracens off Cyprus, about the year 800, the two other generals who were with him abandoned him out of envy, and so he was taken prisoner by the enemy. They carried him off to their own country and shut him in prison. Afterwards they brought him out and, with flatteries and with threats, urged him to deny Christ; and because Theophilus firmly refused to give up his faith they beheaded him, and so he received the crown of martyrdom.

The page gives him no year of birth and no year for his death beyond the «about 800» of the sea battle. It prints an apolytikion and a kontakion for him, both copied here; both of them speak of his end as a crossing of the sea, and the second calls him a ship sealed with Christ.
