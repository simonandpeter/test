# Euphrosynus

The Romanian calendar keeps Euphrosynus on the sixth of March, and the life doxologia prints for him is one sentence long: «Sfântul Mucenic Eufrosin a fost adăpat cu apă fiartă şi aşa s-a săvârşit din viaţă» — Euphrosynus the martyr was given boiling water to drink, and so departed this life. There is no more of it than that.

The page names no country and no city, no emperor and no year, says nothing of who he was before he was arrested or of where he was buried, and prints no hymn for him. What it does not say is not supplied here.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 6 martie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/6-martie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-mucenic-eufrosin); read 19 September 2026.*
