# Euphrosynus

The Romanian calendar keeps Euphrosynus on the sixth of March, and the life doxologia prints for him is one sentence long: «Sfântul Mucenic Eufrosin a fost adăpat cu apă fiartă şi aşa s-a săvârşit din viaţă» — Euphrosynus the martyr was given boiling water to drink, and so departed this life. There is no more of it than that.

The page names no country and no city, no emperor and no year, says nothing of who he was before he was arrested or of where he was buried, and prints no hymn for him. What it does not say is not supplied here.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and adds the two things doxologia did not have: how he died and where a piece of him is. saint.gr gives him one sentence — «Ο Άγιος Ευφρόσυνος μαρτύρησε αφού τον περιέλουσαν με βραστό νερό», the holy Euphrosynus was martyred after they had drenched him with boiling water — and says that a portion of his relic is at the monastery of St Andrew of Kefalonia. It gives him no year, no country, no emperor and no judge, and it prints no hymn. The distich over him turns the scalding into his name: having drunk a boiling draught from a hollow bowl, the martyr Euphrosynus was greatly gladdened.
