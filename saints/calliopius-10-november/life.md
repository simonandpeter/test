# Calliopius (10 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 10 Νοεμβρίου names Calliopius and gives him five words: «Ο Άγιος Καλλιόπιος μαρτύρησε δια ξίφους» — the holy Calliopius was martyred by the sword. That is the whole of the notice: no country on the page, no century, no emperor, no governor, no charge, no burial, no year and no hymn.

What stands beside it is the couplet of the synaxarion, which plays on his name as one with beautiful eyes and on the beauty he came to see: «Ξίφει τράχηλον, Καλλιόπιε, κλίνας, / Κάλλος θεωρεῖς ἀκλινῶς Θεοῦ Λόγου» — having bent your neck to the sword, Calliopius, you behold unwaveringly the beauty of God the Word.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/10/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2969/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
