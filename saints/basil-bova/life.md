# Basil (Bova)

**Eleven years a psalm-reader before he was a priest, and twenty-seven years in the Kazakh steppe.** The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him third of the sixteen it closes with 1937, among the «пресвитеров», and carries no life for him; the life here is what azbyka.ru prints for the civil 5 декабря out of fond.ru, signed by the hegumen Дамаскин (Орловский). Василий Иосифович Бова was born in 1877 in the town of Курган in the province of Тобольск. He finished a church school, and from 1909 to 1914 served as psalm-reader in the church of the village of Новоуральское in the Акмолинская province; in 1915 he was moved as psalm-reader to the Марие-Магдалинская church in the town of Петропавловск. In 1920 he was ordained priest and served in the village of Полудино in northern Kazakhstan.

**From 1929 he lived in Петропавловск and went to the one church in the town that had not gone over.** The article says of the Никольская church there that it was in those years «оплотом Православия в городе», the stronghold of Orthodoxy in the town, during the spread of the Renovationist schism, and that he attended it. He was arrested on 6 November 1937 and charged with «контрреволюционной деятельностью», counter-revolutionary activity. He did not admit himself guilty.

**The sentence was passed on 14 November and carried out three weeks later.** The troika of the regional NKVD administration for North Kazakhstan condemned him on 14 November 1937, and azbyka.ru closes that he was shot on 4 December 1937 — in that year the Julian 21 ноября, the feast of the Entry of the Theotokos, and the day before the one both calendars keep him on. He was glorified by the Jubilee Council of Bishops of the Russian Church in 2000. The whole of what the sources say of him is in these three paragraphs: there is no account of his interrogation, no witness testimony, and no place of burial.
