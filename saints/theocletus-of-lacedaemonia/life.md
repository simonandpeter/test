# Theocletus of Lacedaemonia

An icon of Saint Theocletus survives in a church of Monemvasia, saint.gr says, where the lettering is worn away so that his period cannot be known from it. Some sources inform us that he lived a life of the strictest asceticism, in the ninth century, and that he was distinguished for his effort to raise the moral level of his flock. He was a partisan of [Ignatius](/saints/ignatius-of-constantinople), patriarch of Constantinople, and he took part in the council convoked against Photius, in the years 869 and 870.

He is reckoned a local saint of Lacedaemonia, and his biography, the page says, was composed by somebody anonymous. That is the whole of the entry: no birthplace, no monastery, no miracle recounted although the day's line calls him the wonderworker, no relic and no hymn, and no year beyond the council's. The page links beside him the synaxis of the saints who shone in Laconia, which is a feast and not a part of his life.
