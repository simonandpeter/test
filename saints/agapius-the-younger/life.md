# Agapius the Younger

saint.gr's calendar for 24 December keeps the venerable Agapius the younger, and what it prints for him is a schoolmaster's life. He was Antonios Antonopoulos in the world, known also as Agapios Papantonopoulos, born at Dimitsana in 1753 and dead in 1812. He studied at the school of his own birthplace, where his teachers were Agapios Leonardos and Gerasimos Gounas.

When the school closed in the troubles the page calls the Orlofika, Agapius followed Gerasimos Gounas to Smyrna and went on with his studies at the famous school of that city, under Hierotheos Dendrinos as headmaster. It was at Smyrna that he received the habit of a monk. Later he followed Gounas to Chios, and at last came back to his native Dimitsana, where in August 1781 he took over the direction of his own old school.

The fame of the school of Dimitsana, the page says, is owed chiefly to Agapius the younger, who for thirty-two whole years carried out a headmaster's duties with a conscientiousness, an industry and an honesty that were his own. Another mark of him, which his letters show, is the plainness and frugality of his living: «we live Dimitsana-fashion», he writes to Anthimos Karakallos, who warmly supported the work of the school — sometimes on mallows, sometimes on nettles, sometimes on black bryony, sometimes on buttermilk, sometimes on nothing but bread. Of writings of his, the page says, we have none; but his teaching was the seed from which many excellent teachers and clergy of that time sprang.
