# Zeno (3 September)

Zeno was martyred thrown into a cauldron of boiling lead — rejoicing, the verse says, boiling with love for God. That is the whole of the synaxarion’s notice for the martyr kept on 3 September, with its verse.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 3 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/938/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
