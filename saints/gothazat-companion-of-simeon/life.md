# Gothazat, companion of Simeon of Persia

saint.gr gives him the one story it tells at any length. There was a Christian community in Persia, it says, whose heart was their bishop [Simeon](/saints/simeon-of-persia), head of the churches of Ctesiphon and of Salik in Seleucia; the Persians slandered the Christians to king Shapur II as having revolutionary designs, and though Simeon assured the king that his religion makes law-abiding citizens and not foolish rebels, the king was so prejudiced that he had him imprisoned.

In the prison Simeon came to know Gothazat, or Gouzthadat as the page also spells him, a Christian deserter who had changed his faith to save his head. Simeon found the right way to move his heart towards Christ again; and when Shapur learnt of it he was angry and beheaded Simeon and Gothazat, and with them another 1150 Christians of the church there, together with the presbyter [Audelas](/saints/audelas-companion-of-simeon), and last of all [Phousik](/saints/phousik-companion-of-simeon), his own kouropalates.

His couplet has him choosing: «Ἐπιτραπέντος τοῦ θύειν ἢ τεθνάναι, Θανεῖν Γοθαζὰτ εἵλετο τμηθεὶς ξίφει» — being given the choice of sacrificing or dying, Gothazat chose to die, cut down by the sword. The page gives him no year, no rank and no country of his own beyond Persia.
