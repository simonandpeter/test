# Maximus (17 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 17 November keeps «Άγιοι Γεννάδιος και Μάξιμος Πατριάρχες Κωνσταντινούπολης», Gennadius and Maximus, patriarchs of Constantinople, and says of the pair that both were worthy in every way as shepherds of the Church. Of [Gennadius](/saints/gennadius-patriarch-of-constantinople) it gives a full account. Of Maximus it gives, first of all, an admission.

Now as regards the patriarch Maximus, the page says, it is not sufficiently determined which of the patriarchs of Constantinople who bear that name is the saint. In all probability, however, it is Maximus the Third, who held the throne from 1476 to 1482. He came from the Peloponnese, and worked in many ways for the betterment of manners; and he himself, tirelessly, taught the divine word to the people every Sunday.

That is the whole of the source, and the hedge is part of it. This folder therefore keeps the commemoration the calendar prints and the identification the calendar offers as a probability, without turning the probability into a date: no year is recorded here, and the two-line verse over his name says only that Maximus travailed, and having borne children — pure deeds — departs pure out of life.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 17 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/17/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3028/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
