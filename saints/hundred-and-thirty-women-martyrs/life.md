# The Hundred and Thirty Women

saint.gr's calendar for 21 December keeps a hundred and thirty women together, names not one of them, and gives their whole account in a single sentence: these women believed in Christ through the holy [Juliana](/saints/juliana-of-nicomedia), whom the page refers to the same day, and afterwards all of them were martyred by beheading. The distich above the sentence counts them out in the manner of the synaxaria, three tens of women martyrs offering their heads to the sword with the five-fold twentieth.

That is the entire entry. The page gives no name, no place, no year and no persecutor, and prints no hymn; the feast is marked fixed, «Τύπος εορτής: Σταθερή. Εορτάζει στις 21 Δεκεμβρίου εκάστου έτους». The five hundred martyrs the same calendar keeps on the same day are given Nicomedia by their own distich, and these women are given no city at all, so none is claimed for them here. Because the entry enumerates nobody, the company is held as one. It carries no asterisk and not the sentence by which saint.gr marks an entry it will not vouch for.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 21 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/21/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2818/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
