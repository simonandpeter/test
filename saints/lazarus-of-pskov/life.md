# Lazarus of Pskov

saint.gr says he was born in 1733 of devout and God-loving parents and grew up in the town of Opochka, near the region of Pskov. He became a monk in the monastery of the Dormition of the Mother of God of the Lavra of Pskov, and in 1800 he took up the duties of steward of the house. The page says nothing of the years between his birth and his tonsure.

He was marked out, the page says, for his reverence and his ascetic life, and he reposed in peace in 1824. That is the whole of what the Greek calendar keeps of him: it gives no office after the stewardship, tells no story of his last years, names no relics and prints no hymn. The page glosses his name, as a shortening of Eleazar, as the help and the grace of God.
