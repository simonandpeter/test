# Gerasimus the New of Sourvia

In the army of the venerable a bright place belongs to Gerasimus the New, the wonderworker, of Leontari in the Peloponnese. He laboured at the monastery of the Holy Trinity of Sourvia, which Dionysius of Olympus had built from its foundations some hundred and fifty years before, near the village of Makrinitsa; he is counted the second founder of Sourvia, and his tomb is there. He was born in the middle of the seventeenth century in the village of Leontari of Megalopolis to Theodore and Evangelia; when George was eight they gave him to a teacher to learn the sacred letters, and he especially loved the lives of the saints, so that grown up he went to the monastery of Philosophou nearby, an important centre of Greek learning under the Turks, and became a monk with the name Gerasimus, gaining naturally a good education. With divine longing he went to the Holy Places to venerate, and returning to Greece preached the word of God like another apostle in villages and towns, and so came to the region of Makrinitsa — the synaxarion’s life runs on past the point read here.
