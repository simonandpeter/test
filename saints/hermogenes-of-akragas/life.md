# Hermogenes of Akragas

saint.gr's calendar for 24 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιος Ερμογένης επίσκοπος Ακραγαντίνων» and then says in one sentence that there is nothing: «Ο Άγιος Ερμογένης απεβίωσε ειρηνικά. Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου» — he died in peace, and we have no other details of the life of the saint. The see of the Akragantines is the whole of what places him.

The couplet set over the line turns on the word for race or kind: departing, Hermogenes, from the race of men, you put to shame the arrogance of the race of demons. The page gives him no country of his own, no year, no century, no companions, no relics and no hymn, and the day before this one the same calendar keeps another bishop of the same see, Gregory, without saying how the two stand to each other.
