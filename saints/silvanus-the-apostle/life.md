# Silvanus

Silvanus laboured in the preaching of the word with both of the chief apostles, Peter and Paul, and both of them name him in their letters. The Apostle Peter writes in his catholic epistle: By Silvanus, the faithful brother, as I reckon, I have written briefly to you. And the Apostle Paul writes in the second letter to the Corinthians: The Son of God, Jesus Christ, who was preached among you by us, by me and by Silvanus.

From this, the Romanian life says, it is established that Silvanus served in that time each of those chief apostles, and was a sharer in their labours and in their pains for the gospel of Christ. He was made bishop in Thessalonica, suffered many dangers for the struggles of the faith, and went up to Christ, the giver of crowns.

That is the whole of what doxologia.ro prints of him. The Romanian calendar keeps him on 30 iulie together with Silas, Crescens, Epaenetus and Andronicus, and the site gives one life for the five and a common troparion and kontakion for them all.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 30 July, and in the same company. saint.gr says that Silas, Silouanos, Epaenetus, Crescens and Andronicus were five of the seventy disciples of the Lord, and that all of them served the Gospel of Christ «ἐν κόπῳ καὶ μόχθω, ἐν ἀγρυπνίαις πολλάκις, ἐν λιμῷ καὶ δίψει, ἐν νηστείαις πολλάκις, ἐν ψύχει καὶ γυμνότητι» — in toil and hardship, in watchings often, in hunger and thirst on the long journeys, in fastings, in cold and nakedness — which it takes from the second letter to the Corinthians.

Of Silouanos it says one sentence, and it adds something the Romanian account of him does not: that from the dignity of bishop of Thessalonica — the see this folder already carries — he struggled and was tortured for his faith in the Lord. The synaxarion couplet the page prints for him and Silas together makes them both die for it: «Tους κήρυκάς σου Σιλουανόν και Σίλαν, / Σώτερ θανόντας, πώς ακηρύκτους λίπω;» — your heralds Silouanos and Silas, Saviour, who died: how shall I leave them unheralded?
