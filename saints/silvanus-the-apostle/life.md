# Silvanus

Silvanus laboured in the preaching of the word with both of the chief apostles, Peter and Paul, and both of them name him in their letters. The Apostle Peter writes in his catholic epistle: By Silvanus, the faithful brother, as I reckon, I have written briefly to you. And the Apostle Paul writes in the second letter to the Corinthians: The Son of God, Jesus Christ, who was preached among you by us, by me and by Silvanus.

From this, the Romanian life says, it is established that Silvanus served in that time each of those chief apostles, and was a sharer in their labours and in their pains for the gospel of Christ. He was made bishop in Thessalonica, suffered many dangers for the struggles of the faith, and went up to Christ, the giver of crowns.

That is the whole of what doxologia.ro prints of him. The Romanian calendar keeps him on 30 iulie together with Silas, Crescens, Epaenetus and Andronicus, and the site gives one life for the five and a common troparion and kontakion for them all.
