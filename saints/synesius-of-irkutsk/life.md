# Synesius of Irkutsk

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life. The whole of its text about him is one sentence: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ρώσου Αγίου.» — we have no details about the life of the Russian saint. The day's line calls him «Άγιος Συνέσιος του Ιρκούτσκ», and the head line over his name on his own page adds a year of death and leaves the year of birth blank: «(; - 1787)».

That is everything. The page does not say whether he was a bishop of Irkutsk or a monk there or neither; it names no parents, no monastery, no see, no writings and no manner of death; it records no relics and no burial place, and prints neither apolytikion nor kontakion. It links at the foot to the synaxis of all the saints of Siberia, and that is the only company it puts him in. Nothing here is taken from any other source.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/10/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4257/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
