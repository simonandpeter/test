# George of Constantinople

The Russian church calendar keeps him on 13 мая ст. ст. and gives him a notice of five sentences. He suffered for the veneration of the holy icons at Constantinople in the first half of the ninth century. The emperor Феофил pressed him to renounce the worship of the icons, and the brave confessor refused to do what he was told, declaring to the impious emperor that in bowing to the holy icons we bow to their eternal Prototype.

For that disobedience the emperor ordered his goods taken and plundered, and George himself dragged through the streets of Constantinople with a rope about his neck and thrown into prison. After that he was sent into exile, together with his wife [Irene](/saints/irene-wife-of-george) and their children; and having borne many sorrows in exile, the confessor George died there.

That is the whole of the notice. It gives no year, no place of exile, no manner of death and no account of what became of his household; it does not say how many the children were or name one of them, so nothing is written from that clause. The day page prints hymns for three of the saints it heads, and none under his name.
