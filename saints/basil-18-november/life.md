# Basil (18 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 18 November names «Όσιος Βασίλειος», and its whole notice of him is one sentence: we find his memory only in the Paris codex 1578, with no other addition. There is no country, no century, no monastery, no manner of death and no hymn; there is a name, a rank word, and a manuscript that kept the day.

What else stands on the page belongs to the name and not to the man: an etymology of Basil, from βασιλεύς, the royal one, the founder of a people's happiness, and a remark that many kings, patriarchs, bishops and emperors bore it, chief among them Basil II the Bulgar-slayer. None of that is evidence about this saint, and none of it is recorded here. The folder keeps the commemoration and says plainly how little stands behind it.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3040/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
