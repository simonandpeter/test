# Abercius (26 May)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 26 mai reads «Sfântul Mucenic Averchie» — the holy martyr Abercius — with no epithet, no city, no country and no century, and his own page on the site carries only that line, as heading and as title. There is no Viață tab and no Tropar tab, and nothing that would tell him from any other man of the name.

The display name here carries the day for that reason. The rank word «Mucenic» is the whole of what is said about him: that he died a martyr. This folder asserts only that the Romanian church keeps a martyr named Abercius on 26 May; no year is claimed, no place is pinned, and no other calendar has been checked for him.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and adds a father and a manner of death, both of them on the synaxarists' word rather than its own. saint.gr's whole account is one sentence: «Στους Συναξαριστές αναφέρεται ότι ήταν γιος του Αποστόλου Αλφαίου… και αφού τον άπλωσαν σε μελισσώνα, θανατώθηκε από τις μέλισσες» — in the synaxarists it is recorded that he was the son of the Apostle [Alphaeus](/saints/alphaeus-the-apostle), kept on this same day, and that having been spread out in a beehive he was killed by the bees. Beside it the page sets a couplet that turns the death into an offering: Abercius was set out as food for the bees, he whose honeycomb was shown to be food for the Lord. saint.gr keeps his sister [Helen](/saints/helen-martyr-26-may) on the same day, stoned to death, and gives neither of them a year, a place or a hymn.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 26 mai — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/26-mai) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-averchie), which carries no life and no troparion; read 24 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3938/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026.*
