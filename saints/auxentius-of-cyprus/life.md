# Auxentius of Cyprus

Auxentius is unknown to the synaxaria, the Greek synaxarion says, but known as a wonderworker in the Church of Cyprus. He was a German by birth, and in the time of the Crusades, when the crusaders came near Cyprus, he became a monk together with three hundred other soldiers, the Alaman saints. After going round the island he settled in a cave in the Karpasia, between the villages of Komi Kebir and Eptakomi, where he lived a life pleasing to God. Archimandrite Kyprianos published a service for him, and the Greek calendar keeps him with its apolytikion. The entry gives no year.
