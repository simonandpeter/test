# John (Tomilov)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him in one line with five others for 3 февраля ст. ст. — «Сщмчч. Иоанна, Тимофея, Адриана, Василия пресвитеров, прмч. Владимира, мч. Михаила (1938).» — six who died in 1938, four of them presbyters, one a venerable-martyr and one a martyr. The calendar reckons him among them as a hieromartyr and a priest, and the name link behind the line gives its own fuller form, «Иоанн (Томилов), иерей», which adds the surname. The calendar prints no life page for any of the six, and none is given here: that is what it records of him, and the whole of it.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 3 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260203.html); read 3 October 2026.*
