# Ananias (Aristov)

The Russian church calendar's line for 2 November is the whole of what is recorded of him here: «Сщмчч. Константина и Анании пресвитеров (1918)» — the hieromartyrs Constantine and Ananias, presbyters, with the year 1918 closing the line for both. Its index of names gives the surname and the grade the line withholds, «Анания (Аристов), иерей, сщмч.», a priest, and lists this day and the 26 January synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia against his name.

His forename is a rare one in this corpus, and it is the only thing besides the year and the grade that the calendar gives. There is no life page behind the name, and the name page carries nothing but the two days, so this folder records what the line says and stops there. azbyka.ru, which has carried long lives for others of these years, is a quota this corpus has spent and was not asked again on 4 October 2026.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 2 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261102.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9471.html); read 4 October 2026.*
