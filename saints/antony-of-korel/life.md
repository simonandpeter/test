# Antony of Korel

saint.gr gives the pair one sentence. The venerable Antony and [Felix](/saints/felix-of-korel) of Korel lived in the fifteenth century, it says, and practised asceticism in the monastery of Saint Nicholas which [Euthymius](/saints/euthymius-of-karelia), the Enlightener of Karelia, had founded; they worked as missionaries for the spreading of the word of God and the strengthening of the Orthodox faith, and they fell asleep in peace.

That is the whole of the notice: no parentage, no year of birth or of death, no wonder, no account of the mission, and neither couplet nor hymn. Euthymius's own page on this same day names the two who are commemorated with him as Antony and Eutyches, so even the second name is not settled between saint.gr's two entries.
