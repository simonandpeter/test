# Theopemptus (7 February)

saint.gr names him and then says plainly that it knows nothing else. Its calendar for 7 February prints «Άγιος Θεόπεμπτος και η συνοδεία του» — Theopemptus and his company — and the page behind that line answers with one sentence: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου», we have no details of the saint's life. Not one of the company is named, nor their number, nor a place, nor a century, and there is no hymn.

What the page does print over him is the reading of his name and a couplet. Theopemptus, from θεός and πέμπομαι, is the one sent by God; and the couplet has it that Theopemptus the noble, having contended well, receives the prizes together with his own. Since the page calls him Άγιος and not Μάρτυς, and its couplet's contest is not spelled out as a martyrdom, no rank is recorded for him here.

The corpus keeps a [Theopemptus (2 January)](/saints/theopemptus-2-january) out of the same Greek calendar. That is not taken to be this man: the Greek church prints the two on two days, and two entries on one calendar are two commemorations. Nothing on either page names the other.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3619/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
