# The Two Martyrs from Cyprus

saint.gr's calendar for 18 June names the Two Martyrs from Cyprus and gives no life. All the page says of them is one sentence: the holy Two Martyrs from Cyprus were martyred after their feet were burned. Beside it stands a two-line epigram, and it is the only other thing printed — that having borne the burning of their feet for one hour, the two martyrs rejoice for ever.

The page gives neither of their names, no city of the island, no year, no emperor, no persecutor and no hymn. Because it enumerates nobody it is one entry and one folder for the pair, and nothing here identifies them with the other pairs of two martyrs the calendars keep on other days.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1897/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
