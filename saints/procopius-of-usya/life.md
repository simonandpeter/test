# Procopius of Usya

saint.gr's calendar for 8 Ιουλίου names him and gives no life. The whole of its account is one sentence, «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ρώσου Αγίου παρά μόνο ότι ήταν δια Χριστόν σαλός» — we have no details of the life of this Russian saint except that he was a fool for Christ. The day's line gives him his place, «του Ούσια Βόλογκντα», of Usya in Vologda, and that, with his folly for Christ, is everything the source says of him.

The page names no century, no year, no monastery, no deeds and no manner of death, and it prints no hymn and no synaxarion couplet under his line. The same listing carries a second Russian fool for Christ of the name, [Procopius of Ustyug](/saints/procopius-of-ustyug), with a page and a wonder and a year of his own; the two stand as two separate lines of one day's calendar, and this one is the saint of Usya.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2056/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
