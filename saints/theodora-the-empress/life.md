# Theodora the Empress

On this day, doxologia's life begins, is kept the memory of the holy empress Theodora, who brought back the right faith. She was the wife of the emperor Theophilus, the fighter against the holy icons, but she did not follow her husband in his heresy: keeping the holy icons in secret, she venerated them in right belief. After her husband's death she gathered a council of holy priests and brought the holy icons back into the church.

Then she took pains that the soul of her dead husband should be delivered from its torments by the prayers of many holy fathers — of which, the page says, there is a full account in the Synaxarion for the first Sunday of the great and holy Fast. Having done many good works and pleased God, Saint Theodora left the earthly empire to her son Michael, and passed over to the eternity of heaven.

That is the whole of what the page prints of her. It gives no year, no place of birth or death, and no hymn: the «Tropar» tab holds a sung video whose only words are the invocation «Sfântă împărăteasă Teodora, roagă-te lui Dumnezeu pentru noi!», which is not a troparion with its tone, and so none is recorded here.
