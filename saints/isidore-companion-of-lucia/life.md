# Isidore, companion of Lucia

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him in its fifth entry for 6 июля ст. ст., «Мц. Лукии девы и с нею мчч. Рикса , Антония , Лукиана , Исидора , Диона , Диодора , Кутония , Ароноса (Ориона), Капика и Сатура (301).» — the martyr Lucia the virgin and with her the martyrs Rix, Antony, Lucian, Isidore, Dion, Diodorus, Cottyius, Aronos (Orion), Kapik and Satur, of the year 301. One reading serves the whole company and gives the nine a single closing sentence.

That sentence is the whole of what is said of him. [Lucia](/saints/lucia-of-rome-companion-of-rix), a native of Campania, was carried off young into captivity by [Rix](/saints/rix-the-vicar), a chief of one of the German tribes, who came in time to revere her and then to believe; after twenty years she asked leave to go home, and he left his palace and his family and went with her to Rome, where the prefect Elius sentenced them both to the sword. «Вслед за ними были обезглавлены святые мученики Антоний, Лукиан, Исидор, Дион, Диодор, Кутоний, Аронос, Капик и Сатур ( 301)» — after them the nine were beheaded.

So Isidore stands in the account as one of nine men who followed two confessors to the block, and the calendar gives him a city and nothing else: name page 1115 heads him «Исидор Римский, мч.», Isidore the Roman, martyr, prints «День памяти: 6 июля» and links only to the company's reading. The other eight are in this corpus already from the Greek calendar's own enumeration of this company — [Antoninus](/saints/antoninus-of-the-twenty-four-martyrs), [Lycias](/saints/lycias-of-the-twenty-four-martyrs), [Dion](/saints/dion-of-the-twenty-four-martyrs), [Diodorus](/saints/diodorus-of-the-twenty-four-martyrs), [Cottyius](/saints/cottyius-of-the-twenty-four-martyrs), [Oronus](/saints/oronus-of-the-twenty-four-martyrs), [Papicus](/saints/papicus-of-the-twenty-four-martyrs) and [Satyrus](/saints/satyrus-of-the-twenty-four-martyrs) — and his is the one name that enumeration does not print.
