# John (Pashin)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him for 26 февраля ст. ст. in one line held with another man — «Сщмчч. Иоанна, еп. Рыльского, и Иоанна пресвитера (1938).» — two hieromartyrs of the same forename who died in 1938, a bishop and a priest. The name link behind the first of them carries the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line leaves out: «Святитель Иоанн (Пашин), епископ Рыльский, сщмч.», bishop of Rylsk.

The see is the whole of what the calendar says about his ministry. It prints no life page for him and no hymn under his name, and nothing further is written here — no date of consecration, no place and no manner of death — because the calendar gives none of them, and nothing is reasoned out from the see, the surname or the year.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 26 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260226.html); read 3 October 2026.*
