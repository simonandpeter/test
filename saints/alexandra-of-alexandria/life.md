# Alexandra of Alexandria

saint.gr names the venerable Alexandra on this day and gives her one sentence and a disclaimer, and the life says exactly that: «Η Οσία Αλεξάνδρα ασκήτεψε στην Αλεξάνδρεια και κοιμήθηκε με ειρήνη τον 4ο μ.Χ. αιώνα. Δεν έχουμε περισσότερες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο της Οσίας» — the venerable Alexandra was an ascetic at Alexandria and fell asleep in peace in the fourth century, and we have no further details of her life. The day's own line calls her «η εξ Αλεξανδρείας», the one out of Alexandria.

So three things stand recorded: the city, the century, and that her way was the ascetic one. There is no year of birth and none of repose, no monastery or cell, no elder and no disciple, no account of her ascesis, no relic, no distich and no hymn.

The long etymology block the page prints over her name is about the masculine form of it and the emperors, kings, princes, popes, patriarchs and generals who bore it — Alexander the Great above all — and it says nothing whatever about her. The corpus keeps five other women of this name, of Ancyra, of Diveevo, the empress, and two of the Russian imperial house; none of them stands on this day.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/03/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1732/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
