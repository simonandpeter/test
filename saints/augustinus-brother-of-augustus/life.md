# Augustinus, brother of Augustus

saint.gr's calendar names three men in one line and gives them one sentence. The holy hieromartyr [Flavius](/saints/flavius-of-nicomedia) was Bishop of Nicomedia, it says, and was martyred in the reign of the emperor Diocletian, who held the empire from 284 to 305, together with the brothers [Augustus](/saints/augustus-brother-of-augustinus) and Augustinus.

That is the whole of the page, and it is all that is known here of him: a name that the Greek gives also to the bishop of Hippo, a rank, a brother, a bishop in whose company he died, a reign, and the day. The page's word for the two of them, «των αυταδέλφων», makes them brothers by both parents, and the display name keeps that because the two names are a syllable apart. There is no city, no judge, no manner of death, no relics and no hymn, and no couplet is printed for them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/07/index.aspx) and [the life of the three](https://www.saint.gr/4011/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
