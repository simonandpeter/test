# The Seven Martyrs (30 December)

saint.gr's whole account of them is one sentence: «Οι Άγιοι αυτοί Επτά μάρτυρες ήταν ένας Κόμης και έξι Στρατιώτες οι οποίοι πίστεψαν στον Χριστό, δια του Αγίου Φιλεταίρου και απεβίωσαν ειρηνικά» — these holy seven martyrs were a count and six soldiers who believed in Christ through [Philetairos](/saints/philetairos), and they died peacefully. It names not one of them, and this folder therefore stands for the company and not for seven men.

The page's own line calls them martyrs and its own text says they died in peace, and both are left as it has them. The occasion is on Philetairos's page for the same day: a man of Nicomedia of such beauty that Diocletian wanted him in his retinue, imprisoned for refusing to deny Christ, hunted under Maximian, and at last living in the mountains about Sigriane. The couplet set over this entry counts them and no more: «Eις καλόν απήντηκεν η οδός πέρας, / Tοις επτά τούτοις ουρανούς εφθακόσιν» — the road has come to a good end for these seven, who have reached the heavens. There is no year, no city, no judge and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 30 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/30/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1072/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
