# Anna of Kyiv

saint.gr's calendar for 3 Νοεμβρίου names «Οσία Άννα του Κιέβου» and dates her «(; - 1112)». She was the daughter of the Grand Prince of Kyiv, Vsevolod Yaroslavich, the page says, whose reign it puts at 1078 to 1093, and whose wife was a daughter of the emperor of Byzantium, Constantine Monomachos.

For the sake of the mission in Russia she visited Constantinople; and after her return she became a nun in the monastery of Saint Andrew, which her father had founded. Her example brought other devout souls to the monastic life as well. She laid down the cenobitic system in all the monasteries of Kyiv, the page says, and her part in the teaching of the Russian people was a considerable one.

She fell asleep in peace in 1112 — or in 1113, or in 1116, the page adds, giving three years and choosing none of them. Her relic was found incorrupt; and after the Revolution of 1917, it says, there is no information about what became of it. No hymn is printed, and the page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου».
