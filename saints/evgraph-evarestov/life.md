# Evgraph (Evarestov)

The Сретенский monastery's calendar names him «Сщмч. Евграфа пресвитера (1919)» and carries no life for him; azbyka.ru prints one out of pstgu.ru. Евграф Еварестов was born in 1857 in the province of Самара, the son of a village priest. He finished the Самара seminary and then taught Russian and Church Slavonic in the Самара church school; he finished the Kazan theological academy in 1885 and spent two years as assistant inspector of the Уфа seminary. He was ordained deacon and then priest at Уфа in 1887.

From the day of his ordination to his death he served in one church, the Воскресенский cathedral of Уфа, and went on teaching in the Уфа seminary to the end of his life. He was raised archpriest in 1900 and made rector of the cathedral. The page calls him an outstanding preacher: more than thirty of his sermons and homilies were printed in the diocesan gazette. He was twice elected to the city duma of Уфа as the deputy of the spiritual department, and was treasurer of the Orthodox Missionary Society and head of the mission against the schism. Fifteen children were born to him and his wife Анна Васильевна. In 1914 an order of Saint Vladimir brought the family hereditary nobility.

In 1918 the clergy of Уфа met with bread and salt the White Army that had taken the city from the Bolsheviks that summer, and services of thanksgiving and for the saving of the fatherland were sung in his cathedral; clothing, linen and money were collected there for that army and an infirmary set up, and his wife took part in all of it. Four of his sons served in Kolchak's army, two as volunteers and two conscripted, and two of his daughters left with it. He was arrested for this and was probably let out by Kolchak's counter-offensive in March 1919.

The Reds took Уфа for good on 9 June 1919, and on 18 November Latvian Red Army men came in at the back door of his house, searched the flat, took what was of any value along with his decorations, his papers and his photographs, and led him away. The indictment charged him with «агитации в пользу Колчака» and doubled the charge «ибо он поп» — because he is a priest — listed his donations and collections for the army and his family ties to the Whites, and closed by proposing that he be shot. The sentence was carried out on 7–8 December 1919; he was led to it in his underclothes and barefoot over the snow. His widow was arrested afterwards, and the children were deported in the winter cold to the works at Катав-Ивановск.
