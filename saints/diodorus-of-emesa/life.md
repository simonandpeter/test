# Diodorus of Emesa

saint.gr's calendar for 13 Ιουνίου names him and gives no life. Its whole notice is one line: «Ο Άγιος Διόδωρος ο εξ Εμέσης μαρτύρησε δια σταυρικού θανάτου» — Diodorus of Emesa was martyred by death on a cross. The place is in the day's own line, and that and the manner of his death are all the page has: no emperor, no judge, no century and no year.

Above it stand the two verses of the synaxarion, which say the same thing and give it its point: «Aνήλθε Διόδωρος εις σταυρού ξύλον, / Tιμών Iησούν ου πάθος Σταυρού ξύλον» — Diodorus went up onto the wood of a cross, honouring Jesus, whose passion the wood of the Cross was. The site adds its gloss on the name, from Zeus and gift: the gift of God. There is no hymn for him on the page.
