# Isaac of Armenia

The Охридски пролог gives him five sentences. He was born at Constantinople, at the time when his father was the envoy of the Armenian king at the Byzantine court. He was the tenth archbishop of Armenia, and in that office he governed the church for fifty years. His pastorate he marked, among other things, by the translation of the Holy Scripture into the Armenian language. In a vision it was told him that the Armenians would in time fall away from the pure Orthodox faith.

This notable hierarch reposed peacefully in the year 440 and stood before the Lord. That is all of it: no year of birth, no year of accession, no city of his see, no companion and no wonder. The fifty years and the embassy would between them suggest a birth and an episcopate, and neither is written here, because no book read states either. Of the four calendars this corpus reads he is kept by this one alone, and the other three have not been looked at for him.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 20. новембар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2025-12-03&prolog=1); read 7 October 2026.*
