# John (Plotnikov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and gives no life. Its line for 30 июля ст. ст. reads «Сщмч. Иоанна диакона (1918).» — the hieromartyr John, a deacon, who died in 1918 — and behind that line the calendar's own name page gives the name in full as «Иоанн (Плотников), диакон, сщмч.», with the surname the day line leaves out.

Nothing else is printed. The calendar names no place, no parish, no diocese and no manner of death, and it carries no life page for him at all; the day page's hymns belong to four of the other saints it heads, and there is none under his name. What the calendar keeps of him is the rank, the surname and the year.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 30 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260730.html); read 13 October 2026.*
