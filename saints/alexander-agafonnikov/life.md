# Alexander (Agafonnikov)

The last of the Russian 1 October's three new-martyr lines holds four people under the one year 1937: «Сщмчч. Александра, Георгия, Николая пресвитеров, мч. Иоанна» — three hieromartyr presbyters and one layman. He is the first of the three, and the day line gives him only the baptismal name, so the surname comes from the site's index of names, which heads his page «Александр (Агафонников), протоиерей, сщмч.» and makes him an archpriest.

His fellows on the line are [George (Arkhangelsky)](/saints/george-arkhangelsky), [Nicholas (Kuligin)](/saints/nicholas-kuligin) and the layman [John (Artemov)](/saints/john-artemov). The index keeps all four here and on 26 January with the company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and prints no birth, no diocese, no parish, no charge and no place of death for any of them. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line.

*After the Russian church calendar's [1 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261001.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9252.html); read 4 October 2026.*
