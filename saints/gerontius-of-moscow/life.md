# Gerontius of Moscow

saint.gr's calendar names him Gerontius of Moscow and gives him three sentences: «Ο Άγιος Γερόντιος έζησε τον 15ο αιώνα μ.Χ. Το 1453 μ.Χ. ήταν Επίσκοπος Κολόμνας και στις 29 Ιουνίου 1479 μ.Χ. εξελέγη Μητροπολίτης Ρωσίας με τη συγκατάθεση του μεγάλου πρίγκιπα Ιβάν Γ'. Κοιμήθηκε με ειρήνη το 1489 μ.Χ.» — he lived in the fifteenth century; in 1453 he was bishop of Kolomna, and on 29 June 1479 he was elected metropolitan of Russia with the consent of the grand prince Ivan III; he reposed in peace in 1489.

That is the whole of it. The page tells nothing of his years at Kolomna, nothing of his ten years in the metropolitan see, names no relics and prints no hymn, and nothing more is written here than it prints.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/28/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1768/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
