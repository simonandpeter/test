# The Two Executioners

saint.gr's calendar for 15 December keeps two saints whom it does not name, «Άγιοι Δύο Δήμιοι» — the holy two executioners — and its page is one sentence long. It says that they too suffered martyrdom at Rome under Hadrian, whose years it gives as 117 to 138; that they believed in Christ from the wonders of [Eleutherius](/saints/eleutherius-of-illyricum), whom the same day's calendar keeps; and that they were beheaded.

The page names neither of them and gives no further circumstance, which is why this is one entry here and not two: there is no second name to make a folder from. It gives no year beyond the reign, it names no place but Rome, and it prints no hymn of theirs, the hymns of the day standing on Eleutherius's page and naming Eleutherius.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 15 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/15/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3269/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
