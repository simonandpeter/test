# Macarius of Optina

The Romanian calendar keeps Macarius of Optina on 7 September, one of the elders of Optina, with a troparion that calls him a man perfect in virtue who gave himself to obedience in a life that loved submission, to whom the Lord, seeing his lowliness, gave wisdom and humility — most wise and blessed Macarius, ask great mercy for us; its page carries his saying, humble yourselves in everything and have peace among you, and the Lord’s grace will cover you from every evil, but prints no life. The Russian calendar keeps him the same day of its own reckoning, outside the weeks read here. This entry records the Romanian page and nothing more.
