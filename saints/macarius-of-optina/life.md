# Macarius of Optina

Michael Ivanov was born in 1788 to a noble family of the Orel province and
named for Michael of Tver. His mother died when he was nine, and he was
brought up by a sister and an aunt; at fourteen he was a clerk in the Lgov
treasury, at seventeen head of the treasury at Kursk, and in his free hours
he read and played the violin. His father died in 1806, and in October 1810,
at twenty-two, he went to the poor and remote Ploschansk hermitage and did
not come back, leaving the estate to his brothers on one condition — a stone
church at Turishchev, where their father was buried. Tonsured Melchizedek as
a rassophore that December and Macarius as a monk on 7 March 1815, he was
made deacon within days and priest in 1817. What formed him there was the
schemamonk Athanasius, a disciple of Paisius Velichkovsky, whom he nursed
for years after a fall and who died in his arms in 1825; Athanasius had
copies of Paisius’s translations of the Fathers, and Macarius read and
copied them and would one day print them. In 1828 the elder Leonid came to
Ploschansk and took him as a disciple; when Leonid moved to Optina the next
year Macarius followed by letter, and at last in body, arriving on
5 February 1834 at forty-six to put himself at Leonid’s feet.

He was made confessor of the monastery in 1836 and superior of the skete on
1 December 1839, and never did anything without asking Leonid until the
elder’s death in October 1841, after which Leonid’s children came to him,
and then everyone else. He received visitors from morning to night and kept
up a vast correspondence, and would not curtail either for his own
exhaustion. With Ivan Kireyevsky and his wife Natalia, his spiritual
daughter, he began in 1847 to publish the Paisian manuscripts Optina held —
a life of Paisius first, then sixteen books of the Fathers, Nilus of Sora,
Barsanuphius and John, Symeon the New Theologian, Isaac the Syrian among
them. He gave up the skete on 30 November 1853, took the great schema in
secret two years before his death, fell ill on 26 August 1860, and died at
seven on the morning of 7 September as the ninth ode of the canon for the
departing soul was read; he was buried on the 10th to the right of Leonid.
The Optina elders were glorified for the whole church in August 2000.

The Romanian calendar keeps him on 7 September with a troparion — a man
perfect in virtue who gave himself to obedience, to whom the Lord, seeing
his lowliness, gave wisdom and humility — and his saying, humble yourselves
in everything and have peace among you, and the Lord’s grace will cover you
from every evil; the Russian calendar keeps him the same day of its own
reckoning.
