# Arcadius (12 January)

saint.gr's calendar for 12 Ιανουαρίου names a martyr Arcadius, «Άγιος Αρκάδιος ο Μάρτυρας», and the page behind the line says in one sentence that it has nothing to say: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου Μάρτυρα» — we have no details of the life of this holy martyr.

What the page prints instead, as it does under many of its names, is a note on the name itself: that Arcadius comes from Ἀρκάς, an Arcadian, and that two others bore it — an emperor of Byzantium, whom the page dates 377 to 408, and a distinguished grammarian from Antioch. Neither of those is this martyr. Of him the calendar keeps a name, the rank of martyr, and the day; no city, no reign, no year, and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1408/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
