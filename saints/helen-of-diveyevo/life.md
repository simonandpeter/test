# Helen of Diveyevo

saint.gr says that Helen Vasilievna Manturova, with the blessing of [Seraphim](/saints/seraphim-of-sarov), whom it marks as kept on 2 January, carried on the tradition of the nun [Alexandra](/saints/alexandra-of-diveevo), whom it marks as kept on 13 June. When she was young, the page quotes, «είχε εύθυμο χαρακτήρα, αγάπη για τα εγκόσμια και την έλκυαν οι διασκεδάσεις» — she had a cheerful character, a love of the world, and amusements drew her.

When she was ready to be married she refused the bridegroom for no reason she could name. After that she saw a vision in which a huge serpent was trying to swallow her and the Mother of God saved her, and then she promised to go to the monastery. She renounced the world altogether and began to read the works of the holy fathers, to pray and to work. For three years Seraphim prepared her to become a member of the monastic sisterhood, and this came about in 1825; he foretold the road she would take and urged her to imitate the life of Alexandra. She lived in prayer and in silence.

Her earthly life ended in a way the page calls wonderful: making obedience to Seraphim she died in the place of her brother Michael Vasilievich Manturov. Before her repose she was granted to see in a vision the Lord as though he were fire, together with the Mother of God, who showed her the sisterhood of Diveyevo as it had been and as it would be. She fell asleep in the Lord on 28 May 1832. Seraphim received the word that the nun Helen was near the throne of the Holy Trinity and in the heavenly choir of the virgins of the Kingdom, and her relic remained incorrupt.
