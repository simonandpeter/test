# John (Smirnov)

John was born on 20 March 1873 in the village of Vysokoye in the Mikhailov district of Ryazan province, the son of the psalmist Alexis Smirnov, all five of whose sons became priests. He finished the Ryazan seminary and in 1904 was ordained priest to the cathedral of St Nicholas at Zaraysk, where he settled with his family in a wooden house within the town kremlin; the cathedral then held the famous wonderworking icon of Nicholas of Zaraysk, glorified by many wonders, which drew pilgrims from every end of Russia and the generous gifts of the great saint’s grateful venerators, and kept ancient manuscript and early printed service books — the calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. As rector of the cathedral he actively resisted the closing of churches by the Soviet power; in 1930 he was arrested and exiled for three years, and on his return went on serving; arrested again in 1937 on a charge of counter-revolutionary agitation, he was shot on 9 September 1937 at the Butovo range. The Russian calendar keeps him on 27 August (Julian).
