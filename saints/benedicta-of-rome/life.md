# Benedicta of Rome

saint.gr's calendar names her and gives two sentences. The venerable Benedicta lived in the sixth century after Christ and came of Rome, it says; she became a nun in the monastery of Saint Gallus, whom it sends to 6 November, and she fell asleep in peace. Beside that the page prints only the meaning it gives for the name, from the Latin bene and dictus, the blessed one.

That is the whole of the record here. There is no account of her life in the monastery, no place for it beyond the saint it is named for, no year of her death, no relics and no hymn; and the page neither dates the Gallus it names nor says how a Roman woman came to his house. Nothing is claimed beyond the name, the rank, the city, the century and the day.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1525/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
