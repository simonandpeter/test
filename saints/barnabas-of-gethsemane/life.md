# Barnabas of Gethsemane

saint.gr says the venerable Barnabas, whose name in the world it gives as Basil Ilyich Merkulov, was born on 24 January 1831 in the village of Prudiski in the province of Tula in Russia. In the year 1851 he entered the monastery of the Lavra — the page says the Lavra of Kiev — and was tonsured monk. He lived as an ascetic in a way pleasing to God in the desert of Gethsemane, near the elder Daniel and the hieromonk Gregory, and fell asleep in peace in the year 1906.

That is the whole of the entry, and the page adds only the reading of his name, a Hebrew word, as the son of consolation. It gives no account of his eldership, names no disciple, and prints no relics, no couplet and no hymn. Which house the Gethsemana skete he lived in belonged to is not said on the page beyond the word Lavra, and nothing is supplied here that it does not say.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 17 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/17/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2390/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
