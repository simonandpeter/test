# Phinehas the Priest

Phinehas, son of [Eleazar](/saints/eleazar-the-priest) and grandson of Aaron, was a priest of the Hebrews. For the zeal he showed in putting to death Zimri and Cozbi the Midianite, who shamelessly fornicated in defiance of Moses and the congregation of the sons of Israel, he received the covenant of an everlasting priesthood (Numbers 25:11–13). His memory is kept also on 12 March.

The Russian church keeps him on 12 марта ст. ст., where the Greek keeps him on 2 September, and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery tells the whole of the story the Greek entry compresses into one clause. When the Israelites brought out of Egypt were near the promised land, it says, their neighbours the Moabites and Midianites were filled with envy and fear; having no hope in their own strength they turned to sorcery and hired the magus Balaam to lay a curse on them, but the Lord revealed his will to Balaam, who, filled with the Spirit, blessed the people of God three times instead. Then the Moabites drew Israel into the idolatry of Baal-Peor through fornication, and God punished them for it.

Filled with anger at the man who had led the people astray, the page says, he went into his tent and ran him and the Midianite woman through with a spear, and it quotes the Lord's answer to Moses at length: that he had turned away God's wrath from the sons of Israel by being zealous for him among them, and that for it he was given the covenant of peace and, for him and his seed after him, the covenant of an everlasting priesthood. Afterwards, at God's command, he went at the head of the army of Israel against the Moabites and punished them for their impiety and their guile; and at the death of the high priest, his father, he was chosen high priest with one mind, the high priesthood continuing in his seed by God's promise. He died in deep old age about the year 1500 before Christ — the first year any source read for this folder has given him.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 2 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/915/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 12 марта ст. ст. — [the Russian day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260312.html) and [its life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life623.htm), read 7 October 2026.*
