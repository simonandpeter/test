# Cedd

saint.gr says Cedd came of a devout family, and that he and his three brothers all took up the monastic life and became monks. He studied at the monastery of Lindisfarne, which [Aidan](/saints/aidan-of-lindisfarne) directed. When his studies were finished he was called by Peada, king of Mercia, to preach the Gospel in his kingdom, and he worked as a missionary in the kingdom of the East Saxons as well.

He baptised thousands of pagans, the page says, and founded churches and monasteries; later he was elected bishop, and was ordained by Saint Finnian. He was marked out by his boldness of speech, his spiritual courage and the strictness of his ascetic life. He fell asleep about the year 664, when the plague was cutting down the population.

The day's line calls him «επίσκοπος Σκωτίας», bishop of Scotland, though the notice itself puts his work in Mercia and among the East Saxons; the line's words are given here as the page has them. saint.gr sends the reader on to Saint Chad, and to the synaxis of the saints who shone in Scotland, and prints no hymn for him.
