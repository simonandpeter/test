# Tranquillinus

saint.gr's calendar for 18 December keeps ten people together and names every one of them: Nikostratos and his wife Zoe, Tranquillinus and his wife Marcia, Claudius, Tiburtius, Castor, Castulus, Marcus and Marcellinus. They all suffered, the page says, in the persecution Diocletian set in motion against the Christians, whose years it gives as 284 to 304, and they suffered together with Saint [Sebastian](/saints/sebastian-of-rome), whom the same day's calendar keeps.

Tranquillinus died by stoning. He and his wife [Marcia](/saints/marcia-wife-of-tranquillinus) were pagans, and they went to the prison to turn their two sons from the Christian faith; but in the end, with Sebastian standing by them, they became Christians themselves, and Tranquillinus was ordained bishop by the bishop Gaius and his two sons deacons.

That is the whole of what the page gives for this company: the ten names, the one reign, and for each of them the manner of the death. It names no city and no judge, gives no year of birth for any of them and no year inside the reign, lists no relic and prints no hymn of its own; the day's apolytikion stands on Sebastian's page and names Sebastian. The others of the ten here are [Nicostratus](/saints/nicostratus-18-december), [Marcia](/saints/marcia-wife-of-tranquillinus), [Claudius](/saints/claudius-18-december), [Tiburtius](/saints/tiburtius-18-december), [Castor](/saints/castor-18-december), [Castulus](/saints/castulus-18-december), [Marcus](/saints/marcus-son-of-tranquillinus), [Marcellinus](/saints/marcellinus-son-of-tranquillinus), and [Zoe](/saints/zoe-18-december), whom this corpus already kept from the Romanian calendar.

The Ohrid Prologue keeps him on the same menologion day, inside the heading „Свети мученик Севастијан и други с њим.“ — Sebastian and the others with him — and names him in the body, twice and in two spellings: «Транквилина» where it tells of his healing, «Транквилијан» where it tells of his death.

He was the father of [Marcus](/saints/marcus-son-of-tranquillinus) and [Marcellinus](/saints/marcellinus-son-of-tranquillinus), and he had eleven years of gout and pain in the legs. [Sebastian](/saints/sebastian-of-rome) healed him of it and brought him to baptism together with his whole house. He was drowned in water, the book says, in the same sentence that gives the club to Nicostratus and the spears to his two sons. It names no year, and it does not name his wife.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3289/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026; and the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 18. децембар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2025-12-31&prolog=1), read 7 October 2026.*
