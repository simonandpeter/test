# Epiphanius, Archbishop of Constantinople

Epiphanius succeeded [John II](/saints/john-archbishop-of-constantinople) on the throne of Constantinople. The synaxarion’s joint entry for the two says that John became patriarch after Timothy and held the throne two years and ten months, dying in peace in 520 and leaving Epiphanius as his successor; Epiphanius was patriarch fifteen years and three months and died in peace in 535. His successor was Anthimus the heretic.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 25 August — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2646/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
