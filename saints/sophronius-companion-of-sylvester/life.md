# Sophronius, companion of Sylvester

saint.gr names the holy Sophronius with [Sylvester](/saints/sylvester-companion-of-sophronius) on this day and gives the pair of them one sentence, and the life says exactly that: «Οι Άγιοι Σιλβέστρος και Σωφρόνιος μαρτύρησαν δια ξίφους» — the holy Sylvester and Sophronius were martyred by the sword. No year, no country, no emperor, no judge and no relic stand on the page, and it prints no hymn.

The distich over them is the only other thing there, and it is addressed to his companion rather than to him: not enduring, Sylvester, to practise error, with Sophronius you endured the cutting. So even the couplet says about Sophronius only that he was there, and the site does not say which of them was killed first or whether they were tried together.

The corpus keeps six other men of this name — of Jerusalem, of Cyprus, of Vratsa, of Akhtala, of Soumela, of Cioara — and one more, a bishop, whom saint.gr keeps on 19 February out of a manuscript mention. None of them is on this day and none is named on this page.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3777/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
