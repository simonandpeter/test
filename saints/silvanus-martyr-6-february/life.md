# Silvanus (6 February)

saint.gr names him and gives almost no life. Its calendar for 6 February prints «Άγιοι Φαύστος, Βασίλειος και Σιλουανός», and the page behind the line says only this of the three: that [Faustus](/saints/faustus-martyr-6-february), [Basil](/saints/basil-martyr-6-february) and Silvanus were friends as brothers are, young in years, and were martyred by the sword. There is no homeland, no century, no persecutor and no hymn.

The page then adds its own doubt, that perhaps these are the Faustus, Basil and Lucian kept on 25 October, since the couplet is the same in both places. It is worth noticing that in that company the third name is Lucian and not Silvanus, which is the one point on which the two lines plainly differ; the corpus holds no folder for the October three, and their page was not read here. The couplet plays on the number of them: one end by the sword for three fellow-contenders, who think of God as one and as three.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2810/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
