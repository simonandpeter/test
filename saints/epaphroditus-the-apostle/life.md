# Epaphroditus of Philippi

saint.gr's calendar for 8 December keeps six apostles of the Seventy in one entry and names them all: [Sosthenes](/saints/sosthenes-the-apostle), [Cephas](/saints/cephas-the-apostle), [Apollos](/saints/apollos-the-apostle), [Tychicus](/saints/tychicus-the-apostle), [Caesar](/saints/caesar-the-apostle) and Epaphroditus. Of Epaphroditus it says that the apostle Paul calls him his brother and fellow worker and fellow soldier, and that he carried out his apostolic duties among the Philippians.

Afterwards, the page says, he took the see of Colophon following Sosthenes. The couplet set over his name turns on the right hand: Epaphroditus, keeper of works of the right hand, sees you, Word, at the right hand of the Father. The page gives him no country, no year and no manner of death, and nothing else is claimed here; the Greek calendar's other Epaphroditus, one of the three hundred Alamanoi on 12 October, is another entry and is not joined to this one.

The Ohrid Prologue keeps him on the same menologion day, in a company of seven where the Greek calendar printed six, and it seats him in a different city from the one this folder is named for: in Adrianias of Pamphylia, where this corpus calls him of Philippi after the epistle. Two books, two sees; nothing is renamed on it and neither reading is chosen.

Of all seven together it says that they preached the Gospel of Christ with a burning love, that they suffered torments for his holy name, and that they passed over into the kingdom of everlasting joy. It records that all of them are celebrated on 4 January with the rest of the lesser apostles; for him it gives no second day and no year.

The Russian church calendar keeps him with four of the others on 30 марта ст. ст., and brings the count of cities given him to four. Its longer reading calls him one of the nearest fellow workers and fellow strugglers of the apostle Paul and makes him bishop of Adriaca in Thrace; its shorter one quotes the second chapter of the letter to the Philippians nearly whole — the brother and fellow worker and fellow soldier, their own messenger and minister to Paul’s need, who was sick nigh unto death and whom God had mercy on, and whom they are to receive in the Lord with all gladness, because for the work of Christ he was nigh unto death, not regarding his life — and then seats him at Adriania in Italy. Thrace, Italy, Pamphylia and the Colophon the Greek page gave him make four, and no page chooses between them. The reading that heads the company closes it «Все эти апостолы мирно преставились к Богу (вторая память 30 марта)»: they all fell asleep in peace, and this day is their second memory.
