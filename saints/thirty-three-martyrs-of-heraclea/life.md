# The Thirty-three Martyrs of Heraclea

Thirty-three martyrs from Heraclea who died by fire. Their verse: the roaring fire was to the Lord’s athletes as the dew of Moses upon the grass. Nothing more is recorded.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 28 August — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2212/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
