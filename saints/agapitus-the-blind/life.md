# Agapitus the Blind

saint.gr's whole account of him is one sentence: «Ο Όσιος Αγαπητός ασκήτεψε θεοφιλώς στη μονή Βάλαμο της Φιλανδίας και κοιμήθηκε με ειρήνη το έτος 1905 μ.Χ.» — the venerable Agapitus lived the ascetic life in a manner pleasing to God in the monastery of Valaam in Finland, and fell asleep in peace in the year 1905. The day's line calls him ο Τυφλός, the blind, and the page's heading prints «(; - 1905)».

That is all it has. The page glosses his name — Αγαπητός, the one worthy of love — and stops there. It does not say when or how he lost his sight, names no elder of his and no cell, gives no birth year and no country he came from, lists no relic, and prints neither couplet nor hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/229/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
