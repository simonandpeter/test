# Artemon (9 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 9 Νοεμβρίου keeps Artemon with Narses, and the whole of its notice is that the two were martyred by the sword, with one thing added of his companion only: that Narses was a Persian, and may perhaps be the same man as the Narses of 9 December. Of Artemon himself nothing further is said — no country, no century, no emperor, no trial, no burial and no year — and the page prints no hymn.

The couplet of the synaxarion puts his name in both its lines: «Ναρσῆς σὺν Ἀρτέμονι ἐκκοπεὶς ξίφει, / Σὺν Ἀρτέμονι λαμβάνει καὶ τὰ στέφη» — Narses, cut down by the sword with Artemon, receives the crowns with Artemon too. Kept with him is [Narses](/saints/narses-9-november).

*After saint.gr's calendar for 9 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/09/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2956/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
