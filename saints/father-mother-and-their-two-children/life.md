# The Father, Mother and their two Children

saint.gr's calendar for 29 Ιουλίου keeps a household of four martyrs and names none of them. Its line is «Άγιοι Πατέρας, Μητέρα και τα δύο Παιδιά τους» — the holy Father, Mother and their two Children — and the page behind it is one sentence: «Οι Άγιοι Πατέρας, Μητέρα και τα δύο τους Παιδιά μαρτύρησαν δια πυρός», they were martyred by fire.

Besides that the page prints the couplet of the synaxarion, which makes one word of the household: «Πατήρ εκαύθη και τεκούσα και τέκνα, / Tο πατρομητρότεκνον αθλητών γένος» — the father was burned, and she who bore them, and the children: the father-mother-child race of contestants. There is no country, no century, no emperor, no hymn, and no name for any of the four, so they are left here as the calendar leaves them, counted and unnamed, with no folders of their own.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 29 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/29/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/738/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
