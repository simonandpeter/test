# Zacharias of Vienne

saint.gr names him and gives him one sentence: the holy hieromartyr Zacharias was the second bishop of Vienne and was martyred for Christ in the year 160. That is the whole of the entry, on a page that carries nothing else at all.

There is no birthplace, no account of the episcopate, no persecutor, no manner of death, no relics and no burial place; the page prints neither a synaxarion couplet nor a hymn. What is recorded of him is an order in a list of bishops, a see, a year, and that he died for Christ.
