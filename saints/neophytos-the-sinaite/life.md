# Neophytos the Sinaite

saint.gr's calendar for 20 December names Neophytos the hieromartyr, the Sinaite, and gives no life: his page carries the one sentence «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου» — we have no details of the saint's life. The whole of the entry is the name, the rank of hieromartyr, and the by-name that attaches him to Sinai. The page says nothing of when he lived, nothing of where, and nothing of how he died.

Its feast is marked fixed — «Τύπος εορτής: Σταθερή. Εορτάζει στις 20 Δεκεμβρίου εκάστου έτους» — and no hymn is printed. Above the sentence stands the lexicon note the site gives every Neophytos, that the name is from νέος and φύομαι and means the newly initiated, the one born again. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence by which saint.gr marks an entry whose existence or date it will not vouch for.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 20 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/20/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3614/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
