# Theotime

saint.gr names her and gives no life. Its whole account of her is one sentence: «Η Αγία Μάρτυς Θεοτίμη τελειώθηκε δι' αποκεφαλισμού» — the holy martyr Theotime was perfected by beheading. The page gives her no country, no century, no emperor and no companions, and prints no hymn to her.

What stands beside that sentence is the couplet of the synaxarion, which plays on her name: «Tιμή Θεός πέφυκε τη Θεοτίμη, Hν τιμίαν τίθησι Mάρτυρα ξίφος» — God is the honour of Theotime, whom the sword sets up as an honoured martyr. Nothing further about her is recorded here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 19 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/19/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1635/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
