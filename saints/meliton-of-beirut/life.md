# Meliton of Beirut

saint.gr's calendar for 3 Ιανουαρίου names him and gives no life. Its entire notice is one sentence — the venerable Meliton from Beirut fell asleep in peace in the year 537 — and beside it a gloss on the name, Μελίτων from μέλι, honey, read as the honey-flowing, the sweet one.

That is everything this source holds. It does not say where he lived as a monk, whether he was a priest, what he wrote or whom he taught, and none of that is supplied here. The Greek calendar keeps him on the third of January, calls him venerable, places him at Beirut and dates his death 537, and that is what is recorded.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/03/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1274/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
