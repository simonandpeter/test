# Macarius (Mironov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him second of three in one line for 23 декабря ст. ст.: «Сщмч. Василия пресвитера и прмч. Макария и Ионы Смирнова (1938).» The line declines the forename to «Макария» and gives no surname for him, no place, no year of birth and no word of narrative; the surname it prints at the end belongs to the third man.

Behind that «Макария» stands the calendar's own name page 9223, which carries the fuller form «Макарий (Миронов), иеромонах, сщмч.» — the surname the day's line leaves out, and a hieromonk's grade. The two pages disagree on his rank: the day's line governs him with «прмч.», a venerable-martyr, and the name page closes him with «сщмч.», a hieromartyr. Both are the calendar's own and this folder records both rather than choose.

That page lists two days, this 23 декабря and «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. It carries no life, and neither does the day page.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 23 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261223.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9223.html), which carries no life; read 13 October 2026.*
