# Boris (Uspensky)

The calendar’s own line is the whole of what is recorded of him here. The Сретенский monastery’s calendar for 2 декабря ст. ст. closes its twentieth-century block with him, in three words and a year: «Мч. Бориса (1942).» The grade is «мч.», a martyr and not a hieromartyr, so the calendar makes him a layman; it says nothing else about him at all.

The name page behind his place gives the surname the line leaves out: «Борис (Успенский), мч.» There is no life behind that page, and the day page carries no hymn under his name. The corpus already holds four men of this surname — a Constantine, a John, a Peter and a presbyter of 1930 — and nothing read connects him to any of them; the surname is a common one made from the feast of the Dormition.

So nothing is said here of where he was born, what he did, when or by whom he was arrested, or how and where he died. The year 1942 puts his death in the war rather than in the terror of 1937, and that is an observation about the year and not something the page says.

His name page lists one other day, «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church. One row per church is the schema, so that day is recorded here and he does not render on it.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 2 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261202.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/12986.html); read 7 October 2026.*
