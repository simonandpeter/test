# Winefride of Treffynon

saint.gr's calendar for 3 Νοεμβρίου names «Αγία Winefride του Treffynon» — writing both her name and her place in Latin letters, which it does for only a handful of entries in the year — and the page behind that line says one thing only: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο της Αγίας», we have no details of the saint's life.

So the Greek calendar keeps her on this day and gives nothing of her but the name and the place. There is no year, no account of how she lived or how she died, no relics, no rank word beyond Αγία, and no hymn; and it offers no Greek form of the name either, which is why this folder records the Latin-script form the page prints and transliterates nothing.

The entry carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands, bare as it is.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/03/index.aspx) and [her notice](https://www.saint.gr/2880/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
