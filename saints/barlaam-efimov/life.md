# Barlaam (Efimov)

His line is the last of the three in the Russian 8 October's paragraph of new martyrs, and it stands by itself: «Прмч. Варлаама (конец 1930-х)» — Barlaam, venerable-martyr, the end of the nineteen-thirties. «Прмч.» is the mark of a monastic killed for the faith rather than a priest of the parishes, and the site's index of names adds the surname and nothing else, heading his page «Варлаам (Ефимов), прмч.». He is the only line on the whole of this day to which the calendar gives no year, and the period it gives instead is the only date either page carries.

The two companies of 1937 that the same paragraph names stand before him — the eight shot at Butovo under [Demetrius (Dobroserdov)](/saints/demetrius-dobroserdov) and the eleven under [Jonah (Lazarev)](/saints/jonah-lazarev) — and his own sentence comes after both of them, which is as much as the layout says about whether he belonged to either. His surname is the same as that of the Vasily (Efimov) named in the heading of the first company's life, and nothing on either page connects them.

His index entry keeps him here and on 26 January with the whole company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and it carries no life. The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian saints are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026 even with a browser user-agent, so the line stands as the line: a monk, a surname, and the end of a decade.

*After the Russian church calendar's [8 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261008.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13823.html); read 4 October 2026.*
