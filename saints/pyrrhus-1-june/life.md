# Pyrrhus (1 June)

saint.gr's calendar for 1 Ιουνίου names him and gives one sentence: the venerable Pyrrhus died peacefully. The calendar's own line is where the rest of what is known stands — that he was a bishop, and that he is called the Virgin — and the page's body adds nothing to it at all.

It names no see, no country, no century and no year. What it does add is a distich on the epithet: Pyrrhus raised a great tower of chastity, it says, through which he beholds God, having put the dust beneath him. The corpus keeps another Pyrrhus, of Breti in Georgia, and nothing here connects the two; this folder takes the day for its name because the page gives nothing else to take.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/442/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
