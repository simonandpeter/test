# Theodosius of Oroboi

saint.gr prints a short notice and no more. It says that the venerable Theodosius learned from his own family to contend for the Gospel, which means to contend for the love of God and of his neighbour. When he went to the monastery he laboured honourably for piety within the monastic brotherhood, and likewise for humility and for brotherly love.

And when he became abbot, the page closes, he made his monastery a centre of philanthropic service. The listing for the day names the house he governed, «Όσιος Θεοδόσιος ηγούμενος Ορόβων» — Theodosius, abbot of Oroboi — and saint.gr gives nothing further: no year, no country, no account of his death, and no hymn.
