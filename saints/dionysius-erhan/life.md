# Dionysius (Erhan)

Dionysius Erhan was born in 1868 in the village of Bardar, fifteen
kilometres from Chișinău, to devout parents, Vasile and Andriana, and named
Demetrius at baptism. At fifteen, feeling the longing for God and the wish
to put on the monastic habit, he entered the community of Suruceni
monastery, and from the first showed himself zealous for holy things and
eager for knowledge, striving to form a theological culture by reading the
Scriptures and the works of the Fathers, which guided his life and upheld
him at the turning-points of his own life and the nation’s history; he
passed through all the stages of monastic life — rasophore in 1890, monk in
1899 with the name Dionysius, hierodeacon in 1900, hieromonk in 1904 — and,
remarkable for his spiritual life, gentleness, firmness and dignity in
defending nation and faith, for the culture he gained self-taught and the
esteem of the faithful, was chosen abbot of Suruceni in 1908. He played an
important part in achieving the ideal of national unity before the historic
moment of 27 March 1918, a good part of the members of the Country Council
having him for guide and confessor; in May 1918 he was raised to
archimandrite, and on 22 July 1918 chosen vicar bishop of the archdiocese of
Chișinău and Hotin with the title of Ismail, consecrated in the metropolitan
cathedral at Iași, and in 1934 became bishop of Cetatea Albă and Ismail,
where he shepherded six years, restoring and building churches, founding
institutions for the formation of clergy, supporting asylums and orphanages
and working with great zeal for the spiritual renewal of southern
Bessarabia. After the Ribbentrop–Molotov pact and the cession of Bessarabia
he took refuge in the country, locum tenens of the diocese of Argeș in
1940–1941; on 1 September 1941 he retired and went back to Bessarabia, to
the monastery of his profession, and died on 17 September 1943 in the
central hospital of Chișinău. He was buried in a crypt beside the church of
Suruceni, the house his soul was bound to, and during consolidation works
there on 10 July 2018 his body was found incorrupt. At the proposal of the
synod of the Metropolis of Bessarabia, following the steps taken by the
diocese of southern Bessarabia, the Holy Synod of the Romanian Orthodox
Church entered him in its calendar at its session of 25 October 2018,
weighing the orthodoxy of his faith and his exceptional work as abbot of
Suruceni, and above all as vicar bishop of Chișinău and then bishop of
Ismail and Cetatea Albă. The Romanian calendar keeps him on 17 September
with a troparion that calls him a worthy shepherd of the Romanians of
Bessarabia.
