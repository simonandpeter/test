# Basil, companion of Stephen the New

The Greek calendar keeps on 28 November, beside [Stephen the New](/saints/stephen-the-new), the martyrs who suffered with him for the holy icons. The rage of Copronymus and of those about him, saint.gr says, was not confined to one victim: after Stephen, other confessors of Orthodoxy underwent tortures and deaths as well. Of these the page names two, and Basil is the first of them.

One of them, it says, Basil, was blinded by the executioners, and went on proclaiming his Orthodox faith even so; and then they killed him with kicks. That is the whole of his story on the page. The other it names is [John of the Legatarioi](/saints/john-of-the-legatarioi), exiled to Daphnousia; the rest, it says, were put to death in other barbarous ways or sent into exile and passed their lives in continual martyrdom, and it does not name them.

The page gives Basil no country, no rank, no year and no burial, and prints no hymn. Its couplet over the company says only that, honouring the image of the Word's flesh, the saints die a violent death, and that Stephen, having many fellow-contestants, takes delight in weaving a dance with them.

The Russian calendar names him second of the five it prints on 28 ноября ст. ст., «Мчч. Стефана , Василия , Григория , другого Григория , Иоанна и иных многих (VIII).», and its name page 374 gives him a by-name the Greek page did not: «Василий Византийский», Basil the Byzantine. Its one life for the whole company is two sentences, and they agree with what is above: they suffered for the honouring of the holy icons together with the confessor they were imprisoned with, and after his martyr's end they were put to death. The Roman numeral VIII is the only dating either calendar supplies, and it is recorded here at the full width of the century.
