# Maurice, slain with his five sons

The Охридски пролог gives him one sentence, and it is the whole of what this folder has: he was put to death with his five sons by the emperor Phocas in the year 602. The book heads him «Св. благоверни цар», the holy right-believing emperor, and the rank is the only other thing it says of him.

That is all of it, and nothing has been added here. The entry gives no year of birth, no place of birth and no place of death; it does not name one of the five sons, it does not say in what order they died, and it says nothing of how he reigned or why he fell. No account of the reign has been imported to fill it out, and no other calendar was read for him, so nothing in this folder claims that the Russian, the Romanian or the Greek church keeps him or does not.

He is not the other Maurice whom this same book keeps, on the Julian 22 February: a commander who died at Apamea with seventy of his soldiers about the year 305, and who carries the same Serbian form of the name. Three centuries, two ranks and two deaths apart, and nothing read joins the two accounts.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 28. новембар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2025-12-11&prolog=1); read 8 October 2026.*
