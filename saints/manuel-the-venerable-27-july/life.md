# Manuel (27 July)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 27 iulie is «Sfântul Cuvios Manuel» — the venerable Manuel — and his own page on the site carries only the day and the epistle and gospel appointed for it, the gospel of the easy yoke and the epistle of the fruit of the Spirit, which are the readings of a monastic saint. There is no Viață tab, no troparion, no country, no century.

So what the Romanian calendar sets down of him is his rank, venerable and not a martyr, and his day, the twenty-seventh of July. Nothing else was read, and nothing else is supplied here; the folder is marked by his day because the calendar gives him nothing else to be known by.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 27 iulie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/27-iulie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-manuel), which prints no life; read 24 September 2026.*
