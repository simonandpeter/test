# Orentius

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 25 iunie reads «Sfântul Mucenic Orentie», and the page behind it carries only the day, the epistle appointed for it and the gospel read with it: no country, no century, no companions, no manner of death, no year, no icon and no hymn.

The calendars elsewhere keep an Orentius among six brothers, soldiers exiled to the Caucasus; nothing on this page names brothers or soldiering or any place, and no such claim is made here. What the source gives is a name, the rank of martyr and the day.

The readings appointed are 2 Timothy 2:1–10 and John 15:17–27 with 16:1–2.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and does not keep him alone. saint.gr's line for 25 Ιουνίου reads «Άγιοι Ορέντιος, Φαρνάκιος, Έρωτας, Φίρμος, Φιρμίνος, Κυριάκος, και Λογγίνος», and the page behind it says the seven were brothers, famous for their courage, serving as soldiers in Thrace in the years of the emperor Diocletian, which it dates here to 301. In some battle with the Scythians Orentius killed their hard and brave leader, Mararon, and was honoured for it — and in the same breath invited to join the sacrifices to the idols that were to be offered for the victory. He refused outright and declared that he acknowledged one true God, the God of the Christians; for his bravery's sake he was not punished at once, but he and his six brothers, [Pharnacius](/saints/pharnacius-brother-of-orentius), [Eros](/saints/eros-brother-of-orentius), [Firmus](/saints/firmus-brother-of-orentius), [Firminus](/saints/firminus-brother-of-orentius), [Cyriacus](/saints/cyriacus-brother-of-orentius) and [Longinus](/saints/longinus-brother-of-orentius), were sent on an unwelcome transfer to Armenia. There all seven were interrogated, declared with one mouth that they would stay faithful to the last breath, were condemned to exile in distant and hard places, and died one after another of the hardships, without a murmur from any of them.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 25 iunie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/25-iunie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-orentie); read 24 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 25 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/25/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1974/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
