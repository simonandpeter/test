# Anthony of Georgia

saint.gr's calendar for 1 February names Anthony the hermit and gives him one sentence: that he lived in the sixth century, that he practised his asceticism as a hermit in Georgia, and that he fell asleep in peace. Nothing else is on the page — no birthplace within Georgia, no parentage, no year of birth or of death, no monastery, no disciples, no wonders, and no apolytikion and no kontakion.

The English name here keeps the country rather than the manner of life, because the corpus records the manner of life in its own field and because the great Anthony of Egypt, whom this calendar keeps on 17 January, would otherwise be hard to tell him from. What the source says is the country and the century, and that is what stands.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1347/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
