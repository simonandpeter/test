# Stephen of Rhegium

saint.gr's calendar for 5 Ιουλίου names him and gives no life. The whole of its account is two sentences: «Η μνήμη του αναφέρεται στον Συναξαριστή Delehaye (Synaxaria Selecta σελ. 800, 52 και 804, 37). Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του.» — his memory is recorded in Delehaye's synaxarion, Synaxaria Selecta, at pages 800, 52 and 804, 37; we have no further details of his life. The day's line gives him his rank and his see, metropolitan of Rhegium in Calabria, and that is all the source says of him.

The page names no century and no year, no parentage, no deeds and no manner of death, and it prints no hymn and no synaxarion couplet under his name. The same day's listing carries a second entry beside his, for the commemoration of the dedication of the church of Saint Stephen metropolitan of Rhegium of Calabria, which is a dedication and not a person; the existence of that church is the only other thing the calendar reports of him.
