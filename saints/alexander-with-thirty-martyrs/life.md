# Alexander, with thirty martyrs

The Greek synaxarion keeps Alexander and the thirty martyrs with him on 28 September and says only that they were martyred by the sword. Its verse has the thirty men, beheaded, take Alexander as the leader of their end. It names none of the thirty and gives no date.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 28 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2470/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
