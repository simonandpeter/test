# Nicholas (12 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 12 Νοεμβρίου keeps a new martyr Nicholas, of the parish of the Six Marbles, and says plainly how little it has: «Πολλές πληροφορίες για τη ζωή του δεν έχουμε» — we do not have much information about his life. This glorious new martyr of the Christian faith, it says, was martyred at Constantinople for his piety in the year 1732, and his memory was appointed to be kept on 12 November.

So the parish he came from, the city he died in and the year are the whole of what is recorded: no age, no trade, no family, no account of the arrest or the manner of death, no burial, and no hymn. The page's distich says that he conquers the enemy by his contest and receives the prizes.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3018/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
