# Zeno of Gaza

Zeno and his brothers [Eusebius](/saints/eusebius-of-gaza) and [Nestabus](/saints/nestabus-of-gaza) came from Gaza and lived in the years of Julian the Apostate. Because they were Christians the pagans of the city seized them, dragged them through the streets and tortured them harshly and in many ways; at last they crushed their heads with stones, carried them out of the city and burned them in a furnace. The verse has the three die by the sword for their pious faith. The Greek calendar keeps them in one entry with [Nestor](/saints/nestor-21-september) and [Busiris](/saints/busiris), of whom its notice says nothing beyond their names.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 21 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/1008/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
