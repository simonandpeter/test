# Leo of Mandra

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 11 iulie reads «Sfântul Cuvios Leon din Mandra», and the page the calendar links under that name carries the name again and nothing more: no life, no troparion, no year, no account of the place called Mandra. What the Romanian calendar prints of him on this day is that he is a venerable father, that he is called Leon, and that he is of Mandra; and this folder says no more than that.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 11 July, under the same byname, «Όσιος Λέων ο εν τη Μάνδρα», and has no more to say of him than the Romanian one. saint.gr's whole notice is one sentence: the venerable Leo fell asleep in peace — «Ο Όσιος Λέων απεβίωσε ειρηνικά». Above it the page sets a distich, and that is the only other thing it prints: «Eκ γης απελθών εντρυφά τερπνοίς Λέων, Ων είχεν εν γη τας απορροίας πάλαι» — having gone from the earth, Leo takes his delight in the pleasant things whose overflowings he had on earth long ago. There is no century, no year, no country, no account of his ascesis and no hymn.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 11 iulie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/11-iulie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-leon-din-mandra), which prints the name only; read 24 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 11 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/11/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2121/saint.aspx), read 1 October 2026.*
