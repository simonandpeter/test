# Peter (Reshetnikov)

Peter Reshetnikov was born in 1886 to a peasant family and finished the second-class parish school in 1899. On 28 September 1911 he was ordained deacon to the church of the village of Ilyinskoye in the Perm district; on 2 December 1914 he was ordained priest and appointed to the church of the village of Bym in the Osa district, seconded to the church of the village of Yershi in the same district, and on 22 April 1917 was moved to the church of Plotnikovo in the Kungur district, though in fact he went on serving at Yershi. On 29 August / 11 September 1918, after cruel tortures and mockery, he was shot by Red Army men; he was glorified among the saints at the jubilee Council of Bishops, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 30 August (Julian).
