# Gregory (Nikolsky)

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 27 июня ст. ст. names him on a line of three, «Сщмчч. Григория, Александра и Владимира пресвитеров (1918).», and he is the first of them. The line gives the forenames in the genitive, one grade-class word and one rank word for the three together, and one year, and it gives no surname to any of them. The name link behind his place in it supplies the calendar’s own nominative with the surname and the grade, «Григорий (Никольский), иерей, сщмч.», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from; the parentheses are the calendar’s own.

His name page carries nothing else: it heads «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 27 июня» and stops, with no life, no place of service, no diocese and no account of his death. Nothing is supplied for any of it here. The line’s «Сщмчч.» makes him a hieromartyr and «пресвитеров» a presbyter, and the name link repeats the rank as «иерей», a priest; the year in the line, 1918, is the year of death recorded here and the calendar gives no year of birth.

The second day his name page lists is «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which is a synaxis and not a second feast, so he renders on 27 июня ст. ст. only. The surname stands in the display name because the calendar gives him no epithet and no see to tell him by, and because the line names two other presbyters in the same words. The day page prints five hymns and not one of them is under his name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 27 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260627.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/15019.html); read 9 October 2026.*
