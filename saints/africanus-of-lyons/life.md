# Africanus of Lyons

saint.gr names him and gives no life. Its page for 1 Μαΐου prints the line «Άγιος Αφρικανός Επίσκοπος Λουγδούνου», Africanus bishop of Lugdunum, and under it one sentence: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου» — we have no details of the saint's life. Nothing else is there: no century, no year, no account of how he came to the see or how he left it, no relic, no hymn.

So the record is the name, the rank and the see, and the day the Greek calendar keeps him on. Lugdunum is Lyons, the old see of Gaul; the page gives it in that ancient form and says nothing more of the place either.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1434/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
