# George (Sedov)

The calendar’s own line is the whole of what is recorded of him here. The Сретенский monastery’s calendar for 3 декабря ст. ст. commemorates him alone, in a few words and a year: «Св. Георгия исп (1960).» It is the last clause of the day, and the latest death date anywhere on it.

The name page behind his place gives him the surname the line leaves out together with his grade: «Георгий (Седов), исп.». Behind that page there is no life, and the day page carries no hymn under his name; its six hymns all stand under the names of the ancient saints of the same day.

He is not called a martyr. The line gives him «Св.», holy, and the grade «исп.», a confessor, and his name page repeats «Георгий (Седов), исп.» and nothing else; so this folder records a confessor and no martyrdom, and 1960 is the year of his death and not of a killing. The locale packs carry no office string for a lay confessor, and the calendar names him no house, no church and no grade in one, so he is given no office at all. Of the day’s three twentieth-century saints he is the only one with neither «сщмч.» nor a priestly grade, and the one who outlived the terror.

So nothing is said here of where he was born, where he served, when or by whom he was arrested, or how and where he died. No page read says any of it, and the year 1960 is the year the line closes with.

His name page lists one other day, «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church. One row per church is the schema, so that day is recorded here and he does not render on it.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 3 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261203.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9960.html); read 7 October 2026.*
