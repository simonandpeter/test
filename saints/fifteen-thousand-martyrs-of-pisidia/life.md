# The Fifteen Thousand Martyrs of Pisidia

saint.gr's calendar for 16 July keeps a company it numbers and does not name: «Άγιοι Δεκαπέντε Χιλιάδες Μάρτυρες οι εν Πισιδία», the fifteen thousand martyrs in Pisidia. Its page gives them one sentence — they were martyred by the sword — and the couplet of the Synaxarion set above it says the same thing a second time: «Θείων αθλητών αμφί πέντε και δέκα, / Xιλιάδας τέμνουσιν αμφί γην μίαν», about fifteen thousand of the divine contenders cut down in one land.

That is the whole of the source. No year is given, no emperor, no governor, no town of Pisidia, no name of any one of the fifteen thousand, and no hymn. Because the page names none of them, what the calendar keeps on this day is the company and not its members, and that is what this folder holds; and because it gives no year, no date is recorded for them here.

The Serbian church keeps them on the same 16 July, and in a different country. The Ohrid Prologue of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović gives the company one sentence — they were beheaded with the sword for the faith of Christ in Persia — where the Greek page numbers them and places them in Pisidia. The two books agree on the number, on the day and on the sword, and disagree on the land; both readings are recorded here and neither is preferred. Neither book names one of the fifteen thousand, and neither gives a year, so this folder still keeps the company and not its members, and still carries no date.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 16 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/16/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2101/saint.aspx), which prints only that one sentence; read 1 October 2026; and the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 16. јул ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-07-29&prolog=1), read 6 October 2026.*
