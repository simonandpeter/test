# Theodore the Silent of the Kyiv Caves

Doxologia's calendar names Theodore the Silent for 17 February and gives him two sentences, which are the whole of his life as the page has it. He loved greatly, it says, the labour of silence, and by it he kept the remembrance of the Lord always; and for the greatness of that labour he was counted worthy of the gift of working miracles.

The second sentence adds that his memory is kept also on 28 August, when the synaxis of the saints of the Lavra of the Caves at Kyiv is made. The page gives no century, no parentage and no account of his coming to the Caves, and it prints no troparion; this entry adds nothing to what it says.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 17 februarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/17-februarie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-teodor-cel-tacut-de-la-lavra-pesterilor-din-kiev); read 19 September 2026.*
