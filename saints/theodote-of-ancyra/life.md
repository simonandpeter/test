# Theodote of Ancyra

saint.gr keeps a company of virgin martyrs of Ancyra in Galatia on 18 May, and names eight of them in its calendar line and in its head entries: [Thecusa](/saints/thecusa-of-ancyra), Alexandria, Claudia, Phaeine, Euphrasia, Matrona, Julia and Theodote. They came from Ancyra, it says, gave themselves wholeheartedly to the service of the Gospel, and were among the greatest servants and contenders of the Christian faith; always most ready in works of mercy and charity, they worked together at drawing idolatrous women into the bosom of the Church.

They were denounced for that work and refused to sacrifice to the idols. Theoteknos, the governor of Ancyra, then handed them over to the soldiers to be corrupted; and because by God's grace they were kept pure, all of them were drowned in the depths of the lake there. [Theodotus](/saints/theodotus-of-ancyra), whose aunt Thecusa was one of those holy women, drew their bodies out by night and buried them reverently; he was found out, and because he would not deny Christ he was beheaded after many tortures.

The page's own verse over that narrative is headed for the seven, and calls them a sevenfold company of women cast into a lake — where the calendar line and the head entries name eight. saint.gr does not reconcile the two counts, and nothing is inferred here from either.
