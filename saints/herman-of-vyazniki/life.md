# Herman (Ryashentsev), Bishop of Vyazniki, Hieromartyr (1937)

Herman, bishop of Vyazniki — Nicholas Stepanovich Ryashentsev in the world — was born on 10 November 1883 in the city of Tambov, the son of the merchant of the second guild Stepan Grigorievich Ryashentsev; four days later, on 14 November, the feast of the apostle Philip and of Gregory Palamas, before the Nativity fast, the infant was baptised in the Tambov cathedral of the Nativity of Christ by the archpriest Alexis Petrovsky and named Nicholas for the wonderworker. Already in early childhood Nicholas felt a calling — the calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. He took monastic vows in 1905 and was consecrated bishop in 1919; he was arrested repeatedly for the faith and his church work — in 1921, 1922, 1925, 1928 and 1934 — and served exiles and camp sentences, among them Solovki. Arrested for the last time in 1937 at Syktyvkar on a charge of founding a counter-revolutionary group, he did not admit guilt; the troika of the NKVD sentenced him to be shot, and he was executed on 15 September 1937. He was numbered among the saints in 2001, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 2 September (Julian).
