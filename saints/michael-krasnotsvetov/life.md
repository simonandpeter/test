# Michael (Krasnotsvetov)

The Russian calendar gives him one line on 29 September and no life: «Сщмч. Михаила пресвитера (1937)» — Michael the hieromartyr, presbyter, 1937. Its index of names adds the surname, «Михаил (Красноцветов), пресвитер, сщмч.», and keeps him here and on 26 January with the whole company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia.

The surname is unusual enough to be worth writing down — Красноцветов, red-flowering — and it is the only thing in the source that is particular to him. Where he served, what he was charged with and where he died the calendar does not print. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which refused this reading on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line.

*After the Russian church calendar's [29 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260929.html) and its [index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/15038.html); read 4 October 2026.*
