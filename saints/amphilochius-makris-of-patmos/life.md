# Amphilochius (Makris) of Patmos

The Romanian calendar keeps on 19 September the translation of the relics of Amphilochius Makris of Patmos, and doxologia.ro’s list for the day prints the line and no page under it. This entry records the line and nothing more.

*After the day’s list of doxologia.ro, 19 September 2026 — [the page](https://doxologia.ro/19-septembrie), which prints no life; read 23 August 2026.*
