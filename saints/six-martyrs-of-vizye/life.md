# The Six Martyrs of Vizye

saint.gr's calendar names them and gives almost no life. Its line for the day is «Άγιοι Έξι Μάρτυρες οι εν Βιζύη», the six holy martyrs at Vizye, and the whole of its notice is one sentence about how they died: «Μαρτύρησαν, αφού άλλους συνέτριψαν τα οστά και άλλους ξέσχισαν τις σάρκες.» They suffered martyrdom, some of them having their bones crushed and some their flesh torn. The page names none of the six, and gives no year and no emperor.

Above that sentence it prints the distich the Greek books give them, «Oστών καταγμός και σπαραγμός σαρκίων, / Aρπαγμός εστιν έξ αθληταίς στεμμάτων» — the breaking of bones and the tearing of flesh is, for six contenders, the seizing of crowns.

The page then contradicts its own heading in a closing note. Its listing and its head line put the six at Vizye, in Thrace; the note says that the synaxaristes of the monastery of Dionysiou writes that they suffered in Libya. Neither reading is argued for and nothing on the page chooses between them, so both are set down here and no place is recorded as a fact.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/10/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/774/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
