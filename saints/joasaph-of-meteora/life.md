# Joasaph of Meteora

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The day's line reads «Sfântul Cuvios Ioasaf» and nothing more, and his own page carries no Viață tab: only icons, places of pilgrimage, a section for the Holy Mountain, and the appointed readings.

What says who he is are the titles of the icons on that page, which read «Sfinții Cuvioși Atanasie și Ioasaf de la Meteora» — the venerable Athanasius and Joasaph of Meteora. He is therefore kept here as the companion of [Athanasius](/saints/athanasius-of-meteora), the founder of Meteora, whom the same calendar keeps on this same 20 April; and that identification rests on those captions and on nothing else, which is why it is written out here rather than assumed.

The calendar gives him no years, no country, no office and no troparion, and none is supplied. Of the other men of his name in this corpus — Joasaph of Belgorod, Joasaph son of Abenner, Joasaph of Kubensk, Joasaph of Zographou — none is kept on this day, and nothing on this page identifies him with any of them.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 20 aprilie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/20-aprilie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-ioasaf), which prints no life; read 20 September 2026.*
