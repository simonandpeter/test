# Nikon (Belyaev)

The calendar's own line is the whole of what is recorded of him here, and that line is one of the longest in this calendar's year. The Сретенский monastery's calendar for 27 ноября ст. ст. commemorates an archbishop, thirteen presbyters, eight monastics and a layman in a single clause closing on a single year: «Сщмчч. Николая , архиеп. Владимирского, Василия , Бориса , Феодора , Николая , Алексия , Иоанна , Сергия , Иоанна , Сергия , Николая , Димитрия , Владимира , Иоанна пресвитеров, прмчч. Иоасафа , Кронида , Николая , Ксенофонта , Алексия , Аполлоса , Серафима , Никона и мч. Иоанна (1937).»

He stands twenty-second in it. The name page behind his place gives him the surname the line leaves out together with his grade: «Никон (Беляев), архимандрит, прмч.» — an archimandrite and a venerable-martyr. The calendar carries no life page behind that name page and none on the day itself, and the day page's troparia all stand under other names.

So nothing is said here of where he was born, where he served, when or by whom he was arrested, or where he died: no page read says any of it. The year 1937 is the year the line closes on, and it is given for all twenty-three men at once.

His name page lists one other day, «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church. One row per church is the schema, so that day is recorded here and he does not render on it.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 27 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261127.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13877.html); read 7 October 2026.*
