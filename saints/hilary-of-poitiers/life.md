# Hilary of Poitiers

doxologia.ro's calendar names him on 13 January and gives no life. His page on that site carries his icon, a shelf of articles about him and a shelf of his own texts translated into Romanian, and, standing for a life, four lines: «Sfântul Ilarie (300-368) a fost cel care a reunit episcopatul din Galia, a restaurat credinţa niceană în Aquitania şi a pus temeliile monahismului apusean împreună cu Sfântul Benedict de Nursia. Marea teologie latină a început cu Sfântul Ilarie.»

In English: Hilary, 300 to 368, was the one who reunited the episcopate of Gaul, restored the Nicene faith in Aquitaine, and laid the foundations of Western monasticism together with Benedict of Nursia; and great Latin theology began with him. That is the whole of what the page says of his life, and the pairing with Benedict is the page's own and is left standing as the page has it. Nothing is added here about where he was born, how he came to Poitiers, when he was made bishop, or what he wrote, because the page says none of it.

The day's line prints him without a rank, simply «Sfântul Ilarie de Poitiers», where the other bishop kept on the same day is printed «Sfântul Ierarh Iacob, Episcopul din Nisibe»; so no rank is recorded for him here either.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 13 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/13-ianuarie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-ilarie-de-poitiers); read 19 September 2026.*
