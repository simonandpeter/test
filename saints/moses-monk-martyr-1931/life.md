# Moses (Kozhin)

Moses Nikitich Kozhin was born on 1 May 1868 in the village of Olenitsa
in the Kem district of Archangel province, today in the Tersky district of
the Murmansk region. In his young years he resolved to give his life
wholly to God and went to the Solovki monastery, where he finished the
six classes of the brotherhood school and from about 1897 was a novice,
bearing the obediences of trapper, fisherman and ferryman across the
strait between the Solovki and Anzer islands; he was tonsured at the
Transfiguration monastery of Solovki on 7 April 1907, ordained
hierodeacon on 20 July 1908, and hieromonk on 15 June 1911 by Tryphon
(Turkestanov), bishop of Dmitrov, vicar of Moscow, at the first Liturgy in
the newly consecrated church of the Nativity of Christ of the
Tryphon-Pechenga monastery. His lot was that of many Solovki monks in the
twenties: he laboured at Solovki until the Bolsheviks closed it, and,
expelled from his monastery at its closing, went back to his native
Olenitsa on the Tersky shore of the Kola peninsula, where from 1 August
1925 he was rector of the church of the Nativity of John the Forerunner
and served until his arrest, holding firmly to the Orthodox ways — for
which, in effect, he was persecuted. He was arrested on 16 April 1931 on
the charge that he systematically carried on anti-collective-farm
propaganda under the flag of religion — wrecking a collective-farm meeting
and counter-revolutionary agitation under cover of religion, in a group
case — and on 4 May 1931 the Murmansk district OGPU sentenced him to three
years in a labour camp, and on 18 June the troika of the OGPU for the
Leningrad military district to three years’ exile in the Northern Region
under article 58-10. He died in the prison hospital — the Haas hospital in
Leningrad — on 7 September 1931, of scurvy and enterocolitis, and was
rehabilitated by the Murmansk regional procuracy on 16 April 1989. The
jubilee Council of Bishops glorified him on 20 August 2000, and the Holy
Synod entered him in the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the
Russian Church on 30 July 2003; his memory is kept on the day of his
death, 25 August (Julian), and in the synaxes of the saints of Kola, of
Arkhangelsk and of the new martyrs of Solovki.
