# Gurias, companion of Barsanuphius

saint.gr's calendar for 15 December keeps Saint Gurias, and its page is one sentence long: Saint Gurias, together with Saint [Barsanuphius](/saints/barsanuphius-of-tver-and-kazan), who is honoured on 11 April, laboured to spread the Christian faith among the Muslims. That is the entry entire, and it is the whole of what saint.gr prints about him anywhere.

The page names no country and no city, gives no year of birth and none of death, names no office and no monastery, and prints no hymn for him. The Barsanuphius it sends the reader to on 11 April is the bishop of Tver and Kazan, and that reference is the only thing in the entry which places Gurias at all; nothing further is drafted from it.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 15 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/15/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1880/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
