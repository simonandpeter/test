# Felix of Apollonia

[Isaurus](/saints/isaurus-the-deacon) and those with him were from Athens. Leaving his own country he came to a cave near Apollonia, and there he found [Innocent](/saints/innocent-of-apollonia), Felix and [Peregrinus](/saints/peregrinus-of-apollonia) and Hermias, and taught them not to give themselves to the things of this world that pass away; and they made the word good by the deed, keeping away from the talk and the company of their own kinsmen. Their relatives denounced them to Tripontius the eparch, who seized them and, unable to make them deny Christ, ordered them given over to torture.

[Isaurus](/saints/isaurus-the-deacon), the deacon of Christ, and those with him were handed over to Apollonius, the eparch's son, by whom they were tormented with fire and with water. Delivered from those torments in a wonderful way, they turned many to faith in Christ, among them the foremost men of the city, the brothers Rufus and Rufinus. Then they were condemned to death, and their heads were struck off.

Doxologia's calendar for 17 iunie gives Felix a line of his own, «Sfântul Mucenic Felix», and sends it to the life the whole company shares. He is one of those whom Isaurus found in the cave at Apollonia, and the page says nothing of him apart from the company.
