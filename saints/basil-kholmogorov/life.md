# Basil (Kholmogorov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him on 3 января old style and prints one line only: «Сщмч. Василия пресвитера (1938)» — the hieromartyr Basil, presbyter, 1938. The name link behind that line carries the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line itself omits, «Василий (Холмогоров), протоиерей, сщмч.», which makes him an archpriest; and there the entry ends, for the calendar holds no life page for him.

So what is set down here is what the calendar sets down. A priest named Basil Kholmogorov is kept on this day as a hieromartyr, and the year given for his death is 1938. Of where he was born, where he served, how he was arrested and how he died the calendar says nothing at all, and nothing has been supplied here from any other source.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 3 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260103.html); read 3 October 2026.*
