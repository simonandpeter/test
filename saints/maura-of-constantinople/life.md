# Maura of Constantinople

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps her on 31 октября ст. ст., and the life it serves for her is one sentence long. She struggled in ascetic labour at Tsargrad — the calendar's own name for Constantinople — where she founded a monastery; and in that monastery she died, in the fifth century. There is nothing else: the page gives no year, no name for the house she built, no emperor to date her by, no account of how she came to the city and no word of her relics.

The day's line adds her rank and the century and no more, «Прп. Мавры Константинопольской (V)». The name link behind it adds the one further thing recorded of her anywhere on this calendar, and it is a single word: she was a deaconess. Her page carries no icon and no hymn, on a day whose four other entries are all sung.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 31 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261031.html) and [her life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4622.htm); read 4 October 2026.*
