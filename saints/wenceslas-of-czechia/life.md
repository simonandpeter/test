# Wenceslas of Czechia

The Greek synaxarion keeps Wenceslas, prince of the Czechs, on 28 September, and says that it has no details of the saint’s life. Its heading prints his death as 935. That is all it prints.

The Russian calendar keeps him on the same 28 September, as «Блгв. кн. Вячеслава Чешского» — the right-believing prince Vyacheslav of Czechia, which is the Slavonic form of the name the West prints Wenceslas — and dates his death 935.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 28 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/3799/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026; and the Russian church calendar's [28 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260928.html), read 4 October 2026.*
