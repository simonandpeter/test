# Gregory of Panidos

The memory of the venerable Gregory, saint.gr says, is mentioned in the Patmos codex 266, without any biographical note at all. At all events he lived in the time of the iconoclasm, and he died confessing the right faith; the calendar's own line for him calls him the new confessor. A bishopric of Panidos, the page adds, is met with nowhere; perhaps, it suggests, it is Paniou, which lay in Herakleia of Thrace.

That is the whole of what the page prints about him. It gives no year and no century beyond the iconoclast period, nothing of his birth or his see or his death besides the confession, and no hymn.
