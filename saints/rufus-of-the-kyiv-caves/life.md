# Rufus of the Kyiv Caves

saint.gr's whole account of him is two sentences: «Ο Όσιος Ρούφος έζησε τον 14ο αιώνα μ.Χ. Διακρίθηκε για την υπακοή του και δοξάστηκε σαν λάτρης της εργασίας και της νηστείας» — the venerable Rufus lived in the fourteenth century; he was marked out for his obedience and was glorified as a lover of work and of fasting. The day's line adds where: «εν τη Λαύρα του Κιέβου ασκήσαντος», having lived the ascetic life in the Lavra of Kyiv.

That is all. The page gives no year of birth and none of death, names no abbot he obeyed and no cell of his, tells no incident of his life and no wonder, lists no relic, and prints neither couplet nor hymn. The same 8 April page keeps an Apostle Rufus of the Seventy as a separate entry; nothing on either page joins the two, and the four other Rufuses the corpus holds are all martyrs of the first centuries.

**The Russian church calendar keeps him on the Julian 8 April**, the day the Greek keeps him on, and its line is «Прп. Руфа, затворника Печерского (XIV).» — the rank, the forename, the grade and the century in a Roman numeral, which is the fourteenth century this folder already holds. His name link, page 1888, is titled «Преподобный Руф, затворник Печерский» and lists a second day, 28 августа with the mark «(Печер.(Д))», the synaxis of the fathers of the **Far** caves — a synaxis and not written into the feast, and the companion of the burial place his own life gives.

**The name page carries two readings of him and they are four words apart.** One says he struggled in the Kyiv Caves monastery in the fourteenth century, that he was marked out for his obedience and is glorified as a model of fasters and of lovers of labour, and that he was buried «в Дальних пещерах»; the other says the same in the same words with the subject dropped and the caves named «в Дальних (Феодосиевых) пещерах», the Far or Theodosian caves. That is the whole of both. **It adds nothing to the Greek account above** — the century, the obedience, the fasting and the labour are all there — except the burial place, which is the one new fact of the prose, and the comparison the Greek page did not draw: that he is held up as a pattern to others and not only praised.

**And the hymns give him a by-name no prose page on any of the four calendars does.** Both are headed «преподобного Руфа послушливого, затворника Печерского, в Дальних пещерах» — Rufus **the Obedient** — so the thing the lives report of him as a quality is in the service a name. The by-name is not stored: the name link that gives the form is titled without it, and «послушливый» is in the hymn headings only, which is where a reader can now find it. The troparion plays on the word for obedience being called life, and the kontakion opens on his death being honourable before the Lord and closes by calling him a pattern and a teacher to the obedient. `types` keeps the venerable and the monk it had; the grade «затворник», recluse, which both the line and the name page give him, is recorded and not added.
