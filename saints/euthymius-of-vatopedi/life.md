# Euthymius of Vatopedi, with twelve martyrs

Doxologia.ro's calendar for 4 ianuarie names him and gives no life. The line is «Cuviosul Mucenic Eftimie și cei doisprezece Cuvioși Mucenici de la Mănăstirea Vatoped» — the venerable-martyr Euthymius and the twelve venerable-martyrs of the monastery of Vatopedi — and that is the whole of what the calendar says.

The page the calendar links carries no life and no year. What it offers instead are photographs and articles about the monastery of Vatopedi on the Holy Mountain and about the chapel of the Vatopedi martyrs there. Neither the twelve who suffered with him, nor the occasion of their suffering, nor the emperor under whom they suffered, is named on the page; and nothing has been supplied here from anywhere else.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 4 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/4-ianuarie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/cuviosul-mucenic-eftimie-cei-doisprezece-cuviosi-mucenici-de-la-manastirea-vatoped); read 19 September 2026.*
