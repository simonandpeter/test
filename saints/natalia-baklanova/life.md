# Natalia (Baklanova)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names her in half a line for 18 марта ст. ст.: «Сщмч. Димитрия пресвитера, прмц. Наталии.» The line declines the forename to «Наталии», gives no surname, no place and no year whatever, not even in brackets. The rank word «прмц.» — преподобномученица, which on this calendar is the word for a woman under monastic vows who died a martyr — is the only thing it says about her besides the name.

Behind that «Наталии» stands the calendar's own name page 14238, «Наталия (Бакланова), прмц.», which carries the surname the day's line leaves out and nothing else at all. It lists two days: this 18 марта and «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. That second day is the only thing in the whole entry that places her in the twentieth century, since neither the line nor the page gives a year. Both pages carry no life.

The corpus was searched for the surname Бакланова and holds no folder of it. No monastery, no house and no grade is named, so this folder writes no office and no date, and azbyka.ru was not read for her. So what the calendar prints is the whole of what this folder says: a woman of the Russian church under monastic vows, who died a martyr, and whom this calendar keeps on this day and in the synaxis of the new martyrs.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 18 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260318.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14238.html), which carries no life; read 8 October 2026.*
