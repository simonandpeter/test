# Akepsimas (29 January)

saint.gr's calendar for 29 January names Akepsimas and gives no life. The whole of what his page says of him is one sentence: that he lived as an ascetic and died in peace. There is no homeland here, no century, no year, no monastery, no manner of repose, and neither apolytikion nor kontakion.

What the page adds is a note about who he may be, and it is a question rather than an answer: Homer Eustratiades held that this is the martyr Akepsimas who is kept together with the martyrs Isidore and Leo on 7 December. That is the page's own remark, and it is not acted on here — the day, the name and the one sentence of life are what the Greek calendar records on this day, and a second commemoration on the same calendar is two commemorations until something settles it otherwise. The corpus's other Akepsimas, whom the Romanian calendar keeps on 3 November as a hieromartyr of Persia, is neither this entry nor the December one.

Above the sentence stands a couplet: «Aκεψιμάς την σάρκα χαίρων εξέδυ, / H νεκρικώς σταλείσα την γην εισέδυ» — Akepsimas put off the flesh rejoicing, and it, sent out as a corpse, went down into the earth.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 29 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/29/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3552/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
