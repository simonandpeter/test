# Arsacius (12 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 12 Νοεμβρίου names Arsacius and gives him four words: «Ο Άγιος Αρσάκιος μαρτύρησε δια ξίφους» — the holy Arsacius was martyred by the sword. That is the whole of the notice. There is no country on the page, no century, no emperor, no governor, no charge, no companions, no burial, no year and no hymn.

What stands beside the sentence is the couplet of the synaxarion, which has him glad at the sight of the blade: «Ἔχαιρεν Ἀρσάκιος εἰς ξίφος βλέπων, / Σὺν τῷ ξίφει γάρ, ἦν βλέπων καὶ τὸ στέφος» — Arsacius rejoiced looking at the sword, for along with the sword he was looking at the crown too.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3015/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
