# Demetrius (Blagoveshchensky)

The calendar’s own line is the whole of what is recorded of him here. The Сретенский monastery’s calendar for 2 декабря ст. ст. puts him in one clause with a woman confessor and one year for the two of them: «Сщмч. Димитрия пресвитера и прп. Веры исповедницы (1932).»

The name page behind his place gives the surname the line leaves out with his grade: «Димитрий (Благовещенский), иерей, сщмч.» — a priest and a hieromartyr. There is no life behind it, and the day page carries no hymn under his name. The line pairs him with [Vera (Grafova)](/saints/vera-grafova), and that pairing and the shared year are the only things either page says about the two of them together: nothing read makes them companions in a single arrest, a single prison or a single sentence, and this folder claims no more than the line does.

So nothing is said here of where he was born, where he served, when or by whom he was arrested, or how and where he died.

His name page lists one other day, «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church. One row per church is the schema, so that day is recorded here and he does not render on it.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 2 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261202.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14199.html); read 7 October 2026.*
