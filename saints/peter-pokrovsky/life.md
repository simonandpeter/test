# Peter (Pokrovsky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him first among the hieromartyrs of its last line for 17 декабря and gives no life. The line is «Сщмчч. Петра и Иоанна пресвитеров (1937).» — the hieromartyr presbyters Peter and John, 1937 — and the calendar’s own name page adds the surname the line leaves out: «Петр (Покровский), иерей, сщмч.»

That is the whole of the record. He was a priest; he died in 1937, in the years the Russian church counts its new martyrs; and the calendar keeps him on this day beside [John (Zemlyanoy)](/saints/john-zemlyanoy), the other presbyter of his line. No life page stands behind the line for either of them, so nothing is said here of where he served or at whose hands he died. The day page carries three hymns, and all of them belong to the prophet of its first line and to the three youths with him.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 17 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261217.html); read 13 October 2026.*
