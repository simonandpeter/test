# Alexander (Adrianov)

days.pravoslavie.ru gives him one line and no life page at all; the Russian church calendar at azbyka.ru prints the whole of what is recorded. He was priest and rector of the church of the Three Hierarchs at the Nizhne-Turinsky works in the diocese of Ekaterinburg, and a zealous and worthy pastor: a teacher of the faith, a builder of churches, a member of various committees and societies, whose whole life was filled with the service of his flock and with an active love of those near him.

In the autumn of 1918 the Middle Urals were being fought over by the White and the Red armies. The Nizhne-Turinsky works and the country round it were held by the Bolsheviks, who dealt cruelly with the local people and with the clergy among them. Several priests were killed, and most of those who were left abandoned the places where they served. He was the only one who went on serving his own parish, keeping every appointed service and preaching.

On 8/21 November he was arrested in the church itself, during a festal service, and taken to the station at Выя, which was not far from the works. On 12/25 November he was shot. A former Red Army soldier who took part in the shooting testified afterwards that as the priest was led out he kept repeating «Господи помилуй, Господи помилуй меня, грешного!», quietly, half under his breath. His body was found five days later, lightly covered with snow, with his hands crossed on his breast.

On 7 March 2018 the Holy Synod of the Russian Orthodox Church entered him in the menologion and appointed his commemoration for 25 November — the day he was shot, and the day both Russian calendars keep him on. The day page carries hymns for four of the saints it heads and none under his name, so this folder has none.
