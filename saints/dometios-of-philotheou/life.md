# Dometios of Philotheou

saint.gr's calendar for 7 August names the venerable Dometios the sign-bearer, and gives him one sentence of his own: he kept the ascetic life within the bounds of the monastery of Philotheou, and was perfected in peace. It then borrows another man's life to say more. With him, the page says, lived in stillness the venerable martyr Damian the New, who died in 1568 and whom the same calendar keeps on 14 February.

Damian's biographer is quoted for it: seeking a greater virtue, Damian left the monastery and came to one of those who kept the ascetic life alone, a sign-bearing man, Dometios by name, with whom he spent nearly three years, worn down with fasting and vigil and the rest of the hard discipline. The page offers Dometios no century of his own, no country and no year, and it prints no hymn to him; what it shows is a portable icon of him in the monastery of Philotheou, of 1845.
