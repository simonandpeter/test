# Dionysius (8 May)

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life. Its whole account is one sentence: the holy hieromartyr Dionysius was a bishop, and he was martyred at Vienne after the year 193. The page's head line writes the year as «(; - 193)», and the sentence itself says «μετά το 193», after 193, so the year is a floor and not a date.

The page does not say of what city he was bishop, and it should not be read as saying Vienne: it says only that he suffered there. It names no emperor, no judge, no accusation and no manner of death; it gives no relics and no burial place, and prints neither apolytikion nor kontakion. The many Dionysii this corpus already keeps are other men, on other days and in other places, and none of them is joined to this one by anything on the page.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1537/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
