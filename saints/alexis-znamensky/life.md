# Alexis (Znamensky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Алексий on 20 July and gives no life. Its line is «Сщмч. Алексия пресвитера (1938).» and the calendar's own name page for him adds the surname and the grade: «Алексий (Знаменский), протоиерей, сщмч.»

That is the whole of the record. He was an archpriest; he died in 1938; the calendar keeps him on this day among the new martyrs of Russia. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read, and a surname is not a biography.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 20 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260720.html); read 9 October 2026.*
