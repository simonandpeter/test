# Gregory (Pospelov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him in a single line for 13 марта ст. ст.: «Сщмч. Григория пресвитера (1921).» The line declines the forename to «Григория», gives no surname, no place, no year of birth and no word of narrative; the year it closes with, 1921, is the only year it gives, and the rank word «Сщмч.» that governs the name is the only rank.

Behind that «Григория» stands the calendar's own name page 14237, which carries the fuller form «Григорий (Поспелов), протоиерей, сщмч.» — the surname the day's line leaves out, and an archpriest's grade, which is a step above the «пресвитера» of the line. That page lists three days: this 13 марта, «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and «8 июня (Петерб.)», a local synaxis of the saints of Saint Petersburg. The second of those places his memory in the north, and it is the only hint of a place the calendar gives.

The name page carries no life, and neither does the day page; azbyka.ru, which has lives for some of the martyrs of these years, was not read for him. So what the calendar prints is the whole of what this folder says: an archpriest of the Russian church of that name, who died a martyr in 1921 and whom this calendar keeps on this day.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 13 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260313.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14237.html), which carries no life; read 7 October 2026.*
