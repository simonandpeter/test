# Serapion of Pskov

The Greek synaxarion keeps Serapion of Pskov on 7 September and again on 15 May, and says it has no information about his life. This entry records the line and nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 7 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/1607/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
