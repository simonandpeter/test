# Thomas the Fool for Christ

saint.gr's whole account of him is one sentence: «Ο Όσιος Θωμάς, ο διά Χριστόν σαλός, έζησε κατά τον 6ο αιώνα μ.Χ. και κοιμήθηκε με ειρήνη» — the venerable Thomas, the fool for Christ's sake, lived in the sixth century and fell asleep in peace. The calendar's line for the day gives the same two facts and no third.

So the only thing the page tells about the folly is that he practised it. Where he did so, in what city and among what people, what he did and what was said of him, and whether anybody wrote the story down, it does not say; and it records no relic, prints no couplet and no hymn, and gives him no year within the century. This folder keeps what the calendar keeps and adds nothing to it.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/24/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1465/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
