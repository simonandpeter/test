# John of the Well

John was born in the years of the Church's persecutions by the idolaters, and his devout mother, saint.gr says, gave him the waters of the Christian religion to drink. Later he withdrew to the desert and gave himself to study and to spiritual ascesis; but the place he lived in was a very small cave, and so the devout hermit was driven to use a dry well for his cell. That is how he got the name he is kept under: John in the well.

After a time others came to that place too, and built themselves small cells there. John would not leave the dry well that so many years of his life were bound up with, but he kept in touch with the newcomers and served them as a precious counsellor and guide, so that the little brotherhood was formed and carried forward in Christ by him. He died peacefully in deep old age.

The page gives no year for any of it, no country, no name for the desert or the brotherhood, no relic and no hymn; the folder therefore records no date. Its couplet is on the two wells: «Ὁ ἐν φρέατι ζῶν Ἰωάννης κάτω, Ἄνω πίνει νῦν ζῶντος ὕδατος φρέαρ» — John, who lived below in a well, now drinks above the well of living water.

The Russian church calendar prints «Прп. Иоанна пустынника (IV)» on 29 марта ст. ст., one day before the Greek day, and its life is the same man told at length: born in a time of persecution, taught his prayers by a devout mother, and known to the end by the dry well he lived in. THE TWO WERE READ AS ONE ON THE WELL AND NOT ON THE DAY, which is a day out, and the Russian source adds a country the Greek page does not give — its name page heads him «Иоанн Египетский, пустынник», the hermit, and so names him for that country. THE CORPUS HOLDS ANOTHER MAN UNDER THE ENGLISH OF THAT EPITHET and he is not this one: a confessor beheaded with forty others in 295, whom the Greek calendar keeps on 20 September.

By that account his mother, a widow named «Иулиания», was hiding from the persecution with two small children, and taught them prayer and the reading of the holy books. The boy used to visit a monastery nearby in secret and at some risk, until a devout man he met advised him to look for a more deserted place for his prayers. He went to the hermit «Фармуфий», took his blessing and withdrew into the desert, where he found an empty deep well full of snakes and scorpions; he prayed and threw himself down into it, and was held up unhurt by an angel. At the bottom he spent forty days in prayer with his arms crossed, without food or sleep, until the snakes left the well.

The angel who brought bread to «Фармуфий» began to bring bread for him too, and gave it to the elder rather than to him, so that the young ascetic should not grow proud. The demons took the shape of his mother, his sister and his kinsfolk and came weeping to the mouth of the well one after another, begging him to come out to them; he did not break off his prayer, and at the end of it said, Depart from me, and they vanished. He lived in the well in fasting and unceasing prayer until his death in the fourth century, and a monk named «Хрисихий», thirty years an ascetic in that desert, was brought there by the providence of God to bury him. The night before he died the hermit was at last persuaded to tell «Хрисихий» his whole life, and many wonders happened afterwards at the place of his labours.
