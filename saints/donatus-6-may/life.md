# Donatus (6 May)

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives one sentence: the holy Donatus was martyred, being put to death with arrows. Above it stands a couplet, and it is the only other thing the page has of him — another strange sun is Donatus to me, holding out from his flesh the arrows in the manner of rays, «Ἄλλος ξένος τις ἥλιός μοι Δονάτος, / Σαρκὸς προΐσχων ἀκτίνων δίκην βέλη».

That is the whole of the record. There is no country, no century, no emperor, no place of martyrdom, no relics and no hymn, and the page does not connect him with the Demetrion it names on the same day and kills in the same manner. Nothing is claimed here beyond the name, the rank, the arrows and the day.
