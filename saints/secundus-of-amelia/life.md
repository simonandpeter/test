# Secundus of Amelia

saint.gr's calendar for 1 Ιουνίου names him and gives one sentence. The holy martyr Secundus, it says, contended in the year 304, under the emperor Diocletian, whose reign the page dates 284 to 305, after they threw him into the river Tiber near the city of Amelia in Umbria in Italy.

That is the whole of the source, and the manner of his death is the one thing it is exact about. It gives him no birthplace, no age, no trade, no rank in the Church and no judge; it names no companion, records no wonder, and prints neither hymn nor epigram for him.
