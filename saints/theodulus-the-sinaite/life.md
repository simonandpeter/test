# Theodulus the Sinaite

saint.gr's calendar for 17 Μαρτίου names a hieromartyr Theodulus the Sinaite, and gives him one sentence. It says he was a brother of the monastery of Sinai and served at that monastery's dependency, at St George of Pyrgotos in Cyprus, and that he was perfected in martyrdom by the Turks in the year 1822. The page heads him «Αγιος Θεοδουλος ο Ιερομαρτυρας ο Σιναϊτης (; - 1822)», so the year of his birth is a question mark there too.

That is all that has been read. saint.gr gives no account of his arrest, no place of death beyond the Cypriot dependency, no persecutor by name and no relics, and prints neither a couplet nor a hymn under his name. Nothing has been added here from any other source and nothing from memory. Several other Theoduli are kept on other days and in other calendars, and nothing on this page identifies him with any of them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 17 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/17/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3916/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
