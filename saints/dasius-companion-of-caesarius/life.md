# Dasius, companion of Caesarius

saint.gr's calendar for 1 Νοεμβρίου keeps seven martyrs in one line: «Άγιοι Καισάριος, Δάσιος, και άλλοι πέντε Μάρτυρες, Σάββας, Σαβινιανός, Αγρίππας, Αδριανός και Θωμάς το νήπιο». Each of the seven has a heading of his own on the page and each is called ο Μάρτυρας, the last of them named only as [Thomas the infant](/saints/thomas-the-infant). Beyond the names the whole account is two sentences long.

They were arrested at Damascus, the page says, and punished with various torments so that they should deny Christ; but because they did not deny their Christian faith, all of them received the crown of martyrdom by beheading. There is no year, no emperor and no governor named, and nothing of any one of them apart from the rest — not a trade, not an age, not a city of origin.

The page sets a single epigram over the company, and it names only [Caesarius](/saints/caesarius-of-damascus): with six fellow contenders, friends of the truth, the friends of falsehood cut Caesarius down. For that reason Dasius and the others — [Sabbas](/saints/sabbas-companion-of-caesarius), [Sabinianus](/saints/sabinianus-companion-of-caesarius), [Agrippa](/saints/agrippa-companion-of-caesarius) and [Adrian](/saints/adrian-companion-of-caesarius) — take their English forms from his. No hymn is printed, and the page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου».

The Russian church calendar keeps the company on 1 November too, and its line is shorter than the Greek notice and dated where the Greek is not: «Мчч. Кесария, Дасия и с ними пяти (VII)» — the martyrs Caesarius, Dasius and five with them, in the seventh century. The count agrees with the Greek page's seven exactly, and the century is the first date this folder has held.

He is one of the two men the line names. [Caesarius](/saints/caesarius-of-damascus) is the other, and the remaining five are counted and not named, so only these two take a Russian row and the five the Greek page names are left without one. The index of names gives the two it names a by-name the Greek does not, «Дасий Дамасский» and «Кесарий Дамасский», of Damascus, which is the city the English forms in this corpus already came from. The calendar carries no life page for the seven.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/01/index.aspx) and [the notice of the seven](https://www.saint.gr/2861/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 1 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261101.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/587.html), read 4 October 2026.*
