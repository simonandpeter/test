# Stephen the Presbyter (7 June)

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives him one sentence: «Ο Όσιος Στέφανος ο Πρεσβύτερος απεβίωσε ειρηνικά» — the venerable Stephen the Presbyter died peacefully. That is the whole of the life. The page adds no century, no country, no church and no relics, and the only things it says of what he was are the two rank words in the day's line, «Όσιος» and «Πρεσβύτερος».

What it does print above the sentence is the couplet the synaxaria carry for him, and the couplet plays on his name: «Ἐπώνυμος Στέφανος οὗ φορεῖ στέφους, / ὁ πρακτικὴ χεὶρ ἀρετῶν οἶδε πλέκειν» — Stephen is named after the crown he wears, the crown that the practising hand knows how to plait out of the virtues. No hymn is printed for him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/498/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
