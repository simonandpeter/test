# Therinos

saint.gr names three martyrs together for this day and gives one sentence for all of them: the holy martyrs Danax, [Mesiros](/saints/mesiros) and Therinos — or Therianos, for the page prints both forms of his name in the same sentence — were martyred by the sword, and their memory used to be kept at their martyrion at the Deuteron in Constantinople.

He stands first of the three in the page's own head lines, and the couplet set over them names only his companion: both contenders having had their heads struck off, Mesiros lies in the middle without a head. There is no country, no century, no emperor, no persecution named, no relics and no hymn. Nothing is claimed for him here beyond the name in the two forms the page gives it, the rank, the sword and the day.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/06/index.aspx) and [the life of the three](https://www.saint.gr/1518/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
