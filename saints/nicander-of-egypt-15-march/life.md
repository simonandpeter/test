# Nicander of Egypt (15 March)

Nicander lived in the years of Diocletian. A warm zealot for the faith, saint.gr says, he admired with his whole soul those who poured out their blood for it; and as often as he learned of the death of martyrs he would go to the place, take up their holy relics and bury them, watering them with his tears. In the doing of that sacred work, in the year 305, he was arrested and tried.

Because he stayed faithful to his confession of Christ he was condemned to the harshest death. By the tyrant's order they flayed off his skin and then burned it with lighted torches. The two lines the page keeps for him say the same: the cooks of error laid hands on Nicander and skinned him like a lamb. There is no hymn, no relic and no place in Egypt named beyond the epithet of the line itself.

The corpus keeps another Nicander of Egypt, whom the Romanian calendar has on 5 June among ten martyrs tormented by the ruler of Egypt with hunger, thirst and cold; that is a different account on a different day, and this folder is the man the Greek calendar sets on 15 March, for burying the martyrs of Diocletian's last year.
