# Demetrius (Klepinin)

saint.gr keeps Demetrius Klepinin among the Russian saints who ended their lives in France, a synaxis the Ecumenical Patriarchate appointed for 20 July, and the page says of its members that they are in the main new martyrs of the Nazi concentration camps. His own notice is one sentence long: Saint Demetrius Klepinin died in the camp of Dora, because he had tried to save the lives of dozens of Jews from the Nazis by putting into operation a system of forged certificates of baptism.

That is all the page gives of him. It prints no year of his birth or his death, no place but the camp, no word of where he served as a priest, and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 20 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/20/index.aspx) and [the synaxis](https://www.saint.gr/4381/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
