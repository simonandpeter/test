# Alexandra of Ancyra

Alexandra was one of the seven virgins of Ancyra whom the Romanian calendar keeps on 18 mai with Christina, and whose names the calendar itself does not print. They are read out of the life of [Theodotus of Ancyra](/saints/theodotus-of-ancyra) that doxologia prints for the same day, which names them [Thecusa](/saints/thecusa-of-ancyra), Alexandra, [Claudia](/saints/claudia-of-ancyra), [Phaina](/saints/phaina-of-ancyra), [Euphrasia](/saints/euphrasia-of-ancyra), [Matrona](/saints/matrona-of-ancyra) and [Julia](/saints/julia-of-ancyra), and says that these holy virgins suffered on the eighteenth day of the month of May. The life is signed by Saint Nilus, who says he lived with Theodotus and was with him in prison, and writes only what he saw.

In the city, he writes, were seven virgins who from their youth had been strong in sober wisdom and in the fear of God, and had betrothed themselves in maiden purity to Christ, the incorruptible and deathless Bridegroom; and by the time of the persecution they had grown old. The eldest of them was the blessed Thecusa, the aunt of Theodotus. When the governor Theotecnus came to the borders of Ancyra the churches emptied at once, and the hills were full of people who had fled.

He seized the seven and put them to many torments and could not force them to the idolatry. Then he gave them over to some debauched young men to be defiled, mocking the Christian faith. The virgins sighed from the bottom of their hearts and lifted their eyes and prayed: Lord Jesus Christ, as long as it was in our power to keep our virginity clean, Thou knowest with what watching and what care we have kept it unstained until today. Thecusa caught the boldest of the youths by the feet, threw the covering from her head and showed him her grey hairs, and asked him to be ashamed before them and to think of his own mother; and the young men were pricked to the heart, left off their desire, wept, and went away without harming them.

They were drowned afterwards in the lake, with stones at their necks, for refusing to serve the idols at the washing of them. Theodotus, with Polychronius and Theocharides, went out at night to take up their bodies; Thecusa appeared to him in a vision and reproached his sleeping; the soldiers set to watch the lake fled at the sight of an armed man shining like fire, whom the life names as the martyr Sosander, and the wind drove the water to the far bank so that the bodies were seen. They cut the ropes from their necks with sickles, carried them away in carts and buried them beside the church of the holy patriarchs.

In the morning the theft was known, Christians were seized and examined, and Polychronius betrayed the burial. The pagans dug up the relics of the seven and burned them, and Theodotus gave himself up and was beheaded. The life records no year for any of them, and none is recorded here.

The Greek calendar keeps her on the same civil day, in the same company, and adds what they were doing when they were denounced. saint.gr says they came from Ancyra in Galatia, gave themselves wholeheartedly to the service of the Gospel, and were among the greatest servants and contenders of the Christian faith; always most ready in works of mercy and charity, they worked together at drawing idolatrous women into the bosom of the Church. They were denounced for that work and refused to sacrifice to the idols, and Theoteknos, the governor of Ancyra, handed them over to the soldiers to be corrupted; because by God's grace they were kept pure, all of them were drowned in the depths of the lake there. saint.gr spells her name Alexandria where the Romanian line gave Alexandra; the day, the company and the place are the same, and the Greek form is recorded here as the page prints it.

The Russian calendar keeps her a second time, and on a day neither of the other two does: days.pravoslavie.ru's line for 6 ноября ст. ст. is «Мцц. Текусы , Александры , Полактии , Клавдии , Евфросинии , Афанасии и Матроны (III).» — seven names in the genitive, the class «Мцц.» for all seven together, and a century. The name link behind «Александры» gives the calendar's own nominative, «Александра Анкирская (Коринфская), дева, мц.», and her name page lists two days, «18 мая , 6 ноября», with the life of Theodotus behind it: the same account the Greek and Romanian pages gave this folder, told again and at greater length.

**The line and the life do not print the same seven names.** The line's third and sixth are «Полактия» and «Афанасия», where the life names [Phaina](/saints/phaina-of-ancyra) and [Julia](/saints/julia-of-ancyra); and the calendar gives those two their own name pages, 2647 and 2419, each carrying one day, 6 ноября, and no life link at all. So for five of the seven the source itself ties this line to this company — a name page with both days and the company's life behind it — and for two of them it ties nothing. Those two were refused: no Russian row was written for Phaina or for Julia, because identifying «Полактия» with «Фаина» and «Афанасия» with «Иулия» is a reading this page nowhere makes. The two names are reported instead, and this folder's company is left as it stood.

What the Russian life adds to the account already here is mostly circumstance. «Феотекн», appointed to Ancyra in the persecution of Diocletian (284–305), was known for his cruelty before he came; the city emptied, the famine came, and Theodotus kept the homeless in his inn and the Liturgy was served there when the churches were shut. The seven had given themselves to God from their youth in prayer, fasting and good works, and all of them had reached old age. After the youths left them untouched the governor ordered them to take part in the feast of the washing of the idols as the pagan priestesses did, and for refusing it they were sentenced: a heavy stone was tied at each one's neck and all seven were drowned in the lake.

Thecusa appeared to Theodotus in a dream the next night and asked that the bodies be taken up and buried as Christians. He went to the lake with «Полихроний» and others; it was dark and a burning lamp showed the way; the martyr «Сосандр» appeared to the guard the pagans had set on the shore and they fled, and the wind drove the water over to the far side so that the bodies could be reached and carried to the church. When the theft was known «Полихроний» broke under torture and named Theodotus, who was beheaded for it. The page closes by naming the author: «Нил», a contemporary and fellow-struggler of Theodotus and an eyewitness of his death.

Two things on these pages are left as they stand. Five of the seven name pages head her «Анкирская (Коринфская)», of Ancyra and in the same breath of Corinth, and nothing on any of them explains the second epithet; the lives put the company at Ancyra in Galatia and drown them in the lake there. And the day line's «(III)» does not agree with its own life's «начале IV века»; both are in this folder's date and neither is chosen.
