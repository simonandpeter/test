# Joseph (Bespalov), with thirty-seven martyrs

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him on 5 января old style, and it names him at the head of a company: «Мч. Иосифа и с ним 37-ми мучеников (1921)» — the martyr Joseph and with him thirty-seven martyrs, 1921. The name link behind the line gives the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line itself omits, «Иосиф (Беспалов), мч.». Of the thirty-seven it gives no names at all, and so they are not kept here as thirty-seven entries: the company is named in his, as the calendar names it.

Beyond that the calendar prints nothing. It holds no life page for him, and no hymn stands under his name; the hymns on that day are the eve of Theophany's. What can honestly be said is that a martyr named Joseph Bespalov and thirty-seven with him are kept on this day, and that the year given for their death is 1921. Where they died, and who they were, the calendar does not say, and nothing has been supplied here from elsewhere.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 5 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260105.html); read 3 October 2026.*
