# Nicholas (Shirogorov)

The Russian calendar names the hieromartyr Nicholas (Shirogorov), deacon on 11 September of the old style. The calendar links no written life, so this entry records the line and nothing more.

*After the Православный церковный календарь (days.pravoslavie.ru), [11 сентября ст. ст.](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260911.html); read 30 August 2026.*
