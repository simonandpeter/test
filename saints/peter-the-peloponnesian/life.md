# Peter the Peloponnesian

Peter came from Tripoli in the Peloponnese. saint.gr's account of him is short and admits its own gap: it does not know for what reasons he was seized by the Turks at Odemiş in Asia Minor in 1776, only that he was, and that when he was pressed to embrace the Koran he stood unshaken in the faith of his fathers. For that reason they hanged him, and so he received the crown of martyrdom.

The page's epigram says the same thing in two lines: founded upon the rock of faith, blessed Peter, you went up from earth to heaven by the noose. Nothing else about his trade, his age or his burial is printed there, and nothing is added here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/8/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
