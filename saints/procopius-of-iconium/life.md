# Procopius, Metropolitan of Iconium, Hieromartyr (1923)

Procopius Lazaridis was metropolitan of Iconium from 1911 to 1923, in particular conditions: the Young Turks were trying to organise a Turkish-Orthodox Church independent of the Patriarchate, using only the Turkish language, and pressed priests to join the effort, but failed. In Anatolia especially, in the depths of Asia Minor, they had trapped the Orthodox and subjected them to fearful ill-treatment until they should break from the Ecumenical Patriarchate; three hierarchs in particular, headed by the metropolitan of Iconium Procopius, they tried to force into an arbitrary and uncanonical consecration of a bishop, so that their relations with the Patriarchate should be broken and they be emancipated arbitrarily as a self-standing Turkish-Orthodox synod. In the midst of this oppression of his flock and his fellow-shepherds, and of the desperate weeping of the people, Procopius gave up his spirit to the great High Priest and Saviour Christ, fighting for and suffering with the groaning Greek Christians of Anatolia. The Greek calendar keeps him with [Chrysostom of Smyrna](/saints/chrysostom-of-smyrna) on the Sunday before the Exaltation.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 13 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2408/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
