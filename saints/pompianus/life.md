# Pompianus

saint.gr's calendar for 22 Ιουνίου names him and gives no life. The whole of its account is one sentence, «Ο Άγιος Πομπιανός μαρτύρησε αφού τον έριξαν μέσα στη θάλασσα και πνίγηκε» — the holy Pompianus was martyred after they threw him into the sea and he was drowned. The page gives no country, no city, no century, no year, no emperor and no judge, and it prints no hymn under his line.

Besides that sentence it carries only the couplet the synaxaria print for him: «Ὁ Πομπιανὸς ἐκτελεῖ πομπὴν ξένην, / Ἐκ τῆς θαλάσσης πρὸς τὰ τοῦ πόλου πλάτη» — Pompianus makes a strange procession, out of the sea towards the wide spaces of heaven, a play on his name and the Greek word for a procession. That is everything the source says of him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 22 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/22/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/613/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
