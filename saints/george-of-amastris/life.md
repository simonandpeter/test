# George of Amastris

saint.gr says George came from Kromna in the territory of Amastris and was born of devout parents, Theodore and Megetho. When his studies were done he went off to the mountain of Syrike, found an elder living as an ascetic there, was taught the monastic life by him and received the angelic habit from his hands. After his elder's death he moved to the town of Bonysa in Acarnania and practised his asceticism there.

When the bishop of Amastris fell asleep and the see was left empty, the Church, rewarding his virtues and his gifts, appointed him shepherd of the church of Amastris, which he shepherded in a manner pleasing to God; his consecration as a bishop took place at Constantinople. He served the Church, cared for the clergy, and gave himself to the protection of orphans and widows and to the feeding and keeping of the poor and their release from debt; and God, seeing how he lived, counted him worthy of the gift of wonderworking. He fell asleep in peace in the year 805, and the page adds that his memory is kept again on 25 October.
