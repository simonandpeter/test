# George of Amastris

saint.gr says George came from Kromna in the territory of Amastris and was born of devout parents, Theodore and Megetho. When his studies were done he went off to the mountain of Syrike, found an elder living as an ascetic there, was taught the monastic life by him and received the angelic habit from his hands. After his elder's death he moved to the town of Bonysa in Acarnania and practised his asceticism there.

When the bishop of Amastris fell asleep and the see was left empty, the Church, rewarding his virtues and his gifts, appointed him shepherd of the church of Amastris, which he shepherded in a manner pleasing to God; his consecration as a bishop took place at Constantinople. He served the Church, cared for the clergy, and gave himself to the protection of orphans and widows and to the feeding and keeping of the poor and their release from debt; and God, seeing how he lived, counted him worthy of the gift of wonderworking. He fell asleep in peace in the year 805, and the page adds that his memory is kept again on 25 October.

The Russian calendar keeps him on the same day and tells the whole of his course. He came from the town of Kromna, near Amastris on the Black Sea, and his devout and well-born parents gave him a good education in both sacred and secular learning. He withdrew to the Sirikian mountains, took the monastic habit and led a hard ascetic life under the direction of a hermit; and after the elder's death he settled in a monastic house at Bonissa and went on with his labours there.

When the bishop of Amastris died, the clergy and the people chose him, and he was ordained at Constantinople by the archbishop [Tarasius](/saints/tarasius-of-constantinople), whose years it gives as 784 to 806. Coming to Amastris he taught his flock untiringly, took care for the adorning of the churches, was a defender of widows and orphans and a feeder of the poor, and showed everyone the pattern of a life pleasing to God. By the power of his prayer he drove off from Amastris the Saracens who were laying its country waste, and he saved from death some merchants of Amastris unjustly condemned in the city of Trebizond. He died in peace, on 3 марта, among his own flock, in the reign of the emperor Nicephorus I, 802 to 811.
