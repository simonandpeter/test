# Theodore, founder of Chora

saint.gr's notice for him is three sentences long. The venerable Theodore was born in the year 477 at Constantinople and was a kinsman of the emperor Justinian. About the year 529, accompanied by two of his disciples, Theoplastus and Timothy, he went to Jerusalem to venerate the All-holy Sepulchre. He came back to Constantinople and built the monastery of Chora, with two chapels dedicated to Saint Anthimus of Nicomedia and to the Holy Forty Martyrs of Sebaste.

That is the whole of it. The day's line calls him the builder and hegumen of that monastery. The page gives no year for his death, no account of his rule there and no hymn, and nothing is supplied here.
