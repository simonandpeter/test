# Peter (Kuznetsov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names a Peter on 7 июня and gives no life of him. Its line for him is the last of the day and the whole of what it says: «Сщмч. Петра пресвитеров (1919).» — the hieromartyr Peter, and the year 1919. So what the calendar tells is that he was a priest who was counted among the martyrs and that he died in 1919, a year later than the fourteen men the line before his names together.

His own name page on that site, 9770, carries nothing but the heading «Петр (Кузнецов), иерей, сщмч.», which gives the surname the day's line leaves out and grades him иерей, a priest, and the two days «26 января (Новомуч.) , 7 июня». There is no life page for him on the site, no icon and no article, and no page names a parish, a diocese or a place.

The line's rank word is in the plural, «пресвитеров», over one forename and one name link; that is the page's own slip and it is quoted here as it is printed rather than corrected. The 26 января commemoration of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia was not read, and nothing of it is in this folder.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 7 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260607.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9770.html); read 8 October 2026.*
