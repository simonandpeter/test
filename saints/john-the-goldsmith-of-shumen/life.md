# John the Goldsmith of Shumen

This new martyr came from the town of Shumen in Bulgaria and followed the trade of a goldsmith. He was handsome to look at, saint.gr says, and virtuous. Opposite his workshop lived a Turkish family with a young daughter, who tried to draw the eighteen-year-old John to herself; and when she could not, she slandered him, saying that he had attempted to rape her.

The martyr was brought before the judge, and by the sentence issued he had either to turn Turk and marry the girl or be beaten to death. John answered that he would rather die than deny Christ. The tortures that followed were frightful and barbarous: they beat him without mercy, tore his body, burnt his wounds with lighted torches, and at last beheaded him, on 14 May 1802. His martyrdom was written by the hieromonk Nikephoros of Chios; the page's head line gives his years as 1784 to 1802.
