# Basilissa of Sirmium

Basilissa suffered for Christ at Sirmium, in Srem. That is the whole of the Prologue’s notice, which the Serbian calendar keeps on 29 August (Julian); this entry records it and nothing more.

*After the Охридски пролог for 11 September 2026 (29. август ст. ст.), as printed by the Православни подсетник (pravoslavno.rs) — [the day’s page](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-09-11&prolog=1); read 23 August 2026.*
