# Panteleimon (3 January)

The Greek calendar keeps on 3 January a venerable Panteleimon who is not the physician and great-martyr of Nicomedia, and its notice of him is one sentence: he founded the monastery the page calls Κόστυτσεβ in Russia, lived the ascetic life there in a manner pleasing to God, and fell asleep in peace in 1884.

That is the whole of the source. saint.gr prints no birthplace, no baptismal name, no year of birth and no account of the founding, and it gives the monastery's name only in Greek letters, so no Russian or English form of that place is supplied here. Beside the line stands a reading of the name — πᾶς and ἐλεήμων, the man merciful and kind to all.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/03/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1275/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
