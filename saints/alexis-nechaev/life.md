# Alexis (Nechaev)

Alexis was born in 1875 in the village of Rameshki in the province of Tver, the son of a priest. He was ordained after finishing the seminary, and through the persecution of the twenties served in one of the churches of the Tver diocese. In 1929 the authorities demanded of him a tax fixed arbitrarily and so large that he could not have paid it had he sold everything he owned; for not paying it he was sentenced on 31 October 1929 to three years and sent to the Nizhny Novgorod factory colony.

He came back and served in one of the churches of Staritsa. On 20 November 1937 he was arrested and put in the Rzhev prison, and two days later the investigator questioned him. He held himself so that it was clear he would not bear false witness in any circumstances, and the investigator had to content himself with a formal record of questions and answers: «Я, Нечаев, в предъявленном мне обвинении виновным себя не признаю», and, pressed, that he said a second time that he had carried on no agitation at all.

Having nothing of his own to allege, the investigator worked round to the one thing he knew — the conviction of 1929 for unpaid dues — which the priest admitted; and to the question what he would add, he answered that he could add nothing more. The investigation closed that same day. Three days later the troika sentenced him to be shot, and after seven days in the prison he was shot on 27 November 1937. He was canonised for the whole church in August 2000.

The day's line names twenty-seven new martyrs of 1937 together — twenty-three presbyters, a deacon, a hieromonk, a lay martyr and a woman — and the calendar carries a life page for ten of them, of whom he is one. The men of that line were tried separately, in different districts and different prisons, and no one of these lives names another of them, so no relation is recorded between them here.
