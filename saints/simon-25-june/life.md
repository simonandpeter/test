# Simon (25 June)

saint.gr's calendar for 25 Ιουνίου names him and gives almost no life. Its line reads «Όσιος Σίμων», and the page behind it carries two sentences: that works of this ascetic on humility are to be found in the Evergetinos, and that he died in peace. No country is named, no century, no monastery and no year, and the page prints no hymn.

What stands above those two sentences is a couplet of the synaxarion: «Σορός Σίμωνι σαρκός εστιν εστία, Πόλος δε τούτω πνεύματος κατοικία» — for Simon the coffin is the hearth of the flesh, and the vault of heaven the dwelling of his spirit. The page glosses his name as a Hebrew word, the man obedient to God and kind to his fellows. That is the whole of what is printed.
