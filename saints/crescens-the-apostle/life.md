# Crescens

The Apostle Paul names Crescens in the second letter to Timothy — the letter, the Romanian life notes in passing, that was composed in Greek — saying: Crescens in Galatia, sent by me to the preaching. He was bishop in Galatia, and afterwards preached Christ in Gaul. At Vienne, which the life calls a city of Germany, he set his own disciple Zacharias as bishop; then he returned to Galatia, and there ended his life as a martyr in the reign of the emperor Trajan.

That is the whole of what doxologia.ro prints of him, and it gives no year, only the reign. The Romanian calendar keeps him on 30 iulie together with Silas, Silvanus, Epaenetus and Andronicus, and the site gives one life for the five and a common troparion and kontakion for them all.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 30 July, and in the same company. saint.gr says that Silas, Silouanos, Epaenetus, Crescens and Andronicus were five of the seventy disciples of the Lord, and that all of them served the Gospel of Christ «ἐν κόπῳ καὶ μόχθω, ἐν ἀγρυπνίαις πολλάκις, ἐν λιμῷ καὶ δίψει, ἐν νηστείαις πολλάκις, ἐν ψύχει καὶ γυμνότητι» — in toil and hardship, in watchings often, in hunger and thirst on the long journeys, in fastings, in cold and nakedness — which it takes from the second letter to the Corinthians.

Of Crescens it says, last of the five, that he became bishop of Καρχιδονία and from that place laboured and served the divine Gospel. That is Carthage, and it is not the see the Romanian calendar gives him, which is Galatia; the two pages disagree and neither is corrected here. The synaxarion couplet the page prints for him and Andronicus together says where they are now: «Kρήσκης και Aνδρόνικος οι μύσται Λόγου, / Bλέπουσι νυν πρόσωπον του Θεού Λόγου» — Crescens and Andronicus, the initiates of the Word, now look upon the face of God the Word.
