# Barsimaeus of Edessa

Barsimaeus was the man who catechised and baptised [Sarbelos](/saints/sarbel-the-martyr) and his sister [Bebaia](/saints/bebaia-the-martyr), whom the Greek calendar keeps on this same day. For that he was denounced to the governor Lysias. Standing before him, he confessed his faith in Christ boldly; and for that confession the governor ordered the soldiers to beat him without mercy and to put him in prison.

A little later, when the persecutions briefly ceased, he was set free and returned to his church, glorifying the name of the holy God. Barsimaeus lived the rest of his life in a way pleasing to God, saint.gr says, and fell asleep in peace in the year 114.

The day's line calls him the confessor; the notice beneath it opens by calling him a hieromartyr, although what it then describes is a release and a peaceful death. Both readings are the page's own and stand here as it has them. It gives no year for his birth, and prints neither apolytikion nor kontakion.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 29 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/29/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3550/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
