# Theodore, companion of Stephen of Placidianae

Theodore is the middle name of the three the Greek calendar keeps together on 11 Ιανουαρίου: «Άγιοι Στέφανος ο εν Πλακιδιαναίς, Θεόδωρος και Αγάπιος ο Αρχιμανδρίτης» — [Stephen](/saints/stephen-of-placidianae) who was at Placidianae, Theodore, and [Agapius](/saints/agapius-of-apamea) the archimandrite. Of the three he is the one the line gives nothing to at all: no place, no rank, no epithet.

saint.gr's page for them says why. «Η μόνη πληροφορία που έχουμε είναι ότι ο Όσιος Αγάπιος ασκήτεψε στην Απαμεία της Συρίας. Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τους βίους των Αγίων» — the only information we have is that the venerable Agapius lived the ascetic life at Apamea in Syria; we have no other details of the lives of these saints.

What the page prints besides the names is the couplet over all three: that they stand now before the throne of the Trinity, having left the earth and taken up their dwelling in heaven. It gives no year, and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/11/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1088/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
