# Eugenius (21 July)

saint.gr's entry for him is one sentence and a refusal: Saint Eugenius was martyred by the sword, and we have no other details of his life. Above it stands the synaxarion's distich, which cries well done to the bravery of Eugenius, who stood nobly and was struck on the neck. The calendar's line is only «Άγιος Ευγένιος», with no byname.

So the folder holds a name, a day and a manner of death. The page gives no place, no century, no emperor and no hymn, and what stands above the notice — the site's gloss on the name Eugenius, the well-born — belongs to the name and not to the man.
