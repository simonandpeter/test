# Theodora, sister of Hermes

saint.gr's whole notice for the two of them is two sentences: the holy martyrs [Hermes](/saints/hermes-brother-of-theodora) and Theodora came of a distinguished family of Rome, and they suffered a martyr's death in the year 132, under the emperor Hadrian, who reigned from 117 to 138. The day's own line adds one word — αυτάδελφοι, brother and sister of the same parents — and that is the whole of what the Greek calendar says about her.

There is no manner of death, no persecutor but the emperor, no place of burial, no relic, no hymn and not even the distich the site usually prints. The name-gloss printed above the notice is not about her either: it is an entry for the masculine form of the name, listing an architect of Samos, a Neoplatonist philosopher, two ancient physicians and a crowd of rulers and hierarchs.

So what stands recorded is a name, a brother, a city, a rank and a year. The corpus keeps a good many women called Theodora, each on her own day, and none of them is a Roman of a distinguished house killed in 132 with a brother of hers.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1802/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
