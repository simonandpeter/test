# George of Sofia

George came of Sofia in Bulgaria, and saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαρτίου keeps him by that city's name. He was once at Adrianople in Thrace, and there he went in to a Turkish bowyer to have his bow mended. At some point in the business the Turk reviled Christ; George answered him and confessed his own faith, and the Turks standing by seized him, beat him without pity and took him before the governor. Before the governor too he confessed with courage that he believed in Christ.

There followed, the page says, a series of dreadful tortures and of solitary confinement. In the end, on Great Tuesday, 26 March 1437, they burned him alive; he was thirty years old. The page heads him «(; - 1437)» and gives no year for his birth, so the age is left as the page's own statement and not turned into a date here.

Beyond this the source has nothing: no parents, no trade, no name for the governor, no account of where the relics went, and no hymn. The note it prints about the name — «ΓΕΩΡΓΙΟΣ (εκ του γη + εργάζομαι) = ο καλλιεργητής της Γής» — is about the word and not about the man.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3865/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
