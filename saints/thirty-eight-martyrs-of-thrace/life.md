# The Thirty-eight Martyrs of Thrace

Thirty-eight martyrs who suffered in Thrace, all of them by the sword. The synaxarion records nothing more of them, and notes that they may be the same company as the martyrs kept on 20 August. Their verse: a threefold ten of martyrs bore the sword, with a single eight beside them in the contest.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 23 August — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/852/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
