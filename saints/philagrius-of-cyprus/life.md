# Philagrius of Cyprus

saint.gr's calendar for 9 Φεβρουαρίου names three bishops on one line and carries them on one page: [Marcellus](/saints/marcellus-of-sicily) of Sicily, Philagrius of Cyprus, and [Pancratius](/saints/pancratius-of-tauromenium) of Tauromenium. All three, it says, lived in the first century after Christ. Of Marcellus and Pancratius it tells a good deal — they were father and son, and followed the apostle Peter — and of Philagrius it tells almost nothing.

What it says of him is one sentence: «Ο Φιλάγριος διέπρεψε σαν επίσκοπος της Κύπρου», Philagrius was distinguished as bishop of Cyprus. It then closes for all three together: they all fell asleep, having borne many trials in the carrying out of their pastoral duties, and a life of daily martyrdom. No year, no hymn and no relics are printed for him, and nothing on the page joins him to the other two but the day they share.

The distich over the three plays on the Greek for a knot: «Λυθέντες ἄνδρες σαρκικῶν τρεῖς ἁμμάτων, Τῶν τῆς Ἐδὲμ μετέσχον ἐντρυφημάτων» — three men, loosed from the bonds of the flesh, came to share the delights of Eden.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 9 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/09/index.aspx) and [the page for the three of them](https://www.saint.gr/3630/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 9 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260209.html), read 3 October 2026.*
