# Andrew (Benediktov)

Andrew Nikolayevich Benediktov was born in 1885 in the village of Voronino in Nizhny Novgorod province and was priest of one of the churches of Nizhny Novgorod. He was arrested on 6 August 1937 with other Orthodox clergy and laymen, and charged with belonging to a counter-revolutionary church group and with anti-Soviet talk. He did not admit guilt and gave no evidence against others. He was shot on the sentence of 21 August 1937 and later rehabilitated, and the Council of Bishops of the Russian Orthodox Church glorified him in 2000. azbyka.ru’s life uses the Orthodox Encyclopedia.

The Russian calendar keeps him on 21 September old style on one line with the priests [Valens (Nikolsky)](/saints/valens-nikolsky), [Alexander (Belyakov)](/saints/alexander-belyakov), [John (Lazarev)](/saints/john-lazarev), [Peter (Sakharovsky)](/saints/peter-sakharovsky) and [John (Nikolsky)](/saints/john-nikolsky), of 1937. azbyka.ru prints a seventh priest, Basil Nosov, on its line for the day, whom the Sretensky day does not name; it keeps him also with the saints of Nizhny Novgorod, and prints no hymns for him.
