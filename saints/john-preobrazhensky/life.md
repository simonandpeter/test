# John (Preobrazhensky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and gives no life. Its line for 29 мая ст. ст. reads «Сщмч. Иоанна диакона и мч. Андрея (1938).» — the hieromartyr John, deacon, and the martyr Andrew, of the year 1938 — and each name carries a link to a name page and neither to a reading. The page behind his adds his family name, Преображенский, and writes his grade as «протодиакон», protodeacon, where the day line had said deacon. Beyond the title it carries one line, «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 29 мая», and nothing further.

So the calendar sets down a protodeacon of the Russian church killed in 1938, and no more than that. The second of those two days is not a feast of his own: it is the general commemoration of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which this calendar prints beside the proper day of each of them, and it is the one mark on his page that places him among that company.

The line names him with a layman of the same year, and that is the whole of what joins the two on the page: there is no «with him», no place and no story. No folder in this corpus has been made to say that they died together. Nothing else about him was found, and azbyka.ru, which is where an account of him would be, was not read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 29 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260529.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13719.html), which name him and give no life; read 8 October 2026.*
