# Theodosius of Totma

Theodosius Sumorin of Totma was born in Vologda around 1530 and brought up from youth in Christian piety and the fear of God. At his parents’ insistence he married, but family life did not draw him from God — he went diligently to church and prayed much at home, especially at night — and after the death of his parents and his wife he withdrew to the Prilutsky monastery near Vologda, where he carried water, chopped wood, ground flour and baked bread. Sent by the igumen to Totma to oversee the monastery’s salt-works, he asked leave of Tsar Ivan Vasilievich and the blessing of Archbishop Nikandr to found near the town — the Sretensky calendar’s life runs on past the point read here, to the monastery he founded. The Russian calendar keeps on 2 September (Julian) the finding of his relics in 1796.
