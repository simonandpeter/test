# Boisil of Melrose

The venerable Boisil came of Scotland and took the road of the monastic life. He was tonsured a monk at the monastery of Melrose, which lay near the river Tweed, and by an ascesis pleasing to God he reached the heights of the virtues. He prayed without ceasing, calling on the Name of the Holy Trinity, and God blessed him with the gift of foresight.

Sensing his end beforehand he called the brethren of the monastery together and gave them his last spiritual counsels, which saint.gr prints in his own words: «Να ευχαριστείτε πάντοτε τον Θεό, ιδίως για την αγία σας κλήση στο μοναδικό βίο του μοναχού» — give thanks to God always, and above all for your holy calling to the single life of a monk. Avoid self-love and self-justification as your greatest enemies. Pray without ceasing. Struggle to gain purity of heart, for only so can you reach perfection.

He fell asleep in peace in the year 664. The page gives no year for his birth, no relic and no hymn, and names no house of his own beyond Melrose.
