# Anthony Cantacuzene of Meteora

Anthony was the founder and abbot of the monastery of Saint Stephen among the Meteora, in the first half of the fifteenth century. saint.gr is careful about the surname the calendar gives him: the name Cantacuzene «δεν είναι βέβαιο», it is not certain, and it is met with in a note in a liturgical manuscript. A letter of the year 1413, about the giving of the head of Saint Charalambos to the monastery of Saint Stephen of the Meteora, makes mention of «του αρχιμανδρίτου του μοναστηρίου κυρ Αντωνίου (Καντακουζηνού)» — the archimandrite of the monastery, lord Anthony.

The page gives his parentage as the calendar holds it: he was the son of the queen Maria Cantacuzene, who fell asleep after 1359 and was daughter of the emperor John VI Cantacuzene, and of Nicephorus II Orsini, despot of Epirus, who died in 1359. Of the rest of his life the page says one clause: he lived in a manner pleasing to God and fell asleep in peace. It prints no hymn for him. The second founder of the same house, [Philotheus](/saints/philotheus-of-meteora), is kept on this same day.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 17 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/17/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1291/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
