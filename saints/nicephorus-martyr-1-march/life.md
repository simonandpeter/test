# Nicephorus, with Charisius and Agapius

saint.gr names three martyrs together on this day — [Charisius](/saints/charisius-martyr-1-march), Nicephorus and [Agapius](/saints/agapius-martyr-1-march) — and then says in one sentence that it knows nothing about them: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο των Αγίων», we have no details of the saints' lives. The line enumerates all three by name, so each is kept here as a folder of his own rather than as a company; but the whole of what is recorded of any of them is the name and the day.

Over the name the page sets its usual etymology block — Νικηφόρος, the one who holds and gives the victory, the victor, the bearer of the trophy — and that is a note on the word and not a fact about the man. The distich over the three counts rather than describes them: a triad of contenders now stands in heaven before the Trinity that created all things.

There is no year, no country, no emperor, no judge, no manner of death, no relic and no hymn. The corpus keeps five other men of this name, of Antioch, of Corinth, of Crete, the leper, and one on the fourth of May; none of them is on this day and none is named on this page.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3779/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
