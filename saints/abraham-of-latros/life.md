# Abraham of Latros

saint.gr's calendar for 24 Μαρτίου names a venerable Abraham who struggled on Mount Latros. From a small child, the page says, he chose the angelic life of the monk, and so he distributed to the poor whatever property he had and became a pattern of a monk. The name of his great virtue was not long in spreading through the country where he lived his ascetic life, and for that reason many ran to him to profit spiritually.

But Abraham, because display and commotion did not please him, went up to Mount Latros, where with a stricter abstinence, with vigil and with prayer he advanced to great heights of virtue and was of use to the ascetics who were there. So, having lived in that way well-pleasing to God, he departed in peace.

That is the whole of what the page prints. It gives him no birthplace, no year, no monastery by name, no disciple and no relic, and prints neither a couplet nor a hymn under his name; nothing has been taken from any other source and nothing supplied from memory.
