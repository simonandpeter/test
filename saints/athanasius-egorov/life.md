# Athanasius (Egorov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Athanasius last in a company of eight on 7 August and gives no life for him. He is the one member of that company whom the entry marks off with a rank of his own: «Сщмчч. Александра, Петра, Михаила, Иоанна, Димитрия и Алексия пресвитеров, Елисея диакона и прмч. Афанасия (1937).» — the seven before him are a hieromartyr's company, and he is «прмч.», a venerable-martyr, a monastic.

The calendar's own name page adds the surname and the office the line leaves out, and gives him the other rank: «Афанасий (Егоров), игумен, сщмч.», Athanasius Egorov, abbot. The two readings are not reconciled here; both are written down, and the line that keeps him is the one followed.

That is the whole of the record. He was an abbot; he died in 1937; the calendar keeps him on this day, the day after the Transfiguration, among the new martyrs of Russia. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 7 августа ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260807.html); read 13 October 2026.*
