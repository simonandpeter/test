# Ephraim of the Kyiv Caves

saint.gr's calendar for 23 Μαρτίου names a Russian ascetic, Ephraim of the cave, and gives him one sentence: he lived as an ascetic in the monastery of the Caves of Kyiv in the thirteenth century, and fell asleep in peace; we have no further details of the saint's life. That is the whole of the entry, and this life says no more than the page does.

The page gives him no name in the world, no birthplace, no office in the monastery, no wonder and no relic, and prints neither a couplet nor a hymn under his name. Nothing has been taken from any other source and nothing supplied from memory. Several other men of the name are kept on other days and in other calendars, and nothing on this page identifies him with any of them; if a fuller life of his is printed elsewhere, it has not been read.
