# Isaiah of the Kyiv Caves

saint.gr names him and gives three sentences and no more. It says that Isaiah the Wonderworker lived in the tenth and eleventh centuries, that he kept his ascesis in the Lavra of the Caves at Kyiv, and that he fell asleep in holiness and peace in 1115. Its head line prints the same two facts as a pair of dates, «(; - 1115)», with the year of his birth left open.

Nothing else is printed there: no parents, no birthplace, no office, no wonder told out, no year of entering the Lavra, no hymn and no relics. He is not the bishop of Rostov whom saint.gr keeps on the same day and whose page runs to several hundred words; that is another man, dead in 1090, and the two entries stand separately in the calendar. What the page does not say is not set down here.

The Russian calendar's two readings of him are a few sentences each and say the same thing twice. He was a monk of the Kyiv Caves monastery; the work of his life was silence and untiring labour, and for the labour he is called Трудолюбивый, the industrious. He died in 1115 and was buried in the Near Caves, where the second reading says his relics lie. Both readings place his struggle in the eleventh century and the beginning of the twelfth, where the Greek page places it in the tenth and the eleventh; the death year is the same in all of them and the folder's interval was left as the Greek gave it. The second reading adds that he is kept on this day, on 28 September with the fathers of the Near Caves, and on the second Sunday of Lent.
