# Pior the Recluse

The Greek calendar keeps a venerable Pior the Recluse on 4 October, and its entry says only that it has no details of the life of this Russian saint. Nothing else is recorded here; whether the Russian calendar keeps him is for its own pages.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 4 Οκτωβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2622/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
