# Vladimir Moshchansky, Presbyter, Hieromartyr (1938)

Vladimir Moshchansky was born on 15 June 1866 in the village of Zaborovye in the Yesenovichi district of Tver province, the son of the priest Demetrius Moshchansky; his mother Anna, widowed early and very devout, fed the poor and the beggars, and when she died they all came to bury her — only then did many see how many she had helped. After the Tver seminary he married Eudocia, daughter of the priest John Dimitrievsky, and was soon ordained deacon to the church of Rogachevo in the Rzhev district; a daughter and three sons were born to them, and at the birth of the fourth child Eudocia died, leaving him with small children whom her unmarried sister Alexandra helped him raise. After a time he was ordained priest of the Dormition church in the village of Spasskoye in the Kashin district, where he served until the Bolsheviks closed the church in 1924 — at the last service the church was full, all on their knees, many weeping, and the whole parish walked the thirty kilometres to Kashin to see him off. He settled at Vyshny Volochyok — the Sretensky calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. He died in 1938, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 25 August (Julian).
