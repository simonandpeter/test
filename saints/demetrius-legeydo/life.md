# Demetrius (Legeydo)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery gives him a line of his own for 10 марта ст. ст., and the line is four words and a year: «Сщмч. Димитрия пресвитера (1938)» — the hieromartyr Demetrius, a presbyter, 1938. The line declines the forename and gives no surname, no place, no year of birth and no word of narrative.

Behind that «Димитрия» stands the calendar's own name page 9558, which carries the fuller form «Димитрий (Легейдо), иерей, сщмч.» — the surname the line leaves out, and the grade of priest — and lists two days, this 10 марта and «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. That page carries no life either, and azbyka.ru was not read for him. So what the calendar prints is the whole of what this folder says: a priest of the Russian church named Demetrius Legeydo, who died a martyr in 1938 and whom this calendar keeps on this day.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 10 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260310.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9558.html), which carries no life; read 7 October 2026.*
