# Apollonius of Sardis

doxologia.ro prints two sentences about him and no more. He was born in the city of Sardis, which the page calls the former capital of Lydia, in Asia Minor. The holy martyr Apollonius confessed the Saviour Jesus Christ before Perinius, governor of Iconium — a city neighbouring on Lydia, the page adds — and received the crown of martyrdom by crucifixion.

There is no year on the page, no account of his trial and nothing of his life before it; the manner of his death is the one detail it keeps. What is here is what the Romanian calendar has of him.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 10 July, and adds what the Romanian line did not: the place of his trial and the manner of his death. saint.gr says Apollonius came from Sardis and was denounced to the eparch of Iconium because he believed in Christ and was working among the idolaters for the spreading of the Gospel. The eparch — Perinios was his name — after trying in vain to force him to deny Christ, crucified him. That end, whose dreadful agony our Lord Jesus Christ had tasted too, Apollonius bore with great endurance, strengthened by the divine grace, and so won the most glorious crown of martyrdom. Above the notice the page sets a distich: «Aπολλωνίω σταυρός η τιμωρία, Eιπείν δ’ αληθώς, Σταυρός η σωτηρία» — for Apollonius the cross was the punishment, and to speak truly, the cross was the salvation. The page gives no year and no emperor, and prints no hymn.
