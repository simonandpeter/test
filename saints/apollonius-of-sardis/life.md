# Apollonius of Sardis

doxologia.ro prints two sentences about him and no more. He was born in the city of Sardis, which the page calls the former capital of Lydia, in Asia Minor. The holy martyr Apollonius confessed the Saviour Jesus Christ before Perinius, governor of Iconium — a city neighbouring on Lydia, the page adds — and received the crown of martyrdom by crucifixion.

There is no year on the page, no account of his trial and nothing of his life before it; the manner of his death is the one detail it keeps. What is here is what the Romanian calendar has of him.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 10 iulie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/10-iulie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-mucenic-apolonie-0); read 24 September 2026.*
