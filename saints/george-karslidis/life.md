# George Karslidis

saint.gr's calendar for 4 Νοεμβρίου names «Όσιος Γεώργιος Καρσλίδης ο Ομολογητής» and dates him 1901 to 1959. He was born in 1901 at Argyroupolis in Pontos, the seat of the metropolis of Chaldia, and his baptismal name was Athanasios. He was orphaned small — his parents died on the same day — and the signs of his calling and of grace showed at once. Brought up by his most devout grandmother in the exemplary piety of Pontos, he was different from the other children as soon as he could stand and speak, and was given over to God.

As a child he prayed continually and kept fasts, and at seven he went and venerated the Panagia of Soumela; he became a novice monk at only nine years old. He was tonsured a monk in 1919, at eighteen, and then ordained deacon.

In the terrible days of the persecution of the Church by the communists in Georgia, the page says, the young hierodeacon was arrested as «ἐχθρὸς τοῦ λαοῦ», an enemy of the people, and suffered imprisonments, humiliations, degradations, public parades and unheard-of torments; he was even condemned to death and shot, and was saved by a wonder. In 1925 he was ordained presbyter and confessor, and in 1929, after many adventures, he came to Sipsa in Greece, in the settlement of Taxiarches at Drama, where he lived the last thirty of his fifty-eight years.

He was condemned to death a second time, in 1941, by invaders of the same faith from the north, and was saved again by a wonder, so as to continue his holy life until the day of his repose, on 4 November 1959.

The page keeps some of his sayings. He urged his spiritual children to fast rightly, because fasting is a training for the Christian, and said it was a mistake to fast a week at the beginning and a week at the end of a fast; and he once gave this example for it: when you are on a bridge and it is raining hard and you have a rope stretched out and begin to haul it in, you do not cut it and leave because you are getting wet, but wait until you have hauled it all in, and let yourself be rained on. He pressed also that godparents should not quarrel, and that every Christian should baptise at least three children; the godfather's duty, he said, is to teach his godchildren from small to go to church, to communicate regularly, to follow the right road, and to become good men and Christians.
