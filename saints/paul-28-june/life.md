# Paul (28 June)

saint.gr's calendar for 28 Ιουνίου names him and gives him one sentence. Its line reads «Άγιος Παύλος ο ιατρός», Paul the physician, and the page behind that line says only that he died in peace. No country is named, no century, no year, no city he practised in and no relics, and the page prints no hymn for him.

Above the sentence stands a couplet of the synaxarion which turns his calling into a pun on his name: «Έπαυε Παύλος πριν τομή καύσει χρίσει, Πάθη τανύν δε, ταύτα πρεσβεία λύει» — Paul used before to make sufferings cease by cutting, by burning and by anointing; now his intercession undoes them. The page's gloss on the name is the Latin paulus, the very small. That is the whole of the entry, and the day in the name here is the day the calendar keeps him on.

The Ohrid Prologue of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović keeps him on the same twenty-eighth of June and gives him what the Greek calendar did not: a country, a life and a century. He was a Corinthian by birth. After his schooling he withdrew into a monastery and became a monk, and there he had a great struggle with the unclean spirit of fornication.

When he had driven that spirit off by the power of the cross, the book says, it contrived a spite against him instead: it put a dissolute woman up to saying that she had borne a child by Paul. Heretics dragged him out of the monastery at that, put the child into his hands and drove him through the city for the people to spit at him. The child was only a few days old. Paul prayed earnestly and said to the crowd that the child itself should say who its father was; and the child put its hand out of the swaddling bands, pointed at a blacksmith standing there, and said that this man was its father and not Paul the monk.

His accusers were put to shame, and God gave him a great power of healing, so that when he laid his hands on the sick they got well. He reposed peacefully in old age, having pleased God by his life on earth. The entry closes by saying that he lived in the eighth century, which is the only date the three books between them give him.
