# Dosas

saint.gr says that Dosas — whose name the page gives three ways, Dosas or Dosai or Sodas — lived with [Heliodorus](/saints/heliodorus-the-martyr-20-august) under the reign of Sabor, king of the Persians. That king came with his armies and took a city of the Romans, and began pulling down the churches of the Christians and burning the holy icons.

Then Heliodorus, who was ninety-five years old at the time, came before the impious king together with Dosas, and the two made the sharpest remonstrances to him, confessing at the same time that Christ is true God. Sabor then tortured them hard and without mercy. He cut off their noses, and at the last he put them to death by burning their heads.

That is the whole of what the page has. It gives no year, no city, no account of Dosas apart from his companion, and no relics; the one distich it prints is for the pair together, «Hλιόδωρος και Δοσάς, οι γεννάδαι, Ήθλησαν άμφω ανδρικώς Xριστού χάριν» — Heliodorus and Dosas, the noble ones, both contended manfully for Christ's sake. It prints no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 20 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/20/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2304/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
