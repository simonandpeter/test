# Menignus

saint.gr's calendar for 4 Ιουλίου names him and gives no life. The whole of its account is one sentence, «Ο Όσιος Μένιγνος απεβίωσε ειρηνικά» — the venerable Menignus reposed in peace. The page gives no country, no city, no monastery, no century and no year; it says nothing of how he lived or where he was buried, and it prints no hymn under his line.

Besides that sentence it carries only the couplet the synaxaria print for him: «Mένιγνος εκπλεί τον θαλαττώδη βίον, / Eυρών αρίστους τους νόας κωπηλάτας» — Menignus sails out of this sea-like life, having found in his own thoughts the best of oarsmen. That is everything the source says of him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 4 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/04/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1993/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
