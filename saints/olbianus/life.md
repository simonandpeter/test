# Olbianus

Doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 29 mai reads «Sfântul Sfinţit Mucenic Olivian», the holy hieromartyr Olbianus, and the page the calendar links for him carries no Viață tab and no Tropar tab. It prints his name, the date, and the readings appointed — the epistle from Hebrews 13, remember your leaders who spoke to you the word of God, and the gospel from Luke 12, fear not, little flock. Nothing else about him is on the page.

The rank in the line is the whole of what is stated: a man in holy orders who died a martyr, kept on 29 May. No see, no place, no century and no year is entered for him, because the source gives none.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 29 mai — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/29-mai) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-sfintit-mucenic-olivian); read 24 September 2026.*
