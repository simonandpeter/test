# The Two Soldiers with Longinus

saint.gr's calendar for 16 October keeps «Άγιοι Δύο Στρατιώτες», and its whole notice is one sentence: the holy two soldiers were martyred together with Saint [Longinus](/saints/longinus-the-centurion), and it sends the reader to him on the same day. Neither of the two is named, here or there.

What story they have is in his page. They were two of the soldiers who served under the centurion Longinus at the Passion; when he perceived the earthquake and believed that the crucified was the true Son of God, they withdrew with him to his own country to preach the Resurrection of the Lord. When the detachment sent by Tiberius arrived, Longinus entertained them, prepared the grave for himself and for his two faithful soldiers, and the next day all of them were beheaded.

The calendar gives the two no names, no country, no rank beyond soldier and no year, and prints no hymn for them; the two-line verse over their own entry says that a pair of fellow-contestants, having left a perishable soldiering by the sword, were enlisted to the Lord. So they stand here as one folder for the pair, because the source keeps them as a number beside a man it does name.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 16 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/16/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2755/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
