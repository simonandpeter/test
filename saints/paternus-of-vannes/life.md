# Paternus of Vannes

saint.gr gives him three sentences. The holy Paternus was born in Brittany in the year 500, it says, and followed the monastic life as his father had done before him; he founded monasteries and churches, and was elected bishop of the city of Vannes in Brittany; and he fell asleep in peace in the year 565. The page keeps him on 15 April as a fixed feast, and its heading sets those two years down beside his name.

Nothing else is printed of him. It does not name his parents, or the house where he was tonsured, or any of the monasteries and churches it credits him with, or the years he held the see. There is no couplet and no hymn on the page, and the day's own line names him bare, «Άγιος Πάτερνος», without country or office.
