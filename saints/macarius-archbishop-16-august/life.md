# Macarius (16 August)

saint.gr's calendar for 16 Αυγούστου names him and then says what it does not know. Its whole notice is two sentences: that his memory is mentioned in the Jerusalem Kanonarion, at page 103, without his archbishopric being stated; and that perhaps he was one of the archbishops of the throne of Jerusalem. The page marks that second sentence as a guess with its own «Ίσως», and this folder records no see for him on the strength of it.

So there is no country, no century, no year, no manner of death and no hymn, and the one thing the Greek calendar is willing to say about him beyond his name is that he was an archbishop. This folder stands for that line of 16 August and is marked by its day, because the calendar gives him nothing else to be known by. saint.gr does vouch for the memory itself: it names the book the memory stands in, and its page carries neither the asterisk nor the sentence with which the site marks an entry it will not vouch for.
