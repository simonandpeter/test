# Priscus, martyred at Besançon

Priscus was a Roman senior officer and lived in the third century. He was serving in the city of Besançon in Gaul under the orders of Aurelian; but because he was a Christian he was arrested together with his fellow-soldiers and tortured, and all of them were perfected by martyrdom in the year 272. saint.gr names neither the fellow-soldiers nor their number, and the day's own line calls them only «η συνοδεία αυτού», the company with him.

The page adds one thing after the death: that their holy relics were discovered by Saint Germanus of Auxerre, who raised a church in honour of the holy martyrs. It gives no birthplace, no persecutor besides the emperor, no manner of death and no later history of the relics, and prints neither a synaxarion couplet nor a hymn.
