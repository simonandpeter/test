# Autonomus

Autonomus was a bishop in Italy with a rich Christian activity in his diocese. When Diocletian’s persecution began he left his post and went to Soreoi in Asia Minor, where he settled in the house of a Christian, Cornelius, and went on teaching the Gospel; then he went to Lycaonia and Isauria to continue his work, ordaining Cornelius deacon before he set out, and when after a time he came back to Soreoi and saw the great good work he had done — many people had embraced Christianity — he ordained him priest; after visiting many regions on the Euxine he returned again to Cornelius and consecrated him bishop. The God-pleasing work of Autonomus troubled the pagans, and they killed him by stoning as he was celebrating the liturgy in the church, in 313 — a victim set on your table, Word, the verse says, your priest Autonomus, dying by stones. The Romanian Lives say that by his preaching men’s hearts were kindled to right belief and the love of God; the Greek and Romanian calendars keep him on 12 September, the Romanian with a troparion and kontakion.
