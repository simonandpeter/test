# James of Omuch

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him with his companion on 21 октября ст. ст. as «Прпп. Феофила и Иакова Омучских (ок. 1412)», and what it says of him it says of the two of them together. They struggled on the island of Konevets on Lake Ladoga — the notice for the pair says in the company of [Arsenius](/saints/arsenius-of-konevits), whose own day it gives as 12 June, while the notice written for [Theophilus](/saints/theophilus-of-omuch) alone names only the two of them there.

In 1396 the two founded a hermitage in honour of the Dormition of the Mother of God on the river Omucha, in the Pskov diocese not far from Porkhov, and the river's name is the epithet both of them carry. The calendar says their death followed about 1412, and unlike his companion he is given no day of his own and no separate notice.

That is the whole of it: no birth, no country, no account of how either man came to Konevets or to the Omucha, and no hymn under his name — the hymns the day page prints belong to other saints it heads and to a translation of relics.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 21 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261021.html) and [the notice for the pair](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4566.htm); read 4 October 2026.*
