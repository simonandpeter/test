# Dorotheus of Chiliokomion

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives one sentence and no more: the venerable Dorotheus followed the road of the monastic polity, and it appears — the page says «φαίνεται», it appears — that he was the founder and builder of the monastery of Chiliokomion at Amaseia. The page adds only a note on the name, that Dorotheus is the gift of God, and a list of four bearers of it out of antiquity who are not saints at all. It prints no year for him, no hymn and no other notice, and nothing is supplied here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/573/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
