# Vyacheslav (Zankov)

He is the second of the six presbyters the Russian calendar keeps on 30 September in one line, with a deacon, a schemanun and three lay martyrs beside them and 1937 standing for all eleven. The day line prints him as «Вячеслава» and nothing more; the surname is the site's own index of names, which heads his page «Вячеслав (Занков), иерей, сщмч.» — Vyacheslav the hieromartyr, priest — and that is the whole of what the calendar says about him.

The index keeps him here and on 26 January with the company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. It gives no birth, no diocese, no parish, no charge and no place of death, and the calendar carries no life for any of the eleven. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026 even with a browser user-agent, so the folder says what the one line says and no more.

*After the Russian church calendar's [30 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260930.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9545.html); read 4 October 2026.*
