# Heliodorus of Altinum

saint.gr's calendar for 3 Ιουλίου names him in one sentence and that sentence is the whole page: «Ο Άγιος Ηλιόδωρος ήταν Επίσκοπος Αλτίνου της Ιταλίας και θείος του Αγίου Νεποτιανού» — the holy Heliodorus was bishop of Altinum in Italy and the uncle of Saint [Nepotian](/saints/nepotian-the-presbyter), whose day the page gives as 4 May. No century, no year, no deed and no manner of death is printed, so none of those is recorded here.

The nephew's own page, read earlier in this work, is the fuller of the two: it says Nepotian lived in the fourth century and was ordained presbyter by his uncle Heliodorus, bishop of Altinum, and it already gives 3 July as the uncle's day — which is the day this folder now stands on. Beyond that relation the Greek calendar keeps nothing of him.
