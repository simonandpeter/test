# The Two Martyrs, mother and daughter

saint.gr's calendar for 28 January keeps two women martyrs, a mother and her daughter, and names neither of them. The page's one sentence about them says as much about what is not known as about what is: it is unknown where and when the two women martyrs, the mother with her daughter, bore witness by the sword. So there is no year here, no reign, no city, no persecutor, and no troparion or kontakion — and no name, which is the thing a reader will look for first.

What the page sets above that sentence is a couplet, and the couplet is the only detail of the manner of their death: «Τῇ παιδὶ συγκλίνασα μήτηρ τὴν κάραν, / Ξίφει συνεξέπνευσε τῷ θυγατρίῳ» — the mother, laying her head beside her child's, breathed out her life together with her daughter under the sword. That much the Greek calendar records of them, and nothing here is supplied from anywhere else.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/28/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3539/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
