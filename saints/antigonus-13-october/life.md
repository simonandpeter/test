# Antigonus (13 October)

saint.gr's calendar for 13 October names him and gives no life. Its whole notice is one sentence: «Ο Άγιος Αντίγονος μαρτύρησε δια πυρός» — Saint Antigonus was martyred by fire. That is the manner of his death and all the calendar knows of him: no year, no city, no persecutor, no rank beyond the word saint, and no hymn.

The one other thing on the page that belongs to him is the two-line verse set over his name, in which he speaks: I shall die for you, my Christ, rather than for what is there, says Antigonus, choosing the burning here. The notes above it, about Antigone the daughter of Oedipus and the Macedonian kings and sculptors and poets who bore the name, are the site's remarks on the name itself.
