# Marionilla (Tseytlin)

The calendar’s own line is the whole of what is recorded of her here. The Сретенский monastery’s calendar for 2 декабря ст. ст. commemorates her alone, in three words and a year: «Прмц. Мариониллы (1938).» She is the only saint on this day dated 1938, and she stands between a company of eighteen of 1937 and a single layman of 1942.

The name page behind her place gives the surname the line leaves out and her grade: «Марионилла (Цейтлин), прмц.» — a venerable-martyr, which is a monastic grade; the page names no house and gives her no degree in one, so this folder records her as a monastic and gives her no office. There is no life behind that page, and the day page carries no hymn under her name.

So nothing is said here of where she was born, where she lived, when or by whom she was arrested, or how and where she died. The surname is unusual and the corpus holds it nowhere else; nothing read says anything about it.

Her name page lists one other day, «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church. One row per church is the schema, so that day is recorded here and she does not render on it.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 2 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261202.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14365.html); read 7 October 2026.*
