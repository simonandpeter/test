# Kalia (12 February)

The Ohrid Prologue of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović gives her the day's shortest entry and almost nothing but her charity. She was open-handed towards the wretched out of pure Christian mercy, it says, both as a girl and later as a married woman. Her husband was rich but hard; returning once from a business journey he saw that his wife had spent his wealth away on the poor, and he killed her.

But God glorified this merciful soul, the Prologue goes on, in this way: that many sick people were healed from her relics. The patriarch [Anthony of Constantinople](/saints/anthony-of-constantinople), satisfied of it, raised a monastery over them — which is the one thing the Prologue says of her beyond her own house, and the only date it allows her, since it puts that patriarch in the reign of Leo the Wise, 889 to 912. It gives her no year, no city and no rank, and nothing more is written here.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 12. фебруар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-02-25&prolog=1); read 4 October 2026.*
