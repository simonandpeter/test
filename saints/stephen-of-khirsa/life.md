# Stephen of Khirsa

Stephen of Khirsa was one of the twelve disciples whom [John of Zedazeni](/saints/john-of-zedazeni) chose out of his Syrian community and brought with him into Georgia, in obedience to a vision in which the Mother of God told him to take twelve worthy men to strengthen the faith in her newly founded Church there. doxologia.ro prints no separate life for him. Its life of their teacher opens by naming all thirteen together — «Sfântul Cuvios Ioan Zedazneli, dimpreună cu 12 ucenici din Georgia» — and calls them Syrian ascetics and the founding fathers of Georgian monastic life.

When the fathers were divided he was sent into Kakheti, in eastern Georgia, and the life calls him there «cel încununat cu harisme și vedenii mai presus de fire» — crowned with gifts of grace and with visions above nature. That epithet and the place he was sent to are the whole of what this page says about him by himself.

Their teacher sent them out to preach as the apostles did, to baptise in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, and to lead those in darkness to worship Christ; and he divided among them relics of the saints he had brought, so that they might be worthier on the road and might follow the example of those saints. The life says that they suffered for faith and truth as the apostles and the martyrs did, wandering in deserts and in mountains and in caves and in the clefts of the earth; that most of them held themselves to a complete lack of necessities and would not even use clothes, going out to preach in skins through the heat of the day and the cold of the night; that the faithful came to them for teaching and the sick for healing; and that where the idolatrous altars were destroyed churches were raised in their place.
