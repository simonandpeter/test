# Sasanes

saint.gr's calendar for 20 Νοεμβρίου names four men in one line: «Άγιοι Γεϊθαζέτ ο ευνούχος, Σασάνης, Νοηλμάρης και Ζαρουαντίνης», and gives them a single sentence. Because they did not sacrifice to the Sun-god of the Persians, it says, they were all put to death by the spear. For the first of them the page offers four spellings — Γεϊθαζέτ, or Γεϊδαζέτ, or Γαϊσαδέτ, or Βοηθαζάτ — and for the second two, Σασάνης or Σωσάννης.

The couplet set over the line counts them the same way: the enemies of the Trinity kill Geithazat and three fellow contestants together with their spears. The page gives them no year, no city, no king by name, no rank beyond the first one's being a eunuch, and no hymn, and prints no relics for them.

This folder is Sasanes, named second in the line, whom the page also spells Σωσάννης, beside [Geithazat the Eunuch](/saints/geithazat-the-eunuch), [Noelmares](/saints/noelmares), [Zarouandines](/saints/zarouandines).

**The Russian calendar keeps the whole Persian company on one line and one life page.** The Сретенский monastery’s calendar prints for the Julian 20 November a single clause, «Сщмчч. Нирсы еп. и Иосифа, ученика его, Иоанна, Саверия, Исакия и Ипатия, епископов Персидских; мч. Азата скопца, Сасония, Феклы, Анны и иных многих мужей и жен, в Персиде пострадавших (343).», and behind every name in it the same life page stands, life2574, titled for all of them together. saint.gr prints the same day as four separate groups with four separate lives; the Russian page is the first source read here that puts them in one company. Its whole account of them is that they «были казнены во время гонения на христиан в Персии при царе Сапоре II» — that they were put to death during the persecution of Christians in Persia under king Shapur II — and then one short sentence apiece on the manner of each death.

He is in the last sentence of the life, which does not separate him out: «Пытки, мучения и смерть за Христа претерпели также мученики Сасоний, Фекла, Анна и многие другие мужчины и женщины (+ 343)» — tortures, torments and death for Christ were borne also by these and by many other men and women. The year sits at the end of that sentence and is the only one the page prints.
