# Constantine (Bogoyavlensky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Constantine among the hieromartyrs on 14 July and gives no life. Its line is «Сщмч. Константина пресвитера (1918).» — the hieromartyr Constantine the presbyter, 1918 — and the calendar's own name page for him adds only the surname and the grade: «Константин (Богоявленский), иерей, сщмч.»

That is the whole of the record. He was a priest; he died in 1918, in the first year of the persecution; the calendar keeps him on this day among the new martyrs of Russia. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read, and the surname is not a biography.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 14 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260714.html); read 9 October 2026.*
