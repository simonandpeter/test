# Peter (Golubev)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Peter among the hieromartyrs on 21 July and gives no life. Its line is «Сщмч. Петра пресвитера (1938).» — the hieromartyr Peter the presbyter, 1938 — and the calendar's own name page for him adds the surname and keeps the grade the line gives: «Петр (Голубев), иерей, сщмч.», Peter Golubev, priest.

That is the whole of the record. He was a priest; he died in 1938; the calendar keeps him on this day among the new martyrs of Russia. The day page carries troparia and kontakia for four of the memories it heads, and none under his name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 21 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260721.html); read 9 October 2026.*
