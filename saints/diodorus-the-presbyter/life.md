# Diodorus the Presbyter

Diodorus was a priest, and he suffered with [Chrysanthus](/saints/chrysanthus) and [Daria](/saints/daria) at Rome. After those two had been buried alive in a pit on the Salarian way, wonders and healings were worked at the place, and a great number of Christians — men, women and children — used to gather in a cave nearby to keep the martyrs' day with joy, holding the services and communing of the Holy Mysteries.

The pagan authorities heard what they were doing, came to the cave, and stopped up its mouth and threw the earth over it, so that all those inside received the crown of martyrdom too. Two of those martyred in the cave, doxologia's short page says, are Saint Diodorus the priest and Marianus the deacon; the longer life adds that many clergy died there with them, and that the names of the faithful who ended there cannot be counted, for they were beyond number.

That is everything the Romanian calendar prints about him: his rank, his companions, the manner of his death and the persecution it belonged to. It gives him no country, no year of his own and no hymn, and none has been supplied here from anywhere else.
