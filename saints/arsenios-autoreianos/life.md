# Arsenios Autoreianos

saint.gr's calendar for 28 Οκτωβρίου names «Άγιος Αρσένιος ο Αυτωρειανός Πατριάρχης Κωνσταντινούπολης», and dates him «(; - 1273)». He was born and brought up in Constantinople, the page says, of a family of consequence: his father had been honoured with the dignity of «Κριτοῦ τοῦ δρόμου». His name in the world was George, and he was renamed Gennadios when he embraced the monastic life, first in a monastery of the City and afterwards in Bithynia; the page does not say when he took the name Arsenios.

He had the confidence of Theodore II Laskaris, emperor at Nicaea, whose reign the page dates 1254 to 1258, and at the emperor's demand he was elected patriarch in 1254, although the hierarchs would have preferred the election of Nikephoros Blemmydes. After Theodore's death in 1258 he took on the guardianship of the emperor's under-age son John II, whom he tried to protect from the unbridled ambition of Michael Palaiologos.

Michael blinded the lawful heir and usurped the throne; and because the patriarch resisted him stoutly he was exiled to Prokonnesos, where he fell asleep in 1273. Those who had set themselves against Michael's crime followed Arsenios, and so arose the schism of the Arsenites. The Church proclaimed him a saint, the page says, and honours him on 28 October; and his will, the only authentic work of his that survives, is reckoned a source of value, which it cites as PG 140, 947–958.

The page prints no hymn. It carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands.
