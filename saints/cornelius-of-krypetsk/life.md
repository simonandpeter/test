# Cornelius of Krypetsk

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names the venerable Cornelius of Krypetsk on 28 December and gives no life. Its line is «Прп. Корнилия Крыпецкого (1903).» — the venerable Cornelius of Krypetsk, 1903 — and the calendar’s own name page for him is no fuller: «Корнилий Крыпецкий, прп.»

That is the whole of the record. The epithet is the monastery of Krypetsk, near Pskov; the year of death the line gives is 1903; the calendar keeps him on this day, inside the afterfeast of the Nativity. The day page prints two hymns and both belong to the twenty thousand martyrs of Nicomedia; nothing stands under this name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 28 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261228.html); read 13 October 2026.*
