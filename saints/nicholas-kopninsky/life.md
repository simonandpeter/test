# Nicholas (Kopninsky)

**He is the one of the three men of his parish who was not shot, and the calendar keeps him on the day the other two were.** It gives him a line of his own, «Мч. Николая (1938)», and his name page calls him мч. with no grade and no office. azbyka.ru carries no life of his own for him but prints the joint one of the three, out of fond.ru: his priest [Alexander (Vershinsky)](/saints/alexander-vershinsky) and the chairman of the parish council, [Paul (Kuzovkov)](/saints/paul-kuzovkov).

Николай Иванович Копнинский was born on 20 November 1876 at Ивантеевка in the province of Moscow, a worker's son. He finished the village school in 1888 and went into the office of the Лыжин factory, where he worked until he was called up in 1899; he served four years, first as a private and then as a clerk of an infantry regiment quartered at Łomża. He came back to the factory as a clerk in 1903, was a clerk of a Moscow exchange artel from 1908 to 1914, was called up again and served until 1918 in the engineering commissariat at Moscow, and then returned to Ивантеевка and worked his land. From 1921 to 1930 he was a clerk in the factories there. In 1930 he was caught in a railway accident and was left disabled by the injury.

**From 1935 he was secretary of the audit commission of the parish council, and it was for that, the page says, that he was arrested in 1937.** Asked what his relations with the chairman and «попом Вершинским» had been, he answered that he visited the chairman, and the chairman him, only because one was chairman of the parish council and the other, until 17 March 1937, secretary of its audit commission, and that as for the priest, neither of them had ever visited the other. Put to him that he had voiced plainly counter-revolutionary views after a meeting of the parish council in February 1937, he said the meeting had taken place and that he had voiced no such views and did not admit the guilt.

On 1 December 1937 the troika sentenced the priest and the chairman to be shot and him to eight years in a labour camp. He was taken to the Mariinsk camps in the Kemerovo region, and died there on 15 March 1938.
