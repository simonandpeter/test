# Nicholas (Udintsev)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Nicholas among the hieromartyrs on 25 July and gives no life. Its line is «Сщмч. Николая пресвитера (1918).» — the hieromartyr Nicholas the presbyter, 1918 — and the calendar's own name page for him adds the surname and keeps the grade the line gives: «Николай (Удинцев), иерей, сщмч.», Nicholas Udintsev, priest.

That is the whole of the record. He was a priest; he died in 1918, the first year of the persecution this calendar's twentieth-century entries mostly come out of; and the calendar keeps him on this day among the new martyrs of Russia.

The day page carries five troparia and kontakia for other memories it heads, and none under his name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 25 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260725.html); read 13 October 2026.*
