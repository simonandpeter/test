# Nicander (6 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 6 Νοεμβρίου names Nicander and gives him one sentence: «Ο Όσιος Νίκανδρος μαρτύρησε, αφού θανατώθηκε με μαχαίρι» — the venerable Nicander was martyred, having been put to death with a knife. That is the whole of the notice. The page gives him no country, no century, no emperor, no monastery, no companions, no year and no hymn, and it says nothing of how he came to be taken.

What stands beside the sentence is the couplet of the synaxarion, which sets his killing against what he had already won: «Σφαγὴν παθὼν Νίκανδρος ἐχθρὸς τῆς πλάνης, / Νίκην κατ' ἀνδρῶν ἤρατο πλανωμένων» — Nicander, the enemy of error, suffering slaughter, took up victory over men who were in error. The gloss the page gives his name reads it as the victor.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2925/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
