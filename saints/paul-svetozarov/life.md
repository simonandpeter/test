# Paul (Svetozarov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names this martyr on 27 апреля and gives no life of him. Its line for the day reads «Сщмчч. Павла и Иоанна пресвитеров, мчч. Петра , Николая , Авксентия , Сергия и мц. Анастасии (1922).» — two hieromartyr presbyters, four martyrs and a woman martyr, seven people under one year, each with a name link of his own. He is the first of the seven, and the first of the two the line grades «Сщмчч.», hieromartyrs, and «пресвитеров», presbyters.

That name page, 9387, carries nothing but the heading «Павел (Светозаров), протоиерей, сщмч.» and the two days «26 января (Новомуч.) , 27 апреля». What it adds to the line is the surname «Светозаров» and the grade «протоиерей», archpriest, written out beside «сщмч.» — the line gives only «пресвитера». There is no life page for him anywhere on the site, no icon and no article. The day page prints nine hymns — three under the name of the kinsman of the Lord and six under the name of the abbot of the Caves — and none under any of the seven names of this line. The 26 января day page was not read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 27 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260427.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9387.html); read 8 October 2026.*
