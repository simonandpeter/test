# Sisinnius (22 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 22 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιος Σισίνιος ο Ιερομάρτυρας» and gives him a single sentence: he was martyred by the sword. Everything else the page has of him is in the couplet set over the line, which puts the words in his mouth — you had me for a sacrificer, Word; now you have me, Sisinnius, for a strange sacrifice too, by the sword.

That is the whole of it. The page gives him no see, no city, no country, no year, no emperor, no companions and no hymn, and prints no relics for him; even the priesthood his title and his couplet give him comes with no church attached.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 22 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/22/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3072/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
