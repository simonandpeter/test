# Eutychius, companion of Bassus

The Greek calendar keeps four men together on 20 Ιανουαρίου — [Bassus](/saints/bassus-companion-of-eusebius), [Eusebius](/saints/eusebius-companion-of-bassus), Eutychius and [Basilides](/saints/basilides-companion-of-bassus) — and the page behind that line gives each of them an epigram and one sentence about the way he died. Of Bassus it says that the hands of the executioners cut off his hands; of Eusebius that they hung him by the head and cut his body in pieces with axes; of Basilides that they tore open his belly.

Of Eutychius the page says that they set him between four stakes, stretched his body with force and parted him into pieces, and the epigram over him plays on the number: «Εὐτύχιον διεῖλον εἰς μέρη τρία, θείως Θεοῦ Πρόσωπα τιμῶντα τρία» — they parted Eutychius into three parts, who divinely honoured the three Persons of God. So, the page ends, these holy martyrs received the crown of witness. No year, no city and no judge is printed for him, and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 20 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/20/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3323/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
