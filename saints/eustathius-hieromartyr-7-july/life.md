# Eustathius (7 July)

saint.gr's calendar for 7 Ιουλίου names him and gives no life. The whole of its account is one sentence, «Ο Άγιος Ευστάθιος μαρτύρησε δια πυρός» — the holy Eustathius was martyred by fire. The day's line calls him «Ιερομάρτυρας», a hieromartyr, and that is the only rank the source gives him; the page names no country, no city, no century, no year, no emperor and no judge, and it prints no hymn under his line.

Besides that sentence it carries only the couplet the synaxaria print for him: «Προς ευστάθειαν καρδίας Eυσταθίου, / Kαι πυρ συρίζον ηρεμούν ώφθη ύδωρ» — before the steadiness of Eustathius's heart, even hissing fire was seen as quiet water, a play on his name. A note on the name itself remarks on a Neoplatonist philosopher, a lawgiver of Byzantium and an eleventh-century painter who bore it; none of them is a statement about him. That is everything the source says.
