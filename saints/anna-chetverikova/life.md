# Anna (Chetverikova)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps her on 17 февраля ст. ст. in a line of three words: «Мц. Анны (1940).» The rank word is «Мц.», мученица, a woman martyr, and it is the only thing the calendar says of her besides the year; it does not call her a nun, a novice or anything else. The name link behind the line carries the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line itself leaves out, «Анна (Четверикова), мц.», and the year the line prints for her death is 1940, later than that of anyone else the day heads.

The calendar prints no life page under her name and no hymn under it either: the troparia on the day page are for other saints it heads. Nothing further is written here. The line, the rank word, the name the link behind it carries and the year 1940 are the whole of what was read, and in particular she is not identified with any of the other Annas the calendar keeps on other days.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 17 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260217.html); read 3 October 2026.*
