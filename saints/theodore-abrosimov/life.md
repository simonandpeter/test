# Theodore (Abrosimov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Феодор on 20 July and gives no life. Its line is «Прмч. Феодора (1940).» and the calendar's own name page for him adds the surname and the grade: «Феодор (Абросимов), послушник, прмч.»

That is the whole of the record. He was a novice; he died in 1940; the calendar keeps him on this day among the new martyrs of Russia. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read, and a surname is not a biography.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 20 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260720.html); read 9 October 2026.*
