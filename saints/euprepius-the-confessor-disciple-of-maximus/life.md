# Euprepius the Confessor, disciple of Maximus

Euprepius was one of the disciples of [Maximus the
Confessor](/saints/maximus-the-confessor). Like his teacher and his fellow disciples he suffered
for holding to the Orthodox mind against the heresy of the day: his tongue and his right hand
were cut off. After a single year of exile he gave up his soul to the Lord. The Greek calendar
keeps him on 20 September with Maximus and the others.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 20 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/982/saint.aspx); read 26 August 2026.*
