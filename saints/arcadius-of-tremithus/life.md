# Arcadius of Tremithus

saint.gr keeps two bishops of Tremithus in Cyprus together on this day, and it gives them one sentence of narrative apiece. On the authority of the Laurentian Codex I 70, the page says, Arcadius and [Nestor](/saints/nestor-of-tremithus) flourished in the first Christian centuries; they struggled against the idols, and they baptised many idolaters as Christians before they died peacefully. The distich the page prints over them calls them two good shepherds of Tremithus, leaping in the fair meadow of Eden.

To Arcadius alone it adds a further line, on the authority of St Nikodemos the Hagiorite: that he may perhaps have written an encomium for the translation of the relics of the great-martyr George, whose day the page gives as 3 November. The hedge, «ίσως», is the site's own word, and nothing is built on it here.

That is the whole of the page. There is no year — the first Christian centuries is a phrase without bounds, not a date — no city of his birth, no predecessor or successor at Tremithus, no act of his episcopate beyond the two verbs of that one sentence, no relic and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3872/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
