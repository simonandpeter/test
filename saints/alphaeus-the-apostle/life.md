# Alphaeus (26 May)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 26 mai reads «Sfântul Apostol Alfeu» — the holy apostle Alphaeus — and his own page on the site carries that line as heading and title and nothing else: no Viață tab, no Tropar tab, and nothing that says where he preached, when he lived or how he died. The rank the calendar gives him, «Apostol», is the whole of what is said of him, and it is what this folder holds.

He is kept on the same day as the apostle Carpus of the seventy, whose life the same calendar does print, but the page makes no statement joining the two and none is made here. He is not the Alphaeus whom the corpus already keeps as the brother of Alexander and Zosimas, a martyr kept on 28 September in the Greek calendar; that is a different man on a different day. No year is claimed, and no other calendar has been checked for him.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and refuses to say who he was. saint.gr's whole account is that there are no clear biographical particulars for him, and that on that account it cannot pronounce with certainty which Alphaeus is meant. It then sets out the two answers. Some, among them Nikodemos the Hagiorite, take him for James the son of Alphaeus, one of the Twelve and brother of the Evangelist Matthew, whom the calendar keeps on 16 Νοεμβρίου. Others — and this, the page says, is the likelier — take him for the Alphaeus, or Cleopas, who was husband of the Mary who was a kinswoman of the Mother of God, and who lived with his wife and his children working for the spreading of the Gospel. The page adds that a fragment of his relic is kept at the monastery of Esphigmenou on the Holy Mountain, and that [Abercius](/saints/abercius-martyr-26-may) and [Helen](/saints/helen-martyr-26-may), kept on the same day, are his son and his daughter.

The Ohrid Prologue keeps him on the same day and answers in one line the question the other two calendars refuse to answer. He was the father of two apostles of the Twelve, it says — namely James the son of Alphaeus, and Matthew the Evangelist — and he ended his earthly life in peace. It gives no argument for the identification and does not mention any other, and the peaceful end is this book’s alone: neither other page says anything of his death.

That is the first of the two answers the Greek page sets out and declines to choose between, and the Greek page calls the other one the likelier — the Alphaeus, or Cleopas, who was husband of the Mary who was a kinswoman of the Mother of God, and whose son and daughter the same calendar keeps on this same day. The corpus does not choose either. Both readings stand here as what the two books say, and no family tie has been written into this folder on the strength of the Serbian line.

The Russian church calendar keeps him on 26 мая ст. ст. and gives him, with the two martyrs it keeps beside him, a single page of four sentences. He was of the number of the Seventy, it says, came of the Galilean town of Capernaum, and was the father of the apostles [James](/saints/james-son-of-alphaeus), son of Alphaeus, and [Matthew](/saints/matthew-the-apostle).

The martyrs Аверкий and Елена were, by tradition, the children of the same apostle: Аверкий was tied naked in the middle of a beehive yard for confessing the faith in Jesus Christ and died a martyr of the bees’ stings, and his sister Елена was stoned to death. The calendar prints «(I)» against the whole entry and gives him no year, no reign and no manner of death of his own.
