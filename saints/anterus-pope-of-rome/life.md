# Anterus of Rome

The calendar's line is all there is of him. For 5 августа ст. ст. it prints «Сщмчч. Анфира (236) и Фавия (250), пап Римских.» — the hieromartyrs Anterus and Fabian, popes of Rome, with a year apiece — and the name link behind the first forename gives the city and nothing more. There is no life page, no persecution named, no emperor, no manner of death and no hymn.

The second of the two popes on that line the corpus already keeps from the Romanian, the Greek and the Serbian calendars on this same day, where he carries the year the Russian gives him too. Of this one the Russian calendar records a name, a see, a day and the year 236, and nothing else has been read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 5 августа ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260805.html); read 13 October 2026.*
