# Leonides

Leonides was martyred by fire, the flame of his love for God persuading him to bear the flame’s violence. That is the whole of the synaxarion’s notice for the martyr kept on 2 September, with its verse; the Synaxaristes have nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 2 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/920/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
