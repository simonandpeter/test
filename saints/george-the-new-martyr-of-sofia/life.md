# George of Sofia

George was born at Sofia in Bulgaria, of pious and God-loving parents, who had been long without children until God heard their prayer and gave them a son; and they brought him up in the teaching and admonition of the Lord. In the years of his youth, saint.gr says, the Turks were trying by force to make the Christians change their faith.

George refused. The Turks arrested him and brought him before the governor, who, astonished at his courage and his beauty, promised him riches and honours from the sultan Selim, whose reign the page gives as 1512 to 1520. The saint confessed his faith in Jesus Christ with a spiritual manliness and courage and rebuked the Muslim religion; and the Turkish governor ordered him tortured and at last thrown into the fire, where he was perfected, receiving the unfading crown of martyrdom.

Then, the page says, a heavy rain came on suddenly and put the fire out; and when night fell a light shone over what was left of his holy relic. A reverent priest gathered the remains and buried them with honour, and the relic of the holy new martyr George was afterwards found and carried to the city of Sredets in Bulgaria.

The Russian church calendar keeps him on 26 мая ст. ст. and gives him a full life. He was born of a noble Bulgarian family living in the Bulgarian capital, the city of Sredets, which is now Sofia; he was asked of God by the earnest prayer of his parents, Иоанн and Мария, who had stayed childless into their old age, and the child was baptised in the name of the great-martyr whose feast the calendar sets on 23 апреля. He had a good education, studied the Scriptures attentively, and was devout and chaste. His parents died when he was twenty-five.

Bulgaria was at that time under the power of the Turks, who were forcing Christians into the religion of Mahomet, and one day several Muslims tried to convert him: they put on his head a tafya, the round cap in which Muslims enter their houses of prayer, and he threw it on the ground. With blows and abuse they took him to their governor, who was struck by the manful look and the beauty of his face and began to urge him gently to accept their religion, promising every kind of honour and riches from the sultan Selim, whom the calendar dates 1512 to 1520. He confessed the faith in the Lord Jesus Christ boldly and firmly and rebuked the errors of that religion. The governor in his anger ordered him beaten without mercy with staves, and he held firm; the governor ordered the tortures increased, and the passion-bearer bore all the suffering patiently, calling on the Lord Jesus Christ for help. Then they led him through the city to the beating of a drum, with the cry «Не ругай Магомета и не унижай веру мусульман» — do not revile Mahomet and do not degrade the faith of the Muslims.

At the last they lit a great fire in the middle of the city to burn him, and he, weak from his wounds, fell to the ground; they threw him into the fire still living, and threw the carcasses of dogs on top, so that the Christians should not afterwards be able to find the martyr’s remains. But a heavy rain suddenly poured down and put the fire out; and when darkness came the place where the body had been thrown was lit with a bright light. One Christian priest was allowed to take the honoured remains for burial; and the metropolitan Иеремия, told what had happened, went with his clergy to the place of execution. The body was found in the quenched fire and carried into the church of the great-martyr George in the city of Sredets.
