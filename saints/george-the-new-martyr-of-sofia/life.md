# George of Sofia

George was born at Sofia in Bulgaria, of pious and God-loving parents, who had been long without children until God heard their prayer and gave them a son; and they brought him up in the teaching and admonition of the Lord. In the years of his youth, saint.gr says, the Turks were trying by force to make the Christians change their faith.

George refused. The Turks arrested him and brought him before the governor, who, astonished at his courage and his beauty, promised him riches and honours from the sultan Selim, whose reign the page gives as 1512 to 1520. The saint confessed his faith in Jesus Christ with a spiritual manliness and courage and rebuked the Muslim religion; and the Turkish governor ordered him tortured and at last thrown into the fire, where he was perfected, receiving the unfading crown of martyrdom.

Then, the page says, a heavy rain came on suddenly and put the fire out; and when night fell a light shone over what was left of his holy relic. A reverent priest gathered the remains and buried them with honour, and the relic of the holy new martyr George was afterwards found and carried to the city of Sredets in Bulgaria.
