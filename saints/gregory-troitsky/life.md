# Gregory (Troitsky)

Gregory Iosifovich Troitsky was born on 1 October 1870 in the village of
Gromok in the Morshansk district of Tambov province, now in the Bashmakovo
district of Penza region, the son of a churchman. Out of the Stavropol
seminary he was made psalmist of the Presentation church at Pashkovskaya in
the Kuban on 22 July 1892, taught in the church school at Dolzhanskaya from
1894 to 1896, was ordained deacon on 20 August 1895 and priest on 22 July
1896 for the Protection church of Sandata in the Medvezhye district of
Stavropol province, where he ran and taught the church schools and from 1897
was the deanery’s missionary against the sects and, from 1898 to 1901,
against Buddhism; from 28 August 1903 he was priest of the church of the
Archangel Michael at Alexandrovskoye, teaching the law of God in the
ministry schools and serving the diocesan school council’s branch there. On
19 April 1904 he went back to the Kuban as priest of the Trinity church at
Dolzhanskaya, teaching in its ministry school for outsiders and running its
boys’ and girls’ church schools until 1909, from 1906 as the deanery’s
ecclesiastical investigator, and from 1911 to 1913 as a deputy of the white
clergy at the district and diocesan congresses; on 30 May 1913 he was moved
to the Ascension church at Pashkovskaya, ran the church school until 1914,
taught in the men’s and women’s colleges, sat on the Yekaterinodar committee
for popular temperance and the audit committee of the diocesan girls’
school, and held the Red Cross medal for his part in its work in the war
with Japan. From January 1921, the chairman of the board of the diocesan
candle works being under arrest, he served as a member of its board, and in
July Bishop Sergius (Lavrov) of the Kuban formally gave him charge of the
works. On 15 September he was summoned to the Kuban-Black Sea Cheka and
arrested for corresponding with repressed clergy and for
counter-revolutionary agitation; he admitted no guilt, but did not hide that
in his sermons he had condemned informing on those who hid grain or failed
to pay the food tax. On 22 September the Cheka’s assistant plenipotentiary
for the clergy proposed exiling him to Archangel province, his presence in
the Kuban being “inadmissible in the interests of building the Soviet power”
there; on 23 September the college sentenced him to death as an enemy of the
working people, and on 28 September 1921 he was shot with the priests
[Andrew Kovalev](/saints/andrew-kovalev), [Gregory
Konokotin](/saints/gregory-konokotin) and [John
Yakovlev](/saints/john-yakovlev). The Holy Synod entered him among the new
martyrs on 26 December 2003; the life is by the igumen Damascene (Orlovsky);
he is kept on 15 September of the old style, 28 September, and in the
synaxis of the saints of the Kuban metropolia.
