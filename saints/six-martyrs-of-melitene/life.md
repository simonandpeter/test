# The Six Martyrs of Melitene

Six martyrs who suffered at Melitene, drowned in the sea. That is the whole of the synaxarion’s notice, with its verse: a six-starred light of Christ’s martyrs, sinking within the sea, shines out in heaven.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 30 Αυγούστου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/846/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
