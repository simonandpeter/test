# Gimnasius of Byzantium

He has a folder because the Russian calendar prints his name, and almost nothing else. The day line for 27 September reads «Мч. Каллистрата и дружины его: Гимнасия и иных (304)» — Callistratus and his company, Gimnasius and the rest — and the calendar's index of names gives him his own page as «Гимнасий Византийский, мч.», Gimnasius the Byzantine, martyr, with this one day against him and no other.

What is striking is that the long life behind that same line, which the calendar carries, never mentions him. It tells the story of [Callistratus](/saints/callistratus-the-martyr) — the Carthaginian soldier whose grandfather Neoscorus had served under Pontius Pilate and seen the Crucifixion; the leather sack that burst on a rock so that dolphins carried him ashore; the forty-nine soldiers who believed at that, and the hundred and thirty-five more who believed when the bonds fell off them in the pool — and it names not one of the company. So Gimnasius is a name the menologion keeps and the hagiography has lost, and padding that gap is exactly what this corpus will not do.

*After the Russian church calendar's [27 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260927.html), its [index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/516.html) and its [life of Callistratus and his company](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1611.htm); read 4 October 2026.*
