# Dionysius (Shchegolev)

Dionysius Yefimovich Shchegolev was born in 1870 in the village of Raskazn in the Saratov country and served as a priest at the village of Chiganak. In 1931 he was dispossessed as a kulak and sent to a special settlement in Kazakhstan, where he lived in resettlement village No. 21 in the Karaganda region. He was arrested there in August 1932 on the ground, as the file has it, that living in that village he held services and rites and spoke of his unwillingness to accept the Soviet power.

He did not admit the charge, but he did not deny the services. What he said of them is in the file: «Как священник я проводил богослужения в поселке Майкудук и однажды говорил проповедь, где было до 20-и человек, собравшихся спецпереселенцев. Проповедь была собственного сочинения на основании Священного Писания» — a sermon of his own composition, out of the Scriptures, to twenty resettled people. On 13 January 1933 a troika of the OGPU in Kazakhstan sent him to Siberia for three years.

Back from exile he lived in the Saratov country and was arrested again on 26 October 1937. On 14 November a troika of the regional administration sentenced him to be shot for anti-Soviet agitation, and the sentence was carried out on 23 November 1937 in the town of Balashov. The place of his burial is unknown. 10 ноября ст. ст. is that civil day, so the calendar keeps him on the day he died.

**The two hosts spell his surname differently and one letter is the whole of it.** days.pravoslavie.ru's name page 9565 heads him «Дионисий (Щегопев)» and prints no life; azbyka.ru heads him «Дионисий Щеголев», prints the life above, and names its source, the university's database of the new martyrs. The form recorded here is azbyka's, because it is the spelling the life and the source stand behind, and the other is set down in this paragraph rather than chosen against. Nothing on either host puts him in the same place as the archbishop of Kherson whose company the day's line gives him: he was shot at Balashov in the Saratov country, which is why azbyka keeps him with the Saratov saints. He was glorified among the new martyrs and confessors of Russia.
