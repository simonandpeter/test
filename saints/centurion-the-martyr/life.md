# Centurion of Kandavla

Centurion was one of the martyrs Centurion, Theodore, Ammianus, Julian and Oceanus of the village of Kandavli, seized under Maximian in 288; all confessed their Christian faith bravely and were burned alive, and so took the glorious crown of martyrdom. The synaxarion’s verse says he breathed out his soul eagerly into the fire, breathing the fire of divine love. The Russian calendar keeps Theodore, Mianus, Julian and Kion of Kandavla the same day and does not name him.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 4 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2347/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
