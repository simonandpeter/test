# John of Moscow

The Greek calendar keeps him on 3 Ιουλίου and prints no life of him at all. saint.gr's page carries the heading «Άγιος Ιωάννης της Μόσχας (; - 1589)», a gloss on the name John that belongs to the name and not to this man, and then one line of fact under the rubric of holy relics: «Τα Λείψανα του Αγίου βρίσκονται στον Καθεδρικό Ναό Αγίου Βασιλείου Κόκκινης Πλατείας Μόσχας» — the relics of the saint are in the cathedral of Saint Basil on Red Square in Moscow.

So the whole finding is a city, a year of death and a resting place. The page gives him no rank — not monk, not priest, not martyr, not fool-for-Christ — so none is recorded here, and nothing is reconstructed from what the name and the place might suggest. His own Russian calendar has not been read, and his life is there if it is anywhere.

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 3 июля ст. ст. — «Блж. Иоанна , Христа ради юродивого, Московского (1589).» — the same menologion day and the same year as the Greek page this folder was made from, and it does not leave his grade to be guessed at: he is the fool for Christ’s sake. It links a life, which was not opened, and prints five hymns under his name, more than for any other saint of that day.

What those hymns say of him is this: that for Christ’s sake he left his own country, the city of Vologda, of his own will; that he hated all the fair things of this world and came in spiritual longing to the city of Rostov, where he wandered as in a desert, wearing out his flesh with hunger and thirst and an immeasurable burden, keeping fast and prayer in the church porch and galling the hair of his head with ashes and pitch; that he bound his body with crosses and heavy irons for many years; and that he came at the last to Moscow, where he finished his course and was buried by the hierarchs, and where healings are given at his tomb to those who come with faith.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/03/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/659/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026; and after the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 3 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260703.html), read 9 October 2026.*
