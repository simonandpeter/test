# John of Moscow

The Greek calendar keeps him on 3 Ιουλίου and prints no life of him at all. saint.gr's page carries the heading «Άγιος Ιωάννης της Μόσχας (; - 1589)», a gloss on the name John that belongs to the name and not to this man, and then one line of fact under the rubric of holy relics: «Τα Λείψανα του Αγίου βρίσκονται στον Καθεδρικό Ναό Αγίου Βασιλείου Κόκκινης Πλατείας Μόσχας» — the relics of the saint are in the cathedral of Saint Basil on Red Square in Moscow.

So the whole finding is a city, a year of death and a resting place. The page gives him no rank — not monk, not priest, not martyr, not fool-for-Christ — so none is recorded here, and nothing is reconstructed from what the name and the place might suggest. His own Russian calendar has not been read, and his life is there if it is anywhere.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/03/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/659/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
