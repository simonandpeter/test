# Demetrius (Kazansky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and gives no life. Its line for 4 июля ст. ст. reads «Сщмч. Димитрия пресвитера (после 1937).» — the hieromartyr Demetrius, presbyter, after the year 1937 — and the line carries a link to his name and none to a reading. The name page behind it, 9556, adds his family name, Казанский, and writes his grade as «иерей», priest, where the day line says presbyter; beyond the title it prints one line, «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 23 мая (Ростов.) , 4 июля», and nothing else at all.

So the calendar sets down a priest of the Russian church killed at some time after 1937, and no more than that. No year is recorded in this folder, because «после 1937» is not the statement that he died in 1937 and the site offers nothing to narrow it. Of his three days the first is not a feast of his own: it is the general commemoration of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which this calendar prints beside the proper day of each of them, and because both his pages call him a martyr that commemoration is the ground on which the rank of new martyr stands here. The second, 23 мая, is the synaxis of the saints of Rostov, so the calendar numbers him among them.

Nothing else about him was found. Neither of those two days was read, the day page carries no hymn under his name, and azbyka.ru, which is where an account of a parish priest of the 1930s would be, was not read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 4 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260704.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9556.html), which name him and give no life; read 9 October 2026.*
