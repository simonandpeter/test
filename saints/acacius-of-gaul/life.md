# Acacius (Asius)

saint.gr names him in a single sentence and gives no life. The holy hieromartyr Acacius, or Asius, it says, was a deacon, and was martyred in Gaul in the year 303, under the emperor Diocletian, whose reign it gives as 284 to 305. Nothing else stands on the page: no birthplace, no city in Gaul, no manner of death, no relic, and no hymn.

The day's other notice fills in one thing the first does not. [Acelus](/saints/acelus-of-gaul), it says, was a subdeacon, and was martyred in Gaul in the same year 303 under the same emperor, «μαζί με τον Άγιο Ιερομάρτυρα Ακάκιο ή Άσιο» — together with the holy hieromartyr Acacius or Asius. So the two of them suffered together, and each page is the other's only corroboration.
