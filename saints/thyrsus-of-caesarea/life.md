# Thyrsus of Caesarea

Thyrsus, Leucius and Callinicus suffered for Christ under the emperor Decius (249–251) at Caesarea in Bithynia. [Leucius](/saints/leucius-of-caesarea), who had reproached the governor Cumbricius for his unjust persecution of the Christians, was beheaded after torture; Thyrsus, condemned to the cruellest tortures and torments, came through them unharmed and, by God’s will, died in peace; the pagan priest Callinicus, seeing the courage and the miracles of Thyrsus, believed in Christ, confessed the true faith boldly and was beheaded for it. The Russian calendar keeps Thyrsus, Leucius and [Coronatus](/saints/coronatus-of-caesarea) “and their company” on 17 August (Julian).
