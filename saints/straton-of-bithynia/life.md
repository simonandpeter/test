# Straton of Bithynia

Straton lived in Bithynia and was seized by the ruler there and tormented with various tortures; then they bound his hands to the branches of two cedars bent down to the ground, and let them go, so that the saint was torn in two. This happened in the years of the tyrant Licinius, in 315; the verse says the martyr Straton, bound to two cedars, one body, is shown in two parts. The Greek calendar keeps him on 13 September.
