# Straton of Bithynia

Straton lived in Bithynia and was seized by the ruler there and tormented
with various tortures; then they bound his hands to the branches of two
cedars bent down to the ground, and let them go, so that the saint was torn
in two. This happened in the years of the tyrant Licinius, in 315; the verse
says the martyr Straton, bound to two cedars, one body, is shown in two
parts. The Greek calendar keeps him on 13 September.

The Russian calendar knows him as Strator, or Stratonicus, of Nicomedia in
Bithynia, a martyr of the third century torn in two between two bent trees by
order of the governor of Bithynia, and keeps him on 9 and 13 September of the
old style, 22 and 26 September. On the 13th he stands with Seleucus of Galatia,
who after many torments was thrown with his wife to the beasts, and with the
Egyptians Cronides, Leontius and Serapion, who were bound hand and foot and
thrown into the sea, the waves carrying their bodies to land where the
Christians buried them; [Demetrius of Rostov](/saints/demetrius-of-rostov)'s
Menaion carries their memory. On the 9th he stands with the martyr Chariton.
This corpus held the two days as two folders, and they are one record now.

The two calendars do not agree on when he died: saint.gr puts him in the years
of Licinius, 315, and the Russian calendar prints the third century on both its
days. Neither chooses, so neither does this record.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 13 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2421/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026; and the Russian church calendar at azbyka.ru — [the entry](https://azbyka.ru/days/sv-strator-stratonik-nikomidijskij), read 6 September 2026, with the Sretensky day lines for [9 сентября ст. ст.](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260909.html) and [13 сентября ст. ст.](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260913.html).*
