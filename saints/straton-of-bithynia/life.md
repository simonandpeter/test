# Straton of Bithynia

Straton lived in Bithynia and was seized by the ruler there and tormented
with various tortures; then they bound his hands to the branches of two
cedars bent down to the ground, and let them go, so that the saint was torn
in two. This happened in the years of the tyrant Licinius, in 315; the verse
says the martyr Straton, bound to two cedars, one body, is shown in two
parts. The Greek calendar keeps him on 13 September. The Russian calendar
knows him as Strator, or Stratonicus, of Nicomedia in Bithynia, a martyr of
the third century torn in two between two bent trees by order of the
governor of Bithynia, and keeps him on 9 and 13 September of the old style,
22 and 26 September, with Seleucus of Galatia and the Egyptians Cronides,
Leontius and Serapion, who were thrown bound into the sea.
