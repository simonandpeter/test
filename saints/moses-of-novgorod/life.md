# Moses of Novgorod

Moses, whose name in the world was Mitrophanes, lived in the fourteenth century and was born at Novgorod in Russia. From childhood he loved the monastic life, and so he left his father's house secretly and entered the monastery of Otroch in the city of Tver, where he became a monk. His parents searched for him everywhere; when they found him, they begged him to come near them and to live as a monk in some monastery of Novgorod.

With his abbot's blessing he did as they wished, and continued his ascetic life at the monastery of Saint George in Novgorod. There he was ordained presbyter, and afterwards he was sent as archimandrite to the monastery of Yuriev. After the repose of David, archbishop of Novgorod, whom saint.gr keeps on 21 December, Moses was elected archbishop of Novgorod in the year 1325. Four years later he asked leave to withdraw, and to live as an ascetic in silence and stillness.

In 1330 he settled at the monastery of Kolmovo, but did not remain there long. He found a deserted place in the region of Derevyanitsa, took refuge there, and raised a church dedicated to the Resurrection of the Lord. In 1354 Philotheos, patriarch of Constantinople, out of deep respect for him, gave him the privilege of wearing the polystavrion.

Moses continued the work he judged pleasing to God, raising many churches and monasteries; and in one of them, the monastery of the Archangel Michael at Skovorodsk, he fell asleep in peace in the year 1362. His relic, saint.gr says, was preserved incorrupt by the grace of God, and a portion of it is kept at the Lavra of Saint Alexander Nevsky in Saint Petersburg.
