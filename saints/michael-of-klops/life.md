# Michael of Klops

Michael lived in Russia and came of a wealthy family. He was a fool for Christ's sake, and saint.gr says why he took that hard course: to hide his virtues from the world, and to escape the praise of men. So the one thing the calendar records of him is the thing he arranged that nobody should be able to record.

He was a monk at the monastery of Klops near Novgorod, and he fell asleep in peace there in the year 1456. His body was buried at Klops. The calendar calls him a wonderworker but tells none of the wonders, and the page prints no hymn and no further year.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/11/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1084/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
