# Mercurialis of Forlì

saint.gr says he lived in the fourth and fifth centuries and gives him three sentences and nothing more. He was elected the first bishop of the city of Forlì in central Italy, and he was a stout opponent of the pagans and of the Arian heretics; and he fell asleep in peace in the year 406.

The page gives him no birth year, no family, no account of his teaching and no hymn. Beyond the see it names and the two adversaries it names, nothing further about him is recorded here.
