# Laurence (10 May)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 10 mai reads «Sfântul Cuvios Lavrentie», and the page it links for him carries neither a Viață tab nor a Tropar tab — only the feast line "10 Mai" and the readings appointed for the day. No country is named, no century is given, and nothing is told of what he did or how he ended.

The corpus already keeps several saints of this name, none of them on 10 May and none of them called venerable in the same words; nothing read here connects this line to any of them, and the folder stands on the calendar's line alone.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and tells where he came from and what he built. He lived in the fourteenth century, saint.gr says, and was tonsured a monk in the monastery of the Great Lavra of the Holy Mountain. But because the heresy of Barlaam and Akindynos prevailed on the Holy Mountain, and in the Lavra above all, he left there and came to the region of Volos, to the village of Agios Lavrentios, where he began to build a monastery. There was then reigning, the page says, the pious emperor Alexios Komnenos, who sent the venerable man a chrysobull and the gold that was needed and the sacred vessels for the building of the church. The church was finished in the year 1378 — which no reign of an Alexios Komnenos can be made to cover, and the page's two statements are left here as its own.

Afterwards he founded a skete in the name of the Transfiguration of the Saviour and of the Prophet Elijah, which quickly won a great name and became a pole of attraction for many young and educated monks. Laurence passed his life in fasting and prayer and fell asleep in peace in deep old age. The apolytikion the page prints says that the village found in him a great glory, Laurence the divine among mortals, most tried of ascetics among ascetics and a most wonderful physician of the sick; and saint.gr counts him, on its page for the synaxis of the saints of Demetrias, among those who lived, taught, practised asceticism and were martyred in that region.
