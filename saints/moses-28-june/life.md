# Moses (28 June)

saint.gr's calendar for 28 Ιουνίου names him and gives him one sentence. Its line reads «Όσιος Μωυσής ο Αναχωρητής», Moses the anchorite, and the page behind that line says only that he died in peace. No country is named, no century, no desert, no year and no relics, and the page prints no hymn for him.

Above the sentence stands a couplet of the synaxarion which sets him beside his namesake: «Tων αρετών ανήλθε Mωσής εις όρος, Kακεί τέθνηκεν, ως ο Mωσής εις όρος» — Moses went up the mountain of the virtues, and there he died, as Moses died upon a mountain. The page glosses the name itself, from the Hebrew, as him who was saved out of the waters. That is the whole of the entry.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/28/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1915/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
