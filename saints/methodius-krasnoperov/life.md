# Methodius (Krasnoperov)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him in a single line for 4 февраля ст. ст. — «Сщмч. Мефодия, еп. Петропавловского (1921).» — a hieromartyr and a bishop who died in 1921. The name link behind the line gives the calendar's own fuller form, «Святитель Мефодий (Красноперов), епископ Петропавловский», which adds the surname and names the see as Petropavlovsk. The calendar prints no life page for him, and none is given here: the line and the name link are the whole of what it records.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 4 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260204.html); read 3 October 2026.*
