# Theodotus (1 January)

saint.gr's calendar for 1 Ιανουαρίου names Theodotus among the saints of the day and prints almost nothing about him. Its page gives the name, the reading it takes from it — from Θεός and δοτός, the grace and gift of God — and a two-line epigram in which Theodotus says he will not bear it unless his head is cut off for God's sake. Then comes one sentence of narrative: the holy Theodotus was martyred by the sword.

That is the whole of what the source holds. Nothing on the page names a place, a year, an emperor or a companion, and nothing is supplied here. The Greek calendar keeps him on the first day of January and says he died by the sword, and that is what is recorded.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/5/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
