# Arsenius of Tver

saint.gr gives this Arsenius three sentences. He lived in Russia at the end of the fourteenth and the beginning of the fifteenth century; early on he entered the monastic polity of the Lavra of Kyiv, and he was elected bishop of the city of Tver; and he fell asleep in peace, in a holy manner. The head of the page dates his repose to 1409, and a portion of his relic, it says, is kept at the Lavra of St Alexander Nevsky in St Petersburg.

That is all the notice holds. It gives no year for his birth, no name for his parents or his elder, nothing of his years in the see, no foundation, no distich and no hymn.

Two other entries on the same Greek day have particulars that run alongside his almost exactly: [Arethas the Recluse](/saints/arethas-the-recluse), of Tver and of the Lavra of Kyiv, elected bishop and then withdrawn into reclusion, whose repose the site also puts in 1409, and a bare «Όσιος Αρσένιος εκ Ρωσίας», born at Tver, an ascetic of the Lavra of Kyiv and of the monastery of the Dormition of Zheltikovo. Arethas bears another name and is kept here as another man. The second Arsenius bears this one's own name and was not drafted at all, because nothing on either page tells the two apart.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 2 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/02/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3891/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
