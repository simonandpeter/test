# Militsa (Kuvshinova)

The Russian calendar's line is all there is of her. It names her alone on this day, «Мц. Милицы (1938)» — the martyr Militsa, 1938 — and the name behind that line reads «Милица (Кувшинова), мц.», Militsa Kuvshinova, martyr. That is her surname, her rank and the year of her death, and the calendar gives nothing else.

There is no life page behind her name and no hymn for her on the day. Nothing here says where she was born, where she lived, where she was arrested or where she died; the year 1938 is the calendar's own and the only date it prints. She is one of those the Russian church keeps from the Soviet years, named and not described.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 23 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260123.html); read 3 October 2026.*
