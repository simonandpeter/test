# Epimachus (6 July)

saint.gr's calendar for 6 Ιουλίου names him and gives no life. The whole of its account is one sentence, «Ο Άγιος Επίμαχος μαρτύρησε δια ξίφους» — the holy Epimachus was martyred by the sword. The page names no country, no city, no century, no year, no emperor and no judge, it gives him no rank beyond Άγιος, and it prints no hymn under his line.

Besides that sentence it carries only the couplet the synaxaria print for him: «Πλέον τι πάσχειν ήθελον και του ξίφους, / Έφασκε πάσχων Eπίμαχος εκ ξίφους» — I would have wished to suffer something more even than the sword, said Epimachus as he suffered by the sword. That is everything the source says of him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/677/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
