# Alphaeus, companion of Zacchaeus

saint.gr's calendar for 18 November keeps «Άγιοι Ζακχαίος ο Διάκονος και Αλφαίος», and gives each of the two the year 307. The page begins with the deacon: [Zacchaeus](/saints/zacchaeus-the-deacon-martyr), deacon of the Church of Gadeira — a small island of Spain, the page explains, lying in the Atlantic very close to the Spanish mainland — was dragged in chains to the tribunal of the idolaters and confessed Christ with courage, was tortured hard and thrown into prison, and was set for four days and nights upon a special instrument of torture.

Afterwards, it continues, they brought Alphaeus to the tribunal, who was full of the Holy Spirit, and tore his sides. Then they burnt his wounds and threw him into the prison, as they had his fellow contestant Zacchaeus. The next day they beheaded them both, and so their holy souls flew to the throne of God.

The calendar gives Alphaeus no country of his own, no rank in the Church and no hymn; what it gives him is the two-line verse over his name, which says: Alphaeus, endure, if your head is cut off, and you will have for your portion the uncut Trinity. The corpus holds other folders of this name — an apostle, a brother of Alexander, a martyr of 10 May — and none of them is kept on 18 November in any calendar.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3039/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
