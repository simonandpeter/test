# Aquila the Eparch

saint.gr's calendar for 20 Μαρτίου names a martyr Aquila whom it calls the Eparch, and its page for him is one sentence long: he was martyred by the sword, and we have no further details of the saint's life. The office in the day's line is the only thing the page tells of his station, and this life says no more than the page does.

Over him it prints a couplet — thou wast seen as a spotless sacrifice, O Aquila, cut down by the sword for the spotless Master — and beside it the name's two glosses, from the Latin acula, a small needle, one sharp and useful as a needle is, or else from aquila, an eagle. The page gives him no country, no year, no emperor and no relics, and prints no hymn; nothing has been taken from anywhere else and nothing supplied from memory.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 20 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/20/index.aspx) and [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/3929/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
