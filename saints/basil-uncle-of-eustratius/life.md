# Basil, uncle of Eustratius of Mount Olympus

saint.gr's calendar for 9 Ιανουαρίου keeps two men together under one line and one page, «Όσιοι Βασίλειος και Γρηγόριος οι Θαυματουργοί» — Basil and [Gregory](/saints/gregory-uncle-of-eustratius), the wonderworkers. The page says of them that they were the uncles of [Eustratius of Mount Olympus](/saints/eustratius-of-mount-olympus), whom the same day keeps, and that they lived in the ninth century. Eustratius's own page adds that they were his mother's brothers.

It was to these two that Eustratius came. His page tells how he left his parents in his twentieth year and went up to Olympus in Bithynia, to the monastery of Augarus, where his uncles were living as monks; there they received him, and he took up the hard life of a monk beside them.

Of Basil himself saint.gr says only what it says of both: that they were counted worthy to receive from God the gift of working wonders, and that they fell asleep in peace. It gives no year within the century, no office, and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 9 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/09/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1105/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
