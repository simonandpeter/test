# Bessarion, Archbishop of Larissa

Bessarion was born at Porta Panagia near Trikala in 1490, a few years after the fall of Constantinople; though conditions were very hard he was nursed and raised by his devout parents in the sacred waters of the faith and the ideals of the nation, in a family full of the fear of God. From youth he was marked by intelligence and good sense, uprightness of character, purity and sobriety, humility and deep faith and boundless love, and very young showed his bent for monasticism: at only ten he became a novice under the metropolitan of Larissa, Mark, who ordained him deacon and presbyter; in 1517 he was chosen bishop of Elassona, in 1521 took the government of the bishopric of Stagoi, and in March 1527 was raised to the throne of the metropolis of Larissa, holding too, as locum tenens until August 1529, the bishopric — the synaxarion’s life runs on past the point read here. He reposed, the Romanian page says, on 13 September 1541, aged nearly fifty-one, and by tradition his relics — the Greek and Romanian calendars keep him on 15 September with a troparion that calls him Bessarion the merciful, who loved the poor and supplied them.
