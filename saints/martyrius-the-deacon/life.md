# Martyrius the Deacon

saint.gr's calendar for 25 Οκτωβρίου names two venerable men of one name together, Martyrius the Deacon and [Martyrius the Recluse](/saints/martyrius-the-recluse), and then says that it has nothing to tell of either: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τους βίους των Ρώσων Οσίων» — we have no details of the lives of the Russian venerable ones. That they were Russians, and that one of them lived enclosed and the other served as a deacon, is all that the entry carries.

There is no monastery, no century, no year, no teacher and no companions beyond each other; the page prints no couplet for them and no hymn, and lists no relics. This folder says no more than the page does, and the epithet in its name is the page's own.

The Russian calendar keeps him on 25 октября ст. ст. and is the first source this folder has had with anything in it. He laboured in the monastery of the Kyiv caves — one of its pages says in the fourteenth century, the other and the day's line in the thirteenth and fourteenth — and having pleased God by purity and a strict life of fasting he was glorified with the gift of wonderworking while he was still alive. His holy relics rest in the Far caves, which the page also calls the caves of the second of the monastery's founders.

What else it has of him comes out of a service book rather than a life. His holy name, it says, is remembered in the seventh ode of the canon to the venerable of the Far caves, and what is praised there is his industry, his righteousness and his purity of heart, together with the gift of driving out demons and of healing sicknesses.

The day page carries his troparion (глас 1) and his kontakion (глас 2), and both of them are about the diaconate: the one calls him an honourable hierodeacon who served the Lord well in that order and was found as the Lord's servant where his Lord is, the other an honourable deacon, the boast of monastics. [The other Martyrius of these caves](/saints/martyrius-the-recluse), whom the same line keeps with him, has no hymn and no story at all.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 25 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/25/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2616/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 25 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261025.html) and his two pages, [one](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4590.htm) and [the other](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life6731.htm), read 4 October 2026.*
