# Pactobius of Sebasteia

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar for 3 ноября ст. ст. prints a whole company in one line: «Мчч. Аттика, Агапия, Евдоксия, Катерия, Истукария, Пактовия, Никтополиона и дружины их (ок. 320).» — seven names in the genitive, «and their company» after them, and the year about 320. His is the sixth of the seven, and the name link behind it gives the calendar's nominative with his by-name, «Пактовий Севастийский», of Sebasteia. The caption of the icon on the day page repeats all seven in the nominative and closes «и др.», and others.

The calendar prints no life page for the company and no hymn under its line, so nothing of their suffering is told here. What this day does is seat him beside four men this corpus already keeps: [Atticus](/saints/atticus-of-sebasteia), [Agapius](/saints/agapius-2-november), [Eudoxius](/saints/eudoxius-of-sebasteia) and [Carterius](/saints/carterius-of-sebasteia), whom the Greek calendar keeps as soldiers of Sebasteia under Licinius on its own 2 Νοεμβρίου in a line of ten. Four of the seven names printed here are theirs; five of the Greek ten this line does not print at all, and they are the «дружина» it leaves unnamed.

Two of the seven have no folder anywhere in this corpus and are made folders on this day, he and [Istucarius](/saints/istucarius-of-sebasteia). The seventh is left unseated: the corpus holds a senator of Sebasteia from the Greek line whose name may or may not be the same word as the one printed here, and whether it is is a question for the author and not for a reader.

The line gives him his rank and nothing else — «Мчч.», martyrs — so he is recorded a martyr and not a soldier, the soldiering belonging to the Greek notice of the ten and not to anything printed on this day. The year is this line's «ок. 320»; the Greek calendar dates the company 315, and that disagreement is recorded on the four folders that hold the Greek year.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 3 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261103.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/1713.html); read 4 October 2026.*
