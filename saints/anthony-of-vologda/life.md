# Anthony of Vologda

saint.gr's calendar names him on 17 Ιανουαρίου and gives no life. Its page for him carries the years in the heading — «Αγιος Αντωνιος Επισκοπος Βολογκντα Της Ρωσιας (1526 - 1588)» — and then one line of text: «Βλέπε βιογραφία του στις 26 Οκτωβρίου», see his biography on 26 October. So what the day claims of him is this much and no more: that he was a bishop of Vologda in Russia, that he was born in 1526, and that he died in 1588.

The page for 26 October has not been read here, and nothing from it is repeated. No hymn is printed for him on this day and no account of his see. This folder records the line the Greek calendar prints for 17 Ιανουαρίου and the heading behind it, and claims nothing beyond them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 17 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/17/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1293/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
