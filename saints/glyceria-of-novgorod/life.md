# Glyceria of Novgorod

Glyceria of Novgorod lived in the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries, and she was the daughter of Panteleimon, a municipal officer of Novgorod. That is all saint.gr says of her life; she fell asleep in peace in the year 1522. The Greek calendar keeps her on the same day as the second-century martyr [Glyceria of Traianopolis](/saints/glyceria-of-traianopolis), who is another woman altogether and has a page of her own.

Her incorrupt relics, the page continues, were found on 14 July 1572 — the date is from the second Chronicle of Novgorod — near the stone church of Saints Florus and Laurus, and were buried there by Leonid, archbishop of Novgorod. During that burial of her holy relic, healings of the sick and wonders were worked. After the revolution of 1917 there is no information about what became of the relic.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 13 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/13/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1591/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
