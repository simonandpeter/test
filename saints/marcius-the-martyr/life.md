# Marcius

saint.gr's calendar for 27 Ιουνίου keeps the two of them together: «Άγιοι Μάρκιος και Μαρκία». The page's couplet makes the pairing its subject — «Ήνωντο κλήσει Mάρκιος και Mαρκία, Oυς και τομή συνήψεν η διά ξίφους» — Marcius and Marcia were joined in their name, and the cutting by the sword joined them too. It says nothing of where they were from, or when, or what they were to one another beyond that.

Of [Marcia](/saints/marcia-companion-of-marcius) the page says only that she was martyred by the sword. Of Marcius it has a little more: that he was brought before Perinios, the ruler of Iconium, and because he confessed Christ they flayed his body, set him upon a red-hot gridiron, cut out his tongue and at the last beheaded him. No year is given, no emperor, no relics, and the page prints no hymn.
