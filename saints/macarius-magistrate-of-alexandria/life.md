# Macarius the Magistrate of Alexandria

Macarius was a magistrate and Andrew a curator; both, in the persecution of Decius, were seized at Alexandria by the governor Valerian and after many tortures beheaded — dying by the sword, the verse says, both walked the blessed road. The Greek synaxarion keeps the martyrs of Alexandria under Decius in separate entries on 6 September; the Russian calendar counts them as the eleven with the presbyter Faustus and the deacon Abibus.
