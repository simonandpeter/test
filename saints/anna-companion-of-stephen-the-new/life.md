# Anna, companion of Stephen the New

saint.gr keeps the venerable-martyr Anna on 28 November, and what it says of her is one sentence: that she suffered together with [Stephen the New](/saints/stephen-the-new), whose page it keeps on the same day, for the holy icons, after they had beaten her until she died. The rank word it gives her, Ὁσιομάρτυς, says that she was a monastic as well as a martyr, but the page does not name her monastery.

Nothing else is recorded here. It gives her no country, no family, no year and no emperor by name, and prints no hymn for her. Her couplet is the only other thing on the page: «Μάστιξιν Ἄνναν εὐτόνως τετυμμένην, Ἔδειξε Χριστὸς εὐπρεπῶς ἐστεμμένην» — Anna, soundly beaten with scourges, Christ showed becomingly crowned.

The Охридски пролог gives her one short paragraph and it is the whole of her dossier here. She was a well-born woman, it says, who after her husband’s death was tonsured into the monastic life by [Stephen the New](/saints/stephen-the-new). The emperor Copronymus urged her to say that she had had unlawful bodily relations with Stephen, so that he might by that means lower Stephen before the people.

But this holy woman would have no part at all in the emperor’s plot against a man she honoured as her spiritual father. For that she was scourged, and then thrown into prison, where she gave up her holy soul to God. The entry gives her no year, no reign of her own and no century, so nothing has been written into this folder’s dates, which held none before and hold none now. She is not the Ана of this same day’s first entry, who is Stephen’s own mother and has no folder in this corpus.
