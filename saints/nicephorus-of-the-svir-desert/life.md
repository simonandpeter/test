# Nicephorus of the Svir desert

saint.gr's calendar for 9 Φεβρουαρίου names the venerable Nicephorus with [Gennadius](/saints/gennadius-of-the-svir-desert), and the whole of what its page for the two of them says is one sentence: «Οι Όσιοι Νικηφόρος και Γεννάδιος έγιναν μοναχοί και ασκήτεψαν στην έρημο της μονής Σβίρ, στην περιοχή Όλονετς της Ρωσίας, κατά τον 16ο αιώνα μ.Χ. Κοιμήθηκαν με ειρήνη» — they became monks and practised ascesis in the desert of the Svir monastery, in the Olonets region of Russia, in the sixteenth century; they fell asleep in peace.

The day's own line calls the two of them «εν Βολογντά», in Vologda, which is not where the page then puts them; saint.gr does not reconcile the two places and the disagreement is recorded on this folder's Greek row rather than settled. Beyond the century, the region and the peaceful end, nothing is printed: no parentage, no year, no hymn and no relics, and the page adds only its gloss on the name, that Νικηφόρος is the one who holds and gives victory.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 9 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/09/index.aspx) and [the page for the two of them](https://www.saint.gr/3633/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
