# Gabriel of Novgorod and Saint Petersburg

saint.gr's calendar for 26 January names Gabriel, metropolitan of Novgorod and Saint Petersburg, and says in as many words that it has no life to give. The whole of the page's account of him is this: that he fell asleep in peace in the year 1801, and that «Δεν έχουμε περισσότερες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου» — we have no further details of the saint's life.

So what is recorded here is what the Greek calendar records: the day, the name, the two sees it joins in his title, the year of his repose, and its own admission that it knows no more. The page's heading gives no year of birth. It prints no troparion and no kontakion. The Russian calendar for the corresponding day is where this entry could be finished, and it has not been read.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1342/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
