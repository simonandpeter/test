# Gregory of Pelshma

The Greek synaxarion says it has no details of the life of Gregory, a Russian monk, and gives only the year of his death, 1442, in the heading of its entry.

The Russian church calendar keeps him on the Julian 30 September and prints «Прп. Григория Пельшемского, Вологодского чудотворца (1442)» — Venerable Gregory of Pelshma, wonderworker of Vologda — which agrees with saint.gr on the year. Its index of names reverses the emphasis and heads him «Преподобный Григорий Вологодский, чудотворец», for the city rather than the river. The day carries a troparion and a kontakion for him, both of them naming the wilderness he settled and the gift of wonders that followed; neither was taken into this folder.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 30 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/1014/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026; the Russian church calendar keeps him on the Julian 30 September — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260930.html); read 4 October 2026.*
