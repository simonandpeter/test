# Gregory of Pelshma

The Greek synaxarion says it has no details of the life of Gregory, a Russian monk, and gives only the year of his death, 1442, in the heading of its entry.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 30 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/1014/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
