# Hilarion (6 May)

saint.gr names three men together for this day and tells one story for all of them. The venerable [Mamas](/saints/mamas-6-may), [Pachomius](/saints/pachomius-6-may) and Hilarion lived their ascetic life in a desert place, the page says, and passed their monastic days in fasting, in vigils and in works of charity; and having lived in concord of soul and in a conscience set on the good, they fell asleep in holiness and in peace. Their synaxis used to be kept, it adds, «εν Οχείαις», at Ocheiai.

The couplet over them puts the three in one line — Mamas set out with Hilarion and Pachomius on the good departure — and that, with the meaning of each name, is the whole of the page. It gives no country, no century, no monastery and no emperor, and prints no hymn for any of the three. Nothing further is claimed for Hilarion here than the rank, the day and the company.
