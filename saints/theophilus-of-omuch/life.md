# Theophilus of Omuch

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him with his companion on 21 октября ст. ст. as «Прпп. Феофила и Иакова Омучских (ок. 1412)», and carries two short notices which differ about whom the two men kept company with before they founded their own house. The page headed for the pair says they struggled on the island of Konevets together with [Arsenius](/saints/arsenius-of-konevits); the page headed for Theophilus alone says he was on Konevets for a time together with [James](/saints/james-of-omuch), and does not name Arsenius at all.

What both agree on is the foundation. In 1396, on the river Omucha in the Pskov diocese, not far from the town of Porkhov, the two of them founded a hermitage in honour of the Dormition of the Mother of God. The epithet both men carry, Омучский, is that river's.

There, the calendar says, after many labours and ascetic struggles, Theophilus fell asleep in peace; his own page dates it 29 December, about 1412, and the day line gives the year alone as about 1412. Nothing is said of where either man came from, of when he was born, or of what became of the hermitage afterwards.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 21 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261021.html), [the notice for the pair](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4566.htm) and [his own](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life6893.htm); read 4 October 2026.*
