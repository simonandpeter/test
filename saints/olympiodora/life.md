# Olympiodora

saint.gr's calendar for 31 December keeps the holy Olympiodora, and its whole account of her is two sentences: «Η Αγία Ολυμπιοδώρα μαρτύρησε δια πυρός. Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέριες για τον βίο της Αγίας» — the holy Olympiodora was martyred by fire; we have no other details about the saint's life. There is no century here, no country, no city, no judge and no hymn.

The one other thing the page keeps of her is the couplet set over the entry, which answers her name: «Αγώνι προς πυρ της Oλυμπιοδώρας, / Ύμνος το δώρον, ουκ Oλυμπίων πίτυς» — for Olympiodora's contest against the fire, the gift is a hymn, and not the pine of the Olympian games. That is her death named again, and the fire is all the page will say of it.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 31 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/31/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1509/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
