# Leo (1 July)

saint.gr's calendar for 1 Ιουλίου names him a hermit and gives him one sentence of life: «Ο Όσιος Λέων ήταν ασκητής, που ζούσε γυμνός λόγω αυστηρής ασκήσεως. Απεβίωσε ειρηνικά» — the venerable Leo was an ascetic who lived naked on account of the strictness of his asceticism, and he died in peace. No place, no desert, no century and no year is printed, so none is recorded here.

The page adds the distich the synaxaria set over him, which reads his nakedness back to Eden: «Oυ σαρκί πεισθείς πριν θανείν Eύα Λέων, / Ως πρώτος Aδάμ γυμνός ων ουκ ησχύνου» — Leo, not persuaded by the flesh as Eve was before her death, you were naked like the first Adam and were not ashamed.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1964/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
