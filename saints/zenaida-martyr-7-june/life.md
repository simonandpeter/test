# Zenaida (7 June)

doxologia.ro's calendar names her and gives no life. The line for 7 iunie reads «Sfânta Muceniță Zenaida», the holy martyr Zenaida, and her page on the site has neither a Viață tab nor a Tropar tab: it carries only the day's Epistle and Gospel and, under the headings for icons, articles and photographs, a single entry that belongs to the three saints of the day together.

That entry is titled «Sfântul Ierarh Mucenic Teodot, Sfânta Cuvioasă Sebastiana și Sfânta Muceniță Zenaida», which is how the site groups her with [Theodotus](/saints/theodotus-of-ancyra) and [Sebastiana](/saints/sebastiana-venerable-7-june) on this day; it is a title, not a life. So the source gives her name, her rank as a martyr and her day, and nothing else — no country, no year, no manner of death, no hymn.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 7 iunie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/7-iunie) and [her page](https://doxologia.ro/sfanta-mucenita-zenaida); read 24 September 2026.*
