# Daniel (Alferov)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him for 21 февраля ст. ст. in one line that it gives to three — «Сщмчч. Александра, Даниила и Григория пресвитеров (1930).» — three hieromartyr presbyters, all of 1930. The line carries the forenames only, declined, and no surname for any of them.

The name link behind the second of those names carries the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line leaves out: «Даниил (Алферов), иерей, сщмч.», a priest of that name. Three entries on one line are three people, and they are kept apart here by the names and the offices the calendar's own links give them.

It prints no life page for him and no hymn under his name; the hymns it sets on this day belong to two of the older saints it heads and to an icon of the Mother of God. It gives him no parish, no diocese, no place and no manner of death. Nothing further is written here, and nothing is reasoned out from the surname or from the year he shares with the other two.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 21 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260221.html); read 3 October 2026.*
