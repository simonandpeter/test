# Nicholas the Sicilian

Nicholas was born in Sicily, from which he took his name. He loved the monastic life and was led by God’s providence to Mount Neotakos, now called Skoteini, in Euboea; the place shown him had no water, and he struck a rock and water came out.

Later he was captured by Hagarene pirates. When they set out to carry him off by ship with many other Christians, they were in danger from a storm and from thirst; after fervent prayer the saint stilled the storm and turned the salt water sweet for those on board. The Hagarenes, astonished, brought him back to his monastery and freed the captives.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 23 August — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/850/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
