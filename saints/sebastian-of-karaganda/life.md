# Sebastian of Karaganda

Sebastian, Stephen Vasilevich Fomin in the world, was born in the year 1884 in the province of Oryol in Russia. In 1906 he entered the monastery of Optina and was tonsured a monk there with the name Sebastian. He was ordained deacon in 1923 and presbyter in 1927, and he served in the Karaganda region of Kazakhstan; and there, saint.gr says, he fell asleep in peace in the year 1966.

That is the whole of the page. It says nothing of the elders under whom he lived at Optina, nothing of how he came from Oryol to Kazakhstan or of what the years between his ordination and his ministry there were like, and nothing of his burial or his relics. It prints no couplet and no hymn, and this folder does not supply what the calendar does not say.
