# Sergius (Losev)

Sergius Losev was born on 20 September 1893 in the village of Vanilovo in the Bronnitsy district of Moscow province, the son of a psalm-reader. He finished the Kolomna church school in 1908 and the Moscow seminary in 1915, served two and a half months as a psalm-reader, and married Barbara, a schoolteacher who became his faithful helper; that year he was ordained deacon, and a year and a half later priest. From 1926 until his arrest he served at the church of the Nativity of the Theotokos in the village of Kapotnya, and through the years of persecution did all he could to keep it from being closed, more than once asking his parishioners to help pay the taxes the local authorities laid on it.

He was arrested on 27 November 1937 and held in the Taganka prison in Moscow. Questioned, he admitted collecting money but said all of it had gone to the needs of the church, and that services held during the harvest were held on church feasts and Sundays with no aim of keeping anyone from work, and he denied anti-Soviet agitation. The village soviet’s chairman wrote the character reference the NKVD asked for, and on 7 December 1937 the NKVD troika sentenced him to ten years of labour camp, in a camp in Azerbaijan. The same false witnesses then denounced his wife; she was arrested on 16 February 1938, sentenced to death by the troika on 25 February and shot on 7 March 1938 at Butovo. Father Sergius died in the camp on 29 September 1942 and was buried in an unmarked grave.
