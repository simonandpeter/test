# Karpasios

saint.gr says Karpasios lived in Gaul in the fourth and fifth centuries. He loved the monastic life and lived as a hermit on the Lérins islands, and there he was joined in spirit to [Honoratus](/saints/honoratus-of-arles) — whom the same calendar keeps on 16 January and whose own page names him archbishop of Arles — and to Honoratus's elder brother [Venandios](/saints/venandios), whom it keeps on 30 May.

Together the three of them visited the monastic communities of the East, to come to know holy ascetics and be taught by their manner of life. In the course of that pilgrimage Venandios fell asleep in Greece, while the other two returned to their hermitage. There Honoratus founded a monastery, and after his own election as bishop he installed Karpasios in it as abbot.

Having lived in a manner pleasing to God, Karpasios fell asleep in peace in the year 430. The page spells his name two ways — Καρπάσιος in the calendar's line and Καρπάθιος twice in the body — and gives him no year of birth, no wonder and no hymn.
