# Therapon of Cyprus

Doxologia's life of Therapon begins by saying that almost nothing about him can be told. Where he was from, what family he came of, what parents bore him, in what age he lived, against whom he fought and how he won, and in what way he received the crown of martyrdom — none of it can be said, because the memory of him was lost with time. The Romanian calendar names him for 14 mai as a hieromartyr and bishop in Cyprus, and the page is honest that the little it gives rests on an old oral tradition and not on a written life.

What the page does hold to is this. That he chose the monastic life is shown, it says, by his icons, which depict him in that habit and schema. That he was bishop of the island of Cyprus, that he was brought to Christ through blood, and that he finished the struggle of the contest — these were taken from an old story handed down by tradition without being written, and as it was taught by those who came before, the page says, so we believe it.

His honoured relics were brought to Constantinople. The Agarenes were taking counsel to fall upon the island of Cyprus, and the saint himself gave the order by a revelation that he should be moved; and there, where he now lies, he works wonders continually for those who run to him with faith.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 14 mai — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/14-mai) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-sfintitului-mucenic-terapont-episcopul-ciprului); doxologia prints no troparion for him; read 24 September 2026.*
