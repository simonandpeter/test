# Avacum the Deacon

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 17 decembrie reads «Sfântul Mucenic Avacum, diaconul», and the page the calendar links under that name carries neither a Viață tab nor a Tropar tab: the heading, the day, and nothing more. What the line says is that he was a martyr, that he was a deacon, that he is called Avacum, and that the Romanian church keeps him on this day.

The prophet Habakkuk, whose name is also Avacum in Romanian, comes into the long life the day's other saints share, carrying a meal to Daniel in the lions' pit; this is not that man, and the line's «diaconul» is the only thing that is known here about him. No year, no country, no account of his martyrdom and no hymn has been taken from anywhere else, and none has been supplied from memory. If a life for him is printed elsewhere, it has not been read.

The Ohrid Prologue is the first dossier this folder has had: doxologia.ro gave the name and the rank, and the Prologue gives the man. He was the fellow-brother and the deacon of [Paisius](/saints/paisius-of-trnava), abbot of the monastery of Trnava near Čačak. Both of them, as Christians, were impaled on the stake by the Turks, at Belgrade on the Kalemegdan, on 17 December 1814 — the same day of the same month this calendar keeps them on.

Carrying his own stake through the streets of Belgrade, the book says, the brave Avacum sang. And when his mother begged him weeping to turn Turk, so that he might save his own life, this wonderful soldier of Christ answered her — and there the prose of the entry stops, at a colon. The answer stands in the day's verse, where the book put it: "Mother of mine, for the milk I thank you, but no thanks for this counsel of yours: the Serb belongs to Christ, and rejoices in death." Those are the only words of his the book preserves, and they are not a hymn and were never sung as one.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 17 decembrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/17-decembrie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-avacum-diaconul), which prints the name only; read 28 September 2026; and the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 17. децембар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2025-12-30&prolog=1), read 7 October 2026.*
