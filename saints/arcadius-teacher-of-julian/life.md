# Arcadius, teacher of Julian and Euboulos

saint.gr gives the venerable Arcadius three sentences. He was an ascetic in Cyprus in the fourth century, and the page describes him with a phrase out of its own distich, «ὀσφὺν νοητὴν ἀρεταῖς ἐζωσμένος» — his spiritual loins girded about with the virtues. He made many struggles in the years of the reign of Constantine the Great, and did good to many, not only by his life and his virtue but by his teaching and his counsel. And he fell asleep in peace.

What his teaching came to is on another page of the same day. saint.gr's notice for [Julian](/saints/julian-companion-of-euboulos) and [Euboulos](/saints/euboulos-the-physician) calls them disciples of this Arcadius, who came to him and, leaning on his words, took care for their virtue and their growth towards God; they were seized under Julian the Apostate and beheaded, and when Arcadius learned what had happened he grieved, but gave thanks to God who had arranged it so.

The page gives him no city in Cyprus, no monastery or cave, no elder, no year of birth or of repose, no relic and no hymn. Two other men of this name stand close to him and are not him: the same Greek day keeps an [Arcadius](/saints/arcadius-archbishop-of-cyprus), archbishop of Cyprus, on a page of its own, and the corpus keeps [Arcadius the Wonderworker](/saints/arcadius-of-arsinoe), bishop of Arsinoe in Cyprus, whom saint.gr keeps on the twenty-ninth of August. Nothing on any of the three pages connects them, and nothing is decided here.

The Russian church keeps him on this same day and gives him a line whose words would send a careless reader to the wrong folder: «Прп. Аркадия Кипрского (IV).» Of Cyprus — which is the by-name this corpus has given to another Arcadius altogether, the archbishop of the island, who already relates this one. The life behind the line settles it in four sentences and they are this man's: from his youth he gave himself wholly to monastic labours; he laboured on the island of Cyprus in the days of the emperor Constantine the Great, whose reign it gives as 306 to 337; **he was the teacher of the holy martyrs Иулиан the physician and Еввул, put to death by Julian the Apostate, whose reign it gives as 361 to 363**; and having wept for the martyrdom of his own disciples and buried their bodies, he himself soon departed peacefully to the Lord. That is the same man the Greek calendar prints as «Όσιος Αρκάδιος» on this day, and the display name this folder already carries says so.

The Russian pages therefore give him a death this folder had no row for, and two of them agree on it. The line itself closes with (IV), the fourth century, and the life implies the date by arithmetic rather than stating it: a death soon after the burial of disciples killed under a reign that began in 361. azbyka.ru, read for the same civil day, prints the year outright — «прп. Аркадия Кипрского (ок. 361)» — so the death recorded here is about 361, on two Russian sources read against each other, and the `floruit` of 306 to 337 that came off the Greek page is left exactly as it was, because it is the span of his labours on Cyprus and not of his life. Nothing in either Russian page gives him a monastery, a rank beyond прп., a relic or a hymn: the day prints four hymns and none of them is his.

**The two Arcadii of this day are the trap, and the Russian calendar does not help with it.** Both were on Cyprus, both are kept on 6 March, and the one Russian line of the two that exists calls its man «Кипрского» — of Cyprus — where the corpus's `Arcadius of Cyprus` is the archbishop of the see, whom days.pravoslavie.ru does not print on this day at all. The life behind the line is what separates them: an ascetic who taught two martyrs of Julian's reign, against a bishop of the island's throne. The folders already relate one another in both directions, and this reading is why.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3849/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 6 марта ст. ст. — [the Russian day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260306.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life583.htm), which is the only one of the three read that names his disciples and the reign they died under, with [azbyka.ru's calendar for the same civil day](https://azbyka.ru/days/2026-03-19) read for the year, read 6 October 2026.*
