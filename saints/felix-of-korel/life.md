# Felix of Korel

saint.gr gives the pair one sentence. The venerable [Antony](/saints/antony-of-korel) and Felix of Korel lived in the fifteenth century, it says, and practised asceticism in the monastery of Saint Nicholas which [Euthymius](/saints/euthymius-of-karelia), the Enlightener of Karelia, had founded; they worked as missionaries for the spreading of the word of God and the strengthening of the Orthodox faith, and they fell asleep in peace.

That is the whole of the notice: no parentage, no year of birth or of death, no wonder, no account of the mission, and neither couplet nor hymn. His name is not even settled between saint.gr's own two entries for this day: Euthymius's page names the pair Antony and Eutyches, and this one names them Antony and Felix.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1339/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
