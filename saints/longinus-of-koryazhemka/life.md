# Longinus of Koryazhemka

The venerable Longinus of Koryazhemka came from Russia. He practised ascesis first at the monastery of Saint Paul of Obnora, then at the monastery of Saints Boris and Gleb of Solvychegodsk, and then at the monastery of Saint Cornelius in the region of Komel. From there, followed by the monk Symeon, he came to a hermitage that stood on the banks of the river Koryazhemka, and there he raised cells and a church dedicated to Saint Nicholas.

Longinus fell asleep in peace in the year 1540. Sixteen years after his repose, saint.gr adds, his honourable relic was carried into the church. That is the whole of what the page keeps of him: four houses, a river, a dedication, a companion named once, and two dates. No parentage, no hymn and no distich are printed, and the day's line gives him nothing but the bare epithet of a hermit.
