# Dositheus (Vasić)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names the hierarch Dositheus, metropolitan of Zagreb, among the memories of 31 December and gives no life. Its line is «Свт. Досифея , митр. Загребского, исп (1945).» — the hierarch Dositheus, metropolitan of Zagreb, confessor, 1945 — and the calendar’s own name page for him carries the surname and the rank the line abbreviates: «Святитель Досифей (Васич), митрополит Загребский, исповедник».

That is the whole of the record. He was a metropolitan, of Zagreb; the calendar calls him a confessor, which is the word it uses of a hierarch who suffered and was not killed outright; the year of death it gives is 1945. The see is written here and not in this folder’s `office`, which holds the bare rank, because the site’s four language packs carry no translation for a metropolitan of Zagreb and a reader may not invent four.

This is a Russian calendar’s record of a Serbian hierarch, and the corpus holds no other reading of him: nothing in it carried the surname before this folder, and the Serbian row here is left undocumented with a note saying the Ohrid Prologue was not checked for the day. The day page prints one hymn only, a kontakion of the venerable Melania, and nothing under this name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 31 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261231.html); read 13 October 2026.*
