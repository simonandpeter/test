# Demetrius Donskoi

Demetrius Donskoi was born in 1350, and his spiritual upbringing was taken in hand by [Alexis](/saints/alexis-of-moscow), bishop of Moscow. saint.gr says that his piety went together with a capacity for government, and that he gave himself to the uniting of the Russian land and to its freeing from the Tatar-Mongol yoke as much as to the service of the Church.

Preparing for the decisive battle with the hordes of Mamai, he asked the blessing of [Sergius of Radonezh](/saints/sergius-of-radonezh). The elder encouraged the prince and sent him the monks Alexander Peresvet and Andrew Oslyabya to stand at his side. For his victory on the field of Kulikovo, between the rivers Don and Nepryadva, on the day of the feast of the Nativity of the Mother of God, the prince took the name Donskoi.

He built the Uspensky monastery on the river Dubenka, and raised the church of the Nativity of the Mother of God in the cemetery where the soldiers who fell in the battle had been buried. He fell asleep in peace in 1389 and was buried in the church of the Archangels in the Moscow Kremlin, where the page says his relics are kept.

The Russian calendar's own reading of him is short and all of it is public. He was born in 1350 and brought up under the guidance of the metropolitan Алексий of Moscow; the page says his Christian piety went together with a gift for statesmanship, and that he gave himself to the gathering of the Russian lands and to the freeing of Russia from the Tatar yoke. Collecting his forces for the decisive battle against the hordes of Мамай, he asked the blessing of Сергий Радонежский, who put heart into him and sent him two schema-monks for help, Александр Пересвет and Андрей Ослябя. For the victory on the field of Kulikovo, between the Don and the Nepryadva, on the feast of the Nativity of the Mother of God, he came to be called Донской. He founded the Dormition monastery on the river Dubenka and built a church of the Nativity of the Mother of God over the graves of the fallen, and he died on 19 May 1389, which is the day of this feast, and was buried in the Archangel cathedral of the Moscow Kremlin.
