# Platonida of Argeș

Despina Milița was born in 1487 to the Serbian despot's house of Branković - daughter of John Branković, niece of Metropolitan Maxim - and married Neagoe Basarab in 1505. They had six children, of whom two sons and a daughter died young; with her husband she founded the monastery of Curtea de Argeș, standing at its consecration on 15 August 1517, began the Ostrov skete at Călimănești, and helped build Krušedol - a protector of the arts, the life calls her.

Widowhood in 1521 began the long second half of her life: her son Theodosie, enthroned and driven out, died at Constantinople; she took refuge with her daughters in Transylvania and lived quietly at Sibiu under her cousin the King of Hungary's protection, marrying her daughters to princes and receiving them back widowed. After a short return to a Bucharest where nothing of the old splendour remained, she was tonsured in 1545 with the name Platonida, and finished her life at Sibiu on 30 January 1554. The Romanian calendar keeps her beside her husband on this day.
