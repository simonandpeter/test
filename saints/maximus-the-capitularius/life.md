# Maximus the Capitularius

saint.gr's calendar for 22 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιος Μάξιμος ο Καπιτουλάριος» and gives him two sentences. He was the guard of some prison at Rome, the page says, explaining the epithet as δεσμοφύλακας, a keeper of the bound. He came to believe in Christ through the holy [Valerian](/saints/valerian-husband-of-cecilia) and [Cecilia](/saints/cecilia-of-rome), whom the same calendar keeps on the same day; he confessed his faith, and he suffered a martyr's death — «αἰκισθεὶς τελειοῦται», he was made perfect under the scourging, in the words the page quotes.

The couplet set over the notice turns on his trade: Maximus the guard of the watch is beaten, having been straightway a guard of the commandments of God. The page gives him no country of his own beyond that prison, no year, no emperor and no hymn, and prints no relics for him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 22 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/22/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3060/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
