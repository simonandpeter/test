# Leonorus

The Greek calendar keeps him on 1 Ιουλίου under a name it does not even put into Greek letters. saint.gr's line is «Άγιος Leonorus», the name left in Latin script, and the whole of the page beneath it is one sentence: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ουαλού Αγίου Leonorus» — we have no details of the life of the Welsh saint Leonorus. That he is Welsh, and that the calendar keeps him, is the entire finding.

Because the page prints no Greek form of his name, none is recorded here; a transliteration would be the corpus's invention and not the source's reading. The page gives him no rank, no century, no see and no place in Wales, and nothing of that kind is supplied from elsewhere. Whatever life he has belongs to the western calendars, which have not been read.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1965/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
