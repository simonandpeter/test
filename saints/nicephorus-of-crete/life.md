# Nicephorus of Crete

Nicephorus came from the small town of Kritsa in Merambello on Crete, and his father was called Tzanis. He married a Mohammedan woman named Fetma and had two sons by her; and then he changed his faith and became a Muslim, taking the name Ibrahim. But by the providence of God, saint.gr says, he came to himself and repented of his fall, and from that time he lived the Christian faith and the Christian life.

His wife, seeing the change in him, denounced him to the authorities. Nicephorus was seized and brought before the governor. The verses the page prints over him say what came of it: that on the eleventh he contended conspicuously by the noose, and that he now springs up green in the land of Kritsa and lives with the angelic minds in heaven.

The page's own header dates him 1802 to 1832, and it prints an apolytikion and a kontakion for him, with a whole service of the glorious new martyr Nicephorus among its linked texts.
