# Hyacinth, companion of Eugenia

saint.gr's calendar for 24 December keeps Protas and Hyacinth together, on one page which gives each of them a head line of his own. They were, it says, servants and later fellow ascetics of the holy [Eugenia](/saints/eugenia), whom the same calendar keeps on this day, and they were martyred by the sword at Rome. The distich above the sentence speaks to the two of them at once: cut down by the sword, Hyacinth and Protas, you are allotted the first honours of the martyrs of God.

Because the page names both men rather than counting them, each of them keeps a folder here, and each relates the other: this one's companion is [Protas](/saints/protas-companion-of-eugenia). The calendar gives them no year, no household but Eugenia's, no place but Rome and no hymn, and marks the feast fixed. The corpus's three other Hyacinths — the chamberlain of 3 July, the son of Theoclitus and Hyacinth of Vicina — are kept on other days and are other men. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence by which saint.gr marks an entry it will not vouch for.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/24/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3225/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
