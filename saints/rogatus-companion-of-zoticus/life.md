# Rogatus, companion of Zoticus

Rogatus is the second of the four martyrs the Greek calendar names in one line on 12 Ιανουαρίου: «Άγιοι Ζωτικός, Ρογάτος, Μοδεστός και Καστούλιος οι Μάρτυρες». The page behind the line gives them one sentence between them — [Zoticus](/saints/zoticus-companion-of-rogatus), Rogatus, [Modestus](/saints/modestus-companion-of-zoticus) and [Castulus](/saints/castulus-companion-of-zoticus) were soldiers, and they were martyred in Africa — and then says it has no further details of their lives.

So of Rogatus the calendar keeps a Latin name in Greek letters, the soldier's trade he shared with the other three, the continent where they died, and the day. There is no emperor, no city, no year, and no hymn.
