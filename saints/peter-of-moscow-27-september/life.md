# Peter of Moscow (27 September)

The Greek synaxarion keeps Peter, Patriarch of Moscow, a hieromartyr, on 27 September, and says it has no details of the saint’s life. It prints no date and nothing more. He is kept apart from the metropolitan Peter whom the Greek calendar keeps on 24 August, because this entry calls him a hieromartyr and a patriarch; which Peter it means is not settled here.

The Russian calendar names the office the Greek page overstated. saint.gr has «Άγιος Πέτρος Πατριάρχης Μόσχας Ιερομάρτυρας», patriarch of Moscow; days.pravoslavie.ru prints «Сщмч. Петра, митрополита Крутицкого (1937)» and its index of names «Священномученик Петр (Полянский), митрополит Крутицкий» — metropolitan of Krutitsy, and in its long life of him the locum tenens of the patriarchal throne, which is the office he actually held and never the patriarchate itself. The day, the rank and the see all agree across the two pages, which is why they are read as one man and the difference is recorded here rather than resolved.

That life gives his name in the world as Peter Fyodorovich Polyansky, born on 28 June 1862 in the village of Storozhevoye in the Korotoyak district of the Voronezh diocese, a parish priest's son, and heads him «(1862-1937)». He finished the Voronezh seminary in the first class in 1885 and was made a psalm-reader; came to the Moscow academy as an external student and left it in 1892 a kandidat of theology for a dissertation on the pastoral epistles; took his master's in 1897 on the first epistle to Timothy; and spent ten years from 1896 as warden of the church school at Zhirovitsy, which an inspector's report called brilliant. Moved to Petersburg in 1906 to the Synod's education committee, he took a salary two and a half times smaller and lost the lodging the school had given him, and it did not rise again until 1915.

After [Tikhon of Moscow](/saints/tikhon-of-moscow) died the government set about splintering what he left, and the life is largely the record of that: Metropolitan Peter held in the GPU's cells, fed news in doses by Tuchkov so that he would act on a false picture, persuaded in prison to sign the church's government over to a college of bishops that was never formed, and then to hand the locum tenancy to Metropolitan Agathangel — which he did on 9 June 1926 with the saving clause that if Agathangel would not or could not take it, the office returned to him. It did. He was moved to the political isolator at Suzdal and kept in a single cell, cut off, and the page says the greater pain was never his own conditions but what was being done to the Church he answered for.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 27 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2682/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026; and the Russian church calendar's [27 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260927.html), read 4 October 2026.*
