# Peter, companion of Marcian

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαρτίου prints one line for a company of five and their companions: «Άγιοι Πέτρος, Μαρκιανός, Ιωάννης, Θέκλα, Κασσιανός οι Μάρτυρες και οι συν αυτών μαρτυρήσαντες» — the holy martyrs Peter, Marcian, John, Thekla and Cassian, and those who were martyred with them. Peter stands first in the line. The page behind it says two things and stops.

The first is where they suffered: «Οι Άγιοι αυτοί Μάρτυρες Μαρτύρησαν στην Ρώμη» — these holy martyrs were martyred at Rome. The second is an admission: «Δεν έχουμε περισσότερες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο και το μαρτύριο των Αγίων» — we have no further details of the life and the martyrdom of the saints. There is no year, no emperor, no judge, no manner of death, no relic and no hymn, and nothing at all that belongs to Peter rather than to the company.

He is recorded here as one of the five the source names, beside [Marcian](/saints/marcian-companion-of-peter), [John](/saints/john-companion-of-peter), [Thekla](/saints/thekla-companion-of-peter) and [Cassian](/saints/cassian-companion-of-peter). Those who suffered with them the line counts and does not name, so they have no folder of their own. The note the page attaches to the name Πέτρος, from πέτρα, the rock, is about the word and not about him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3869/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
