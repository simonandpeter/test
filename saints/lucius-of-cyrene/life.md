# Lucius of Cyrene

saint.gr says that Lucius the senator put on the crown of martyrdom in the years of the emperor Diocletian, in the year 299. He came of Cyrene in Libya and was one of its councillors; and although he was an idolater he followed the law of conscience, and was marked out for his fairness and his kindness to other people.

So when the bishop of Cyrene, the hieromartyr [Theodore](/saints/theodore-of-cyrene), showed in his martyrdom the admirable treasures of his most Christian soul, Lucius was drawn to it and received holy baptism. Once the Christian flame had been lit in him he was able to bring the eparch Dignianus too to the truth of the Gospel. Afterwards the two of them went to Cyprus, where they worked for the spread of the faith.

But there too the persecution against the Christians was at its height. And so Lucius, having been arrested there, took the martyr's crown by beheading. The page prints one distich for him, built on his seat in the council: «Βουλὰς ὅλας παρῆλθες τὰς Ἀχιτόφελ, Βουλῇ μιᾷ Λούκιος ἀθλήσας ξίφει» — you passed by all the counsels of Ahithophel, Lucius, contending by the sword on one resolve. It prints no hymn, and it gives neither the place of his death in Cyprus nor any relics.
