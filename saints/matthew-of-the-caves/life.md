# Matthew of the Caves

He too had the gift of seeing into the spiritual world, and what his life records is what he did with it: the elder told the brethren his visions, turning them away from acts dangerous to the soul. So the gift is reported as a warning rather than as a wonder, and that is nearly all of him.

The Russian calendar keeps him on 5 October in one line with [Damian the Healer](/saints/damian-the-healer) and [Jeremiah of the Caves](/saints/jeremiah-of-the-caves), and the lives of all three were written by [Nestor the Chronicler](/saints/nestor-the-chronicler), kept on 27 October. **The one site gives him two different years**: the day line «Матфея (ок. 1085)» and the life it links from that very line «+ ок. 1088». Both are on his folder and neither is chosen.

The calendar also quotes the Iconographic Original on how he is to be painted, which is the only description of his person anywhere in the entry: «Матфей прозорливый подобием стар, изчерна сед, брада аки Власиева, в клобуке черном, риза преподобническая, руки прижаты к сердцу» — old, grey going to black, a beard like Blaise's, a black klobuk, a monastic robe, hands pressed to the heart.

The Serbian church's Ohrid Prologue keeps the same three together on this day, under a single heading and in a single sentence: «Преп. Дамјан, Јеремија и Матеј. Печерски прозорљивци и чудотворци. Сви из XI столећа.» — Damian, Jeremiah and Matthew, seers and wonderworkers of the Caves, all three of the eleventh century. It gives the century where the Russian calendar gives the years, and [Damian](/saints/damian-the-healer) and [Jeremiah](/saints/jeremiah-of-the-caves) are in it with him; of Matthew himself it says nothing further, and it does not name Nestor.
