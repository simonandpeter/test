# Laurence (9 January)

saint.gr's calendar for 9 Ιανουαρίου names a martyr Laurence, and the page behind the line says at once what is not known of him: «Ο Άγιος Λαυρέντιος είναι άγνωστος στους Συναξαριστές και τα Μηναία» — he is unknown to the synaxaria and to the Menaia. What the Church has of him is one manuscript of the Great Lavra on Athos, the Lavriotic Codex Γ 74, folio 112a, where two idiomela are written for him.

Those two hymns are the whole of the record, and saint.gr quotes them. They say that he finished the good fight in the stadium; that he rose like a star out of the western parts and lit up the whole world; that he stood in the midst of the idolatrous persecutors and brought down the strengthlessness of their words with the sling of the divine words; and that, adorned with the crown of victory, he beholds the unattainable beauty of Christ.

The page gives no year, no city and no emperor, and prints no apolytikion. The calendar keeps him on 9 January and says nothing further.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 9 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/09/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1099/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
