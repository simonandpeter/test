# Anatolius of the Cave

The Greek calendar keeps him on 3 Ιουλίου and says in so many words that it does not know him. saint.gr's line is «Όσιος Ανατόλιος ο εν τω σπηλαίω» — the venerable Anatolius in the cave — and the whole of the page beneath it is one sentence: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ρώσου αυτού Οσίου», we have no details of the life of this Russian venerable. The epithet and the word «Ρώσου» are the finding.

No century, no year, no monastery and no name of a cave is printed, and nothing of that kind is supplied here from elsewhere; in particular he is not identified with any of the several Anatolii the Greek calendar keeps on their own days, nor with the fathers the same calendar calls «οι εν τω σπηλαίω» on 28 September, because the page draws no such connection. His own Russian calendar has not been read.

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps an Anatolius of the Caves on 3 июля ст. ст., which is this folder’s day in the Greek calendar too. ITS LINE NAMES TWO MEN OF THE NAME AND NOT ONE: «Прпп. Анатолия (XII) и другого Анатолия затворника (XIII), Печерских» — one of the twelfth century in the Near Caves and another, a recluse, of the thirteenth in the Far Caves, with a name page and a share of the day’s hymns for each. Which of the two this folder holds cannot be read off anything the corpus has, and nothing here was decided: the row records the day, and the name, the century, the hymns and the second man are all left open.
