# Nicholas (Podyakov)

Nicholas Podyakov was archpriest of the Nativity of the Theotokos church at Podosinovets in the Vologda diocese. In 1918 he read Patriarch Tikhon's epistle from the ambo - the confessor-Patriarch's word naming sin and calling to repentance - and it enraged the militant atheists enough that his parishioners set a permanent watch at the church, fearing for his life.

On 24 September 1918 he sang a funeral and walked with the mourners to the cemetery. A nun who served at the church came running in lay clothes: hide, they are coming today to shoot you. He smiled at her: "You came to save me in nothing but a skirt?" The punitive detachment reached the village after noon, in red sashes and matching red shirts, and no one would point him out - so they threatened to take his youngest son, and at that Father Nicholas came home and gathered his children for a last conversation, calm, entering into every detail of how they were to live, motherless as they already were, and keep the faith. The executioners burst in during it, shot him, saw he was only wounded, and left; "Well, glory to God," he said, and crossed himself. When they returned they drove out the doctor, carried him on a stretcher to a hayfield by the river, and there tortured him to death - eleven bayonet wounds beside the bullets, the examination later found - and threw the body into a pit unburied. He was glorified among the New Martyrs and Confessors of Russia in 2000.
