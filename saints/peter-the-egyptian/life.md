# Peter the Egyptian

saint.gr's calendar for 27 January names Peter the Egyptian and gives him one sentence of life: that he was a disciple of Abba Lot, and that he died in peace at a very deep old age. The page says nothing else about him — no birthplace beyond the epithet Egyptian, no year, no monastery, no account of his asceticism, and no troparion or kontakion.

The couplet set above that sentence takes up the old age: «Ὡς ὥριμός τις σῖτος ἐκ γήρως Πέτρε, / Οἶνον ταμείῳ συγκομίζει τῷ τάφῳ» — like some grain ripened by age, Peter, he gathers the wine into the storehouse of the tomb. That figure, the name of his elder, the day and the epithet are the whole of what this calendar holds of him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 27 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/27/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3345/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
