# Theodore (Bogoyavlensky, 27 September)

The Russian calendar gives him a line of his own on 27 September and no life: «Сщмч. Феодора пресвитера (1937)» — Theodore the hieromartyr, presbyter, 1937. Its index of names adds the surname, «Феодор (Богоявленский), иерей, сщмч.», a priest, and keeps him here and on 26 January with the whole company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia.

He is the second priest of this name the corpus holds, and the calendar itself is what keeps them apart: name page 13806 is this man, a hieromartyr of 1937 on 27 September; name page 10402 is a confessor of 1933, kept on 28 January, who has his own folder here. Nothing read links them. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which refused this reading on 4 October 2026, so neither of them has more than his line.

*After the Russian church calendar's [27 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260927.html) and its [index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13806.html); read 4 October 2026.*
