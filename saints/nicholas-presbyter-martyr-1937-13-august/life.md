# Nicholas the Presbyter (13 August)

Nicholas Orlov was born on 15 February 1881 in the village of Rybnitsy in the Bolshesolsky district of Kostroma province, of a family of hereditary priests — the Orlovs had served in that church and the neighbouring village of Bor for more than a hundred years, his father forty-four of them. After the Yaroslavl seminary he taught at the people’s school in Petropavlovskoye, some fifteen kilometres away; he married, and in 1911 was ordained to the church of his native village.

When the persecutions reached him in the 1930s he asked the churchwarden, so as not to bring trouble on the parishioners with whom he lodged in turn, to fit out the lean-to of the church for living: in the old storeroom with one window bunks were made, and the priest’s family moved in. He was repressed three times; the last arrest came on 5 June 1937, on a charge of taking part in a counter-revolutionary group and anti-Soviet gatherings, which he denied. On 25 August 1937 the troika of the Yaroslavl NKVD sentenced him to death, and on the 26th he was shot. He was glorified in 2000. The Russian calendar keeps him on 13 August (Julian) with [Seraphim](/saints/seraphim-of-dmitrov), bishop of Dmitrov, the presbyter [James](/saints/james-presbyter-martyr-1937) and the deacon [Alexius](/saints/alexius-deacon-martyr-1937); the Sretensky calendar prints no life for him, and this one follows azbyka.ru.
