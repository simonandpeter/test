# Marius of Sebaste

saint.gr's calendar for 13 Μαρτίου names him «Άγιος Μάριος επίσκοπος Σεβαστείας», the holy Marius bishop of Sebaste, and the page behind that line says where the entry comes from and that there is nowhere else to go: his memory is mentioned in one line in the «Μικρόν Ευχολόγιον ή Αγιασματάριον» published by the Apostoliki Diakonia in 1956, with no further information, and nowhere else is his memory mentioned at all.

So the see and the day are the whole of it. The page gives no century, no persecution, no account of his shepherding, no relic and no hymn; the rest of what it prints is a note on the name, from the Latin mare, the sea, and an icon of him by a living painter. What is recorded here is the commemoration the Greek calendar keeps and the single book it rests on, which the page itself names.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 13 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/13/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/39/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
