# Malphetha

saint.gr's calendar for 12 October names her and gives no life. Its whole notice is one sentence: «Η Αγία Μαλφεθά μαρτύρησε, αφού τη θανάτωσαν με τοξευόμενα βέλη» — Saint Malphetha was martyred, having been put to death with shot arrows. That is the manner of her death, and it is all the calendar knows of her: no year, no city, no persecutor, no rank beyond the word saint, and no hymn.

The one other thing on the page is the two-line verse the synaxaria set over her name, which plays on the sound of it — that Malphetha has nothing soft to look at, with the bow drawn hard against her. The site prints no note on the name and nothing more.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2718/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
