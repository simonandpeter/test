# Alexander (Krylov)

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar for 7 ноября ст. ст. carries fifteen names on one line and his is the fourth of them: «Сщмч. Кирилла , митр. Казанского, Михаила , Александра , Александра , Михаила , Александра , Николая , Алексия , Павла , Василия , Павлина пресвитеров, Иоанна и Вениамина диаконов, мч. Николая , мц. Елисаветы (1937).» The line sorts the fifteen into four classes with one year for all of them: «Сщмч.» heads the metropolitan, the eleven priests and the two deacons, «пресвитеров» marks the eleven, «диаконов» the two, and «мч.» and «мц.» the layman and the woman who close it. The name link behind his place in it supplies the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line leaves out, «Александр (Крылов), иерей, сщмч.», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from.

That is very nearly everything the calendar records of him. He has no life page, no icon, no hymn, no place, no age and no parish anywhere on this calendar, and the name page behind his place on the line carries its title, two days and no text at all. That page also lists «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which is a synaxis and not a second feast; this folder renders on 7 ноября ст. ст. only. Nothing further is written here, and in particular nothing is reasoned from the surname.

The line names the class and the link names the grade, and both are kept: the line's «Сщмч.» and «пресвитеров» make him a hieromartyr and a presbyter, and the link's «иерей» is recorded as his office, Priest.

He is given no relation to the fourteen others the line names, and the reason is in the one life the line has. Its heading claims a company — «Убиение святителя Кирилла, митрополита Казанского и ихже с ним», the slaying of the metropolitan of Kazan and those with him — but that life says the metropolitan was shot in the Fox ravine outside Chimkent in southern Kazakhstan, together with two men who are not on this line at all and have no folder in this corpus. Nothing on the day page or on any name page read says that the other fourteen died with him or with one another. A shared line, a shared class and a shared year are not a company unless a page says they died together, so no `related` row is written here in either direction.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 7 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261107.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9429.html); read 4 October 2026.*
