# Euphrosyne of Suzdal

The Russian calendar keeps her on 25 September, the day she died, and prints her baptismal name beside the one she took in the monastery: Theodulia in the world, Euphrosyne in the cloister. Its life page makes her the eldest daughter of the martyr Michael of Chernigov and his wife Theophania, born in 1212 after the couple had long been childless and had gone again and again to the Caves monastery at Kyiv to pray for a child.

She was brought up in that household's piety, and the page credits the learned [Theodore, boyar of Michael of Chernigov](/saints/theodore-boyar-of-chernigov), commemorated with her father on 20 September, with much of her schooling. She was betrothed to the prince Theodore, brother of Alexander Nevsky, and he died on the day of the wedding itself. She stayed where the marriage had brought her, in the Suzdal convent of the Deposition of the Robe, and was tonsured there under the name of Euphrosyne of Alexandria.

The life has her outstripping the other sisters in steadiness and in abstinence while still very young, and being visited by Christ, who told her to keep watch. People came from Suzdal and beyond to hear her on love, prayer, obedience and humility, and the abbess herself took her advice. At her asking the sisters were divided into two halves, the virgins and the widows. When the abbess died the community was given to her.

The page says she was told beforehand, in a revelation, both of her father's martyrdom and of the Tatar invasion. In 1238 the Mongol armies did come, and Suzdal was burnt to the ground; her monastery, it says, was the one thing in the city that stood, and it stood by her prayers. She died on 25 September 1250, and the sick went on being healed at her tomb. On 18 September 1698, by the blessing of Patriarch Adrian, Metropolitan Hilarion of Suzdal proclaimed her a saint.

*After the Russian church calendar's [25 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260925.html) and its [life of her](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1603.htm); read 4 October 2026.*
