# Alexander (Levitsky, 24 November)

The calendar's own line is the whole of what is recorded of him here. The Сретенский monastery's calendar for 24 ноября ст. ст. names him first of five priests in «Сщмчч. Александра , Алексия , Иоанна , Корнилия и Митрофана пресвитеров (1937)», and the name page behind that first name gives him his surname and his grade: «Александр (Левицкий), протоиерей, сщмч.» — an archpriest and a hieromartyr. The calendar carries no life page for him, and azbyka.ru, which prints lives for three of the eight new martyrs this day keeps, prints none for him either; its own line for the civil 7 декабря names him and stops.

So nothing is said here of where he was born, where he served, when or by whom he was arrested, or where he died: no page read says any of it. The year 1937 is the year the day line closes the clause with, and it is given for all five men at once. He is not the archpriest the corpus already keeps as «Александр (Левитский)», with т, on 26 сентября ст. ст.: that is a different name page and a different day.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 24 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261124.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14353.html); read 7 October 2026.*
