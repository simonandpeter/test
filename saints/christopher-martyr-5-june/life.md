# Christopher (5 June)

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives him two sentences: «Ο Άγιος Χριστόφορος από τη Ρώμη μαρτύρησε δια ξίφους. Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου.» — the holy Christopher from Rome was martyred by the sword; we have no other details of the saint's life. The page says so of itself, and nothing here goes beyond it: no year, no emperor, no judge, no relics and no hymn.

The couplet it prints above the sentence is the one comment the synaxaria make on him: «Μὴ δωρεὰν σοι τέμνομαι Χριστοφόρος; / Δώσεις ὁ Χριστός οἶδα πολλά μοι στέφη» — am I, Christopher, cut down for you for nothing? You will give me, Christ, many crowns, I know.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1816/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
