# Straton (Stratonicus)

Straton, whom the calendar also calls Stratonicus, was of Nicomedia in Bithynia, and suffered for the faith in the third century. The life the Russian calendar keeps for him is one line long: by order of the governor of Bithynia, after tortures, he was bound to two trees bent down to the ground, and torn in two when they were released.

The Russian calendar names him on 9 September of the old style together with the martyr Chariton, and keeps him again on 13 September among the martyrs Seleucus, Stratonicus, Cronides, Leontius and Serapion, from whose common life this line is taken.

*After the Православный церковный календарь (days.pravoslavie.ru), [the martyrs' common life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1523.htm); read 30 August 2026.*
