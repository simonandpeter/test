# Straton (Stratonicus)

Straton, or Stratonicus, was a Christian of Nicomedia in Bithynia who
suffered in the third century with Seleucus of Galatia and the Egyptians
Cronides, Leontius and Serapion, the five being kept together on 13
September of the old style, 26 September. After cruel torments for the
confession of Christ they were savagely put to death: Cronides, Leontius and
Serapion were bound hand and foot and thrown into the sea, and the waves
carried their bodies to land, where the Christians buried them; Seleucus
suffered in Galatia, where after many torments he was thrown with his wife
to the beasts; and Stratonicus, by order of the governor of Bithynia, was
tortured and then bound to two bent-down tree trunks, which tore his body in
two. Demetrius of Rostov’s Menaion carries their memory. The Russian
calendar keeps Stratonicus also on 9 September of the old style, 22
September, with the martyr Chariton, which is the line this entry began
from.
