# Malchus the Syrian

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar for 26 марта ст. ст. carries a full life of him, and this is the whole of it. He was the only son of a farmer who lived not far from Antioch in Syria. When he came of age his parents began to press him to marry; he left his father’s house secretly instead, and was tonsured a monk in one of the monasteries, where he kept his obedience for many years. Then he learned that his father had died, and decided to visit his widowed mother.

The abbot of the house would not bless the intention. He disobeyed, joined a party of travellers and set out for his own country. On the road Saracens fell upon them, took the whole company captive and made them slaves. His master forced him to marry one of the slave women, and by agreement with her he kept the vow of chastity he had made. With this wife, his wife in spirit only, he fled from captivity.

The master pursued them, and the two hid in a cave which proved to be a lioness’s den. The lioness did not touch them, and tore the pursuers to pieces. At his wife’s own asking he sent her to a convent of women and himself went back to his monastery, where he no longer found the abbot alive; and he never again went outside its walls. He would often tell the monks, for their instruction, of the misfortunes his one disobedience had brought upon him. He laboured on there humbly to the end of his life and died in peace, in the fourth century.

The calendar’s own name page for him lists this day and no other, and gives no year, no monastery by name and no hymn. The day page’s three hymns all belong to the archangel it heads.
