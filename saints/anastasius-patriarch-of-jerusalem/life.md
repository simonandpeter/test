# Anastasius of Jerusalem

saint.gr's calendar for 10 Φεβρουαρίου keeps Anastasius, patriarch of Jerusalem, and the page behind that line is mostly an argument about which Anastasius the day belongs to. Various synaxaristes and the Menaia, it says, put on this day by oversight the memory of Anastasius patriarch of Constantinople — the man who succeeded the patriarch Germanus with the backing of Leo III and worked with him against the holy icons.

That Anastasius was so hated among the Orthodox, the page reports out of Dositheus of Jerusalem, that at the very moment he was being made patriarch the pious and modest women of Constantinople rushed into the church with stones and sticks, calling him a bought traitor and a wolf, and drove him out. Such a wretched hierarch, saint.gr concludes, naturally has no place in the order of the saints.

The honour belongs instead to another Anastasius, patriarch of Jerusalem, who was a pious man and was distinguished for a life pleasing to God, and who lost his throne for upholding the decisions of the Fourth Ecumenical Council, held at Chalcedon in 451. This right opinion, the page adds, we owe to the wise patriarch Constantius I. No year of his birth or his repose is printed, and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/10/index.aspx) and [his page](https://www.saint.gr/1208/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
