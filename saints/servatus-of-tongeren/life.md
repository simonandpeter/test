# Servatus of Tongeren

saint.gr's calendar for 13 May names him and gives him four sentences. Saint Servatus lived in the fourth century and was bishop of the city of Tongeren in Belgium. He gave hospitality to [Athanasius the Great](/saints/athanasius-of-alexandria) when Athanasius was in exile in the West because of the persecutions of the followers of the heretic Arius. He defended saint Athanasius and the Orthodox faith, particularly at the synod of Sardica, at Sofia, in the year 343. He fell asleep in peace in 384.

Nothing else is printed: no birthplace, no parents, no length of episcopate, no relics and no hymn. He is one of four bishops of the Latin West whom the Greek calendar keeps on this day, and the only one of them whose entry attaches him to a council and a name the whole Church knows.
