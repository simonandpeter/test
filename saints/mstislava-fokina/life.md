# Mstislava (Fokina)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names her for 25 февраля ст. ст. at the end of a line it gives to two: «Сщмч. Александра пресвитера, прмц. Мстиславы.» — a hieromartyr, a presbyter, and a venerable-martyr, which is the calendar's word for a monastic who died a martyr's death. The name link behind her carries the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line leaves out: «Мстислава (Фокина), прмц.».

The line gives no year, which is unusual on this calendar's twentieth-century entries and is why no date is recorded here; it gives no monastery, no place and no manner of death either, and there is no life page and no hymn under her name. Nothing is reasoned out from the surname, and nothing further is written, because the line and the name link are the whole of what was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 25 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260225.html); read 3 October 2026.*
