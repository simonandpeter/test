# Martyrius the Recluse

saint.gr's calendar for 25 Οκτωβρίου names two venerable men of one name together, Martyrius the Recluse and [Martyrius the Deacon](/saints/martyrius-the-deacon), and then says that it has nothing to tell of either: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τους βίους των Ρώσων Οσίων» — we have no details of the lives of the Russian venerable ones. That they were Russians, and that one of them lived enclosed and the other served as a deacon, is all that the entry carries.

There is no monastery, no century, no year, no teacher and no companions beyond each other; the page prints no couplet for them and no hymn, and lists no relics. This folder says no more than the page does, and the epithet in its name is the page's own.

The Russian calendar keeps him on 25 октября ст. ст. and has two sentences about him, which is two more than the Greek one had: he lived and laboured in the thirteenth and fourteenth centuries in the monastery of the Kyiv caves, and his holy relics rest in the Far caves, which the page also calls the caves of the second of the monastery's founders.

That is the whole of it. There is no story, no place of origin, no elder, no gift and no year, and the day page, which prints a troparion and a kontakion for [the deacon of his name](/saints/martyrius-the-deacon) whom the same line keeps with him, prints nothing under his. What he is called by the calendar — the enclosed one — is the only thing that tells the two of them apart in its line, and in the name form this folder stores it cannot even do that.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 25 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/25/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2616/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 25 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261025.html) and [his own page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life6732.htm), read 4 October 2026.*
