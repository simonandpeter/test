# Leo Styppes

saint.gr's calendar for 12 Νοεμβρίου keeps Leo, patriarch of Constantinople, and begins by saying which Leo he is: the one surnamed Styppes. He held the patriarchate in the years of the emperor John Komnenos, from 1134 to 1143 — the page gives those years on the authority of Meletios, citing the third volume of his work at page 17.

Before that he was a presbyter and oikonomos of Hagia Sophia, and he succeeded on the patriarchal throne John the Beloved. Having governed his flock in a godly manner, he died in peace. The page gives no year of birth, no place, no acts of his patriarchate and no hymn; its couplet says Leo drives off the demons of the air, having gone out of this life like a lion out of the reeds.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3016/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
