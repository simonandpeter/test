# John (Blinov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him in one line for 19 марта ст. ст.: «Исп. Иоанна (1932).» The line declines the forename, gives no surname, no place and no year of birth, and the rank word «Исп.» — исповедник, confessor — is the only rank. It is a confessor's line and not a martyr's: the calendar does not say he was killed, only that he confessed and that he died in 1932.

Behind that «Иоанна» stands the calendar's own name page 9973, which carries the fuller form «Иоанн (Блинов), псаломщик, исп.» — the surname the day's line leaves out, and a grade: псаломщик, the psalm-reader of a parish, which is a layman's service and not holy orders. That page lists two days, this 19 марта and «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. It carries no life, and neither does the day page.

The corpus was searched for the surname Блинов and holds no folder of it, and azbyka.ru, which has lives for many of the confessors of these years, was not read for him. No office is written, because the corpus's four locale packs carry no word for a psalm-reader and a reader should not have to invent four translations for one folder; the grade is recorded in his attestation and here. So a parish psalm-reader of the Russian church who confessed the faith and died in 1932 is the whole of what this folder says.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 19 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260319.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9973.html), which carries no life; read 8 October 2026.*
