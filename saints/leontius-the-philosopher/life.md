# Leontius the Philosopher

saint.gr's calendar for 19 October names «Άγιος Λεόντιος ο φιλόσοφος», Leontius the philosopher, and its whole notice of him is one sentence: the holy Leontius the philosopher died in peace. The calendar gives him no country, no century, no rank in the Church and no hymn, and nothing beyond the epithet by which it tells him apart.

The only other thing on the page is the two-line verse over the entry, which addresses him as father and turns his epithet against the learning it names: you, father, convicted even your living thoughts as counterfeit, and when you had come to know the art of syllogisms you were rid of it. That is all the source has, and this folder says so rather than filling the silence.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 19 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/19/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2772/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
