# Dorotheus (5 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 5 Νοεμβρίου keeps Dorotheus the presbyter, and the whole of its account of him is one sentence. He lived in the years of the emperor Maximian, when the governor of Palestine was Urbanus — the page sets the year 298 beside the governor's name — and Dorotheus, who was a modest priest, was martyred after being handed over as food to the wild beasts. The page gives him no city, no church, no year of his own, no burial and no hymn.

What stands beside the sentence is the couplet of the synaxarion, which turns on his name as a gift of God: «Ὁ Δωρόθεος ἐκδοθεὶς τοῖς θηρίοις, / Δῶρον προσήχθη τῷ Θεῷ προσδεκτέον» — Dorotheus, handed over to the beasts, was brought to God as a gift fit to be received. The same day's calendar keeps several other martyrs under the same governor of Palestine, each in an entry of its own.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2892/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
