# Nirus

saint.gr's calendar for 10 Νοεμβρίου names Nirus and gives him one sentence: «Ο Άγιος Νίρος μαρτύρησε δια ξίφους» — the holy Nirus was martyred by the sword. Nothing else of him is on the page: no country, no century, no emperor, no companions, no burial, no year and no hymn, and the name itself is given no gloss.

What the page prints beside the sentence is his couplet, which turns on the head the sword took: «Ὑπῆρξεν ἡμῖν ἡ κεφαλή σου, Νίρε, / Θείου κεφαλὴ Μάρτυρος διὰ ξίφους» — your head became for us, Nirus, the head of a divine martyr, by the sword.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/10/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2970/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
