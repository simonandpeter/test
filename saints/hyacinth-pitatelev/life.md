# Hyacinth (Pitatelev)

The Russian calendar names him in a line of four and gives no life at all. Its 17 October old style has «Сщмчч. Неофита и Анатолия пресвитеров, прмчч. Иакинфа и Каллиста (1918)» — the hieromartyrs [Neophytus](/saints/neophytus-lyubimov) and [Anatolius](/saints/anatolius-ivanovsky), presbyters, and the monk-martyrs Hyacinth and [Callistus](/saints/callistus-oparin), 1918 — and because the line names all four of them, each has a folder of his own. He is the first of the two the calendar marks прмч., the rank of a monk who died a martyr, against the two presbyters named before him.

His page in the calendar's index of names adds his surname: «Иакинф (Питателев), прмч.», Hyacinth Pitatelev, venerable-martyr. It gives the second day he is kept on, 26 January, among the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and then it stops. There is no life link on either page, so the rank and the surname are the whole of what the calendar holds of him besides the year.

His number in the index of names stands immediately beside that of the other monk of the company, which means only that the two entries were added to the calendar at the same time. What monastery they belonged to, whether they died together, and how, the calendar does not say, and nothing is supplied for it.

azbyka.ru is the source that carries lives for the new martyrs of the twentieth century, and it would not be read: asked for its page for this run of days, it answered 403 to the fetcher this corpus uses, on 28 October 2026. The refusal is recorded because it is the reason this page is short rather than a judgement about the man.

*After the Russian church calendar of the Sretensky monastery (days.pravoslavie.ru) for 17 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261017.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13833.html), which is all there is; read 30 October 2026. The same menologion day was read in the other three calendars and does not keep him — [saint.gr](https://www.saint.gr/10/17/index.aspx), [doxologia.ro](https://doxologia.ro/17-octombrie) and the [Ohrid Prologue](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-10-30&prolog=1).*
