# Philaret of Kyiv

saint.gr keeps Philaret, metropolitan of Kyiv, on the second of December, and the whole of what it prints about him is a heading and a sentence. The heading reads «Άγιος Φιλάρετος Μητροπολίτης Κιέβου (; - 1857)», giving the see and the year of death and no year of birth; and the sentence under it says «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ρώσου Αγίου» — we have no details of the life of the Russian saint.

So three things are attested here and nothing else: that the Greek calendar keeps him on this day, that he held the see of Kyiv, and that he died in 1857. The page gives him no name in the world, no birthplace, no monastery, no account of his episcopate, no relic, no couplet and no hymn. Beside the entry it links the synaxis of all the saints of Siberia and an English-language calendar; neither was read, and nothing from either is written here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 2 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/02/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4254/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
