# Nectarios in the Cave

saint.gr keeps on 29 November a venerable Nectarios whom it calls, in Greek and in quotation marks, «ἐπίσκοπος ἐν τῷ Σπηλαίῳ» — bishop in the Cave. The whole of its entry for him is the sentence that follows: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ρώσου Οσίου», we have no details of the life of the Russian venerable father.

So two things are attested here and nothing else: that the Greek calendar keeps him on this day, and that he is Russian and is remembered as a bishop belonging to a cave — which is how the Greek sources habitually render the Caves of Kyiv, though this page does not say so and no such identification is made in this folder. It gives him no century, no name in religion, no see, no relic, no couplet and no hymn.

The Russian calendar keeps him on the same day and says plainly what he was. Its line is «Прп. Нектария Печерского (XII).» and its life is three sentences: Nectarius of the Caves, a monk of the Kyiv Caves monastery, struggled in the twelfth century; for carrying out the will of the elder brethren without a word of argument, and for his zeal in the work, he was called «послушливый», the obedient; and he is buried in the cave of Antony.

One word in this wants stating rather than smoothing over. saint.gr's line for 29 Νοεμβρίου calls him «Ὅσιος Νεκτάριος “ἐπίσκοπος ἐν τῷ Σπηλαίῳ”», a bishop in the Cave, and this folder's `bishop` type and its display name both rest on that word. This calendar, on the same day and under the same by-name — Печерский is what «ἐν τῷ Σπηλαίῳ» renders — calls him not a bishop but an obedient monk, ὑπήκοος rather than ἐπίσκοπος, and gives him no see. The day, the by-name and the one monastery agree, so the Russian row is written here and not on a second folder; but the Greek page's word is left standing as that page's, and which of the two the Church means is a question for a reader with both open.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 29 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/29/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3109/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 29 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261129.html) and [the lives](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/id1590.htm), read 7 October 2026.*
