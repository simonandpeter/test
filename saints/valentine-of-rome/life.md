# Valentine of Rome

saint.gr's whole entry for the hieromartyr Valentine is one sentence: he was a presbyter and was martyred at Rome under the emperor Claudius, whose reign the page gives as 41 to 54. The page prints no life beyond that line, no hymn and no relic. It keeps a second Valentine on the same 14 February, the bishop of Terni, on a page of his own, so the two are two men and not one entry told twice.

One thing is added from elsewhere on the site. The page of the martyrs [Proclus](/saints/proclus-companion-of-apollonius), [Apollonius](/saints/apollonius-companion-of-proclus) and [Ephebius](/saints/ephebius-companion-of-proclus) says that they were arrested when they went to bury the holy relic of the hieromartyr Valentine, referring the reader back to the same day; so his burial is what their martyrdom hangs on.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 14 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/14/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3705/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
