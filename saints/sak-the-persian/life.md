# Sak the Persian

saint.gr's calendar for 17 November names «Άγιος Σακ ο Πέρσης», Sak the Persian, and its whole notice of him is two sentences, both about where he comes from. This saint, it says, is unknown to the synaxarists, and is found in the Paris codex 1578 without any notice attached. Then: perhaps he is the same as the martyr Saktos who is kept on the twenty-fifth of July.

That is everything. The calendar gives him no century, no reign, no manner of death, no companion and no hymn; what it gives is a name in a manuscript, a nation, and a guess that it marks as a guess. This folder keeps the commemoration the codex preserves and leaves the identification where the page leaves it, as a possibility and not a finding.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 17 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/17/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3031/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
