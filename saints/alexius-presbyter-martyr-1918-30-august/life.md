# Alexius (Velikoselsky)

Alexius Alexandrovich Velikoselsky was born in 1865 to a family of clergy.
After the Yaroslavl seminary in 1888 he served as psalmist, teacher and
catechist of the parish school at the church of the Procession of the
Precious Wood of the Cross in the village of Krasnoye on the Sheksna in
the Mologa district of Yaroslavl province — since drowned by the Rybinsk
reservoir; he married Zinaida Arsenyevna, born 1870, and on 9 June 1891
was ordained priest for the church of the Smolensk icon of the Mother of
God at Nikolskoye-on-the-Tropa in the Rybinsk district, where he also ran
the Oboltino school of letters and taught the Law of God there. In April
1903, at his own request, he was moved to the Saviour church of the
village of Ivashevo in the Rostov district — now in the Ilyinsky district
of the Ivanovo region — teaching the Law of God at the Ivashevo and
Channikovo zemstvo schools and church singing at the Ivashevo school; and
on 12 November 1912 Tikhon (Belavin), archbishop of Yaroslavl and Rostov
and the future patriarch, moved him to the church of the Smolensk icon at
Diyevo-Gorodishche in the Yaroslavl district, then made him rector of the
Trinity church of the same village. In 1918, during the Yaroslavl rising,
a White detachment occupied Diyevo-Gorodishche, which lies on the border
of the Yaroslavl and Kostroma provinces; on 10 June the Reds took the
village by storm and arrested about a hundred of its people, among them
the rector of the Trinity church, on whose bell-tower a machine-gun had
been found. The Bolsheviks took the priest for a White Guard, and on
30 August 1918 shot him at Kostroma; word of the shooting reached the
commission on the persecution of the Church at the Holy Council of
1917–1918 from the members of the Yaroslavl diocesan council. He was
glorified among the new martyrs and confessors of Russia by the Council of
Bishops of 13–16 August 2000 and added to their synaxis by the Holy Synod
on 26 December 2001; the Russian calendar keeps him on 30 August (Julian)
— 17 August of the old style, its short notice says — and in the synaxes
of the saints of Kostroma and of Rostov and Yaroslavl.
