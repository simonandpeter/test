# Heliodorus (20 August)

Doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 20 august reads «Sfântul Mucenic Eliodor» — the holy martyr Heliodorus — and the page that line links to carries neither a Viață tab nor a Tropar tab. No country, no persecution, no emperor, no year and no company is printed beside the name, and nothing has been brought in from elsewhere to fill it out.

The corpus already holds two other men of this name, on other days and out of other sources — Heliodorus the companion of Mark the Shepherd, and Heliodorus (Golovanitsky) — and no claim is made here that this is either of them. The identification rests on the Romanian day alone, and the other three churches' rows say only that their calendars were not read for the corresponding day.

The Greek calendar keeps Heliodorus on the same day, 20 August, gives him a companion and tells the story. saint.gr says that he and [Dosas](/saints/dosas) — a name it also gives as Dosai and as Sodas — lived under the reign of Sabor, king of the Persians; that Sabor came with his armies and took a city of the Romans, and began pulling down the churches of the Christians and burning the holy icons; and that Heliodorus, who was ninety-five years old at the time, came before the impious king together with Dosas and made the sharpest remonstrances to him, confessing at the same time that Christ is true God.

Sabor then tortured the two of them hard and without mercy. He cut off their noses, and at the last he put them to death by burning their heads. The page gives no year and no city, and its one distich is for the pair together: «Hλιόδωρος και Δοσάς, οι γεννάδαι, Ήθλησαν άμφω ανδρικώς Xριστού χάριν» — Heliodorus and Dosas, the noble ones, both contended manfully for Christ's sake.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 20 august — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/20-august) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-eliodor), which is empty of both life and hymn; read 24 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 20 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/20/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2304/saint.aspx), read 1 October 2026.*
