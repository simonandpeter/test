# Joannicius II

Joannicius, patriarch of Serbia, came from Prizren. He was first secretary to the king — Dušan, the Prologue says — and from 1339 governed the Church as archbishop; in 1346 the council of all the Serbian bishops, at which the patriarch of Bulgaria was present, at King Dušan’s wish elected him the first patriarch of the Serbian Church. A zealous shepherd and orderer of the Serbian Church, a great establisher of the Church’s laws, he reposed on 3 September 1349 and was buried at Peć, where his relics lie.
