# Demetrianus of Tamassos

Of the very few pieces of information we have about him, saint.gr says, we learn that Demetrianus was born at Tamassos in Cyprus and lived there in the first years of Christianity; that he gave himself to the Christian faith from a small age; and that he was ordained presbyter by the bishop of Tamassos. He lived a holy life, and Christ endowed him with the gift of working wonders. That is the whole of the biographical notice, and it carries no year.

His own service, printed on the same page, says more and says it differently. It calls him the glory of the Cypriots and the president of the people of Tamassos, a chief shepherd and a hierarch, and it says that he succeeded to the throne of Heracleides; so the hymns hold him bishop of the see in which the notice has him only a presbyter, and both readings are the page's and are set down here unreconciled. The hymns also record the one wonder the page names: that he once gave light to a blind man, who cried out to the God who had enlightened him through his prayer.

saint.gr prints for him two apolytikia and a kontakion, all three copied here, and a megalynarion besides, which this corpus has no field for and which greets him as the hierarch of Tamassos who destroyed the error of the idols with wise words and increased the Lord's planting with the streams of his wonders.
