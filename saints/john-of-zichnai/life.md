# John of Zichnai

saint.gr's calendar for 12 December keeps the venerable John, bishop of Zichnai, whom the page also calls Joakeim. He lived in the years of the emperors Andronikos II Palaiologos, which it dates 1282 to 1328, and Andronikos III, 1328 to 1341. At two years old he was left an orphan of both parents, and an uncle of his, a monk called Ioannikios, took him and brought him up according to the commands of the Gospel.

When he came to a suitable age he became a priest, and because of his great virtue he was chosen bishop of Zichnai. When his uncle died he resigned the bishopric and stayed at the holy monastery of the Honourable Forerunner. He extended that monastery, which lay near the Menoikion mountain of eastern Macedonia, north-east of Serres, and built a magnificent church in the name of John the Baptist.

There, having lived in a way pleasing to God and given much to the district, he reposed in peace on 12 December 1333. His relics, the page says, are at that same monastery of the Honourable Forerunner at Serres.
