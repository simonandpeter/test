# Cleopas

Cleopas is numbered among the Seventy Apostles, and doxologia.ro's page for him is short: the Orthodox Church commemorates him on 30 October, and what it prints about him is a handful of testimonies rather than a narrative life. He is the disciple to whom the Saviour appeared on the road to Emmaus a few days after the Resurrection, as he walked there with another disciple; Luke tells it in the twenty-fourth chapter of his Gospel.

The page observes that the way the evangelist Luke mentions Cleopas suggests he was well known to Luke's readers, that is to Theophilus and to the converts from the nations. It then cites the historian Eusebius of Caesarea, who quotes the historian Hegesippus: writing about the year 180, Hegesippus said that many years before he had been able to speak with a great-nephew of the Apostle Jude, and had learned in that way that Cleopas was the brother of the Righteous Joseph, the betrothed of the Virgin Mary.

This testimony, the page says, would make it easier to identify the Cleopas of Luke 24 with the Cleopas or Clopas — in Greek Κλόπας, in the Semitic form Qlopha — who was the husband, or perhaps the father, of the "Mary of Clopas" named in John 19:25, the mother of James the Less and of Joses in Mark 15. The corpus keeps that woman as [Mary of Cleopas](/saints/mary-of-cleopas). By the same testimony carried down from Hegesippus by Eusebius, the son of Cleopas, the Apostle Symeon, was chosen second bishop of Jerusalem after the martyrdom of the Apostle James the Just.

The page ends with how he is painted. The Hermeneia of Dionysius of Fourna sets him down as an old bishop with a pointed beard; and he is shown besides in the icons of the road to Emmaus and of the breaking of bread there, seated at table with Saint Luke, with Christ between them holding a loaf in his hand and blessing it.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 30 octombrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/30-octombrie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-apostol-cleopa); read 25 September 2026.*
