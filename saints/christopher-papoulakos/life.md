# Christopher Papoulakos

Christopher was born about 1780 in the village of Armpounas, in the mountains and thick woods of the province of Kalavryta. doxologia.ro's life describes him as a peaceable, upright, unselfish man, simple in his manners and very merciful, who loved study, prayer and the monastic life from childhood; and so he went to the Monastery of the Great Cave, where he was tonsured a monk, and preached the Christian faith in a hard time for Orthodoxy in Greece.

He returned to his own village and built a monastery dedicated to the Mother of God. After several attempts he obtained the licence to become a preacher, and with hard work and sacrifice began his mission, teaching against the atheist education then being spread in the Peloponnese and opposing the western influences imposed under the Bavarian occupation, which was pulling down the Byzantine churches. He was persecuted for it and went on preaching.

He followed, the life says, the way of a confessor, of a martyr and of an ascetic, and the faithful loved him and honoured him as a saint. He went to the Lord on 18 January 1861, and was canonised by the Ecumenical Patriarchate on 30 August 2024.
