# Julian (2 September)

Julian was martyred struck on the head with a club, his mind fixed whole on God to the end. That is the whole of the synaxarion’s notice for the martyr kept on 2 September, with its verse; the Synaxaristes have nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 2 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/924/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
