# Justus, companion of Matthew

The holy Justus and [Matthew](/saints/matthew-companion-of-justus), saint.gr says, together with [Theophilus](/saints/theophilus-companion-of-trophimus) and [Trophimus](/saints/trophimus-companion-of-theophilus) — and the page sends its reader to its own entry for those two, on this same 21 July — were martyred under Diocletian. Because they would not deny Christ they were stoned, they were wounded, they were thrown into the fire, and at the last they were beheaded.

That is the whole of the page's notice for the two of them. Above it stands the synaxarion's distich, which says that two men, giving up their necks to the sword, received with a common end a common crown as well. The page gives no year for them, only the emperor's name; the entry it points to for Theophilus and Trophimus sets that martyrdom in the years of the emperor Diocletian, 284 to 304. It prints no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 21 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/21/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2021/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
