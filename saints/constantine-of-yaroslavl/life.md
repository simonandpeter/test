# Constantine of Yaroslavl

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps the two princes of Yaroslavl together on 3 июля ст. ст. and gives them one reading in two parts. It opens with the year 1236, when «свирепый Батый» came into Rus with countless hordes and gave everything to fire and sword, and the Mongols laid waste Vladimir and the appanages of Rostov and Yaroslavl with many other towns of the north. Among the new stars of that cruel time, the reading says, two princes shone meekly, tried by disasters as gold is tried in a furnace.

The brothers ruled their patrimony in the fear of God, took no part in the quarrels of the Russian princes, cared only for the good of their own inheritance, healed the wounds the raids had made and restored the ruined churches. Constantine was the younger, and he inherited his brother’s princedom for a short time when the elder fell ill at Vladimir in the winter of 1249 and died there. The nomads of the steppe went on raiding from the south, and in 1252 they laid waste the principality of Suzdal.

On 3 July 1267 Tatar detachments came up to Yaroslavl. The prince went out to meet them with his retinue, and on the rising ground by the river Которосль there was a bloody battle in which a great many men fell on both sides. In that fight, the reading says, the young prince «принял … мученическую кончину» — received a martyr’s end — giving his life for the Orthodox faith and the independence of his homeland; and his body was buried with honour beside his brother’s. Two and a half centuries later, when trenches were dug for a new cathedral, the incorrupt relics of the two were found, and wonders and healings began at them.

The calendar sets him on three days: 23 мая for Rostov, 8 июня for the finding of the relics, and this one, which is the day of the battle. One row to a church is the schema, so the other two are recorded in the row and he does not render on them. Under the two names together the day page prints four hymns — a troparion and a kontakion of the finding of the relics, a second troparion and a kontakion of their falling-asleep — and all four are in this folder and in his brother’s, [Basil of Yaroslavl](/saints/basil-of-yaroslavl), whose row this same day gave him.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 3 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260703.html) and [the life of the two brothers](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life6492.htm); read 9 October 2026.*
