# Marcellus

doxologia.ro's calendar for 1 martie names Marcellus and gives no life for him: his page carries no Viață tab and no Tropar tab, only a single sentence of summary. That sentence says that the saints Marcellus and [Antony](/saints/antony-the-martyr-1-march), kept on 1 March, were martyred after being thrown into the fire — «Sfinții Marcel și Antonie prăznuiți pe 1 martie au fost martirizați după ce au fost aruncați în foc».

That is the whole of the source. It names no country and no city, no emperor and no year, and doxologia prints no hymn for him.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, pairs him with [Antony](/saints/antony-the-martyr-1-march) on one page and adds the one thing doxologia did not give — the manner of their death. saint.gr says in a single sentence that the holy Marcellus and Antony were martyred after they had been thrown into the fire, and prints a distich over the pair of them: the furnace is a crucible for two contenders, who shine in it brighter than gold. It gives no year, no country, no emperor, no judge and no relic, and it prints no hymn; and it does not say which of the two men the distich's first line belongs to, because it belongs to both.

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar keeps the two of them together on the Julian 1 March, and the whole of what it gives is the line «Мчч. Маркелла и Антония .» — the grade of martyr, the two names in the genitive, and nothing more. There is no life page behind either name, no century and no year, where every other line of that day but one closes with a date; no place is named; and the day prints no hymn under either name. The name link behind each gives the bare forename and no surname, no toponymic and no epithet.

So the Russian calendar adds to this folder the Russian form of the name, the grade it already had from the Greek and the Romanian, and the fact that this calendar keeps him too, and nothing else. It does not name [Antony](/saints/antony-the-martyr-1-march) as a companion in any clause; it simply prints the two on one line, as saint.gr does, and the relation this folder already recorded is not strengthened by that and not weakened either.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 1 martie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/1-martie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-marcel); read 19 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 1 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3776/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 1 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260301.html), which keeps the two of them on one line, prints no life for either and gives them no year, read 5 October 2026.*
