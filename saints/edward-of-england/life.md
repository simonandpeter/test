# Edward of England

Doxologia's life of Edward, king of England, is four sentences long, and this is all of it. In spite of his place in society, it says, he was marked out by his piety and his love for God. He was killed three years after coming to the throne, in the year 978, by his stepmother Elfrida, and was buried very deep in an unknown place.

His holy relics were found incorrupt when a heavenly light shone above the place of his grave. Today, the page says, the reliquary holding them is kept by Russian Orthodox monks in a monastery near London. Nothing further has been read for him here: no year of birth, no account of the killing beyond that one clause, and no hymn, for the page prints none.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and tells the same short story, adding his father's name: saint.gr makes him the son of Edgar, king of England. It says that for all the greatness of his office he was marked out by his purity and his piety; that he was murdered three years after coming to the throne by his stepmother Elfrida and buried very deep in an unknown place; and that his relic was found whole when a heavenly light shone above the spot of his grave. It too says that the relic is kept today by Russian Orthodox monks of the Diaspora at a monastery near London, and it sets his martyrdom in the year 978, leaving the year of his birth a question mark in its heading. No hymn is printed under his name.
