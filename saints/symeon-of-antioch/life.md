# Symeon of Antioch

saint.gr's calendar for 16 December keeps Saint Symeon, archbishop of Antioch, and the whole of what its page says is where his name is to be found: the memory of Saint Symeon is recorded in Delehaye's synaxarion, at page 361, 53, together with that of Saint [Memnon](/saints/memnon-of-ephesus), bishop of Ephesus, whom the same day's calendar keeps. There is no life after it and nothing before it.

So the page gives him no year of birth and none of death, no place beyond the see of Antioch, no circumstance of his life, no relic and no hymn. What it does give is the company his memory keeps in that one manuscript tradition, and the see; and this entry says that and no more.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 16 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/16/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3276/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
