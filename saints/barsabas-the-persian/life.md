# Barsabas the Persian

saint.gr's calendar for 11 December names the holy Barsabas and gives him one sentence of life: he was a Persian, and he confessed the Christian faith with courage before the Persian ruler, with the result that he was beheaded. The page names neither the ruler nor the year, and it joins him to no other name on the day, although three of the day's other entries are also Persian.

What it adds is the couplet set over his name, and the couplet is a refusal: sacrifice, Barsabas, and live, delivered from the sword — I long to live above; let the sword come quicker. That is the whole of the entry, and nothing further is claimed here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/11/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3168/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
