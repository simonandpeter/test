# Abramius of Ephesus

saint.gr's calendar for 28 Οκτωβρίου names «Άγιος Αβράμιος επίσκοπος Εφέσου». He followed the monastic life, the page says, and became metropolitan of Ephesus after the year 533 — the day's line calls him bishop of that city and the notice calls him its metropolitan, and the page does not reconcile the two words. It identifies him with the abba of the same name whom John Moschos mentions, citing PG 87, 2956, of the monasteries of the Abrahamites at Constantinople and of the Byzantines at Jerusalem.

Two homilies survive under his name, the notice adds, «εἰς τὸν Εὐαγγελισμόν» and «εἰς τὴν Ὑπαπαντήν» — on the Annunciation and on the Meeting of the Lord — published in 1913 by M. Kraschaninnikov and M. Jugie, and of great importance for patrological study. That is the whole of it: no birthplace, no year of death, no relics and no hymn. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/28/index.aspx) and [his notice](https://www.saint.gr/2829/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
