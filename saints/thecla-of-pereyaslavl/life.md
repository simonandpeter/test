# Thecla of Pereyaslavl

saint.gr's account of the venerable Thecla is three sentences, and it places her by her son. She lived in Russia in the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries and was the mother of the venerable Daniel of Pereyaslavl, whose memory the page keeps on 7 Απριλίου. She followed the monastic life and became a nun in the monastery of the Holy Trinity of Pereyaslavl, and she fell asleep in peace.

That is all the page holds. It gives her no monastic name other than Thecla, no year of birth and no year of death, no account of her ascesis, no relics and no hymn. What is recorded of her is a century, a son, a house and a peaceful end.
