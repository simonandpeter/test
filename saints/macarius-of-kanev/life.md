# Macarius of Kanev

Macarius was born at Ovruch in Volhynia in 1605 into the Tokarevsky family, who
were known for holding to Orthodoxy, and after his parents died he entered the
Dormition monastery there as a novice. He moved to the Kupyatichi monastery near
Pinsk in 1625, was ordained deacon in 1630 and priest in 1632, and was sent in
1637 to Metropolitan Peter Mogila of Kiev, who made him superior of one house
after another — Kamenets, Kupyatichi, Pinsk, and at last his own monastery at
Ovruch.

There he spent more than ten years in the long struggle over the faith, leaving
only in 1671 when the Tatars had emptied the town, and going to the Kiev Caves
Lavra. He was needed elsewhere and was sent to Kanev, into the same fight. When
the Turks attacked the monastery in September 1678 he met them on the church
porch holding a cross. They hung him up by his hands and feet between two posts,
and two days later beheaded him.
