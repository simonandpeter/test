# Vladimir (Pasternatsky)

He was born on 2 July 1885 at Dudichi in the Igumen district of the Minsk province, in a family of hereditary gentry who had gone over into the clergy. Having finished the Minsk theological seminary he was appointed rector of the church of the holy Apostles Peter and Paul at Pesochnoye in the Slutsk district, in the place of his wife's aged father. He and his wife had eight children, and the notice says the household lived in great concord.

In the twenties he was made dean of all the churches of the Kopyl district. The taxation laid on the Church ruined his family, who had even their cow taken from them, the thing that fed the youngest children. In 1932 he moved to Kopyl and went on serving in the church of the Ascension there. He was called in to the N. K. V. D. and told to renounce his orders in public; his grown children were dismissed from their work and the younger ones expelled from school.

He was the only priest over an immense stretch of country, riding round the parishes and not seeing his own house for months together. He was arrested first in March 1936, and after a short release arrested again on a charge of working with what the indictment called German counter-intelligence. On 5 January 1938 he was sentenced to be shot, and the sentence was carried out soon afterwards.
