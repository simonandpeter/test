# Censorinus

Censorinus bore witness under the emperor Claudius II, whose years saint.gr gives as 268 to 269. He held the rank of magister and of first man of the senate. After slanders against him he was arrested and shut in prison; and in spite of the tortures he went on insisting on his faith in Christ.

From the wonders he worked in that prison, the page says, twenty soldiers came to believe in Christ, and were beheaded together with him. The page names none of the twenty and gives them no line of their own on the day, so no folder is made for them here; what is recorded is that they were twenty, that they were soldiers, and that they died with him.

The page gives him no year of birth, no city, and no apolytikion and no kontakion. Its couplet makes his neck the whetstone of his fellow-contestants: Censorinus, stretching out his neck to the sword, is as a hone to the razor for those who contend beside him.
