# Obadiah

Doxologia's life gives him as of the land of Shechem, from a village called Bethacharam, and a servant at the courts of the kings of Israel, steward of the royal house of Ahab. From his youth he feared the Lord greatly; and when the people of Israel had gone away from God and were worshipping the idol Baal, Obadiah served the true God of his fathers, who had saved Israel out of Egypt and led them through the Red Sea.

When Jezebel, the lawless queen, was seeking to kill all the prophets of the Lord, Obadiah took a hundred men who were prophets and hid them fifty in each of two caves, feeding them with bread and water through the famine that was in the days of the Prophet [Elijah](/saints/elijah-the-tishbite). The account that follows is of Ahab sending him out through the land, and of his meeting with Elijah.

He came to the prophet in humility, knelt before him and begged him: Man of God, spare my soul and the souls of thy servants. Elijah spared him and raised him up, and went with him to the king. From that time Obadiah left the king's service and followed the Prophet Elijah, and received the spirit of prophecy, as one who had kept and fed the prophets of the Lord and had followed the prophet. Then, dying, he was buried with his fathers. The page gives no year.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 19 November, and saint.gr adds what the Romanian line does not. It glosses his name — a Hebrew word meaning servant of the Lord, or one who confesses — and sets him among the twelve lesser prophets, from Shechem, out of the field of Bethocharam. It quotes his own book against pride, that a man who nests among the stars like an eagle will still be brought down, and draws the lesson that humility is the groundwork of the virtues. It also makes him a captain of fifty under king Ahaziah, sent to fetch [Elijah](/saints/elijah-the-tishbite) down from the mountain, who then laid his command down, followed the prophet and served him; he was buried in the tomb of his fathers.
