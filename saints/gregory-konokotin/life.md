# Gregory (Konokotin)

Gregory Konokotin was born on 1 April 1869, a priest’s son, at
Novoyegorlykskoye in Stavropol province, and served as priest and teacher of
the law of God at Takhtinskoye, at the Protection church of Medvedskoye in
the same province, and at Moldavskoye in the Kuban region, with church and
state decorations, among them the order of St Anne for his work in the
schools. He was married and had five children. He was arrested at his home
in Krasnodar on 8 September 1921 “for flight from the Soviet power”; the
case was heard at a disposing session of the college of the Kuban-Black Sea
Cheka on 23 September, which resolved to hold the crime proved and to shoot
him as an enemy of the working people, and the sentence was carried out at
two in the morning of 28 September 1921 in the presence of members of the
college, the same night as [Andrew Kovalev](/saints/andrew-kovalev),
[Gregory Troitsky](/saints/gregory-troitsky) and [John
Yakovlev](/saints/john-yakovlev). He is kept on 15 September of the old
style, 28 September, and in the synaxis of the saints of the Kuban
metropolia.
