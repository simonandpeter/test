# Nicholas (Pisarevsky)

His line in the calendar is four words and a year, and the same four words stand on another day of the same calendar for another man. The Сретенский monastery’s calendar for 21 апреля ст. ст. prints «Св. Николая исп., пресвитера (1933)» — a confessor of that name, a presbyter, 1933 — and the name page behind the forename gives the surname the line leaves out: «Николай (Писаревский), иерей, исп.»

The doubling is worth setting down, because the line by itself cannot be trusted here. The same calendar prints the identical words for 19 августа ст. ст., and the name page behind that forename is a different page for a different man, Николай (Лебедев), a hieromartyr, whose days are 26 января and the nineteenth of August. This priest’s own page gives 26 января and the twenty-first of April and no other day. Two men, one printed line, two days, and the name links are the only thing on the site that parts them.

Beyond the surname there is nothing. There is no life page for him on the day page and none on his name page, and 1933 is the only year either of them gives. Nothing is said of where he was born, what parish he served, when or by whom he was arrested, or where and how he died.

His name page lists one other day, «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church, and that day page was not read. One row per church is the schema, so the day is recorded on his Russian row and he does not render on it.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 21 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260421.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9721.html); read 8 October 2026.*
