# John (Popov)

John Petrovich Popov was born on 20 June 1863 in the village of Burdukovo in
the Gryazovets district of Vologda province, the son of the deacon of the St
Nicholas church. In 1883 he finished the Vologda church school and two
classes of the seminary, and could go no further for family reasons; on 25
October 1885 he was made psalmist of the Transfiguration church at Shezham
in the Yarensk district, and from 1889 to 1900 taught church singing without
pay at the zemstvo school at Aikino. On 30 May 1901 he was moved to the
church of the Mother of God at Vazhkurya in the Ust-Sysolsk district,
confirmed in the post in 1906, and taught the law of God and singing in the
parish school and the zemstvo school there. In 1922 he was ordained priest
and served at the church of John the Forerunner at Kerchomya in the
Ust-Kulom district of the Komi autonomous region, the last priest Kerchomya
had in the twentieth century. In 1932 he was arrested for concealing church
valuables and the case was dropped for want of a crime; on 2 September 1937
he was arrested for systematic counter-revolutionary agitation against the
measures of the Soviet power and the building of collective farms, spreading
slanderous rumours and regretting the shooting of enemies of the people, and
held in the prison at Syktyvkar. On 21 September 1937 the special troika of
the Komi NKVD sentenced him to death, and he was shot on 23 September 1937
near Syktyvkar. He was numbered among the new martyrs by the Holy Synod on 6
October 2001, and is kept on 10 September of the old style, 23 September,
and in the synaxis of the Komi saints.
