# Pacianus of Barcelona

Pacianus lived in the fourth century in the city of Barcelona, in Catalonia, in Spain. He had been a married man, but God took his companion to himself, and Pacianus gave his life to the Church. saint.gr calls him a man of broad education, with a great ability in the handling of speech and with an adherence and an exactness in the life and tradition of the Church, who offered the gifts God had given him in the service of the local Church. He was ordained bishop of Barcelona about the year 363.

From that place, the page says, the bishop Pacianus doubled his zeal and increased the talents his Lord had entrusted to him, and spent himself on the shepherding of his rational sheep. In an age when the heresies were plundering the field of Christ he wrote theological works against them and in defence of the faith, and taught the faithful to keep the piety of their fathers and not to be defiled by contact with the heretics — with the Arians and the Novatianists above all — but to stay firmly attached to the Orthodox dogmatic tradition of the holy Church.

He fell asleep in peace in the year 390. The page heads him «(; - 390)», so that the year of his birth stands as a question mark; it names no relic, no writing of his by its title, and no place of his but Barcelona, and it prints no hymn.
