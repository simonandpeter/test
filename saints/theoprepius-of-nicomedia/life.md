# Theoprepius of Nicomedia

Theoprepius was one of the martyrs kept together on 22 August — Agathonicus, Zoticus, Theoprepius (Bogolep), Acindynus, Severian, Zeno and others — who died for Christ under Maximian. Agathonicus, of the noble family of the Hippasii, lived at Nicomedia; learned in the Scriptures, he turned many pagans to faith in Christ, among them the first member of the Senate, the princeps.

The count Eutolmius, sent to the Pontic country, crucified at Karpen the disciples of the Christian Zoticus who refused to sacrifice to the idols, and brought Zoticus to Nicomedia, where — the Prologue carries on — he seized and bound Agathonicus, Principus, Theoprepius, Acindynus, Severian, Zeno and many others, and led them bound towards Byzantium; on the way Zoticus, Theoprepius and Acindynus died of their wounds and exhaustion, Severian was killed near Chalcedon, and Agathonicus with the rest was taken to Thrace, to Selymbria, where after torture before the emperor himself they were beheaded.

The Romanian calendar keeps him on 22 August with his company, and doxologia.ro prints one life under five of their names. He is named among the Christians whom the count Eutolmius bound at Nicomedia together with [Agathonicus](/saints/agathonicus-of-nicomedia) and with Princeps, whom Agathonicus had taught the faith, and led into Thrace, where the emperor Maximian had come, to be tortured there. In the country called Potama the count killed Theoprepius with [Zoticus](/saints/zoticus-of-nicomedia) and [Acindynus](/saints/acindynus-of-nicomedia), because they could no longer walk for the beatings they had been given; [Severian](/saints/severian-of-nicomedia) was killed near Chalcedon, and the rest at Selymbria.
