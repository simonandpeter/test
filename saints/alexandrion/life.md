# Alexandrion

saint.gr's calendar for 6 Ιουλίου names him and gives no life. The whole of its account is one sentence, «Ο Άγιος Αλεξανδρίων μαρτύρησε ως εξής: αφού τον έδεσαν σ' ένα τροχό, κατόπιν τον άφησαν να κυλήσει με δύναμη σε μια μεγάλη κατηφοριά και έτσι ο Αλεξανδρίων, που ήταν δεμένος στον τροχό βρήκε τραγικό θάνατο» — he was martyred thus: they bound him to a wheel and then let it roll with force down a long slope, and so, bound to the wheel, he met a terrible death. The page names no country, no city, no century, no year, no emperor and no judge, and it prints no hymn under his line.

Besides that sentence it carries only the couplet the synaxaria print for him: «Aλεξανδρίων και κατωφερώς τρέχων, / Aνωφερώς υψούτο προς Θεόν Λόγον» — Alexandrion, even while running downhill, was lifted upward towards God the Word. That is everything the source says of him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/678/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
