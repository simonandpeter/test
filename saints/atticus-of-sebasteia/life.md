# Atticus of Sebasteia

saint.gr's calendar for 2 Νοεμβρίου keeps ten soldiers in one line: «Άγιοι Εύδοξιος, Αγάπιος και άλλοι οκτώ μάρτυρες, Αττικός, Μαρίνος, Ωκεανός, Ευστράτιος, Καρτέριος, Νικοπολιτιανός, Στύραξ και Τωβίας». The notice behind it gives each of the ten a line of his own under «Άγιοι που εορτάζουν», and each line is dated «(; - 315)». They were all soldiers in the city of Sebasteia, the notice says, in the years of the emperor Licinius.

In the year 315 they were examined by Auxanios, the governor of Sebasteia, by the duke Marcellus, and by Marcus Agricolaus. They confessed their Christian faith with courage and were cruelly tortured in various ways; and at the end they were thrown into the fire and burned alive, so taking the unfading crown of martyrdom together. The epigram over them counts them: ten champions who endured the fire, and the ten came to the best of ends.

The prose of that notice names no one of the ten; the names stand in the heading and in the list, and nothing distinguishes this Atticus from his nine companions but his place in them. The corpus holds two other men of the name — Atticus the martyr, whom saint.gr keeps on 26 Αυγούστου with Sisinius, and Atticus of Constantinople — and they are other entries on other days, so other men.

The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands, bare as it is.

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar keeps the same company one day later than the Greek, on 3 ноября ст. ст., in a single line of seven names and a phrase: «Мчч. Аттика, Агапия, Евдоксия, Катерия, Истукария, Пактовия, Никтополиона и дружины их (ок. 320).» His is the first of the seven, and the name link behind it gives «Аттик Севастийский», which is the first time any page read for this folder has put his place into his name. Four of the seven are men this corpus already held from the Greek line of ten; five of that ten the Russian line does not print, and they are the «дружина», the company, it leaves unnamed. The calendar gives the company no life page and no hymn.

Two of the three names the corpus did not hold were made folders on this day, [Istucarius](/saints/istucarius-of-sebasteia) and [Pactobius](/saints/pactobius-of-sebasteia); the third was left unseated, as a question about whether it is a word the Greek list already prints under another spelling. The Russian year is «ок. 320» against the Greek's 315, so his death is recorded here as 315 or 320, with neither chosen.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 2 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/02/index.aspx) and [the notice of the ten](https://www.saint.gr/1051/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 3 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261103.html), read 4 October 2026.*
