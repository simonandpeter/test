# Nicodemus of Lake Kozha

saint.gr keeps him on 3 Ιουλίου and says that it has nothing to tell. Its heading is «Όσιος Νικόδημος της Λίμνης Κόζα (; - 1640)» and the body of the page is one sentence: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέριες για τον βίο του Ρώσου Αγίου», we have no details of the life of the Russian saint — the page's own spelling of «λεπτομέρειες» slipping as it goes. The lake, the year and the word Russian are the finding.

No monastery, no tonsure, no elder and no century beyond the one year is printed, and the page gives him the rank «Όσιος» and no other, so that is all this folder claims of him. He is not identified here with any of the other Nicodemi the calendars keep. His own Russian calendar has not been read.

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 3 июля ст. ст. — «Прп. Никодима Кожеезерского (1640).» — the same menologion day, the same epithet and the same year as the Greek page this folder was made from. It links a life, which was not opened, and prints four hymns under his name, two troparia and two kontakia.

Those hymns have him leave the imperial city and a great monastery in it, and, steered by the providence of the divine understanding, set out for the country beside the sea; he settled in the wilderness to get away from the noise of the world, drove his enemies off with the weapon of the Cross, and finished his life in fasting and unceasing prayer, emulating, the first of them says, «великим отцем Антонию, и Онуфрию, и Павлу Фивейскому». The second has him live on earth as one without a body and humble the enemy by his own humility and by dwelling in a pit; the last names the wilderness he went out to, Khozyuga.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/03/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4140/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026; and after the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 3 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260703.html), read 9 October 2026.*
