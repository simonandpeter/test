# Theoctistus of Novgorod

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 23 декабря ст. ст. as «Свт. Феоктиста, архиеп. Новгородского (1310)», and its life page says that before he received the see he was abbot of the monastery of the Annunciation near Novgorod. When the archbishop before him died in 1300 the people of Novgorod chose him as their bishop, and the metropolitan ordained him with two Russian bishops assisting.

His chief care, the page says, was the restoring and the building of churches. He consecrated two cathedrals himself, one to a pair of princely martyrs of Rus and one to the Fathers of the First Ecumenical Council, and under him the monastery on Valaam was set in good order. In 1307 his health failed and he withdrew to the monastery of the Annunciation, where he lived until his death, giving himself to the labour of silence.

Healings at his relics led to his glorification in 1664, and in 1786 the relics were carried to Yuriev, where an archimandrite built a side chapel in his honour in the cathedral there. The day page prints a troparion and a kontakion to him, both of which call him the praise of Great Novgorod.
