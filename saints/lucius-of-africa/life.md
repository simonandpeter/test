# Lucius the African

saint.gr names him and gives no life. Its whole account of the company is one sentence: «Οι Άγιοι Μάρτυρες Κουϊντιανός, Λούκιος και Ιουλιανός μαρτύρησαν μαζί με άλλους δέκα εννέα Χριστιανούς, άνδρες και γυναίκες, στην Αφρική, το 430 μ.Χ., από τους αιρετικούς Αρειανούς» — the holy martyrs Quintianus, Lucius and Julian were martyred together with nineteen other Christians, men and women, in Africa, in the year 430, by the Arian heretics.

The page gives no city, no account of the trial and no manner of death, and of the nineteen it gives not one name. It prints no hymn to them. He is named on the day together with [Quintianus](/saints/quintianus-of-africa) and [Julian](/saints/julian-of-africa), and nothing further about any of them is recorded here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 23 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/23/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4058/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
