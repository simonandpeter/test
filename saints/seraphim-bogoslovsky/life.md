# Seraphim (Bogoslovsky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Seraphim first in a pair of hieromonks on 29 July. Its line is «Прмчч. Серафима и Феогноста (1921).» — the venerable martyrs Seraphim and Theognostus, 1921 — and the calendar's own name page for him adds the surname: «Серафим (Богословский), иеромонах, сщмч.», Seraphim Bogoslovsky, hieromonk.

The line and the name page do not agree about what kind of saint he is: the line calls him a venerable martyr and the name page a hieromartyr. This folder writes the line's reading, keeps the name page's rank, and records the disagreement rather than settling it. The second of the pair is [Theognostus (29 July)](/saints/theognostus-29-july), whom the calendar gives no surname at all.

This corpus had already written him down once, without a folder. The life of [Eudocia (Tkachenko)](/saints/evdokia-tkachenko) names him as the spiritual father of the sisters of the John and Mary convent at Stavropol, over whose wishes a superior was set in 1913; that sentence comes from another source and is why the two folders carry a link to each other.

A reading stands behind this day's entry and was not opened. The day's two hymns for the pair, which are in this folder, call them the God-given protectors of Alma-Ata and place their struggle in the mountains of the Tian Shan; nothing has been written into this life or into a `location` from them, a hymn not being a life. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 29 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260729.html); read 13 October 2026.*
