# Simitrius

saint.gr names him and gives him no life. Its whole account is one sentence — «Ο Άγιος Μάρτυς Σιμίτριος μαρτύρησε μαζί με άλλους είκοσι τρείς Χριστιανούς το 159 μ.Χ.», the holy martyr Simitrius was martyred together with another twenty-three Christians in the year 159. The page gives no place, no emperor, no persecutor and no manner of death, and prints neither a synaxarion couplet nor a hymn.

The twenty-three are counted and none of them is named, either in the day's line or on this page, and so no folder is made for any of them: what the source gives is a number, and a number is not a name. What is kept on 26 Μαΐου under his own name is one man, one year, and the company he died in.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3942/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
