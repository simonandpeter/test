# Theophilus of Antioch

The Ohrid Prologue keeps him on the Julian 6 December and gives him four lines, which are the whole of what this folder knows. He had been well schooled in Hellenic philosophy; and having read the Holy Scripture he was baptised and became a great protector of the Christian faith. His work On the Faith, the book says, has been preserved to this day. He governed the church of Antioch for thirteen years, and reposed in the year 181.

That is all. The book gives him no birthplace, no parentage, no account of his episcopate and no manner of death, and it names nobody beside him. The thirteen years are a length and not a date, and no span of years has been made out of them here; the 181 is the one year printed. Where the source has a few lines, this folder says what they say and no more.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 6. децембар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2025-12-19&prolog=1); read 7 October 2026.*
