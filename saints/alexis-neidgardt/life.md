# Alexis (Neidgardt)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him last of three on 24 октября ст. ст., in a line that keeps a bishop, a priest and him together under the year 1918. The line declines his name and gives it without a surname; the title on the name link behind his place supplies the surname and the rank, «Алексий (Нейдгардт), мч.».

What the line does say about him it says by its rank words. It calls the first two Сщмчч., hieromartyrs, and him alone мч., a martyr — so he is a layman, and the link behind his place carries no grade, no office and no see to add to that. He is the second of the two men called Алексий on the line, and it is the rank words and not the surnames that separate them there.

The calendar carries a life for the bishop of the three, and that life does not name him once: it gives the sentence, the garden, the grave already dug, the Russian soldiers who refused and the bodies thrown into the Volga, and it speaks of two men throughout. So his year is the line's 1918 and not the 6 November the other two have, and nothing on any page read says where he was born, what he did or how he died.

The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line. The day page's thirteen troparia and kontakia belong to the four older entries it heads and to the icon it keeps, and none of them to this line of 1918.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 24 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261024.html); read 4 October 2026.*
