# Hesychius (10 May)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 10 mai reads «Sfântul Cuvios Isihie Mărturisitorul», and the page it links for him carries neither a Viață tab nor a Tropar tab — only the feast line "10 Mai" and the readings appointed for the day. No country is named, no century is given, and nothing is told of what he did or how he ended.

The corpus already keeps a martyr Hesychius from the Greek synaxarion for 2 September; this line calls its man «Cuvios» and «Mărturisitor», venerable and confessor, not a martyr, and nothing read here connects the two. The folder stands on the calendar's line alone.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and gives him no life there. saint.gr's page for the tenth of May prints his name, the meaning of it — from ἥσυχος, the quiet and calm one, the maker of calm — a couplet, and one sentence of direction: the memory of the venerable Hesychius the Confessor is honoured on the sixth of March, where his life is also to be found. This reader did not open that page, and nothing is set down here from it. The couplet says that Hesychius, leading his life quietly, passes over to God in quietness: «Τὸν βίον Ἡσύχιος ἄγων ἡσύχως, / Ἐν ἡσυχίᾳ πρὸς Θεὸν διαβαίνει».

The Russian church calendar keeps a Hesychius on 10 мая ст. ст., and unlike the Romanian and Greek lines this folder was written from, it gives him a life — and a different rank. It calls him «Мч. Исихия Антиохийского», the martyr Hesychius of Antioch, of the fourth century, and makes him a man who held a high and honoured office at the imperial court in the city of Antioch in the reign of Максимиан Галерий, which it dates 305 to 311.

Maximian published an edict depriving all Christians of military rank and putting them out of the service: those who would not change their faith he ordered to take off their soldiers’ belts and their marks of honour and to go over to the standing of hired servants. Hesychius was one of these. The emperor had him strip off a dignitary’s dress, put on coarse clothing and keep company with the women servants; and some days later called him and asked whether he was not ashamed to remain in such dishonour. «Честь, которую я имел от тебя, временна» was the answer — the honour I had from thee is a passing thing. Maximian then ordered him drowned in the river with a millstone tied about his neck. The life closes by saying that the year of the martyr’s death is not exactly known.

Whether the martyr of Antioch and the venerable confessor the Romanian and Greek calendars keep on this day are one man is a question this folder states and does not close. The three calendars agree on the day and on the name and differ on the rank, and the Russian is the only one of them that tells a story; so its story stands here, and the ranks the other two give are left as they were.
