# Hesychius (10 May)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 10 mai reads «Sfântul Cuvios Isihie Mărturisitorul», and the page it links for him carries neither a Viață tab nor a Tropar tab — only the feast line "10 Mai" and the readings appointed for the day. No country is named, no century is given, and nothing is told of what he did or how he ended.

The corpus already keeps a martyr Hesychius from the Greek synaxarion for 2 September; this line calls its man «Cuvios» and «Mărturisitor», venerable and confessor, not a martyr, and nothing read here connects the two. The folder stands on the calendar's line alone.
