# Timothy (Ulyanov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names a Timothy on 10 июня and gives no life of him. Its line is the last of the day and the whole of what it says: «Сщмч. Тимофея пресвитера (1940).» — the hieromartyr Timothy, a presbyter, and the year 1940. So what the calendar tells is that he was a priest counted among the martyrs and that he died in 1940, which is late for this calendar's new martyrs and later than any other entry of the day.

His own name page on that site, 13724, carries nothing but the heading «Тимофей (Ульянов), иерей, сщмч.», which gives the surname the day's line leaves out and grades him иерей, a priest, and the two days «26 января (Новомуч.) , 10 июня». There is no life page for him on the site, no icon and no article, and no page names a parish, a diocese or a place.

The 26 января commemoration of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia was not read, and nothing of it is in this folder. The day page prints hymns for four of the commemorations it heads and none under his name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 10 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260610.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13724.html); read 8 October 2026.*
