# Juliana (4 December)

saint.gr keeps a holy Juliana on the fourth of December, and the whole of what it says of her is six words: «Η Αγία Ιουλιανή μαρτύρησε δια ξίφους» — the holy Juliana was martyred by the sword. There is no country, no century, no emperor, no judge, no companion and no burial, and no relic; and the page prints no hymn for her.

What it prints besides is her couplet, which asks the Word to receive Juliana as a dove — and, if she is not cut down quickly, as one twittering. The page's note on the name is about Julius Caesar and not about her. Nothing else is recorded here, because nothing else is there; what the Greek calendar attests is that a woman of that name is kept on this day and that she died by the sword.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 4 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/04/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3146/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
