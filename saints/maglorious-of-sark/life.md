# Maglorious of Sark

saint.gr's calendar for 24 Οκτωβρίου names Maglorious of Sark, and the page behind the name is two sentences: he came from Ireland, it says, and fell asleep in 575; and we have no details of the saint's life. The listing and the heading both print his name and his island in Latin letters and give no Greek form of either.

Nothing else is printed for him at all: no see, no monastery, no companions, no relics, no couplet and no hymn. The page's heading gives the year of his death and leaves the year of his birth unknown, and there is no rank word in the line, so this folder carries no `types`.
