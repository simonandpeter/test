# Barsimos, with his two brothers

saint.gr's calendar for 24 Ιανουαρίου names «Άγιος Βάρσιμος και τα δύο αδέλφια του» — Barsimos and his two brothers — and the page behind that line gives one sentence: Saint Barsimos and his two brothers were martyred by the sword. The two brothers are commemorated with him, and the page heads them «Αγια Δυο Αδελφια Του Αγιου Βαρσιμου», the two holy brothers of Saint Barsimos; but it never gives either of them a name.

The distich printed over the three is «Τοὺς τρεῖς Ἀδελφοὺς θεῖος εἷς συσχὼν πόθος, Θεῖον ποθεῖν ἔπειθεν ἐκ ξίφους τέλος» — one divine longing held the three brothers, and persuaded them to long for a divine end by the sword. No country, no year, no emperor, no hymn and no relics are printed for any of them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/24/index.aspx) and [the page for the three of them](https://www.saint.gr/3522/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
