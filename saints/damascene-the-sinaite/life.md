# Damascene (Tzangares)

saint.gr's line for him is «Άγιος Δαμασκηνός ο Ιερομάρτυρας ο Σιναΐτης», and the page behind it is a single sentence: Damascene — the family name is given in brackets, Tzangares — was a brother of the monastery of Sinai, and was martyred by the Mezenides in the year 1623 outside the monastery's own wall.

That is the whole source. It gives him the rank of hieromartyr, the house he belonged to, his killers, the year, and the place just outside the enclosure; it gives no birth, no account of the martyrdom, no hymn and no verses. The corpus's other Damascenes are a Bulgarian hieromonk hanged at Svishtov in 1771, a Romanian entry of 13 November that names nobody, and a Russian bishop shot in 1937; this is none of them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3613/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
