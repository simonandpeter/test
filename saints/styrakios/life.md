# Styrakios

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life. The whole of its notice is one sentence about his death and one about the books: «Ο Άγιος Στυράκιος μαρτύρησε δια ξίφους.» The holy Styrakios suffered martyrdom by the sword. And then: «Ορισμένα αγιολόγια μαζί με τη μνήμη του Aγίου Στυρακίου, αναφέρουν και αυτή κάποιου μάρτυρα Ερμολάου» — some menologia set the memory of a certain martyr Hermolaus beside his.

Above those sentences the page prints the distich the Greek books give him, which plays on his name and the storax tree: «Aνήκε τμηθείς ο Στυράκιος ξίφει, / Oσμήν νοητήν ως νοητός τις στύραξ» — cut down by the sword, Styrakios sent up a spiritual fragrance, like some spiritual storax. The page gives no year, no country, no emperor and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2239/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
