# Augustine of Hippo

Born at Thagaste in Roman Numidia to a pagan father and a Christian mother, and
educated in rhetoric at Carthage, Augustine taught in Rome and Milan and passed
through Manichaeism and Neoplatonism before his baptism in 387 at the age of
thirty-two. He was made bishop of the small port of Hippo Regius in 395 and
stayed there for thirty-five years.

The *Confessions* and *The City of God* are the two works that carried him
beyond his own century, but the bulk of what he wrote was occasional: sermons,
letters, and the long controversies with the Donatists over the validity of
sacraments administered by unworthy clergy, and with Pelagius over grace and the
will. His positions in the second of these shaped Western theology so heavily
that the Reformation was in large part an argument between readings of him.

He died with the Vandals besieging his city. He is one of the few Latin fathers
kept in the Eastern calendar, though on a different day and with a more
qualified title.

The Ohrid Prologue of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović keeps him on the Julian 15
June, the same menologion day his Russian row already carries, and the
Православни подсетник serves that day at
[pravoslavno.rs](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-06-28&prolog=1);
read 5 October 2026. Its heading calls him Блажени — blessed rather than holy —
and bishop of Hippo, which is the qualified title the paragraph above names.

What that book gives is four lines. He was turned from paganism to Christianity,
it says, by the counsels, the tears and the prayers of his mother
[Monica](/saints/monica-mother-of-augustine). He was a great teacher of the
Church and an influential writer, with certain unapproved extremes in his
teaching. He glorified the Lord as bishop of Hippo for thirty-five years, and
lived on earth seventy-six years in all, which that book sets between 354 and
430 — the two years this folder already held from other books, and the only two
it takes from this one.
