# Meliton (30 June)

saint.gr's calendar for 30 Ιουνίου names him and gives him almost no life. Its whole account is one sentence: «Ο Άγιος Μελίτων μαρτύρησε δια ξίφους. Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του» — the holy Meliton was martyred by the sword, and we have no further details of his life. The page prints no place, no reign and no year, so none is recorded here; the sword is the one thing it says about him.

What else it prints is the distich the Greek synaxaria carry for him, built on his name, which comes from μέλι, honey: «Eί που Mελίτων και θεόρρυτον μέλι, / Eχρήν εκεί βάψαντα σην τομήν γράφειν» — if ever Meliton and God-flowing honey are found together, there one should dip the pen and write of your beheading.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 30 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/30/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/647/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
