# Manatho

saint.gr's calendar for 20 Οκτωβρίου names Manatho the virgin and gives her three lines. She suffered, the page says, in the years of Maximian, whose reign it dates 286 to 305. She was arrested at Scythopolis in Palestine because she was a Christian; she was dragged naked through the city; and at the last she was burned alive. The page gives no year inside the reign, and nothing of her family, her trade or her words.

Over the entry stands a couplet which asks what the fire could have had left to cleanse in one who was a virgin in spirit and in body alike. The page adds that her memory is kept again on 12 November, and it prints no hymn for her.

The Russian calendar keeps her too, and on a day of its own. days.pravoslavie.ru's line for 13 ноября ст. ст. reads «Мц. Манефы (ок. 308).», and the name link behind it, page 2583, gives «Манефа Кесарийская (Палестинская), дева, мц.» — a virgin and a martyr, of Caesarea in Palestine, where saint.gr named Scythopolis in Palestine as the place of her arrest. The two cities are a difference the pages leave standing, and no place is proposed in this folder for either. The Russian prints no life for her and no hymn under her name; the day page's troparion and kontakion both belong to the great hierarch who heads the day.

It dates her «ок. 308», which falls outside the reign of Maximian saint.gr dates her by, so the death interval here is widened to hold both readings with both sources' words in its note. The line above hers on the same Russian day reads «Мчч. Антонина , Никифора и Германа (ок. 308).» — three of the four martyrs saint.gr says keep their memory with her on the Greek 12 November. Sharing a day is not a relation, and neither host says she was arrested or killed with them, so no relation is written.

The Ohrid Prologue gives the four of them one entry and divides it as the deaths divided. The three men saw the heathen at one of their festivals bowing to idols with shouting and dancing, and came out fearlessly before the crowd and began to preach the one God in Trinity; the governor of Caesarea in Palestine, where this happened, was so exasperated that he ordered their heads struck off at once.

Of her it says this much, and it is more than this folder had: she was a Christian virgin, and she followed the martyrs as they were led to the place of execution. She too was seized, and after bitter examinations was burned in a fire, where the three men were beheaded. All of them suffered in 308, which falls inside the interval this folder already holds. The book spells her name «Манета», a third spelling beside the Greek and the Russian the folder already carries.
