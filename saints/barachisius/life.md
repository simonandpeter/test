# Barachisius

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 29 March is «Sfântul Mucenic Varahisie», and its page for him carries no Viață: only the service texts, which it prints for him and for [Jonah](/saints/jonah-martyr-29-march) together — a canon, a troparion and a kontakion «al Sfinţilor Mucenici Iona şi Varahisie şi al celor împreună cu dânşii», to the holy martyrs Jonah and Barachisius and those with them.

So the calendar keeps the two of them on the same day, with companions it does not number or name, and neither hymn says anything particular of either man: the troparion is the common of martyrs, and the kontakion calls them enlightened by the true Grace and setting the ends of the earth aright. That is the whole of what the Romanian source prints, and this entry records it and nothing more.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and tells his end apart from his companion's. saint.gr dates the martyrdom to about the year 330, under Shapur king of the Persians and Constantine the Great. He and [Jonah](/saints/jonah-martyr-29-march) were ascetics who left their monastery for a small town called Marbiabokh, or Marmiabokh, and there visited the nine martyrs held in its prison — [Zanithas](/saints/zanithas-companion-of-jonah), [Lazarus](/saints/lazarus-companion-of-jonah), [Marouthas](/saints/marouthas-companion-of-jonah), [Narses](/saints/narses-companion-of-jonah), [Elias](/saints/elias-companion-of-jonah), [Mares](/saints/mares-companion-of-jonah), [Abibus](/saints/abibus-companion-of-jonah), [Simiathes](/saints/simiathes-companion-of-jonah) and [Sabbas](/saints/sabbas-companion-of-jonah) — to encourage them for their martyrdom, and were arrested on the spot. Three Persian magistrates, Masdrath, Siro and Marmises, told the two to deny Christ and worship the fire, the water and the sun; when they would not, both were bound, beaten, dragged over rough rods and left out in the frost all night. Barachisius was then dragged naked through thorns, thrown into a pit, and boiling pitch was poured down his throat, and so his life was ended. The relics of the whole company were bought by a devout Christian named Audeisotes for five hundred miliarisia and buried with reverence. The page puts the nine martyrs' perfecting on 27 March and his own and Jonah's on 29 March, and its couplet makes the pitch a cup: «Διψῶν Βαραχήσιος Ἀθλητῶν τέλους, Χανδὸν ζεούσης ἐκπίνει πίσσης σκύφον» — thirsting for the martyrs' end, Barachisius drinks off at a gulp a cup of boiling pitch.

The Russian church calendar keeps the company one day earlier than the Greek, on 28 марта ст. ст., and its life gives what the Romanian source has not. He and [Jonah](/saints/jonah-martyr-29-march) were brothers and Christians, living in the village of Ясы in Persia under king Sapor, whose reign it puts at 310 to 331 and whom it calls a cruel persecutor of Christians. Hearing that Christians were being tormented in the town of Варавох, they went there, found nine martyrs in the prison and urged them to hold firmly to the faith to the end; and for that the two brothers were seized.

They were brought before the Persian princes Масдраф, Сироф and Мармисий, who pressed them to worship the sun, the fire and the water. They confessed their faith in Christ and refused, and were given over to the cruellest torments. His brother suffered first; then red-hot irons were put on his own hands, molten lead was poured into his nose, his ears and his mouth, and he was torn while bound to a turning wheel. He gave up his spirit to God after boiling pitch had been poured into his mouth. A devout Christian named Авдисот buried the bodies of the two brothers and of the others put to death with them.
