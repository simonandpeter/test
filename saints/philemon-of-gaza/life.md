# Philemon of Gaza

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. His own page on the site carries his name, the day — 14 Februarie — and the Apostle and Gospel appointed to be read, and nothing else: there is no Viață tab, no account of his see or his times, no year of birth or death, no place named beyond Gaza itself, and no hymn. What the calendar's line says is the whole of the record here: «Sfântul Filimon, Episcopul Gazei» — Saint Philemon, bishop of Gaza.

The readings the page prints beside the name are those the Church appoints for a hierarch — from the Epistle to the Hebrews, that such a high priest became us, holy, guileless, undefiled and separated from sinners; and from John, the Lord's words that he is the door, and that the good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep. They belong to the day's service and are not a statement about this man, and they are noted here only because they are what the page holds in the place where a life would be.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 14 februarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/14-februarie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-filimon-episcopul-gazei), which carries no life; read 19 September 2026.*
