# Philemon of Gaza

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. His own page on the site carries his name, the day — 14 Februarie — and the Apostle and Gospel appointed to be read, and nothing else: there is no Viață tab, no account of his see or his times, no year of birth or death, no place named beyond Gaza itself, and no hymn. What the calendar's line says is the whole of the record here: «Sfântul Filimon, Episcopul Gazei» — Saint Philemon, bishop of Gaza.

The readings the page prints beside the name are those the Church appoints for a hierarch — from the Epistle to the Hebrews, that such a high priest became us, holy, guileless, undefiled and separated from sinners; and from John, the Lord's words that he is the door, and that the good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep. They belong to the day's service and are not a statement about this man, and they are noted here only because they are what the page holds in the place where a life would be.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, with the same see, and has no more of his life than the Romanian did: «Η μνήμη του Αγίου Φιλήμωνα αναφέρεται μόνο στον Πατμιακό Κώδικα 266» — his memory is mentioned only in Patmos Codex 266, saint.gr says, and we have no further details of the saint's life. The page prints two distichs over him. The first is in his own voice, that being present in the middle of the furnace and bearing witness he wrote his calling in the sky; the second, that Philemon, having been jealous for the glory of the Apostle, was willing to die worthily for Christ. The page carries no year, no place of death and no hymn.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 14 februarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/14-februarie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-filimon-episcopul-gazei), which carries no life; read 19 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 14 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/14/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3699/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026.*
