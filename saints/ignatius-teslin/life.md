# Ignatius (Teslin)

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him ninth of twenty-four and gives no life; the life here is the one azbyka.ru prints for the civil 2 декабря, out of pstgu.ru and the Orthodox Encyclopaedia. Ignatius Stepanovich Teslin was born in 1873 in the village of Studenets in the Syzran district of the province of Simbirsk. He came of a peasant family and kept a grocer’s shop; afterwards he finished the seminary and was ordained priest in 1916.

In 1930 he was held for a short time by the OGPU at Syzran, and on release moved to Moscow. On 15 December 1934 he was arrested again, this time charged with sympathy for the killing of Kirov; he was held in the Butyrka prison and admitted nothing. On 10 February 1935 a special conference of the NKVD sentenced him to three years of exile in Kazakhstan, and he lived at the settlement of Mayskoye in the Beskaragay district of the Pavlodar region as a workman of the Maysky mine.

On 25 November 1937 he was arrested together with the archimandrite Gennadius (Rebeza), charged with counter-revolutionary work carried on «совместно с другими служителями религиозного культа». He admitted nothing, but on 1 December 1937 the special troika of the East Kazakhstan region sentenced him to be shot and he was executed the next day. He lies in an unmarked grave. The Jubilee Council of Bishops of 2000 glorified him.
