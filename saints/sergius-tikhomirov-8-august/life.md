# Sergius (Tikhomirov), 8 August

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Sergius second of a pair of priests on 8 August and gives no life for either of them. Its line is «Сщмчч. Николая и Сергия пресвитеров (1930).» — the hieromartyrs Nicholas and Sergius, presbyters, 1930 — and the calendar's own name page for him adds the surname the line leaves out: «Сергий (Тихомиров), иерей, сщмч.», Sergius Tikhomirov, priest.

This corpus already keeps a priest of the same forename and the same surname, shot in 1922 and kept on 13 May, and these are two men. The calendar hangs the two names on two different pages of its own index, 4778 and 15022, and gives the two of them two different years. The day in the folder's title is here to keep them apart, because the calendar's line gives nothing else that would.

That is the whole of the record. He was a priest; he died in 1930; the calendar keeps him on this day, within the afterfeast of the Transfiguration, among the new martyrs of Russia. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 8 августа ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260808.html); read 13 October 2026.*
