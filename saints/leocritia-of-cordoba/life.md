# Leocritia of Cordoba

Leocritia was a young Christian woman of Cordoba in Spain, in the years when the city stood under the Mohammedan conquerors and took the first place, saint.gr says, in the Spanish martyrology of the ninth century. Her own parents wanted her to take the religion of Mohammed, and she was sheltered and hidden by [Eulogius](/saints/eulogius-of-cordoba), who had just been chosen bishop of Cordoba; and it was for that shelter that he was arrested, accused not only of abduction but of corrupting her.

The page keeps his defence, that no shepherd has ever refused his support to any member of his flock, and that it is the duty of a priest of Christ to teach the faithful that if they must choose between God and their parents they are to choose God. He was beheaded in the year 859, and of Leocritia the page says this: «Η Λεωκρητία αποκεφαλίσθηκε την επόμενη Τετάρτη, όπως διασώζουν τα Συναξάρια» — she was beheaded on the following Wednesday, as the synaxaria preserve it, and she added one more crown of glory to the martyrology of the Orthodox Church of Spain.

That is the whole of what is recorded of her. The page gives her no age, no family name, no account of her own trial and no hymn, and it heads her, as it heads Eulogius, with a question mark for the year of her birth.
