# Alexander (Gnevushev)

The Сретенский monastery's calendar prints him last of the five entries it puts on the Julian 15 April, and the whole of the entry is one line: «Сщмч. Александра пресвитера (1930).» — the hieromartyr Alexander, a presbyter, and the year 1930. There is no life under his name on the day page and none behind it. The name link carries a fuller form than the line does, «Александр (Гневушев), иерей, сщмч.», with a surname the line leaves out altogether and the word иерей, a priest, for the grade he held.

That is the whole of what the source gives. No year or place of birth, no parish, no diocese, no house, no arrest, no sentence, no word of his own and nothing of the years between: the bracketed year is the year of death, and the grade is the only thing said about his state in life. It is written here as the calendar has it rather than filled out from elsewhere, and a reader who wants more of him will have to go to a source this folder has not read.

His second day on that page is 26 января, marked as the new martyrs', which is the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia — a commemoration of a whole company rather than a second day of his own, so it is not recorded here as a feast. The page prints an icon of him and nothing else. The day's single hymn under «Молитвы» is a kontakion of the apostles, belonging to the three of the Seventy the day is headed for, and there is nothing under his name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 15 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260415.html), which gives him one line and no life, and [the calendar's page for his name](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14246.html); read 7 October 2026.*
