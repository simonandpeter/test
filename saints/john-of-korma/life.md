# John of Korma

The Russian calendar names him and gives him no life page. Its line for 18 мая ст. ст. is «Прав. Иоанна Кормянского (1917)» — the righteous John of Korma, with the year and nothing else — and the day page hangs no reading on it. What the calendar does carry is a paragraph on his own name page, which is more than most of its twentieth-century entries get, and it is made of four things he was remembered for.

He liked to repeat the words «Вера без дел мертва» — faith without works is dead — and the page says he gave the example himself, caring in a fatherly way for widows and orphans. Once he gave a poor widow and her five children the cow his own family lived on. When the possessed were brought to him they fell flat on the ground as soon as he made the sign of the cross over them. And he called people to repentance without tiring, foreseeing prophetically that the abomination of desolation was near and that streams of blood would soon water the Russian land.

That is all the calendar prints of him: no surname, no birthplace, no year of birth, no grade in the clergy. The year 1917 is in the day line alone.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 18 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260518.html), which names him and gives no life, and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14952.html), which carries the paragraph above; read 8 October 2026.*
