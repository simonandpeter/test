Ананьевского, Константина , Димитрия , Нестора , Феодора , Константина , Виктора , Илии , Павла пресвитеров, Иосифа диакона и прмч. Алексия (1937).» One year stands for all eleven, «Сщмчч.» heads the ten clergy and «прмч.» the monk who closes the line, and the grades it prints sort them into a bishop, eight presbyters, a deacon and a hieromonk. The name link behind his place supplies the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line leaves out, «Святитель Парфений (Брянских), епископ Ананьевский, сщмч.», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from.

**days.pravoslavie.ru gives this company nothing but that line.** Each of the eleven name pages carries a title, a list of days and no text at all, and not one of them has a `/Life/` link of any kind; the day page's hymns are the two martyrs' of 284–305, the venerable Matrona's, the Mother of God's before the Скоропослушница icon and the wonderworker of Aegina's, and nothing is sung under any of these eleven names. The life below is azbyka.ru's, read in the in-app browser because that host answers no fetcher, and the paragraphs are this corpus's own.

In the world he was Пётр Арсеньевич Брянских, born in 1881 into the family of a hereditary honorary citizen of Irkutsk who managed the industrial and trading firm «Немчинов и сын». After the Theological Academy he went to Germany to continue his studies at the university of Berlin, where he followed a course in the biblical sciences. He took the monastic habit and was ordained priest in 1911, and was very soon raised archimandrite.

Before the revolution of 1917 he taught in the theological schools of Zhitomir and was head and teacher of Old Testament in the Pastoral Missionary Seminary attached to the Grigoriev-Bizyukov monastery in the Kherson country. After the revolution he moved to Kherson, where he lived in the bishop's house with [Procopius (Titov)](/saints/procopius-titov) and acted as his secretary. In 1921, in the open persecution of the Church, he was raised to the episcopate: vicar of the Odessa diocese, first bishop of Novomirgorod and then of Ananyev, and he spoke out plainly against the Renovationists.

He was arrested at the end of 1921 and released after half a year, and went to Kiev; detained there again in 1922, he went on release to Moscow and lived at the Danilov monastery with no right of departure. In 1925 he was arrested in the case of [Peter (Polyansky)](/saints/peter-polyansky). The charge was that he «ставил себе задачей нанесение ущерба диктатуре пролетариата», and the investigation accused him of belonging to a so-called «Даниловский синод» — several bishops living at that monastery who, after the patriarch's death that year, might have formed a synod to elect a new primate.

He and [Ambrose (Polyansky)](/saints/ambrose-polyansky) were charged with having most influence over the other bishops, and in particular with resolving to defend the emigrant part of the Russian Church by denying that it was engaged in anti-Soviet activity. He was sentenced to three years' exile in the Komi-Zyryan country. He returned to Moscow in 1928 and to the same confinement at the Danilov monastery, and was arrested a year later for resisting the closing of one of that monastery's churches.

That sentence was three more years of exile. On the road from the Butyrka prison to the town of Uil he was beaten, and lay in the prison hospital at Samara. Released in 1933, he could get neither a passport nor a registration and for a time had nowhere to live; he found out where his mother was living and in 1934 went to her at Kimry in the Tver country. He was arrested there the same year for anti-Soviet agitation — he «устраивал на своей квартире тайные богослужения» — and sentenced to five years' exile in the Northern country.

In exile at Arkhangelsk he was arrested on 4 August 1937 and shot on 22 November of the same year. 9 ноября ст. ст. is the civil 22 November, so the calendar keeps him on the very day he was shot. **The office this folder owes and cannot have is “Bishop of Ananyev”**, the see the day's own line gives him: no locale pack holds Ananyev and it cannot be composed from a row they do hold, so the `office` here is the bare rank and the see is in this paragraph. Novomirgorod before it is the same case, and he held the two in succession rather than in dispute.
