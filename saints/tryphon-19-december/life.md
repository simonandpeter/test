# Tryphon (19 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 19 December keeps Saint Tryphon, and the whole of what its page says of him is one sentence: Saint Tryphon was martyred by hanging, above a willow tree. There is nothing before that sentence and nothing after it at all — no year, no emperor, no judge, no city, no relic and no hymn.

What the page carries besides is an epigram, and it repeats the one detail there is: Tryphon went up in the body to a willow, and in the spirit to the great height of heaven. The willow is the whole of his story as the Greek calendar keeps it, and nothing further is drafted from the epigram.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 19 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/19/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3301/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
