# Erasmus

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 18 iunie reads «Sfântul Cuvios Erasm», and the page behind it carries neither a Viață tab nor a Tropar tab: there is no account of him at all, no country, no century, no manner of life beyond the rank the line gives him, that of a venerable — a monastic. Nothing more is recorded here, because nothing more was printed.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 18 June, and saint.gr has no more of his life than the Romanian calendar does: the whole of its account is the sentence «Ο Όσιος Έρασμος απεβίωσε ειρηνικά», the venerable Erasmus reposed in peace. Beside it the page prints a two-line epigram — dying, Erasmus says, I am glad, for dying is a lovely thing to me — which plays on his name, and the page's own note that Erasmus comes from the verb to love and means the lovable. Under that note it prints a line of general information about the name, that it belonged to a distinguished Dutch writer and grammarian; that is a remark about the name and not about this saint, and nothing of it is taken into this life. There is no country, no year and no hymn.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 18 iunie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/18-iunie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-erasm); read 24 September 2026; saint.gr's calendar for 18 Ιουνίου and [its life](https://www.saint.gr/1896/saint.aspx), read 1 October 2026.*
