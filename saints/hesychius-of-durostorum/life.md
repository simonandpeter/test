# Hesychius of Durostorum

doxologia.ro's page for Hesychius is four sentences long, and this is what they say. The Romanian Orthodox Church keeps him on 15 June. He was a Christian soldier and belonged to the legion of the Roman army on the Danube, stationed in the fortress of Durostorum in the province of Moesia Inferior, in the days of the emperors Diocletian and of his son-in-law Maximian Galerius. He suffered together with five other holy martyrs: Pasicrates, Valentio, Nicander, Marcian, and Julius the veteran. Hesychius met his martyr's death on 15 June, by the cutting off of his head.

The page gives no year and prints no troparion, and nothing further about him is recorded here.
