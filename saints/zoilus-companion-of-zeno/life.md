# Zoilus, companion of Zeno

saint.gr names the venerable Zoilus with [Zeno](/saints/zeno-companion-of-zoilus) on this day and gives the pair of them one sentence, and it is a sentence about what the site does not know: «Είναι άγνωστο που και πότε τέλεσαν τον δρόμο της ασκήσεως οι Όσιοι Ζήνων και Ζωίλος, οι οποίοι κοιμήθηκαν με ειρήνη» — it is unknown where and when the venerable Zeno and Zoilus finished the road of the ascesis, and they fell asleep in peace.

So two things stand recorded: that he was an ascetic, and that his end was peaceful. No century, no country, no monastery, no elder, no relic and no hymn; and because the page says outright that it has neither the place nor the time, no date is written into this folder at all. The site does not even print over his name the etymology block it sets over his companion's.

The distich over the pair plays on the sound of both names and says nothing about either man: the loosing of life for Zeno and for Zoilus was the occasion of a better life. No other bearer of this name stands anywhere in the corpus.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/03/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1730/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
