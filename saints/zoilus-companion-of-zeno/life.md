# Zoilus, companion of Zeno

saint.gr names the venerable Zoilus with [Zeno](/saints/zeno-companion-of-zoilus) on this day and gives the pair of them one sentence, and it is a sentence about what the site does not know: «Είναι άγνωστο που και πότε τέλεσαν τον δρόμο της ασκήσεως οι Όσιοι Ζήνων και Ζωίλος, οι οποίοι κοιμήθηκαν με ειρήνη» — it is unknown where and when the venerable Zeno and Zoilus finished the road of the ascesis, and they fell asleep in peace.

So two things stand recorded: that he was an ascetic, and that his end was peaceful. No century, no country, no monastery, no elder, no relic and no hymn; and because the page says outright that it has neither the place nor the time, no date is written into this folder at all. The site does not even print over his name the etymology block it sets over his companion's.

The distich over the pair plays on the sound of both names and says nothing about either man: the loosing of life for Zeno and for Zoilus was the occasion of a better life. No other bearer of this name stands anywhere in the corpus.

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar keeps the two of them together on the Julian 3 March, and the whole of what it gives is the line «Св. Зинона и Зоила .» — the bare mark of a saint, the two names in the genitive, and nothing more. It is the one line of the day that closes with no year and no century, where the other three all carry one. There is no life page behind either name; the name link gives the bare forename with no toponymic, no surname and no epithet, and not even the grade the other lines carry; no place is named; and the day prints no hymn under either name.

So what this calendar adds to the folder is the Russian form of the name and the fact that it keeps him too, and nothing else. It does not say what either man was. saint.gr had called them both «Όσιοι», which is the venerable this folder records, and the Russian «Св.» is the general mark the calendar uses where it states no grade at all, so nothing is added to `types` and nothing taken away. Its line names [Zeno](/saints/zeno-companion-of-zoilus) on the same line, as saint.gr did, and the relation this folder already recorded is neither strengthened nor weakened by that.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/03/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1730/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 3 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260303.html), which keeps the two of them on one line, prints no life for either, gives them no year and states no grade, read 6 October 2026.*
