# The Holy Infant (18 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 18 November keeps «Άγιο Νήπιο», a holy infant, and dates it 304 — the year it gives the Romanos of the same day. Its whole notice is one scene. When Saint [Romanos](/saints/romanus-the-deacon-of-caesarea) said to the governor Asklepiades that even the children know that the idols are not gods, the governor asked an infant which god it believed in. This holy infant answered miraculously, with clear articulation of voice: Christ. And it was beheaded at once.

The calendar gives the child no name, no parents, no city and no age, and does not say whether it was a boy or a girl; «Νήπιο» is neuter in Greek, and the page leaves it so. It prints no hymn. The two-line verse over the entry puts the child where the Church puts it: an infant that gained the bosom of Abraham by the sword was seen seated with the infants of Bethlehem.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3037/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
