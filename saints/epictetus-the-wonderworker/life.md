# Epictetus the Wonderworker

saint.gr's calendar for 12 October keeps «Άγιος Επίκτητος ο θαυματουργός», and its page makes him one of the three hundred Alamanoi, the company of saints the Cypriot tradition counts as having come to the island together. For his story it cites Leontios Machairas, the chronicler of Cyprus, who says that Epictetus went on from place to place until he came to the north of Cyprus and settled in the district of Kaza Pifani, the Kazafani of today, some four miles from the town of Kyrenia.

There, the page says, he lived the ascetic life in a way pleasing to God and fell asleep in peace. In the cave where he lived one may still see the saint's stone bed and a stone shaped like a human head which the ascetic used for a pillow, and in one corner of it is his grave.

Above that cave, towards the end of the twelfth century, the village built its church in his name, and the inhabitants held it in great reverence; the saint's icon was carried into it from the cave and set there in 1856. The calendar gives no year for his birth or his death and no account of his end beyond the word peace, and the note it prints above the life about a Stoic philosopher of 50 to 120 belongs to the name Epictetus and not to this saint.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2727/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
