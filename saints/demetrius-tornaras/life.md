# Demetrius Tornaras

saint.gr's calendar for 19 Μαρτίου keeps a new martyr, Demetrius Tornaras, and gives him four sentences. From an early age, it says, he kept company with Turks, and he used to reproach them for their unbelief. When he was pressed to become a Mohammedan he refused with boldness, and he refused again before the judge, who then ordered him tortured harshly until he should change his faith.

But when they saw the saint's wonderful persistence they beheaded him, in the year 1564 — granting him in that way, the page says, the heavenly kingdom he had longed for. The couplet over him turns on the exchange: Demetrius, having given his head, receives unnumbered crowns from the hand of the Lord.

That is the whole of what has been read. The page heads him «(; - 1564)», so the year of his birth is a question mark; it gives him no birthplace, no place of martyrdom, no relics and no hymn, and cites the Synaxaristes of the Church of Greece. Nothing has been added here from any other source or from memory.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 19 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/19/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3922/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
