# Paul (3 February)

saint.gr names him and gives no life. Its calendar for 3 February prints «Άγιοι Παύλος και Σίμων οι Μάρτυρες», and the page behind the line says only that the two of them were martyred by the sword and that no further details of their lives are held: «Δεν έχουμε περισσότερες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο των Αγίων Μαρτύρων». So there is no homeland here, no century, no persecutor, no account of the trial, and no hymn — only the rank, martyr, and the manner of death.

The one thing the page adds is a couplet, and it is about this very silence: Paul too must be written down here, and Simon, lest they escape notice, they whose heads were cut off. Which Paul of the many the Church keeps this is, the page does not say, and no other calendar was read for him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/03/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1194/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
