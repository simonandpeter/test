# Mary (5 August)

She is the last of the four, and the only one of them the calendar gives no surname. The calendar's line is all there is of her and of the three women it names with her: for 5 августа ст. ст. it prints «Мцц. Евдокии , Дарии , Дарии и Марии (1919).» — the martyrs Eudocia, Daria, Daria and Mary, 1919 — and behind each forename a name link gives the nominative and, for three of the four, a surname. There is no life page behind the line, no place, no diocese, no trade, no age and no manner of death.

The three women the calendar keeps with her are [Eudocia (Sheykova)](/saints/eudocia-sheykova), [Daria (Ulybina)](/saints/daria-ulybina) and [Daria (Timagina)](/saints/daria-timagina), and the line is the only thing read that joins them: it gives all four the martyr's grade without a monastic or a priestly one, and the one year 1919 for them together. Nothing beyond that has been read, and nothing beyond that should be read out of this folder.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 5 августа ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260805.html); read 13 October 2026.*
