# Theophilus (4 July)

saint.gr's calendar for 4 Ιουλίου names him and gives no life. The whole of its account is one sentence, «Ο Άγιος Θεόφιλος μαρτύρησε δια ξίφους» — the holy Theophilus was martyred by the sword. The day's line calls him «ο Ιερομάρτυρας», a hieromartyr, and that is the only rank the source gives him. The page names no country, no city, no century, no year, no emperor and no judge, and it prints no hymn under his line.

Besides that sentence it carries only the couplet the synaxaria print for him: «O Θεόφιλος εξαφαιρείται κάραν, / Θεόν φιλήσας και πλέον ζωής φίλης» — Theophilus has his head taken from him, having loved God more than his own dear life, a play on his name. That is everything the source says of him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 4 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/04/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1992/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
