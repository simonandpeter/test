# Dioscorus (13 October)

saint.gr's calendar for 13 October keeps «Άγιος Διόσκορος», and gives him a short notice with a date in it. He was martyred, it says, in the years of the emperor Diocletian, in the year 288. He came of the people called the Skenopolites and in rank he was a councillor.

He threw all his worldly ranks and honours onto the rubbish heap, the page says, presented himself before the magistrate Lucianus and reproved him most sharply for his persecutions against the Christians. Thunderstruck at Dioscorus's bearing, the magistrate tried with flatteries and with threats to turn his mind; and since he could not manage it, he tortured the saint hard and in the end beheaded him.

That is the whole of the calendar's account. It names no city, gives him no family and prints no hymn; beside the life it sets a two-line verse saying that Dioscorus, beheaded, puts Zeus to shame, who never had his fill of destroying souls.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 13 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/13/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2737/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
