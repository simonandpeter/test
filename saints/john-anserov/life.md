# John (Anserov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and gives no life. Its line for 23 апреля ст. ст. reads «Сщмч. Иоанна пресвитера (1940).» — the hieromartyr John, presbyter, of the year 1940 — and the line carries a link to his name and none to a reading. That is the shape this calendar has for a great many of the new martyrs of the twentieth century, and it is the whole of what can honestly be written here.

The name page behind the line adds two things the line leaves out. It gives his family name, Ансеров, which is why this folder is called by it; and it writes his grade out as «иерей», priest, beside the abbreviation «сщмч.», hieromartyr. It heads two days, «26 января (Новомуч.) , 23 апреля»: the second is the day he is kept on here, and the first is the general commemoration of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church, which is why that rank stands in this folder without a day of its own. That January day page was not read.

The day he is printed on is one of the great days of the calendar — the great-martyr George and the company martyred with him fill most of it, and it carries four hymns, none of them his. His line is the seventh of eight, and the year 1940 is the only year he has. No other calendar was read for him, and the Romanian, Greek and Serbian rows of his attestation name the day that was not checked.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 23 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260423.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9593.html), which name him and give no life; read 8 October 2026.*
