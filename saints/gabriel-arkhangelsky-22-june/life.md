# Gabriel (Arkhangelsky, 22 June)

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 22 июня ст. ст. names two deacons on one line, «Сщмчч. Феодора и Гавриила диаконов (1938).», and he is the second of them. The line gives the forenames in the genitive, one grade-class word and one rank word for the two together, and one year, and it gives no surname to either. The name link behind his place in it supplies the calendar’s own nominative with the surname and the grade, «Гавриил (Архангельский), диакон, сщмч.» (name page 14465), and that is where the name form in this folder is read from.

His name page carries nothing else: «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 22 июня» and then no life, no place of service and no account of his death in 1938. Nothing is supplied for that here. The line’s «Сщмчч.» makes him a hieromartyr and «диаконов» a deacon, and the name link repeats the rank as «диакон».

One thing has to be said plainly. This calendar names a bishop of Penza and two of his clergy on the Russian 22 августа ст. ст. under the year 1937, and the surnames in that line are the same two that stand in this one; the corpus holds all three of those men, and one of them, `gabriel-presbyter-martyr-1937`, stores the Russian form this folder stores. The two entries differ in everything the source states — the day, the year, the grade, and the name page, this man’s own page listing 22 июня and no other proper day. So the calendar keeps two entries; whether it keeps two men is not settled here, and azbyka.ru, which carries lives for the Penza company, was not read.

The second day his name page lists is the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which is a synaxis and not a second feast, so he renders on 22 июня ст. ст. only. The day of the menologion is in his display name because the calendar gives him no epithet to tell him by. The day page prints one hymn, for the hierarch its first line heads, and none under his name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 22 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260622.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14465.html); read 9 October 2026.*
