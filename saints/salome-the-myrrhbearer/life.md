# Salome the Myrrhbearer

Salome the Myrrhbearer was the wife of Zebedee and the mother of the two apostles James and [John the Evangelist](/saints/john-the-theologian). She asked the Saviour for the honour that they should be set one at His right hand and one at His left in His kingdom, as Matthew records at 20:20-22. Salome was hoping, as many of the Jews did, for an earthly kingdom of Jesus, and only later, the Romanian life says, was she assured of the high meaning of the answer she was given then.

She was present at the passion and the crucifixion of our Lord Jesus Christ, as Matthew records at 27:56, and on the morning of the holy Resurrection she was at the tomb with the other myrrhbearing women, to whom Christ the Saviour appeared. Afterwards the myrrhbearers with Salome were seized and imprisoned together with other Christians; then they were put aboard a ship and left in the middle of the sea. The ship came to the coasts of Provence, in what is now France, and the myrrhbearers spread the word of the Lord in that country. Mary of Cleopas and Salome settled on an island at the mouth of the Rhône, and stayed there until their death.

That is the whole of what doxologia.ro prints of her, and it gives no year and no hymn.

The Greek calendar keeps her on the same day, 3 August, and saint.gr gives her a fuller line than the Romanian one. She was, it says, first cousin of the Panagia; her husband was Zebedee and her sons James and John. She was one of the women who followed Christ and who gave into the common purse of the apostolic brotherhood, and it was she who asked the Lord, as He went up to Jerusalem for the last time, that her sons be honoured with the first places — supposing that He was about to raise up the throne of David there. Yet at the hour of the Passion, when disciples and friends had scattered in fear, she stood by with a few other faithful women; and she was counted worthy to be one of the myrrhbearers to whom the angel made known the Resurrection. Her heart was torn on the day Herod beheaded her firstborn James, but hope in Christ strengthened her, and she gave up her soul in peace.
