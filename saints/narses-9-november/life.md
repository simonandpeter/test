# Narses (9 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 9 Νοεμβρίου keeps Narses and Artemon in one entry, and says of them only that they were martyred by the sword. Of Narses it adds two things: that he was a Persian, and that he may perhaps be the same man as the Narses kept on 9 December — «ίσως να είναι ο ίδιος», the page says, and leaves it there. Nothing else of him is recorded: no city, no century, no emperor, no trial, no burial, no year and no hymn.

The couplet the page prints names them both twice over, once for the sword and once for the crowns: «Ναρσῆς σὺν Ἀρτέμονι ἐκκοπεὶς ξίφει, / Σὺν Ἀρτέμονι λαμβάνει καὶ τὰ στέφη» — Narses, cut down by the sword with Artemon, receives the crowns with Artemon too. Kept with him is [Artemon](/saints/artemon-9-november).

*After saint.gr's calendar for 9 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/09/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2956/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
