# Marcian of Constantinople

Marcian's forebears had come to Constantinople from old Rome, and he lived in the days of the emperors Marcian and Pulcheria. He built the church of Saint Irene by the sea and joined to it the chapel of Saint Isidore. He built also the church of Saint Anastasia in the bay of Domninus, and saved that church from the great fire at Constantinople: while the flames had taken hold of its upper part he stood on the roof with his hands lifted to heaven, and at his prayer the blaze died down.

He did other Christian works besides. He would walk at night through the streets and the ruins, and wherever he found a dead Christian he washed the body with his own hands, clothed it and lifted it, saying, "Rise, brother, and let us give one another the last kiss"; and then he buried it in good order. His miracles, his beggarly clothing and his life among the poor — for he was by birth a rich man — are told at length, the page says, in the history of his life.

His memory is kept on this day also for the great earthquake that happened on it, about the beginning of the reign of Basil, when the church of the most holy Mother of God at Stigma fell, and many other churches and houses with it. doxologia.ro gives no year for him.
