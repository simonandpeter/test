# Euxitheos of Thessalonica

saint.gr's calendar for 16 July keeps two bishops of Thessalonica on one line, and Euxitheos is the second of them. The page behind the line is given over almost entirely to [Anastasius](/saints/anastasius-of-thessalonica), his predecessor, and Euxitheos appears in it twice: in its heading, «Αγιος Ευξιθεος Επισκοπος Θεσσαλονικης», with no years at all beside his name, and at the close of its account of the Fourth Ecumenical Council.

What it says there is this. At the first session of the council at Chalcedon, on 8 October 451, the bishop of Thessalonica was represented by Quintillus of Heraclea as locum tenens for Anastasius; at the third session, on 13 October, by the presbyter Andrew. From that point it is no longer Anastasius who is named as bishop of Thessalonica but Euxitheos — or Eudoxius, the page gives both forms — who succeeded him, evidently after his death, which the page surmises came about towards the end of September 451.

The page adds one guess about the man who stood for him. The presbyter Andrew who represented Euxitheos at the sessions of the council may possibly be the same as the later bishop Andrew who succeeded Euxitheos on the archiepiscopal throne of Thessalonica. Beyond that it gives him no birthplace, no year of birth or death, no account of his years in the see and no hymn, and it credits the metropolis of Thessalonica as its source.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 16 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/16/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2104/saint.aspx), which he shares with Anastasius; read 1 October 2026.*
