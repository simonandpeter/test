# Pachomius (Rusin)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Pachomius second in the last entry of 29 July and gives no life. Its line is «Сщмч. Алексия пресвитера, прмч. Пахомия (1938).» — the hieromartyr Alexis the presbyter and the venerable martyr Pachomius, 1938 — and the calendar's own name page for him adds the surname: «Пахомий (Русин), иеромонах, сщмч.», Pachomius Rusin, hieromonk.

The line grades him a venerable martyr and his own name page a hieromartyr, the third time the two halves of this calendar disagree about a man on this one day. This folder writes the line's reading, keeps the name page's rank, and records the disagreement rather than settling it.

The first of the pair is [Alexis (Krasnovsky)](/saints/alexis-krasnovsky), a parish priest where this man was a monk in orders. The surnames are not on the line at all but on the two numbered name pages. He died in 1938; the calendar keeps him on this day among the new martyrs of Russia. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 29 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260729.html); read 13 October 2026.*
