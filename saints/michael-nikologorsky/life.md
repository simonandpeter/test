# Michael (Nikologorsky)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 17 февраля ст. ст. in a line that names two priests together and gives the year they died: «Сщмч. Михаила, Павла пресвитеров (1938).» He is the first of the two. The rank word is «Сщмч.», hieromartyr, and «пресвитеров» gives them as presbyters; the name link behind the line carries the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line itself leaves out, «Михаил (Никологорский), иерей, сщмч.».

The calendar prints no life page under his name and no hymn under it either: the troparia on the day page are for other saints it heads. Nothing further is written here. The line, the rank word, the order of service, the name the link behind it carries and the year 1938 are the whole of what was read, and in particular he is not identified with any saint of the same name whom the calendar keeps on another day.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 17 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260217.html); read 3 October 2026.*
