# Agathangelus (5 November)

Agathangelus is kept with Castor in one entry of saint.gr's calendar for 5 Νοεμβρίου, and the page's account of the two of them is one sentence: Castor, whom uncorroborated information made a bishop, was martyred by fire, and Agathangelus by the sword. That is all. The page gives him no country, no century, no emperor, no companions beyond Castor, no year and no burial, and it prints no hymn for the entry.

What it does print is the couplet the synaxarion gives him, which turns on his name as a good angel: «Χριστάγγελός τις ψῆφος Ἀγαθαγγέλῳ / Ἡ τῆς τελευτῆς ψῆφος ἦν ἐκ τοῦ ξίφους» — a Christ-angel's verdict for Agathangelus was the verdict of his end, which came of the sword. The man kept with him is [Castor](/saints/castor-5-november).

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2897/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
