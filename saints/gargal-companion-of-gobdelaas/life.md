# Gargal, companion of Gobdelaas

Gargal was the chief priest of the Persian religion, and he suffered with [Dadas](/saints/dadas-companion-of-gobdelaas) and [Gobdelaas](/saints/gobdelaas-son-of-sapor) because he came to believe in Christ. That is the whole of what the Serbian church's Ohrid Prologue says of him, in the closing line of its entry for the company: with them suffered — because they believed in Christ — Gobdelaas's sister [Casdoa](/saints/casdoa-sister-of-gobdelaas) and the chief priest Gargal. The Prologue dates the whole company to the fourth century.

He is named by this one calendar and by no other. The Greek synaxarion's line for the day is «Άγιοι Γοβδέλαος, Δάδας, Κάσδοος και Κασδόα», four names and no Gargal; the Russian calendar prints three, «Мчч. Дады, Гаведдая и Каздои», and no Gargal either. Nothing else of him is recorded, and nothing else is written here.

*After the Serbian church's [Ohrid Prologue for the Julian 29 September](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-10-12&prolog=1); read 4 October 2026.*
