# Chaeremon the Ascetic

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Νοεμβρίου names «Όσιος Χαιρέμων» and begins by saying what does not have him: he is not mentioned by the Menaia nor by the Synaxaristes of Saint Nikodemos. He is mentioned on this day, though, by the Paris Codex 1621, with a short notice which says that he lived in purity and sober-mindedness.

His life in the desert, the page adds out of that same notice, was of the most ascetic, and so he died. That is the whole of the entry: no country, no city, no monastery, no century, no year, no companions, no relics and no hymn, and the page sets no couplet over him either.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3090/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
