# Isacius (20 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 20 Νοεμβρίου prints one line for six: «Άγιοι Ισάκιος, Ιωάννης, Σαβώριος, Ωνάμ, Παππίας και Ισαάκιος». Its whole notice is two sentences. The first three of them, it says, were martyred by stoning in 343, together with the ascetic Onam, at the hands of the king of the Persians; and the next two by beheading.

The couplet set over the notice names the stoning and agrees with that order: with John and Saborius, the servants of stones kill Isacius with stones. So Isacius, John and Saborius went under the stones with Onam the ascetic, and Papias and Isaacius to the sword. The page's own heading dates four of them, John, Saborius, Onam and Isacius, to 343, and leaves Papias and Isaacius without a year.

Two of the six carry nearly the same name — Ισάκιος and Ισαάκιος — and the page keeps them apart in its heading as two men, so the corpus keeps two folders. It adds that in the Paris Codices 1578 and 1624 they are called bishops. It gives them no city, no see, no emperor by name beyond the king of the Persians, and no hymn, and prints no relics.

This folder is Isacius, whom the line names first and the couplet puts under the stones, beside [John](/saints/john-martyr-20-november), [Saborius](/saints/saborius), [Onam](/saints/onam), [Papias](/saints/papias-20-november) and [Isaacius](/saints/isaacius-20-november).

**The Russian calendar keeps the whole Persian company on one line and one life page.** The Сретенский monastery’s calendar prints for the Julian 20 November a single clause, «Сщмчч. Нирсы еп. и Иосифа, ученика его, Иоанна, Саверия, Исакия и Ипатия, епископов Персидских; мч. Азата скопца, Сасония, Феклы, Анны и иных многих мужей и жен, в Персиде пострадавших (343).», and behind every name in it the same life page stands, life2574, titled for all of them together. saint.gr prints the same day as four separate groups with four separate lives; the Russian page is the first source read here that puts them in one company. Its whole account of them is that they «были казнены во время гонения на христиан в Персии при царе Сапоре II» — that they were put to death during the persecution of Christians in Persia under king Shapur II — and then one short sentence apiece on the manner of each death.

The life gives him and Ипатий one sentence between them, «Та же участь постигла и святителей Исакия и Ипатия» — the same fate befell them, the fate being the beheading of the two named before. That sentence is why this folder now carries the new one beside it in `related`. The clause ranks him with the bishops, where saint.gr gave him «μάρτυρας» flat.
