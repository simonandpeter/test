# Cassian the Recluse of the Kyiv Caves

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life beyond one sentence. The venerable Cassian lived in the thirteenth and fourteenth centuries, it says, and practised asceticism as a recluse — ὡς ἔγκλειστος — in the Lavra of the Caves of Kyiv; and he fell asleep in holiness and in peace. The page adds only that the name comes from the Latin cassis, a helmet, and means the brave champion.

That is all of it. There is no year of birth and none of death, no family, no abbot, no account of the enclosure itself, no relics and no burial place, and the page prints neither apolytikion nor kontakion for him. Nothing is added here from anywhere else, and the four other Cassiani the corpus keeps are other men on other days.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1535/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
