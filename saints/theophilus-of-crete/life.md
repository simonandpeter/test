# Theophilus of Crete

saint.gr's calendar for 31 Μαρτίου keeps «Άγιος Θεόφιλος ο Μάρτυρας και οι συν αυτώ μαρτυρήσαντες εν Κρήτη», the holy martyr Theophilus and those martyred with him in Crete, and the page behind the line begins by admitting that the synaxaristae do not know him. His memory, it says, is found in the Paris Codex 1575 and in the Lavriotic codices H 76, D 25 and D 45, where there is also a complete service of his; and it is out of that service, not out of a narrative, that the little it says is drawn.

Theophilus was martyred with his family in Crete. The first sticheron of Vespers gives the information about his wife's martyrdom — «…καὶ νυμφῶνος θείου ἐχώρησας ἔνδον, νενυμφευμένην τῷ Χριστῷ διὰ βασάνων τοῦ σώματος τὴν σύζυγον ἀγόμενος…», you entered within the divine bridal chamber, leading your wife, wedded to Christ through the tortures of her body — and the first troparion of the third Ode of the Canon speaks of the martyrdom of his children. Evidently, the page concludes, this is a case of family martyrdom like that of the Great Martyr Eustathius, whose day it puts on 20 September, and of St Hesperus, on 2 May.

Beyond that it will not go, and neither will this folder. The date is left where the page leaves it: most probably, it says, the saints were martyred in the era of the ancient Church's persecutions — which is a guess about a century and not a year, so no date is recorded here. The wife and the children the calendar line counts are named nowhere and numbered nowhere, so they have no folder of their own; they are in his life because they are in his service.
