# Pachomius of Keno

saint.gr names him and gives two sentences and no more. It says that Pachomius of Keno lived in the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries in Russia, and that he kept his ascesis at the monastery of the Transfiguration near the country of Lake Keno, in the province of Kargopol. It says that he fell asleep in holiness and peace.

Nothing else is printed there: no year of birth or of death, no parents, no birthplace, no office, no wonder told out, no hymn and no relics. He is one of four men named Pachomius whom the same Greek day keeps, and the only one of the four whose page carries neither a life nor a cross-reference to another day. What the page does not say is not set down here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 15 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/15/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1609/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 11 января ст. ст. — [the Russian day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260111.html), read 3 October 2026, which keeps him as «Прп. Пахомия Кенского (XVI)» and prints no life for him.*
