# Nicholas (Kazansky)

The Russian calendar's 7 October ends with a line of four words and a year: «Сщмч. Николая пресвитера (1942)» — Nicholas, presbyter, hieromartyr, 1942. «Сщмч.» is the mark of a hieromartyr, a priest killed for the faith, and that abbreviation is the whole of what the day says about how he died. The site's index of names supplies the surname and an office the day line leaves at «пресвитера», heading his page «Николай (Казанский), протоиерей, сщмч.» — archpriest. The line is printed in a paragraph of its own, below the day's seven older commemorations.

His index entry keeps him here and on 26 January with the whole company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and it carries no life. The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian saints are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026 even with a browser user-agent, so the line stands as the line: a priest dead in 1942, with no place, no diocese and no manner of death recorded on either page that was read. [Jonah of Hankou](/saints/jonah-of-hankou), who died in Manchuria seventeen years before him, is the other saint of the Soviet century that this day keeps.

*After the Russian church calendar's [7 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261007.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9372.html); read 4 October 2026.*
