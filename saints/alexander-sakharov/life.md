# Alexander (Sakharov)

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him second of the fifteen and gives no life; the life here is azbyka.ru’s for the civil 3 декабря, out of fond.ru. He was born on 15 August 1875 in the village of Кривандино in the Yegoryevsk district of the province of Ryazan, son of a deacon, Нестор Сахаров. In 1890, after the ecclesiastical school at Zaraysk, he entered the Ryazan seminary, and in December 1895, in his sixth year, the bishop of Ryazan Иустин (Полянский) vested him in the stikharion to preach the word of God from the church ambo.

In 1896, out of the seminary, he was appointed schoolmaster and teacher of the Law of God at a primary school in the Yegoryevsk district; the building fell into such decay that it had to be closed, and in September 1897 he was moved to another. That year, by the blessing of the bishop of Ryazan and Zaraysk Мелетий (Якимов), a church in honour of the Kazan icon of the Mother of God was begun between two villages of the same district, and the peasants sent the bishop a petition asking him to «назначить нам в настоящее время священника к Казанской церкви окончившего курс семинарских наук Александра Сахарова». He petitioned to the same end, and the bishop approved both. On 13 November 1897 he was ordained deacon and on the next day priest, in the cathedral of the Nativity of Christ.

To the care of the building was added the teaching of the Law of God at the village school from 1898. At first, while the church and the clergy houses were going up, he and his family lived in peasant huts hired by the parishioners themselves and he drew no salary from the treasury. The church was consecrated on 25 May 1900. In October 1907 the clergy of the district elected him to the deanery council of the second Yegoryevsk district; in 1920 he was given the pectoral cross, in 1925 raised archpriest and made dean, and in 1931 given the mitre. In 1930 he was sentenced to six months’ imprisonment and five years’ exile for employing a housekeeper, but he contested the decision and was acquitted by the district court; in 1931 the authorities took the family’s last cow.

When he carried home food given him for the services, and met a child in particular need, he would stop and give the whole of it away to the children. At Pascha and the patronal feast he went round the parishioners’ houses with the blessing of water. In the summer of 1937 the local communists held meetings in the village club about closing the Kazan church and turning it into a school, and the bells were thrown down. He expected his own arrest and said to an acquaintance: «Меня, наверное, скоро возьмут» — they will take me soon, I expect.

He was arrested in the night of 15–16 November 1937 and held in a cell at Шатура, then moved to the Yegoryevsk prison. Asked how long he had served as a minister of a religious cult, he answered: «Священником я служу 40 лет, то есть с 1897 года». Asked to list all the acquaintances he met outside the services, he said he had none. Told that the investigation knew he gathered round the church a counter-revolutionary element of monastics and holy fools, he answered: «Никогда у меня бродячих священников и монашествующих не было. Антисоветской агитацией я не занимался и виновным себя в этом не признаю». On 27 November 1937 the troika of the NKVD sentenced him to be shot and he was taken to the Taganka prison; he was shot on 3 December 1937 at the Butovo range near Moscow and buried in a common unmarked grave.
