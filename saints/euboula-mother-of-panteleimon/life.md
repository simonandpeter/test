# Euboula, mother of Panteleimon

doxologia.ro's calendar names her and gives no life. Its line for 30 March is «Sfânta Cuvioasă Euvula, mama Sfântului Pantelimon» — the venerable Euboula, mother of Saint [Panteleimon](/saints/panteleimon-the-healer) — and the page the calendar links for her carries no Viață and no troparion, only icons, relics, places of pilgrimage and articles. The one thing the Romanian source states of her is the thing that names her: that she was the mother of the holy unmercenary physician of Nicomedia.

Nothing else here is taken from anywhere else. Her son's life, which the corpus holds separately, is the place where the Romanian calendar's readers will find her.

The Greek calendar keeps her on the same day and in one sentence, and the sentence says no more than the Romanian line does: she was the mother of St Panteleimon, whose memory saint.gr puts on 27 July, and she died peacefully. There is no year, no city, no account of her death, no relic and no hymn. What the Greek page adds is a couplet, and what the couplet adds is a word: «Ἐν οὐρανοῖς σύνεστιν ἀθλητῇ Τέκνῳ, Ἀθλητομήτωρ καλλίτεκνος Εὐβούλη» — in the heavens Euboula is together with her contending Child, she the mother of a contender, blessed in her child. The word ἀθλητομήτωρ, mother-of-a-martyr, is coined for her and is the only thing in the Greek record that is hers rather than her son's.

The Russian church calendar keeps her on the same day and is the first of the three to give her a year and a city. Its line reads «Св. Еввулы , матери вмч. Пантелеимона (ок. 303).», its name link heads her «Еввула Никомидийская», and the whole of its life of her is one sentence: the holy Euboula, mother of the great martyr Panteleimon, whose memory it puts on 27 июля, fell asleep in peace about the year 303, before her son’s sufferings. That last clause is the one thing of substance all three calendars agree on without any of them saying how it is known.

It is also the first source to print a service of her own. The day page carries a troparion and a kontakion headed «праведной Еввулы, матери великомученика Пантелеимона» — of the righteous Euboula — and both of them are hers: the troparion calls holy children a gift of all joy from God to holy women and names her son as that gift to her, and the kontakion addresses her directly as the mother of the glorious Panteleimon, standing with him before the throne of God. The rank word in those headings is the only place any page read for this folder gives her one, and it is where this folder’s `righteous` comes from. The same day page prints her son’s own troparion, kontakion and megalynarion as well; those name him and not her, so none of them is here.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 30 martie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/30-martie) and [her page](https://doxologia.ro/sfanta-cuvioasa-euvula-mama-sfantului-pantelimon); read 19 September 2026; and after saint.gr's calendar for 30 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/30/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/141/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026; and after the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 30 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260330.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life739.htm), read 8 October 2026.*
