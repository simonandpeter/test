# Stephen of Montenegro

Stephen was born in Montenegro, in Serbia, and saint.gr places him in the seventeenth century. From a young age he followed the monastic life, and became a monk in the monastery of Morača. Because of the Turks he was forced to leave the monastery and to live hidden among the shepherds of the district for seven years.

In 1660 he took up his ascetic life again in a cave, and inside it he lived in unceasing prayer for thirty years. Four years after his holy falling asleep the faithful found his honourable relic by the light that was lighting the cave in which he had come to rest. The page gives no year for his birth and none for his death, names no monastery of his own, and prints no hymn to him.
