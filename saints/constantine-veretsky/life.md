# Constantine (Veretsky)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him in a single line for 10 февраля ст. ст. — «Сщмч. Константина Верецкого пресвитера (1918).» — a hieromartyr and a priest who died in 1918. The line is one of the few on the day that prints the surname itself, and the name link behind it gives the calendar's own nominative, «Константин (Верецкий), иерей».

The calendar prints no life page for him, and none is given here: the line and the name link are the whole of what it records. The day page's hymns carry nothing under his name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 10 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260210.html); read 3 October 2026.*
