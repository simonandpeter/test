# Asklepios, disciple of Polychronius

saint.gr's calendar for 27 Φεβρουαρίου names two ascetics in one line — «Όσιοι Ασκληπιός και Ιάκωβος» — and says that their life was written by Theodoret of Cyrrhus in his Philotheos Historia. Of Asklepios it gives one sentence: he was the disciple of St Polychronius, whom the page keeps on 23 February, who had served the venerable Zebinas, and he imitated his own elder in everything in his asceticism.

Of [Jacob](/saints/jacob-of-nimouzan), whom the line names beside him, it gives a sentence of his own, and it closes for the two of them together: so having lived, they fell asleep in holiness and in peace. Nothing else is printed here — no birthplace, no year, no relics, no hymn — and the couplet over the pair says that two righteous men stood together before the Master who judges all the earth righteously. Because the line names each of them, each is kept here. The Polychronius the page sends its reader to on 23 February is not the martyr and presbyter of that name whom this corpus keeps on another day, so no link is made to him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 27 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/27/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3594/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
