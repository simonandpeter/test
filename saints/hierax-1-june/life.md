# Hierax (1 June)

saint.gr's calendar for 1 Ιουνίου prints eight names in one line — [Justus](/saints/justus-1-june), [Justin](/saints/justin-companion-of-chariton), [Chariton](/saints/chariton-companion-of-justin), [Charito](/saints/charito) the virgin, [Euelpistus](/saints/euelpistus), Hierax, [Paion](/saints/paion) and [Liberianus](/saints/valerian-companion-of-justin), whom the page says others call Valerianus — and the life behind the line is one paragraph. These, it says, received the martyrs' crowns together with [Justin the philosopher and apologist](/saints/justin-the-philosopher), whom the same calendar keeps on the same day, in the persecution against the Christians under Marcus Aurelius.

They belonged to the brave phalanx of the Christians of Rome, where only genuine Christian courage could keep a man from being dismayed by the power of the idol-worship that the palace and the army upheld. Having confessed Christ repeatedly, they were condemned to die by beheading; and all of them underwent that martyrdom untroubled and joyful, in the conviction that the immortal goods of heaven awaited them, which the heavenly Father gives to all who have lived on earth in faith and love.

That paragraph is the whole of the account, and it is shared by every folder in the company. Beside it the page prints a short epigram over each name, and the one over Hierax plays on his, which is the Greek for a hawk: Hierax rushes to the contest like a hawk, and beheaded he hunts the salvation of his soul. Nothing else about him is on the page, and it prints no hymn.
