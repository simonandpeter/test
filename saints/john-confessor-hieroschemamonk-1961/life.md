# John (Kevroletin)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him on 14 января ст. ст. and gives no life. The whole of its account is one line, «Прп. Иоанна Кевролетина исп (1961)» — the venerable John Kevroletin, confessor, 1961 — and behind that line the calendar's own name link carries the rank the line abbreviates, «Иоанн (Кевролетин), иеросхимонах, исп.»: a hieroschemamonk, that is a priest-monk of the great schema, and a confessor rather than a martyr.

The calendar names no monastery, no place, no manner of his confession and no year but the one in its bracket, 1961, which is the year of his death. It is the latest date on this day's list by three decades, and the rank it gives him, «исп.» and not «сщмч.», is the only thing the page says about how he ended: a confessor, not a martyr. The day page prints no hymn under his name.

That is everything the source says of him.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 14 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260114.html); read 3 October 2026. The calendar prints no life page for him.*
