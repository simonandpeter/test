# Alexander (Ipatov)

Alexander Ipatov was born in 1874 at Kazan, in a family of the townsman
class, and in March 1888 finished the Kazan four-class city college. In
May 1898 he sat the examination for psalmist before the commission at the
Perm Stephen school, and from 27 May 1898 to 24 March 1906 served as
psalmist at the Presentation church of the Artinsky works in the
Krasnoufimsk district; in 1899 the Perm consistory admitted him to the
clerical estate and confirmed him in the post, and on 14 September 1900
Bishop Peter (Losev) of Perm and Solikamsk made him a reader. On 24 March
1906, “at the petition of the clergy, the warden and the parishioners”, he
was moved to the Trinity cathedral of Krasnoufimsk, and on 17 September
1906 Bishop Nicanor (Nadezhdin) ordained him deacon to it; on 31 March
1908 he was moved to the Saviour church of Solikamsk. From 17 February
1911 he taught the Law of God at the Popovskoye primary college, with the
thanks of the diocesan authorities on 5 September 1914 for his diligent
and skilful teaching, and in 1916–1917 at the Chuvash zemstvo primary
college. On 21 September 1918, the feast of the Nativity of the Mother of
God, in the mass repressions in Perm province, the deacon Alexander Ipatov
was shot by Red Army men, and with him the priest of his church,
[Gregory Garyaev](/saints/gregory-presbyter-martyr-1918), whom the
calendar keeps beside him. The jubilee Council of Bishops of 2000
glorified him among the new martyrs and confessors of Russia; the Russian
calendar keeps him on 9 September of the old style, 22 September, and in
the synaxis of the saints of Perm.

[the entry](https://azbyka.ru/days/sv-aleksandr-ipatov), read 5 September 2026; until
then this entry had only the Sretensky calendar’s line, and spelt the
surname Ilatov.*
