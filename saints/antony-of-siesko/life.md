# Antony of Siesko

saint.gr's calendar for 6 December names the venerable Antony, the New and wonderworker «ἐν Σιήσκῳ», and then says in one sentence that it has no details of the Russian venerable's life. That is the whole of the page: no century, no monastery beyond the place in the day's line, no manner of death, no relic, no hymn, and none of the synaxaristic couplets the site sets over most of its entries.

What the entry does say is carried in the line itself, and it is set down here as the line has it: that he is reckoned a Russian ascetic, that the calendar calls him the New to distinguish him from the elder Antonys it keeps, that it calls him a wonderworker, and that it places him at Siesko. Nothing further is claimed.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3138/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
