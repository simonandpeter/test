# Theodore, martyred at Antioch

saint.gr's calendar for 24 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιος Θεόδωρος που μαρτύρησε στην Αντιόχεια» and puts him at a precise moment. He was martyred in the years of the emperor Julian the Apostate, in 361 by the page's reckoning, when Sallustius was eparch; and the occasion was the translation of the relics of [Babylas](/saints/babylas-of-antioch), bishop of Antioch, from the suburb of Daphne into the city.

At that procession Theodore sang the hymn of David, and he gave a particular emphasis to the verse «αἰσχυνθήτωσαν πάντες οἱ προσκυνοῦντες τοῖς γλυπτοῖς» — let all those who worship carved images be put to shame. He was arrested for it and scourged with whips made of ox sinews, and they tore his sides; and then they let him go.

But he was denounced again, for going on mocking the idols, was arrested a second time and beheaded. The couplet set over the notice turns on his name: they cut you with the sword, blessed one, a gift of God, who were bringing God the blood of your own throat as a gift. The page gives him no country of his own, no relics and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/24/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2987/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
