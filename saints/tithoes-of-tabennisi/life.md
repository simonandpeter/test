# Tithoes of Tabennisi

Tithoes was a disciple of Pachomius and great among the ascetics of Egypt, abbot of the monastery at Tabennisi, who passed his whole life in complete purity. A brother once asked him which road leads to humility, and he answered: the road to humility is temperance, prayer, and counting oneself lower than every creature. He reached a very high degree of perfection, and whenever he raised his hands in prayer his spirit went into ecstasy. He reposed in the fourth or fifth century; the Prologue, which the Serbian calendar follows on 26 August (Julian), remembers with him the venerable Ibistion.
