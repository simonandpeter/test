# John (Sarv)

**The day gives him a forename and the case is named after him.** The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him eighth of the fifteen, among the ten it calls «пресвитеров», and gives no life; the life here is the article azbyka.ru prints for the civil 3 декабря out of pstgu.ru, which it hangs behind his page and behind those of five others of the same company. He was born in 1867 in the province of Livland, son of a landless peasant. Out of the Riga seminary he got a psalm-reader’s post and was soon ordained priest to the church of the village of Ольгин Крест in the Gdov district of the province of St Petersburg.

Wanting an education at the Theological Academy, he petitioned the metropolitan Палладий (Раев) for a place in one of the churches of St Petersburg, and in 1896 was moved to the capital, to the Greek embassy church. Entering the St Petersburg Theological Academy he gave much time, besides his studies and his serving, to school work, teaching the Law of God in a number of the city’s institutions; in 1902 he finished the Academy with the degree of candidate of theology. From 1904 he served at the Transfiguration church on the Забалканский — now the Московский — prospect, and in 1912, already an archpriest, became its rector; he served in that church until 1927. At the same time he directed the Вонифатьевское temperance society.

At the end of the twenties he was expelled from Leningrad. He went to Тихвин and went on with his pastoral work there. In 1934 he was arrested, and at the interrogations **he refused to give any testimony at all** — the article says he «оказался единственным среди арестованных в то время в Тихвине по церковным делам, кто вел себя подобным образом», the only one among those arrested in Тихвин on church matters at that time who behaved so. He was given a suspended sentence and released, and was by then the dean of the churches of the town. In 1937 a new wave of arrests rolled over Тихвин and its neighbourhood as over the whole country.

The article names the company and the case. Fourteen people were shut in the Tikhvin prison as accused together with the dean: «7 священников, игумения Тихвинского Введенского монастыря и 6 мирян» — seven priests, the abbess of the Tikhvin convent of the Entry, and six laymen. All of them were declared one counter-revolutionary group acting in the town of Тихвин, the dean was declared its head, and the whole case was titled after him, «Сарв Иван Романович и другие». Every one of the clergy in the case was shot; the rest were given ten years in the camps.

**And the article says what five of the priests and the abbess did under interrogation.** They «отказались, не взирая на применявшиеся пытки, признать свою вину и оговорить проходивших по делу лиц, явив редкий пример единодушного христианского мужества» — refused, notwithstanding the tortures used on them, to admit their guilt or to speak against the others in the case, showing a rare example of unanimous Christian courage. The seven whom the day remembers «были расстреляны в Тихвине 3 декабря 1937 года».
