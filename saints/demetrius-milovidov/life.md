# Demetrius (Milovidov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Demetrius fifth in a company of eight on 7 August and gives no life for him. The entry is «Сщмчч. Александра, Петра, Михаила, Иоанна, Димитрия и Алексия пресвитеров, Елисея диакона и прмч. Афанасия (1937).» — eight hieromartyrs of the year 1937 — and the calendar's own name page adds the surname the line leaves out: «Димитрий (Миловидов), иерей, сщмч.», Demetrius Milovidov, priest.

That is the whole of the record. He was a priest; he died in 1937; the calendar keeps him on this day, the day after the Transfiguration, among the new martyrs of Russia. Two of the eight have a reading behind their names and he is not one of them. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 7 августа ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260807.html); read 13 October 2026.*
