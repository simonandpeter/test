# Basil (Yagodin)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him on 9 декабря in a line that names two priests and gives neither a life. The line is «Сщмчч. Василия и Александра пресвитеров (1937).» — the hieromartyrs Basil and Alexander, presbyters, 1937 — and the calendar’s own name pages add the surnames the line leaves out: «Василий (Ягодин), протоиерей, сщмч.» and «Александр (Буравцев), иерей, сщмч.»

That is the whole of the record. He was an archpriest, which is what the name page’s «протоиерей» says where the line says only presbyter; he died in 1937; and he is set down with [Alexander (Buravtsev)](/saints/alexander-buravtsev) in one line and under one year, the two surnames being unlike and the calendar saying nothing of what joined the men.

The day page carries seven hymns, for the day’s own feast and for others whom it heads, and not one of them is under either name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 9 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261209.html); read 13 October 2026.*
