# Dositheus of Verkhny Ostrov

The Russian calendar's 8 October prints «Прп. Досифея Верхнеостровского, Псковского (1482)», and the life it links is two sentences long. He was a disciple of [Euphrosynus of Pskov](/saints/euphrosynus-of-pskov), of the Saviour and Eleazar monastery, whose memory the page puts on 15 May. In 1470 he founded the monastery of Saints Peter and Paul on the Upper Island in Lake Pskov, and was its abbot there.

That is the whole of the record. The page gives no year of birth and no year of death — the 1482 on this folder is the calendar's day line, not the life's — and it says nothing of how he came to Euphrosynus, nothing of the island, and nothing of his end. His index entry keeps him on this day and on no other, and adds only the office, «игумен», and a second epithet, «Псковский».

*After the Russian church calendar's [8 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261008.html), [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/655.html) and [the life it reaches](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1692.htm); read 4 October 2026.*
