# Domninus (5 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 5 Νοεμβρίου names Domninus and those martyred with him. Of Domninus himself it says that he lived in the years of the emperor Maximian, when the governor of Palestine was Urbanus — the page sets the year 298 beside the governor's name — and that after many tortures he was burnt alive. With him, it adds, a number of Christian virgins were martyred too; it names none of them and gives no number, so nothing can be recorded of them beyond that sentence.

The page gives him no country, no see, no burial and no year of his own, and prints no hymn. What stands beside the notice is the couplet of the synaxarion, which sets the fire he was given against the other fire he feared: «Ἀνασκοπῶν πῦρ τῆς γεέννης Δομνῖνος, / Τὸ τῇ δε πῦρ ἔφριττεν οὔμενουν ὅλως» — Domninus, looking up at the fire of Gehenna, did not shudder at all at the fire here.

The same day's calendar keeps several other martyrs whom it places under the same governor of Palestine, each in an entry of its own.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2890/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
