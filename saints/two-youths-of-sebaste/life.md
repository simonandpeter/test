# The Two Youths of Sebaste

saint.gr's calendar for 11 Φεβρουαρίου keeps two youths whose names it does not give, and the whole of what its page for them says is one sentence: «Μαζί με τον Άγιο Βλάσιο ήσαν στην φυλακή και δύο νέοι, τους οποίους ο Άγιος κατήχησε και βάπτισε. Και οι δύο συνάθλησαν μετά του Αγίου και κατέστησαν κοινωνοί του μαρτυρικού θανάτου αυτού και της δόξας του Θεού» — two young men were in prison together with Saint [Blaise](/saints/blaise-of-sebaste), whom the saint catechised and baptised; and both contended along with him and became sharers of his martyr's death and of the glory of God.

Because the page never names either of the two, this folder is one entry for the pair rather than two entries, and there is nothing further to record: no parentage, no year of their own, no hymn and no relics. The distich over them is all the character the calendar gives them: «Βαβαὶ τοσαύτης παιδίων εὐθαρσίας! Σπεύδουσιν ἄμφω, ποῖον ἐκτμηθῇ φθάσαν» — what courage in children! Both are in a hurry, each to be the first cut down.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/11/index.aspx) and [the page for the two of them](https://www.saint.gr/3636/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
