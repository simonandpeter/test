# Kentigern of Glasgow

Kentigern came from Scotland, and saint.gr is careful to say that much of his childhood is told as legend rather than as record — «περί των παιδικών του χρόνων θρυλούνται πολλά». It is said that he was the child, born out of wedlock, of a king's daughter whose offended father put her into a boat and set it adrift on the open sea; the boat ran aground near the monastery of Culross, and the abbot, Saint Serf, took pity on mother and child and undertook the infant's protection.

The saint was also called Mungo, which the page glosses as "dear, beloved", or, as others have it, "little dog, puppy", because he used to follow his protector Serf about.

He settled at Glasgow, where he also became bishop. His life was very ascetic: his clothing was of animal skins, and he lived in a cave, labouring at prayer. He is held to have fallen asleep, venerably, in peace in the year 612, at the age of eighty-five. There is no hymn.
