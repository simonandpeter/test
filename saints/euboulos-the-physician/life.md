# Euboulos, companion of Julian

saint.gr keeps the holy martyrs [Julian](/saints/julian-companion-of-euboulos) and Euboulos the physician on one page, and begins by saying whose they were: they were disciples of the venerable [Arcadius](/saints/arcadius-teacher-of-julian), the Cypriot ascetic whom the same day keeps. Coming to him and leaning on his words, the page says, they took care for their virtue and for their growth towards God. His trade is the one word the site gives him for himself, ὁ ἰατρός, the physician, and nothing more of it is told — no patients, no city he practised in, no healing.

They were seized by the idolaters in the reign of the impious Julian the Apostate, and after being tortured excessively they were beheaded. When the venerable Arcadius learned what had happened he grieved, but he gave thanks to God, who had arranged it so.

The distich the site prints over the pair is addressed to him: come, let us die by the sword, says Julian to Euboulos, bringing in good counsel — εὐβουλία against Εὔβουλος. The page gives neither man a country or a city of his own, no year of birth, no name for the judge, no burial and no relic, and it prints no hymn. The long etymology block over his name lists one of the Danaids, a hero of Eleusis, a comic poet and an Athenian orator, all four of them notes on the word and none of them this man; and no other bearer of the name stands anywhere in the corpus.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3853/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
