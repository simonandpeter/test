# Sophia (18 September)

Sophia and [Irene](/saints/irene-martyr-18-september) were martyred by the
sword; that is the whole of the Greek synaxarion’s notice for 18 September,
with its verse — their heads cut off, Irene and Sophia saw you, Word, the
peace beyond understanding. The Orthodox Church in America keeps the two with
[Castor](/saints/castor-the-martyr), whom the Greek calendar lists beside
them, and says the three suffered at Alexandria; the place is the one thing
its line adds.

The Russian calendar keeps Sophia and Irene on 18 September old style, in the third century, and Castor the same day. The Russian church calendar at azbyka.ru says the two were beheaded after torture for confessing Jesus Christ, under Aurelian, by Claudius, the governor of Egypt, and of Castor that little is known beyond his name and that he died for confessing the faith openly.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 18 Σεπτεμβρίου —
[the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2474/saint.aspx); the Orthodox Church in
America’s line for 18 September — [the day’s lives](https://www.oca.org/saints/all-lives/2026/09/18);
read 5 September 2026; the Russian church calendar at azbyka.ru — [the entry](https://azbyka.ru/days/svv-sofija-egipetskaja-irina-egipetskaja), read 17 September 2026.*
