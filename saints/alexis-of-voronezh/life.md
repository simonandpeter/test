# Alexis of Voronezh

saint.gr's calendar for 12 Φεβρουαρίου names «Άγιος Αλέξιος ο Ιερομάρτυρας Επίσκοπος Βορονέζ», Alexis the hieromartyr, bishop of Voronezh, and heads his page «(; - 1930)». The whole of what it prints is one sentence: «Ο Άγιος Ιερομάρτυρας Αλέξιος, Επίσκοπος Βορονέζ της Ρωσίας, μαρτήρησε το έτος 1930 μ.Χ. Δεν έχουμε περισσότερες λεπτομέρειες για τον Βίο του Αγίου» — he was martyred in the year 1930; we have no further details of the saint's life.

So the see, the rank and the year are the whole of what the Greek calendar keeps of him. He is not Mitrophan of Voronezh, the seventeenth-century bishop of the same see whom this corpus already keeps. No parentage, no account of the martyrdom, no hymn, no relics and no distich are printed, and the page adds only its gloss on the name, that Αλέξιος is from ἀλέξω, the defender.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/12/index.aspx) and [his page](https://www.saint.gr/1232/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
