# Or (22 August)

Doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 22 august reads «Sfântul Mucenic Or» — the holy martyr Or — and the page that line links to carries neither a Viață tab nor a Tropar tab. Nothing is recorded here but the name, the rank of martyr and the day, because nothing more is printed.

The corpus holds no other saint of this name to be confused with him, and doxologia's own life of Agathonicus and his company, which it prints for four of the other names on this day, does not name him. He is entered on the Romanian line alone.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 22 august — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/22-august) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-or), which is empty of both life and hymn; read 24 September 2026.*
