# Alexander (Chekalov)

Alexander was born on 19 March 1888 in the village of Romanovo in the Kuvshinovo district of the province of Tver, the son of a priest. He finished the Tver seminary and was ordained priest. In 1930 his house and everything in it were taken from him, and, expecting arrest, he blessed his two sons and two daughters to go away so as not to be persecuted as a priest's children, staying himself with his wife and his youngest daughter, who was nine.

He was arrested that same year and sentenced to banishment from the village where he served. Unwilling to go far from his own country, he settled at Zheleznikovo in the neighbouring Staritsa district, and his wife and daughter came with him. They had nothing; he served in the church and rented a room for the three of them. He knew from his own experience, the page says, that in a state professing militant godlessness as its new religion he would always be hunted and could be taken at any moment, and he went on serving, ready for confession and, if the Lord should call him, for martyrdom.

On 21 November 1937, after the Liturgy on the feast of the bodiless powers, the NKVD arrested him. He was questioned on 23 November and denied the charges, with one exception he would not deny: «Были случаи, когда я говорил, что скоро грядет война, тогда добра не будет, а о гибели населения нигде ничего не говорил.» The troika sentenced him on 25 November and he was shot on 27 November 1937. He was canonised for the whole church in August 2000.

The day's line names twenty-seven new martyrs of 1937 together — twenty-three presbyters, a deacon, a hieromonk, a lay martyr and a woman — and the calendar carries a life page for ten of them, of whom he is one. The men of that line were tried separately, in different districts and different prisons, and no one of these lives names another of them, so no relation is recorded between them here.
