# Paisius of the Caves Lavra

saint.gr's calendar for 19 Ιουλίου names the venerable Paisius of the Lavra of the Caves, and his own page says in a single line that we have no details of the venerable one's life. It heads his feast as a fixed one, kept on 19 July each year, and it prints nothing else at all.

So the folder records a name and a day and a house, and no more: no year, no century, no city for the Lavra, no hymn, and no story. The page does not say which Lavra of the Caves it means, and this entry is not the fool-for-Christ Paisius whom the same site keeps, with his years, on 17 April.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 19 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/19/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2624/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
