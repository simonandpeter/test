# Jonah of Pesonsa

saint.gr's calendar for 15 June names Jonah of the monastery of Pesonsa and gives no life. The whole of what the page says is one sentence: he was a Russian monk, a fool for Christ's sake, and we have no further details of the saint's life. Beside the name the page prints its usual note on the name Jonah itself, that it is a Hebrew word meaning the dove that carries the message, the one sent by God. There is no year, no epigram and no hymn.

The monastery's name is given here only in the Greek spelling the page uses, «Πεσόνσα», and this life does not identify it with any house whose Russian name it might be. Nothing more of him is recorded than that he was a monk of that monastery, that he bore the folly for Christ, and that the Greek calendar keeps his memory on 15 June.
