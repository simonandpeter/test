# Poimen of Georgia

saint.gr's calendar for 16 Μαρτίου names him «Όσιος Ποιμήν ο διά Χριστόν Σαλός εκ Γεωργίας», the venerable Poimen, the fool for Christ, out of Georgia; and the page behind that line holds one sentence about him: that the venerable Poimen lived in Georgia in the thirteenth century after Christ, and that he fell asleep in peace. The rank is in the line and nowhere else on the page, and there is no account of the folly he took on.

No year is given, no town of Georgia, no monastery, no relic and no hymn. The one thing the page adds is a picture: the icon it prints shows him together with [Anthony Meskhi](/saints/anthony-meskhi), whom the calendar keeps on this same day and in the same century, and whose own page says he worked as a missionary under Demetrius II. What is recorded here is the commemoration, the country, the century and the rank.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 16 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/16/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3905/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
