# Theoctistus of St Sabbas

saint.gr's calendar for 13 Μαρτίου names him «Άγιος Θεόκτιστος ο Οσιομάρτυρας», and heads him «(; - 797)». The page behind that line holds one sentence: that the venerable Theoctistus lived in the eighth century after Christ and lived as an ascetic in the monastery of St Sabbas, and that he fell asleep in peace in the year 797. No parents, no country, no office, no relic and no hymn are given, and the rest of the page is a note on the name, Θεός and κτίσμα, the creature of God.

The calendar's line calls him a venerable-martyr while the page has him die in peace, and the two are left as they stand here, with no rank recorded for him on the strength of the line alone. What is certain from the page is the lavra he lived in, the century, and the year of his repose; and the monastery of St Sabbas in the Judaean desert is where the Greek calendar places him.
