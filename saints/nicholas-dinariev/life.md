# Nicholas (Dinariev)

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar for 3 ноября ст. ст. names him first of two on one line: «Сщмч. Николая и мч. Павла (1918).» — a hieromartyr and a martyr, two forenames in the genitive, and the year 1918. The line gives him the rank word «Сщмч.» and no grade and no surname; the name link behind his forename supplies the calendar's own nominative with both, «Николай (Динариев), протоиерей, сщмч.», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from.

That is everything the calendar records of him. He has no life page, no icon, no hymn, no place, no age and no parish anywhere on this calendar, and the name page behind his place on the line carries two days and no text at all. The name page behind his place on the line also lists «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. That is a synaxis and not a second feast, and he does not render on it. Nothing further is written here, and in particular nothing is reasoned from the surname.

The line's rank word is all the line gives, so hieromartyr is the whole of his types, and the link's «протоиерей» is recorded as his office, Archpriest. The second name on the line carries «мч.» and no grade, and no relation is written between the two of them: the calendar prints one line, one year and nothing that says they died in one place.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 3 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261103.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9370.html); read 4 October 2026.*
