# Antonia (Timofeyeva)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery gives her the last line of 23 октября ст. ст., and the whole of it is «Прмц. Антонии (1942).» The line declines her name and gives it without a surname; the title on the name link behind it supplies both the surname and the rank, «Антония (Тимофеева), прмц.». Her line is her own and not part of the company of six priests above it: a different rank word and a year five years later.

«Преподобномученица» is the rank of a monastic who died a martyr, and that is the whole of what the calendar says about her state of life. It gives her no monastery, no office and no second name in religion, and nothing on either page says where she was born, where she lived, when in 1942 she died or how.

The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line. The day page's five troparia and kontakia all belong to the three older entries it heads, and none of them to her.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 23 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261023.html); read 4 October 2026.*
