# Nerdon

saint.gr's calendar for 24 Οκτωβρίου names Nerdon and gives him no life. One sentence is the whole of the narrative: «Ο Άγιος Νέρδων μαρτύρησε διά πυρός» — he was martyred by fire. The couplet above it turns the furnace into a cup, saying that Nerdon, thrown into the furnace's bowl of fire, drinks off the cup of a divine end.

The page gives him no country, no city, no year, no emperor and no companions; it prints no hymn and no relics, and nothing in the corpus answers to the name.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/24/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2534/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
