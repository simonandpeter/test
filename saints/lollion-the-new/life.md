# Lollion the New

saint.gr's calendar for 27 Απριλίου names him and gives no life. Its whole notice of him is one sentence — «Ο Άγιος Λολλίων μαρτύρησε αφού βίαια τον έσυραν κατά γης» — that Lollion was martyred after they violently dragged him along the ground. Nothing else is printed: no century, no country, no emperor and no judge, and nothing that says why he is called the New or whom he is new against.

The couplet under his name turns on the manner of his death and on dust: «Κονίεται τὸ σῶμα, Μάρτυς Λολλίων, / Κόνιν φύρεσθαι τὴν ἀπ' αὐτοῦ δοὺς κόνιν» — the body is dusted over, O martyr Lollion, having given the dust that came of it to be mixed with dust. The feast type the page gives is the plain fixed one, «Σταθερή. Εορτάζει στις 27 Απριλίου εκάστου έτους».

*After saint.gr's calendar for 27 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/27/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/344/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
