# John (Smolichev), Presbyter, Hieromartyr (1937)

John Smolichev was born on 27 January 1889 in the village of Knyazhichi in the Glukhov district of Chernigov province, a priest’s son, and his road was a hard one: he finished the Chernigov seminary in 1912, studied two years at the St Petersburg Academy, served as a medical orderly in the army in 1915–1917, entered Tomsk university in 1918 and heard courses in some subjects, lived in Nizhyn from 1917 to 1920 working as a clerk, was mobilised into Kolchak’s army in 1919 as an orderly, served a year from the end of 1919 as a medical assistant in the Red Army, and from 1920 to 1924 taught at Krolevets. In May 1924 he was ordained priest to the church of the town of Glukhov by Bishop [Damascene (Tsedrik)](/saints/damascene-of-starodub), and served there zealously six years; in 1930 — the calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. He was arrested twice for counter-revolutionary activity — in 1930, five years in the camps, and in 1936 in the case of Bishop Damascene, five years more; in the Karaganda camp he went on secretly celebrating the services, was arrested there in 1937 for taking part in a counter-revolutionary group, and was shot on 15 September 1937. The Russian calendar keeps him on 2 September (Julian).
