# John (Smolichev)

John Smolichev was born on 27 January 1889 in the village of Knyazhichi
in the Glukhov district of Chernigov province, a priest’s son, and his
road was a hard one: he finished the Chernigov seminary in 1912, studied
two years at the St Petersburg Academy, served as a medical orderly in the
army in 1915–1917, entered Tomsk university in 1918 and heard courses in
some subjects, lived in Nizhyn from 1917 to 1920 working as a clerk, was
mobilised into Kolchak’s army in 1919 as an orderly, served a year from
the end of 1919 as a medical assistant in the Red Army, and from 1920 to
1924 taught at Krolevets. In May 1924 he was ordained priest to the church
of the town of Glukhov by Bishop
[Damascene (Tsedrik)](/saints/damascene-of-starodub), and served there
zealously six years; in 1930 he was arrested for counter-revolutionary
activity and sentenced to five years in a labour camp, which he served in
the Svir camp in the Leningrad region, and freed in 1933 he went on
serving as priest at Malaya Suna in the Zuyevka district of the Vyatka
region. In 1936 came the second arrest, in the case of Bishop Damascene,
with the same charge of counter-revolutionary activity and “taking part
in a counter-revolutionary group” added, and five more years in the camps;
on 4 November 1936 he was sent under escort to Karaganda, at the disposal
of the head of the Karlag, where the educated priest worked as a
book-keeper. On 14 August 1937 he was arrested in the camp, in the “case
of the counter-revolutionary group of Bishop Damascene (Tsedrik), Karlag,
1937” opened against the priests who thought with him: together with the
prisoner Tsedrik, the indictment said, he held services on the religious
feasts in the presence of all the prisoners of the barrack, Tsedrik
blessed him, and under Tsedrik’s guidance he carried on the
counter-revolutionary idea and busied himself composing religious prayers
in manuscript. Asked whether he knew that religion was the opium of the
people, he answered: I do not agree that religion is the opium of the
people; on the contrary, I maintain that the idea of religion rightly
understood necessarily ennobles a man. On 10 September 1937 the troika of
the Karaganda NKVD sentenced him to the highest penalty, and on 15
September 1937 — 2 September of the old style, the day the Russian
calendar keeps him — he was shot; the Russian calendar keeps him also in
the synaxis of the saints of Vyatka.
