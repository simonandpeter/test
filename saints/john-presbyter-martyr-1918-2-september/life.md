# John the Presbyter (2 September)

John Fyodorovich Ivanov was born in 1864 and served from 1904 as priest of
the Ferapontov convent, twenty kilometres from the Kirillo-Belozersky
monastery. When in May 1918 the Kirillov executive committee ordered a fresh
inventory of every monastery’s property, the abbess [Seraphima
(Sulimova)](/saints/seraphima-abbess-martyr-1918) asked him to announce the
commission’s coming, and after the Liturgy on Sunday 6 May he said:
Orthodox! today or tomorrow some people will come to us to inventory the
convent’s property, but the inventory has already been made by the council;
to admit them or not is your affair, but if they wrong the church and the
sisters, defend them. The parishioners answered that no more inventory was
needed and that they would not allow it. The commissioners came during the
vigil that evening; a noise rose outside, and seeing the congregation drawn
by it the priest said aloud, with God is eternity, with God is beauty — and
all this noise, all this is temporary and passing, and went on serving; when
the service ended three of the four commissioners had fled under a few shots
fired to frighten them and the crowd was about to lynch the fourth, and Fr
John came out on the porch, interceded, and the crowd let him go. The same
day the local committee telephoned Kirillov that the commission’s expulsion
was the fruit of the priest’s agitation against the Soviet power. On 9 May,
back at evening from a service, needs and a procession to the next village,
he was arrested by a squad of armed Red Guards, most of them men of
Ferapontovo who had deserted from the front and remembered that in the
autumn of 1917 he had rebuked them in a sermon for running from the front
like hares; they hurried him, struck him several times, would not let him
dress warmly, and he went out in a thin cassock with his pectoral cross,
blessed them and got into the cart; on the road, with a pistol and a rifle
trained on him, they offered him his life if he would renounce God, and he
refused, and was put in the Kirillov prison on a charge of calling the
people to violence against the commission. [Barsanuphius
(Lebedev)](/saints/barsanuphius-of-kirillov) had the case investigated and
the priest’s complete innocence shown, and the dean, Alexander Fomin, laid
the findings before the executive committee; the authorities answered by
opening a formal inquiry, moving him to the Cherepovets prison, and
rejecting every witness in his favour on the pretext that he had written to
them from prison, which made them accomplices, while hearing only those who
wanted the church and convent shut. On 13 May the dean asked the
parishioners to stand up for their priest and, paralysed by fear of the
terror, they refused, and only a month later began to feel the dishonour of
having given up an innocent pastor without a word; at Pentecost, 10 June,
two thousand of them signed a petition for his release, which a godless
peasant of the settlement below the monastery got hold of and destroyed, and
a second, signed by hundreds from twelve villages, went to the Cherepovets
revolutionary tribunal on 1 July — arrested innocently, by a
misunderstanding; in the church he was bound to announce the commission’s
coming, as we ourselves had resolved, and in the street he was urging the
crowd to do the members of the commission no violence; we ask the tribunal
to free him at once and hold him acquitted. It did not. On 15 September the
bishop, the abbess and four laymen were shot on the Goritsy road, and a few
days later, on 19 September 1918 — 6 September of the old style — the priest
John Ivanov was shot too. With [Barsanuphius
(Lebedev)](/saints/barsanuphius-of-kirillov), the abbess [Seraphima
(Sulimova)](/saints/seraphima-abbess-martyr-1918) and the martyrs [Seraphima
(Sulimova)](/saints/seraphima-abbess-martyr-1918), [Nicholas
Burlakov](/saints/nicholas-martyr-1918-2-september), [Anatoly
Barashkov](/saints/anatoly-martyr-1918), [Michael
Trubnikov](/saints/michael-martyr-1918-2-september), [Philip
Maryshev](/saints/philip-martyr-1918) he was numbered among the new martyrs
and confessors of Russia by the jubilee Council of Bishops of August 2000,
for veneration by the whole church; the Russian calendar keeps the seven
together on 2 September of the old style, 15 September. The life is by
Igumen Damascene (Orlovsky).
