# Didymus of Cyprus

saint.gr's calendar for 20 Φεβρουαρίου names three men in one line — «Άγιοι Δίδυμος, Νεμέσιος και Ποτάμιος» — and the page standing under it says of them only that they are local saints of Cyprus whose memory is written in the Roman Martyrology. Because the line names each of them, each is kept here: Didymus, with [Nemesius](/saints/nemesius-of-cyprus) and [Potamius](/saints/potamius-of-cyprus).

The page adds that they are counted by Kyprianos among the martyrs of the Church of Cyprus, and by Delehaye among those of the Church of Alexandria: «Απαριθμούνται από τον Κυπριανό, μεταξύ των μαρτύρων της Κυπριακής Εκκλησίας και από τον Delehaye μεταξύ της Αλεξανδρινής Εκκλησίας». So the two authorities it cites do not put them in the same church, and no place is recorded in this folder on that account. It gives no years, no account of their suffering, no relics, no couplet and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 20 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/20/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3767/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
