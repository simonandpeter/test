# The Four Martyrs of Perge

Four martyrs of Perge in Pamphylia, who died after being made to run through fire. The synaxarion’s verse says that the demons, willing or not, were persuaded of their defeat; Nikodemos the Hagiorite, it notes, glosses the couplet so, and remarks that the Synaxaristes of the Dionysiou monastery reads “the fires” there.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 31 Αυγούστου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/864/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
