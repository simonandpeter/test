# Matrona of Hurezi

doxologia.ro gives her life in five sentences, and this is all of it. She was brought up from the age of five at the monastery of Sărăcinești in Vâlcea and tonsured a nun there. In 1868 she was sent to the monastery of Horezu, where she went on with her labours, and in 1922 the sisters elected her abbess.

After falling gravely ill in 1926 she was healed by prayer to the Mother of God. In the summer of 1935, feeling her end near, she called her disciples to her and urged them to love, humility and obedience; the next day, while she was reciting the akathist of the Annunciation, she gave up her spirit with a serene face.

She was canonised by the Holy Synod of the Romanian Orthodox Church at its session of 1 July 2025, under the title «Sfânta Cuvioasă Matrona de la Hurezi» and with her commemoration on 5 May. Her troparion was sung by the Tronos choir on 6 February 2026, at the general proclamation of the canonisation of the sixteen Romanian women saints; doxologia's page for it is a recording, and prints no text.
