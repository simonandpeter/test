# John (Malinovsky)

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him sixth of twenty-four and gives no life; the life here is the one azbyka.ru prints for the civil 2 декабря, out of pstgu.ru. Ivan Matveyevich Malinovsky was born in 1881 in the village of Poymichye in the province of Yaroslavl. He finished the seminary, was ordained priest and served in the church of the village of Novye Gorki in the Lezhnevo district of the Ivanovo region, and was later raised archpriest. In the thirties the persecution reached him: charged with counter-revolutionary agitation, he was sentenced to five years of exile in Kazakhstan.

The place appointed was the settlement of Mayskoye in the Beskaragay district of the Pavlodar region, where a priest no longer young had to work at the mine in hard conditions. He was arrested there on 25 November 1937 and went into the group case of the archimandrite Gregory (Rebeza) and others at Pavlodar. Asked by the investigator what counter-revolutionary work he had done in exile he answered: «Контрреволюционной работой я при нахождении в ссылке в поселке Майском не занимался, богослужений не проводил». The troika of the East Kazakhstan region sentenced him to death on 1 December 1937 and the sentence was carried out at two in the morning on 2 December. He was glorified on 20 August 2000.
