# Parthenius (Krasivsky)

Parthenius Petrovich Krasivsky, priest, was born on 25 January 1859 in the city of Tomsk, the brother of the hieromartyr [Basil Krasivsky](/saints/basil-presbyter-martyr-1919); in 1866 the family moved to the stanitsa of Urdzhar in the Lepsinsk district of the Semirechye region, and he studied at the Urdzhar Cossack school. On 1 January 1876 Bishop Sophonias of Turkestan and Tashkent made him sacristan of the Turkestan cathedral — the calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. He died a martyr in 1919, in the repressions against the clergy of Semirechye; he was glorified by the jubilee council of 2000, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 3 September (Julian).
