# Parthenius (Krasivsky)

Parthenius Petrovich Krasivsky, priest, was born on 25 January 1859 in
the city of Tomsk, a deacon’s son and the brother of the hieromartyr
[Basil Krasivsky](/saints/basil-presbyter-martyr-1919); in 1866 the
family moved to the stanitsa of Urdzhar in the Lepsinsk district of the
Semirechye region, and he studied at the Urdzhar Cossack school. On 1
January 1876 Sophonias, bishop of Turkestan and Tashkent, made him
sacristan of the Turkestan cathedral, and on 17 September 1876 gave him
the sticharion; on 25 August 1878 he was enrolled as psalmist of the
cathedral, on 20 April 1879 moved to the St Sophia church of
Bolshaya Almatinskaya, on 18 November 1880 sent to the St Demetrius church
of Zaitsevskoye, and on 1 February 1882, at his own petition, moved to the
Holy Trinity church of Przhevalsk. On 7 June 1904 he was moved as
psalmist to the Kazan church of Ivanovskoye, on 15 August 1904 ordained
deacon and on 18 August moved to the Holy Trinity church of the city of
Verny, the present Almaty; moved to the Kazan church of the Malaya
Stanitsa at Verny and made catechist of its parish school, he was
ordained priest on 12 March 1911. In 1919 he was burned by the men of
Kikhtenko’s detachment at the left porch of the Kazan church of Verny —
on the same day, the short notice says, that his brother Basil, priest of
the prison church of Peter and Paul in the same city, was beaten, tied to
a horse’s tail and so tortured to death by criminals let out of the
prison — and until the Kazan church was closed in 1936 a memorial cross
stood on the spot. The jubilee Council of Bishops of 2000 glorified him
among the new martyrs and confessors of Russia; the Russian calendar
keeps him on 3 September (Julian). The life was compiled by the igumen
Damascene (Orlovsky) from the Almaty registry archive and the calendar of
the new martyrs of Kazakhstan.
