# Sabbas of Sourozh

saint.gr says Sabbas lived in the twelfth century in the Crimea and was elected bishop of the city of Sourozh, which is Sudak today. What is known of him at all, the page says, has survived in notes in a Greek Menaion of the twelfth century: «Ότι γνωρίζουμε γι' αυτόν έχει σωθεί σε υποσημειώσεις σε ένα ελληνικό Μηναίο του 12ου αιώνος μ.Χ.» Beyond that the page tells no incident of his life.

Five kilometres from the city of Sourozh there is a mountain called St Sabbas, where the remains of a church and of a cave are kept. There, the page says, the saint evidently died and was buried; and in the year 1872 an icon of him was found there. The day's line calls him archbishop of Sourozh and the page's own text says he was elected its bishop. He is given no year of birth and no year of death beyond the century, no monastery, no couplet and no hymn.
