# Snandulia the Persian

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar for 3 ноября ст. ст. names her in three words and a century — «Прав. Снандулии (IV).» — the rank word «Прав.», righteous, the name in the genitive, and the figure IV. The name link behind the line gives the calendar's own nominative with a second name in brackets and a by-name, «Снандулия (Яздундокта) Персидская», and that page carries her day and nothing else: no body, no icon, no hymn, no place, no parentage and no year.

The calendar prints no life for her, and what is said of her story here is not taken from it. This corpus's own lives of [Joseph the Presbyter](/saints/joseph-presbyter-of-persia) and [Aithalas the Deacon](/saints/aithalas-the-deacon), written from the Romanian calendar, name a faithful and holy woman of Arbela who fed in secret the Christians held in the prisons there. She gave the guards much gold to let the two broken men out to her house for one night, washed and anointed their wounds and wept over them; and she was afterwards dragged to the place of execution and forced to throw stones at Joseph, and said she would sooner drive the spear into her own heart. Those lives spell her Sinandulia.

That woman and this one are read here as the same: one name, one city, one company, and one day, since the Russian calendar keeps her on the day it keeps the three Persian martyrs. Nothing beyond that is claimed. The century is the line's «(IV)» and is recorded as the span she lived in rather than as a year of death.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 3 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261103.html) and [her name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/2670.html); read 4 October 2026.*
