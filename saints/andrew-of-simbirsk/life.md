# Andrew of Simbirsk

The calendar's line and his name page are the whole of what is recorded of him here. The Сретенский monastery's calendar for 27 ноября ст. ст. reads «Блж. Андрея Симбирского (1841).» — the blessed Andrew of Simbirsk — and the name page behind that name gives him the surname the line leaves out and tells what kind of blessedness the abbreviation means: «Андрей (Огородников) Симбирский, Христа ради юродивый», a fool for Christ's sake.

There is no life page for him on the site. So nothing is said here of when or where he was born, of what he did in the city whose name he carries, of who knew him or of how he died. The year 1841 is the year his line closes with, and the calendar gives him no other.

His name page keeps one day besides this one, 21 мая, and marks it as the finding of his relics rather than a second commemoration of his death; and it links a parish church dedicated to him in his own country, which is the only sign the page gives of how he is honoured there now.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 27 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261127.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14260.html), which carries no life; read 7 October 2026.*
