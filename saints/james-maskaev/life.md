# James (Maskaev) of Barnaul

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps on 16 July the archbishop of Barnaul and three men shot with him. Its reading is a long one, built out of the investigation file: the heading names five, of whom the day's line names four.

[James (Maskaev) of Barnaul](/saints/james-maskaev) was born on 13 October 1879 at Uralsk, to peasants of the village of Edelevo in the Syzran district of the Simbirsk province, and named for the apostle James son of Alphaeus. He finished the Orenburg seminary in 1901, married in his last year there, was ordained priest that same year to the village of Zobovo a hundred and eighty kilometres from Orenburg, and had nine children, his wife dying at the birth of the last of them in 1918. The reading dwells on his giving: a new church built in the village, a parish school at Voronina in 1909, collections for the army and the fleet in the Japanese war and the first German one which were always larger than any other parish's in the diocese. Tonsured with the name James, in honour of James the brother of the Lord, he was consecrated bishop in 1923 by Antonin (Granovsky) — who turned out to be one of the leaders of the renovationist schism, so that the lawfulness of the consecration was questioned, and he laid a prohibition on himself and ceased to serve until Patriarch Tikhon had received him into communion and his consecration had been recognised as valid, the consecrators being of the old appointment. On 4 April 1933, after a camp, he was appointed to the see of Barnaul with temporary charge of Biysk as well, and in 1935 he was raised archbishop.

Arrested with him were the priests [Peter (Gavrilov)](/saints/peter-gavrilov) and [John (Mozhirin)](/saints/john-mozhirin), the monk [Theodore (Nikitin)](/saints/theodore-nikitin) and the layman Ivan Protopopov. The interrogations the reading prints at length put to the archbishop that he had taken an instruction to raise insurgent organisations in the Altai districts against the day of a war with Japan; he answered that no such conversations had taken place, that he had received no instruction, and that he could not confess himself guilty of it, and when the investigator wrote that he had confirmed the testimony against him, he refused outright to sign the record of the interrogation.

On 3 July 1937 Stalin signed the order for mass shootings and for the sentencing of such cases administratively by troikas. On 25 July 1937 a troika of the NKVD sentenced the archbishop, the archpriest Peter, the priest John, the monk Theodore, Ivan Protopopov and others to be shot. The four were shot on 29 July 1937 and buried in an unmarked common grave; the layman was shot on 4 August. All were numbered among the New Martyrs and Confessors of Russia at the Jubilee Council of the Russian Church in August 2000.

This folder is the archbishop. The day's line names him first and alone by his see, «Иакова , архиеп. Барнаульского», and the other three stand under «и с ним», and with him.
