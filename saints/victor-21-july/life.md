# Victor (21 July)

saint.gr's calendar for 21 Ιουλίου names «Άγιος Βίκτωρ ο Στρατιώτης», Victor the soldier, and his own page says in one line that we have no details of the life of Saint Victor. It does not say where he served, under what emperor, or whether he died for the faith at all.

So the folder holds a name, a day and a trade. What stands above the notice is the site's gloss on the name — from the Latin victor, the conqueror — and a line about two Byzantine iconographers who bore it, which belongs to the name and not to this man. The page prints no hymn for him.
