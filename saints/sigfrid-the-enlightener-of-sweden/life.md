# Sigfrid the Enlightener of Sweden

Doxologia's page for Sigfrid has no life tab and no troparion tab; what it carries is a short notice and a photograph. The notice says that he is the one who brought the light of the Gospel of Christ into what is Sweden today, and that this is why he is also called the Enlightener of Sweden; and that in the year 1008, at Husaby in the east of the province of Gothland, he baptised King Olof Skötkonung, Queen Estrid, their children and their court.

The photograph is of the well at which that baptism was given. Its caption repeats the year and the names, and puts his repose at 1045. That is the whole of what doxologia prints for him: no life, no hymn, and nothing of where he came from or by what road he reached the north.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 15 februarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/15-februarie) and [the page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-ierarh-sigfrid-luminatorul-suediei); read 19 September 2026.*
