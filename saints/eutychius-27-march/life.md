# Eutychius (27 March)

saint.gr's calendar for 27 Μαρτίου names a venerable Eutychius and then says one thing about him: «Ο Όσιος Ευτύχιος απεβίωσε ειρηνικά» — the venerable Eutychius died peacefully. The line carries no epithet, no see, no monastery and no country; the page gives no year, no parents, no teacher, no disciple, no wonder, no relic and no hymn. What the source keeps of him is a name, a rank and a manner of dying, and this folder keeps exactly that and no more.

The one other thing the page prints for him is a couplet: «Εὐτυχὲς Εὐτύχιος ἦλθεν εἰς πέρας, Γῆθεν μεταστὰς καὶ κατοικήσας πόλον» — happily did Eutychius come to his end, passing from earth and taking up his dwelling in heaven. It plays, as the calendar's couplets like to, on the name itself, which the page glosses «ΕΥΤΥΧΙΟΣ (από το ευτυχής) = ο μακάριος», the blessed, the fortunate.

Because nothing distinguishes him but the day, the day is in his name here. He is not any of the Eutychii the corpus already keeps: not the patriarch of Constantinople, nor the bishop of Melitene, nor the abbot of Nursia, nor the soldier among the Forty of Sebaste, nor the subdeacon of Alexandria whom the Greek calendar keeps on 26 March. Where a later reader finds a source that gives this man a place or a century, the folder should take it.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 27 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/27/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1262/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
