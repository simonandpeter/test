# Ignatius of Rostov

saint.gr says he lived in the thirteenth century and was elected bishop of the city of Rostov. He shepherded his flock in the love of God for twenty-six years and reposed in peace in 1288. The page gives him no year of birth, no monastery before the see, and no story of his election, and it names no place but the city itself.

On the day of his funeral service, it goes on, some of the faithful saw his honourable relic raise itself and bless them. God blessed him with the gift of working wonders, and many wonders, the page says, are worked at his tomb. That is the whole of what the Greek calendar keeps of him.
