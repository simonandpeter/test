# Emilian (11 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 11 December keeps four martyrs in one entry and names all four: [Terentius](/saints/terentius-martyr-11-december), [Vincentius](/saints/vincentius-martyr-11-december), Emilian and [Bebaia](/saints/bebaia-martyr-11-december). The whole of the life it gives them is one sentence — that the holy Terentius, Vincentius, Emilianus and Bebaia were martyred by the sword. No country, no reign, no persecutor and no year is printed for any of them.

The couplet set over the entry counts them and reads the count: cut down, three and one, they show forth the three persons of God and the one nature. Nothing is said of Emilian apart from his name, his place third in the list, and the sword; and the corpus keeps several other men of this name on other days, to none of whom this page joins him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/11/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3169/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
