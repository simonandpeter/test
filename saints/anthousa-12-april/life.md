# Anthousa (12 April)

doxologia.ro's calendar names her and gives no life. The entry for 12 April reads «Sfânta Cuvioasă Antuza» — Anthousa, with the rank the Romanian calendar gives to those who lived the monastic life — and the page behind that line carries no Viață tab and no Tropar tab, only the day's Epistle and Gospel, icons and articles. A name, a rank and a day is the whole of what this source has.

No century is given, no place, no parentage and no manner of death; nothing here says whether she was a martyr, an abbess or a solitary. This folder records what the Romanian calendar keeps on 12 April and stops there, and nothing further should be read into the bare name.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 12 aprilie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/12-aprilie) and [the saint's page](https://doxologia.ro/sfanta-cuvioasa-antuza-0); read 20 September 2026.*
