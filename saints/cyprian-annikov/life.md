# Cyprian (Annikov)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him last of five on 21 октября ст. ст., in the line «Сщмчч. Павлина , архиеп. Могилевского, Аркадия , еп. Екатеринбургского, и с ними Анатолия и Никандра пресвитеров и мч. Киприана (1937)». His rank word is the line's second one, «мч.», martyr, which separates him from the two bishops and the two priests before him: he was a layman. The title on the name link behind his place adds the surname and repeats the rank, «Киприан (Анников), мч.»

The company is headed by an archbishop, [Paulinus (Kroshechkin)](/saints/paulinus-kroshechkin), who was shot that day with a group of clergy in the Kemerovo camp; the calendar carries a life for him and for none of the other four, and says nothing about where the rest died or whether they died together. Nothing on the page says what this layman's part in it was, or whether he belonged to any of their parishes.

Nothing further is written here — not a birth, not a trade, not a parish, not a charge, not a place of death. The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 21 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261021.html); read 4 October 2026.*
