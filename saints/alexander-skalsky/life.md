# Alexander (Skalsky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 4 января old style, and it keeps him in company: the line reads «Сщмчч. Александра, Стефана и Филиппа пресвитеров (1933)» — the hieromartyrs Alexander, Stephen and Philip, presbyters, 1933 — so that three priests who died in the same year are named together in one entry. The name links behind the line give the calendar's own nominatives with the surnames the line itself leaves out, and his is «Александр (Скальский), протоиерей, сщмч.».

Beyond the line and the year the calendar prints nothing: it holds no life page for him, and no hymn. What can honestly be said is that a priest of this name is kept as a hieromartyr on this day, that the year given for his death is 1933, and that the calendar names him with [Stephen (Ponomarev)](/saints/stephen-ponomarev) and [Philip (Grigoriev)](/saints/philip-grigoriev). Where he was born, where he served and how he died are not printed there, and nothing has been supplied here from elsewhere.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 4 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260104.html); read 3 October 2026.*
