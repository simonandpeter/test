# Serapion (24 May)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 24 mai reads «Sfântul Mucenic Serapion» — the holy martyr Serapion — with no epithet, no city and no century, and his own page on the site carries only that line, as heading and as title. There is no Viață tab and no Tropar tab; the page is a name and nothing more.

The display name here carries the day, because the calendar gives nothing else to tell him by. The corpus already keeps three men of that name — Serapion of Rome, Serapion of Pskov and the Serapion who was a disciple of Cronides — and none of the three is kept on this day in any calendar the corpus has read, so none of them has been identified with this one. Whether he is the same as any of them is an open question this page cannot settle, and no other calendar was checked.

The Greek calendar keeps a Serapion on this same day and gives him what the Romanian line withholds: an epithet, a company and a death. saint.gr names him «Σεραπίων ο Αιγύπτιος», the Egyptian, among the martyrs who suffered with [Meletius the Stratelates](/saints/meletius-the-stratelates) in the years of the emperor Elagabalus, who reigned from 218 to 222. He was an idolater, the page says, and when he saw the holy Meletius break the idols of the temple by his prayer he believed; and having confessed Christ steadfastly he was beheaded, together with [Callinicus](/saints/callinicus-the-magus) the magus, who had believed on seeing Meletius take no harm from the deadly poison.

The same page names, on the same day and in the same company, [John](/saints/john-companion-of-meletius) and [Stephen](/saints/stephen-companion-of-meletius), the two soldiers who were denounced with Meletius; the three women [Marciane](/saints/marciana-martyr-24-may), [Palladia](/saints/palladia-companion-of-meletius) and [Susanna](/saints/susanna-companion-of-meletius); the two infants [Cyriacus](/saints/cyriacus-the-infant) and [Christian](/saints/christian-the-infant); twelve komites and tribunes whom it names one by one; and a multitude beyond them whose number it puts at eleven thousand two hundred and eight. That the Serapion of the Romanian line is this Serapion is a match on the day and on the name, which is all either calendar gives to match on, and it is recorded here as such.

The Russian church calendar keeps him on 24 мая ст. ст. with the company of Meletius, and gives him a paragraph of his own at the end of their life. He was born in Egypt. He came to the province of Galatia and was a witness of the martyr’s contest of Meletius and his company; and seeing the courage with which those who believed in Christ died for him, he came to believe himself, and for that was shut up in prison. In the prison an angel of God came down to him and consecrated him bishop.

That is where this folder’s episcopate comes from: the Greek calendar that first made it gave him no rank at all, and the Russian both names him «Святитель … епископ, сщмч.» on his name page and tells on its life page how he came by the office. The company he died with was the regiment of the military commander of Galatia under the emperor the calendar dates 218 to 222: the shrines pulled down, the commander dead under torture, the two tribunes Стефан and Иоанн beheaded, 1218 soldiers with their wives and children put to the sword, and Феодор and Фавст burned with many others.
