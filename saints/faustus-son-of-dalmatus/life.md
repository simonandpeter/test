# Faustus, son of Dalmatus

Faustus was the one son whom [Dalmatus](/saints/dalmatus-of-constantinople) took with him when, after serving as a soldier in the reign of Theodosius the Great and standing in honour with the emperor, he despised all worldly things for God's sake and left his wife and his other children. Father and son went to [Isaacius](/saints/isaac-of-constantinople), who had a monastery in the town before Constantinople, and Isaacius tonsured them both into the monastic order.

The two lived there together in a way pleasing to God, striving well in the labours of the desert; and afterwards, when Isaacius died in a deep old age, Dalmatus was set as abbot in his place. Of Faustus himself the day's page says only that he passed through all that is pleasing to God and through the whole monastic struggle, and, finishing well the ascetic course, went over to the eternal dwellings and was numbered with the venerable fathers in the life that has no death.

That is all doxologia.ro prints of him: no year, no place of his own, and no hymn but the one it gives to the three of them together.
