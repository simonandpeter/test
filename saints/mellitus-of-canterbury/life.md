# Mellitus of Canterbury

Mellitus lived at the end of the sixth century and the beginning of the seventh. He was a member of the second band of monks whom Gregory the Dialogist, bishop of Rome, sent to Britain in support of the archbishop Augustine. In the year 604 he was consecrated bishop of the East Saxons with his seat at London, where he drew king Saberht to the Orthodox faith; and the king helped him greatly in his missionary work.

In the year 619 Mellitus was elected archbishop of Canterbury, and he fell asleep in peace in the year 624. That is all saint.gr has of him: no year of birth, no parentage, no account of his years at London beyond the king's conversion, no relic, no couplet and no hymn.
