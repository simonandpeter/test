# Gregory (Lebedev)

Gregory was born in the ancient town of Kolomna in Moscow province on 12 (24) November 1878; his father, the archpriest Alexis Mikhailovich Lebedev (1843–1914), served at the Dormition convent in Kolomna, and his mother Maria was the daughter of the local priest Theodore Ostroumov. The infant was named Alexander; the family was large — four brothers and three sisters — and the good and devout mother planted in the boy’s soul the seeds of faith and prayer, but died early of pneumonia, and his upbringing fell wholly to his father — the calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. He finished the Kazan Academy and took monastic vows; in 1923 he was consecrated bishop and became superior of the Alexander Nevsky Lavra, fought actively against Renovationism and was arrested twice in the twenties; not accepting the Declaration of Metropolitan Sergius he retired and occupied himself with theological work. Arrested in 1937 at Kashin on a charge of founding a fascist-monarchist organisation, he was shot on 17 September 1937; he was glorified in 2000, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 4 September (Julian).
