# Damasus

saint.gr keeps a saint Damasus on the fourth of December, and the whole of its entry for him is one sentence: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Πορτογάλου Αγίου» — we have no details of the life of the Portuguese saint. There is no notice behind the line beyond the bare name, no couplet, no relic, no icon and no picture of any kind.

Two things are therefore attested and no more: that the Greek calendar keeps him on this day, and that the site calls him Portuguese. It gives him no century, no see, no monastery, no rank and no hymn, and it does not identify him with any of the Damasuses the western calendars keep; the folder claims none of them. Anything further would have to come from a source this folder has not read.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 4 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/04/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3140/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
