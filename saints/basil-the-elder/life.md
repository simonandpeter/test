# Basil the Elder

doxologia.ro's page for him carries no Viață tab and no Tropar tab, and what it prints about him is a single paragraph, which is the whole of the source for this folder. He was the father of [Basil the Great](/saints/basil-the-great) and the son of Saint Macrina the Elder, and he lived at Caesarea in Cappadocia, in Asia Minor; during the persecutions he had to withdraw with his family towards the shores of the Black Sea.

Known as an advocate and an orator, he was marked out by virtue and by faith. He married [Emilia](/saints/emilia-mother-of-basil-the-great), the daughter of a martyr, and together they had a blessed family. Five of their ten children became saints: Basil the Great, kept on 1 January; [Gregory of Nyssa](/saints/gregory-of-nyssa), on 10 January; [Peter of Sebaste](/saints/peter-of-sebaste), on 9 January; the venerable Naucratius, on 8 June; and Macrina the Younger, on 19 July.

He fell asleep in the Lord in the year 350, and his commemoration is on 8 May, together with that of his wife Emilia. Nothing else about him is printed there.
