# The Thirty-three Martyrs of Palestine

Thirty-three martyrs of Palestine who died by the sword; their verse calls them a steadfast army and a great shield-wall, fallen by the sword, a match for the host of demons. That is the whole of the Greek synaxarion’s entry for 16 August. The Russian calendar keeps them on the same day by the Julian reckoning, 29 August civil, and prints no life; the Russian church calendar at azbyka.ru gives only the date.

The Greek calendar keeps the company on the same menologion day and with the same number and country, and says one thing about them: «Μαρτύρησαν όλοι δια ξίφους», they were all martyred by the sword. saint.gr names none of the thirty-three, gives no century, no emperor and no year, and prints no hymn. The couplet of the synaxarion above the notice makes a battle of it: «Στερρὸς στρατός τε καὶ συνασπισμὸς μέγας, / Ξίφει πεσών, στράτευμα δαιμόνων πρέπει» — a firm army and a great locking of shields, fallen by the sword, befits an army of demons, that is, routs it.
