# Cosmas of Zographou

Cosmas was a Bulgarian who fled his parents' house in secret rather than be married, and made for the Holy Mountain. On the road the devil tried to turn him back with a vision of the sea round Athos as a bottomless deep, and long, ardent prayer got him past it. At Zographou he was a novice a long time, then tonsured and made ecclesiarch; at the feast of the Annunciation at Vatopedi the Mother of God let him see her as a Queen attending the services and the refectory, with all the monks ministering to her - the Mistress of the Mountain among her own.

Ordained deacon and then priest, he asked her in prayer how he should be saved, and was answered: silence. He took a blessing and withdrew to a cave as a hermit, where he laboured many years, was given discernment and grace against the demons, and foretold his own end. When he died - in 1323, the life says - the birds themselves gathered at his cave and cried at his burial. After forty days the brethren opened the grave to translate his relics to the monastery, as the custom was, and they were not to be found: God had hidden them.
