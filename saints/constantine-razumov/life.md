# Constantine (Razumov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him in a line of four and gives no life. Its line for 31 июля ст. ст. reads «Cщмч. Иоанна пресвитера, св. Константина исп., пресвитера, мц. Анны и св. Елисаветы исп (после 1937).» — with [John (Rumyantsev)](/saints/john-rumyantsev) a presbyter who died a hieromartyr, [Anna (Serova)](/saints/anna-serova) a martyr and [Elizabeth (Rumyantseva)](/saints/elizabeth-rumyantseva) a confessor. Behind the line the calendar's own name page gives his name in full as «Константин (Разумов), иерей, исповедник».

The calendar calls him a confessor and not a martyr, and a presbyter: a priest who was held and not killed for it, as far as the line goes. The year is given as «после 1937», after 1937, which is a lower bound and not a year of death.

Nothing else is printed. The calendar names no parish, no diocese, no place and no end for any of the four, and carries no life page and no hymn for them. Two of the four share the surname Rumyantsev with the martyr of 1928 whom the previous line names, and the calendar says nothing of any kinship between them.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 31 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260731.html); read 13 October 2026.*
