# Pimen the Iconographer

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 3 noiembrie reads «Sfantul Cuvios Pimen Iconarul» — the venerable Pimen the iconographer — and the page the calendar links for him carries neither a Viață tab nor a Tropar tab, so the line, the date in the page head and the readings appointed for the day are the whole of what the source prints under his name.

Nothing else is there. His country is not named, no monastery, no century and no year; the word «Cuvios» in the calendar's line is all the source says about his manner of life, and «Iconarul» all it says about his work. The corpus holds several other men of this name on other days — Pimen the Great, Pimen the Faster of the Kyiv Caves, Pimen of Ugresha, and others — and nothing on this page connects him with any of them.

No other calendar has been read for him.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 3 noiembrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/3-noiembrie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-pimen-iconarul); read 25 September 2026.*
