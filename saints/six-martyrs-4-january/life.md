# The Six Martyrs (4 January)

saint.gr's whole notice for them is two sentences and a cross-reference, and the first sentence is an admission: it is unknown when or where these martyrs contended. In the synaxarists, the page says, they are called martyrs, but are said to have been perfected in peace — the two things at once, and it does not reconcile them. It adds that their memory is repeated on 15 January, and sends the reader on to a line it calls «Άγιοι Έξι Πατέρες», the Holy Six Fathers.

They are one folder here and not six, because the calendar names none of them and there is no name to give a member. The page's two-line verse counts them all the same: six clear souls, having flown from this life, stand together with the six-winged minds. No year, no place and no hymn is printed, and nothing is supplied here.
