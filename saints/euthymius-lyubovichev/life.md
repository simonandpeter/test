# Euthymius (Lyubovichev)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and gives no life. Its line for 6 июля ст. ст. reads «Прмч. Евфимия (1931).» — the venerable-martyr Euthymius, of the year 1931 — and behind the name there is a link to a name page and to no reading at all. That page, 14289, prints «Евфимий (Любовичев), иеромонах, прмч.», Euthymius (Lyubovichev), hieromonk, venerable-martyr, and beneath it «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 6 июля», and nothing besides.

So the calendar sets down a hieromonk of that surname who died in 1931 and is kept twice: on this day, which is his own, and on the Julian 26 January with the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. Of where he was professed, where he served, how he was taken or how he died it says not one word, and nothing here is supplied from elsewhere.

The day page carries nineteen hymn headings and none under his name. azbyka.ru, which might hold an account of him, was not read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 6 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260706.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14289.html), which name him and give no life; read 9 October 2026.*
