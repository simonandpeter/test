# Sergius of Nurma

The Russian calendar's 7 October prints «Прп. Сергия Нуромского (Вологодского) (1412)», and the two lives the site keeps behind that line call him Sergius of Obnora, of Nurma, the wonderworker of Vologda. He began his monastic labours on the Holy Mountain of Athos, and was tonsured there. Then he came to Russia and settled in the monastery of the Life-giving Trinity under the direction of [Sergius of Radonezh](/saints/sergius-of-radonezh), whose memory the page puts on 5 July and 25 September.

After some years, with the blessing of his abbot, he went into the forests of Vologda and stopped on the bank of the river Nurma. There he set up a cross and built a chapel with a cell, and lived in it in deep silence, «равноангельскую жизнь проводя» — leading a life equal to the angels — bearing patiently the trials that came to him from demons and from unkind men. The second life says robbers as well as demons, and gives his withdrawal a motive: the love of stillness.

It pleased the Lord to call him out of that solitude, so that he, filled with wisdom and spiritual experience, might serve others to their salvation. Forty men gathered to him from various places, hungry for a godly life. By their common effort the brethren built a large church in honour of the Procession of the Honourable Wood of the Life-giving Cross of the Lord, and cells were raised around it. Not far from the Nurma monastery [Paul of Obnora](/saints/paul-of-obnora) was keeping his own struggle, and Sergius often visited him for conversation profitable to the soul; the second life calls Paul his friend, his fellow-talker and his spiritual son, and dates Paul's death to 1429.

The two lives do not agree on when he died, and this folder does not choose between them. The one the day's own link reaches says that he fell asleep on 7 October 1412 and that his church veneration began in 1546; the other says that his repose followed in 1421, and that after his death he was glorified by many healings, as many as eighty of his miracles having been written down by 1584. The day line prints 1412. Neither year is preferred here and the interval carries both.

The day prints his own troparion and kontakion, both in tone 8, and both name him. The kontakion opens «Яко звезда пресветлая, от Святыя Горы провозшедши» — having come forth like a most radiant star from the Holy Mountain — which is the Athos tonsure the lives give him, and closes by calling him «пустынный жителю», dweller in the wilderness.

*After the Russian church calendar's [7 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261007.html), [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/1971.html) and the two lives it reaches — [the first](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1686.htm) and [the second](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life6831.htm); read 4 October 2026.*
