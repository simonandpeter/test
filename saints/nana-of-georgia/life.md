# Nana of Georgia

The Romanian calendar keeps Nana and [Mirian](/saints/mirian-of-georgia), rulers of Georgia, together, and its life tells their turning to Christ through Nina, called equal to the apostles for giving her whole life to the conversion of Georgia. Nina healed many, and her name reached the Iberian queen Nana, who called her to the palace hoping to be cured of a grave illness; Nina cured her, but refused the rich reward of king Mirian III, saying her power to heal was a gift of God. Out hunting, Mirian lost his way and was kept by a strange mist from finding it; he called on all the gods known in Iberia, and was saved only when he called on the God of Nina, and so he became a Christian.

He made Christianity the religion of the state. On Nina’s advice he sent envoys to Constantine the Great asking for priests, and Constantine sent the archbishop Eustathius of Antioch, who baptized the royal family, the soldiers and the nobles, and the whole of Kartli was baptized quickly and peacefully, save the mountain people of the Caucasus. Mirian was buried in the church of the monastery of Samtavro, and two years later Nana was buried beside him. The life gives no years.
