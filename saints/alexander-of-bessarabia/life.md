# Alexander of Bessarabia

Alexander was born on 14 April 1861 into a priest's family in Bessarabia, and became himself the priest of the church of Alexander Nevsky at Călărași-sat, in what is today the Republic of Moldova. He and his wife raised two adopted children, Vsevolod and Margareta. doxologia.ro's page, which is short, names him only as Alexandru din Basarabia and gives no surname.

He was, the page says, a tireless promoter of Romanian culture and of the Romanian identity of the province. From 1903 to 1925 he presided over the diocesan congress, and in 1917 he became the single representative of the Church in the Sfatul Țării, the country's council. In that capacity, on 27 March 1918, he voted for the union of Bessarabia with Romania, and he was afterwards named to the committee for the unification of the churches of Greater Romania.

After the Soviet Union annexed Bessarabia on 28 June 1940 he stayed with his parishioners, although the rest of his family took refuge across the Prut. He was arrested very soon and condemned for counter-revolutionary activity and enmity towards the Soviet Union; before his interrogators he acknowledged that he had no love for the Bolshevik revolution, nor for the fact that before the union Romanians could not use their mother tongue in worship. He was deported to Kazan, in what is now Tatarstan, and died there soon after, on 7 August 1941.
