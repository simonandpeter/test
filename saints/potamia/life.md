# Potamia

saint.gr's calendar for 7 August names the venerable Potamia the wonderworker, and gives her one sentence: «Η Όσια Ποταμία η Θαυματουργή μαρτύρησε δια ξίφους» — she was martyred by the sword. There is nothing else on the page: no century, no country, no emperor, no judge, no year of her death, no place of it, and no relic of hers anywhere. The listing calls her venerable and the sentence calls her a martyr, and both words are kept.

Above the sentence the page sets a couplet which turns her name into her praise: slain by the sword for Christ, the wonderful one, Potamia pours out rivers of wonders. The page prints no hymn to her. Where the source has one line, the life says exactly that line.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/764/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
