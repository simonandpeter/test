# Andrew of Totma

saint.gr's calendar for 10 Οκτωβρίου names Andrew of Totma and gives him three lines. He was born, the page says, in 1638; he was a fool for Christ — «δια Χριστόν σαλός» — and he fell asleep in 1673. Then it says plainly «Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου»: we have no further details of the life of the saint. The head of the page repeats the two years as «(1638 - 1673)».

So nothing is written here of where he was born, of the town of Totma beyond its name, of what his folly took the form of, or of how he died, because the source read for this folder says none of it. The page prints no hymn for him. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/10/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2711/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
