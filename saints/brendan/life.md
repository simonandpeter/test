# Brendan

saint.gr keeps a saint Brendan on 29 November, printing the name in Latin letters inside a Greek line — «Άγιος Brendan» — and the whole of its entry for him is one sentence: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ιρλανδού Αγίου», we have no details of the life of the Irish saint. There is no «Άγιοι που εορτάζουν» notice behind that line, no couplet and no picture.

Two things are therefore attested and no more: that the Greek calendar keeps him on this day, and that he is Irish. The page gives him no century, no monastery, no see, no rank, no relic, no couplet and no hymn, and it does not say which Brendan of the Irish calendars he is; the name belongs to several, and this folder claims none of them. Anything further would have to come from an Irish source this folder has not read.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 29 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/29/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3107/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
