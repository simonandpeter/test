# Philemon (6 July)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 6 iulie reads «Sfântul Mucenic Filimon», and the page the calendar links under that name carries the name again and nothing more: no life, no troparion, no year, no place, no companions. What the Romanian calendar prints of him on this day is that he is a martyr and that he is called Filimon, and this folder says no more than that.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 6 July, in one line with [Archippus](/saints/archippus-6-july) and [Onesimus](/saints/onesimus-6-july). saint.gr says the three had a warm love for our Lord Jesus Christ and a perfect devotion to his Gospel, and that when they were ordered to deny Christ and sacrifice to the idols all three answered with one voice in the words of the Apostle Paul, that none of us lives to himself and none dies to himself, and that whether we live or die we are the Lord's. Putting their own words into practice, it says, all three died a martyr's death, and each differently: Philemon by crucifixion, Archippus of the wounds he suffered being dragged behind a wild horse through stones and thorns, and Onesimus beheaded with the sword. Over Philemon it sets the couplet «Σταυροί ψυχρόν φίλημα Xριστόν Φιλήμων, / Σταυροί δε και σε φίλτρον αυτού το ζέον» — Philemon crucifies a cold kiss for Christ, and the burning love of Christ crucifies him in turn.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 6 iulie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/6-iulie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-filimon-0), which prints the name only; read 24 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 6 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/681/saint.aspx), read 1 October 2026.*
