# Philemon (6 July)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 6 iulie reads «Sfântul Mucenic Filimon», and the page the calendar links under that name carries the name again and nothing more: no life, no troparion, no year, no place, no companions. What the Romanian calendar prints of him on this day is that he is a martyr and that he is called Filimon, and this folder says no more than that.
