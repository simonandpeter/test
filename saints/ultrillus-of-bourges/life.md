# Ultrillus of Bourges

Ultrillus was born in the city of Bourges and practised his ascesis in the monastery of Saint Nicetius of Lyon, whose memory saint.gr keeps on 2 Απριλίου. In 612 he was elected bishop of Bourges, the city of his birth, and he fell asleep in peace in 624.

That is the whole of the page: two sentences, a birthplace, a monastery, a year of election and a year of death. It gives him no parents, no account of his twelve years in the see, no relics, no couplet and no hymn. He is one of three Western bishops saint.gr keeps on this day, with Theodore of Pavia and Anastasius of Brescia, and the only one of the three who is also a monk.
