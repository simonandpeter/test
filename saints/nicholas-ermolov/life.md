# Nicholas (Ermolov)

He is the second half of one line on the Russian calendar's 13 October: «Сщмчч. Иннокентия и Николая пресвитеров (1937)» — Innocent and Nicholas, hieromartyrs, presbyters, 1937. The day line gives the pair one grade between them, and it is his index entry that separates them: «Николай (Ермолов), протоиерей, сщмч.», an archpriest, where the other man's page says «иерей». That one word is everything the calendar adds to his name.

Neither page gives a birth, a diocese, a parish, a charge or a place of death, and 1937 is the year in which almost the whole of a diocese's clergy could be taken at once. His index entry keeps him here and on 26 January with the whole company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and carries no life. The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian saints are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026 even with a browser user-agent, so the line stands as the line.

[Innocent (Kikin)](/saints/innocent-kikin) is the other half of it.
