# Eutropia

saint.gr keeps three women together on 25 Ιουνίου — [Leonis](/saints/leonis-25-june), [Livia](/saints/livia-the-martyr) and Eutropia, each of them called a venerable-martyr — and gives them no narrative at all, only a couplet apiece and one closing sentence. That sentence is the whole account of their end: «Μαρτύρησαν, η μεν πρώτη δια πυρός, η δε δύο άλλες δια ξίφους» — they were martyred, the first of them by fire and the other two by the sword.

The couplets say as much again and no more. Of Leonis: she leaps into the fire of the furnace as a lioness, very hungry, leaps upon her food. Of Livia: a bride of the Lord, she takes a dye for her adorning, giving her neck to the sword. Of Eutropia: he who boasted that he held the world fast like a nest is made sport of by Eutropia, who has been cut down. No country is named, no century, no persecutor and no year, and the page prints no hymn.

What stands beside this on the Romanian calendar of the same day is a passage inside the life of [Fevronia](/saints/fevronia-of-sivapol), where the abbess Bryene strengthens her disciple by recalling earlier martyrs, «Livia și Leonida, cele două surori» — Livia and Leonida, two sisters, of whom Livia was beheaded with the sword and Leonida thrown into the fire. The Greek couplets put the fire and the sword the same way round, and name a third woman the Romanian passage does not.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 25 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/25/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1976/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
