# Sava III

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 26 iulie is «Sfântul Sava al III-lea» — Saint Sava the Third — and his own page on the site has no Viață tab and no troparion, only icons, the epistle and gospel of the day, and one sentence about him: that Saint Sava the Third had an important part in the development of mediaeval architecture in Serbia.

That single sentence is all that was read. It does not give his rank, though the epistle appointed for the day is a hierarch's, nor his century, nor his see, nor the day of his death; and this folder therefore carries no rank and no dates. He is not Sava of Serbia, the first archbishop, whom this corpus already keeps on another day: the calendar's line numbers him third, and nothing more than that was established here.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 26 iulie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/26-iulie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-sava-al-iii-lea), which prints no life; read 24 September 2026.*
