# Sergius (Voskresensky)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him for 26 февраля ст. ст. in five words — «Сщмч. Сергия пресвитера (1933).» — a hieromartyr, a presbyter, who died in 1933. The name link behind the line carries the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line leaves out: «Сергий (Воскресенский), иерей, сщмч.», a priest of that name, one of the new martyrs of the Russian church in the Soviet years.

The corpus keeps two other men of that surname, a deacon and a presbyter, martyred in other years and on other days; the forename here is different from both, and no connection between them is asserted. The calendar prints no life page for him and no hymn under his name, and nothing further is written here, because the calendar gives nothing further.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 26 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260226.html); read 3 October 2026.*
