# Benjamin the Recluse

saint.gr's calendar for 13 October keeps «Όσιος Βενιαμίν ο Έγκλειστος ο “εν τω Σπηλαίω”» — Benjamin the enclosed, the one in the cave — and its whole notice is three sentences. He lived in the fourteenth century, it says, and before he became a monk he was a considerable merchant.

Then at some time, taking to heart the Lord's words that a rich man will hardly enter into the Kingdom of Heaven, he repented; and having divided what he had among the poor he became a monk, begging the Lord to forgive him. That is all. The calendar gives no country and no monastery, does not say which cave the epithet means, and prints neither a verse nor a hymn for him; the only other thing on the page is a note that the name Benjamin is Hebrew and means a showing of the divine power.

The Russian calendar keeps him on the same day and in the same century, and has the whole of the life the Greek notice lacks. Before he took the monastic habit he «был купцом великим», was a great merchant. Then one day at a service the Saviour's words went deep into his heart — that a rich man shall hardly enter the kingdom of heaven, Matthew 19:23 — and he gave his estate away to the poor and became a monk of the Kyiv Caves lavra, «угождаючи Господу Богу в постах и молитвах даже до смерти», pleasing the Lord God in fasting and prayer even until death. He is buried in the Far, or Theodosius, caves.

The two hymns the calendar prints for him are built on that one turn of his life. The troparion calls him a good merchant who gave away corruptible wealth and bought the incorruptible with it; the kontakion has him leave the trade of this life as worthless and buy instead the pearl of great price.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 13 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/13/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2605/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026; and the Russian church calendar's [13 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261013.html) and the two lives its index page links, [one](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life6501.htm) and [the other](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1730.htm), read 4 October 2026.*
