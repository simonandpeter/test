# Ninnoca of Brittany

saint.gr says that the venerable Ninnoca lived in Britain in the fifth century after Christ, and that by tradition she is held to be the daughter of Saint Brenthacius of Brecknock. She followed Saint Germanus of Auxerre, and was later tonsured a nun and became abbess of a monastery in Armorica in Brittany.

She fell asleep in peace in the year 467, and God gave her, after her falling asleep, the gift of working wonders; and so many of the faithful ran to the monastery of Quimper in Brittany to be healed. That is the whole of it: a country, a century, a father given on the strength of tradition, a teacher, a house she governed, a year, and a shrine that drew the sick after she was dead. The page names no wonder in particular, gives her no rule and no disciple, and prints neither verse nor hymn. Her father and Germanus have no folder in this corpus, so neither is linked here.
