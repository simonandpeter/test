# Peter of Capitolias

Peter was born and raised in the city of Capitolias, an ancient city of eastern Jordan on the road to Damascus, the Greek synaxarion says, and was a man of great wisdom and prudence. He was married with three children and afterwards became a monk, and was honoured with the priesthood by the metropolitan of Bostra. He was denounced to the ethnarch of the Agarenes as a teacher of the Christians, and because he confessed his faith boldly before him, they cut off his hands and feet, then put out his eyes, crucified him, and at last beheaded him; his martyred body they burned and threw into the river. Its verse has Peter, a well-sharpened sword of the Spirit, unafraid of the sword’s sharp edge.

The Greek calendar keeps him on 4 October. The entry gives no year.
