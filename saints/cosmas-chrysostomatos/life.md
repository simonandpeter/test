# Cosmas Chrysostomatos

saint.gr's page for him is a note about a manuscript before it is a life. His memory, it says, is recorded in the Typikon of the Cypriot monastery of Chrysostomos, preserved in the National Library of Paris under number 402 Coislin φ, 101, and the Typikon sets it down in these words: «Ιστέον ότι κατά ταύτην την ημέραν επιτελούμεν μνημόσυνα του οσίου πατρός ημών Κοσμά του Χρυσοστόματος, ωσαύτως και τα μνημόσυνα του πρεσβυτέρου και καθηγουμένου κυρ Νικηφόρου» — that on this day the memorials are kept of our venerable father Cosmas Chrysostomatos, and likewise those of the presbyter and abbot lord Nicephorus.

From that, the page says, Cosmas was a monk of the Cypriot monastery of Saint Chrysostomos, from which he took the surname Chrysostomatos, and having lived a holy and ascetic life he died peacefully and is kept as a feast in his own monastery. No century, no birthplace and no year of repose is given, and no hymn is printed for him.
