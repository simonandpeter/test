# Theodore of Edessa

saint.gr gives the venerable Theodore's father as Symeon and his mother as Maria. He went to school and was distinguished in the learning of philosophical knowledge, and in rhetoric and philosophy as well. At ten years old he was left an orphan; half of his parents' property his sister took, so that she might be established in the world, and the other half he took himself, shared it out among the poor, and set off for Jerusalem.

There he venerated the Holy Places, and in the end he became a monk in the Lavra of Saint Sabbas. There he trained himself in virtue to a great degree and was a famous ascetic. Because of his great virtues the Patriarch of Jerusalem made him bishop of Edessa, which the page places in the Middle East and does not describe further.

As a shepherd of his people he was excellent, with his much theological knowledge, his many virtues, and above all with his great love, which he had in abundance for his flock. When he had called together the clergy and the people of Edessa and blessed them, he gave up his holy soul to the heavenly Father. The page adds that his life was written by Basil, bishop of Emesa, and that a life of him in Greek survives at the Monastery of Iviron. It gives no year at any point.
