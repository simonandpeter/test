# Misael of Abalatsk

saint.gr's calendar for 17 December keeps Saint Misael of Abalatsk, and says of him only that it has nothing of his life: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ρώσου Αγίου» — we have no details of the life of the Russian saint. The heading above that sentence gives the year of his death as 1797 and no year of birth, and that heading is the only place a year appears at all.

So the whole of the Greek entry is four things: the fixed day, the place Abalatsk, the word that he is a Russian saint, and the year 1797. The page gives him no rank beyond saint, names no monastery and no office, lists no relic, prints no hymn, and sends the reader on to the synaxis of all the saints of Siberia and to an English calendar, neither of which was read for this folder.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 17 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/17/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4252/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
