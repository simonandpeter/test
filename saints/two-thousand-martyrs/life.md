# The Two Thousand Martyrs

The Greek calendar keeps two thousand martyrs on 1 Ιουλίου and prints one sentence about them: «Οι Άγιοι Δύο Χιλιάδες Μάρτυρες μαρτύρησαν δια ξίφους» — the two thousand holy martyrs were martyred by the sword. There is no name among them on the page, no place, no emperor and no year, so nothing of that kind is recorded here; the number and the sword are what the source gives.

Above the sentence the page sets the distich the synaxaria carry, which plays the two thousand against the cowardice they did not have: «Ξίφος πατάσσει χιλιανδρίας δύω, / Σεπτών αθλητών ων μακράν δειλανδρία» — the sword strikes down two thousands of men, revered contestants from whom unmanliness stood far off.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1958/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
