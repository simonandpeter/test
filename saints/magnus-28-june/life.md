# Magnus (28 June)

saint.gr's calendar for 28 Ιουνίου names him and gives him one sentence. Its line reads «Όσιος Μάγνος», and the page behind that line says only that the venerable Magnus died in peace while at prayer. No country is named, no century, no monastery, no year and no relics, and the page prints no hymn for him.

The couplet of the synaxarion above the sentence makes that end its whole subject: «Eν τη προσευχή θείος εκπνεύσας Mάγνος, Yπήρξε δήλος λήξιν αιτήσας βίου» — the divine Magnus, breathing his last at prayer, was plainly a man who had asked for the end of his life. That is all the entry holds, and the day in the name here is the day the calendar keeps him on and not an epithet of his own.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/28/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1919/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
