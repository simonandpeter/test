# Patrick (Petrov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him in a line of four words and a year for 11 марта ст. ст., «Прп. Патрикия исп (1933)» — the venerable Patrick, a confessor, 1933. The line declines the forename, gives no surname, no place, no year of birth and no word of narrative, and the rank words it does give are the only ones there are: venerable, which is the monastic grade, and confessor, which in this calendar's usage means a man who suffered for the faith and was not killed for it.

Behind that «Патрикия» stands the calendar's own name page 9238, which carries the fuller form «Патрикий (Петров), иеромонах, исп.» — the surname the line leaves out, and the grade of hieromonk — and lists two days, this 11 марта and «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. That page carries no life either, and azbyka.ru was not read for him. So what the calendar prints is the whole of what this folder says: a hieromonk of the Russian church named Patrick Petrov, who died in 1933 and whom this calendar keeps among its confessors on this day.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 11 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260311.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9238.html), which carries no life; read 7 October 2026.*
