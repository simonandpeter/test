# Evagrius of Georgia

saint.gr names him and gives no life. Its calendar for 4 February prints «Όσιοι Ευάγριος και Σίος εκ Γεωργίας» — the venerable Evagrius and Shio, out of Georgia — and the page behind that line carries no account of either man: only the site's own note on what the name Ευάγριος means, and then the three words «Βλέπε 4 Ιανουαρίου», see 4 January. So what is recorded of him here is what the calendar records: the rank Όσιος, venerable; the country, Georgia; and the company, [Shio](/saints/shio-of-mgvime), whom the corpus already keeps and whom the Romanian calendar puts on 9 May.

Nothing else is on the page — no century, no monastery, no death, no hymn and no verses — and the 4 January page it points to was not read for this entry. The Greek day quoted is the day page that names him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 4 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/04/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1281/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
