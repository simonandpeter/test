# Gabriel of Lesnovo

Gabriel of Lesnovo was born in the village the page spells Ossoke, in the region of Kriva Palanka in Serbia, in the eleventh century. He loved the monastic polity, and was tonsured a monk at the monastery of Lesnovo. He fell asleep in peace at the beginning of the twelfth century.

That is the whole of what saint.gr prints of him: three sentences, no year within either century, no account of his labours, no abbacy, no relics, and no hymn. What the page spends its other words on is the name — Gabriel, the man and the instrument of God, one of the seven chief archangels, who announced to the Mother of God that she would bring Christ into the world.
