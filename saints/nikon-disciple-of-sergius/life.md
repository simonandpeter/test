# Nikon, disciple of Sergius

saint.gr's calendar for 17 November keeps «Όσιος Νίκων ο θαυματουργός μαθητής του Αγίου Σεργίου» — Nikon the wonderworker, disciple of Saint Sergius — and dates his falling asleep 1427. The venerable Nikon, the page says, was a disciple of Saint [Sergius](/saints/sergius-of-radonezh), and it sends the reader to the twenty-fifth of September for him. Then it says plainly that we have no other details of the Russian venerable's life except that he fell asleep in 1427.

The one other thing it records is where he lies: the relics of the saint are at the Lavra of the Holy Trinity and Saint Sergius at Moscow. It prints no hymn for him and names nobody else beside him. So this folder keeps a discipleship, a year and a resting place, and says that the calendar has nothing further — the epithet wonderworker included, for which the page gives no miracle of its own.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 17 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/17/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3034/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
