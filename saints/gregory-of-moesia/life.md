# Gregory of Moesia

saint.gr's notice for him is a single sentence, and the page's whole content besides it is a note on the name Gregory — the wakeful one — and a list of other bearers of it. Saint Gregory, bishop of Moesia, fell asleep in peace in the year 1012.

The page gives no birthplace, no account of his see, no manner of life and no hymn, and nothing is supplied here. He is one of three men called Gregory whom this Greek day keeps, and the only one of them the calendar makes a bishop.
