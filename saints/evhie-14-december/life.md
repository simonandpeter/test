# Evhie (14 December)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 14 decembrie reads «Sfântul Mucenic Evhie», and the page the calendar links under that name carries the name only: no life, no troparion text, no year, no place and no companions. The long life which the other martyrs of the day share — the martyrs of Caesarea in Bithynia under Decius, and the martyrs of the Thebaid under Diocletian — does not mention him anywhere in it, so the Romanian calendar keeps him on this day without telling his story.

What his page does carry, under its Tropar tab, is a recording made for the martyrs of the day together, whose title names seven of them: «Troparul Sfinților Mucenici Calinic, Filimon, Apolonie, Tirs, Evhie, Arian și Levchie (14 decembrie)», with the sung petition that they pray to God for us. That is the whole of what doxologia.ro prints of Evhie — the rank of a martyr, a name, a day, and a place in that company — and this folder says no more than that.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 14 decembrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/14-decembrie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-evhie), which prints the name only; read 28 September 2026.*
