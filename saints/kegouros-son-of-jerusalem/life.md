# Kegouros, son of Jerusalem of Beroea

Kegouros was one of the three sons of [Jerusalem](/saints/jerusalem-of-beroea),
the widow of Alexandria who, after her husband’s death, went about many
places preaching Christ as far as Rome, then came to Beroea, became a nun
and brought monasticism to the region, and led many pagans to the faith.
For that she was denounced to the duke of Thessalonica, Kindianos, who came
to Beroea himself to question her, and seeing her steadfastness had her
tortured. Her three sons were put to death cruelly before her — one dragged
behind horses, the second beaten to death, the third made to wear a red-hot
helmet; the synaxarion does not say which son died which death — and then
the mother herself was beheaded, between 276 and 282. Her skull is kept at
the shrine church of St Anthony at Beroea, and she and her sons are kept
with a festival on 4 September there and at Kampochori near Alexandria in
Imathia; the metropolitan of Beroea has published her life with her
festal service. His brothers are [Secundus](/saints/secundus-son-of-jerusalem)
and [Secundinus](/saints/secundinus-son-of-jerusalem).
