# Kegouros, son of Jerusalem of Beroea

Kegouros was one of the three sons of [Jerusalem](/saints/jerusalem-of-beroea), the widow of Alexandria who brought monasticism and the faith to Beroea; when the duke of Thessalonica, Kindianos, interrogated and tortured her, her children were put to death cruelly — one dragged behind horses, the second beaten to death, the third made to wear — the Greek synaxarion’s life runs on past the point read here, and says which son died which death only there.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 4 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2339/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
