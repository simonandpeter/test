# Erotiis, companion of Capitolina

saint.gr's calendar for 27 Οκτωβρίου names Capitolina and Erotiis together and tells one story for them. They lived in the time of the emperor Diocletian, the page says, when Zilikinthios was governor in Cappadocia, about the year 289. [Capitolina](/saints/capitolina) was of noble birth and rich, but gave no weight at all to earthly riches: she shared out her whole property among the poor and set her slaves free, and then presented herself before the governor Zilikinthios and confessed her faith in Christ. He ordered her shut in the prison, and the next day she was beheaded.

Erotiis, who had been Capitolina's slave, snatched up stones and threw them at the governor; and he, enraged, ordered his guards to beat her mercilessly with rods. But by the grace of Christ, the page says, she stayed unharmed, so that the governor then ordered her beheaded with the sword; and thus both of them found a martyr's death. The couplet above the entry says that they kill a slave and a mistress by the sword, both of them slaves of the Trinity, the true Mistress.

The Russian calendar keeps the two of them on the same day and gives her a shorter telling and a different standing. She is «ее служанка», her serving-maid, where the Greek page makes her her slave; she was beaten with staves for throwing a stone at the governor in defence of her mistress — one stone, where the Greek page has her snatch up several — and, having come through the beating unhurt, she too was beheaded. The province is in her name instead of in the prose, «Еротиида Каппадокийская».

The year differs as well and is not settled here. This page closes «Святые мученицы пострадали в 304 г.»; saint.gr dates them to about 289. Both readings are now in the date, and neither is chosen. No life page follows her own name on the day page — the life the day links for this entry is headed for her mistress — and her name page prints two icons, the one day, and the teaser of an article on Православие.Ru about the two of them together, which quotes her mistress and not her.
