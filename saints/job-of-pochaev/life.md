# Job of Pochaev

Job of Pochaev died on 28 October 1651. On 28 August 1833 his relics were solemnly opened for general veneration, and in 1902 the Holy Synod decreed that on this day the holy relics be carried round the Dormition cathedral of the Pochaev Lavra after the Divine Liturgy. That is the whole of the Sretensky calendar’s note for the day, which the Russian calendar keeps as the finding of his relics, with a troparion and kontakion — an incorrupt treasure, the kontakion says, shone from the hiding-place of the earth.
