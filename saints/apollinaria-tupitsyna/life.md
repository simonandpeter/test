# Apollinaria (Tupitsyna)

She is the last of the eleven names the Russian calendar keeps in one line on 30 September, and the year the line carries for all of them, 1937, is printed immediately after hers: «мц. Аполлинарии (1937)». «Мц.» is martyr in the feminine, a laywoman with no rank in the Church and no office, which distinguishes her from the one monastic of the eleven, [Alexandra (Chervyakova)](/saints/alexandra-chervyakova), whom the line marks «прмц.»

The site's index of names heads her page «Аполлинария (Тупицына), мц.» and keeps her here and on 26 January with the company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. It prints no birth, no trade, no parish, no charge and no place of death. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026 even with a browser user-agent, so the line stands as the line.

*After the Russian church calendar's [30 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260930.html) and [its index entry for her](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13812.html); read 4 October 2026.*
