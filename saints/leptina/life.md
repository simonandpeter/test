# Leptina

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Οκτωβρίου names Leptina and gives her no life. One sentence is the whole of the narrative: «Η Αγία Λέπτινα μαρτύρησε συρόμενη στη γη μέχρι θανάτου» — she was martyred by being dragged along the ground until she died. The couplet over it says that, dragged on the earth by the hands of the impious, Leptina left the thickness of her flesh to the earth.

The page gives her no country, no city, no year, no emperor and no companions; it prints no hymn and no relics, and nothing in the corpus answers to the name.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2652/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
