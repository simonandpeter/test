# Silvanus (5 November)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 5 noiembrie reads «Sfântul Mucenic Silvan», and the page it points to carries the name, the day, the epistle appointed for the day and the gospel read with it, and nothing else at all: no country, no century, no companions, no manner of death, no year, no icon, no hymn, and no Viață tab to open.

Nothing on the page joins him to the Silvanus of Emesa the corpus already keeps, the bishop whom the same calendar holds on 29 January, or to the Silvanus who is the companion of Bianor on 10 July. The calendar simply names a martyr of this name on 5 November, and that is what is recorded here.

The readings appointed for the day are 2 Timothy 2:1–10 and John 15:17–27 with 16:1–2.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 5 November, and adds the little the Romanian page lacked. saint.gr puts him in the years of the emperor Maximian, when the governor of Palestine was Urbanus — the page sets the year 298 beside the governor's name — and says that he was condemned to be worn down in the mines until he died, at the place called Phano. The couplet it prints for him plays on the mines and on changing: «Ἐν τοῖς μετάλλοις Σιλβανὸς βεβλημένος, / Λείπει μέταλλα καὶ μεταλλάττει βίον» — Silvanus, thrown into the mines, leaves the mines and changes his life. The page gives him no year of his own, no see, no companions and no hymn; several other martyrs it places under the same governor stand in separate entries of the same day.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 5 noiembrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/5-noiembrie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-silvan); read 25 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 5 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2894/saint.aspx), read 1 October 2026.*
