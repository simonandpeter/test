# Theosteriktos the Hymnographer

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 17 martie reads «Sfântul Cuvios Teostirict imnograful», and the page it links to carries neither a Viață tab nor a Tropar tab: there is the heading, the day, and nothing further. What the line says is that he was a monastic and a hymnographer, and that the Romanian church keeps him on this day; that is the whole of what has been read here.

No year, no place, no account of his life and none of his hymns has been taken from anywhere else, and none has been supplied from memory. If a life of his, or a canon of his, is printed elsewhere, it has not been read, and this page will say only what doxologia says until it has been.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 17 martie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/17-martie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-teostirict-imnograful); read 19 September 2026.*
