# Sios of Gareji

saint.gr's calendar for 1 Ιουνίου names him and gives two sentences. Sios, whom the page also calls Garegeli, lived in Georgia in the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries. He lived the ascetic life in a manner pleasing to God in the desert of the venerable David in eastern Georgia, and he was perfected in martyrdom by the Muslims of the Caucasus in the year 1700.

That is the whole of the source. It gives him no birthplace and no year of birth, names no monastery within that desert, no abbot and no companion, and tells nothing of how he died beyond who killed him. It prints no hymn and no epigram for him.

The desert it names is the Gareji desert, which carries the name of the venerable David who settled it; the by-name the page puts in brackets after Sios says as much. That is a place-name and not a companionship, so this folder claims no association with him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4402/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
