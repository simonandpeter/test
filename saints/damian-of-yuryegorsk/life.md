# Damian of Yuryegorsk

**Three names and two days, and the calendar explains the second day itself.** The Сретенский monastery’s calendar closes the Julian 20 November with «Прп. Дамиана (в схиме Диодора) Юрьегорского (1633).», and the name link behind it, name page 575, gives «Дамиан (в схиме Диодор) Юрьегорский, Каргопольский, прп.» and prints two days of memory, «Дни памяти: 20 ноября , 27 ноября». Its life page reverses the two names in its own title, «Преподобный Диодор (Дамиан) Юрьегорский», and gives him a third: his parents Иерофей and Мария called him Диомид. He was born in the village of Турчасово on the river Онега.

At fifteen he went on pilgrimage to the Соловецкий monastery and stayed there as a novice; at nineteen he was tonsured there by the abbot Антоний; he lived with hermits on the desert islands, and then withdrew to Водл-озеро, where he spent seven years in the ascetic struggle with his disciple Прохор. Resolving to found a monastery in the name of the Most Holy Trinity on Юрьева гора, he went to Moscow, where he got permission from the tsar Михаил Феодорович — whose years the page gives as «(1613 - 1645)» — and the money for the building from the tsar’s mother, the nun-elder Марфа.

Not long before his end he had to go to Каргополь on the business of the house. Taking leave of the brethren he foretold his own death soon; and he died and was buried at Каргополь, «(+ 27 ноября 1633 года)». Two years later his incorrupt body was carried to the Trinity house and buried on the south side of the cathedral church.

**The day this folder stands on is not the day he died, and the page says why.** «Память преподобного Диодора совершается 20 ноября, так как преставление совпадает с празднеством Божией Матери ради Ее иконы Знамения» — his memory is kept on 20 November because his repose falls together with the feast of the Mother of God for her icon of the Sign. So the `russian` row carries the 20th, which is the day page that listed him, and the 27th, the day of the death, is in the row’s note and in `dates`. The second epithet of the name page, «Каргопольский», is the place of the burial and not of the house he founded.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 20 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261120.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life2571.htm); read 6 October 2026.*
