# Charisius, with Nicephorus and Agapius

saint.gr names three martyrs together on this day — Charisius, [Nicephorus](/saints/nicephorus-martyr-1-march) and [Agapius](/saints/agapius-martyr-1-march) — and then says in one sentence that it knows nothing about them: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο των Αγίων», we have no details of the saints' lives. The line enumerates all three by name, so each is kept here as a folder of his own rather than as a company; but the whole of what is recorded of any of them is the name and the day.

The distich over the three is the only other thing on the page, and it counts rather than describes them: a triad of contenders now stands in heaven before the Trinity that created all things. There is no year, no country, no emperor, no judge, no manner of death, no relic and no hymn. Charisius alone of the three has an image on the site and no etymology block; the blocks the page prints are for the other two names.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3779/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
