# Strategius

saint.gr's calendar for 19 August names Strategius beside [Eutychian the soldier](/saints/eutychian-the-soldier), and the whole of what it says about the two of them is one sentence: «Οι Άγιοι Ευτυχιανός ο στρατιώτης και Στρατήγιος μαρτύρησαν δια πυρός» — the holy Eutychian the soldier and Strategius were martyred by fire. There is no year, no city, no emperor and no judge.

What the page adds is the pair of synaxarion distichs it prints, one for each man. The one for Strategius plays on his name, which is the word for a general: «Ὁ Στρατήγιος, Χριστὸν ὡς ὅπλον φέρων, Κατεστρατήγει καὶ πυρᾶς διηρμένης» — Strategius, bearing Christ as his weapon, out-generalled even the pyre when it was heaped up high.

The page gives him no life beyond this, no place, no relics and no hymn, and it does not say whether he was a soldier too; only the distich's pun stands between him and complete silence. The listing's own line for the entry puts him second, «Άγιοι Ευτυχιανός ο στρατιώτης και Στρατήγιος», while the page's own head line puts him first.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 19 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/19/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2216/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
