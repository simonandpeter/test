# The Seventy Martyrs slain with Rhipsime

The Greek synaxarion says only that the seventy martyrs were martyred with [Rhipsime](/saints/rhipsime), and sends the reader to her entry, which says that seventy men hiding in the parts near Ararat died with her, with [Gaiane](/saints/gaiane-companion-of-rhipsime) and the [thirty-two virgin martyrs](/saints/thirty-two-virgin-martyrs), in 292. Its verse says that seventy men died by the sword, ready to die many times over if need be. The Romanian life of Rhipsime and Gaiane counts seventy Christian men among those killed with them, and gives them no entry of their own.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 30 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/1020/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026; the Romanian life is at doxologia.ro — [the page](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfintei-mucenite-ripsimia).*
