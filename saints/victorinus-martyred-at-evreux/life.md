# Victorinus, martyred at Évreux

Maximus and Victorinus were born in the city of Brescia in Italy and were martyred in the city of Évreux in Gaul in 384. They were sent, saint.gr says, by Damasus I, bishop of Rome, whose years it gives as 366 to 384, to preach the Gospel to the idolaters; and according to the tradition — the page's own phrase, «Σύμφωνα με την παράδοση» — Maximus was a bishop and Victorinus a deacon.

They were seized by the idolaters and beheaded, and so, the page ends, they received the unfading crown of glory and of martyrdom. That is the whole of the entry: a city of birth, a city of death, one year, one sender, two ranks held only by tradition, and one manner of death. The page prints no synaxarion couplet and no hymn to either of them, and names no relics.
