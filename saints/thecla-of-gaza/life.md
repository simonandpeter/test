# Thecla of Gaza

Thecla and [Agapius](/saints/agapius-of-gaza) suffered for Christ at Gaza in Palestinian Caesarea in 304, the year and the city in which [Timothy](/saints/timothy-of-gaza) the teacher was burned: they were given to the beasts. That is all the Sretensky calendar’s life says of them; the Prologue keeps the three together for the Serbian calendar.

*After the life printed by the Sretensky calendar (days.pravoslavie.ru) — [the entry](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4479.htm); The Ohrid Prologue for 1 September 2026 (19. август ст. ст.), as printed by the Православни подсетник (pravoslavno.rs), tells it for the Serbian calendar — [the day’s page](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-09-01&prolog=1); read 23 August 2026.*
