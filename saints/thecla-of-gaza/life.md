# Thecla of Gaza

Thecla and [Agapius](/saints/agapius-of-gaza) suffered for Christ at Gaza in Palestinian Caesarea in 304, the year and the city in which [Timothy](/saints/timothy-of-gaza) the teacher was burned: they were given to the beasts. That is all the Sretensky calendar’s life says of them; the Prologue keeps the three together for the Serbian calendar.

The Romanian calendar keeps the three of them on 19 August, and doxologia.ro prints one short notice under all three names. It tells first of [Timothy](/saints/timothy-of-gaza), of Palestine by birth, perfect in life and in word, who was seized, stood at Gaza before Urban, declared himself on the Christians' side, was beaten and tortured in every way and at last put into the fire. Then it says that in the same city [Agapius](/saints/agapius-of-gaza) and Thecla of Byzia, holding to the faith of Christ, were given to the beasts and took the crown of martyrdom through them.

The Greek calendar keeps the three on the same day, 19 August, and sets the martyrdom in a history. saint.gr says they were martyred at Gaza, which was an ancient city of Canaan, and that from the first years of Christianity a church had been founded in that city which the idolaters fought continually — but the more they fought it, the more it grew, and more and more became Christians. From time to time great persecutions were raised against them.

In one such outbreak, by order of the eparch Ourbanos, Timothy was seized; and having been put to many hard and inhuman tortures he was thrown into the fire, where he was martyred. Seeing his martyrdom, Agapius and Thecla said that they too wished to be martyred for their Christ, and the idolaters threw them to the wild beasts. The page gives each of the three a synaxarion distich: Timothy's calls the furnace a censer carrying a most fragrant spice in its midst, Agapius's has him bearing the beast's bite while the soul-biting beast is bitten in the heart, and Thecla's speaks in her own voice — «Ἔμοιγε κλῆσις Θέκλα· πατρὶς Βιζύη. Γάζης τόπος θέατρον, ἆθλον θὴρ δάκνων» — my name is Thecla, my homeland Bizye; my place the theatre of Gaza, my contest a biting beast.
