# Psoes

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life. The whole of its notice is one sentence: «Ο Όσιος Ψόης απεβίωσε ειρηνικά προσευχόμενος.» The venerable Psoes departed in peace, at prayer. The page gives no year, no country, no monastery and no hymn, and it does not even offer the gloss on the name that it prints for most of the others of the day.

Above that sentence it sets the distich the Greek books give him, which turns his name into a weapon: «Mάστιξιν ευχών καν τέλει βίου Ψόης, / Παίει νοητάς δαιμόνων δεινάς ψόας» — with the scourges of his prayers, even at the end of his life, Psoes strikes the dread spiritual loins of the demons. That pun on ψόαι, the loins, and his own name is all the character the source gives him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 9 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/09/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2293/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
