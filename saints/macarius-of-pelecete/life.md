# Macarius

Macarius was born in Constantinople and lost his parents as a boy. He read the word of God with such zeal and was so filled with it that he resolved to give his whole life to God, and entered the monastery of Pelecete in Bithynia, whose igumen was then the famous ascetic Hilarion; after Hilarion’s death the brethren chose Macarius igumen with one voice. In the reigns of Leo V the Armenian (813–820) and Michael II the Stammerer (820–829) he suffered as a confessor for the veneration of the holy icons, and was exiled to the island of Aphousia, where he died around 830.
