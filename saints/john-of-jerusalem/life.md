# John of Jerusalem

saint.gr's calendar for 30 Μαρτίου names a holy John, patriarch of Jerusalem, and the page behind the line is a single sentence: he was one of the first patriarchs of Jerusalem, and he died peacefully. There is no year, no place of birth, no monastic house, no number in the succession, no council, no work, no relic and no hymn. Three Johns stand on this one Greek day — the author of the Ladder, the hermit of the well, and this patriarch — and only the see tells this one from the other two.

It is a thin record and it is left thin here. «From among the first patriarchs of Jerusalem» is a claim about where in a list he stands and not a date, and no century is written into this folder on the strength of it; the several early patriarchs of Jerusalem named John are not sorted out by this page and are not sorted out here either.

What the page prints besides is its couplet, which plays on the two Sions: «Ὁ τῆς Σιὼν πρόεδρος αἰσθητοῦ θρόνου, Πρὸς τὴν Σιὼν ἀπῆρε τὴν νοουμένην» — the president of Sion's visible throne departed to the Sion that is perceived by the mind. The four numbered notes under the name Ιωάννης are the site's encyclopaedia entry on the word, and none of them is this patriarch.
