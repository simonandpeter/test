# Anatolius (Ivanovsky)

The Russian calendar names him in a line of four and gives no life at all. Its 17 October old style has «Сщмчч. Неофита и Анатолия пресвитеров, прмчч. Иакинфа и Каллиста (1918)» — the hieromartyrs [Neophytus](/saints/neophytus-lyubimov) and Anatolius, presbyters, and the monk-martyrs [Hyacinth](/saints/hyacinth-pitatelev) and [Callistus](/saints/callistus-oparin), 1918 — and because the line names all four of them, each has a folder of his own. He is the second of the two presbyters.

His page in the calendar's index of names adds his surname: «Анатолий (Ивановский), иерей, сщмч.», Anatolius Ivanovsky, priest and hieromartyr. It gives the second day he is kept on, 26 January, among the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and then it stops. There is no life link on either page, and no account of him anywhere in the calendar.

So what is known here is a priest of the Russian church killed in 1918 and remembered by name on the Julian 17 October with three others who died in the same year. His number in the index of names stands far from the other three of the company, which means only that his entry was added to the calendar at a different time; nothing further is read out of it. Where he served and how he died the calendar does not say.

azbyka.ru is the source that carries lives for the new martyrs of the twentieth century, and it would not be read: asked for its page for this run of days, it answered 403 to the fetcher this corpus uses, on 28 October 2026. The refusal is recorded because it is the reason this page is short rather than a judgement about the man.

*After the Russian church calendar of the Sretensky monastery (days.pravoslavie.ru) for 17 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261017.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14519.html), which is all there is; read 30 October 2026. The same menologion day was read in the other three calendars and does not keep him — [saint.gr](https://www.saint.gr/10/17/index.aspx), [doxologia.ro](https://doxologia.ro/17-octombrie) and the [Ohrid Prologue](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-10-30&prolog=1).*
