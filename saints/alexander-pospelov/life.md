# Alexander (Pospelov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him among five presbyters on 13 декабря and gives no life for him. Its line is «Сщмчч. Владимира, Александра, Иакова, Алексия, Григория пресвитеров (1937).» — five hieromartyr presbyters, 1937 — and the calendar’s own name page adds the surname the line leaves out: «Александр (Поспелов), протоиерей, сщмч.»

That is the whole of the record for him. The one reading the entry links belongs to the first of the five and says nothing of the rest, so nothing of it is borrowed here. He was a priest; he died in 1937, in the years the Russian church counts its new martyrs. The day page carries troparia and kontakia for five of the memories it heads, and none under his name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 13 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261213.html); read 13 October 2026.*
