# Basiliscus the Elder

saint.gr's calendar for 29 Ιουλίου names him and gives no life. Its whole notice is one sentence: «Ο Άγιος Βασιλίσκος ο γέρων μαρτύρησε δια ξίφους» — Saint Basiliscus the elder was martyred by the sword. There is no country, no century, no emperor, no judge and no hymn, and the byname «ο γέρων», the old man, is all the page offers to tell him apart from the others of the name.

Besides that it prints the gloss on the name — ΒΑΣΙΛΙΣΚΟΣ, το βασιλόπουλο, the king's little son — and the couplet of the synaxarion, which plays the name against the serpent of the psalm: «Nαι Bασιλίσκε ψαλμικώς επ' ασπίδα, / Kαι βασιλίσκον βαίνε την τομήν φέρων» — yes, Basiliscus, in the psalm's own words tread upon the asp and the basilisk, bearing the cut of the sword.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 29 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/29/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/740/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
