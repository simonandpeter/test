# The Mother of Celsus

saint.gr's calendar for the eighth of January carries a line that gives no name at all: «Αγία Γυναίκα», the holy woman. Its notice is two sentences. She was the wife of the ruler who put [Julian](/saints/julian-of-antinoopolis) to death and the mother of [Celsus](/saints/celsus-son-of-marcian); she believed in Christ because of the wonders that happened during the tortures, and afterwards she was beheaded.

The page's two-line verse says the same thing again: pierced with longing for Christ, the mother of Celsus despised her husband and the torments. That is everything printed about her. She has no name here because the calendar gives her none, and none is invented; her husband is named in the notice of the company as Marcian, governor of Egypt, and her son and [Basilissa](/saints/basilissa-of-antinoopolis) and the rest are kept on the same day.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/124/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
