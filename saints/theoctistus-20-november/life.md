# Theoctistus (20 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 20 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιος Θεόκτιστος ο Ομολογητής», glosses the name as God's own creature, Θεός and κτίσμα, and gives him three lines. He lived in the ninth century, the page says. He suffered much at the hands of the iconoclasts for his struggles on behalf of Orthodoxy; but in the reign of the empress Theodora he was raised to the dignity of patrikios and honoured with many other distinctions besides.

The couplet set over the notice turns on the two senses of the word: the patrician who loved the laws of the Fathers is now ranked with the choirs of the Fathers. The page gives him no city, no year of birth or death, no see and no hymn, and prints no relics for him.
