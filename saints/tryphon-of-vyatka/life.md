# Tryphon of Vyatka

saint.gr's calendar for 8 Οκτωβρίου names Tryphon of Vyatka and then says, in one sentence, that nothing is known: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ρώσου Αγίου» — we have no details of the life of the Russian saint. The head of the page writes his dates as «(; - 1612)», with the year of his birth unknown, and that year of death is the whole of what the site records about him.

The page names no monastery, no office and no manner of death, and prints no hymn. The only other saint it sets beside him is Prokopios of Vyatka, the fool for Christ, whose page it links as a neighbour; saint.gr says nothing that joins the two beyond the city, so nothing of Prokopios is read into this folder. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4319/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
