# Constantine (Chekalov)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him first of fifteen on 21 октября ст. ст., at the head of a line of eleven priests, two deacons and two monks who died in 1937. The line's own form of his name is the genitive «Константина» with the rank word «Сщмчч.» at the head and «пресвитеров» after the eleventh of them; the title on the name link behind his place adds the surname and the grade, «Константин (Чекалов), иерей, сщмч.» — a priest, not an archpriest.

Ten of the fifteen have life pages on this calendar and he is not one of them: the name link behind his place carries no life, and the day page says nothing of him beyond his rank, his name and the company's year. The ten whose lives are given were all sentenced by the same NKVD troika on 1 November 1937 and shot on 3 November, which is 21 October old style, and that is what the day keeps them on; nothing on the page says whether this priest died with them or where.

Nothing further is written here — not a birth, not a parish, not a charge, not a place of death. The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line. The day page's troparia and kontakia all belong to other saints it heads and none to this company of 1937.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 21 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261021.html); read 4 October 2026.*
