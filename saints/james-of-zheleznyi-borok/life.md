# James of Zheleznyi Borok

saint.gr's whole account of him is three clauses: «Ο Όσιος Ιάκωβος του Ζελέζνιυ - Μπορόκ γεννήθηκε στο Γκάλιτς, στην περιοχή της Κοστρομά και ήταν υιός οικογένειας Βογιάρων. Εκάρη μοναχός στη μονή της Αγίας Τριάδος του Αγίου Σεργίου του Ραντονέζ και αργότερα ασκήτεψε σε μονή της Μόσχας. Κοιμήθηκε με ειρήνη το έτος 1442 μ.Χ.» — he was born at Galich, in the country of Kostroma, and was the son of a family of boyars; he was tonsured a monk in the monastery of the Holy Trinity of Saint Sergius of Radonezh and afterwards lived the ascetic life in a monastery of Moscow; and he fell asleep in peace in the year 1442.

The house he is named for is not in that account at all. Zheleznyi Borok stands in his name in the day's line and on the page of [James of Bryleyevo](/saints/james-of-bryleyevo), his disciple, whom the same calendar keeps on the same day and who is said there to have been a monk of the monastery of Zheleznyi Borok — spelt Ζέλεζνιυ-Μπορόκ in one clause of that page and Ζελενί-Μπορ in the next. Nothing is decided here between the readings.

Nothing else is told. The page gives no year of birth and no name in the world, does not say which monastery of Moscow he went to or whether he ever founded one, lists no relic, and prints neither couplet nor hymn.

**The Russian calendar keeps him on the same menologion day, and it is the calendar of his own church: it prints two lives of him, by different hands, where the Greek page this folder was written from printed a line and a year.** Both agree that he was born in the second half of the fourteenth century, which is the folder's first birth date, and both end on 11 April 1442, the day and the year, where saint.gr gave only the year. The second of the two adds that he was a son of the boyars Anosov, or Amosov, who held lands at Galich in the Kostroma country.

The two differ over how he came to [Sergius of Radonezh](/saints/sergius-of-radonezh), and the difference is recorded rather than resolved. The first says that after his parents' death he came to Sergius and ascetised some years in his monastery; the second says he came as a youth, received the monastic tonsure from Sergius himself, and lived some years in the Trinity house. In 1392 he withdrew to a place called Zhelezny Borok, by the iron workings on the bank of the little river Tebza, thirty versts from Galich, in dense forest, and lived there more than twenty years alone; God granted him the gift of discernment, lovers of silence began to gather, and in time a monastery formed.

The second life gives the one dated miracle. In 1415 Sophia Vitovtovna, wife of the grand prince Basil Dmitrievich, whose reign it dates 1389 to 1425, was gravely ill before her confinement, and the grand prince sent to ask James to pray for her and whether she would live. James told them to pray to the martyr [Longinus](/saints/longinus-the-centurion) and foretold the safe birth of a son, Basil. The grateful prince gave him the means to build a monastery on the place of his labours, with a church in the name of the prophet [John the Forerunner](/saints/john-the-baptist); and the page adds in parenthesis that in 1450 the grand prince Basil Vasilievich, after his victory over prince Dmitry Shemyaka, visited the house and prayed there in thanksgiving.

In 1429 the Kazan Tatars laid waste the country round Galich. James and his disciples hid in the deep forest, and came back to find the house in ruins. He rebuilt it, raised a church in the name of [Nicholas](/saints/nicholas-of-myra), and dug ponds with the brothers; the strict coenobitic rule was brought in after the pattern of the Trinity-Sergius monastery, and a great many hungry and poor people whom the Tatars had ruined were fed there. After many years of common labour the monks asked him to be their abbot; he submitted humbly, went to Moscow, and was vested in the sacred rank.

He died a very old man on 11 April 1442 and was buried in the Forerunner church of the house he had founded. **His relics were found on 5 May 1613**, and that is a second day this calendar keeps for him, printed on the life page as «5 мая (обретение мощей)»; one row per church is the schema, so it is recorded in the Russian row's note and he does not render on it.
