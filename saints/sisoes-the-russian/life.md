# Sisoes the Russian

saint.gr's calendar for 6 Ιουλίου names him and gives no life. The whole of its account is one sentence, «Ο Όσιος Σισώης ήταν Ρώσος μοναχός και κοιμήθηκε εν ειρήνη τον 18ο αιώνα μ.Χ. Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του» — the venerable Sisoes was a Russian monk and fell asleep in peace in the eighteenth century; we have no further details of his life. That is the whole of the page: no monastery, no province, no year, no deeds, no hymn and no synaxarion couplet.

The same day's listing carries a second entry of the name, [Sisoes the Great](/saints/sisoes-the-great), who is the ascetic of the Egyptian desert headed «(; - 429)» and has a page and an apolytikion of his own. The two stand as two separate lines of one day's calendar, and this one is the Russian monk of the eighteenth century.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/671/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
