# Martyrius, companion of Sisinnius

saint.gr's calendar for 29 Μαΐου prints the deacon Sisinnius with Martyrius and Alexander, and the life behind the line is two sentences. The holy martyrs Sisinnius the deacon, Martyrius and Alexander, it says, were martyred in the year 397 at Milan in Italy, by the idol-worshippers. Of their martyrdom, the page adds, [Ambrose of Milan](/saints/ambrose-of-milan), bishop of that city, and [Jerome](/saints/jerome-of-stridon) both make mention.

That is the whole of the source. It gives no birthplace, no age and no manner of death, names no judge and no emperor, and prints no hymn. It does name the three men separately, each with his own heading and his own rank, which is why each has his own folder here.

This folder is for Martyrius, whom the page names a martyr and nothing further. His companions [Sisinnius](/saints/sisinnius-of-milan), the deacon, and [Alexander](/saints/alexander-companion-of-sisinnius) have folders of their own, carrying the same life.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 29 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/29/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1668/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
