# Victoria of Cordoba

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names her and gives no life. Its line for the Julian 17 November reads «Мчч. Ацискла и Виктории, Кордубских (IV).», and the name link behind her place in it gives «Виктория Кордубская, мц.» — a title and a list of days with no text under it. There is no life page anywhere on that calendar for either of them, so what the source says is the whole of what stands here: a woman martyred at Cordoba in Spain in the fourth century, commemorated on this day.

The line names the two of them together, which is why each of these folders relates the other and why neither claims anything more. It does not say they were kin, nor how they died, nor under whom, and this corpus does not supply what a page withholds. [Acisclus of Cordoba](/saints/acisclus-of-cordoba) is kept here on the same day and out of the same line.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 17 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261117.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14540.html), which carries no life; read 5 October 2026.*
