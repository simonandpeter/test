# Genesius of Clermont

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives him two sentences. The line for 3 June reads «Άγιος Γενέσιος Επίσκοπος Κλερμόντ», Saint Genesius, bishop of Clermont, and the page behind it says this: Saint Genesius lived in the seventh century after Christ and was bishop of the city of Clermont; he was distinguished for the austerity of his life, for his love of men and for his love towards the poor and the suffering; and having shepherded his flock in a manner pleasing to God, he fell asleep in peace, in the year 662.

So what is recorded of him is a see, a century, a year, and three things said of his character — austerity in himself and kindness towards others. The page gives him no birthplace, no predecessor, no foundation and no wonder, and prints neither verse nor hymn. Its second sentence calls him venerable where its first called him a saint, and both words are left as the page has them.
