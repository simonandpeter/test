# Anna the Princess

The Greek calendar keeps a venerable Anna the Princess on 4 October. Its entry gives her death as 1056 and sends the reader to her life under 10 February, where its list for that day prints her again; the page itself carries no life, and nothing more is recorded here.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 4 Οκτωβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/1219/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
