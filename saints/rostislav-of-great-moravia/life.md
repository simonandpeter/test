# Rostislav of Great Moravia

The Russian church calendar keeps him on 11 мая ст. ст., the day it keeps the teachers of the Slavs, and the life it gives him is three sentences long. He was prince of Moravia and lived in the ninth century. He furthered the spread of Christianity among the Slavs by the holy Кирилл and Мефодий, and he was the first among his own people to show them high honour and a deep sympathy with their holy work. The day line closes him with the year 870, which is the only year the calendar gives him; the life itself says no more than the century.

That is the whole of what this source says. It does not say where he was born or where he died, how long he reigned, how the two brothers came to him or what became of his land afterwards; it does not name his kin, and it gives him no hymn of his own on a day whose page prints a troparion, two kontakia and a величание for the two teachers he received. The calendar calls him равноапостольный, equal to the apostles, which is the rank it gives those through whom a people was brought to the faith, and that rank is the reason his name stands on this day at all.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 11 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260511.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life6812.htm); read 8 October 2026.*
