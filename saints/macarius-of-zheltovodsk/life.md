# Macarius of Zheltovodsk

saint.gr's whole notice of him is one sentence: we have no details from the life of this Russian saint. The day's line names him «Όσιος Μακάριος Ζελτοβόδας ο Θαυματουργός», the venerable Macarius of Zheltovodsk the wonderworker, and the head of his page sets the years 1349 to 1444 beside the name; the page glosses the name Macarius as the happy one. Beyond that it prints no life, no country but the word Russian, no distich, no relics and no hymn.

Its heading gives the feast as fixed, kept on 25 July of every year, and the entry carries neither an asterisk nor the sentence by which saint.gr marks an entry it will not vouch for. The Greek calendar keeps a number of Russian saints in just this way — a name, a byname and a pair of years, and nothing else — and this folder says no more than the page does.

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar for 26 июня ст. ст., which keeps [Dionysius of Suzdal](/saints/dionysius-of-suzdal) on the day of the namesake whose name he bore in the world, names him in both of its readings as the teacher under whom this saint was brought up, «Учениками святого Дионисия были преподобный Евфимий Суздальский … преподобный Макарий Желтоводский». That is the whole of what it says of him here, and a related row is written on this folder for it.
