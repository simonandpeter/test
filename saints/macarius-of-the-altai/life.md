# Macarius of the Altai

saint.gr names him and says in so many words that it has nothing to tell. Its line for 16 Μαΐου reads «Άγιος Μακάριος των Αλταΐων», Macarius of the Altai; its head line gives him one date, «(; - 1847)», with the year of his birth left open; and the whole of the body under it is the sentence «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ρώσου Αγίου» — we have no details of the Russian saint's life.

So nothing is set down here beyond that: no birthplace, no parents, no office, no monastery, no manner of death, no wonder, no hymn and no relics. The page sends its reader on to the synaxis of all the saints who shone in Siberia, which names nobody in particular, and to a page of the Orthodox Church in America. He is the second such entry in two days, the calendar keeping Aretas of the Altai on 15 May in exactly the same words, and like that one he is recorded here as the Greek calendar records him: a name, a day, a country and one sentence of honest ignorance.

The Russian calendar keeps him on this day and prints no life for him; what it gives is the line «Прп. Макария Алтайского (1847)» and a name page that heads him Макарий Глухарев of the Altai, archimandrite. The page's own summary of the article it links says two things about him: that he founded the Altai spiritual mission, and that he translated the Holy Scriptures into the Altai language; and that he brought many souls to the true faith not only by ardent preaching but by the example of his own righteous life. The article was not opened here, because an essay on the site is not the calendar's own life page.

The same page lists three further days beside this one — 23 января for Kostroma, 10 июня for Siberia and 27 июля for Smolensk — which are the synaxes of the saints of those places and not feasts of his own. The rank of archimandrite is recorded here rather than in this folder's fields, because the word is not one the site's four languages carry.
