# John, companion of Peter

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαρτίου prints one line for a company of five and their companions: «Άγιοι Πέτρος, Μαρκιανός, Ιωάννης, Θέκλα, Κασσιανός οι Μάρτυρες και οι συν αυτών μαρτυρήσαντες» — the holy martyrs Peter, Marcian, John, Thekla and Cassian, and those who were martyred with them. John is named third. The page behind the line says two things about the five and stops.

The first is where they suffered: «Οι Άγιοι αυτοί Μάρτυρες Μαρτύρησαν στην Ρώμη» — these holy martyrs were martyred at Rome. The second is an admission that nothing more is known: «Δεν έχουμε περισσότερες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο και το μαρτύριο των Αγίων». There is no year, no emperor, no judge, no manner of death, no relic and no hymn, and the page keeps him only as a name inside a list.

He is recorded with [Peter](/saints/peter-companion-of-marcian), [Marcian](/saints/marcian-companion-of-peter), [Thekla](/saints/thekla-companion-of-peter) and [Cassian](/saints/cassian-companion-of-peter), and that companionship is the only thing that tells him from the many other saints of his name; the unnamed companions of the line have no folder. The four numbered notes the page attaches to the name Ιωάννης — the Forerunner, the son of Zebedee, Chrysostom, and many other saints, kings and hierarchs — are an encyclopaedia entry on the word, and none of them is this martyr.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3869/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
