# Timothy (5 November)

Timothy is the first of three martyrs saint.gr's calendar keeps in one entry on 5 Νοεμβρίου. The page says of all three that they lived in the years of the emperor Maximian, when the governor of Palestine was Urbanus — it sets the year 298 beside the governor's name — and that they were young men, of good presence. They were martyred, it says, by being beaten with fists until they died. That is the whole of the account: no country of their own, no trial, no burial, no year for the men themselves, and no hymn.

The couplet the Greek books give the three plays on Timothy's name, which is read as honouring God: «Τὸν Τιμόθεον, καὶ συναθλητὰς δύω / Κτείνουσι πυγμαί, καὶ Θεὸς τιμᾷ Λόγος» — fists kill Timothy and his two fellow-contenders, and God the Word honours him. The two kept with him are [Theophilus](/saints/theophilus-5-november) and [Theotimus](/saints/theotimus-5-november).

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2891/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
