# Simeon (Kulgavets)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him in one line with three other priests for 8 февраля ст. ст. — «Сщмчч. Симеона, Андрея, Сергия и Петра пресвитеров (1938).» — four hieromartyrs and presbyters who died in 1938. The name link behind his place in the line gives the calendar's own nominative, «Симеон (Кульгавец), иерей», which adds the surname the line leaves out.

The calendar prints no life page for him, and none is given here: the line and the name link are the whole of what it records. The three priests named with him are not set down as his companions in anything but the day; the line puts the four of them together and says nothing further about any of them.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 8 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260208.html); read 3 October 2026.*
