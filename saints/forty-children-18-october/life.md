# The Forty Children (18 October)

saint.gr's calendar for 18 October keeps «Άγια Σαράντα Παιδιά», the holy Forty Children, and its whole notice of them is one sentence: the holy Forty Children were martyred by the sword. The calendar names not one of the forty, gives them no country, no reign and no year, and prints no hymn for them.

What else the page has is the two-line verse over the entry, and it only turns the number over: a double score of children has died by the sword, honouring Christ who is double in essence. Because the source keeps them as a number and enumerates nobody, they stand here as one folder for the whole company.

The Greek calendar keeps another company of forty children, with Melasippus, Kassine and Antonius of Ancyra on 7 November, and the corpus holds that one already. These are a separate entry on a separate day, and nothing on either page joins them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2762/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
