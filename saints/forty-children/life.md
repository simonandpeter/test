# The Forty Children

saint.gr's calendar for 7 Νοεμβρίου keeps forty children with Melasippus, Cassine and their son Antonius of Ancyra, and all it tells of them is one sentence inside Antonius's passion. The duke Agrippinus had tortured the young man and then put him inside a lighted oven, and by the help of divine grace he stayed unharmed; and seeing that wonder, forty young children came to the faith of Christ, whom the king's servants beheaded at once.

The page names not one of them, gives no number of boys and girls, no city of their own beyond the one the family is given, and no year — the year 361, the time of Julian the Apostate, stands against the names of the three kept with them and not against theirs. It prints no distich for them and no hymn. Because the notice enumerates nobody, this is one entry and not forty.

The three kept with them are [Melasippus](/saints/melasippus), [Cassine](/saints/cassine) and [Antonius](/saints/antonius-son-of-melasippus), at whose deliverance from the fire they believed.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2934/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
