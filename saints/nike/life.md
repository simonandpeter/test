# Nike

saint.gr's whole account of her is two sentences. Nike was among those who came to believe in Christ from the wonders that the great martyr [George](/saints/george-the-trophy-bearer) worked in the course of his own glorious martyrdom, under Diocletian, whose reign the page dates here 284 to 304. Afterwards she suffered death by beheading, together with others, in the year 303.

Who the others were the page does not say — this is the fourth day running on which the Greek calendar sends somebody back to George's passion for the whole of their story, and she is the only one of her group that it names. It gives her no country, no city, no parentage, no judge and no relic, and it prints no hymn.

Its couplet plays on her name, which is the Greek word for victory: the awarder of victory gives the prizes of victory to Nike who was cut down, as to one who bears victory. That is everything the calendar has of her.
