# Mardarije of Libertyville

Mardarije was born at Podgorica on 22 December 1889, the son of Pero and Jela. He finished elementary school at Cetinje and began the lycée there, going on with it at Belgrade, and from there withdrew to the monastery of Studenica. In 1906, with the blessing of Bishop Sava (Barać) of Žiča, he received the monastic habit and was ordained deacon. He finished the seminary at Kishinev, where he published a collection of sermons, and went from there to Saint Petersburg, graduating from the Theological Academy in 1916.

In 1917 he was sent to America and appointed administrator of the Serbian Orthodox diocese for America and Canada, with its seat at Chicago. He built a monastery at Libertyville, Illinois, and in 1925 was elected the first bishop of the Serbian Orthodox Church for America. Gravely ill though he was, doxologia's page says, he was a tireless missionary.

He went to the Lord after ten years of pastorate, on 12 December 1935, and was buried at his own foundation, the monastery of Saint Sava at Libertyville. The Serbian Church canonised him in 2015, and his relics were found intact in 2017.
