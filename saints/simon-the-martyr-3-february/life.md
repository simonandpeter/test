# Simon (3 February)

saint.gr names him and gives no life. Its calendar for 3 February prints «Άγιοι Παύλος και Σίμων οι Μάρτυρες», and the page behind that line says only that the two were martyred by the sword, and then that no further details of their lives are held: «Δεν έχουμε περισσότερες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο των Αγίων Μαρτύρων». No homeland, no century, no persecutor, no trial and no hymn — the rank, martyr, and the manner of death are the whole of it.

What the page does print is a couplet about the silence itself: that Simon, with [Paul](/saints/paul-the-martyr-3-february), must be written down here so that the two of them, whose heads were struck off, do not go unnoticed. Whether he is one of the Simons some other calendar keeps on another day the page does not say, and no other calendar was read for him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/03/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1194/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
