# Simeon (Lileev)

He is the fourth of the six presbyters the Russian calendar keeps in one line on 30 September, all eleven of that line dated 1937. The day line prints only «Симеона»; the surname and the rank come from the site's index of names, which heads his page «Симеон (Лилеев), протоиерей, сщмч.» — Simeon the hieromartyr, archpriest. The calendar's own abbreviation on the day makes him a presbyter and its index makes him an archpriest, which is a difference of rank rather than of person.

The index keeps him here and on 26 January with the company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and prints nothing further: no birth, no diocese, no parish, no charge, no place of death. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026 even with a browser user-agent, so what is written here is what the calendar wrote.

*After the Russian church calendar's [30 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260930.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13809.html); read 4 October 2026.*
