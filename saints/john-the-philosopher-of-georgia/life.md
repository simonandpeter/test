# John the Philosopher

saint.gr's calendar for 10 Φεβρουαρίου names Saint John the Philosopher, and the whole of what its page for him says is one sentence: «Ο Άγιος Ιωάννης, ο επιλεγόμενος Φιλόσοφος λόγω των εξαιρετικών σπουδών του στη θεολογία και φιλοσοφία, έζησε κατά τα τέλη του 11ου και αρχές του 12ου αιώνα μ.Χ. στη Γεωργία. Κοιμήθηκε οσίως με ειρήνη» — John, called the Philosopher on account of his exceptional studies in theology and philosophy, lived at the end of the eleventh and the beginning of the twelfth century in Georgia, and fell asleep in holiness and peace.

So the epithet, the two disciplines, the country, the turn of those two centuries and the peaceful end are the whole of what the Greek calendar keeps of him. Nothing else is printed: no parentage, no monastery, no year, no hymn, no relics and not even a distich, and the page adds only its usual gloss on the name Ιωάννης and its list of the great Johns the name recalls.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/10/index.aspx) and [his page](https://www.saint.gr/1215/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
