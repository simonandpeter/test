# Valerian (25 October)

doxologia.ro's calendar names Valerian for 25 octombrie and gives no life. His page has no Viață tab and no Tropar tab, and carries no notice of any kind: the name, the date, and the readings appointed for the day are the whole of it.

Those readings are the ones the Romanian calendar appoints for a martyr — the Apostle from II Timothy 2, where Timothy is told to be strong in the grace that is in Christ Jesus and to endure hardness as a good soldier, and the Gospel from John 15 and 16, where the Lord tells the disciples that the world will hate them as it hated Him, and that the hour comes when whoever kills them will think he does God service.

So what is recorded of him is the name, the rank the calendar's line gives him — martyr — and the day. There is no place, no century, no companion named, and no hymn. Nothing here should be read as more than that.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 25 October, and pairs him with another martyr. saint.gr's whole narrative is one sentence — «Οι Άγιοι Ουαλλέριος (ή Oυαλλερίνος) και Χρυσάφιος (ή Xρύσαφος) μαρτύρησαν διά ξίφους», they were martyred by the sword — and the couplet over it says that for the two contestants the punishment was a sword, and that to men who led the way the punishment was a delicacy. The parentheses are the page's own, and they are the reason a separate entry on the same day's listing, «Άγιος Ουαλλερίνος», is taken here for the same man rather than another. His companion is [Chrysaphus](/saints/chrysaphus).

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 25 octombrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/25-octombrie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-valerian-1), which prints no life; read 24 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 25 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/25/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2804/saint.aspx), read 1 October 2026.*
