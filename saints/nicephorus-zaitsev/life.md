# Nicephorus (Zaitsev)

The Russian church calendar names him and gives no life. Its list for 8 мая ст. ст. reads «Мч. Никифора (1942)» — the martyr Nicephorus, with the year of his death — and the name page behind that line carries one thing more than the day gives: his surname. It heads him «Никифор (Зайцев), мч.», and then prints his days, «26 января (Новомуч.), 8 мая», and nothing else at all.

So what the source says of him is this: that his family name was Зайцев, that he died in 1942, that he is numbered among the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, whose synaxis is the January day, and that he was a martyr and not a priest — the abbreviation it gives him is «мч.» and not «сщмч.», which is a distinction this calendar keeps carefully. There is no birth year, no place, no trade, no diocese, no account of his arrest or of how he died, and the day page prints no troparion or kontakion under his name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 8 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260508.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14418.htm), which gives no life; read 8 October 2026.*
