# George of Kratovo

The holy neomartyr George was born in the town of Kratovo in Serbia, of pious parents, Demetrius and Sarra. From a very young age he gave himself to the study of the divine word, and later learned the goldsmith's craft. He lost his father young, and fearing that his good looks might get him carried off to the court of the sultan Bayezid II, he came into Bulgaria and stayed at Sofia near a priest named Peter, who provided him every means of being taught the sacred letters; and so he lived a life pleasing to God and ascetic.

The Turks tried to make him a Muslim, and for that purpose employed an experienced Muslim teacher, who as a pretext for approaching George brought him gold to make a piece of jewellery. In their dealings George proved that the only true faith is the Christian one and convicted the Muslim religion of falsehood, and for that he was taken before the judge. Despite flatteries, promises and threats the martyr stayed firm in his fathers' piety.

The priest Peter visited him in prison, embraced him and said: rejoice, George, today you have glorified Christ as the protomartyr Stephen once did, and the new Stephen in the time of the iconoclasm, and many other saints, for you have done a like work. He was brought before the judge again, who promised to adopt him and give him countless riches and glory if he would deny Christ, and again he declared his readiness to be martyred and confessed Christ.

Then the judge, giving way to the pressure of the mob, handed the martyr over to the raging crowd, who bound him and paraded him through the streets of the city. So Saint George was shown to be a martyr of the faith, and suffered death by fire in the year 1515 at Sofia in Bulgaria.
