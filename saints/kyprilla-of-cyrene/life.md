# Kyprilla of Cyrene

saint.gr's calendar for 4 Ιουλίου keeps three women of Cyrene in one line — Kyprilla, Aroa and Loukia — and its life page tells each of them apart. Kyprilla, it says, came from the same homeland as [Theodore](/saints/theodore-of-cyrene), bishop of Cyrene, that is from Cyrene itself. She married, and lived with her husband only two years, for he died; and so she remained a widow for twenty-eight years.

Because she suffered dreadful headaches she went to the holy Theodore, who was then in prison for the faith of Christ, and he healed her. From that time Kyprilla stayed there together with [Loukia](/saints/loukia-companion-of-kyprilla) and [Aroa](/saints/aroa-companion-of-kyprilla) and served the saint. After Theodore's martyric end she too was denounced to the governor; and because they could not make her sacrifice to the idols they hung her on a piece of wood, where they tore her flesh, and so she gave up her blessed soul.

Her honourable relic Loukia and Aroa received and buried with honours, and later the governor Dignianus put both of those women to death as well. That is all saint.gr says of the three. It prints no hymn for any of them, only the synaxarion couplets: «Tονοί Kυπρίλλαν προς πάλην την προς ξέσεις, / O συμπαλαίσας Iακώβ Θεός πάλαι» — the God who once wrestled with Jacob braces Kyprilla for her struggle against the scrapers.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 4 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/04/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1990/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
