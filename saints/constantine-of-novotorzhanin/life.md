# Constantine of Novotorzhanin

saint.gr says at the outset that there is not enough hagiological material for Constantine the fool for Christ's sake. What it does give is this: he lived in Russia, in the district of Novotorzhanin, in the sixteenth century, and he fell asleep in peace. It adds no parentage, no place of tonsure, no account of the folly he took on himself, no miracle and no relics.

It does say where a reader should go for more. Various hagiological particulars about him, the page says, are given by Archbishop Dimitri Sambikin in his Menologion and Paterikon of Tver. saint.gr prints no synaxarion couplet and no hymn to him, and this folder records only what the page itself holds.
