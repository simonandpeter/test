# Charalampus (31 May)

Doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 31 mai reads «Sfântul Mucenic Haralambie», the holy martyr Charalampus, and the page the calendar links for him carries no Viață tab and no Tropar tab: it prints his name, the date, the readings appointed, and links to devotional articles about healings. Nothing on it says where he lived, or when, or how he died.

The rank in the line is the whole of what is stated: a man who died a martyr, kept on 31 May. He is kept apart here from the hieromartyr whom the same calendar prints on 10 februarie as «Sfântul Sfințit Mucenic Haralambie», on a different day, with a different rank and on a page of his own; nothing read here makes the two one man, and nothing read here makes them two, so this folder holds only what the 31 May line states. No place and no year is recorded, because the source gives none.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and gives what the Romanian line lacked. The holy martyrs Eusebius and Charalampus, saint.gr says, came from Nicomedia, and it is unknown when they were martyred. Because they were Christians they were arrested, and refusing to sacrifice to the idols they suffered a martyr's death upon the pyre together with the holy martyrs Romanus, Teletius — or Meletius — and Christina, whom the same calendar keeps on 30 May. The distich printed over the two of them says that two contenders, burnt with the fire of zeal, are not made dizzy at all by the fire that burns.
