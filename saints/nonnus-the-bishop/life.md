# Nonnus

saint.gr's calendar for 10 Νοεμβρίου keeps Nonnus, and names him by what he did for somebody else: the catechist of the venerable Pelagia. He was a bishop, the page says, and preached the divine word at Antioch, and so drew to the road of God [Pelagia](/saints/pelagia-of-antioch), who was then a harlot and whose memory it puts on 8 October. Having been catechised as she needed, she came to hate the works of darkness and fled to Jerusalem, and on the Mount of Olives finished her life in repentance and ascesis.

Of Nonnus himself the page says nothing further — no see, no century, no emperor, no manner of death, no year — and prints no hymn. Its couplet calls him full of the knowledge of God and able to turn even the worse things to good: «Ὅλος γέμων ἦν γνώσεως Θεοῦ Νόννος, / Εἰς καλὸν εἰδὼς καὶ τὰ χείρονα τρέπειν».

The Ohrid Prologue gives him the years this folder did not have and a see before the one it names him for. He had become famous as a great ascetic in the monastery of Tabenna in Egypt, and for that was chosen bishop in 448, for the diocese of Edessa; later he passed to the diocese of Heliopolis. There, it says, he turned thirty thousand Arabs to the faith of Christ.

After the death of the bishop of Edessa he returned to that diocese and stayed there until his death, which it puts in 471. Both years are new to this folder, which carried no dates at all before this reading. It closes on the one thing the Greek page had already given him: by his prayers the notable sinner [Pelagia](/saints/pelagia-of-antioch) was turned to the faith of Christ, she who afterwards became renowned for the holiness of her life, and it sends the reader to its own 8 October for her.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/10/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2966/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026; and the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 10. новембар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2025-11-23&prolog=1), read 7 October 2026.*
