# Apollonius, senator of Rome

The Охридски пролог gives him three sentences and they are the whole of this folder. He was a Roman senator. Accused for his faith in Christ, he confessed that faith before the whole Senate; and for that he was beheaded with the sword, at Rome, in the year 186.

That is all of it, and nothing has been added here. The entry names no accuser and no judge, does not say under which emperor he stood or what was said in the Senate, and gives him no year of birth, no family and no place of burial. No account of a Roman senator of that name has been imported from memory to fill it out, and no other calendar was read for him, so this folder claims nothing either way about the Russian, the Romanian or the Greek church.

He is not any of the nine other bearers of the name this corpus holds. Not one of them is a senator, not one of them stands at Rome, and not one carries this year: two are bare Greek lines of July with no dates, one stands with Proclus in February, one is a bishop of Brescia, one is of Sardis, one belongs to a company of twenty-four who died in 301, one is a hermit of the fourth century, one is a reader with a floruit of 290 whose Serbian name form is the same string as this folder’s, and the last is a Russian new martyr of the twentieth century.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 29. новембар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2025-12-12&prolog=1); read 8 October 2026.*
