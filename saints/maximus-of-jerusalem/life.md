# Maximus of Jerusalem

The holy Maximus became Patriarch of Jerusalem in the year 333, saint.gr says, and succeeded on that throne the Patriarch Macarius, who had held it from 314. In the years before, under the emperor Maximinus, who reigned from 307 to 313, when the Christians were being persecuted, he had been condemned to the mines; and there they put out his right eye and maimed his left foot.

In 335 he took part in the Synod of Tyre, but he did not sign the condemnation of [Athanasius the Great](/saints/athanasius-of-alexandria); and in 349 he received with great joy that same Athanasius, who was then returning from exile. Maximus fell asleep in peace in the year 350.

That is the whole of what the page holds. It gives no year and no place for his birth, names no family, says nothing of his election, nothing of what he built or wrote in the seventeen years of his patriarchate, and nothing of where he was buried; it records no relics, and prints neither apolytikion nor kontakion for him.
