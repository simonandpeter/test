# Doulas of Cilicia

saint.gr's calendar for 15 June names Doulas and gives him a short life. He came, the page says, from a village of Praetoriada in Cilicia called Zephyri, and he was marked out for his works of charity, for his zeal for Christ, and for his unceasing effort to light other souls upon the road of true salvation. He was denounced for this to the eparch Maximus, and when they could not bend his Christian mind they put him to fearful tortures.

They poured scalding oil over his head, laid red-hot irons on his back and crushed his limbs, without winning from him the least yielding of his faith or of his courage. Then the eparch in his brutality tore open his belly, so that his entrails poured out; and half dead as he was they forced him to run some twenty miles, until the saint fell dead and his holy soul flew away to the tabernacles of the righteous, victor over the arbitrary tyranny of the idolaters. The page gives no year, names no emperor and prints no hymn.
