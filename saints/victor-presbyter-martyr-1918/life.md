# Victor

The calendar prints no surname for him: he was the priest of the parish neighbouring Podosinovets in the Vologda diocese, and on 24 September 1918 the punitive detachment that had come for archpriest Nicholas (Podyakov) was holding him in the village soviet. When they had tortured Father Nicholas to death in the hayfield by the river, they led Father Victor to the pit and ordered him to sing the funeral over the murdered priest. He sang it to the end, and as it finished one of the executioners shot him in the back of the head.

In the spring his sons came, and together with Father Nicholas's children begged the authorities' leave to bury the two priests properly; they took Father Victor's body home to the parish where he had served. He was glorified among the New Martyrs and Confessors of Russia in 2000.

*After the Православный церковный календарь (days.pravoslavie.ru), [Священномученики Николай (Подьяков) и Виктор](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4866.htm); read 30 August 2026.*
