# Juliana, mother of Saturninus

saint.gr's calendar for 22 Ιουνίου names her and gives no life. The whole of its account of the two of them is one sentence, «Η Αγία Ιουλιανή και ο γιος της Σατορνίνος μαρτύρησαν δια πυρός» — the holy Juliana and her son [Saturninus](/saints/saturninus-son-of-juliana) were martyred by fire. The page gives no country, no city, no century, no year, no emperor and no judge, and prints no hymn under their line.

Besides that sentence it carries only the couplet the synaxaria print for them: «Ἰουλιανὴν καὶ τὸ ταύτης παιδίον, / Ἴουλον ἀνθοῦν ἄρτι πῦρ καταφλέγει» — the fire burns up Juliana and her child, whose first down was only just in flower, which is as near as the source comes to telling us how young the boy was. That is everything it says of either of them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 22 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/22/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/611/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
