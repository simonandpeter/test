# Fevronia of Murom

[Peter](/saints/peter-of-murom) was the second son of Yuri Vladimirovich, prince of Murom, and came to the throne of Murom in 1203. Some years before, he had fallen ill with leprosy, which no one could cure; in a dream it was revealed to him that he could be healed by the devout maiden Fevronia, a beekeeper’s daughter, a peasant of the village of Laskovaya in the Ryazan land. He sent his men to the village, and when he saw her he so loved her for her piety, wisdom and goodness that he vowed to marry her when he was healed; she healed him and married him, and the holy couple carried their love for each other through every trial. The proud boyars would not have a peasant woman as princess — the Sretensky calendar’s life, read as far as that, tells the rest of the well-known story: exile together, return, the monastic tonsure of both as David and Euphrosyne, and their death on the same day. The Russian calendar keeps on 31 August (Julian) the translation of their relics in 1992; their principal feast is 25 June.
