# Abraham of Pechenga

saint.gr keeps Abraham in one line with [Copris](/saints/copris-of-pechenga), and gives the two of them two sentences. The venerable fathers Abraham and Copris lived the ascetic life about the year 1485 in the monastery of the Transfiguration of the Saviour at Pechenga-Grazovesk. They fell asleep in peace, and their holy relics were carried to the small town of Vladimirskoye of Pechenga, in the Vologda region of Russia.

The page adds, in its own note on relics, that part of Abraham's relics is in the church of the village of Vladimirsko-Pechengsky in Vologda. Of everything else — where he was born, who tonsured him, which of the two was the elder, what year either died in — it says nothing, and it prints no hymn and no verses.
