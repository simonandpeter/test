# Peter of Tobolsk

saint.gr names Peter, metropolitan of Tobolsk, on this day, and then says in one sentence that it knows nothing about him: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ρώσου Αγίου» — we have no details of the Russian saint's life. What the page does carry is in its heading, which dates his repose to 1820, and in the day's own line, which gives him his see.

So three things stand recorded and no more: the name, the see of Tobolsk in Siberia, and the year. There is no place of birth and no year for it, no monastery, no predecessor or successor, no deed as metropolitan, no manner of repose, no relic, no distich and no hymn; and the body of the page repeats not even the year its own heading gives.

What the site sets beside his name is the synaxis of all the saints who shone in Siberia, and that is a company and not a person, so it is not kept here. Of the many men of this name the corpus holds, none stands on this day and none is named on this page.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 4 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/04/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4253/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
