# John of Chaldia

saint.gr says that father John Triantaphyllides was born on 10 February 1836 in the village of Loria, also called Mouzena, in the province of Trebizond, of devout parents, Triantaphyllos and Kyriaki. Because there was no school in his own country he learned his letters from a literate man in six months, being very quick-witted. At fourteen he was left fatherless, and so had to go abroad to find work along the coasts of Pontus, where he worked in a bakery in the winter and at farm work in the summer. At seventeen he married a modest and devout young woman named Helen, by whom he had a son and daughters.

One summer, the page says, he and his wife were walking to his village when they met three angels in the form of men on the road. John was walking ahead; the angels looked at him carefully but did not speak to him. Then they met his wife, and the first said to her: your villagers are waiting for John to become a priest; this is God's will. The second said: after thirty years you will be granted to venerate the Holy Places. And the third: after his repose he will be numbered with the saints. Helen asked in astonishment how they, being men, could know the future, what would happen after thirty years; and they answered that they were not men but angels of God, and had come for that.

The head of the page brackets him «(1836 - 1903)» and calls him the new Chrysostom and the merciful. The page names its source for all of it: what is known of Saint John Triantaphyllides, the new merciful one, was preserved by his grandson Spyridon Triantaphyllides in manuscript notes in katharevousa — Spyridon had been a schoolmaster at Trebizond and fell asleep at Thessalonica in 1942 — and Spyridon's son John gave them to Panagiotis Klementides, who made them over for the writing of the book «Ασκητές μέσα στον κόσμο».

Its apolytikion and kontakion both name him and both speak of healings drawn from his relics, and its megalynarion calls him a divine spring of Chaldia and a newly-lit luminary.
