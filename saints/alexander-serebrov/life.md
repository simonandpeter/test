# Alexander (Serebrov)

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him eighth of twenty-four and gives no life; the life here is the one azbyka.ru prints for the civil 2 декабря, out of pstgu.ru and the Orthodox Encyclopaedia. Alexander Alexandrovich Serebrov was born in 1868 in the village of Klyuchishchi in the Buguruslan district of the province of Samara. He finished the seminary and was in holy orders from 1889, and lived at Ulyanovsk. On 24 June 1937 he was exiled to Kazakhstan for five years and was a workman at the Maysky mine in the Beskaragay district of the Pavlodar region.

He was arrested on 25 November 1937 with the other clergy exiled to that mine — the archimandrites Gregory (Rebeza) and Benjamin (Zykov), the archpriests John Malinovsky and Constantine Mikhailovsky, the priests Alexander Mishutin, Ignatius Teslin, Simeon Krivosheev, John Piramidin, John Florovsky and Nicholas Amasiysky, and Valentine Kornienko — and charged with counter-revolutionary agitation. On 1 December all of them but the priest Nicholas Amasiysky were sentenced, and the next day they were shot. The Council of Bishops of 2000 glorified him among the new martyrs and confessors of Russia.
