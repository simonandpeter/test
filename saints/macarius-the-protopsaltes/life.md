# Macarius the Protopsaltes

Macarius the Protopsaltes — a translator and composer of church chant and a zealous patriot, the Romanian life says, and at the same time a humble monk advanced in the spiritual life, a good servant of the Church of Christ and a distinguished speaker — is counted the founder of Romanian psaltic music, because he translated the church chants from the Greek, fitting them to the singing of the Romanian people and making them easy for all to understand. He was born in 1763 and grew up in a devout family; the life, read only so far, goes on to his work and his end. The Romanian calendar keeps him on 31 August, marked as a feast of the day, with a troparion and a kontakion.

*After the Viețile Sfinților as printed by doxologia.ro — [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-cuvios-macarie-protopsaltul); read 23 August 2026.*
