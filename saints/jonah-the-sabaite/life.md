# Jonah the Sabaite

Jonah was a presbyter in the year 829, and the father of the confessors Theodore and Theophanes, called the Graptoi, whose faces the iconoclast Theophilus had cut with writing. He went to the Lavra of Saint Sabbas and became a monk, grew in reverence towards God and in every Christian virtue, and reached a great age before he died in peace. The verse says he left the place of an unsteady life and found a place that stands firm.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 21 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/1010/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
