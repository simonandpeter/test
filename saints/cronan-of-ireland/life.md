# Cronan of Ireland

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives him one sentence. The line for 3 June reads «Όσιος Κρονανός o εξ Ιρλανδίας», the venerable Cronan of Ireland, and the page behind it says this and no more: the venerable Cronan lived in the seventh century after Christ and belonged to the company of Saint [Kevin](/saints/kevin-of-ireland), whom the same day keeps; he fell asleep in peace, in the year 617.

So what is recorded of him is a country, a century, a companion, a death in peace and a year — and that the year is one before the year the same calendar gives Kevin, who outlived him. The page names no monastery, though Kevin's names the region of the Two Lakes; it gives Cronan no office, no wonder and no relic, and prints neither hymn nor verse for him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/03/index.aspx) and [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/4415/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
