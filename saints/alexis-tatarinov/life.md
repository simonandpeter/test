# Alexis (Tatarinov)

The new martyrs of the Slobodskaya country of Ukraine, near Kharkov, are kept together on 19 Μαΐου. saint.gr names twenty-seven of them at the head of one page and then gives each man a single sentence of his own: a name in the world, a patronymic and a surname, in most cases a birthplace and a birth date, and the month and the year in which he was killed. They are hieromonks, protopresbyters, deacons and priests of the Kharkov country, and all but one of them died between 1937 and 1941.

The page tells no story of their arrest or of their end. It records what a list can record, and it records it man by man, so that the company is a company of names and dates rather than of a shared narrative. Its own head lines and its body do not always agree about a year, and where they differ the difference is set down here rather than resolved. saint.gr prints no hymn for them and names no place where they were buried.

The sentence saint.gr gives him in that list is this: «Ο Άγιος Ιερομάρτυς Αλέξιος Νικολάεβιτς Ταταρίνωφ, ο οποίος γεννήθηκε στις 30 Μαρτίου 1885 στο χωριό Κούνε της επαρχίας του Χάρκωβ και μαρτύρησε τον Δεκέμβριο του 1937.» — the holy hieromartyr Alexis Nikolayevich Tatarinov, who was born on 30 March 1885 in the village of Kune in the province of Kharkov and was martyred in December 1937. That is the whole of what the page records of him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 19 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/19/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1641/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
