# Acholius of Thessalonica

Acholius — or Ascholius, as saint.gr also spells him — lived and worked in the years 360 to 383. He came of Caesarea, though the page does not rule out, on other testimonies, that he came of Achaia, since in those he is mentioned as a monk in Achaia; and it remarks that if that information is exact then the monastic life must already have been established in Achaia in the fourth century.

He took part in the Second Ecumenical Council, and he was marked out, the page says, for the holiness of his life and his Orthodox spirituality. A letter written to him calls him a luminary of the Church and praises him. He fell asleep in peace between the years 383 and 384. No year of his birth is printed, and no hymn.
