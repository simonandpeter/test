# Nicephorus (Kuchin)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 4 апреля ст. ст. beside one companion, «Прмчч. Вениамина и Никифора (1928).», and the name link behind the line, page 9232, gives his grade and his surname: «Никифор (Кучин), иеромонах, сщмч.» The calendar has no life for him. azbyka.ru does, inside the life it gives to the two of them together, and the paragraph that is his own is short.

In the world he was Николай Иванович Кучин, born in the Сольвычегодский uyezd of Vologda province; azbyka gives him no year of birth and names no parents. He served in the army and rose to the rank of unter-officer, and after leaving it entered the Solovki monastery in 1909. He was tonsured there in 1913 and given the name Nikifor, finished five classes of the brothers’ school, kept the store of old clothing as his obedience, and was ordained hieromonk.

When the persecution of the Church began he became the closest helper of [Benjamin (Kononov)](/saints/benjamin-kononov), the abbot of the house, and the rest of what is told of him is told of the two of them at once: the hiding of the church silver in the walls of the cathedral and over the altar of the Nicholas church, the arrest at the end of 1920 for concealing the monastery’s valuables and keeping arms, the timber workings at Kholmogory, the release in 1922, the Arkhangelsk metochion and the pharmacist who took them in, and then the forest.

In the summer of 1926 the two of them built a cell in deep forest forty versts from the nearest village, on the high bank of a lake, and lived there nearly two years on a kitchen garden and the fish they caught. On the evening of 17 April 1928, the Tuesday of Bright Week, two men who had got the way to the cell from local hunters came to the windows while the paschal service was being kept inside, waited for the light to go out, and fired at where they knew the beds to stand; then, afraid to go in, they robbed the loft, found kerosene there, poured it on the walls and set them alight, and heard cries from inside, the two priest-monks being still alive.

Their friend found the burnt cell and two charred skeletons on 9 June 1928. The calendar’s line closes «(1928)». The hymns azbyka prints are for the two of them together, a troparion in глас 5 and a kontakion in глас 7, and both name them both. azbyka lists the moveable synaxis of the New Martyrs on 7 February and the synaxis of the Arkhangelsk metropolia on 1 November beside this day; both are synaxes and neither is written into the feast.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 4 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260404.html); and the life azbyka.ru’s calendar for the civil 17 April serves — [the life](https://azbyka.ru/days/svv-veniamin-kononov-nikifor-kuchin); read 5 October 2026.*
