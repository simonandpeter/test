# The Two Leontii of Athos

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Οκτωβρίου names two venerable men of one name on the Holy Mountain, and then says that it has nothing to tell of either: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο των Οσίων» — we have no details of the lives of the venerable ones. Its own list of those celebrating prints the same line twice over, «Όσιος Λεόντιος ο εν Άθω» and «Όσιος Λεόντιος ο εν Άθω», and gives no epithet, no monastery, no century and no other word by which one of them could be told from the other.

Because the page distinguishes them in no way, this folder is kept for the two of them together rather than split into two folders that would have to invent the difference between them. There is no year, no house on the Mountain, no couplet and no hymn; and nothing in the corpus's Leontius folders answers to a pair kept on this day.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2657/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
