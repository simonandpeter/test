# Damian of Esphigmenou

Damian lived in the thirteenth century and holds an important place, doxologia's life says, among the saints of the monastery of Esphigmenou on the Holy Mountain. Wanting to be a monk while he was still young, he went to Athos, to the Garden of the Mother of God, and took up a life of hard spiritual labour on Mount Samaria, which lies between the monasteries of Esphigmenou and Hilandar, near the cave where [Anthony of the Kyiv Caves](/saints/anthony-of-the-caves) had struggled before he founded the Lavra of the Caves at Kyiv.

It is told of him that he went once to [Cosmas of Zographou](/saints/cosmas-of-zographou) and stayed so long that the sun had set. Cosmas asked him to spend the night there, but Damian would not, for it was his habit to pass the night in his own cell. Halfway back a storm came on so heavily that he could see nothing and stood in danger; and lifting the eyes of his mind to the Mother of God, he prayed to be delivered out of it. While he was still praying he found himself caught up and set down at the door of his own cell.

He fell asleep in peace on 23 February in the year 1280, and for forty days after his passing a fragrance beyond telling was felt through the whole valley around the monastery. The page prints no hymn for him.
