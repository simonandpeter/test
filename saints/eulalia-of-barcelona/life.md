# Eulalia of Barcelona

Eulalia lived in Spain near the city of Barcino — Barcelona — and was brought up by her parents in the Christian faith and piety; at fourteen she was already living a secluded life in her parents’ house, with some girls of her age, in prayer, the reading of Scripture and handiwork. In the persecution under Diocletian and Maximian the governor Dacian came to Barcino to destroy the Christians; hearing of it the girl slipped out of the house at night and by morning was in the city, where, pushing with difficulty through the crowd, she boldly rebuked the judge for forcing people to worship idols — where is your Christ now, to save you, he asked her under the torches, and she answered, he is here with me, but you cannot see him for your uncleanness. She was stripped and beaten with rods, bound to a tree in the shape of a cross and burned with candles, and gave her spirit to God in great torment; those present saw a white dove come from her mouth, and snow fell suddenly and covered her naked body like a white garment; on the third day Felix came and wept before the hanging body, and a smile appeared on the dead face. Her parents and the other Christians buried her, at the beginning of the fourth century.
