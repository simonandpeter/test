# Theodotus of Ephesus

saint.gr's calendar for 12 October names him and gives no life. Its whole notice is one sentence: «Ο Άγιος Θεόδοτος ήταν επίσκοπος Εφέσου και απεβίωσε ειρηνικά» — Saint Theodotus was bishop of Ephesus and died peacefully. That is the see and the manner of his end, and nothing else: no year, no birthplace, no account of his episcopate, no relic and no hymn.

Beside it the page prints a two-line verse of the kind the synaxaria set over each name — that Theodotus, having left an earthly throne, found the bright throne of the glory above — and a note on the name itself, that Theodotus is from Θεός and δοτός and means God-given, the grace and the gift of God. The line above that, about two Greek kings of Bactria, is the site's remark on who else has borne the name and says nothing about this saint.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2717/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
