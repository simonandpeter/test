# Zeno (22 August)

Doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 22 august reads «Sfântul Mucenic Zinon» — the holy martyr Zeno — and the page that line links to carries neither a Viață tab nor a Tropar tab. No place, no persecution, no emperor and no year is printed beside the name.

The day on which the Romanian calendar keeps him is the day of Agathonicus of Nicomedia and his company, and Zeno stands in that company in other calendars; but doxologia's own life of Agathonicus, which it prints under four of those names, does not name a Zeno among them, so no such connection is asserted here. Nor is he identified with the Zeno of Nicomedia the corpus already holds, who belongs to a different company on a different day. He is entered on the Romanian line alone.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 22 august — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/22-august) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-zinon), which is empty of both life and hymn; read 24 September 2026.*
