# Zeno (22 August)

Doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 22 august reads «Sfântul Mucenic Zinon» — the holy martyr Zeno — and the page that line links to carries neither a Viață tab nor a Tropar tab. No place, no persecution, no emperor and no year is printed beside the name.

The day on which the Romanian calendar keeps him is the day of Agathonicus of Nicomedia and his company, and Zeno stands in that company in other calendars; but doxologia's own life of Agathonicus, which it prints under four of those names, does not name a Zeno among them, so no such connection is asserted here. Nor is he identified with the Zeno of Nicomedia the corpus already holds, who belongs to a different company on a different day. He is entered on the Romanian line alone.

The Greek calendar keeps the company on the same day, 22 August, and gives the march that gathered them. saint.gr says that when the count Eutolmios was returning from his circuit in Pontus, where he had gone to hunt Christians down, he stopped at Karpe, and there he found [Zoticus](/saints/zoticus-of-nicomedia) and put him to death together with his disciples. At Karpe he learned that the prince of the city had been made a Christian by a certain [Agathonicus](/saints/agathonicus-of-nicomedia), who was from Nicomedia.

So he arrested the prince and Agathonicus, and having punished them along with other Christians he led the whole party off to the king, who was then in Thrace. But on the road, near a village called Potamos, he killed Zeno, [Theoprepius](/saints/theoprepius-of-nicomedia), [Acindynus](/saints/acindynus-of-nicomedia) and [Severian](/saints/severian-of-nicomedia), because from the many wounds in their feet they could no longer walk.

When he reached the village of Ammous near Silyvria he beheaded Agathonicus, the prince and the other Christians by royal order, bound as they were. So all of them followed the example of the slain Lamb, Christ, and will worthily be numbered with those who are «γεγραμμένοι εν τω βιβλίω της ζωής του αρνίου», written in the book of the life of the Lamb. The page names neither the prince nor the disciples who died with Zoticus, and gives no year; it records the skull of Agathonicus at the church of the Forerunner at Kavala and a portion of his relic at the monastery of Mega Spilaio at Kalavryta.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 22 august — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/22-august) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-zinon), which is empty of both life and hymn; read 24 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 22 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/22/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/832/saint.aspx), read 1 October 2026.*
