# Gregory the Recluse of the Caves

saint.gr's whole notice for him is one sentence: the venerable Gregory lived as a recluse in the monastery of Saint Theodosius of the Great Lavra of Kyiv in the fourteenth century. Above it the page notes where his relic is — «Το Λείψανο του Αγίου βρίσκεται αδιάφθορο στη Λαύρα των Σπηλαίων», incorrupt in the Lavra of the Caves.

He is not the other Gregory the Greek calendar keeps on this same day, the venerable-martyr and wonderworker who was also an ascetic of that monastery of Saint Theodosius and who died in 1094: two entries on one calendar are two commemorations, and the pages give them different centuries and different ends. No year, no birthplace and no hymn is printed for this one.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/114/saint.aspx) and, for the wonderworker this one is not, [his page](https://www.saint.gr/115/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
