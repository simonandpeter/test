# Maidulf

saint.gr's calendar for 17 May names him and gives him two sentences. The venerable Maidulf came from England and lived as a hermit. Having worked as a missionary in his own country, he fell asleep in peace at Malmesbury in the year 673; the page's head line dates him as an unknown birth and that death.

That is the whole of the entry. It says nothing of his parents, nothing of where in England he was born or where his hermitage was, nothing of a monastery or a rule, and it prints no relics and no hymn. The Greek calendar keeps an English hermit of this name on 17 May, and nothing further is set down here.
