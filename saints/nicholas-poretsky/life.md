# Nicholas (Poretsky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Nicholas among the hieromartyrs on 14 July and gives no life. Its line is «Сщмч. Николая пресвитера (1933).» — the hieromartyr Nicholas the presbyter, 1933 — and the calendar's own name page for him adds the surname and a higher grade than the line does: «Николай (Порецкий), протоиерей, сщмч.», Nicholas Poretsky, archpriest.

That is the whole of the record. He was a priest, by the name page an archpriest; he died in 1933; the calendar keeps him on this day among the new martyrs of Russia. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 14 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260714.html); read 9 October 2026.*
