# Abercius (Kedrov)

saint.gr says that the holy hieromartyr Abercius, Polycarp Kedrov in the world, was born on 2 March 1879 in the town of Yaransk in the province of Vyatka, of devout and God-loving parents, the hieromartyr [Nicholas](/saints/nicholas-kedrov) and Elizabeth. He studied at the theological school of Vyatka and at the theological academy of Saint Petersburg. On 2 July 1910 he was made a monk by Sergius Stragorodsky, archbishop of Finland, and given the name Abercius, and on the fifth of the same year he entered the priesthood. He took up the duties of rector of the church school of Zhytomyr, and on 27 June 1915 he was elected bishop of that region.

He was arrested for the first time by the Soviet regime in 1922 and exiled to Uzbekistan; in that period, the page says, he contended against the autocephaly of the Church of Ukraine. In 1925 he was living in Moscow and was present at the funeral of Patriarch [Tikhon](/saints/tikhon-of-moscow). He was arrested again in 1926 and imprisoned at Zhytomyr, and then moved to the Butyrki prison in Moscow.

Abercius the steadfast, the page says, together with his brother Archbishop [Pachomius](/saints/pachomius-kedrov), signed a letter against the «κρατικὴ θρησκεία», the state religion the godless regime was trying to impose. In 1929 he was imprisoned in Butyrki, and in February 1930 exiled to the city of Arkhangelsk. From 1933 to 1934 he was in exile in the town of Birsk in Bashkiria, where he was arrested and, in 1937, sentenced to death by shooting.
