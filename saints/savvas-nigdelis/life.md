# Savvas Nigdelis

saint.gr's calendar for 12 Νοεμβρίου keeps the new martyr Savvas Nigdelis, called also Samoladas, and its notice is two sentences. He came from Nigde in Asia Minor and was martyred for the Christian faith at Constantinople, beheaded on 12 November 1726 at the place called Kutsuk Karamani.

The reason for his arrest, the page says, was the envy of the Turks: by his honest work the saint had become very rich, and they wanted to seize his property. Nothing else of him is recorded — no age, no family, no trial, no burial — and the page prints no hymn. Its distich says that Savvas, the glory of the martyrs, cut off in the head, is joined by that head to Christ, the head of all.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3017/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
