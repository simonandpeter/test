# Neophytus (5 May)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 5 mai reads «Sfântul Mucenic Neofit» — Neophytus the martyr — with no country, no century and no epithet, and his page carries only the day and the readings appointed for a martyr.

That is the whole of the record here. The martyrs Gaius and Gaianus stand on the same day with pages as bare as his.

The Greek calendar joins to him the two men whose Romanian pages stood beside his and said nothing. saint.gr prints one line for the three — «Άγιοι Νεόφυτος, Γάιος και Γαϊανός οι Μάρτυρες» — with a couplet that counts them: three martyrs take death most manfully for the sake of the Holy Trinity. Under it comes the one sentence the page has for all of them. The holy martyrs Neophytus, [Gaius](/saints/gaius-5-may) and [Gaianus](/saints/gaianus-5-may) were perfected by the sword, and their synaxis used to be kept at the church of the Holy Unmercenaries Cosmas and Damian, in the quarter of Dareios. There is still no year, no emperor and no place of martyrdom, and the Greek prints no hymn for them either.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 5 mai — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/5-mai) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-neofit-0); read 20 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 5 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/05/index.aspx) and [the life of the three](https://www.saint.gr/389/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026.*
