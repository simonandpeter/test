# Neophytus (5 May)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 5 mai reads «Sfântul Mucenic Neofit» — Neophytus the martyr — with no country, no century and no epithet, and his page carries only the day and the readings appointed for a martyr.

That is the whole of the record here. The martyrs Gaius and Gaianus stand on the same day with pages as bare as his.
