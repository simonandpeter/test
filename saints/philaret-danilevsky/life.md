# Philaret (Danilevsky)

The Russian calendar names Philaret (Danilevsky) on 9 September of the old style, among the sixteen Glinsk fathers the day enumerates, each with his year. The calendar links no written life, so this entry records the line and nothing more.

*After the Православный церковный календарь (days.pravoslavie.ru), [9 сентября ст. ст.](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260909.html); read 30 August 2026.*
