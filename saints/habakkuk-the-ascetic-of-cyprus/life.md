# Habakkuk of Kalamithasa

saint.gr's prose for the venerable Habakkuk the ascetic is a single sentence: he was a Cypriot ascetic, one of the Alamans — «Ο Όσιος Αββακούμ ο ασκητής ήταν ένας Κύπριος ασκητής εκ των Αλαμανών». The Alamans are the company of fathers whom Cyprus remembers as having come to the island together and settled apart from one another, and the page does not say more about them here.

Everything else this folder knows comes from the apolytikion printed beneath that sentence, which is taken here. It calls him the glory of the Cypriots and the boast of Kalamithasa; it says he was shown a wonderworker, and shone over Soleas like a most clear lamp; and it says what the healings are — that he heals the sick and the deaf alike.

The page gives him no century, no monastery, no relic and no couplet, and no year of any kind, so this folder carries no dates. He is not the prophet Habakkuk, whom the same second of December also keeps, and not the Habakkuk of Thessalonica of the Greek calendar's other day; the day's own line distinguishes him by the one word «ο ασκητής».

*After saint.gr's calendar for 2 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/02/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3131/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
