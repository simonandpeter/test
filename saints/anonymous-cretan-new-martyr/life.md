# The Anonymous Cretan

saint.gr keeps on 31 Ιουλίου a new martyr whose name it does not know, and its line for him is simply «Άγιος Ανώνυμος Κρητικός Νεομάρτυρας» — the holy anonymous Cretan new martyr. This new martyr, the page says, was from Crete and born of pious parents. At twenty years old he found himself in the service of a Turk at Alexandria in Egypt.

One night that Turk wanted to take advantage of the young man. The pious youth refused, and in his desperation knifed the Turk, with the result that the man died. The next day the dead man's friends, as soon as they heard what had happened, seized the young Christian and took him before the judge; but because there was no testimony against him they let him go free.

Two days later they seized him again, and after torturing him hard he confessed the whole truth. Then the judge put it to him that to save his life he should deny Christ and become a Muslim. The new martyr rejected the judge's proposal and confessed with courage that he had been born a Christian, was one, and wished to die one. So on one of the days of July in the year 1811 they put him to death by hanging. His honourable relic hung two days on the gallows; then they took it down and buried it in the sand.
