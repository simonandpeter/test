# Peter (Popov)

The Russian church calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 12 мая ст. ст. reads «Сщмч. Петра , пресвитера (1937)» — the hieromartyr Peter, presbyter — and behind that line stands a name page, and nothing more: «Петр (Попов), иерей, сщмч.», with two days of commemoration, 26 января among the new martyrs and this day. So the calendar says that he was a priest, that his surname was Попов, that he is numbered among the new martyrs of Russia, and that he died in 1937. It says nothing else: not where he served, not where he was born, not how he died.

That is set down here as it stands. The year is on the day line alone, and the 26 января is the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, a feast of the whole company rather than a day of his own. The day page prints hymns for four of the saints it heads and none under his name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 12 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260512.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9776.html); read 8 October 2026.*
