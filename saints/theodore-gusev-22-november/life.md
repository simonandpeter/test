# Theodore (Gusev) (22 November)

**Thirty-three years in one village church: psalm-reader, then deacon, then — for thirteen months — its priest.** The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him sixth of the sixteen it closes with 1937, among the «пресвитеров», and carries no life for him; the life here is what azbyka.ru prints for the civil 5 декабря out of fond.ru. He was born on 2 February 1875 in the village of Никольское in the Клин district of the province of Moscow, son of its priest, Евдоким Гусев. He finished the Дмитров church school, served two years in the army as a private from 1899, and in 1903 became psalm-reader of the Dormition church of the village of Изосимье in the Клин district, where he served all his life. In 1912 the metropolitan of Moscow ordained him deacon.

**The church he served was closed twice and the parishioners got it open twice.** In November 1929 its priest was arrested and exiled for three years for not meeting the grain deliveries; there was nobody left to serve, and in December the church was shut. On 31 March 1930, on the parishioners’ petition, it was opened again, and priests from the neighbouring parishes served in it by turns. On 15 May 1934 it was shut again for unpaid taxes, and in August of the same year the parishioners got it opened once more. On 8 November 1936 the bishop of Волоколамск ordained the deacon Феодор priest to that church.

**The one thing he admitted was about taxes.** He was arrested on 21 November 1937 and shut in the Таганская prison in Moscow. The investigator asked whom he kept up connections with in the district; he named the priest of the Грибово church, then under arrest himself, and said the connection was that he had often gone to him for advice about the services. Told that he was under arrest for counter-revolutionary anti-Soviet activity among the population and that the investigation required frank testimony, he answered that in July 1937, on the question of tax policy, he had said among the collective farmers: «Советская власть на нас налагает непосильные налоги. Нет больше сил платить» — the Soviet power lays taxes on us beyond bearing, there is no strength left to pay — and that he had never held other conversations against the Soviet power among the community of believers and did not admit himself guilty of it.

**Three days from the sentence to the pit.** On 1 December 1937 the troika of the NKVD condemned him to be shot; on 4 December he was shot and buried in an unmarked common grave at the Бутово range outside Moscow. That day was in 1937 the Julian 21 ноября, the day before the one the calendars keep him on. **He is not this corpus’s `theodore-martyr-1940`**, who holds the same Russian form off the same calendar’s 19 января ст. ст.: that one is a layman, a martyr and no hieromartyr, with no office and no life, whom the calendar dates to 1940.
