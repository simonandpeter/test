# Joannicius (Gomolko)

Joannicius — John Gomolko in the world — was born in 1842, the son of an
officer, and came to the Glinsk hermitage in 1865 after finishing the
district school at Gomel. From his entry he waged the inner war with the
passions, and in time reached a constant spiritual recollection — if the
mind does not bridle the senses, he said afterwards, the eyes run
everywhere from curiosity, the ears love to hear vain things, and the lips
become unrestrainable. In 1874 he was tonsured with the name Isaiah, in
1880 ordained hierodeacon and in 1884 hieromonk; given the obedience of
clerk and attorney, he mastered the science of conducting the monastery’s
affairs competently and successfully without harm to his ascesis, and on
the strength of that experience was elected superior in 1888 and made
igumen. Theophan the Recluse, with whom he corresponded, called him an
elder of much-experienced wisdom. As abbot he opened a House of Industry
at the hermitage in 1890 to teach orphan boys letters and trades, raised
the Far Skete in 1893 on the place of the labours of
[Iliodor](/saints/heliodorus-golovanitsky), and set the hermitage’s
publishing going in 1891; it was he who tested and received the young
[Andronicus (Lukash)](/saints/andronicus-lukash) in 1905. In 1906 he took
the schema with the name Joannicius. Defeated in the inner war with the
saint, the devil raised outward persecution: General P. Mitropolsky, who
rented a house from the hermitage, obtained through the Synod the
replacement of the diocesan bishop, and the new bishop, who had not known
him in earlier years, believed the slander that the abbot managed the
house’s affairs incompetently — its flourishing at that very time proved
the opposite — and in March 1912 he was dismissed from the government of
the hermitage. Leaving it, he signed the cross over the river swollen with
the spring flood and crossed on the water to the other bank, repeating the
wonder once worked by Joannicius the Great. Until lately his further fate
was thought unknown; it is now established that a peasant family took him
in, that in their house he lived the life of a recluse, and that after his
death he was buried in the brethren’s cemetery of the hermitage. On 8 May
2008 the Holy Synod of the Ukrainian Church numbered the
schema-archimandrite Joannicius, superior of Glinsk, among the locally
venerated saints, and on 30 November 2017 the Council of Bishops of the
Russian Church glorified him for the whole church, with his memory on 22
September — 9 September of the old style — in the synaxis of the Glinsk
fathers.
