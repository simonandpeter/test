# Euphrosyne (Dzhurinskaya)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names her on 9 декабря in a line that names two people and gives neither a life. The line is «Сщмч. Владимира пресвитера и мц. Евфросинии (1920).» — the hieromartyr Vladimir the presbyter and the martyr Euphrosyne, 1920 — and the calendar’s own name pages add the surnames the line leaves out: «Евфросиния (Джуринская), мц.» and «Владимир (Джуринский), протоиерей, сщмч.»

That is the whole of the record. She was a martyr, which is the one thing the calendar says of her; she died in 1920; and she is set down with [Vladimir (Dzhurinsky)](/saints/vladimir-dzhurinsky), whose surname her own is the feminine of, in one line and under one year. How the two were related, where they lived and how they died the calendar does not say, and nothing is supplied here.

The day page carries seven hymns, for the day’s own feast and for others whom it heads, and not one of them is under either name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 9 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261209.html); read 13 October 2026.*
