# Euthymius (Krugovykh)

Euthymius Georgievich Krugovykh served as psalmist at the cathedral of the Entry of the Theotokos and was later ordained second priest of the church of the Kazan icon of the Mother of God in the city of Verny — today Almaty. He was a steadfast fighter against Renovationism, and in 1924 was hanged at the monastery farmstead near the settlement of Zarya Vostoka, today within Almaty, with four martyrs, for staying faithful to Patriarch Tikhon; the burial place of the tortured men remains unknown. He and the four martyrs were glorified by the jubilee council of 2000, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 3 September (Julian).
