# Alexis of Bortsurmany

His line in the calendar is four words and a year. The Сретенский monastery’s calendar for 21 апреля ст. ст. prints «Прав. Алексия Бортсурманского (1848)» — a righteous man of that name, of Bortsurmany, 1848 — and the name page behind the forename gives the surname the line leaves out and the grade it does not mention: «Алексий (Гневушев) Бортсурманский, иерей», a priest.

That is the whole of it. There is no life page for him on the day page and none on his name page, and 1848 is the only year either of them gives. Nothing is said of when he was born, in what diocese he served, how long he was at Bortsurmany, or how he died. The rank of righteous is the line’s and the priesthood is the name page’s, and both are set down among his ranks rather than written out as a biography he has not been given.

His name page lists one other day, «4 августа (обретение мощей)», the finding of his relics, and that day page was not read. One row per church is the schema, so the day is recorded on his Russian row and he does not render on it. The day page carries one hymn, and it is headed for two martyrs of other centuries and not for him.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 21 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260421.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/10108.html); read 8 October 2026.*
