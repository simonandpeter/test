# Elpidephorus

Doxologia.ro's calendar for 3 aprilie names him and gives no life. The line is «Sfântul Mucenic Elpidifor», the holy martyr Elpidephorus, and his own page on the site carries no life tab and no troparion — only the name, the date, and the readings appointed for a martyr, from the second letter to Timothy and from the Lord's farewell discourse in John.

That is the whole of what the source gives. It names no country and no city, no year and no emperor, no judge and no manner of death, and no companions, although martyrs of this name are commonly kept with others. The calendar asserts two things and no more: that he was a martyr, and that the Romanian church keeps him on this day.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and is no fuller than the Romanian. saint.gr's whole account is one clause: «Ο Άγιος Ελπιδηφόρος (ή Ελπιδοφόρος) μαρτύρησε δια ξίφους» — the holy Elpidephorus, or Elpidophorus, was martyred by the sword. It gives no year, no place, no emperor and no judge, names no relics and prints no hymn. What it does print beside the line is a couplet: «Ξίφους ἀληθὴς μάρτυς Ἐλπιδηφόρος, / Θεὸν φέρει ἄψευστον εὐελπιστίαν» — a true witness of the sword, Elpidephorus bears God as a hope that does not deceive.

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on the same menologion day the Romanian and Greek books do, 3 апреля ст. ст., and gives him three companions the other two never named: «Мчч. Елпидифора , Дия , Вифония и Галика (III).» The life behind that line is titled for the four of them and is four sentences long. They suffered, it says, for confessing the faith in Jesus Christ, and then each death is given separately: his own was by the sword, and [Dius](/saints/dius-companion-of-elpidephorus) was stoned, [Bithonius](/saints/bithonius-companion-of-elpidephorus) drowned in the sea and [Galicus](/saints/galicus-companion-of-elpidephorus) given to the beasts.

The «(III)» that closes the line is the first date this folder has held: neither doxologia nor saint.gr gave him a year or a century, and the third century is written off that mark and nothing narrower. The four men have four different name pages under the one line, his being number 773, which is what makes them four and not one heading; and the three companions are new folders written from this day. The day page prints no hymn for any of them.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 3 aprilie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/3-aprilie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-elpidifor-0); read 19 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 3 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/03/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3803/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 3 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260403.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life771.htm), read 5 October 2026.*
