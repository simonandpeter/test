# Elpidephorus

Doxologia.ro's calendar for 3 aprilie names him and gives no life. The line is «Sfântul Mucenic Elpidifor», the holy martyr Elpidephorus, and his own page on the site carries no life tab and no troparion — only the name, the date, and the readings appointed for a martyr, from the second letter to Timothy and from the Lord's farewell discourse in John.

That is the whole of what the source gives. It names no country and no city, no year and no emperor, no judge and no manner of death, and no companions, although martyrs of this name are commonly kept with others. The calendar asserts two things and no more: that he was a martyr, and that the Romanian church keeps him on this day.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 3 aprilie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/3-aprilie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-elpidifor-0); read 19 September 2026.*
