# The Five Maidens of Lesbos

saint.gr says that although their names and the year of their martyrdom are not known to us, it is a fact that they came from Lesbos and that they were martyred on the island by the sword. The calendar's line for them is «Αγίες Πέντε Κόρες από την Λέσβο», the holy five maidens from Lesbos, and the page keeps them as one company and names not one of the five.

The local hagiological tradition, the page says, has it that they lived at Gavatha near Antissa, and that on Mount Ordymnos there was a monastery dedicated to them, which about the year 1331 was flourishing together with two others, of St John the Theologian and of St Theophano. An old chapel dedicated to their memory stands in the community of Pterounta.

The page gives them no century and no year, and names no relics. Its couplet counts them rather than naming them: «Ἀθληφόρους τίθησι Λεσβίας κόρας, / Μίαν, δύο, τρεῖς, τέσσαρας πέντε, ξίφος» — the sword makes prizewinners of the maidens of Lesbos: one, two, three, four, five. Because the source names nobody, they are kept here as one company and not as five folders.
