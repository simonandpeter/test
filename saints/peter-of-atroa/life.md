# Peter of Atroa

Peter “in Atroa” — by others “in Agrea” — lived in the years of the emperor Nicephorus I (802–811) and Stauracius. He came from Asia and from a young age had a calling to the monastic life; he went to Atroa in Bithynia, where he passed his life in the strictest ascesis and died in peace — broken by the loosing of the flesh, the verse says, Peter passed to God, the unbreakable rock. The Greek calendar keeps him on 13 September, and an [Andronicus of Atroa](/saints/andronicus-of-atroa) the day before.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 13 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2427/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
