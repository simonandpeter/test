# Pitirim of Tambov

Pitirim of Tambov was born at Vyazma in 1645 — the shorter of his calendar's two readings of him gives 27 February and allows 1644 instead — and was called Prokopy in the world, baptised for [Procopius of Decapolis](/saints/procopius-of-decapolis), whose name he kept until his tonsure and whom he honoured all his life. He was taught his letters as a child, read the fathers and the lives of the saints, painted icons, knew church singing well and was reckoned the best reader in the town. The strict Forerunner monastery at Vyazma drew him, and he entered it when his schooling was done.

He was tonsured in his twenty-first year, in 1665, and took the name of a disciple of the great Egyptian ascetic. His longer life adds that the fifteenth-century missionary bishop [Pitirim](/saints/pitirim-of-perm) of Great Perm was very likely the example the name was chosen for, and that the two men's work turned out alike. He was made abbot of his house, raised archimandrite in 1684, and on 15 February 1685 the patriarch of Moscow called him to the capital and consecrated him bishop of Tambov.

The diocese was three years old, poor, and in large part not yet Christian: Mordva, Cheremis and Meshchera among the heathen, many Tatars hostile to the faith, and among the Christians schismatics and exiles. He began a two-storey stone cathedral of the Transfiguration in Tambov and worked on the building with his own hands, opened a school where clergy were trained under him, and gathered a library. In 1690 he founded a convent of the Ascension whose first superior was his own sister, and in deep forest he set up the Tregulyaev monastery of the Forerunner together with his friend [Mitrophan of Voronezh](/saints/mitrophan-of-voronezh).

He worked on schismatics and unbelievers by patience and brotherly kindness rather than by denunciation, and his life says that many were brought to the faith by it. He fell gravely ill in 1695, after ten years in the see, and recovered; three years later, on 28 July 1698, the feast of the Smolensk icon which this same day of the calendar keeps, he died, aged fifty-three. He was buried in the lower storey of his cathedral, his grave was venerated from the first, the healings granted there were written down from 1819, and on 28 July 1914 he was numbered among the saints.
