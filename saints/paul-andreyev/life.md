# Paul (Andreyev)

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar for 3 ноября ст. ст. carries twelve names on one line and his is the eleventh of them: «Сщмчч. Василия, Василия, Александра, Владимира, Сергия, Николая, Викентия, Иоанна, Петра, Александра, Павла, Космы пресвитеров (1937).» The line gives the forenames in the genitive, the grade-class «пресвитеров» for all twelve together, and one year, 1937, and it gives no surname to any of them. The name link behind his place in it supplies the calendar's own nominative with the surname, «Павел (Андреев), протоиерей, сщмч.», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from.

That is everything the calendar records of him. He has no life page, no icon, no hymn, no place, no age and no parish anywhere on this calendar, and the name page behind his place on the line carries two days and no text at all. The name page behind his place on the line also lists «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. That is a synaxis and not a second feast, and he does not render on it. Nothing further is written here, and in particular nothing is reasoned from the surname.

The line names the class and the link names the grade, and both are kept: the line's «Сщмчч.» and «пресвитеров» make him a hieromartyr and a presbyter, and the link's «протоиерей» is recorded as his office, Archpriest. He is given no relation to the eleven others on the line. They share a line, a grade-class and a year, and the calendar prints nothing at all that says they died in one place or knew one another; a shared day is not a relation here.

One thing the calendar does not print is in the Russian church calendar at azbyka.ru, read for another man on another day, and it is why this folder now carries two relations. azbyka's life of the new martyrs of Возмище says that in 1937 he was appointed rector of the church of the Nativity of the Mother of God in the village of Возмище in the Volokolamsk district and dean of the Orthodox parishes of the Volokolamsk, Shakhovskaya and Lotoshino districts; that [Alexander (Zverev)](/saints/alexander-zverev) served in the same church; and that [Demetrius (Rozanov)](/saints/demetrius-rozanov-12-november), who had met Zverev in exile and come to the district on his invitation, lived at Возмище waiting for a vacant place and served with the two of them as he was able.

The three were arrested at the end of September and the beginning of October 1937, together with nuns living in the village who sang in the church choir. On 14 November 1937 one NKVD troika sentenced the three of them together to be shot. He and Zverev were shot on 16 November, which is 3 ноября ст. ст. and the day this calendar keeps them on, and Rozanov on 25 November, which is 12 ноября ст. ст. and the day it keeps him on; all three were buried in an unmarked common grave on the Butovo range near Moscow. One troika decision naming the three of them is what the two relations rest on, and they are the only relations written here: the eleven other names on this day's line are still a line and not a company.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 3 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261103.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/12872.html); read 4 October 2026; and the life of the new martyrs of Возмище printed by the Russian church calendar at azbyka.ru, [the entry](https://azbyka.ru/days/sv-aleksandr-zverev), read 5 October 2026.*
