# Sergius (Stanislavlev)

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar for 3 ноября ст. ст. names him alone on the last line of the day: «Сщмч. Сергия диакона (1942).» — the rank word «Сщмч.», the forename in the genitive, the grade «диакона», and 1942, which is the latest year anything on this day carries. The name link behind the forename supplies the calendar's own nominative with the surname and a fuller grade, «Сергий (Станиславлев), протодиакон, сщмч.»

That is everything the calendar records of him. He has no life page, no icon, no hymn, no place, no age and no parish anywhere on this calendar, and the name page behind his place on the line carries two days and no text at all. The name page behind his place on the line also lists «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. That is a synaxis and not a second feast, and he does not render on it. Nothing further is written here, and in particular nothing is reasoned from the surname.

The line's grade word and the link's differ, and both are kept in the way this wave keeps them: «диакона» is the line's word, so deacon stands in his types beside the hieromartyrdom, and «протодиакон» is the link's grade, so it is recorded as his office, Protodeacon. Nothing else is recorded, and 1942 is the year the line prints and the only thing on this page that places him in a decade.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 3 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261103.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14484.html); read 4 October 2026.*
