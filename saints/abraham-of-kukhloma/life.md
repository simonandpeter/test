# Abraham of Kukhloma

saint.gr's calendar for 20 Ιουλίου names the venerable Abraham of Kukhloma and heads him with one year, 1375, the year of his birth left as a question mark. Of his life it says only this: we have no details of the Russian monk's life. So the folder holds a name, a day, a place-name in the page's own Greek spelling, and the year he died.

The page prints no hymn for him and tells nothing of where Kukhloma is, when he entered the monastic life, or what house he belonged to. What stands above the notice is the site's gloss on the name Abraham and a sentence about the patriarch of the Hebrew nation, which belongs to the name and not to this monk.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 20 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/20/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3367/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
