# Ignatius (Sadkovsky)

The Russian calendar's line is all there is of him. It names him alone on this day, «Сщмч. Игнатия, еп. Скопинского (1938)» — the hieromartyr Ignatius, bishop of Skopin, 1938 — and the name behind that line reads «Святитель Игнатий (Садковский), епископ Скопинский, сщмч.», Ignatius Sadkovsky, bishop of Skopin, hieromartyr. That is his surname, his see, his rank and the year of his death.

There is no life page behind his name and no hymn for him on the day. Skopin is the see the calendar gives him and the only place it names; nothing here says when or where he was consecrated, where he was arrested or where he died. The year 1938 is the calendar's own and the only date it prints.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 28 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260128.html); read 3 October 2026.*
