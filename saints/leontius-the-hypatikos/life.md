# Leontius the Hypatikos

saint.gr's calendar for 7 Οκτωβρίου names Leontius the hypatikos — the consular — and gives him no narrative of his own. Its page for him says that he is spoken of in the life of Saints [Julian](/saints/julian-companion-of-caesarius) and [Caesarius](/saints/caesarius-companion-of-julian), who are honoured on the same day, and that he is the man who came to believe when he saw the miracle of Jesus Christ that shattered the idols after Caesarius had prayed.

The life of the two martyrs, which the same calendar links, gives the rest. Caesarius had come from Africa to the city of Tarracina in Italy and rebuked the people he found sacrificing to idols; he was arrested, imprisoned, and taken under guard to the temple of Apollo, and at his prayer the temple fell. Seeing that, Leontius the hypatikos fell at the saint's feet and asked to be made a Christian. Julian the presbyter baptised him, and as soon as he had communicated of the immaculate mysteries he breathed his last.

Over his name the page sets a distich: «Λιπών ο Λεόντιος αρχήν την κάτω, / Eιρηνικώς εφεύρε τιμήν την άνω» — having left the rule below, Leontius peacefully found the honour above. saint.gr gives him no year, no place of burial and no hymn, and it carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2694/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
