# Gaius (5 May)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 5 mai reads «Sfântul Mucenic Gaie», and the page it points to opens no Viață tab: it carries the day and the readings appointed for a martyr, and no text of its own.

The same day prints «Sfântul Mucenic Gaian» and «Sfântul Mucenic Neofit», whose pages are as bare. Nothing on any of them joins the three, and nothing is claimed here beyond the name, the rank and the day.

The Greek calendar joins the three, where the Romanian left them apart. saint.gr prints one line and one page for «Άγιοι Νεόφυτος, Γάιος και Γαϊανός οι Μάρτυρες», with a couplet that counts them — three martyrs take death most manfully for the sake of the Holy Trinity — and gives each of the three a head line of his own, his reading «Αγιος Γαϊος ο Μαρτυρας». The one sentence of story is for all of them: the holy martyrs [Neophytus](/saints/neophytus-5-may), Gaius and [Gaianus](/saints/gaianus-5-may) were perfected by the sword, and their synaxis used to be kept at the church of the Holy Unmercenaries Cosmas and Damian, in the quarter of Dareios. Beyond that the Greek adds no year, no emperor, no place and no hymn.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 5 mai — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/5-mai) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-gaie); read 20 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 5 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/05/index.aspx) and [the life of the three](https://www.saint.gr/389/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026.*
