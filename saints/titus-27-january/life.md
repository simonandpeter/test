# Titus (27 January)

saint.gr's calendar for 27 January names Titus the soldier, and his own page on that site prints no life for him. The page heads the day as his, «Ημερομηνία Εορτής: 27/01», and says the feast is a fixed one kept on 27 January every year; and then, in place of a life, it sends the reader elsewhere: for Titus the soldier, see 27 February.

That other day has not been read for this folder, so nothing is written here about who he was, where he served, when he lived or how he died. What is recorded is what this calendar records on this day: the name, the epithet of soldier, and the page's own cross-reference. Everything else about him waits on the February page, and on the Russian and Romanian calendars, none of which has been consulted.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 27 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/27/index.aspx) and [his page](https://www.saint.gr/2609/saint.aspx), which prints no life; read 30 September 2026.*
