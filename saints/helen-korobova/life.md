# Helen (Korobova)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names her and gives no life. Its line for 25 мая ст. ст. reads «Прмц. Елены (1938).» — the venerable-martyr Helen, of the year 1938 — and the line carries a link to her name and none to a reading. The name page behind it adds her family name, Коробова, and nothing else: its one line is «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 25 мая».

So the calendar sets down a woman of the monastic state in the Russian church who died in 1938, and no more than that. The second of those two days is not a feast of her own: it is the general commemoration of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which this calendar prints beside the proper day of each of them, and it is the one mark on her page that places her among that company. She is kept here on the day the calendar gives her for her own, and the synaxis is recorded on her attestation.

The rank it gives her, преподобномученица, is the rank of someone under monastic vows who died a martyr; but the calendar does not say what her monastic degree was, or where she lived, or how she died, so no office has been written into this folder. Nothing else about her was found: no folder in this corpus held the name before, and azbyka.ru, which is where an account of her would be, was not read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 25 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260525.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14681.html), which name her and give no life; read 8 October 2026.*
