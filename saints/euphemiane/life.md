# Euphemiane

saint.gr's calendar for 12 December keeps two women in one entry and names both: Euphemiane and [Phoebe](/saints/phoebe-12-december). Of Euphemiane the page says that she is unknown to the synaxaria, and that her memory is recorded in the Jerusalem Kanonarion at page 120, accompanied by that of Saint Phoebe, who is likewise an unknown saint.

That is the whole of the entry for either of them. No country, no century, no office, no manner of death, no relic, no hymn and no synaxaristic couplet is printed; the page does not even say whether they were martyrs or ascetics, and so nothing of the kind is written down here. What the calendar keeps on this day is the pair of names and the one book they stand in.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3242/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
