# Eugenia (Lysova)

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar for 4 ноября ст. ст. names her on a line of its own among the day's last four: «Прмц. Евгении (1935).» The line gives the forename in the genitive and no surname at all. The name link behind it supplies the calendar's own nominative with the surname, «Евгения (Лысова), инокиня, прмц.», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from.

That is everything the calendar records of her. She has no life page, no icon, no hymn, no place, no age and no parish anywhere on this calendar, and the name page behind the line carries two days and no text at all. The name page also lists «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which is a synaxis and not a second feast; this folder renders on 4 ноября ст. ст. only. Nothing further is written here, and in particular nothing is reasoned from the surname.

The line's «Прмц.» makes her a venerable martyr, and the link's «инокиня» is both the ground for the monastic type and her office, Nun. She is the only woman on the day and the only one of the four whose line gives no grade of the priesthood. Nothing is written of where, or at whose hands, or at what age.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 4 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261104.html) and [her name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14522.html); read 4 October 2026.*
