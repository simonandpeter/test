# Gregory of Nicomedia

saint.gr says Gregory was born in the year 1190 and came from the parts of Bithynia. From a child he lived by the commands of the Gospel, and in his studies he showed himself the quickest and the most diligent; that learning he put to the use of the Church and to the building up of his neighbour in the spirit.

After some time he withdrew to one of the most celebrated monasteries of his day, and there he shone by his virtue. But some of the monks, out of envy, put about the slander that he had stolen sacred vessels of the house. His innocence was proved; even so he left that monastery and went to another, where a brother of his was living as a monk.

There he grew further in knowledge and in virtue, so much that they made him a priest. Afterwards he went to a neighbouring village, where his learning and his great spirituality were a help to the people about him. Satan, through his instruments, set many traps for him, but by the grace of God Gregory got past them all untouched. He died at the age of fifty on 2 April 1240, having contended with his learning for his neighbour to his last breath.

The page names no monastery, no village and no bishop, and prints no hymn under his name. It adds a couplet, which tells a wonder its own narrative does not: «Έφιππος άλα Γρηγόριε καθάπερ, / Xέρσον διήλθες, ω τεραστίου ξένου!» — over the brine on horseback, Gregory, you passed as over dry land: what a strange wonder.
