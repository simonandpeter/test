# Onesimus (10 May)

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life. Its whole account of him is one sentence: the holy martyrs [Erasmus](/saints/erasmus-10-may) and Onesimus were martyred together with fourteen other martyrs, under the emperor Decius, who reigned from 249 to 251, along with the holy martyrs [Alpheus](/saints/alphaeus-martyr-10-may), [Cyprinus](/saints/cyprian-martyr-10-may) and [Philadelphus](/saints/philadelphus-martyr-10-may).

That is all of it: no country, no judge, no accusation, no manner of death, no relics, no burial place, no apolytikion and no kontakion. The page's note on the name says only that Ονήσιμος comes from ὀνίνημι, to profit, and means the useful one. A venerable man of the same name baptised the three brothers, according to their own page on this same day; the calendar does not say that he and this martyr are one, and nothing here says it either.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/10/index.aspx) and [the life of the two](https://www.saint.gr/1541/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
