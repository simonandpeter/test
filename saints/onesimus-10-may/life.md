# Onesimus (10 May)

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life. Its whole account of him is one sentence: the holy martyrs [Erasmus](/saints/erasmus-10-may) and Onesimus were martyred together with fourteen other martyrs, under the emperor Decius, who reigned from 249 to 251, along with the holy martyrs [Alpheus](/saints/alphaeus-martyr-10-may), [Cyprinus](/saints/cyprian-martyr-10-may) and [Philadelphus](/saints/philadelphus-martyr-10-may).

That is all of it: no country, no judge, no accusation, no manner of death, no relics, no burial place, no apolytikion and no kontakion. The page's note on the name says only that Ονήσιμος comes from ὀνίνημι, to profit, and means the useful one. A venerable man of the same name baptised the three brothers, according to their own page on this same day; the calendar does not say that he and this martyr are one, and nothing here says it either.

The Russian church calendar keeps him on 10 мая ст. ст. in one line with four companions, «Мчч. Алфия, Филадельфа, Киприана, Онисима, Еразма и иных (251)», and gives the five of them a single life. It tells more of him than the Greek line this folder was first written from: the calendar’s name link makes him a presbyter and a hieromartyr, «Онисим Бастийский, Римский, пресвитер, сщмч.», and the life makes him the one who baptised the three brothers Алфий, Филадельф and Киприан, the sons of Вителий the governor of Italy. That is why he stands in their company.

When Christians were ordered sought out and given over to torture, the brothers were sent to Rome with him, with Еразм and with fourteen other Christians. At Rome his breast was crushed under a heavy stone and he died of it, and Еразм and the fourteen were beheaded; the three brothers were sent on to Sicily and suffered at Месополь in the territory of Леонтины. The life dates the brothers to 251 and the reign of Декий, having opened by setting the persecution under Ликиний, and says their relics were found at Леонтины in 1517.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/10/index.aspx) and [the life of the two](https://www.saint.gr/1541/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026; and after the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 10 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260510.html) and [the life the five share](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1031.htm), read 8 October 2026.*
