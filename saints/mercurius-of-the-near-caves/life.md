# Mercurius of the Near Caves

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 7 August, eleventh of the day's entries, and its line is «Прп. Меркурия Печерского, еп. Смоленского (1239).» — the venerable Mercurius of the Caves, bishop of Smolensk. The calendar's own name page gives him more fully, and with two more ranks than the line: «Святитель Меркурий Смоленский, Печерский, епископ, сщмч.»

The reading behind his name is short and is the whole of what the calendar says of him. He was a monk of the Kyiv Caves monastery, and on the see of Smolensk he was, the page says, probably the successor of bishop Lazarus, since he went through the terrors of Batu's invasion together with his flock. It then quotes the description of the Kyiv Caves Lavra, which dates his killing by Batu to a year the day line does not: «Святой Меркурий, епископ Смоленский, убит Батыем в 1347 году».

The page closes with the wonder for which the Near caves keep him. After his blessed end his relics came up the Dnieper to Kyiv of themselves and were laid in the Near — the Antonievy — caves, and it is there that the calendar's own troparion and kontakion place him. The same page lists a second memory for him, 28 September with the fathers of those caves; the day page for 7 August does not repeat it, so it is recorded and not kept here as a feast.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 7 августа ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260807.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life6740.htm); read 13 October 2026.*
