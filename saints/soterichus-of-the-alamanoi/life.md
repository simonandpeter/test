# Soterichus, one of the Three Hundred Alamanoi

saint.gr's calendar for 18 July keeps nine saints on one line — «Άγιοι Ονησιφόρος, Παμμέγιστος, Παμφοδίτης, Παφνούτιος, Πηγών, Πολέμιος, Σωζόμενος, Σωτήριχος και Φώτιος» — and Soterichus is one of them. The page behind the line gives no life for any of the nine. What it says of them it says once, in a single clause under all nine names: that they are saints of the Church of Cyprus who are counted among the three hundred holy Alamanoi.

Of Soterichus in particular it records nothing at all: no birthplace, no year, no rank, no office, no account of how he lived or how he died, and no hymn. The one further thing on the page concerns the relic of the first of the nine, Onesiphorus, a portion of which it says is kept at the monastery of Kykkos in Cyprus.

So this folder holds what the calendar holds: a name, a company, a church and a day. The nine are kept together and are named one by one, which is why each of them stands here as his own folder rather than the nine being gathered into one.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2159/saint.aspx), which the nine share and which prints no life for any of them; read 1 October 2026.*
