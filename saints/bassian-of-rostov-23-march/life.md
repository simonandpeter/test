# Bassian of Rostov (23 March)

saint.gr's calendar for 23 Μαρτίου names a bishop of Rostov in Russia, and gives him two sentences: the holy Bassian lived in Russia in the fifteenth century; he was elected bishop of the city of Rostov and fell asleep in peace in the year 1481. The page heads him «(; - 1481)», so the year of his birth is a question mark there too.

That is the whole of what has been read. saint.gr gives him no birthplace, no name in the world, no monastery, no account of his episcopate and no relics, and prints neither a couplet nor a hymn under his name. Nothing has been added here from any other source and nothing from memory. The corpus keeps two other men called Bassian, one of Tiksna in the Totma country who reposed in 1624 and one whom the Romanian calendar names without a life on 10 octombrie, and nothing on this page identifies him with either; a third, an archbishop of the same see who reposed in 1516, saint.gr keeps on 25 Ιανουαρίου, and the two Rostov entries are kept apart here as the Greek calendar keeps them apart.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 23 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/23/index.aspx) and [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/80/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
