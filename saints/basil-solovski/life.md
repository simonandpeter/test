# Basil Solovski

saint.gr's calendar for 12 Ιανουαρίου names «Άγιος Βασίλειος Σολόβσκι ο Ιερομάρτυρας», and the page behind the line has one sentence for him and one refusal: «Ο Άγιος Ιερομάρτυς Βασίλειος Σολόβσκι, τιμάται υπό της Εκκλησίας της Ταλλίνης και Εσθονίας. Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον Βίο του Ιερομάρτυρα.»

That is: the holy hieromartyr Basil Solovski is honoured by the Church of Tallinn and Estonia, and we have no details of the hieromartyr's life. The calendar gives no year, no see, no parish and no manner of death, and prints no hymn; what it does record is the one church that keeps him, and the rank it keeps him in.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1404/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
