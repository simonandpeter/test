# Stephen of Constantinople

Stephen was the son of the emperor Basil the Macedonian and of Eudokia, and was born in the year 867. He had been a pupil and synkellos of the great [Photius](/saints/photius-of-constantinople), and after Photius's second patriarchate he took the patriarchal throne, in the year 886, while his own brother Leo the Wise was reigning — Leo held the empire from 886 to 912.

What he had in the way of income, saint.gr says, he used so far as he could for the help of the poor. He died in May 893, at the age of twenty-six, and was buried in the monastery of Saint George «in the Second», the house called the Sykiotes. It is thought probable, the page adds, that the first register of the bishops of the Ecumenical Throne was issued during his patriarchate; that is its own supposition and is recorded here as one.
