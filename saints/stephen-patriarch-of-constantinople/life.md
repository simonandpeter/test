# Stephen of Constantinople

Stephen was the son of the emperor Basil the Macedonian and of Eudokia, and was born in the year 867. He had been a pupil and synkellos of the great [Photius](/saints/photius-of-constantinople), and after Photius's second patriarchate he took the patriarchal throne, in the year 886, while his own brother Leo the Wise was reigning — Leo held the empire from 886 to 912.

What he had in the way of income, saint.gr says, he used so far as he could for the help of the poor. He died in May 893, at the age of twenty-six, and was buried in the monastery of Saint George «in the Second», the house called the Sykiotes. It is thought probable, the page adds, that the first register of the bishops of the Ecumenical Throne was issued during his patriarchate; that is its own supposition and is recorded here as one.

The Ohrid Prologue keeps him on the Julian 17 May, a day earlier than the Greek calendar, and its whole entry is three sentences. It says he was the son of the emperor Basil the Macedonian and the brother of the emperor Leo the Wise; that he came to the patriarchal throne after [Photius](/saints/photius-of-constantinople) and governed the Church of God from 889 to 893; and that he ended peacefully and stood before the Lord whom he greatly loved. The two books agree on the 893 and differ by three years on the beginning: saint.gr puts his accession in 886 and the Prologue in 889. Both are set down and neither is chosen here, and neither is a year this corpus has a field for.

The Russian calendar's notice of him is five sentences and three of them are facts the other books in this folder do not give. It makes him the younger son of the emperor Василий Македонянин and the full brother of the emperor Лев Премудрый, and says he received holy orders under the patriarch Фотий. When Фотий was compelled in 886 to leave the patriarchal throne, he was raised to the see of Constantinople. The calendar then says what kind of bishop he was: watchful on guard over his spiritual flock, merciful, a defender of the defenceless, a carer for widows and orphans, and marked by extreme abstinence. He died in peace in 893 and was buried in the Sikellion monastery.
