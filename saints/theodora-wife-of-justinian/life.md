# Theodora, wife of Justinian

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps her on the Julian 14 November, in one line with her husband: «Правоверного царя Иустиниана (565) и царицы Феодоры (548).» The life behind that line is headed for the two of them together, and almost all of it is his. Of her it gives a single sentence, at the very end, and this folder is that sentence: she was numbered among the saints together with the emperor, as his wife and of one mind with him, and she died in 548.

What that sentence adds is a shape rather than a story. She was at first, it says, a great sinner and a partisan of the monophysites; afterwards she repented and lived a virtuous life, keeping purity of soul and of body. The calendar gives her no birthplace, no family, no year of birth, and no part in any of the works it credits to [Justinian](/saints/justinian-the-great) — not the Fifth Council, not the churches, not the Code. The day page prints no troparion and no kontakion for her, nor for him: its hymns belong to three other saints it heads.

She is not the woman the same calendar keeps on the Julian 11 February. Both stand under one calendar name string, «Феодора Греческая, царица», but behind two different name pages — 2706 in February, 2707 here — and the February woman is the restorer of the icons, who died in 867, three centuries after this one. Nothing on this page connects them.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 14 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261114.html) and [the life of the two of them](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life2537.htm); read 5 October 2026.*
