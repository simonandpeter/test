# Paisius of Kyiv

saint.gr says the venerable Paisius, called Prokopios Grigorevich-Zaroskiy in the world, was born on the eighth of July 1821 in the town of Lubny in the province of Poltava. He studied at the ecclesiastical school of Kyiv-Podolskiy, and gave himself particularly to the study of holy Scripture and of the Lives of the saints. Early on the longing for the monastic state was cultivated in his heart, and so he entered the monastery of the Lavra of Kyiv and was tonsured a monk, taking the name Paisius.

He fought the good fight, the page says, following the way of folly for Christ's sake and reaching the high achievements of the ascetic life; and he fell asleep in peace in the year 1893. That is the whole of the notice: it gives not one act of the folly, not one saying, no elder, no disciple and no wonder, and it prints neither couplet nor hymn.
