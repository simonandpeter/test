# Alexis (Vvedensky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him second of two in one line on 10 декабря and gives neither a life. The line is «Сщмч. Николая и Алексия пресвитеров (1938).» — the hieromartyrs Nicholas and Alexis, presbyters, 1938 — and the calendar’s own name pages add the surnames the line leaves out: «Алексий (Введенский), протоиерей, сщмч.» and «Николай (Розов), протоиерей, сщмч.»

That is the whole of the record. He was an archpriest, which is what the name page says where the line says only presbyter; he died in 1938, the second of the two worst years of the persecution in which the Russian church counts its new martyrs; and he is set down with [Nicholas (Rozov), 10 December](/saints/nicholas-rozov-10-december) in one line and under one year, the two surnames being unlike and the calendar saying nothing of what joined the men.

The day page carries eleven hymns and not one is under either of these names. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 10 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261210.html); read 13 October 2026.*
