# Boniface of Ross

saint.gr's calendar for 14 Μαρτίου names him «Άγιος Βονιφάτιος Επίσκοπος Ρος της Σκωτίας», and heads him «(; - 630)». The page says that the holy Boniface — it gives the Latin form of the name in brackets — was born in Italy and went over to Scotland for the spreading of the divine word; that by his missionary work he reformed the manners of the inhabitants of various Scottish provinces; that the Church chose him bishop of the city of Ross; and that he fell asleep in holiness in the year 630.

That is the whole of it. The page gives no city of Italy, no account of his preaching, no church of his, no relic and no hymn, and the rest of what it prints is a note on the name, from the Latin bonus and fatus, the blessed one. What is recorded here is the commemoration, the country he came from, the country he went to, the see, and the year of his repose.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 14 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/14/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/53/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
