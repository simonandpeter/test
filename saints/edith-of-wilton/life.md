# Edith of England

The Greek synaxarion keeps the English saint Edith — Edithac, it spells her — on 16 September and says it has no details of her life. This entry records the line and nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 16 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2451/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
