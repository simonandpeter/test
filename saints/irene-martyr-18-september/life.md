# Irene (18 September)

Irene and Sophia were martyred by the sword; that is the whole of the Greek synaxarion’s notice for 18 September. Their heads cut off, the verse says, Irene and Sophia saw you, Word, the peace beyond understanding. This entry records it and nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 18 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2474/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
