# Julian

Julian came from the village of Crinteus near Ancyra in Galatia and was a priest worthy of great respect. When Licinius proclaimed persecution against the Christians (307–323) he fled with forty-two Christian fellow-townsmen to the mountains and hid; but once he came down from the mountains to fetch water, and the pagans offering sacrifice at the temple of Hecate there saw him, seized him and led him to the ruler of Ancyra, who asked where the rest of his Christian townsmen were hiding; he refused to betray them and was horribly tortured, a red-hot helmet set on his head, and at last beheaded as he prayed — mingling contests with ascetic sweat, the verse says, Julian takes a double crown.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 12 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2412/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
