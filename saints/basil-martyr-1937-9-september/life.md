# Basil (Shikalov)

Basil Shikalov, a layman, was killed in the terror of 1937. The Russian calendar keeps him on 9 September of the old style, in one line with the hieromartyrs Zachariah, archbishop of Voronezh, the priests Sergius, Joseph and Alexius, and the deacon Demetrius; the calendar's written life for the day is the common life of Alexius (Uspensky) and Basil, from which this entry is taken.

*After the Православный церковный календарь (days.pravoslavie.ru), [the common life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4743.htm); read 30 August 2026.*
