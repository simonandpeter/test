# Helen of Dečani

saint.gr's whole account of Helen the princess is three sentences, and each of them fixes her by somebody else. She was the sister of the Serbian king Stefan III Uroš, whose reign the page gives as 1321 to 1331. She was distantly related to [Joasaph of Meteora](/saints/joasaph-of-meteora), whom the page keeps on 20 Απριλίου. And she lived in holiness, and her holy relics are kept with reverence at the monastery of Dečani.

The page adds nothing else: no year of birth, no year of death, no account of the holiness it attributes to her, no monastic name and no hymn. Of her whole life what is recorded here is a brother, a kinsman, a manner of living and a resting place.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 21 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/21/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1700/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
