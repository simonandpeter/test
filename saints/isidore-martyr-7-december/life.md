# Isidore (7 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 7 December keeps three martyrs in one entry and names all three: Isidore, [Akepsimas](/saints/akepsimas-martyr-7-december) and [Leo](/saints/leo-martyr-7-december). The whole of the life it gives them is one sentence — that the holy Isidore, Akepsimas and Leo were martyred by fire. No country, no reign, no persecutor and no year is printed for any of them.

The couplet the page sets over the entry is addressed to Isidore by name: be burned together with your two fellow contestants, Isidore, for you have obtained gifts from God equal to theirs. That is the only thing said of him apart from the shared sentence, and nothing further is claimed here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3186/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
