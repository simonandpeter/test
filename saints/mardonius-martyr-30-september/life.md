# Mardonius (30 September)

The Greek synaxarion says only that Mardonius was martyred after his eyes were burned out with red-hot coals; its verse speaks of the seal of burning he bears as the token of his passion. It gives no date and no place, and nothing in it makes him the martyr of that name at Nicomedia who suffered with the bishop Anthimus.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 30 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/1017/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
