# Nicholas (Postnikov)

His line in the calendar is six words long, and it is the whole of what is recorded here. The Сретенский monastery’s calendar for 28 марта ст. ст. prints «Св. Николая , исп., пресвитера (1931)» — the holy Nicholas, confessor, presbyter, 1931 — and the name page behind that forename gives the surname the line leaves out: «Николай (Постников), протоиерей, сщмч.»

The two pages do not agree about him. The day line calls him a confessor, which is how this calendar prints a man who died in confinement rather than under sentence of death; the name page calls him a hieromartyr. Nothing here settles which is meant, and the stored rank follows the line that is cited. The name page also makes him a protopriest, and the office recorded here is the plainer `Priest`, which is the word this site can render in all four of its languages.

So nothing is said of where he was born, where he served, when or by whom he was arrested, or where he died. There is no life page for him on the day and none on his name page, and 1931 is the only year the calendar gives. His name page adds two days to this one — the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church, and the synaxis of the saints of Ryazan, which is the only thing in either page that points to a part of Russia.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 28 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260328.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13708.html); read 8 October 2026.*
