# Auxentius of Laodicea

saint.gr's whole notice of him is one sentence: we have no details of the life of the holy martyr. The day's line names him and places him — «Άγιος Αυξέντιος ο εν Λαοδικεία Φρυγίας μάρτυρας», Auxentius the martyr in Laodicea of Phrygia — and the page adds a gloss on the name, which it takes from αυξάνω, the great one. Beyond the place and the rank it gives no century, no emperor, no judge, no manner of death, no relics and no hymn, and it does not even print a distich for him.

Its heading gives the feast as fixed, kept on 28 July of every year, and the entry carries neither an asterisk nor the sentence by which saint.gr marks an entry it will not vouch for. This folder says exactly what the page says: that the Greek calendar keeps a martyr of that name on this day in that town, and that nobody wrote down his life.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/28/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2138/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
