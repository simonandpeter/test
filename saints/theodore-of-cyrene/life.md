# Theodore of Cyrene

saint.gr's calendar for 4 Ιουλίου keeps Theodore, bishop of Cyrene in Libya. The page says he lived in the years of the emperor Diocletian and came from Cyrene itself, and that he was a copyist of sacred books, which he distributed to the churches for the enlightenment of the faithful. His own son Leo denounced him — probably in the year 299, the page says — to the local governor Dignianus, charging that he was converting many of the idolaters to the Christian faith by means of his books.

The governor summoned him, and Theodore came before him accompanied by many Christians, among whom were [Kyprilla](/saints/kyprilla-of-cyrene), [Aroa](/saints/aroa-companion-of-kyprilla) and [Loukia](/saints/loukia-companion-of-kyprilla). He was told to hand over his books and to deny Christ. He did not obey the governor's order, and so he suffered hard tortures: first they scourged him without mercy, then they crucified him, and then they scraped his wounds with hides. Afterwards they cut out his tongue and threw him into prison, where he gave up his last breath.

The page adds that his synaxis is held in the martyrium of the holy martyr Theodore at Rhesion. It prints no hymn under his name, and besides the account above it carries only the synaxarion couplet: «Eπί σκότους θανόντι τω Θεοδώρω, / Tο του Προφήτου πρώιμον φως ερράγη» — for Theodore, who died in darkness, the early light of the Prophet broke.
