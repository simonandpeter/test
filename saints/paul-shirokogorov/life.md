# Paul (Shirokogorov)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him for 21 февраля ст. ст. in one line that it gives to two — «Сщмч. Константина пресвитера, Павла диакона (1938).» — a hieromartyr presbyter and a hieromartyr deacon, both of 1938. The line carries the forenames only, declined, and no surname for either of them.

The name link behind the second of those names carries the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line leaves out: «Павел (Широкогоров), диакон, сщмч.», a deacon of that name. Two entries on one line are two people, and they are kept apart here by the names and the offices the calendar's own links give them.

It prints no life page for him and no hymn under his name; the hymns it sets on this day belong to two of the older saints it heads and to an icon of the Mother of God. It gives him no parish, no diocese, no place and no manner of death. Nothing further is written here, and nothing is reasoned out from the surname or from the year he shares with the other.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 21 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260221.html); read 3 October 2026.*
