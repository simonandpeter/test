# Marcus, son of Tranquillinus

saint.gr's calendar for 18 December keeps ten people together and names every one of them: Nikostratos and his wife Zoe, Tranquillinus and his wife Marcia, Claudius, Tiburtius, Castor, Castulus, Marcus and Marcellinus. They all suffered, the page says, in the persecution Diocletian set in motion against the Christians, whose years it gives as 284 to 304, and they suffered together with Saint [Sebastian](/saints/sebastian-of-rome), whom the same day's calendar keeps.

Marcus and [Marcellinus](/saints/marcellinus-son-of-tranquillinus) they killed with lances. The two of them were brothers and the sons of [Tranquillinus](/saints/tranquillinus) and [Marcia](/saints/marcia-wife-of-tranquillinus), and they were ordained deacons by the bishop Gaius when their parents came to the faith.

That is the whole of what the page gives for this company: the ten names, the one reign, and for each of them the manner of the death. It names no city and no judge, gives no year of birth for any of them and no year inside the reign, lists no relic and prints no hymn of its own; the day's apolytikion stands on Sebastian's page and names Sebastian. The others of the ten here are [Nicostratus](/saints/nicostratus-18-december), [Tranquillinus](/saints/tranquillinus), [Marcia](/saints/marcia-wife-of-tranquillinus), [Claudius](/saints/claudius-18-december), [Tiburtius](/saints/tiburtius-18-december), [Castor](/saints/castor-18-december), [Castulus](/saints/castulus-18-december), [Marcellinus](/saints/marcellinus-son-of-tranquillinus), and [Zoe](/saints/zoe-18-december), whom this corpus already kept from the Romanian calendar.

The Ohrid Prologue keeps him on the same menologion day, inside the heading „Свети мученик Севастијан и други с њим.“ — Sebastian and the others with him — and names him in the body. He and his brother [Marcellinus](/saints/marcellinus-son-of-tranquillinus) were in prison for Christ and already on the edge of denying him and bowing to the idols, it says, when [Sebastian](/saints/sebastian-of-rome) steadied them in the faith and made them brave for martyrdom.

It was to these two brothers that Sebastian was speaking when his face shone: as he was telling them not to fear death for Christ, all who were present saw his face bright as the face of an angel of God. Their father [Tranquillinus](/saints/tranquillinus) was healed by him of eleven years of gout and pain in the legs and baptised with his whole house. The two brothers were tortured and pierced through with spears. The book gives no year for their death, and names no place for it but the Rome of the rest of the entry.
