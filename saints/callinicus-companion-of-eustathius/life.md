# Callinicus, companion of Eustathius the Roman

The Greek synaxarion keeps [Eustathius the Roman](/saints/eustathius-the-roman) and Callinicus together on 28 September and says only that they were martyred by the sword, probably at Rome. Its verse plays on the name: a Roman, and robust besides, is the martyr Eustathius, contending to the sword. It gives no date.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 28 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2469/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
