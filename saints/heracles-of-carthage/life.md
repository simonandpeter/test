# Heracles of Carthage

saint.gr's calendar for 11 Μαρτίου names him with his companion, «Άγιοι Ηρακλής και Ζωσιμάς», and the page behind that line holds one sentence about the two of them: that the holy martyrs Heracles and Zosimas were martyred at Carthage. To that it adds, in its own words, that we have no further details of the life and the martyrdom of the saints. There is no year, no persecutor, no manner of death and no hymn.

So what is recorded here is the commemoration, the city, and the name Heracles, which the page writes «Ηρακλής» — not the Ηράκλειος of the other men of that family of names in the corpus, but Ἡρακλῆς. [Zosimas of Carthage](/saints/zosimas-of-carthage) is kept with him on the same day and in the same line, and the two of them are one entry of the Greek calendar split into the two names it prints.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/11/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3819/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
