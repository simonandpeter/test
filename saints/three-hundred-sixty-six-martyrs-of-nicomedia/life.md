# The Three Hundred and Sixty-six Martyrs of Nicomedia

Three hundred and sixty-six martyrs of Nicomedia who died by the sword — the number of a leap year, the synaxarion’s verse remarks, set down at the year’s end. Nothing more is recorded.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 31 Αυγούστου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/860/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
