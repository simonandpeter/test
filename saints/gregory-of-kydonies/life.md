# Gregory

Gregory was metropolitan of Kydonies from 22 July 1908 to 3 October 1922, and before that of Tiberiopolis and Strumica (1902–1908); Anastasius Antoniadis or Saatsoglou in the world — Orologas, by his own rendering — he was born at Magnesia in Asia Minor in 1864, and as a preacher was among the first to use the demotic language. In all three sees he worked with zeal and success for the defence of the Greek national cause, working for it especially with the metropolitan of Drama, [Chrysostom Kalafatis](/saints/chrysostom-of-smyrna), later the martyred metropolitan of Smyrna; at Tiberiopolis and Strumica he struggled not only against the Turks but especially against the Bulgarian committee, whose members tried many times to murder him, and when the Turkish government learned of his national work it forced the Patriarchate to move him to the newly founded see of Kydonies, where he went on. In 1918 he was charged by the Turks with high treason, tried twice before the court-martial of Smyrna, condemned and imprisoned; freed on 16 October 1918, he stayed in his province after the Greek army took Kydonies in 1919, and when the Greek authorities withdrew he urged the elders to move the inhabitants to Mytilene to escape massacre, but was not heeded — the tragedy began on 22 August 1922, when irregular Turkish troops slaughtered four thousand Greeks of Kydonies near Phraneli. Despite the humiliations he suffered from the Turkish authorities he visited them and struggled to save and feed his flock, and when on 15 September he learned of the slaughter of [Ambrose of Moschonisia](/saints/ambrose-of-moschonisia) and six thousand of his people he struggled superhumanly and got the Turks to let Greek ships come from Mytilene under the American flag, guaranteed by the American Red Cross, and take off twenty thousand of the thirty-five thousand Greeks of Kydonies. He refused to leave; on 30 September the Turks arrested and imprisoned him, tortured him horribly in prison, and on 3 October put him to death with other priests and notables of Kydonies who had also been arrested.
