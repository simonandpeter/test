# Pantoleon (17 September)

The synaxis of Charalampus, Pantoleon and their company is celebrated “in the Deuteron”, and the Greek synaxarion has no more details of their lives; its verse says Charalampus was rejoicing as he went to the slaughter, and Pantoleon was a lion before the knife. The Greek calendar keeps them on 17 September; this entry records it and nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 17 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2457/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
