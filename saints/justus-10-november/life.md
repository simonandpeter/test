# Justus (10 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 10 Νοεμβρίου names Justus and gives no life. Its line for the day is «Άγιος Justus», with the name in Latin letters and no Greek form of it anywhere on the page, and the whole of the notice behind that line is one sentence: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Άγγλου Αγίου» — we have no details of the English saint's life.

So the name, the day and the one word English are the whole of what is recorded here. The page gives him no century, no see, no monastery, no rank, no manner of death, no year, no couplet and no hymn, and quotes nothing of him from any other book.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/10/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2975/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
