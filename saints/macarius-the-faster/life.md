# Macarius the Faster of the Kyiv Caves

The Greek calendar's line for him on 19 Ιανουαρίου is «Όσιος Μακάριος ο Νηστευτής εκ Ρωσίας», the venerable Macarius the Faster, out of Russia, and the page behind it is two sentences long. It says that he was a monk in the monastery of [Saint Anthony](/saints/anthony-of-the-caves) of the Great Lavra of Kyiv, and that he lived and fell asleep in peace in the twelfth century.

The epithet the calendar gives him — the Faster — is all it says of his manner of life. No year, no birthplace, no office and no miracle is printed for him, and no hymn. The Greek day keeps a second Macarius out of Russia beside him, [the Deacon](/saints/macarius-the-deacon), of a later century and another of the Kyiv houses; the two are distinct lines with distinct pages, and nothing is carried from one to the other.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 19 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/19/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1133/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 19 января ст. ст. — [the Russian day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260119.html), read 3 October 2026, which keeps him as «Прп. Макария, постника Печерского (XII)».*
