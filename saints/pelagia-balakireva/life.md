# Pelagia (Balakireva)

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 17 июня ст. ст. prints her in three words and a year: «Мц. Пелагии (1943).». The line gives the forename in the genitive and the class word «Мц.», a woman martyr, and no surname. The name link behind her place in it supplies the calendar’s own nominative with the surname, «Пелагия (Балакирева), мц.», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from.

The name page carries no text. It heads «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 17 июня», offers an icon under «Иконы», and stops: there is no life, no article, no place, and no account of her death in 1943. The calendar’s line and that heading are the whole of what this source gives her, and the icon was not opened. Nothing is supplied for them here.

The line’s «Мц.» makes her a martyr, and the name link gives her no grade and no monastic rank, so none is written. The second day the name page lists is the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, a synaxis and not a second feast, so she renders on 17 июня ст. ст. only. Hers is the latest of the three twentieth-century names this day carries.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 17 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260617.html) and [her name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13727.html); read 9 October 2026.*
