# Abercius (5 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 5 December names Abercius and gives him one line of life: the holy Abercius was martyred by the sword. The page prints no country, no persecutor, no reign and no year, and it joins him to no other name on the day. The couplet set over his name is all it adds: great, Abercius, is the prize of your beheading, for your crown is no wreath of wild olive but heaven itself.

The Greek calendar keeps other men of this name on other days, and this page offers nothing by which to join this one to any of them. What is recorded here is the 5 December line and the couplet, and no more than that.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3158/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
