# Eleutherius the Cubicularius

saint.gr's calendar for 15 December keeps a second Eleutherius on the same day as the hieromartyr, Eleutherius the Cubicularius, and its page makes him a wholly different man. He was a man of standing in Constantinople, a senator in rank, who had been brought up and educated inside the palace. Some of the synaxaristes, the page says, put the height of his glory in the time of Maximian, whose years it gives as 286 to 305, and say that he was on friendly terms with him; but that, it adds in its own voice, does not seem likely.

Eleutherius wanted to become a Christian, and pleading reasons of health he took himself away from the noise of the court and settled in a village near the river Sangarios, where he built an underground house of prayer. There he was baptised, and there he received the immaculate Mysteries.

When the king learnt of him he came to that place, found him out, and tried in many ways to bring him back to the worship of the idols; and failing in it, he had him beheaded. That is the whole of the page. Because it doubts the one reign it reports, no years are recorded for him here, and no hymn: the hymns of the day stand on the hieromartyr's page and name the hieromartyr.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 15 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/15/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3268/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
