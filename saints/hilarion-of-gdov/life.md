# Hilarion of Gdov

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 21 октября ст. ст. as «Прп. Илариона Псковоезерского, Гдовского (1476)» — of the Pskov lake and of Gdov — and carries two notices of him, which agree on everything of substance. He was a disciple of [Euphrosynus of Pskov](/saints/euphrosynus-of-pskov), whose day the calendar gives as 15 May.

About 1460 he founded a monastery in honour of the Protection of the Mother of God on the bank of the river Zhelcha, which runs into Lake Peipus, not far from Gdov, and laboured and prayed there. The house stood on the frontier of the Livonian Order, so the monks were under constant attack from it; and in spite of that and of a want of means, the calendar says he established a high standard of devotion in the monastery and carried out large works to fortify and improve it.

He fell asleep on 28 March 1476 and was buried by the north doors of the iconostasis in the church of the Protection of the Mother of God in the monastery he had founded. A church of the Nativity of Christ was built in the house afterwards, and its left aisle was named for him as the founder of the Gdov monastery.
