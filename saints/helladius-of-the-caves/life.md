# Helladius of the Caves

The Russian calendar keeps him on 4 October in one line with [Onesimus of the Caves](/saints/onesimus-of-the-caves) — «Прпп. Елладия и Онисима Печерских (XII-XIII)» — and that line, plus his entry in the site's index of names, is the whole of what it has. The index heads him «Елладий Печерский, затворник, постник», which gives two things the day line does not: he was a recluse, and he was a faster, one of those whose asceticism was remembered for abstinence in particular.

It also keeps him on 28 September with the synaxis the abbreviation «Печер.(Б)» marks, the saints of the Near Caves, which is where in the Kyiv Caves lavra he is to be looked for. No life is carried for him on the site: the page his companion's life sits on names only Onesimus. The two centuries the day gives, the twelfth or the thirteenth, are not narrowed anywhere, so they stand here as an interval rather than a guess.

*After the Russian church calendar's [4 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261004.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/769.html); read 4 October 2026.*
