# Melanippus

Melanippus was martyred by fire. That is the whole of the synaxarion’s notice for the martyr kept on 2 September, with its verse; the Synaxaristes have nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 2 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/917/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
