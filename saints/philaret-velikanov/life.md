# Philaret (Velikanov)

The Russian calendar's 11 October is an Optina day and an apostle's day, and its last paragraph is one line of five words that belongs to neither: «Сщмчч. Филарета и Александра пресвитеров (1918)» — Philaret and Alexander, hieromartyrs, presbyters, 1918. «Сщмч.» is the mark of a priest killed for the faith. The site's index of names supplies the surnames the day line leaves out and the grade it does not give, heading his page «Филарет (Великанов), иерей, сщмч.» — a priest of the simple grade.

1918 is the first year of the campaign against the clergy, and the calendar does not say what it did to him: no birth, no diocese, no parish, no charge, no place of death. His index entry keeps him here and on 26 January with the whole company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and carries no life. The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian saints are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026 even with a browser user-agent, so the line stands as the line. [Alexander (Grivsky)](/saints/alexander-grivsky) is the other half of it, and the calendar gives him one day more than it gives this one.

*After the Russian church calendar's [11 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261011.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9824.html); read 4 October 2026.*
