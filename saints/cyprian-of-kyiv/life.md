# Cyprian

Cyprian, metropolitan of Kyiv, was of Serbian descent and lived as an ascetic on the Holy Mountain; for his virtuous life the patriarch of Constantinople Philotheus, kept on 11 October, appointed him metropolitan of Kyiv, and he reposed in 1406. That is the whole of the Greek synaxarion’s notice for 16 September; this entry records it and nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 16 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2449/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
