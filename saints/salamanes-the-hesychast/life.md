# Salamanes the Hesychast

Salamanes came of a small town built on the western bank of the river Euphrates and called Kapersana. Because he loved the life of the desert he followed the road of the monastic state, and set up his cell near the Euphrates. saint.gr's account then turns on how completely he kept silence: whatever was done to him or about him, he neither resisted nor spoke, and the page says that he made himself altogether dead to the present life, struggling only to keep the will of God, who so loved man that he gave himself up for man's salvation.

So he lived in a manner pleasing to God, the page ends, and fell asleep in peace. The distich over him reads «Οἴχῃ χαμερποῦς καὶ χαμαιζήλου βίου, Ὑψηλὲ πράξει καὶ λόγῳ Σαλαμάνη» — thou art gone from the life that creeps and covets the ground, O Salamanes, high in deed and word. No year of his birth or of his repose is printed for him, and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 23 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/23/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3375/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
