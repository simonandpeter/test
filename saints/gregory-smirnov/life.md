# Gregory (Smirnov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Gregory on 29 December and gives no life. Its line is «Сщмчч. Павла пресвитера и Григория диакона (1918).» — the hieromartyrs Paul the presbyter and Gregory the deacon, 1918 — and the calendar’s own numbered name page for this memory carries the surname the line leaves out: «Григорий (Смирнов), диакон, сщмч.»

That is the whole of the record. He was a deacon; the year of death the line gives is 1918; the calendar keeps the memory on this day, inside the afterfeast of the Nativity. The line names two men and both are in this draft. The day page prints seven hymns — two for the gravedigger of the Kyiv Caves, two for the abbot of the Unsleeping and three for the infants of Bethlehem — and none under this name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 29 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261229.html); read 13 October 2026.*
