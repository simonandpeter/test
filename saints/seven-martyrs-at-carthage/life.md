# The Seven Martyrs at Carthage

saint.gr's whole entry for them is two sentences, and the first is uncertain about where it happened: the seven holy martyrs bore their witness at Carthage, or, by other accounts, in Chaldia — which the page glosses, in brackets, as Chalcedon. And then: we have no details of the life of the holy martyrs.

Above the notice stands the synaxarion's distich, which says that seven contenders, put to death by the sword, stand now with Christ the Holy One. The page gives no name, no year, no century, no emperor and no hymn: what the corpus holds of them is a number, a manner of death and two candidate cities.
