# Lucia of Rome, companion of Rix

Doxologia's calendar for 6 iulie names her and prints only a short notice, which is all this folder holds. The holy martyr Lucia, it says, was seized by Rix, the deputy of the governor of the province of Campania in Italy, who tried to compel her to sacrifice to the gods. She refused outright, and confessed our Lord Jesus Christ with such courage that Rix himself received the Christian teaching and holy baptism.

The governor, hearing what had happened, ordered the two of them beheaded, and so they received the crown of martyrdom. The page gives no year, no place of burial and nothing of her life before her arrest. She is not the Lucia the Greek calendar keeps on 17 September, the widow of Rome betrayed by her son Eutropius, whom this corpus holds under her own name.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 6 iulie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/6-iulie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfintei-mucenite-lucia); read 24 September 2026.*
