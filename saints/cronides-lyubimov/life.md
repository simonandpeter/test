# Cronides (Lyubimov)

Constantine Petrovich Lyubimov was born in 1859 at Левкиево in the Волоколамский district of the province of Moscow, and took the name Cronides in religion. In 1915 he was appointed superior of the Trinity-Sergius Lavra, and so it fell out that he was its last superior before it was closed. For seventeen years after the closure he lived at Загорск, going first to the church of Peter and Paul and, when that was taken by the Renovationists, to the Кукуевская church, where he liked to sing in the choir.

He remained, in fact if not in law, the head of the house the Bolsheviks had shut: he kept the seal of the superior of the Lavra and used it, and by his blessing some fifty of its hieromonks, come back from camps and prisons, were given parishes in the country around Загорск. That is what the case against him was made of.

He was arrested in November 1937, by which time he was entirely blind, and held in the Таганская prison in Moscow. Fifteen people were tried on his case, ten of them monks who had been of the Lavra; eleven were shot and four were given ten years in the corrective labour camps. The charge against him was «leadership of a counter-revolutionary monarchist group of monks and clergy». He was shot on 27 November — 10 December by the new reckoning — 1937 at the NKVD range at the settlement of Бутово outside Moscow and buried in a common grave, and he was numbered among the new martyrs and confessors of Russia by the jubilee council of bishops in August 2000.

His name page lists one other day, «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church. One row per church is the schema, so that day is recorded here and he does not render on it.
