# Theodosius of the East

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαρτίου names him second in a company of four entries: «Άγιοι Κοδράτος, Θεοδόσιος, Μανουήλ και άλλοι Τεσσαράκοντα μάρτυρες από την Ανατολή» — the holy Quadratus, Theodosius, Manuel and forty other martyrs from the East. The page tells the passion of all of them in one paragraph, in the plural throughout, and gives no year, no city, no emperor and no judge by name: they lived, it says, in the years of the Church's persecutions under the idolatrous emperors.

Seized and pressed to deny Christ, they repeated their confession many times and declared that they would never desert the standard of the Gospel. The judge, at his wits' end, ordered their sides laid open, and when that torture too proved powerless against their devotion to Christ he ordered them beheaded.

His own couplet the page heads «Eις τον Θεοδόσιον»: «Θεοδόσιος τῷ Θεῷ ζῶν καὶ μόνῳ, Ζωὴν δι' αὐτὸν ἐκ ξίφους καταστρέφει» — Theodosius, living to God and to him alone, for his sake ends his life by the sword. Of the name the page says only «ΘΕΟΔΟΣΙΟΣ: Ο θεόδοτος, το θεϊκό δώρο», the God-given, the divine gift. His fellows are [Quadratus](/saints/quadratus-of-the-east), [Manuel](/saints/manuel-of-the-east) and [the forty](/saints/forty-martyrs-of-the-east). No relic and no hymn are recorded.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3861/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
