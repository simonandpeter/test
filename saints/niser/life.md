# Niser

saint.gr's calendar for 6 December keeps the holy Niser, and begins by saying how little is known of him: he was a martyr unknown to the Synaxaristes of Saint Nicodemus and to the printed Menaia, and he is written in the Parisian Codex 1621, where there is also a short biographical notice of him. What the life here says is that notice, as the page reports it, and nothing besides.

According to it, then, he lived in the years of Maximian, which the page dates 286 to 305, and because he was a Christian he was brought before Julius and Aelianus. When he was asked whether he persisted in the worship of Christ, Niser answered that he did, until death. Then they stripped him and scourged his body with red-hot pellets, and afterwards imprisoned him. But when he was examined again he confessed his faith in Christ, and they stripped him at once, flayed him alive, and then threw him into the fire, where he found a martyr's end for the glory of Christ.
