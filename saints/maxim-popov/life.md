# Maxim (Popov)

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 17 июня ст. ст. prints him in one short line: «Прп. Максима исп (1934).». The line gives the forename in the genitive, the class word «Прп.» and the abbreviation «исп» for исповедник, a confessor, and no surname. The name link behind his place in it supplies the calendar’s own nominative with the surname and the grade, «Максим (Попов), иеромонах, прп., исповедник», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from.

The name page carries nothing else. It heads «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 17 июня» and then stops: no life, no article, no monastery, no account of his confession or of his death in 1934. The calendar’s line and that heading are the whole of what this source gives him. Nothing is supplied for them here, and in particular the surname is not read as a biography.

The line’s «Прп.» makes him venerable and «исп» a confessor; the name link adds «иеромонах», a hieromonk. The second day the name page lists is the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, a synaxis and not a second feast, so he renders on 17 июня ст. ст. only. The day page prints two hymns, both for the martyrs its first line heads, and none under his name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 17 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260617.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9226.html); read 9 October 2026.*
