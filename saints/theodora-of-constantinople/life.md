# Theodora of Constantinople

The Охридски пролог gives this Theodora four lines on the Julian 30 December, and they are the whole of what this folder records. She was a nun, and the novice of [Basil the New of Constantinople](/saints/basil-the-new-of-constantinople), the ascetic of that city who saw into the secrets of men and foretold what was to come. The book heads her „Преподобна Теодора Цариградска.“ and cross-references his own day, 26 March, so that the reader may find him.

After her death she appeared to Григорије, another of Basil's disciples, and described to him all twenty of the toll-houses through which her soul had passed before it entered into eternal rest by the prayers of her elder. That vision, written down, is the whole reason her name is remembered: it is the account the Church has read for a thousand years on what the soul meets after death. She reposed on 30 December 940.

The entry says nothing else, and this folder therefore says nothing else. It names no city of her birth, no monastery, no year of birth and no age; it does not enumerate the twenty toll-houses, only counts them; and it does not say which Gregory of that name received the vision, so his name stays here as the book prints it. The Prologue prints no hymn for her, or for any saint of the day.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 30. децембар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-01-12&prolog=1); read 8 October 2026.*
