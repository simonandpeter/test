# Joachim of Novgorod

Joachim of Novgorod is one of the twelve hierarchs of Novgorod whom saint.gr's calendar keeps together on 10 Φεβρουαρίου, and the page gives the year of his repose as 1030. The feast, it says, was established after Saint [John](/saints/john-of-novgorod), archbishop of Novgorod, who fell asleep on 7 September 1186, appeared to Saint Euthymius, archbishop of Novgorod, who fell asleep on 11 March 1458, and told him to keep a vigil in the cathedral church of the Holy Wisdom for the blessed hierarchs of that local church who had pleased God by their lives and their labours.

The same feast, the page adds, is kept also on 4 October, the day of the memory of Saint Hierotheus, bishop of Athens, according to what Saint John had said, and on the third Sunday after Pentecost. The twelve are then named in one list, each with the year he fell asleep: Ιωακείμ (κοιμήθηκε 1030 μ.Χ.), Λουκάς (1060), Γερμανός (1096), Αρκάδιος (1162), Γρηγόριος (1193), Μαρτύριος (1199), Αντώνιος (1231), Βασίλειος (1352), Συμεών (1421), Γεννάδιος (1505), Ποιμήν (1571), Αφφώνιος (1652). They are twelve entries enumerated by name, and so twelve folders rather than one heading.

Of Joachim himself the page records the name, the see and the year, and nothing else: no parentage, no account of his episcopate, no hymn and no relics. Its heading for him is «Αγιος Ιωακείμ (; - 1030)», which leaves the birth open. The page's gloss on the name is that Ιωακείμ is the Hebrew for the one raised up by God.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/10/index.aspx) and [the page for the twelve hierarchs](https://www.saint.gr/1366/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
