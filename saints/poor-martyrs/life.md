# The Poor Martyrs

saint.gr's calendar for 18 August keeps a company it names only by their poverty: the Poor Martyrs. The page gives them no names, no number, no city and no year, and tells their story entirely through a temple they pulled down. That temple had been built by the twin stonemasons [Florus](/saints/florus-of-illyricum) and [Laurus](/saints/laurus-of-illyricum), whom the same calendar keeps on the same day.

The money for it, the page says, was given to the two brothers by the king — Licinius — for the raising of the building, and the brothers did not keep it. They gave it away to this crowd of poor men. Afterwards, at the urging of Florus and Laurus, the poor destroyed the idols of the temple and turned it, in a manner of speaking, into a Christian one.

For that Licinius had them put to death by fire. The synaxarion distich the page prints plays on the two words of their story, their poverty and the flame: «Πένητες ἄνδρες πλοῦτον εὗρον ἀθρόον, Τὸ σύντροφον δός, εἰς τὸ πῦρ λελοιπότες» — poor men who found wealth all at once, having left in the fire the thing they were reared with. saint.gr prints nothing further about them, and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2274/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
