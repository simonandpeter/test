# Joel

Joel was of the tribe of Reuben, from the village of Metomor, and the life doxologia.ro prints of him is a single short paragraph. He prophesied the desolation of Jerusalem, and he gave counsel about fasting, as the best means by which God is made merciful. He prophesied also of the coming of the Holy Spirit upon all flesh, and of the dread Judgement to come.

Then he ended his life in peace, and was buried in his own land. The Romanian life sets him eight hundred years before the birth of Christ, and reads his name as meaning the love of God, or else the firstfruits, or the beginning of God.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 19 October. saint.gr says Joel was one of the twelve are called the lesser prophets, the son of Bathuel, of the tribe of Reuben — adding that others would have him of the tribe of Gad — and that he prophesied when Joash was king over the tribe of Judah, whose reign it gives as 878 to 838 before Christ. His prophetic book, the page says, has been called poetic, finely wrought and vivid, an ornament of Hebrew letters, and it quotes his word on repentance: return to me with your whole heart, with fasting and weeping, and rend your hearts and not your garments. In its three chapters he foretells the pouring out of the gifts of the Holy Spirit upon the Church and the signs that will go before the Lord's second coming. He died in peace, and the page ends by saying that the prophet Joel kept on 30 March is another man.
