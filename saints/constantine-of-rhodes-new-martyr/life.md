# Constantine of Rhodes

Doxologia's life gives him as born on the island of Hydra in Greece, the son of Michael and Marina. At the age of eighteen he left Hydra for the island of Rhodes, and there entered the service of the Turkish commander Hasan Kapetan. In Rhodes he went over to Islam and took the name Hasan, in honour of his master; for three years he received great honours and was content with what he had become.

Later, however, he felt remorse at having denied Christ. He gave alms to the Orthodox, prayed with bitter tears, and showed great reverence to every Orthodox priest he met, and he resolved to expiate his sin by martyrdom. So he found a spiritual father, confessed to him, and asked his blessing to seek martyrdom; and the priest, seeing how young he was, counselled him to wait. Constantine obeyed, and left Rhodes.

He travelled to the town of Krimi, then to Constantinople, and from there to the Holy Mountain. At the monastery of Iviron he prepared himself for martyrdom, and with the blessing of the fathers he returned to Rhodes and presented himself to the commander, confessing Christ with great courage. The torments he endured were terrible, the page says; and at the last he was hanged, on 14 November 1800.

Today, doxologia adds, there is a beautiful church dedicated to him at his birthplace on Hydra, where his holy relics are kept.
