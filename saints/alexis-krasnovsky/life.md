# Alexis (Krasnovsky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Alexis first in the last entry of 29 July and gives no life. Its line is «Сщмч. Алексия пресвитера, прмч. Пахомия (1938).» — the hieromartyr Alexis the presbyter and the venerable martyr Pachomius, 1938 — and the calendar's own name page for him adds the surname and keeps the grade the line gives: «Алексий (Красновский), иерей, сщмч.», Alexis Krasnovsky, priest.

The line grades its two men differently, which a joint line on this calendar does not always do: a hieromartyr and a venerable martyr, a parish priest and a monk in orders. The second of them is [Pachomius (Rusin)](/saints/pachomius-rusin), and the surnames are not on the line at all but on the two numbered name pages.

That is the whole of the record. He was a priest; he died in 1938; the calendar keeps him on this day among the new martyrs of Russia. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 29 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260729.html); read 13 October 2026.*
