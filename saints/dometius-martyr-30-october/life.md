# Dometius (30 October)

saint.gr's calendar for 30 Οκτωβρίου names «Άγιος Δομέτιος», and the page behind that line says one thing only: the holy Dometius was martyred by the sword. There is no city, no persecutor, no year, nothing of who he was or what he did, and nothing of his relics. The calendar keeps him, and that is the whole of what it tells.

The page sets an epigram over the name, which turns on the executioner rather than the martyr: the swordsman is eager to bring the blade down on Dometius, and it is he who looks on something terrible. This folder takes its name from the day because the calendar gives it no epithet to take it from, and because the corpus already keeps eight other men of this name on other days.

No hymn is printed. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands, bare as it is.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 30 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/30/index.aspx) and [his notice](https://www.saint.gr/2846/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
