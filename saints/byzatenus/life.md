# Byzatenus

The Greek synaxarion says that Byzatenus is unknown to the synaxaria: he is named only in the Kanonarion of Jerusalem, on page 112 of Archimandrite Kallistos’s edition. It says nothing more of him, and gives no date.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 25 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2515/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
