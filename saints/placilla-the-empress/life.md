# Placilla (Flacilla) the Empress

Placilla was the wife of Theodosius the Great (379–395), very devout and charitable. All her life she cared for the poor and gave without stint to those in need what they required; she visited the sick and nursed them with her own hands, and so, having lived as a good Samaritan, died in peace — leaving the corruptible crown of earth, the verse says, Placilla found an incorruptible crown in heaven. The Greek calendar keeps her on 14 September, the feast of the Exaltation.
