# Castus the African

saint.gr gives him and his fellow one sentence between them, and no life: «Οι Άγιοι Μάρτυρες Κάστος και Αιμίλιος μαρτύρησαν το 251 μ.Χ. στην Καρχηδόνα επί Σεπτιμίου Σεβήρου» — the holy martyrs Castus and Aemilius were martyred in the year 251 at Carthage under Septimius Severus. The page's own year and the reign it names do not agree, and it does not reconcile them.

It adds only where the story comes from: «Το μαρτύριό τους αναφέρεται από τον Άγιο Κυπριανό και τον Ιερό Αυγουστίνο» — their martyrdom is recorded by Saint Cyprian and by the holy Augustine. How they died the page does not say, and it prints no hymn to them. He is named on the day together with [Aemilius](/saints/aemilius-of-africa), and nothing else about either of them is recorded here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 22 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/22/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1744/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
