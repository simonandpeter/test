# Zosimas (Verkhovsky)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him on 24 октября ст. ст. and carries no life for him. Its whole entry is «Прп. Зосимы (1833).» — a rank word, a forename in the genitive and a year; and the title on the name link behind it supplies both the surname and his state of life, «Зосима Верховской, схимонах, прп.», a schemamonk, which is the great habit and the last degree of the monastic life.

That name page carries two days for him — this one and «24 августа (Моск.)», marked as the Moscow church's — and a link to his icons, and nothing else. No `Life/` href follows his name on the day page, and nothing on either page says where he was born, where he lived, what he founded or taught, or when in 1833 he died.

The long lives of the Russian saints of the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line. The day page's thirteen troparia and kontakia belong to the four entries it heads with hymns and to the icon it keeps, and none of them to him.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 24 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261024.html); read 4 October 2026.*
