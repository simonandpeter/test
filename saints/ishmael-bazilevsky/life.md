# Ishmael (Bazilevsky)

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar for 4 ноября ст. ст. names him on a line of its own among the day's last four: «Сщмч. Исмаила пресвитера (1941).» The line gives the forename in the genitive and no surname at all. The name link behind it supplies the calendar's own nominative with the surname, «Исмаил (Базилевский), иерей, сщмч.», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from.

That is everything the calendar records of him. He has no life page, no icon, no hymn, no place, no age and no parish anywhere on this calendar, and the name page behind the line carries two days and no text at all. The name page also lists «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which is a synaxis and not a second feast; this folder renders on 4 ноября ст. ст. only. Nothing further is written here, and in particular nothing is reasoned from the surname.

The line's «Сщмч.» and «пресвитера» make him a hieromartyr and a presbyter, and the link's «иерей» is recorded as his office, Priest. 1941 is the latest year anything on this day carries. The corpus already holds an Ishmael of this wave, a presbyter of 1938 under the surname «Кудрявцев» and the spelling «Измаил»; this man's forename is spelt «Исмаил» here and the surname is a different one, and no relation or identity is proposed between them.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 4 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261104.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9654.html); read 4 October 2026.*
