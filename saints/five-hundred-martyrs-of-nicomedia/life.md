# The Five Hundred Martyrs of Nicomedia

saint.gr's calendar for 21 December keeps five hundred martyrs together, names not one of them, and gives their whole account in a single sentence: these holy martyrs believed in Christ through the holy [Juliana](/saints/juliana-of-nicomedia), whom the page refers to the same day, and afterwards all of them were martyred by the sword. The distich printed above that sentence says that Nicomedia saw five hundred victorious martyrs cut down.

That is the entire entry. The page gives no name, no year, no emperor and no governor, does not divide the five hundred into men and women, and prints no hymn; the feast is marked fixed, «Τύπος εορτής: Σταθερή. Εορτάζει στις 21 Δεκεμβρίου εκάστου έτους». Because it enumerates nobody, the company is held here as one entry and not as five hundred, which is this corpus's rule for a synaxis that names no member. It carries no asterisk and not the sentence by which saint.gr marks an entry it will not vouch for.

The Охридски пролог puts them in its heading for 21. децембар ст. ст. and tells them in one sentence of the body. When the eparch of Nicomedia had the virgin cast into a heated furnace and the fire did not scorch her, many who saw the wonder believed in Christ God, and the book counts them: five hundred men and a hundred and thirty women. The eparch condemned the whole number to death and had them put to the sword, and their souls, it says, passed over into Paradise. The entry names not one of them, gives them no year of their own, and the year 304 that closes it belongs to the sentence about her own beheading.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 21 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/21/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2817/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026; and the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 21. децембар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-01-03&prolog=1), read 8 October 2026.*
