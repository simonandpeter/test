# Gaius of Ephesus

Gaius is one of the five apostles of the Seventy whom saint.gr's calendar for 5 Νοεμβρίου keeps in a single entry, all of them, the page says, true shepherds of the rational flock of the Church. He is among those it names out of the Apostle Paul's letter to the Romans. What it tells of his own ministry is one clause: Gaius shepherded at Ephesus after Timothy. Nothing else of his life, his death or his years is on the page, and it prints no hymn for the entry.

The Greek books count him in the couplet they give three of the five: «Ἑρμᾶς, Πατρόβας καὶ Γάϊος τρεῖς ἅμα, / Ἀπόστολοι θνῄσκουσιν, οἷς τριὰς φίλη» — Hermas, Patrobas and Gaius, three together, die as apostles, to whom the Trinity is dear. The other four of the entry are [Hermas](/saints/hermas-5-november), [Patrobas](/saints/patrobas), [Linus](/saints/linus-of-rome) and [Philologus](/saints/philologus-of-sinope).

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2889/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
