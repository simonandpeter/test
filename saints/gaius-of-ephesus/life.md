# Gaius of Ephesus

Gaius is one of the five apostles of the Seventy whom saint.gr's calendar for 5 Νοεμβρίου keeps in a single entry, all of them, the page says, true shepherds of the rational flock of the Church. He is among those it names out of the Apostle Paul's letter to the Romans. What it tells of his own ministry is one clause: Gaius shepherded at Ephesus after Timothy. Nothing else of his life, his death or his years is on the page, and it prints no hymn for the entry.

The Greek books count him in the couplet they give three of the five: «Ἑρμᾶς, Πατρόβας καὶ Γάϊος τρεῖς ἅμα, / Ἀπόστολοι θνῄσκουσιν, οἷς τριὰς φίλη» — Hermas, Patrobas and Gaius, three together, die as apostles, to whom the Trinity is dear. The other four of the entry are [Hermas](/saints/hermas-5-november), [Patrobas](/saints/patrobas), [Linus](/saints/linus-of-rome) and [Philologus](/saints/philologus-of-sinope).

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar for 5 ноября ст. ст. keeps him as one of five named on a single line: «Апп. от 70-ти Патрова , Ерма , Лина , Гаия , Филолога (I).». The line gives the five forenames in the genitive, the class «Апп. от 70-ти» for all five together, and the century, and the name link behind his place in it supplies the calendar's own nominative with the see, «Святитель Гаий, епископ Ефесский, апостол от 70-ти». That page carries the title and two days and **no life link at all**, so the line and the link are the whole of what this calendar has of him, and the «(I)» at the line's end is the first date this folder has carried. The name page also lists «4 января (70 ап.)», the synaxis of the Seventy Apostles, which is a synaxis and not a second feast; this folder renders on 5 ноября ст. ст. only.

The see the link gives, Ephesus, is the one already in this folder's `office`. The form «Гаий» is now in two folders of this corpus, deliberately: the other is `gaius-pope-of-rome`, a martyr and pope of Rome who died in 296 and whom this same calendar keeps on 11 августа ст. ст. A different day, a different century and a different see, and no identity is proposed between the two.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2889/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 5 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261105.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/455.html), read 4 October 2026.*
