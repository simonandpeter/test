# Ulpian of Tyre

The Ohrid Prologue's third entry for this day is four sentences long and the Prologue is the only source read for him. He was a youth, it says, of the city of Tyre; and he was tortured for Christ by the governor Urban — the same man the Prologue names as the tormentor of the martyr it keeps on the day before, which is the one thing in the entry that places him in a persecution and a place at once.

The end it gives him is particular. At the last they tied him into a sack together with a dog and a snake, and threw the sack into the sea; and so, the entry says, he suffered and was glorified in the year 306.

That is the whole of it. The entry names no parents, no trade and no age beyond calling him a youth; it gives no trial, no words of his, no relic and no burial, and prints no hymn. The corpus holds no other folder of this name in any spelling, and none of the six folders it keeps on this menologion day is a youth of Tyre, which is why this entry stands as a folder of its own.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 3. април ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-04-16&prolog=1); read 4 October 2026.*
