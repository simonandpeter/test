# Benjamin (29 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 29 December keeps the venerable Benjamin, and says of his end only that he died in peace: «Ο Όσιος Βενιαμίν απεβίωσε ειρηνικά.» To that it adds two things out of other books. Saint Nikodemos, it says, reports that he died of dropsy; and the Evergetinos carries a saying of his, which the page cites to its page 622.

The saying is the one thing of his own that the entry keeps: «Tην βασιλικήν οδόν πορεύεσθε και τα μίλια μετρείτε, και ουκ ολιγωρείτε» — walk the royal road and count the miles, and do not lose heart. The page gives him no century, no country, no monastery and no hymn, and its couplet says only that Benjamin is to be numbered with those who left this life well in old age.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 29 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/29/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1063/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
