# Leo (18 August)

The Romanian calendar keeps a martyr named Leo on 18 August, and doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for the day reads «Sfântul Mucenic Leon», and the page that line links to carries neither a life nor a troparion, so nothing more is recorded here than the name, the rank of martyr and the day. He is not to be confused with any of the other bearers of the name whom the corpus keeps on other days; the identification rests on the day alone.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 18 august — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/18-august) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-leon), which is empty of both life and hymn; read 24 September 2026.*
