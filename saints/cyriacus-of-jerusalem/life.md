# Cyriacus of Jerusalem

saint.gr's calendar for 28 Οκτωβρίου names «Άγιος Κυριάκος ο Ιερομάρτυρας», and his page opens with the one thing it remembers him for: it was he who showed the Precious Cross to [Helen](/saints/helen-the-empress), and afterwards he was raised to be bishop of Jerusalem. Of his life before that, and of how long he held the see, the page says nothing at all.

In the year 361, the notice continues, Julian the Apostate marched against the Persians and passed through Jerusalem. There he heard of Cyriacus, and having seized him pressed him to sacrifice to the idols. Cyriacus not only refused but rebuked Julian to his face. For that his right hand was cut off; then molten lead was poured into his mouth, and he was laid on a bed of red-hot bronze. Last they put him into a cauldron full of boiling oil and pierced him there with a spear, and so, in torment but in glory, he gave up his spirit.

His mother [Anna](/saints/anna-mother-of-cyriacus) is kept on the same day, and her own notice says that when she learned of her son's torments she ran to see him and to put heart into him, and was taken and killed for it. saint.gr prints no hymn for Cyriacus. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/28/index.aspx) and [his notice](https://www.saint.gr/2824/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
