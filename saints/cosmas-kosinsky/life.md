# Cosmas Kosinsky

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Cosmas second in a pair on 29 July. Its line is «Прпп. Константина и Космы Косинских, Старорусских (XIII).» — the venerable Constantine and Cosmas of Kosino, of Staraya Russa, thirteenth century — and the calendar's own name page for him gives «Косма Косинский, Старорусский, прп.» and nothing further.

The first of the pair is [Constantine Kosinsky](/saints/constantine-kosinsky), and the day's troparion, which the calendar prints under both their names, calls Cosmas blessed and a child of obedience whom Constantine brought up. That is a hymn and not a life, and nothing beyond the relation of the two men has been taken from it.

A reading stands behind the entry and was not opened, so what is here is the line, the name page and the hymn. The calendar gives him no rank in the church and no year, only the century. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 29 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260729.html); read 13 October 2026.*
