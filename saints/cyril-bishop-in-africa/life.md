# Cyril (8 March)

saint.gr's whole notice for him is one sentence, and it is a citation rather than a life: according to the Roman Martyrology, the holy hieromartyr Cyril was a bishop and was martyred in Africa at an unknown time. That is the whole of it. There is no see — the page says he was a bishop and never says of where — no city, no persecutor, no manner of death, no relic, no hymn, and not even the distich the site usually prints.

The year is not merely absent but expressly unknown, which is the page's own word, so no date is recorded here at all. Three things stand: the name, the rank, and the continent. The corpus keeps a good many men named Cyril, on their own days and in other calendars, and nothing on this page would tell him apart from any of them; he is recorded because the Greek calendar prints him on this day, and for nothing more.
