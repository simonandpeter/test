# Nicander of Gorodnoezero

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar for 4 ноября ст. ст. names «Прп. Никандра Городноезерского (XVI).», and his life page says that he founded a hermitage on the shore of Lake Gorodnoye, forty-seven versts from the town of Borovichi and four from the churchyard of Sherokhovichi. The line and the life do not agree about his century: the line ends «(XVI)» and the life has him found the house at the beginning of the seventeenth. This folder records both readings and chooses neither.

The place was not new ground. An earlier Resurrection monastery had stood there, and the life says plainly that when it was founded nobody knows; a monastic inventory of 1581 recorded that only three elders were living in its ten cells. What the page credits to Nicander is the renewal of that house, and it says he was buried in the hermitage he had restored.

The hermitage was abolished in 1764, and in the church of the suppressed house a side chapel was dedicated to him. That is the whole of the account. The calendar gives him no parentage, no place of birth, no year of birth or of death, no tonsure and nothing at all of his life before the lake, and it prints no hymn under his name. Two parish churches on the day page's own list are dedicated to him, in the village of Izvoz and the settlement of Khvoinaya.

His life page also lists «15 марта». The day page for 15 марта ст. ст. was not read, so that day is recorded here as a note and not as a feast, and this folder renders on 4 ноября ст. ст. only.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 4 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261104.html) and [his life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life651.htm); read 4 October 2026.*
