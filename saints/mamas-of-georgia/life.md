# Mamas of Georgia

saint.gr names him and gives him two sentences. Mamas, or Mamai, lived in the eighth century and served as patriarch of Georgia, its page says, and he fell asleep in peace in the year 744. That is the whole of the notice.

Nothing else stands: no year of birth, no city, no monastery he came from, no length for his patriarchate, no act or word of his, no wonder, no relic and no hymn. The page glosses his name as it glosses every other, «ο στοργικός», the affectionate one, and prints the second form of it in brackets without saying which is the older.
