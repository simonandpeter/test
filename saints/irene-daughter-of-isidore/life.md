# Irene, daughter of Isidore

saint.gr's calendar for 18 October keeps a priest of Crete and his two children: [Isidore](/saints/isidore-of-vali), and his children [George](/saints/george-son-of-isidore) and Irene. According to the testimonies, the page says, the priest Isidore and his children after the flesh were martyred by the Ottomans inside the church in which he was celebrating the Divine Liturgy.

They cut off their heads and threw them away; devout Christians gathered them up and buried them at the feet of the martyrs' bodies, exactly as they were found on the day the graves were opened. The saints themselves, the page says, appeared in dreams to many people and made known the manner of their martyrdom and the place where their holy relics lay. The translation of the relics took place on 18 October 1953, at Vali; today only a part of them survives, since an irreverent nun stole the greater part.

The calendar gives the three no year for the martyrdom, no reign and no hymn, and the day it keeps is the day of the translation. Its authority at the foot of the page is the Metropolis of Gortyna and Arkadia in Crete, which has a church of the three at Vali and the tomb in the seventeenth-century church of Saint Demetrios there.
