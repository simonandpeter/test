# Maximus of Asia

The Russian church calendar keeps him on 14 мая ст. ст. and gives him a notice of five sentences. He suffered under the emperor Декий, whom the page dates 249 to 251. He was a layman and lived by trade, a devout Christian who brought many of the heathen to faith in Christ the Saviour and urged them to be baptised; and that, with what follows, is everything the calendar knows of him.

One day the heathen gathered to offer a human sacrifice to their gods. Maximus was outraged, and unable to bear the sight of it he ran in among them, loudly rebuking their impiety and their error and calling their idols soulless things that men had made. The enraged heathen stoned him to death.

The notice gives no city, no family, no age and no manner of burial, and the day line adds only the approximate year. The name page calls him Азийский, of Asia, which is a province and not a place this folder can put on a map, so none was recorded. The day page prints a troparion and a kontakion for two of the saints it heads and nothing under his name.
