# Calodote of Alexandria

Calodote was the wife of one Cyrus, of Alexandria in Egypt; seized in the persecution of Decius she was beheaded by the governor and curator Valerian while she was with child — her head cut off by the sword, the verse says, she stands before the Word, the giver of good things. The Greek synaxarion keeps the martyrs of Alexandria under Decius in separate entries on 6 September; the Russian calendar counts them as the eleven with the presbyter Faustus and the deacon Abibus.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 6 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2359/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
