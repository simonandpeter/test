# Platilla of Rome

Platilla lived in the first century at Rome. According to the tradition, saint.gr says, she was baptised by the Apostle [Peter](/saints/peter-the-apostle); she was present at the martyrdom of the Apostle [Paul](/saints/paul-the-apostle); and she is held to be the mother of Saint Domitilla, whom the page keeps on 12 Μαΐου. She was perfected by martyrdom in the year 67. The page says nothing of how she died, and gives her no place of burial.

What it gives instead is how she is painted. She is shown offering her veil to the Apostle Paul at his martyrdom; and Paul, the page says, appeared to her after his death and gave her the veil back. That act is worked into the bronze doors of the basilica of Saint Peter at Rome. The page prints no hymn to her.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 20 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/20/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1681/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
