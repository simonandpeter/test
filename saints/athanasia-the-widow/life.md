# Athanasia, mother of Theodote, Theoctiste and Eudoxia

Athanasia was a widow, saint.gr says, and was arrested in the persecution of Diocletian together with her three daughters, [Theodote](/saints/theodote-daughter-of-athanasia), [Theoctiste](/saints/theoctiste-daughter-of-athanasia) and [Eudoxia](/saints/eudoxia-daughter-of-athanasia). The news of it troubled the unmercenaries [Cyrus](/saints/cyrus-of-alexandria) and [John](/saints/john-of-edessa), who feared that the women might lose heart at the hardness of the tortures, and who hurried to them to give them courage while preparing themselves for martyrdom as well.

Brought before the governor, they declared their faith in God with boldness. He tried in vain to bend the mother's courage by showing her her daughters and laying their guilt upon her. She turned to them instead and strengthened them, saying that beauty of body lasts a little while, but that in eternity the beauty of a person's soul is kept undying. And they answered their mother that they felt great joy, because they were to leave this vain world along with her for the love of Christ and never be parted from her.

The governor was enraged and ordered them put to many hard tortures; and after the tortures Cyrus and John were beheaded with the sword in the year 292, and Athanasia with her three daughters bore witness in the same way. Their life and martyrdom, the page says, was written by Sophronius the Sophist, and their synaxis was kept in the martyrion raised in their honour in the quarter of Phorakion.

The couplet the page sets over the four of them praises her first: «Μήτηρ ἀρίστη, καὶ τριὰς θυγατέρων, / Πόθῳ Πατρὸς θνῄσκουσι τοῦ πάντων ξίφει» — an excellent mother, and a trinity of daughters, die by the sword out of longing for the Father of all.
