# Peter the Ascetic

saint.gr's calendar for 11 December keeps two martyrs in one entry and names both: Peter the ascetic and [Akepsimas](/saints/akepsimas-martyr-11-december). The whole of what it has to say of them is where it has them from and where they died: the holy Peter the ascetic and Akepsimas are recorded in the Synaxaristes of Delehaye and in the Parisian Codex 1578 as having been martyred in Persia.

No century, no king, no city, no manner of death, no relic, no hymn and no synaxaristic couplet is printed, and the page gives Peter no office beyond the word ascetic in the day's line. Of the two names it is his that carries that word, and that is the only thing distinguishing him from his companion here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/11/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3170/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
