# Camelia of Ecoulives

doxologia.ro gives Camelia no life page, only the few lines of summary that stand under her name, and this is the whole of them. She was born at Civitavecchia in Italy, and was converted to Christianity by Saint Germanus, bishop of Auxerre in France, whom the same page keeps on 31 July.

It was she who accompanied the coffin with the body of that hierarch, who had died suddenly at Ravenna, on its journey back to Auxerre. At Ecoulives, near Auxerre, there was a church dedicated to Saint Camelia, in which her relics were kept.

The page gives no year, no account of her way of life and no hymn, and nothing further is added here.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 3 martie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/3-martie) and [her page](https://doxologia.ro/sfanta-cuvioasa-camelia-din-ecoulives); read 19 September 2026.*
