# Iltud

saint.gr's calendar for 6 Νοεμβρίου names Iltud and gives no life. Its line for the day is «Άγιος Iltud», with the name in Latin letters and no Greek form of it anywhere on the page, and the whole of the notice behind that line is one sentence: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ουαλού Αγίου Iltud» — we have no details of the life of the Welsh saint Iltud.

So the name, the day and the one word Welsh are all that is recorded here. The page gives him no century, no monastery, no rank, no manner of death, no year, no couplet and no hymn, and nothing of his is quoted from any other book.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2931/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
