# Hosea

The prophet Hosea was the son of Beeri of Belemoth, of the tribe of Issachar, a man who feared God. In that time many of the people of Israel had turned aside from God and forgotten His benefits and His wonders and were worshipping idols; but he served God, who made heaven and earth and who brought their fathers out of Egypt with a strong hand, and he taught the people who had strayed from the truth and turned them back by his wise counsels to the right faith of their forefathers.

Filled with the Holy Spirit, he prophesied much concerning Israel and concerning the coming of Christ and His resurrection, as is seen in his book; for he wrote down his prophetic words, fourteen chapters in all, which are reckoned in Holy Scripture as spoken through the Holy Spirit.

Hosea lived many years, the page says, eight hundred and twenty-two years before the birth of Christ; and his name is interpreted saviour, keeper or one who overshadows. He died in deep and honourable old age and was buried in peace in his own land. That is the whole of what doxologia.ro prints of him.
