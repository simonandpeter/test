# Basil of Yaroslavl

The Greek calendar keeps him on 3 Ιουλίου as a prince of Yaroslavl, and knows nothing else about him. saint.gr's heading is «Άγιος Βασίλειος του Γιαροσλάβλ ο πρίγκιπας (; - 1249)», and the whole of the page beneath it is the sentence «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου» — we have no details of the saint's life. The city, the title and the year 1249 are the entire finding.

The page adds only that the finding of his relics is kept as a commemoration of its own, on a day it does not give here. Nothing of the thirteenth-century history of Yaroslavl is written into this folder from elsewhere, and his own Russian calendar, which will have his life if any calendar does, has not been read.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/03/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4030/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
