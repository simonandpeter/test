# Basil of Yaroslavl

The Greek calendar keeps him on 3 Ιουλίου as a prince of Yaroslavl, and knows nothing else about him. saint.gr's heading is «Άγιος Βασίλειος του Γιαροσλάβλ ο πρίγκιπας (; - 1249)», and the whole of the page beneath it is the sentence «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου» — we have no details of the saint's life. The city, the title and the year 1249 are the entire finding.

The page adds only that the finding of his relics is kept as a commemoration of its own, on a day it does not give here. Nothing of the thirteenth-century history of Yaroslavl is written into this folder from elsewhere, and his own Russian calendar, which will have his life if any calendar does, has not been read.

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps the two brothers on 3 июля ст. ст. — «Блгвв. кн. Василия и Константина Ярославских (XIII).» — which is the same menologion day as the Greek page this folder was made from, and gives them one reading between them. It has the two of them rule their patrimony in the fear of God after Batu’s invasion of 1236, taking no part in the quarrels of the princes, healing the wounds of the raids and rebuilding the ruined churches; it sends the elder to the Golden Horde in 1242 and again the year after; and it has him fall ill at Vladimir in the winter of 1249 and die there on 8 February, to be laid in the Dormition cathedral of Yaroslavl.

The calendar sets him on three days, a Rostov commemoration on 23 мая, the finding of the relics of both brothers on 8 июня, and this one; and under the two names together it prints four hymns, two of the brothers’ memory and two of the finding of their relics. His brother, [Constantine of Yaroslavl](/saints/constantine-of-yaroslavl), came into this corpus on the same day as this row.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/03/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4030/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026; and after the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 3 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260703.html), read 9 October 2026.*
