# Kyriake (19 May)

doxologia.ro's calendar for 19 mai names her «Sfânta Muceniță Chiriachi», and her page prints one sentence about her and nothing more: that she was martyred together with six other young Christian women at Nicomedia in the year 307, after they had been burned alive, in the reign of Maximianus Galerius. There is no Viață tab, and the page gives no family, no trial and no names for the six who suffered with her.

What it does print is her condac, in the second tone, and that hymn is the only other thing the calendar says of her. It calls her valiant in mind and turns on her name — Chiriachi, the Lord's own — to say that by that name she is a lady over her thought and over the unseemly passions. Because the six are not named, this corpus keeps one folder here and not seven.

The Greek calendar keeps her on the same day and confirms the two facts the Romanian line gives, while adding two more. saint.gr's whole account of her is one sentence: «Η Αγία Μάρτυς Κυριακή μαρτύρησε μαζί με άλλες έξι Χριστιανές νέες στη Νικομήδεια το 307 μ.Χ., αφού τις έκαψαν ζωντανές, επί Μαξιμιανού Γαλερίου» — she was martyred together with six other young Christian women at Nicomedia in 307, after they had been burned alive, under Maximian Galerius. So the manner of the death and the name of the emperor come from the Greek, and the six are as nameless there as they are on the Romanian page.
