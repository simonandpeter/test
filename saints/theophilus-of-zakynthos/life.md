# Theophilus of Zakynthos

saint.gr says Theophilus was born on Zakynthos in 1617 and was handsome and strong in body. He worked as a sailor, and he would not serve aboard a Turkish ship; setting himself against the will of the Turkish captain, he was slandered by him with a charge that he had supposedly put a Turkish covering on his head. He was taken by force before the judge, where flattery and threats were used in turn to make him a Muslim.

Theophilus confessed Christ with courage, and for that the Turks circumcised him by force, meaning to send him as a gift to the Sultan's palace. While he was still on Chios he escaped and went over to Samos, where he stayed for a good while. When he came back to Chios the Turks recognised him, arrested him and brought him again before the judge, who saw that he held to his faith and sentenced him to death by fire.

Undaunted, the page says, this brave martyr of our faith made the sign of the cross and said: into your hands, my Christ, I give up my soul. He walked into the fire by himself, and there gave up his holy soul to God on 24 July 1635. What the fire left of his remains was bought by Christians and laid with honours in the church of the Great Martyr George on Chios. An account of his martyrdom was first written by George Koresios of Chios.

Above the notice the page sets a distich: «Φιλῶν τὸν Θεόν, Θεόφιλος προθύμως, πρὸς πῦρ δι’ Αὐτόν, κινεῖται θαρσαλέως» — loving God, Theophilus moves eagerly and boldly towards the fire for his sake. The page also ranks him with the new martyrs who suffered after the fall of Constantinople.
