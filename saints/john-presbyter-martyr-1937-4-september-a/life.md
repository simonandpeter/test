# John the Presbyter, Hieromartyr (1937, 4 September, the first)

John Vasilevsky was born on 18 March 1869 in the Krasny Kholm settlement of the Vesyegonsk district of Tver province, the son of a psalmist, Nicholas Vasilevsky; after the Tver seminary he was ordained and served at the cathedral of Vesyegonsk. When the godless came to power he was arrested in 1922, charged with resisting the seizure of church valuables and sentenced to a year; his appeal succeeded, he was freed and returned to the cathedral, where he served until February 1924, then moved to the church of Selikhovo, where he served until October 1926 — the Sretensky calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. He was shot in 1937, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 4 September (Julian) with the bishops Gregory and Sergius and their company.
