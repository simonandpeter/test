# Febronia, daughter of Heraclius

saint.gr's calendar for 28 Οκτωβρίου names «Αγία Φεβρωνία» and gives her three lines and no more. She was the daughter of Heraclius, the emperor of Byzantium, the page says, whose reign it dates 610 to 641; she lived with the spirit of God, and she died peacefully. That is the whole of the notice. The epigram set above it adds only that Febronia, being quick of mind, preferred the Kingdom of Heaven to her father's own.

The page gives her no year of her own — the only years on it are her father's reign — so no date is recorded here. It names no place but Byzantium, tells nothing of how or where she lived, records no relics and prints no hymn. It carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands, bare as it is.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/28/index.aspx) and [her notice](https://www.saint.gr/2823/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
