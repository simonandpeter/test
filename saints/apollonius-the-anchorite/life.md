# Apollonius the Anchorite

The Greek calendar names him on 21 Ιανουαρίου as «Όσιος Απολλώνιος ο Αναχωρητής», the venerable Apollonius the anchorite, and the whole of the page behind that line is one sentence: «Ο Όσιος Απολλώνιος έζησε περί τον 4ο αιώνα μ.Χ. και κοιμήθηκε με ειρήνη» — he lived about the fourth century and fell asleep in peace. The rest of the page is a note on the name, which it says was borne by a Rhodian epic poet and by a mathematician of Alexandria.

So the day claims a century, the rank *Όσιος*, and the epithet anchorite, which is all it says of his manner of life. No country, no desert, no teacher, no year and no hymn is printed. This folder records that much; anything fuller would have to come from a source that has not been read here.
