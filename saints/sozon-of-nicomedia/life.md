# Sozon of Nicomedia

saint.gr's calendar for 7 August names Saint Sozon from Nicomedia, and gives him two sentences: they threw him into the fire, and by divine grace he came out of it unharmed; and afterwards he died peacefully. That is the whole of the entry — no emperor, no judge, no year, no relic and no word about what came between the fire and the peaceful end.

The couplet the page sets above those sentences calls him a martyr, and says that the martyr Sozon was another salamander against the flame, whose end was without fire. The prose and the couplet do not quite agree, and this folder records neither as a rank: the page says he survived the fire and reposed in peace, and that is what the life says too. He is not the Sozon of Pompeiopolis nor the shepherd boy Sozon of Cyprus, both of whom the same calendar keeps on 7 September.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/761/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
