# Gregory the Elder

Before he entered the clergy Gregory was a senior public official, in a post where a man could take money and where, saint.gr says, his colleagues did so as a rule. His honesty did not let him make an unlawful profit of so much as one drachma. At first he belonged to a religious sect whose followers were called Hypsistarians, and it was his wife [Nonna](/saints/nonna-mother-of-gregory-the-theologian), devout and educated, who led him into the Orthodox faith; he was still a young man then.

He was baptised in 325, was raised to the bishopric of Nazianzus in 328, and was bishop there for forty-five years. The page says he lived through that episcopate in a manner worthy of being numbered among the saints, and that he would have deserved it on one ground alone: from him and from Nonna came two sons and a daughter all reckoned saints — [Gregory the Theologian](/saints/gregory-the-theologian), Caesarius the physician, and [Gorgonia](/saints/gorgonia). He gave up his soul to God peacefully and in holiness. The page prints no year for his death and none is worked out here.
