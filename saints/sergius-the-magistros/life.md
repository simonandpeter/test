# Sergius the Magistros

saint.gr's calendar for 28 Ιουνίου keeps him as «Άγιος Σέργιος ο δίκαιος ο Μάγιστρος», Sergius the just, the magistros, and gives him a career rather than a martyrdom. He came, the page says, from the little town of Niketia near Amastris on the Black Sea. His family was of aristocratic descent and was joined by kinship to that of the empress Theodora, wife of the emperor Theophilus. He made brilliant studies and came quickly to high military and civil offices.

Although Theophilus was a warm supporter of the iconoclasts, Sergius stayed faithful to the Orthodox faith and made great efforts for the setting up again of the holy icons; and in the persecution Theophilus raised he was shown to be the protector of many defenders of them. After the emperor's death he contributed the most and spent all his influence to strengthen Theodora's resolve to call an ecumenical council for the restoration of the icons.

He fell asleep in peace in Crete and was buried in the monastery of the Magistros. Later his holy relics were carried with great honours and buried in the monastery of the Most Holy Theotokos which he had himself built on the gulf of Nicomedia, called the monastery of the Niketianos because its founder came from the town of Niketia. The page gives no year and prints no hymn, and its couplet plays on his office: Sergius, who was a magistros before in this life, is now shown God's magistros in the vault of heaven.
