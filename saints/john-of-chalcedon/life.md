# John of Chalcedon

saint.gr's calendar for 18 July keeps two bishops together, [Stephen the Second](/saints/stephen-ii-of-constantinople), from Amasea, archbishop of Constantinople, and John, metropolitan of Chalcedon; and of John it says at once that it has nothing. «Όσο για τον Μητροπολίτη Χαλκηδόνας άγιο Ιωάννη, δεν έχουμε βιογραφικά του στοιχεία» — as for the holy John, metropolitan of Chalcedon, we have no biographical details of him. There is no year beside his name, no birthplace, no account of his episcopate and no hymn.

What the page offers in place of a life is a guess, and it says so. The synaxaria mention him as a preacher; from that, it reasons, we may conclude that he lived in the years of the iconoclasts; and so he must be the man of whom [Theodore the Studite](/saints/theodore-the-studite) said that he had carried off the bright crown of confession. That chain is the page's and not this folder's: no year and no rank of confessor are recorded here on the strength of it.

Above the two notices the calendar sets one couplet for the pair: «Λυθέντες εκ γης οι δύω Eπίσκοποι, / Πάρεισιν ήδη τω νόων Eπισκόπω» — loosed from the earth, the two bishops stand now before the Bishop of minds.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2154/saint.aspx), which he shares with Stephen II; read 1 October 2026.*
