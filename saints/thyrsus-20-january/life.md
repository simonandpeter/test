# Thyrsus (20 January)

saint.gr keeps him on 20 Ιανουαρίου with [Agnes](/saints/agnes-20-january), and the page behind the line begins by saying what is not known: «Είναι άγνωστο που και πότε μαρτύρησαν οι Άγιοι Θύσρος και Αγνή» — it is unknown where and when the saints Thyrsus and Agnes bore witness. What it does record is where they were kept: their synaxis was held near the Elenianai, between Psomatheia and the Xerolophos of Constantinople. Over the two of them stands the epigram «Θύρσος συν Aγνή Χριστόν ηγαπηκότες, Θάνατον ηγάπησαν ασμένως πάνυ».

Because the page names neither place nor time, this folder does not join him to any other Thyrsus the calendars keep on other days, and nothing from those folders is carried over to him. No year, no judge, no manner of death and no hymn is printed here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 20 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/20/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3326/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
