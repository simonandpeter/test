# Victor (Basov)

Victor Petrovich Basov was born on 29 October 1892 in the village of Gorodets in Nizhny Novgorod province, a deacon’s son. After the seminary he served some years as psalmist; in 1918 he was ordained deacon and in 1925 priest, and served in one of the villages of the Arzamas district, and from 1931 at the church of the village of Efimyevo in the Bogorodsk district. On 17 April 1935, on the denunciation of a villager, he was arrested and imprisoned at Gorky, charged with speaking against the collective farms, preaching anti-Soviet sermons and helping the peasants draw up complaints against wrongful taxation; at the sitting of the special collegium of the regional court on 1 June — the calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. Sentenced to five years in the camps, he was arrested again in the Karaganda camp in 1937 in the case of Bishop [Damascene (Tsedrik)](/saints/damascene-of-starodub), for taking part in the singing of church hymns, sentenced by the troika to be shot and executed on 15 September 1937. He was numbered among the saints in 2000, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 2 September (Julian).
