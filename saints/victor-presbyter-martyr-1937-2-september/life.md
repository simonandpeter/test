# Victor (Basov)

Victor Petrovich Basov was born on 29 October 1892 in the village of
Gorodets in Nizhny Novgorod province, a deacon’s son. After the seminary
he served some years as psalmist; in 1918 he was ordained deacon and in
1925 priest, and served in one of the villages of the Arzamas district,
and from 1931 at the church of Efimyevo in the Bogorodsk district. On 17
April 1935, on the denunciation of a villager, he was arrested and
imprisoned at Gorky, charged with speaking against the collective farms,
preaching anti-Soviet sermons and helping the peasants draw up complaints
against wrongful taxation; at the sitting of the special collegium of the
regional court on 1 June he said: I do not admit myself guilty … I hold
firmly to the rule of not speaking against any authority whatever — and
was sentenced to five years in the camps. On 30 August 1937 he was arrested
in the Karaganda camp as an adherent of the counter-revolutionary group of
the hieromartyr Bishop [Damascene (Tsedrik)](/saints/damascene-of-starodub),
charged with systematically carrying on counter-revolutionary activity
aimed at discrediting the policy of the party and government and at the
overthrow of the existing order; the occasion of the arrest was his
singing of church songs — the canon of Pascha — with the hieromartyr
[John (Melnichenko)](/saints/john-presbyter-martyr-1937-2-september-a). He
did not admit guilt of counter-revolutionary activity, and by sentence of
the troika of the NKVD for the Karaganda region of 10 September was shot on
15 September 1937 — 2 September of the old style, the day the Russian
calendar keeps him. He was glorified by the jubilee Council of Bishops in
2000; the life is by Igumen Damascene (Orlovsky), and the calendar keeps
him also in the synaxis of the saints of Nizhny Novgorod.
