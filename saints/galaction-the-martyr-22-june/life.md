# Galaction (22 June)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 22 iunie reads «Sfântul Mucenic Galaction», and the page it points to carries the day, the epistle appointed for the day and the gospel read with it, and nothing else at all: no country, no century, no companions, no manner of death, no year, no icon and no hymn.

He is not the Galaction of Vologda the corpus already keeps, and he is not joined by anything on this page to the Galaction whom the calendars commemorate with Epistimi; the calendar simply names a martyr of this name on 22 June, and that is what is recorded here.

The readings appointed for the day are 2 Timothy 2:1–10 and John 15:17–27 with 16:1–2.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 22 June, and adds the one thing the Romanian calendar withheld: how he died. saint.gr's whole account is a single sentence, «Ο Άγιος Γαλατίων (ή Γαλακτίων) μαρτύρησε αφού τον έριξαν μέσα στη θάλασσα και πνίγηκε» — the holy Galaction was martyred after they threw him into the sea and he was drowned. It still gives no country, no city, no century, no year and no judge, and prints no hymn under his line; and besides that sentence it carries only the couplet the synaxaria print for him, «Ὕδωρ θαλάσσης ἁλμυρὸν Γαλατίων, / Ὡς ἡδὺ μᾶλλον εἶχεν οἴεσθαι γάλα» — Galaction could think the salt water of the sea sweeter than milk, which is a play on his name.
