# Galaction (22 June)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 22 iunie reads «Sfântul Mucenic Galaction», and the page it points to carries the day, the epistle appointed for the day and the gospel read with it, and nothing else at all: no country, no century, no companions, no manner of death, no year, no icon and no hymn.

He is not the Galaction of Vologda the corpus already keeps, and he is not joined by anything on this page to the Galaction whom the calendars commemorate with Epistimi; the calendar simply names a martyr of this name on 22 June, and that is what is recorded here.

The readings appointed for the day are 2 Timothy 2:1–10 and John 15:17–27 with 16:1–2.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 22 iunie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/22-iunie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-galaction-0); read 24 September 2026.*
