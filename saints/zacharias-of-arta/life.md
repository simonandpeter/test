# Zacharias of Arta

Zacharias came of the parts of Arta. At a small age he was made a Muslim, and he came to Old Patras, where he practised the trade of a furrier. But he repented of having changed his faith; and having come to repentance he found a spiritual father, confessed to him the sin of his denial, and asked his blessing to go to martyrdom.

The spiritual father, fearing that the martyr might lose heart in the middle of the torments, tried to turn him from it. Then Zacharias answered him: «Έχω τόση δίψα να βασανισθώ για τον Χριστό, όπου επιθυμώ να λάβω, αν ήταν δυνατόν, και περισσότερα βασανιστήρια, από αυτά που μου ανέφερες» — I have such thirst to be tormented for Christ that I would wish, if it were possible, for more torments than those you have named to me. Before those words the priest gave him the immaculate Mysteries and blessed him.

He went to his workshop, sold everything he had and gave it in alms to the poor, and presented himself before the judge, in whose presence he confessed his faith in Christ. For all the flatteries used on him he stood firm in that confession, and so they shut him in prison and tormented him for many days. He bore the dreadful torments with spiritual manliness and admirable endurance, and gave up his spirit in 1782. The local Church of Arta keeps his memory, and an icon of him is kept at the monastery of Kato Panagia of Arta; the local Church of Patras keeps his feast too, since he had worked there.
