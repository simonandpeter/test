# Domitilla

saint.gr's calendar for 12 May names her and gives her three sentences, which are the whole of what follows. The holy martyr Domitilla lived in the first century at Rome. She was the wife of the Roman official Titus Flavius Clemens, and the daughter of the sister of the emperor Domitian, who reigned from 81 to 96. She was perfected by martyrdom because she refused to sacrifice to the idols.

The page says no more: it names no place of death, no year within the century, no judge and no manner of execution, and it prints no hymn. This folder records the Greek calendar's line for 12 May and the little that stands behind it.
