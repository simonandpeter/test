# Audiesius, with the martyrs who suffered with him

saint.gr sets the company in Persia under Shapur II, whose reign it gives as 325 to 379. In the thirtieth year of that reign, it says, the king overran the country it calls Byzate and put to death everyone who bore arms; the women, the children, the old men, the bishop Heliodorus, the presbyters Desan and Mariab and all their clergy he left alive. When Heliodorus understood that he was going to die he ordained the presbyter Desan bishop, and Desan carried the Christian preaching further, to the Persians themselves.

At that the chief magus Adephar accused Desan to the king, saying that he and all the Christians about him were blaspheming the Persian religion; and Shapur gave orders that they all be seized and made to worship the sun and the fire. Because these Christians would not do it, they were all beheaded — only five of them, the page says, lost heart on the road and accepted the religion of the idols.

Audiesius belonged to that company of beheaded Christians in Persia. But the blow he took was not mortal, and in the night he got up, and afterwards went on preaching Christ boldly again; whereupon some fanatical idolater rushed at him and killed him with a knife. The couplet over his name says that he, a captive by his lot, was set free by giving his slaughter as a ransom; and the couplet for the martyrs says that in the land of the fire-worshippers, in Persia, the neck-severed phalanx of the worshippers of Christ contends.

The page never says how many suffered with him, and beyond Heliodorus, Desan and Mariab it names none of them, so the calendar's one line is kept here as one entry.

**The Russian calendar does for this company what no other source read for this folder does: it names three of them in its own day line and counts the rest.** Its line for 9 апреля ст. ст. is «Мчч. Дисана еп., Мариава пресвитера, @7[Авдиеса] и прочих 270-ти (362-364).», the martyrs Disan the bishop, Mariab the presbyter, Audiesius and the other 270 — and the brackets it closes with are years, about 362 to 364, where the Greek entry that made this folder heads the same company «(; - 355)». The interval in `dates` now carries both readings and chooses neither.

Two of the three names in that line carry links to name pages of their own and **his carries none**: where Дисана and Мариава are each a link, his name is printed as a template token that failed to expand, «@7[Авдиеса]», which is how the page renders it to a reader. The life page’s title gives the nominative that this folder’s `ru` form is taken from, «Мученики епископ Дисан, пресвитер Мариав, Авдиес и прочие 270». The life itself is two sentences: the company suffered under the Persian king Shapur II, and in captivity they refused to deny the Christian faith.

Because this calendar enumerates where the Greek one did not, the two presbyters it names now have folders of their own — [Desan of Persia](/saints/desan-of-persia), whom the dying Heliodorus ordained bishop and whom the Russian name page calls a bishop too, and [Mariab of Persia](/saints/mariab-of-persia). The reading recorded on this folder from the Greek side was that a heading whose narrative names some and lists none is one entry; that reading stands for the Greek calendar, and it is this calendar’s own line, which names and counts, that makes the other two.

The Russian life adds a fourth name from inside the company and sends it elsewhere: «В их числе была и мученица Ия, которая воспоминается 11 сентября» — among them was the martyr Ia, who is commemorated on 11 September. That is [Ias of Persia](/saints/ias-of-persia), whom this corpus already keeps on that day, and whose own Сретенский line prints «(362-364)», the same bracket this one does. So the page that says the company is unnumbered beyond 270 also says that one of the 270 is kept by name in another month.
