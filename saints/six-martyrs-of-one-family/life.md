# The Six Martyrs of One Family (9 March)

saint.gr's calendar for 9 Μαρτίου names them by their kinship and by nothing else. Its line reads «Άγιοι Παππούς, Γιαγιά, Πατέρας, Μητέρα και τα Δύο Παιδιά τους» — the holy grandfather, grandmother, father, mother and their two children — and the page behind that line carries no personal name for any of the six, no place, no year and no emperor. What it says of them is one sentence: «Μαρτύρησαν δια ξίφους», they were martyred by the sword.

Beside that sentence the page prints two iambic lines, which say the same thing in the same compass: «Διὰ ξίφους ἤθλησε συγγενὲς γένος, / Ἡ παππόμμαμος πατρομητροτεκνία» — by the sword a kindred stock contended, the grandfather-grandmother-father-mother-and-children household, the second line being a single coined word for a whole family. Three generations of one house are commemorated together, and that they were of one house is the whole of what is recorded. The page gives no hymn and no relic.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 9 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/09/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/31/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
