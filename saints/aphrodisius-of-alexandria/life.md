# Aphrodisius of Alexandria

saint.gr's calendar for 30 Απριλίου names him and gives one sentence: «Ο Άγιος Ιερομάρτυρας Αφροδίσιος ήταν πρεσβύτερος της Εκκλησίας και μαρτύρησε στην Αλεξάνδρεια μαζί με άλλους τριάντα Χριστιανούς» — Aphrodisius the hieromartyr was a presbyter of the Church and was martyred at Alexandria along with thirty other Christians. Nothing else is printed: no century, no emperor, no judge and no manner of death.

The thirty who suffered with him have a heading of their own on the page and no names at all, so the corpus keeps none of them: a company is one folder for each member the source names, and a member the source leaves unnamed cannot be one. The gloss above the notice is about the goddess Aphrodite and the derivation of her name, and is not about the martyr. The feast type is the plain fixed one, «Σταθερή. Εορτάζει στις 30 Απριλίου εκάστου έτους».

*After saint.gr's calendar for 30 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/30/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3998/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
