# Germanus, founder of the monastery of Eikosiphoinissa

saint.gr's calendar for 22 Νοεμβρίου names «Όσιος Γερμανός Ιδρυτής Ιεράς Μονής Εικοσιφοινίσσης» — Germanus, founder of the holy monastery of Eikosiphoinissa — glosses the name from the Latin germanus as the brother, the true, the perfect, and then says the whole of what it knows in two sentences. The venerable Germanus lived about 880. There are no further details of the life of the saint.

So the calendar keeps him by the house he founded and by one approximate year, and this entry says only that. The page gives him no country, no city, no year of birth or of death, no companions, no relics and no hymn, and sets no couplet over him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 22 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/22/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3074/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
