# Apollos (10 June)

saint.gr's calendar for 10 Ιουνίου names him and gives no life. Its whole notice is one sentence: «Ο Άγιος Απολλώς (ή Απόλλων) ο Επίσκοπος απεβίωσε ειρηνικά. Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του» — that Apollos, or Apollon, the bishop, died peacefully, and that we have no further details of his life. The page names no see, no century and no place, and gives him no year.

What it does print is the couplet of the synaxarion: «Ως χρηστός όντως ο ζυγός σου Xριστέ μου, / Oν ζων υπήλθον Aπολλώς θανών λέγει» — how good indeed is your yoke, my Christ, says Apollos in death, the yoke he went under while he lived. The Greek calendar keeps a second Apollos on 25 January, a hermit under Julian the Apostate whom the corpus holds separately; this bishop is his own commemoration and his own entry.
