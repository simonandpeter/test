# Hilarion the New, Monk-martyr of Crete

Hilarion came from Herakleion in Crete and was called John in the world, one of
five children of Frantzeskos and Aikaterini, brought up carefully and taught the sacred letters.
An uncle who was a physician took him to Constantinople but neither taught him medicine nor
troubled himself about his nephew, so John left and was taken on by a merchant from Chios. When
the merchant returned from a journey he reckoned thirty piastres missing, though no stock had
been counted before he left, and his suspicion fell on John. The uncle would not receive him. In
his despair John went to the sultan's palace, and there the agha who received him turned the
occasion to his own use. The Greek calendar keeps him on 20 September.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 20 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/976/saint.aspx); read 26 August 2026.*
