# Quartus

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 10 noiembrie reads «Sfântul Apostol Cvart», and the page it points to has no Viață tab at all: it carries the name, the day, and a hymn — a common of the apostles, headed «Troparul Sfinților Apostoli Olimp, Rodion, Sosipatru, Erast, Terțiu, Cvart» for the six of them together and naming none of them in its text.

So the calendar calls him an apostle and sets him in that company of six kept on this day, and says nothing further: no see, no country, no century, no year, no manner of death. His is a name the corpus has not held before, and the name sweep found nothing in it to confuse him with.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 10 noiembrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/10-noiembrie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-apostol-cvart); read 25 September 2026.*
