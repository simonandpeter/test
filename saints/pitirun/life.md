# Pitirun

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 29 noiembrie reads «Sfântul Cuvios Pitirun» — the holy venerable Pitirun — and the page that stands under his name carries neither a Viață tab nor a Tropar tab: there is the name and nothing else. The rank word «Cuvios» is all that is said of his manner of life, and it is the only thing entered here besides the name and the day.

He is given here in the form doxologia prints, because no English form of the name was found on any page that was read, and none is invented.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 29 noiembrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/29-noiembrie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-pitirun); read 25 September 2026.*
