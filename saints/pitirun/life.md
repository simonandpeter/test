# Pitirun

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 29 noiembrie reads «Sfântul Cuvios Pitirun» — the holy venerable Pitirun — and the page that stands under his name carries neither a Viață tab nor a Tropar tab: there is the name and nothing else. The rank word «Cuvios» is all that is said of his manner of life, and it is the only thing entered here besides the name and the day.

He is given here in the form doxologia prints, because no English form of the name was found on any page that was read, and none is invented.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 29 November, and saint.gr gives the story the Romanian page had none of. He became a disciple of [Anthony the Great](/saints/anthony-the-great), it says, and took something of that man's virtues and gifts, and his own self-control is pictured as of the strictest asceticism: many a time he went entirely without food, and neither his health suffered for it nor his spiritual endurance and readiness lessened.

Often, it says, the monks would talk about visions in which demons appeared to them; and then he would say: «εγώ φοβάμαι περισσότερα τα δαιμόνια, πού φωλιάζουν την υπερηφάνεια, τη φιλαργυρία, τη φιληδονία και άλλα παρόμοια πάθη» — I am more afraid of the demons that nest in pride, in love of money, in love of pleasure and other passions like them; those are the more dangerous demons, and great attention must be paid to them. The venerable Pitirun died in peace. The page gives him no year and prints no hymn.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 29 noiembrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/29-noiembrie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-pitirun); read 25 September 2026; and after saint.gr's calendar for 29 Νοεμβρίου — [the Greek day](https://www.saint.gr/11/29/index.aspx) and [its life](https://www.saint.gr/3113/saint.aspx), read 2 October 2026.*
