# Elizabeth (Samovskaya)

The Russian church calendar's line for 1 November is the whole of what is recorded of her here: «Сщмчч. Александра и Димитрия пресвитеров, мц. Елисаветы (1937)» — the hieromartyrs Alexander and Demetrius, presbyters, and the martyr Elizabeth, with the year 1937 closing the line for all three. Its index of names gives her surname, «Елисавета (Самовская), мц.», and lists this day and the 26 January synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia against her name.

She is the one lay person on a line of two priests: the calendar gives her no grade, no office and no place, and there is no life page behind her name. So this folder records the year of her death, the day the Church keeps her on and nothing else. azbyka.ru, which has carried long lives for others of these years, is a quota this corpus has spent and was not asked again on 4 October 2026.
