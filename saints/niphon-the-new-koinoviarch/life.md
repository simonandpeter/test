# Niphon the New, founder of Evangelistria

saint.gr's calendar for 28 December keeps the venerable Niphon the New, whom it calls Κοινοβιάρχης, the founder and head of a coenobium. In the world he was Nicholas, «εκ της γενεάς των Νικολαράδων» — of the stock of the Nikolarades — and he was born in 1736 at Patrika on Chios. He became a monk at the monastery of the Great Lavra and at the skete of the Pantokrator on the Holy Mountain.

He went afterwards to Patmos, to Leipsoi and to Ikaria, and there, in 1775, at the place called Lefkada, he founded the monastery of the Evangelistria. Among the monks of that house on Ikaria was Gregory Chatzistamatis, a man of Skiathos, who after his father's death inherited a great property on Skiathos; and having it, he persuaded Niphon that the two of them should cross to green Skiathos and build a new monastery there.

So it was. The building began in 1794, the house received the dignity of a stavropegion in 1797, and the whole complex was finished in 1806; and it became, the page says, the centre of the Kollyvades. The venerable Niphon fell asleep in 1809. The page gives no more of him than this, and prints no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/28/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4297/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
