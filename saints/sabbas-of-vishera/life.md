# Sabbas of Vishera

The Greek synaxarion keeps Sabbas of Vishera, the wonderworker, and says only that he was a Russian monk, born in 1381 and fallen asleep in 1461, and that it has no other details of his life.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 1 Οκτωβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2564/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
