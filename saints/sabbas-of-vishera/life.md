# Sabbas of Vishera

The Greek synaxarion keeps Sabbas of Vishera, the wonderworker, and says only that he was a Russian monk, born in 1381 and fallen asleep in 1461, and that it has no other details of his life.

The Russian church calendar keeps him on the Julian 1 October and agrees with saint.gr on the year, 1461: «Прп. Саввы Вишерского, Новгородского (1461)». It names him twice over, for the river he settled on and for the city whose land it ran through, and its own life page adds «чудотворец», wonderworker, as the Greek heading does. A troparion for him is printed on the day and was not taken into this folder.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 1 Οκτωβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2564/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026; the Russian church calendar keeps him on the Julian 1 October — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261001.html); read 4 October 2026.*
