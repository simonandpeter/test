# Charalampos of Kalyviani

Charalampos was unknown until his grave was found. In the days of Timotheos, metropolitan of Gortyna and Arkadia in Crete, while the courtyard of the monastery of Panagia Kalyviani was being laid out, a grave was opened close beside the old church; a fragrance came from it, and relics were found.

The saint then revealed himself to many people and made known that he came from the village of Dafnes, that he had come to Kalyviani and dedicated himself to the monastery, that he had lived an ascetic life and had himself received a miracle from the Mother of God. Among the miracles worked through him was one for the wife of the agha of Kalyvia, after which the whole family was baptised. Metropolitan Timotheos built a church in his honour, and Metropolitan Makarios consecrated it, to him and to the monastery’s martyred monks and other saints, on 29 September 2012.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 23 August — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/849/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
