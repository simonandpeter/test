# Macarius (Solovyov)

He is the fifth of the six presbyters the Сретенский monastery’s calendar names on one line for the Julian 16 November: «Сщмчч. Иоанна , Николая , Виктора , Василия , Макария и Михаила пресвитеров (1937).» That calendar prints no life page behind his name, so the life here is the one the Russian church calendar at azbyka.ru prints from the database of the St Tikhon’s university. Macarius Arsenievich Solovyov was born in 1876 in the village of Skornevo in the Maksatikha district of the Tver country. Of the whole of his life from his birth to his first arrest, the page says, practically nothing is known: only that he chose the way of the Church and was ordained priest.

On 16 August 1930 he was arrested in the village of Dmitrievskoye in the Likhoslavl district. The page notes that charges at the trials of clergy in those years commonly came down to article 58-10 of the criminal code, propaganda or agitation containing a call to overthrow, undermine or weaken the Soviet power, and that the investigators in his case were no more original than the rest. The trial lasted under a month, and on 10 September he was sentenced to three years’ exile.

His second arrest, on 13 November 1937, was his last. Fourteen days later, on 27 November, the troika of the УНКВД for the Kalinin region sentenced him on a charge of anti-Soviet agitation to the highest measure of punishment, which is shooting, and on 29 November 1937 he was counted worthy of the martyr’s crown. He was glorified by the Russian Orthodox Church among the New Martyrs and Confessors of Russia in August 2000. Nothing else is recorded here: the page gives him no parish between the exile and the second arrest, no family and no place of death.
