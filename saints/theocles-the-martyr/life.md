# Theocles

Theocles was martyred by the sword; that is the whole of the Greek synaxarion’s notice for 14 September. Theocles too comes to the calling above, its verse says, with the cut of the sword for his chariot. This entry records it and nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 14 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2395/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
