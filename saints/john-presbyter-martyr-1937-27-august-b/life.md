# John (Lebedev)

John was born on 9 October 1869 in the village of Vorontsovo in the Yegoryevsk district of Ryazan province, the son of the psalmist Theodore Lebedev. After the Ryazan seminary in 1892 he was made catechist of the Pazhino parish school; in 1896 he married Nadezhda, daughter of the priest Martin Sampsonovich Lipyagov, former rector of the church of the Nativity of Christ in the village of Popovichi, later Pronyukhlovo, in the Zaraysk district, and soon Bishop Polyeuctus of Mikhailov ordained him priest to that church. The church had stood in the village since the sixteenth century; by the beginning of the nineteenth the building was much decayed, and by tradition the peasants miraculously found a cross, which was taken as a command to renew — the calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. After the revolution he was persecuted repeatedly for the faith; in 1930 he was exiled for three years, and on his return went on serving; arrested in 1937 on a charge of counter-revolutionary agitation, he was shot on 9 September 1937 at the Butovo range near Moscow. The Russian calendar keeps him on 27 August (Julian).
