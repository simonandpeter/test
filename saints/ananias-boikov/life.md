# Ananias (Boikov)

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him on the Julian 16 November as one of the two martyrs killed with a presbyter: «Сщмч. Феодора пресвитера и с ним мчч. Анании и Михаила (1929).» He was the warden of the church of the Transfiguration in the town of Kimry in the province of Tver, whose rector was [Theodore (Kolerov)](/saints/theodore-kolerov). When the order came in May 1929 to close the church, and the parishioners would not give it up but kept watch about it for three days, he was arrested with the priest and with [Michael (Boldakov)](/saints/michael-boldakov).

Twenty were charged in that case. The accused were taken to the prison at Tver and then brought down the Volga by night on an open barge to Kimry, where the trial opened on 20 October 1929 with the Moscow papers invited and admission by ticket. None of them admitted guilt. On 27 October the priest, the warden and Michael were sentenced to be shot, with two members of the church council whose sentences were afterwards cancelled, and they were shot at eleven at night on the Julian 16 November, the 29th by the civil reckoning. The funeral of the three was held in the cathedral of Kimry before a great gathering of the faithful.

That is the whole of what the two hosts give for him, and neither gives a year of birth, an age, a trade or a birthplace: he has no page of his own on either, and both tell him only inside the priest’s life. The office this corpus would have written for him is church warden, and there is no such key in the five locale packs, so none is written. The spelling of the surname is the calendar’s own and is not quite steady: the title on the name link and the title of the Sretensky life both read «Бойков», as azbyka.ru does throughout, while the last paragraph of that same Sretensky life writes «Войкова» once.
