# Julian of the Dogazou

saint.gr's calendar for 12 Ιουνίου names him and gives no life. Its whole notice is four words — «Ο Όσιος Ιουλιανός απεβίωσε ειρηνικά», the venerable Julian died peacefully — and the day's line adds the only other thing the site says of him, the place he is named for: «εν τοις Δογάζου», in the Dogazou. What that was, and where, the page does not say.

Above the notice stand the verses of the synaxarion, which say no more than that he was buried: «Ιουλιανός, θάπτεται Πατήρ μέγας, / Tάφου τον ουδόν … ημερών πλήρης φθάσας» — Julian, a great father, is buried, having reached the threshold of the grave full of days; and the page glosses the parenthesis itself, that the word means the step and the way. There is no century here, no monastery, no manner of life and no hymn.
