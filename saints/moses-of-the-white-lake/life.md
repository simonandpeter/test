# Moses of the White Lake

saint.gr gives the venerable Moses two sentences and the life says no more than they do. He lived about the end of the fifteenth century and the beginning of the sixteenth, and he was an ascetic in the monastery of the Holy Trinity of the White Lake in Russia; and he fell asleep in peace, in a holy manner. The day's line calls him simply «εκ Ρωσίας», the one out of Russia.

The page gives no place of birth, no year for it, no year for his repose, no abbot, no office in the house, no relic and no hymn. Two other men of this name stand on the same Greek day, on pages of their own — one on the mountain of Ramas and one a disciple of Polychronius — and none of the three is told in terms of the others.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 23 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/23/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3693/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
