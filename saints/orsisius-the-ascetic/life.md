# Orsisius

saint.gr's calendar for 15 June names Orsisius — the page writes «Ορτίσιος» and adds «ή Ορσίσιος» beside it — and says he was one of the famous ascetics of the desert, and that sayings of his are preserved in the Paradise of the Fathers of Palladius. It gives no country, no monastery, no year and no hymn, and closes by saying only that he reposed in peace. What it does print at length is two of the sayings.

The first: I think that if a man does not keep his own heart well, he forgets and neglects all the good things he has heard, and so the enemy, finding a place in him, throws him down. For as a lamp that has been trimmed and is shining goes out little by little if it is neglected and no oil is taken for it, and thereafter the darkness grows strong against it, so too, when a soul is neglectful, the Holy Spirit withdraws by so much, until at last its warmth is quenched.

The second: an unbaked brick laid in a foundation near a river does not last a single day; but if it has been fired, it remains like stone. So a man who has a carnal mind, and has not been fired with the fear of God as Joseph the All-comely was, is dissolved when he comes forward into office. For many are the temptations of such men when they are in the midst of people; and it is good that one who knows his own measure should flee the weight of office.
