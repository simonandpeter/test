# Demetrius (Semenov)

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 14 июня ст. ст. carries five names on one line and his is the second of them: «Сщмч. Иосифа и Димитрия пресвитеров (1918) Сщмчч. Александра , Павла пресвитеров и Николая диакона (1938).» The line gives the forenames in the genitive, two grade-class words for the five together, and two years — 1918 for the first two names and 1938 for the last three — and it gives no surname to any of them. The name link behind his place in it supplies the calendar’s own nominative with the surname, «Димитрий (Семенов), пресвитер, сщмч.», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from.

That is everything this calendar records of him. He has no life page, no icon, no hymn, no place, no age and no parish anywhere on it, and the name page behind his place on the line carries two days and no text at all. The second of those days is «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia; that is a synaxis and not a second feast, and he does not render on it. Nothing is reasoned here from the surname.

The line’s «Сщмч.» and «пресвитеров» make him a hieromartyr and a presbyter, and the link repeats the grade as «пресвитер». He is given no relation to the four others on the line: they share a line, a grade-class and a year, and the calendar prints nothing at all that says they died in one place or knew one another.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 14 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260614.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/15072.html); read 9 October 2026.*
