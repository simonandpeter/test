# Tamara (Satsi)

Her line in the calendar is three words and a year. The Сретенский monastery’s calendar for 18 апреля ст. ст. prints «Прмц. Тамары (1942)» — a venerable-martyr of that name, 1942 — and the name page behind the forename gives both the surname the line leaves out and an office the line does not mention: «Тамара (Сатси), игумения, прмц.», an igumena, the superior of a women’s house.

That is the whole of it. There is no life page for her on the day page and none on her name page, and 1942 is the only year either of them gives. Nothing is said of where she was born, which house she governed, when or by whom she was arrested, or where she died. The office is the one thing the name page knows that the line does not, and it is set down among her ranks rather than written out as a biography she has not been given.

Her name page lists one other day, «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church, and that is what the rank of new martyr here rests on. One row per church is the schema, so that day is recorded on her Russian row and she does not render on it.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 18 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260418.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14454.html); read 8 October 2026.*
