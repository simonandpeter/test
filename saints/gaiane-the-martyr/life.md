# Gaiane

The synaxaria have no biographical detail of Gaiane, the Greek synaxarion says — only the phrase that she was perfected with her back burned. Its verse bids her give her back to be burned, so that she may escape the back of the flaming sword. It gives no date.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 27 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2522/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
