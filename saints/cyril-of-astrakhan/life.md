# Cyril of Astrakhan

saint.gr's calendar for 18 Μαρτίου keeps a Russian abbot, Cyril of Astrakhan, and heads his page «(; - 1576)». It says he lived in the sixteenth century in Russia, followed the monastic life and was tonsured a monk at the monastery of the Holy Trinity at Astrakhan. In 1568, when he became its abbot, he renewed the house and built its churches of the Holy Trinity, of the Entry of the Theotokos and of St Nicholas.

The page adds that he enjoyed the honour and the respect not only of the Christians of the region but of its Muslims as well. Cyril fell asleep in peace in the year 1576, and his icon was painted in 1676 on the testimony of one Aemilianus Pafentiev, whom the saint had saved from drowning in the river Volga.

That is the whole of what has been read. saint.gr gives him no year of birth and no name in the world, names no relics, and prints neither a couplet nor a hymn under his name; nothing has been added here from any other source or from memory. Several other Cyrils are kept on this same 18 Μαρτίου and on other days, and nothing on this page identifies him with any of them.
