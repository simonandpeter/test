# Peter from the Soldiers

saint.gr's calendar for 9 Οκτωβρίου names Peter «ο από στρατιωτών», Peter the one from among the soldiers, and gives him a soldier's beginning. He lived in the years of the iconoclast emperor Theophilus, which the page dates 829, and came from the province of the Galatians in Asia Minor. He was the son of Theophilus and Eudocia, and his first name was Leo; and because he was handsome and strong in body and sound in judgement the emperor made him a count.

After campaigning for many years and performing many feats of courage, the page says, he came to despise all of it and became a monk in the monastery of Daphnon, with the name Peter. From there he went to Olympus, and from Olympus to Jerusalem; and he did not stay there either, but left for Laodicea and for Attaleia. Having borne with manly endurance the labours of the road and of asceticism, and the wild anger of the Ishmaelites he met on the way, he returned at last to Olympus.

Because the height of his virtue became known to the emperors, the page says, the emperor Basil the Macedonian in the year 867 persuaded him to settle in the monastery of Saint Phocas; and there, after many spiritual labours, he gave up his soul to the Lord in peace. saint.gr gives him no year of death and prints no hymn for him. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands.

The Russian calendar keeps him on the same day as the Greek, its own 9 October, and names him from the other end of the same life: «Прп. Петра Галатийского (IX)», Peter the Galatian, where saint.gr has Peter «ο από στρατιωτών», the one from among the soldiers. Its index page adds a second epithet, «Константинопольский», of Constantinople, and its century — the ninth — is the century the Greek page's two years fall in. The Russian carries no life for him at all, so everything above this paragraph is still the Greek page's, and what the Russian adds is the province he came from, raised into his name.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 9 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/09/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1029/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026; and the Russian church calendar's [9 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261009.html) with [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/1773.html), read 4 October 2026.*
