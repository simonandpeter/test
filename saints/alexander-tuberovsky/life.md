# Alexander (Tuberovsky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him second of thirteen in one line on 10 декабря, and he is the only one of the thirteen it gives a life. He was born on 8 March 1881 into a priest’s family in the village of Makaveevo, in the Kasimov district of the Ryazan province. He finished the Kasimov church school in 1896 and the Ryazan seminary in 1902, entered the Moscow Theological Academy the next year as an external student, and from 16 August 1907 held its professorial stipend.

On 28 August 1907 he was appointed to teach fundamental, dogmatic and moral theology at the Kaluga seminary, and from 16 August 1911 he acted as docent of the Moscow Academy in the chair of dogmatic theology. On 11 October 1917 he defended a dissertation called «Воскресение Христово. Опыт Православно-мистической идеологии догмата» — the Resurrection of Christ, an essay in the Orthodox mystical reading of the dogma — which the page calls the only work a theologian of modern times devoted to the chief dogma of the Christian Church.

The defence had begun on 1 September 1915 and provoked, the page says, the sharpest dispute in the whole history of that Academy, with every leading theologian of the day taking part. He was made master after it and appointed docent, and a month later extraordinary professor; the dissertation was given the second Makarius prize.

In 1919, the Academy having been closed, he went home to Makaveevo and married a merchant’s daughter who had a university education. Between 1922 and 1924, at the height of the campaign against the church, he accepted the priesthood as a confessor would and served alongside his own father in that village. He was arrested on 13 (26) September 1937, and on 10 (23) December of the same year twenty-three people, he among them, were shot within the bounds of the Ryazan region. He was numbered among the New Martyrs and Confessors of Russia at the jubilee council of August 2000.

The day page carries eleven hymns and not one is under any of the thirteen names.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 10 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261210.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4735.htm); read 13 October 2026.*
