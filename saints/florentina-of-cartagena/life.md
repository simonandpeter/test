# Florentina of Cartagena

Florentina was born at Cartagena, in Spain, in the middle of the sixth century — doxologia's life writes the city as Cartagina and names the country. She came of a blessed household: her parents were Severianus and Theodora, and her brothers were Leander, Fulgentius and Isidore, who all three became bishops afterwards. Orphaned young, she was given into the care of her eldest brother Leander, who was then at the beginning of the monastic life.

Under his guidance, and raised in the Christian values, she chose the way of ascetic labour and of a life given to God. She put her brother's counsels into practice with much zeal: he used to urge monks and young nuns to feed on the reading of Holy Scripture, to persevere in prayer and fasting, to cultivate love of neighbour and to be of use to those around them, and to keep measure and discernment in every part of life.

She became in time abbess of the monastery of Saint Mary of the Valley, near the town of Écija. The life says she founded as many as forty monasteries and had the direction of more than a thousand nuns. After a life given to the service of God she passed to eternity about the year 612, and her day of commemoration is 20 June. Her relics are in the church of San Juan Bautista, in the province of Cáceres, in the autonomous community of Extremadura in Spain. The calendar prints no hymn for her.
