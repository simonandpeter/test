# Ignatius (7 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 7 December names the venerable Ignatius and gives him one line of life: he lived as a monk in a cell near the Blachernae, and he reposed in peace. That is the whole of the page. No country, no century, no monastery, no abbot, no relic and no hymn is printed, and the only place named is the quarter of Constantinople his cell stood near.

The couplet set over his name is the rest of what there is: you went away full of good deeds, father, out of a life that was empty and full of evils. The gloss the site prints under the name belongs to a Byzantine writer of that name and not to this ascetic, and nothing is claimed here from it.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3194/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
