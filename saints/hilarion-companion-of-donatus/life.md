# Hilarion, companion of Donatus

saint.gr's calendar for 4 Ιουλίου keeps two men in one line, «Άγιος Δονάτος επίσκοπος Λιβύης και Ιλαρίων οσιομάρτυρας», and its life page tells them both at once. Of Hilarion it says only that he was a monk and how he died. The page opens by disputing the see the listing gives [Donatus](/saints/donatus-bishop-4-july): in M. Galanos's opinion, it reports, the man in question is the Donatus who was bishop of Arezzo in Etruria, and not of Libya at all.

Donatus's parents, it says, had themselves died for Christ, and their son, together with a certain monk Hilarion, had taken refuge in that city, of which Donatus was afterwards made shepherd. But in the persecution set in motion by Julian the Apostate the prefect Quadratianus ordered the two of them to sacrifice to the idols. They not only refused but protested vigorously to the public authorities about that error of theirs.

Then they beat Hilarion with rods until he died, and Donatus, after many tortures, they beheaded. The Christians took up their holy relics from the place of execution, where they had been left, and buried them with honours. The page gives no year for any of it, no country for Hilarion and no monastery, and it prints no hymn under either name; the only verse on it is a couplet for Donatus.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 4 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/04/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1997/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
