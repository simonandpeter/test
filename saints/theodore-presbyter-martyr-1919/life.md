# Theodore (Antipin)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him on 11 января ст. ст. in one line with two fellow priests and gives no life for any of the three. The whole of its account is «Сщмчч. Николая, Феодора и Владимира пресвитеров (1919)» — the hieromartyrs Nicholas, Theodore and Vladimir, presbyters, 1919 — and behind that line the calendar's own name links carry the surnames the line leaves out.

His is «Феодор (Антипин), иерей»: the priest Theodore Antipin. The two the calendar keeps with him are [Nicholas (Matsievsky)](/saints/nicholas-presbyter-martyr-1919-11-january) and [Vladimir (Fokin)](/saints/vladimir-presbyter-martyr-1919). The calendar names no diocese, no parish, no place of death and no manner of it, and the 1919 in its bracket is the whole of what it dates the three of them by; it is the year of their deaths, in the civil war.

The day page carries troparia for others of the saints and feasts it heads, and none under their names. That is everything the source says of him.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 11 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260111.html); read 3 October 2026. The calendar prints no life page for him.*
