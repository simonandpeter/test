# Zeno of Diospolis

Zeno, one of the Seventy, is the same, the Greek synaxarion says, as Zenas the lawyer, a true and approved worker of the Gospel, who helped in Crete together with Apollos; which is why the apostle Paul so warmly charges [Titus](/saints/titus-the-apostle) to take such care of the two that they lack nothing (Titus 3:13). He was afterwards a distinguished bishop of Diospolis. The synaxarion keeps him on 27 September with [Mark](/saints/mark-of-byblos) and [Aristarchus](/saints/aristarchus-of-apamea), and gives no date.

The Romanian calendar keeps him on 27 April, and its short life adds the one thing the Greek entry does not say: that it was the apostle Peter who set him bishop in Diospolis. It agrees that the apostle Paul remembers him in the letter to the Romans, and closes as it closes for his two companions — that he suffered many evils from unbelievers for rebuilding churches and pulling down idols, and that, himself becoming a worker of miracles and of healings, he departed to the Lord. It gives no year.

The Russian calendar keeps him on the same 27 September, third in a line of three of the Seventy — «Апп. от 70-ти Марка, Аристарха и Зины (I)» — and its index of names is the most explicit of the four calendars about who he was: «Святитель Зина Законник, епископ Диоспольский (Лиддский), апостол от 70-ти», Zenas the lawyer, bishop of Diospolis or Lydda, apostle of the Seventy. The other two are [Mark of Byblos](/saints/mark-of-byblos) and [Aristarchus of Apamea](/saints/aristarchus-of-apamea).

The Serbian church's Ohrid Prologue keeps the three of the Seventy under one heading on this day and agrees with the Russian about who he was in half a line: «Св. Зинон, или Зина назван од ап. Павла закоником» — Zeno, or Zenas, called the lawyer by the apostle [Paul](/saints/paul-the-apostle), citing Titus 3:13 as the Greek entry does, and bishop in Lydda of Palestine, which is the Diospolis of this folder's name. Of the three together it says that they shone like stars in the darkness of heathendom and turned many to the faith of Christ, and that they shine now as stars in the kingdom of the Christ they loved. It gives no date for any of them.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 27 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2520/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026; and doxologia.ro's calendar for 27 aprilie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/27-aprilie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-apostol-zinon), read 20 September 2026; the Russian church calendar's [27 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260927.html), read 4 October 2026; and the Serbian church's [Ohrid Prologue for the Julian 27 September](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-10-10&prolog=1), read 4 October 2026.*
