# Basil (Ratishvili)

saint.gr's calendar gives him one sentence and a date that does not construe. The venerable Basil, surnamed Ratishvili, the Wonderworker, lived between the eighteenth and the ninth century in Georgia — that is what the page says — and practised asceticism at the monastery of Kabeni, whose ancient name, it adds, was Gethsemane. He fell asleep in holiness and in peace.

Nothing else is printed of him: no parentage, no year that can be read, no teacher, no wonder of his own and no burial place. The page prints neither couplet nor hymn, and the two centuries it gives are left here exactly as it has them rather than mended in one direction or the other.
