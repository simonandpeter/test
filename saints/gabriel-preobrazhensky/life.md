# Gabriel (Preobrazhensky)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 13 февраля ст. ст. in a line of six words that names two priests together and gives the year they died: «Сщмч. Василия и Гавриила пресвитеров (1919).» The rank word is «Сщмч.», hieromartyr, and «пресвитеров» gives the two of them as presbyters. The name link behind the line carries the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line itself leaves out, «Гавриил (Преображенский), иерей, сщмч.».

The calendar prints no life page for either of them and no hymn under either name; the troparia on the day page are for other saints it heads. Nothing further is written here, and in particular he is not identified with any saint of the same name whom the calendar keeps on another day. The line, the two rank words and the year 1919 are the whole of what was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 13 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260213.html); read 3 October 2026.*
