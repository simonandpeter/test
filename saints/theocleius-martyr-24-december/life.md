# Theocleius (24 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 24 December keeps Sossius and Theocleius on one page, and begins by saying where they come from: they are unknown to the Synaxaristes of Saint Nikodemos, and are mentioned in the Parisian Codex 1621 with a few biographical details. What follows is what that codex gives. They were martyred, it says, under Maximian, whose reign the page dates 286 to 305, and under Magnentius.

They were seized as Christians by the governor Baudos, who was governor of Adrianople of Macedonia; and because they were not persuaded to deny Christ they were tortured without mercy in the most frightful way. Their torments were so many, the page says, that it is impossible to count them, and a man wonders how they managed to stay alive through them. In the end they were beheaded, and so received the incorruptible crowns of martyrdom.

Because the page names both men instead of counting them, each of them keeps a folder here, and each relates the other: this one's companion is [Sossius](/saints/sossius-martyr-24-december). No hymn is printed for either, no year of birth is given, and the feast is marked fixed. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence by which saint.gr marks an entry whose existence or date it will not vouch for.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/24/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3231/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
