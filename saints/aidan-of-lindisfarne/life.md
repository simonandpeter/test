# Aidan of Lindisfarne

Aidan fell asleep in 651, a little after the death of his friend and the successor of St Oswald, the holy king Oswine, and was buried at the monastery of Lindisfarne. At the moment of his death an innocent young shepherd on the hills above the monastery — Cuthbert, later bishop of Lindisfarne — saw his blessed soul rising to heaven in an unearthly light, and the vision moved him to give himself to God as a monk. So the Greek synaxarion, which keeps him on 31 August among the saints of Britain.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 31 Αυγούστου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/859/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
