# Peter of Olympus

saint.gr keeps a martyr Peter on 28 November and gives him two sentences. He was an ascetic on Olympus, it says, and he was imprisoned by Copronymus together with [Stephen the New](/saints/stephen-the-new), whose page it keeps on the same day, because he venerated the holy icons. Afterwards they beat him until he died, and so he received the crown of martyrdom.

That is the whole of the page's account. It gives him no country, no monastery, no year and no companions besides Stephen, and prints no hymn for him; what it prints besides is his couplet, which plays on his name — «Ἂν εἰκόνων τύπτωσι τὸν Πέτρον χάριν, Εὕρωσιν αὐτὸν πέτρινoν τῷ σαρκίῳ»: if they strike Peter for the sake of the icons, they will find him stone in his very flesh.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/28/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3100/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
