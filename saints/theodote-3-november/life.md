# Theodote (3 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 3 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιοι Δάσιος, Σέβηρος, Άνδρωνας, Θεόδοτος και Θεοδότη», and the page behind that line says one thing only: the holy [Dasius](/saints/dasius-3-november), [Severus](/saints/severus-3-november), [Andron](/saints/andron), [Theodotus](/saints/theodotus-3-november) and Theodote were martyred by the sword. There is no city, no persecutor, no year, nothing of who the five were to one another, and nothing of their relics.

What the page does say about her it says in the epigram, and it is the only distinction it draws inside the company: «Σύμμαρτυς ἔστω καὶ γυνὴ τοῖς ἀνδράσι, τετμημένοις τέσσαρσι συντετμημένη» — let the woman too be a fellow martyr to the men, cut down along with the four who were cut. So she is the one woman among five, and the four others are the men.

This folder takes its name from the day, because the calendar gives it nothing else to take a name from. No hymn is printed. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands, bare as it is.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/03/index.aspx) and [the notice of the five](https://www.saint.gr/2874/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
