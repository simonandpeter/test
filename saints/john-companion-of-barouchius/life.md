# John, companion of Barouchius

saint.gr's calendar for 27 Μαρτίου names two martyrs on one line, «Άγιοι Ιωάννης και Βαρούχιος οι Μάρτυρες», and the page behind the line carries a single sentence about them: «Οι Άγιοι Μάρτυρες Βαρούχιος και Ιωάννης τελειώθηκαν διά ξίφους» — the holy martyrs Barouchius and John were perfected by the sword. That is the whole of the source. It gives no year, no city, no emperor, no judge, no relic and no hymn.

The one thing it adds is a couplet that sets the two of them against each other in courage: «Οὐ θάρσος ἧττον Ἰωάννου πρὸς ξίφος, Βαρουχίῳ ὑπῆρχεν, ἀλλὰ καὶ πλέον» — [Barouchius](/saints/barouchius-companion-of-john) had no less boldness than John before the sword, but even more. The couplet names John first in its first line and gives the palm to his companion in the second.

Nothing else on the page is about him: the four numbered notes under the name Ιωάννης — the Forerunner, the son of Zebedee, Chrysostom, and many other saints, kings and hierarchs — are the site's encyclopaedia entry on the word. Because the page gives this John no place, no date and no epithet, the companionship of Barouchius is what identifies him, and it is what his name in this corpus carries.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 27 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/27/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1265/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
