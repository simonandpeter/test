# Achillas of Alexandria

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives him one sentence. The line for 3 June reads «Άγιος Αχιλλάς Επίσκοπος Αλεξανδρείας», Saint Achillas, bishop of Alexandria, and the page behind it says only this: Saint Achillas, 311 to 312, was bishop of Alexandria and fell asleep in peace. The two years stand in a parenthesis with nothing to say whether they are his life or his episcopate, and since the page does not say, no year is recorded for him here.

So what there is of him is a name, a see, a death in peace and that parenthesis. The page gives him no birthplace, no predecessor and no successor, nothing of the Church of Alexandria in those years, and no hymn and no verse. The heading above the entry is a lexicon note on the name Achilles and its bearers, from the hero of the Trojan war to a mathematician of the fourth century before Christ, and it is the page's own furniture rather than a word about this bishop.
