# Martha (Kovrova)

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names her first of the two people of 1938 it puts on one line for the Julian 3 March, «Прмц. Марфы и мч. Михаила (1938).», and prints no life for either. Martha Stepanovna Kovrova was born in 1887 in the village of Galygino in the Korobovo district of the Moscow region and came of a peasant family. Resolving to give her life to the service of God, she became a novice of the women’s monastery of Saints Alexander and Maria in the village of Yevlevo in the Yegoryevsk district of the province of Ryazan, and she lived in that house until it was closed.

On 26 February 1938 she was arrested. She was charged with agitation against the collective farms, and on 9 March 1938 the troika of the НКВД for the Moscow region passed its final sentence, the highest measure of punishment: to be shot. She was shot on 16 March 1938 at the Butovo range. That is the whole of what either host gives for her — no interrogation, no witness, no word of her own, and nothing of the thirty years between her entering the monastery and her arrest.

Two dates follow the death. She was rehabilitated on 20 July 1989 by the prosecutor of the Moscow region, under the head of the repressions of 1937, and she was numbered with the new martyrs and confessors of Russia by a determination of the Holy Synod of the Russian Orthodox Church of 17 July 2006. Her page is one of the few of this range that azbyka.ru credits neither to Игумен Дамаскин (Орловский) nor to fond.ru but to pstgu.ru. Her monastic grade is that heading’s, «послушница», a novice, and the calendar’s name link reads «послушница» too, so the two hosts agree and the office is written.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 3 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260303.html), which names her on one line with one other and prints no life under her name; and the Russian church calendar at azbyka.ru for 16 марта — [the life](https://azbyka.ru/days/sv-marfa-kovrova); read 6 October 2026.*
