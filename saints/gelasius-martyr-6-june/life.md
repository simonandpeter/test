# Gelasius (6 June)

When the persecution against the Christians began, this holy martyr shared out all his property among the poor, put on white clothes and went to the martyrs of Christ who were lying in the prisons. Seeing how bitterly they were tortured, he kissed their wounds, asked them what they wished for, and urged them to stay brave in the face of martyrdom.

So the pagans arrested him and brought him before the archon. There Gelasius confessed his faith in Christ boldly, and preaching to them he showed the error of the worship of idols. At first the archon despised him, but then he ordered him to be beaten and to be beheaded; and so the holy martyr Gelasius gave up his soul into the hands of the Lord.

That is the whole of the life the calendar prints. It gives no country, no year, no name for the archon and no place of the martyrdom, and the page carries no troparion. The corpus holds another bare-named Gelasius, a martyr with no recorded life on the Greek 26 August; nothing read here makes the two one man, and nothing read here makes them two, so they are kept apart and the question is left open.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and tells the same thing in its own words. saint.gr says that when the persecution against the Christians began this saint distributed everything he had to the poor, put on a white garment and went to the martyrs of Christ; and that seeing their frightful tortures he kissed their wounds, asked their prayers and urged them to stand bravely in their martyrdom. So the idolaters seized him and brought him before the ruler, where he confessed Christ with courage and preached the error of the idols. At first the ruler despised him, but afterwards he ordered that he be beaten, and then had him beheaded. The couplet on the page plays on the name: you laugh the blessed laughter, blessed one, your head cut off, O upright-minded Gelasios.
