# Basil (Kolosov) (9 May)

The Russian church calendar names him and gives no life. Its list for 9 мая ст. ст. reads «Сщмч. Василия пресвитера (1939)» — the hieromartyr Basil, a presbyter, with the year of his death — and the name page behind that line carries one thing more than the day gives: his surname. It heads him «Василий (Колосов), иерей, сщмч.», then prints his days, «26 января (Новомуч.), 9 мая», and nothing else at all.

So what the source says of him is this: that his family name was Колосов, that he was a priest, that he died in 1939, and that he is numbered among the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church, whose synaxis is the January day. There is no birth year, no place, no diocese, no parish, no account of his arrest or of how he died, and the day page prints no troparion or kontakion under his name.

This corpus keeps another priest of exactly the same name and the same grade, whom the calendar prints on 26 ноября ст. ст. and dates 1937. That they are two men and not one is the calendar’s own reading rather than this site’s: it keeps a separate name page for each, and each page lists a different day beside the common January synaxis. Nothing further distinguishes them, because nothing further is given about either, and that is said here so that a reader who comes to one of these two folders knows the other exists and knows on what the distinction rests.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 9 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260509.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13716.html), which gives no life; read 8 October 2026.*
