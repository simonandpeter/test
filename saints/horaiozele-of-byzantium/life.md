# Horaiozele of Byzantium

saint.gr says Horaiozele lived in the first century after Christ. Her country was Byzantium and her parents were Greek idolaters, and at first she was an idolater herself; afterwards she became a Christian through the preaching of the Apostle [Andrew](/saints/andrew-the-first-called). Because she was an educated woman, the page says, she was able to learn all the truths of the Gospel properly, so as to teach them to other women as well, and she managed to draw many of them to Christ.

When under the emperor Domitian a persecution was set in motion against the Christians, Horaiozele was arrested, and because she stayed faithful to the love of Christ she was beheaded and was ranked in the honourable choir of the Martyrs. Her dead body they threw into the fire. Above the notice the page sets a distich: «Eις πυρ βληθείσα Ωραιοζήλη Λόγε, Άπασι βρύει τους κρουνούς των θαυμάτων» — cast into the fire, O Word, Horaiozele pours out for all the streams of her wonders.

That is the whole of the Greek notice: no year of her own, no judge named, no relics and no hymn. Her page links the page of [Jerusalem](/saints/jerusalem-companion-of-horaiozele), whom the same calendar keeps on this same day and whose notice calls her Horaiozele's friend and housemate, taken and beheaded with her.
