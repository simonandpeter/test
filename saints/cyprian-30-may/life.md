# Cyprian (30 May)

saint.gr's calendar for 30 Μαΐου names him and gives one sentence. The holy Cyprian, it says, came from Antioch, and he fell asleep in peace. That is the whole of the source: it gives him no rank beyond the word holy, no century, no year, no office, no monastery and no manner of life, and it prints no hymn and no epigram for him.

The same calendar keeps another Cyprian of Antioch, the hieromartyr and bishop, on 2 October, and that man is a martyr where this one dies in peace. Two entries on one calendar are two commemorations, so the two are kept apart here, and this folder is identified by nothing but the day the Greek calendar prints him on.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 30 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/30/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2320/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
