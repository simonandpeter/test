# Hira

The Romanian calendar keeps the martyr Hira on 26 September, and the one line its life prints is her whole record: she finished her course by the cutting of her head with the sword. This entry records that line and nothing more.

*After doxologia.ro, [Viaţa Sfintei Muceniţe Hira](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfintei-mucenite-hira); read 30 August 2026.*
