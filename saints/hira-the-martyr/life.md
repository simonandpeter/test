# Hira

The Romanian calendar keeps the martyr Hira on 26 September, and the one line its life prints is her whole record: she finished her course by the cutting of her head with the sword. This entry records that line and nothing more.

The Greek synaxarion keeps her on 26 September too, and its one line is that she was martyred, put to death with a knife (μαχαίρι). Its verse turns on her name, which in Greek means a widow: slain, the widow is wedded to the Word, having written the marriage contract in her blood.
