# Vartan of Armenia

saint.gr's calendar for 7 August names Saint Vartan, the martyr from Armenia, and then says that it has nothing to tell: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου» — we have no details of the saint's life. The listing's own line is therefore the whole of what is recorded of him here: that he was a martyr, and that he was from Armenia.

There is no century on the page, no emperor, no judge, no manner of death, no relic and no hymn. Where the source gives a name and a line, the life says exactly that, and this is such a case.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/756/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
