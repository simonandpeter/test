# Joseph of Alaverdi

Joseph of Alaverdi was one of the thirteen Assyrian Fathers sent to Georgia to preach the holy Gospel; he reposed in peace in the Lord in 570, and his holy relics — the Romanian page runs on past the point read here. The Romanian calendar keeps him on 15 September.
