# Joasaph

Joasaph was born at Priluki in Poltava province on 8 September 1705, the feast of the Nativity of the Theotokos, and named Joachim at baptism, of the old and devout Little Russian family of the Gorlenkos. In 1712 his father gave the seven-year-old to the Kyiv Academy, within whose walls he felt the pull of the monastic life; for seven years the future hierarch tested himself, and at last opened his mind to his parents, who long begged their firstborn not to take the tonsure — but in 1725, secretly from them, he took the rasophore with the name Hilarion at the Mezhigorsky monastery in Kyiv, and on 21 November — the Sretensky calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. The Russian calendar keeps on 4 September (Julian) the finding of his relics in 1911, with troparia and a kontakion.
