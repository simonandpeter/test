# Gregory Palamas

Gregory was born at Constantinople in 1296. His father rose to be a prominent dignitary at the court of Andronicus II Palaeologus but died early, and the emperor himself took a hand in raising and schooling the fatherless boy. He was able and diligent and mastered the whole course of what then counted as higher education, and Andronicus hoped he would give himself to the work of the state.

Instead, barely twenty, he withdrew in 1316 to Mount Athos and entered Vatopedi as a novice under the elder Nikodemos, was tonsured there and began the ascetic life; a year later the Evangelist John appeared to him in a vision and promised his protection. His mother and sisters also became monastics.

After Nikodemos died he spent eight years under the elder Nikephoros, and after his death moved to the Lavra of Saint Athanasius, serving in the refectory and then as a church singer. Three years later he settled in the small skete of Glossia, seeking a greater perfection, and there its head began to teach him the method of unceasing prayer. He was afterwards archbishop of Thessalonica.

The Romanian calendar keeps him on 14 noiembrie, and keeps his family with him on the same day. The life doxologia prints there gives him as born at Constantinople in 1296, the eldest of the seven children of [Constantius](/saints/constantius-father-of-gregory-palamas) and [Kalloni](/saints/kalloni-mother-of-gregory-palamas), courtiers of the emperor Andronicus II who had fled Asia Minor before the Turkish invasions; his teacher, it says, thought he was hearing Aristotle himself. Around 1316 he took his mother, two brothers — [Theodosius](/saints/theodosius-brother-of-gregory-palamas) and [Macarius](/saints/macarius-brother-of-gregory-palamas) — and two sisters — [Epicharis](/saints/epicharis-sister-of-gregory-palamas) and [Theodota](/saints/theodota-sister-of-gregory-palamas) — with him into the monastic life, and went on foot to Athos.
