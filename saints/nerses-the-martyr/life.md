# Nerses

saint.gr's calendar for 15 June names Nerses and then says in so many words that we have no details of the saint's life. The only other thing the page prints for him is a two-line epigram, which says that Nerses departed by way of martyrdom, to where the choirs of the contenders are in heaven. So the calendar keeps him as a martyr and tells nothing else: no country, no year, no persecutor, no manner of death and no hymn.

This life records what the source records and no more. The name is borne by several bishops and martyrs of Armenia, but saint.gr's entry for 15 June does not say which of them, if any, this is, and nothing here should be read as identifying him with them.
