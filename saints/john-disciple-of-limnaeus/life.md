# John, disciple of Limnaeus

saint.gr keeps four ascetics together on this day and gives them one page and two sentences. The venerable John was a disciple of [Limnaeus](/saints/limnaeus), whom the site keeps on 22 February, and he lived as an ascetic on a mountain near the city of Targala. He stayed out in the open and bore the burning heat of the day and the cold of winter, girded about with irons and fed most sparely; and so, having lived, he fell asleep in peace.

The venerable [Antiochus](/saints/antiochus-of-ramas), [Antoninus](/saints/antoninus-of-ramas) and [Moses](/saints/moses-of-ramas) imitated John in his ascesis and in his way of life. They passed the time of their lives in severe ascesis, in prayer and in fasting, on the high peak of the mountain of the city of Ramas, and they fell asleep in peace.

The distich over the four of them counts them as a company: a four-numbered array of honourable men is drawn up for thee, and departs from this life. The page gives no year for any of them, no emperor, no monastery, no relic and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 23 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/23/index.aspx) and [the page for the four of them](https://www.saint.gr/3685/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
