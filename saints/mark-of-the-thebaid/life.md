# Mark of the Thebaid

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him for 27 октября ст. ст. in six words — «Мч. Марка и иже с ним (304).» — a martyr, and those with him, who died in 304. The name link behind the line carries the calendar's own nominative and the one thing the line does not give, which is where he was from: «Марк Фиваидский, мч.», Mark of the Thebaid, the Egyptian country up the Nile. No life page follows his name on the day page, and the name page prints the title, the one day, and nothing else at all.

A second Russian calendar, azbyka.ru, keeps him on the same day and adds two sentences, which are the whole of his recorded life: he suffered for preaching Christianity in Asia, and after cruel torments he was put to death. The page gives the two sentences twice, in slightly different words, and no more — no city, no emperor, no governor, no year, and no hymn.

**The two things that page says do not sit easily together, and both are left standing.** The by-name the calendars give him is of the Thebaid, which is in Egypt, and the province it says he suffered in is Asia. Neither page explains the distance between them, no third source was read, and nothing is chosen here: a man may be named for where he came from and die somewhere else, but that is a reading and not something either page states.

The companions the line grants him are granted no names. «И иже с ним» — and those with him — is this calendar's way of heading a company it does not enumerate, so the entry is one folder and not one per man, by the ruling that a synaxis naming nobody is a single entry. How many they were and whether any of them is in this corpus already under some other day cannot be read off either page.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 27 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261027.html); and azbyka.ru's calendar for the same day — [his page](https://azbyka.ru/days/sv-mark-fivaidskij); both read 4 October 2026.*
