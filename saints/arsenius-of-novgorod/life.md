# Arsenius of Novgorod

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life beyond two sentences. The venerable Arsenius of Novgorod, the fool for Christ's sake, fell asleep in peace on the twelfth of July in the year 1570. The keeping of his memory on the eighth of May, the page goes on, was established on account of the translation of his holy relics in the year 1785, and also for the other saints who bore the same name and who are commemorated on this day.

That is the whole of it. The page tells nothing of his birth, nothing of his family, nothing of the manner of his folly for Christ and nothing of where in Novgorod he lived or was buried; it prints no apolytikion and no kontakion for him, and it names no monastery. Whatever else the Russian calendar holds about him is not on this page, and nothing here is taken from anywhere else.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1533/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
