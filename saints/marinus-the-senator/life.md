# Marinus the Senator

saint.gr's calendar for 16 December keeps Saint Marinus, who received the martyr's crown, it says, at the end of the third century after Christ. He was born and grew up at Rome and took the rank of senator; but his many good works towards the Christians roused the suspicions of his colleagues, who looked into it, discovered that Marinus belonged to the Christian Church, and denounced him.

He was arrested by imperial order, and because he would not sacrifice to the idols he was condemned to death. While they were leading him to the place of execution he saw some of his friends weeping, and said to them gently: why do you weep and grieve? Know that I am going from darkness into the eternal light, from the ground of the stadium to the prizes, and from death into everlasting life.

A little after, the page ends, his head fell and his soul flew to the dwellings of the righteous. That is all it prints of him: no emperor is named, only an imperial order; no year is given within the century; no relic is listed and no hymn.
