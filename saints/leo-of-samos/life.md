# Leo of Samos

Leo lived in the ninth century, and saint.gr's whole notice of him is a character out of his synaxarion rather than a history: he was plain, it says, devout, humble, a lover of his fellow men, a defender of the Orthodox faith and of its tradition, and a teacher of piety. In this wonderworking saint, the page adds, the distinction of the monastic form of life exists alongside the holiness of a bishop. He fell asleep in holiness and in peace. No year, no birthplace, no monastery and no account of a single act of his are printed.

What the page does record is the history of his feast. His memory was originally kept on 29 April; today he is honoured on 26 April by the local Church of Samos, of which he is held to have been the bishop. That is all saint.gr gives of the see itself — «της οποίας θεωρείται Επίσκοπος», of which he is *considered* bishop — and the hedge is the page's own and is kept here. His feast type is the plain fixed one, «Σταθερή. Εορτάζει στις 26 Απριλίου εκάστου έτους».
