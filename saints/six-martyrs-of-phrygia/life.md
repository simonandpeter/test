# The Six Martyrs of Phrygia

saint.gr names them and gives no life. Its calendar for 7 February prints «Άγιοι έξι Μάρτυρες καταγόμενοι από τη Φρυγία» — six holy martyrs whose descent was from Phrygia — and the page behind that line holds a single sentence about them: that the six holy martyrs were martyred by fire. There is no name among the six, no city within Phrygia, no century, no emperor and no hymn.

Over that sentence the page prints the couplet, which is all the rhetoric the entry has, and it turns on the province. Not having dreaded the strength of fire, it says, the six Phrygians are for the land of the Phrygians a tower of strength. Since the calendar names none of them, they are kept here as one entry and not as six.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3622/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
