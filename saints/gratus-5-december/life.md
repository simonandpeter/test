# Gratus (5 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 5 December names Gratus and gives him two sentences. The venerable Gratus reposed in peace; and in certain synaxaria, the page says, he is mentioned as bishop of Ephesus, and mentioned together with the venerable [Nonnus](/saints/nonnus-5-december), whom the same calendar keeps on the same day. No country, no reign and no year is printed, and the page does not itself call him a bishop.

The couplet set over his name is the whole of the rest: while he lived it was hoped that he was by nature an angel, but when he died Gratus was convicted of being a man. The see of Ephesus is written down here as the page writes it, as the reading of some synaxaria and not as the calendar's own, and nothing further is made of it.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3156/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
