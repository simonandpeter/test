# Christodula, mother of the three children

Christodula was the honourable Christian mother of the three boys — Urban, Prilidian and Epolonius — whom the bishop [Babylas](/saints/babylas-of-antioch) of Antioch taught, and she suffered for Christ herself. When the emperor, shamed by Babylas at the church door, took his revenge, the children were tortured too: each was given as many blows as he had years, then imprisoned, and at last all three were beheaded, the bishop in chains watching and heartening them, before he laid his own head under the sword; he was buried in one tomb with them, about 250 — 251, the calendars print. The Russian calendar keeps the three children and their mother with Babylas on 4 September; the Greek synaxarion keeps the three children with him.
