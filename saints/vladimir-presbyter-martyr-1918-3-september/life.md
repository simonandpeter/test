# Vladimir (Dmitrievsky)

Vladimir Fyodorovich Dmitrievsky was born in 1876 in Novgorod province, a
psalmist’s son. He finished two classes of the Novgorod seminary and became
a teacher at the school of Kuvizino in the Valdai district — on 1 October
1884, as the calendar prints it, a date that cannot stand beside a birth
in 1876; on 25 March 1895 he was made psalmist at the church of
Kolpakovskoye in the Lepsinsk district of the Semirechye region, and on 4
October 1902 was moved to the town of Pishpek, today Bishkek. On 18 June
1903 Bishop Paisius (Vinogradov) of Turkestan and Tashkent ordained him
deacon and on 22 September priest, and appointed him to the parish of
Arasan-Kopal in Semirechye, where he also taught the Law of God in the
Kopal girls’ school; in 1910 he was rector of the church of St Nicholas in
the stanitsa of Kopal, and in 1917 acting dean of the Kopal district. In
1918 he was killed when a procession of the cross collided with a
Bolshevik demonstration. He was glorified by the jubilee Council of
Bishops in 2000; the Russian calendar keeps him on 3 September of the old
style, 16 September, and in the synaxes of the saints of Novgorod and of
the Central Asian metropolitan district.
