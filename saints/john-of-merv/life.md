# John of Merv

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names John of Merv among the martyrs on 14 July and gives no life. Its line is «Мч. Иоанна Мервского.» — the martyr John of Merv — with no year, no place of suffering and no account of it, and the calendar's own name page for him repeats the heading and adds nothing.

So what is recorded here is what the calendar records: that he was a martyr, that his by-name is Merv — the city of Khorasan, on the old road east — and that this church keeps his memory on this day. Nothing further is written, because nothing further was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 14 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260714.html); read 9 October 2026.*
