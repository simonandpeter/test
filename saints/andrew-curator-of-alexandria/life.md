# Andrew the Curator of Alexandria

Andrew was a curator and [Macarius a magistrate](/saints/macarius-magistrate-of-alexandria); both, in the persecution of Decius, were seized at Alexandria by the governor Valerian and after many tortures beheaded — dying by the sword, the verse says, both walked the blessed road. The Greek synaxarion keeps the martyrs of Alexandria under Decius in separate entries on 6 September; the Russian calendar counts them as the eleven with the [presbyter Faustus](/saints/faustus-presbyter-of-alexandria) and the [deacon Abibus](/saints/abibus-deacon-of-alexandria).

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 6 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2361/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
