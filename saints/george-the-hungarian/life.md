# George the Hungarian

saint.gr's calendar for 28 January names George the martyr and gives him two sentences. It says that he came from Hungary, but lived in the city of Rostov in Russia, and that he was in the service of the prince Boris Vladimirovich; and that he bore witness as a martyr in the year 1015.

That is the whole of it. The page gives no year of birth, no account of how he came from Hungary to Russia, no manner of death, no place of burial, and no troparion or kontakion. The epithet in the English name here is taken from the one thing the page says about where he was from. The Russian calendar, which keeps the prince he served, is where this entry could be finished, and it has not been read.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/28/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3543/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
