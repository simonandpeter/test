# Neonilla

Neonilla lived in the city doxologia.ro's life calls Lingonia, in Gaul, and was the sister of Faustus, a senator of the city the same page calls Edua. Her son had died in unbelief, and she had kept silence about Christ while he lived for fear that he would turn his sons from the way; after his death she brought up his three sons, born at a single birth, and asked her brother for help in making them Christians. Faustus sent her the presbyter Benignus, whom Polycarp of Smyrna had sent into Gaul.

The day Benignus came, her grandsons were at a village called Pasmasia sacrificing to an idol named Nemesis, and brought the remains of the offering home; she threw it to the dogs and set about teaching them, of the God who made heaven and earth and of the idols that are nothing. [Speusippus](/saints/speusippus), [Eleusippus](/saints/eleusippus) and [Meleusippus](/saints/meleusippus) asked her why she had covered so great a thing with silence, and she told them, and Benignus baptised them.

When the three had been taken and the judges could not move them, they sent for Neonilla and told her to go and persuade her grandsons to set up again the gods they had broken. She said she would go and counsel them what befitted their deliverance; and finding them unmoved she was filled with joy, kissed each of them, and charged them to be steadfast, since the kingdoms of this world soon take their end and the unseen kingdom of God is everlasting. After the brothers died, a woman named Jovilla confessed Christ in the crowd; she and Neonilla were taken to Urvat and both beheaded.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 16 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/16-ianuarie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfintei-mucenite-neonila); read 19 September 2026.*
