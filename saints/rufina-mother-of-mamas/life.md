# Rufina, mother of Mamas

Rufina and her husband [Theodotus](/saints/theodotus-father-of-mamas), devout and noble Christians of Paphlagonia, confessed their faith openly and were imprisoned by the pagans at Caesarea in Cappadocia, where Theodotus died before the tortures, as he had prayed. Rufina followed him, dying in giving birth prematurely to a son whom she commended to God in prayer, asking him to be the orphan’s keeper and defender; God heard her, and the rich Christian widow Ammia buried the parents with honour and raised the child, [Mamas](/saints/mamas-of-caesarea). The Russian calendar keeps the parents with the son on 2 September (Julian).
