# Solomon of Ephesus

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 2 decembrie reads «Sfântul Ierarh Solomon, Arhiepiscopul Efesului» — the holy hierarch Solomon, archbishop of Ephesus — and the page that stands under his name carries neither a Viață tab nor a Tropar tab. The line gives his rank and his see, and those, with the day, are all that is entered here.

Nothing is said there of when he held Ephesus, of what he did in it or of how he died, and nothing of the kind is written here.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 2 decembrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/2-decembrie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-ierarh-solomon-arhiepiscopul-efesului); read 25 September 2026.*
