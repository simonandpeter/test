# Solomon of Ephesus

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 2 decembrie reads «Sfântul Ierarh Solomon, Arhiepiscopul Efesului» — the holy hierarch Solomon, archbishop of Ephesus — and the page that stands under his name carries neither a Viață tab nor a Tropar tab. The line gives his rank and his see, and those, with the day, are all that is entered here.

Nothing is said there of when he held Ephesus, of what he did in it or of how he died, and nothing of the kind is written here.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 2 December, with the same rank and the same see, and it has no more to tell than the Romanian line had: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου» — we have no details of the saint's life — with a cross-reference to the first of December. What it adds is his couplet, which distinguishes him from his namesake: Solomon dies, not the offspring of David, but Solomon the chief shepherd of shepherds. On the day it refers to, saint.gr keeps [Onesimus of Ephesus](/saints/onesimus-of-ephesus), whose own page says that in certain sources Onesimus is called Onesiphoros together with this Solomon, and that of the entries on those two days only two were really archbishops of Ephesus: Onesimus and Solochon or Solomon. It prints no hymn.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 2 decembrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/2-decembrie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-ierarh-solomon-arhiepiscopul-efesului); read 25 September 2026; and after saint.gr's calendar for 2 Δεκεμβρίου — [the Greek day](https://www.saint.gr/12/02/index.aspx) and [its life](https://www.saint.gr/3125/saint.aspx), read 2 October 2026.*
