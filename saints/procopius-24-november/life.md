# Procopius (24 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 24 Νοεμβρίου names two men in one line, «Άγιοι Προκόπιος και Χριστόφορος», and gives them a single sentence: they were martyred by the sword in the year 274. The page's heading carries «(; - 274)» against both names and gives neither a year of birth. Beyond that year there is nothing in the notice at all: no country, no city, no emperor, no manner of arrest, and no word about who the two were to each other.

What it adds is the couplet, and the couplet gives Procopius a voice: as I do, Christophoros, he says, put your own neck too to the cutting. The page gives them no country, no city, no emperor, no relics and no hymn, and it does not say whether they were kin or companions — only that they went to the same death, and that one of them encouraged the other to it.

This folder is Procopius, named first in the line and the speaker of the couplet, who died by the sword with [Christophoros](/saints/christopher-companion-of-procopius).

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/24/index.aspx) and [the life the two share](https://www.saint.gr/2992/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
