# Basil (Krylov, 18 June)

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 18 июня ст. ст. names four presbyters on one line, «Сщмчч. Василия , Александра , Василия и Сергия пресвитеров (1938).», and he is the third of them. The line gives the forenames in the genitive, one grade-class word and one rank word for the four together, and one year, and no surname to any of them. The name link behind his place in it supplies the calendar’s own nominative with the surname and the grade, «Василий (Крылов), иерей, сщмч.», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from.

His name page carries nothing else: «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 18 июня» and then no life, no place and no account of his death. Nothing is supplied for that here. The line’s «Сщмчч.» makes him a hieromartyr and «пресвитеров» a presbyter, and the name link repeats the rank as «иерей».

This calendar prints the identical string «Василий (Крылов), иерей, сщмч.» on three of its own name pages, and each of the three has one proper day and no other: 9496 is this day, 9497 is 6 ноября and 13718 is 18 мая. The corpus already holds the other two. Three pages, three days, and two years between them — 1938 on this day and on 9497, 1942 on 13718 — is the whole of what the source gives; 9496 and 9497 were fetched and read side by side on 9 October 2026 and neither carries a sentence of narrative. Whether the two pages that share the year 1938 are one man is not settled here, and azbyka.ru was not read.

The second day his name page lists is the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which is a synaxis and not a second feast, so he renders on 18 июня ст. ст. only. The day of the menologion is in his display name because the calendar gives him no epithet to tell him by.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 18 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260618.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9496.html); read 9 October 2026.*
