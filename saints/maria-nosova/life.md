# Maria (Nosova)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Maria Nosova on 27 апреля and gives no life of her. Its line for the day is the whole of what it says: «Прмц. Марии (1938).» — the venerable-martyr Maria, and the year 1938 — with a link to her name and none to any reading. So what the calendar tells is that she was a novice, that she was counted among the venerable-martyrs, and that she died in 1938.

Her own name page on that site, 14250, carries nothing but the heading «Мария (Носова), послушница, прмц.», which gives the surname the day's line leaves out and the grade «послушница», a novice, and the two days «26 января (Новомуч.) , 27 апреля». There is no life page for her anywhere on the site, no icon and no article, and no page names a monastery, a diocese or a place of any kind. The day page prints nine hymns, three for the kinsman of the Lord and six for the abbot of the Caves, and none under her name. The 26 января day page was not read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 27 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260427.html) and [her name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14250.html); read 8 October 2026.*
