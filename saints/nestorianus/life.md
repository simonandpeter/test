# Nestorianus

saint.gr names the holy Nestorianus on this day and gives him one sentence, and the life says exactly that: «Ο Άγιος Νεστοριανός μαρτύρησε διά ξίφους» — the holy Nestorianus was martyred by the sword. The page gives no year, no country, no emperor, no judge and no relic, and it prints no hymn. It does not even carry the etymology block on the name that it sets over almost every other entry of this day.

The one thing beside the sentence is the distich, and it describes the posture of the killing rather than the man: his neck drawn back behind him, Nestorianus carries the slaughter forward. Nothing else is written here, and no other bearer of this name stands anywhere in the corpus.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3778/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
