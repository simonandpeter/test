# Zeno, disciple of Silvanus

saint.gr's calendar for 19 June names Zeno, and gives him one sentence of life and one of death. He left the world, the page says, and became a disciple of the great elder Silvanus; and for his exceeding obedience and his extreme ascesis and possessionlessness God counted him worthy to work wonders, and he drove many demons out of men. So, having passed his life in ascesis and struggles, he reposed in peace at the age of sixty-two.

The page gives no country, no monastery, no year and no hymn, and the age is the only number in it. Which of the elders named Silvanus is meant it does not say, and this life does not decide it. Beside the entry stands a two-line epigram — Zeno, what have you in common with mortal life? Go away to the angels, who are above life — and the page's general note on the name, which lists two ancient philosophers who bore it and is a remark about the name only.
