# Aetherius (18 June)

saint.gr's calendar for 18 June names Aetherius, and what it prints of him is praise rather than narrative. He belongs too, the page says, to the endless and glorious phalanx of the martyrs of the Christian faith; divine grace made him a citizen of heaven while he was still on earth, his mind and his heart were free of every grossness and full of spirituality, and being dead to the world, that is to sin, he lived for Jesus Christ alone.

Then, in the persecution under Diocletian, Aetherius was arrested. Neither threats nor promises, the page goes on, nor beatings nor scourgings nor the opened wounds with the blood running from them were able to bend his faith, and faithful unto death he departed from this world to where the gladness of Paradise is. Beside this stands a two-line epigram: for many torments, Aetherius, you receive your recompense in the breadth of the upper air.

The page gives no country, no city, no year and no hymn, and it says nothing of a bishopric. He is not the bishop Aetherius of Cherson whom the calendars keep on 7 March; nothing here is to be read as identifying the two.
