# Ephrem, Bishop of Selenginsk, Hieromartyr (1918)

Ephrem — Epiphanius Andreyevich Kuznetsov in the world — was born in 1876 into a family of Transbaikal Cossacks; orphaned early, he was a shepherd in his village until the local priest noticed the boy, made him a reader and sent him to the Chita church school and then the Irkutsk seminary, which he finished in 1898, taking holy orders. After his wife’s death in 1903 he finished the Kazan Academy and in 1904 was posted to the Transbaikal mission at Chita, where he brought many Mongols, Buryats and Koreans to Christ, who loved him for his fervent preaching; he was made archimandrite in 1909 and on 20 November 1916 consecrated bishop of Selenginsk, vicar of Transbaikalia, and took part in the Local Council of 1917–1918. On 20 May 1918 he was arrested in Moscow in the flat of the archpriest [John Vostorgov](/saints/john-vostorgov) and imprisoned with him in the Butyrka; both were shot on 23 August 1918 and he was buried in the Bratskoye cemetery in Moscow, demolished at the end of the twenties.
