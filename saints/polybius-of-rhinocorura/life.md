# Polybius of Rhinocorura

The Russian church calendar keeps him on 12 мая ст. ст. and gives him a notice of five sentences. From his young years he was the disciple of [Epiphanius of Cyprus](/saints/epiphanius-of-cyprus); he went with his teacher on all his journeys, and he wrote down his life and his wonders. His teacher's own notice says where that record came from: a first disciple kept it until he fell sick and died, and Polybius was the second who carried it on.

He was with his teacher when the old man left Constantinople rather than sit in the council called against Иоанн Златоуст, and he was with him when he died at sea on the way home. Dying, his teacher charged him: «Иди в Египет, а я и по смерти буду заботиться о тебе» — go into Egypt, and I will care for thee after my death as well. Polybius obeyed with humility and did not even wait for the burial of the body, but set out for Egypt, where he was made bishop of the city of Rhinocorura.

For his virtuous and ascetic life, the calendar says, he was granted the gift of wonderworking: once at his prayer the Lord sent rain in a drought and multiplied the harvest in the fields. He departed to God in deep old age in the fifth century. That is the whole of the notice; it gives no year, names no other see and no kin, and the day page, which prints a troparion and a kontakion for two of the hierarchs it heads, carries nothing under his name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 12 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260512.html), [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1046.htm) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/1817.html); read 8 October 2026.*
