# Pankosmios

saint.gr keeps a venerable Pankosmios on 29 November, and the entire life it prints for him is four words: «Ο Όσιος Παγκόσμιος απεβίωσε ειρηνικά» — the venerable Pankosmios died in peace. There is nothing else. No country, no century, no monastery, no teacher, no relic and no hymn, and the page does not say where the memory comes from.

The only other thing on it is his couplet, which plays on the name — Pankosmios means something like all-the-world — and says that the whole world was nothing to Pankosmios, for he loved Eden, and Eden alone, which is what he receives. What the Greek calendar attests is that a venerable father of that name is kept on this day and that he was not a martyr.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 29 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/29/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3114/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
