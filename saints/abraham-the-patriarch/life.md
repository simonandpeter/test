# Abraham

The Ohrid Prologue keeps the holy forefathers Abraham, Isaac and Jacob on 21 August (Julian) for the Serbian calendar, and says of them only that they are remembered too on the Sunday of the Forefathers before the Nativity, as righteous men and servants of God. Their story is the book of Genesis; nothing more is recorded here.

The Greek calendar keeps him on 9 October, and keeps him in one entry with his nephew: «Δικαίων Αβραάμ και Λωτ του ανεψιού του», the righteous Abraham and [Lot](/saints/lot) his nephew. saint.gr's page is a long retelling of the Genesis narrative with its chapters and verses — Abram the son of Terah, brother of Nahor and Harran, of the tribe of Shem but dwelling in Ur of the Chaldeans among idolaters; the name Abraham read as father of a people or of a multitude; the title friend of God out of 2 Chronicles 20.7; Sarah his wife, his sister by his father and not by his mother; Isaac born of Sarah, Ishmael of Hagar, and six more sons of Keturah. It gives no year anywhere, and no hymn, only a distich for him and another for Lot.

The Russian calendar keeps him on its own 9 October with his nephew, «Прав. Авраама праотца и племянника его Лота (2000 до Р.Х.)» — the righteous Abraham the forefather and his nephew [Lot](/saints/lot), about two thousand years before Christ. That is the only date the page offers, and it is an era rather than a year.

The life behind the line is one sentence, and it is a reference rather than a life: of the righteous Abraham the forefather and his nephew Lot, it says, see the book of Genesis, chapters 12 to 25. The Russian calendar does not paraphrase the patriarchs, and this folder does not paraphrase it. His index page keeps him on this one day.
