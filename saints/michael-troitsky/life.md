# Michael (Troitsky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Michael among the hieromartyrs on 23 July and gives no life. Its line is «Сщмч. Михаила пресвитера и мч. Андрея (1938).» — the hieromartyr Michael the presbyter and the martyr Andrew, 1938 — and the calendar's own name page for him adds the surname and keeps the grade the line gives: «Михаил (Троицкий), иерей, сщмч.», Michael Troitsky, priest.

The line names him together with [Andrew (Argunov)](/saints/andrew-argunov), to whom it gives no priestly grade, and the two share one year and one entry on this day. The surnames are not on the line at all: each man's own numbered name page carries his, which is how it is known that they are two men and not one.

That is the whole of the record. He was a priest; he died in 1938; the calendar keeps him on this day among the new martyrs of Russia. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 23 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260723.html); read 13 October 2026.*
