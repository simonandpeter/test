# Baradatus

The venerable Baradatus came of Alexandria, and at a young age he chose the life of the desert. When Theodotus, patriarch of Antioch, whose years saint.gr gives as 418 to 427, learned of his rare virtues, he took care to draw him out of his ascetic life in order to use him in the many-sided social and charitable work of the church.

Baradatus accepted the offer, the page says, and in the seasons of fasting went about the cities, and by his holy life breathed reverence into men for God the creator and for his commandments, and breathed patience into them for the hard turns of life. So, in a manner befitting a holy man, he fell asleep on one of those journeys.

The distich over him turns on the apostle's word: having put his members to death on earth, as Paul says, Baradatus had a share of life in heaven. The page gives no year for his death and prints no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 22 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/22/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3676/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
