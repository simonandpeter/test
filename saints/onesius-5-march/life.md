# Onesius (5 March)

The Сретенский monastery's calendar gives him one line for the Julian 5 March, «Мч. Онисия (I).», and the life behind it is two sentences: «Святой мученик Онисий жил в Палестине. За исповедание веры во Христа был усечен мечом.» He lived in Palestine, and for confessing the faith in Christ he was beheaded with the sword. That is the whole of it, and this folder says so rather than filling it.

What the calendar does not give him is a year, a reign, a city, a family, a companion, a relic or a hymn: the day page prints hymns under four headings and none of them is his. The (I) at the end of the line is the first century, and it is the only dating of him anywhere read; the life does not repeat it.

**The two halves of the calendar's own entry disagree about where he was from.** The line gives no place. The name link behind it, page 1663, heads him «Онисий Усуровский (Исаврийский), мч.» — of Усура, and of Isauria — where the life says Palestine. Both are recorded, the index's in the stored Russian form and the life's here, and neither is chosen; the display name therefore tells him apart by the day the calendar keeps him on, as this corpus does for a saint it can place no other way, and no `locations` is written.

He stands on a day of fourteen entries, and two other people on it are called Palestinians by the same calendar and beheaded in the same words: Евлогий Палестинский, whose pagan parents died and who gave away his goods and went about Palestine converting pagans before he was seized, tortured and beheaded, and Евлампий Палестинский, who lived in Palestine and was beheaded. **Three beheaded Palestinians on one page of a calendar are not a company.** No line names another, the three lives are separate pages that do not cross, and the shared province and the shared manner of death are not a relation. So this folder carries no `related` row at all, and the two others are named here in the calendar's own words rather than linked.

The name is worth one sentence of its own, because the corpus is full of its neighbours. Онисий is not Онисим and not Онисифор: eight Onesimus folders and five Onesiphorus folders stand in this corpus, from the apostle of the letter to Philemon to a bishop shot in 1937, and not one of them is kept on this day or named on this page.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 5 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260305.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life569.htm); read 6 October 2026.*
