# Basil (Erekaev)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Basil first in a company of eight on 28 July and gives no life for any of them. The entry is «Прмч. Василия , прмцц. Анастасии и Елены , мчч. Арефы , Иоанна , Иоанна , Иоанна и мц. Мавры (1937).» — a hieromonk, two nuns, four laymen of whom three bear one name, and a laywoman, all with the single year 1937 — and each of the eight has his own numbered name page, which is the whole of what tells them apart, the line itself giving only forenames.

His own name page is «Василий (Эрекаев), иеромонах, сщмч.», Basil Erekaev, hieromonk. The line calls him a venerable-martyr and the name page a hieromartyr, and this folder keeps the line's reading and records the other; nothing read resolves them. He was a monk in priest's orders, he died in 1937 with the seven others of this entry, and the calendar keeps them together on this day among the new martyrs of Russia.

The company is recorded here as `related` on all eight folders. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 28 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260728.html); read 13 October 2026.*
