# Thaddeus of Tver

saint.gr's calendar for 18 December keeps Saint Thaddeus, archbishop of Tver, and the whole of what its page says of him is one sentence: Saint Thaddeus was archbishop of Tver and was martyred in the year 1937. The heading above that sentence gives the same year for his death and gives none for his birth, and there is nothing else on the page at all.

So the Greek entry is four things and no more: the fixed day, the see of Tver, the martyrdom, and the year. The page names no place of death and no court, tells nothing of how he died, lists no relic, prints no hymn, and gives nothing of his life before the see.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3298/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
