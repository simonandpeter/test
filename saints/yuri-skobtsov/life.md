# Yuri (Skobtsov), son of Maria

saint.gr keeps Yuri Skobtsov among the Russian saints who ended their lives in France, a synaxis the Ecumenical Patriarchate appointed for 20 July, and his notice on that page is a single sentence: Saint Yuri died in a concentration camp, as his mother did. His mother is [Maria (Skobtsova)](/saints/maria-skobtsova), the nun of Paris whom the same page keeps in the same synaxis and whose own notice is long.

The page gives him nothing else at all — no year of birth or death, no camp named, no word of what he did in the occupation — and it prints no hymn for him. The kinship is the whole of what it says about him, and it is why the display name carries it.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 20 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/20/index.aspx) and [the synaxis](https://www.saint.gr/4381/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
