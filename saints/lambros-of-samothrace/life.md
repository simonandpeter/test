# Lambros of Samothrace

saint.gr's calendar for 2 Ιουλίου names him a neomartyr of Samothrace and gives him a date, a place and a fall. «Ο Άγιος Λάμπρος μαρτύρησε στη Μάκρη του Νομού Έβρου στις 2 Ιουλίου του 1835 μ.Χ.» — he was martyred at Makri in the province of Evros on 2 July 1835. He had been forced by the Turks, the page says, and had denied the faith; afterwards he repented, and was martyred for his faith in Christ.

The page names five co-martyrs with him — «τον Θεόδωρο, τον άλλο Θεόδωρο, τον Γεώργιο, τον Ιωάννη και τον Μιχαήλ», Theodore, the other Theodore, George, John and Michael — but it gives them no entry of their own on this day and no further word, and its own list of the five does not agree with the roll of the five neomartyrs of Samothrace that the calendar keeps elsewhere. So no link is made to any of them here, and none of the five is identified with a saint the corpus already holds; the disagreement is the source's and is left standing.

His apolytikion sets him apart from that other company in so many words: «Τῶν μέν Πεντάθλων σύ ἔγνως τό μαρτύριον, τῶν ἐκ τῆς Ἄγαρ δέ τήν πίστιν ἐξέβαλες» — you knew the martyrdom of the Five Contenders, and you cast out the faith of the sons of Hagar.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 2 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/02/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/652/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
