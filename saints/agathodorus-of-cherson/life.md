# Agathodorus of Cherson

Agathodorus is one of the bishops of Cherson whom the Romanian calendar keeps together on the seventh of March. In the life doxologia prints for the seven of them, he comes into the story after [Basileus](/saints/basileus-of-cherson), the first bishop there, had been killed by a mob at Cherson. A disciple of Basileus, putting in for a night with his ship on the coast of the Hellespont, found three bishops there labouring at the good news of Christ: [Eugene](/saints/eugene-of-cherson), [Elpidius](/saints/elpidius-of-cherson) and Agathodorus.

The three went with him to Cherson and took up the work their predecessor had left unfinished. But the devil armed the Jews and the Greeks of the city against them just as he had against Basileus: a crowd came together, seized the bishops, dragged them bound along the road and beat them with sticks and with stones, and so they ended their lives.

doxologia gives no year for any of it, and nothing about where Agathodorus came from or where his body was laid. It prints for him the same troparion it prints for the other bishops of Cherson.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, inside the company of seven, and gives him one sentence. saint.gr says that after the martyr's death of St Basileus the bishops Agathodorus, Elpidius and Eugene were sent to Cherson as heralds of the Gospel and of the faith, and that they too were killed by the unbelieving. It gives no year for any of the three, and the distich it prints over them names none of them singly: a fellow-striving three, it says, speaking the Prophet's word, I gave my back to scourges. The page glosses his name as the precious gift of God, which is an etymology and not a fact about the man.
