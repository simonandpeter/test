# Anna (13 February)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps her on 13 февраля ст. ст. inside a single line that names fifteen sufferers of 1938 together — ten presbyters, a deacon, a layman and three nuns — and she stands in it as the first of the three women the line closes with, «прмцц. Анны». The line's rank word over the three of them is «прмцц.», the plural of преподобномученица, a venerable-martyr, which is how the calendar marks a nun. The name link behind the line gives her as «Анна, прмц.» and no further: the calendar has no surname for her, where it has one for each of the other two women.

The calendar prints no life page under this name and no hymn under it either: the one life the line carries belongs to the archpriest at its head, and the troparia the day page prints are for other saints it heads. Nothing further is written here. The line, the rank word, the name the link behind it carries and the year 1938 are the whole of what was read, and in particular she is not identified with any saint of the same name whom the calendar keeps on another day.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 13 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260213.html); read 3 October 2026.*
