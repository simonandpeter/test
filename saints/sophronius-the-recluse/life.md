# Sophronius the Recluse

saint.gr's calendar for 11 Μαρτίου names him «Όσιος Σωφρόνιος ο Έγκλειστος», the venerable Sophronius the recluse, and the page behind that line holds one sentence and a bracket. The sentence: that the venerable Sophronius lived in Russia in the thirteenth century and lived as an ascetic in the Lavra of St Theodosius of Kyiv, that he wore hair garments and a heavy iron belt, and that he read the whole Psalter every day; and that he fell asleep in holiness and in peace.

The bracket says that his memory is repeated on 11 May, and the Greek calendar therefore keeps him twice; this folder records the day whose page is quoted here. Beyond the hair cloth, the iron belt and the daily Psalter the page tells nothing of him — no year, no office, no relic and no hymn — and the recluse's name is the whole of what the calendar has kept.
