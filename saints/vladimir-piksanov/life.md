# Vladimir (Piksanov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and carries no life. Its line for the twentieth of March, old style, reads «Сщмч. Владимира пресвитера (1918).» — the hieromartyr Vladimir, presbyter — and the name page behind that «Владимира» is headed «Владимир (Пиксанов), сщмч.» and gives the surname, which the line itself does not print. That page lists two days: «26 января», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church, and this twentieth of March.

Nothing else is said of him on either page. There is no place, no diocese, no parish, no year of birth and no account of how he died; the 1918 the line closes with is the whole of his chronology, and he is one of the many priests of those years whom this calendar keeps by a name and a day. The second source for the Russian new martyrs, azbyka.ru, was not read for him.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 20 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260320.html); read 8 October 2026.*
