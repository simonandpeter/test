# Bousiris (31 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 31 December keeps the holy Bousiris, and its whole account of him is one sentence: «Ο Άγιος Βούσιρις μαρτύρησε, αφού τον θανάτωσαν γυναίκες με σαΐτες που υφαίνουν» — the holy Bousiris was martyred, women having killed him with the shuttles they weave with. It says no more: no century, no country, no city, no judge, no reason why the women did it, and no hymn.

The couplet set over the entry makes the same death into a weaving: «Bουσίριδος σαρξ, ιστός ένθα κερκίσιν, / Ύφασμα καινόν εξυφάνθη Kυρίω» — the flesh of Bousiris was the loom whereon, with the shuttles, a new web was woven for the Lord. That is the whole of what this calendar keeps of him, and the shuttles are in both halves of it. The same calendar names a Bousiris on 21 September too, in one entry with the brothers of Gaza, and tells nothing of him there; the corpus keeps that one apart from this.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 31 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/31/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1510/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
