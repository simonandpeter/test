# Valerian the Infant

Valerian the infant was martyred by the sword; that is the whole of the Greek synaxarion’s notice for 14 September. To little Valerian, its verse says, the great God gave in heaven an all-great crown. This entry records it and nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 14 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2396/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
