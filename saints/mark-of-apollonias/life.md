# Mark of Apollonias

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its page for him is a single sentence: «Sfântul Sfinţitului Mucenic Marcu, episcopul Apoloniadei, s-a săvârşit prin pietre spânzurate de mâinile lui» — the holy hieromartyr Mark, bishop of Apollonias, met his end by stones hung from his hands. The page gives no year, no emperor, no place of trial, no burial and no troparion, and nothing further about him is recorded here.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 16 iunie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/16-iunie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-sfintit-mucenic-marcu-episcopul-apoloniadei); read 24 September 2026.*
