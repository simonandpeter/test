# Mark of Apollonias

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its page for him is a single sentence: «Sfântul Sfinţitului Mucenic Marcu, episcopul Apoloniadei, s-a săvârşit prin pietre spânzurate de mâinile lui» — the holy hieromartyr Mark, bishop of Apollonias, met his end by stones hung from his hands. The page gives no year, no emperor, no place of trial, no burial and no troparion, and nothing further about him is recorded here.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 16 June, and saint.gr's account is a single sentence with a two-line epigram beside it. The sentence says that Mark, hieromartyr and bishop of Apollonias, was martyred after they hung him upside down, tied boulders to his hands, and then left him swinging in the void. The epigram plays on the same picture: you are a balance, Mark, with the weight of the stones, your hands hanging down below like the pans of the scales. The page gives no year, names no emperor and prints no hymn.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 16 iunie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/16-iunie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-sfintit-mucenic-marcu-episcopul-apoloniadei); read 24 September 2026; saint.gr's calendar for 16 Ιουνίου and [its life](https://www.saint.gr/1872/saint.aspx), read 1 October 2026.*
