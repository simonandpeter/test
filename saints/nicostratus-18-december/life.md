# Nicostratus (18 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 18 December keeps ten people together and names every one of them: Nikostratos and his wife Zoe, Tranquillinus and his wife Marcia, Claudius, Tiburtius, Castor, Castulus, Marcus and Marcellinus. They all suffered, the page says, in the persecution Diocletian set in motion against the Christians, whose years it gives as 284 to 304, and they suffered together with Saint [Sebastian](/saints/sebastian-of-rome), whom the same day's calendar keeps.

He and his wife [Zoe](/saints/zoe-18-december) died of suffocation, the page says: they had been hung upside down and a special hearth of fire was built below them, so that much smoke should come up and go straight into their faces.

That is the whole of what the page gives for this company: the ten names, the one reign, and for each of them the manner of the death. It names no city and no judge, gives no year of birth for any of them and no year inside the reign, lists no relic and prints no hymn of its own; the day's apolytikion stands on Sebastian's page and names Sebastian. The others of the ten here are [Tranquillinus](/saints/tranquillinus), [Marcia](/saints/marcia-wife-of-tranquillinus), [Claudius](/saints/claudius-18-december), [Tiburtius](/saints/tiburtius-18-december), [Castor](/saints/castor-18-december), [Castulus](/saints/castulus-18-december), [Marcus](/saints/marcus-son-of-tranquillinus), [Marcellinus](/saints/marcellinus-son-of-tranquillinus), and [Zoe](/saints/zoe-18-december), whom this corpus already kept from the Romanian calendar.

The Ohrid Prologue keeps him on the same menologion day, inside the heading „Свети мученик Севастијан и други с њим.“ — Sebastian and the others with him — and names him in the body. He was the jailer, it says, and his wife [Zoe](/saints/zoe-18-december) had been dumb for six years; [Sebastian](/saints/sebastian-of-rome) healed her of it, and brought her and the whole house of Nicostratus to baptism.

His own death the book gives in one clause: he was killed with a club. It is the same death the entry gives Sebastian at the end, after the arrows had not killed him. No year is given for either.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3289/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026; and the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 18. децембар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2025-12-31&prolog=1), read 7 October 2026.*
