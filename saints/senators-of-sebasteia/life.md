# The Senators of Sebasteia

saint.gr's calendar for 2 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιοι Συγκλητικοί» — the holy Senators — and begins by saying that their names are the one thing it cannot give. They came from Sebasteia, the page says, and suffered under Auxanon, the governor of that city, under the duke Marcellus and under Marcus Agricolaus. «Τα ονόματά τους δεν διασώθηκαν στη γη, λάμπουν όμως αθάνατα στον ουρανό»: their names were not preserved on earth, but they shine immortal in heaven.

When Licinius, at war with Constantine the Great, proclaimed a heavy persecution against the Christian faith in the year 315, a group of senators had the courage to tell the emperor openly that his order was a crime against God, who would not be slow to send the punishment that teaches. Licinius called them insolent and ordered them put to death.

They accepted it bravely, the page says, although they were leaving families behind them who were exposed to the same fury of death; and they died all together in the fire, encouraging one another and calling one another blessed. The epigram above the notice turns on their rank: senators longing for an incorruptible reward, cut down, refuse the rewards of the senate. No hymn is printed, and the page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου».

*After saint.gr's calendar for 2 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/02/index.aspx) and [their notice](https://www.saint.gr/1052/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
