# Philoromus of Galatia

The Ohrid Prologue says Philoromus lived and struggled in Galatia in the fourth century. They say of him, it goes on, that he was so perfect in all the virtues that he looked more like an angel than a man; and he was glorified above all for his patience. He was persecuted by the emperor Julian the Apostate and suffered much for Christ.

But after the death of that wicked persecutor of Christ, the entry says, Philoromus lived in peace and was of use to many; and he fell asleep in his eightieth year. That is the whole of it. It names no city in Galatia, no monastery and no disciple, gives no year for his birth or his repose, says nothing of what the suffering under Julian consisted in, and prints no hymn.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 9. март ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-03-22&prolog=1); read 4 October 2026.*
