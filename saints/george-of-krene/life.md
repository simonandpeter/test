# George of Krene

saint.gr's calendar for 25 Ιουνίου keeps him as a new martyr and gives his whole story in five short paragraphs. He was born in the parts of Attaleia, of rich and devout parents, and while still an infant was seized by one Aga Prousalis, in the ordinary snatching of Christian children; he was made a Muslim and named Mehmet. When he came to the age of marriage he married that Aga's daughter.

At the urging of his God-fearing parents, a Christian servant of his called Maria told him of his descent and of the manner of his islamisation. On the pretext of going on pilgrimage to Mecca, George came with Maria to the Holy Land, where he stayed two years. Afterwards he reached the town of Krene in Asia Minor, where he married Helen Mavrogianne.

Seeking martyrdom, George went to the governor's house and helped his own former father-in-law, who was passing through Krene, to get down from his horse; and a little after confessed to him his faith in Christ. The Turks then threw him into prison, where they beat him hard, put his feet in the stocks and set a red-hot copper vessel on his head. In the end, on 25 June 1823, they hung him on the wall of the house of a notable called Pantelakis Pharmakis and strangled him there.

The page prints no hymn for him, and the only other thing it carries is a link to the synaxis of the new martyrs who suffered after the fall of Constantinople.
