# Beronice, companion of Speratus

saint.gr's calendar for 17 July names two together, «Άγιοι Σπεράτος και Βηρονίκη», and then says plainly that it knows nothing about either of them: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τους βίους του Αγίου Σπεράτου και της Αγίας Βηρονίκης (ή Βερονίκης)» — we have no details of the lives of Saint [Speratus](/saints/speratus-the-martyr) and Saint Beronice. Her name it gives in those two spellings and settles on neither. There is no year, no place, no emperor, no judge and no hymn.

The one thing beyond the names is the couplet of the Synaxarion set over the notice: «Συν αίμασι Σπεράτε τοις εξ αυχένος, / Aίμα τραχήλου μιγνύει Bηρονίκη» — with your blood from the neck, Speratus, Beronice mingles the blood of her own throat. So they were beheaded, and they were beheaded together; that is said in verse and nowhere in prose, and it is the whole of what the Greek calendar records of her.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 17 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/17/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/721/saint.aspx), which the two share and which prints only that one sentence; read 1 October 2026.*
