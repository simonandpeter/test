# Mitrophan of Astrakhan

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 23 июня ст. ст. prints him alone on a line of four words and a year: «Сщмч. Митрофана , архиеп. Астраханского (1919).». The line gives the forename in the genitive, the grade-class word «Сщмч.» and the see, «архиеп. Астраханского», and no surname. The name link behind his place in it supplies the calendar’s own nominative with the rank and the see, «Святитель Митрофан, архиепископ Астраханский, сщмч.» (name page 13588), and the name form in this folder is read out of that string with the rank words left off.

His name page carries no life. It heads «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 23 июня» and then lists two parish websites under «Сайты приходов» — both of them churches of a different hierarch of the same forename, the wonderworker of Voronezh, which the site matches on the name and which say nothing about this man. No place, no age, no account of his death in 1919 and no surname is given him anywhere on this source, and nothing is supplied for them here.

The line’s «Сщмч.» makes him a hieromartyr and the see makes him an archbishop. The corpus holds seven other folders of this forename and not one of them is an archbishop of Astrakhan. The second day his name page lists is the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which is a synaxis and not a second feast, so he renders on 23 июня ст. ст. only. The day page prints twelve hymns, for a boy and a hierarch and a woman martyr and three icons and a synaxis, and none under his name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 23 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260623.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13588.html); read 9 October 2026.*
