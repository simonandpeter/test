# Michael (Denisov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him on 7 июня inside a single line that names fourteen men together and gives them one year, and it gives no life of any of them. The line is the whole of what it says: «Сщмч. Александра , Александра , Валентина , Вениамина , Виктора , Александра , Владимира , Игнатия , Михаила , Николая , Павла , Николая , Николая пресвитеров и мч. Александра (1918).» — thirteen forenames covered by its «Сщмч.», the grade of a hieromartyr, and a fourteenth man marked off by its own «мч.»; and the year 1918.

He is the ninth name in it. His own name page on that site, 9683, carries nothing but the heading «Михаил (Денисов), иерей», which gives the surname the day's line leaves out and grades him иерей, and the two days «26 января (Новомуч.) , 7 июня». There is no life page for him on the site, no icon and no article, and no page names a parish, a diocese or a place of any kind.

What stands beside his line on the day is the archbishop of Perm shot in the same year, and the one hymn the day page prints belongs to a martyr of Ancyra and not to him. The 26 января commemoration of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia was not read, and nothing of it is in this folder.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 7 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260607.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9683.html); read 8 October 2026.*
