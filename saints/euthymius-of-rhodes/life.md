# Euthymius of Rhodes

saint.gr says that the glorious hieromartyr Euthymius was elected Metropolitan of Rhodes when the Ottomans took the island, in the year 1523, and that he shepherded the Orthodox flock in a manner pleasing to God through those hard years. In 1529 he was arrested as the chief instigator of a conspiracy against the Turks, and he was impaled together with other clergy and leading men of the community of Rhodes.

The Christians honoured the metropolitan as a martyr immediately after his martyr's end, the page says. For evidence of it, it cites a report sent to the Roman Propaganda by the Latin missionary Peter Phangonis, vicar general of the Latin archbishop Alfonso Gonzaga: the tomb of the blessed Euthymius had become the pre-eminent place of pilgrimage of the Rhodians, and those who suffered from the quartan fever, malaria, with the shivering that comes with it, would go and sit at it and be healed.

That report, written by a man of the other church, is the whole of what saint.gr offers for the veneration; of the saint's own life before 1523 it gives nothing at all, and no year of birth. The day itself is named in the page's own distich: «Ἐνάτῃ Εὐθύμιος Ῥοδίων ποιμὴν τιμάσθω ὕμνοις» — on the ninth let Euthymius, shepherd of the Rhodians, be honoured with hymns.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 9 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/09/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4187/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
