# Celestine (25 May)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 25 mai reads «Sfântul Mucenic Celestin» — the holy martyr Celestine — with no epithet, no city, no country and no century, and his own page on the site carries only that line, the date and the day's scripture readings. There is no Viață tab and no Tropar tab, and nothing that would place him or tell him from anyone else of the name.

The display name here carries the day for that reason. This folder asserts one thing only: that the Romanian church keeps a martyr named Celestine on 25 May. No year is claimed, no place is pinned, and no other calendar has been checked for him.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and gives him a paragraph where the Romanian gives a line. saint.gr sets him among those who walked the way of the Cross after Christ with a wonderful endurance, and says that his burning desire was the quicker prevailing of the true religion: that desire drove him to preach the Gospel of salvation openly and to hunt for souls, and his continual struggles against falsehood, against the heresies and against the foul idols enraged the priests of the idols. He confessed his faith in the author of life and of death with a young man's vigour, and bore the various tortures with a wonderful endurance, thanking and glorifying the God who had counted him too worthy of martyrdom; and his tormentors, enraged that they could not tear Christ out of his soul, put him to death with red-hot iron. The page gives him no year, no place and no emperor, and the couplet it sets over him has the irons pierce his heels, so that in treading he crushes the serpent that strikes at the heel.
