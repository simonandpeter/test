# Polydorus of Nicosia

Polydorus, a Cypriot of Nicosia, was raised by devout parents, Loukas and Lourdanou, learned letters and travelled to Egypt and other lands as a merchant. In Egypt in 1793 he was taken on as secretary by a Zakynthian who had renounced Christ, and at a carousal with him got drunk and denied Christ; coming to himself he repented bitterly, went to Beirut and confessed to the bishop there, withdrew to a monastery on Mount Lebanon, where he rested through Great Lent, and then went to Ptolemais — Acre — where he announced — the synaxarion’s life runs on past the point read here; its line for the day gives his martyrdom in 1794.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 3 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/931/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
