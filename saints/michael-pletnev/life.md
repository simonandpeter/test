# Michael (Pletnev)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him last of four in one line on 10 декабря and gives none of the four a life. The line is «Сщмчч. Иакова и Александра пресвитеров, сщмч. Евграфа пресвитера и сына его мч. Михаила (1918).» — the hieromartyrs James and Alexander, presbyters, the hieromartyr Eugraphus the presbyter and his son the martyr Michael, 1918. He is the son, and the calendar’s own name page for him reads «Михаил (Плеьнев), мч.», with a soft sign standing where a т belongs; his father’s page two links earlier reads «Евграф (Плетнев)».

That is the whole of the record. He was a martyr with no priestly grade, which the line and the name page agree on; he died in 1918, the first year of the persecution in which the Russian church counts its new martyrs; and the calendar says nothing of his age, of where the two of them were, or of how they died. Nothing is supplied here.

The day page carries eleven hymns, for a company of martyrs of the fourth century and for three of the saints it heads, and not one of them is under any of these four names. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 10 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261210.html); read 13 October 2026.*
