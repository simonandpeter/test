# Basilides, companion of Gerontius

saint.gr's whole notice for the two of them is one sentence: the holy [Gerontius](/saints/gerontius-the-martyr) and Basilides — or, as others have it, Basiliades — were martyred by the sword. The variant is the page's own, offered as another reading of the same name. There is no year here, no city, no persecutor, no rank beyond martyr, no relic and no hymn; the manner of death is the whole of what is recorded, and the order of the two names is the whole of what tells this man from any other.

Over them the page prints a distich, which says that Gerontius, by the sword together with Basilides, came in his old age to share in the Word who is King of all. The Romanian calendar prints Gerontius on this day and not his companion, which is why this corpus kept one of the pair and not the other until the Greek calendar was read.

The corpus holds two other men named Basilides — the companion of Bassus, and one of the Ten of Crete — and neither of them is kept on this day by any calendar it reads, nor is either the companion of a Gerontius. That is a thin distinction, and it is the one this folder rests on.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/158/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
