# Claudius of Rome

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 7 июня in a line with the pope he died beside, and the one reading it carries is the pope's. It tells that Маркеллин was pope of Rome at the height of the persecution under Диоклетиан and Максимиан, who reigned from 284 to 305, when seventeen thousand people were put to death in a single month; that the pope himself was seized, and in terror of the torments burned incense and sacrificed to the idols, and that the emperor called him his friend and dressed him in costly clothes.

Gnawed by his conscience, the reading says, he wept bitterly that he had urged many to suffer for Christ and had himself given his flock an example of faint-heartedness. A council of a hundred and eighty bishops and presbyters was sitting at that time in the town of Синуесса in Campania. The pope came to it in sackcloth with ashes on his head and asked to be judged for his denial, and the fathers answered: condemn yourself with your own mouth; out of your mouth came the sin, let the sentence be spoken by your mouth too. They reminded him that Петр also denied Christ out of fear and wept and was received again.

Then he passed sentence on himself: I own myself stripped of the holy rank, of which I am unworthy; let my body not be buried after my death but thrown to the dogs, and cursed be he who dares to bury it. Back in Rome he went to the emperor, threw the gift of the costly robe down at his feet and said that he bitterly repented his denial of Christ. The enraged emperor had him tortured and sentenced him to death; and having prayed to the Lord Jesus Christ, who receives penitents in mercy, the pope put his head under the sword.

«С ним были обезглавлены и святые мученики Клавдий, Кирин и Антонин (+ 304)» — with him were beheaded also the holy martyrs Claudius, Cyrinus and Antoninus, in 304. That one sentence is everything the long reading says of the three of them. The pope's body lay by the road thirty-six days until the apostle Петр appeared to the newly made pope Маркелл and told him to bury it, saying: he that humbleth himself shall be exalted; and it was laid in the tomb a noble Roman woman had made for the bodies of martyrs.

He is the Клавдий of that sentence, and the sentence is all there is: the day line names him second of the four, his name page gives the heading and the one day, and the index behind it leads back to the same reading of the pope.

The two beheaded with him are [Cyrinus of Rome](/saints/cyrinus-of-rome), [Antoninus of Rome](/saints/antoninus-of-rome), and the pope they died beside is [Marcellinus of Rome](/saints/marcellinus-of-rome).
