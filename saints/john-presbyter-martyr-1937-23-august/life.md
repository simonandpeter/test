# John (Karabanov), Presbyter, Hieromartyr (1937)

John was born in 1885 in the village of Gerasimovo in the Shablykino district of the Karachev county of Oryol province, the son of the peasant Theodore Karabanov. Finishing the Karachev teachers’ seminary in 1905 he became a people’s teacher, teacher and head of the school of the ministry of education in the village of Znamenskoye in the Bolkhov district; mobilised in 1915 he served in the 203rd infantry regiment, was sent on 1 January 1916 to the Alexander military school and on finishing it, on 1 May 1916, made a platoon commander, then sent as a company commander to the front near Dvinsk. Demobilised in 1917 he returned — the calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. After ordination he served as priest in the Oryol region; in 1932 he was arrested for resisting the closing of the church and for counter-revolutionary agitation against collectivisation and sentenced to eight years in the camps, and in 1937 in the camp a new case was fabricated without interrogations, and the troika of the NKVD sentenced him to be shot. He was executed on 5 September 1937; the Russian calendar keeps him on 23 August (Julian).
