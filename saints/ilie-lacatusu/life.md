# Ilie Lăcătușu

Ilie Lăcătușu was born on 6 December 1909 in the village of Crăpăturile, in Vâlcea county, of devout parents. From childhood he wanted to serve God, and in 1934 he finished the Faculty of Theology in Bucharest. He was ordained priest on 1 September 1934 for the parish of Osica de Jos in Olt county, and served that church until November of the same year, when he was sent on to the village of Buicești, also in Olt. The page says he served without ceasing, in church and in his family.

From 1942 he was sent as a missionary priest across the Dniester, into Odessa and the district of Râbnița; the next year he came home to his wife and children. He was arrested in 1952 and taken into Constanța county, to the Galeș and Peninsula colonies, and released in 1954. From 1959 to 1964 he was arrested again and sentenced to hard labour in the Delta, at Periprava, where he met Father Iustin Pârvu; things happened there, the page says, worthy of the lives of the saints.

After his release he was settled by force at Bolintin, where he worked as a bricklayer. Between 1965 and 1970 he served a parish in Teleorman county, and in 1970 he was transferred into Ilfov county, to Cucuruz, where he was pensioned in January 1978. What he had suffered had told badly on his health, and he spent the end of his life in hospital. He passed to eternity on exactly 22 July 1983.

On 28 September 1998, at the burial of his wife, those present came upon something they had not looked for: the saint's body was incorrupt, dry and of a pleasant fragrance.
