# Acacius of Tver

The Greek calendar prints him on 14 Ιανουαρίου as «Άγιος Ακάκιος εκ Ρωσίας», Acacius from Russia, and the page behind the line gives him four sentences. He lived in Russia about the sixteenth century. He received the monastic habit from [Joseph of Volokolamsk](/saints/joseph-the-wonderworker-of-volokolamsk), and — as the page has it — was named Alexander. In 1522 he was elected bishop of the region of Tver and Kashin.

He fell asleep, venerably, in peace in the year 1567, and his holy relics were moved to the monastery of Zheltikov. That is the whole of the entry: no birthplace, no year of birth, no account of his forty-five years in the see, and no hymn. Beside the line the page glosses his name from the privative alpha and κακός: the one who has no malice, and so the virtuous one.
