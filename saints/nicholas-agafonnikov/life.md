# Nicholas (Agafonnikov)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him first of six priests of 1937 on 23 октября ст. ст. The line declines his name and gives it without a surname; the title on the name link behind his place supplies both the surname and the grade, «Николай (Агафонников), протоиерей, сщмч.». Two of the six are called Николай, and nothing on the line tells them apart: it is the links behind the two places that do it, by their surnames.

The line calls the six of them пресвитеров together, and the link behind his place alone says протоиерей — an archpriest among the priests, which is why this folder's office says so and its types do not. His name page carries no life, but it does carry something the others' do not: the parish site of a church dedicated to him, «Храм в честь священномученика Николая Подольского», at Podolsk in the Moscow diocese. Nothing on either page says where he was born or served, or what he was charged with.

The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line. Of the six, the calendar carries a life for one only, and his is not it. The day page's five troparia and kontakia all belong to the three older entries it heads, and none of them to this company of 1937.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 23 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261023.html); read 4 October 2026.*
