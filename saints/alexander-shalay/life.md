# Alexander (Shalay)

The Russian church calendar keeps him on 1 November with another priest and a woman martyr, in the line «Сщмчч. Александра и Димитрия пресвитеров, мц. Елисаветы (1937)», and its index of names gives the surname and the grade the line leaves out: «Александр (Шалай), протоиерей, сщмч.» The life the calendar serves for him is not his own but a collective one, of the twenty-three hieromartyrs of the Minsk diocese among the new martyrs of the Belarusian land, and his place in it is a single paragraph.

He was born on 20 November 1879 in the town of Slutsk. In the 1930s he was rector of the Holy Trinity church in the village of Blon, in the Pukhovichi district of the Minsk region. His church was closed in 1935, and he went on performing the offices in people's houses. When the all-union census came, he organised a collection of signatures among the people of Blon for the reopening of their church.

He was arrested soon afterwards, in August 1937, charged with counter-revolutionary activity directed at breaking up the collective farms, and in October of the same year sentenced to be shot. The sentence was carried out at Bobruisk on 14 November 1937, new style, which is this Julian day in new style. The calendar keeps him also on 26 January with the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 1 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261101.html), [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9267.html) and [the collective life of the Minsk hieromartyrs](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4766.htm); read 4 October 2026.*
