# Demetrius (Volkov)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 19 февраля ст. ст. in a line of three words: «Мч. Димитрия (1942).» The rank word is «Мч.», мученик, a martyr, and it is the only thing the calendar says of him besides the year; it gives him no order of service and no monastic rank. The name link behind the line carries the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line itself leaves out, «Димитрий (Волков), мч.», and the year the line prints for his death is 1942, the latest of any the day heads.

The calendar prints no life page under his name and no hymn under it either: the two hymns the day page carries are the apostle Archippus's. Nothing further is written here. The line, the rank word, the name the link behind it carries and the year 1942 are the whole of what was read, and in particular he is not identified with any of the other Demetriuses the calendar keeps on other days.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 19 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260219.html); read 3 October 2026.*
