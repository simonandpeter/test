# Gaius (7 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 7 December keeps two martyrs in one entry and names both: Gaius and [Gaianus](/saints/gaianus-martyr-7-december). The whole of the life it gives them is one sentence — that the holy Gaius and Gaianus were martyred by fire. No country, no reign, no persecutor and no year is printed for either of them.

The couplet set over the entry puts the two men in the furnace together and makes the second speak: though you be thrown into the midst of the furnace, Gaius, Gaianus says, I will not leave you. That is all the page has of their story, and the order of the names in the entry is the only ground for calling Gaius the first of the two.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3188/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
