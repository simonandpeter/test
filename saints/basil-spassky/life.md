# Basil (Spassky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him first of three in one line for 23 декабря ст. ст.: «Сщмч. Василия пресвитера и прмч. Макария и Ионы Смирнова (1938).» The line declines the forename to «Василия», gives him the grade of presbyter and no surname, no place, no year of birth and no word of narrative. The surname at the end of the line belongs to the third man and not to him.

Behind that «Василия» stands the calendar's own name page 9511, which carries the fuller form «Василий (Спасский), иерей, сщмч.» — the surname the day's line leaves out. That page lists two days, this 23 декабря and «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and it carries an icon of him and no life. Neither does the day page.

So this folder says what the calendar says and no more: a Russian priest of that name, who died a martyr in 1938 and whom this calendar keeps on this day with a hieromonk and a monk.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 23 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261223.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9511.html), which carries no life; read 13 October 2026.*
