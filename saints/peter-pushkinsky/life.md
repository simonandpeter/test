# Peter (Pushkinsky)

The Russian calendar keeps eleven people on 30 September under one line and one year: six presbyters, a deacon, a schemanun and three lay martyrs, all of them 1937. He is the first of the six, and the day line gives him only his baptismal name — «Сщмчч. Петра, Вячеслава, Петра, Симеона, Василия, Александра пресвитеров …» — so the surname has to be taken from the site's index of names, which prints him «Петр (Пушкинский), протоиерей, сщмч.» and makes him an archpriest rather than a priest.

That index keeps him on this day and on 26 January with the whole company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and it prints nothing else: no birth, no diocese, no parish, no charge, no place of death. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, browser user-agent and all, so the line stands as the line. Two Peters are on this page and they are two men; the second is [Peter (Solovyov)](/saints/peter-solovyov).

*After the Russian church calendar's [30 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260930.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9390.html); read 4 October 2026.*
