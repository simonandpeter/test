# Dometian of Melitene

Dometian lived in the time of the emperor Justin the Younger. His parents, Theodore and Eudocia, were right-believing and well-to-do people, and he was brought to the end of human learning and of the study of the holy Scripture. He married, and his wife died a short while after; and giving himself wholly to the wisdom that is God's, he was made bishop of the holy Church of Melitene when he was thirty years old.

Joining the wisdom of life in the world to the life of the hermit, he became a cause of salvation not only to his own flock but to his whole people. The emperor sent him more than once as an envoy to the Persians, at the time when Chosroes, emperor of the Persians, had been driven out by Varam and had become a tributary of the Greeks. Dometian grew to be a friend of the emperor Maurice and of the empress, and the large sums of money he obtained from them he spent on the building of holy churches and of shelters for the poor.

Going once more to Constantinople, he departed to the Lord there. Honoured as was fitting by the whole assembly of the court and of the Church, he was laid in a coffin and carried with candles and singing to his own country, Melitene. He worked many miracles, the life says, both while he lived and after he had gone from the earth. doxologia.ro's page gives no year for any of this.
