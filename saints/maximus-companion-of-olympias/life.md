# Maximus, companion of Olympias

saint.gr gives him one sentence and no life. The holy martyrs Maximus and [Olympias](/saints/olympias-companion-of-maximus), it says, were martyred in Persia in the years of the emperor Decius, which it dates 249 to 251. That is the whole of the notice: it names no city, no judge and no manner of death, and it gives neither of them a year of their own, so this folder carries no dates. The page prints no couplet and no hymn for them, and adds only the meaning of his name, «ο μέγιστος», the greatest.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 15 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/15/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1323/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
