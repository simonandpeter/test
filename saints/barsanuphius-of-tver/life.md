# Barsanuphius of Tver

saint.gr keeps four venerable men of Russia together on this day and gives them two sentences between them: the venerable Barsanuphius, [Sabbas](/saints/sabbas-of-tver), [Sabbatius](/saints/sabbatius-of-tver) and [Euphrosynus](/saints/euphrosynus-of-tver) lived in Russia in the fifteenth century and were ascetics in the region of Tver; and the venerable ones fell asleep in peace. Because the line names all four, each is kept here as a folder of his own rather than as a company.

Of this one, beyond the name, that is everything: the century, the country, the district and the manner of his repose. The page gives no year of birth and none of death, no monastery and no cell, no elder and no disciple, no office in any house, no order of precedence among the four and nothing at all about what they were to one another. It names no relic, prints no distich and gives no hymn.

The corpus keeps four other men of this name — of Kirillov, of Optina, of Zographou, and the Great — and none of them stands on this day or is named on this page.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 2 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/02/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3890/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
