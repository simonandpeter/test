# Stachys of Byzantium

doxologia.ro's calendar names Stachys among the apostles it keeps on 31 October, and the life it prints for him is four sentences. He was set as bishop in Byzantium by the holy Apostle Andrew, and together with Andrew he built a church at Argyropolis. Then he gathered many of the faithful and taught them the life that saves.

Having laboured sixteen years in that struggle for the salvation of men, he reposed in peace. That is the whole of the page: it gives no year, no account of the sixteen years beyond the number, and names nobody beside the apostle who ordained him. doxologia.ro prints no separate troparion for him. The hymn on his page is the one it gives to all six apostles it names on this day — Apelles, Stachys, Ampliatus, Urban, Aristobulus and Narcissus together — a short common of apostles in the third tone, asking them to entreat the merciful God for the forgiveness of our offences.

The Greek calendar keeps the six of them together on the same day, 31 October, and saint.gr gives each of them his see. All six belonged to the seventy Apostles of the Lord, it says, and all of them were «Χριστοῦ εὐωδία τῷ Θεῷ ἐν τοῖς σωζομένοις» — a fragrance of Christ, pleasing to God, among those being saved — which it takes from 2 Corinthians 2.15. Of this one it says that he became the first bishop of Byzantium, and that after sixteen years in the apostolic preaching he rested in peace in the Lord; its heading dates him «(; - 54)». The epigram over his name plays on it, as the Greek for an ear of corn: Stachys, like an ear of corn, is reaped out of this present life by the sickle of his consecration.

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps the same six apostles of the Seventy on the Julian 31 October, and its one life for them adds to his notice the man who ordained him: the holy Apostle [Andrew the First-Called](/saints/andrew-the-first-called) laid hands on him for the rank of bishop of Byzantium, where he was a hierarch for sixteen years — the same sixteen the Greek page gave — preaching the Gospel of Christ zealously and turning the pagans to the true faith. The day's line closes the six of them with «(I)», the first century, which is weaker than the year saint.gr gave him and does not displace it. The calendar prints a troparion and a kontakion for the six together; the kontakion names all six by name.
