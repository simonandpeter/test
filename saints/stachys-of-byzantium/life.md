# Stachys of Byzantium

doxologia.ro's calendar names Stachys among the apostles it keeps on 31 October, and the life it prints for him is four sentences. He was set as bishop in Byzantium by the holy Apostle Andrew, and together with Andrew he built a church at Argyropolis. Then he gathered many of the faithful and taught them the life that saves.

Having laboured sixteen years in that struggle for the salvation of men, he reposed in peace. That is the whole of the page: it gives no year, no account of the sixteen years beyond the number, and names nobody beside the apostle who ordained him. doxologia.ro prints no separate troparion for him. The hymn on his page is the one it gives to all six apostles it names on this day — Apelles, Stachys, Ampliatus, Urban, Aristobulus and Narcissus together — a short common of apostles in the third tone, asking them to entreat the merciful God for the forgiveness of our offences.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 31 octombrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/31-octombrie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-apostol-stahie); read 25 September 2026.*
