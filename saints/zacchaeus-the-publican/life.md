# Zacchaeus

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The page for 20 April prints his line — «Sfântul Apostol Zaheu» — and his own page carries icons, places of pilgrimage and the appointed readings, but no Viață tab, no troparion and no notice of his life at all.

What stands in place of a life on that page is the Gospel it appoints, the passage from Luke in which he is first met: «Iată un bărbat, cu numele Zaheu, şi acesta era mai-marele vameşilor şi era bogat. Şi căuta să vadă cine este Iisus, dar nu putea de mulţime, pentru că era mic de statură» — a man named Zacchaeus, the chief of the publicans, and rich; and he sought to see who Jesus was, and could not for the crowd, because he was short of stature.

That is all the calendar gives. Where he preached afterwards, where he died and in what year, the Romanian calendar does not say here, and nothing further is supplied in this folder; it prints only the rank it gives him, apostle.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, and saint.gr sends the reader to the Gospel for him: his story, it says, is in Luke 19:1-10. He is the well-known chief publican of Jericho who climbed up into the sycamore — because his height did not favour him — in order to see Jesus passing by; the Lord marvelled at his faith, bade him come down from the tree and went with him to his house, having forgiven all the sins he had committed up to that moment.

What the page adds, it adds as report: «Λέγεται», it is said, that after the Ascension of the Lord Zacchaeus followed the apostle Peter, and that by him he was afterwards ordained bishop of Caesarea. It gives no year and no account of his end, and it prints no hymn. Its couplet turns the Gospel's own word about: not come down, but come up, Christ says to thee, Zacchaeus, calling thee to heaven.
