# Helladius (27 May)

Doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 27 mai reads «Sfântul Sfințit Mucenic Eladie», the holy hieromartyr Helladius, and the page the calendar links for him carries no Viață tab at all — only service texts: a canon, a troparion and a kontakion, with icons and video of the hymn. Nothing there says where he lived, or when, or how he died. The rank in the line is the whole of what is stated about him: a man in holy orders who died a martyr.

The kontakion is the only text on the page that names him, calling him a great setter-right for us through his prayers to God, and addressing him as consecrated sufferer, ever-glorious. The troparion doxologia prints under his name is the common of hieromartyrs, the same text it prints for Therapon of Sardis, who shares the day with him. No year, no see and no place is recorded here, because the source gives none.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 27 mai — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/27-mai) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-sfintit-mucenic-eladie); read 24 September 2026.*
