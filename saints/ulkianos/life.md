# Ulkianos

saint.gr's calendar for 28 Ιουνίου names him and gives almost no life. Its line reads «Όσιος Ουλκιανός», and the page behind that line carries two short sentences: that the venerable Ulkianos died in peace, and that there are no other particulars of his life to be had — «Δεν έχουμε άλλες πληροφορίες για τον βίο του Οσίου». No country is named, no century, no monastery and no year, and the page prints no hymn.

Above the two sentences stands a couplet of the synaxarion addressed to him: «Eίσελθε χαίρων Oυλκιανέ παμμάκαρ, Tην Oυρανών ήνοιξε Xριστός σοι πύλην» — enter rejoicing, all-blessed Ulkianos; Christ has opened the gate of the heavens to you. The page does not even gloss the name, as it does for most of the saints it keeps.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/28/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1912/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
