# Constantine (Lebedev)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and gives no life. Its seventh entry for 9 июля ст. ст. reads «Сщмч. Константина пресвитера (1918).» — the hieromartyr Constantine, presbyter, of the year 1918 — and behind the name stands a link to a name page and to no reading at all. That page, 13745, prints «Константин (Лебедев), иерей, сщмч.», Constantine (Lebedev), priest, hieromartyr, and beneath it «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 9 июля», and nothing else whatever.

So the calendar sets down a parish priest of that surname who died in 1918, the first year of the persecution, and keeps him twice: on this day, which is his own, and on the Julian 26 January with the synaxis of the New Martyrs and Confessors of Russia. Where he served, how he was taken and how he died it does not say, and nothing here is supplied from anywhere else.

The day page carries nine hymn headings, every one of them for a hierarch of the day, and none under his name. azbyka.ru, which might hold an account of him, was not read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 9 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260709.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13745.html), which name him and give no life; read 9 October 2026.*
