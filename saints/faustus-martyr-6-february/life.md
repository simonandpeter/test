# Faustus (6 February)

saint.gr names him and gives almost no life. Its calendar for 6 February prints «Άγιοι Φαύστος, Βασίλειος και Σιλουανός», and the page behind that line says one thing about the three of them: that Faustus, [Basil](/saints/basil-martyr-6-february) and [Silvanus](/saints/silvanus-martyr-6-february) were friends as brothers are, young in years, and were martyred by the sword. There is no homeland here, no century, no persecutor, and no hymn.

Then the page adds a doubt of its own, which is recorded here because it is the source's and not the reader's: perhaps, it says, these are the same as the saints Faustus, Basil and Lucian who are kept on 25 October, since the couplet printed over them is the same. The third name is not the same in the two lines, the corpus holds no folder for that October company, and the 25 October page was not read. The couplet itself turns on the number: one end by the sword for three fellow-contenders, who think of God as one and as three.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2810/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
