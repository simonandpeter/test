# Gerasimus of Tismana

Gerasimus was born on 21 January 1912 in the village of Poduri in the county of Bacău, to the Christian household of Elena and Grigore Iscu, where he was brought up in the Christian faith and in the fear of God; and while he was still young he went to the monastery of Bogdana in the same county, where he stayed as a brother. Between 1925 and 1928 brother Grigore followed the courses of the theological seminary at the monastery of Neamț.

In 1928, when that seminary was closed, he had to move to the Prince Ferdinand lyceum in Bacău and then to the theological seminary at the monastery of Cernica; afterwards he enrolled at the faculty of theology in Bucharest, which he only finished in 1942. In 1932 he went to the monastery of Tismana, where he was tonsured a monk and received the name Gherasim, and not long after was ordained hierodeacon and then hieromonk.

On 15 April 1937 he was made abbot of the monastery of Arnota, which lies a little above the monastery of Bistrița in the county of Vâlcea, and between 1937 and 1939 he worked at repairing the monastic buildings, which a fire had damaged. In 1939 he gave up the abbacy of Arnota and returned to Cernica, where he was made librarian and accountant of the theological seminary within its walls.

On 1 April 1942 Nifon, then metropolitan of Oltenia, sent him across the Prut to strengthen the Orthodox faith and to comfort the weaknesses of the brethren there; and there he raised and repaired churches, and served as priest, as professor and as schoolmaster. Returning to the country in 1943 he went back to Tismana, the monastery of his profession, and was made exarch over all the monasteries of Oltenia, a charge he fulfilled between June 1943 and April 1945.

From 1943 he was also abbot of Tismana and received the honour of archimandrite; and in that charge he restored the monastery, which had been severely affected in the period when it was used as a prison for legionary priests. With the coming of the communist government the persecution began of all who set themselves against the new order; and in the autumn of 1947 a group of the anti-communist resistance found shelter and food at Tismana.

For that help he was arrested on 26 September 1948, accused of «social disorder», and after the interrogations he was sentenced to ten years of hard prison. Taken to the prison at Craiova, he began to be persecuted without a scrap of mercy, and in 1949 he was taken to the prison at Aiud; at Craiova he had been able to keep the monastic habit, but at Aiud it was forbidden him.

After some time he was taken to Poarta Albă to dig at the Canal. Because of the harsh conditions he fell ill with tuberculosis, and for that was sent to the prison at Târgu Ocna. One of the tormentors who persecuted him there was Vasilescu, a prisoner who had been through re-education. It is said that Gerasimus knew the day of his death beforehand; and in his last days his own tormentor Vasilescu, badly stricken with tuberculosis, was brought to the bed beside his.

On 25 December 1951 he asked insistently to be carried to Vasilescu's bed. The meeting with the father made Vasilescu repent of all the sins he had committed at the Canal, and he confessed and communicated; and that night both of them passed in peace to eternity. doxologia.ro's calendar keeps him on 26 decembrie, the day after the death its own life for him gives, and the page the site keeps for his troparion is an audio recording, sung by Father Vlad Roșu, and prints no text.
