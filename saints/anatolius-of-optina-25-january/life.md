# Anatolius of Optina (25 January)

saint.gr's calendar for 25 January names Anatolius of Optina and dates him 1824 to 1894. His name in the world, the page says, was Alexios Moiseevich Kopev, and he was born in the village of Bobyli in the province of Kaluga in Russia in 1824. In 1832 he attended the lessons of the church school of Borovsk, and in 1836 those of the church seminary of Kaluga.

In 1853 he withdrew, in order to live as a monk in stillness and in silence, to the desert of Optina; and there, saint.gr says, he fell asleep in peace in the year 1894. That is the whole of the page. It says nothing of his eldership, nothing of the disciples who came to him, and prints no troparion and no kontakion. The corpus already keeps another elder of the same monastery and the same name, whom the Romanian calendar sets on 30 July and calls the Younger; the two are distinguished here by their days, and the years given for each are the ones their own sources print.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 25 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/25/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1164/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
