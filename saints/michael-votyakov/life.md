# Michael (Votyakov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names a priest Michael on 5 июня and prints no life of him. Its line for the day is short: «Сщмч. Михаила пресвитера (1931).» — the hieromartyr Michael, a presbyter, and the year 1931. His name page, 14270, adds the surname Вотяков, grades him иерей, a priest, and gives two days, «26 января (Новомуч.) , 5 июня».

That page carries no reading of his life but a link to an article on Pravoslavie.Ru by Марина Шмелева, and only the two lines the name page itself prints of it were read. They are these. The article is titled with a sentence from what was said against him — «Вотяков доводит до плача присутствующих в церкви. Его надо изолировать», Votyakov brings those present in the church to tears, he must be isolated. And its opening says that his last word before he was shot was spoken to the people of his own village, calling them to forgive the persecutors and the executioners as he himself had forgiven them.

Nothing further of that article is in this folder: no year of birth, no village, no diocese, no date within 1931. The day page prints eleven hymns and none under his name, and the 26 января commemoration of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia was not read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 5 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260605.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14270.html); read 8 October 2026.*
