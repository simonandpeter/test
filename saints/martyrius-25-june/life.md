# Martyrius (25 June)

saint.gr's calendar for 25 Ιουνίου names him and gives no life. Its line reads «Όσιος Μαρτύριος ο επίσκοπος», and the page behind it says two things only: that it is unknown of which bishopric he was bishop — «Άγνωστο ποιας επισκοπής ήταν επίσκοπος» — and that his memory is mentioned in the Jerusalem Kanonarion, at page 96.

Nothing else is printed: no country, no century, no year, no manner of death and no hymn. The page glosses the name itself, from the word for a witness, as one who accepts torments for Christ's sake. The see is left open here because the source leaves it open.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 25 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/25/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1977/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
