# Hilary of Carcassonne

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives him one sentence. The line for 3 June reads «Άγιος Ιλάριος Επίσκοπος Καρκασσόν της Τουλούζης», Saint Hilary, bishop of Carcassonne of Toulouse, and the page behind it says this: Saint Hilary lived in the fourth century after Christ and was bishop of the city of Carcassonne of Toulouse in Gaul; he fell asleep in peace.

So what is recorded of him is a name, a see, a century and a death that was not a martyrdom. The page says nothing of his birth, his education, his predecessor or his flock, and prints neither verse nor hymn for him. The heading above the entry is a note on the name, from ιλαρός, the cheerful or the serene, and it is the page's own furniture. He is not the Hilary of Poitiers whom this corpus already keeps, a doctor of the Western church with a life of his own.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/03/index.aspx) and [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/4411/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
