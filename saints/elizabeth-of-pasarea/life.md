# Elizabeth of Pasărea

The venerable Elizabeth (Lazăr) of Pasărea was a great hermit and ascetic, known for her life of prayer and for the spiritual gifts she received from God, and held to be a worker of wonders. She was born on 7 May 1970 in the commune of Moldova Sulița, in the county of Suceava, and in civil life bore the name Rodica Lazăr.

At the age of only fifteen, in 1985, she entered the community of the Monastery of Pasărea, near Bucharest. After three years of monastic life, in 1988, she was tonsured a nun and received the name Theodora. In 2003, feeling the call of a more withdrawn life, she went into solitude on Mount Giumalău, where she lived as a hermit in harsh ascetic labour, in prayer and in meditation. In 2007 she received the great schema, taking the name Elizabeth.

On 6 June 2014 she passed to the Lord, remembered by those who had known her as a humble and enlightened ascetic, the hermit of Mount Giumalău. In its working session of 1 July 2025 the Holy Synod of the Romanian Orthodox Church approved her canonisation under the title of Saint Elizabeth of Pasărea, with her commemoration on 5 June.
