# Theodulus, executioner converted by Hermione

Theotimus (or Timothy) and Theodulus were the executioners who believed in Christ through [Hermione](/saints/hermione-daughter-of-philip), the daughter of the apostle Philip, and died in peace. That is the whole of the Greek synaxarion’s notice; its verse says Theotimus died with Theodulus, giving the Lord a servant’s honour with him.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 4 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2343/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
