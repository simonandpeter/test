# Basil (Voskresensky)

Basil Voskresensky was a deacon of the church of the Transfiguration in the town of Solikamsk, in Perm province. During the mass repressions in the Perm region he was shot by Red Army men on 7 October 1918. He was glorified by the Jubilee Council of Bishops of the Russian Church in 2000. azbyka.ru, after Hegumen Damascene (Orlovsky), prints nothing more of his life, and no hymn.
