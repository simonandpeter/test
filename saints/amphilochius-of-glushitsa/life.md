# Amphilochius of Glushitsa

He came to the Glushitsa monastery in the Vologda country in 1417, already a monk and already in priestly orders, out of one of the houses of Ustyug the Great, having heard what was done there. [Dionysius of Glushitsa](/saints/dionysius-of-glushitsa), who had founded the place, told the newcomer plainly how severe and how hard its life was, and it did not put him off. «Если хочешь пребывать здесь, то сотворим завет — не разлучаться друг с другом, пока останемся на земле» — if you wish to remain here, let us make a covenant, not to be parted from one another while we are on the earth. He agreed gladly, and promised to keep the house's rule.

He kept it for twenty years under his teacher, in fasting, prayer and obedience, copying him in everything and working beside him on the building of the monastery. The second of the calendar's two lives for him adds the only two words either page has for his character, meekness and patience, and nothing more about them.

When Dionysius died he was made abbot and governed the house fifteen years longer. He died in peace on 12 October 1452 and was buried beside his teacher, which is how the Russian calendar's icon shows the two of them, side by side. The troparion the calendar prints on this day is not his alone — it is sung to four of the Glushitsa fathers together, him named first among them, and its kontakion ends by calling on two, his teacher and himself.

*After the Russian church calendar's [12 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261012.html) and the two lives its index page keeps for him, [the first](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1717.htm) and [the second](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life6407.htm); read 4 October 2026.*
