# Barnabas of the Vetluga

saint.gr's calendar for 11 Ιουνίου names him and gives no life. Its whole notice is one sentence: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέριες για τον βίο του Ρώσου μοναχού Βαρνάβα» — we have no details of the life of the Russian monk Barnabas. What the day's line adds is the river he is named for, «του ποταμού Βετολούγκα», the Vetluga; and the page's gloss on his name, which is Hebrew and means son of consolation.

So the Greek calendar keeps of him a name, a nation, a monastic habit and a river, and nothing more. There is no century on the page, no place of birth, no monastery, no manner of death, no synaxarion verse and no hymn. Two other Barnabases stand on the same Greek day — the apostle of Cyprus, and a wonderworker of Limassol whose entry the site itself marks as uncorroborated — and this monk is neither of them.
