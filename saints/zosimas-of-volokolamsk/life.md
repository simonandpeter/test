# Zosimas of Volokolamsk

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life beyond one sentence for the two of them. The venerable Zosimas and [Adrian](/saints/adrian-of-volokolamsk) of Volokolamsk, founders of the monastery of Sestrin at the mouth of the river Sestra, lived in the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries. They fell asleep in peace, and their holy relics were laid in the church of the Dormition of the Mother of God of that same monastery of Sestrin.

That is all of it. The page gives no year of birth and none of death for either man, says nothing of where they came from or under whom they were tonsured, nothing of the founding itself and nothing of what became of the house afterwards; it prints neither apolytikion nor kontakion for them. It does give each of the two his own head line, which is why they stand here as two and not as one.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/08/index.aspx) and [the life of the pair](https://www.saint.gr/1538/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
