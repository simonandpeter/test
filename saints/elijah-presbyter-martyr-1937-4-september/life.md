# Elijah (Izmailov), Presbyter, Hieromartyr (1937)

Elijah was born in 1882 in the village of Khormaly in the Tsivilsk district of Kazan province — today the Ibresi district of Chuvashia — to the peasant Ishmael, one of five children; he studied at the parish school, sang in the church choir from childhood and, grown up and experienced, came to lead it. Called up in 1916 he came home at the war’s end; taken with the idea of freedom he became chairman of the volost land committee in 1918 and served in the volost executive committee until 1920. In 1925, without marrying, he received the priesthood from Herman (Kokel), bishop of Alatyr, then in exile at Kozya Sloboda near Kazan, where the ordination took place; the then priest of Khormaly had joined the Renovationists, and Bishop Herman, arriving in Chuvashia, with the Orthodox drove — the calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. He was arrested twice: in 1932 in the case of a group of the True Orthodox Church, three years’ exile in Kazakhstan, and in 1937 for holding secret services and petitioning for the opening of the church; he did not admit guilt, was sentenced by the troika of the NKVD to be shot, and was executed on 17 September 1937 at Alatyr. The Russian calendar keeps him on 4 September (Julian).
