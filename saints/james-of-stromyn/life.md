# James of Stromyn

saint.gr's whole account of him is two sentences: «Ο Όσιος Ιάκωβος έζησε τον 14ο αιώνα μ.Χ. και ασκήτεψε στη μονή της Κοιμήσεως της Θεοτόκου του Στρομύν, κοντά στη Μόσχα. Κοιμήθηκε με ειρήνη περί το έτος 1392 μ.Χ.» — James lived in the fourteenth century and practised asceticism in the monastery of the Dormition of the Mother of God of Stromyn, near Moscow; he fell asleep in peace about the year 1392.

There is nothing else. The calendar's line for the day calls him only «Όσιος Ιάκωβος», with no epithet to tell him apart, and the page's heading adds only the year. It names no parents, no elder, no abbot, no office, no relic and no wonder, and it prints neither couplet nor hymn. The monastery it names is the only thing that distinguishes him, and it is what the English form here is built on.
