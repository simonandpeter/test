# Nicodemus of the Cave

The Greek synaxarion keeps the venerable [Spyridon](/saints/spyridon-of-the-cave) and Nicodemus «in the cave» together on 28 September, and says that it has no details of the lives of these Russian venerable fathers; their memory is repeated, it says, on 31 October and on the Sunday of Meatfare. That is all it prints, and it gives no date.

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps the two of them on 31 октября ст. ст., and it has what the Greek page said it did not: a life. For thirty years the two fulfilled one obedience, the baking of the prosphora, in the monastery of the Caves. Of this one the page says two things and no more — that he laboured together with [Spyridon](/saints/spyridon-of-the-cave), and that he led the same strict life. Their relics lie together in the cave of Antony.

What the page tells at length belongs to the other man: his coming to the monastery under the abbot Pimen (1132–1141) already not young, the unceasing prayer and psalmody over the work, the burning bakery put out with a mantle that stayed whole, the three fingers of his right hand folded in the sign of the cross. The day's line dates the pair to the twelfth century, and the calendar's two hymns for them name this one beside the other in both. It keeps them again on 28 сентября with the saints of the Near Caves and on the second Sunday of Great Lent.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 28 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/1035/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 31 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261031.html) and [the life of the two](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4621.htm), read 4 October 2026.*
