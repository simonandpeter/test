# Joannicius (Lipovac)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 4 июня and marks the entry «(Серб.)», a saint of the Serbian church. Its line for the day is short: «Сщмч. Иоанникия , митр. Черногорско-Приморского (1945) (Серб.).» — the hieromartyr Joannicius, metropolitan of Montenegro and the Littoral, and the year 1945. His name page, 14511, adds the surname Липовац and repeats the see and the one day.

That page carries no life of the kind this calendar usually prints, but a link to an article on Pravoslavie.Ru by the hieromonk Игнатий (Шестаков). Only its opening paragraph was read, and that paragraph says this much: that his coming to the see of the metropolitans of Montenegro was the beginning of an ascent to Golgotha, and that his four years of archpastoral service — from December 1940 to November 1944 — were filled with constant hardship and upheaval, with suffering, and with an unceasing and desperate struggle for the Church and for the people.

Nothing further of that article is in this folder: no birth, no place, no account of his arrest or of his death, and no day within 1945. The day page prints no hymn under his name, and the see itself is written here rather than in the folder's `office`, because the four languages this site reads in have no settled form of it.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 4 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260604.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14511.html); read 8 October 2026.*
