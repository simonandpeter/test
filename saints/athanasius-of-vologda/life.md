# Athanasius of Vologda

The line the Greek calendar prints for him on 18 Ιανουαρίου is «Όσιος Αθανάσιος εκ Ρωσίας», Athanasius out of Russia, and the page behind it holds three sentences. It calls him «ο Όσιος Αθανάσιος του Σγιαντέμσκϊυ», says that he lived in the sixteenth century, and says that he began the ascetic life from the monastery of Valaam and ended at the monastery of the same surname in the region of Vologda in Russia, where he passed the remainder of his monastic course in a manner pleasing to God.

He fell asleep in peace in 1550. No year of birth is given, no abbacy, no miracle and no hymn. The English form used here keeps the region the page names; the surname the page prints is Greek script for a Russian form, and no Russian spelling is set down in this folder because none was read.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1129/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
