# Januarius, with Maxime and Macaria

saint.gr's whole account of the three is one sentence: «Οι Άγιοι Μάρτυρες Ιανουάριος, Μαξίμη και Μακαρία μαρτύρησαν στην Αφρική» — the holy martyrs Januarius, [Maxime](/saints/maxime-with-januarius-and-macaria) and [Macaria](/saints/macaria-with-januarius-and-maxime) were martyred in Africa. The day's line names all three together and calls them οι Μάρτυρες, and the page gives each of the three names its own heading and a gloss — Ιανουάριος bare, Μάξιμος from the Latin maximus, the greatest, and Μακάριος, the happy one.

That is all. The page names no city in Africa, no emperor and no governor, gives no year, says nothing of how any of the three died, tells nothing of what bound them to one another, lists no relic, and prints neither couplet nor hymn. Because the line names the three and enumerates them, this is one folder for each; nothing here says which of them, if any, was the first or the chief.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1843/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
