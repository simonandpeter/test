# George (Mkheidze)

The Russian calendar keeps the venerable George (Mkheidze), archimandrite and confessor, on 8 September of the old style, together with John (Maisuradze) - one line for the two of them, marked as saints of the Georgian Church, with the years 1957 and 1962. The calendar links no written life for either, so this entry records the line and nothing more.

*After the Православный церковный календарь (days.pravoslavie.ru), [8 сентября ст. ст.](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260908.html); read 29 August 2026.*
