# Parthenius of Kiev

The venerable Parthenius was born on 24 August 1790 in the village of Somovo, which lay under the government of Tula, and his name in the world was Peter Ivanovich Krasnopevkev — saint.gr, writing in Greek, gives it as «κατά κόσμο Πέτρος Ιβάνοβιτς Κρασνοπέβκεφ». He practised ascesis in the Lavra of the Caves of Kiev, and in the year 1838 he was clothed in the great schema, the μεγαλόσχημος habit that the page names. He fell asleep in peace in the year 1855.

That is the whole of the notice. saint.gr prints four sentences and heads him «(1790 - 1855)»; it gives no account of his parents, his schooling, his coming to Kiev, his obediences in the Lavra or his disciples, and it records no wonder, no relic and no hymn. It glosses the name instead: «ΠΑΡΘΕΝΙΟΣ (από το παρθένος) = ο αγνός, ο παρθενικός» — from the word for virgin, the pure, the virginal.

He is a Russian saint kept by the Greek calendar, and this folder records only what the Greek page prints. The Russian sources that would carry his life at length were not read for it, and what they add is another reader's to bring.

The Russian church keeps him on 17 марта ст. ст., eight days before the day the Greek calendar gives him, and the two are not a style conversion of each other; each calendar simply keeps its own day. The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery gives him one line and no life, and the name page behind that line adds the two things the line leaves out: the surname Краснопевцев, and the house — he was a hieroschemamonk of the Lavra of the Caves at Kyiv. Those are what identify him as the man the Greek page describes, and the year both give is the same.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 25 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/25/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1775/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 17 марта ст. ст. — [the Russian day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260317.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13454.html), which carries no life, read 7 October 2026.*
