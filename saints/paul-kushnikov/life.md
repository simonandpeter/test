# Paul (Kushnikov)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him for 23 февраля ст. ст. in five words — «Сщмч. Павла пресвитера (1918).» — a hieromartyr, a presbyter, who died in 1918. The name link behind the line carries the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line leaves out: «Павел (Кушников), иерей, сщмч.», a priest of that name, one of the new martyrs of the Russian church in the first year of the persecution.

The calendar prints no life page for him and no hymn under his name. Nothing further is written here — no parish, no diocese, no place and no manner of death — because the calendar gives none of them, and nothing is reasoned out from the surname or from the year.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 23 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260223.html); read 3 October 2026.*
