# John of Irenopolis

John was Bishop of Irenopolis — a Byzantine city, saint.gr explains in a parenthesis, which lay near the river Saros and the Taurus mountain in Rough Cilicia — and one of the three hundred and eighteen holy Fathers of the First Ecumenical Council, which met at Nicaea in Bithynia in the year 325 to condemn the heretical opinions of Arius. He fell asleep in holiness and in peace.

That is the whole of the page: the see, the council, and the manner of his death. No year of birth, no year of death, nothing of what he said or wrote at Nicaea, and no hymn. Over him it sets one couplet, that John too, for orthodoxy's sake, rebuked the impious with plain speech.
