# Bodfanos of Wales

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives almost no life. The entry for 2 June is two sentences long: the venerable Bodfanos came from Wales and lived in the seventh century after Christ; he fell asleep in peace. That is the whole of it — a name, a country, a century, and a death that was not a martyrdom.

There is no monastery here, no bishop who tonsured him, no companion, no wonder and no relic; there is no year, and the seventh century is the page's own word for when he lived. The page prints no hymn for him and no verse. The name is stored only in the Greek form the page prints: whatever he was called in his own country, this source does not say, and the corpus does not reconstruct a Welsh name out of a Greek transcription.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 2 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/02/index.aspx) and [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/290/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
