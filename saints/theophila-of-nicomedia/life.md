# Theophila of Nicomedia

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Theophila among the martyrs of Nicomedia on 28 December, in the same line as [The Twenty Thousand Martyrs of Nicomedia](/saints/twenty-thousand-martyrs-of-nicomedia). The reading behind it, life 3189, places her in the women’s monastery of the abbess [Agatha of Nicomedia](/saints/agatha-of-nicomedia), the house that sheltered [Domna of Nicomedia](/saints/domna-of-nicomedia) after she had feigned madness to be sent out of the palace.

When the emperor’s soldiers ruined that house, the sisters were imprisoned, tortured and insulted, and the reading says not one of them was defiled. Theophila it names in particular: given over to a brothel, she kept her virginity there by the help of an angel of the Lord, and the angel led her out of the place.

She died in the burning of the church of Nicomedia with the twenty thousand who would not sacrifice — the reading calls her, in that sentence, the one wonderfully saved out of the brothel. The day line closes «(302)» for the whole company; the reading gives no year of its own, only the reign of Maximian. The day page prints two hymns, a troparion and a kontakion of the martyrs, and both are the company’s own; nothing stands under this name alone.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 28 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261228.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life3189.htm); read 13 October 2026.*
