# John of Pateleria

saint.gr's calendar for 3 August names the venerable John the Confessor, abbot of the monastery of Pateleria, and then says plainly that it has no life to give: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Οσίου» — we have no details of the venerable one's life. What the entry carries besides the name is the title it gives him, confessor, and the house it puts him over, the monastery of Pateleria. It gives no century, no country and no story of any kind.

What the page does print is a gloss on the name and a couplet. The name, it says, means the grace and favour of God towards men, and it lists the famous bearers of it: the son of the priest Zacharias and Elisabeth, the Forerunner and Baptist of Jesus; the son of Zebedee and Salome, the disciple and Evangelist, who died in deep old age on Patmos; John Chrysostom, greatest of the Church's orators; and many other saints, kings and hierarchs. The couplet then turns the gloss on this John: that of the divine grace, blessed John, he was shown by his very calling to be a worthy house. The page prints no hymn to him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/03/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2201/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
