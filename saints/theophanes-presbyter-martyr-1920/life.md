# Theophanes (Sokolov), Archpriest, Hieromartyr (1920)

The mitred archpriest Theophanes Petrovich Sokolov was born in 1875. He is known to have been inspector of the parish schools of the Semirechye region, a member of the consistory and rector of the Protection church of the city of Verny, today Almaty. In the autumn of 1920 the venerable archpriest was shot by the new godless power; he was numbered among the saints at the jubilee Council of Bishops of the Russian Church in 2000, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 3 September (Julian) with [Andrew Dalnikov](/saints/andrew-presbyter-martyr-1920) and the other martyrs of Semirechye.
