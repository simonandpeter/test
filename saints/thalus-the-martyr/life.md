# Thalus

Thalus was martyred by the sword together with
[Artemidorus](/saints/artemidorus-the-martyr-20-september). That is the whole of the Greek notice
for 20 September.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 20 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/981/saint.aspx); read 26 August 2026.*
