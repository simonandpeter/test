# Solev the Egyptian

saint.gr's calendar for 5 August names Saint Solev the Egyptian, and gives him one sentence: «Ο Άγιος Σόλεβ ο Αιγύπτιος μαρτύρησε δια τοξευόμενου βέλους» — he was martyred by an arrow shot from a bow. That is the whole of the entry: no century, no city, no judge, no year and no relic.

Over the sentence the page sets a couplet which plays on his name: if you are spelled out backwards, O Solev, you will gather βέλος, an arrow, the instrument of your end. It prints no hymn to him. Where the source has one line, the life says exactly that line and no more.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2228/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
