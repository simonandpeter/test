# Clotilde

saint.gr's calendar names her and gives her two sentences. The line for 3 June reads «Αγία Γλοτίλδη η εκ Γαλλίας», Saint Clotilde of Gaul, and the page behind it says this: Saint Clotilde was born in the city of Lyons in Gaul in the year 474; she saw to the baptism of the king her husband and of her children, and contended for the spreading of the Gospel; and she fell asleep in peace, in the year 545.

So what is recorded of her is a birthplace, two years, a husband who was a king and was baptised through her, children who were baptised with him, and a labour for the Gospel named in one clause. The page does not name the king, does not say what kingdom it was, does not name a child, and says nothing of her widowhood or of where she died. It prints no verse for her and no hymn.
