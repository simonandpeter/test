# Serapion of Kozheozero

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 27 июня ст. ст. names him «Прп. Серапиона Кожеезерского (1611).», and the index page it links carries two readings, both read here. He was a Kazan Tatar, the murza Турташ Гравирович, brought to Moscow in 1551 among the Kazan prisoners; he was baptised with the name Sergius and lived in the house of the Moscow boyar Захария Плещеев. He took the faith so sincerely, the calendar says, that he resolved to give himself wholly to God.

On a desert island of lake Kozha, in 1560, he met the hermit monk Нифонт and stayed to live with him, sharing the hard labours of that life. At his own pressing request Нифонт gave him the monastic habit with the name Serapion.

After his teacher’s death in 1584 he went to Moscow and asked the tsar for a charter of land for a house; coming back, he and the monks who had gathered cleared the forest for tillage and raised two churches, one of the Holy Theophany and one of the hierarch Nicholas, and the patriarch gave him holy antimensia. The other reading says simply that the community of the common life was established by him in 1584, after his teacher had died.

In 1608, being then very old, he set his disciple Авраамий as abbot in his own place. He died in 1611 and was buried in the church of the house on lake Kozha. In 1613 a monk of that monastery, Боголеп, wrote the account of its founding and of its first builder, and composed his life. The day page prints no hymn under his name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 27 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260627.html) and [the index of his lives](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/id1961.htm); read 9 October 2026.*
