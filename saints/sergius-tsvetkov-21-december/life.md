# Sergius (Tsvetkov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him in one line for 21 декабря ст. ст.: «Сщмч. Сергия диакона (1937).» The line declines the forename to «Сергия» and gives no surname, no place, no year of birth and no word of narrative. The rank word «Сщмч.», the grade «диакона» and the year it closes with, 1937, are the whole of what it says of him.

Behind that «Сергия» stands the calendar's own name page 14499, which carries the fuller form «Сергий (Цветков), диакон, сщмч.» — the surname the day's line leaves out. That page lists two days: this 21 декабря and «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. It carries no life, and neither does the day page.

The same calendar keeps a second man of this name on 9 марта ст. ст., a priest who died in 1938, on its own numbered name page. The two names are identical and nothing else about them is, so this folder is the deacon of 1937 and that one is the priest of 1938, and the day stands in this slug to keep them apart.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 21 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261221.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14499.html), which carries no life; read 13 October 2026.*
