# Jordan (2 February)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its page for him prints the line «Sfântul Mucenic Iordan» over the date, 2 Februarie, and nothing besides: no homeland, no century, no account of his suffering, and no troparion. The rank the calendar gives him is mucenic, martyr, and the English form of his name here is the one an English reader would look for.

He is kept on the day of the Meeting of the Lord, together with two other martyrs the same calendar names and leaves equally unwritten, Agatodor and Gavriil. Whether he is a martyr some other calendar keeps on another day is a question this page does not answer, and no other calendar was read for him.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and calls him «ο εκ Τραπεζούντας», and where doxologia left him unwritten saint.gr gives the whole story. He was from Trebizond, and on marrying settled at Galata in Constantinople, aged forty. Playing a game one day with some Ottoman countrymen of his, one of the players jeered in Greek, «Άγιε Νικόλα ψωριάρη, βοήθησε με να νικήσω» — mangy St Nicholas, help me win — and Jordan answered in kind, at Mohammed's expense. Next day one of the company denounced him as a blasphemer. Brought before the Vizier and pressed to accept Islam to escape death, he held to his love for the «γλυκύτατο Ιησού»; and at the place of execution, with the headsman ready, the Vizier's messenger whispered that he need only say publicly that he was turning Turk and could then go and live as a Christian wherever he liked. He thanked the Vizier and said he would never do it, and was beheaded at Constantinople on 2 February 1650 — others say 1651. That night his kinsmen bought the body from the prefect and buried it at Beyoğlu; I. Karyophylles, Great Logothete of the Great Church, and Meletios Syrigos wrote his martyrdom, and the page prints no hymn.
