# Eleutherius, disciple of Dionysius the Areopagite

Eleutherius was a disciple of [Dionysius the Areopagite](/saints/dionysius-the-areopagite), the Greek synaxarion says, a deacon, Rusticus being a priest; the two may even have been Athenians. Eleutherius and [Rusticus](/saints/rusticus-disciple-of-dionysius) were martyred by the sword together with Dionysius in the persecution under Domitian (81–96), and the entry’s verse says that the sword set Eleutherius and Rusticus free of earthly things as well. The Greek calendar keeps the two on 3 October under one heading.

doxologia.ro’s life of Dionysius, for the Romanian calendar, has Clement of Rome send Dionysius to Gaul with the bishop Lucian, the priest Rusticus and the deacon Eleutherius, and the governor Sisinnius try the three together at Paris: they confessed one God in heaven, were beaten without mercy and imprisoned, and after Dionysius had come unharmed through fire and beasts they were beaten again and beheaded with him. The Romanian day lists Dionysius alone.

The Serbian church's Ohrid Prologue keeps him with [Dionysius](/saints/dionysius-the-areopagite) in its first entry for this day, and the account of the three of them going to Gaul and being beheaded together is the same as the Greek's. What it adds is the year inside the reign: Dionysius was ninety when the three were seized, it says, and they suffered under Domitian in 96, where saint.gr gives only the reign 81–96. The recorded death on this folder is narrowed to that year and both readings stand in the note.

**The Russian church calendar puts the martyrdom in Gaul and raises his rank.** It keeps him on the Julian 3 October in one line with Rusticus, «Сщмч. Рустика пресвитера и Елевферия диакона (96)», and its index of names heads him «Елевферий Парижский, диакон, сщмч.» — Eleutherius of Paris, deacon, hieromartyr. The two sources agree that he was a deacon and differ on what that made him at his death: a martyr to saint.gr, a hieromartyr to the Russian. This folder still prints the Greek reading and records the Russian one in its Russian row.
