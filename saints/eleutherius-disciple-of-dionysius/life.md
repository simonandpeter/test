# Eleutherius, disciple of Dionysius the Areopagite

Eleutherius was a disciple of [Dionysius the Areopagite](/saints/dionysius-the-areopagite), the Greek synaxarion says, a deacon, Rusticus being a priest; the two may even have been Athenians. Eleutherius and [Rusticus](/saints/rusticus-disciple-of-dionysius) were martyred by the sword together with Dionysius in the persecution under Domitian (81–96), and the entry’s verse says that the sword set Eleutherius and Rusticus free of earthly things as well. The Greek calendar keeps the two on 3 October under one heading.

doxologia.ro’s life of Dionysius, for the Romanian calendar, has Clement of Rome send Dionysius to Gaul with the bishop Lucian, the priest Rusticus and the deacon Eleutherius, and the governor Sisinnius try the three together at Paris: they confessed one God in heaven, were beaten without mercy and imprisoned, and after Dionysius had come unharmed through fire and beasts they were beaten again and beheaded with him. The Romanian day lists Dionysius alone.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 3 Οκτωβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2580/saint.aspx); the life of Dionysius printed by doxologia.ro — [the entry](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-sfintit-mucenic-dionisie-areopagitul); read 17 September 2026.*
