# Arsenius, companion of Elias the Cave-dweller

Arsenius and [Elias the Cave-dweller](/saints/elias-the-cave-dweller-of-calabria) lived about the ninth century and were monks in southern Italy, but because of the Arab raids took refuge for a time at Patras; the Greek calendar keeps them on 11 September, among the saints of Achaea. That is the whole of the notice, which this entry records and nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 11 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/4065/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
