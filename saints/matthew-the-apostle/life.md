# Matthew

Matthew was the brother of James, son of Alphaeus, and was called to the apostleship from the tax booth. He was the first to write the life of Christ, setting down the Holy Gospel in the Hebrew tongue. He preached Christ throughout all Ethiopia and in the parts of the man-eaters, where, in the city called Myrmene, he passed from this life as a martyr.

Doxologia prints this as one paragraph of its account of the Synaxis of the Twelve Apostles, which the Romanian calendar keeps on 30 June. The page gives no year, so none is recorded here.
