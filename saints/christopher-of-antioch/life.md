# Christopher of Antioch

Christopher was elected patriarch of Antioch in 960, in the fourteenth year of the caliph al-Muti, whose reign saint.gr gives as 946 to 974 — the page rests that dating on the Arab chronicler Yahya, and names him for it. He held the see seven years. On the night of Monday to Tuesday in 967 he was killed by the Arabs, having been accused of being in communication with the emperor of Byzantium, Nikephoros Phokas, who was then campaigning against Antioch.

The page adds where it happened and what followed. The killing was done in the house of a man the patriarch had himself done a kindness to, Ibn Manik, and the honourable relic was thrown into the river Orontes. Eight days later the Christians found it and buried it with reverence on an islet of the river, and afterwards carried it to the monastery of Saint Arsenius outside the city. The page prints no hymn and no synaxarion couplet for him.
