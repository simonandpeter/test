# Julian, companion of Modestus

saint.gr's calendar for 12 Φεβρουαρίου names the martyrs Julian and [Modestus](/saints/modestus-companion-of-julian) together, and the page behind that line prints no life at all: «Οι Άγιοι Μάρτυρες Ιουλιανός και Μόδεστος μαρτύρησαν στην Καρθαγένη. Δεν έχουμε περισσότερες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο των Αγίων» — they were martyred at Carthage; we have no further details of the saints' life.

So the pair, the city and the word martyr are the whole of what the Greek calendar keeps of them. No year, no emperor, no manner of death, no hymn, no relics and not even a distich are printed, and the page adds only its glosses on the names: that Ιουλιανός is from Ἰούλιος and means the glorious, like Julius Caesar, and that Μόδεστος is from the Latin modestus, the temperate and sober one. The same day keeps a second pair martyred at Carthage, Plotinus and Saturninus, on a page of their own; the calendar does not join the four.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/12/index.aspx) and [the page for the two of them](https://www.saint.gr/1239/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
