# Anthousa of Seleucia

Anthousa, a native of Seleucia in Syria, was the daughter of noble pagans. Learning the teaching of Christ she went, on the pretext of visiting her nurse, to [Athanasius](/saints/athanasius-of-tarsus), bishop of Tarsus, and received baptism from him — angels appeared at her baptism, the Prologue says. Her parents were angry that she had become a Christian, and taking the monastic tonsure from Athanasius she withdrew into the desert, where she spent twenty-three years in ascetic labours and died in peace at the end of the third century, kneeling in prayer on a stone under which she asked to be buried. The martyrs [Charisimus](/saints/charisimus-servant-of-anthousa) and [Neophytus](/saints/neophytus-servant-of-anthousa), baptised with her, were her servants and died for Christ.
