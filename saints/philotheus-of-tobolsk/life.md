# Philotheus of Tobolsk

saint.gr's calendar for 31 Μαΐου names him and then says it has nothing to tell. Its line is «Άγιος Φιλόθεος Μητροπολίτης Τομπόλσκ», the holy Philotheus, metropolitan of Tobolsk, its heading dates his death to 1727, and the whole of its body is one sentence: we have no details of the life of this Russian saint.

What the page does beside that is place him and point elsewhere. It files him under the synaxis of all the saints who shone in Siberia, and it names the Orthodox Church in America's Feasts and Saints as a source where more would be found. Neither of those was read for this folder, and nothing has been added here from anywhere else: the see, the rank and the year of death are the whole of what is entered, because they are the whole of what was read.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 31 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/31/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4255/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
