# Paraskeva (Fedorova)

Her line in the calendar is three words long, and it is the whole of what is recorded here. The Сретенский monastery's calendar for 28 ноября ст. ст. prints «Мц. Параскевы (1938).» — the martyr Paraskeva, 1938 — and the name page behind that forename gives her the surname the line leaves out and nothing else: «Параскева (Федорова), мц.» She is a martyr and not a monastic martyr: the calendar gives her no monastic grade and no office, which is how it prints a laywoman.

So nothing is said here of where she was born, what she did, when or by whom she was arrested, or where she died. The calendar carries no life page for her, and the year 1938 is the only year it gives. She stands apart from the company of six the same day names, whose line closes a year earlier.

Her name page lists one other day, «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church. One row per church is the schema, so that day is recorded here and she does not render on it.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 28 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261128.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14439.html); read 7 October 2026.*
