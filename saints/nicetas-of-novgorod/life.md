# Nicetas of Novgorod

Nicetas came from Russia and was the brother in the flesh of the venerable Nikon, abbot of the Lavra of Kyiv, whom saint.gr says is honoured on 23 March. He became a monk in the monastery of the Caves at Kyiv, but the disobedience of his character led him to leave the common life and the fathers and to live as a solitary monk in a cell he made for himself. Nikon, who had not given his blessing to that withdrawal, saw what his brother had done with grief and waited in fear for the punishment of his disobedience.

The trial was not slow in coming. One day, while Nicetas was at prayer, the devil appeared before him transfigured as an angel of the Lord and told him to pray no more but to study the holy Scriptures, the work of prayer being taken over on his behalf by the false angel. He yielded to the temptation, supposing that by God's permission and on account of his ascetic labours this great gift had been given him. So he stopped praying and began to read — and study of the word of God does not happen without prayer, so his heart fell into listlessness and spiritual torpor, and he could not even finish his reading of the Scripture.

He began suddenly to prophesy only coming evils: thefts, crimes, wicked doings, all the things the devil, the father of lies and of malice, contrives. His fame spread everywhere and everyone marvelled at the exactness with which his words came true. The fathers of the monastery began to understand that he had fallen into delusion and been led by Satan onto the road of perdition, and they set to fervent prayer that his mind might be enlightened and he be saved. By the prayer of his fellow ascetics his mind was enlightened; he began to pray again and to live the true fruits of the monastic state, and God not only accepted his repentance but counted him worthy of the gift of wonderworking.

Because of the holiness of his life he was elected bishop of Novgorod in the year 1096. Having shepherded his flock worthily and in a manner pleasing to God, he fell asleep in peace in 1109, and his holy relic was buried in the church of the holy and righteous ancestors of God, Joachim and Anna, in Novgorod; the page says it lies incorrupt in the church of Saint Nicholas there.

The Russian calendar tells the same fall differently. In its reading the young monk of the Caves asked for the reclusion too early, the abbot warned him that the labour was premature, and he trusted his own strength and did not listen. The devil appeared as an angel and told him that, being now perfect, he need not pray but only read and teach others, and stood beside him pretending to pray in his place. So deceived, Nicetas outdid everyone in the knowledge of the books of the Old Testament, while of the Gospel he would neither speak nor hear; and the elders of the Caves came to him, prayed, and drove the demon away. Afterwards, with their blessing, he left the reclusion and lived in strict fasting and prayer, exercising himself above all in obedience and humility. The Russian life adds that he governed Novgorod thirteen years, that the city's fire was quenched and the rain brought down at his prayer, that he was glorified in 1558 under Tsar Ivan Vasilyevich, and that his relics rest at Novgorod in the church the Russian life names.
