# Marcian (11 July)

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life. Its whole notice of him is one sentence: the holy Marcian was martyred, having been put to death with a knife — «Ο Άγιος Μαρκιανός μαρτύρησε, αφού τον θανάτωσαν με μαχαίρι». The day's line is as bare: «Άγιος Μαρκιανός», with no country, no see, no century and no companion.

The one thing the page adds is the distich above the notice, which says the same thing in verse and nothing more: «Yπήρξε θήκη Mαρκιανέ σος φάρυγξ, Xωρούσα την μάχαιραν, ην κατεκρίθης» — your throat, Marcian, was a sheath, taking in the knife you were condemned to. There is no year, no emperor, no place, no hymn and no further sentence.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/11/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2122/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
