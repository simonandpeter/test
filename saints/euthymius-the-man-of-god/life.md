# Euthymius the Man of God

Euthymius was a university professor and a considerable man of learning from Orthodox Georgia, saint.gr says, with a great spiritual and scholarly contribution — a real fighter in the service of scholarship, even while he was in exile — above all in history and ethnography; among other things he was one of the founders of the university of Tbilisi.

He came into conflict with the atheist regime of the USSR over his support for the autocephaly of the Church of Georgia, and was exiled to France in 1921. There his wife Nino died of starvation in 1931, and he himself was brought to the edge of it. Ten years after his own death his body was found incorrupt, and his clothes with it.

On 17 October 2002 the Orthodox Church of Georgia recognised him as a saint under the name the Man of God. He is kept on 3 January, the day of his birth — which the page itself calls a strange thing, since as a rule the saints are honoured on the day of their falling asleep.
