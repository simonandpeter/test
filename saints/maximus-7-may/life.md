# Maximus (7 May)

saint.gr says he lived in the hard but glorious years of the persecutions the Church suffered from the idolaters, and dates him no more closely than that. A genuine believer, the page says, he took his cross upon his shoulders, preached Christ everywhere and brought numerous idolaters to him; and for that he was hunted and ill-used, and passed his life in continual danger and continual battle.

At the last he fell, stoned by fanatical idolaters, and so put on the unfading crown of martyrdom. The couplet above the notice keeps to the stones: having mounted upon them, great-minded Maximus went up rejoicing into the breadth of heaven. Beyond that the page names no country, no century, no emperor, no city and no judge, and prints no relics and no hymn; the day is the only thing that tells him from the other saints of his name.
