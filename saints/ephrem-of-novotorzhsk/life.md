# Ephrem of Novotorzhsk

saint.gr's calendar for 28 January names Ephrem of Russia, and his own page calls him Ephrem of Novotorzhsk. What it says of him is three things: that he was born in Russia; that he was tonsured a monk at the monastery of Saints Boris and Gleb of Torzhok; and that, having lived in a way pleasing to God, he fell asleep in peace in the year 1053. The page also records that a portion of his relic is at the Lavra of Saint Alexander Nevsky in Saint Petersburg.

Nothing else is on the page: no year of birth, no parentage, no account of what he did at Torzhok or of his repose, and no troparion or kontakion. The same calendar keeps two other Ephrems on this day — the Syrian, and the one from Kiev — and this is neither of them. The Russian calendar is where this entry could be finished, and it has not been read.

The Russian calendar keeps him on the same menologion day as «Прп. Ефрема Новоторжского (1053)», with the same year, and it gives him the service the Greek page lacked: two troparia and a kontakion, all three of them about the shrine of his relics and the healings given there, the last calling him a star of Russia shown forth by God.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/28/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3544/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 28 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260128.html), read 3 October 2026.*
