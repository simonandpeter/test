# Lykarion of Hermoupolis

saint.gr says he came from Hermoupolis in Egypt. He was arrested for confessing Christ and shut up in a foul prison. After some days they brought him out, tore him with irons, and then nailed him upon a cross; afterwards they burned his body with red-hot iron rods, and then threw him into a lighted furnace, where the saint stayed three days and came out unharmed by the grace of God.

Then they gave him poisons, from which again he was unharmed, so that the idolater who had made them believed in Christ and was beheaded. After that they cut the martyr's sinews and threw him into a cauldron of boiling water; then they flayed his head, and at the last they beheaded him, and so he took the unfading crown of martyrdom. The page's couplet says that Lykarion came to the sword without a sound, like a lamb before the shearer.
