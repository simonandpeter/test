# Methodius of Nivritos

saint.gr's calendar for 25 Ιουνίου keeps him as an ascetic of Crete. The page says he was born at Rethymno under the Arab domination of the island, that the exact place of his birth is not known, and that the monastery of his tonsure is unknown too — perhaps, it says, the monastery of Preveli. What is known, it adds, is the name of his elder: Euthymius, who lived as an ascetic in the mountains of Nivritos, spelt also Nyvritos and, in the older writing, Nivritos.

There in Nivritos Methodius too lived as an ascetic, joining meekness, patience and self-restraint to many other virtues, and living the purely monastic life, or more exactly the anchoretic one. The page sets him in the high mountains of Nivritos, which run out towards the south-eastern slopes of Psiloritis, and says he was full of days when he departed to the Lord, as he had longed to do from a child. His relic is kept at the monastery of Epanosifi.

The page is careful about what it does not know. It calls it bold even to report that Methodius had met [Nicholas Kourtaliotis](/saints/nicholas-kourtaliotis), who lived as an ascetic in a neighbouring district; it records that some hold Nicholas to have lived in a cave near the monastery of Saint Nicholas by the village of Zaros; and it says that some confuse the two saints and present them as one and the same person, and that all of this needs much research. A church in Nivritos stands under the name of Methodius, and the hymnographer of his service is unknown.
