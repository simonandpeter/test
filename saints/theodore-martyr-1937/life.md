# Theodore

Fyodor Dmitrievich Zakharov was born on 13 April 1874 in the settlement of Uglitsky in the Chelyabinsk district of Orenburg province, to a family of hereditary Cossacks. In 1919 he was arrested by White officers and let go the next day; until 1920 he lived in Vladivostok, and in 1920 was arrested at Verkhneudinsk for crossing from Vladivostok through the White zone, and released after thirty-two days.

In 1937 he was arrested for the last time, in the group case of Archbishop Alexis (Orlov) and others at Chimkent. The file holds a note that he led a wandering life, went about Kazakhstan spreading anti-Soviet slander of the leaders of party and government, and acted as the direct executant of the counter-revolutionary work of the metropolitan of Tashkent and the local bishops — the liaison between separate groups of churchmen and their leaders; the indictment of 23 August in case no. 723, against Metropolitans Kirill (Smirnov) and Joseph (Petrovykh), Archbishop Alexis and others, called him the liaison between the centre and its cells in Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan and Kirghizia, an agitator among the people, and a teacher of the Law of God. He answered that he belonged to no counter-revolutionary organisation and had done no such work. In August the South Kazakhstan troika sentenced him to death, and on 27 August 1937 he was shot in the Lisya Balka at Chimkent. He was glorified at the Council of 2000. The Russian calendar keeps this company on 14 August (Julian): the presbyters [Vladimir](/saints/vladimir-presbyter-martyr-1937) and [Nicholas](/saints/nicholas-presbyter-martyr-1937-14-august), the monk-martyr [Eleutherius](/saints/eleutherius-monk-martyr-1937), the abbess [Eve](/saints/eve-the-abbess-1937), the nun [Eudocia](/saints/eudocia-nun-martyr-1937) and the martyr [Theodore](/saints/theodore-martyr-1937), all shot at Chimkent on 27 August 1937. The Sretensky calendar prints no life for any of them, and the year beside their names there, 1927, is a slip; these lives follow azbyka.ru.
