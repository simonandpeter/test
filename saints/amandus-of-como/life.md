# Amandus of Como

saint.gr's whole account of him is one sentence: «Ο Άγιος Αμάνδος ήταν Επίσκοπος της πόλεως Κόμο της Ιταλίας και κοιμήθηκε με ειρήνη το έτος 440 μ.Χ.» — Amandus was bishop of the city of Como in Italy and fell asleep in peace in the year 440. The heading of the page prints «(; - 440)», and the day's line is shorter still: «Άγιος Αμάνδος», with no see and no epithet.

That is all the calendar has. It gives no birth year and no country he came from, names no predecessor and no successor in the see, tells nothing of his episcopate, lists no relic, and prints neither couplet nor hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/230/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
