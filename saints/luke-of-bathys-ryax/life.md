# Luke, Abbot of Bathys Ryax

Luke, from the province of the Lycaonians, became a monk and then abbot — the third since the foundation — of the monastery of Bathys Ryax at Triglia, dedicated to the name of Christ the Saviour, whose first abbot and founder was Basil, kept on 1 July, and whose second was Ignatius, kept on 27 September. A type of every virtue, the verse says, he stood apart from every earthly attachment. The Greek calendar keeps him on 7 September with [Peter](/saints/peter-of-bathys-ryax).

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 7 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/943/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
