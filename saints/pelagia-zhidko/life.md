# Pelagia (Zhidko)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps her in a single line for 13 июня ст. ст. — «Мц. Пелагии (1944).» — a martyr who died in 1944. The name link behind that line, name page 9904, gives the calendar's own fuller form, «Пелагия (Жидко), инокиня», which adds the surname the line leaves out and makes her a nun.

That name page heads itself «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 13 июня», two days, the first of them the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia rather than a day of her own. The calendar prints no life page for her and no hymn under her name, and none is given here: those are the facts it records, and the whole of them.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 13 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260613.html) and [her name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9904.html); read 9 October 2026.*
