# Nestor (26 April)

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Απριλίου names him and gives no life. Its whole notice of him is one sentence — «Ο Όσιος Νέστωρ αφού εγκατέλειψε τους γονείς του, έγινε μοναχός και απεβίωσε ειρηνικά» — that the venerable Nestor, having left his parents, became a monk and died in peace. Nothing else is printed: no century, no country, no monastery, and no name for the parents he left behind him.

The gloss the page prints above that sentence, under the heading ΝΕΣΤΩΡ, is about the name and not about the saint: it makes Nestor the son of Neleus and Chloris, king of Pylos, who took part in the Argonautic expedition and in the war at Troy. That is the Nestor of Homer, printed as an etymology in the way saint.gr glosses many names, and nothing in it belongs to this monk.

His couplet says «Τοὺς οὐρανοὺς ἱδρῶσι Νέστωρ ἐπρίω, / Δι' οὓς φύσιν τύραννον ἠρνήσω θέσει» — Nestor bought the heavens with his sweats, and for their sake denied nature the tyrant by a choosing of his own. The feast type the page gives is the plain fixed one, «Σταθερή. Εορτάζει στις 26 Απριλίου εκάστου έτους».

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/335/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
