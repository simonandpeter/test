# Bessarion of Lainici

The life doxologia.ro prints begins with his schooling: the primary classes in the village where he was born, and then the school for church singers at Roman between 1900 and 1904. Together with his good friend Mihai Bâtâcă, the future archimandrite Maxim Bâtâcă, he was a disciple of Bishop Gherasim Safirin, and while they studied the two of them served as singers in the cathedral at Roman. The page does not name his birthplace, his family or the year he was born.

The two brothers in Christ entered as novices the lavra of the monastery of Frăsinei, in the time of the archimandrite Porfirie Bucurescu as superior. In 1908 they laid the foundations of psaltic chant at Frăsinei. In 1910 the superior Porfirie tonsured him with the name Bessarion; and being of a gentle and humble nature he began a still harder ascetic labour. He chanted at the stall out of the depth of the heart, so that many wept to hear his voice.

Seeing his spiritual ascent and his life full of virtues, Porfirie proposed him for ordination in 1922. In the summer of 1929 Bartholomew Stănescu, Bishop of Râmnic and New Severin, sent him as superior to the monastery of Lainici. There he began little by little to establish the hesychast rule he had found at Frăsinei, and after ten years as superior he had gathered a brotherhood of more than twenty monks and brothers.

During the works at Bumbești–Livezeni, opened by the communist regime in 1949, father Toia — as the page calls him — organised a hospital for those crippled in the works, and was always near them. According to the Securitate he was in this way countering the Marxist-Leninist campaign of education being carried on in the region; more than that, he organised pilgrimages together with the miners of the Jiu valley, and stubbornly refused to let the cells be occupied by the Securitate troops who were fighting the partisans in the mountains. Those acts drew the attention of the organs of repression.

He was ill-treated by the Securitate and could not be saved even in hospital, so that on 8 November 1951 he passed to eternal life. The calendar keeps him on 10 noiembrie. His page carries no Tropar text — the Tropar tab holds only a recording of it sung by a priest — so the folder has no hymn.
