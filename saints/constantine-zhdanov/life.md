# Constantine (Zhdanov)

The Сретенский monastery's calendar prints him third of the four entries it puts on the Julian 16 April, and the whole of the entry is one line: «Сщмч. Константина пресвитера (1919).» — the hieromartyr Constantine, a presbyter, and the year 1919. There is no life under his name on the day page and none behind it. The name link carries a fuller form than the line does, «Константин (Жданов), иерей, сщмч.», with a surname the line leaves out altogether and the word иерей, a priest, for the grade he held.

That is the whole of what the source gives, and the page behind the link gives less than most: no life, no icon, no parish, no year or place of birth, no diocese, no arrest, no sentence and no word of his own. The bracketed year is the year of death and the grade is the only thing said about his state in life. It is written here as the calendar has it rather than filled out from elsewhere, and a reader who wants more of him will have to go to a source this folder has not read.

His second day on that page is 26 января, marked as the new martyrs', which is the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia — a commemoration of a whole company rather than a second day of his own, so it is not recorded here as a feast. Of the two hymns the day prints under «Молитвы», one is the kontakion of the three sisters the day is headed for and the other a troparion of an icon of the Mother of God; there is nothing under his name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 16 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260416.html), which gives him one line and no life, and [the calendar's page for his name](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/15015.html); read 7 October 2026.*
