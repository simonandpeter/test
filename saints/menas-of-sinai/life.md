# Menas of Sinai

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 5 января old style, and its whole life of him is one sentence: the venerable Menas struggled fifty years in the monastery of Sinai and died in peace in the second half of the sixth century. The day's line is shorter still — «Прп. Мины (VI)» — and it is the name link behind it that adds the mountain, «Мина Синайский».

Nothing else is printed there. The calendar gives no birthplace, no year, no office in the monastery and no account of what those fifty years held, and it prints no hymn under his name; the hymns on that day are the eve of Theophany's. What is recorded here is therefore what the calendar records: half a century at Sinai, and a peaceful end.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 5 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260105.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life160.htm); read 3 October 2026.*
