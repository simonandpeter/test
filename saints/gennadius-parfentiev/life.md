# Gennadius (Parfentiev)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him for 28 октября ст. ст. in four words — «Прмч. Геннадия (1919)» — and the name link behind the line adds the surname the line leaves out: «Геннадий (Парфентьев), прмч.», one of the new martyrs of the Russian church. The abbreviation is venerable-martyr, which says that he was a monk and that he was killed, and it is the only thing the calendar says about his manner of life. The link gives him no grade, so this folder records none. There is no life page behind the line, and his name page carries no text, no icon and no hymn.

The year is 1919, the second of the years in which the Russian monastics and clergy were killed in numbers, and it is the whole of what is dated. His name page lists a second day, 26 января, the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, on which he is commemorated with the rest of that company rather than alone. The second Russian calendar, azbyka.ru, which has given this corpus a full life and its archival references for others of these men, refused the request on the day he was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 28 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261028.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/15035.html); read 4 October 2026.*
