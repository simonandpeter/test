# Maris of Cyrus

Maris came from the city of Cyrus. saint.gr says he was handsome in appearance and had a fine voice, that he chanted in the churches, and that he lived in self-restraint and modesty. When he resolved to leave the things of the world behind, he came to a village called Omirou, or Nitis, and built himself a house there, and in that house he remained thirty-seven whole years in fasting and prayer. He lived ninety years in all, and died in peace in the fourth century.

The page says he loved simplicity and wholly detested every kind of cunning, and that he loved poverty and the owning of nothing. So through the ninety years of his life he wore clothing made of goat's hair, and met his need of food with bread and a little salt.

Because he had been many years in that deserted place, he came to long to see the Divine Liturgy celebrated, and the longing was satisfied. A priest visited him at that very time and performed the holy rite, using the hands of deacons in place of an altar table. His heart, the page says, was filled with a joy and gladness beyond describing, and he felt that he was looking on the heavenly throne of Christ himself. saint.gr says his life was written by Theodoret of Cyrus, and credits the Apostoliki Diakonia of the Church of Greece.
