# John of Nitria

saint.gr's calendar for 20 Νοεμβρίου names «Όσιος Ιωάννης» and tells three things about him and no more. He was an ascetic on the mountain of Nitria. Having lived with holiness of life, he died in peace. And then the page refuses the one long source there is: «Τα περί της ζωής του, που αναφέρονται στο Λαυσαϊκό δεν ανταποκρίνονται στην πραγματικότητα» — what is reported of his life in the Lausiac History does not correspond to reality.

So the calendar keeps him and will not tell his story, and this entry says only what the calendar says. It gives him no city beyond the mountain, no year of birth or of death, no companions and no hymn, prints no relics, and sets no couplet over him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 20 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/20/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3049/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
