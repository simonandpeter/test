# Dionysius of Olympus

doxologia.ro keeps Dionysius on 23 ianuarie, and the life it prints for him is three sentences long. He was born towards the end of the fifteenth century at Sklataina, in the region of Karditsa in Greece. From childhood he was used to prayer and to the reading of spiritual books, and after his parents had passed to eternal life he took the monastic way. The site's own note on him adds that he was one of the hesychast saints who lived in the sixteenth century, and that he was not only a notable representative of the hesychast life but a missionary and a man of letters.

That is the whole of it. The page gives no account of where he was tonsured, nothing of the Holy Mountain or of the mountain his name is bound to, nothing of his death, and no year at either end of his life — only the century for each. It prints no troparion and no kontakion for him.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 23 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/23-ianuarie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-cuvios-dionisie-din-olimp); read 19 September 2026.*
