# Alexius (Medvedkov)

saint.gr keeps Alexius Medvedkov among the Russian saints who ended their lives in France, a synaxis the Ecumenical Patriarchate appointed for 20 July; and of the five it names he is the one the page sets apart, since he fell asleep before the war. Born in 1867, he was a priest in a small village in the region of Saint Petersburg, but in 1918, with the atheist regime established, he was arrested, and afterwards escaped to Estonia, where he worked as a coal miner.

In 1930 he left for France, where Metropolitan Eulogius Gueorguievskii appointed him parish priest to the Russian community of Ugine. There he served his people with self-denial, and fell asleep, ill with cancer, in 1934. He was a true hesychast, the page says, a deep worker of prayer, meek and almost shy, who did not defend himself even when parties among his parishioners moved slanderously against him, charging that he was not as he ought to be because he wore poor vestments and the like — in truth, it says, because they could not win him over to their political groups.

His body, with his vestments, was found incorrupt when the cemetery of Ugine was renovated in 1956. That is the whole of the page's notice; it prints no hymn for him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 20 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/20/index.aspx) and [the synaxis](https://www.saint.gr/4381/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
