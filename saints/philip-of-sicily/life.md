# Philip of Sicily

saint.gr's calendar for 12 May names him and gives him no life. What its page says is this: the holy hieromartyr Philip the Argyrius, as the Roman Martyrology has him, was a presbyter and came from Sicily. He is unknown, the page adds, to the compilers of the synaxaria and to the Menaia; his memory is met in the Sinai Codex 647 and in Codex Δ. α. I.Χ. of the monastery of Cryptoferrata at Rome, where his service is also found.

That is the whole of the entry. It gives no year, no emperor, no manner of death and no hymn, and it does not explain the epithet «ο Αργύριος» that the calendar line hangs on him. This folder records what the Greek calendar keeps on 12 May and no more than that.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1554/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
