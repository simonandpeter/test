# Berius

saint.gr's calendar for 29 Ιουλίου names two martyrs in one line, «Άγιοι Βενιαμίν και Βήριος» — the holy Benjamin and Berius — and the page behind it gives them no life. Its whole notice is two sentences: «Η σύναξη τους γινόταν κοντά στα παλάτια του «Έβδομου». Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο τους» — their synaxis was kept near the palaces of the Hebdomon; we have no other details of their life.

So what the Greek calendar keeps of Berius is a name, a companion and a place of commemoration outside the City, and nothing else at all: no country, no century, no emperor, no manner of death and no hymn. His companion [Benjamin (29 July)](/saints/benjamin-the-martyr-29-july) the corpus already keeps from the Romanian calendar of the same day, which names him alone and gives him no life either. Berius has a folder here because saint.gr's line names him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 29 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/29/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/736/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
