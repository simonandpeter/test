# The Unknown Maiden of Alexandria

The Ohrid Prologue's third entry for this day is a person with no name. It calls her an unknown maiden of a rich house in Alexandria, and gives her a father and a mother who went two different ways. Her father was a good man who suffered much and made a bad end; her mother was a wicked woman who lived well, died in peace, and was buried with honour. The maiden was left in doubt which of the two she ought to take for her example.

A vision settled it. It showed her the state of her father and of her mother in the other world: she saw her father in the Kingdom of God, and her mother in darkness and torment. That decided her to give the whole of her life to God and, like her father, to hold to the law of God whatever contrariness and hardship she might have to bear for it. With God's help she held it to the end, the Prologue says, and was counted worthy of the heavenly kingdom, where she met her God-loving father again.

That is the whole of the entry. It gives her no name, no year, no century, no parentage beyond the two examples set before her, and no hymn, and it is the only source read for her.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 3. март ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-03-16&prolog=1); read 4 October 2026.*
