# Isidora (17 October)

saint.gr's calendar for 17 October keeps two women on one line — «Αγίες Ισιδώρα και Νεοφύτη» — and Isidora is one of them. The page behind the line gives each of them a heading of her own, hers being «Αγια Ισιδωρα», and then prints one sentence for both, which is the whole of what the calendar has.

That sentence is also a warning. These two — the holy Isidora and [Neophyte](/saints/neophyte-17-october) — are unknown to the synaxarists, it says; they are mentioned in the Morsellian Synaxaristes, and it refers the reader to Gedeon's *Byzantine Eortologion*, page 45, first column. So the calendar does not say when she lived, or where, or whether she was a martyr or a nun; it says that a synaxaristes names her on this day, and it says which, and where that can be checked.

Of Isidora in particular, then, there is a name, a day and a reference. The corpus's isidora-of-tabennisi, the fool for Christ kept on 1 May, is another woman on another day. The «ΙΣΙΔΩΡΟΣ (ίσο δώρο, κοινώς Σίδερης)» line is saint.gr's note on the name. This folder holds that and nothing more: no year, no country, no office, no account of a life or a death, and no hymn — and it says plainly, as the calendar does, that the synaxarists do not know her.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 17 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/17/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2542/saint.aspx), which the two share; read 1 October 2026.*
