# Basil of Georgia

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives him two sentences: «Ο Όσιος Βασίλειος έζησε τον 11ο αιώνα μ.Χ. στη Γεωργία και ήταν υιός του βασιλέως Μπαγράτ Γ'. Κοιμήθηκε με ειρήνη.» — the venerable Basil lived in the eleventh century in Georgia and was the son of the king Bagrat III; he reposed in peace. The day's line calls him simply Basil out of Georgia.

That is the whole of it. The page gives no monastery, no year within the century, no place in Georgia and no hymn, and nothing more is written here than it prints.
