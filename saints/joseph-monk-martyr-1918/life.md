# Joseph (Baranov), Hieromonk, Monk-martyr (1918)

Joseph (Baranov), hieromonk, was born in 1861. He is known to have served at the metochion of the Yaroslavl Tolga monastery in the town of Rybinsk in Yaroslavl province, and to have acted as its manager. On 20 August 1918 he was taken from the metochion, and the next day was found killed on the river Cheremkha; the news that he had been shot by the godless power reached the Commission on the persecution of the Russian Church at the Holy Council from the members of the Yaroslavl diocesan council. He was canonised by the Council of Bishops of 2000, by decree of the Holy Synod of 17 July 2001, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 20 August (Julian).
