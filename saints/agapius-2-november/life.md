# Agapius (2 November)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 2 noiembrie reads «Sfântul Mucenic Agapie» — the holy martyr Agapius — and the page the calendar links for him carries neither a Viață tab nor a Tropar tab, so that the line, the date in the page head and the readings appointed for the day are the whole of what the source prints under his name.

Nothing else about him is there. His country is not named, no century is given, no persecution and no emperor, and the manner of his martyrdom is not told; the word «Mucenic» in the calendar's line is all the source says about how he died. He is not one of the five martyrs of Persia whom the same day keeps — Acindynus, Pegasius, Anempodistus, Aphthonius and Elpidephorus — whose shared life the calendar does print, and who are named on their own pages and in their own troparion; the page for Agapius stands apart from theirs and says nothing.

No other calendar has been read for him, and the corpus holds several other men of this name on other days, none of whom the source connects with him.

The Greek calendar keeps him with nine others on the same day, 2 November, and makes a soldier of him. saint.gr's line is «Άγιοι Εύδοξιος, Αγάπιος και άλλοι οκτώ μάρτυρες, Αττικός, Μαρίνος, Ωκεανός, Ευστράτιος, Καρτέριος, Νικοπολιτιανός, Στύραξ και Τωβίας», and each of the ten has a heading of his own, dated «(; - 315)». They were all soldiers in the city of Sebasteia, the notice says, in the years of the emperor Licinius; and in 315 they were examined by Auxanios, the governor of Sebasteia, by the duke Marcellus and by Marcus Agricolaus. They confessed their Christian faith with courage, were cruelly tortured in various ways, and at the end were thrown into the fire and burned alive, taking the unfading crown of martyrdom together. The epigram counts them: ten champions who endured the fire, and the ten came to the best of ends. The page gives them no further circumstance and prints no hymn.

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar keeps the same company one day later than the Greek, on 3 ноября ст. ст., in a single line of seven names and a phrase: «Мчч. Аттика, Агапия, Евдоксия, Катерия, Истукария, Пактовия, Никтополиона и дружины их (ок. 320).» His is the second of the seven, and the name link behind it gives «Агапий Севастийский», which is the first time any page read for this folder has put his place into his name and the first distinguishing mark he has had beyond his position in a list. Four of the seven are men this corpus already held from the Greek line of ten; five of that ten the Russian line does not print, and they are the «дружина», the company, it leaves unnamed. The calendar gives the company no life page and no hymn.

Two of the three names the corpus did not hold were made folders on this day, [Istucarius](/saints/istucarius-of-sebasteia) and [Pactobius](/saints/pactobius-of-sebasteia); the third was left unseated, as a question about whether it is a word the Greek list already prints under another spelling. The Russian year is «ок. 320» against the Greek's 315, so his death is recorded here as 315 or 320, with neither chosen.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 2 noiembrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/2-noiembrie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-agapie); read 25 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 2 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/02/index.aspx) and [the notice of the ten](https://www.saint.gr/1051/saint.aspx), read 1 October 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 3 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261103.html), read 4 October 2026.*
