# Theodotus of the monastery of Publius

saint.gr's calendar for 25 January names Theodotus, abbot of the monastery of Publius, and gives almost no life. The page says that this venerable man is mentioned by Theodoret of Cyrus, and that according to what Theodoret wrote, Theodotus became abbot of the monastery of [Publius](/saints/publius-of-zeugma) — the same Publius of Zeugma whom the Greek calendar keeps on this day — lived in holiness, and died in peace. That is the whole of what it prints of him.

No year is given, either for his birth or for his death, no birthplace, and no troparion or kontakion. What else the page carries is the etymology it gives for the name Theodotus, from Θεός and δοτός, the gift and the grace of God, and a line about two Greek kings of Bactria who bore the name. Both are notes on the name and not on the man.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 25 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/25/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1153/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
