# Matthew (Gusev)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him on 5 января old style and prints one line only: «Мч. Матфея (1938)» — the martyr Matthew, 1938. The name link behind that line carries the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line itself omits, «Матфей (Гусев), мч.»; and there the entry ends, for the calendar holds no life page for him and prints no hymn under his name.

So what is set down here is what the calendar sets down. A man named Matthew Gusev is kept on this day as a martyr, and the year given for his death is 1938. The calendar gives him no rank in the Church, so he is recorded simply as a martyr and not as a priest or a monk; of his birth, his home, his arrest and his end it says nothing, and nothing has been supplied here from any other source.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 5 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260105.html); read 3 October 2026.*
