# Michael (Viktorov)

His line in the calendar is six words long, and it is the whole of what is recorded here. The Сретенский monastery’s calendar for 29 марта ст. ст. prints «Св. Михаила , исповедника, пресвитера (1933)» — the holy Michael, confessor, presbyter, 1933 — and the name page behind that forename gives the surname the line leaves out: «Михаил (Викторов), протоиерей, исповедник»

The two pages agree about him, which is not always so on this calendar: both call him a confessor, and neither calls him a martyr. The calendar uses that word for a man who died in confinement rather than under sentence of death, and nothing here says which it was. The name page also makes him a protopriest, and the office recorded here is the plainer `Priest`, which is the word this site can render in all four of its languages.

So nothing is said of where he was born, where he served, when or by whom he was arrested, or where he died. There is no life page for him on the day and none on his name page, and 1933 is the only year the calendar gives. His name page adds one day to this one, the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church; one row per church is the schema, so that day is recorded on his Russian row and he does not render on it.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 29 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260329.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14241.html); read 8 October 2026.*
