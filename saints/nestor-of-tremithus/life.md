# Nestor of Tremithus

saint.gr keeps two bishops of Tremithus in Cyprus together on this day, and all it says of them it says of the pair. On the authority of the Laurentian Codex I 70, the page reports, [Arcadius](/saints/arcadius-of-tremithus) and Nestor flourished in the first Christian centuries; they struggled against the idols, and they baptised many idolaters as Christians before they died peacefully. The distich the page prints over them calls them two good shepherds of Tremithus, leaping in the fair meadow of Eden.

Of Nestor apart from his fellow bishop there is nothing at all. The page's one further line — that Arcadius may perhaps have written an encomium for the translation of the relics of the great-martyr George — belongs to the other man. So what stands recorded here is a name, a see, a shared struggle against the idols, many baptisms, and a peaceful end.

There is no year: the first Christian centuries is a phrase without bounds and not a date. There is no city of his birth, no predecessor or successor at Tremithus, no relic and no hymn. The name-gloss the site prints above the notice is a lexicon entry about Homer's king of Pylos and says nothing about the bishop.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3872/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
