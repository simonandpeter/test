# Nicholas the Presbyter (4 September, the second)

Nicholas Sretensky was born on 15 April 1867 in the village of Tolstikovo in the Bezhetsk district of Tver province, the son of the priest Alexis Ivanovich Sretensky. At ten his parents sent him to the Bezhetsk church school, and from there he went to the Tver seminary; after it he worked half a year as a teacher in a zemstvo school, then some months as a spiritual supervisor, and in 1890, at twenty-three, was ordained to the church of the Annunciation monastery at Bezhetsk, where — the Sretensky calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. He was shot in 1937, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 4 September (Julian) with the bishops Gregory and Sergius and their company.
