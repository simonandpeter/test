# Isidore of Cordoba

The Greek calendar keeps three martyrs together on 30 Απριλίου, Isidore, Elias and Paul, and saint.gr's whole notice of them is one sentence: «Οι Άγιοι Μάρτυρες Ισίδωρος, Ηλίας και Παύλος μαρτύρησαν στην Κορδούη της Ισπανίας» — the holy martyrs Isidore, Elias and Paul were martyred at Cordoba in Spain. Nothing else is printed of any of them: no century, no emperor, no judge, no manner of death, no kinship and no trade.

What stands above that sentence on the page is three glosses on the three names — that Ισίδωρος means an equal gift, that Ηλίας is a Hebrew word for one who has divine power, that Παύλος is the Latin paulus, the very small, and that besides the chief apostle of the name many hierarchs and rulers have borne it. None of that is about these men. The page carries no couplet for them, no hymn and no relic, and its feast type is the plain fixed one, «Σταθερή. Εορτάζει στις 30 Απριλίου εκάστου έτους».

saint.gr heads him «Άγιος Ισίδωρος ο Μάρτυρας», and the other two of the three are [Elias](/saints/elias-of-cordoba) and [Paul](/saints/paul-of-cordoba).

*After saint.gr's calendar for 30 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/30/index.aspx) and [the entry for the three](https://www.saint.gr/3997/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
