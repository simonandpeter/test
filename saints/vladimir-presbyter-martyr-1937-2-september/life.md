# Vladimir (Morinsky), Presbyter, Hieromartyr (1937)

Vladimir Alexandrovich Morinsky was born on 24 June 1883 in St Petersburg; his father sang in church choirs, and the boy, orphaned early, was given to be raised in a private church choir under patronage. In 1903 he moved with the choir to Moscow and sang in the Moscow churches; in 1912 he got a place as singing teacher at Klin in Moscow province, and for half a year was also precentor of the cathedral choir, then moved to the village of Spas-Kvashonki in the Kalyazin district of Tver province, where he taught singing in the school and led the church choir; in 1916 he was called up and served as a private in the 238th reserve — the calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. A precentor and singing teacher, he was ordained priest in 1934; arrested in 1935 for anti-Soviet agitation and sentenced to three years in the camps, he was arrested again in the Karaganda camp in 1937 in the case of Bishop [Damascene (Tsedrik)](/saints/damascene-of-starodub) for taking part in secret services and counter-revolutionary activity, did not admit guilt, and was sentenced by the troika to be shot and executed on 15 September 1937. He was numbered among the saints in 2000, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 2 September (Julian).
