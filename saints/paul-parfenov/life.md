# Paul (Parfenov)

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar for 3 ноября ст. ст. names him second of two on one line: «Сщмч. Николая и мч. Павла (1918).» The line gives him the rank word «мч.», martyr, with no grade at all, and no surname; the name link behind his forename supplies the calendar's own nominative with the surname and repeats the rank and nothing else, «Павел (Парфенов), мч.» He is the one layman of the two the line names, and the absence of a grade is all that is read into it.

That is everything the calendar records of him. He has no life page, no icon, no hymn, no place, no age and no parish anywhere on this calendar, and the name page behind his place on the line carries two days and no text at all. The name page behind his place on the line also lists «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. That is a synaxis and not a second feast, and he does not render on it. Nothing further is written here, and in particular nothing is reasoned from the surname.

No office is recorded, because the calendar gives him none, and no relation is written to the archpriest the line names before him: they share a line and a year, and the calendar prints nothing that says they died in one place or knew one another.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 3 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261103.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/10013.html); read 4 October 2026.*
