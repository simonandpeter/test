# Cleopas of Russia

saint.gr's calendar for 9 Μαρτίου names him «Όσιος Κλεόπας εκ Ρωσίας», the venerable Cleopas out of Russia, and heads him «(; - 1778)». The page behind that line holds one sentence about him: that the venerable Cleopas was a disciple of the venerable [Paisius Velichkovsky](/saints/paisius-of-neamt), whom the same site keeps on 15 Νοεμβρίου, and that he fell asleep in peace in the year 1778.

That is the whole of it. The page gives him no place in Russia, no monastery, no office and no account of his life, and prints no hymn and no relic; the year of his birth stands in its heading as a question mark. What is recorded here is the commemoration, the year of his repose, and the one thing the site thought worth saying of him, which is whose disciple he was.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 9 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/09/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1250/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
