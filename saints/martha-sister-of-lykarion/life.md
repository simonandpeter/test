# Martha, sister of Lykarion

Martha and [Mary](/saints/mary-sister-of-lykarion) were sisters by the same mother, and doxologia's life says that, burning with right faith and with love towards Christ, they were like the Martha and Mary of the Gospel; their brother was the child martyr Lykarion. They lived in the parts of Asia, in the times when the Church of Christ was persecuted by pagan emperors and magistrates who worshipped idols, and the two of them kept their virginity, serving Christ and wishing to die for him.

It happened once that the pagan governor passed by their house. Martha opened the door and cried out with her sister after him, «We are Christians.» The governor and his servants took no notice and went on, and they called out after him again, «We are Christians.» He answered them: «I spare you for your youth, for I do not wish to kill you.» Martha said to him: «O governor, a martyr's death is not death but life.» At that he grew very angry and ordered her to be seized; Mary came out and said that what her sister had spoken she confessed as well, and after the sisters the child Lykarion came out and cried that he too was a Christian.

The governor ordered the three of them to be hung upon crosses. Their mother stood by and said to them: «Be saved, my dear children, and receive the crown from Christ.» Mary, hanging on her cross, said: «Be thou saved also, our mother, with the fruit of thy womb, for thou hast brought us to Christ.» A soldier ran all three through with a sword as they hung, and they gave up their spirits into the hands of the Lord. The page gives no year and no hymn.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 8 februarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/8-februarie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfintei-mucenite-marta); read 19 September 2026.*
