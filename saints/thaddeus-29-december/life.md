# Thaddeus (29 December)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 29 decembrie reads «Sfântul Cuvios Tadeu», and the page the line links carries neither a life nor a troparion nor any other text about him: no country, no century, no office and no deed. The rank word the line gives him, Cuvios, is the one this calendar uses for a monastic saint, and that is the whole of what the source says.

He is kept here as the calendar keeps him, by his name, his rank and his day. The same calendar keeps the apostle Thaddeus of the Seventy on 26 mai, with the rank of an apostle, and Thaddeus of Stepantsminda on 7 mai, among the Syrian fathers of Saint John Zedazneli; the monastic Thaddeus of this day it names and no more.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 29 December, and gives the life the Romanian line did not. saint.gr calls him Thaddeus the Confessor, says he was a Scythian and a servant of [Theodore the Studite](/saints/theodore-the-studite), and that he became a monk in Theodore's monastery and was distinguished there for the strictness of his asceticism. Its distich makes the same point about his birth: «Eι και γένει βάρβαρος ην ο Θαδδαίος, / Αλλ' ασεβείς ήλεγχε συν παρρησία» — though Thaddeus was a barbarian by race, yet he rebuked the impious with boldness.

Once, when he was accompanying his abbot Theodore to the palace, he rebuked the iconoclast emperor before the senate for his impiety towards the holy icons. The page names that emperor Michael, whose reign it dates 820 to 829, and adds in a parenthesis that M. Galanos names Leo V instead. The emperor then tried to force him to tread on the icon of Christ; and the venerable one not only would not do it but called the emperor a paid tyrant and unclean. For that he was cruelly tortured and dragged by the feet through the streets of the City, and after three days he died.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 29 decembrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/29-decembrie) and [his own page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-tadeu), which carries no life; read 28 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 29 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/29/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1065/saint.aspx), read 2 October 2026.*
