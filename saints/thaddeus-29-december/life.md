# Thaddeus (29 December)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 29 decembrie reads «Sfântul Cuvios Tadeu», and the page the line links carries neither a life nor a troparion nor any other text about him: no country, no century, no office and no deed. The rank word the line gives him, Cuvios, is the one this calendar uses for a monastic saint, and that is the whole of what the source says.

He is kept here as the calendar keeps him, by his name, his rank and his day. The same calendar keeps the apostle Thaddeus of the Seventy on 26 mai, with the rank of an apostle, and Thaddeus of Stepantsminda on 7 mai, among the Syrian fathers of Saint John Zedazneli; the monastic Thaddeus of this day it names and no more.
