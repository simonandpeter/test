# Alexis (Skorobogatov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and carries no life. Its line for the twenty-third of March, old style, reads «Сщмч. Василия , Стефана пресвитеров, прмч. Илии , прмц. Анастасии , Варвары , мч. Алексия (1938).», and the name page behind «Алексия» is headed «Алексий (Скоробогатов), мч.», which gives the surname the line leaves out. That page lists the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church on «26 января» and this twenty-third of March.

Nothing else is said of him on either page: no place, no diocese, no parish, no year of birth and no account of how he died. The 1938 the line closes with is the whole of his chronology. The second source for the Russian new martyrs, azbyka.ru, was not read for him.

He is the last of the six the line of 1938 names and the only one of them the calendar gives no rank in the Church at all: «мч.» and nothing else, so he is recorded as a layman, with no `office`.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 23 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260323.html); read 8 October 2026.*
