# Euplus (30 May)

saint.gr's calendar for 30 Μαΐου names him and gives one sentence and a distich. He was martyred, the page says, after they put him to death wrapped up inside an ox-hide and left him exposed to the burning sun. The distich above it turns on the same thing: they burn Euplus, rolled in a hide, in the sun — they who do not see the light of the sun of the mind.

That is the whole of the source. It gives no birthplace, no century, no year, no judge and no emperor; it names no companion; it records no wonder and prints no service. What it does do is keep him apart from the better-known Euplus the deacon of Catania, whom the same calendar holds on 11 August with the title of great-martyr, and whose death was by the sword and not in a hide.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 30 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/30/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/427/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
