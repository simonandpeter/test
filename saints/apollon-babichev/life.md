# Apollon (Babichev)

Apollon Xenophontovich Babichev was born on 30 March 1874 at Kaluga. He finished the town school and served as psalmist in a city church. The authorities arrested him on 16 October 1937, and from the first he took a firm line: he insisted that his answers be written down exactly and signed under each one.

Told that he was charged with counter-revolutionary activity and required to speak to the point, he said he had carried on none. Told that his denials were pointless and that the investigation had enough to convict him of activity carried on together with churchmen, he said again that he had carried on none and did not admit the guilt. Told that he was known to be hostile to the Soviet order and closely tied to an active counter-revolutionary element among churchmen, he said he did not consider himself hostile and had done no anti-Soviet work. Asked to name people hostile to the Soviet power, he said he knew none — and said it again when he was asked a second time.

Told he had been among a group close to the archbishop [Augustine (Belyaev)](/saints/augustine-belyaev), working on the minds of believers in an anti-Soviet spirit, he said he had held no anti-Soviet conversations; that his tie with churchmen was the church service and nothing else. At the last he was read extracts from the evidence of men who had admitted belonging to a monarchist church organisation and had named him as a member, and asked whether he now admitted his guilt. He said no.

On 19 November 1937 an NKVD troika sentenced him with the archbishop, the archimandrite [Joannicius (Dmitriev)](/saints/joannicius-dmitriev), the archpriest [John (Speransky)](/saints/john-speransky), the psalmist [Alexis (Gorbachev)](/saints/alexis-gorbachev) and [Michael (Arefyev)](/saints/michael-arefyev) of a church council, to be shot; they were shot on 23 November 1937 and buried in one unmarked grave. 10 ноября ст. ст. is that civil day. He was glorified in August 2000 by the jubilee council of bishops.
