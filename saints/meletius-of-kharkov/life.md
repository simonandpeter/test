# Meletius of Kharkov

The venerable Meletius, in the world Michael Ivanovich Leontovich, was born on 6 November 1784 in Russia. He studied at the theological academy of Saint Petersburg and became a monk in the year 1820, at the Bratsk monastery of Kiev. On 19 October 1826 he was elected bishop of Chigirinsk of Kiev, and he was distinguished for his ascetic life and his charity.

He fell asleep in peace and was buried at the monastery of the Panagia of Kharkov. In the year 1948 his honourable relic was carried into the cathedral church of the Theophany at Kharkov. The Church honours his memory also on 28 February, saint.gr adds, and its heading gives his years as «(1784 - 1840)»; the day's line names him for Kharkov, where he was buried, rather than for Chigirinsk, the see it says he was elected to.
