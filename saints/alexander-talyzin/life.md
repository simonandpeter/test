# Alexander (Talyzin)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him in a single line for 7 февраля ст. ст. — «Сщмч. Александра пресвитера (1938).» — a hieromartyr and a priest who died in 1938. The name link behind the line gives the calendar's own nominative, «Александр (Талызин), иерей», which adds the surname the line leaves out. The calendar prints no life page for him, and none is given here: the line and the name link are the whole of what it records.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 7 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260207.html); read 3 October 2026.*
