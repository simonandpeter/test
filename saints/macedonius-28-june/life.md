# Macedonius (28 June)

saint.gr's calendar for 28 Ιουνίου names him and gives him one sentence. Its line reads «Άγιος Μακεδόνιος», and the page behind that line says only that the holy Macedonius was martyred after they had cut off the fingers of his hands and of his feet. No country is named, no century, no persecutor, no year and no relics, and the page prints no hymn.

The couplet of the synaxarion standing above it says the same thing in verse: «Mακεδονίου Mάρτυρος τους δακτύλους, Tέμνουσιν ανδρών δάκτυλοι μιαιφόνοι» — the blood-stained fingers of men cut off the fingers of Macedonius the martyr. That is the whole of the entry, and the day in the name here is the day the calendar keeps him on and not an epithet of his own.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/28/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1911/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
