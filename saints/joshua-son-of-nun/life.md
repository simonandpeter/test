# Joshua the Son of Nun

Joshua, son of Nun, of the tribe of Ephraim, was the leader of the people of Israel after the death of the prophet Moses; he had been called Hoshea, the Greek synaxarion says, and Moses sent him to spy out the land of Canaan. He conquered the Promised Land and brought the Hebrew people into it, and through him the Lord showed great wonders: the people crossed the Jordan as on dry ground; the Archangel Michael appeared to him; the walls of Jericho fell of themselves after the Ark of the Covenant had been carried round the city for seven days; and in battle, at God’s command, he stopped the sun and lengthened the day until the victory was won. When the war was over he divided the land among the twelve tribes, governed them — twenty-seven years, the Greek says — and died at a hundred and ten, around 1440 BC; of the hundreds of thousands who came out of Egypt, the Prologue notes, only he and Caleb entered the land.
