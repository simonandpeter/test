# Martin (10 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 10 Νοεμβρίου names the venerable Martin the bishop and says it has no details of his life. The rank in the line is the whole of what is stated of him: a bishop, of no named see, in no named country or century, with no year of birth or death, no account of his end and no hymn.

The page does take care to say who he is not. A note under the entry warns that the Martin kept on this day is not the same as the venerable Martin, bishop of Tarakine, whom the same calendar keeps two days later on 12 November. Its couplet for him plays the shortness of a life against the length of the course it ran: «Βίου διαστάς, οὗ βραχύς τις ὁ δρόμος, / Τὸν πρὸς πόλον Μαρτῖνος ἔδραμε δρόμον» — parting from a life whose course is short, Martin ran the course towards heaven.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/10/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2976/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
