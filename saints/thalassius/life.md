# Thalassius

doxologia.ro's calendar names him for 22 februarie, and the life it prints under his own name gives him a single sentence before it turns to his disciple: Thalassius made himself a solitary's dwelling on a certain mountain, and surpassed all men in the purity of his life and in the humility of his mind. The page gives no country, no years, no account of his death, and no hymn.

What it does record is what came of him. Limnaeus, drawn while still very young to the solitary life, went to Thalassius and learned from him how a hermit lives; from there he went on to the ever-blessed Maron, and in zeal for that man's life resolved to live under no roof at all. The rest of that account belongs to [Limnaeus](/saints/limnaeus), whose page on doxologia prints these same words.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and on one page with [Limnaeus](/saints/limnaeus), and rests the two of them on Theodoret of Cyrrhus and on the Synaxarion of Constantinople. saint.gr sets Thalassius's hermitage on a small mountain of a village of Cyrrhus called Tillimas, where he prayed, studied and worked a small fertile plot of ground, and from where he would go down to the nearby villages and teach the word of God. Limnaeus, who was the younger of the two, heard of him and came to him, and took from that good teacher the pattern of a genuine and true monastic life; it was only after Thalassius's repose that he went off to [Maron](/saints/maron-the-hermit), whom the site keeps on 14 February. The distich over the pair plays on their names — a harbour bears Limnaeus and Thalassius, men who escaped this life as one escapes the sea.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 22 februarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/22-februarie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-cuvios-talasie); read 19 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 22 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/22/index.aspx) and [the page for the two of them](https://www.saint.gr/3677/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026.*
