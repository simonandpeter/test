# Thalassius

doxologia.ro's calendar names him for 22 februarie, and the life it prints under his own name gives him a single sentence before it turns to his disciple: Thalassius made himself a solitary's dwelling on a certain mountain, and surpassed all men in the purity of his life and in the humility of his mind. The page gives no country, no years, no account of his death, and no hymn.

What it does record is what came of him. Limnaeus, drawn while still very young to the solitary life, went to Thalassius and learned from him how a hermit lives; from there he went on to the ever-blessed Maron, and in zeal for that man's life resolved to live under no roof at all. The rest of that account belongs to [Limnaeus](/saints/limnaeus), whose page on doxologia prints these same words.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 22 februarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/22-februarie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-cuvios-talasie); read 19 September 2026.*
