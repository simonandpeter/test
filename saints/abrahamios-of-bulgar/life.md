# Abrahamios of Bulgar

saint.gr says the holy new martyr Abrahamios came of Bulgaria and lived in Russia. He was a Muslim at first; when he heard the preaching of the Gospel he embraced the Orthodox faith, and he was a generous man and merciful to the poor. In the city of Bolgar, on the lower reaches of the river Volga, he began to teach his own countrymen about the true God.

For that they seized him and tried to force him to deny Christ. He stayed firm, the page says, in the confession of the piety he had received; and so in the year 1229 they cut him in pieces and then struck off his honourable head. The Russian Christians who lived in the city buried his holy relic in the Christian cemetery.

The translation of the relics followed on 6 March 1230, at the hands of the grand prince of Vladimir, George, whose own day the page gives as 4 February; he laid them in the church of the Dormition of the Mother of God at the Knyatzinin monastery. That is the whole of the page: no year of birth, no hymn, and not even the distich the site usually prints.
