# Nicephorus (13 May)

saint.gr's calendar for 13 May names him and gives him almost nothing. It is unknown, the page says, in what age the venerable Nicephorus lived. The short notice on him in the Lavriotic Codex I 70, folio 239, says simply that on account of his virtuous and God-pleasing manner of life he was made abbot of the populous monastery of Ephapsis, and that he fell asleep in peace. Some synaxaria, the page adds, commemorate with him a venerable Agapios, of whom it says nothing further.

The couplet above the notice is the only other thing printed: having turned the phalanxes of the demons to flight, Nicephorus departed to God in deed a victor — a play on his name, which means the bearer of victory. No year, no country, no hymn. This folder records what the Greek calendar keeps on 13 May.
