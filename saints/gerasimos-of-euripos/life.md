# Gerasimos of Euripos

saint.gr's calendar for 7 December keeps the venerable Gerasimos of Euripos, whom it calls a missionary of Greece in the years of the Frankish rule, about 1320. He was born on the island of Euripos, which is Euboea, of wealthy and noble parents, and his parents came, the page says, of the western Frankish family of Rigas Fatzos.

In the second half of the thirteenth century Gerasimos came to Sinai, to the monastery of Saint Catherine. There he came to know what the page calls the surpassing quality of the virtue of [Gregory](/saints/gregory-of-sinai) the Sinaite, attached himself to him, and became one of his disciples; and so he reached such a height of practice and of contemplation that he became for others a pattern and archetype of all good things, and a mould of the virtues.

When Gregory the Sinaite left Sinai his disciple Gerasimos followed him, and when they reached Greece they put in for a while at the Good Harbours of Crete, from which Gregory went on to the Holy Mountain while Gerasimos landed on the Greek mainland and worked there as a missionary. In the end, the page says, he returned as the monk Gerasimos the Sinaite to his own country, Euboea.
