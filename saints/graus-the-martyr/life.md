# Graus

saint.gr's calendar for 15 June names Graus and gives no life. All the page says of her is one sentence: the holy Graus was martyred by the sword. Beside it stands a two-line epigram, and that is the only other thing the page prints about her. It is set as a question and an answer — Do you sacrifice to the gods, Graus, or shall the sword be stretched out against you? I rejoice; let it be stretched out, for I do not sacrifice to the gods.

The page gives no country, no year, no emperor, no persecutor and no hymn, and the name it prints is the Greek word for an old woman. Nothing further can be said of her out of this source than that she refused and was put to the sword.
