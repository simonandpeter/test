# Dionysius the Reader of Alexandria

Dionysius was a reader, martyred at Alexandria in the persecution of Decius with [Cyriacus](/saints/cyriacus-attendant-of-faustus), the attendant of the presbyter [Faustus](/saints/faustus-presbyter-of-alexandria), and [Andrew](/saints/andrew-soldier-of-alexandria) — or Andronicus — the soldier: the sword, the verse says, killed the two who were of one mind with Dionysius together with him. The Greek synaxarion keeps the martyrs of Alexandria under Decius in separate entries on 6 September; the Russian calendar counts them as the eleven with the presbyter Faustus and the deacon [Abibus](/saints/abibus-deacon-of-alexandria).

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 6 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2363/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
