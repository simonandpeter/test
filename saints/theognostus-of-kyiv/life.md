# Theognostus of Kyiv

Theognostus was a Greek by descent and lived as a monk at Constantinople. In the year 1328 he was elected metropolitan of Kyiv, after the repose of Peter of Kyiv, whom saint.gr keeps on 21 December; and he settled at Moscow. There he supported the ruler of Moscow, John, against the ruler of Tver, Alexander Mikhailovich, whom he excommunicated; and when Alexander, after his breach with the Golden Horde, took refuge in the city of Pskov, Theognostus laid an anathema on the people of Pskov as well.

The page has him standing against measures aimed at the Church of Russia, and keeps one answer of his. When he was accused before the ruler of the Mongols of not rendering him honours, the saint answered that Christ had come to teach us the truth and to redeem us from sin and from error; and why should he, as a bishop, render such honour to worldly rulers and to idolatry?

He fell asleep in peace in the year 1353, having first chosen for his successor [Alexis](/saints/alexis-of-moscow), the bishop of Vladimir. The page records that his relic lies incorrupt in the church of the Dormition of the Theotokos in the Kremlin, and heads him with a question mark where the year of his birth would be. It prints no hymn.

**The Russian church calendar keeps him on the Julian 5 October with the synaxis of the hierarchs of Moscow**, seventeen names in one line under one polyeleos service, marked «(Моск. Свт.)» and not expanded: Peter, Theognostus, Alexis, Cyprian, Photius, Jonah, Gerontius, Joasaph, Macarius, Philip, Job, Hermogenes, Tikhon, Peter, Philaret, Innocent and Macarius — two and a half centuries of metropolitans, then the patriarchs, then the metropolitans of Moscow and Kolomna, ending with a man shot in 1937. He is the second of them. The Russian index of names gives him both sees where the Greek gives one, «Святитель Феогност Киевский и Московский, митрополит», and lists two further days: 14 March, his repose, and «10 октября (Волын.)», a Volhynian commemoration.
