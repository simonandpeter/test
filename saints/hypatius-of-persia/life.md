# Hypatius of Persia

**The Russian calendar keeps the whole Persian company on one line and one life page.** The Сретенский monastery’s calendar prints for the Julian 20 November a single clause, «Сщмчч. Нирсы еп. и Иосифа, ученика его, Иоанна, Саверия, Исакия и Ипатия, епископов Персидских; мч. Азата скопца, Сасония, Феклы, Анны и иных многих мужей и жен, в Персиде пострадавших (343).», and behind every name in it the same life page stands, life2574, titled for all of them together. saint.gr prints the same day as four separate groups with four separate lives; the Russian page is the first source read here that puts them in one company. Its whole account of them is that they «были казнены во время гонения на христиан в Персии при царе Сапоре II» — that they were put to death during the persecution of Christians in Persia under king Shapur II — and then one short sentence apiece on the manner of each death.

He is the fourth of the four bishops the clause lists after the disciple, «Иоанна, Саверия, Исакия и Ипатия, епископов Персидских», and the name link behind his place in it, name page 1070, gives «Святитель Ипатий Персидский, епископ, сщмч.» — a bishop and a hieromartyr, with «Персидский» for an epithet and no see. The name page prints one day of memory, «День памяти: 20 ноября», and one life, the company’s.

The life gives him half a sentence, shared with the bishop named before him: «Та же участь постигла и святителей Исакия и Ипатия» — the same fate befell them also, and the fate is the beheading of Нирс and his disciple two sentences earlier. That is the whole of what is said of him. He is the one member of the company the clause names for whom this corpus held no folder: saint.gr’s four groups for 20 Νοεμβρίου have no Hypatius in them, so the Russian calendar supplies a name the Greek day does not.

No place is written. «Персидский» is a country and not a see, the page gives no coordinate for anything, and the `office` field is left empty rather than filled from an epithet that names an empire. The year is the company’s «(343)», taken from the close of the clause and the close of the life page, and no year of birth is given anywhere.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 20 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261120.html) and [the life the whole company shares](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life2574.htm); read 6 October 2026.*
