# Peter (Skipetrov)

saint.gr's calendar for 1 February names Peter the hieromartyr, and his page gives one sentence: that Peter Skipetrov was a presbyter and bore witness as a martyr in the year 1918. The surname is on his own page and not in the day's line, which gives only the name and the rank.

That is the whole of it. There is no year of birth, no parish, no city, no manner of death and no place of burial, and no apolytikion and no kontakion. The corpus keeps a dozen other men of the name, and this is none of them; the surname in brackets is the corpus's way of telling one twentieth-century Russian priest from another, and it is the page's own spelling transliterated back.

The Russian calendar, which keeps the new martyrs of 1918 with their own dossiers, is where this entry could be finished, and it has not been read.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1344/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
