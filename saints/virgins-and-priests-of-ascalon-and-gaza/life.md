# The Virgins and Priests of Ascalon and Gaza

saint.gr's calendar for 29 Μαρτίου prints them at the end of a line that begins with two named men: «Άγιος Μάρκος επίσκοπος Αρεθουσίων, Κύριλλος διάκονος, και των εν Ασκάλωνι και Γάζη παρθένων γυναικών και ιερωμένων ανδρών» — and on the page behind the line they stand as an entry of their own, with a couplet of their own, and not one of them is named. The page does not count them either: «αρκετές παρθένες γυναίκες στην Ασκάλωνα και τη Γάζα, καθώς και μερικοί ιερωμένοι», a number of virgin women at Ascalon and at Gaza, and some men in holy orders.

What it tells of them is the manner of their death and the company they died in. Under Julian the Apostate, in 363, the deacon [Cyril](/saints/cyril-of-heliopolis), the boast of the Church of Phoenicia, stood unmoved in the Christian confession and preached against the idols, and the idolaters in their fury opened his belly with swords so that his entrails were poured out. By that same death, the page says, these women of Ascalon and Gaza and these men in orders ended their lives, and their memory is kept together with his on this day. The couplet is as blunt as the account: «Κεῖνται γύναια βρώσεως χοίροις σκάφαι, Γαστρὸς παθοῦσαι ῥῆξιν ἐκχοιροφρόνων» — the women lie as troughs of food for swine, having suffered the tearing of the belly at the hands of men with swine's minds.

This is one folder and not many, because the source enumerates nobody: it says how many is «several» and «some», and gives no name, no age, no house and no see. [Mark](/saints/mark-of-arethusa), bishop of Arethusa, whom the same line names first, is kept with them, and the one apolytikion the page prints is for the whole company, naming the bishop, the deacon, the God-bearing priests and the modest women in turn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 29 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/29/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/102/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
