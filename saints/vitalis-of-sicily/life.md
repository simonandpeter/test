# Vitalis of Sicily

Vitalis was born in the city of Castronovo in Sicily, in the tenth century, of parents who were well off and devout, Sergios and Chrysonike, and who took care for his upbringing according to God and for his education. From a young age he loved Christ and the monastic polity, and for that he went away to the monastery of St Philip at Agkyra. There he was tonsured a monk and received the angelic habit, and lived in ascesis, advancing in virtue, for fifteen years.

In the course of those years he went on pilgrimage to Rome, to Calabria and to other holy places. In the end he returned to Calabria and lived as an ascetic on the mountain of Liporachos, where he met the ascetic Antonios and stayed with him a little while; then he moved from mountain to mountain, and came at last to the monastery of St Elias. After a good while he went to a cave between the mountains of Tourri and Armento, and there he struggled day and night at prayer and came to such heights that even the wild beasts of the mountain would come up to him quietly to take his blessing.

There he was visited by the venerable Luke of Demenna in Sicily, whom saint.gr keeps on 13 Οκτωβρίου, and the two of them talked of spiritual things and prayed together. Later Vitalis went to Bari, and with his nephew Elias came to the mountain of Tourri, where he built a church. His last station was the region of Rapolla, where he raised a monastery and passed the rest of his ascetic life, gathering a multitude of monks about him. He fell asleep in peace in the year 994.
