# Anna, mother of Cyriacus

saint.gr's calendar for 28 Οκτωβρίου names «Αγία Άννα μητέρα του Αγίου Κυριακού», and her notice is two sentences long. Anna was the mother of [Cyriacus of Jerusalem](/saints/cyriacus-of-jerusalem), whom the same calendar keeps on the same day — the page sends the reader to «the same day» for him — and when she learned of her son's torments she ran to see him and to encourage him.

For that she was arrested herself. They hung her up by the hair of her head, the page says, and burned her alive with lighted torches; and the epigram above the notice turns the torches round, so that Anna, burned in the flesh with torches, became herself an ever-shining torch of the Lord. The page gives her no year and names no place, records no relics and prints no hymn. It carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/28/index.aspx) and [her notice](https://www.saint.gr/2825/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
