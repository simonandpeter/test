# Celestine of Rome

Doxologia.ro's calendar for 8 aprilie names him and gives no life. The line is «Sfântul Ierarh Celestin, episcopul Romei» — the holy hierarch Celestine, bishop of Rome — and his own page on the site carries no life tab and no troparion: an icon, the readings appointed for the day, and articles that treat him together with the apostles Rufus and Agabus, who share the day.

The line asserts two things and no more: that he was a hierarch, and that his see was Rome. It gives no year, no council, no emperor and no account of how he governed or how he died, and this entry adds none.
