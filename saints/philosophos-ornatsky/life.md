# Philosophos Ornatsky

saint.gr says the hieromartyr Philosophos Nikolayevich Ornatsky lived in the nineteenth and twentieth centuries. In 1885 he finished the theological academy of Saint Petersburg and married Helen Zaozerkov. He was ordained presbyter and worked as a pastor, building up an enormous work of charity and mission, and he was joined in spirit to Patriarch Tikhon. Through the first world war he stood beside the wounded soldiers and their families.

His two sons were officers. [Nicholas](/saints/nicholas-son-of-philosophos) served with senior rank in the ninth battalion of the Russian army, and [Boris](/saints/boris-son-of-philosophos) had been appointed head of the twenty-third artillery brigade and fought bravely on the Austro-Hungarian front.

After the revolution of 1917 the hieromartyr Philosophos was arrested, on 9 August 1918, together with his sons, by men of the state security, who took them to the prisons of Kronstadt. There they were executed by shooting, and so gave the witness of their faith in our Lord and God. That is the whole of the page: it gives no year of birth for any of the three, records no wonder, and prints no hymn.
