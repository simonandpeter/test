# Beryllus of Catania

saint.gr's calendar for 21 Μαρτίου keeps a bishop of Catania in Sicily. Beryllus came from Antioch of Syria, the page says, and was a disciple of the Apostle [Peter](/saints/peter-the-apostle), whose memory it puts on 29 Ιουνίου; when he went with him to the West he was ordained by him bishop of Catania. There he shepherded his flock in the best way and brought a good number to the Christian faith, and was counted worthy to work wonders as well.

There was once a spring at Catania, the page goes on, which gave bitter water, and the saint by his prayer turned the bitter water sweet. A fanatical idolater who saw that wonder believed in Christ, and a good many others with him. Beryllus died in deep old age, and his relic was buried with honours at Catania, where to this day it pours out myrrh and heals the sicknesses of those who come to it in faith.

The page adds that a service for him was written by Theophanes and by [Joseph the Hymnographer](/saints/joseph-the-hymnographer) — it does not say which Theophanes, and none has been chosen here. Its couplet says that Beryllus, dying, found a double crown, as having mastered both the passions and the demons. No year is given for any part of his life, and none has been inferred from the apostle's.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 21 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/21/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3335/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
