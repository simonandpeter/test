# Matthias (Voznesensky)

The Russian calendar names him and nothing more. Its line for 19 мая ст. ст. reads «Сщмч. Матфия пресвитера (1919)» — the hieromartyr Matthias the presbyter, with the year — and the name link behind it supplies the surname the line leaves out, «Матфий (Вознесенский), иерей, сщмч.» That page carries a heading, the grade and two days, and not a sentence of narrative: no birthplace, no year of birth, no parish, nothing of his arrest or of his death. The first of its two days, 26 января, is the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which is a company's feast and not a day of his own.

So this folder asserts what the calendar asserts: that the Russian church keeps a priest of this name and surname as a hieromartyr on this day, and that he died in 1919. The year sets him apart from everything else the day holds — the rest of its twentieth-century entries are of 1937, 1938 and 1940 — and the calendar does not say where or how. What a second source would add is everything, and azbyka.ru was not read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 19 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260519.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9674.html), which carries no life; read 8 October 2026.*
