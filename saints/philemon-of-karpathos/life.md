# Philemon of Karpathos

saint.gr's calendar for 24 Ιανουαρίου names Saint Philemon, bishop of Karpathos, and the page behind that line has no life at all: «Η μνήμη του Αγίου Φιλήμωνα αναφέρεται στον Πατμιακό Κώδικα 266, χωρίς βιογραφικό υπόμνημα» — his memory is recorded in Patmos Codex 266, without any biographical note. The day, the see and the codex that carries him are everything the calendar keeps of him.

The page adds nothing else: no year, no hymn, no relics, not even a distich, and only the usual gloss on the name, that Φιλήμων comes from φίλημα, a kiss, and means the one who loves and is loved. He is not the venerable Philo whom the same day keeps as bishop of Carpasia in Cyprus: Karpathos is the island between Crete and Rhodes, Carpasia was a city on Cyprus, and it is only the two names and the two sees that look alike.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/24/index.aspx) and [his page](https://www.saint.gr/3525/saint.aspx), which prints no life; read 30 September 2026.*
