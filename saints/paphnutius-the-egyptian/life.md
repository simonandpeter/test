# Paphnutius the Egyptian

An Egyptian who laboured as an ascetic in the desert, in the persecutions of Diocletian - when the governor Adrian ordered him brought in, he did not wait for the soldiers sent to fetch him but presented himself, to confess Christ and receive the torments. Of the soldiers who tortured him, Dionysius and Callimachus, seeing the power with which God kept the martyr unharmed, believed in Christ themselves and were beheaded. Thrown into prison, he brought forty of the prisoners to Christ, and they were burned alive; released and taken in by a Christian named Nestorius, he led that whole household to a martyr's end; counsellor and strengthener of many, the life counts five hundred and forty-six who confessed Christ through him, some by the sword and some by fire.

The executioners threw him into a river with a stone hung at his neck, and the life records the wonder by which he did not stay there. The Romanian calendar keeps him on this day.
