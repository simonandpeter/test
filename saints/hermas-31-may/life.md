# Hermas (31 May)

Doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 31 mai reads «Sfântul Apostol Ermie», the holy apostle Hermas, and the page the calendar links for him carries no Viață tab and no Tropar tab: it prints his name, the date, a link to a devotional article, and the epistle appointed for an apostle, 1 Corinthians 4, God has set forth us the apostles last, as men doomed to death. Nothing else about him is on the page.

The rank in the line is the whole of what is stated: an apostle, kept on 31 May. The same day's calendar prints a second «Ermie» a line below, a martyr with a full life of his own, and doxologia gives the two men separate pages; this folder is the apostle. He is entered under the day and not under a place, because the source names none, and no year is recorded, because the page gives none.

The Ohrid Prologue keeps him on the same day and gives this folder its whole life, where the Romanian calendar gave only the rank. He was one of the Seventy, it says, and is the Hermas whom the apostle Paul greets in his letter to the Romans, at the fourteenth verse of the sixteenth chapter. He was a Greek by birth but lived a long time at Rome; he was bishop in Philippopolis; and he ended his life as a martyr. He put together the very instructive book called the Shepherd, following the revelations of an angel of God.

Hermas had been a rich man, the entry goes on, but through his own sins and the sins of his sons he fell into the last poverty. Once at prayer a man appeared to him in white clothing with a staff in his hand, who told him that he was the angel of repentance, sent to him for the rest of his life; and the angel gave him twelve commandments.

They are these, in the Prologue’s own numbering. To believe in God. To live in simplicity and innocence, to ill-treat nobody, and to give alms to everyone who asks. To love the truth and to avoid the lie. To keep chastity in one’s thoughts. To learn patience and magnanimity. To know that beside every man there stands one good spirit and one evil one. To fear God and not to fear the devil.

And the last five. To do every good thing and to hold back from every evil deed. To pray to God out of the depth of the soul, with faith that our prayer will be heard. To guard against sorrow, as the sister of doubt and of anger. To test prophecies, which are true and which false. And to guard against every evil desire. The entry gives no year of any kind, so the folder stays undated.

The Russian church calendar keeps him on 31 мая ст. ст. and gives him two readings, the first of three sentences and the second a heading for six of the Seventy together. The first says that he was bishop in Philippopolis of Thrace; that «Святой апостол Павел» greeted him in the Epistle to the Romans, at «Рим. 16, 14»; and that preaching the Gospel he bore many sorrows from the pagans, but died in peace.

That last is not the reading this folder already carried, which makes him a martyr, and neither has been preferred here. The second reading is headed «Апостолы: Иродион, Агав, Асинкрит, Руф, Флегонт и Ерм» and says only that the six of them belong to the number of the Seventy whom Christ chose and sent out to preach, pointing to the synaxis of the Seventy on 4 января. His pages set him on three days — that synaxis, 8 апреля, which is the day the six are kept together, and this one — and the calendar does not say which is his own.
