# Alexander (Arkhangelsky)

days.pravoslavie.ru names him third of seven on one line for 12 ноября ст. ст. and prints no life for him; the Russian church calendar at azbyka.ru prints one. He was born on 29 July 1891 in the village of Rykova Sloboda in the Ryazan district of the Ryazan province, the son of a psalmist, Ivan Arkhangelsky. In 1916 he entered the theological seminary but had no time to finish it, the revolution beginning; in 1917 he entered pastoral courses, and on completing them served as a psalmist. In 1921 he was ordained deacon and then priest, and served in the church of the village of Pashnevo in the Orekhovo-Zuyevo district of the Moscow region.

In the persecution of 1937 the authorities resolved to arrest him, and on 7 October one of the false witnesses was questioned. What he reported was a sermon of September 1937, spoken at the burial of the priest's own wife before more than fifty collective farmers from the villages round about: that if one looked at the Old and the New Testament and at how the pagans made persecutions against the Christians, and compared that with the present moment under the Soviet power — where the Church was as though separated from the state and every citizen's faith, of whatever nationality, as though free — then on looking into the matter it was plain that there was a persecution of religion.

The communists, he was reported as saying, were taking a census of the people and dividing it into believers and unbelievers, and meant by that to frighten them into renouncing their faith by force; they wanted to close the churches, laying taxes on the priests and on the Orthodox Church itself, and were taking down the bells. And let the communists make what persecution of the Church they would, religion would go on existing; sooner or later an end would come to that power.

On 11 November 1937 he was arrested and imprisoned in the Taganka prison in Moscow. Told that the investigation had material showing he had carried on anti-Soviet agitation in a counter-revolutionary spirit while living at Pashnevo, and asked whether he confirmed it, he answered «Я это отрицаю, так как никакой антисоветской агитации я не проводил». The interrogator went on pressing him to agree with the charges laid against him and to confirm what the false witnesses had said, and he rejected both categorically.

On 21 November the troika sentenced him to be shot. He was shot on 25 November 1937 and buried in a common unmarked grave on the Butovo range near Moscow. The day page carries no hymn under his name, and no relation is written to the six others of 1937 on his line: he was arrested on his own charge, held in his own prison and sentenced by his own troika on a date of its own.
