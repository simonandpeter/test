# The Forty Martyrs of the East

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαρτίου prints one line for the whole company — «Άγιοι Κοδράτος, Θεοδόσιος, Μανουήλ και άλλοι Τεσσαράκοντα μάρτυρες από την Ανατολή», the holy Quadratus, Theodosius, Manuel and forty other martyrs from the East — and on the life page behind it the forty stand as an entry of their own, «Αγιοι Τεσσαρακοντα Μαρτυρες Απο Την Ανατολη». The page names none of the forty. It gives no year, no city, no emperor and no judge.

What it does give is their passion, told with that of the three named men in a single paragraph and in the plural. They all lived in the years of the Church's persecutions under the idolatrous emperors. When they had been seized they were pressed to deny Christ; they repeated their confession many times over and declared that they would never desert the standard of the Gospel. The judge, at his wits' end, ordered their sides to be laid open, and when that torture too proved powerless against their devotion to Christ, he ordered them beheaded.

Their couplet, headed «Eις τους τεσσαράκοντα», reads «Τεσσαράκοντα Μάρτυρες διὰ ξίφους, Θεῷ προσηνέχθησαν, ὢ τῆς ἀνδρίας!» — forty martyrs were offered to God by the sword; O, the courage of it. This is one folder and not forty, because the source enumerates nobody: it counts them and stops. The three it does name are [Quadratus](/saints/quadratus-of-the-east), [Theodosius](/saints/theodosius-of-the-east) and [Manuel](/saints/manuel-of-the-east). No relic and no hymn are recorded.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3861/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
