# Mark (29 November)

The Greek calendar keeps a venerable Mark on 29 November, and saint.gr says where the memory comes from before it says anything else: it is in Delehaye's Synaxarist, with a short notice. What the notice holds is this. Mark left wife, children and relatives and became a monk; and he went about the desert, the cities and the villages, until he came at last to Egypt.

There he was found dead in some church, wearing heavy irons upon him. When the people saw it they marvelled at his asceticism, and they made a wooden case and laid his body in it together with the irons he had worn while he lived. The page gives him no century, no monastery, no name of the church and no name for the city; it prints no hymn and not even a couplet for him, and nothing of the kind is written here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 29 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/29/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3110/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
