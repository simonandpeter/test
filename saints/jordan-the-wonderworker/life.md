# Jordan (2 May)

saint.gr names him and says in so many words that it knows nothing else. Jordan the Wonderworker fell asleep in peace, its page reads, and we have no further details of the life of the venerable one. That is the whole of the notice: no century, no country, no monastery, no year, no account of a single wonder, no relic and no hymn.

What the page does print is a couplet that turns his name into the wonders: «Kαν εις μυχόν γης ερρύης Iορδάνη, / Eκχείς εκείθεν θαυμάτων Iορδάνην» — though thou hast flowed away into the innermost part of the earth, O Jordan, from there thou pourest out a Jordan of wonders. The epithet and the couplet are all that stands behind his being called a wonderworker at all.
