# Nicholas Kourtaliotis

Nicholas, gifted with an exceptional grace of wonderworking, lived the ascetic life in southern and central Crete in the middle or second half of the seventeenth century, and his falling asleep is placed around 1670. What is known of him survives only in the oral tradition of the island, above all in the province of Agios Vasileios in the prefecture of Rethymno, where the Kourtaliotiko gorge runs, with the river Kourtaliotis flowing through it to the Libyan Sea; in the middle of the gorge, near its floor, stands the saint’s small chapel, reached by a stair of many stone steps, and nearby, tradition says — the synaxarion’s life runs on past the point read here.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 1 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/886/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
