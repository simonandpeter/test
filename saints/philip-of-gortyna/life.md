# Philip of Gortyna

saint.gr's calendar for 8 Οκτωβρίου names Philip, bishop of Gortyna, and places him in the second half of the second century, in the years of Marcus Aurelius, 161 to 180, and of his successor Lucius Aurelius Commodus, 180 to 182. It makes him a contemporary of the eminent bishop of Corinth, the hieromartyr Dionysius, whose feast it gives as 29 Νοεμβρίου, and the recipient of a letter from him.

In that letter, saint.gr says, Dionysius praises Philip and the church of Gortyna for the courage they had shown in the persecutions, and counsels them to keep themselves from «τήν διαστροφήν τῶν αἱρετικῶν», the perversity of the heretics. A summary of the letter is preserved by Eusebius of Caesarea, and the page adds that [Jerome](/saints/jerome-of-stridon) also speaks of Philip of Gortyna.

The page's last note is from the fifth-century mosaics of the Rotonda at Thessalonica, where Philip is depicted together with [Cyril of Gortyna](/saints/cyril-of-gortyna) the hieromartyr — which means, it says, that he had entered the calendar of the Church very early. saint.gr prints no hymn for him and gives no year of death. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4464/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
