# Carthage of Lismore

saint.gr's calendar for 14 May names him and gives him three sentences. Saint Cartage — the page adds the Greek form Καρτέγιος in a parenthesis — came from Ireland and was the founder of the famous monastery which it writes as Rathanoffaly, a house that had some eight hundred and sixty monks. In the year 635 he was forced to abandon the monastery and to settle at Lismore, where he was elected bishop.

Having lived and shepherded his flock in a manner pleasing to God, he fell asleep in peace in the year 637. The page says nothing of why he had to leave his own foundation, nothing of his parents or his birthplace beyond the country, and prints no hymn and no relics. The Greek calendar keeps an Irish abbot and bishop on 14 May, and that is the entry.
