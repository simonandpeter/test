# Zacharias (22 October)

saint.gr's calendar for 22 Οκτωβρίου names Zacharias the martyr and gives him no life. One sentence is the whole of the narrative — «Ο Άγιος Ζαχαρίας μαρτύρησε διά πνιγμού μέσα στη θάλασσα», he was martyred by drowning in the sea — and the couplet over it calls him a divine diver and bids the reader name him so, having dived down inside the deep.

The page then argues the identity itself, as it does on the day before: the Zacharias kept today, it says, is not the same as the one of 21 October, because the two have different couplets and because one is entitled a venerable-martyr and the other a martyr. This folder is the martyr of the two. It is given no country, no year, no companions and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 22 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/22/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2779/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
