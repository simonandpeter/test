# Philip (Shatsky)

The archpriest Philip Shatsky was a teacher at the missionary school in the stanitsa of Sarkand in the Kopal district of the Semirechye region. In 1918 the Bolsheviks who took the village shut him in the school and set the building on fire; the priest’s charred bones were found and buried. He was numbered among the saints at the jubilee Council of Bishops of 2000, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 3 September (Julian) among the martyrs of Semirechye. That is the whole of the calendar’s notice; this entry records it and nothing more.
