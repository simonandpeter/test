# The Three Hundred Martyrs of Dudikvati and Papati

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names three hundred martyrs on 29 апреля and gives no account of them. Its line for the day is the whole of what it says: «Трехсот мучеников , в горах Дудиквати и Папати (Турция) пострадавших (XVII-XVIII) (Груз.).» — three hundred martyrs who suffered in the mountains of Dudikvati and Papati, in Turkey, in the seventeenth or eighteenth century; and the «(Груз.)» at the end of it is how this calendar marks a commemoration it keeps from the Georgian church.

So what the calendar tells is a number, two mountains, a country and a span of two centuries. It names nobody. Its own page under their name carries the heading «Триста мучеников, в горах Дудиквати и Папати пострадавшие» and the line «День празднования: 29 апреля», and nothing else — no reading, no icon, no article. Because the line enumerates no one, this is one folder for the company rather than three hundred folders; and because a range of mountains in which three hundred people were killed is not a point on a map, no place is recorded here.

The day page prints four hymns, two for the nine martyrs of Cyzicus and two for the wonderworker of Ostrog, and none for these. That the Georgian church keeps them and that the mountains are in Turkey is the whole of what the two pages say about where and among whom they died, and nothing more is claimed here.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 29 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260429.html) and [their name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14251.html); read 8 October 2026.*
