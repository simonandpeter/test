# Damian of Georgia

saint.gr says he was called Demetrius in the world and was king of the Georgians, from the year 1125, and the son of King David II, called the Strong or the Restorer, who reigned from 1089 to 1124. He lived in the eleventh and twelfth centuries; and having abandoned worldly power he became a monk, taking the name Damian. He fell asleep in peace in the year 1156.

That is the whole of the page's account of him. It gives him no birth year, no monastery, no account of his reign or of his abdication, no companions, and no hymn. Nothing further about him is recorded here.
