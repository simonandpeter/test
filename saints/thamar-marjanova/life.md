# Thamar (Marjanova)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names her on 10 июня and gives no life of her. Its line is «Прп. Фамари Марджановой, исп (1936) (Груз.).» — the venerable Thamar Marjanova, a confessor, the year 1936, and the parenthesis «(Груз.)» by which this calendar marks a saint of the Georgian Church. The abbreviation «исп» is printed without its full stop, as it is for other confessors on these pages.

Her own name page on that site, 14953, carries the heading «Фамарь (Марджанова), прп., исп.» and the one day «10 июня», and no life page, no icon and no place. It links two articles on Pravoslavie.Ru, and **not one word of either was read**; their headings and the teasers the page prints under them are page content and are quoted here, and nothing beyond them is in this folder. The first is headed «Святая схиигумения Фамарь (Марджанова; 1868–1936)», by Мария Тоболова, which gives her rank as a schema-abbess and the two years 1868 and 1936; under it the page prints: «Княжна Тамара вошла в храм светской красавицей, а вышла инокиней. У нее было всё для земного человеческого счастья, но она оставила это ради служения Богу.» The second is headed «"Маленькая в мирском и большая в духовном"», and under it: «Как только она вошла в монастырь, у нее сразу же в голове промелькнула мысль: "И я поступлю в монастырь…".»

So the calendar grades her venerable and confessor and not martyr, which is why `new-martyr` is not in her types although 1936 is the heart of the persecution; and what it tells of her is a Georgian princess named Tamara in the world who went into a monastery and died in 1936 under the monastic name Thamar. The day page prints no hymn under her name, and the articles that would say more were left closed.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 10 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260610.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14953.html); read 8 October 2026.*
