# Elias (Fondaminsky)

saint.gr keeps Elias Fondaminsky among the Russian saints who ended their lives in France, a synaxis the Ecumenical Patriarchate appointed for 20 July, whose members, the page says, are in the main new martyrs of the Nazi concentration camps. His notice is two sentences. Saint Elias was strongly political, a man of the left, who nevertheless supported Christian undertakings in France.

He was baptised Orthodox a little before the Nazis sent him to Auschwitz, and there he met his death on 19 November 1942, at the age of sixty-two. The page's own heading gives his years as 1882 to 1942, which does not quite agree with that age. It tells nothing else of him and prints no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 20 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/20/index.aspx) and [the synaxis](https://www.saint.gr/4381/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
