# Timon the Hermit

saint.gr's calendar for 25 Μαρτίου names the venerable Timon the hermit, and the page behind the line gives one sentence of life: «Ο Όσιος Τίμων έζησε και ασκήτεψε κατά τον 10ο αιώνα μ.Χ. Κοιμήθηκε με ειρήνη» — the venerable Timon lived and practised ascesis in the tenth century after Christ, and fell asleep in peace. Beyond that the source says nothing at all: no birthplace, no desert, no house, no teacher, no wonder, no relic and no hymn.

What the page prints around the sentence is about the name and not about the man. It glosses «ΤΙΜΩΝ (από την λέξη τιμή) = ο τίμιος, ο έντιμος» — from the word for honour, the honourable man — and then lists the two pagan Greeks who carried it, the Athenian philosopher of the fifth century before Christ who was nicknamed the misanthrope, and a tragic poet of the fourth. Neither has anything to do with the hermit.

The feast is marked fixed, «Σταθερή. Εορτάζει στις 25 Μαρτίου εκάστου έτους», kept on 25 March of every year, the day of the Annunciation, on which the Greek calendar sets him beside the great feast without a service of his own that the page records.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 25 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/25/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1774/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
