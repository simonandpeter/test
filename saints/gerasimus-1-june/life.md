# Gerasimus (1 June)

saint.gr's calendar for 1 Ιουνίου names him and then says that it knows nothing. His memory is recorded, the page says, in Codex 53 of the Monastery of Vlatades; and we have no other information at all about the life of this saint.

So the whole of what this folder can hold is the day, the name and the manuscript that keeps the commemoration. The page gives him no rank beyond the word holy — not even the Όσιος that would make him a monastic — no century, no country, no manner of death and no hymn. The corpus keeps seven other men of the name, from the Jordan to Kefalonia to Tismana, and nothing on this page connects him to any of them; that is why the folder is identified by its day.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/440/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
