# Michael (Vinogradov)

The Russian calendar names him and nothing more. Its line for 18 мая ст. ст. reads «Сщмч. Михаила пресвитера (1932)» — the hieromartyr Michael the presbyter, with the year — and the name link behind it gives the surname the line leaves out, «Михаил (Виноградов), иерей, сщмч.» That page carries a heading and two days and not a sentence of narrative: no birthplace, no year of birth, no parish, no account of his arrest or of his death. The second of its two days, 26 января, is the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which is a company's feast and not a day of his own.

So this folder asserts what the calendar asserts: that the Russian church keeps a priest of this name and surname as a hieromartyr on this day, and that he died in 1932. The corpus holds nine other folders bearing the same surname, scattered over eight other days between 1931 and 1942, and none of them is a Michael. What a second source would add is everything, and azbyka.ru was not read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 18 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260518.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14510.html), which carries no life; read 8 October 2026.*
