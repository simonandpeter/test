# Secundus, brother of Romylus

saint.gr's calendar for 24 Μαρτίου names two brothers together, and gives each of them a heading of his own and the two of them one sentence: the holy martyrs Romylus and Secundus were martyred in Mauretania of Africa, and we have no further details of the saints' life and martyrdom. That is the whole of the entry, and this life says no more than the page does.

The day's line calls them αυτάδελφοι, brothers of the same parents, which is all that is said of the kinship between Secundus and [Romylus](/saints/romylus-brother-of-secundus). The page gives them no emperor, no year, no city of Mauretania, no manner of death and no relics, prints no couplet and no hymn, and nothing has been taken from any other source or supplied from memory.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/24/index.aspx) and [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/1630/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
