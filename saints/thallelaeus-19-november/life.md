# Thallelaeus (19 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 19 Νοεμβρίου prints one line for five people: «Άγιοι Άνθιμος, Θαλλελαίος, Χριστόφορος, Ευφημία και τα παιδιά τους και Παγχάριος» — Anthimus, Thallelaeus, Christopher, Euphemia and their children, and Pancharius. Of the lives behind the names the page says in one sentence that there is nothing to be had: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο των Αγίων», we have no details of the life of the saints.

What it does print is the couplet, and the couplet counts them: receive five athletes, Christ, and give five crowns as the prizes of their contests. Five are named and the children are not, and so the corpus keeps a folder for each name the page gives and none for the children it leaves unnamed. The page glosses three of the names — Anthimus from ἄνθος, the flower, one beautiful in body and in spirit; Christopher as the one who uncovers Christ by his works and his words; Euphemia from good report, the renowned, the glorious — and that is the whole of its commentary. Thallelaeus it does not gloss.

It gives them no country, no city, no year, no emperor, no manner of death and no hymn, and prints no relics. This folder is Thallelaeus, named second in the line, beside [Anthimus](/saints/anthimus-19-november), [Christopher](/saints/christopher-19-november), [Euphemia](/saints/euphemia-19-november) and [Pancharius](/saints/pancharius-19-november).

*After saint.gr's calendar for 19 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/19/index.aspx) and [the life the five share](https://www.saint.gr/2910/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
