# John (Kastorsky)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him for 22 февраля ст. ст. in one line that it gives to four — «Сщмчч. Иосифа и Владимира пресвитеров, Иоанна диакона и мч. Иоанна (1918).» — two hieromartyr presbyters, a hieromartyr deacon and a martyr, all of 1918. The line carries the forenames only, declined, and no surname for any of them.

The name link behind the third of those names carries the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line leaves out: «Иоанн (Касторский), диакон, сщмч.», a deacon of that name. Four entries on one line are four people, and they are kept apart here by the names and the offices the calendar's own links give them.

It prints no life page for him and no hymn under his name; the only hymns it sets on this day belong to a finding of relics with which it heads the day. It gives him no parish, no diocese, no place and no manner of death. Nothing further is written here, and nothing is reasoned out from the surname or from the year he shares with the other three.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 22 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260222.html); read 3 October 2026.*
