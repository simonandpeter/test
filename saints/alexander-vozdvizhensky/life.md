# Alexander (Vozdvizhensky)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him second of ten people on one line for 31 октября ст. ст. and gives him no life. The line carries a rank word, seven forenames in the genitive, two more rank words, three more forenames and one year for all ten; his own name appears in it as «Александра» and nothing else. The name link behind it adds the surname the line leaves out and the grade the line gives only in the plural: «Александр (Воздвиженский), иерей, сщмч.», a priest of that name, one of the new martyrs of the Russian church.

So the year is the whole of what is recorded of him, and the year is 1937. There is no life page behind his name, and his name page carries no text, no icon and no hymn — only his name and his days, of which the second is 26 января, the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, where he is commemorated with the rest of that company rather than alone.

The surname returns no other folder in this corpus. Two of the ten on this line have lives on the calendar, and both of those were shot on 13 November 1937 in the Tver region; the page says nothing of the kind about him, and nothing is said for him here. The second Russian calendar, azbyka.ru, which has given this corpus full lives and archival references for others of these men, refused the request with a 403 on the day he was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 31 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261031.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9422.html); read 4 October 2026.*
