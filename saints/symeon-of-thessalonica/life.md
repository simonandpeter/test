# Symeon, Archbishop of Thessalonica

Symeon lived in the fifteenth century, and of his life not much is known: he must have been a hieromonk, and archbishop of Thessalonica he must have become after 1410. Besides his important pastoral work he developed a rich writing — letters, commentaries, encomia, dialogues and hymnographic texts. He died in September 1429 and was numbered among the saints on 14 April 1981. The Romanian life counts him the last disciple of Gregory Palamas and an important theologian of his time, archbishop from 1410 until his death, about 1381–1429; the Greek and Romanian calendars keep him on 15 September.
