# Valerian (4 May)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 4 mai reads «Sfântul Cuvios Valerian» — Valerian the venerable — and his page carries only the day and the readings this calendar appoints for a monastic saint, the epistle to the Galatians on the fruit of the Spirit and the gospel of the easy yoke.

The rank «Cuvios» is the whole of what the source says about him, and it is what distinguishes him from the martyr of Tomis whom the same calendar keeps on 13 September. No century, no country and no hymn is printed here.
