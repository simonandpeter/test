# Valerian (4 May)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 4 mai reads «Sfântul Cuvios Valerian» — Valerian the venerable — and his page carries only the day and the readings this calendar appoints for a monastic saint, the epistle to the Galatians on the fruit of the Spirit and the gospel of the easy yoke.

The rank «Cuvios» is the whole of what the source says about him, and it is what distinguishes him from the martyr of Tomis whom the same calendar keeps on 13 September. No century, no country and no hymn is printed here.

The Greek calendar keeps a Valerian of this day, and unlike doxologia it says what he did — though it calls him a martyr where the Romanian line calls him a monk, and the two ranks are not reconciled here. saint.gr names him fourth in a company of six: [Aphrodisius](/saints/aphrodisius-of-scythopolis), [Misdas](/saints/misdas), [Macrobius](/saints/macrobius-of-scythopolis), Valerian, [Leontius](/saints/leontius-of-scythopolis) and [Antoninus](/saints/antoninus-of-scythopolis), martyrs, with others who suffered with them at Scythopolis in Syria.

These saints, it says, presented themselves of their own accord before the ruler of their country and reproached him most sharply for his inhuman doings against the Christians. Then they broke the altars of the idols to pieces and provoked the fury of the pagans, who set on them with stones and clubs and killed them all. The Christians took up their bodies, buried them with honours, and raised a church over them; and their synaxis used to be kept at the apostoleion of James the Brother of God, which stands inside the venerable house of the Mother of God at the Chalkoprateia. Of Valerian himself the page says nothing apart from the company, and its couplet pairs him with Macrobius: a martyr's roof of fire was fixed over the heads of the two of them.
