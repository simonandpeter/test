# Barsanuphius

Barsanuphius (Lebedev), bishop of Kirillov, vicar of the Novgorod diocese,
was born in 1873. In 1894 he finished the Novgorod seminary, in 1895 was
tonsured and ordained hierodeacon, from 1896 was the Novgorod diocese’s
missionary-preacher, in 1909 was raised to archimandrite, and from 1911 was
superior of the Kazan monastery of the Transfiguration. On 8 January 1917 he
was consecrated bishop of Kirillov, vicar of Novgorod. Together with the
priest [John Ivanov](/saints/john-presbyter-martyr-1918-2-september), the
abbess of the Ferapontov monastery [Seraphima
(Sulimova)](/saints/seraphima-abbess-martyr-1918) and four laymen —
[Nicholas Burlakov](/saints/nicholas-martyr-1918-2-september), [Anatoly
Barashkov](/saints/anatoly-martyr-1918), [Michael
Trubnikov](/saints/michael-martyr-1918-2-september) and [Philip
Marychev](/saints/philip-martyr-1918) — he was shot on 2 September 1918 of
the old style, 15 September. Before the execution, until the bishop had
finished his prayer, the Red Army men could not shoot him; he went to his
death with his arms opened in a cross, and not one bullet touched him, until
the executioners in their fury fell on him and stabbed him with their
bayonets. He was buried not far from the Kirillo-Belozersky monastery; in
the 1960s the grave was destroyed and desecrated, and a pigsty was built on
the spot. He was glorified as a locally venerated saint of the Vologda
diocese in 1999, and the hieromartyr Barsanuphius, bishop of Kirillov, with
those who suffered with him — the priest John Ivanov, the abbess Seraphima,
Nicholas Burlakov, Anatoly Barashkov, Michael Trubnikov and Philip Marychev
— were numbered among the new martyrs and confessors of Russia by the
jubilee Council of Bishops of August 2000 for veneration by the whole
church. The Russian calendar keeps the seven together on 2 September
(Julian), and Barsanuphius also on 26 January with the new martyrs.
