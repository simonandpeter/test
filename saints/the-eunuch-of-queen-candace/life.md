# The Eunuch of Queen Candace

The Prologue keeps this man without a name of his own, as the eunuch of Candace queen of the Ethiopians. His baptism it gives in a single line, pointing to the eighth chapter of the Acts of the Apostles, where [Philip the Deacon](/saints/philip-the-deacon) meets him on the road that goes down from Jerusalem and baptises him; and after his baptism, it says, he returned home and began to preach Christ.

It calls him the first apostle of the faith in Ethiopia, and says that he ended his life as a martyr and was counted worthy of the kingdom of God. No year is given, no place of his death and no circumstance of it, and the calendar records nothing of his life before the road out of Jerusalem.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 4. јануар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-01-17&prolog=1); read 3 October 2026.*
