# Martinian the Jailer

The Ohrid Prologue of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović keeps him on the Julian 11 April with his fellow, and what it gives is five sentences and no more. They were jailers at the Roman prison in which the apostles [Peter](/saints/peter-the-apostle) and [Paul](/saints/paul-the-apostle) were held. Hearing the apostles' words and seeing their wonders, the two men were baptised, and they let the apostles out of the prison.

The apostles went out of Rome; but the Lord met Peter on the road, walking towards the city. Where are you going, Lord? Peter asked him. And the Lord answered that He was going to Rome to be crucified again. Ashamed, the apostles turned back into the city, where they were seized and killed; and with them these two brave martyrs, [Processus](/saints/processus-the-jailer) and Martinian, were beheaded.

That is the whole of the account. The entry gives them no year, no birthplace, no relics and no hymn, and it says nothing of either man apart from the other: the two are named together in its heading and told of together in its body, and nothing in it distinguishes them. He is not the Martinian this same Prologue keeps on 13 February, nor any of the four other men of the name the corpus holds; none of them is a jailer and none is Roman.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 11. април ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-04-24&prolog=1); read 4 October 2026.*
