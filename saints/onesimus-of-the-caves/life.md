# Onesimus of the Caves

His life on the Russian church calendar is two sentences long and says only where he was and where he stayed. Struggling in the Kyiv Caves lavra in the twelfth or thirteenth century, he shut himself into the Near Caves — the caves of Saint Anthony — and his holy relics are buried in the place of his labours. That is the entry entire.

He is kept on 4 October in one line with [Helladius of the Caves](/saints/helladius-of-the-caves), and the site's index of names gives him two further days, 21 July and the synaxis of the Near Caves on 28 September, and one word the day line has not: «затворник», recluse. He is not the apostle Onesimus of Philemon's letter, nor any of the other men of that name this corpus holds; the day and the lavra are what distinguish him.

*After the Russian church calendar's [4 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261004.html), [his life there](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4284.htm) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/1667.html); read 4 October 2026.*
