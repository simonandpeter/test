# Ampliatus of Diospolis

doxologia.ro's calendar names Ampliatus among the apostles it keeps on 31 October, and the life it prints for him is three short sentences. He was made bishop by the holy Apostle Andrew; he was bishop in Diospolis; and preaching Christ and destroying idols he raised the Jews and the Greeks against himself, and being killed by them he wove himself a martyr's crown.

Nothing further is given: no year, no account of his preaching, no manner of death and no burial place, and nobody is named beside him but the apostle who ordained him. doxologia.ro prints no separate troparion for him. The hymn on his page is the one it gives to all six apostles it names on this day — Apelles, Stachys, Ampliatus, Urban, Aristobulus and Narcissus together — a short common of apostles in the third tone, asking them to entreat the merciful God for the forgiveness of our offences.
