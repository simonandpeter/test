# Nicholas (Kedrov)

saint.gr names him and gives four sentences. It says that the holy hieromartyr Nicholas Kedrov was a presbyter in the town of Yaransk, near the province of Vyatka in Russia, and that of his marriage to the presbytera Elizabeth he had three sons and a daughter: the hieromartyrs [Pachomius](/saints/pachomius-kedrov) and [Abercius](/saints/abercius-kedrov), whom the same Greek day keeps beside him, Michael, bishop of the town of Braslav in Poland, and Vera. It says that he suffered martyrdom in 1936.

Nothing more is printed there. The page gives no year of his birth, no account of his parish or his arrest, no manner of death and no place of death, no hymn and no relics; and of Michael and Vera it says only what stands above. What the page does not say is not set down here.
