# Nicander (Chernelevsky)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 21 октября ст. ст. as the fourth of five, in the line «Сщмчч. Павлина , архиеп. Могилевского, Аркадия , еп. Екатеринбургского, и с ними Анатолия и Никандра пресвитеров и мч. Киприана (1937)». He is the second of the two presbyters, named «Никандра» and nothing more; the title on the name link behind his place adds the surname and the grade, «Никандр (Чернелевский), иерей, сщмч.», a priest.

The company is headed by an archbishop, [Paulinus (Kroshechkin)](/saints/paulinus-kroshechkin), who was shot that day with a group of clergy in the Kemerovo camp; the calendar carries a life for him and for none of the other four, and says nothing about where the rest died or whether they died together. His own forename is uncommon enough that the corpus holds no other of it from this century, and the surname is the only thing beyond the grade that the calendar gives him.

Nothing further is written here — not a birth, not a diocese, not a parish, not a charge, not a place of death. The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 21 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261021.html); read 4 October 2026.*
