# Proclus of Bologna

saint.gr's calendar for 1 Ιουνίου names him and gives one sentence. The holy martyr Proclus, it says, was a Roman official, and he was martyred in the year 304, under the emperor Maximian, whose reign the page dates 285 to 305, in the city of Bologna in Italy.

That is the whole of the source. It gives him no birthplace, no age, no office more exact than official, no judge and no manner of death; it names no companion, records no wonder, and prints neither hymn nor epigram. The word it uses for his station is not a military one, so this folder does not make a soldier of him.
