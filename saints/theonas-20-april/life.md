# Theonas (20 April)

Theonas is one of nine martyrs saint.gr prints together on 20 April, and the page makes the cause of all nine the same: «αίτια του ενδόξου μαρτυρίου τους, ο μεγαλομάρτυς και τροπαιοφόρος Γεώργιος» — the cause of their glorious martyrdom was the great martyr and trophy-bearer [George](/saints/george-the-trophy-bearer). They all suffered, it says, in the years of the king Diocletian, whose reign it dates 284 to 305.

Theonas was one of four who were the king's own bodyguards — Christopher, Theonas, Caesarius and Antoninus. When these saw that George had raised a dead Greek by his prayer, they threw off the insignia of their rank and, before the king and the crowd, confessed Christ true God. They were imprisoned at once.

After some days they were brought before Diocletian, and there they were hung up and torn, and burned with lighted torches; and at the last they were thrown into the furnace of fire, and so received the glorious crown of martyrdom. It was the year 303. The couplet the page sets over them says that, filled with the soot of the furnace, those about Christopher came to Christ.

The page gives Theonas no country, no city, no rank beyond that of the guards, and no relic, and it prints no hymn. Its whole account is the shared one, and the nine stand in it as they stand in the calendar's line: [Victor](/saints/victor-20-april), [Acindynus](/saints/acindynus-20-april), [Zoticus](/saints/zoticus-20-april), [Zeno](/saints/zeno-20-april), [Severian](/saints/severian-20-april), [Christopher](/saints/christopher-20-april), [Caesarius](/saints/caesarius-20-april) and [Antoninus](/saints/antoninus-20-april).
