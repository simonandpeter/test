# Peter Ordynsky of Rostov

The Greek calendar keeps him on 30 Ιουνίου and knows nothing about him. saint.gr's heading is «Άγιος Πέτρος ο πρίγκιπας του Ορντύνσκϊυ, Ροστώβ (; - 1290)» — Peter the prince of the Horde, of Rostov, died 1290 — and the body of the page is the one sentence «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου», we have no details of the saint's life. The year of death and the two place-words are therefore everything this source gives.

What those words carry is a Russian saint read into a Greek calendar: Rostov is the city on the lake north-east of Moscow, and «Ορντύνσκϊυ» is the Russian epithet of the Horde, borne by a prince who came out of the Tatar Horde to the Russian church. The page neither tells that story nor vouches for it, so nothing of it is recorded here beyond the words it prints. His Russian calendar is the source that will have his life, and it has not been read.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 30 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/30/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4029/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
