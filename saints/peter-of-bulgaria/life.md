# Peter of Bulgaria

Peter was tsar of Bulgaria from 927 to 967, saint.gr says, and the son of the emperor Symeon. He had married Maria, the granddaughter of the emperor Romanos Lekapenos, whose reign the page dates 920 to 944; she was renamed Irene, for the peace that held between the Byzantines and the Bulgarians.

He contended against the heresy of the Bogomils, the page says, following the teaching of [John of Rila](/saints/john-of-rila), whom the same calendar keeps on 19 October. After an unsuccessful war with Hungary and with Russia, he fell asleep in the year 967.

That is the whole of the notice. It gives him no year of birth, no place of death and no burial, and prints no apolytikion and no kontakion. The rank of tsar is in the day's own line for him; the Russian, Romanian and Serbian calendars have not been read for this folder.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 30 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/30/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3570/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
