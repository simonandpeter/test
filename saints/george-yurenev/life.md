# George (Yurenev)

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar for 7 ноября ст. ст. carries three names on one line and his is the third of them: «Сщмчч. Сергия , архиеп. Елецкого, Николая пресвитера и мч. Георгия (1937).» The name link behind his place in it supplies the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line leaves out, «Георгий (Юренев), мч.», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from.

That is everything the calendar records of him. He has no life page, no icon, no hymn, no place, no age and no parish anywhere on this calendar, and the name page behind his place on the line carries its title, two days and no text at all. That page also lists «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which is a synaxis and not a second feast; this folder renders on 7 ноября ст. ст. only. Nothing further is written here, and in particular nothing is reasoned from the surname. The one life the entry has is the archbishop's, and it does not name him.

**The calendar gives him no office, and that is what marks him off from the two clergy before him on the line.** Its class for him is «мч.», not «Сщмч.», and his name page says «Георгий (Юренев), мч.» and nothing more: a layman, with no grade, no parish and no place.

He is given no relation to the two others the line names. The line's «Сщмчч.» is plural and sets the three of them together, and the archbishop's own life — the only life the entry has — says he was shot in the Karaganda camp and names neither the presbyter nor the layman, nor anybody else who died with him. A shared line, a shared class and a shared year are not a company unless a page says they died together, so no `related` row is written here in either direction.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 7 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261107.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9961.html); read 4 October 2026.*
