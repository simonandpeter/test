# Leonid (Salkov)

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him twenty-third of twenty-four and gives no life; the life here is the one azbyka.ru prints for the civil 2 декабря, and this one comes out of grad-petrov.ru rather than pstgu.ru. Leonid Vasilyevich Salkov was born in 1886 in the province of Taurida and had a higher technical education at the university of Moscow. From 1910 to 1917 he served in the tsar’s army; in 1917 he gave up the service and went about the monasteries of Crimea, the Caucasus, the Stavropol country and Odessa, and in March 1919 the elder Alexis of the Teklauk monastery blessed him to be a wanderer.

In 1927 he was arrested on a charge of spying for the counter-revolutionary clergy and sentenced to three years of concentration camps; he was sent to the Solovki camp of special purpose and came out of it in 1930. In 1935 he was arrested at Vologda for counter-revolutionary activity and sentenced to five years of camps, and was sent to the Karlag of the NKVD in the Karaganda region. In 1937 he was arrested again inside the camp. The case papers say that while serving his sentence in Karlag he performed religious rites, passed prayers he had written himself among the prisoners, spoke of the near fall of the Soviet power, and together with one Krasnopevtsev served a panikhida on 8 November 1937 for the murdered imperial family.

He did not admit any guilt, and said that he was «не подлец и не предатель», and that he did not buy his own freedom with other men’s blood. The page ends by saying he was shot in Karlag at the end of 1937 or the beginning of 1938, and gives no day; the Sretensky calendar keeps him on this Julian 19 November with the company, and his `dates` hold the range the source states and no more.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 19 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261119.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9993.html), which carries no life; and the life printed by the Russian church calendar at azbyka.ru for 2 декабря out of grad-petrov.ru — [the entry](https://azbyka.ru/days/sv-leonid-salkov); read 6 October 2026.*
