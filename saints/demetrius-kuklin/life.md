# Demetrius (Kuklin)

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him thirteenth of twenty-four and gives no life; the life here is the one azbyka.ru prints for the civil 2 декабря, out of pstgu.ru. Dmitry Konstantinovich Kuklin was born in 1870 at Vologda. After the seminary he took holy orders and went to serve in the town of Veliky Ustyug in the Vologda region, and was in time raised archpriest. He served there until 1936, when he was arrested together with the archbishop of Veliky Ustyug, Nicholas (Klimentyev).

Charged with taking part in «контрреволюционной группировке церковников в г. Устюге», he was sentenced to five years of exile in Kazakhstan, and served it in hard conditions in the village of Borodulikha in the Semipalatinsk region. What held him up, the page says, was an exchange of letters with the archbishop Nicholas, who had been exiled to Chimkent, and the services the exiled clergy kept among themselves. That could not go unnoticed, and on 22 November 1937 he was arrested again; a group case was made up, «Дело протоиерея Дмитрия Куклина и др. г. Семипалатинск, 1937 г.», which is the case four more of these twelve went into.

The indictment charged him with «проведение религиозных обрядов и нелегальных сборищ», at which the forming of groups of believers and the opening of a church were discussed, and with agitation against the collective-farm order. He did not admit the agitation, and said at his questioning that the local people had come to him for advice about registering a group of believers, and that he had told them to gather a settled group and apply to the district executive committee, and then he, or whoever else they chose, would perform the rites. He said also that he had christened a child once and that no other clergyman had been there. He was sentenced to be shot on 27 November 1937 and died at Semipalatinsk on 2 December; where he is buried is not known.
