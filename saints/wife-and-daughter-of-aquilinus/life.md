# The Wife and Daughter of Aquilinus

saint.gr's calendar for 19 Νοεμβρίου names «Αγίες Γυναίκα και Θυγατέρα» — a holy woman and a holy daughter — and names neither of them. Its one sentence of narrative says instead whose they were: the wife and the daughter of the eparch Aquilinus, who believed in Christ through [Azes](/saints/azes-19-november), the saint the same calendar keeps on the same day, and who were martyred by the sword.

Azes's own notice says what moved them. The eparch had him bound to a wheel with a fire lit beneath it, and the flame rose and then went out all at once; it was the sight of that which drew his own wife and daughter to Christianity. The couplet set over their line has the woman and the daughter leaving Aquilinus behind and finding Christ for a bridegroom by the sword.

The page gives them no names, no country, no year and no hymn, and prints no relics for them. Beside them the same day keeps [a hundred and fifty soldier martyrs](/saints/hundred-and-fifty-soldier-martyrs) and [twelve soldier martyrs](/saints/twelve-soldier-martyrs-19-november) more, all of them, as it tells the story, Azes's.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 19 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/19/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2907/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
