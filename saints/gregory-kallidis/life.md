# Gregory Kallidis

Gregory Kallidis was born in 1844 at Cumbao in Eastern Thrace. He served as Metropolitan of Heraclea and Rhaedestus, of Ioannina and of Thessalonica, and it was at Thessalonica that he fell asleep, on 25 July 1925. That is the whole of what the Romanian calendar sets down of his life: the year and place of his birth, the three sees he held, and the day of his death.

At his exhumation on 20 October 1979, when his relics were moved from the cemetery of the Annunciation in Thessalonica into the holy church of Saint Demetrius, it was found that they gave off a sweet fragrance; and from then until today, the page says, he has worked many wonders for those who have prayed to him with faith.

He was recognised as a saint of the Orthodox Church of Greece by the corresponding act of canonisation of the Patriarchate and of the Holy Synod, dated 22 May 2003. He is kept on 25 July, and on 20 October, the day of the translation of his honourable relics.
