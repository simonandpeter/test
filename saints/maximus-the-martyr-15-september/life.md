# Maximus (15 September)

Maximus was martyred by the sword; that is the whole of the Greek synaxarion’s notice for 15 September. Seeing Christ hold out a crown to you, its verse says, you hold out your head to the sword, Maximus. This entry records it and nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 15 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2436/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
