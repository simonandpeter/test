# Vladimir (Khirasko)

The Russian calendar keeps him on 11 января ст. ст. as «Св. Владимира исп., пресвитера (1932)», and the life it links is not his own but the collective account of the twenty-three new martyrs of Minsk, where he has a paragraph. He was born on 11 January 1873 at Podolsk in Ukraine — the calendar's own day for him is his birthday — and after his ordination in 1899 he served the churches of the villages of Omelno and Yurevichi.

From 1911 he was rector in Minsk of the church of the icon of the Mother of God «Всех скорбящих Радосте», the Joy of All Who Sorrow. In 1925 he was arrested for having stood up for Metropolitan Melchizedek, whom the authorities had expelled from Minsk, and was himself banished to the town of Orel. He came back from that exile and went on serving in the same Minsk church.

For his frequent preaching he was arrested again in 1929 and sent to Siberia, and in three years of camp life he went very nearly blind. Released in 1932, gravely ill, he died a few months afterwards. The Synod of the Belarusian Exarchate numbered the Minsk martyrs among the locally venerated saints of its diocese in 1999, and the Jubilee Council of Bishops of August 2000 glorified them for the whole church.

The day page gives him the rank of confessor, «исп.», while the Minsk page heads his paragraph «Священномученик Владимир Хираско, иерей»; the calendar's own line is followed here. The day page prints no hymn under his name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 11 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260111.html) and [the Minsk new martyrs](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4766.htm); read 3 October 2026.*
