# Synesius of Lysi

saint.gr names the venerable Synesius on this day and then says, in one sentence, that it knows nothing about him: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Οσίου» — we have no details of the venerable one's life. So the whole of what stands recorded is what the day's own line says, «Όσιος Συνέσιος ο εν Λύση της Κύπρου αθλήσας»: the venerable Synesius, who contended at Lysi in Cyprus. Lysi is a village of the Mesaoria plain, and that is the only place the site puts him.

There is no century here, no year of birth or of repose, no monastery, no elder, no account of the ascesis the word ἀθλήσας points at, no relic, no distich and no hymn. The etymology block the page sets over the name says Συνέσιος is the prudent one and then adds that a writer of Cyrene of the fourth century bore it; that is the site's note on the word and on another man, nothing on the page attaches it to this venerable one, and no date is taken from it.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3786/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
