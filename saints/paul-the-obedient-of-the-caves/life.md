# Paul the Obedient, of the Cave

Paul was an ascetic of the Far Caves at Kyiv. Having taken the schema there,
the Orthodox Church in America’s life says, he bore without a murmur the very
heavy obediences the superior sent him on, and was never idle: when no
obedience was on him he ground grain under the millstone, wearing his body
down with the work and coming by it to unceasing inner prayer. He is kept on
10 September, the day of his namesake [Paul](/saints/paul-bishop-of-nicaea),
bishop of Nicaea. The Greek synaxarion keeps him the same day, “in the
Cave”, and says it has no details of his life; the Russian calendar keeps
him the same day of its own reckoning.

*After the life printed by the Orthodox Church in America for 10 September —
[the day’s lives](https://www.oca.org/saints/all-lives/2026/09/10); the Greek
entry is the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 10 Σεπτεμβρίου —
[the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2629/saint.aspx); read 5 September 2026.*
