# Paul the Obedient, of the Cave

The Greek synaxarion keeps Paul the Obedient, “in the Cave”, on 10 September and says it has no details of his life. This entry records the line and nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 10 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2629/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
