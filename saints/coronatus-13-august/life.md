# Coronatus (13 August)

saint.gr's calendar for 13 Αυγούστου names him and says one thing about him. Its whole notice is the sentence «Ο Άγιος Κορωνάτος μαρτύρησε δια ξίφους» — Saint Coronatus was martyred by the sword. There is no country, no city, no century, no emperor, no year, and no hymn. The couplet of the synaxarion printed above it says no more than the same thing twice over.

That couplet runs «Προθείς τράχηλον εις τομήν Kορωνάτος, / Kτείνει πολυτράχηλον ύδραν την πλάνην» — Coronatus, offering his neck to the cutting, slays error, the many-necked hydra. This folder stands for the Greek line of 13 August alone, and is marked by its day because the calendar gives him nothing else to be known by.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 13 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/13/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/974/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
