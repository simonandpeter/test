# Papas the New

saint.gr's calendar for 1 Αυγούστου names him and gives no life. Its whole notice of him is one sentence: «Ο Άγιος Πάπας μαρτύρησε αφού τον έβαλαν μέσα σε ένα σάκο και τον έριξαν στη θάλασσα» — Saint Papas was martyred after they had put him into a sack and thrown him into the sea. There is no country, no century, no emperor, no judge and no hymn. What the Greek calendar keeps of him is therefore a name, a day and a manner of death, and nothing else at all.

Besides that the page prints the couplet of the synaxarion, which is the only other thing it says of him: «Σάκκος Πάπαν ἔκρυψε, καὶ σάκκον θίβη, / Καὶ τὴν θίβην ῥοῦς, καὶ Πάπας Θεοῦ πέλας» — a sack hid Papas, and a basket the sack, and the current the basket — and Papas is near to God.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2182/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
