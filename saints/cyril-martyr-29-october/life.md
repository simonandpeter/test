# Cyril (29 October)

saint.gr's calendar for 29 Οκτωβρίου names «Άγιοι Κύριλλος, Μίνης και Μιναίος», and the notice behind that line is a single sentence: the holy Cyril, Mines and Minaeos were martyred by the sword. There is no city, no persecutor, no year, and nothing of the three men beyond the manner of their death. The page sets an epigram over them instead, and the one for Cyril says that he knew the threat of the sword to be the promise of a crown to come.

The page adds one thing more: that the memory of Mines and Minaeos is kept again on 1 August, and it names no second day for Cyril. Those two have their own entry on that other day and are read there; this folder is Cyril's alone, and it takes its name from this day because the calendar gives him no epithet to take it from.

saint.gr prints no hymn for him. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands, bare as it is.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 29 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/29/index.aspx) and [the notice of the three](https://www.saint.gr/2836/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
