# Cyril (29 October)

saint.gr's calendar for 29 Οκτωβρίου names «Άγιοι Κύριλλος, Μίνης και Μιναίος», and the notice behind that line is a single sentence: the holy Cyril, Mines and Minaeos were martyred by the sword. There is no city, no persecutor, no year, and nothing of the three men beyond the manner of their death. The page sets an epigram over them instead, and the one for Cyril says that he knew the threat of the sword to be the promise of a crown to come.

The page adds one thing more: that the memory of Mines and Minaeos is kept again on 1 August, and it names no second day for Cyril. Those two have their own entry on that other day and are read there; this folder is Cyril's alone, and it takes its name from this day because the calendar gives him no epithet to take it from.

saint.gr prints no hymn for him. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands, bare as it is.

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery does not name him on its own line for 29 октября ст. ст., but the first of the two lives it carries for [Anastasia the Roman](/saints/anastasia-the-roman) does, and it is the fullest account of him any calendar in this corpus gives. While she was being tortured she grew faint from the bleeding and asked for water, and «некто Кирилл», a certain Cyril standing by, pitied her and gave her a drink; and the torturers, deciding that a man who would do that must be a secret Christian, seized him and put him to death as well. The calendar gives him no day of his own, no name link and no year, so this folder keeps the Greek row it had and takes no Russian one.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 29 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/29/index.aspx) and [the notice of the three](https://www.saint.gr/2836/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 29 октября ст. ст., which names him inside its life of Anastasia and not on its own line — [that life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life6413.htm), read 4 October 2026.*
