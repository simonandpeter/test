# Mary of Cleopas

doxologia.ro's calendar names her and gives no life. Its line for 23 mai reads «Sfânta Mironosiță Maria lui Cleopa», and the page that line links to carries no Viață tab and no Tropar of her own — only the name, the day, the readings appointed for it, and a link to the paraclis of the holy and glorious myrrh-bearing women as a company. What the calendar tells of her is the two things its line holds: that she is a myrrh-bearer, and that she is Mary of Cleopas.

It gives her no years, no country, no kinship beyond the name Cleopas, and no account of her death, and this corpus does not supply from elsewhere what the page it cites does not print.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 23 mai — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/23-mai) and [her page](https://doxologia.ro/sfanta-mironosita-maria-lui-cleopa), which prints no life; read 20 September 2026.*
