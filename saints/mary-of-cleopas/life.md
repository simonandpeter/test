# Mary of Cleopas

doxologia.ro's calendar names her and gives no life. Its line for 23 mai reads «Sfânta Mironosiță Maria lui Cleopa», and the page that line links to carries no Viață tab and no Tropar of her own — only the name, the day, the readings appointed for it, and a link to the paraclis of the holy and glorious myrrh-bearing women as a company. What the calendar tells of her is the two things its line holds: that she is a myrrh-bearer, and that she is Mary of Cleopas.

It gives her no years, no country, no kinship beyond the name Cleopas, and no account of her death, and this corpus does not supply from elsewhere what the page it cites does not print.

The Greek calendar keeps her on the same day and names her household. saint.gr says she was the wife of Cleopas and one of the women who followed our Lord Jesus Christ and served in his work; that when the dread sacrifice of Golgotha was being accomplished and the disciples were hiding and scattered, she was watching at the place of the condemnation and stood by through the agony on the Cross and afterwards the burial; and that she was one of the myrrh-bearers who were counted worthy to hear on the Sunday morning the glad message of the Resurrection.

The sons of this Mary, the page says, were Joses and James, and the latter was numbered among the twelve apostles and was called the Less, to tell him apart from the other James, the brother of John the Theologian. She was present also at the descent of the Holy Spirit in the upper room; and when the first Church was formed in Jerusalem she went on offering it her services, for the spreading of the true faith and for every good and charitable work. The page adds that a portion of her holy relics is kept at the monastery of the Panagia Tourliani on Mykonos.
