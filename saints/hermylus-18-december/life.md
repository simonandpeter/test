# Hermylus (18 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 18 December keeps two martyrs together, Phocas and Hermylus, and the whole of what its page says of them is one sentence: the holy Phocas and Hermylus were martyred by the sword. There is nothing before that sentence and nothing after it — no year, no emperor, no judge, no place, no relic and no hymn.

What the page carries besides is an epigram in which the two of them argue over who shall go first: I shall be cut first, says Hermylus; no, says Phocas, but Phocas, Hermylus. Nothing is drafted from it. Because the entry names them both, each has a folder here and each relates the other, [Phocas](/saints/phocas-18-december) beside this one.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3292/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
