# Anna (Ivashkina)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names her first of two in one line on 10 декабря and gives neither a life. The line is «Свв. Анны и Татианы испп (1948).» — the holy confessors Anna and Tatiana, 1948 — and the calendar’s own name pages add the surnames the line leaves out: «Анна (Ивашкина), исп.» and «Татиана (Бякирева), исп.»

That is the whole of the record. The word the calendar uses of her is исповедница, a confessor: one who suffered for the faith and was not killed for it. She died in 1948, a decade after the worst of the persecution and in the years the Russian church counts its confessors from; and she is set down with [Tatiana (Byakireva)](/saints/tatiana-byakireva) in one line and under one year, the calendar saying nothing of what joined the two women.

The day page carries eleven hymns and not one is under either name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 10 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261210.html); read 13 October 2026.*
