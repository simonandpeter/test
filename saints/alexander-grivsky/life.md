# Alexander (Grivsky)

He is the second name on the one line the Russian calendar's 11 October gives to the Soviet years: «Сщмчч. Филарета и Александра пресвитеров (1918)» — Philaret and Alexander, hieromartyrs, presbyters, 1918. The day line gives only the forenames; the site's index of names is what tells the two apart, heading his own page «Александр (Гривский), иерей, сщмч.», a priest of the simple grade and a hieromartyr, which is to say a priest killed for the faith.

His index entry gives him one day more than [Philaret (Velikanov)](/saints/philaret-velikanov) has: besides this one and 26 January with the whole company of the new martyrs, it keeps him on 8 June with the saints of Pskov. That is the only thing on either page that puts him anywhere at all, and it is a commemoration rather than a diocese, so nothing further is read off it. There is no life. The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian saints are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026 even with a browser user-agent, so what is here is a forename, a surname, a grade, a year and three days.

*After the Russian church calendar's [11 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261011.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13825.html); read 4 October 2026.*
