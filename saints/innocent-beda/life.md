# Innocent (Beda)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him in one line for 24 декабря ст. ст.: «Прмч. Иннокентия (1928).» The line declines the forename to «Иннокентия» and gives no surname, no place, no year of birth and no word of narrative. The rank word «Прмч.» that governs the name and the year it closes with, 1928, are the whole of what it says of him.

Behind that «Иннокентия» stands the calendar's own name page 9172, which carries the fuller form «Иннокентий (Беда), архимандрит, сщмч.» — the surname the day's line leaves out, and an archimandrite's grade. The two pages do not agree on his rank: «прмч.» on the day, a venerable-martyr, and «сщмч.» on the name page, a hieromartyr. Both are the calendar's own and this folder records both rather than choose.

That page lists two days, this 24 декабря and «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. It carries no life, and neither does the day page.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 24 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261224.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9172.html), which carries no life; read 13 October 2026.*
