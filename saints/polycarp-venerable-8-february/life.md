# Polycarp (8 February)

saint.gr names him with one companion and gives the pair a single sentence. Its calendar for 8 February prints «Όσιοι Φιλάδελφος και Πολύκαρπος», and the page behind that line says that the venerable fathers [Philadelphus](/saints/philadelphus-venerable-8-february) and Polycarp were ascetic monks and fell asleep in peace. There is no monastery here, no wilderness, no country, no century, no relics and no hymn.

Over that sentence the page prints a couplet on the two of them: the end of life was indeed a friend, it says, both to Philadelphus and to Polycarp. Πολύκαρπος, the much-fruited, it glosses as the very fruitful man; and that is the whole of what it offers by way of a character.

He is not the hieromartyr Polycarp of Smyrna, the disciple of John, whom both the Greek and the Romanian calendars keep in the same month on 23 February; nothing on this page is a bishop's, and its two fathers are monks and ascetics who died in peace. The same page records that their memory is repeated on 25 October. That day's page was not read, and the day kept here is the day page that was.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/532/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
