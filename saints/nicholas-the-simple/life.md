# Nicholas the Simple

saint.gr's calendar for 14 October keeps «Όσιος Νικόλαος ο απλός και θαυματουργός δούκας Τζερνηγοβιγίας “ὁ ἐν τῷ Σπηλαίῳ”» — Nicholas the simple and wonderworking, duke of Tzernigovia, the one in the cave — and then says that we have no further details of the venerable one's life. That sentence is the whole of the page.

So what the calendar holds of him is the line: a rank, Όσιος, the venerable; two epithets, the simple and the wonderworker; a title in the world, duke of Tzernigovia; and a cave, which the page names and does not place. It gives no year and no century, no monastery, no account of how he left his dukedom or how he died, and prints neither a verse nor a hymn for him. This folder holds that and does not fill it in.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 14 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/14/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2753/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
