# Acepsimas (3 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 3 Νοεμβρίου names «Όσιος Ακεψιμάς», and he is not the Acepsimas the same day keeps as a bishop and a martyr: this one lived in the fourth century, the page says, in the time of the emperor Theodosius I the Great, and died in peace. After a life in the world, in which he was marked out for his piety, he withdrew to the desert life and built his house inside a cave.

He lived on the scant produce of nature, the notice goes on, and sometimes passers-by and devout people brought him food; of that he kept a little, only what was strictly needed to feed him, and gave the rest to the poor. Then he would invite his benefactors into the cave where he lived, and there he paid their kindness back with teaching in the word of God.

The venerable one worked many wonders, the page says, and gave up his spirit to the Lord in peace. The epigram set over him makes the same point: having melted himself away with abstinence and with labours, Acepsimas passed over to where there are no labours. No place is named and no year is given beyond the reign; no hymn is printed; and the page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου».

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/03/index.aspx) and [his notice](https://www.saint.gr/2871/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
