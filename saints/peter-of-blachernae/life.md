# Peter of Blachernae

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 16 mai reads «Sfântul Mucenic Petru din Vlaherne» — the holy martyr Peter of Blachernae — and that is the whole of what the Romanian calendar prints about him. His own page on the site carries the name twice, as heading and as title, and nothing else: no Viață tab, no Tropar tab, no Acatist, no note on his century, his country or his death.

So this folder holds one fact and the admission that there is only one: that the Romanian church keeps a martyr named Peter on 16 May, and calls him of Blachernae, after the quarter of Constantinople. Nothing further has been read for him, and nothing further is asserted here. Another calendar may say more, and no other calendar was checked.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 16 mai — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/16-mai) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-petru-din-vlaherne), which carries no life and no troparion; read 24 September 2026.*
