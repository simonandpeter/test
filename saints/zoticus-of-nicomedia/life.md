# Zoticus of Nicomedia

Zoticus was one of the martyrs kept together on 22 August — Agathonicus, Zoticus, Theoprepius (Bogolep), Acindynus, Severian, Zeno and others — who died for Christ under Maximian. Agathonicus, of the noble family of the Hippasii, lived at Nicomedia; learned in the Scriptures, he turned many pagans to faith in Christ, among them the first member of the Senate, the princeps.

The count Eutolmius, sent to the Pontic country, crucified at Karpen the disciples of the Christian Zoticus who refused to sacrifice to the idols, and brought Zoticus to Nicomedia, where — the Prologue carries on — he seized and bound Agathonicus, Principus, Theoprepius, Acindynus, Severian, Zeno and many others, and led them bound towards Byzantium; on the way Zoticus, Theoprepius and Acindynus died of their wounds and exhaustion, Severian was killed near Chalcedon, and Agathonicus with the rest was taken to Thrace, to Selymbria, where after torture before the emperor himself they were beheaded.

The Romanian calendar keeps him on 22 August with his company, and doxologia.ro prints one life under five of their names, headed for the four of them «Zotic, Teoprepie, Severian, Achindin». It says the count Eutolmius, sent into Pontus to kill Christians, came by ship to the place called Carpin and there found Zoticus with his disciples confessing Christ; that he condemned them to death on the cross and took Zoticus with him back to Nicomedia, where [Agathonicus](/saints/agathonicus-of-nicomedia) was already taken. The prisoners were led into Thrace, and in the country called Potama the count killed Zoticus together with [Theoprepius](/saints/theoprepius-of-nicomedia) and [Acindynus](/saints/acindynus-of-nicomedia), because they could no longer walk for the beatings they had been given.
