# Paregorius of Patara

saint.gr's calendar for 18 February prints them on one line, «Άγιοι Λέων και Παρηγόριος οι Μάρτυρες οι εν Πατάροις της Λυκίας Αθλήσαντες» — the martyrs Leo and Paregorius who did their contending at Patara in Lycia — and the page behind it says the synaxaristes record that they were seized there. Paregorius was seized first, and was martyred through many and various torments; and Leo used to pray at the place where they had thrown Paregorius's body, weeping for the death of his beloved friend and martyr, and longing to follow the same road of martyrdom himself. And the occasion was found.

Of Paregorius's own martyrdom the page says only that it came through many and various torments, and that his body was thrown out somewhere; it is [Leo](/saints/leo-of-patara)'s grief at that place, and his longing to follow, that the account turns on. Leo afterwards broke the candlesticks and lamps in the temple of the idols at a heathen feast, confessed Christ before the governor, and was beaten with bull's sinews and thrown from a precipice.

The page gives neither of them a homeland, a year, an emperor or an office, and prints no hymn for either. Its couplet for Paregorius turns on his name, which is the word for consolation: Paregorius, having given his flesh to the torments, has the crowns for a great consolation. He is not the Paregorius this corpus keeps among the martyrs of Samosata; nothing on this page names Samosata, and the two are kept on two different days.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3735/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
