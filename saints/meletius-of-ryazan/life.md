# Meletius of Ryazan

saint.gr's calendar for 14 Ιανουαρίου names «Άγιος Μελέτιος επίσκοπος του Ριαζάν», Meletius bishop of Ryazan, and the page behind the line says only that it has nothing to say: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ρώσου Αγίου» — we have no details of the life of this Russian saint.

What the page holds besides that is its header, which dates his death to 1900, a gloss on his name — Meletius, the man of study — and a link to the synaxis of all the saints who shone in Siberia. There is no birthplace, no year of birth, no account of his episcopate, no manner of death, and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 14 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/14/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4251/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
