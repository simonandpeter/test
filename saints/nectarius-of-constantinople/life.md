# Nectarius of Constantinople

saint.gr's calendar for 11 Οκτωβρίου keeps three patriarchs of Constantinople in one entry, and Nectarius is the first of them. He came, the page says, from Tarsus of Cilicia; he was holy and devout in his life and a senator in rank. At the Second Ecumenical Council, after the resignation of Gregory the Theologian and the deposition of the heretical patriarch Maximus, he was elected patriarch of Constantinople by the common vote of people and clergy and with the judgement of the emperor Theodosius — although he was a layman, and indeed unbaptised, so that he was baptised first.

The page gives his patriarchate as 381 to 397, and says that having shepherded the church with great piety he fell asleep in peace. Its distich for him plays on his name: «Ὁ Νεκτάριος οὐ τὸ τοῦ μύθου Λόγε, / Ἀληθινὸν δέ, σῆς τρυφῆς νέκταρ πίνει» — Nectarius drinks not the nectar of the fable, O Word, but the true nectar of your delight.

The two kept with him are his brother [Arsacius](/saints/arsacius-of-constantinople) and [Sisinnius](/saints/sisinnius-of-constantinople), each with his own paragraph and his own distich on the same page. saint.gr prints no hymn under the entry. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/11/index.aspx) and [the life the three patriarchs share](https://www.saint.gr/2086/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
