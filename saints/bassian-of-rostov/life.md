# Bassian of Rostov

saint.gr's calendar for 25 January names Bassian, archbishop of Rostov in Russia, and gives a life of three sentences. It says that he lived in Russia between the fourteenth and the fifteenth century; that from a young age he loved the monastic life and became a monk at the monastery of Saint Paphnutius of Borovsk; that in 1506 he was elected archbishop of the city of Rostov; and that he fell asleep in peace in the year 1516.

The page's heading dates him «(; - 1516)» and gives no year of birth. Its own sentence setting his life between the fourteenth and the fifteenth century sits oddly beside the two years it then prints, and both readings are recorded here as the page has them rather than reconciled. It prints no troparion and no kontakion, and says nothing of his episcopate beyond its beginning and its end.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 25 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/25/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1314/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
