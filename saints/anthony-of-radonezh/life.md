# Anthony of Radonezh

The venerable Anthony of Radonezh, Andrew Medvedev in the world, was born on 6 October 1792 in the town of Lyskovo in the province of Novgorod, into a family in the service of the Georgian prince George. Filled with love for the monastic life, he entered in 1818 the monastery of the Dormition of the Mother of God at Sarov, and in 1822 the house of Vysokogorsky at Arzamas, where he was ordained presbyter.

In 1831 he was called by Philaret, metropolitan of Moscow, to the Lavra of Saint Sergius, where he was marked out for the holiness of his life and for his virtues. He fell asleep in peace in 1877. The canonical act of his glorification was performed by Alexy, patriarch of Moscow, in 1997, and his relics are kept in the Lavra of the Holy Trinity and Saint Sergius at Moscow.
