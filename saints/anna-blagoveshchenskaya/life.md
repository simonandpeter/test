# Anna (Blagoveshchenskaya)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names her for 26 февраля ст. ст. in three words — «Прмц. Анны (1937).» — a venerable-martyr, which is the calendar's word for a monastic who died a martyr's death, in 1937. The name link behind the line carries the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line leaves out and with her state in religion: «Анна (Благовещенская), инокиня, прмц.». «Инокиня» is a nun, and it is the only thing the calendar says of her place in the church.

It prints no life page for her and no hymn under her name. No monastery, no place and no manner of death is recorded here, because the calendar names none, and nothing is reasoned out from the surname or from the year; she is one of the new martyrs of the Russian church in the Soviet years and the line is the whole of what was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 26 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260226.html); read 3 October 2026.*
