# Martin, Pope of Rome

The Greek calendar names Martin, Pope of Rome, on 20 September together with
[Maximus the Confessor](/saints/maximus-the-confessor), and refers the reader to 13 April, where
his own commemoration and his life stand. That is the whole of the notice for this day.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 20 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/983/saint.aspx); read 26 August 2026.*
