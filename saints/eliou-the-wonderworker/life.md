# Eliou the Wonderworker

Eliou was one of the great teachers and masters of the desert. saint.gr tells him in a series of refusals: he saw hunger, and took no account of it; the cold, and made nothing of it; sleeplessness, and despised it; tyrants, and looked them in the face; ten thousand persecutions, and put them to shame. What, then, did he fear?

Three things, the page says he used to answer: the hour in which his soul was to go out of his body; the time in which he was to appear before God; and the moment in which the sentence upon him would come out from the God who judges justly and loves mankind. Eliou the Wonderworker fell asleep in peace.

The page gives no year, no country and no monastery, and prints no hymn. Over him it sets the couplet «Ἄλλος δέδεικται θαυματουργὸς Ἠλίας, / Ὁ θαυματουργὸς οὗτος ὄντως Ἠλίας» — another Elijah has been shown a wonderworker; this wonderworker is truly Elijah.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1396/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
