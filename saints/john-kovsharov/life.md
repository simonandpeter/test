# John (Kovsharov)

Ivan Mikhailovich Kovsharov came from Odessa, was a lawyer by training and had been a barrister. He was legal adviser to the Lavra in Petrograd, and a layman: the calendar gives him no rank in the Church, and no year of birth, where it gives a year to each of the other three shot with him. Three sentences in the life the four of them share are the whole of what it keeps of his life before the trial.

He was tried with [Benjamin of Petrograd](/saints/benjamin-of-petrograd) in the case opened on 10 June 1922, after the metropolitan's arrest on 29 May over the seizure of church valuables, with eighty-six others in the dock. The tribunal gave sentence on 5 July, and in the night of 12 to 13 August 1922 he was shot on the edge of Petrograd with the metropolitan, with [Sergius (Shein)](/saints/sergius-shein) and with the other layman of the parish society, [Yuri (Novitsky)](/saints/yuri-novitsky).

A cross stands over the symbolic grave of the Russian new martyrs in the brotherhood cemetery of the Lavra in Petrograd, and the day page prints no hymn under his name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 31 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260731.html) and [the life the four share](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life356.htm); read 13 October 2026.*
