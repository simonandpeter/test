# Irenarchus

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 28 noiembrie reads «Sfântul Mucenic Irinarh» — the holy martyr Irenarchus — and the page that stands under his name carries no Viață tab at all, only a troparion in the fourth tone, and that troparion is the common of a martyr with his name set into it. Nothing is said there of his country, his century, the persecution in which he suffered or the manner of his death, and nothing of the kind is written here.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 28 noiembrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/28-noiembrie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-irinarh); read 25 September 2026.*
