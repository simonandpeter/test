# Mark, companion of Mokianos

saint.gr keeps the two together on 3 Ιουλίου and gives them four sentences. They were pressed by the prefect Maximus to sacrifice to the idols, and because they refused and confessed Christ instead they were beheaded. The page names no city, no reign and no year, so none is recorded here; the prefect's name and the sword are the whole of the circumstance it gives. The other of the pair is [Mokianos](/saints/mokianos).

What the page does keep of them is two domestic details. As Mokianos was walking to the place of execution his wife and children followed him weeping; and the saint, instead of losing heart, told his family not to weep but to rejoice over the martyrdom God was counting him worthy of. Mark's wife, on the other hand, when her husband had been beheaded, took his head in her hands with joy, as a precious treasure. The distich over the entry has Mark offering his neck to the sword first and so cutting a road for Mokianos to his own passion.

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps the two of them on 3 июля ст. ст. — «Мчч. Мокия и Марка (IV).» — on the same menologion day as the Greek page this folder was made from, and sets them in the fourth century, which is the first year of any kind any calendar has given them. It gives this man no epithet anywhere, in the line or on his name page, and no life and no hymn.
