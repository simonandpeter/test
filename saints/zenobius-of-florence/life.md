# Zenobius of Florence

Zenobius came from Greece. He was a sophist and taught rhetoric at Florence, and he embraced the Christian faith against the stout resistance of his own parents — who, saint.gr says, were soon baptised themselves and became members of the Church of Christ. He was elected bishop of Florence in Italy.

He was bound in a spiritual friendship with [Ambrose](/saints/ambrose-of-milan), bishop of Milan, and with Damasus, bishop of Rome, whose years the page gives as 366 to 384 and who sent him to Constantinople on account of Arianism and the troubles the heretics were making in the Church. The God of the Trinity counted him worthy of the gift of wonderworking, and he fell asleep in peace in 390, the page says, under Honorius, emperor of the West — a reign that had not begun in 390, and the page is left as it stands rather than corrected here.
