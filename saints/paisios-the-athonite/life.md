# Paisios the Athonite

Paisios was born to devout parents, Prodromos and Eulampia Eznepidi, at Pharasa
in Cappadocia on 25 July 1924, a few days before the people of Pharasa left their ancestral
ground for Greece. At his baptism Arsenius of Cappadocia, the priest of Pharasa, named him
Arsenius — so as to leave a monk behind in his own place, as he put it. The family settled in
Greece, and he came in time to the Holy Mountain, where he is called the Athonite. The Greek
calendar keeps him on 20 September.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 20 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/3200/saint.aspx); read 26 August 2026.*
