# Christopher of Georgia

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life. Its whole account is two sentences: the venerable Christopher was a monk in the monastery of Saint John the Baptist in the desert of David-Gareji, which the page calls the Thebaid of Georgia; and having practised asceticism in a way pleasing to God, he fell asleep in peace in the year 1771. The head line over his name adds nothing but the same year and a blank where the year of his birth would be.

The page gives him no name in the world, no birthplace, no family, no abbot and no disciples; it tells nothing of his asceticism beyond the one adverb, records no relics and no burial place, and prints neither apolytikion nor kontakion for him. It notes of the name itself only that Χριστόφορος is Christ and φέρω together, the one who reveals Christ and his teaching by his works and his words.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/11/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2245/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
