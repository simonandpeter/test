# John of Monemvasia

saint.gr's calendar for 21 Οκτωβρίου names John, the new martyr from Monemvasia, and heads his page «(1755 - 1773)». He was a priest's son, the page says, born in the village of Geraki in the Monemvasia country. When the Albanians came into the Peloponnese in 1770 they cut his father's throat, and carried him and his mother off to Larissa; John was fifteen years old. There he was sold together with his mother to a Turk.

Because that man had no children he wanted to adopt John, having first made a Muslim of him; and the great and various efforts he made to that end bore no fruit at all. The young man answered, always and in the same words, «εγώ Τούρκος δεν γίνομαι, εγώ είμαι χριστιανός και χριστιανός θέλω να πεθάνω» — I will not become a Turk, I am a Christian and a Christian I mean to die.

Then the Turk began to torture him cruelly inside his own house. In the fortnight of the Dormition fast he pressed him to break it, and even set his mother to beg him to eat of the tempting dishes; but John answered that he was a priest's son and must keep the laws and customs of the Church better than laymen's sons do. Enraged at this, the man stabbed him in the heart, and he died on 21 October 1773. The page puts his skull and parts of his hands and feet at the monastery of Zerbitsa near Sparta.
