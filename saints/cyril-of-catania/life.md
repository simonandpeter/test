# Cyril of Catania

The Ohrid Prologue's entry for the Julian 21 March is the whole of what this folder records, and it is four sentences long. Cyril was from Antioch by birth and a disciple of the apostle [Peter](/saints/peter-the-apostle). He governed the flock of Christ well as bishop of Catania in Sicily. He had also the gift of working wonders by prayer: there was a bitter water in that place which could not be drunk, and in summer there was no other water to be had there, and by his prayer he turned it into sweet water fit to drink. He reposed in peace.

That is all. The Prologue gives no year and no century for him, nothing of how he came from Antioch to Sicily, and no account of his death beyond its peacefulness; and nothing else has been read for this folder, so it records that one entry and no more. The Greek calendar keeps another bishop of Catania on the same menologion day, [Beryllus](/saints/beryllus-of-catania), who is a different man with a different name. The Prologue prints no hymn for him, or for any saint of the day.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 21. март ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-04-03&prolog=1); read 4 October 2026.*
