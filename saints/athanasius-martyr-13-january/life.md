# Athanasius (13 January)

saint.gr's calendar for 13 Ιανουαρίου names a martyr Athanasius, and the page behind the line holds one fact and one refusal: «Ο Άγιος Αθανάσιος μαρτύρησε βασανιζόμενος με ραβδιά. Δεν έχουμε περισσότερες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου» — he was martyred, tormented with rods; we have no further details of his life.

The couplet the page prints over him says the same thing twice over: «Ῥάβδοις Ἀθανάσιε σαυτὸν ἐκδίδως, / Σπεύδων θανεῖν μέν, ζῆν δὲ πολλῷ κρειττόνως» — to the rods, Athanasius, thou givest thyself up, hastening indeed to die, but to live far better. There is no city, no emperor, no year, and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 13 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/13/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1413/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
