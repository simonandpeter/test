# Secundinus of Numidia

The Greek calendar keeps a company of martyrs of Numidia on 29 Απριλίου: the hieromartyrs [Agapius](/saints/agapius-of-numidia) and Secundinus, the virgins [Tertulla](/saints/tertulla-of-numidia) and [Antonia](/saints/antonia-of-numidia), and with them an unnamed woman martyr together with her two children. saint.gr's whole notice of them is a single sentence, and it is about the two bishops.

Agapius and Secundinus were born in Spain, it says, and were bishops in the region of Numidia in Africa; and they suffered a martyr's death in the year 259, under the emperor Valerian, whose reign the page dates 253 to 259, together with the virgins Tertulla and Antonia and an anonymous woman martyr with her two children. The page heads every one of the five entries «(; - 259)», with no year of birth for any of them, and nothing else is printed: no city in Numidia, no judge, no manner of death, and no name at all for the woman or for her children.

saint.gr heads this one «Άγιος Σεκουνδίνος Ιερομάρτυρας Επίσκοπος Νουμιδίας».

*After saint.gr's calendar for 29 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/29/index.aspx) and [the entry for the company](https://www.saint.gr/364/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
