# Nikodemos of Meteora

saint.gr's calendar for 16 Αυγούστου names him and then says plainly that it knows nothing more. Its whole notice is two sentences: that the new venerable-martyr Nikodemos, from Meteora, was martyred for his piety on 16 August 1551; and that we have no further details of the venerable-martyr's life. There is no birthplace, no monastery, no manner of death and no hymn. The line itself calls him a monk, and that is all the station the calendar gives him.

The couplet of the synaxarion printed above the notice puts the question to him and lets him answer it: «Tί δαί συ Nικόδημε; ειπέ σον πάθος. / Tεθανάτωμαι δι' αγάπην Kυρίου» — and you, Nikodemos? tell your passion. I have been put to death for love of the Lord. The page sets him among the new martyrs who suffered after the fall of Constantinople, which it keeps as a synaxis of its own.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 16 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/16/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/799/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
