# Claudius (9 November)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 9 noiembrie reads «Sfântul Mucenic Claudiu», and the page it points to has no Viață tab: it carries the name, the day, and a Tropar — a common of the martyrs, sung to no one of them by name.

What the site does say is whom he is counted with. The hymn page is headed «Troparul Sfinţilor Mucenici Claudiu, Castor, Sempronian şi Nicostrat; al Sfinţilor Mucenici Onisifor şi Porfirie», which sets him in one company of four with [Castor](/saints/castor-martyr-9-november), [Sempronian](/saints/sempronian) and [Nicostratus](/saints/nicostratus), and distinguishes that company from the two martyrs kept the same day. That grouping is the whole of what the calendar gives beyond the name: no country, no century, no year, no emperor, no governor and no manner of death.

He is not the Claudius of Corinth the corpus already keeps on 31 ianuarie, nor the Claudius who is the companion of Susanna, nor the one of the Forty of Sebaste; nothing on this page joins him to any of them.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 9 noiembrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/9-noiembrie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-claudiu-0); read 25 September 2026.*
