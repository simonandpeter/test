# Sergius (Kochurov)

The calendar's own line is the whole of what is recorded of him here. The Сретенский monastery's calendar for 29 ноября ст. ст. prints «Сщмч. Сергия пресвитера (1941).» — the hieromartyr Sergius, a presbyter, 1941 — and the name page behind that forename gives him the surname the line leaves out together with his grade: «Сергий (Кочуров), иерей, сщмч.», a priest and a hieromartyr.

So nothing is said here of where he was born, where he served, when or by whom he was arrested, or where he died: no page read says any of it. The calendar carries no life page for him, and the one hymn the day page prints stands under another name. The year 1941 is the only year it gives him, and he is the one saint of the Soviet years on a day otherwise made of the third, the sixth and the twelfth centuries.

His name page lists one other day, «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church. One row per church is the schema, so that day is recorded here and he does not render on it.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 29 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261129.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14527.html), which carries no life; read 7 October 2026.*
