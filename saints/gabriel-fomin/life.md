# Gabriel (Fomin)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him on 9 апреля ст. ст. in three words, «Мч. Гавриила (1942).», and carries no life for him; the name link behind the line, page 14453, gives the surname in the brackets this calendar uses for one and the rank the line leaves out, «Гавриил (Фомин), мч.», and lists beside this day only the moveable synaxis of the new martyrs on 26 января. azbyka.ru’s calendar for the same civil day has the life, taken there from kolomna-hram.ru, and adds a second moveable synaxis of its own, the Собор Московских святых — synaxes both, and neither written into the feast.

He was born in 1882 in the village Ивачево in the Зарайский uyezd, the son of a peasant, Харитон Фомин. A deeply believing man, the page says, he helped the priest in church and did the duty of watchman there, which suited his household circumstances, since he was unmarried. He sometimes sang on the kliros in the Захариино-Елизаветинская church of the village Горетово, from which some who came to church rarely took him for a psalmist — a mistake he had to answer in court for, eleven years later, in the last words any source read here gives him.

In 1925 and 1926 he lived in the village Городец in the Коломенский district and helped the priest of the Никольский church there. In 1928 he was deprived of the franchise; in 1932 he was arrested and sentenced to three years for not surrendering grain requisitions to the state, and the whole of his property, house and outbuildings together, was confiscated, so that he was left — the page says it in those words — literally without a roof. Coming back from imprisonment he settled in Сушково in the Коломенский district, now Луховицы.

There he began to help [Sergius (Lyubomudrov)](/saints/sergius-lyubomudrov), the priest serving in the Kazan church of that village, whom this corpus already keeps on 8 февраля ст. ст. and who was shot at Butovo in February 1938. **As a faithful man**, the page says — and that is the whole of the reason it gives — he was arrested on 7 September 1938 and accused of spreading counter-revolutionary slander. The same day the investigator charged him with conversations about the imminent fall of the soviet power: among passengers at Сушково station in June 1937, and among kolkhozniks in November 1937.

Because he would not admit charges he held to be false, «свидетели» were called — some of them, the page says, served as secret collaborators of the NKVD — and they confirmed their own written denunciations. He was interrogated again after that and again did not confirm what the false witnesses had said; face-to-face confrontations were then arranged between him and them, and in those too he showed firmness and courage. The investigation closed on 2 October 1938.

The indictment put the result in a sentence that concedes the whole of it: «допрошенный по делу в качестве обвиняемого Фомин Г. X. виновным себя не признал, но достаточно изобличается тремя свидетельскими показаниями и тремя очными ставками» — questioned in the case as the accused, Fomin did not admit his guilt, but is sufficiently incriminated by three witness statements and three confrontations.

He was held afterwards in the Taganka prison in Moscow. On 25 April 1939 a doctor examined him and established tuberculosis of the right ankle joint, which, the page says, left him little chance of surviving his imprisonment. He spent almost a year in that prison neither sentenced nor acquitted, while the authorities rebuilt the system of «троек» and brought back court hearings — held, the page notes, in the same closed regime as the sittings of the troikas had been. The hearing in his case came only on 16 August 1939.

What he said at it, the page gives whole: «Я в церкви был сторожем, и были случаи, пел на клиросе. Виновным себя не признаю, потому что никаких контрреволюционных действий никогда не производил. Со свидетелями, которые вызваны по моему делу, — я их не знаю и в ссорах никогда с ними не состоял, и те разговоры, которые указаны в обвинительном заключении, я никогда нигде не разглашал.»

I was a watchman in the church, and there were occasions when I sang on the kliros. I do not admit my guilt, because I never carried out any counter-revolutionary actions. As for the witnesses called in my case — I do not know them and was never in a quarrel with them, and those conversations set down in the indictment I never divulged anywhere. And the last of it goes back to the kliros: he had never been a psalmist in the church, the witnesses had said so because there had been occasions when he sang there, and that is why they think him one.

The same day the court sentenced him to five years in a corrective labour camp and three years of «поражения в гражданских правах», deprivation of civil rights. He died on 22 April 1942 in the Унженский camp of the NKVD and was buried in an unmarked grave. **22 April 1942 is the civil date of the Julian 9 April**, so this calendar keeps him on the day he died, and the three words of its line are the only thing it says about him.
