# Eudocia the Child

The memory of this little girl is recorded in the Jerusalem Kanonarion, page 107, and is unknown to the Synaxaristes; that is the whole of the Greek notice for 10 September. This entry records it and nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 10 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2372/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
