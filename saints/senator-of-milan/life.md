# Senator of Milan

saint.gr says he came of Milan in Italy and attended the sessions of the Fourth Ecumenical Council, which met at Chalcedon in 451, as a presbyter. He was afterwards elected bishop of Milan, and he reposed in peace in 480. That is the whole of the page's account of him, in two sentences, and the council at Chalcedon is the only event in it.

It gives him no year of birth, tells nothing of the twenty-nine years between the council and his death, names no relics and prints no hymn. Nothing more is written here than the Greek calendar prints.
