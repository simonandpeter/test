# Zosimas of Cilicia

Zosimas was a monk of Cilicia, and he lived in the desert among the wild beasts. Domitian the governor took him and tortured him for Christ; and while Zosimas was under torture a lion ran out of the desert to the spectacle and spoke with a human voice, putting fear on everyone who stood there. The calendar's own line for the day is no more than «Sfântul Mucenic Zosima».

[Athanasius the Commentarisius](/saints/athanasius-the-commentarisius), seeing it, knew the power of Christ, the one true God, and believed in him. The two of them then made their end in the mountains, where a rock split open and they went into it and gave up their souls to the Lord. Doxologia.ro prints this same short account on both men's pages and adds nothing to it: no year, no place but Cilicia and the mountains, and nothing of either man before or after.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 4 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/4-ianuarie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-mucenic-zosima); read 19 September 2026.*
