# Theagenes

The Greek synaxarion says only that Theagenes, or Theogenes, was martyred by fire, and prints a verse: when Theagenes came close to the pyre he went into it, not troubled by the sight. The Greek calendar keeps him on 3 October; no year is given.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 3 Οκτωβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2584/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
