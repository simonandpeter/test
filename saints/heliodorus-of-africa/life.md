# Heliodorus of Africa

saint.gr's calendar names two men and counts the rest. The holy martyrs Heliodorus and [Venustus](/saints/venustus-of-africa) were martyred, the page says, together with seventy-five other Christians in Africa, in the reign of the emperor Diocletian, who held the empire from 284 to 305. That is the one sentence the page has for them, and beside it only the meaning it gives for his name, from the sun and a gift.

None of the seventy-five is named, and neither is the place in Africa, the governor who tried them or the manner of their death; there are no relics and no hymn. The page's line calls them «οι συν αυτοίς μαρτυρήσαντες», those who were martyred together with them, and stops. Nothing is claimed here beyond the two names, the rank, the number, the province, the reign and the day.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/06/index.aspx) and [the life of the company](https://www.saint.gr/1526/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
