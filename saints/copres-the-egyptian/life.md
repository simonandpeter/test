# Copres the Egyptian

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 9 iulie reads «Sfântul Cuvios Coprie Egipteanul», and the page the calendar links under that name carries the name again and nothing more: no life, no troparion, no year, no place, no teacher. What the Romanian calendar prints of him on this day is that he is a venerable father, that he is called Coprie, and that he was an Egyptian.

The same site keeps a Copris on 24 septembrie, and the corpus holds him: a man born on a dung-heap beside the monastery of Theodosius the Cenobiarch and brought up by those monks, in Palestine. This folder is kept apart from that one on the strength of this line's own word, Egipteanul, and of the different day; the page offers nothing further either way.

The Ohrid Prologue keeps him on the Julian 9 July, the same menologion day the Romanian calendar keeps him on — and it is the Romanian calendar’s word «Cuvios», a venerable, that says which of this day’s two entries is his. The book prints the same two names twice: a pair of venerable-martyrs beheaded under Julian the Apostate, and then this pair, of whom it says that though of the same names they were different men from the first. These two were Egyptians, venerables and wonderworkers, and they reposed in the Lord at the end of the fourth century, in deep old age — which is the first date this folder has ever carried, the Romanian page having printed his name and nothing more.

Of his companion the book tells a conversion. That Patermuthius was first the leader of a band of robbers: he had climbed onto the roof of a God-fearing woman’s house, meaning to get in from the roof and plunder it, when sleep fell upon him, and in the sleep he saw Someone who threatened him to do evil no more and to repent. He was not only baptised but tonsured. That man has no folder in this corpus and takes none here, the two Patermuthii it does hold being a martyr of another persecution and the first pair’s elder, whom this entry exists to tell him apart from.

Both of them, it says, were great wonderworkers: by the grace of God they healed people of every torment and sickness, turned sinners to the right way, and prophesied. A sinner lying on his deathbed begged Patermuthius to lengthen his life so that he might repent, and after the prayer the saint told him that God was adding three years to his life. The sinner repented, and died exactly three years later.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 9 iulie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/9-iulie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-coprie-egipteanul), which prints the name only; read 24 September 2026; and the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 9. јул ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-07-22&prolog=1), read 6 October 2026.*
