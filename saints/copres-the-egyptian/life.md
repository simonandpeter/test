# Copres the Egyptian

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 9 iulie reads «Sfântul Cuvios Coprie Egipteanul», and the page the calendar links under that name carries the name again and nothing more: no life, no troparion, no year, no place, no teacher. What the Romanian calendar prints of him on this day is that he is a venerable father, that he is called Coprie, and that he was an Egyptian.

The same site keeps a Copris on 24 septembrie, and the corpus holds him: a man born on a dung-heap beside the monastery of Theodosius the Cenobiarch and brought up by those monks, in Palestine. This folder is kept apart from that one on the strength of this line's own word, Egipteanul, and of the different day; the page offers nothing further either way.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 9 iulie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/9-iulie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-coprie-egipteanul), which prints the name only; read 24 September 2026.*
