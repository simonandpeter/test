# Peter of Sebaste

doxologia.ro's calendar for 9 ianuarie names him «Sfântul Ierarh Petru al Sevastiei» and gives him no life of his own. What the site holds about him is one article about his family, and that is the whole of what is set down here. He was one of the children of the household of [Basil the Great](/saints/basil-the-great), which the article calls one of the best known families in the history of the Orthodox Church.

In the fourth century, it says, that household gave a remarkable example of how holiness brings forth holiness: the parents Basil and Emilia, the grandmother Macrina the Elder, and five of the children — Basil the Great, [Gregory of Nyssa](/saints/gregory-of-nyssa), Peter of Sebaste, the venerable Naucratius, a great hermit and wonderworker in the desert of Sinai, and Macrina the Younger — became eight saints in the calendar of the Church. Of Peter's own see at Sebaste, of when he was made its bishop or when he died, the page says nothing, and nothing has been supplied here from elsewhere.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day. saint.gr dates him 349 to 392 and says he was born at Caesarea in Cappadocia, brother of [Basil the Great](/saints/basil-the-great) and of [Gregory of Nyssa](/saints/gregory-of-nyssa), and that it was his sister [Macrina](/saints/macrina-sister-of-basil-the-great) who guided him into the strict ascetic life. He became a monk, and then abbot of the coenobium his brother Basil had founded; Basil ordained him presbyter in 370 and bishop of Sebaste in Greater Armenia in 380. He took part in the Second Ecumenical Council at Constantinople in 381, and he reposed in peace in the year 392.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 9 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/9-ianuarie) and [the article on his family](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-ierarh-petru-al-sevastiei); read 19 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 9 Ιανουαρίου — [the Greek day](https://www.saint.gr/01/09/index.aspx) and [the Greek life](https://www.saint.gr/1101/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026.*
