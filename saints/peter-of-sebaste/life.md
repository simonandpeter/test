# Peter of Sebaste

doxologia.ro's calendar for 9 ianuarie names him «Sfântul Ierarh Petru al Sevastiei» and gives him no life of his own. What the site holds about him is one article about his family, and that is the whole of what is set down here. He was one of the children of the household of [Basil the Great](/saints/basil-the-great), which the article calls one of the best known families in the history of the Orthodox Church.

In the fourth century, it says, that household gave a remarkable example of how holiness brings forth holiness: the parents Basil and Emilia, the grandmother Macrina the Elder, and five of the children — Basil the Great, Gregory of Nyssa, Peter of Sebaste, the venerable Naucratius, a great hermit and wonderworker in the desert of Sinai, and Macrina the Younger — became eight saints in the calendar of the Church. Of Peter's own see at Sebaste, of when he was made its bishop or when he died, the page says nothing, and nothing has been supplied here from elsewhere.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 9 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/9-ianuarie) and [the article on his family](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-ierarh-petru-al-sevastiei); read 19 September 2026.*
