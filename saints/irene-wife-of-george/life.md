# Irene, wife of George of Constantinople

The Russian church calendar names her in one clause of one line, as the wife of a confessor, and that clause is the whole of what it says of her. Its line for 13 мая ст. ст. reads «Свв. Георгия исп. с супругою Ириною и чадами (IX)» — the saints George the confessor with his wife Irene and their children, in the ninth century — and behind it stands a name link of her own, headed simply «Ирина Константинопольская», with no rank word after it.

The life page is her husband's, and she enters it at its end. [George of Constantinople](/saints/george-of-constantinople) refused the emperor Феофил's demand that he renounce the veneration of the icons, had his goods taken and was dragged through the streets with a rope about his neck and imprisoned; and after that, the page says, he was sent into exile with his wife Irene and their children, and died there having borne many sorrows. So the calendar says that she followed him into exile and that the Church counts her a saint, and nothing else: not when or where she died, not how many the children were, not one of their names.

That is set down here as it stands. She is kept on this day because she is named on it, and no rank was carried across to her from her husband's. The day page prints hymns for three of the saints it heads, and none under her name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 13 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260513.html), [her husband's life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1060.htm) and [her name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/6947.html); read 8 October 2026.*
