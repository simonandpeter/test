# Gregory, uncle of Eustratius of Mount Olympus

saint.gr's calendar for 9 Ιανουαρίου keeps two men together under one line and one page, «Όσιοι Βασίλειος και Γρηγόριος οι Θαυματουργοί» — [Basil](/saints/basil-uncle-of-eustratius) and Gregory, the wonderworkers. The page says of them that they were the uncles of [Eustratius of Mount Olympus](/saints/eustratius-of-mount-olympus), whom the same day keeps, and that they lived in the ninth century. Eustratius's own page adds that they were his mother's brothers.

Gregory is the one the page places by name: «Ο Όσιος Γρηγόριος ασκήτεψε στη Μονή των Αυγάρων» — he lived the ascetic life at the monastery of Augarus, on Olympus in Bithynia. That is the monastery to which Eustratius came in his twentieth year, and where his uncles received him.

Of the end of his life saint.gr says what it says of both men: that they were counted worthy to receive from God the gift of working wonders, and that they fell asleep in peace. It gives no year within the century, and prints no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 9 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/09/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1105/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
