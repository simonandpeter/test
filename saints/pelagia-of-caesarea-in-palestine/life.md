# Pelagia of Caesarea in Palestine

saint.gr's calendar for 25 Μαρτίου names the holy martyrs Pelagia and Theodosia together on one line, and the page behind that line carries one sentence of narrative and nothing else: «Οι Αγίες Μάρτυρες Πελαγία και Θεοδοσία, μαρτύρησαν στην Καισάρεια της Παλαιστίνης το έτος 361 μ.Χ. και τελειώθηκαν διά ξίφους» — the holy martyrs Pelagia and Theodosia were martyred at Caesarea in Palestine in the year 361, and were perfected by the sword. That is the whole of what the source says about her.

Above the sentence the page prints the menaion's couplet for the two of them, «Θεοδοσία και Πελαγία ξίφει, Πέλαγος εύρον δωρεών Θεού Λόγου» — Theodosia and Pelagia, by the sword, found an ocean of the gifts of God the Word — which makes the play on her name that the page then glosses outright: «ΠΕΛΑΓΙΑ (από το πέλαγος) = η ικανή να αντιμετωπίζει το τρικυμισμένο πέλαγος της ζωής», the woman able to face the storm-tossed ocean of life.

The page heads her «(; - 361)». It gives no city of birth, no account of the arrest or the trial, no emperor, no relic and no hymn. Her fellow martyr [Theodosia](/saints/theodosia-of-caesarea-in-palestine) stands beside her in the calendar line, in the couplet and in the one sentence, and the two women share a single page.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 25 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/25/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/95/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
