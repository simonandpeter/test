# Euthymius the Wonderworker

saint.gr begins by saying where he is not to be found. The venerable Euthymius is unknown to the Synaxarist of Saint Nikodemos and to the Menaia, it says; he is met with in the Synaxarist of Delehaye, but without any biographical matter, and his biographical details exist in the Lavriotic codex I 70, folio 1526. Everything the page then prints comes from that codex.

According to it, Euthymius longed for the ascetic life from his childhood. He was pressed hard by his parents to marry, but he broke away from their will and took refuge in a monastery, where he was tonsured a monk. At first he was given the service of the cook and the keeping of the gate; and having gone through the whole round of the services, giving to the brethren and teaching them by his obedience and his humility, he was chosen abbot of the house by the fathers of the monastery.

But because he did not want the passing glory of men or the vanity of this life, he left the monastery secretly and settled in a place desert and quiet, where he went on with the work of prayer and asceticism. There he cultivated the virtues and was counted worthy by God of the gift of working wonders. He came to a great age and fell asleep in peace. The page names no monastery, no country, no century and no year, and it prints neither couplet nor hymn.
