# Iconius, Bishop of Gortyna

Iconius, bishop of Gortyna, took part in the Third Ecumenical Council, summoned at Ephesus in 431 to condemn the heretical opinions of Nestorius of Constantinople. He signed the acts of the council with the bishops Anderius of Chersonesus, Paul of Lampe and Zenobius of Knossos; the acts record that he spoke twice at the council, rejecting the heresy of Nestorius and making an Orthodox confession of faith, and what he and the three other Cretan bishops said is written in them. The Greek calendar keeps him on 9 September with the memory of the council.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 9 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/4470/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
