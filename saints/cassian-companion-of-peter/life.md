# Cassian, companion of Peter

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαρτίου prints one line for a company of five and their companions: «Άγιοι Πέτρος, Μαρκιανός, Ιωάννης, Θέκλα, Κασσιανός οι Μάρτυρες και οι συν αυτών μαρτυρήσαντες» — the holy martyrs Peter, Marcian, John, Thekla and Cassian, and those who were martyred with them. Cassian is named last of the five. The page behind the line says two things and stops.

The first is where they suffered: «Οι Άγιοι αυτοί Μάρτυρες Μαρτύρησαν στην Ρώμη» — these holy martyrs were martyred at Rome. The second is the site's own admission: «Δεν έχουμε περισσότερες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο και το μαρτύριο των Αγίων», we have no further details of the life and martyrdom of the saints. There is no year, no emperor, no judge, no manner of death, no relic and no hymn.

He is recorded here with [Peter](/saints/peter-companion-of-marcian), [Marcian](/saints/marcian-companion-of-peter), [John](/saints/john-companion-of-peter) and [Thekla](/saints/thekla-companion-of-peter); the companions the line adds without naming them have no folder. The gloss the page gives the name — «ΚΑΣΣΙΑΝΟΣ (Από το λατινικό cassis = περικεφαλαία) = ο γενναίος πρόμαχος», from the Latin for a helmet, the brave champion — is about the word and not about him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3869/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
