# Theotimus (5 November)

Theotimus is the third of the martyrs whom saint.gr's calendar keeps in one entry on 5 Νοεμβρίου, and the page tells the three of them as one story. They lived in the years of the emperor Maximian, when the governor of Palestine was Urbanus, and against the governor's name the page sets the year 298. They were young in age and of good presence, and they were martyred by being beaten with fists until they died. Nothing else of them is recorded: no country, no trial, no burial, no year of their own, and no hymn.

The couplet of the synaxarion for the entry names the first of them and counts the other two: «Τὸν Τιμόθεον, καὶ συναθλητὰς δύω / Κτείνουσι πυγμαί, καὶ Θεὸς τιμᾷ Λόγος» — fists kill Timothy and his two fellow-contenders, and God the Word honours him. Theotimus is one of the two; the others are [Timothy](/saints/timothy-5-november) and [Theophilus](/saints/theophilus-5-november).

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2891/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
