# Nilus of Athos the Myrrh-streaming

The page doxologia.ro prints for him is short, and this is what it says. He was born at Morea; and being already a hieromonk he went with his uncle to the Holy Mountain of Athos, and there struggled to the end of his days as a recluse in a mountainous and desert place that was called «Pietrele Sfinte», the Holy Stones. The page places his departure to the Lord in the seventeenth century. It names neither his uncle nor his parents, and gives no year.

When he had passed to the Lord, myrrh flowed from his holy relics — a stream that ran down from the summit of that mountain and poured into the sea. The page calls the wonder one never seen before, and says it brought the gravely ill to the place from everywhere. Their numbers so disturbed the stillness of one of his disciples that, at that disciple's prayer to the saint, the myrrh ceased at once to flow.

Doxologia ends by setting him among the great ascetics of the world, like the great desert fathers and wonderworkers of the first Christian ages. Its page for him carries no Tropar tab, so the calendar gives him no hymn.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 12 noiembrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/12-noiembrie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-nil-athonitul); read 25 September 2026.*
