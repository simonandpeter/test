# Theodore (Nedosekin)

The Сретенский monastery's calendar prints him last of the nine entries it puts on the Julian 17 April, and the whole of the entry is one line: «Сщмч. Феодора пресвитера (1942).» — the hieromartyr Theodore, a presbyter, and the year 1942. There is no life under his name on the day page and none behind it. The name link carries a fuller form than the line does, «Феодор (Недосекин), иерей, сщмч.», with a surname the line leaves out altogether and the word иерей, a priest, for the grade he held.

That is the whole of what the source gives, and the page behind the link gives nothing beyond it: no life, no icon, no parish, no article, no year or place of birth, no diocese, no arrest, no sentence and no word of his own. The bracketed year is the year of death and the grade is the only thing said about his state in life. It is written here as the calendar has it rather than filled out from elsewhere, and a reader who wants more of him will have to go to a source this folder has not read.

His second day on that page is 26 января, marked as the new martyrs', which is the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia — a commemoration of a whole company rather than a second day of his own, so it is not recorded here as a feast. Of the four hymns the day prints under «Молитвы», two belong to the abbot of Solovki the day keeps and two to the finding of another saint's relics; there is nothing under his name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 17 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260417.html), which gives him one line and no life, and [the calendar's page for his name](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9818.html); read 7 October 2026.*
