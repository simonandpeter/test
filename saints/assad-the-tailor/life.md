# Assad the Tailor

saint.gr's calendar names him and says in the same breath that it has nothing to tell: «Ο Άγιος Ασσάντ μαρτύρησε το έτος 1218 μ.Χ. Δεν έχουμε περισσότερες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου.» — the holy Assad was martyred in the year 1218, and we have no further details of his life. The day's line calls him «ο Ράπτης», the tailor, which is the only thing said of him besides the year. There is no place, no manner of death, no hymn, and nothing is supplied here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1080/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
