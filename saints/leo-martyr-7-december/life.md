# Leo (7 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 7 December keeps three martyrs in one entry and names all three: [Isidore](/saints/isidore-martyr-7-december), [Akepsimas](/saints/akepsimas-martyr-7-december) and Leo. The whole of the life it gives them is one sentence — that the holy Isidore, Akepsimas and Leo were martyred by fire. No country, no reign, no persecutor and no year is printed for any of them.

The couplet the page sets over the entry is addressed to Isidore and calls the other two his fellow contestants, so nothing is said of Leo apart from his name and the shared sentence. The gloss the site prints under the name — the brave and impetuous, like the lion — is its standing note on the name itself and not on this man.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3186/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
