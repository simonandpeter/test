# Nestor the Unlearned

saint.gr's calendar for 29 Οκτωβρίου names «Όσιος Νέστωρ ο αμαθής» — Nestor the Unlearned — and the page behind that line says one thing only: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ρώσου Οσίου», we have no details of the life of the Russian venerable one. So the Greek calendar keeps him on this day, calls him Russian, calls him unlearned, and tells nothing else about him at all.

There is no year on the page, no monastery, no place in Russia, no account of his death and no hymn. The only other thing printed under his name is the site's usual gloss of the name Nestor, which is about the king of Pylos in Homer and not about this man. The entry carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands, bare as it is.

The Russian calendar is the first source this folder has that says anything about him at all. saint.gr, which keeps him on 29 October, said only that he was Russian, that he was unlearned and that it had no details; this calendar gives him the monastery, the caves his relics lie in, and the century — «Нестора Некнижного, Печерского (XIV)» — and then two hymns, which are the only account of him anywhere.

They are an unusual pair, because what they praise him for is what the Greek page could only call him. He was clean and upright in heart and holy in soul, the troparion says, and simple, and could not read books himself; so he listened closely to those who read them, kept what he heard in himself, and made haste to put it into practice — and having fulfilled as an understanding man what he had taken in, he found the Kingdom of heaven. The kontakion says it more sharply still: wonderful in his saints, the Lord was wonderful in this Nestor particularly, who although bookless and simple surpassed the learned and put to shame the scribes who read and do not do.

No life page follows his name on the day page, and his name page prints the two days and the two hymns and nothing else. The second Russian calendar, azbyka.ru, repeats the same line and places his relics in the Far caves; it answered 403 to this corpus's fetcher for his own page on 4 October 2026 and was not worked around.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 29 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/29/index.aspx) and [his notice](https://www.saint.gr/2619/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 27 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261027.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/1602.html), read 4 October 2026.*
