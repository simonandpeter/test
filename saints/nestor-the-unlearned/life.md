# Nestor the Unlearned

saint.gr's calendar for 29 Οκτωβρίου names «Όσιος Νέστωρ ο αμαθής» — Nestor the Unlearned — and the page behind that line says one thing only: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ρώσου Οσίου», we have no details of the life of the Russian venerable one. So the Greek calendar keeps him on this day, calls him Russian, calls him unlearned, and tells nothing else about him at all.

There is no year on the page, no monastery, no place in Russia, no account of his death and no hymn. The only other thing printed under his name is the site's usual gloss of the name Nestor, which is about the king of Pylos in Homer and not about this man. The entry carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands, bare as it is.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 29 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/29/index.aspx) and [his notice](https://www.saint.gr/2619/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
