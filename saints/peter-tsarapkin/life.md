# Peter (Tsarapkin)

The calendar's own line is the whole of what is recorded of him here, and it is four words long. The Сретенский monastery's calendar for 26 ноября ст. ст. prints «Мч. Петра (после 1937).» — a martyr, after 1937 — and the name page behind that forename gives him the surname the line leaves out together with his grade: «Петр (Царапкин), мч.». He is a martyr and not a hieromartyr: the calendar gives him no holy order and no office, which is how it prints a layman.

So nothing is said here of where he was born, what he did, when or by whom he was arrested, or where he died: no page read says any of it. The calendar carries no life page behind his name page and none on the day itself. And the year is not a year but a bound — «после 1937», after 1937 — so the record says that he did not die before 1937 and does not say when he did.

His name page lists one other day, «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church. One row per church is the schema, so that day is recorded here and he does not render on it.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 26 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261126.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/10025.html); read 7 October 2026.*
