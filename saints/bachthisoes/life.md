# Bachthisoes

saint.gr names him last of three and gives two sentences for all of them together. Its line for 16 Μαΐου reads «Άγιοι Ισαάκιος, Συμεών και Βαχθισόης», and its page says that Saints [Isaac the Persian](/saints/isaac-the-persian-of-metsovo), [Simeon](/saints/simeon-martyr-16-may) and Bachthisoes were perfected by fire, and that the Synaxarion of Constantinople records their contest in the years of the emperor Constantine the Great, whose reign it dates 306 to 337, and of the Persian king Shapur II, whose reign it dates 309 to 379, after they had been brought before the king and had confessed their faith in Christ.

The synaxarion verse says only that the three of them breathed out their lives with one mind into a single fire. Of Bachthisoes in particular the page says nothing at all: no city, no year, no office, no parentage, no relics and no hymn. He has a name, a day, a manner of death and two companions, and that is the whole of what is set down here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 16 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/16/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/415/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
