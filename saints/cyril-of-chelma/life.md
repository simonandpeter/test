# Cyril of Chelma

The Russian calendar keeps him under two readings of one life, and the two say the same things in almost the same words. He was the enlightener of the Chud, born in the town of Beloozero, and he was tonsured in the house of the venerable Antony the Roman, where he went through various obediences for six years. Then, after three years of wandering in the wildernesses, he settled in the wild country of Kargopol.

There, by a direction from above, he chose Mount Chelma for his lasting dwelling. He had much sorrow to bear from the Chud; but his shining ascetic life and his mild instruction disposed many of them to receive holy baptism. Towards the end of his life he set up a house and a church in honour of the Theophany of the Lord. He spent fifty-two years on the Chelma mountain and died in deep old age, at eighty-two.

The day line closes with 1368 and neither page repeats it; the age and the span are what the pages give, and this folder does not subtract one from the other to reach a year of birth. The day page carries four hymns and two of them are his, a troparion in the fourth tone and a kontakion to which the page gives no tone at all; this folder keeps that kontakion without one rather than supplying a tone the calendar does not print.
