# Papas of Laranda

Papas came from the city of Laranda in Lycaonia and bore the most fearful tortures without ceasing for a moment to pray to God. When Galerius let loose a savage persecution against the Christians he suffered one of the harshest martyrdoms: first they struck him on the jaws with stones until they broke them, then hung him upside down and scourged him without mercy, then tore his sides and burned the wounds with lighted candles; but he bore it all with astonishing patience and glorified God to his last breath — he was ashamed, the verse says, to groan at the marks, having God near as helper. The Greek calendar keeps him on 14 September and says he is probably the same saint as the one kept on 16 March; the Romanian calendar keeps him on that 16 March.

Doxologia's life for 16 martie tells it more shortly. Seeing the world in danger of sinking into idolatry, Papas presented himself before the ruler of the place where he lived and shamed him by his great boldness in Christ. He was thrown to the ground, beaten first on the body and then on the face with rods, tormented in many ways, and so gave up his spirit; and his relics, it says, were taken up by the people of Lycaonia, who rejoice and are glad in having them among them.
