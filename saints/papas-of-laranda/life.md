# Papas of Laranda

Papas came from the city of Laranda in Lycaonia and bore the most fearful tortures without ceasing for a moment to pray to God. When Galerius let loose a savage persecution against the Christians he suffered one of the harshest martyrdoms: first they struck him on the jaws with stones until they broke them, then hung him upside down and scourged him without mercy, then tore his sides and burned the wounds with lighted candles; but he bore it all with astonishing patience and glorified God to his last breath — he was ashamed, the verse says, to groan at the marks, having God near as helper. He is probably the same saint as the one kept on 16 March; the Greek calendar keeps him on 14 September.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 14 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2394/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
