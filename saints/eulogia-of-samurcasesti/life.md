# Eulogia of Samurcășești

doxologia.ro's life of her opens in 1927, with Ecaterina already at work. She gave herself to mission and to colportage, carrying the magazine «Cuvânt Bun» from village to village with books and small icons. The page tells one story out of that work — a young man who made fun of it, and whose sleeve then caught fire — and quotes the priest Nicodim Bujor, who called her a sister and a spiritual mother and valued the stand she took against the cohabitation common in the society of her day.

In 1942 she was tonsured a nun at the monastery of Ciorogârla and received the name Evloghia. Her asceticism was severe: she slept on her knees, on rags, without a fire in winter, and prayed without ceasing. Obstacles did not stop the mission, which she carried as far as the Cadrilater. The page's own links to further reading call her «monahia Evloghia Țârlea» and a Macedonian; the life itself says nothing of where she was born or when.

Her end was a martyr's. In the village of Ulmeni she was beaten with brutality and trampled underfoot, and she gave up her soul at the monastery of Samurcășești‑Ciorogârla, after receiving the Holy Mysteries, on 19 December 1949. In its working session of 1 July 2025 the Holy Synod of the Romanian Orthodox Church approved her canonisation under the title Sfânta Cuvioasă Muceniță Evloghia de la Samurcășești, with her commemoration on 19 December.
