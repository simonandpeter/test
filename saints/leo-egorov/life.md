# Leo (Egorov)

Leo — Leonid Egorov in the world — was born in 1898 in Novgorod province;
his father owned a co-operative of draymen, and after their parents’ death
he and his two brothers were brought up by an uncle who managed the
Alexander Nevsky market in St Petersburg. One brother, Gurias, became a
metropolitan, of Minsk, then of Leningrad, and at last of Simferopol. Leonid
finished the historical and philological faculty of St Petersburg university
and entered the theological academy, where he studied until it was closed in
the spring of 1918; the brothers took monastic vows together in 1915, became
together the spiritual sons of the hieroschemamonk Seraphim (Muravyov), the
future elder of Vyritsa, and laboured together at the Alexander Nevsky Lavra
until 1922. The hieromonk Leo was one of the leaders of the Orthodox
brotherhoods of Petrograd, was arrested for it in 1922 and exiled, and came
back to Leningrad in 1924, was made archimandrite, taught at the theological
and pastoral school and took up the affairs of the Alexander Nevsky
brotherhood again; from 1926 he was rector of the cathedral of the Theodore
icon of the Mother of God on Poltavskaya street, was arrested again in 1927
and freed after nearly a year, and stayed rector until 1932, teaching
Russian literature at the theological courses, performing more than twenty
tonsures, and corresponding with Metropolitan Joseph (Petrovykh) in defence
of Metropolitan Sergius’s church policy. In 1932 more than forty members of
the brotherhood were arrested, he among them, and he was given ten years in
the camps: first the Siblag, then Novokuznetsk, where his term was
lengthened and he was put in the punishment isolator, then the Siblag again
in 1936, and in 1937, in the mass camp purges, a death sentence. At his last
interrogation he said: I do not admit guilt; though by conviction I am an
anti-Soviet man, I carried on no counter-revolutionary agitation among the
prisoners; I am a deeply religious man who has given his whole life to the
service of God, and the aim of my life is the propagation of religion among
the masses — I have carried it on, I carry it on, and I shall. He was shot
in 1937. The Church keeps him on 7 September of the old style, 20 September,
and in the synaxes of the Novgorod and St Petersburg saints and of the
Kuzbass saints.
