# Philemon, companion of Fortunianus

The Greek synaxarion keeps Philemon the bishop and [Fortunianus](/saints/fortunianus-companion-of-philemon) together on 27 September and says it has no details of their lives. Its verse has Philemon, the priest who loves Christ as Christ loves him, contend beside the beloved Fortunianus. That is all it prints, and it gives no date.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 27 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2521/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
