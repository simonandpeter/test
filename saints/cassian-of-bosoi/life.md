# Cassian of Bosoi

saint.gr's calendar for 11 Φεβρουαρίου names the venerable Cassian from Russia, and the whole of what its page for him says is two sentences: «Ο Όσιος Κασσιανός του Μποσόι έζησε και ασκήτεψε στη μονή της Κοιμήσεως της Θεοτόκου Βολοκολάμσκ της Ρωσίας, όπου ήταν ηγούμενος ο όσιος Ιωσήφ. Ο Όσιος Κασσιανός κοιμήθηκε με ειρήνη το έτος 1532 μ.Χ.» — Cassian of Bosoi lived and practised ascesis at the monastery of the Dormition of the Mother of God at Volokolamsk in Russia, where [Joseph](/saints/joseph-the-wonderworker-of-volokolamsk) was abbot; and he fell asleep in peace in the year 1532.

So the by-name, the house, the abbot he lived under and the year are the whole of what the Greek calendar keeps of him. Nothing else is printed: no parentage, no office, no hymn, no relics and not even a distich, and the page adds only its gloss on the name, that Κασσιανός is from the Latin cassis, a helmet, and means the brave champion.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/11/index.aspx) and [his page](https://www.saint.gr/3645/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
