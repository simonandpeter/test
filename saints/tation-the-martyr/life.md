# Tation

Tation came from Mantineion, in the metropolis of Claudiopolis in the province of Honorias. He was arrested as a Christian and brought before the governor Urbanus, who asked whether he persisted in confessing Christ; he answered boldly that he would confess him to his last breath.

They beat him savagely and tore him with iron claws, and then dragged him through Claudiopolis until he died, after he had first made the sign of the cross. The Romanian Lives give the same outline.
