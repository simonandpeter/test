# Gregory the Wonderworker of the Caves

saint.gr says this Gregory came from Russia and lived his ascetic life in the monastery of Saint Theodosius of the Great Lavra. By the exactness of his monastic living and by his spiritual wrestlings he was counted worthy, very early, to work all kinds of wonders in the name of Christ. God gave him a particular gift against the evil spirits, which he rebuked and drove away from men. He was martyred in the year 1094, under the prince Rastislav.

That is the whole of the notice. He is not the other Gregory this same Greek day keeps, the recluse of that same monastery of Saint Theodosius whom the page puts in the fourteenth century: two entries on one calendar are two commemorations, and the two pages give different centuries and different ends. The page prints no hymn for him and no place in Russia.
