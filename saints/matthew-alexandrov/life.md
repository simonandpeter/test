# Matthew (Alexandrov)

The calendar’s own line is the whole of what is recorded of him here. The Сретенский monastery’s calendar for 2 декабря ст. ст. commemorates him alone, in four words and a year: «Сщмч. Матфея пресвитера (1921).» He is the first of the twentieth-century martyrs this day keeps and the only one of them dated 1921.

The name page behind his place gives him the surname the line leaves out, together with his grade: «Матфей (Александров), иерей, сщмч.» — a priest and a hieromartyr. Behind that page there is no life, and the day page carries no hymn under his name.

So nothing is said here of where he was born, where he served, when or by whom he was arrested, or how and where he died. No page read says any of it, and the year 1921 is the year the line closes with.

His name page lists one other day, «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church. One row per church is the schema, so that day is recorded here and he does not render on it.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 2 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261202.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9673.html); read 7 October 2026.*
