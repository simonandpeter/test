# Constantine the Presbyter

Little is known of Constantine Ivanovich Popov. He served at the church of the village of Lyagushinskoye in the Verkhoturye district of Perm province, and on 13 August 1918 — 26 August by the civil calendar — he was shot in the village of Yegorshino by the Bolsheviks who had seized power there. He was numbered among the new martyrs and confessors of Russia by the Council of 2000 and the Synod’s decision of 17 July 2002. The Russian calendar keeps him on 13 August (Julian) with the presbyters [John](/saints/john-presbyter-martyr-1918-13-august) and [Joasaph](/saints/joasaph-presbyter-martyr-1918); the Sretensky calendar prints no life for him, and this one follows azbyka.ru.
