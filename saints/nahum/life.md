# Nahum

Nahum was from Elkosh, on the far side of the Jordan over against Begabar, of the tribe of Simeon. After the prophet [Jonah](/saints/jonah-the-prophet) he prophesied to the Ninevites that their city would be destroyed with water and with fire, and so it came about. For the Ninevites, who had repented for a little while at Jonah's preaching, seeing that his prophecy had not been fulfilled upon them, went back to their first evil deeds and stirred God's long patience to anger again.

Their destruction came in this way. Beside Nineveh was a great lake of water that went round the city; and a great earthquake happened, and the city sank into that lake, while the part of it that was left standing on the hill was burnt by fire that came out of the wilderness. So Nahum's prophecy was fulfilled, and God punished a sinful people by his righteous judgment: those he had spared in the days of the prophet Jonah for their repentance, those same he destroyed at the last when they had turned back.

He prophesied other things also that were to come, and fell asleep in peace forty-five years from his birth, and was buried with honour in his own land.
