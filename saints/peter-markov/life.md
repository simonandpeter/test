# Peter (Markov)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him in one line with three other priests for 8 февраля ст. ст. — «Сщмчч. Симеона, Андрея, Сергия и Петра пресвитеров (1938).» — four hieromartyrs and presbyters who died in 1938. The name link behind his place in the line gives the calendar's own nominative, «Петр (Марков), протоиерей», which adds both the surname the line leaves out and the rank of archpriest; unlike the other three links, it carries no rank word of its own, and the «Сщмчч.» of the day's line is what names him a hieromartyr.

The calendar prints no life page for him, and none is given here: the line and the name link are the whole of what it records. The three priests named with him are not set down as his companions in anything but the day.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 8 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260208.html); read 3 October 2026.*
