# Bacchus of the monastery of Saint Sabbas

saint.gr's whole account of him is one sentence: «Ο Άγιος Οσιομάρτυς Βάκχος ασκήτεψε στη μονή του Αγίου Σάββα και τελειώθηκε μαρτυρικά κατά τον 8ο αιώνα μ.Χ.» — the holy venerable-martyr Bacchus lived the ascetic life in the monastery of Saint Sabbas and was made perfect by martyrdom in the eighth century. The day's line says the same and adds nothing.

That is all. The page does not say where he came from, who tonsured him, at whose hands he suffered or by what death, and gives no year within the century it names. It lists no relic and prints neither couplet nor hymn. The Bacchus the corpus already keeps is the soldier who suffered with Sergius four centuries earlier; nothing joins the two but the name.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/11/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1875/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
