# John (Korotkov)

John Petrovich Korotkov was born in 1885 into a peasant family of Yaroslavl province, one of the few laymen, his life says, who confessed Christ to death under the godless state. He did not join the collective farm and kept his own holding, and in 1935 his family, with six children, was taxed beyond its means and stripped of its livestock and goods for not paying. In the summer of 1936 he found work at the Masalsk hospital, but was dismissed a few months later for preaching the Christian faith: he knew the Scriptures well and gathered believers about him by his preaching. He was arrested in 1937 and sentenced to ten years of labour camp, and on 30 September 1941 he died in Kargopollag, in Arkhangelsk province, of a crushed skull.

The Russian calendar keeps him on 17 September old style.
