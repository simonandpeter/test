# Plotinus, companion of Saturninus

saint.gr's calendar for 12 Φεβρουαρίου names Plotinus and [Saturninus](/saints/saturninus-companion-of-plotinus) together — «Άγιοι Πλωτίνος και Σατορνίνος» — and the page behind that line gives one sentence of life: «Οι Άγιοι Πλωτίνος και Σατορνίνος μαρτύρησαν δια ξίφους στην Καρχηδόνα», the holy Plotinus and Saturninus were martyred by the sword at Carthage. That is the whole of it: a pair, a city, a manner of death.

No year, no emperor, no governor, no hymn and no relics are printed for either of them. The distich over the two is grim and unusually flat: «Τὰ θνησιμαῖα ταῦτα τῶν τετμημένων, Σατορνίνου πέφυκε καὶ τοῦ Πλουτίνου» — these carcasses of the beheaded are Saturninus's and Plotinus's. Its spelling of the first name, Πλουτίνου, is not the day line's Πλωτίνος, and the name form kept here is the day line's.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/12/index.aspx) and [the page for the two of them](https://www.saint.gr/1224/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
