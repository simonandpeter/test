# Michael of Kyiv

The Greek synaxarion keeps Michael as the first metropolitan of Kyiv and all Russia and a wonderworker. It says it has no details of the life of this Russian saint, and gives only the year of his death, 992, in the heading of its entry.

The Russian church calendar keeps him on the Julian 30 September, and its index of names says what kind of day it is: he has two, «15 июня» and «30 сентября (Перенесение мощей)», so this is the translation of his relics and his repose is in June. The day line gives the year as 992, which agrees with saint.gr. The page carries two troparia and two kontakia for him in two slightly different readings; the kontakion calls him a second Moses who carried the vine of the mind out of Egyptian idol-service into a land foretold, and it was not taken into this folder.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 30 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/1015/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026; the Russian church calendar keeps him on the Julian 30 September — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260930.html); read 4 October 2026.*
