# Michael of Kyiv

The Greek synaxarion keeps Michael as the first metropolitan of Kyiv and all Russia and a wonderworker. It says it has no details of the life of this Russian saint, and gives only the year of his death, 992, in the heading of its entry.

The Serbian church's Ohrid Prologue keeps him third of the three it enumerates on this day, and it tells what the Greek entry says it does not know. At the request of the Grand Prince [Vladimir](/saints/vladimir-the-great) he was sent by the Patriarch of Constantinople into Russia, to baptise the heathen people and to establish and order the church there.

He baptised the people at Kyiv, at Novgorod, at Rostov and in many other towns and villages; he ordered the church and appointed bishops and presbyters; he laid the foundation of the Michael monastery at Kyiv; and he sent missionaries among the Bulgars and the Tatars, winning many of them too for Christ. All this and much else of profit, the Prologue says, this hierarch did in a bare four years.

He reposed in peace in 992, which is the year the Greek heading gives, and his relics rest in the Caves Lavra.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 30 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/1015/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026; and the Serbian church's [Ohrid Prologue for the Julian 30 September](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-10-13&prolog=1), read 4 October 2026.*
