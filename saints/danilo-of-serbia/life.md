# Danilo of Serbia

The Охридски пролог gives him one dense paragraph for 20. децембар ст. ст., and it is the whole of what is read here. He was the son of rich and God-loving parents and was well brought up in his youth. King [Stephen Milutin](/saints/stephen-milutin) took him to his own court, but out of great love for God he fled it and was tonsured in the monastery of Končul beside the Ibar. Later he was abbot of Hilandar, and there, the book says, he suffered much from the plundering Latin crusaders.

He was bishop of Banja, then of Hum, and at the last archbishop of the Serbs. From the beginning to the end he was a strict ascetic, and the entry singles out one gift of his: he had a particular gift of tears. He reconciled King Dragutin with Milutin, and afterwards Milutin with [Stephen of Dečani](/saints/stephen-of-decani). He fought strongly against the Latins and against the Bogomils alike.

Under his oversight the monasteries of Banja and Dečani were built, and he renewed and raised many churches besides. He wrote the Родослов, the genealogy of the Serbian kings and saints. Untiring in the service of God to the end of his life, he reposed peacefully in the days of the Emperor Dušan, in the night between the 19th and the 20th of December 1338 — which is why this day keeps him. The entry closes by calling him «Велики јерарх, велики подвижник, велики трудољубац и велики родољуб», a great hierarch, a great ascetic, a great toiler and a great lover of his own people.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 20. децембар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-01-02&prolog=1); read 8 October 2026.*
