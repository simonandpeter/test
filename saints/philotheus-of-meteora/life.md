# Philotheus of Meteora

The calendar's line for him is two words, «Όσιος Φιλόθεος», and the page behind it says who he was: the second founder and restorer of the monastery of Saint Stephen of the Meteora, a hieromonk, from Sthlataina or Sklataina, the village called Rizoma today in the district of Trikala. He is borne witness to in the first half of the sixteenth century. He was the son of a well-off family, and he laid out the whole of his patrimony on the renewal and the adorning of the monastery and on the enriching of it.

saint.gr says plainly that the historical material for him is not sufficient, and that the chief source is a sigillion of the Ecumenical Patriarchate issued by Jeremias I in the year 1545. Having lived in a manner pleasing to God, the page ends, he fell asleep in peace. No hymn is printed for him. The first founder of the same house, [Anthony](/saints/anthony-of-meteora), is kept on this same day.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 17 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/17/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1290/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
