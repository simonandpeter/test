# Mardarius the Recluse

saint.gr's calendar for 13 December keeps the venerable Mardarius the Recluse, and its page gives no life. It says that he was a monk in the Lavra of the Caves in the thirteenth century, that in his troparion he is called «μη-άπληστος», not greedy, and that the inscription on his relics reads «χωρίς κελί», without a cell. That is the whole of what saint.gr prints of him; it gives no year for his birth and none for his death, and it prints no hymn of his, only reports what the troparion calls him.

The same day's listing on saint.gr also keeps a martyr Mardarius, one of the five who suffered under Diocletian with Eustratius. Those are a separate entry on the same calendar day, and therefore a separate commemoration and a different man; nothing on this page joins the two, and the only thing they share is the name.

The Russian calendar keeps him on 13 декабря ст. ст., the same menologion day as the Greek calendar here, and its line is «Прп. Мардария, затворника Печерского (XIII).» — the venerable Mardarius, recluse of the Caves, the thirteenth century, which is the century saint.gr gives above. The day page carries a troparion and a kontakion under his name, both of them proper to him: each names him, and each titles him of the Far Caves of the Lavra. They are written here. The readings the entry links were not opened.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 13 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/13/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2623/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 13 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261213.html), read 13 October 2026.*
