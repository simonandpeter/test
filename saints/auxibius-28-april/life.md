# Auxibius (28 April)

Auxibius came from Cyprus and lived in the fourth century, saint.gr says, and was bishop of Soloi. What the page has of him is a signature and an attendance: he signs first, among twelve Cypriot bishops, the acts of the Council of Sardica in the year 343, and earlier than that, in the year 325, he had taken part in the First Ecumenical Council which met at Nicaea. He fell asleep in peace.

Nothing else is printed — no year of birth, no year of death, no kindred, no act of his episcopate and no wonder. His couplet turns on his name, which means increase: «Όντως χορώ δίδωσι των σεσωσμένων, / Aύξησιν Aυξίβιος εκστάς του βίου» — Auxibius, stepping out of this life, truly gives increase to the choir of the saved. The feast type the page gives is the plain fixed one, «Σταθερή. Εορτάζει στις 28 Απριλίου εκάστου έτους».
