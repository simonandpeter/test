# Antonina (Stepanova)

The calendar’s own line is almost the whole of what is recorded of him here, and that line is one of the longest in this calendar’s year. The Сретенский monastery’s calendar for 2 декабря ст. ст. commemorates eleven presbyters, two hieromonks, four women monastics and a laywoman in a single clause closing on a single year: «Сщмчч. Иоанна, Константина, Николая, Сергия, Владимира, Иоанна, Феодора, Николая, Николая, Павла, Сергия пресвитеров, прмч. Данакта, Космы прмцц. Маргариты, Тамары, Антонины и Нимфодоры, мц. Матроны (1937).»

She stands sixteenth. The name page behind her place gives the surname the line leaves out together with the grade: «Антонина (Степанова), прмц.». There is no life behind that name page and none on the day itself. The line gives her the grade «прмц.», a venerable-martyr, which is a monastic grade, and no word naming her house or her degree in it; so this folder records her as a monastic and gives her no office.

So nothing is said here of where she was born, in what house she lived, when or by whom she was arrested, or how and where she died. The year 1937 is the year the line closes on and it is given for all eighteen at once.

Eighteen people are named in it and this folder is one of eighteen, each read off the name page behind its own place in the line. The day page carries no hymn under any of their eighteen names; its four troparia and four kontakia all stand under the names of the ancient saints of the same day. Sharing a line is not a relation, so none of the eighteen is given a `related` row to the others on that ground alone.

Her name page lists two other days, «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church, and «26 апреля (Бут.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs who suffered at Butovo. One row per church is the schema, so both are recorded here and she renders on neither.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 2 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261202.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14364.html); read 7 October 2026.*
