# Marcian of Cyrrhus

Marcian was born and brought up in the city of Cyrrhus. He set the things of the world at nothing and went away into the desert, where he shut himself into a very narrow cell with severe fasting and prayer. His manner of life won him the reputation of a saint, and a multitude of the faithful took refuge in his spiritual protection and sought peace and rest of him. He fell asleep in peace.

saint.gr names the source of that account: his life was written by Theodoret of Cyrrhus in the work called the *Philotheos Historia*. The calendar gives him no year of birth and none of death, and prints no hymn for him. The day page also carries a note that some synaxaristae mention him here superfluously and sends the reader to 2 November; that page has not been read here.
