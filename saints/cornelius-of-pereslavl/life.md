# Cornelius of Pereslavl

The Ohrid Prologue keeps him on the Julian 22 July and gives him six sentences, which are the whole of what this folder knows. In his fifteenth year he was made a monk by the elder Paul. Later he withdrew into the desert for silence. Thirty years he passed in silence, not speaking a word with anyone, so that many took him for a mute. By fasting he had dried himself out so far that he was like a skeleton. Before his death he received the great schema, and he reposed on the twenty-second of July in the year 1693.

The entry gives him no birth year, no family, no country and no monastery; the by-name in its heading is the whole of what places him, and the thirty years of silence are the whole of what it says he did.
