# Alexander (17 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 17 December keeps three venerable-martyrs together, Patermuthius, Copris and Alexander, and Alexander is the third of them. The page says that they were Christians full of piety and zeal, that they were tortured and at last beheaded by order of Julian the Transgressor, whose years it gives as 361 to 363, and that this happened while Julian was on campaign against the Persians.

Of Alexander the narrative says nothing apart from the three of them together: that [Copris](/saints/copris-17-december), the young one, faltered a little and that [Patermuthius](/saints/patermuthius-17-december) strengthened him, and that all three underwent the martyr's death chanting hymns to Christ. What the page adds for Alexander is in its epigram, which gives him a double contest and double crowns, for his ascesis and for the blade — which is also why the calendar line calls the three of them venerable-martyrs rather than martyrs.

That is the whole of the entry. The page names no city and no province, no judge and no prison; it gives no year of birth and dates the martyrdom only by the reign; it lists no relic and prints no hymn for any of the three.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 17 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/17/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3283/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
