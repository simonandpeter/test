# Neon (1 June)

saint.gr's calendar for 1 Ιουνίου names him and gives one sentence: the holy Neon was martyred by the sword. That is all the prose the page carries for him — no birthplace, no century, no year, no judge, no emperor and no companion.

What it adds is a distich, and the distich is about the blood: the Scripture tells us of a red sea, it says, and Neon, beheaded, makes the earth red as well. The corpus keeps another Neon, one of the company of Mark the Shepherd, and nothing on this page connects the two; this folder is identified by the day the Greek calendar prints him on, because the page offers nothing else.
