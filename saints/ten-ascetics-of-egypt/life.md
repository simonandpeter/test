# The Ten Ascetics of Egypt

saint.gr's calendar names them and gives no life. The whole of its notice is one sentence and a disclaimer: «Οι Άγιοι Δέκα Όσιοι και Ασκητές από την Αίγυπτο απεβίωσαν ειρηνικά. Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέριες για τον βίο τους.» The ten venerable ascetics from Egypt reposed in peace, and we have no further details of their life. The page names none of the ten, gives no year, and gives no place in Egypt beyond the country itself.

Above that sentence it prints the distich the Greek books give the day, «Βλαστήματα θνῄσκουσιν Αἰγύπτου δέκα, / Πληγαῖς ἐκείναις ἰσάριθμοι ταῖς δέκα» — ten shoots of Egypt die, equal in number to those ten plagues. It prints no hymn of any kind.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2237/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
