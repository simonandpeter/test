# Revocatus

saint.gr names him and gives one sentence of life. Its calendar for 6 February prints «Άγιοι Θεόφιλος, Σατουρνίνος και Ρεβοκάτος», and the page behind the line says that [Theophilus](/saints/theophilus-martyr-6-february), [Saturninus](/saints/saturninus-martyr-6-february) and Revocatus were martyred in Spain in the reign of the emperor Decius, which the page dates 249 to 251. No city is named, no trial, no manner of death; there is no hymn and no couplet.

The name is a rare one, and the corpus holds no other bearer of it. It does occur, with Saturninus, among the companions of Perpetua and Felicity at Carthage, whose lives this corpus keeps and whose passion is placed two generations earlier; the Greek line puts these three in Spain under Decius, and on that reading they are other men. No other calendar was read for him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3616/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
