# Antonina of Nicomedia

saint.gr's calendar for 9 Ιανουαρίου names Antonina, and the page behind the line gives her one sentence and no life: «Η Αγία Αντωνίνα καταγόταν από τη Νικομήδεια και τελειώθηκε στη θάλασσα» — she came from Nicomedia, and she was perfected in the sea. The heading the day prints over that line is «Αγία Αντωνίνα που μαρτύρησε στη Νικομήδεια», Antonina who was martyred at Nicomedia.

That is all. The page names no emperor and no judge, gives no year, says nothing of how she was taken or what was asked of her, and prints no hymn. What it offers to tell her apart from the other martyrs of this name whom the calendar keeps on other days is the city she came from and the manner of her death, and nothing besides.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 9 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/09/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1100/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
