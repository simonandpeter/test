# Peter

Peter, bishop of Nicaea, defended the veneration of icons against the
iconoclasts under the emperor Leo — the Armenian, on the years the Orthodox
Church in America’s life gives, 813 to 820, though the life names him the
Isaurian — and suffered for it; he died no earlier than 823. Four letters of
Theodore the Studite to him survive, written between 816 and 823. The Greek
synaxarion keeps him on 10 September and says only that he died in peace,
with a verse — once president of the Nicaeans, Peter, in dying he becomes a
fellow of the council of fishermen. The same day it keeps a
[Paul](/saints/paul-bishop-of-nicaea), bishop of Nicaea, of whom nothing is
recorded; the Russian calendar keeps the two together on its own reckoning.

*After the life printed by the Orthodox Church in America for 10 September —
[the day’s lives](https://www.oca.org/saints/all-lives/2026/09/10); the Greek
entry is the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 10 Σεπτεμβρίου —
[the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2371/saint.aspx); read 5 September 2026.*
