# Peter, Bishop of Nicaea

Peter, bishop of Nicaea, died in peace. That is the whole of the Greek synaxarion’s notice for 10 September; its verse says that once president of the Nicaeans, Peter, in dying he becomes a fellow of the council of fishermen. The Greek calendar keeps the same day a Paul, bishop of Nicaea, of whom it has no details.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 10 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2371/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
