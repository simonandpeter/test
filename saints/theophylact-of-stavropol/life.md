# Theophylact of Stavropol

The holy Theophylact, in the world Theodore Gubin, was born into a priestly family in the village of Makovets, near Tarussa, in the province of Kaluga, saint.gr says. In 1838 he studied at the theological seminary of Kaluga, and later he entered the theological academy of Moscow. On the eighth of March 1842 he was tonsured a monk and received the name Theophylact; on the fifteenth of March of the same year he was ordained deacon, and on the twenty-eighth of June presbyter.

Having taught in various theological schools, he was elected Bishop of Stavropol on the tenth of February 1863. He showed himself a true shepherd, the page says, and was distinguished in his ascetic life. Theophylact fell asleep in peace in the year 1872.

That is the whole of it. The page gives no year for his birth, names neither his father nor his mother, says nothing of which schools he taught in or of what he did in the nine years of his episcopate, records no writings, no relics and no burial place, and prints neither apolytikion nor kontakion for him. Of the name it notes only that Θεοφύλακτος means the one guarded by God.
