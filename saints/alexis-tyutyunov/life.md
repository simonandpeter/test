# Alexis (Tyutyunov)

The calendar's own line is the whole of what is recorded of him here. The Сретенский monastery's calendar for 24 ноября ст. ст. names him second of five priests in «Сщмчч. Александра , Алексия , Иоанна , Корнилия и Митрофана пресвитеров (1937)», and the name page behind that second name gives him his surname and his grade: «Алексий (Тютюнов), иерей, сщмч.» — a priest and a hieromartyr. The calendar carries no life page for him, and azbyka.ru's own line for the civil 7 декабря names him and stops.

So nothing is said here of where he was born, where he served, when or by whom he was arrested, or where he died: no page read says any of it. The year 1937 is the year the day line closes the clause with, and it is given for all five men at once.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 24 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261124.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14354.html); read 7 October 2026.*
