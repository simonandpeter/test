# Agrippina (Lesina)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Agrippina in this company on 26 December and gives no life. Its line is «Сщмч. Григория пресвитера, прмцц. Августы и Марии , мц. Агриппины (1938).» — the hieromartyr Gregory the presbyter, the venerable-martyrs Augusta and Maria, and the martyr Agrippina, 1938 — and the calendar’s own name page for this memory carries the fuller form the line leaves out: «Агриппина (Лесина), мц.»

That is the whole of the record. She was a martyr and the line gives her no grade; the year of death the line gives is 1938; the calendar keeps the memory on this day, the second day of the Nativity. The line names four people and all four are in this draft. The day page carries no troparion and no kontakion under this name; the three it prints belong to the day’s own feasts and to one of its icons. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 26 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261226.html); read 13 October 2026.*
