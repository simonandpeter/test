# Gregory the Painter of the Caves

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for the day is «Όσιος Γρηγόριος ο ζωγράφος ο εν τω σπηλαίω» — the venerable Gregory the painter, the one in the cave — and the whole of its notice beneath that line is a disclaimer: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ρώσου Οσίου.» We have no details of the life of the Russian venerable one. So the two epithets of the listing, the painter and the cave, and the one word Russian are everything the source gives.

The rest of the page is a gloss on the name, which it reads from the verb to be awake, «ο άγρυπνος, άρα ενήμερος και φωτιομένος», and a remark that many saints, bishops and writers have borne it, among them Gregory the Theologian and the patriarch Gregory the Fifth. The page gives no year, no place, no account of his death and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2244/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
