# James the Confessor

James loved the hermit's life from his youth, the whole of doxologia's short life for the day says, and became a monk, labouring in fasting, in vigils and in many toils; and afterwards he was counted worthy of the rank of bishop. The page does not name his see, and does not say where he was born or where he lived.

In the time of the enemies of the icons, under the emperor Constantine Copronymus, he was pressed to renounce the veneration of the holy images. He would not submit, and for that he endured many trials — banishments, imprisonments, dangers beyond telling, hunger and thirst and many other cruel torments. Then he gave up his blessed soul into the hands of God, for whom he had struggled until death, and from whom he received the Kingdom of heaven.

That is the whole of what the Romanian calendar prints about him: his monastic beginning, his bishopric, his confession under the iconoclasts, and his death from what he suffered. It gives him no year of his own beyond the emperor's reign, and nothing has been added here from anywhere else.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 21 martie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/21-martie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-ierarh-iacob-marturisitorul); read 19 September 2026.*
