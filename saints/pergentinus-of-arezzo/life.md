# Pergentinus of Arezzo

saint.gr's calendar keeps him with his brother and gives the two of them one sentence between them. The line for 3 June reads «Άγιοι Περγεντίνος και Λαυρεντίνος οι Μάρτυρες οι αυτάδελφοι» — the holy martyrs Pergentinus and Laurentinus, brothers of the same parents — and the page behind it says that the holy martyrs Pergentinus and Laurentinus bore witness in the year 251, in the city of Arezzo in Italy, under the emperor Decius, who reigned from 249 to 251.

So what is recorded of him is a name, a brother, a rank, a year, a reign and an Italian city. The page does not say how old they were, what they were accused of, what they suffered, or which of the two died first; it prints no verse for them and no hymn. His brother is [Laurentinus](/saints/laurentinus-of-arezzo), and the two folders carry the same sentence because the source gives them nothing apart.
