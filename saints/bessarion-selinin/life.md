# Bessarion (Selinin)

His line in the calendar is four words and a year, and it is nearly the whole of what is recorded here. The Сретенский monastery’s calendar for 18 апреля ст. ст. prints «Сщмч. Виссариона пресвитера (1918)» — a hieromartyr of that name, a presbyter, 1918 — and the name page behind the forename gives the surname the line leaves out and nothing besides: «Виссарион (Селинин), иерей, сщмч.»

The two agree in every word they share. The line calls him пресвитера, a presbyter, and the page иерей, a priest; both call him сщмч., a hieromartyr; and 1918 is the only year either of them gives. So nothing is said here of where he was born, where he served, when or by whom he was arrested, or how he died. There is no life page for him on the day page and none on his name page.

Two things the name page adds to the line. It lists one other day, «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church, and that is what the rank of new martyr here rests on. And it carries a link headed «Сайт прихода», a parish website, which it names «Храм Пророка Божия Илии с. Урджар (Митрополичий округ в республике Казахстан)» — a church of the Prophet Elijah in a village of Kazakhstan. The page does not say what joins him to that parish, so no place of birth, service or death is claimed from it.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 18 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260418.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9523.html); read 8 October 2026.*
