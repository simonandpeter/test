# John of Kazan

saint.gr's calendar for 24 Ιανουαρίου names «Άγιος Ιωάννης ο Μάρτυρας εκ Καζάν», John the martyr of Kazan, and heads his page «(; - 1529)». The whole of what it prints is a single sentence: «Ο Άγιος Μάρτυς Ιωάννης ο Θαυματουργός, μαρτύρησε στο Καζάν της Ρωσίας το έτος 1529 μ.Χ. Δεν έχουμε περισσότερες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου» — the holy martyr John the Wonderworker was martyred at Kazan in Russia in the year 1529; we have no further details of the saint's life.

So the calendar gives him a place, a year and two titles, martyr and wonderworker, and nothing else: no parentage, no account of the martyrdom, no hymn and no relics. It prints under his name only its usual gloss on Ιωάννης, that the name is the Hebrew for God's grace and favour towards men, and the list of the great Johns it recalls — the son of Zacharias and Elizabeth, the son of Zebedee, and Chrysostom.

The Russian calendar keeps him on the same menologion day as «Мч. Иоанна Казанского (1529)», with the same year, and it gives him a troparion and a kontakion of his own. They name his persecutors — «многия муки от неверных агарян приял еси», many torments thou didst receive from the unbelieving Hagarenes — call him a passion-bearer and a champion of the Trinity, and say that the earth which drank his blood is blessed.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/24/index.aspx) and [his page](https://www.saint.gr/3533/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 24 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260124.html), read 3 October 2026.*
