# Nicholas (Florov)

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 20 июня ст. ст. prints him in four words and a year: «Св. Николая исп., пресвитера (1933).». The line gives the forename in the genitive, the class word «Св.», the grade «исп.» for исповедник, a confessor, and the rank «пресвитера», and it gives no surname. The name link behind his place in it supplies the calendar’s own nominative with the surname, «Николай (Флоров), иерей, исповедник», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from.

His name page carries nothing else. It heads «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 20 июня» and then stops: no life, no article, no place of service, no account of his confession and none of his death in 1933. The calendar’s line and that heading are the whole of what this source gives him, and nothing is supplied for them here.

He is graded a confessor and not a martyr, which is what the line says and what this folder records; the name link repeats the rank as «иерей». The second day his name page lists is the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which is a synaxis and not a second feast, so he renders on 20 июня ст. ст. only. The day page prints eleven hymns, for four of the saints and feasts it heads, and none under his name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 20 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260620.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/15018.html); read 9 October 2026.*
