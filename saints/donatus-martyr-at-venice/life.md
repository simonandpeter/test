# Donatus, martyred at Venice

saint.gr names him with two others and gives the three of them a single sentence between them. Its calendar for 17 February prints «Άγιοι Ρωμύλος, Σεκουνδιανός και Δονάτος», and the page behind that line says that the holy martyrs Romylos, Secundianus and Donatus were martyred at Venice in the year 304, under the emperors Diocletian, whose reign it gives as 284 to 305, and Maximian, whose reign it gives as 285 to 305.

That is the whole of it. The page gives no homeland for any of the three, no office, no order among them, no manner of death, no relics, no couplet and no hymn; its headings repeat the year 304 under each name and leave the year of birth a question mark. His companions are [Romylos](/saints/romylos-martyr-at-venice) and [Secundianus](/saints/secundianus-martyr-at-venice), and the day, the city and the year are what the Greek calendar keeps of all three alike.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 17 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/17/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2386/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
