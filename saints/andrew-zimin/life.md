# Andrew (Zimin)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him on 5 января old style and prints one line only: «Сщмч. Андрея пресвитера (1920)» — the hieromartyr Andrew, presbyter, 1920. The name link behind that line carries the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line itself omits, «Андрей (Зимин), сщмч.»; and there the entry ends, for the calendar holds no life page for him and prints no hymn under his name.

So what is set down here is what the calendar sets down. A priest named Andrew Zimin is kept on this day as a hieromartyr, and the year given for his death is 1920. Of his birth, of where he served, of his arrest and of his end the calendar says nothing, and nothing has been supplied here from any other source.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 5 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260105.html); read 3 October 2026.*
