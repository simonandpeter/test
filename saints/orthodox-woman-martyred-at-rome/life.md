# The Orthodox Woman Martyred at Rome

saint.gr's calendar for 7 December keeps an Orthodox woman who was martyred at Rome, and it does not give her name. She was burned alive at Rome, the page says, because she would not accept Arianism and chose Orthodoxy instead. Nothing else is printed of her: no family, no age, no magistrate, no emperor, no year and no relic.

The couplet set over the entry is the rest of what there is, and it is an exclamation at her: a woman hating the teaching of the evil-minded underwent a strange burning — O what courage! Her entry is written down here as the calendar has it, under the words the calendar itself uses, because it gives no name to use instead.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3193/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
