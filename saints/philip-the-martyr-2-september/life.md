# Philip (2 September)

Philip was martyred by the sword — a swift horse, the verse says, carrying him to the Master who loved him. That is the whole of the synaxarion’s notice for the martyr kept on 2 September, with its verse; the Synaxaristes have nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 2 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/923/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
