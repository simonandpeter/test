# Peter of Cetinje

The Ohrid Prologue of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović keeps him on the Julian 18 October and gives him a life in one paragraph, with both ends of it dated. He was born on the first of April 1749 in the village of Njeguši, and entered the monastic state in his twelfth year. On the death of Metropolitan Sava in 1782 he became metropolitan and lord of Montenegro.

His whole life, knightly and holy, this glorious man devoted to his people. At home he worked with all his strength to reconcile the tribes that were at feud with one another; abroad, to defend the land and the people from rapacious attackers. He succeeded, the book says, in both the one task and the other, and he was glorified above all by his victory over the army of Napoleon in Boka and in Dalmatia.

Towards himself he was harsh, and towards everyone else just and forbearing. He lived in one cramped cell, like a simple monk, although he was a prince over a whole people. He fell asleep on the eighteenth of October 1830, and his wonderworking relics rest incorrupt in the monastery of Cetinje. The Lord glorified him in heaven and on earth, the entry ends, as his faithful and patient servant.
