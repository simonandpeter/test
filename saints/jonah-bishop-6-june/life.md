# Jonah (6 June)

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives him two sentences: «Ο Όσιος Ιωνάς ήταν Ρώσος επίσκοπος. Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου.» — the venerable Jonah was a Russian bishop; we have no other details of the saint's life. Its head line adds the year of his death, and nothing else: «Οσιος Ιωνας (; - 1470)».

So the Greek calendar keeps a Russian bishop of that name who died in 1470 and says it knows nothing further. It names no see and no monastery, gives no year of birth, tells nothing of how he came to the episcopate or how he died, names no relics and prints no hymn. Nothing is supplied here that the page does not print, and in particular no see is guessed at for him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/485/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
