# Patermuthius the Glorious

Patermuthius and [Elias](/saints/elias-martyr-with-patermuthius), noblemen,
suffered with the bishops [Peleus](/saints/peleus-bishop-and-hieromartyr)
and [Nilus](/saints/nilus-bishop-and-hieromartyr), the presbyter Zeno and a
hundred and fifty-one others — a hundred and fifty-six martyrs in all, most
of them Egyptians, some Palestinians — under Maximian Galerius, in the
persecution of 305 to 311. Firmilian, governor of Palestine, arrested them;
their eyes were put out and the tendons of their feet cut, and after every
kind of torture [a hundred](/saints/hundred-egyptian-martyrs) were beheaded
and the rest, Patermuthius and Elias among them, burned. That is the
Orthodox Church in America’s account. The Greek synaxarion keeps the two on
17 September with the fire alone and a verse — falling into the furnace,
Patermuthius urges Elias to equal zeal.

*After the life printed by the Orthodox Church in America for 17 September —
[the day’s lives](https://www.oca.org/saints/all-lives/2026/09/17); the Greek
entry is the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 17 Σεπτεμβρίου —
[the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2459/saint.aspx); read 5 September 2026.*
