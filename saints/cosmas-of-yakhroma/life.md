# Cosmas of Yakhroma

saint.gr says the venerable Cosmas of Yakhroma was born in Russia in the fifteenth century after Christ. At first he was the servant of some nobleman, whom he cared for during the time of his illness and to whom he read books. Once, when he was travelling, the most holy Mother of God appeared to him in the wooded country of the river Yakhroma and told him to become a monk and to raise up a monastery.

So he went to Kiev, where he was tonsured monk at the monastery of the Caves, and afterwards he came back to Yakhroma to build a church dedicated to the Dormition of the Mother of God. With the help of the Christians of that country he built the church and founded a monastery, of which he was chosen abbot. He fell asleep in peace in a deep old age in the year 1492, and was buried in the monastery he had founded.

The page gives him no day of death of its own, no relics beyond that grave, no couplet and no hymn. Beside his entry it links a synaxis of the most holy Mother of God of Yakhroma, which is the icon of the appearing and not a person, and is not kept here.
