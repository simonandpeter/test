# Malachi

Malachi was born at Sofi after the return from the captivity at Babylon, of the tribe of Zebulun. From his youth, the life doxologia.ro prints says, he had a pure and blameless life, and he prophesied of the coming of the Lord, of the fearful judgement, of the change of the ordinance and of the law of Moses, and of the new sacrifice.

The name Malachi, which is interpreted „angel”, was given him by the whole people: partly because he was of a great comeliness, like an angel, and partly because he kept an angelic purity and holiness and had friendship with the angels. He often spoke with the angel who uncovered to him the mysteries of God and taught him the knowledge of what was to come. Those who were worthy knew this to be so, for they heard the angel's voice although they could not see him, and Malachi spoke with him face to face as a man speaks with his friend.

He was the last of all the prophets, the life says, for after him there was no other in Israel. He died young and was buried in the same place as his fathers. Doxologia.ro gives no year for any of it.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same third of January. saint.gr says he was one of the twelve so-called minor prophets, born of the tribe of Levi at Sopha in the years when the Hebrews returned to Jerusalem from the captivity of Babylon; that he lived about the fifth century before Christ, in the days of Nehemiah, and worked at Jerusalem after the prophets Haggai and Zechariah. The page says he was given the name Malachias — which in Greek means angel — for three reasons, and the first it gives is that whatever he prophesied a divine angel at once confirmed to the people: the strange part being that the unworthy could not see the angel, though worthy and unworthy alike heard his voice.
