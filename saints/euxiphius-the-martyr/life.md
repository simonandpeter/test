# Euxiphius

The memory of Euxiphius is mentioned in a line in the Small Euchologion or Hagiasmatarion published by the Apostoliki Diakonia in 1956, without further information, and nowhere else; perhaps he is confused with Auxibius, kept on 17 February. That is the whole of the Greek notice for 17 September; this entry records it and nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 17 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2465/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
