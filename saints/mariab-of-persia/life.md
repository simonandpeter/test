# Mariab of Persia

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him second in the company of two hundred and seventy-three it heads the Julian 9 April with: «Мчч. Дисана еп., Мариава пресвитера, @7[Авдиеса] и прочих 270-ти (362-364).» — the martyrs Disan the bishop, Mariab the presbyter, Audiesius and the other 270, about 362 to 364. The name link behind his place in that line, name page 1451, gives the calendar’s own nominative with the epithet and the rank, «Мариав Персидский, сщмч.», and lists one day for him and only one, 9 апреля. Its single Жития link is the company’s, which is the same page the other two names link to.

That life is two sentences long and they are the whole of what this calendar records. The holy martyrs bishop Disan, the presbyter Mariab, Audiesius and the other 270 suffered martyrdom at the hands of the Persian king Shapur II; in captivity they refused to deny the Christian faith. Neither this page nor azbyka.ru’s copy of it says how the two hundred and seventy are counted, and neither distinguishes anything this man did from what the company did. He is a presbyter on both the name page and the day line, and he is a presbyter still where the Greek entry names him.

That entry is the one the corpus already keeps under the name of [Audiesius, with the martyrs who suffered with him](/saints/audiesius-of-persia), and it is the only account read here that gives the company a story. In the thirtieth year of his reign Shapur II overran the country saint.gr calls Byzate and killed everyone who bore arms, sparing the women, the children, the old men, the bishop Heliodorus, the presbyters «Δησάν, Μαριάβ» and all their clergy; the dying Heliodorus ordained [Desan](/saints/desan-of-persia) bishop in his place, and Desan preached on to the Persians themselves.

The chief magus Adephar then accused Desan to the king of blaspheming the Persian religion, and Shapur ordered that he and all the Christians about him be seized and made to worship the sun and the fire; refusing, they were all beheaded, five of them having lost heart on the road. **The page does not name Mariab again after the list of those spared**, so what it says of him is that he was a presbyter of Byzate, that he was spared the sword that took the men under arms, and that he was of the company that was beheaded. The English form here follows its «Μαριάβ», which is also the form this corpus had already printed.

The company’s own Russian life closes by sending the reader to another day: among them, it says, was the martyr Ia, who is commemorated on 11 September — [Ias of Persia](/saints/ias-of-persia), whom this corpus keeps on that day. She is the fourth name any source read here puts inside this company, and the only one of the four who was kept somewhere else before this day was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 9 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260409.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life819.htm); and azbyka.ru’s calendar for the civil 22 April, read in the in-app Browser pane — [the company](https://azbyka.ru/days/svv-mariav-persidskij-avdies-persidskij); and saint.gr’s entry for 9 Απριλίου, read for the Greek spelling of the name — [that life](https://www.saint.gr/215/saint.aspx); read 6 October 2026.*
