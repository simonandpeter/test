# Pontius the Senator

The Ohrid Prologue of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović gives him the third and longest entry of its Julian 5 August, and it is the whole of what is written here. He was the son of a senator, Marcus, and of Marcus’s wife Julia; and Julia, who was barren, conceived only after twenty-two years of married life, and bore Pontius. He was baptised by pope Pontian, together with the friend who afterwards wrote his life, and he brought his own father to the faith of Christ, and the emperor Philip with his son, and many other Romans of standing besides.

He protected and helped the church greatly as a senator, and was a great friend of pope [Fabian](/saints/fabian-of-rome), with whose help, the day’s second entry says, many idols and many temples of the idols were thrown down. When the persecution of Decius began, Pontius fled from Rome and hid under the Alpine mountains. But in the time of Valerian and Gallienus he was caught and tormented with terrible torments, under which many wonders of God were shown and many turned to Christ.

He was cut down with the sword in 257, the entry ends, and buried by his friend. That year is the only date in the entry: there is no year for the birth, and the twenty-two years of his parents’ marriage fix none without a wedding to count them from.
