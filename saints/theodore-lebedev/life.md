# Theodore (Lebedev)

Theodore Pavlovich Lebedev was born on 15 February 1875 in the village of
Stebachevo in Vladimir province, a priest’s son, finished the Vladimir
seminary in 1899 and was ordained deacon and then priest; he served first at
a church near Yuriev-Polsky and then at Kuznetsovo in the Shuya district,
and in 1932 was made dean. He was arrested by the Shuya NKVD on 17 September
1937 and questioned from the day of his arrest for three days and nights
without a break, the interrogators changing and resting and the priest
allowed no rest, the whole reckoning being that a man would sign within the
first three days; he refused. Informers had written that he said nothing
would come of so wasteful a system, that everything was taken from the
peasant, that wreckers and kulaks were hunted everywhere by those who did
the wrecking, and that the collective farms were built only to draw the
people from the church and from faith in God and made them work on feasts.
Asked what he had said about collectivisation and the life of the collective
farmers he said, nothing; told that the investigation had evidence of
counter-revolutionary talk in June 1937, that he had said nothing to anyone;
when another prisoner’s testimony was read to him, that he denied it
entirely; told that his conduct showed him an enemy of the people hiding his
own counter-revolution and other people’s, that he had engaged in none; and
threatened with confrontations, that whatever witnesses’ testimony they read
him and however many confrontations they held, he would not admit himself
guilty of counter-revolution. The investigation closed the same day, 20
September; two days later the troika of the Ivanovo NKVD sentenced all the
arrested priests to death, and at eleven at night on 25 September 1937 he
was shot with the others, [John Prudentov](/saints/john-prudentov) among
them. He is kept on 12 September of the old style, 25 September, and in the
synaxes of the Ivanovo metropolia and of the Vladimir saints.
