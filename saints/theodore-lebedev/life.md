# Theodore (Lebedev)

The Russian calendar names the hieromartyr Theodore (Lebedev), archpriest on 12 September of the old style, in the one line the day keeps for its four new martyrs of 1937. The calendar links no written life, so this entry records the line and nothing more.

*After the Православный церковный календарь (days.pravoslavie.ru), [12 сентября ст. ст.](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260912.html); read 30 August 2026.*
