# Jacob (Bobyrev)

Yakov Ivanovich Bobyrev was born on 25 November 1883 in the village of Bukreevka in the Kursk province, into a peasant family whose Orthodox habits had held and whose temper, the calendar says, was ascetic: his father's brother Sergius had gone off to the Kalyazin monastery in Tver province and been tonsured there as Alexy. Yakov finished five classes of the parish school and a two-class college, which in 1901 was enough to make him a parish schoolmaster at Shchetino. In 1903 he became psalm-reader at the church of Plaksino and served there until 1915, when he was called into the army; he came back to the same village in 1918 and took up the same post, and two years later was ordained deacon while going on reading the psalms.

At the end of the twenties, with the persecution of the Church under way, he was sentenced to three years' banishment from the Kursk region for not meeting the grain requisitions. He went to the village of Vysokoye in the Tver region, where his uncle the monk Alexy had settled in the hamlet of Nikulino after the Kalyazin monastery was closed. He lived as an exile under supervision and had to report twice a month at the district militia office. In February 1930, still an exile, he was ordained priest to the church at Vysokoye, and the peasants came to love him for the seriousness with which he served; when he was suddenly arrested on 13 December 1931 it took them by surprise.

In 1937 the charges were the ordinary charges of that year, and his answers are in the record. That he had agitated against the elections to the Supreme Soviet, saying only communists would vote and only communists be elected: no, there was no agitation of mine in an anti-Soviet spirit about those elections. That he had threatened collective farmers in August 1937 with the inevitable change of the existing order in the USSR: no, I threatened nobody, and I do not admit the fact. That he had agitated against subscribing to the defence loan: I deny it — that was the impression the chairman of the village soviet formed, because I once stopped to talk with a man of Vysokoye, but I said nothing to him about not subscribing.

The NKVD troika sentenced him to be shot on 15 October. He was shot two days later, on 17 October 1937.
