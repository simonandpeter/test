# John (Plekhanov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him for 12 марта ст. ст. in the line «Сщмч. Иоанна, Константина пресвитеров, прмч. Владимира (1938).» The line declines the forename to «Иоанна» and gives no surname, no place, no year of birth and no word of narrative; the year it closes with, 1938, is the only year it gives.

Behind that «Иоанна» stands the calendar's own name page 9627, which carries the fuller form «Иоанн (Плеханов), иерей, сщмч.» — the surname the day's line leaves out, and a priest's grade — and lists two days, this 12 марта and «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. It carries no life, and neither does the day page; azbyka.ru was not read for him. So what the calendar prints is the whole of what this folder says, and it says that rather than fill the silence.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 12 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260312.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9627.html), which carries no life; read 7 October 2026.*
