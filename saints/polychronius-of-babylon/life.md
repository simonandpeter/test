# Polychronius of Babylon

The Охридски пролог for the Julian 30 July is the whole of what this folder records. When the emperor Decius took Babylon he seized Polychronius, its bishop, together with three presbyters, two deacons and two baptised princes, Авдон and Сенис. Polychronius would answer the emperor nothing at all, but kept silence, while the holy presbyter Парменије answered for them all — and Bishop Nikolai's verse on the day is built on that silence, the emperor shouting in his fury and the saint saying nothing.

The emperor took the bishop and the priests away into Persia, to the city of Corduba, and had them beheaded with axes. The princes he took with him to Rome, where he threw them first before the wild beasts and afterwards put them to death with the sword. They all suffered honourably in 251.

The book gives no other date, no place of birth and no word of his life before the siege; and of the eight men taken with him it names only three — the presbyter who spoke and the two princes, the first of whom it spells two ways on the one page. This folder's `office` is recorded as the bare rank and the see stands in the name, because the four languages this corpus is read in carry no translation of the longer form. The Prologue prints no hymn for him, or for any saint of the day.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 30. јул ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-08-12&prolog=1); read 8 October 2026.*
