# Mitrophan, Bishop of Voronezh

Mitrophan, bishop of Voronezh — Michael in the world — was born on 8 November 1623. In the memorial book that belonged to him the list of names begins with men in priestly orders, which gives ground to think he was born into a family of hereditary priests, and his spiritual testament says he was born of devout parents and raised by them in the blameless piety of the Eastern Church, in the Orthodox faith. Until forty he lived in the world: married, with a son John, a parish priest at the village of Sidorovskoye — the Sretensky calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. The Russian calendar keeps on 4 September (Julian) the second finding of his relics in 1964 and their translation in 1989, with a troparion and kontakion.
