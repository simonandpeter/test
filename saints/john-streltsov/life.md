# John (Streltsov)

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him third of five presbyters on the one line it gives to fourteen people of 1938 for the Julian 1 March. John Lavrentyevich Streltsov was born on 21 May 1872 in the village of Gridino in the Bronnitsy district of the province of Moscow, the son of the psalmist Lavrenty Ivanovich Streltsov. He finished the Kolomna theological school in 1888 and the Moscow theological seminary in 1894, and on 24 August 1898 he was ordained priest to the church of the Nativity of the Most Holy Mother of God in the village of Kuzovlevo in the Bronnitsy district.

In 1922 he was appointed rector of the church of the Ascension in the village of Ryblovo in the same district, and he served there until his arrest. During the persecution of the end of the 1920s the authorities, wanting the services in the church stopped, demanded an individual tax of him, then wrote him down as a kulak and plundered his property. In 1931, reckoning that a priest stripped of his goods would have nothing to pay with and would therefore stop serving, they demanded twelve hundred roubles. The church council got word of it and collected the money among the faithful, and he was arrested for it and sentenced to three years’ exile on the charge of having collected the sum by deceit. When the term was out he came back to the village and went on serving.

The officers of the НКВД arrested him on 28 November 1937 and he was held in the Taganka prison in Moscow. After the duty witnesses had been heard — the chairman of the Ryblovo village soviet among them — he was questioned about secret gatherings at his house, which he denied; about what he had said to the chairman in August 1937 when he applied for leave to hold a procession, to which he answered that he had gone to the soviet about a deacon from Bronnitsy being allowed to serve in their church and about nothing else; and about a loan, which he said he had signed for on the spot when it was offered to him. He admitted nothing at all.

On 3 December 1937 the troika of the НКВД sentenced him to ten years in a corrective labour camp — of the five presbyters of this line the only one not shot. He died in a corrective labour camp in the Amur region on 14 March 1938 and was buried in an unmarked grave, on the same day of the same year as the others of the line, which is the Julian 1 March the calendar keeps them all on.

The day’s line calls the five of them «пресвитеров» together and azbyka.ru heads him «пресвитер», while the name link behind his name reads «иерей», a priest, which is what the office here records.
