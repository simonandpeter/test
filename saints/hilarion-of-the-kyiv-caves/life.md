# Hilarion of the Kyiv Caves

saint.gr's calendar for 21 Οκτωβρίου names Hilarion of the Lavra of the Caves and gives him three sentences. He was a disciple of the venerable [Theodosius](/saints/theodosius-of-the-kyiv-caves), the page says, sending the reader to 3 May for his teacher; he prayed day and night with tears in his eyes; and his fellow ascetics knew him as a chronicler, who wrote the life of the master who had taught him.

That is the whole of the entry. The page gives no year of his birth or death, no office in the monastery, no relics and no hymn, and it prints no couplet for him.

The Russian calendar keeps him on the same day, as «Прп. Илариона , схимника Печерского (XI)», and carries two short notices which together say rather more than the Greek line did. He shared a cell with [Theodosius of the Kyiv Caves](/saints/theodosius-of-the-kyiv-caves), whose repose the calendar dates to 1074, and was his fellow-struggler and his disciple; he was known to his contemporaries as a copyist of books, and worked at the copying day and night in Theodosius's own cell while his teacher sang psalms and spun wool. He was marked out by a particularly strict abstinence, and imitating his teacher he prayed with tears day and night and kept a strict fast.

The first of the two notices says he fell asleep in peace in 1066 and was buried in the Far Caves, and the second gives only the eleventh century. Both add two more commemorations beyond this day: 28 августа with the fathers of the Far Caves, and the second Sunday of Great Lent. The day page carries a troparion and a kontakion for him; the troparion turns on his name, saying that he was rightly called Hilarion because his temper was quiet and meek as his name is.
