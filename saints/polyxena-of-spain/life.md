# Polyxena

The Romanian synaxarion keeps one short life for the two sisters together. Polyxena lived in Spain in the time of the emperor Claudius, the sister of Xanthippe, wife of the governor Probus. Travelling to the eastern part of the Roman empire, she received baptism from the Apostle Andrew together with her friend Raveca, and returned to Spain in the company of the Apostle Onesimus - at which her sister too was baptized, and with her she preached our Lord Jesus Christ to the end of her life.
