# Mamas (6 May)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 6 mai reads «Sfântul Cuvios Mamant» — Mamas the venerable — and his page opens no Viață tab, carrying only the day and the readings appointed for a monastic saint.

The rank «Cuvios» is the whole of the record, and it is what separates him from the shepherd martyr of Caesarea whom the same calendar keeps on 2 September. No century, no country, no monastery and no hymn is printed here.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and is the first source here to say anything of him beyond the name. saint.gr prints one line and one page for three men — «Όσιοι Μάμας, Παχώμιος και Ιλαρίων» — and one sentence of story for all of them. The venerable Mamas, [Pachomius](/saints/pachomius-6-may) and [Hilarion](/saints/hilarion-6-may) lived their ascetic life in a desert place, it says, and passed their monastic days in fasting, in vigils and in works of charity; and having lived in concord of soul and in a conscience set on the good, they fell asleep in holiness and in peace. Their synaxis used to be kept «εν Οχείαις», at Ocheiai. The couplet over them puts the three in one line: Mamas set out with Hilarion and Pachomius on the good departure. There is still no year, no country, no monastery and no hymn.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 6 mai — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/6-mai) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-mamant); read 20 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 6 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/06/index.aspx) and [the life of the three](https://www.saint.gr/1516/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026.*
