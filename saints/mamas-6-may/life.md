# Mamas (6 May)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 6 mai reads «Sfântul Cuvios Mamant» — Mamas the venerable — and his page opens no Viață tab, carrying only the day and the readings appointed for a monastic saint.

The rank «Cuvios» is the whole of the record, and it is what separates him from the shepherd martyr of Caesarea whom the same calendar keeps on 2 September. No century, no country, no monastery and no hymn is printed here.
