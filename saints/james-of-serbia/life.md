# James of Serbia

saint.gr names him and gives one sentence of life: «Ο Άγιος Ιάκωβος έζησε στην Σερβία και εξελέγη Αρχιεπίσκοπος αυτής κατά τον 13ο αιώνα μ.Χ.» — James lived in Serbia and was elected its Archbishop in the thirteenth century. That is the whole of the page. It gives no year of election, no year of death, no monastery, no account of his rule, and no hymn or verses.

The see is the one the Greek calendar calls Αρχιεπισκοπή Σερβίας, and the one thing the entry fixes about him besides the century. The Serbian calendar was not read for him, and on this reading the corpus records only what the Greek page prints.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/03/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1202/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
