# Epaenetus

The Apostle Paul names Epaenetus in the letter to the Romans, saying: Greet Epaenetus, my beloved, who is the firstfruits of Achaia unto Christ. He was bishop in Carthage. Those two sentences are the whole of what doxologia.ro prints of him, and the corpus does not add to them; no year is given, no parentage, and nothing of how he died.

The Romanian calendar keeps him on 30 iulie together with Silas, Silvanus, Crescens and Andronicus, under one line and one life for the five, and the service of the day gives them a common troparion and a common kontakion rather than anything of his own.
