# Epaenetus

The Apostle Paul names Epaenetus in the letter to the Romans, saying: Greet Epaenetus, my beloved, who is the firstfruits of Achaia unto Christ. He was bishop in Carthage. Those two sentences are the whole of what doxologia.ro prints of him, and the corpus does not add to them; no year is given, no parentage, and nothing of how he died.

The Romanian calendar keeps him on 30 iulie together with Silas, Silvanus, Crescens and Andronicus, under one line and one life for the five, and the service of the day gives them a common troparion and a common kontakion rather than anything of his own.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 30 July, and in the same company. saint.gr says that Silas, Silouanos, Epaenetus, Crescens and Andronicus were five of the seventy disciples of the Lord, and that all of them served the Gospel of Christ «ἐν κόπῳ καὶ μόχθω, ἐν ἀγρυπνίαις πολλάκις, ἐν λιμῷ καὶ δίψει, ἐν νηστείαις πολλάκις, ἐν ψύχει καὶ γυμνότητι» — in toil and hardship, in watchings often, in hunger and thirst on the long journeys, in fastings, in cold and nakedness — which it takes from the second letter to the Corinthians.

Of Epaenetus it says one sentence, and it adds the suffering the Romanian account does not: that from the dignity of bishop of Carthage — the see this folder already carries — he struggled and was tortured for his faith in the Lord. The synaxarion couplet the page prints for him sets a word of Paul's beside it: «Σε αγαπητόν Παύλος ιστορεί μέγας, / Kαλών απαρχήν της Aχαΐας όλης» — great Paul records you as his beloved, the first-fruits in good things of the whole of Achaia.
