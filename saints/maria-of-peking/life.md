# Maria, betrothed of Isaiah of Peking

saint.gr names her among the Chinese martyrs of 1900. The day's line calls her «η νύφη του», the bride of the house of father [Metrophanes Ji-Sung](/saints/metrophanes-of-peking); the account behind it says plainly that she was the betrothed of his son [Isaiah](/saints/isaiah-of-peking), and that she was nineteen years old. The head of the page brackets her «(1881 - 1900)». Nothing else of her life is given.

When the Boxers loosed their persecution on the Christians of Peking on the night after 10 June 1900, she had come to father Metrophanes's house with the wish to die together with the family of the man she was to marry. When the Boxers surrounded the house she helped the others to escape, getting them over the wall, and then faced the men who had broken in, accusing them of murdering the innocent without trial. They did not dare to kill her; they wounded her in the hands and pierced her feet. To those who pressed her to go away she answered: «Γεννήθηκα κοντά στην εκκλησία της Παναγίας Θεοτόκου και εδώ θα πεθάνω!» — I was born beside the church of the Most Holy Theotokos, and here I shall die. When the Boxers came back, they killed her.

The page counts [two hundred and twenty-two](/saints/two-hundred-twenty-two-chinese-martyrs) others who received the crown of martyrdom in those days, out of three hundred lost from the thousand souls of the Russian mission at Peking.
