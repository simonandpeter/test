# Laurence of Turov

saint.gr's calendar for 29 January names Laurence of Kiev and gives him five sentences. He was born in Russia, it says, and was tonsured a monk at the monastery of Saint Demetrius; later he lived as a monk in the Lavra of Kiev; and in his ascetic life he followed the holy manner of living of the ascetics Isaac and Nikitas.

In 1182, the page says, God counted him worthy to become bishop of the city of Turov in the region of Minsk, and so he went on living as an ascetic while a shepherd in his own province. He fell asleep in peace in the year 1194.

The page's heading gives no year of birth, and it prints no apolytikion and no kontakion. The two ascetics it names beside him have no folders in this corpus, so neither is linked here. The Russian calendar is where this entry could be finished, and it has not been read.
