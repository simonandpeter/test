# Nirsas

saint.gr's calendar for 20 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιοι Νιρσάς ο Επίσκοπος και Ιωσήφ ο μαθητής του και Επίσκοπος» — Nirsas the bishop, and Joseph his disciple, a bishop too. Today our church honours the saints Nirsas and Joseph, the page begins. Nirsas was a bishop and eighty years old; Joseph was his pupil and also a bishop, and he was eighty-nine.

Both were leaders of small Christian flocks and sleepless spiritual fathers to them, and they taught by their own example not only the life lived according to Christ but the martyrdom suffered for him. In the persecutions of the Christians in the years of Shapur II they were added to the glorious ranks of the martyrs, and were honoured so with the incorruptible crown of martyrdom. The couplet over the notice has the two of them dying together, Nirsas and Joseph, by the murder of the sword, as the Apostle says.

The page gives neither of them a see, a country, a year of birth or a hymn, and prints no relics. This folder is Nirsas, the elder of the two, whose disciple [Joseph](/saints/joseph-disciple-of-nirsas) was.

**The Russian calendar keeps the whole Persian company on one line and one life page.** The Сретенский monastery’s calendar prints for the Julian 20 November a single clause, «Сщмчч. Нирсы еп. и Иосифа, ученика его, Иоанна, Саверия, Исакия и Ипатия, епископов Персидских; мч. Азата скопца, Сасония, Феклы, Анны и иных многих мужей и жен, в Персиде пострадавших (343).», and behind every name in it the same life page stands, life2574, titled for all of them together. saint.gr prints the same day as four separate groups with four separate lives; the Russian page is the first source read here that puts them in one company. Its whole account of them is that they «были казнены во время гонения на христиан в Персии при царе Сапоре II» — that they were put to death during the persecution of Christians in Persia under king Shapur II — and then one short sentence apiece on the manner of each death.

Of him the life says only that he and his disciple were beheaded, «Святитель Нирс и его ученик мученик Иосиф были обезглавлены». The clause gives him the one grade word «еп.» that this folder already held from the Greek, and the name link adds a see the Greek page does not give.
