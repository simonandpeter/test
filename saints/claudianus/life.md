# Claudianus

saint.gr's calendar for 27 January names Claudianus and gives no life. The whole of what his page says of the man is one sentence: that he was a monk, and that he fell asleep in holiness and in peace. There is no homeland, no monastery, no century, no year of birth or of death, no account of his tonsure or of his repose, and neither troparion nor kontakion.

What stands above that sentence is a couplet, which addresses him in a slightly different form of the name: «Ψυχῆς ἰδὼν σῆς κάλλος ἐξῃρημένον, / Ὁ ψυχεραστὴς λαμβάνει σε Κλαυδῖνε» — seeing the surpassing beauty of your soul, the lover of souls takes you to himself, Claudinus. That, with the name and the day, is everything the Greek calendar records of him, and it is recorded here as it stands.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 27 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/27/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3344/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
