# Leontius (16 October)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its page for 16 octombrie prints the line «Sfântul Mucenic Leontie», and the life under his name is a single sentence: «Sfinții Mucenici Leontie, Dometie, Terentie și Domnin prin foc s-au săvârșit» — the holy martyrs Leontius, Dometius, Terentius and Domninus made their end by fire. The same one sentence stands under each of the four names, word for word.

So what is recorded of Leontius here is what the calendar records: that he is kept on this day with [Dometius](/saints/dometius-the-martyr-16-october), [Terentius](/saints/terentius-the-martyr-16-october) and [Domninus](/saints/domninus-the-martyr-16-october), and that the four of them died in the fire. The page gives no country, no emperor, no year, no account of their trial and no troparion, and nothing is added to it.

The Greek calendar keeps the four of them on the same day, 16 October, and adds one thing to the name: how they died. saint.gr gives them a single page with a heading for each — «Αγιος Λεοντιος», «Αγιος Δομνινος», «Αγιος Δομετιος», «Αγιος Τερεντιος» — and then one sentence for all four together, that the holy Leontius, Dometius, Terentius and Domninus were martyred by fire. It names no emperor, no governor, no city and no year, and prints no hymn; its two-line verse says that Leontius, having three fellow-contestants, was something lion-hearted in the middle of the pyre.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 16 octombrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/16-octombrie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-leontie); read 24 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 16 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/16/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2756/saint.aspx), which the four share, read 1 October 2026.*
