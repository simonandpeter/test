# Antony (Pankeyev)

The Russian calendar keeps fourteen people in one line on this day and he stands at the head of it: «Сщмчч. Антония , еп. Белгородского, и с ним Митрофана , Александра , Михаила , Ипполита , Николая , Василия , Николая , Максима , Александра , Павла , Павла , Георгия пресвитеров и мч. Михаила (1938)» — the hieromartyr Antony, bishop of Belgorod, and with him twelve presbyters and one layman. The line gives their forenames and their ranks and the year, and nothing else; the surnames are in the name links behind the forenames, and his own is «Святитель Антоний (Панкеев), епископ Белгородский, сщмч.»

There is no life page behind the line, and none behind any of the fourteen name links. His own page carries a heading, the see, the grade and two days, of which the second, 26 января, is the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, a company's feast and not a day of his own. So this folder asserts what the calendar asserts: that the Russian church keeps a bishop of Belgorod of this name and surname as a hieromartyr on this day, that he died in 1938, and that thirteen others of his diocese are kept with him — Митрофан, Александр, Михаил, Ипполит, Николай, Василий, Николай, Максим, Александр, Павел, Павел, Георгий and the layman Михаил, each of whom has his own folder here and carries this one as a companion.

What a second source would add is everything: the dates, the places, the trial and the manner of their deaths. azbyka.ru was not read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 19 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260519.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9130.html), which carries no life; read 8 October 2026.*
