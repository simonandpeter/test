# Boris, son of Vladimir

saint.gr's whole notice of the two brothers is two sentences, and this is all of them. The holy martyrs Borisos and Glievos — the Greek page's transcription of their Slavonic names — were sons of Saint [Vladimir](/saints/vladimir-the-great), whom the page sends the reader to look up on 15 July, and by holy baptism they were renamed Romanos and David. We have no further information about the life of these saints, the page says, and there it stops.

The Greek calendar keeps the two of them together on 24 July, and this folder is made from that line and the page behind it and from nothing else. The page gives no year, no place, no account of how they died, no relics and no hymn; it does not call them princes, and it names no third brother. His brother is the corpus's [Gleb](/saints/gleb-prince-and-passion-bearer), whom the Russian calendar keeps alone on 5 September under the baptismal name David that this page also gives him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/24/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2071/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
