# Anna (Shashkina)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Anna Shashkina on 28 апреля and gives no life of her. Its line for the day is the whole of what it says: «Мц. Анны (1939).» — the martyr Anna, and the year 1939 — with a link to her name and none to any reading. So what the calendar tells is that she was counted among the martyrs and that she died in 1939.

Her own name page on that site, 10058, carries nothing but the heading «Анна (Шашкина), мц.», which gives the surname the day's line leaves out, and the two days «26 января (Новомуч.) , 28 апреля». There is no life page for her anywhere on the site, no icon and no article, and no page names a monastery, a diocese or a place of any kind. The rank the calendar gives her is мученица and not преподобномученица: it grades her a martyr with no monastic or clerical standing, and nothing was added to that. The day page prints four hymns, two for the apostles of the day and two for the bishop of Turov, and none under her name. The 26 января day page, the general commemoration of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, was not read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 28 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260428.html) and [her name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/10058.html); read 8 October 2026.*
