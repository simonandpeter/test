# Sabbas of Sicily

Sabbas was born in the first half of the tenth century in Sicily, of godly and virtuous parents, Christopher and Kale. He was tonsured a monk in the monastery of St Philip, where his father Christopher and his brother Macarius were monks already. When the Saracens raided Sicily he fled with his father and his brother to Calabria, and there founded the monastery of the Archangels, of which he became abbot.

But the Saracen raids into the country of Calabria forced him out of that house too, and he took refuge in a district near the river Sinnos, where he founded the monastery of St Laurence. After his father's repose he took over the governing of the house. The holiness of his life made him known throughout Italy, and for that, the page says, he was surnamed Sabbas the Younger.

He fell asleep in peace in the year 995. saint.gr prints no hymn for him and no verses, and says nothing of where he was buried.
