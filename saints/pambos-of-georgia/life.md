# Pambos of Georgia

saint.gr names him and cites one book. The whole of its notice for him reads: the memory of Saint Pambos is mentioned in the Jerusalem Kanonarion, page 88. Nothing else is printed — no century, no year, no city, no length of office, no act, no wonder, no relic and no hymn — and even the office comes not from the notice but from the day's listing, which calls him «Άγιος Πάμβος «Καθολικός Γεωργίας»», Pambos, Catholicos of Georgia, in the site's own quotation marks.

So the record is a name, a day, an office the source itself holds at arm's length, and a page reference in one liturgical book. That is said here rather than filled in, because filling it in is the one thing this corpus exists not to do.
