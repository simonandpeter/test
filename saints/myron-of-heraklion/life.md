# Myron of Heraklion

saint.gr's calendar for 20 Μαρτίου keeps a new martyr of Crete. Myron came from the Great Castle of Crete, the page says — the Heraklion of today — and was born of devout and God-loving parents; his father was called Demetrius and was a just and virtuous man. Myron himself was modest and temperate, and loved virginity and asceticism beyond measure.

He worked as a tailor in Heraklion, and there the Turks, who envied him, slandered him with the charge of having seduced a Turkish girl. In court he answered the slander and rejected it, but the dilemma was put to him of Islam or death. The martyr answered with boldness that he did not deny his faith and was ready to endure every torture for the love of Christ, since he had been born a Christian and wished to die a Christian.

For that they beat him without mercy and threw him into prison. When they brought him out they led him before the judge again, where he kept repeating that he wished to die a Christian, and so he was condemned to death by hanging. A little before the martyrdom he asked leave of the executioners and went up to his father, fell at his feet and kissed his hand; and having received his blessing he came before the executioners, and shortly after received the crown of martyrdom. It was the year 1793.

The couplet over him turns on his name: thou wast seen as spiritual myrrh out of the noose, O Myron, prizewinner, the glory and the boast of Crete.
