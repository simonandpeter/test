# Demetrius (Vlasenkov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and gives no life. Its line for 22 апреля ст. ст. is the last of the eight the day prints and reads «Мч. Димитрия (1942).» — the martyr Demetrius, of the year 1942 — with a link to his name and none to a reading. That is the whole of what the calendar says of him, and this folder says no more than the calendar does.

The name page behind the line adds his family name, Власенков, which the day’s line leaves out and which is why this folder is called by it. It heads two days, «26 января (Новомуч.) , 22 апреля»: the second is the day he is kept on here, and the first is the general commemoration of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church, which is why that rank stands in this folder without a day of its own.

The grade the calendar gives him is «мч.», мученик, a martyr and not a hieromartyr, and neither page gives him any clerical rank; so nothing was written here about an office or a priesthood, and the year 1942 is the only year he has. No other calendar was read for him, and the Romanian, Greek and Serbian rows of his attestation name the day that was not checked.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 22 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260422.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9555.html), which name him and give no life; read 8 October 2026.*
