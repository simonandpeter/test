# Jonah of Novgorod

saint.gr's calendar for 5 Νοεμβρίου names Jonah, the wonderworking archbishop of Novgorod, and says in so many words that it has no life for him: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου» — we have no details of the saint's life. What the page carries besides the name is the year of his death in its head line, «(; - 1470)», with no year of birth; the see, Novgorod; and the title of wonderworker that the day's line gives him.

So nothing is recorded here of his birth, his monastery, his election, his years in the see or his burial, and the page prints no couplet and no hymn for him. He is one of the Russian saints the Greek calendar keeps, and the Greek page is the only source read for this folder; what the Russian calendar makes of his day has not been checked.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2899/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
