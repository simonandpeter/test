# Gabriel of Białystok

Gabriel was born in the year 1684 in the village of Zvierki, which lies south of the city of Białystok in the district of Zabłudów, at a time — saint.gr says — when persecutions and discriminations were being carried on against the Orthodox Church by the Uniates in Poland. When he was six years old he was carried off and taken to the city of Białystok, and there he was martyred, in the year 1690, by the enemies of the Church.

When in the eighteenth century the Christians opened his grave, they found his holy relic incorrupt. The holy body was taken to the city of Białystok in Poland, where it remained until the Second World War, when it was carried into Russia, to the church of the Most Holy Protection at Grodno. With the blessing of the Russian metropolitan Philaret, on 21 September 1992, the holy relic was translated back with honours and reverence to the city of Białystok.

That is the whole of the page. It does not say who took the child, or before whom he was brought, or how he was put to death; it names no judge and no companion, and it prints no couplet and no hymn.
