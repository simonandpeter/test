# Diodorus, companion of Andromachus

saint.gr's calendar for 12 October keeps «Άγιοι Ανδρομάχος και Διόδωρος» on one line, and the page behind it gives the two of them a heading each and then a single sentence for both: «Οι Άγιοι Ανδρομάχος και Διόδωρος μαρτύρησαν δια πυρός» — Saints [Andromachus](/saints/andromachus-companion-of-diodorus) and Diodorus were martyred by fire. That is the whole of what the calendar knows of them.

It gives no year and no reign, no city and no province, no persecutor, no rank beyond the word saint, and no hymn. The two-line verse it sets over the pair tells Andromachus to fight against the enemies of the Lord and to die together with Diodorus out of the fire, which is the same thing the sentence says. The note printed under his name — about a historiographer from Sicily — is the site's remark on who else has borne the name Diodorus, and belongs to the name and not to this saint.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2722/saint.aspx), which the two share; read 1 October 2026.*
