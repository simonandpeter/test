# Felix, companion of Eusebius

doxologia.ro's calendar names Felix and gives no life. The one line it prints for him is the same line it prints on the page of [Eusebius](/saints/eusebius-companion-of-felix), the deacon he is named with: «Sfinţii Felix, preotul şi Eusebie, diaconul de sabie s-au săvârşit» — Saints Felix the priest and Eusebius the deacon were put to death by the sword.

That is the whole of it. The page does not say where they suffered, or when, or under whom, and it prints no troparion for either of them. What is recorded is that Felix was a priest, that Eusebius was a deacon, that they are remembered together, and that they died by the sword.

The Greek calendar keeps the two on the same day, 19 October, and its whole notice of them is two sentences. The holy Felix the presbyter and Eusebius the deacon, it says, were martyred by the sword — the same ranks the Romanian calendar gives them — and then: perhaps they are the same as those who are kept on 7 October as Saints Eusebius the presbyter and Felix. The second sentence is the page's own guess and it is left as that. It swaps the two ranks over between the entries, and the calendar prints the two days as two entries; so this folder stays where it is, with [Eusebius](/saints/eusebius-companion-of-felix) beside it, and the identification the page suggests is recorded and not acted on. Beyond the manner of death the page gives no year, no city and no hymn.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 19 octombrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/19-octombrie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-felix-preotul); read 24 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 19 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/19/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2773/saint.aspx), read 2 October 2026.*
