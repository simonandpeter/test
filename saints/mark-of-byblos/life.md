# Mark of Byblos

Mark, who was also called John — not, the Greek synaxarion is careful to say, the Evangelist — was one of the Seventy. He was ordained bishop of Byblos and worked there as an apostle, and as with Peter (Acts 5:15), the sick on whom his shadow alone fell were healed. The synaxarion keeps him on 27 September with [Aristarchus](/saints/aristarchus-of-apamea) and [Zeno](/saints/zeno-of-diospolis), and gives no date.

The Romanian calendar keeps him on 27 April, and its short life says the same of him in three sentences: that Mark was set bishop by the apostles in Vivlopole — Byblos — and that Saint Luke remembers him in the Acts of the Apostles; that he suffered many evils from unbelievers for rebuilding churches and pulling down idols; and that, himself becoming a worker of miracles and of healings, he departed to the Lord. It gives no year. The Evangelist Mark, whom the same calendar keeps two days earlier on 25 April, is a different man.

The Serbian church's Ohrid Prologue keeps the three of the Seventy under one heading on this day, as the Greek and the Russian do, and says of Mark the one thing neither of them says: that it was in the house of his mother Mary in Jerusalem that the holy apostles gathered for prayer, citing Acts 12:12, and that he preached the Gospel with the apostles [Paul](/saints/paul-the-apostle) and [Barnabas](/saints/barnabas-the-apostle), citing Acts 12:25. After that, it says, he was bishop in the city of Byblos. The Greek synaxarion is careful on the same day to say that he is not the Evangelist; the Prologue does not raise the question, and identifies him instead with the John Mark of the Acts.

Of the three together the Prologue says that they shone like stars in the darkness of heathendom and turned many to the faith of Christ, and that they shine now as stars in the kingdom of the Christ they loved. It gives no date for any of them, as the Greek does not.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 27 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2520/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026; and doxologia.ro's calendar for 27 aprilie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/27-aprilie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-apostol-marcu), read 20 September 2026; and the Serbian church's [Ohrid Prologue for the Julian 27 September](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-10-10&prolog=1), read 4 October 2026.*
