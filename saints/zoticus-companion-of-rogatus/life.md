# Zoticus, companion of Rogatus

saint.gr's calendar for 12 Ιανουαρίου names four martyrs in one line, «Άγιοι Ζωτικός, Ρογάτος, Μοδεστός και Καστούλιος οι Μάρτυρες», and Zoticus stands first of them. The page behind the line has one sentence for all four: «Οι Άγιοι Ζωτικός, Ρογάτος, Μοδεστός και Καστούλιος ήταν στρατιωτικοί και μαρτύρησαν στην Αφρική» — Zoticus, [Rogatus](/saints/rogatus-companion-of-zoticus), [Modestus](/saints/modestus-companion-of-zoticus) and [Castulus](/saints/castulus-companion-of-zoticus) were soldiers, and they were martyred in Africa.

The page then says it has no more details of the lives of these martyrs. So the record is four names, a trade, a continent and a day: no emperor, no city, no year, no manner of death, and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1409/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
