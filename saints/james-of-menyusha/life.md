# James of Menyusha

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 24 июня ст. ст. names two children on one line, «Правв. отроков Иакова и Иоанна Менюжских, Новгородских (1566-1569).», and gives them one life page between them of three sentences. They were brothers in the flesh, it says, the children of devout parents, Исидор and Варвара; and they were killed by evildoers, the one at three years old and the other at five.

Between 1682 and 1689 their incorrupt relics were found and laid under the floor of the church of the Trinity in the village of Менюж, on the river Менюга in the diocese of Novgorod, on the site of a monastery of the Trinity that had once stood there. That is the whole of what this source says of them. It does not say who killed them, or why, or in what year; the interval in this folder is the one the day line prints beside their names, and the life page gives no year for the death at all.

He is kept here in his own folder beside [his brother](/saints/john-of-menyusha), because the line names each of them. The calendar grades them «Правв. отроков», righteous youths, and gives them no clerical or monastic rank. The day page prints one troparion for the two together, which is copied into both folders.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 24 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260624.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1390.htm); read 9 October 2026.*
