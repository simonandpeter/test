# Sisoes of the Kyiv Caves

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 24 октября ст. ст. as the second of three recluses of the caves of Kyiv, and his own page is three sentences long: he laboured in the monastery of the Kyiv caves in the twelfth and thirteenth centuries, and the relics of the saint rest in the Near caves, which the page also calls the caves of the first of the monastery's founders.

The only thing said of him beyond that is a phrase out of the service. The life the three of them share reports that in the common service to the venerable of those caves he is called «просиявшим в посте» — one who shone in fasting. He has no story, no place of origin, no elder and no year; the day's line gives him the thirteenth century and his own page the twelfth and thirteenth, and this folder keeps both readings rather than choosing between them.

His name is read off the heading of that page of his own, because the name link behind his place in the day's line points at a page headed for all three together and so offers no nominative for him alone. The day page carries troparia and kontakia for four of the saints it heads, and none under his name. [Arethas](/saints/arethas-of-the-kyiv-caves), the first of the three, has the one story the three of them share between them, and [Theophilus](/saints/theophilus-of-the-kyiv-caves), the third, is as bare as he is.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 24 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261024.html), [his own page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life6850.htm) and [the life the three share](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4583.htm); read 4 October 2026.*
