# Helladius of Auxerre

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life. Its whole account is one sentence: the holy Helladius was bishop of the city of Auxerre, and he fell asleep in peace in the year 387. The head line of the page repeats the year and leaves the year of his birth blank, writing only «(; - 387)».

That is everything the page holds. It does not say where he was born, when or by whom he was consecrated, how long he held the see, what he did in it, or where he was buried; it names no writings, no council and no relics, and it prints neither apolytikion nor kontakion for him. Three other Helladii stand in this corpus on other days and none of them is this bishop.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1536/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
