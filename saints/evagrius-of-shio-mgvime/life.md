# Evagrius, disciple of Shio of Mgvime

saint.gr says the venerable Evagrius and [Shio](/saints/shio-of-mgvime) of Mgvime lived in Georgia in the sixth century. Evagrius was at first Duke of Zichantini and the head of the greatest estate at the court of the kingdom of Kartli; then he became one of Shio's first Georgian disciples, and afterwards hegumen of the monastery Shio founded.

He meant to become a monk, the page says, after he saw a wonder while out hunting: a dove bringing food to the hermit Shio. Shio was against the decision at first, because it was too hasty. But Evagrius insisted, and in the end Shio told him to go home, settle all his affairs, take leave of his own people, and then go to the banks of the river Mtkvari and put into the water a staff that Shio would give him. If the river dried up before Evagrius's eyes, that would be a divine sign to begin the monastic life; otherwise the would-be monk was to give up his purpose.

Evagrius did so, and by the will of God he stayed with Shio. From then on the number of ascetics around them began to multiply, and in that way the monastery was born. Evagrius bought the village of Saltemba with its attached lands for the brotherhood at his own expense. A little while after, Shio, with his spiritual father's blessing, shut himself in a cave and appointed Evagrius hegumen of the monastic brotherhood.
