# Germanus, companion of Zebinas

saint.gr's calendar for 20 Οκτωβρίου names four martyrs in one line and tells a single story for all of them. [Zebinas](/saints/zebinas-20-october), Germanus, [Nicephorus](/saints/nicephorus-companion-of-zebinas) and [Antoninus](/saints/antoninus-companion-of-zebinas) suffered, the page says, under Maximian, whose reign it dates 286 to 305. Antoninus was an old man; the other three were young. They were arrested at Caesarea because they were Christians, by a man the page names only as Mazos, and they were beheaded there.

That is the whole of what saint.gr prints of them. It gives no country beyond the city of their arrest, no trades, no families, no words of their own, and no year inside the reign it names. The couplet set over the entry plays on one of the four names: the sword, it says, shows Zebinas, Germanus and Nicephorus, together with Antoninus, to be bearers of victory. No hymn is printed for them.

The page closes by noting that their memory is kept again on 12 November, there together with the virgin Manatho, whose name it says is wrongly given as Maratho on that day. Both days are the Greek calendar's own; this folder records the day page read here, and the second day is kept as a note on the attestation.

The Russian calendar keeps this company too, and on a day of its own. days.pravoslavie.ru's line for 13 ноября ст. ст. reads «Мчч. Антонина , Никифора и Германа (ок. 308).» — three of saint.gr's four, with Zebinas not among them — and the name link behind his place in it, page 510, gives «Герман Кесарийский (Палестинский), мч.» It names the province that saint.gr leaves at the bare city: Caesarea in Palestine. The Russian calendar prints no life for the three of them and no hymn under any of their names; the day page's troparion and kontakion both belong to the great hierarch who heads the day.

It dates the martyrdom «ок. 308», which falls outside the reign of Maximian saint.gr dates them by. Neither host is preferred: the death interval is widened to hold both readings and both sources' words stand in its note. The next line of the same Russian day, «Мц. Манефы (ок. 308).», is the virgin kept with the four on the Greek 12 November; the Russian gives her a line of her own, and nothing on either host says she was taken or killed with them, so no relation is written to her. The Russian rank word for him is «мч.», a martyr, which is what this folder already held.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 20 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/20/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/810/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery, 13 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261113.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/510.html), read 5 October 2026.*
