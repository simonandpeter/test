# Gerasimus Palladas

Gerasimus Palladas was born in the year 1633 in the village of Skillous in the Pediada of Crete, of noble and devout parents; his father Theodore was a protopapas. saint.gr's header dates him 1633 to 1714, and its account of the years between — his learning, his sees, and his patriarchate of Alexandria — is far longer than what is set down here. This life does not attempt it: what is written here is what was read.

What the page's closing lines record is that his official recognition as a saint was made by the Patriarchate of Alexandria on 17 September 2002, and that the honoured skull of the saint is at the monastery of Vatopedi on the Holy Mountain. It is by that recognition, and not by long custom, that the Greek calendar keeps him on 15 January.

The page prints no hymn for him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 15 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/15/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3321/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
