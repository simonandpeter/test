# Elijah (Vyatlin)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and carries no life. Its line for the twenty-third of March, old style, reads «Сщмч. Василия , Стефана пресвитеров, прмч. Илии , прмц. Анастасии , Варвары , мч. Алексия (1938).», and the name page behind «Илии» is headed «Илия (Вятлин), иеромонах, прмч.», which gives the surname the line leaves out. That page lists the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church on «26 января» and this twenty-third of March.

Nothing else is said of him on either page: no place, no diocese, no parish, no year of birth and no account of how he died. The 1938 the line closes with is the whole of his chronology. The second source for the Russian new martyrs, azbyka.ru, was not read for him.

The line’s rank word for him is «прмч.», venerable-martyr, and the name page’s grade «иеромонах»: a monk in priestly orders, which is what tells him from the two presbyters named before him in the same line.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 23 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260323.html); read 8 October 2026.*
