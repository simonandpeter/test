# Callinike (9 May)

saint.gr's calendar names her and gives no life beyond one sentence for the two of them. She and [Aquilina](/saints/aquilina-9-may) were harlots, the page says, sent by the emperor Decius to entice the martyr [Christopher](/saints/christopher) and draw him into idolatry. Instead they believed in Christ through Christopher, and were martyred: they were put to death with spits driven through them from their feet to their shoulders.

The page adds nothing else about her at all — no city, no judge of their own, no burial place, no relics, no apolytikion and no kontakion — and the year 251 appears only in the head line over her name, the body giving no year but only the emperor who sent them. It notes of the name itself that Καλλινίκη is κάλλος and νίκη together, the beautiful and glorious victor.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 9 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/09/index.aspx) and [the life of the pair](https://www.saint.gr/1497/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
