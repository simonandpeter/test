# Basil (Pariysky)

The Сретенский monastery's calendar names him fourth in its clause of fifteen for 25 ноября ст. ст. and carries no life for him; azbyka.ru prints one out of fond.ru. He was born on 28 July 1894, the day the Church keeps the memory of the prince Vladimir — whose own baptismal name was Basil — in one of the villages of the Александров district of the province of Vladimir, the son of a psalm-reader also called Василий Парийский.

He finished the Переяславль church school in 1912 and entered the Vladimir seminary, but completed only its first year and became psalm-reader of the church of his own village. He served in the army from 1919 to 1922 and went back to the psalm-reader's place; he married Вера Ивановна and they had three children. He was ordained deacon in 1925 and priest in 1928 to the church of the Tikhvin icon at Титовское in the province of Moscow, where he served until 1936, and then moved to the Dormition church of Красное in the Куровской district, where he stayed until his arrest. Like every clergyman of those years he and his family were stripped of the vote, and in the collectivisation they were turned out of their house and their property taken.

He was arrested on 27 November 1937 on a charge of counter-revolutionary agitation and held in the Taganka prison. The page prints the whole of the exchange: the investigator said the investigation had material that he had carried on systematic counter-revolutionary agitation among his neighbours; the priest answered «Я это не подтверждаю, контрреволюционной агитации я не проводил»; the investigator demanded truthful evidence; and the priest said he could give no other. He admitted none of the charges. On 1 December the judicial troika sentenced him to be shot, and he was executed on 8 December 1937 and buried in an unmarked common grave on the Butovo range outside Moscow.
