# Dometius, disciple of Dionysius

His own page on doxologia.ro carries only his name, the day and the readings appointed for it, and no life at all. What the calendar prints of him it prints inside the life of [Dionysius](/saints/dionysius-of-athos), whom it names on the same day: a hieromonk called Dometius, foreseeing and full of the Spirit, who lived at the church of the Most Holy Theotokos on the Holy Mountain, and to whom the man who had seen a light night after night on the mountain came at last to tell the cause of it. That the two lines on one day are these two men is read off that life and off nothing else.

When Dionysius had died, his monks came back from the city with the alms and the blessing of the emperor and the bishop and told the brethren of their father's passing, and all wept for the loss of such a man. Dometius comforted them; and when he would have gone away to his own cell they all fell at his feet, begging him with tears not to leave them orphaned but to stay and shepherd them as before.

So that he should not be disobedient, the life says, he stayed, and shepherded them in a way pleasing to God and with much diligence; and coming full of days he went to the Lord. No year, no place of burial and no hymn is printed for him, and none is recorded here.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 25 iunie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/25-iunie), [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-dometie) and [the life of Dionysius](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-cuvios-dionisie); read 24 September 2026.*
