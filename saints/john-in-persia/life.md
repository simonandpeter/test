# John in Persia

The Greek calendar keeps on 29 November a hieromartyr John whom it calls «ὁ ἐν Περσίδι», the one in Persia, and the whole of saint.gr's account of him is a single reported sentence: in many Menologia this John is said to have come of the city of Damascus and to have been martyred in Persia. That is where the epithet comes from, and it is the only thing said of him besides his rank.

The page gives him no century, no emperor, no shah, no manner of death and no burial, and it prints no hymn. The couplet over his entry says only that to John too, the sacrificer of Christ, rewards are measured out richly from him. Nothing more is recorded here; anything further would have to come from the Menologia the page names and does not quote.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 29 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/29/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3118/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
