# Michael (Skobelev)

Michael was born on 14 November 1887 in the village of Bikalikha in Tver province to devout peasants, Stephen and Eudocia Skobelev, who sent him to the teachers’ school at Novgorod. In 1910 he was ordained deacon to the church at Rameshki, to which his whole life was bound, and in 1925, when many had already died as martyrs, he was ordained priest to the church where he had served fifteen years as deacon. In 1929 he was arrested for not paying taxes and sentenced to two years of camp and five of exile, and in 1935 he came back to serve at Rameshki.

The persecution began again at once, with taxes and tasks set so that he could not meet them, and he asked his parishioners from the ambo to help him with the money. In his Christmas sermon of 1937 he told them not to believe the blasphemers who say there is no God and yet count their years from the Nativity of Christ, which was later charged against him. When the authorities proposed in June 1937 to close the church as unsafe, the parishioners went to the district executive committee to defend it; the authorities gave way, but arrested Father Michael on 17 July and closed the church for want of a priest. He was questioned harshly in the Tver prison, it seems under torture; on 27 September the NKVD troika sentenced him to death, and he was shot on 1 October 1937.

The Russian calendar keeps him on 18 September old style, and he is also remembered on 26 January with the new martyrs.
