# Nicholas (Tsikura)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him in a single line for 5 февраля ст. ст. — «Мч. Николая (1918).» — a martyr who died in 1918. The name link behind the line gives the calendar's own nominative, «Николай (Цикура)», which adds the surname the line leaves out. The calendar prints no life page for him, and none is given here: that is what it records, and the whole of it.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 5 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260205.html); read 3 October 2026.*
