# Gennadius the Dionysiate

saint.gr says Gennadius was a monk of the monastery of Dionysiou on the Holy Mountain. At the urging of his abbot he went to Constantinople as the companion of Boniphatius and Eudokimos, who were walking towards martyrdom. But those two lost their nerve before the tortures, denied Christ, and denounced Gennadius as the cause of their journey to martyrdom.

The Turks then seized Gennadius, imprisoned him and tortured him in one way after another. He stayed steadfast in his faith, and was beheaded on 6 April 1818. A portion of the relics of the venerable martyr is at the monastery of Dionysiou, where he had trained himself in the virtues and in the faith.

That is the whole of what the page gives. It names no country and no year of birth for him, gives no family, says nothing more of the two who denounced him, and prints no couplet and no hymn under his name; what it links beside him is the synaxis of the new martyrs who suffered after the taking of Constantinople, which is a company and not a person and is not kept here.
