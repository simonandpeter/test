# Concordius of Spoleto

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery makes him the son of a presbyter Гордиан, brought up in piety and in the faith of Christ, and says that the bishop of Rome Пий made him a subdeacon for it. He kept the fast and the prayer with his father and gave alms generously, and then, with his father's leave, settled near Rome with a kinsman Евтихий and spent his days in prayer and in good works.

The repute of that life reached Торкват, the governor of the Tuscan region, who called him in and urged him to deny Christ, promising to make him a priest of the pagan gods; the answer was that the governor should turn to the true God instead. He was beaten and thrown into prison. A bishop Анфим, a friend of the governor's, begged to have the prisoner released to him, and he lived with the bishop a long while and was ordained presbyter there.

Called a second time and asked what he thought of his life, he said that life for him was Christ. He was bound to a post of shame, then shut in prison with his neck and hands chained to the wall. Three days later the governor sent armed men to tell him that if he did not sacrifice he would be put to death; he said «Слава Тебе, Господи Иисусе Христе», spat on the idol of Zeus they had brought in, and bent his own head under the sword. His end followed about the year 175, and the calendar says his relics rest in Italy not far from the city of Spoleto.
