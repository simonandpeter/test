# The Three Virgin Martyrs of Chios

saint.gr's calendar for 9 June keeps three virgin martyrs of Chios and names none of them. The one sentence it prints about them is the whole of what is recorded: the three holy virgin martyrs from Chios bore witness by the sword. There is no year here, no emperor, no persecutor, no place on the island and no account of how they were taken — and no name, which is the first thing a reader looks for.

The couplet the page sets above that sentence is the only other detail it gives them, and what it gives is a colour: three brides of God, whom the sword took away, were dyed with the hue of their own blood. The corpus keeps two other such unnamed companies of women from this calendar, the three of Magnesia and the three new martyrs of Vrachori, and this is a third; what tells them apart is the island and the day, and nothing else the source offers.
