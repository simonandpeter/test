# Anatolius (Smirnov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Anatolius among the venerable martyrs on 29 July and gives no life. Its line is «Прмч. Анатолия (1930-1935).» — the venerable martyr Anatolius, 1930 to 1935 — and the calendar's own name page for him adds the surname and keeps the grade the line gives: «Анатолий (Смирнов), иеромонах, прмч.», Anatolius Smirnov, hieromonk.

The year is a span and not a date, which is rare on this calendar: it does not know when he died, only that it was within those six years, and this folder records the span and does not narrow it. He was a monk in priest's orders; the calendar gives him no office beyond that rank.

That is the whole of the record. Of the twenty-one folders this corpus keeps whose slug carries his surname, not one is a man of his forename. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 29 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260729.html); read 13 October 2026.*
