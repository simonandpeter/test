# Gerebernus of Geel

saint.gr keeps Gerebernus — Gerebran, as the page also spells him, and «Γκέρεμπραν» in its Greek — on this day together with [Dymphna](/saints/dymphna-of-geel), and everything it says of him it says inside her story. He was her spiritual father, and when her own father, the pagan king of some region of Ireland, resolved after his wife's death to marry his daughter, it was with Gerebernus's help that she fled.

The king pursued them, the page says, found them in a small village called Cheel, or Geel, near the present-day Antwerp, and killed them both. It was 15 May, somewhere between 620 and 640. The wonders that followed at Geel, and the tradition of charity and of care for the mentally ill that grew up there, the page tells of her tomb and not of his.

Nothing else is printed for him: no birthplace, no parents, no age, no year of birth, no office beyond the word «πνευματικός», no hymn and no relics. His is one of the two head names the day's single line carries, and what the page does not say is not set down here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 15 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/15/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4264/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
