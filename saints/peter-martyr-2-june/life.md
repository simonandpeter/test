# Peter (2 June)

saint.gr's calendar names him with a companion and gives them one sentence between them. The line for 2 June reads «Άγιοι Μαρκελλίνος και Πέτρος οι ιερομάρτυρες», and the page behind it says that the holy hieromartyrs Marcellinus and Peter bore witness in the year 304, in the years of the emperor Diocletian, who reigned from 284 to 305; and that a church of theirs was built by Constantine the Great, whom the same calendar keeps on 21 May, in Rome.

So what is recorded of Peter is a name, a rank, a year, a reign, a companion and a church in Rome that was raised over their memory. The page does not say where they suffered, what they suffered, what office either of them held, or which of them was the elder. It prints no verse and no hymn. His companion Marcellinus has no folder here, and the reason is not that the page withholds him: it is that the corpus already keeps a Marcellinus of Rome who died in the same persecution, on a neighbouring day, and this source says nothing that would show whether the two are one man or two.
