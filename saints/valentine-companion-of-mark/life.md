# Valentine, companion of Mark

doxologia.ro's calendar names Valentine for 24 octombrie and gives no life. The Viață tab of his page carries one sentence, and it is the same sentence that stands on the pages of the two men he is named with: «Sfinţii Marcu, Sotirih şi Valentin, pe pământ fiind târâţi, aşa s-au săvârşit» — Saints Mark, Soterichus and Valentine, being dragged along the ground, so ended their lives.

That is everything. The page does not say where they suffered, or when, or under whom, or what they were dragged behind, and it gives them no rank beyond the word martyr in the calendar's line. His companions are [Mark](/saints/mark-companion-of-soterichus) and [Soterichus](/saints/soterichus-companion-of-mark).

His page is the only one of the three that carries a Tropar tab, and what it serves there is the troparion headed for Saint Aretas the Great Martyr and those with him, who is kept on the same day. The text names nobody: it is a common of martyrs. It is copied here as the page has it, and the heading is recorded with it.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 24 octombrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/24-octombrie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-valentin); read 24 September 2026.*
