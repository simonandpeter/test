# Gildas the Wise

Gildas was born about the year 500 in Wales, saint.gr says, and was a pupil of Saint Iltud, whom the same calendar keeps on 6 November. He followed at first the married life, and when his wife died he became a monk. He was one of the very learned men of his time, and it is for that that he is called the wise.

He is held to have written a work in which he accuses the rulers of his time, and the clergy, and the people, of their impiety and their lawlessness. In the last years of his life he lived in Brittany, and he fell asleep in holiness about the year 570.

That is the whole of what the Greek calendar prints of him. It names no monastery, gives the title of no book, and prints no apolytikion and no kontakion; the day's own line spells his name in Latin letters, and the Greek form used here is the page's own transcription of it in the life.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 29 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/29/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3554/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
