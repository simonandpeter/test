# Ethelburga of East Anglia

saint.gr's calendar for 11 Οκτωβρίου names «Αγία Ethelburga», writing the name in Latin letters as the English write it, and gives her one sentence: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο της Αγίας παρά μόνο ότι έζησε τον 7ο αιώνα μ.Χ. και ήταν κόρη του βασιλιά της Ανατολικής Αγγλίας» — we have no details of the life of the saint except only that she lived in the seventh century and was the daughter of the king of East Anglia.

That is the whole of the record. The page does not name her father, or her house, or the place where she lived or died; it gives her no rank, no abbacy and no year beyond the century; and it prints no hymn for her. The Greek form of her name is not printed either: the line carries the English spelling only, so no Greek name form is recorded for this folder. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands.
