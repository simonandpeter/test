# Symeon beyond the Anaplous

saint.gr's whole notice of him is two sentences about where his memory is recorded, and it has no life to give. He is unknown, the page says, to the Synaxarion of Saint Nicodemus. His memory is noted in the Patmos Codex 266 and in the Vienna manuscript Theol. gr. 300, and also in Delehaye's Synaxarion, without any biographical note.

The day's line gives him the two titles the page knows him by, archimandrite and stylite, and the byname «ο επέκεινα του Ανάπλου» — he beyond the Anaplous, the strait above the city. Beyond that the page prints no country, no century, no monastery, no account of his repose, no relics, no distich and no hymn, and its heading gives the feast as fixed, kept on 26 July of every year. This folder says no more than the page does, which is that the Church keeps his memory and that nobody wrote down his life.
