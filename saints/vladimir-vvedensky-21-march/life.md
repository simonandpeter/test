# Vladimir (Vvedensky, 21 March)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and carries no life. Its line for the twenty-first of March, old style, reads «Сщмч. Владимира пресвитера (1931).» — the hieromartyr Vladimir, presbyter — and the name page behind that «Владимира» is headed «Владимир (Введенский), иерей, сщмч.», which gives both the surname the line leaves out and the grade the line words differently. That page lists two days, the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church on «26 января» and this twenty-first of March.

This calendar keeps a second priest of the same name and surname, and he is not this man. It gives him a name page of his own, a day of his own on the twenty-first of October, old style, a life, a birth at Pogost-Arkhangelsky in the Kalyazin uyezd of the province of Tver on 14 July 1881, and a death by shooting on 3 November 1937. This man is a name page with no life and the year 1931. Two pages, two days and two years are the whole of what tells the two apart, and nothing read puts them in one sentence.

Nothing else is said of him on either page: no place, no diocese, no parish, no year of birth and no account of how he died. The 1931 the line closes with is the whole of his chronology, and it is earlier than most of the years this calendar gives the priests it keeps by a name and a day alone. The second source for the Russian new martyrs, azbyka.ru, was not read for him. The day page carries seven hymns and all seven are under another of the day's saints; none is his.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 21 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260321.html); read 8 October 2026.*
