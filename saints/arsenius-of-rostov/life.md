# Arsenius (Matsievich)

saint.gr's calendar for 28 Φεβρουαρίου names him with his see — its line reads «Άγιος Αρσένιος Μητροπολίτης Ροστώβ» — and the page standing under it gives one sentence: the venerable Arsenius, whom it names in a parenthesis as Matsievich, was born in Russia, was elected metropolitan of the city of Rostov, and fell asleep in peace in the year 1772.

That is the whole of the entry. It gives him no year of birth — its heading leaves the first of his two dates a question mark — no place in Russia, no account of his episcopate, no relics, no couplet and no hymn; and what stands above the sentence is only saint.gr's standing gloss on the name Arsenius and on two earlier bearers of it, a monk who taught the children of Theodosius the Great and a Byzantine general, which belongs to the name and not to this man.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/28/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1942/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
