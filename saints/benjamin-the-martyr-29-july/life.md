# Benjamin (29 July)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 29 iulie is «Sfântul Mucenic Veniamin» — the holy martyr Benjamin — and his own page on the site carries only the day and the epistle and gospel appointed for it, the readings of a martyr. There is no Viață tab, no troparion, no country, no century and no account of his suffering.

The two men of this name the corpus already keeps are Russian new-martyrs of the twentieth century, named in their calendars with their surnames, and neither is claimed here. This folder stands for the Romanian line of 29 July alone, and is marked by its day because the calendar gives him nothing else to be known by.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 29 July, and gives him one thing the Romanian line does not: a companion. Its entry is «Άγιοι Βενιαμίν και Βήριος» — the holy Benjamin and Berius — and the page behind it has only two sentences for the pair: their synaxis was kept near the palaces of the Hebdomon, and we have no other details of their life. That is all the addition amounts to. There is still no country, no century, no emperor, no manner of death, no year and no hymn. [Berius](/saints/berius) has his own folder, made from this same line.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 29 iulie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/29-iulie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-veniamin), which prints no life; read 24 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 29 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/29/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/736/saint.aspx), read 1 October 2026.*
