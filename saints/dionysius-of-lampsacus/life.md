# Dionysius of Lampsacus

Dionysius comes into the life doxologia prints for this day as one sentence and leaves it at the killing. While [Peter](/saints/peter-of-lampsacus) was being tortured and beheaded at Lampsacus, a city of the Hellespont, under the governor Optimus, a Christian man named Dionysius was seized and held bound in the prison there. The same persecution had brought in two soldiers of Mesopotamia, Andrew and Paul, and a man called Nicomachus, who confessed Christ under the iron claws and then denied Him at the last.

The people, hearing the martyrs speak against their gods, shouted to the governor to give them into their hands. He had them beaten first and then handed over; and the crowd tied ropes to their feet, dragged them with much noise out of the city and stoned them there. The virgin Christina, hearing of it, ran to the place crying and weeping and threw herself upon the bodies, saying, Let me die with you on earth that I may live with you in heaven; and the governor had her pulled away and killed with the sword.

So they died, the life ends: Peter by many torments, Dionysius and Andrew and Paul by stones, and the virgin Christina by the sword. This was done at Lampsacus in the reign of the emperor Decius. The calendar for 18 mai names Dionysius on his own, without an epithet; the epithet used here is the city his one sentence gives him.
