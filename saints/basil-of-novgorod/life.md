# Basil of Novgorod

Basil is one of the twelve hierarchs of Novgorod whom saint.gr's calendar keeps together on 10 Φεβρουαρίου, and he is the only one of them the same day gives a page of his own. He was elected to Novgorod in 1329, that page says, and was ordained in the church of Saints Cosmas and Damian; he wrote the well-loved reading «Ο παράδεισος επί της γης», Paradise upon earth; and he fell asleep in peace in the year 1352.

The two places saint.gr names him disagree about his rank. The day's line calls him «Άγιος Βασίλειος Αρχιεπίσκοπος Νόβγκοροντ της Ρωσίας», archbishop, and his own page says «εξελέγη Μητροπολίτης του Νόβγκοροντ», was elected metropolitan. The disagreement is recorded on this folder's Greek row rather than settled, and no hymn and no relics are printed for him.

The synaxis feast, the page for the twelve says, was established after Saint [John](/saints/john-of-novgorod), archbishop of Novgorod, appeared to Saint Euthymius, archbishop of Novgorod, and told him to keep a vigil in the cathedral church of the Holy Wisdom for the blessed hierarchs of that local church. The twelve are named there with the year each fell asleep: Ιωακείμ (κοιμήθηκε 1030 μ.Χ.), Λουκάς (1060), Γερμανός (1096), Αρκάδιος (1162), Γρηγόριος (1193), Μαρτύριος (1199), Αντώνιος (1231), Βασίλειος (1352), Συμεών (1421), Γεννάδιος (1505), Ποιμήν (1571), Αφφώνιος (1652).

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/10/index.aspx) and [the page for the twelve hierarchs](https://www.saint.gr/1366/saint.aspx) and [his own page](https://www.saint.gr/1216/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
