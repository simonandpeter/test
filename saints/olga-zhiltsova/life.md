# Olga (Zhiltsova)

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names her fourth of the six women it grades прмц. on the one line it gives to fourteen people of 1938 for the Julian 1 March, and prints no life for her; azbyka.ru prints one life for her and for the two others of her village. Olga Yegorovna Zhiltsova was born in 1887 in the village of Goretovo in the Lukhovitsy district of the province of Ryazan, the daughter of the peasant Yegor Zhiltsov, and at eighteen she entered the Kazan monastery in the city of Ryazan as a novice.

After the house was closed she went home and lived there alone with her mother. When the threat of the church’s closing hung over the village she went round to some of the faithful to persuade them not to forget the house of God and to give what help they could towards its repair, since otherwise it might be closed and there would be nowhere to pray. That, and the collection the warden made, is the whole of what the case against the three was built on.

In the autumn of 1937 an officer of the НКВД came to the secretary of the village Komsomol at Goretovo, called the warden in and required of her a list of who was the church’s priest, who its psalmist and who the members of its church council, and took the list and went away. Some time after he left, a denunciation reached the head of the Lukhovitsy district section saying that a conspiratorial meeting of ministers of religion had been held at [Eudocia (Arkhipova)](/saints/eudocia-arkhipova)’s — «Собрание происходило с 9 часов утра до 16 часов дня, причем помещение было закрыто изнутри и занавешены окна».

On 15 February 1938 the officers asked a neighbour of the warden to knock at her door; he agreed, she opened to him as a neighbour, and she was arrested. On 26 February Olga and [Basil (Arkhipov)](/saints/basil-arkhipov) were arrested and held in the prison at Kolomna. [Eudocia (Arkhipova)](/saints/eudocia-arkhipova) was asked why she abused the collective farm and urged people to give to the church and not subscribe to the state loan, and answered that the one thing she owned was collecting money to repair the church. Olga was asked whether she had drawn collective farmers into a group of believers, collected money for the priest and agitated against the loan, and said she admitted nothing at all and knew nothing of it, and with that her questioning ended.

«Вы, как псаломщик», the investigator said to the psalmist, charging him with agitating among the people to draw collective farmers into a group of believers, with telling them that the Soviet power was given them as a punishment, and with agitating against the constitution — that there was a constitution and that in fact the Orthodox Church was persecuted. He answered that he had not agitated, had carried on no fight against the constitution and no agitation against the Soviet power, and did not admit himself guilty of it.

The false witness called was the secretary of the district Komsomol committee, who said that on 22 November an illegal meeting of priests and nuns of Goretovo had been held in the former nun’s lodging; that the whole pack of them carried on counter-revolutionary work among the collective farmers, so that discipline on the farm was bad and on the religious feasts they agitated for people not to work but to go to church; that they did it openly; and that the former nun, now church warden, had under the priest’s direction made unlawful collections of money among the people for a thorough repair of the church — and that the church now stood thoroughly repaired.

The investigation was closed that same day, 26 February 1938, and on 8 March the troika of the НКВД sentenced the two novices and the psalmist — [Eudocia (Arkhipova)](/saints/eudocia-arkhipova), Olga and [Basil (Arkhipov)](/saints/basil-arkhipov) — to be shot. After the sentence all three were taken to the Taganka prison in Moscow, where on 13 March the prison photographer photographed them, so that among the multitude of people condemned to death it could be checked that the right ones were being led out to the execution. They were shot on 14 March 1938 and buried in a common unmarked grave at the Butovo range outside Moscow.
