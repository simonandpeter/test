# John the Soldier

saint.gr says that John lived in the years of Julian the Apostate, and that he was a soldier «εις τα νούμερα των καλουμένων Ταϊφάλων» — in the companies of those called the Taifali. He had been sent out with other soldiers to hunt down Christians. He hunted them in appearance only: in secret he helped them to get away, to hold out under the torments, to have the bare necessities for dealing with their sicknesses, and other things besides.

Having passed his life in that way, pleasing to God, John rested in peace and was buried in the Pandektes, the place where strangers too were given burial. His synaxis, the page says, was kept in the first apostolic church of Saint John the Theologian. There is no hymn for him on the page.

The same calendar keeps him a second time on 12 Ιουνίου, as «Άγιος
Ιωάννης ο Στρατιώτης», and prints no life there at all: «Δεν έχουμε
λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου Ιωάννη του Στρατιώτη», we have no details
of the life of Saint John the Soldier. What that day keeps of him is a name, a
trade and the couplet of the synaxarion, which turns the trade into the whole of
the praise: «O στρατιώτης νυν χορεύει εν πόλω, / Όπου στρατιαί Aγγέλων
Iωάννης» — the soldier John now dances in heaven, where the armies of the
angels are.

This corpus held the two days as two folders, and the July folder said in so
many words that it was saying nothing about whether the two are one man. The
author ruled on 3 October 2026 that they are, and this record is both days. The
ruling adds nothing to what is known of him: the June page had nothing to give.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 29 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/29/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/737/saint.aspx), and its 12 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/12/index.aspx) and [the page](https://www.saint.gr/466/saint.aspx), which prints none; all read 1 October 2026.*
