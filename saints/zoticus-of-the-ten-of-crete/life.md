# Zoticus, one of the Ten of Crete

When Decius held the sceptre of the empire of Rome he set over the island of Crete a governor like himself in name and in manner, who matched his emperor in harshness, in tyranny and in the persecution of Christians. Coming to the island he ordered that all who believed in Christ be sought out for torture, and those who were strong in the faith were brought before him and destroyed, after many trials and torments, by deaths of every kind. In that time a company of ten chosen men was taken and brought to the man who tormented them.

They were of different cities. The life gives [Theodulus](/saints/theodulus-of-the-ten-of-crete), [Saturninus](/saints/saturninus-of-the-ten-of-crete), [Euporus](/saints/euporus-of-the-ten-of-crete), [Gelasius](/saints/gelasius-of-the-ten-of-crete) and [Eunicianus](/saints/eunicianus-of-the-ten-of-crete) to the city of Gortyna, Zoticus to Hios, [Pompeius](/saints/pompeius-of-the-ten-of-crete) and [Agathopus](/saints/agathopus-of-the-ten-of-crete) to Epinia, [Basilides](/saints/basilides-of-the-ten-of-crete) to Chidonia and [Evaristus](/saints/evaristus-of-the-ten-of-crete) to Iracleea — «Teodul, Satornic, Evpor, Ghelasie și Evnichian erau din cetatea Gortiniei; Zotic din Hios, Pompie și Agatopul din Epinia, Vasilid din Chidonia și Evarest din Iracleea» — and says that all of them hastened towards the city that is in heaven.

Standing before the torturer they answered with great courage and showed much manliness. What road of martyrdom did they not travel, what torment did they not overcome by endurance? They were beaten, tortured, dragged on the ground, stoned, mocked, spat upon, and bore every kind of contempt for thirty days.

On the twenty-third day of the month of December a day of judgement was prepared, and as the governor sat in court the sufferers were brought to their last questioning, full of boldness and of spirit. He looked at them with an angry face and asked what madness was theirs, that neither need nor time had been able to teach them what was to their profit; then, as though to frighten them with his anger, he said that they should sacrifice to the gods, and that otherwise he would say nothing more to them, but they would learn who Decius was, whom they refused to obey.

The martyrs answered that in all that long time they had proved to him well, by word and by deed, that they would neither sacrifice to the gods nor submit to his order. Cutting their words short, he asked whether they took no account of torments either. They said that they were not afraid of torments at all, but thanked him very much for having prepared them such a spiritual feast and set it before them; and he answered that it would not be so, for they would learn how great was the power of the gods they blasphemed.

A word of Theodulus pleased them all, that the true contender is the one who at the end shows no fear and no sadness or change of face; and they first sang to God, «Blessed be the Lord, who has not given us over to be hunted by their teeth. Our soul like a bird has been delivered from the snare of the hunters», and the rest of that psalm. Coming to the place appointed, each of them made his prayer: «Forgive, Lord, your servants, and receive the shedding of our blood for ourselves, for our kinsfolk and friends and for our whole country, that it may escape from the darkness of ignorance and see the right faith with clean eyes and know you, the true Light, O eternal King».

So, each of them praying, their holy and victorious heads were struck off, and their souls went up with joy to Christ God. When the executioners had gone, certain acquaintances stayed behind to gather their bodies and buried them with honour. Afterwards, when the persecution ceased and the holy faith shone out, the blessed Paul, bishop of the new Rome — the city of Constantine the Great — came to the island of Crete, found the relics of those saints, ten in number, incorrupt, and brought them with great honour to the imperial city, for its defence and for the help of all who had need of help.

Of the ten, Zoticus is the sixth the life names, and it gives him to Hios. doxologia.ro's line for the day, «Sfinții 10 Mucenici din Creta», names nobody and gives no year; the ten names, his among them, are read out of the life the day page links.

The Greek calendar keeps the ten together on the same day and gives each of them a home. saint.gr has Zotikos from Knossos, and it sets their martyrdom in the third century, when the emperor was Decius. All ten, it says, worked with great zeal for the spreading of the Gospel on the island. They were denounced to the prefect of Crete, who bore the emperor's own name and was called Decius too; and when he saw their youth in flower and their manly bearing he tried to draw them off with many promises of worldly enjoyments and pleasures. When nothing succeeded he ordered them scourged, and after that they were stoned. They bore the torments, the page says, remembering the words of the psalmist, «Ἀνδρίζεσθε καὶ κραταιούσθω ἡ καρδία ὑμῶν»; and then, at the prefect's order, the soldiers took off their honourable heads with their swords.

The Охридски пролог keeps the ten together on 23. децембар ст. ст. under one heading, „Светих десет мученика на Криту.“, and names every one of them in the body, his own name among them as «Зотик»: «Њихова су имена: Теодул, Сатурнин, Евпор, Геласије, Евникијан, Зотик, Помпије, Авгатопус, Василид и Еварест.» They suffered for Christ the Lord, the book says, in the time of the persecution of Decius, in the year 250.

All of them were distinguished and honourable citizens, «најбољи међу најбољим», the best among the best, the book says. When they were led out to the place of execution they were exceedingly joyful, and argued over which of them should be beheaded first, for each wished to be the first to go to the beloved Christ. Then they prayed: „Прости, Господе, слугама Твојим, и прими изливену крв нашу за нас, за сроднике наше и пријатеље и за све отечество, да се сви ослободе од таме незнања и Тебе познаду, истиниту светлост, о Царе вечни!“ — forgive, Lord, your servants, and receive the shedding of our blood for ourselves, for our kinsfolk and friends and for our whole country, that all may be freed from the darkness of ignorance and know you, the true Light, O eternal King. They were beheaded, and passed over into the kingdom of glory, to rejoice for ever.

The book sets their passion in the year 250, where this folder holds the third century, read off the Greek calendar, which names the same reign. The one year lies inside the other and does not contradict it, and it is recorded here beside the century rather than put in its place.
