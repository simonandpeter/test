# Michael (Bogoroditsky)

The Сретенский monastery's calendar names him second of the two in «Сщмчч. Евгения и Михаила пресвитеров (1937)» and carries no life for him; azbyka.ru prints one out of pstgu.ru. Богородицкий Михаил Трофимович was born in 1872 in the village of Студенецкие Выселки in the Липецк district of the province of Тамбов. He served thirty-three years in the church of the icon of the Mother of God «Всех скорбящих Радосте» in the town of Козлов, and with his wife Мария brought up eleven children in the faith.

In 1930 he was arrested on a charge of counter-revolutionary agitation and shut in the prison of Тамбов; on 19 March 1930 a troika sentenced him to five years' exile. He could not return to his own church afterwards, the renovationists having taken it, and was appointed instead to the village of Беломестная Двойня in the Тамбов district. At the beginning of October 1937 the chairman of the village soviet warned him: «Богородицкий, уезжайте, Вас должны арестовать» — leave, you are to be arrested. He answered «А на кого же я оставлю храм и прихожан?», and to whom shall I leave the church and the parishioners.

After that conversation he went to the town of Мичуринск, where his wife and his thirteen-year-old son were living in a private flat; he mended her stove with what he had learned to do in the camp, patched his own clothes for hard wear, and said goodbye to her at the station for good. He was arrested on 12 November 1937 and imprisoned, and on 20 November a troika of the NKVD administration for the Тамбов region charged him with systematic counter-revolutionary agitation and with using the pulpit of the church for propaganda, and sentenced him to the highest measure of punishment. He was shot on 7 December 1937.
