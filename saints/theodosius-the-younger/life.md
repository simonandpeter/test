# Theodosius the Younger

saint.gr keeps him as the pious king. Theodosius II took up the royal sceptre in the year 408, when he was only seven years old, as heir to his father [Arcadius the Emperor](/saints/arcadius-the-emperor); he was called the Small, the page says, so that he should be told apart from his grandfather, [Theodosius the Great](/saints/theodosius-the-great). It was his sister [Pulcheria the Empress](/saints/pulcheria-the-empress) who handed on to him the Christian piety, and so he came to have a great reverence and devotion for the Orthodox faith.

When Theodosius the Small had taken up the royal sceptre, the page goes on, he warmly upheld, with his sister's help, the truth of the Orthodox faith and the security of its creed. So it was that by a royal decree of 19 November 430 the Third Ecumenical Council gathered at Ephesus on 22 June 431, and condemned the heretical opinions of Nestorius. For his warm piety and for that great service of his to Orthodoxy, the page ends, the Church numbered him in the choir of her saints.

That is the whole of the notice. It gives no account of his death and no hymn, and the years it sets against his name — 401 and 450 — stand in its head line and nowhere in its prose.
