# Patrobas

Patrobas is one of the five apostles of the Seventy whom saint.gr's calendar keeps together on 5 Νοεμβρίου, and the page calls all five true shepherds of the rational flock of the Church. He is named, it says, among those the Apostle Paul greets in his letter to the Romans. The whole of what is told of his ministry is one sentence: he rendered great services to the faith as bishop of Puteoli in Italy.

The page adds one thing more, which is where some of him is now: a fragment of his holy relic is at the monastery of Kykkos in Cyprus. It gives him no year, no account of his death and no hymn. The couplet the Greek books set over three of the five names him in it: «Ἑρμᾶς, Πατρόβας καὶ Γάϊος τρεῖς ἅμα, / Ἀπόστολοι θνῄσκουσιν, οἷς τριὰς φίλη» — Hermas, Patrobas and Gaius, three together, die as apostles, to whom the Trinity is dear.

Those kept with him are [Hermas](/saints/hermas-5-november), [Linus](/saints/linus-of-rome), [Gaius](/saints/gaius-of-ephesus) and [Philologus](/saints/philologus-of-sinope), each with his own sentence on the same page.

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar for 5 ноября ст. ст. keeps him as one of five named on a single line: «Апп. от 70-ти Патрова , Ерма , Лина , Гаия , Филолога (I).». The line gives the five forenames in the genitive, the class «Апп. от 70-ти» for all five together, and the century, and the name link behind his place in it supplies the calendar's own nominative with the see, «Святитель Патров, епископ Неапольский и Путеольский, апостол от 70-ти». That page carries the title and two days and **no life link at all**, so the line and the link are the whole of what this calendar has of him, and the «(I)» at the line's end is the first date this folder has carried. The name page also lists «4 января (70 ап.)», the synaxis of the Seventy Apostles, which is a synaxis and not a second feast; this folder renders on 5 ноября ст. ст. only.

This calendar gives him **two sees where saint.gr gave one**: «епископ Неапольский и Путеольский», bishop of Neapolis and of Puteoli, against the Puteoli alone that stands in this folder's `office`. The fuller reading is recorded here in the prose and the `office` field is left as it was, because the tool that applies an upgrade cannot set it; the correction is one line of hand work and is named in this batch's report.
