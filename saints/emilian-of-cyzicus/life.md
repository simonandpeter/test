# Emilian of Cyzicus

saint.gr opens its notice on Emilian with the Apostle Paul's word about confession, «Ἔχοντες οὒν ἀρχιερέα μέγαν διεληλυθότα τοὺς οὐρανούς, Ἰησοῦν τὸν υἱὸν τοῦ Θεοῦ, κρατῶμεν τῆς ὁμολογίας» — since we have a great High Priest who has passed through the heavens, Jesus the Son of God, let us hold fast the confession of our faith in him. Emilian, the page says, was such an example of confession, and he lived between the eighth and the ninth century.

He was bishop of Cyzicus after Nicholas, in the years 787 to 815. He contended with all the strength God gave him for the honourable veneration of the holy icons, in the reign of the iconoclast emperor Leo the Fifth. An athlete of Orthodoxy, he spoke of it most warmly and strengthened the faithful to bear the impious persecutions with endurance.

He suffered many hardships and afflictions, the page closes, and he died at last in exile, as a true confessor of the right faith. saint.gr prints no year for his birth and none for his death, and gives no place beyond the see itself.
