# Basil (6 February)

saint.gr names him and gives almost no life. Its calendar for 6 February prints «Άγιοι Φαύστος, Βασίλειος και Σιλουανός», and everything the page says of Basil it says of the three together: that [Faustus](/saints/faustus-martyr-6-february), Basil and [Silvanus](/saints/silvanus-martyr-6-february) were friends as brothers are, young in years, and were martyred by the sword. No homeland, no century, no persecutor, no hymn.

The page then raises a doubt of its own about the three, and it is set down here because it is the source's: perhaps they are the same as the Faustus, Basil and Lucian kept on 25 October, since the couplet over them is the same. The third name differs between the two lines; the corpus holds no folder for the October company; and the 25 October page was not read for this entry. Of the many saints called Basil, which this one is the page does not say.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2810/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
