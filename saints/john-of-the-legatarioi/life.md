# John of the Legatarioi

The Greek calendar keeps on 28 November, beside [Stephen the New](/saints/stephen-the-new), the martyrs who suffered with him for the holy icons. The rage of Copronymus and of those about him, saint.gr says, was not confined to one victim: after Stephen, other confessors of Orthodoxy underwent tortures and deaths as well. Of these the page names two, and John is the second of them.

Another afterwards, it says, John of the Legatarioi, was exiled to Daphnousia, where he died of continual beatings. That is the whole of his story on the page, and the epithet is all it tells of where he came from. The other it names is [Basil](/saints/basil-companion-of-stephen-the-new), blinded and killed with kicks; the rest, it says, were put to death in other barbarous ways or sent into exile and passed their lives in continual martyrdom, and it does not name them.

The page gives John no country beyond that epithet, no rank, no year and no burial, and prints no hymn. Its couplet over the company says only that, honouring the image of the Word's flesh, the saints die a violent death, and that Stephen, having many fellow-contestants, takes delight in weaving a dance with them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/28/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3102/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
