# Agapius of Apamea

Agapius is the third of the three the Greek calendar keeps together on 11 Ιανουαρίου: «Άγιοι Στέφανος ο εν Πλακιδιαναίς, Θεόδωρος και Αγάπιος ο Αρχιμανδρίτης» — [Stephen](/saints/stephen-of-placidianae) who was at Placidianae, [Theodore](/saints/theodore-companion-of-stephen), and Agapius the archimandrite. He is also the only one of the three about whom saint.gr says it knows anything beyond the name.

What it knows is one sentence: «Η μόνη πληροφορία που έχουμε είναι ότι ο Όσιος Αγάπιος ασκήτεψε στην Απαμεία της Συρίας» — the only information we have is that the venerable Agapius lived the ascetic life at Apamea in Syria. The page then adds that there are no other details of the lives of these saints.

The calendar gives him the rank of archimandrite and no year. Over the three of them the page prints a couplet: that they stand now before the throne of the Trinity, having left the earth and taken up their dwelling in heaven. There is no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/11/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1088/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
