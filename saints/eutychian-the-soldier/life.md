# Eutychian the Soldier

saint.gr's calendar for 19 August names Eutychian the soldier beside [Strategius](/saints/strategius), and the whole of what it says about the two of them is one sentence: «Οι Άγιοι Ευτυχιανός ο στρατιώτης και Στρατήγιος μαρτύρησαν δια πυρός» — the holy Eutychian the soldier and Strategius were martyred by fire. There is no year, no city, no emperor and no judge.

What the page adds is the pair of synaxarion distichs it prints, one for each man. The one for Eutychian turns his name into the point: «Εὐτυχιανὸς καίεται, κρίνας μέγα Τὴν καῦσιν εὐτύχημα, μικρὸν τὸ πάθος» — Eutychian is burned, having judged the burning a great good fortune and the suffering a small thing. His name means the fortunate one, and the couplet is built on that and on nothing else the page knows about him.

saint.gr gives him no life beyond this, no relics, and no hymn. He is not the Eutychian of Nicomedia nor the Eutychian the Greek calendar keeps on 2 September, both of whom the corpus already holds on their own days; the only thing this page says of him is the fire, the soldier's trade and the companion he died with.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 19 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/19/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2216/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
