# Gregory of Akritas

saint.gr says the venerable Gregory was born in Crete in the year 755. His parents were called Theophanes and Juliana, and the page says that both of them were very devout people. At the age of twenty-six the young Gregory, with his parents' leave, went to Palestine, burning with the longing to venerate the holy places and to live some years where the remembrance of Christ the Saviour is born again and is moving.

From the holy city he decided, after a time, to go to Rome; the page places this in the patriarchate of Nicephorus I, under Michael Rangabe as emperor of Constantinople, and dates it 811. There he was tonsured a monk. Not long afterwards [Michael](/saints/michael-of-synada) the Confessor, metropolitan of Synada, persuaded Gregory to come with him to Constantinople, and enrolled him in the monastery of Akritas.

That monastery, saint.gr explains, stood on the Astakenos gulf in Bithynia, on the headland of the same name. There, used also in the inward mission of the metropolis, Gregory ended his life in the year 820 — a pattern and example, the page says, of warm faith, of a clean and peaceable life, and of love.
