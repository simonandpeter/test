# Nicholas (2 September)

Nicholas Burlakov, a layman, was shot on 2 September 1918 of the old style,
15 September, near the Kirillo-Belozersky monastery, with the hieromartyr
[Barsanuphius (Lebedev)](/saints/barsanuphius-of-kirillov), bishop of
Kirillov and vicar of the Novgorod diocese, and with [John
Ivanov](/saints/john-presbyter-martyr-1918-2-september), [Seraphima
(Sulimova)](/saints/seraphima-abbess-martyr-1918), [Anatoly
Barashkov](/saints/anatoly-martyr-1918), [Michael
Trubnikov](/saints/michael-martyr-1918-2-september) and [Philip
Marychev](/saints/philip-martyr-1918). The Sretensky calendar’s life is the
bishop’s, and gives of his companions only their names and callings: the
priest John Ivanov, the abbess of the Ferapontov monastery Seraphima
(Sulimova), and four laymen, Nicholas Burlakov, Anatoly Barashkov, Michael
Trubnikov and Philip Marychev. Of the death it says this: before the
execution, until the bishop had finished his prayer, the Red Army men could
not shoot him; he went to his death with his arms opened in a cross, and not
one bullet touched him, until the executioners in their fury fell on him and
stabbed him with their bayonets. He was buried not far from the
Kirillo-Belozersky monastery; in the 1960s the grave was destroyed and
desecrated, and a pigsty was built on the spot. All seven were numbered
among the new martyrs and confessors of Russia by the jubilee Council of
Bishops of August 2000, for veneration by the whole church, the bishop
having been a locally venerated saint of the Vologda diocese since 1999; the
Russian calendar keeps them together on 2 September (Julian).

*After the life printed by the Sretensky calendar (days.pravoslavie.ru) —
[the entry](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4771.htm), read 5 September 2026 — this
time whole; the earlier reading had stopped partway.*
