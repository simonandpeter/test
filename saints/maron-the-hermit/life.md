# Maron

The whole of doxologia's life of Maron is a single short paragraph, and this is it. Embracing the life under the open sky, this venerable man went up to the top of a mountain which the ancient Greeks had honoured in a particular way; and finding there a temple of the demons which they had built, he consecrated it to God and lived there, making himself a small tent under which he only rarely went in.

The blessed man lived in great labours, and, not content with these, found others greater still. And God, the Giver of rewards, gave him the measure of his grace according to the measure of his labours: sicknesses could be seen quenched at his prayer, the shaking of the earth stilled, and demons driven out by his prayer alone. He founded many monasteries, bringing many to God through the ascetic struggle; and labouring in this way at the divine work, and healing souls together with bodies, after a short illness he departed this life in peace.

That is all. The page names no country, no emperor and no year, and it prints no hymn for him; the mountain, the pagan temple he consecrated, the tent, the healings and the monasteries are everything it gives.
