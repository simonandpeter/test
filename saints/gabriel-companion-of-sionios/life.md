# Gabriel, companion of Sionios

The Greek calendar keeps him on 21 Ιανουαρίου with [Sionios](/saints/sionios-companion-of-gabriel), and the whole of the page behind that line is one sentence: «Οι Άγιοι Μάρτυρες Γαβριήλ και Σιώνιος μαρτύρησαν μαζί με άλλους κατά την περίοδο του ηγεμόνα των Βουλγάρων Μουρτάγονος κατά τον 9ο αιώνα μ.Χ.» — the holy martyrs Gabriel and Sionios bore witness together with others in the time of the ruler of the Bulgarians Mourtagon, in the ninth century. That page names neither the others nor the city nor the manner of death, and prints no hymn.

The same calendar names the two of them again on the following day, 22 Ιανουαρίου, in the company of [Manuel](/saints/manuel-of-adrianople), Metropolitan of Adrianople, and there it tells the whole of it: the Bulgarians came down against Byzantium in 815 under Leo the Armenian, took Adrianople under Krum, and after Krum and his successors Doukoumos and Ditzengos it was Mourtagon — Omurtag — who killed the bishops and the rest with his own hands, and «τους Γαβριήλ και Σιώνιο τους αποκεφάλισε», beheaded Gabriel and Sionios. Two entries on one calendar are two commemorations, and these are read here as two commemorations of one man rather than as two men.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 21 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/21/index.aspx), [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1299/saint.aspx) and, for the Bulgarian war and the taking of Adrianople, [Manuel of Adrianople's page](https://www.saint.gr/1148/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
