# Donald

saint.gr's calendar for 15 July names Donald and his nine daughters together, and the page behind the line gives a short notice of him. He lived at the beginning of the eighth century in Scotland — the page's own sentence has a word missing where the part of the country would stand — and after the death of his wife he and [his nine daughters](/saints/nine-daughters-of-donald) took up an ascetic life, working the land with their hands, eating very little bread and drinking water once a day.

After his repose the daughters went to live in a monastery at Abernethy, where they became known as the Nine Maidens, or the Nine Holy Virgins. That is where the page's account ends. It gives no year for his birth or his death, no hymn, and nothing of what became of him beyond that his daughters outlived him; his name it prints in Latin letters, with no Greek form of it anywhere.
