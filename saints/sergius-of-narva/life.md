# Sergius (Druzhinin), Bishop of Narva, Hieromartyr (1937)

The future bishop — Ivan Prokhorovich Druzhinin in the world — was born on 20 June 1863 in Novoye Selo in the Bezhetsk district of Tver province to a well-off peasant family, with only a home education, having taught himself his letters. In 1881 he came to live in St Petersburg, where his cousins were nuns of the Resurrection Novodevichy convent; in the capital he worked for a time as a horse-tram driver, and in 1881 entered the Valaam monastery of the Transfiguration as a novice, living there about six years; on 9 September 1887 he was received into the Holy Trinity St Sergius hermitage, known for the labours of Ignatius (Brianchaninov) — the calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. He was confessor to the grand dukes Konstantinovich; in 1924 he was consecrated bishop of Narva, and was an active member of the Josephite movement that rejected the Declaration of Metropolitan Sergius; arrested repeatedly, he was sentenced in 1931 to five years in the camps, and after his release in 1935 exiled to Yoshkar-Ola. Arrested in 1937 on a charge of founding a counter-revolutionary monarchist organisation, he was shot on 17 September 1937; the Russian calendar keeps him on 4 September (Julian).
