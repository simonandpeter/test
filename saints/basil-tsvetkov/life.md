# Basil (Tsvetkov)

He is the fifth and last name on the Russian calendar's 1937 line for 4 October, and the line's grammar sets him apart from the other four: they are «сщмчч. … пресвитеров», hieromartyr presbyters, and he is «прмч. Василия» — a venerable-martyr, the rank a monastic gets who dies a martyr. The site's index of names supplies the rest: «Василий (Цветков), архимандрит, прмч.», an archimandrite, which makes him the senior man of the five.

One other thing is his alone of the five. Their name pages all carry this day and «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia; his carries a third, «10 июня (Ряз.)», an abbreviation the calendar uses and does not expand, and it is written down here as it was read rather than guessed at.

He and [Michael (Tverdovsky)](/saints/michael-tverdovsky) are the two of the five for whom the day carries no life, so what monastery he was archimandrite of, what he was charged with and where he died are not recorded. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026 even with a browser user-agent.

*After the Russian church calendar's [4 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261004.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13815.html); read 4 October 2026.*
