# Demetrius (Plyshevsky)

He was born in 1880 into the family of a priest whose fathers had been priests before him, and in 1905 he finished the Minsk theological seminary. He then served at the church of Saint Nicholas in Smolevichi, in the Minsk district. The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 8 января ст. ст. with the line «Сщмч. Димитрия пресвитера (1938).», and its notice of the twenty-three hieromartyrs of Minsk is where the rest of this is read.

He was arrested on 26 September 1937, charged with taking part in what the indictment called a counter-revolutionary insurgent spy organisation. Under interrogation, the notice says, he bore himself with dignity. On 19 November 1937 he was sentenced to be shot, and he was killed at Minsk on 19 January 1938.

That is the whole of what the calendar sets down about him. The day page's hymns carry nothing under his name, and the name link behind the line gives the calendar's own nominative, «Димитрий (Плышевский), иерей», which adds the surname the day's line leaves out.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 8 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260108.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4766.htm); read 3 October 2026.*
