# Valantios, one of the Three Hundred Alamanoi

saint.gr's calendar for 12 October keeps twelve saints on one line — «Άγιοι Βαλάντιος, Βαρλαάμ, Βαρνάβας ο μοναχός, Βασίλειος ο επίσκοπος, Γεώργιος ο Βαβατσινιώτης, Γεώργιος ο επιτηδειώτης, Γεώργιος ο περαχωρίτης, Γεώργιος ο Σαλαμάνης, Δημητριανός ο επίσκοπος, Ειρηνικός, Ελπίδιος, Επαφρόδιτος» — and Valantios is one of them. The page behind the line gives each of the twelve a heading of its own, his being «Αγιος Βαλαντιος Εκ Των Αλαμανων», and then prints a single sentence for all twelve together. That sentence is the whole of what the calendar says of them: that they are of the three hundred holy Alamanoi of Cyprus, and that we have no details of their lives.

Of Valantios in particular the page records nothing at all: no year, no birthplace, no rank or see beyond what stands in his own heading, no account of how he lived or how he died, and no hymn. His heading adds only the company, «Εκ Των Αλαμανων», of the Alamanoi.

So this folder holds what the calendar holds — a name, a company, a church and a day. The twelve are kept together on one line and are named one by one, which is why each of them stands here as his own folder rather than the twelve being gathered into a number. The only variation the line itself carries is for the tenth of them, whom its text gives as «Ειρηνικός ή Αρνιακός».

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2726/saint.aspx), which the twelve share and which prints no life for any of them; read 1 October 2026.*
