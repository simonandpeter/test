# David, brother of Tarichan

saint.gr's calendar for 18 May keeps two brothers on one page and gives them one sentence between them. The holy martyrs David and [Tarichan](/saints/tarichan) were brothers, and they were martyred in the year 693, in the years of the Persian king Abdul, because they refused to change their faith and embrace Islam.

That is the whole of the entry. The page gives no country, no parents, no trade, no judge, no manner of death, no relics and no hymn; its head lines date each of them «(; - 693)» and nothing more. The Greek calendar keeps two brothers of those names on 18 May, and this folder carries the line and the sentence behind it.

The Russian calendar's notice of the two brothers is two sentences. They were Georgians by descent and brothers of one blood, and they met a martyr's end at the hands of the Persian governor Абдула for refusing to go over to Islam. The calendar marks the entry «(Груз.)», which is how it flags a saint it keeps from another local church, and calls them both «отроков», boys.

Its two statements of the year do not agree. The notice says 693, which is the year already recorded above; the day line prints 683. Ten years lie between them on the same page, nothing on it chooses, and nothing here was changed.
