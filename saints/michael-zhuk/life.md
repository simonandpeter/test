# Michael (Zhuk)

The calendar's line is all there is of him. It names three men of 1937 in one breath, and the reading it hangs behind that line is the life of the other two only: a hieromonk of his name appears nowhere in it. So what the calendar records of him is his forename, his surname out of the name link, the order of hieromonk, the day and the year.

The two halves of the entry give him two martyr grades, both the source's: the day line has «Прмч.», the grade of a monastic martyr, and the name link «сщмч.», a priest's. A hieromonk is both monk and priest, and this folder keeps both rather than choosing. Nothing else about him has been read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 4 августа ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260804.html); read 13 October 2026.*
