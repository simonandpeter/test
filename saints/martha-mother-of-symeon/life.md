# Martha, mother of Symeon the Stylite

Martha was the mother of [Symeon the Stylite](/saints/symeon-the-stylite),
and died in peace; the Greek synaxarion, which keeps her with him on 1
September, says that much and no more. The Martha of 4 July, whose fuller
life the Russian church calendar prints, is another woman — the mother of
Symeon the Younger, the stylite of the Wonderful Mountain, who died in 551
and was buried beside her son there — and the two are easily run together.
The Romanian life has her living in Cilicia at the turn of the fourth and
fifth centuries, of a poor family, with her husband Susotion. The Russian
calendar prints her death at about 428 beside her son’s; the Prologue
remembers her twice climbing to the pillar to see him, and her son sending
her away until the other world.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 1 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/893/saint.aspx); The Romanian life is at doxologia.ro — [the page](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfintei-cuvioase-marta-mama-sfantului-cuvios-simeon-stalpnicul); The Sretensky calendar’s life is at days.pravoslavie.ru — [the entry](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1426.htm); read 23 August 2026; the Russian church calendar’s page for the other Martha (azbyka.ru) — [the entry](https://azbyka.ru/days/sv-marfa-antiohijskaja), read 6 September 2026, is what keeps the two apart.*
