# Martha, mother of Symeon the Stylite

Martha was the mother of [Symeon the Stylite](/saints/symeon-the-stylite), and died in peace; the Greek synaxarion, which keeps her with him on 1 September and again on 4 July, says that much and no more. The Romanian life has her living in Cilicia at the turn of the fourth and fifth centuries, of a poor family, with her husband Susotion. The Russian calendar prints her death at about 428 beside her son’s; the Prologue remembers her twice climbing to the pillar to see him, and her son sending her away until the other world.
