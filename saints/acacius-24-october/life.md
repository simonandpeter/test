# Acacius (24 October)

saint.gr's calendar for 24 Οκτωβρίου names Acacius the presbyter and gives him no life. One sentence is the whole of the narrative: «Ο Άγιος Ακάκιος ο Πρεσβύτερος, μαρτύρησε διά ξίφους» — he was martyred by the sword. The couplet over the entry plays on the name, which the page glosses as the man without malice, and says that Acacius, having offered the Word even his soul, hastens for his sake to give that soul up to the sword.

The page gives him no country, no city, no year, no emperor, no church of his own and no companions, and it prints no hymn and no relics for him. Of the thirteen Acacius folders the corpus already keeps, none is a presbyter kept on this day.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/24/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2533/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
