# Eustathius Kataphloros, Archbishop of Thessalonica

Eustathius lived in the twelfth century. He studied letters and theology at
Constantinople and was ordained deacon at Hagia Sophia, and he was one of the great scholars of
Byzantium. In 1174 he was elected Metropolitan of Myra and the next year raised to the
archbishopric of Thessalonica, where his work was much in advance of its time and met
resistance. He was taken by the Normans in 1185 and his life was in danger from a conspiracy in
1191. He died in 1197 or 1198, leaving a body of writing behind him. The Greek calendar keeps
him on 20 September.
