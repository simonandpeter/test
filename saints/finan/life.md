# Finan

saint.gr's calendar for 21 Οκτωβρίου names Finan, and the page behind the name is a single sentence: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ιρλανδού Αγίου Φινάν (Finan)» — we have no details of the life of the Irish saint Finan. The listing writes the name in Greek letters and the page's own heading writes it in Latin ones.

Nothing else is printed for him at all: no century, no see, no monastery, no companions, no relics, no couplet and no hymn. That he was Irish and that the Greek calendar keeps him on this day is the whole of what the source gives, and this folder says no more than that.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 21 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/21/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2545/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
