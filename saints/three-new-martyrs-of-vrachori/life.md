# The Three New Martyrs of Vrachori

The three new martyrs were Peloponnesians who traded in the parts of Ioannina, the Greek synaxarion says, where they learned to speak Turkish too. In 1786 they decided to go home to the Morea. On the way they came to Vrachori in Aetolia, where Turkish tax-gatherers were collecting the tax; to avoid the haraç the three greeted them in Turkish and passed freely into the town, taken for Turks. Afterwards they were found out, arrested and tortured to make them deny Christ, but they stayed steadfast in their faith and took the crown of martyrdom by hanging. The entry names none of the three.
