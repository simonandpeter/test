# Euphemia (4 January)

saint.gr keeps [Chrysanthus](/saints/chrysanthus-4-january) and Euphemia together on the fourth of January, and puts an asterisk against both names. The asterisk is explained at the head of the notice: the saint's existence, or the biographical particulars, or the date of the feast is not cross-checked. That caveat is the page's own and is repeated here because it is the most important thing the page says about her.

What else it says is two sentences. The two of them were martyred for the faith of Christ, but we have no details of their lives; and of Euphemia it is simply said «της πλησίον του Αγίου Ακακίου», of the place near Saint Acacius, from which the page concludes that what is meant is her tomb. The verse over the notice says that the word of good report counted Euphemia — whose name means good report — plainly worthy of the immortal one. She is not the great martyr Euphemia the corpus keeps on 16 September: this is another entry on another day, with no story attached to it.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 4 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/04/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/477/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
