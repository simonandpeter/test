# Constantine (Cherepanov)

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar for 9 ноября ст. ст. carries eleven names on one line and he is the sixth of them: «Сщмчч. Парфения , еп. Ананьевского, Константина , Димитрия , Нестора , Феодора , Константина , Виктора , Илии , Павла пресвитеров, Иосифа диакона и прмч. Алексия (1937).» One year stands for all eleven, «Сщмчч.» heads the ten clergy and «прмч.» the monk who closes the line, and the grades it prints sort them into a bishop, eight presbyters, a deacon and a hieromonk. The name link behind his place supplies the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line leaves out, «Константин (Черепанов), иерей, сщмч.», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from.

**days.pravoslavie.ru gives this company nothing but that line.** Each of the eleven name pages carries a title, a list of days and no text at all, and not one of them has a `/Life/` link of any kind; the day page's hymns are the two martyrs' of 284–305, the venerable Matrona's, the Mother of God's before the Скоропослушница icon and the wonderworker of Aegina's, and nothing is sung under any of these eleven names. The life below is azbyka.ru's, read in the in-app browser because that host answers no fetcher, and the paragraphs are this corpus's own.

**The two hosts swap the two Constantines of this line.** days.pravoslavie.ru puts Константин (Немешаев) second, next to the bishop, and Константин (Черепанов) sixth, between Феодор Чичканов and Виктор Климов; azbyka.ru's line for the same day reverses them, and nothing on either line says which reading is which man. Neither host is preferred here on its own authority, and the question is settled on the hosts' own life and name pages.

**What settles it is azbyka's own life for Черепанов.** It says he «проходил по коллективному делу вместе с протоиереем Нестором Паниным, протоиереем Феодором Чичкановым и иереем Виктором Климовым» — he went through a collective case together with those three — and was shot with them at two in the morning. His place on a line must therefore be the one among those three, which is the sixth place and is where days.pravoslavie.ru puts him. azbyka's own life for Немешаев has him arrested in the Moscow country, sentenced by a troika there and shot at Butovo, and names none of the three. Two of azbyka's pages contradict azbyka's line; the Сретенский order stands, and the name pages 9659 and 9662 keep the two men apart whichever way the line is read.

Константин Васильевич Черепанов was born in 1878 in the Yekaterinburg country of the Perm province. He was educated at the Perm theological seminary and ordained priest to the cathedral of the Resurrection at Semipalatinsk. He was arrested on 28 August 1937 on the charge that he was «член контрреволюционной шпионской организации церковников», a member of a counter-revolutionary spy organisation of churchmen.

He went through a collective case together with three other priests of the city, was sentenced with them to the highest measure of punishment, and was shot at two in the morning on 22 November. 9 ноября ст. ст. is the civil 22 November, so the calendar keeps him on the very day he was shot. He was numbered among the new martyrs and confessors of Russia by the jubilee Council of Bishops of August 2000.

**These four are a company and the pages say so in as many words.** Черепанов's life names the collective case he shared with [Nestor (Panin)](/saints/nestor-panin), [Theodore (Chichkanov)](/saints/theodore-chichkanov) and [Victor (Klimov)](/saints/victor-klimov); Климов's life says he was shot «вместе со священномучениками прот. Нестором Паниным, прот. Феодором Чичкановым и свящ. Константином Черепановым» on the strength of one decision of the troika of 19 November 1937; and Чичканов's life prints the decision itself, naming six men sentenced as members of one fabricated organisation and carrying the sentence out on 22 November 1937 at Semipalatinsk. One case, one sentence, one day, one city: `related` rows are written between all four, both ways.
