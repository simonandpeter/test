# Taurion

Taurion was martyred at Amphipolis of Macedonia, which saint.gr puts near Kavala, together with Auctus and with Thessalonike, the daughter of the idol-priest Kleon, whose passion the page tells first: she would not deny Christ at her father's begging, was scourged by four men with whips of raw hide, had her ribs broken and her property taken, and was exiled, and died in exile confessing Christ.

Taurion and Auctus then accused her brutal torturer and murderer, and were for that denounced to the consular Thorybios. He ordered them stoned and then put to a series of horrible tortures; and when the two came through all of it whole and unharmed in a wonderful way, he ordered them beheaded, and so they took the glorious crowns of martyrdom.

The couplet for the pair makes bulls of them, on his name: «Ἐφεῦρον Αὖκτος, Ταυρίων εὐκτὸν τέλος, / Ξίφει τμηθέντες, οἷα ταῦροι Κυρίου» — Auctus and Taurion found the end they prayed for, cut down by the sword like bulls of the Lord. The page gives no year and prints no hymn. Kept with him are [Auctus](/saints/auctus) and [Thessalonike](/saints/thessalonike).

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2935/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
