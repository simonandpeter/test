# Basil (Smirnov, 18 June)

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 18 июня ст. ст. names four presbyters on one line, «Сщмчч. Василия , Александра , Василия и Сергия пресвитеров (1938).», and he is the first of them. The line gives the forenames in the genitive, one grade-class word and one rank word for the four together, and one year, and it gives no surname to any of them. The name link behind his place in it supplies the calendar’s own nominative with the surname and the grade, «Василий (Смирнов), протоиерей, сщмч.», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from.

His name page carries nothing else: it heads «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 18 июня» and stops, with no life, no place of service and no account of his death. Nothing is supplied for that here. The line’s «Сщмчч.» makes him a hieromartyr and «пресвитеров» a presbyter, and the name link raises the rank to «протоиерей», an archpriest, which is what this folder records.

One thing has to be said plainly. The corpus already holds a presbyter whose stored Russian form is «Василий Смирнов», and the two forms fold onto one key. That man is kept by this same calendar on 22 августа ст. ст., named with a bishop of Penza under the year 1937, and his folder was written out of azbyka.ru and cites no name page of this calendar at all. This man’s own name page lists 18 июня and nothing else. The calendar therefore keeps two entries; whether it keeps two men is not settled here, and azbyka.ru was not read for either of them.

The second day his name page lists is the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which is a synaxis and not a second feast, so he renders on 18 июня ст. ст. only. The day page prints no hymn under his name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 18 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260618.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9285.html); read 9 October 2026.*
