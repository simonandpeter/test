# Maria the Methymopoula

Maria, called the Methymopoula, was born at Kato Fourni of Merambello in Crete of parents devout and God-loving, and saint.gr calls her the fair virgin new-martyr of the faith. A Turco-Albanian gendarme fell in love with her and used every means to draw her into his defiling passion; and the more he tried, the more the blessed Maria turned away from him. So the impious man decided to kill her.

He found her one day gathering leaves for the feeding of the silkworms, attacked her, and struck her mortally in the heart; and so the all-fair bride of the Lord received the crown of her contest. The page says she is reported to have been martyred about the year 1826, and it gives no year for her birth, names no relic, and adds nothing else to the story.

What it does give is her service, and the hymns keep saying the same two things — the field and the bird. She refused the love of an unbeliever, the apolytikion says, and took from him a deadly blow, and like a small bird spread the wings of her soul towards her Creator; and the kontakion calls her a most fragrant flower blossomed out of Crete. Its couplet is plainer still: «Θήρατρα ἔρωτος ἔφυγες ἀπίστου / Ἔρωτι μαρία τετρωμένη θείῳ» — you fled the snares of an unbeliever's love, Maria, being wounded with a love divine.
