# Marcia, wife of Tranquillinus

saint.gr's calendar for 18 December keeps ten people together and names every one of them: Nikostratos and his wife Zoe, Tranquillinus and his wife Marcia, Claudius, Tiburtius, Castor, Castulus, Marcus and Marcellinus. They all suffered, the page says, in the persecution Diocletian set in motion against the Christians, whose years it gives as 284 to 304, and they suffered together with Saint [Sebastian](/saints/sebastian-of-rome), whom the same day's calendar keeps.

Of Marcia the page says what it does not know: that for Tranquillinus's wife Marcia we find no evidence at all that she was martyred, only that she became a Christian. She and [Tranquillinus](/saints/tranquillinus) were pagans who went to the prison to turn their two sons from the faith, and were turned themselves with Sebastian standing by them.

That is the whole of what the page gives for this company: the ten names, the one reign, and for each of them the manner of the death. It names no city and no judge, gives no year of birth for any of them and no year inside the reign, lists no relic and prints no hymn of its own; the day's apolytikion stands on Sebastian's page and names Sebastian. The others of the ten here are [Nicostratus](/saints/nicostratus-18-december), [Tranquillinus](/saints/tranquillinus), [Claudius](/saints/claudius-18-december), [Tiburtius](/saints/tiburtius-18-december), [Castor](/saints/castor-18-december), [Castulus](/saints/castulus-18-december), [Marcus](/saints/marcus-son-of-tranquillinus), [Marcellinus](/saints/marcellinus-son-of-tranquillinus), and [Zoe](/saints/zoe-18-december), whom this corpus already kept from the Romanian calendar.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3289/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
