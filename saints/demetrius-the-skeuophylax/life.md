# Demetrius the Skeuophylax

saint.gr's calendar for 25 January names Demetrius the Skeuophylax and gives almost no life. The whole of what the page says about the man is one sentence: that he lived in the eighth century and fell asleep in peace. It prints no birthplace, no parentage, no year, and no account of his office beyond the title that names it — skeuophylax, the keeper of a church's sacred vessels. It prints no troparion and no kontakion.

What else stands on the page is not biography. There is the etymology saint.gr gives for the name Demetrius, and there is a couplet of verse: «Των γηΐνων ουκ ην τι τω Δημητρίω, / Ήδη βλέποντι προς μόνην αϋλίαν» — nothing of earthly things belonged to Demetrius, who looked already toward the immaterial alone. Both are set down here as what the source prints, and neither adds a fact about his life.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 25 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/25/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1155/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
