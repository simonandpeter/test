# Martha of Monemvasia

saint.gr's calendar names her and then says outright that it knows nothing of her: «Δεν έχουμε πληροφορίες για τον βίο της Οσίας» — we have no information about the life of the venerable one. The line for 5 Μαΐου reads «Οσία Μάρθα Μονεμβασίας», Martha of Monemvasia, and beside it the page prints only the meaning of her name, which it gives from the Hebrew as lady, or mistress of the house.

What stands under the notice is not a life but two pointers: the synaxis of the saints who shone in Laconia, and the Metropolis of Monemvasia and Sparta, the local church whose calendar keeps her. There is no century, no place beyond the see her epithet names, no account of how she lived, no relics and no hymn. Nothing is claimed here beyond the name, the rank and the day.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/6352/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
