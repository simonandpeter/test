# Alexander of Voskiy

saint.gr's calendar for 27 Μαρτίου names the venerable Alexander «του Βόσκιϋ», and the page behind the line says only that it knows nothing: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέριες για τον βίο του Ρώσου μοναχού Αλέξανδρου» — we have no details of the life of the Russian monk Alexander. So what the source keeps is that he was Russian, that he was a monk, and that the calendar attaches a place to his name; there is no year, no monastery, no abbot, no wonder, no relic and no hymn.

The place itself is only half-recorded. Βόσκιϋ is saint.gr's Greek spelling of a Russian name, and no Russian page was read to find how that name is written at home, so the epithet in this folder follows the Greek and claims nothing more. A later reader with the Russian calendar open should expect to correct the spelling, and may find the life the Greek page does not have.

Everything else on the page belongs to the name and not to the man: that Αλέξανδρος is from ἀλέξω and ἀνήρ, the defender of his country and vanquisher of its enemies, and that scores of emperors, kings, princes, popes, patriarchs, bishops and generals have borne it, the greatest of them Alexander of Macedon. None of that is about a Russian monk kept on 27 March.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 27 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/27/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3362/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
