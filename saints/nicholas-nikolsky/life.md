# Nicholas (Nikolsky)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him second of two priests of 1937 on 24 октября ст. ст.: «Сщмчч. Иоанна и Николая пресвитеров (1937).» The line declines his name and gives it without a surname; the title on the name link behind his place supplies both the surname and the grade, «Николай (Никольский), иерей, сщмч.», and that link is the whole of what the calendar has for him, because no life page follows his name.

His is the name the word «пресвитеров» stands after, which is what fixes the grade of the man before him as well as his own, and the link behind his place says иерей, the same thing. The corpus already holds seven other men of this surname, and not one of them of this forename, so nothing has to be read to tell him apart from any of them.

Nothing on either page says where he was born or served, what he was charged with, or whether he died with [John](/saints/john-smirnov-24-october), the first of the two names on his line.

The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line. The day page's thirteen troparia and kontakia belong to the four older entries it heads and to the icon it keeps, and none of them to this line.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 24 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261024.html); read 4 October 2026.*
