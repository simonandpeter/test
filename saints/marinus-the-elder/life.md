# Marinus the Elder

Doxologia.ro’s calendar for 18 octombrie names him «Sfântul Mucenic Marin cel Bătrân», and the life on his page is two sentences long. The holy martyr Marinus the Elder, it says, was in the time of Diocletian and of Aristobulus; and standing before the governor of Cilicia he was cruelly beaten with rods, and his feet were bound with irons and he was put into prison.

The next day he was brought out again, hung by the feet on a piece of wood and beaten without mercy; and taken down from the wood, he suffered death by the sword and gave up his spirit to God. The page gives no year, no homeland and no name beyond those two, and prints no troparion; nothing further is added here.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 18 October, and names his city and his judge. saint.gr says Marinus the Elder was from Tarsus in Cilicia and lived in the years of Diocletian, whose reign it gives as 284 to 304; that he was arrested because he was a Christian and brought before Lysias, the ruler of the place, and that when Lysias could not bring him back to the worship of idols by many hard tortures he had him beheaded at the last. The two-line verse over the entry says that the old man Marinus refutes an old error, young in daring, and is perfected by the sword.
