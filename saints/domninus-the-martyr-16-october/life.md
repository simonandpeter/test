# Domninus (16 October)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its page for 16 octombrie prints the line «Sfântul Mucenic Domnin», and the life under his name is a single sentence: «Sfinții Mucenici Leontie, Dometie, Terentie și Domnin prin foc s-au săvârșit» — the holy martyrs Leontius, Dometius, Terentius and Domninus made their end by fire. The same one sentence stands under each of the four names, word for word.

So what is recorded of Domninus here is what the calendar records: that he is kept on this day with [Leontius](/saints/leontius-the-martyr-16-october), [Dometius](/saints/dometius-the-martyr-16-october) and [Terentius](/saints/terentius-the-martyr-16-october), and that the four of them died in the fire. The page gives no country, no emperor, no year, no account of their trial and no troparion, and nothing is added to it.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 16 octombrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/16-octombrie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-domnin-0); read 24 September 2026.*
