# Askiot of Georgia

Askiot was born in Georgia and reigned there in the ninth century, saint.gr says. The devout ruler raised many churches and a good number of monasteries. He was killed by the Arabs inside the church of his own castle at Artanuji in Georgia, which he had himself built. In the Georgian synaxaria, the page adds, he is also called Kouropalates.

That is the whole of what the Greek calendar prints of him: no year of birth, no year of death, no parentage, no length of reign, and neither troparion nor kontakion. The name is given here only in the form the page prints it, and the Georgian sources it mentions have not been read.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 27 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/27/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3349/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
