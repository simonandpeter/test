# Demetrius (Kryuchkov)

He is a confessor rather than a martyr, and the calendar marks the difference in the one line it gives him on 26 September: «Св. Димитрия исп., пресвитера (1952)» — not «сщмч.» but «исп.», not killed but one who held out. Its index of names adds the surname, «Димитрий (Крючков), иерей, исп.», a priest, and keeps him here and on 26 January with the new martyrs and confessors of Russia.

1952 is late — after the camps and after the war — and a priest dying in that year has usually survived a sentence rather than been shot for one. The calendar says none of that: it prints the name, the rank and the year, and nothing else. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which refused this reading on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line.

*After the Russian church calendar's [26 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260926.html) and its [index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9557.html); read 4 October 2026.*
