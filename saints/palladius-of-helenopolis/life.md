# Palladius of Helenopolis

Palladius was born in the year 363 in Galatia, in Asia Minor. The Romanian page, which is a short notice rather than a life, says he was tonsured at Jerusalem in 386, went after three years to Alexandria and stayed there another three, and then spent nine years in Nitria. In the year 400 he was bishop at Helenopolis in Bithynia.

He was, the page says, one of the closest friends of [John Chrysostom](/saints/john-chrysostom). To support him he went to Rome at the head of a delegation of priests, asking the pope to take the part of the great man whom the emperor Arcadius had exiled. On the way back he was exiled himself, to the Thebaid in Egypt, for six years, from 406 to 412.

After the exile the notice finds him bishop at Aspuna in Galatia, where he shepherded until his death. It gives no year for that death and nothing of his writings, and nothing further is set down here.
