# Photius of Yuriev

saint.gr's calendar for 27 Φεβρουαρίου names him as «Όσιος Φώτιος εκ Ρωσίας», the venerable Photius from Russia, and the page standing under it gives one sentence: the venerable Photius practised his asceticism at the monastery of Yuriev of Novgorod and fell asleep in peace in the year 1838. That is the whole of the entry, and the monastery is the only thing in it that tells him apart from the other Photii the calendar keeps.

The page gives him no year of birth — its heading leaves the first of his two dates a question mark — no office, no relics, no couplet and no hymn; and what stands above the sentence is only saint.gr's standing gloss on the name Photius, the man who carries the spiritual light, which belongs to the name and not to this man.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 27 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/27/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3601/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
