# Elijah (Berezovsky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him on 21 января ст. ст. and gives no life. The whole of its account is one line, «Сщмч. Илии пресвитера (1938)» — the hieromartyr Elijah, a presbyter, 1938 — and behind that line the calendar's own name link carries the surname the line leaves out, «Илия (Березовский), иерей, сщмч.»: the priest Elijah Berezovsky.

The calendar names no birthplace, no diocese, no parish, no place of death and no manner of it. The 1938 in its bracket is the whole of what it dates him by, and it is the year of his death, in the last and heaviest year of the great terror. He stands on this day among the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and he is the only twentieth-century name on it.

The day page carries troparia and kontakia for others of the saints and for the icons it heads, and none under his name. That is everything the source says of him.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 21 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260121.html); read 3 October 2026. The calendar prints no life page for him.*
