# Athanasius of Paros

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 24 iunie reads «Sfântul Atanasie din Paros», and his page carries the day, the epistle and gospel appointed for it, and no biography at all — no century, no monastery, no office, no year of death and no hymn. The island in the name is the only thing the calendar itself says of him beyond the name.

What the page carries instead of a life is a text of his own and an account of a wonder, both filed under the same heading: a word urging the reader to pray to Saint Spyridon with hope and with faith, and the promise that he will certainly help. The page gives no date and no setting for it.

The readings appointed are Galatians 5:22–26 with 6:1–2, on the fruit of the Spirit, and Matthew 11:27–30. No rank is printed for him on the day's line, so none is recorded here.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 24 June, and saint.gr gives the life doxologia.ro withheld. It makes him a Parian, born at Kostos on Paros in 1722, whose real surname was Toulios and who exchanged it for «Πάριος» out of love for his birthplace: a theologian and one of the great teachers of the Greek nation. He studied at Smyrna from 1745 and entered the Athonias school on the Holy Mountain in 1752, under Neophytos Kausokalyvites and then Eugenios Voulgaris. He was made to leave the Athonias over his part in the quarrels of the Kollyvades, directed the school of Thessalonica until 1786, and then brought the school of Chios to a great flowering between 1788 and 1811. At about ninety he withdrew to the little monastery of Saint George Reuston, where he died on 24 June 1813; the page lists sixty-nine of his writings under eight headings, dates his canonisation to 1995, and prints an apolytikion for him that the Romanian calendar does not.
