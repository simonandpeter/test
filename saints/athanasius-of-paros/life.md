# Athanasius of Paros

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 24 iunie reads «Sfântul Atanasie din Paros», and his page carries the day, the epistle and gospel appointed for it, and no biography at all — no century, no monastery, no office, no year of death and no hymn. The island in the name is the only thing the calendar itself says of him beyond the name.

What the page carries instead of a life is a text of his own and an account of a wonder, both filed under the same heading: a word urging the reader to pray to Saint Spyridon with hope and with faith, and the promise that he will certainly help. The page gives no date and no setting for it.

The readings appointed are Galatians 5:22–26 with 6:1–2, on the fruit of the Spirit, and Matthew 11:27–30. No rank is printed for him on the day's line, so none is recorded here.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 24 iunie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/24-iunie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-atanasie-din-paros); read 24 September 2026.*
