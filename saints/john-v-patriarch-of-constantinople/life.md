# John V

John V was patriarch of Constantinople from 669 to 674, and [George I](/saints/george-i-patriarch-of-constantinople) from 678 to 683, both under the emperor Constantine Pogonatus (668–685). That is the whole of the Sretensky calendar’s life for the two hierarchs kept together on 18 August (Julian).
