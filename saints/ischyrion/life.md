# Ischyrion

saint.gr's calendar for 1 Ιουνίου names him and gives two sentences. The holy martyr Ischyrion, it says, came from Egypt and was an officer. He was martyred in the year 250, under the emperor Decius, whose reign the page dates 249 to 251, together with five of his own soldiers, because they confessed their faith in Christ.

The page adds that the Church honours his memory also on 22 December. It gives him no birthplace in Egypt, no year of birth, no judge and no manner of death, and it names not one of the five who died with him; they have a heading of their own on the page with no name and no number in it, so this corpus makes them no folder and leaves the question open.

It prints no hymn and no epigram for him. The rank in the folder is the page's own word for him, an officer, and the five being called his soldiers.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4404/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
