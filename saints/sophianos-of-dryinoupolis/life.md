# Sophianos of Dryinoupolis

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιος Σοφιανός επίσκοπος Δρυϊνουπόλεως καί Αργυροκάστρου» and heads his page with the year 1711. He was an important religious figure of his age in the region of Epirus, the page says, and is reckoned the forerunner of [Kosmas of Aetolia](/saints/kosmas-of-aetolia). He was born most probably in the village of Polytsiani, in the Pogoni district of northern Epirus, and he became known from the moment he took up the bishopric of Dryinoupolis — the district of Argyrokastro, Delvino and Himarra.

In 1672 he founded a school at the local monastery of Saint Athanasius. In the time he lived, conversions to Islam were a particularly common thing, and he, wanting to meet that situation, went from village to village to persuade people to keep their traditions and their religion. In 1711, a few months before his death, he resigned his priestly duties and became a monk, so as to give himself entirely to his work of preaching and mission.

Because of his character he was held in particular respect, the page says, even beyond his own flock. The page's own links call him «ΟΣΙΟΣ ΣΟΦΙΑΝΟΣ Ο ΣΗΜΕΙΟΦΟΡΟΣ», the sign-bearer, and date his repose 26 November 1711.
