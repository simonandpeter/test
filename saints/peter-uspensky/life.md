# Peter (Uspensky)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 16 февраля ст. ст. in a line of four words: «Сщмч. Петра пресвитера (1938).» The rank word is «Сщмч.», hieromartyr; «пресвитера» gives him as a presbyter, which is the one thing the calendar says about his service; and the year the line prints for his death is 1938. The name link behind the line carries the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line itself leaves out, «Петр (Успенский), иерей».

The calendar prints no life page under his name. The day page carries one hymn, a kontakion of martyrs headed under no name at all, and nothing under his. Nothing further is written here. The line, the rank word, the order of service, the name the link behind it carries and the year 1938 are the whole of what was read, and in particular he is not identified with any saint of the same name whom the calendar keeps on another day.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 16 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260216.html); read 3 October 2026.*
