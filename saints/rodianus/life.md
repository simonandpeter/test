# Rodianus

saint.gr's calendar for 20 Μαρτίου names a martyr Rodianus, and its page for him is one sentence long: he was martyred by the sword, and we have no further details of the saint's life. That is the whole of the account, and the life here says no more than the page does.

Over him the page prints a couplet which plays on his name against ῥόδον, a rose: Rodianus was brought to thee, O Christ, as a red rose, his body dyed. It gives him no country, no year, no persecutor and no relics, and prints no hymn. Nothing has been taken from any other source and nothing supplied from memory; if a life of his is printed elsewhere, it has not been read.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 20 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/20/index.aspx) and [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/3928/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
