# Terentius (17 October)

saint.gr's calendar for 17 October keeps five saints on one line — «Άγιοι Αντίγονος, Λουκιανός, Τερέντιος, Νικομήδης και Θεοφάνης» — and Terentius is one of them. The page behind the line gives each of the five a heading of its own, his being «Αγιος Τερεντιος», and then prints one sentence for all five together, which is the whole of what the calendar has.

That sentence is also a warning. The five of them — he and [Antigonus](/saints/antigonus-17-october), [Lucian](/saints/lucian-17-october), [Nicomedes](/saints/nicomedes-17-october) and [Theophanes](/saints/theophanes-17-october) — it says, are unknown to the Synaxaria and the Menaia; they are mentioned in the Paris Codex 1578, without biographical details of them. So the calendar does not say when they lived, or where, or what they suffered, or whether they were martyrs at all; it says only that a manuscript names them on this day, and it names the manuscript.

Of Terentius in particular, then, there is a name and a day and a codex.  This folder holds that and nothing more: no year, no country, no office, no account of a life or a death, and no hymn — and it says plainly, as the calendar does, that the older service books do not know him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 17 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/17/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2541/saint.aspx), which the five share; read 1 October 2026.*
