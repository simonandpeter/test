# Donatus (4 July)

saint.gr's calendar for 4 Ιουλίου keeps two men in one line, «Άγιος Δονάτος επίσκοπος Λιβύης και Ιλαρίων οσιομάρτυρας», and its life page tells them both at once. The page opens by disputing the see the listing gives: «Σύμφωνα με την άποψη του Μ. Γαλανού, πρόκειται για τον Δονάτο επίσκοπο Arerro της Ετρουρίας» — in M. Galanos's opinion the man in question is Donatus, bishop of Arezzo in Etruria, and not of Libya at all. It leaves the two readings standing side by side and settles neither.

His parents, it says, had themselves died for Christ, and their son, together with a certain monk [Hilarion](/saints/hilarion-companion-of-donatus), had taken refuge in that city, of which he was afterwards made shepherd. But in the persecution set in motion by Julian the Apostate the prefect Quadratianus ordered the two of them to sacrifice to the idols. They not only refused but protested vigorously to the public authorities about that error of theirs.

Then they beat Hilarion with rods until he died, and Donatus, after many tortures, they beheaded. The Christians took up their holy relics from the place of execution, where they had been left, and buried them with honours. The page gives no year for any of it and prints no hymn under either name; the only verse on it is the couplet for Donatus, «Δονάτος έργοις πριν δονήσας Λιβύην, / Ήδη κατοικεί ου δόνησις ουκ ένι» — Donatus, who once shook Libya by his deeds, now dwells where no shaking is.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 4 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/04/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1997/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
