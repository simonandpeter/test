# Gabriel of Egypt

saint.gr's calendar for 18 October keeps two new martyrs of Egypt together, Gabriel and [Kyrmidoles](/saints/kyrmidoles-of-egypt), and gives each of them the years 1497 to 1522. Both were young, the page says, both very well educated, and both came of pious parents. They were slandered before Khaer Mek, the emir of Egypt, with the charge that they had thrown refuse and filth into a Turkish mosque.

They were led bound to the governor's house, where they were asked to accept Islam so as to escape torture. Because they confessed Christ steadily instead, they were handed over to the judge; and there too they stayed unshaken in their faith, for all the flatteries, and underwent dreadful tortures. So both were martyred in the twenty-fifth year of their age: Kyrmidoles was put to death with knives and stones, Gabriel by beheading.

Their martyred bodies the Turks put into the fire twice; they were burnt to ash and the ash was scattered. Only the relics of the head of the martyr Gabriel were saved. The Service of the two is in codex 379 of the Patriarchal library of Cairo, and their entry carries a verse of two lines: Kyrmidoles and Gabriel, the two of them, contend together and are crowned together.
