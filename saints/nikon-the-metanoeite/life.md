# Nikon the Metanoeite

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Νοεμβρίου names «Όσιος Νίκων ο «Μετανοεῖτε»» — Nikon the Repent-ye — and makes him patron of Sparta. He was born between 920 and 925, the page says, came of Polemoniac Pontus and was the son of a magnate. While still young he left his father's house and became a monk; and because a holy zeal and a great gift of teaching marked him out, he went round the whole East as his monastery's envoy preaching the Gospel, repeating the cry that had sounded first in the desert of Judaea and by the banks of the Jordan: «Μετανοεῖτε», repent ye.

Afterwards he went to Crete, where he stayed teaching for twenty years. For the strengthening of religious feeling he built churches without number there; the page names in particular the church of Saint Photini, which Nikon built three days' journey from Gortyna.

Then he went to the Peloponnese, and ended in the city of the Laconians. There he preached, worked various miracles and built a church in the name of the Saviour Jesus Christ, and his moral influence on the inhabitants was great. In that country, which he loved more than his own homeland, he gave up his last breath, in the year 998.

It is worth noting at the end, the page adds, that Nikon visited Euboea too, and busied himself there, as he did everywhere, with preaching. He took his occasion from the phenomenon of the tide and spoke on the fluidity and changeableness of this world's life and on the unsteady ways of disordered men, preaching from the high fortress of Euripos at Chalkis. He worked a wonder there, saving a child who had fallen from a height off the fortress: everyone thought it was killed, and when the child came to itself it said, I was not killed, because that monk who says «Repent ye» held me. He also healed a demon-possessed woman, praying for her cure publicly with the people taking part. The couplet set over his line says the same in a pun: let Lakedaimon in no way get a demon, for Nikon drives him off with his prodigies.
