# Sabainos, servant of Chryse

saint.gr's calendar for 30 January names Sabainos and gives him two sentences, and both of them are about how he died. He was the servant of the martyr [Chryse](/saints/chryse-the-martyr), whom the same calendar keeps on the same day. The idolaters struck him on the neck with heavy shot, then hung him on a piece of wood and burned out his entrails; and so he gave up his holy soul to the Lord.

Nothing else is on the page: no homeland, no year of birth or of death, no emperor, no city, and no apolytikion or kontakion. Chryse's own notice dates her martyrdom to about 269 under the emperor Claudius, and his belongs with hers; but his own page states no year, and none is set down here.

The couplet above the account turns the torture back on the torturers: «Σπλάγχνα φλέγουσι Σαβαΐνου λαμπάσι, / Τὰ παμπόνηρα τέκνα τῆς ἀσπλαγχνίας» — the utterly wicked children of pitilessness burn Sabainos's entrails with torches.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 30 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/30/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3566/saint.aspx) and, for the year his own page does not give, [Chryse's](https://www.saint.gr/3565/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
