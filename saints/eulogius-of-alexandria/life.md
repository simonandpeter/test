# Eulogius of Alexandria

Doxologia's life of him is a single short paragraph. This saint lived in the reign of the emperor Heraclius, and was archbishop of Alexandria before John the Merciful. He worked many wonders, and the page tells one of them at length, as the thing worth remembering about him.

The most venerable pope Leo had written for the Council of Chalcedon the Epistle of Orthodoxy; and Eulogius, reading it, not only praised it and received it but preached it to everyone. God, wishing to comfort them both, sent an angel in the shape of Leo's archdeacon to thank Eulogius for having received that letter; and Eulogius spoke with the angel of God as with a man, taking him for the pope's deacon. After the angel had become invisible to him, he gave thanks the more to God for the wonder, and gave up his soul into his hands.

That is all the page prints. It gives no year of birth or death, names no place but Alexandria, and carries no hymn for him.
