# Michael (Lektorsky)

The Russian calendar's 14 October gives him five words and a year: «Сщмч. Михаила пресвитера (1921)» — Michael, hieromartyr, presbyter, 1921. «Сщмч.» is the mark of a priest put to death for the faith, and «пресвитер» is the simple priesthood. The site's index of names supplies the surname the day line withholds and says the grade a second way, heading his page «Михаил (Лекторский), иерей, сщмч.», and that is the whole of what the calendar holds.

1921 sets him apart from the other two priests on this day: it is the year of the civil war and of the requisitioning of church valuables, not of the terror of the thirties that took [Peter (Lebedev)](/saints/peter-lebedev) in 1937 and [Maximilian (Marchenko)](/saints/maximilian-marchenko) in 1938. Neither page gives a birth, a diocese, a parish, a charge or a place of death, and neither carries a life. The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian saints are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026 even with a browser user-agent, so the line stands as the line.

His index entry keeps him here and on 26 January with the whole company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia.

*After the Russian church calendar's [14 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261014.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13829.html); read 4 October 2026.*
