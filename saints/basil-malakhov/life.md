# Basil (Malakhov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him in a line of five words and a year for 11 марта ст. ст., «Св. Василия исп., пресвитера (1937)» — saint Basil, a confessor, a presbyter, 1937. The heading matters: this calendar marks its martyred priests «Сщмч.» and it does not mark him so, which is the one thing the line says about how he died. It declines the forename and gives no surname, no place and no year of birth.

Behind that «Василия» stands the calendar's own name page 14235, which carries the fuller form «Василий (Малахов), иерей, исповедник» — the surname the line leaves out, and the grade of priest — and lists two days, this 11 марта and «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. That page carries no life either, and azbyka.ru was not read for him. So what the calendar prints is the whole of what this folder says: a priest of the Russian church named Basil Malakhov, who died in 1937 and whom this calendar keeps among its confessors on this day.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 11 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260311.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14235.html), which carries no life; read 7 October 2026.*
