# John the Exarch of Bulgaria

The Охридски пролог gives him four sentences and no more, and they are the whole of what is written here. He was a notable Bulgarian priest and theologian, it says, from the time of the emperor Симеон, whose reign the page prints as 892 to 896. He put into the Slavonic tongue the «Шестоднев Василијев», the Hexaemeron on the six days of creation, and the «Богословље Дамаскиново», the exposition of the faith of the Damascene. And he reposed peacefully in the Lord.

That is all. This book, which elsewhere gives a saint a page, gives him a paragraph: no birthplace, no year of his birth and none of his death, no see and no monastery, no martyrdom and no wonder — only the two great books he carried over into the language of his own people, and a quiet end. The calendar names him and gives him almost no life, and the folder says so rather than filling the silence from anywhere else.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 31. јул ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-08-13&prolog=1); read 8 October 2026.*
