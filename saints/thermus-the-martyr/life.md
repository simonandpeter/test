# Thermus

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life. Its whole account is one clause — «Ο Άγιος Θέρμος τελειώθηκε διά πυρός», the holy Thermus was perfected by fire. The page gives him no century, no country, no city, no emperor and no judge; it gives him no year, names no relics, prints no hymn, and does not gloss his name as it glosses so many others.

What it prints beside the line is a couplet that turns on the name, θέρμη, warmth: «Θέρμην ἔρωτος ἐνθέου Θέρμος φέρων, / Θέρμην πυρὸς φλέγοντος ὡς ψύξιν κρίνει» — bearing the warmth of a love that is God's, Thermus reckons the warmth of the burning fire as a coldness. That is the whole of the source, and this folder says so rather than filling the gap.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3976/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
