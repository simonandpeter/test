# Gabriel (2 February)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its page for him prints the line «Sfântul Mucenic Gavriil» over the date, 2 Februarie, with an icon and the readings appointed for a martyr — the Apostle from the second letter to Timothy and the Gospel from John — and nothing besides: no homeland, no century, no account of his suffering, and no troparion. The rank the calendar gives him is mucenic, martyr.

He is kept on the day of the Meeting of the Lord, together with two other martyrs the same calendar names and leaves equally unwritten, Agatodor and Iordan. Whether he is one of the martyrs of that name whom other calendars keep on other days is a question this page does not answer, and no other calendar was read for him.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 2 februarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/2-februarie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-gavriil); read 19 September 2026.*
