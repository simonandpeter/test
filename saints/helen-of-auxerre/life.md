# Helen of Auxerre

saint.gr names her and gives no life beyond two sentences. Its whole account is this: «Η Αγία Ελένη αναφέρεται στα συγγράμματα του Αγίου Αγαπητού της Ωξέρρης (τιμάται 1 Μαΐου). Ήταν εκείνη που τον διακονούσε, όταν ο Άγιος ήταν ασθενής. Κοιμήθηκε μετά το 418 μ.Χ.» — Saint Helen is mentioned in the writings of Saint Agapitus of Auxerre, who is honoured on 1 May; she was the one who served him when the saint was sick; she fell asleep after the year 418.

That is all the page has of her, and she is remembered for that one thing: the woman who looked after a sick bishop and is known because he wrote of her. The page gives her no birth year, no rank, no family and no place but the city her name carries, and it prints no hymn to her. Nothing further about her is recorded here.
