# John of Antioch

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The page for 18 April prints his line — «Sfântul Ierarh Ioan, Arhiepiscopul Antiohiei» — and under it only the readings appointed for a hierarch: the Epistle from the seventh and eighth chapters of Hebrews, that such a high priest became us, holy, guileless, undefiled; and the Gospel of the good shepherd from the tenth chapter of John. His page carries no Viață tab, no troparion and no notice of any kind.

So this folder records what the Romanian calendar keeps and nothing further: that on 18 April it commemorates a hierarch named John, archbishop of Antioch. Which of the bishops of that see he is, the calendar does not say, and nothing written here should be read as having decided it.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 18 aprilie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/18-aprilie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-ierarh-ioan-arhiepiscopul-antiohiei), which prints no life; read 20 September 2026.*
