# Peter (Grudinsky)

He was born in 1877 at Glusk in the Minsk province, in a family of townspeople; his father rented church land and he helped him at it. He got a fair education, and in 1905 the peasants elected him their deputy to the second State Duma. He took holy orders as a grown man, knowing, the notice says, that he was choosing a thorny way of serving the Lord in a time when the persecutions had already begun, and he served the parish of Timkovichi in the Kopyl district.

In 1930 he was arrested and shut up in the house of correction at Slutsk. Under the weight of it his wife Irina begged him to renounce his orders, and he wrote her a letter full of sympathy for her weakness and firm in the confession of the faith: «Отречься от веры во Христа, Который составляет смысл всей моей жизни, от Которого я видел столько благодеяний, и оставить Его в то время, когда я приближаюсь к могиле?!» — renounce the faith in Christ, who is the meaning of my whole life, from whom I have seen so many mercies, and leave him now, when I am drawing near the grave?

He did not stay alive long after that letter. He was shot on 23 February 1930. The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 10 февраля ст. ст. in one line with another priest shot the same day.
