# Michael, companion of Platon of Reval

saint.gr's calendar for 1 Ιανουαρίου names three men in one line: the hieromartyr Platon, and with him the presbyters Michael and Nicholas who were martyred alongside him. Of Michael its page prints only the name, the reading it gives the name — a Hebrew word, meaning one who is like God — and the year of his death, 1919. It gives him no surname, no parish, no birthplace and no account of how he died.

That is everything the source holds about him, and nothing has been added to it. He is recorded here because the Greek calendar names him, and he is recorded with exactly what it names him with: a presbyter, a companion of [Platon](/saints/platon-kulbusch), bishop of Reval in Estonia, killed with him in 1919.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1271/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
