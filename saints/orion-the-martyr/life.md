# Orion

saint.gr's calendar for 10 Νοεμβρίου names Orion and gives him one sentence, which is all it knows of his end: «Ο Άγιος Ωρίων μαρτύρησε, αφού τον έθαψαν ζωντανό στη γη» — the holy Orion was martyred, having been buried alive in the earth. The page gives him no country, no century, no emperor, no charge, no companions, no year and no hymn.

What stands beside the sentence is the couplet of the synaxarion, which sets his name against the constellation that bears it: «Κἂν ζῶν ἐβλήθη Μάρτυς εἰς γῆν Ὠρίων, / Ὑπὲρ τὸν Ὠρίωνα λάμπει τοῦ πόλου» — though the martyr Orion was cast living into the earth, he shines brighter than the Orion of the sky.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/10/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2971/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
