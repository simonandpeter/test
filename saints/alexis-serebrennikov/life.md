# Alexis (Serebrennikov)

He is one of the two men the Russian calendar's 30 September line calls simply «мчч.» — martyrs, with no rank in the Church and no office — after six presbyters, a protodeacon and a schemanun, all eleven under the one year 1937. The day line names him «Алексия» and the site's index of names heads his page «Алексий (Серебренников), мч.». His fellow layman on the line is [Matthew (Solovyov)](/saints/matthew-solovyov).

The index keeps him here and on 26 January with the company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and prints nothing further: no birth, no trade, no parish he belonged to, no charge, no place of death. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026 even with a browser user-agent, so the line stands as the line.

*After the Russian church calendar's [30 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260930.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9936.html); read 4 October 2026.*
