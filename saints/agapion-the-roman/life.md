# Agapion the Roman

saint.gr's calendar for 22 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιος Αγαπίων ο Ρωμαίος» — Agapion the Roman — and gives him one sentence. Because he confessed Christ, the page says, they threw him to the wild beasts for food; but he came out unharmed, miraculously, and died in peace. So the calendar keeps him among its martyrs' company and by its own account he did not die a martyr's death, which is why this folder calls him a confessor and nothing more.

The couplet set over the notice says why: Agapion went away unhurt by the beasts, for the nature of beasts loved him too — a play on the love in his name. The page gives him no year, no emperor, no companions and no hymn, prints no relics for him, and says nothing of Rome beyond the epithet in his line.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 22 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/22/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3070/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
