# Germanus of Paris

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and gives no life. Its line for 28 мая ст. ст. reads «Свт. Германа , епископа Парижского (576).» — the hierarch Germanus, bishop of Paris, of the year 576 — and the line carries a link to his name and none to a reading. The name page behind it prints his see twice over, «Святитель Герман епископ Парижский, епископ Парижский», which is the page’s own duplication, and the one line «День памяти: 28 мая», and nothing further.

So the calendar sets down a bishop of Paris who died in 576, and no more than that. It gives him one day only and no mark of the Russian synaxis of the new martyrs, which is what the page carries for the twentieth-century names on the same day; he belongs to the Western church of the sixth century, and this calendar keeps him without telling his story. The see is in this folder’s name and in this sentence rather than in its office field, a see-bearing office costing four translations a reader may not invent.

Nothing else about him was found: no folder in this corpus held the name, and neither azbyka.ru nor the Latin tradition his own church keeps him in was read.
