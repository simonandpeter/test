# Eutychius of Mesopotamia

saint.gr's calendar for 14 Μαρτίου names him «Άγιος Ευτύχιος ο Μάρτυρας και οι συν αυτώ μαρτυρήσαντες», the holy martyr Eutychius and those who were martyred with him, and heads him «(; - 741)». The page behind that line holds one sentence: that the holy martyr Eutychius was martyred together with other Christians during the invasion of the Arabs into Mesopotamia, in the year 741.

Neither the number nor the name of any of those others is given, so they are commemorated here only in the line that keeps them, and no folder stands for them. Of Eutychius himself the page says nothing further — no city of Mesopotamia, no rank, no manner of death, no relic and no hymn — and the rest of it is a note on the name, from εὐτυχής, the blessed one. What is recorded is the commemoration, the country, the invasion and the year.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 14 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/14/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1388/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
