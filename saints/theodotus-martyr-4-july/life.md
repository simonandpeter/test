# Theodotus (4 July)

saint.gr's calendar for 4 Ιουλίου names him and gives no life. The whole of its account is one sentence, «Ο Άγιος Θεόδοτος μαρτύρησε δια ξίφους» — the holy Theodotus was martyred by the sword. The page names no country, no city, no century, no year, no emperor and no judge; the only other matter under his name is a note on the name itself, which explains it as the gift of God and remarks that two Greek kings of Bactria bore it. Neither remark is about him.

Besides that sentence it carries only the couplet the synaxaria print for him: «Δους την κεφαλήν τω Θεώ Θεόδοτε, / Ζωήν έλαβες ήτις ουκ έχει πέρας» — having given your head to God, O Theodotus, you received a life that has no end. The page prints no hymn under his line. That is everything the source says of him.

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on the same menologion day as saint.gr, 4 июля ст. ст., and adds three things the Greek page had not: a year, 108; an epithet, «Римский», of Rome, on its name page for him; and a companion. Its line reads «Мчч. Феодота и Феодотии (108).», the martyrs Theodotus and Theodotia, and the woman’s own name page gives her the same epithet and the same single day — she is [Theodotia of Rome](/saints/theodotia-of-rome). The year is not written into this folder, because the calendar offers no reading behind the line to carry it, and the Greek page which does carry a reading gives no year at all. The Russian calendar prints no hymn under his name either.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 4 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/04/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1995/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026; and after the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 4 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260704.html), read 9 October 2026.*
