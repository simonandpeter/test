# Sabbatius of Solovki

The Greek synaxarion keeps the venerable Sabbatius «of Solovki» on 27 September and says that it has no details of the Russian saint’s life. Its heading prints his death as 1435. That is all it prints.

The Russian calendar keeps him on the same 27 September, as «Прп. Савватия Соловецкого чудотворца», the wonderworker of Solovki, and dates his repose 1435.

The Serbian church's Ohrid Prologue keeps him fourth of the four it enumerates on this day and is as short as the other two, but it is the only one of the three to say when in the year he died rather than only in which year — «Упокојио се у Господу 27. септ. 1435. год.», on the 27th of September itself. It adds the one fact neither of the others prints: that he is commemorated together with [Zosimas](/saints/zosimas-of-solovki) on 17 April. Of his life it tells nothing at all, and none of the three calendars that keep him here does.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 27 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2525/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026; the Russian church calendar's [27 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260927.html), read 4 October 2026; and the Serbian church's [Ohrid Prologue for the Julian 27 September](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-10-10&prolog=1), read 4 October 2026.*
