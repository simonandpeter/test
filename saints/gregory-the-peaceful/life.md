# Gregory the Peaceful

saint.gr's calendar for 7 December keeps the venerable Gregory who is surnamed the Peaceful, and also the New, the page says, so that he may be told apart from his own great teacher the venerable [Gregory](/saints/gregory-of-sinai) the Sinaite. He lived as an ascetic on the Holy Mountain and founded the monastery of the venerable Gregory, formerly the monastery of Saint Nicholas, in the fourteenth century.

He fell asleep in peace, and his holy relics, the page says, are at the monastery of Gornjak in Serbia and at his own monastery of Gregoriou. The couplet set over his name says that he was seen to be the founder of a monastery of Nicholas, and that from there he dwells in the monasteries of heaven. The Church also keeps his memory on 19 May, which the page records and which is written here as a note on his Greek feast and not as a second entry.

The Ohrid Prologue keeps him on the same menologion day and identifies him beyond doubt by his monastery — the founder of the Athonite monastery of Saint Nicholas, which is known because of him by the name of Gregoriou. It calls him by a different by-name from the Greek page's: the Silent, where that page has the Peaceful, and this folder is displayed under the second of the two. It says that he was a Serb by origin, and that a quarter of an hour from the monastery stands his cell, where he did penance and prayed in silence.

This man of God, it says, reposed in peace in the year 1406 — the first year of any kind this folder has carried, and six years past the century in which the Greek page dates the founding of his house; neither reading is adjusted to the other. And it supplies the cause of something that page reported only as a fact: in 1761 there was a great fire at the monastery, and on that occasion some of the monks took up his relics and carried them into Serbia. That is how the relics the Greek page records at the monastery of Gornjak came to be there.
