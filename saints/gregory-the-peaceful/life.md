# Gregory the Peaceful

saint.gr's calendar for 7 December keeps the venerable Gregory who is surnamed the Peaceful, and also the New, the page says, so that he may be told apart from his own great teacher the venerable [Gregory](/saints/gregory-of-sinai) the Sinaite. He lived as an ascetic on the Holy Mountain and founded the monastery of the venerable Gregory, formerly the monastery of Saint Nicholas, in the fourteenth century.

He fell asleep in peace, and his holy relics, the page says, are at the monastery of Gornjak in Serbia and at his own monastery of Gregoriou. The couplet set over his name says that he was seen to be the founder of a monastery of Nicholas, and that from there he dwells in the monasteries of heaven. The Church also keeps his memory on 19 May, which the page records and which is written here as a note on his Greek feast and not as a second entry.
