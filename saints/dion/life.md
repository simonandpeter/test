# Dion

saint.gr's whole notice for him is two sentences, and the second is an admission. The holy martyr Dion, it says, was perfected by the sword; and we have no further details of the saint's life. That is all: no year, no city, no persecutor, no rank beyond martyr, no relic, no hymn. The site's name-gloss above the notice is not about him either — it lists a king of Laconia, a Platonist philosopher of Syracuse, Dio Cassius the historian, a philosopher of Prusa and the Nereid Dione, which are lexicon entries for the name and not facts about the saint.

What the page does print over him is a distich, and it turns on the one thing known: though the terrible right hand of the deceiver slaughters you, the right hand of the Master crowns you, Dion. The corpus holds nobody else of this name, and nothing here would tell him apart from another man if it did.
