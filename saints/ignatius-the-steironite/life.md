# Ignatius the Steironite

saint.gr's whole notice of him is one sentence: the venerable Ignatius the Steironite died in peace. The day's line names him «Όσιος Ιγνάτιος ο Στειρωνίτης», and the page gives no country, no monastery, no year and no age, no account of his life and no hymn. Its heading gives the feast as fixed, kept on 26 July of every year, and the entry carries no asterisk.

Above the notice the page sets a distich, which plays on his byname, and that is the only other thing it prints of him: «Πριν γης απελθείν αρετών καρπούς φύσας, Tο στείρον εκπέφευγεν ο Στειρωνίτης» — having brought forth the fruits of the virtues before he departed the earth, the Steironite escaped barrenness.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2163/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
