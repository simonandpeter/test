# Nectarius of Bezhetsk

saint.gr says Nectarius lived in Russia and was a monk of the monastery of the Holy Trinity of Sergius. In the middle of the fifteenth century he settled in a dense forest, on the highest ground of the district of Bezhetsk, and there practised asceticism in a way pleasing to God. The calendar's own line for him is «Όσιος Νεκτάριος του Μπεζέτσκ», the venerable Nectarius of Bezhetsk.

A crowd of monks gathered to him in that place, and in a short time they built a church dedicated to the Entry of the Mother of God. The new monastery was one of the poorest of them all, and according to the monastery's own chronicle it was built with tears, with fasting and with keeping vigil. By the unanimous decision of all the brothers of the house, Nectarius was chosen as its abbot.

Nectarius fell asleep in peace in the year 1492. That is the whole of what the page says: it gives him no year of birth, no family, no place he came from, tells no incident of his abbacy, names no relics, and prints no couplet and no hymn under his name.
