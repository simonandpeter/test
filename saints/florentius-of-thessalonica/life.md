# Florentius of Thessalonica

Florentius was from the city of Thessalonica. Being a Christian and zealous for what is good, he reproached and decried the gods of the Greeks before everybody, strengthened the Christians in their faith in Christ and urged them in every way to the working of the divine commandments. Doing so, he was seized by the governor of the city; and being questioned, he confessed Christ boldly and without fear before them all, as God before the ages and the Maker of all things.

The gods of the pagans, he said, are wood and stone, gold, silver, brass and iron, idols without soul and without feeling. For saying it they beat him cruelly, hung him on a piece of wood and raked him; and kindling a great fire they threw him into the midst of it, and he, rejoicing and praying secretly and giving thanks to God, made his end.

That is the whole of what doxologia.ro prints of him — three sentences, with no year, no name for the governor and no word of his burial. Its calendar for 13 octombrie names him «Sfântul Mucenic Florentie» on the same day as the martyrs of Thyatira, and his page’s Tropar tab leads to their troparion, headed for Carp, Papil and those with them; since it is theirs and not his, it is not recorded here.
