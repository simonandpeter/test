# Leontios the Myrrh-gusher

Leontios came to the monastery of Dionysiou in 1537 and struggled there beyond nature for sixty-eight years, never once leaving the house. His cell was a small cave beside the monastery's water cistern. Full of the grace of the Holy Spirit, he received the gifts of foresight and of clairvoyance: doxologia quotes his life as saying that, being full of these divine gifts, he saw clearly the thoughts and the deeds of those who came to him, and that they, learning from the saint the hidden things of their own hearts, went forward in the struggle, and many of them were saved.

He knew his end beforehand and fell asleep in peace after a short illness on 16 March 1605. After his burial, when his honourable relics were being moved, the brothers saw with their own eyes that whole body pouring out holy myrrh, and for that he was called the Myrrh-gusher.

His biographer, who wrote the life not long after his repose, is unknown; the life is kept in a manuscript of the monastery of Dionysiou, itself a reworking of the original. The page adds that his day of commemoration is 18 June, which is not the day of his repose.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 18 iunie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/18-iunie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-leontie-dionisiatul-izvoratorul-de-mir); read 24 September 2026.*
