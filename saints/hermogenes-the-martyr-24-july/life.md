# Hermogenes (24 July)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 24 iulie is «Sfântul Mucenic Ermoghen» — the holy martyr Hermogenes — and his own page on the site carries only the day and the epistle and gospel appointed for it: no Viață tab, no troparion, no city, no century and no account of his martyrdom.

The corpus already keeps three men of this name, and none of them is claimed here: the martyr of Nicomedia is kept on 1 September, the bishop of Samos on 5 October, and Hermogenes of Tobolsk in the Russian calendar in August. With nothing on the page but a name and a rank, there is no ground to say that the Romanian 24 July is any of them, and this folder stands for that day's line alone, marked by its day because the calendar gives him nothing else to be known by.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 24 iulie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/24-iulie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-ermoghen-0), which prints no life; read 24 September 2026.*
