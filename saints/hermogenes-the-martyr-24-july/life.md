# Hermogenes (24 July)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 24 iulie is «Sfântul Mucenic Ermoghen» — the holy martyr Hermogenes — and his own page on the site carries only the day and the epistle and gospel appointed for it: no Viață tab, no troparion, no city, no century and no account of his martyrdom.

The corpus already keeps three men of this name, and none of them is claimed here: the martyr of Nicomedia is kept on 1 September, the bishop of Samos on 5 October, and Hermogenes of Tobolsk in the Russian calendar in August. With nothing on the page but a name and a rank, there is no ground to say that the Romanian 24 July is any of them, and this folder stands for that day's line alone, marked by its day because the calendar gives him nothing else to be known by.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 24 July, and its line is as bare as the Romanian one — «Άγιος Ερμογένης» — but saint.gr adds the one thing the Romanian line did not say, which is how he died. The holy Hermogenes was martyred, the page says, after all his teeth had been torn out, and he died of the infection and of a bleeding that could not be stopped. Above the notice it sets a distich: «Aνηλεώς οδόντας εκκρουσθείς όλους, Bρυγμούς οδόντων, Eρμόγενες, εκφύγης» — having had all your teeth knocked out without mercy, Hermogenes, you escaped the gnashing of teeth. The page still gives no country, no city, no year, no emperor and no judge, and prints no hymn; it adds only a note on the name, which it derives from Hermes and γένος, a man descended from Hermes.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 24 iulie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/24-iulie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-ermoghen-0), which prints no life; read 24 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 24 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/24/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2067/saint.aspx), read 1 October 2026.*
