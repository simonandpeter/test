# The Mother with her Two Children

The Greek calendar keeps on 3 January a mother and her two children, and does not give any of the three a name. saint.gr's whole account is one sentence: the holy Mother and her two children were martyred by fire. Beside it stands a two-line epigram, and it is the only picture the source offers — the mother having been set alight, the children run chirping towards the flame like the young of a sparrow.

Because the calendar names nobody, this is one entry and not three: there is no name to give a child of hers, and none is invented here. No place, no persecutor and no year is printed on the page, and the epithet that heads it — the Martyrs — is all the rank it claims for them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/03/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/359/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
