# Anatolius of Raithu

saint.gr's calendar for 21 Φεβρουαρίου names two men in one line — «Όσιοι Ανδρέας και Ανατόλιος» — and the page standing under it says that the venerable fathers [Andrew](/saints/andrew-of-mytilene) and Anatolius lived and practised their asceticism in the Church of Jerusalem in the fifth century, and that they were among the first disciples of [Euthymius the Great](/saints/euthymius-the-great), whom it keeps on 20 January.

Of the two of them separately it gives one sentence: Andrew came from Mytilene and Anatolius from Raithu. They fell asleep in peace. There is no more than that — no years, no account of their labours, no relics, no couplet and no hymn. The page adds one thing of Anatolius alone, that his memory is kept again on 26 April.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 21 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/21/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3747/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
