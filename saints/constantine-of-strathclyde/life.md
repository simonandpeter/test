# Constantine of Strathclyde

saint.gr's calendar for 11 Μαρτίου names him «Άγιος Κωνσταντίνος ο βασιλεύς του Στραθκλάϊντ», Constantine the king of Strathclyde, and heads him «(570 - ;)». The page says he was born in the year 570 and was the son of the king Rhydderch Hael of Strath Clyde and of the queen Languoreth; that he was guided to Christianity by St Columba; and that he contended for the Orthodox faith in England and in Ireland. It says he fell asleep in peace about the year 640.

That is the whole of the page. It gives no account of his reign, no church of his, no relic and no hymn, and the rest of what it prints is a note on the name itself and on its imperial bearers. The Greek calendar keeps another royal Constantine of the British west two days earlier, on 9 March, the martyr king of Dumnonia in Cornwall, born about 520 and killed in 576; that is a second entry with a second set of parents and years, and the corpus keeps the two of them apart as the calendar does.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/11/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3821/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
