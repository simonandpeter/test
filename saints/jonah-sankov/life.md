# Jonah (Sankov)

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 21 июня ст. ст. prints him in the line «Прмч. Ионы (1938).», and the name link behind his place in it supplies the calendar’s own nominative with the surname, «Иона (Санков), иеромонах, прмч.» (name page 13730). That is where the name form in this folder is read from; the line gives the forename in the genitive and no surname at all.

His name page carries nothing else: «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 21 июня» and then no life, no monastery and no account of his death in 1938. Nothing is supplied for that here, and in particular the surname is not read as a biography. The line’s «Прмч.» makes him a venerable-martyr and the name link adds «иеромонах», a hieromonk; he is alone on his line.

The second day his name page lists is the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which is a synaxis and not a second feast, so he renders on 21 июня ст. ст. only. The day page prints six hymns — two for the martyr its first line heads, two for another martyr of the same name whom it does not list at all, and two for the finding of a hierarch’s relics — and none under his name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 21 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260621.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13730.html); read 9 October 2026.*
