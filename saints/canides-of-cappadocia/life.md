# Canides of Cappadocia

saint.gr says he lived in the years of the emperor Theodosius the Great, in the year 379, and that he was the son of devout parents, Theodotus and Theophane, who lived in Cappadocia. The page gives the tradition of his infancy as tradition: that his mother ate no fatty food during the nine months she carried him, and that while he was still a child he was fed with restraint. Nothing else is said of his family, and the page names no town.

While he was still small he had a leaning towards the monastic life, and at the age of seven he went away to a mountain, where he shut himself into a cave and there schooled himself with fasting and prayer. From the great damp of that cave, the page says, the hairs of his head and of his beard fell out. Having lived thus, in asceticism and holiness, for seventy-three years, he died in peace.

That is the whole of saint.gr's account. It prints above it the two verses of the synaxarion — «Γην και τα εν γη εκλιπών ο Kανίδης, / Άφθαρτον εύρεν αντιμισθίαν άνω» — that Canides, forsaking the earth and the things on the earth, found an incorruptible recompense above. The page prints no hymn for him.
