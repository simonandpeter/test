# Peter (Lebedev)

«Сщмч. Петра пресвитера (1937)» is the whole of him on the Russian calendar's 14 October: a rank, a forename, a grade and a year. The site's index of names adds the surname and says the grade again, heading his page «Петр (Лебедев), иерей, сщмч.» — a priest of the simple grade, put to death for the faith in the worst year of the Soviet campaign against the Church.

He has one day more than the two other priests the day names. His index entry keeps him here, on 26 January with the whole company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and on «7 июня (Иван.)», the synaxis of the saints of Ivanovo — the only thing on either page that puts him anywhere, and recorded here as a commemoration rather than as a diocese, because a synaxis is not a see.

Neither page gives a birth, a parish, a charge or a place of death, and neither carries a life. The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian saints are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026 even with a browser user-agent, so the line stands as the line. [Michael (Lektorsky)](/saints/michael-lektorsky) and [Maximilian (Marchenko)](/saints/maximilian-marchenko) are the other two.
