# Stephen (27 February)

saint.gr's calendar for 27 Φεβρουαρίου names him with no epithet at all — its line reads «Όσιος Στέφανος» — and the page standing under it gives one sentence: the venerable Stephen was chamberlain to the emperor Maurice; he lived as an ascetic and founded the old people's home of Armatios, where there was also a church of the Theotokos and of the Sagma; and he fell asleep in peace.

That is all of it. The page gives him no birthplace, no year, no relics and no hymn, and its couplet plays his name against his charity: Stephen, in old age the provider of a livelihood, whom the Provider of the crown crowns when he dies — «Γήρει Στέφανος πρύτανις ζωῆς πόρου, Ὃν περ θανόντα Πρύτανις στέφους στέφει». Because the calendar gives him nothing to tell him apart from the many Stephens it keeps, the day stands in this folder's name and the office is recorded in its own field.

The Serbian church keeps him on the same day, and the Ohrid Prologue of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović gives him three sentences which say what the Greek line says and add a year. He was first a court official under the emperor Maurice; then he left the court service and, driven by the love of Christ, raised a house of mercy for the aged at Constantinople. He ended peacefully in 614.
