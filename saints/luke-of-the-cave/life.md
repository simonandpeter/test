# Luke of the Cave

saint.gr's calendar for 6 Νοεμβρίου names Luke «ὁ ἐν Σπηλαίῳ», Luke in the Cave, and says plainly that it has nothing of his life: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ρώσου Οσίου» — we have no details of the Russian venerable one's life. So the whole of what is recorded here is the name, the epithet of the cave, the day, and that the page calls him a Russian saint.

There is no century on the page, no monastery named beyond the cave of the epithet, no year of birth or death, no couplet and no hymn. The second Luke the same day keeps, the Sicilian of Tauromenium, has a life of his own on another page and is another man; nothing on either page joins them.

days.pravoslavie.ru places the cave. saint.gr had kept him on 6 Νοεμβρίου as «Όσιος Λουκάς «ὁ ἐν Σπηλαίῳ»», Luke in the cave, with nothing else; this calendar's line is «Прп. Луки , эконома Печерского (XIII).» and its life page is three sentences long. He lived as a monk at the Kyiv Caves monastery in the thirteenth century and carried out the obedience of steward, «эконом». Nothing more of his life is known, the page says, and his relics lie in the Near Caves, the caves of Antony.

His `office` is therefore owed and is not written here: the calendar's own word for him is «эконом», the steward of the house, and the tool that applies an upgrade cannot set that field. It is named in this batch's report. The day page prints no hymn under his name.
