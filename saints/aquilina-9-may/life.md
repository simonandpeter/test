# Aquilina (9 May)

saint.gr's calendar names her and gives no life beyond one sentence for the two of them. She and [Callinike](/saints/callinike-9-may) were harlots, the page says, sent by the emperor Decius to entice the martyr [Christopher](/saints/christopher) and draw him into idolatry. Instead they believed in Christ through Christopher, and were martyred: they were put to death with spits driven through them from their feet to their shoulders.

That is the whole of it. The page names no city, no judge of their own and no burial place; it gives them no relics and prints neither apolytikion nor kontakion for them. The year 251 stands only in the head line over each of their names, and the body of the page gives no year at all — only the emperor who sent them. The couplet above says that Satan has set a new table again, and that his two deceivers spit women on skewers: «Έχει τράπεζαν Σατανάς πάλιν νέαν, / Γύναια σουβλίζουσιν οι πλάνοι δύω».

*After saint.gr's calendar for 9 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/09/index.aspx) and [the life of the pair](https://www.saint.gr/1497/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
