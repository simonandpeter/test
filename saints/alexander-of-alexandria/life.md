# Alexander of Alexandria

Doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 29 mai reads «Sfântul Ierarh Alexandru, Arhiepiscopul Alexandriei», the holy hierarch Alexander, archbishop of Alexandria, and the page the calendar links for him carries no Viață tab and no Tropar tab: it prints his name, the date, icons of him, links to articles, and the readings appointed for a hierarch — the epistle from Hebrews 7 and 8, such a high priest it became us to have, and the gospel from John 10, I am the door and the good shepherd.

The rank and the see are the whole of what this source states: an archbishop of Alexandria, kept on 29 May. No year and no place beyond the see is entered for him, because the page gives none, and nothing here is taken from any source other than the one cited below.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 29 mai — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/29-mai) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-ierarh-alexandru-arhiepiscopul-alexandriei); read 24 September 2026.*
