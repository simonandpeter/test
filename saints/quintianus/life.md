# Quintianus

The Greek calendar keeps two martyrs together on 29 Απριλίου, Quintianus and Atticus, and saint.gr's whole notice of them is a statement of where their commemoration comes from. They are unknown, it says, to the synaxaristes of Saint Nikodemos. They are mentioned in the synaxaristes of Delehaye in these words, «Άθλησις των αγίων μαρτύρων Κυντιανού και Αττικού» — the contest of the holy martyrs Quintianus and Atticus — at page 640, line 6. No other information about their lives is recorded.

So nothing is printed of either man: no century, no country, no persecutor, no manner of death and no kinship between them beyond the line that keeps them together. The page carries no couplet for them, no hymn and no relic, and its feast type is the plain fixed one, «Σταθερή. Εορτάζει στις 29 Απριλίου εκάστου έτους». This is a statement about thin attestation and not a doubt about the men, so the two are kept.

saint.gr's line names him with [Atticus](/saints/atticus-29-april), and that is the whole of what either of them has.
