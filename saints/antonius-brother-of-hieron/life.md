# Antonius, brother of Hieron

saint.gr's calendar for 7 Νοεμβρίου keeps Antonius with his brother Matronianus in one short entry, and the whole of the notice is this: they were brothers of the holy martyr Hieron — the first of the thirty-three martyrs «ἐν Μελιτινῇ», kept the same day — and they too were martyred by beheading in the city of Melitene. The page gives them no year, no trial and no burial.

What the page of [the thirty-three](/saints/thirty-three-martyrs-of-melitene) adds is the household: Hieron was of Tyana in Cappadocia; their father died early and their mother Stratonike brought up the three brothers alone; and when the persecution of Diocletian began, the eparch Agricolas arrested Hieron for preaching Christ to the labourers on Sundays and feast days, taking his two brothers with him and thirty more of his fellow-workers. The enumerated names of the thirty-three do not include the two brothers, which is why they stand in an entry of their own.

There is no distich for them and no hymn. The other brother kept with him is [Matronianus](/saints/matronianus-brother-of-hieron), and a different Antonius, the son of Melasippus, is kept the same day in another entry.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2933/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
