# Tatiana (5 January)

saint.gr's calendar names her and gives no life. Its whole notice is one sentence: the venerable Tatiana was a nun and an ascetic, and she died peacefully. The two-line verse the page prints above it says that Tatiana, who wasted away long ago with fasting, is now in the company of the angels, who are the friends of fasting. The page gives no year, no place and nothing further about her, and nothing is supplied here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/576/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
