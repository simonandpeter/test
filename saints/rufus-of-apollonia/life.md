# Rufus of Apollonia

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him in its sixth entry for 6 июля ст. ст., «Мчч. Исавра диакона, Иннокентия , Филикса , Ермия , Василия , Перегрина , Руфа и Руфина Македонских (III-IV).» — the martyrs Isaurus the deacon, Innocent, Felix, Hermias, Basil, Peregrinus, Rufus and Rufinus of Macedonia, of the third to fourth century. The reading behind the line is two sentences long and serves the whole company, and he is in its second sentence.

The first sentence puts the other six at the Macedonian city of Apollonia: [Isaurus](/saints/isaurus-the-deacon), [Innocent](/saints/innocent-of-apollonia), [Felix](/saints/felix-of-apollonia), [Hermias](/saints/hermias-companion-of-isaurus), [Basil](/saints/basil-companion-of-isaurus) and [Peregrinus](/saints/peregrinus-of-apollonia), Athenians who suffered for Christ «при императоре Нумериане (283-284)», under the emperor Numerian. The second is his own. «Вместе с ними были усечены уверовавшие во Христа два градоначальника - Руф и Руфин.» — together with them were put to the sword two city magistrates who had come to believe in Christ, he and [Rufinus](/saints/rufinus-of-apollonia).

That is the whole of it: a man in the city's magistracy who believed and was beheaded with the Athenians he had before him. Name page 1885 heads him «Руф Аполлониадский (Македонский), мч.», prints «День памяти: 6 июля» and carries no reading of its own at all, not even the company's. The day's own line is wider than the reading — it gives the third to fourth century where the reading names one reign inside it — and nothing is narrowed from the one by the other.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 6 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260706.html), [the company's reading](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4167.htm) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/1885.html); read 9 October 2026.*
