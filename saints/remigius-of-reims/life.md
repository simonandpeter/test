# Remigius of Reims

Remigius was born in the year 437 in the city of Laon in Gaul. Made bishop of the city of Reims, he sold his great estate and distributed the money to the poor and to various other good works. He gave himself with a divine zeal to the study of the divine word, to the fight against the heretics, and to missionary labour; his first care, saint.gr says, was the preaching of the truth of Jesus Christ to the heathen.

The great achievement of his episcopate was the baptism of Clovis I, king of the Gauls, on Christmas Day of the year 496, together with the king's two brothers and three thousand women and children. For the enormous scale of that missionary work he was called «Απόστολος της Γαλλίας», the Apostle of Gaul.

With the passing of time Remigius lost his sight, and he fell asleep, venerably, in peace in the year 533. The calendar prints no hymn for him.
