# Basil (Malinin) (28 March)

His line in the calendar is four words long, and it is the whole of what is recorded here. The Сретенский monastery’s calendar for 28 марта ст. ст. prints «Сщмч. Василия пресвитера (1938)» — the hieromartyr Basil, presbyter, 1938 — and the name page behind that forename gives the surname the line leaves out: «Василий (Малинин), протоиерей, сщмч.» The office recorded here is the plainer `Priest`, which is the word this site can render in all four of its languages.

Two men of this calendar carry that name, and the calendar itself keeps them apart. Its other name page of the surname heads a priest, not a protopriest, gives his days as the synaxis of the new martyrs and 10 сентября, and carries a life: a son of a family that had given priests to the Kalyazin district of Tver since the eighteenth century, born in 1898 and shot in 1937. This page heads a protopriest, gives his days as the synaxis and 28 марта, carries no life at all, and the day closes him with 1938. Two pages, two ranks, two days, two years. The day in the heading of this entry is what tells them apart for a reader.

So nothing is said of where he was born, where he served, when or by whom he was arrested, or where he died. There is no life page for him on the day and none on his name page, and 1938 is the only year the calendar gives.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 28 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260328.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13709.html); read 8 October 2026.*
