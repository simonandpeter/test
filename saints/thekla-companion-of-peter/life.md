# Thekla, companion of Peter

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαρτίου prints one line for a company of five and their companions: «Άγιοι Πέτρος, Μαρκιανός, Ιωάννης, Θέκλα, Κασσιανός οι Μάρτυρες και οι συν αυτών μαρτυρήσαντες» — the holy martyrs Peter, Marcian, John, Thekla and Cassian, and those who were martyred with them. She is the one woman the line names. The page behind it says two things and stops.

The first is where they suffered: «Οι Άγιοι αυτοί Μάρτυρες Μαρτύρησαν στην Ρώμη» — these holy martyrs were martyred at Rome. The second is that nothing further is known: «Δεν έχουμε περισσότερες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο και το μαρτύριο των Αγίων». No year, no emperor, no judge, no manner of death, no relic and no hymn; the source keeps her name, her sex and her company, and that is all.

One thing on the page has to be set aside. Under her name it prints «ΘΕΚΛΑ (από το Θεός + κλέος) = αυτή που έχει θεϊκή δόξα» and then «Κόρη του Αυτοκράτορα Θεόφιλου», daughter of the emperor Theophilus — an item from the site's notes on the name itself, like the philosophers listed under the men's names on the same page. It is not said of this martyr, who is dated nowhere near a ninth-century emperor, and none of it is recorded here. Her fellows are [Peter](/saints/peter-companion-of-marcian), [Marcian](/saints/marcian-companion-of-peter), [John](/saints/john-companion-of-peter) and [Cassian](/saints/cassian-companion-of-peter).

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3869/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
