# Felicissimus of Todi

Felicissimus, Heracles and Paulinus were martyred in the year 303, under the emperor Diocletian, whose reign saint.gr gives as 284 to 305, in the city of Todi in the district of Umbria in Italy. That one sentence is the whole of the page's account of the three of them, and the day's own line gives their three names and their rank and nothing else.

The page carries no birthplace for any of them, no rank in the Church and none in the army, no persecutor by name, no manner of death, no relics and no burial place; and it prints neither a synaxarion couplet nor a hymn. Each of the three has his own head line on it, each dated «(; - 303)», and beyond the three names, the year, the emperor and the city there is nothing further recorded here.
