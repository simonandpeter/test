# Papias

Papias was born in the fourth century, doxologia.ro's short notice says, in the days of the emperor Diocletian. He inherited the love of God from those before him and handed it on in his turn. He was denounced and seized to be tried by the worshippers of idols, and he refused to submit to them; instead he rebuked the governor to his face.

He was beaten with raw sinews and thrown into a cauldron of boiling oil, and the fire wrapped him about as though it were a garment, without harming him. He was tortured until he passed to eternal life, and by his suffering itself he drew many to faith in God.

Doxologia prints this much and no more. It names no city, no governor and no year of his death, and the page carries no troparion, so none is recorded here.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 28 iunie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/28-iunie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-papias-drumul-spre-sfintenie); read 24 September 2026.*
