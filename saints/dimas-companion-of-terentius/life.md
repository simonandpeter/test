# Dimas, companion of Terentius

Dimas is the last of the six names doxologia.ro's calendar prints for 10 April — «Sfinții Mucenici Terentie, Pompie, African, Maxim, Macarie și Dima» — and he is named again in the heading of the troparion the site gives the day, a hymn sung to the whole company. The life the calendar links does not name him. It tells of the martyrs of Africa under the emperor Decius and the governor Fortunatianus, of four who were set apart, and of thirty-six others among whom it names Zeno, Alexander and Theodore; Dimas is in neither list.

The company he is counted with refused to sacrifice when the instruments of torture were set out in front of the people, and forty of them held together, reminding one another not to fear those who kill the body. The governor had four of them — [Terentius](/saints/terentius-of-africa), Africanus, Maximus and Pompeius — shut in the innermost prison, and the rest beaten with heavy staves and dried sinews until they died. The four were chained over iron nails, fed by an angel who broke their chains, left unharmed by the serpents loosed on them, and at the last beheaded and buried two stadia outside the city.

This folder records that the Romanian calendar keeps Dimas on this day among those martyrs, and says no more than the calendar does. [Macarius](/saints/macarius-companion-of-terentius), the fifth name on the line, is in the same case.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 10 aprilie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/10-aprilie), [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfintilor-mucenici-terentie-pompie-african-maxim-macarie-dima) and [the troparion](https://doxologia.ro/troparul-sfintilor-mucenici-terentie-pompei-african-maxim-macarie-dima-al-celor-impreuna-cu-dansii); read 20 September 2026.*
