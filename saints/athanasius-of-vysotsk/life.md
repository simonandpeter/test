# Athanasius of Vysotsk

One name covers a master and his disciple here, and the calendar's own page carries both without saying so: the line prints the year 1395, which belongs to Athanasius the Younger, and links a life which is the story of his teacher, the founder. This entry keeps the one Serpukhov commemoration and tells that story plainly.

The founder - Andrew in the world, a priest's son from the far Novgorod lands - walked to the Trinity on Makovets to become a disciple of Sergius of Radonezh, and was tonsured Athanasius after the Great one of Alexandria. His beloved obedience was the copying of books, which the fathers set beside the holy icons as bearers of the Church's mind. In 1374, when Prince Vladimir the Brave of Serpukhov asked Sergius for a monastery in his lands, Sergius came with his favourite disciple, founded the monastery of the Conception of the Theotokos on the high bank of the Nara - "the monastery on the Height", Vysotsky - and blessed Athanasius to build and then govern it. Monks, he taught there, are voluntary martyrs: the martyrs suffered and finished in an hour, but a monk endures every day, from his own flesh and the enemies of the mind, to his last breath. It was to him that Sergius sent Nicon, his own future successor, for tonsure.

When Metropolitan Cyprian, driven out by the Grand Prince, was upheld by Sergius and Athanasius in his long canonical struggle, Athanasius went with him to Constantinople, bought himself a cell at the monastery of the Forerunner, and spent some twenty years there translating and copying - ten icons of the best Greek painting sent to his own Vysotsky, the "four hundred chapters" of Maximus the Confessor, Symeon the New Theologian, and at the very end of his life the church typikon known in Russia as the "Eye of the Church". He left the Vysotsky abbacy to his disciple Athanasius the Younger, who died in 1395 and whose year the calendar prints; the founder himself died in Constantinople in deep old age, in 1401 or after, remembered by the chronicles as an elder "virtuous, learned, knowing the Holy Scriptures".

*After the Православный церковный календарь (days.pravoslavie.ru), [Преподобный Афанасий Серпуховской](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1518.htm); read 30 August 2026.*
