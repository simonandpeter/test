# Theodotia of Rome

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names her and gives no life. Its line for 4 июля ст. ст. reads «Мчч. Феодота и Феодотии (108).» — the martyrs Theodotus and Theodotia, of the year 108 — and it carries a link to each of the two names and none to a reading. The name page behind hers, 2720, prints «Феодотия Римская, мц.», Theodotia of Rome, martyr, and one line beneath it, «День памяти: 4 июля», and nothing at all besides.

So the calendar sets down a woman martyred under the name of Rome in the year 108, and names her in one breath with [Theodotus (4 July)](/saints/theodotus-martyr-4-july), whose own name page gives him the same epithet and the same single day. The two are a pair in the line and nothing further is said of either. The day page prints twenty hymns under other names and none under hers, and azbyka.ru, which might carry an account of the pair, was not read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 4 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260704.html) and [her name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/2720.html), which name her and give no life; read 9 October 2026.*
