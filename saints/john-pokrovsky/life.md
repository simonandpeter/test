# John (Pokrovsky)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 13 февраля ст. ст. inside a single line that names fifteen sufferers of 1938 together — ten presbyters, a deacon, a layman and three nuns — and he stands in it as the fourth of the ten presbyters, as «Иоанна». The line's rank word over him is «Сщмч.», hieromartyr.

The calendar prints no life page under this name and no hymn under it either: the one life the line carries belongs to the archpriest at its head, and the troparia the day page prints are for other saints it heads. Nothing further is written here. The line, the rank word, the name the link behind it carries and the year 1938 are the whole of what was read, and in particular this is not identified with any saint of the same name whom the calendar keeps on another day.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 13 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260213.html); read 3 October 2026.*
