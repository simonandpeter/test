# Maximinus of Trier

saint.gr says Maximinus was born in the village of Silly, near the city of Poitiers in France, and came of a noble family. In the year 332 he became bishop of Trier, and there he showed himself an enemy of Arianism. He received and cared for [Athanasius of Alexandria](/saints/athanasius-of-alexandria) and for Paul, patriarch of Constantinople, when they came to him.

He took part in the Council of Sardica in the year 343, together with Pope Julius the First and Hosius of Córdoba — [Hosius](/saints/hosius-of-cordoba), whom the Greek page calls «τον Κορδούης Όσιο» — and there upheld Orthodoxy with zeal. He must have resigned the episcopal throne, the page says, since by 347 he had been succeeded by Paulinus.

Maximinus fell asleep in peace in the year 352, and [Jerome](/saints/jerome-of-stridon) describes him as one of the courageous bishops of his time. That is the whole of what the page prints of him; it records no wonder, and it prints no hymn.
