# Galaction, disciple of Martinian of White Lake

saint.gr's calendar for 12 Ιανουαρίου names «Όσιος Γαλακτίων ο διά Χριστόν σαλός», the venerable Galaction, fool for Christ's sake, and gives him one sentence: he was the disciple and obedient son of the venerable [Martinian](/saints/martinian-of-white-lake), whom the same day keeps and whose death the page marks at 1483, and he himself fell asleep in peace in the year 1506, in Russia.

That is all the page holds of him. It names no monastery of his own, tells none of the acts by which he was reckoned a fool for Christ, and prints no hymn. Beside the line it glosses his name from γάλα, milk: the tender one, good and white in soul and body as milk is.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1401/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
