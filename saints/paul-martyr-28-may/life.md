# Paul (28 May)

saint.gr's calendar for 28 Μαΐου names three martyrs on one line and on one page: [Crescens](/saints/crescens-martyr-28-may), [Dioscorides](/saints/dioscorides) and this man. Each of the three is given a head line of his own, and each of the three is dated the same way, «(; - 244)», with no year of birth. Beyond the names and that year the page keeps nothing of them but a couplet.

The page's whole account of the three is the couplet the synaxaria carry for them, which sets them beside the three youths in the Babylonian furnace: «Ἐν τῇ καμίνῳ Σεδράχ Διοσκορίδης, / Μισὰχ δὲ Παῦλος, Αὐδεναγὼ δὲ Κρήσκης» — in the furnace Dioscorides is Shadrach, Paul is Meshach, and Crescens is Abednego. It prints no prose life, no place, no judge and no hymn, and the only year it gives is in the three head lines, «(; - 244)», one for each man.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/28/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1762/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
