# Alexis (Drozdov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and gives no life. Its line for 1 июля ст. ст. reads «Сщмч. Алексия диакона (1942).» — the hieromartyr Alexis, deacon, of the year 1942 — and the line carries a link to his name and none to a reading. The name page behind it adds his family name, Дроздов, and raises his grade to «протодиакон», protodeacon, where the day line says deacon. Beyond the title it prints one line, «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 1 июля», and nothing else at all.

So the calendar sets down a protodeacon of the Russian church killed in 1942, and no more than that. The first of those two days is not a feast of his own: it is the general commemoration of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which this calendar prints beside the proper day of each of them, and because both his pages call him a martyr that commemoration is the ground on which the rank of new martyr stands in this folder.

Nothing else about him was found. The day page carries four hymns under two headings and none under his name, and azbyka.ru, which is where an account of a deacon of 1942 would be, was not read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 1 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260701.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14283.html), which name him and give no life; read 9 October 2026.*
