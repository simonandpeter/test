# Isaac I of Optina

Isaac — Ivan Ivanovich Antimonov in the world — was born at Kursk on 31 May 1810 into a patriarchal merchant family respected by the townspeople for its unimpeachable honesty and strict Christian way of life, its mercy to the poor and its adornment of the town church. The future great elder of Optina grew up in an atmosphere of love, obedience to his parents, firm keeping of the church’s rule and moral strictness: a modest, kind boy, quiet and reserved but not gloomy, not without a joke, witty and simple in company. The thought of the monastery ripened in him over long years while he had to help his father in trade — the Sretensky calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. He died in 1894.
