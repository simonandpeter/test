# Sophia (18 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 18 December keeps Saint Sophia the wonderworker, and says of her only that it has nothing of her life: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο της Αγίας» — we have no details of the life of the saint. That one sentence is the whole of the entry, and the by-name is the only thing in it besides the name and the day.

What the page carries besides is an epigram: Sophia loved the wisdom of God, and from there she received the grace of wonders as well. It gives her no year of birth and none of death, no homeland, no manner of death, no relic, and it prints no hymn for her.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3294/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
