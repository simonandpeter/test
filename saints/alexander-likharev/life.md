# Alexander (Likharev)

The Сретенский monastery's calendar gives him one line for the Julian 4 March, «Сщмч. Александра пресвитера (1938).», grades him сщмч., and prints no life under his name; the surname is on the name link behind the line and on azbyka.ru's line for the same day. Alexander Likharev was born on 22 January 1876 in the village of Синьково, in the Софьинская волость of the Бронницкий district of the province of Moscow, the son of a priest, Пётр Лихарев.

He finished the Moscow theological seminary in 1896 and went as a teacher into the parish school at Иславское in the Zvenigorod district. On 5 May 1899 he was ordained deacon to the Тихвинская на Бережках church in Moscow, and on 26 June 1920 priest to the same church — **twenty-one years a deacon in one church, and then its priest, and from 24 August 1928 its rector**. On 22 July 1930 he was moved to the Богоявленский cathedral in Дорогомилово and on 25 March 1932 raised to archpriest.

Then the moves came quickly, as they did for a great many priests in those years: on 23 November 1932 to the Богородице-Владимировская church in Мытищи, and on 22 October 1935 to the Николаевская church in the settlement of Подсолнечное in the Солнечногорск district. He was arrested there on 27 November 1937, shut in the Taganka prison in Moscow, and questioned. The investigator told him the investigation had evidence that he had carried on counter-revolutionary activity among the population, and he answered that he did not admit himself guilty of counter-revolutionary activity.

**What he was accused of was a remark about the constitution, and it is a witness who reports it.** The investigator questioned witnesses, and one of them, a priest, testified that Alexander had said to him that the Soviet power had issued the Stalin constitution and that one would think there should be complete freedom, and instead the opposite comes out, and in the matter of religion above all: priests are driven from district to district and nowhere registered, one of them had been sent to a fifteenth place, and what comes out of it is Bolshevik empty talk and not a constitution. **His own words in the file are that denial and nothing else**; the charge is a reported conversation.

On 3 December 1937 the investigation was closed, and on the fifth the NKVD troika sentenced him to ten years in a corrective labour camp. On 11 December it was announced to all the men under investigation — many of whom, after so short an investigation, had been expecting release or at worst exile — that they had been sentenced to ten years of hard labour, and they were loaded into wagons and sent to the Krasnoyarsk region. On 9 January 1938 they reached the station of Суслово, and some of the priests, he among them, were sent to work on a pig farm of the Бамлаг of the NKVD.

He had fallen ill on the journey; it seemed a simple cold at first, but when the convoy reached its final point the conditions were so hard that a body weakened on the road could not deal with the illness any longer. All the prisoners were put in temporary dugouts. The Siberian frosts stood, it was cold, and there was not firewood enough even to boil water, and an epidemic of dysentery began in the camp. There were not places enough in the hospital and many of the sick died in the dugouts.

He took the dysentery too, and the imprisoned priests who shared his dugout set about petitioning to have him put in the hospital. **Archpriest Alexander Likharev died in the camp hospital on 17 March 1938**, which is the Julian 4 March on which the calendar keeps him, so his day of death and his feast are one day. A priest imprisoned with him wrote to his children: «Похоронен он... в братской могиле, близ мельницы... ушел родитель ваш в другой мир, но никого не оскорбил он ни на службе ранее, ни в заключении...» — buried in a common grave, near the mill; your parent has gone into another world, but he offended nobody, neither in his service before nor in prison.

**One thing in the life does not sit with the rest of it, and it is the source's own.** The page sends the convoy to the Krasnoyarsk region and names the camp administration Бамлаг, while listing him among the saints of Kuzbass — and Суслово is the station where the corpus's `michael-stroev`, kept on the Julian 3 March, died in a camp of Сиблаг a day earlier in the same month of the same year. The two lives are by the same hand and the same host and name the place the same way and its administration differently. Nothing read here resolves it, and no relation between the two men is recorded. The moveable days azbyka gives him, and which this corpus cannot hold, are the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church on 7 февраля, the synaxis of the saints of Kuzbass on 29 августа and the synaxis of the saints of Moscow on 5 сентября.

The life azbyka serves is credited to Игумен Дамаскин (Орловский), «Жития новомучеников и исповедников Российских ХХ века. Март» (Тверь, 2006), pages 49 to 51, by way of fond.ru. It records no rehabilitation, no date of glorification and no relic, and names no companion in the case; the one other man in it, the priest who testified, is not named.
