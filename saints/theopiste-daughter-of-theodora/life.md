# Theopiste, daughter of Theodora of Thessalonica

saint.gr keeps Theopiste with her mother on one page and under one heading, «Οσίες Θεοδώρα εξ Αιγίνης και Θεοπίστη η θυγατέρα της», and what it prints of her is that she was [Theodora](/saints/theodora-of-thessalonica)'s daughter and that she was with her in the monastery. Theodora, it says, was born on Aegina; after her marriage she came to Thessalonica because of the raids of the Saracens; and when her husband died she embraced the monastic life and entered the Monastery of Saint Stephen, taking her daughter Theopiste with her.

There the mother lived with much virtue, the page says, and fell asleep in 892. That year is the mother's, and the page gives the daughter none, so none is recorded here for her. The site glosses her name as it glosses her mother's, out of the word and not out of a life: Θεόπιστος, the one who is faithful to God. It prints a single distich, and the distich names Theodora alone.

The page closes with the reason the two women are not found on their own day. They are commemorated on 29 August, it says, but their feast was transferred to 3 August because of the feast of the Beheading of the Honourable Head of Saint John the Forerunner. That is the whole of what the Greek calendar prints about Theopiste: a name, a mother, a convent in Thessalonica, and a day that gives way to a greater one.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/03/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2207/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
