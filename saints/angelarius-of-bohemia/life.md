# Angelarius of Bohemia

The venerable Angelarius was a fellow-worker and helper of the holy equals-to-the-apostles [Cyril](/saints/cyril-the-philosopher) and [Methodius](/saints/methodius-of-moravia), saint.gr says, and he lived and worked in the ninth century. The day's own line calls him Archbishop and enlightener of Bohemia. He came to Bulgaria, where he fell asleep in holiness after countless persecutions and ill usages at the hands of the Frankish clergy and the German soldiers.

The page adds one thing more, and it is a bibliographical note rather than a life: in the year 1742 there was published at Moschopolis in Macedonia a «Service of the seven-numbered saints, made by Gregory of Moschopolis, hieromonk», in which it is written that the memory of the holy equals-to-the-apostles Cyril and Methodius and of those with them is kept on the seventeenth of July.

That is everything. The page gives no year of birth and none of death, no birthplace, no account of his consecration or of his see, no writings, no relics and no burial place, and it prints neither apolytikion nor kontakion for him. It does not name the seven of that service's title either.
