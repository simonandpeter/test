# Niphon, Patriarch of Constantinople

A Greek by birth, Niphon became a monk in his youth and lived the ascetic life first outside the Holy Mountain and then on Athos itself, in several monasteries, longest at Vatopedi and Dionysiou, where he was loved by all for a rare wisdom and an unusual meekness.

Against his will he was made bishop of Thessalonica. After two years he went on church business to Constantinople, and there he was elected to the vacant patriarchal throne. The sultan banished him to Adrianople, where he lived as an exile until the Wallachian prince Radu obtained his release and made him archbishop of Wallachia. When Radu transgressed, Niphon left Wallachia for Athos and the monastery of Dionysiou, and there he lived out his days: the Prologue says he reached his ninetieth year, and gives 1460 as the year he passed into the kingdom of God. He composed the prayer of absolution that is read at the funeral service.
