# Alexander, martyred at Corinth

saint.gr's calendar for 24 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιος Αλέξανδρος» and gives him one sentence: «Ο Άγιος Αλέξανδρος μαρτύρησε στην Κόρινθο το 360 μ.Χ. Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου» — he was martyred at Corinth in 360, and we have no other details of the life of the saint. The city and the year are the whole of what it places him by.

The couplet set over the line says only what kind of death it was: Alexander contended in a godly mind, showing plainly how one ought to love Christ. The page gives him no country of origin, no emperor, no manner of death, no companions, no relics and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/24/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2991/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
