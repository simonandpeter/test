# The Ten Thousand Martyrs of Antioch

saint.gr's calendar for 1 Ιουνίου names them by their number and gives two sentences. The holy Ten Thousand Martyrs, it says, were martyred at Antioch under the emperor Decius. Not one of them is named, and the page tells nothing of how they died, of who condemned them, or of what year it was.

The second sentence is about the sources rather than the martyrs, and it is worth keeping. Their memory, saint.gr says, is not mentioned in the synaxarion of Saint Nikodemos, but it is written in Delehaye's and in Codex 53 of the Monastery of Vlatades. So the company stands on those two witnesses and not on the one a Greek reader would reach for first, and the page says so plainly rather than passing it over.

Because the calendar names no member of the company, this is one folder for the ten thousand and not one folder each. No year is entered for them here: the page names an emperor and not a date, and the number is the source's and not a count anybody made.
