# Suquias, with nineteen martyrs

saint.gr gives him one sentence. The holy martyr Suquias and the Christians who were martyred together with him, it says, contended in the second century in Georgia. The day's line numbers his companions — «Άγιος Σούκιος ο Μάρτυρας και οι συν αυτώ δέκα εννέα Μάρτυρες», Suquias the martyr and the nineteen martyrs with him — and the page's own heading sets them down as the holy nineteen martyrs; but it names not one of them, so the nineteen have no folder of their own here.

The page says nothing else: no city or district in Georgia, no persecutor, no manner of death, and no year within the century it gives. It prints no couplet and no hymn, and the only other thing on it is a picture of the saint with the company.
