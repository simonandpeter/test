# Sergius (Florinsky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him third among the hieromartyrs of its third line for 17 декабря and gives no life. The line is «Сщмчч. Александра и Николая и Сергия пресвитеров (1918).» — the hieromartyr presbyters Alexander and Nicholas and Sergius, 1918 — and the calendar’s own name page adds the surname and the grade the line leaves out: «Сергий (Флоринский), протоиерей, сщмч.», an archpriest where the line says presbyter.

That is the whole of the record. He was an archpriest; he died in 1918, the first year of the persecution the Russian church counts its new martyrs from; and the calendar keeps him on this day beside [Alexander (Savelov)](/saints/alexander-savelov) and [Nicholas (Beptyukov)](/saints/nicholas-beptyukov), the two its line joins to him. No life page stands behind the line for any of the three, so nothing is said here of where he served or how he died, because the source does not say it. The day page’s three hymns all belong to the prophet of its first line and to the three youths with him.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 17 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261217.html); read 13 October 2026.*
