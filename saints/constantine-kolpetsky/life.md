# Constantine (Kolpetsky)

Of some of the new martyrs, the life the calendar links says, almost nothing has survived - the place they served, the time of arrest, a short interrogation record, and the document confirming the sentence was carried out. The priest Constantine Kolpetsky is one of these. He was born on 15 May 1885 in the village of Frolovskoye in the Tver province, a priest's son; he studied at the Tver seminary and was ordained - to which church, the life admits, is not known. He was imprisoned twice over the taxes levied on clergy, six months in 1931 and a year in 1935, and came back from the second sentence to serve at Sukromlya.

He was arrested on 5 August 1937, before Stalin's order had even reached the provincial administrations. In the village soviet where the arrested were first gathered he paced the room, sensing he would never be free again, and the guard beat him for refusing to sit. The one interrogation was two questions long, and he denied the agitation twice. On 20 September the troika sentenced him, and he was shot on 23 September 1937. He was glorified among the New Martyrs and Confessors of Russia in 2000.

*After the Православный церковный календарь (days.pravoslavie.ru), [Священномученик Константин (Колпецкий)](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4840.htm); read 30 August 2026.*
