# Photas

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives him one sentence: «Ο Όσιος Φώτας απεβίωσε ειρηνικά» — the venerable Photas died peacefully. That is the whole of the life. The page adds no century, no country, no monastery and no relics, and it prints no hymn; the only thing it says of what he was is the rank word «Όσιος» in the day's line.

What it does print above the sentence is the couplet the synaxaria carry for him, and the couplet plays on his name: «Τῶν ἀρετῶν τὰ φῶτα Φώταν τὸν μέγαν, / καὶ νεκρὸν αὐγάζοντα πᾶσι μηνύει» — the lights of the virtues show great Photas shining upon all men even in death.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/486/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
