# John the Faster, of the Cave

saint.gr's calendar for 7 December names the venerable John the Faster, «ὁ ἐν τῷ Σπηλαίῳ» — he who is in the Cave — and then says in one sentence that it has no details of the Russian venerable's life. That is the whole of the page: no century, no monastery, no abbot, no manner of death, no relic, no hymn, and none of the synaxaristic couplets the site sets over most of its entries.

What the entry says is carried in the line itself, and it is set down here as the line has it: a Russian ascetic whom the Greek calendar calls the Faster and places in the Cave. The Greek calendar also keeps a John the Faster who was patriarch of Constantinople, on 2 September, whom the corpus already holds; that is another entry and another man, and nothing here is claimed from it.

The Russian calendar supplies the cave the Greek one only gestured at, and in one sentence it is the whole of what that calendar knows: he struggled in the monastery of the Caves at Kyiv in the twelfth century, and he is buried in the Near Caves. His life page gives him a second day besides this one, the September commemoration of the venerable fathers who lie in those same Near Caves, and it prints no hymn for him; the day page's nine hymns stand under other names.
