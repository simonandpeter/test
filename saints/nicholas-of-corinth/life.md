# Nicholas of Corinth

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. His own page on the site carries his name and the day — 14 Februarie — and after that only headings that lead elsewhere: holy relics, places of pilgrimage, monasteries and churches, articles about the saint, photographs. There is no Viață tab and no troparion, no year, no emperor or judge, and nothing at all of how or when or where he suffered.

What the calendar's line says is all there is: «Sfântul Mucenic Nicolae din Corint» — the holy martyr Nicholas of Corinth. Under the heading for holy relics the page links an article titled «Odoarele Mănăstirii Mega Spileo», the treasures of the monastery of Mega Spilaion, which is a pointer to another page and not an account of him. Anything further about this martyr would have to come from a source that this folder has not read.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and gives him a life. saint.gr has him of the village of Psari of Corinthia, his parents John and Kali, pious and God-loving people; left fatherless at twelve he went away to Selymbria, where he later married and had children, and from there to Constantinople, where he kept a pedlar's grocery. Fellow tradesmen accused him of having insulted the religion of Mohammed, and he was arrested under the sultan Suleiman I and brought before the judge. Nicholas confessed his faith in Christ and reproved the religion of the Turks as false, and gave way neither to the torments nor to the flatteries; so they threw him first into the fire and then beheaded him, in the year 1554. His head is kept at the monastery of the Great Meteoron, and a service for him was written by the hieromonk Damaskenos, afterwards bishop of Lite and Rendina, and later published by Ezekiel, metropolitan of Thessaliotis and Fanariofersala.
