# Paphnutius the Recluse of the Kyiv Caves

saint.gr names him and gives him one sentence, and the life says exactly that: «Ο Όσιος Παφνούτιος έζησε στην Λαύρα των Σπηλαίων και είχε το χάρισμα των δακρύων» — the venerable Paphnutius lived in the Lavra of the Caves and had the gift of tears. The day's line adds the one further word the site gives him, ὁ Ἔγκλειστος, the recluse.

That is the whole of it. The page gives no year of birth or of repose, no place he came from, no abbot he lived under, no relic, and no hymn; it does not even say which of the caves at Kyiv he was shut up in. Nothing more is written here than the site prints.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 15 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/15/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2628/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
