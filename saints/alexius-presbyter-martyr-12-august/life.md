# Alexius the Presbyter, Hieromartyr

Alexius Lopatin was born in 1868 in the settlement of Kukarka in Vyatka province and finished the Vyatka seminary. From 1890 to October 1916 he served at the Transfiguration church of the village of Verkhovino — dean of his district from 1906, teacher of religion in the local schools, a deputy to the diocesan congresses and a member of the diocese’s missionary and temperance societies; in October 1916 he was made rector of the Transfiguration cathedral of Slobodskoy and archpriest, and in February 1917 dean of the town’s churches.

In January 1918 Bishop Nikandr sent him the Church Council’s appeal to the Orthodox people on the decree on freedom of conscience, to be read in the churches of Slobodskoy; on 30 January he called the clergy together, spoke against the persecutions that had begun and asked them to consider how to defend the Church, and the firmness of the town’s clergy owed much to his. On Sunday 21 April 1918, preaching in the cathedral on the life of Mary of Egypt, he compared the Russian people’s life to her first years — we have left the precepts of our forebears and ceased to keep the Christian duties. That day the Red Guards arrested him twice; freed at the parishioners’ petition, he was seized again at midnight and taken to the Vyatka house of correction, where he denied every charge with courage; at the bishop’s petition he was released for Pascha. On 23 May his flat was searched and he was arrested again; the Urals Cheka sentenced him to death, and he was shot in Vyatka on 25 August 1918. He was rehabilitated in 1996, and on 25 July 2024 the Holy Synod entered his name in the Synaxis of the New Martyrs and Confessors, with his memory on 12/25 August. The Sretensky calendar prints no life for him and no year; this one follows azbyka.ru.
