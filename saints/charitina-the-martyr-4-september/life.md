# Charitina (4 September)

Because her memory is carried with those of Petronius, of Hermione and of Eutychis, the daughters of the apostle Philip, the Greek synaxarion supposes that Charitina too may have been martyred at Trajan’s passing through Ephesus on his way to the Persian war; or she may be the Charitina of 5 October, who has the same verse — what she had asked in prayer, release from the flesh, she received through prayer.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 4 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2345/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
