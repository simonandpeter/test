# Gerontius the Canonarch

saint.gr's whole notice for him is three short sentences. The venerable Gerontius lived in the fourteenth century and was an ascetic in the Lavra of the Caves of Kyiv, where the obedience of canonarch had been laid upon him — the brother who announces the tones and leads the chanting of the appointed verses. He was distinguished, the page says, for his ascetic life and for his obedience; and he fell asleep in peace and was buried in the Lavra of Kyiv.

That is the whole of it: no birthplace, no teacher, no abbot, no year, no wonder, no relic beyond the burial, and no hymn. This corpus has no rank in its list for a canonarch, so the one office the page does give him is kept in his name and in this paragraph instead.

The same Greek day also keeps a martyr named Gerontius, killed by the sword with a companion, whom this corpus held already from the Romanian calendar. He is not this man: two entries on one day of one calendar are two commemorations, and nothing about a Kyivan monk who died in peace in the fourteenth century belongs to a martyr with no date at all.
