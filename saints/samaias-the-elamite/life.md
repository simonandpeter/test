# Samaias the Elamite

saint.gr's whole notice for him is two sentences of scripture. He was one of the prophets whose book is lost, it says, as we learn from the second book of Chronicles: «και λόγοι Ροβοάμ οί πρώτοι ουκ ιδού γεγραμμένοι εν τοις λόγοις Σαμέου του προφήτου;» — are not the first acts of Rehoboam written in the words of Samaias the prophet? He is also the one who stopped Rehoboam, Solomon's son, from making war on the twelve tribes, and so protected them; the page cites the twelfth chapter of the third book of Kings.

The page's two-line verse says that Samaias no longer prophesies what is to come on earth, because that prophetic tripod of his is now above. There is no year, no place beyond the epithet «ο Ελαμίτης» that the day's line gives him, and no hymn.
