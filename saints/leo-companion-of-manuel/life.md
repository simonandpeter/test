# Leo, companion of Manuel of Adrianople

In the year 815, under Leo the Armenian the iconoclast, the Bulgarians came down to make war on Byzantium. Their leader was Krum, and they took Adrianople; for three days, saint.gr says, the bloodthirsty slaughtered the Christians, and after Krum's death his successors Doukoumos, and after him Ditzengos, showed the same beastly behaviour to the unhappy Christians. Ditzengos was succeeded in power by Mourtagon — Omurtag.

Leo is named in the day's list among the company taken with [Manuel](/saints/manuel-of-adrianople), Metropolitan of Adrianople, without a rank; the life calls him one of the two generals — «τους στρατηγούς Λέοντα και Ιωάννη» — whom Mourtagon beheaded with his own hands, the other being [John](/saints/john-companion-of-manuel).

He is not the [Leo](/saints/leo-of-nicaea) of the same list who was bishop of Nicaea and whose belly Mourtagon ripped open with a sword; the day names the two separately and the life gives them different offices and different deaths. No year, no city and no hymn is printed for either.

The Russian calendar names him in the company's one line on the same menologion day as Леонт, a second form of the name, which is how it keeps him apart from the bishop Леонтий earlier in the same line; the Greek day distinguishes the two the same way, by rank and by place in the list. It calls him of Adrianople and prints no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 22 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/22/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1148/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 22 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260122.html), read 3 October 2026.*
