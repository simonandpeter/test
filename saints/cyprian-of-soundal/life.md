# Cyprian of Soundal

The Greek synaxarion keeps Cyprian «του Σουντάλ» on 2 October and says it has no details of the life of this Russian saint, only that he lived as a fool for Christ. It gives no date and no more. Whether he is a saint the Russian calendar keeps is for its own 2 October old style, civil 15 October, to say; that page has not been read.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 2 Οκτωβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2573/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
