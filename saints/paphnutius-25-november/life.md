# Paphnutius (25 November)

The Ohrid Prologue gives him four sentences under the Julian 25 November, and they are the whole of this folder. He never drank wine. Once he was caught by brigands, and their chieftain forced him to drink a cup of it. Seeing the good nature with which he took it, the chieftain repented and gave up his trade of robbery altogether.

That is all of it, and nothing has been added here. The book gives him no year of birth and no year of death, no century, no country, no monastery and no office; it does not say where he was caught, how he came to be on that road, or what became of the chieftain after he left off robbing. The rank it heads him with is Преподобни, venerable, and that one word is the whole of what this folder takes from the heading.

He is not the one-eyed confessor bishop of the Thebaid whom this same book keeps on the Julian 11 September, the man who lost an eye and had his leg broken by heretics and who spoke at the First Ecumenical Council against forbidding marriage to priests living in the world; and he is not any of the eight other bearers of the name this corpus holds, none of whose dossiers has wine or brigands in it. He is the Serbian calendar’s alone, and it is the only one of the four calendars this corpus reads that was read for him.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 25. новембар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2025-12-08&prolog=1); read 8 October 2026.*
