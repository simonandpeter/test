# Theodosia of Caesarea in Palestine

saint.gr's calendar for 25 Μαρτίου names the holy martyrs Pelagia and Theodosia together on one line, and the page behind that line carries one sentence of narrative and nothing else: «Οι Αγίες Μάρτυρες Πελαγία και Θεοδοσία, μαρτύρησαν στην Καισάρεια της Παλαιστίνης το έτος 361 μ.Χ. και τελειώθηκαν διά ξίφους» — the holy martyrs Pelagia and Theodosia were martyred at Caesarea in Palestine in the year 361, and were perfected by the sword. That is the whole of what the source says about her.

The page prints the menaion's couplet for the pair above that sentence, and it puts her name first: «Θεοδοσία και Πελαγία ξίφει, Πέλαγος εύρον δωρεών Θεού Λόγου» — Theodosia and Pelagia, by the sword, found an ocean of the gifts of God the Word. Of her name it says only what it says of the masculine form, «ΘΕΟΔΟΣΙΟΣ: Ο θεόδοτος, το θεϊκό δώρο» — the God-given, the divine gift.

She is headed «(; - 361)», with no year of birth. The page gives no city of birth, no account of the arrest or the trial, no emperor, no relic and no hymn, and it does not say which of the two suffered first. Her fellow martyr [Pelagia](/saints/pelagia-of-caesarea-in-palestine) shares the calendar line, the couplet and the single page with her.

The Ohrid Prologue keeps her on the same menologion day and heads three holy women together where the Greek calendar heads two. Of these two it says that after imprisonment and torments they were beheaded together with the sword; of the third, a servant, that she suffered alone and came to her end in the city of Nicomedia. The entry closes by saying that God transplanted three white roses, watered with a martyr's blood, into his heavenly garden.

It gives no year, no reign, no persecutor and no city for these two, so the year this folder already holds from the Greek page stands as it was.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 25 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/25/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/95/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026; and the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 25. март ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-04-07&prolog=1), read 4 October 2026.*
