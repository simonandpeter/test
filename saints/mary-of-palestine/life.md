# Mary of Palestine

Mary of Palestine was first a reader of the Psalter at the church of the Resurrection in Jerusalem, the Serbian church's Ohrid Prologue says, and that is where the little it tells of her begins. She was fair of face, and many who looked at her were scandalised into sinful thoughts; so that she should not be the occasion of other men's sin, she went away into the desert of Souka, taking with her one basket of beans and one jug of water.

Eighteen years she lived there, and by the power of God neither the beans nor the water ran out. The disciples of [Cyriacus the Anchorite](/saints/cyriacus-the-anchorite), whom the Prologue keeps first on this same day and who ended his own course in the monastery of Chariton nearby, found her still alive, and afterwards buried her.

That is the whole of what the Prologue prints, and it gives no year, so none is given here. No other of the four calendars keeps her on this day.

*After the Serbian church's [Ohrid Prologue for the Julian 29 September](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-10-12&prolog=1); read 4 October 2026.*
