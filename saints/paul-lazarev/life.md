# Paul (Lazarev)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and gives no life. Its line for 20 мая ст. ст. reads «Сщмч. Павла пресвитера (1919).» — the hieromartyr Paul, presbyter, of the year 1919 — and the line carries a link to his name and none to a reading. The name page behind it prints his family name, Лазарев, and writes his grade out as «иерей», priest, beside the abbreviation «сщмч.», hieromartyr. Beyond the title it carries one line, «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 20 мая», and nothing further.

So the calendar sets down a priest of the Russian church killed in 1919 and no more than that. The second of those two days is not a feast of his own: it is the general commemoration of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which this calendar prints beside the proper day of each of them, and it is the one mark on his page that places him among that company rather than among the martyrs of an older persecution. He is kept here on the day the calendar gives him for his own, and the synaxis is recorded on his attestation.

Nothing else about him was found. No folder in this corpus held the name before, and the one other source that would be likely to carry an account of a parish priest of 1919 — azbyka.ru — was not read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 20 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260520.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/15016.html), which name him and give no life; read 8 October 2026.*
