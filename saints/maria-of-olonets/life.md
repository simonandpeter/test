# Maria of Olonets

saint.gr says the venerable Maria was born in the nineteenth century in the village of Perentino in the province of Staraya of Novgorod, of devout and God-loving parents. Her father was called Basil Sophronov and was granted four more children besides her, two sons and two daughters. From a small age Maria was taught the Horologion and the Psalter; on winter evenings she would sit and read the Lives of the Saints, and at night she would get up secretly and go almost without a sound before the holy icons and make prostrations.

The years passed and she would not decide to make a family of her own. She visited regularly the monasteries and the ascetics who lived in the forests beyond the river Lovat, and one of those holy elders advised her to walk north, to the lake Veiz, where her uncle the hermit father Isaiah lived, who would guide her on the road of salvation. So she set out for Olonets to take father Isaiah's blessing, and there she stayed, to live as an ascetic, inside a hut.

After three years of ascesis and prayer new temptations came. She was forced to leave her hut and to wander in the forests and the wilderness, and so, after many hardships, she came on pilgrimage to Stavropol of the Caucasus, looking for a quiet refuge in a ravine. After much ascesis and prayer the time came to give up her holy soul to the Lord whom she had loved so much; and her soul was parted from her body in the year 1860. The page gives her no day of death of its own, no relics, no couplet and no hymn.
