# Lupus of Troyes

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Lupus among the hierarchs on 29 July and gives no life. Its line is «Свт. Лупа , епископа Труа (479).» — the hierarch Lupus, bishop of Troyes, 479 — and the calendar's own name page for him carries nothing but the same words over again, printing the see twice and the rank three times: «Святитель Луп епископ Труа, епископ Труа, свт.»

This corpus had already written him down once, without a folder. The life of [Palladius of Scotland](/saints/palladius-of-scotland) says that in 429 pope Celestine, at Palladius's urging, sent two bishops to the British Isles against the Pelagian preaching of Agricola, and that they made the heresy disappear by steady mission; Lupus of Troyes was one of the two. That sentence is a Greek source's and not this calendar's, and it is why the two folders carry a link to each other.

Of his own life this calendar says nothing at all beyond the see and the year. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 29 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260729.html); read 13 October 2026.*
