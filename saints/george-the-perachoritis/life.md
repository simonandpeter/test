# George the Perachoritis

saint.gr's calendar for 12 October keeps twelve saints on one line — «Άγιοι Βαλάντιος, Βαρλαάμ, Βαρνάβας ο μοναχός, Βασίλειος ο επίσκοπος, Γεώργιος ο Βαβατσινιώτης, Γεώργιος ο επιτηδειώτης, Γεώργιος ο περαχωρίτης, Γεώργιος ο Σαλαμάνης, Δημητριανός ο επίσκοπος, Ειρηνικός, Ελπίδιος, Επαφρόδιτος» — and George the Perachoritis is one of them. The page behind the line gives each of the twelve a heading of its own, his being «Αγιος Γεωργιος ο Περαχωριτης Εκ Των Αλαμανων», and then prints a single sentence for all twelve together. That sentence is the whole of what the calendar says of them: that they are of the three hundred holy Alamanoi of Cyprus, and that we have no details of their lives.

Of George the Perachoritis in particular the page records nothing at all: no year, no birthplace, no rank or see beyond what stands in his own heading, no account of how he lived or how he died, and no hymn. The one thing his heading distinguishes him by is the epithet «ο Περαχωριτης», which is the only thing that tells him from the three other Georges on the same line.

So this folder holds what the calendar holds — a name, a company, a church and a day. The twelve are kept together on one line and are named one by one, which is why each of them stands here as his own folder rather than the twelve being gathered into a number. The only variation the line itself carries is for the tenth of them, whom its text gives as «Ειρηνικός ή Αρνιακός».

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2726/saint.aspx), which the twelve share and which prints no life for any of them; read 1 October 2026.*
