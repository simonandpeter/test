# Wistan

saint.gr says Wistan was the son of Wimund, son of the king of Mercia. When his father and his grandfather had fallen asleep the young Wistan came to the throne, under the guardianship of his grandfather's brother; and meanwhile he grew and advanced in the grace and the wisdom of God, and strove to keep the commandments of the Gospel.

But Berhtwulf, the regent of the throne, whose rule the page dates from 840 to 852, conspired with his own son against him, so as to be rid of the lawful heir; and they murdered him in the year 849, at the moment when he was coming up to his cousin to greet him. His butchered body was buried by his mother with reverence, and many wonders, the page says, were worked by his intercession.

That is the whole of it. The page names no kingdom of his own — Mercia is his grandfather's — and gives him no year of birth, no wife, no heir and no hymn. It calls him a most devout king and never a martyr, and this folder keeps to the word it uses.
