# Mildred of Minster

saint.gr says Mildred was the daughter of king Merewalh and of Ermenburga, a princess of Kent. She took the road of the monastic state and was tonsured a nun at the monastery of Minster, where she also served as abbess, having been established there by [Theodore of Tarsus](/saints/theodore-of-tarsus-archbishop-of-canterbury), archbishop of Canterbury, the Greek of Tarsus in Cilicia whom the page sends its reader to look up on 19 Σεπτεμβρίου.

She was marked out, the page says, for the gentleness of her character and for her charitable work, and she fell asleep in peace in the year 700. It gives her no year of birth, names no relics, and prints neither couplet nor hymn under her name; what it credits at the foot of the entry is the Apostoliki Diakonia of the Church of Greece.
