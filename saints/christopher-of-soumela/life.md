# Christopher of Soumela

saint.gr says plainly that no clear biographical details of Christopher survive, and that only from the *Neon Leimonarion* do we learn that he and the two fathers kept with him reposed in peace in the year 412. He came from Trebizond, and he was the new founder of the monastery of Soumela after it had been laid waste.

The monastery itself had been founded by [Barnabas](/saints/barnabas-of-soumela), an Athenian by descent, whose nephew [Sophronius](/saints/sophronius-of-soumela) was with him; the Greek calendar keeps the three together on 18 August and the service sings all three in one hymn. Christopher's own synaxarion distich calls him the initiate of Christ, who went up rejoicing into the undefiled dwellings to receive the reward of his labours.

Beyond that the page prints nothing of his life — nothing of his birth beyond Trebizond, nothing of the desolation he repaired, and nothing of his death. Its year is reported here as the page gives it, on the authority of the *Neon Leimonarion*; doxologia.ro, which the corpus read first, puts Barnabas and Sophronius in the tenth century instead.
