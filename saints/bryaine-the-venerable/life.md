# Bryaine

Bryaine died in peace; she may be, the synaxarion says, the mother of [Phantinus](/saints/phantinus-the-wonderworker), kept on the same day. Its verse: from earth Bryaine, a divine spring of graces, was channelled to the living fountain above.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 30 Αυγούστου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/844/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
