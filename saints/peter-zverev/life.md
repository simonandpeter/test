# Peter (Zverev)

saint.gr's calendar for 27 January names Peter Zverev as a hieromartyr and says in as many words that it has no life to give. The whole of the page's account of him is this: that the holy hieromartyr Peter bore witness in the year 1929, and that «Δεν έχουμε περισσότερες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου» — we have no further details of the saint's life.

So what is recorded here is what the Greek calendar records: the day, the name with its surname, the rank of hieromartyr, the year in which he was killed, and the page's own admission that it knows no more. It gives no year of birth, no see, no place and no manner of death, and prints no troparion and no kontakion. The Russian calendar, which keeps the new martyrs of those years with their own dossiers, is where this entry could be finished, and it has not been read.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 27 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/27/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3350/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
