# Aetherius (12 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 12 December keeps the holy Aetherius, and begins with where it has him from: the memory of Saint Aetherius is recorded in the Parisian Codex 1611. He was martyred under Maximian, the page says, whose reign it dates 286 to 305; and because he would not deny Christ and sacrifice to the idols, they put red-hot irons into his ears so that the pupils of his eyes started out.

After that they bound him to a wheel and put a bridle in his mouth. Then, having burned him with lighted torches, they beheaded him at the last. That is the whole of the entry: no country, no city, no magistrate, no relic and no hymn, and not even one of the synaxaristic couplets the site sets over most of its entries. The gloss it prints under the name — the ungraspable, the airy one — is its standing note on the name itself.
