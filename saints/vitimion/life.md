# Vitimion

saint.gr's calendar for 24 December names the venerable Vitimion, and says of him in its own voice only that he was an ascetic of the desert who died in peace. Then it quotes the whole of what Nikodemos the Hagiorite sets down in his Synaxaristes, which is one saying of the fathers and nothing more: in the Paradise of the Fathers, Nikodemos notes, this venerable one appears under the name Vitimios.

Apples were given him once to take to the elders in the Skete. He went and knocked at the door of the cell of Abba Achillas, meaning to give him some of them, and Achillas answered him: at this hour, brother, I would not have had you knock at my door, not even if you wished to give me manna from heaven; and go to no other man's cell either. So Vitimion carried the apples to the church, and himself withdrew to his own cell. The calendar gives him no year and no hymn, and marks the feast fixed. It carries no asterisk and not the sentence by which saint.gr marks an entry it will not vouch for.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/24/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3229/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
