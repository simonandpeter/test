# Theopemptus (11 June)

saint.gr's calendar for 11 Ιουνίου names him and gives no life. The whole of its notice is one line — «Όλοι μαρτύρισαν δια ξίφους», they were all martyred by the sword — with the two verses of the synaxarion above it: «O Θεόπεμπτος τέσσαρας διά ξίφους, / Eυρών συνάθλους πέμπτος αυτός ευρέθη», that Theopemptus, finding four fellow-contestants by the sword, was himself found the fifth. The page names no place, no emperor and no judge, and gives no year.

His four companions the page counts and does not name, and it keeps them as an entry of their own, «Άγιοι Τέσσερις Μάρτυρες που μαρτύρησαν μαζί με τον Άγιο Θεόπεμπτο»; they are held here as [the four martyrs with him](/saints/four-martyrs-with-theopemptus). The Greek calendar keeps three other men of this name on other days, and this is not one of them. There is no hymn on the page.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/11/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/569/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
