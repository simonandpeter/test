# Castor

The Greek synaxarion keeps Castor on 18 September and says it has no details of his life; its verse says Castor was no beast but a brave man — or rather, a statue before torments. The Romanian calendar keeps him the same day with the readings for a martyr and no life. This entry records the lines and nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 18 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2476/saint.aspx); The Romanian page is at doxologia.ro — [the page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-castor); read 23 August 2026.*
