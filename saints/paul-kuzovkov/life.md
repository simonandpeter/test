# Paul (Kuzovkov)

**The calendar's last name in the clause of fifteen is a layman's**, «и мч. Павла (1937)», and his name page calls him мч. with no grade and no office. He was a mill hand of the village he was born in. azbyka.ru carries no life of his own for him but prints the joint one of the three men of his parish, out of fond.ru: his priest [Alexander (Vershinsky)](/saints/alexander-vershinsky) and the secretary of the parish audit commission, [Nicholas (Kopninsky)](/saints/nicholas-kopninsky).

Павел Васильевич Кузовков was born on 15 June 1875 at Ивантеевка in the province of Moscow, a worker's son. He finished the village school, went into a factory in 1887 on odd jobs, became a skilled hand and stayed there until 1904, worked two years in a carriage-repair works, and from 1907 to 1932 was a cloth-fuller at the Лыжин factory. From 1932 to the day of his arrest he was on duty at a water-pumping station. **On 1 January 1937 he was elected chairman of the parish council of the church of the Smolensk icon of the Mother of God, and it was for that, the page says plainly, that he was arrested.**

The questioning it prints is the whole of his confession. The investigator put it to him that Soviet society knew from the teaching of Lenin that religion is a drug for the people, and asked how it was that he was a fierce partisan and defender of religion and the Church. He answered: «Я ярым защитником религии и Церкви не являюсь, я только был народом выбран председателем церковной двадцатки» — he was no fierce defender, only a man the people had elected chairman of the parish twenty. He admitted going to the diocesan office in Moscow that spring to ask what could be done to keep the church from being closed, and going to the district executive committee for leave to hold a meeting to tell the faithful of the closing; and he denied that counter-revolutionary talk had gone on in his flat.

On 1 December 1937 the troika of the NKVD sentenced the priest and the chairman of the parish council to death, and the member of the parish council to eight years in the camps. He was shot on 8 December 1937 and buried in an unmarked common grave on the Butovo range outside Moscow.
