# Nicholas (Beptyukov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him second among the hieromartyrs of its third line for 17 декабря and gives no life. The line is «Сщмчч. Александра и Николая и Сергия пресвитеров (1918).» — the hieromartyr presbyters Alexander and Nicholas and Sergius, 1918 — and the calendar’s own name page adds the surname and the grade the line leaves out: «Николай (Бепьтюков), протоиерей, сщмч.», an archpriest where the line says presbyter.

The surname is written here as the calendar prints it, and the way it prints it looks like a slip: «Бепьтюков», with a п where «Бельтюков» would have a л. It occurs once on the page and nowhere else in it, and no correction has been made, because correcting a name is reconstructing it and this corpus records what it read.

That is the whole of the record. He was an archpriest; he died in 1918; and the calendar keeps him on this day beside [Alexander (Savelov)](/saints/alexander-savelov) and [Sergius (Florinsky)](/saints/sergius-florinsky), the two its line joins to him. The day page’s three hymns all belong to the prophet of its first line and to the three youths with him.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 17 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261217.html); read 13 October 2026.*
