# Glycon

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Οκτωβρίου names Glycon and gives him no life. One sentence is the whole of the narrative: «Ο Άγιος Γλύκων μαρτύρησε διά ξίφους» — he was martyred by the sword. The couplet over it plays on the sweetness in his name, saying that the martyr Glycon, holding out his neck to the sword, pours out his blood as a sweet libation to Christ.

The page gives him no country, no city, no year, no emperor and no companions; it prints no hymn and no relics, and nothing in the corpus answers to the name.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2653/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
