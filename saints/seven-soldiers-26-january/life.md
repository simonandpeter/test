# The Seven Soldiers (26 January)

saint.gr's calendar for 26 January keeps seven soldiers together with the priest [Ananias](/saints/ananias-26-january) and the gaoler [Peter](/saints/peter-the-gaoler), and gives the seven a heading of their own on the page without naming one of them. What it records of them is the one thing that is recorded: that they were brought to faith in Christ by what they saw in the prison in Phoenicia, and that they died for it.

Ananias, having been scourged and burned with heated spits and having had vinegar and salt thrown on his cut limbs, was fed in prison by God for whole days together. That wonder, the page says, drew the gaoler Peter and these seven soldiers to Christ, and the governor Maximus drowned them all in the sea together with Ananias, in the years the page dates 295, under the emperor Diocletian. The page prints no troparion and no kontakion for them, and its couplet counts them: Peter with the seven went down into the sea.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1172/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
