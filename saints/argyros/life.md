# Argyros

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 11 mai reads «Sfântul Mucenic Arghir», and the page kept under his name carries no Viață tab and no Tropar tab — only the scripture readings appointed for the day and an icon. The caption of that icon, a twentieth-century Greek lithograph in the Iaşi collection of the Synaxarion of the saints of the day, names him beside the hieromartyr of the same day: «Sfântul Sfințit Mucenic Mochie și Sfântul Nou Mucenic Arghiros». That he is called a new martyr is the whole of what the page says about when he lived; it says nothing of where he was from, nothing of how he died, and gives no year.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 11 mai — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/11-mai) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-arghir); read 20 September 2026.*
