# Ursula

saint.gr's calendar for 21 Οκτωβρίου names Ursula and says in one sentence that it has no life for her: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο της Βρεταννής Αγίας Ούρσουλας (Ursula)» — we have no details of the life of the British saint Ursula. The page's heading dates her «(; - 383)», leaving the year of her birth unknown, and it prints her Latin name beside the Greek.

The only other thing it records is where she is honoured: part of her relics, it says, is in the Roman Catholic church of her name at Cologne. There is no couplet, no hymn and no narrative of any kind, and the page says nothing of the companions with whom the Western tradition keeps her.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 21 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/21/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2546/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
