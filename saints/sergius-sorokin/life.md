# Sergius (Sorokin)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him among the venerable martyrs on 10 декабря and gives no life. Its line is «Прмч. Сергия (1937).» — the venerable martyr Sergius, 1937 — and the calendar’s own name page adds both the grade and the surname the line leaves out: «Сергий (Сорокин), иеромонах, прмч.», Sergius the hieromonk.

That is the whole of the record. He was a monk in priest’s orders; he died in 1937, in the years in which the Russian church counts its new martyrs; and the calendar keeps him on this day alone, his line joined to no one else’s. The day page carries eleven hymns and not one is under his name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 10 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261210.html); read 13 October 2026.*
