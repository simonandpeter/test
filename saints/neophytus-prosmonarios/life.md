# Neophytus the Prosmonarios of Vatopedi

Neophytus was prosmonarios — the monk who keeps the church and its icon — of the monastery of Vatopedi on the Holy Mountain, and he was sent to the monastery's dependency in Euboea. There, when he fell gravely ill, he prayed before the icon of the most holy Theotokos that she would let him return to his monastery and die there. Then he heard the voice of the most holy Theotokos telling him to go back to the monastery, and that he would be ready to die in a year's time.

Neophytus was made well at once and returned to his monastery. A year later he heard the voice of the most holy Theotokos again, out of the mouth of her holy icon, telling him that the hour had come for him to fall asleep. He fell gravely ill once more, and having asked forgiveness of his brethren he gave up his soul to the Lord. Over him the page sets the epigram «O Nεόφυτος Παρθένου πέλων φίλος, Φωνήν ακούει της δε· Ω θαύμα ξένον!» — Neophytus, being a friend of the Virgin, hears her voice: O strange wonder. No year is printed for him, and no hymn.
