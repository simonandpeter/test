# John of Manglisi

saint.gr's calendar for 28 Μαρτίου names the venerable John of Georgia, and the page behind the line is a single sentence: he was bishop of the city of Manglisi in eastern Georgia, and he fell asleep in peace in the year 1751. The heading adds nothing but a question mark where the year of birth would stand. There is no monastic house, no year of consecration, no account of his see, no wonder, no relic and no hymn.

Manglisi lies in Kvemo Kartli, south-west of Tbilisi, and its cathedral is one of the older sees of the Georgian Church; the page says nothing of any of that, and nothing of it is written into the folder. What the source gives is a name, a rank, a see and a year, and that is what is kept.

The four numbered notes the page prints under the name Ιωάννης — the Forerunner, the son of Zebedee, Chrysostom, and many other saints, kings and hierarchs — are the site's encyclopaedia entry on the word, and none of them is this bishop. Because the calendar's own line calls him only of Georgia, the see is what tells him from the other Johns, and the see is what his name here carries.
