# Nazarius (Gribkov)

The calendar's own line is the whole of what is recorded of him here. The Сретенский monastery's calendar for 26 ноября ст. ст. names him third of ten priests in a clause that closes with an archimandrite and a single year, «Сщмчч. Иоанна , Георгия , Назария , Василия , Василия , Илии , Василия , Даниила , Михаила , Николая пресвитеров, прмч. Тихона (1937).», and the name page behind that third name gives him the surname the line leaves out together with his grade: «Назарий (Грибков), протоиерей, сщмч.» — an archpriest and a hieromartyr.

So nothing is said here of where he was born, where he served, when or by whom he was arrested, or where he died: no page read says any of it. The calendar carries no life page behind his name page and none on the day itself, and the day page's troparia all stand under other names. The year 1937 is the year the day line closes the clause with, and it is given for all eleven men at once.

His name page lists one other day, «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church. One row per church is the schema, so that day is recorded here and he does not render on it.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 26 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261126.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9360.html); read 7 October 2026.*
