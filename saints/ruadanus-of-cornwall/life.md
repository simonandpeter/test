# Ruadanus of Cornwall

saint.gr's calendar for 1 Ιουνίου names him Ruadanus of Cornwall and then gives two sentences that widen the ground under that name. He came from Brittany, the page says, and lived in the sixth century. He lived the ascetic life in a manner pleasing to God in the region of Tavistock, of Cornwall and of Brittany, and he fell asleep in peace in the town of Locronan in Brittany.

So the epithet the calendar gives him is one of three countries the page puts him in, and the one place it is exact about is where he died and not where he laboured. Nothing else is on the page: no monastery, no teacher, no disciple, no wonder, no year of birth or death, and no hymn. This folder keeps the calendar's epithet because that is the name the source prints, and leaves the three places to the reader.
