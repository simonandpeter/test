# Augustine (Belyaev)

Augustine was born Alexander Alexandrovich Belyaev on 28 February 1886 at Kamyshin in the Saratov province, a priest's son. He studied at the Kineshma church school and the Kostroma seminary, finished the Kazan theological academy in 1911 with a candidate's degree in theology, and from 1911 to 1920 taught in the men's and women's schools of Penza. He was ordained priest in 1920 to a church of that city, and in 1920 and 1922 was briefly arrested, once for agitation against the Soviet power and once for resisting the seizure of the churches' valuables.

Tonsured a monk with the name Augustine and ordained hieromonk, he was consecrated bishop of Ivanovo-Voznesensk on 8 September 1923, a vicar of the Vladimir diocese, and was almost at once expelled from that city and lived at Kineshma. He was arrested twice more in 1924. By the end of November 1924 he was in Moscow, where he took part in the burial of the patriarch and signed the act handing on the highest church authority. Arrested again at Ivanovo-Voznesensk in 1926, he was given three years' exile in Central Asia and served it at Khodzhent.

From 1930 he held the see of Syzran, and in 1931 was arrested by the Syzran OGPU and given three more years in the camps, which he served in the Svir camp in the Leningrad country. There is evidence, the page says, that after the declaration of 1927 he was for a time in secret opposition to its author and did not commemorate his name at the services. Freed again, he was bishop of Kaluga and Borovsk from 1934 to 1937; in 1935 he was drawn into an investigation but not arrested; in 1936 he was raised to archbishop. He was arrested at Kaluga in September 1937 and condemned to death a month later.

On 19 November 1937 an NKVD troika sentenced him with the archimandrite [Joannicius (Dmitriev)](/saints/joannicius-dmitriev), the archpriest [John (Speransky)](/saints/john-speransky), the psalmists [Alexis (Gorbachev)](/saints/alexis-gorbachev) and [Apollon (Babichev)](/saints/apollon-babichev), and [Michael (Arefyev)](/saints/michael-arefyev) of a church council, to be shot; the sentence was carried out on 23 November 1937 at Kaluga and the six were buried in one unmarked grave. The hieromonk [Seraphim (Gushchin)](/saints/seraphim-gushchin), condemned the same day by the troika of another district, was shot on the same day and, his life says, buried as they were. 10 ноября ст. ст. is that civil day.

**The see this folder owes it cannot have.** The day's line calls him archbishop of Kaluga and his own life page archbishop of Kaluga and Borovsk, which is the fuller and the truer; no locale pack holds either place and the label cannot be composed from a row they do hold, so the `office` stands at the bare rank until the author sets it. Two of the people he is bound to on this same day are elsewhere in it: [Olga (Maslennikova)](/saints/olga-maslennikova), who helped keep up the church where he served and whose own folder had to call him by his rank alone because this one did not yet exist, and the deacon and the bookkeeper of the day's third 1937 entry, whose folders come after this one. He was glorified in August 2000 by the jubilee council of bishops.
