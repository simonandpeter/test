# Damian (Voskresensky)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 21 октября ст. ст. in a line of his own, «Сщмч. Дамиана , архиеп. Курского (1937)» — a hieromartyr, archbishop of Kursk, dead in 1937. The title on the name link behind the line adds the surname and repeats the rest: «Святитель Дамиан (Воскресенский), архиепископ Курский, сщмч.»

The see is the one thing the calendar says about him beyond the name, the rank and the year, and it is a real diocese and not a commemoration, so it is recorded as his office. 1937 is the year of the Terror, and this day carries two other lines of clergy killed in it, one of fifteen people and one of five, which his is not part of.

The calendar prints no life page under his name and no hymn; the hymns the day page carries belong to other saints it heads and to a translation of relics. The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line — no birth, no earlier sees, no charge, no place of death.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 21 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261021.html); read 4 October 2026.*
