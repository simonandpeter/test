# Constantine the New, Emperor

This seems to be, the synaxarion says, the fourth son of the emperor Heraclius, who reigned in 641 and is called by some Constantine Heraclius and by others Constantine the New: a very faithful emperor who reigned six months and was, according to Meletius, poisoned. His memory is kept “at the Holy Apostles”; the verse says Christ the King crowned the pious Constantine in heaven.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 3 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/934/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
