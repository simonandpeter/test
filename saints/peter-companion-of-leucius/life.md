# Peter, companion of Severus and Leucius

saint.gr's calendar for 11 Ιανουαρίου names three martyrs in one line, «Άγιοι Πέτρος, Σεβέριος και Λεύκιος οι Μάρτυρες», and the page behind the line has one sentence for all three: «Οι Άγιοι Πέτρος, Σεβέριος και Λεύκιος, μαρτύρησαν στην Αλεξάνδρεια» — Peter, [Severus](/saints/severus-companion-of-leucius) and [Leucius](/saints/leucius-companion-of-peter) were martyred at Alexandria.

That is the whole record: the city, the fact of the martyrdom, and the three names in that order. The calendar names no emperor and no governor, gives no year, says nothing of how they died or what they were accused of, and prints no hymn for them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/11/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1093/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
