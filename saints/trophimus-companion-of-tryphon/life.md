# Trophimus, companion of Tryphon

The Greek synaxarion keeps [Tryphon](/saints/tryphon-companion-of-trophimus), Trophimus and [Dorymedon](/saints/dorymedon-companion-of-tryphon) together on 29 September, and says that the synaxaria record of them only the phrase that their synaxis is kept near the church of St Anna in the Deuteron. Its verse plays on two of the names: Trophimus and Tryphon, with Dorymedon, found the delight above to be their nourishment. It gives no date and says nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 29 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2400/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
