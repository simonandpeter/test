# Isaac the Persian of Metsovo

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 16 mai reads «Sfântul Mucenic Isaachie Persul din Meţovo» — the holy martyr Isaac the Persian, of Metsovo — and his own page on the site carries only that line, as heading and as title. There is no Viață tab and no Tropar tab, and nothing on the page says when he lived, how he died, or how a man called the Persian came to be named after a town in Epirus.

The two epithets the calendar gives him are therefore the whole of the record kept here, and they are kept exactly as the page prints them rather than resolved into a story. No year is claimed, no place is pinned, and no other calendar has been checked for him.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 16 mai — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/16-mai) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-isaachie-persul-din-metovo), which carries no life and no troparion; read 24 September 2026.*
