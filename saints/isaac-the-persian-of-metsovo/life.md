# Isaac the Persian of Metsovo

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 16 mai reads «Sfântul Mucenic Isaachie Persul din Meţovo» — the holy martyr Isaac the Persian, of Metsovo — and his own page on the site carries only that line, as heading and as title. There is no Viață tab and no Tropar tab, and nothing on the page says when he lived, how he died, or how a man called the Persian came to be named after a town in Epirus.

The two epithets the calendar gives him are therefore the whole of the record kept here, and they are kept exactly as the page prints them rather than resolved into a story. No year is claimed, no place is pinned, and no other calendar has been checked for him.

The Russian calendar names him second of three and gives the three one notice. They were Christians and lived, it says, in the third century in Persia under king Шапур, a cruel persecutor; they were pressed to renounce Christ and to turn to the superstition of the fire-worshippers, and answered that they would not renounce the Creator of creation and would not bow to the sun and to the fire. They were cruelly tortured, thrown into prison and left seven days without food, and at the last beheaded with the sword.

What the Russian adds of him in particular is a rank and a see. Its own index heads him a hierarch and a bishop and a hieromartyr, of Бет-Селевкия — Beth-Seleucia — and calls him the Persian; and that is the first office any calendar in this folder has given him. It does not sit easily beside the epithet the Romanian line gave him, of Metsovo in Epirus, which is the one this folder's display name keeps and which no page read for it has ever explained. The calendar's day line prints the fourth century where its notice says the third, and the king it names reigned across both, so no year and no century is recorded here.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 16 mai — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/16-mai) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-isaachie-persul-din-metovo), which carries no life and no troparion; read 24 September 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 18 мая ст. ст. — [the Russian day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260518.html), [the Russian notice of the three](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1095.htm) and [his Russian name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/1095.html), read 8 October 2026.*
