# Anastasius of Brescia

Anastasius was bishop of Brescia in Lombardy, and saint.gr says that he contended stoutly against the Arian heretics. He fell asleep in peace in 610. That is the whole of the entry, and the page says nothing more: no birthplace, no parents, no monastery, no account of the contest itself, no relics.

It prints neither a synaxarion couplet nor a hymn to him. He is one of three Italian and Frankish bishops saint.gr keeps on this day, with Theodore of Pavia, dead in 778, and Ultrillus of Bourges, dead in 624 — none of the three carrying the asterisk with which the site marks the Western entries it will not vouch for.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 20 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/20/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1684/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
