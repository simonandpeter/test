# Acacius of Latros

saint.gr says the memory of the venerable Acacius the Wonderworker is recorded in the Paris manuscript Coislin 361, folio 88, and that is the whole of its authority for him. He was a monk, and he founded on the mountain of Latros the Great Lavra of the Most Holy Theotokos of Myrsinon. He fell asleep in peace.

The page spends the rest of its space on the mountain rather than the man. The region of Latros was first called Latmos and lay near Miletus in Asia Minor; because a multitude of monks lived there as ascetics it was renamed Latros, and the crowded monastic settlements that were there before the reign of the emperor Leo the Wise were what gave Latros, in the reign of Constantine Porphyrogennetos, the name of the Holy Mountain by Ephesus. No year is printed for Acacius and none is supplied here.
