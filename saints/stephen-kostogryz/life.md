# Stephen (Kostogryz)

Stephen Grigorievich Kostogryz was born in 1887 at Berislav in Kherson
province, and from 1915 was psalmist of the church of the Archangel Michael
at Slobodzeya in the Tiraspol district of the same province; he was ordained
deacon probably in 1924 and priest probably in 1933, and made rector of the
same church. In 1935 he was arrested for trying to cross the state frontier
and for counter-revolution, given seven years, and sent to the Karaganda
camp; in 1937 he was arrested in the camp for systematic agitation among the
prisoners and praise of the tsarist order. The priest [Alexander
Aksyonov](/saints/alexander-aksyonov) of the Vyatka diocese was in the same
camp, one group case was opened on the two of them for systematic
counter-revolutionary agitation among the prisoners, and they were sentenced
to death and shot together in the first Volkovo section of the Karlag on 26
September 1937. He is kept on 13 September of the old style, 26 September.
