# Andrew

Andrew lived in the years of the emperor Basil I (867), the Greek synaxarion says, and was already an old man when the Agarenes, who then ruled all of Africa and had reached as far as Sicily, took him captive. They brought him before their savage ruler Athrachim, and before him Andrew confessed Christ with great boldness. The ruler kept him in prison for many years, and when he saw that Andrew held to his faith he set him up in front of himself as a target, galloped at him on his horse and struck him a mortal blow with his lance; then he had him beheaded. The entry’s verse sets the two lances that pierced Andrew beside the one lance that pierced the Word.

The Russian calendar keeps the four on one line on 23 September old style, in the ninth century, calling John’s younger son Antoninus. Its life says they suffered under Ibrahim, the cruel ruler of Africa, who after taking and destroying the Sicilian city of Syracuse carried [John](/saints/john-of-syracuse) and his two children, [Peter](/saints/peter-son-of-john-of-syracuse) and [Antony](/saints/antony-son-of-john-of-syracuse), captive to Africa and had the boys taught Arabic and the sciences. As they grew up he came to love them so for their wisdom and virtue that he called Antoninus his kinsman and made Peter his chief steward; but learning that the young men secretly confessed Christ, he flew into a rage and had them put in iron stocks and beaten with knotted sticks. After a long beating Antoninus was tied with straps onto an ass and led round the city, beaten and reviled, and bore it all giving thanks to God; Peter was beaten and thrown into prison. Their father was brought, and Ibrahim seized him by the neck with his left hand and drove a knife into his throat with his right; the father’s body was thrown down by his children’s, and at Ibrahim’s order a great fire was lit and the martyrs’ bodies thrown into it. The old man Andrew the tormentor starved for a long time and then ran through the breast with a spear, and when the martyr began to give thanks to God he ran him through again, and as he bled they cut off his head with a sword.
