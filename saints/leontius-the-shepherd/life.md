# Leontius the Shepherd

saint.gr's calendar for 18 June names Leontius the shepherd of the house lying beyond the City, and the whole of what the page says about him is one sentence: the venerable Leontius reposed in peace. Beside it stands a two-line epigram, which turns on his calling — being a shepherd you now stand before the Shepherd, before the Chief Shepherd, upright-minded Leontius — and the page's usual note on the name, that Leontius comes from the lion and means the strong, the lordly, like the lion.

There is no country, no year, no monastery named beyond that phrase of place, no detail of his rule and no hymn, and this life adds none. He is none of the other men of the name whom the calendars keep, and in particular not the soldier martyr of Tripoli nor the myrrh-gusher of Dionysiou, both of whom this same 18 June keeps as separate entries with lives of their own.
