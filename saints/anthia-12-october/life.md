# Anthia (12 October)

saint.gr's calendar for 12 October names her and gives no life. Its whole notice is one sentence: «Η Αγία Ανθία βρήκε μαρτυρικό θάνατο μέσα σε χάλκινο πυρακτωμένο βόδι» — Saint Anthia found a martyr's death inside a red-hot brazen ox. That is the manner of her death and all the calendar says of her: no year, no city, no persecutor, no family, no rank beyond the word saint, and no hymn.

The one other thing on the page is the two-line verse set over her name, which turns on the flowers her name is made of: thrown into the fiery ox she withered, and in heaven she flowers brightly, Anthia. The site prints no note on the name and nothing more.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2719/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
