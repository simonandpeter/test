# Maximilian (Marchenko)

The Russian calendar's 14 October closes its twentieth century with three words and a year: «Прмч. Максимилиана (1938)» — Maximilian, venerable martyr, 1938. «Прмч.» stands for преподобномученик, a monk put to death for the faith, as against the two priests named before him on the same day. The site's index of names supplies the surname and one further word, heading his page «Максимилиан (Марченко), игумен, прмч.»: he was an abbot.

Which monastery he governed is not printed, and neither is a birth, a tonsure, a charge or a place of death. 1938 is the second of the two years in which the Soviet state took most of the monastic clergy it had not already taken, and the calendar says nothing of how it took him. His index entry keeps him here and on 26 January with the whole company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and carries no life. The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian saints are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026 even with a browser user-agent, so the line stands as the line.

[Michael (Lektorsky)](/saints/michael-lektorsky), of 1921, and [Peter (Lebedev)](/saints/peter-lebedev), of 1937, are the other two the day keeps under the same silence.

*After the Russian church calendar's [14 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261014.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14339.html); read 4 October 2026.*
