# Anastasia (23 March)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names her and carries no life. Its line for the twenty-third of March, old style, reads «Сщмч. Василия , Стефана пресвитеров, прмч. Илии , прмц. Анастасии , Варвары , мч. Алексия (1938).», and the name page behind «Анастасии» is headed «Анастасия, прмц.», which gives no surname either. That page lists the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church on «26 января» and this twenty-third of March.

Nothing else is said of her on either page: no place, no diocese, no parish, no year of birth and no account of how she died. The 1938 the line closes with is the whole of her chronology. The second source for the Russian new martyrs, azbyka.ru, was not read for her.

Hers is the only one of the nine name pages that gives no surname, so there is nothing to tell her by but the day, and the day is in her display name for that reason. «Прмц.» is the whole of what the calendar says she was.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 23 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260323.html); read 8 October 2026.*
