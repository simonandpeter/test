# Eutychius (Kachur)

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him twelfth of the fifteen, second of the three it calls «прмчч.», and gives no life; the life here is azbyka.ru’s for the civil 3 декабря, out of fond.ru. In the world Евфимий Викторович Качур, he was born on 26 December 1891 in the settlement of Алексеевка in the Biryuch district of the province of Voronezh — and the page makes a point of the day, which is the memory of the hieromartyr [Euthymius of Sardis](/saints/euthymius-of-sardis), who stood against the iconoclast heresy through long years of exile.

Before the revolution he was tonsured with the name Евтихий and was a monk of the Богородицкий скит at the Kyiv Bratsky monastery of the Theophany — called «Церковщина» in the case file. His ordination to the priesthood was apparently after 1917. Where he served in the twenties is not known, but a certificate of April 1929 awarding him the pectoral cross, issued by the bishop of Vinnytsia Варлаам (Козуля), shows him already superior of the Маркиановский скит of the Vinnytsia district, and awarded it «за усердное исполнение священно-пастырских обязанностей и твердое стояние во Святом Православии» — for zealous fulfilment of his priestly and pastoral duties and firm standing in the holy Orthodoxy. Some while later he was raised abbot.

The authorities persecuted him everywhere. He was arrested twice before, in 1924 and in 1931, and held forty-five days the first time and two months the second. By the time of the arrest of 1937 he was living in the town of Зарайск in the Moscow region and working as a gardener and flower-grower at the «Москож» factory. He was arrested on 22 November 1937 for anti-Soviet agitation and held in the prison of the Kolomna district. Asked whether he admitted carrying on active counter-revolutionary activity among the people of Зарайск, he answered: «я это не признаю, так как никакой контрреволюционной деятельности не вел». He admitted none of the other invented crimes either.

On 27 November 1937 the troika of the NKVD sentenced him to be shot. «Спустя некоторое время приговор был приведен в исполнение. Место его погребения неизвестно» — some time later the sentence was carried out, and the place of his burial is unknown. His name was included in the Synaxis of the New Martyrs and Confessors of Russia by the determination of the Holy Synod of 27 December 2000.
