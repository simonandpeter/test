# Antony of Vilnius

saint.gr's whole account of the three is one sentence: «Οι Άγιοι Μάρτυρες Αντώνιος και Ιωάννης ήταν αδέλφια και μαζί με τον Ευστάθιο μαρτύρησαν το έτος 1347 στη Βίλνα της Λιθουανίας» — the holy martyrs Antony and John were brothers, and together with Eustathius they were martyred in the year 1347 at Vilna in Lithuania. The calendar's line for the day says the same and no more, calling the two αυτάδελφοι, brothers of one womb.

The calendar names Antony first of the two brothers, and [John](/saints/john-of-vilnius) second; the third of the company, [Eustathius](/saints/eustathius-of-vilnius), it joins to them without saying how. saint.gr heads Antony «Αγιος Αντωνιος (; - 1347)», which is the year of his death and the only year it gives him.

Nothing else is printed of him: no parentage, no trade, no city of birth, no account of the trial and no manner of death, no judge and no hymn. What the page adds under his name is where his body lies. Its closing sentence puts the relics of all three «στην ιερά μονή Αγίας Τριάδος Βίλνας», at the holy monastery of the Holy Trinity of Vilna, while its own list of relics gives him, separately, the church of the Holy Spirit at Vilnius in Lithuania; the page sets the two down side by side and does not reconcile them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 14 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/14/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/262/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
