# Alexis (Vorobyov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Alexis last of the six presbyters in a company of eight on 7 August, and gives no life for him. The entry is «Сщмчч. Александра, Петра, Михаила, Иоанна, Димитрия и Алексия пресвитеров, Елисея диакона и прмч. Афанасия (1937).», and the calendar's own name page adds the surname the line leaves out: «Алексий (Воробьев), протоиерей, сщмч.», Alexis Vorobyov, archpriest.

That is the whole of the record, though the calendar keeps an icon of him where it keeps no life. He was an archpriest; he died in 1937; the calendar keeps him on this day, the day after the Transfiguration, among the new martyrs of Russia. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 7 августа ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260807.html); read 13 October 2026.*
