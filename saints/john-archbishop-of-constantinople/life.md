# John, Archbishop of Constantinople

John II became patriarch of Constantinople after Timothy and held the throne two years and ten months; he died in peace in 520 and left [Epiphanius](/saints/epiphanius-of-constantinople) as his successor, who was patriarch fifteen years and three months and died in peace in 535. That is the whole of the synaxarion’s joint entry for the two; its verse for John laments that at the death of good John a lamp of the Church of God goes out.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 25 August — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2646/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
