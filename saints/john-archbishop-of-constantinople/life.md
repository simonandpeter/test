# John

John II became patriarch of Constantinople after Timothy and held the throne two years and ten months; he died in peace in 520 and left [Epiphanius](/saints/epiphanius-of-constantinople) as his successor, who was patriarch fifteen years and three months and died in peace in 535. That is the whole of the synaxarion’s joint entry for the two; its verse for John laments that at the death of good John a lamp of the Church of God goes out.

The Romanian calendar keeps him on 30 August, not on 25, and keeps him with two other patriarchs of the city — [Alexander](/saints/alexander-patriarch-of-constantinople) and [Paul the New](/saints/paul-the-new-patriarch-of-constantinople) — under one shared life. That life names him «Sfântul Ioan care se numea Capadoc - căci era cu neamul din Capadocia», says he took the throne of Constantinople after Timothy and was raised to it against his will, and that the emperor meant to drive him out but died first; it quotes the Greek church historian Nicephorus Callistus for that. It ends by saying the three hierarchs, Alexandru, Ioan and Pavel, are honoured together on this day.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 25 August — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2646/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026; and doxologia.ro's calendar for 30 august — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/30-august) and [the shared life of the three hierarchs](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-ierarh-ioan-patriarhul-constantinopolului), read 24 September 2026.*
