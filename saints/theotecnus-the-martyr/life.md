# Theotecnus

The Greek synaxarion says only that Theotecnus was martyred by stoning, and prints a verse in which he too says “once was I stoned”, as Paul said writing to the Corinthians. The Greek calendar keeps him on 3 October; no year is given.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 3 Οκτωβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2585/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
