# Michael (Tverdovsky)

He is the second of the four presbyters the Russian calendar keeps in one line on 4 October, with an archimandrite after them and 1937 printed once for all five: «Сщмчч. Николая, Михаила, Иакова и Тихона пресвитеров, прмч. Василия». The day line gives him only «Михаила»; the site's index of names heads his page «Михаил (Твердовский), иерей, сщмч.» and keeps him here and on 26 January with the whole company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia.

Three of his four companions have lives on this site and he does not: the day's life links reach [Nicholas (Vereshchagin)](/saints/nicholas-vereshchagin), [Jacob (Bobyrev)](/saints/jacob-bobyrev) and [Tikhon (Arkhangelsky)](/saints/tikhon-arkhangelsky), while he and [Basil (Tsvetkov)](/saints/basil-tsvetkov) are left with the line. So nothing is recorded here of where he served, what he was charged with, or where he died. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026 even with a browser user-agent.

*After the Russian church calendar's [4 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261004.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9695.html); read 4 October 2026.*
