# John (Nikolsky)

John Nikolayevich Nikolsky was born on 8 October 1868 in the village of Kladbishchi in the Sergach district of Nizhny Novgorod province, into a priest’s family. He finished the Nizhny Novgorod seminary in 1891 and was ordained priest. In 1933 he spent three and a half months under arrest, and his last years he served in the Transfiguration church of the settlement of Karpovka on the edge of the city of Gorky. On 6 August 1937 he was arrested with a group of priests and laymen of Gorky and held in the city prison, charged with belonging to a “counter-revolutionary fascist organisation” supposedly led by Theophanes (Tulyakov), metropolitan of Gorky. He did not admit guilt and refused to give evidence against others. On 21 September 1937 a special troika of the Gorky NKVD sentenced him to be shot; he was executed on 4 October 1937 and buried in an unmarked common grave at the Bugrovskoye cemetery in Gorky. The Jubilee Council of Bishops of the Russian Orthodox Church glorified him in 2000.

The Russian calendar keeps him on 21 September old style on one line with the priests [Valens (Nikolsky)](/saints/valens-nikolsky), [Alexander (Belyakov)](/saints/alexander-belyakov), [John (Lazarev)](/saints/john-lazarev), [Andrew (Benediktov)](/saints/andrew-benediktov) and [Peter (Sakharovsky)](/saints/peter-sakharovsky), of 1937. azbyka.ru prints a seventh priest, Basil Nosov, on its line for the day, whom the Sretensky day does not name; it keeps him also with the saints of Nizhny Novgorod, and prints no hymns for him.
