# Nestor the Chronicler

saint.gr's calendar for 27 Οκτωβρίου names Nestor the Chronicler, «ὃ ἐν τῷ Σπηλαίῳ», and heads him «(1050 - 1114)». Its whole notice is one sentence: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ρώσου Οσίου παρά μόνο ότι γεννήθηκε στο Κίεβο το 1050 μ.Χ. και κοιμήθηκε περί το 1114 μ.Χ.» — we have no details of the life of the Russian venerable one, except that he was born at Kyiv in 1050 and fell asleep about 1114.

Beside that the page records one thing more, that his relic lies incorrupt in the Lavra of the Caves at Kyiv. There is no couplet, no hymn, no teacher and no companions; the chronicle his epithet is taken from is not named, and nothing here supplies a title the page does not print.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 27 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/27/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2666/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
