# Serapion (21 March)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 21 martie reads «Sfântul Cuvios Serapion», and the page it links to carries neither a Viață tab nor a Tropar tab: there is the heading, the day, and nothing more. What the line says is that he was a monastic — «Cuvios» — and that the Romanian church keeps him on this day; that is the whole of what has been read here.

No year, no country, no account of his life and no hymn has been taken from anywhere else, and none has been supplied from memory. The corpus already holds three men of this name, kept on other days and in other calendars, and nothing on this page identifies him with any of them. If a life for him is printed elsewhere, it has not been read, and this page will say only what doxologia says until it has been.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 21 martie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/21-martie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-serapion); read 19 September 2026.*
