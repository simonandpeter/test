# Rufianus

Rufianus and Rufus were martyred by the sword. That is the whole of the Greek synaxarion’s notice for 8 September; its verse has Rufianus bowing to the sword and saying, I wait for you, Rufus — do not delay, follow. The Greek calendar keeps them on the feast of the Nativity of the Theotokos.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 8 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/954/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
