# Constantine, son of Prince Theodore of Yaroslavl

Constantine was born at Sarai, at the court of the Golden Horde, to the holy
prince [Theodore](/saints/theodore-prince-of-yaroslavl) of Smolensk and
Yaroslavl and to Anna, the khan’s daughter baptised for the marriage — the
Orthodox Church in America’s life of his father tells it. He died, it seems,
before his brother [David](/saints/david-son-of-prince-theodore), who ruled
Yaroslavl after their father and died in 1321. On 5 March 1463 the three were
found in one grave at the Saviour monastery of Yaroslavl, the sons laid
beside their father, and they have been kept together since. The Greek
synaxarion keeps them on 19 September and says it has no details of the
lives of these Russian saints; the Russian calendar keeps them the same day
of its own reckoning.

*After the life printed by the Orthodox Church in America for 19 September —
[the day’s lives](https://www.oca.org/saints/all-lives/2026/09/19); the Greek
entry is the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 19 Σεπτεμβρίου —
[the entry](https://www.saint.gr/3846/saint.aspx); read 5 September 2026.*
