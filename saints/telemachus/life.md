# Telemachus

Telemachus was a monk of the fifth century. saint.gr says he travelled from Asia to Rome in the reign of the emperor Honorius, wanting to save the city, and that he set himself against the gladiatorial combats held in its amphitheatres. He went down into the Colosseum himself and shouted to the gladiators to stop in the name of Christ; the crowd began to laugh and to jeer at him.

One of the gladiators struck him in the stomach with his sword and he fell. He got up and called out to the two men again to stop, and a gladiator ran him through, so that he died on the floor of the amphitheatre, soaked in his own blood, murmuring the same words a last time. The crowd fell silent and emptied the Colosseum.

Because of this saint, the page says, the last gladiatorial combat in the history of the Roman Empire took place on 1 January 404: three days after his death an imperial decree abolished the games. The page names Wikipedia among its sources for the account.
