# Nicholas (Rozanov)

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 21 июня ст. ст. prints him in the line «Сщмч. Алексия , Павла и Николая пресвитеров (1938).», and the name link behind his place in it supplies the calendar’s own nominative with the surname, «Николай (Розанов), протоиерей, сщмч.» (name page 13728). That is where the name form in this folder is read from; the line gives the forename in the genitive and no surname at all.

His name page carries nothing else: «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 21 июня» and then no life, no place and no account of his death in 1938. Nothing is supplied for that here. The line’s «Сщмчч.» makes him a hieromartyr and «пресвитеров» a presbyter, and the name link raises the rank to «протоиерей», an archpriest. The corpus holds five other folders whose stored forms carry «Розанов» — a Basil, a Sergius and three Demetriuses, the last three themselves told apart by the day — and not one of them is a Nicholas.

The second day his name page lists is the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which is a synaxis and not a second feast, so he renders on 21 июня ст. ст. only. He is given no relation to the two others on his line, who share with him a line, a grade word and a year and nothing further that the calendar states.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 21 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260621.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13728.html); read 9 October 2026.*
