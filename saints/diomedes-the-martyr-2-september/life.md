# Diomedes (2 September)

Diomedes was martyred by the sword, beaten to death with it, as the verse says, iron against iron. That is the whole of the synaxarion’s notice for the martyr kept on 2 September, with its verse; the Synaxaristes have nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 2 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/925/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
