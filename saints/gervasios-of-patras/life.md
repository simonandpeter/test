# Gervasios of Patras

Gervasios Paraskevopoulos was born at Nymfasia in Gortyna on 1 January 1877. Having a strong faith from childhood, he entered the monastery of Kernitsa as a novice at the age of thirteen. In 1897 he came to the monastery of the Dormition of the Mother of God, the Girokomeio of Patras, where he remained to the end of his life; he was tonsured a monk at twenty-six.

In 1905 he finished the Rizarios seminary, where he came to know the hierarch [Nektarios](/saints/nektarios-of-aegina) and became his disciple. At thirty-three he became a priest, and four years later he defended his doctoral thesis in the faculty of theology. He served as a military chaplain during the Balkan wars of 1912 and 1913.

After the catastrophe of Smyrna in Asia Minor in 1922 he stayed close to the more than seven thousand refugees who settled at Patras. His work of charity, his gentle presence, his self-sacrifice and his tireless service made him a true model of a clergyman and an example of a Christian. He departed to the Lord on 30 June 1964, at the age of eighty-seven.
