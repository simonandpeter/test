# Sophia (Selivestrova)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps her on 15 февраля ст. ст. inside a single line that names six sufferers of 1938 together — three presbyters, a deacon, a hieromonk and a novice — and she stands in it as the last name in the line, «и прмц. Софии», under a rank word of her own, преподобномученица, a venerable-martyr; the name link adds that she was a послушница, a novice. The name link behind the line carries the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line itself leaves out, «София (Селивестрова), послушница, прмц.».

The calendar prints no life page under this name and no hymn under it either: the one life the line carries belongs to the hieromonk among the six, and the troparia the day page prints are for other saints it heads. Nothing further is written here. The line, the rank word, the order of service, the name the link behind it carries and the year 1938 are the whole of what was read, and in particular she is not identified with any saint of the same name whom the calendar keeps on another day.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 15 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260215.html); read 3 October 2026.*
