# John (Rybin)

His line on the Russian 6 October is four words: «Сщмч. Иоанна пресвитера (1937)» — John the hieromartyr, presbyter, 1937. The site's index of names gives the surname, heading his page «Иоанн (Рыбин), иерей, сщмч.», and keeps him here and on 26 January with the whole company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. Nothing else is printed: no birth, no diocese, no parish, no charge, no place of death.

He stands on the day beside [Michael (Soyuzov)](/saints/michael-soyuzov), who is marked «исп.», a confessor, and dated 1922. The two abbreviations are the whole of the difference the calendar records between them, and they are worth reading as the difference they are: one man came out of what was done to him and one did not. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026 even with a browser user-agent, so for both of them the line stands as the line.

*After the Russian church calendar's [6 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261006.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9635.html); read 4 October 2026.*
