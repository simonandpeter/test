# John the New of Ephesus

saint.gr's calendar for 3 August names two archbishops of Ephesus together, John the monk and John the New, and gives them no life at all. What it gives instead is where their memory is kept. It stands, the page says, in two codices, the Patmian 266 and the Parisian 152 Sap, in this form: «Τῶν ἐν ἁγίοις πατέρων ἠμῶν καὶ ἀρχιεπισκόπων γενομένων Ἐφέσου Ἰωάννου τοῦ μονάχου καὶ Ἰωάννου τοῦ νέου» — of our fathers among the saints, who were archbishops of Ephesus, John the monk and John the New.

This folder is for the second of the two, the one the codices call the New. Of him the page says nothing further: no century, no country, no deed and no repose, and not even what it was that made him the newer of the two. The day's listing calls the pair bishops of Ephesus, while the codex it quotes calls them archbishops, and it is the codex's word that is recorded here. The page adds that in a Parisian codex their memory is repeated on 4 August as well. It prints no hymn to either of them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/03/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2204/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
