# Athre

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 8 June reads «Όσιος Αθρέ», the venerable Athre, and the page behind it says only this: the venerable Athre, or Atre, died peacefully; we have no other details of the venerable one's life. So what is recorded here is a name with a variant beside it, the rank of a venerable, and a death in peace.

The couplet the page sets above that sentence is the only other thing it gives him, and it plays on the name as though it were the Greek verb to behold: having wisely spat out the beauties of earth, Athre, you behold the beauty of your own Master. There is no century here, no country, no desert and no monastery; no companion, no wonder, no relic and no hymn. Whether Athre or Atre is the older form of the name, the page does not say, and this folder does not choose between them: it stores the one the calendar's own line prints.
