# Menignus the Fuller

saint.gr's calendar for 22 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιος Μένιγνος ο κναφέας» — Menignus the fuller, a whitener of cloth, as the page explains the trade. He came from the city of Parium in the Colonia of the Hellespont, between Cyzicus and Lampsacus, and lived in the years of the emperor Decius, about 251. When the persecution began he defied the hard penalty and confessed his faith in Jesus in the market place, urging the Christians to stand faithful to the Lord and unmoved by the impious imperial decrees.

He was arrested, and because he would not embrace the idols he was taken to the place of beheading. When his wife learnt of it she ran to him and followed him weeping; and he comforted her, telling her that his death would secure their union for ever, because those who stay joined to God are the ones it is impossible ever to part. A little while afterwards his head fell, and he was buried there at the place of his martyrdom.

The page then prints at length the account Nikodemos the Hagiorite gives in his Synaxaristes. There it was the deliverance of the imprisoned Christians of his city that made Menignus burn for martyrdom: they had prayed in the dark of the prison to the Master who loosed Peter, and a heavenly light shone, their bonds melted like wax, the doors opened of themselves, and in the morning the soldiers found the seals whole and the cells empty and cried that the Nazarene had come by night and stolen the prisoners.

Hearing it, Menignus was filled with faith, and while he was washing clothes at the river a voice called him twice to martyrdom; he did not wait for a third call, but gave back the garments that were not his and made himself ready. When the ruler came and sat in judgement and began reading out the emperor's letters, Menignus leapt into the middle, snatched them out of his hands, cut them small and trod them underfoot, saying that in the name of Jesus Christ his God he trampled on asp and basilisk and on the lawless orders of Decius.

They threw him down and trampled him, hung him on a tree and tore his flesh with iron claws until his entrails showed through his ribs, and when the ruler ordered the fingers that had cut the decrees to be cut off, milk came out instead of blood. The next day he confessed Christ before them all and was sentenced to beheading; and those who stood by saw what looked like a clean turtle-dove come out of the martyr's mouth and fly up to heaven. The proconsul left the body unburied under guard, to see whether his God would bury him; his brothers took it away by night, forgot the head, and came back to find a star shining over it.

The Russian calendar prints one clause for him, «Мч. Менигна (250).», and a life of its own behind it. He was a plain craftsman, it says, whose trade was the bleaching of linen — «белильник», the word the calendar’s own name page files him under, and the fuller this folder is named by. Twice in his life he heard a voice from heaven calling him to suffer for Christ. In the persecution under Decius, whose reign the page dates 249–251, a miracle happened: an angel led the Christians out of the prison. Hearing of it Menignus rejoiced and loved the Saviour with his whole heart, and remembering the call he had heard he tore down on the spot the decree of «нечестивого Декия» that hung in the square ordering the persecution of Christians, and declared himself a follower of Christ. He was seized for it and beheaded after cruel tortures, and a snow-white dove flew out of the martyr’s mouth.

The Охридски пролог makes him a man of the Hellespont who bleached cloth for his living, and who was by-named the Bleacher from it. In the time of the emperor Decius he tore up the imperial decree ordering the persecution of the Christians. For that he was thrown into prison, where the Lord himself appeared to him and encouraged him, saying: do not fear, I am with you. In that same hour his fetters melted like wax, the prison opened of itself, and he walked out.

He was taken again and tried, and tortured inhumanly: they cut the fingers from his hands and from his feet, and then beheaded him. His head shone at night like a candle. The reign is the only dating the entry gives, and the «250 or 251» this folder already holds from two calendars sits inside it, so nothing in `dates` is moved.
