# Eleazar of Anzersky

The Greek calendar prints him on 13 Ιανουαρίου as «Όσιος Ελεάζαρος εκ Ρωσίας», the venerable Eleazar from Russia, and the page behind the line names him more exactly: Eleazar of Anzersky. He came from Russia and lived the ascetic life on the island the page calls Solovski, and he fell asleep in peace in the year 1656.

That is the whole of the entry: two sentences and a gloss on his name, which the page takes from the Hebrew for the help of God. It gives no year of birth, no monastery he founded or entered, no teacher and no disciple, and no hymn.
