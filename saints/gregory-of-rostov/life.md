# Gregory of Rostov

saint.gr names him and gives him a sentence and a half. Gregory was born in Russia in the fourteenth century, its page says, and was abbot of the monastery of the Transfiguration of the Saviour on the island of Kamenny in lake Kubenskoe, near the country of Vologda; he was elected archbishop of the city of Rostov, and he fell asleep in peace in the year 1416.

That is all. There is no year of birth, no account of his abbacy or of his years in the see, no wonder, no relic and no hymn. The day's listing gives him the fuller title of that see, «Αρχιεπίσκοπος Ροστώβ, Γιαροσλάβλ και Λευκής Λίμνης» — of Rostov, Yaroslavl and the White Lake — while the notice itself names only Rostov.
