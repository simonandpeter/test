# Mocius of Emesa

Mocius was a reader at Emesa, and doxologia.ro prints one life for him and for the two taken with him. In the days of the emperor Numerian, in the year 284, it says, a persecution was raised against the Christians, and word was sent to the governor of the city of Emesa to seize [Silvanus](/saints/silvanus-of-emesa), the bishop there; and he was seized at once together with [Luke](/saints/luke-of-emesa) the deacon and Mocius the reader.

Bound, the three stood before the governor. He questioned them closely, and hearing that they confessed Christ to be true God and put the servants of the idols under anathema, he fell into great rage and fury, put them to many and various torments, and at the last gave sentence that they should fight with beasts.

So the saints were brought out into the assembly of the people and many kinds of beasts were let loose. The saints prayed God that their life might end there, and God took the souls of his servants at once; and then the wild beasts, holding the relics of the saints in awe, drew back and did not touch their bodies at all. When night came the bodies were taken and buried with honour, with thanks given to God. The Romanian page names no persecutor beyond the emperor and gives nothing of Mocius's life before that day.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 29 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/29-ianuarie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-mucenic-mochie-citetul); read 19 September 2026.*
