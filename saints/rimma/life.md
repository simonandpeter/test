# Rimma

Rimma was one of three martyrs whom doxologia.ro's calendar keeps together on 20 ianuarie, with [Inna](/saints/inna) and [Pinna](/saints/pinna), and whose life it tells in a single paragraph. They were of a country in the north, the page says, and were taken by idol-worshipping barbarians and brought before the ruler of the place; seeing that they confessed Christ, he condemned them to die by freezing. The page gives no year and does not name their country.

They were bound to stakes driven into the water of a river, and it was winter. The water froze and became as motionless as the motionless bodies of the saints, and so they ended their lives and gave up their blessed souls into the hands of God. Nothing in the Romanian page tells one of the three from another, and nothing further is added here.

The Greek calendar keeps the three together on the same day and says where they came from and what was done to them. saint.gr says that [Innas](/saints/inna), [Pinnas](/saints/pinna) and Rimmas were from the region of Scythia and were called «οι κρυστάλλινοι», the crystalline ones; that they bore witness after being bound to a piece of wood, in a time of terrible frost, and set in a frozen river; that dreadful and unbearable were the pains their naked flesh was tried with there, but that their souls cast off nothing of their warm devotion, and so they received the crown of witness. The epigram over them plays on the same word: «Θάλψις δεχέσθω τοὺς Ἀθλητὰς τοῦ κρύους, Ἰννᾶν, Πιννᾶν, Ῥιμμᾶν τε τοὺς κρυσταλλίνους». The page gives them no year.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 20 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/20-ianuarie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfintilor-mucenici-pin-rim); read 19 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 20 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/20/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3324/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026.*
