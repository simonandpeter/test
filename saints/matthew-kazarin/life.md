# Matthew (Kazarin)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him in five words and gives no life. Its line for 30 октября ст. ст. reads «Сщмч. Матфея диакона (1942)» — a rank word, a forename in the genitive, a grade and a year — and the name link behind the line adds the surname the line leaves out and raises the grade it gives: «Матфей (Казарин), протодиакон, сщмч.», a protodeacon of that name, one of the new martyrs of the Russian church.

So the year is the whole of what is recorded, and the year is 1942, the latest death on this day's list. There is no life page behind the line, and the name page carries no text, no icon and no hymn — only his name and his days. The two pages disagree about his grade, deacon on the one and protodeacon on the other; both are kept here and neither is preferred.

His name page lists a second day, 26 января, the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, on which he is commemorated with the rest of that company rather than alone. The second Russian calendar, azbyka.ru, which has given this corpus a full life and its archival references for others of these men, refused the request with a 403 on the day he was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 30 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261030.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9834.html); read 4 October 2026.*
