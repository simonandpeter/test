# John the Tailor of Thasos

saint.gr's calendar for 20 December keeps John the tailor, a new martyr of the island of Thasos, and calls him throughout «το ηρωικό αυτό παιδί», this heroic child. He was born in the village of Mariai on Thasos; at fourteen years of age he came to Constantinople, and was learning the trade of the tailor at Galata when the thing happened that the page tells.

He went one day to the shop of a Jewish merchant to buy thread, and quarrelled with him. The merchant, the page says, took his chance while the hodja was up on the minaret, and accused John of insulting the faith and the worship of the Turks. Brought before the vizier and pressed to deny Christ, the boy answered with courage: «I will never deny my most sweet Jesus Christ, not if you do me ten thousand torments and give me the whole of your kingdom».

Seeing that the child's mind was not to be moved, the vizier ordered him beheaded; and at the place the page calls the Çarşı of the furriers the executioner cut off his head, in martyrdom and a little at a time, on 20 December 1652. It closes by naming the two men who wrote his life, John Karyophylles and Meletios Syrigos.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 20 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/20/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3179/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
