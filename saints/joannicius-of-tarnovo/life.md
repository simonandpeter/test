# Joannicius of Tarnovo

saint.gr's calendar for 14 Ιανουαρίου names «Άγιος Ιωαννίκιος Αρχιεπίσκοπος Τυρνόβου», and the page behind the line gives him one sentence: «Ο Άγιος Ιωαννίκιος έζησε κατά τον 13ο αιώνα μ.Χ. Εξελέγη Μητροπολίτης Τυρνόβου της Βουλγαρίας και κοιμήθηκε με ειρήνη» — he lived in the thirteenth century, was elected metropolitan of Tarnovo in Bulgaria, and fell asleep in peace.

The day's line calls him archbishop and the page's own sentence calls him metropolitan; both readings are the calendar's, and neither is corrected here. Beyond the century and the see there is nothing: no year, no birthplace, no monastery, no account of his episcopate, and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 14 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/14/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1284/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
