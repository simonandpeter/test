# Peter, Abbot of Bathys Ryax

Peter came from the second province of the Cappadocians and was distinguished for his strict piety and God-loving life; having lived in ascesis he died in peace. He is said to have been a disciple of Basil, the founder of the monastery of the Saviour at Bathys Ryax, and to have taken the abbacy after him — though by other sources the second abbot was Ignatius, kept on 27 September; his memory is not in the Synaxaristes of Nikodemos. The Greek calendar keeps him on 7 September with [Luke](/saints/luke-of-bathys-ryax), the third abbot.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 7 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/944/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
