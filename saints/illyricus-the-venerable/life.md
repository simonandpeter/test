# Illyricus

Doxologia.ro's calendar for 3 aprilie names him and gives no life. The line is «Sfântul Cuvios Ilirie», and his own page on the site carries no life tab, no troparion and no text at all beyond his name, the date and the readings appointed for the day. There is no country, no century, no monastery and no account of anything he did.

The one thing the source does assert is that he was a monastic: «Cuvios» is the Romanian for the rank the Greek calls ὅσιος, and the readings set beside his name are the monastic ones — the fruit of the Spirit from Galatians, and the Lord's yoke that is easy and burden that is light from Matthew. The English form of the name here is a chosen one; doxologia prints only «Ilirie».

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and adds the one thing the Romanian line has not got: the place. saint.gr's whole account is a sentence — «Ο Όσιος Ιλλυριός ασκήτεψε στο όρος του Μυρσινώνος και απεβίωσε ειρηνικά», he practised asceticism on the mountain of Myrsinon and died peacefully. It gives him no year, no monastery, no country and no relics, and prints no hymn. Its couplet turns on the same place, and spells it a second way: «Ἐκ Μυρσιῶνος, Ἰλλυριέ, πρὸς πόλον, / οὐχ ὡς ὁ Χριστὸς ἐξ Ἐλαιῶνος φέρῃ» — from Myrsion, Illyricus, you are borne to heaven, though not as Christ was from the Mount of Olives.

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 3 апреля ст. ст. in two words, «Прп. Иллирика .», and the life behind them is two sentences. It gives him the one thing the Romanian and Greek books did not: a place. He struggled, it says, on mount Марсион in the Peloponnese — and the name link behind the line says the same, «Иллирик Марсионский (Пелопонесский), прп.» Then it says outright that the time of his life and his labours is unknown, which is why this folder still holds no date.

The life page is titled «Преподобный Иллирик чудотворец», a wonderworker, where the day’s own line and the name link both give only the venerable’s abbreviation. A grade that stands in a life’s title and not in the calendar’s own line is written here and not into `types`, which is how «чудотворец» was refused on another folder of this wave. No `locations` is recorded either: the page names the mountain and the peninsula and gives no coordinate. The day page prints no hymn under his name.
