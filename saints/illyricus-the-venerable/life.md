# Illyricus

Doxologia.ro's calendar for 3 aprilie names him and gives no life. The line is «Sfântul Cuvios Ilirie», and his own page on the site carries no life tab, no troparion and no text at all beyond his name, the date and the readings appointed for the day. There is no country, no century, no monastery and no account of anything he did.

The one thing the source does assert is that he was a monastic: «Cuvios» is the Romanian for the rank the Greek calls ὅσιος, and the readings set beside his name are the monastic ones — the fruit of the Spirit from Galatians, and the Lord's yoke that is easy and burden that is light from Matthew. The English form of the name here is a chosen one; doxologia prints only «Ilirie».

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 3 aprilie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/3-aprilie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-ilirie); read 19 September 2026.*
