# Marianus, companion of Emilian

saint.gr keeps three martyrs together on this day and gives them a single sentence: the holy [Emilian the Roman](/saints/emilian-the-roman), [James](/saints/james-companion-of-emilian) and Marianus were martyred at Rome in the year 259. That is the whole notice, and the page's head repeats the year over each of the three as «(; - 259)». There is no manner of death, no persecutor, no rank, no relic, no hymn and not even the distich the site usually prints.

So three things stand recorded here and no more: the name, the two men he died with, and the year, which falls in the persecution of Valerian that the page does not name. Nothing else about him is on the page, and nothing is brought in here from anywhere else.

The corpus already keeps a Marianus the Deacon, whom the Romanian calendar names on 19 March. That man has a rank this page's Marianus does not, and a day of his own; two entries are not one person because they share a name, so the two are kept apart and the question is recorded in the reader's report.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3876/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
