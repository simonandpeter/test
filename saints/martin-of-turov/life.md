# Martin of Turov

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 27 июня ст. ст. names him «Прп. Мартина Туровского (после 1146).», and the index page it links carries two readings of him, both of which were read and both of which head «Дни памяти: 8 июня (Белорус.) , 8 июня , 27 июня». They tell one story and the second has more of it.

He was cook to the bishops of Turov — the second reading names four of them, «Симеоне, Игнатии, Иоакиме (1144 - 1146) и Георгии» — and the last of them discharged him for his age. He would not part from the monastery, the bishops living at the house of the holy princes Boris and Gleb at Turov in the Minsk country, and so he took the monastic habit there.

He had overstrained himself in his former labours and was often ill. Once he lay unable to move and crying out with pain, and for three days, helpless and fainting with thirst, he called earnestly on the help of the holy passion-bearers «Борис и Глеб», in whose honour the monastery stood. On the third day the holy princes appeared to him, gave him water to drink and healed him of his sickness. One reading says he lived a year more after that healing; the other says only that he died after 1146, which is what the day’s line gives and what is recorded here.

The princes are named in this life because the calendar’s own account of the healing names them, and an appearance is not a relation, so no related row is written for either of them. The day page carries a troparion under his name and it tells the same story in four lines.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 27 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260627.html) and [the index of his lives](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/id1489.htm); read 9 October 2026.*
