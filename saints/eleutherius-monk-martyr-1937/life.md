# Eleutherius

Eleutherius Pechennikov was born in 1870 in the village of Pripechino in the Cherikov district of Mogilev province, to a peasant family. He was called up in 1892 and served as a private; when he was ordained is not exactly known, but he was a regimental priest, and from 1896 a monk of the Trinity monastery in Smolensk.

He was arrested on 5 December 1922 by the Smolensk GPU as an opponent of the Renovationists and a convinced Tikhonite, and released five days later on an undertaking not to leave; on 20 March 1924 arrested again for membership of a counter-revolutionary organisation and held in the Smolensk house of detention until the charge was dropped on 3 April; and on 26 February 1930 a third time, in the case of the “Smolensk cathedral brotherhood”, and on 28 May sentenced by the OGPU collegium to ten years in the camps — Solovki first, and from 1932 the station of Mankent on the Turkestan–Siberian railway in South Kazakhstan. On 23 June 1937 he was arrested in the camp, with the archpriest Vladimir Smirnov, the abbess Eve (Pavlova), the archpriest Nicholas Tolgsky and others, in the case brought against Metropolitans Kirill (Smirnov) and Joseph (Petrovykh) and Archbishop Alexis (Orlov); the schema-archimandrite Eleutherius was charged with counter-revolutionary activity as leader of a secret monastery at Mankent that tonsured people into secret monasticism, and admitted nothing. Sentenced by the South Kazakhstan troika on 26 July, he was shot on 27 August 1937 in the Lisya Balka near Chimkent and buried in an unmarked common grave. He was glorified at the Jubilee Council of 2000. The Russian calendar keeps this company on 14 August (Julian): the presbyters [Vladimir](/saints/vladimir-presbyter-martyr-1937) and [Nicholas](/saints/nicholas-presbyter-martyr-1937-14-august), the monk-martyr [Eleutherius](/saints/eleutherius-monk-martyr-1937), the abbess [Eve](/saints/eve-the-abbess-1937), the nun [Eudocia](/saints/eudocia-nun-martyr-1937) and the martyr [Theodore](/saints/theodore-martyr-1937), all shot at Chimkent on 27 August 1937. The Sretensky calendar prints no life for any of them, and the year beside their names there, 1927, is a slip; these lives follow azbyka.ru.
