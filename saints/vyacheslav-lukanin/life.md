# Vyacheslav (Lukanin)

The calendar's line is all there is of him. For 3 августа ст. ст. it prints «Сщмч. Вячеслава диакона (1918).» — the hieromartyr Vyacheslav the deacon, 1918 — and behind the forename the name link gives his surname and repeats his order, so that the calendar's own fullest form of him is Вячеслав (Луканин), диакон. There is no life page, no diocese, no parish, no place and no manner of death.

Two other men of the same surname are kept by this calendar on two other days of the same year of killing, and nothing read here says whether they were his kin. This folder says only what the calendar says, which is a name, an order, a day and a year.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 3 августа ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260803.html); read 13 October 2026.*
