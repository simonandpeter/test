# Ares

saint.gr's calendar for 13 December keeps the venerable Ares, and its page says only this much of him: that he was one of the holy and wise ascetics of the Egyptian desert, that sayings of his are preserved in the Evergetinos, and that he died in peace. It gives no year for his birth and none for his death, and it names no monastery and no settlement.

For the rest the page prints what [Nicodemus the Hagiorite](/saints/nicodemus-the-hagiorite) wrote about him in his Synaxaristes, out of the Evergetinos. A brother came to Ares while Abba Abraham was present and asked him, tell me what I am to do to be saved. Ares told him to go and spend that year eating bread and salt every evening, and then to come back and he would speak to him. When the year was out the brother came to the elder again, and Abba Abraham happened to be there again.

The elder told the brother to go and spend this year eating once in two days. Abraham then asked him why he laid a light yoke on the other brothers and loaded a heavy burden on this one. The elder answered that he spoke to the brothers as they came asking; but this one was a worker, and whatever he told him he did in earnest, and so he spoke to him according to his strength. From that counsel, Nicodemus says, it is shown how discerning this venerable Ares was.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 13 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/13/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3256/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
