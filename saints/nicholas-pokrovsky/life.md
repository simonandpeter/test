# Nicholas (Pokrovsky)

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him seventh of the fifteen, among the ten it calls «пресвитеров», and gives neither a life nor a year of birth; the name link, name page 13863, gives «Николай (Покровский), иерей, сщмч.», a priest and a hieromartyr, and that is the whole of what the source of record says about him. **The life is one shared article and gives this person no biography of their own.** days.pravoslavie.ru carries no life for any of the fourteen of this line; azbyka.ru hangs behind the pages of the Tikhvin company a single article out of pstgu.ru, headed for the dean of Тихвин and «с ним пострадавшие», which gives a full life to him alone and to the other six a place in the case, the tortures, the refusal and the day. What is written here is that and the rank words on the name page, and nothing is added.

**And he is the one of the fifteen for whom azbyka.ru has no page.** Its own index for the civil 3 декабря names him in plain text with no link behind it, and the slug that name would take on that site belongs to a different Nicholas Pokrovsky — born in 1864, out of the Simbirsk seminary, kept on 18 марта and with the saints of Simbirsk, and with no 3 декабря among his days. So the shared article is the only text read for him, and it is cited here from the page of the dean it is written about.

The article names the company and the case. Fourteen people were shut in the Tikhvin prison as accused together with the dean: «7 священников, игумения Тихвинского Введенского монастыря и 6 мирян» — seven priests, the abbess of the Tikhvin convent of the Entry, and six laymen. All of them were declared one counter-revolutionary group acting in the town of Тихвин, the dean was declared its head, and the whole case was titled after him, «Сарв Иван Романович и другие». Every one of the clergy in the case was shot; the rest were given ten years in the camps.

**And the article says what five of the priests and the abbess did under interrogation.** They «отказались, не взирая на применявшиеся пытки, признать свою вину и оговорить проходивших по делу лиц, явив редкий пример единодушного христианского мужества» — refused, notwithstanding the tortures used on them, to admit their guilt or to speak against the others in the case, showing a rare example of unanimous Christian courage. The seven whom the day remembers «были расстреляны в Тихвине 3 декабря 1937 года».
