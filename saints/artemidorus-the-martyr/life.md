# Artemidorus

Artemidorus was martyred by fire. That is the whole of the Greek synaxarion’s notice for 8 September; its verse says that how strongly Artemidorus could leap he showed in deed, even with the fire burning. The Greek calendar keeps him on the feast of the Nativity of the Theotokos.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 8 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/952/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
