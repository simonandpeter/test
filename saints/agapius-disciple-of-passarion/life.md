# Agapius, disciple of Passarion

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life. The whole of what it says about him is one clause at the end of another man's account: the venerable Agapius and [Philemon](/saints/philemon-disciple-of-passarion), who are carried along with him, were disciples of the venerable [Passarion](/saints/passarion-the-presbyter) and his fellow-ascetics. The memory of all three, it adds, is recorded in the Jerusalem Kanonarion, at page ninety of Archimandrite Kallistos's edition.

Nothing else is there. The page gives him no year of birth and none of death, no birthplace, no monastery, no account of his asceticism, no relics and no burial place, and it prints neither apolytikion nor kontakion for him. It does not even say whether he outlived his master. Passarion it dates by the years of Patriarch Juvenal of Jerusalem; it dates the two disciples not at all, and no year is carried across to them here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/10/index.aspx) and [the life of the three](https://www.saint.gr/1544/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
