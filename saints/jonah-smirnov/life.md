# Jonah (Smirnov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him last of three in one line for 23 декабря ст. ст.: «Сщмч. Василия пресвитера и прмч. Макария и Ионы Смирнова (1938).» He is the only one of the three whose surname the line gives, and the rank word that governs his name, «прмч.», makes him a venerable-martyr; of place, year of birth and narrative the line says nothing.

Behind that «Ионы» stands the calendar's own name page 14727, which carries «Иона (Смирнов), прмч.» and settles the surname as his rather than the other two men's — the first is «Василий (Спасский)» on name page 9511 and the second «Макарий (Миронов)» on 9223. That page lists two days, this 23 декабря and «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and carries no life; neither does the day page.

So this folder says what the calendar says and no more: a Russian monastic of that name, who died a martyr in 1938 and whom this calendar keeps on this day with a priest and a hieromonk.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 23 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261223.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14727.html), which carries no life; read 13 October 2026.*
