# Apollos (under Julian)

saint.gr says that the life of Apollos was written by Saint Jerome, whom it keeps on 15 June, and that Apollos lived in the years of the emperor Julian the Apostate, 361 to 363. From a young age he followed the monastic life in the region of Egypt. He practised his asceticism in the desert, and God counted him worthy to reach great heights of holiness, granting him the gift of foresight and the gift of working wonders. Apollos, the page says, fell asleep in peace.

That is the whole of what saint.gr prints of him. There is no birthplace beyond Egypt, no year of birth and none of death, no place of burial, and no troparion or kontakion. Above the life the page sets a couplet of verse, «Χριστὸν βιώσας μέχρι καὶ τέλους βίον, / Θραύει πονηροῦ πᾶν Ἀπολλὼς τὸ θράσος» — having lived a life in Christ to the very end, Apollos breaks every insolence of the evil one. The page names Jerome as the source of the life and credits the Apostoliki Diakonia of the Church of Greece.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 25 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/25/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1158/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
