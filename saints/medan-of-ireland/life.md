# Medan of Ireland

saint.gr's calendar names him with two companions and gives the three of them one sentence between them. The line for 4 June reads «Όσιοι Κρουϊδανός, Δαγανός και Μεδανός οι εξ Ιρλανδίας», the venerable Cruidan, Dagan and Medan of Ireland, and the page behind it says only this: the venerable fathers Cruidan, Dagan and Medan came from Ireland and lived in the sixth century after Christ; they were disciples of Saint [Petroc](/saints/petroc-of-cornwall), whom the same day keeps; and they fell asleep in peace.

So what is recorded of him is a country, a century, a master and a death in peace. The page gives him no monastery of his own, no office, no wonder and no relic, and no year at all; it prints neither verse nor hymn. His fellow disciples are [Cruidan](/saints/cruidan-of-ireland) and [Dagan](/saints/dagan-of-ireland), and the three folders carry the same sentence because the source gives them nothing apart.
