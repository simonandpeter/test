# Paul

Nothing of Paul’s life has come down. The Greek synaxarion keeps him on
10 September, bishop of Nicaea, and says it has no details; the Orthodox
Church in America, which keeps him with [Peter](/saints/peter-bishop-of-nicaea)
of Nicaea the same day, says the same — no account of his life is preserved,
and his name first appears in the so-called Petrine Greek Prologue of the
eleventh century. That is the whole of it. The Russian calendar keeps the
two bishops together on its own reckoning, and
[Paul the Obedient](/saints/paul-the-obedient-of-the-caves) of the Kyiv
Caves is kept on this day as his namesake.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 10 Σεπτεμβρίου —
[the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2630/saint.aspx); the Orthodox Church in
America’s line for 10 September — [the day’s lives](https://www.oca.org/saints/all-lives/2026/09/10);
read 5 September 2026.*
