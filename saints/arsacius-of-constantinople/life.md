# Arsacius of Constantinople

saint.gr's calendar for 11 Οκτωβρίου keeps three patriarchs of Constantinople in one entry, and Arsacius is the second of them. He too came from Tarsus of Cilicia, the page says, and he was the brother of the patriarch [Nectarius](/saints/nectarius-of-constantinople). He was a presbyter of the church of Constantinople, and he was called to the patriarchal throne as the successor of [John Chrysostom](/saints/john-chrysostom) when he had already passed his eightieth year.

The page gives his patriarchate as 404 to 405, and says that having lived quietly and according to Christ he fell asleep in peace after a short patriarchate. Its distich for him reads «Τὸν Χριστὸν ἐν γῇ Ἀρσάκιος δοξάσας, / Πρὸς αὐτόν ἐλθών, ἀντεδοξάσθη πλέον» — having glorified Christ on earth, Arsacius came to him and was glorified the more in return.

The third patriarch of the entry is [Sisinnius](/saints/sisinnius-of-constantinople). saint.gr prints no hymn under the entry, and gives Arsacius no birthplace beyond Tarsus and no year but the two of his patriarchate and the 405 of its head line. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/11/index.aspx) and [the life the three patriarchs share](https://www.saint.gr/2086/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
