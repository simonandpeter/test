# Cronion, companion of Alexander

saint.gr's calendar for 30 Οκτωβρίου keeps «Άγιοι Αλέξανδρος, Κρονίων, Ιουλιανός, Μακάριος και άλλοι Δεκατρείς Μάρτυρες» — four named men and thirteen more. All of them were martyred in the years of the emperor Decius, in the year 250, at Alexandria, the page says, and it gives them no other circumstance: no trial, no governor, and nothing of who any of them was before.

Cronion and [Alexander](/saints/alexander-companion-of-cronion) were the first two: they were put to death after hard torments, having boiling lime poured over them. The next two, [Julian](/saints/julian-companion-of-macarius) and [Macarius](/saints/macarius-companion-of-julian), were tortured just as inhumanly and beheaded at the end. Of the other thirteen martyrs, some were burned alive and some beheaded.

The page's epigram for this pair makes a pun of the name: Cronion bears the boiling lime with Alexander, honouring no son of Kronos. The thirteen are named nowhere, so they are one entry here and not thirteen folders. saint.gr prints no hymn for the company, and carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου».

The Serbian church keeps him on this day, where the Greek calendar keeps his company on 30 Οκτωβρίου, and the Ohrid Prologue of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović names him inside another man's entry: [Julian](/saints/julian-companion-of-macarius) the gouty, who could neither stand nor walk and was carried on a litter before the court for the faith of Christ, was burned alive on the pyre with his disciple — this man — in the time of the emperor Decius, at Alexandria. That is all the Prologue says of him, and it is the only source read that calls him anyone's disciple.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 30 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/30/index.aspx) and [the notice of the company](https://www.saint.gr/2854/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026; and the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 27. фебруар ст. ст. — [the Serbian day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-03-12&prolog=1), read 4 October 2026.*
