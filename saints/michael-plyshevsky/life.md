# Michael (Plyshevsky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Michael third in a company of eight on 7 August, and his is one of the two names in that company with a reading behind it. The entry is «Сщмчч. Александра, Петра, Михаила, Иоанна, Димитрия и Алексия пресвитеров, Елисея диакона и прмч. Афанасия (1937).», and the calendar's name page gives him as «Михаил (Плышевский), протоиерей, сщмч.», Michael Plyshevsky, archpriest.

The reading is the calendar's notice of the twenty-three hieromartyrs of Minsk. He was born in 1862 in the village of Voronichi in the Igumen district of the Minsk province, into a family of hereditary clergy. After the Minsk theological seminary he was ordained priest and appointed rector of the parish church of Shatsk in the same district.

After the Bolsheviks came to power he was arrested more than once. He was offered his freedom for renouncing his priesthood and could not be frightened into it. In 1937 he was arrested again on a charge of counter-revolutionary activity and held in the NKVD prison at Cherven, and there, on the feast of the Transfiguration, he was shot. The calendar keeps him on the day after that feast.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 7 августа ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260807.html) and [the notice of the Minsk hieromartyrs](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4766.htm); read 13 October 2026.*
