# Damian of Pavia

saint.gr says Damian came of distinguished parents and was born in the first half of the seventh century. He became a monk, and in the year 680 came up to the episcopal throne of Pavia in Lombardy. As bishop, having fought hard against the Monothelite heretics, he worked energetically for the reconciliation of Byzantium and the Lombards; and his service to the poor and the sick, in some epidemic that fell upon Lombardy, was admirable. He fell asleep in peace in the year 710.

That is the whole of the page. It glosses his name from δαμάω, to tame — the tamer — and gives no other detail: no birth year within the half-century it names, no city he came from, no monastery, no council he took part in against the Monothelites, no successor in the see, and no relic. It prints neither couplet nor hymn, and the day's line adds only the see.
