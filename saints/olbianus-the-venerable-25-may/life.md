# Olbianus (25 May)

saint.gr names him and gives him no life. Its whole account of the venerable Olbianus is one sentence — «Ο Όσιος Ολβιανός, αφού ασκήτεψε θεοφιλώς, κοιμήθηκε με ειρήνη», the venerable Olbianus, having practised his ascesis in a God-loving way, fell asleep in peace. The page gives him no country, no century, no monastery, no teacher and no relics, and prints no hymn to him.

What stands beside that sentence is a couplet of the synaxarion, which plays on his name — ὄλβιος is blessed, and πανόλβιος wholly blessed: «Tον Oλβιανόν ου πανόλβιος βίος, Πανολβίαν λαχόντα και λήξιν γράφω» — I write of Olbianus, whose life was not wholly blessed, that he obtained a wholly blessed end as well. That is the whole of the entry.
