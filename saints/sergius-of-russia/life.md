# Sergius of Russia

saint.gr's calendar names him and says outright that it has no life to give: «Ο Όσιος Σέργιος (Γιανόβσκυ) κοιμήθηκε με ειρήνη το έτος 1876 μ.Χ. Δεν έχουμε περισσότερες λεπτομέρειες για τον Βίο του Οσίου.» — the venerable Sergius fell asleep in peace in the year 1876, and we have no further details of his life. The day's line calls him «ο εκ Ρωσίας», the one from Russia, and the notice adds a surname in Greek letters, Γιανόβσκυ, which is not turned into Latin letters here because the page prints no such form. There is no hymn and nothing else.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1078/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
