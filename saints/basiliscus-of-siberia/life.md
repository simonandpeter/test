# Basiliscus of Siberia

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Basiliscus of Siberia on 29 December and gives no life. Its line is «Прп. Василиска Сибирского (1824).» — the venerable Basiliscus of Siberia, 1824 — and the calendar’s own numbered name page for this memory carries the surname the line leaves out: «Василиск Сибирский, прп.»

That is the whole of the record. The calendar gives him no grade at all beyond «прп.», venerable, and no `office` was invented for him; the year of death the line gives is 1824; the calendar keeps the memory on this day, inside the afterfeast of the Nativity.  The day page prints seven hymns — two for the gravedigger of the Kyiv Caves, two for the abbot of the Unsleeping and three for the infants of Bethlehem — and none under this name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 29 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261229.html); read 13 October 2026.*
