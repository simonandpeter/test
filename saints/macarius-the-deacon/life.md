# Macarius the Deacon of the Kyiv Caves

The Greek calendar's line for him on 19 Ιανουαρίου is «Όσιος Μακάριος ο Διάκονος εκ Ρωσίας», the venerable Macarius the Deacon, out of Russia, and the page behind it holds four short sentences. It says that he lived between the thirteenth and the fourteenth century, that he was a monk in the monastery of [Saint Theodosius](/saints/theodosius-of-the-kyiv-caves) of the Caves of Kyiv, that God counted him worthy of the gift of working wonders for his spiritual struggle, and that he fell asleep in peace.

No year, no birthplace and no wonder in particular is printed for him, and no hymn. The Greek day keeps a second Macarius out of Russia beside him, [the Faster](/saints/macarius-the-faster), of an earlier century and another of the Kyiv houses; the two are distinct lines with distinct pages, and nothing is carried from one to the other.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 19 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/19/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1143/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
