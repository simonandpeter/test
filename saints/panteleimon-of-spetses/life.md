# Panteleimon of Spetses

saint.gr's calendar for 14 November keeps «Άγιος Παντελεήμων ο Νεομάρτυρας, εν Κρήτη», Panteleimon the new martyr, in Crete, and its whole notice of him is one sentence: the holy Panteleimon came from Spetses and was martyred in Crete in 1848. The head line of the page repeats the year by itself, giving him no year of birth.

That is the whole of the source. The calendar does not say how he died, under whom, or at what age; it prints no hymn for him and names nobody with him. So this folder says what the line says and no more: an islander of Spetses who died for Christ on Crete in 1848, and whom the Greek calendar keeps on 14 November among the new martyrs.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 14 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/14/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/170/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
