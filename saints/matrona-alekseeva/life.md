# Matrona (Alekseeva)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names her in one line for 19 марта ст. ст.: «Прмц. Матроны (1938).» The line declines the forename, gives no surname, no place and no year of birth, and the rank word «Прмц.» — преподобномученица, which on this calendar is the word for a woman under monastic vows who died a martyr — is the only thing it says about her besides the name and the year.

Behind that «Матроны» stands the calendar's own name page 14239, «Матрона (Алексеева), прмц.», which carries the surname the day's line leaves out and nothing else at all. It lists two days: this 19 марта and «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. Both pages carry no life, and the day page prints no hymn for her.

The corpus was searched for the surname Алексеева and holds no folder of it. The year 1938 is the worst of the years this calendar's March gives, and the one it gives most often. No monastery and no grade is named, so this folder writes no office, and azbyka.ru was not read for her. So what the calendar prints is the whole of what this folder says: a woman of the Russian church under monastic vows, who died a martyr in 1938, and whom this calendar keeps on this day and in the synaxis of the new martyrs.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 19 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260319.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14239.html), which carries no life; read 8 October 2026.*
