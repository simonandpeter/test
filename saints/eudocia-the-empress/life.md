# Eudocia, wife of Theodosius the Younger

saint.gr says that Eudocia was the daughter of the Athenian philosopher Leontius, and that she was born in the year 401. She studied grammar, rhetoric and philosophy in the best manner there was. When Leontius died he left the whole of his property to his sons, and to her only a hundred gold coins. So she came to Constantinople to claim her rights of inheritance, and there she was married to [Theodosius the Younger](/saints/theodosius-the-younger), through his sister [Pulcheria the Empress](/saints/pulcheria-the-empress), who had been carried away by the rare gifts of the Athenian girl. So she was baptised a Christian and took the name Eudocia, having been called Athenais before.

Eudocia was by her nature a modest woman, the page goes on, and she did not meddle at all in the affairs of the palace. What drew her more was the truth of Christ, and for that reason she sought to visit the Holy Places. When her purpose was accomplished she felt her soul take wing towards the throne of God. Her return to the Reigning City, however, held surprises in store.

Her relations with Theodosius cooled, the page says, because of slanders. So with his leave she went back to Jerusalem, where she founded many monasteries; and with prayer, with study, and «ἐν πάσῃ εὐσεβείᾳ καὶ σεμνότητι» — in all piety and gravity, which the page cites from the first letter to Timothy — she brought her life to its end. saint.gr gives no year for that and prints no hymn to her, and this folder records none.
