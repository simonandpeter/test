# Vera (Morozova)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps her on 13 февраля ст. ст. inside a single line that names fifteen sufferers of 1938 together — ten presbyters, a deacon, a layman and three nuns — and she stands in it as the second of the three women the line closes with, «Веры». The line's rank word over the three of them is «прмцц.», the plural of преподобномученица, a venerable-martyr, which is how the calendar marks a nun.

The calendar prints no life page under this name and no hymn under it either: the one life the line carries belongs to the archpriest at its head, and the troparia the day page prints are for other saints it heads. Nothing further is written here. The line, the rank word, the name the link behind it carries and the year 1938 are the whole of what was read, and in particular she is not identified with any saint of the same name whom the calendar keeps on another day.

**The Russian calendar’s 7 апреля ст. ст. keeps a novice of the Страстной monastery whose household she shared**, and this folder gains its first `related` row from it. azbyka.ru’s life of [Eudocia (Pavlova)](/saints/eudocia-pavlova), read for the civil 20 April on 6 October 2026, says that after the Страстной house was broken up some of its nuns and novices rented a half-basement room on Тихвинская street in Moscow and lived there sewing quilts, keeping their monastic rules and sometimes inviting priests to serve molebens; and it names the household — Eudocia, her sister Пелагия, and the novices «Вера Морозова», «Мария Носова» and «София Селиверстова». They were arrested in one case, which the indictment of 16 November 1937 calls a group of nuns settled in one flat.

**The identification is a reading and rests on two points**: azbyka’s own footnote against that name says «Преподобномученица Вера (Морозова); память празднуется 13/26 февраля», which is the name and the day this folder was read on, and both women are novices of the same decade. Nothing on the Сретенский calendar’s 13 февраля line says it, and the question is put to the author in the batch that wrote this row.
