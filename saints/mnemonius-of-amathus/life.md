# Mnemonius of Amathus

saint.gr's calendar for 16 June names Mnemonius, bishop of Amathus in Cyprus, and prints two sentences about him. The first is that he ordained [Tikhon](/saints/tikhon-of-amathus) deacon, and the page adds in the same breath that Tikhon is honoured on 16 June, the same day as himself. The second is that we have no other information about his life, except that he is referred to in the biography of Saint Tychon.

So the whole of him, in this source, is that he was bishop of Amathus, that he saw what Tikhon was and ordained him, and that Tikhon afterwards succeeded him in the see. The page gives no year, no epigram and no hymn. Under the picture it prints «Η αγιογραφία παρατίθεται με επιφύλαξη», the iconography is given with reserve, which is said of the image and not of the entry.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 16 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/16/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1867/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
