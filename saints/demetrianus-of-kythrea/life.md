# Demetrianus of Kythrea

Demetrianus is unknown to the synaxarists, saint.gr says, but known in the church of Cyprus as bishop of Kythrea — the page gives the see's name three ways, Κηθηρία or Κυθραίων or Χυτρών. He lived in the years of the emperor Theophilus the iconoclast, whose reign the page puts at 829 to 843, and he was born in the village of Syka in the district of Kythrea, of a father who was a priest.

He became a monk, and was then made presbyter by Eustathius, bishop of Chytroi, whom he afterwards succeeded on that throne. Demetrianus shared in the captivity of his flock when the Egyptians carried them off, and afterwards returned with them to Cyprus, where he lived a holy life and died in deep old age. The page gives him no year of his own and prints no hymn for him.

It adds that a fragment of his holy relic is at the monastery of Kykkos in Cyprus.
