# Xanthippe

Xanthippe and [Polyxena](/saints/polyxena-of-spain) were sisters who lived
in Spain in the days of the apostles — under the emperor Claudius, the
Romanian synaxarion says — and first heard the divine teaching of Christ the
Saviour from the apostle Paul when he preached in their country. Xanthippe
and her husband Probus, the governor of Spain, received Christianity;
Polyxena was still a pagan when a man taken with her extraordinary beauty
carried her off by force to Greece, and the Lord kept her unharmed: on the
ship she heard the preaching of the apostle Peter and believed, and reaching
Greece she put herself under the protection of the Christians, who hid her
at Patras in Achaea, where she was baptised by the apostle Andrew the
First-called himself. She saw his wonders, and the patience and meekness
with which he bore his tortures, and stood by the cross on which he was
crucified; after his martyrdom she returned to Spain, where with her elder
sister she turned many pagans to Christ — for about forty years, the Russian
church calendar says, she preached the Gospel in Spain and confirmed
multitudes in the faith, and Xanthippe shared her sister’s labours and
preached in the populous city of Toledo. The Romanian synaxarion tells it a
little differently: Polyxena was baptised by Andrew with her friend Raveca,
and the two came home in the company of the apostle Onesimus, at which
Xanthippe too was baptised, and she preached our Lord Jesus Christ to the
end of her life. Polyxena died about 109, a virgin to the end. The Russian
calendar keeps the two sisters on 23 September of the old style, 6 October,
and the Romanian calendar on 23 September.

*After the life printed by the Russian church calendar at azbyka.ru — [the entry](https://azbyka.ru/days/sv-ksanfippa-toledskaja), read 6 September 2026; the Romanian life at doxologia.ro — [Viața Sfintei Cuvioase Xantipa și a Sfintei Cuvioase Polixenia](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfintei-cuvioase-xantipa-sfintei-cuvioase-polixenia), read 30 August 2026, was all this entry had until then.*
