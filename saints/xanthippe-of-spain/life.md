# Xanthippe

The Romanian synaxarion keeps one short life for the two sisters together. Xanthippe lived in Spain in the time of the emperor Claudius, the wife of Probus, the governor of Spain. It was her sister Polyxena who reached the eastern part of the Roman empire and was baptized there by the Apostle Andrew, together with her friend Raveca; when the two returned to Spain in the company of the Apostle Onesimus, Xanthippe too received baptism, and preached our Lord Jesus Christ to the end of her life.
