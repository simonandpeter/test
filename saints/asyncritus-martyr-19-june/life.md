# Asyncritus (19 June)

saint.gr's calendar for 19 June names Asyncritus the hieromartyr, and prints one sentence about him: he was martyred after being put to death with a knife, and, as Saint Nikodemos the Hagiorite informs us, the saint's synaxis is held «in the martyr church of Saint Isidore within Hagia Eirene which is by the sea». Beside it stands a two-line epigram: slain out of longing for the Lord, Asyncritus is worthily crowned with incomparable crowns.

The page gives no country, no year, no persecutor, no see and no hymn. It carries too the site's note on the name, that Asyncritus means one so much better and higher than all else that nothing can be compared with him. That is the whole of the entry, and this life adds nothing to it.
