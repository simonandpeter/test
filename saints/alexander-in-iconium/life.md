# Alexander in Iconium

saint.gr's calendar for 21 August names the venerable Alexander under the epithet «ο εν Ικονίω», the one in Iconium, and the whole of what it has to say about him is a sentence and a manuscript: «Η μνήμη του Οσίου Αλέξανδρου αναγράφεται στο Σιναϊτικό κώδικα 631. Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του» — his memory is written in the Sinai codex 631, and we have no other details of his life.

So there is no life to tell. The page gives no century, no parents, no monastery, no manner of death and no relics; it does not say whether Iconium is where he was born, where he lived or where he was buried. It does not even print a synaxarion distich for him, as it does for most of the bare entries on this day's neighbours.

What it does do is name its source, which is why this folder exists at all. The entry carries no asterisk and not the sentence saint.gr puts on entries it will not vouch for; it says instead where the commemoration is written down, and leaves the rest unsaid.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 21 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/21/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/825/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
