# Valerinus

saint.gr keeps a martyr Valerinus on 29 November, and the whole of what it says of him is one sentence: the holy Valerinus — or Valerian, it adds in brackets — was martyred by the sword. «Ο Άγιος Ουαλερίνος (ή Ουαλεριανός) μαρτύρησε δια ξίφους.» No country, no century, no emperor, no judge, no companions and no burial.

His couplet is the only other thing on the page, and it is put in his own mouth: for an all-honoured crown I will bear even the sword, the martyr Valerinus cried out. The page prints no hymn for him and no relic. The uncertainty over his name is the page's own, and both forms are recorded here as it has them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 29 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/29/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3112/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
