# Cyprian of Nicomedia

Cyprian was one of four martyrs of Nicomedia — Straton, Philip, Eutychian and Cyprian — who suffered around 303. Going to the public spectacles, they taught the people to abandon the worship of idols and turned many pagans to Christ; the governor, noticing that the shows had begun to empty, summoned them, and when they confessed their faith in Christ firmly he had them thrown to the beasts. The beasts did not touch them; then they were tortured and thrown into the fire.

The Romanian calendar keeps him on 17 August. Doxologia.ro's short life says he suffered in the persecution of the emperor Diocletian (284–305); that he was seized while preaching Christ and shut up with wild beasts, and came out of it unharmed by divine providence; and that in the end he was burned alive in the city of Nicomedia in Asia Minor.
