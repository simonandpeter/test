# Nicholas (Krylov)

The calendar's own line is the whole of what is recorded of him here. The Сретенский monastery's calendar for 28 ноября ст. ст. prints «Сщмч. Николая пресвитера (1941).» — the hieromartyr Nicholas, a presbyter, 1941 — and the name page behind that forename gives him the surname the line leaves out and a grade the line does not: «Николай (Крылов), протоиерей, сщмч.», an archpriest.

So nothing is said here of where he was born, where he served, when or by whom he was arrested, or where he died: no page read says any of it. The calendar carries no life page for him. The year 1941 is the only year it gives, and it sets him four years after the company of six the same day names and three after the one laywoman.

His name page lists one other day, «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church. One row per church is the schema, so that day is recorded here and he does not render on it.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 28 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261128.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9375.html); read 7 October 2026.*
