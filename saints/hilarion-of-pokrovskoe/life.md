# Hilarion of Pokrovskoe

saint.gr's calendar for 28 Μαρτίου names the venerable Hilarion of Russia, and the page behind the line is one sentence long: he practised ascesis in the monastery the page calls Pokrovskoe Ozerskoe, in Russia; he was a disciple of the venerable Euphrosynus the Wonderworker, whose memory the page puts on 15 May; and he fell asleep in peace in the year 1476. A second sentence adds that his memory is repeated on 21 October.

That is the whole of the source. There is no year of birth, no account of how he came to the monastery, no office, no wonder, no relic and no hymn, and nothing at all of what the teacher taught or what the disciple did after him. The Greek page gives the monastery's name only in Greek letters, and no Russian source was read to find how it is written at home; the epithet this folder carries follows the Greek and claims nothing more than that.

Euphrosynus has no folder in this corpus, so he is named here without a link, and the reader who wants that teacher's life will not find it on this page either: it gives him a day and a surname and no more. The note the page attaches to the name — «ΙΛΑΡΙΩΝ (από την λέξη ιλαρός) = ο φαιδρός, ο αισιόδοξος», the cheerful, the hopeful — is about the word.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/28/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2639/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
