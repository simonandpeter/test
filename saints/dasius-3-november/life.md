# Dasius (3 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 3 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιοι Δάσιος, Σέβηρος, Άνδρωνας, Θεόδοτος και Θεοδότη», and the page behind that line says one thing only: the holy Dasius, [Severus](/saints/severus-3-november), [Andron](/saints/andron), [Theodotus](/saints/theodotus-3-november) and [Theodote](/saints/theodote-3-november) were martyred by the sword. There is no city, no persecutor, no year, nothing of who the five were to one another, and nothing of their relics.

The page's epigram adds the one distinction it makes among them, which is of sex and not of name: let the woman too be a fellow martyr to the men, cut down along with the four who were cut. Each of the five has a heading of his or her own on the page, which is why there are five folders here and not one.

This folder takes its name from the day, because the calendar gives it nothing else to take a name from. No hymn is printed. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands, bare as it is.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/03/index.aspx) and [the notice of the five](https://www.saint.gr/2874/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
