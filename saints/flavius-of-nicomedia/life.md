# Flavius of Nicomedia

saint.gr's calendar names three men in one line and gives them one sentence. The holy hieromartyr Flavius was Bishop of Nicomedia, it says, and was martyred in the reign of the emperor Diocletian, who held the empire from 284 to 305, together with the brothers [Augustus](/saints/augustus-brother-of-augustinus) and [Augustinus](/saints/augustinus-brother-of-augustus).

That is the whole of the page. It gives the see and the reign and no more: no city of his birth, no judge, no manner of death for any of the three, no relics and no hymn, and no couplet such as it prints for most of this day's martyrs. The word it uses of the two who died with him, «των αυταδέλφων», makes them brothers by both parents; it does not say they were kin to the bishop. Nothing is claimed here beyond the name, the rank, the see, the reign, the company and the day.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/07/index.aspx) and [the life of the three](https://www.saint.gr/4011/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
