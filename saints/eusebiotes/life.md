# Eusebiotes

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives him a single sentence: «Ο Άγιος Ευσεβιώτης (ή Ευβίοτος) μαρτύρησε δια πυρός» — the holy Eusebiotes, or Eubiotos, was martyred by fire. The page offers the two forms of the name side by side and settles on neither, and it says nothing further: no year, no city, no emperor and no judge.

Above that sentence it sets the two-line couplet the synaxaria carry for him: «Εὐσεβιώτη προσδοκῶντι τὰ στέφη, / τὸ πυρπολοῦν πῦρ ὡς Ἀερμὼν ἦν δρόσος» — to Eusebiotes, who was looking for the crowns, the burning fire was as the dew of Hermon. That is the whole of what the Greek calendar keeps of him, and the couplet is the only comment it makes on his death.
