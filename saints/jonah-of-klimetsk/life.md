# Jonah of Klimetsk

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery gives him a short reading and hangs the whole of it on one night. In the world he was called Иоанн; he took the monastic habit and founded the Trinity monastery of Klimetsk by a vow. In 1490, it says, he was caught by a storm on Lake Onega, and when there was no hope left of being saved he called on the Lord, asking Him to keep his life for repentance and for the service of God. The waves threw the boat onto a shoal by the shore.

There, the reading goes on, he heard the voice of the Lord commanding him to build a monastery in the Name of the Life-giving Trinity, and found Her holy icon wonderfully on a juniper bush. He did what he had been told and built the house with two churches, one in the Name of the Most Holy Trinity and one in honour of святитель Николай, the protector of those who sail and travel. Offered the rank of abbot he refused it and remained in his own monastery a simple monk.

He died on 6 June 1534, which is the day the calendar keeps him on; afterwards a church in honour of Захария and Елисавета was built over his relics. His name page spells the epithet two ways, Климецкий and Клименецкий, and adds Олонецкий for the region; it also lists a second day, 28 марта, which was not read here. The troparion the day page prints for him adds one thing the reading does not have at all: that the fatherland he left was «великий Новград».

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 6 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260606.html), [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1248.htm) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/1041.html); read 8 October 2026.*
