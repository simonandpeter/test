# Agapius of Gaza

Agapius and [Thecla](/saints/thecla-of-gaza) suffered for Christ at Gaza in Palestinian Caesarea in 304, the year and the city in which [Timothy](/saints/timothy-of-gaza) the teacher was burned: they were given to the beasts. That is all the Sretensky calendar’s life says of them; the Prologue keeps the three together for the Serbian calendar.

The Romanian calendar keeps the three of them on 19 August, and doxologia.ro prints one short notice under all three names. It tells first of [Timothy](/saints/timothy-of-gaza), of Palestine by birth, perfect in life and in word and made a teacher of the right faith, who was seized, stood at Gaza before Urban, declared himself on the Christians' side, was beaten and tortured in every way and at last put into the fire. Then it says that in the same city Agapius and [Thecla](/saints/thecla-of-gaza) of Byzia, holding to the faith of Christ, were given to the beasts and took the crown of martyrdom through them.

*After the life printed by the Sretensky calendar (days.pravoslavie.ru) — [the entry](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4479.htm); The Ohrid Prologue for 1 September 2026 (19. август ст. ст.), as printed by the Православни подсетник (pravoslavno.rs), tells it for the Serbian calendar — [the day’s page](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-09-01&prolog=1); read 23 August 2026; and doxologia.ro's calendar for 19 august — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/19-august) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-cuvios-agapie), read 24 September 2026.*
