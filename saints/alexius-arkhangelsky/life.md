# Alexius (Arkhangelsky)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him in a single line for 13 июня ст. ст. — «Сщмч. Алексия пресвитера (1918).» — a hieromartyr and a presbyter who died in 1918. The name link behind that line, name page 13725, gives the calendar's own fuller form, «Алексий (Архангельский), иерей», which adds the surname the line leaves out and the rank of priest.

That name page heads itself «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 13 июня», two days, the first of them the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia rather than a day of his own. The calendar prints no life page for him and no hymn under his name, and none is given here: those are the facts it records, and the whole of them.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 13 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260613.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13725.html); read 9 October 2026.*
