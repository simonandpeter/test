# Clement

Clement is mentioned in the letter to the Philippians (4:3) as a fellow-athlete of the apostle Paul; he became bishop of Sardis and died after many sufferings for the support of his flock and the spread of the truth of the Gospel. The Greek calendar keeps him on 10 September with [Apelles](/saints/apelles-the-apostle) and [Luke](/saints/luke-apostle-of-laodicea).
