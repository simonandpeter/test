# Clement

Clement is the fellow-worker Paul names in the letter to the Philippians
(4:3), just after the women Euodia and Syntyche who had laboured with him in
the gospel — one of those, the apostle says, whose names are in the book of
life. He became bishop of Sardis (the Orthodox Church in America’s life
spells it Sardica) and died after many trials borne for the sake of his
flock, to keep them firm and hand the truth of the gospel down to them.
Eusebius, in the third book of his history, mistook him for Clement of Rome.
The Greek calendar keeps him on 10 September with
[Apelles](/saints/apelles-the-apostle) and
[Luke](/saints/luke-apostle-of-laodicea), and the three are kept together
again on 22 April; the Russian calendar keeps them the same day of its own
reckoning.
