# Theodore of Moscow

saint.gr says Theodore was born at Moscow in the year 1557 and was the son of the tsar Ivan the Terrible, whom he succeeded on the throne on 18 March 1584. He studied church matters, the page says, rather than political ones. In spite of the weakness of his nature, under his rule the standing of Russia was raised: the kingdom of Kazan was annexed to it, Siberia was subdued, and diplomatic and commercial relations with the European states were developed.

Theodore looked after the Greek clergy who had fled their own country, and the page names two who were in Russia in his time besides the rest — Ignatius, archbishop of Cyprus, and Saint Arsenius, bishop of Elassona, honoured on 13 April, who took up the pastoral charge of the see of Suzdal. Theodore fell asleep in the year 1598 after an illness, and left the government of the state in the hands of his wife Irene.

That is the whole of saint.gr's notice. The day's line calls him «ο Πρίγκιπας», the prince, though the notice itself has him succeeding his father on the throne; no hymn is printed for him.
