# Simon of Moscow

Simon was a monk in the monastery of the Holy Trinity of Saint Sergius, saint.gr says, and in September of the year 1495 he was elected metropolitan of Moscow. He fell asleep in peace in the year 1511 and was buried in the church of the Dormition of the Mother of God in the Kremlin. That is the whole of the page's account of him: two sentences, an election and a burial.

Nothing else is printed — no year of birth, no kindred, no act of the sixteen years he held the see, no wonder and no word of his. The page adds only its gloss on the name, that Σίμων is a Hebrew word for one obedient to God and loving to men, and heads him «(; - 1511)» with the year of his birth left as a question mark. The feast type is the plain fixed one, «Σταθερή. Εορτάζει στις 30 Απριλίου εκάστου έτους».
