# Dositheia of Russia

saint.gr names her and gives no life. Its calendar for 4 February prints «Αγία Δοσιθέα Βασίλισσα της Ρωσίας», Dositheia, queen of Russia, and the page behind that line is one sentence: «Η Αγία Δοσιθέα κοιμήθηκε με ειρήνη το έτος 1810 μ.Χ.» — she fell asleep in peace in the year 1810. Everything else on the page is the site's own dictionary note on what the name Δοσίθεος means.

So what the corpus holds of her is what the calendar holds: the name, the year of her repose, that it was peaceful, and the title the Greek line gives her, Βασίλισσα της Ρωσίας. No birth, no place, no account of her life, no hymn and no verses; and the Russian calendar, which would be the one to read next for a saint the Greek describes this way, was not read.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 4 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/04/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1375/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
