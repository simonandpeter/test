# Eata of Hexham

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Οκτωβρίου names Eata of Hexham and gives him four sentences. He was one of the twelve boys whom Saint Aidan trained at Lindisfarne, the page says, sending the reader to 31 August for Aidan. Eata became a monk, then a priest, and at last abbot of Melrose, and he founded the monastery of Ripon in Yorkshire. He fell asleep in 686 and was buried near the church of Wilfrid at Hexham.

That is the whole of the entry: no couplet, no hymn, no relics and no see, and nothing of what he did at Hexham itself, which the line names him after. The page writes his name, his teacher's and all four places in Latin letters and gives no Greek form of any of them.
