# Vladimir (Terentyev)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 18 февраля ст. ст. in a line of four words: «Прп. Владимира исповедника (1933).» The rank word is «Прп.», venerable, which the calendar uses of a monastic, and the line adds «исповедника», confessor — one who suffered for the faith and did not die of it. The name link behind the line carries the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line itself leaves out, and one more thing the line does not say: «Владимир (Терентьев), игумен, прп., исповедник», an abbot.

The calendar prints no life page under his name and no hymn under it either: the troparia on the day page are for two of the older saints it heads. Nothing further is written here — not which house he was abbot of, and not what his confession cost him. The line, its two rank words, the office the name link adds, the name itself and the year 1933 are the whole of what was read, and in particular he is not identified with any saint of the same name whom the calendar keeps on another day.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 18 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260218.html); read 3 October 2026.*
