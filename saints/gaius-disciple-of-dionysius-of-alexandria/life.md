# Gaius, disciple of Dionysius of Alexandria

Gaius was one of the eight disciples of [Dionysius of Alexandria](/saints/dionysius-of-alexandria), bishop of that city, who were martyred with him under Valerian in 254, the Greek synaxarion says: disciples in every way worthy of their teacher in the fervour of their piety and the firmness of their faith, who were bound with him and shut in a narrow place, harshly tortured there, and so gave up their souls to God. The entry names six of the eight — Faustus, Peter, Paul, Eusebius and Chaeremon beside Gaius — and gives none of them a story of his own.

The Greek calendar keeps Gaius with Dionysius and the others on 3 October. The entry says his memory is repeated on 4 October with Faustus, Eusebius and Chaeremon.

saint.gr’s own entry for 4 October calls Faustus, Gaius, Eusebius and Chaeremon deacons and disciples of Dionysius, and says that Eusebius and Chaeremon in particular buried the precious relics of the holy martyrs, and that in the end they too boldly confessed Christ and were beheaded; its verse has the four deacons joined to Christ. It gives each of them a death in 264, where the 3 October entry dates Dionysius’s martyrdom, and theirs with him, to 254, so the dates here run across both.
