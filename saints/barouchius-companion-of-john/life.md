# Barouchius, companion of John

saint.gr's calendar for 27 Μαρτίου names two martyrs on one line, «Άγιοι Ιωάννης και Βαρούχιος οι Μάρτυρες», and the page behind it has one sentence for them both: «Οι Άγιοι Μάρτυρες Βαρούχιος και Ιωάννης τελειώθηκαν διά ξίφους» — the holy martyrs Barouchius and John were perfected by the sword. The source gives no year, no city, no emperor, no judge, no relic and no hymn, and the sentence is all the narrative there is.

Its couplet, however, singles him out: «Οὐ θάρσος ἧττον Ἰωάννου πρὸς ξίφος, Βαρουχίῳ ὑπῆρχεν, ἀλλὰ καὶ πλέον» — Barouchius had no less boldness than [John](/saints/john-companion-of-barouchius) before the sword, but even more. It is a small thing to know about a man, and it is the only thing the Greek calendar says of him beyond his name, his companion and his death.

The page writes the name Βαρούχιος, which is not the Βαρούχ of the prophet the corpus keeps in September; nothing here links the two, and no other form of the name is recorded, because the page prints none.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 27 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/27/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1265/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
