# George of Azkuri

saint.gr names him and gives almost no life. Its whole account is one sentence and a half: «Ο Άγιος Γεώργιος (Μαζκουερέλι) έζησε μεταξύ του 9ου και του 10ου αιώνα μ.Χ. στη Γεωργία και ήταν Επίσκοπος Αζκουρίας. Κοιμήθηκε οσίως με ειρήνη.» — George, surnamed Mazkuereli, lived between the ninth and the tenth century in Georgia and was bishop of Azkuri; he fell asleep in holiness, in peace.

That is all the page says. It gives him no year of birth and no year of death, names no monastery, no metropolitan and no see beyond Azkuri itself, tells no incident of his life, names no relics, prints no couplet and prints no hymn. The note above his line on the page is the site's standing etymology of the name Γεώργιος and is not about this man.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 2 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/02/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3359/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
