# Paul (Favoritov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him third among the seven hieromartyrs of its seventh line for 16 декабря and gives him no life. The line is «Сщмчч. Аркадия, еп. Бежецкого, Илии, Павла, Феодосия, Владимира, Александра пресвитеров, прмч. Макария (1937).» — a bishop, five presbyters and a hieromonk, 1937 — and the calendar’s own name page adds the surname and the grade the line leaves out: «Павел (Фаворитов), иерей, сщмч.»

That is the whole of the record for him. Six readings hang behind the line, and every one of them belongs to one of the other six men and says nothing of a third priest of that name. He was a priest; he died in 1937, in the years the Russian church counts its new martyrs; and nothing is said here of where he served, or when he was taken, because the source does not say it.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 16 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261216.html); read 13 October 2026.*
