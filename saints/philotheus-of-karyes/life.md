# Philotheus of Karyes

saint.gr's calendar for 5 December keeps Philotheus of Karyes, and what it prints of him is his office and his character rather than a narrative. He was the elder of the Lavriote cell of the Holy Archangels, the cell of Iagaris, at Karyes of the Holy Mountain, which the page says survives to this day; and he was the elder of the venerable [Nectarios](/saints/nectarios-of-bitola), whose memory the same calendar keeps on the same day.

He was, in the words the page quotes, a layman as far as learning went, but in virtue exceedingly well trained; and he was adorned with the gift of discernment and of foreknowledge. He died, it says, in deep old age, the truly God-loving Philotheus, and went away to the Christ he had longed for. He was buried in his own cell. A full service in his honour was afterwards composed by a hymnographer of the Holy Mountain.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3161/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
