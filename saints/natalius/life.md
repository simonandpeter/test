# Natalius

Doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 30 mai reads «Sfântul Mucenic Natalie», the holy martyr Natalius, and the page the calendar links for him carries no Viață tab and no Tropar tab. It prints his name, the date, and the readings appointed — the epistle from 2 Timothy 2, be strong in the grace that is in Christ Jesus, and the gospel from John 15 and 16, if the world hates you, know that it hated me before it hated you. Nothing else about him is on the page.

The rank in the line is the whole of what is stated: a man who died a martyr, kept on 30 May. The Romanian «Natalie» is a masculine form here, and the line's «Sfântul Mucenic» agrees with it. No place, no century and no year is entered for him, because the source gives none.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and adds one fact. The holy martyr Natalius, saint.gr says, was martyred by the sword. Its distich over him says that the books bear witness that Natalius came into the choir of the martyrs by the sword, and that is the whole of it: no place, no year, no judge and no companion.
