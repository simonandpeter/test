# Marciana (24 May)

doxologia.ro's calendar names her and gives no life. The line for 24 mai reads «Sfânta Muceniță Marciana» — the holy martyr Marciana — with no epithet, no city, no country and no century, and her own page on the site carries that line as heading and title and nothing else: no Viață tab, no Tropar tab, nothing that would place her or tell her from another woman of the name.

The display name here carries the day for that reason. This folder asserts one thing only: that the Romanian church keeps a woman martyr named Marciana on 24 May. No year is claimed, no place is pinned, and no other calendar has been checked for her.

The Greek calendar keeps a Marciane on this same day and gives her what the Romanian line withholds: a company and a death. saint.gr names her among the martyrs who suffered with [Meletius the Stratelates](/saints/meletius-the-stratelates) in the years of the emperor Elagabalus, who reigned from 218 to 222. She is one of three women — with [Palladia](/saints/palladia-companion-of-meletius) and [Susanna](/saints/susanna-companion-of-meletius) — who believed at the sight of the martyrdom of Meletius and of [Serapion](/saints/serapion-martyr-24-may) the Egyptian and [Callinicus](/saints/callinicus-the-magus) the magus, and gave their lives for Christ; and the couplet the synaxarion sets over the three of them says they are killed with wood for not sacrificing to Pallas.

The same page names, on the same day and in the same company, [John](/saints/john-companion-of-meletius) and [Stephen](/saints/stephen-companion-of-meletius), the two soldiers denounced with Meletius; the two infants [Cyriacus](/saints/cyriacus-the-infant) and [Christian](/saints/christian-the-infant); twelve komites and tribunes whom it names one by one; and a multitude beyond them whose number it puts at eleven thousand two hundred and eight. That the Marciana of the Romanian line is this Marciane is a match on the day and on the name, which is all either calendar gives to match on, and it is recorded here as such.

The Russian church calendar keeps the whole of this company on 24 мая ст. ст. Its line names seven and then «и с ними 1218 воинов с женами и детьми (ок. 218)», and the life page behind that line enumerates part of the 1218 by name. This is one of the names that list gives of the women and children who suffered: «Из пострадавших жен и детей известны имена святых мучениц Маркианы, Сосанны, Палладии, младенцев Кириака и Христиана.» The calendar says nothing else of her at all — no page of her own, no epithet, no year, and no sentence of narrative.

What it does give is the company. Their commander was the military governor of the province of Galatia under the emperor the calendar dates 218 to 222. He was a Christian and prayed that the Lord would put an end to the pagan error; the demons that lived in the shrines, terrified by his prayer, went into dogs whose howling put the province in fear; and he destroyed the maddened dogs and pulled down the shrines with his soldiers, was taken before the governor Максимиан, was tortured for refusing to sacrifice and died confessing Christ. The tribunes Стефан and Иоанн were beheaded. The rest of the regiment, who also declared themselves Christians, were put to the sword with their wives and children, 1218 of them, or by some sources, the calendar says, eleven thousand; and Феодор and Фавст were burned with many others.
