# Elizabeth of Adrianople

doxologia.ro gives Elizabeth no life of her own. The page under her name prints the same four sentences that stand under each of the four women's names, and they are the synaxarion notice of the bishop who converted them.

Alexander, it says, was a bishop, and teaching and converting and baptising many he was seized by the governor; tormented much and pressed to sacrifice to the idols, he would not submit. Heraclius the soldier, seeing his endurance, believed in Christ; he too was tormented first at length, and then they cut off his head.

Alexander went on working wonders and was healed of his wounds, and by the help and grace of God he drew to the faith of Christ four women — Theodota, Glyceria, Anna and Elizabeth — who stood before the governor and rebuked the deceit of the idols, and were beheaded. Then Alexander himself was ended by the sword.

That is everything doxologia prints. It names no place and no year; the calendar's line for Glyceria alone adds Adrianople. The other three women are [Theodota](/saints/theodota-of-adrianople), [Glyceria](/saints/glyceria-of-adrianople), [Anna](/saints/anna-of-adrianople). There is no troparion on any of the four pages.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 22 octombrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/22-octombrie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/sfanta-mucenita-elisaveta); read 24 September 2026.*
