# Leontius the Canonarch

saint.gr's calendar for 18 June names Leontius the Canonarch, and gives him two sentences. He settled, the page says, in the Lavra of the Caves at a young age; and because of his beautiful voice, once he had been taught the sacred letters, he received the office of canonarch, which the page glosses as chanter. He fell asleep young, in the fourteenth century.

That is all the page prints: no birthplace, no name of a teacher, no year, no epigram and no hymn. The fourteenth century is the page's own dating and the only one it gives, and no closer year is inferred here. He is not the myrrh-gushing Leontius of Dionysiou nor the shepherd nor the soldier martyr of Tripoli, all three of whom the same 18 June keeps as entries of their own.

The Russian calendar gives him a short life of its own, and it agrees with the Greek one in everything the two share while adding three things. It says he entered the Kiev Caves monastery in his young years and was tonsured there, so that the house the Greek page calls the Lavra of the Caves is named; that for his selfless labour of salvation the Lord glorified him with the gracious gift of working wonders; and that the relics of the ascetic lie in the Дальние пещеры, the Far Caves. It repeats the fourteenth century and his dying young, and gives no closer year.

The Russian day page carries four hymns for him, which is more than it gives most of the saints it heads: a troparion and a kontakion of his own, and a troparion and a kontakion it marks «общий», common, to the two canonarchs who rest in the Far Caves, himself and «Геронтий». Both of the common pair address a holy pair and are his as much as the other man's, and both are copied here with that said in their source notes. The life adds that his memory is kept also on 28 августа, in the synaxis of the Kiev Caves saints, which is a synaxis and not a second feast.
