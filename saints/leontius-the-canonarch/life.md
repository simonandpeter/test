# Leontius the Canonarch

saint.gr's calendar for 18 June names Leontius the Canonarch, and gives him two sentences. He settled, the page says, in the Lavra of the Caves at a young age; and because of his beautiful voice, once he had been taught the sacred letters, he received the office of canonarch, which the page glosses as chanter. He fell asleep young, in the fourteenth century.

That is all the page prints: no birthplace, no name of a teacher, no year, no epigram and no hymn. The fourteenth century is the page's own dating and the only one it gives, and no closer year is inferred here. He is not the myrrh-gushing Leontius of Dionysiou nor the shepherd nor the soldier martyr of Tripoli, all three of whom the same 18 June keeps as entries of their own.
