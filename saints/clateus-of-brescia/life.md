# Clateus of Brescia

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives him one sentence. The line for 4 June reads «Άγιος Κλατέος ο Ιερομάρτυρας Επίσκοπος Βρεσκίας», Saint Clateus the hieromartyr, bishop of Brescia, and the page behind it says this: the holy hieromartyr Clateus was the first or the second bishop of Brescia in Lombardy, in Italy, and suffered a martyr's death in the year 64, under the emperor Nero, who reigned from 54 to 68.

So what is recorded of him is a see, a place in its succession the page will not fix, a year, a reign and the rank of a hieromartyr. It does not say who consecrated him, how long he held the see, what he was accused of or how he died; it prints neither verse nor hymn for him. The year 64 is the year of the fire at Rome and of Nero's persecution, and the page makes nothing of that beyond naming the emperor.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 4 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/04/index.aspx) and [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/4419/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
