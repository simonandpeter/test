# Tatiana (Besfamilnaya)

She was born on 10 January 1866 in Moscow, and at the age of five her mother gave her to the orphanage of the Nativity convent. She lived there until the convent was closed in 1923, and in all that time, the page says, she did not once go outside its walls. She laboured at various obediences and finished the convent's parish school, and in 1920 she was tonsured a nun of the lesser habit. Her surname, Besfamilnaya, means «without a surname», which is what the orphanage gave the children it took in.

After the house was closed she lived in a flat near it until her arrest, keeping to her monastic rule and earning her living by needlework and sewing. She was arrested on 29 September 1937 with [Demetrius (Dobroserdov)](/saints/demetrius-dobroserdov), archbishop of Mozhaysk, and seven others, and she admitted no guilt. The life records no interrogation of her beyond that: of the nine people it covers she is the one it quotes not at all.

The NKVD troika sentenced the company to be shot on 17 October 1937, and on 21 October she was shot at the Butovo range outside Moscow and buried with the others in a common unmarked grave. She was numbered among the new martyrs and confessors of Russia for church-wide veneration at the Jubilee Council of Bishops in August 2000.
