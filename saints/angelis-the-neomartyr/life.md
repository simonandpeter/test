# Angelis the New Martyr of Constantinople

Angelis was a goldsmith by trade. Once, making merry in Constantinople with acquaintances who had renounced Christianity, he put a Turkish turban on his head as a joke; the Turks took it as a renunciation of the Christian faith and an acceptance of Islam, and pressed him to convert. He rejected their enticing proposals with loathing and accepted martyrdom for the love of Christ without counting his wife and six children: do what you will, he told the vizier — beat, cut, slaughter, burn me, throw me to the beasts, drown me. The synaxarion’s life runs on past the point read here; its verse says he was a man in nature and, beheaded, was seen an angel with the angels.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 1 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/885/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
