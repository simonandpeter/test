# Inna

Inna was one of three martyrs whom doxologia.ro's calendar keeps together on 20 ianuarie and whose life it tells in a single paragraph. They were, the page says, of a country in the north, and were taken by idol-worshipping barbarians and brought before the ruler of the place; and he, seeing that they confessed Christ, condemned them to die by freezing. The page gives no year, no emperor and no name for their country.

The three were bound to stakes driven into the water of a river. It was winter. The water froze and became as motionless as the motionless bodies of the saints, and so they ended their lives and gave up their blessed souls into the hands of God. The other two are [Pinna](/saints/pinna) and [Rimma](/saints/rimma), and nothing in the Romanian page distinguishes one of the three from another.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 20 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/20-ianuarie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfintilor-mucenici-pin-rim); read 19 September 2026.*
