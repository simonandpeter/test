# Inna

Inna was one of three martyrs whom doxologia.ro's calendar keeps together on 20 ianuarie and whose life it tells in a single paragraph. They were, the page says, of a country in the north, and were taken by idol-worshipping barbarians and brought before the ruler of the place; and he, seeing that they confessed Christ, condemned them to die by freezing. The page gives no year, no emperor and no name for their country.

The three were bound to stakes driven into the water of a river. It was winter. The water froze and became as motionless as the motionless bodies of the saints, and so they ended their lives and gave up their blessed souls into the hands of God. The other two are [Pinna](/saints/pinna) and [Rimma](/saints/rimma), and nothing in the Romanian page distinguishes one of the three from another.

The Greek calendar keeps the three together on the same day and says where they came from and what was done to them. saint.gr says that Innas, [Pinnas](/saints/pinna) and [Rimmas](/saints/rimma) were from the region of Scythia and were called «οι κρυστάλλινοι», the crystalline ones; that they bore witness after being bound to a piece of wood, in a time of terrible frost, and set in a frozen river; that dreadful and unbearable were the pains their naked flesh was tried with there, but that their souls cast off nothing of their warm devotion, and so they received the crown of witness. The epigram over them plays on the same word: «Θάλψις δεχέσθω τοὺς Ἀθλητὰς τοῦ κρύους, Ἰννᾶν, Πιννᾶν, Ῥιμμᾶν τε τοὺς κρυσταλλίνους» — let warmth receive the contenders of the frost, Innas, Pinnas and Rimmas, the crystalline. The page gives them no year.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 20 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/20-ianuarie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfintilor-mucenici-pin-rim); read 19 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 20 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/20/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3324/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 20 января ст. ст. — [the Russian day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260120.html), read 3 October 2026, which keeps the three of them as «Мчч. Инны, Пинны и Риммы (I-II)» and makes each of them «Мученик», a man.*
