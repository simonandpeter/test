# Michaela (Ivanova)

She is the second of two named in one line of the Russian calendar for 28 September: «Прмч. Илариона, прмц. Михаилы (1937)». The calendar's index of names gives her as «Михаила (Иванова), схимонахиня, прмц.» — a schemanun, under a name in religion that is the feminine of Michael, and «прмц.» rather than «мц.», which marks a monastic killed for the faith rather than a layman.

That the calendar keeps her with [Hilarion (Gromov)](/saints/hilarion-gromov) in one line is all it says about how the two were connected. It prints no monastery, no birth year and no account of her death. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which refused this reading on 4 October 2026, so her folder holds her name, her rank, her habit and the year.

*After the Russian church calendar's [28 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260928.html) and its [index entry for her](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13808.html); read 4 October 2026.*
