# Michael (Kobzev)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him among the hieromartyrs on 10 декабря and gives no life. Its line is «Сщмч. Михаила пресвитера (1937).» — the hieromartyr Michael the presbyter, 1937 — and the calendar’s own name page adds the surname the line leaves out: «Михаил (Кобзев), протоиерей, сщмч.»

That is the whole of the record. He was an archpriest, which is what the name page says where the line says only presbyter; he died in 1937; and although the line before his names thirteen people under the same year, his own line stands alone, so nothing joins him to them here but the day. The day page carries eleven hymns and not one is under his name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 10 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261210.html); read 13 October 2026.*
