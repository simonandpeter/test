# Macarius of Paphos

saint.gr names him and gives him one sentence with a year in it. Its calendar for 8 February prints «Όσιος Μακάριος Επίσκοπος Πάφου», and the page behind that line says that the venerable Macarius was bishop of the city of Paphos in Cyprus and fell asleep in peace in the year 1688. It gives no year of birth — its own heading leaves that date a question mark — and nothing of how he came to the see, how long he held it, or what he did in it; no relics are named, and there is no hymn.

What the page prints besides is a couplet that turns on the meaning of his name, which it glosses simply as the happy man: the president of Paphos was hidden away within a tomb, and Macarius, it says, was blessed in the rest as well, and not in the calling only.

The same page records that his memory is repeated on 25 October. That day's page was not read, and the day kept here is the day page that was.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/531/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
