# Anastaso of Leukadion

saint.gr's calendar for 18 May names her and says almost nothing. The venerable Anastaso fell asleep in holiness and in peace: that is the whole of her life on the page. Everything else it prints is about the place her name carries — Leukadion, or Laukation, lay along the Bithynian shore, not far from the district of Akritas, very probably near Dakibyze.

So the Greek church keeps on this day a woman whose name it remembers, whose holiness it affirms, and whose story it does not have; and the one thing it can still say about her is where she lived. The couplet above the notice reads her name as a promise: Anastaso lives, even though she is buried by the common law, awaiting the common resurrection of the race.
