# The Four Co-ascetics

saint.gr's whole entry for them is one sentence: the venerable Four — according to others forty — co-ascetics reposed in peace. Above it stands the synaxarion's distich, which calls them a four-numbered summit of monastic men, standing together with the four-formed minds. The page names none of the four, and the number it reports twice over and differently, four in its own heading and forty on the authority of others.

It gives them no place, no year and no century, and no hymn. They are not martyrs: the one verb the entry has for them is that they fell asleep in peace.
