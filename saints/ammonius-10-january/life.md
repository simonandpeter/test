# Ammonius (10 January)

saint.gr's calendar for 10 Ιανουαρίου keeps a venerable Ammonius, and the page behind the line finishes by saying that it does not know which Ammonius this is: «Ποιος όμως από τους τρεις μνημονεύεται εδώ δεν γνωρίζουμε». Three hermits of that one name are remembered, it says, and all three were distinguished for the holiness of their lives; the calendar does not choose between them, and neither does this page.

One of the three was a disciple of [Antony the Great](/saints/anthony-the-great). One was a disciple of Abba [Pambo](/saints/pambo-of-egypt), and cut off his own ear so that he should not be made a bishop. The third built cells for the young monks who kept coming out to the desert. All of them, the page says, lived in the desert in a venerable manner and died in peace.

No year is given for any of the three, and no country beyond the desert. The page prints the couplet «Τῆς ἀρετῆς ὁ θεῖος ἐσβέσθη λύχνος, / Λύχνων μυσάντων σαρκικῶν Ἀμμωνίου» — the divine lamp of virtue was put out when the fleshly lamps of Ammonius closed — and it prints no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/10/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1107/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
