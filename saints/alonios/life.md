# Alonios

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives him two sentences, the first of them hedged. The line for 4 June reads «Όσιος Αλώνιος», the venerable Alonios, and the page behind it says this: it seems that the venerable Alonios was one of the ascetics of the desert famous for virtue; sayings of his are to be found in the Evergetinos; he died peacefully.

So what is recorded of him is a name, a way of life given in one word, a book where his words survive, and a death in peace — and the page's own «it seems» over the first of those. There is no century here, no desert named, no monastery, no disciple and no wonder; no year, and no hymn. The couplet the page sets above the entry plays on his name, which is the Greek for a threshing floor: Alonios sweetens the husbandman of souls, having set a rick of good things on the threshing floor of the soul.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 4 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/04/index.aspx) and [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/1800/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
