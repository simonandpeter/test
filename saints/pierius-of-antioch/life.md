# Pierius of Antioch

saint.gr's calendar for 27 Ιουνίου names him and gives him one sentence. Its line reads «Άγιος Πιέριος Ιερομάρτυρας Πρεσβύτερος Αντιοχείας», and the page behind it says only that this hieromartyr, a presbyter of Antioch, was martyred by fire. No century is named, no persecutor, no year and no place but the see in his title, and the page prints no hymn.

Above that sentence stands a couplet of the synaxarion with a gloss of its own: «Ως ιερείον τον Πιέριον Λόγε, Eνήγισάν σοι (ήτοι κατέκαυσαν) δυσσεβείς επ’ ανθράκων» — the impious offered Pierius to you as a sacrificial victim, O Word, that is, burned him, upon coals. The page adds one note on the company he is kept in: certain synaxaria commemorate, with the memory of Pierius, that of his brother Isidore. The day's own line names only Pierius, and nothing further is said of the brother here.
