# Aprion of Cyprus

saint.gr names him and gives almost no life. Its calendar for 7 February prints «Άγιος Απρίων Επίσκοπος Κύπρου», and the page behind that line says this much and no more: that the venerable Aprion, or Euprion — the page allows both forms — bishop of Cyprus, who is mentioned in the Synaxarion of Constantinople, fell asleep in peace. There is no century here, no see within the island, nothing of how he governed it, and no hymn.

Over that sentence the page prints a couplet, and it is a summons rather than an account: Christ calls you, Aprion, make haste, go, to the prizes of the inheritance above.

One other entry of the same Greek day touches him, and the touch is a doubt of the source's own. saint.gr's page for the venerable Serapion of Cyprus, whom it calls unknown to the synaxarists and knows only from a Patmos codex, ends by saying that he may perhaps be the same man as Aprion and that the two names are being confused. Nothing on this page repeats that, and nothing here resolves it.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3621/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
