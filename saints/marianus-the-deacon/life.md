# Marianus the Deacon

Marianus was a deacon, and he suffered with [Chrysanthus](/saints/chrysanthus) and [Daria](/saints/daria) at Rome. Doxologia gives him the same few sentences it gives [Diodorus](/saints/diodorus-the-presbyter) the priest: in a cave near the place of execution the Christians gathered to keep the memory of the martyrs' death, and they held the services there and communed of the Holy Mysteries.

The pagan authorities heard of it, came to the cave and closed its mouth, so that those inside received the crown of martyrdom as well; and two of those martyred in the cave are Diodorus the priest and Marianus the deacon. The longer life of Chrysanthus and Daria says that many clergy died there with them, and that the faithful who ended in the cave were past counting.

That is the whole of what the Romanian calendar prints about him: his rank, his companions and the manner of his death. It gives him no country, no year of his own and no hymn, and none has been supplied here from anywhere else.

The Greek calendar keeps him with [Diodorus](/saints/diodorus-the-presbyter) the presbyter on the same day, and says less than the Romanian does. saint.gr's whole entry for the two is one sentence: they were martyred after being shut up hermetically inside a cave. Over them it prints a couplet which makes the cave a sanctuary — the cave is as a bema, for within it it bears a sacrificer of Christ and a Levite with him, which is the deacon's own order. The page gives no emperor, no year and no country, and no hymn stands under either name.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 19 martie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/19-martie), [his own page](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-mucenic-marian-diaconul) and [the life of Chrysanthus and Daria](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-mucenic-hrisant); read 19 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 19 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/19/index.aspx) and [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/3921/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026.*
