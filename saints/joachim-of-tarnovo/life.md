# Joachim of Tarnovo

Joachim came of Bulgaria and lived in the thirteenth century. He was taught the monastic life of the Holy Mountain, and he was elected Archbishop of Tarnovo. saint.gr names the occasion on which that see was raised: when John Ducas Vatatzes, emperor of Nicaea, whom the page dates 1222 to 1254 and whom the Church honours as a saint on 4 November, made alliance with the ruler of the Bulgarians John Asen II, whom it dates 1218 to 1241, and celebrated at Lampsacus in Asia Minor the marriage of his son Theodore to Asen's daughter Helen, the Archbishop of Tarnovo was proclaimed Patriarch by an imperial and synodal decree.

He fell asleep in peace in the year 1248. The page gives him no year of birth, nothing of his monastery on the Holy Mountain, nothing of his patriarchate after that proclamation, and it prints no hymn for him.
