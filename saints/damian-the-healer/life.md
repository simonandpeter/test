# Damian the Healer

Damian was a monk of the Kyiv Caves Lavra whose guide was [Theodosius of the Caves](/saints/theodosius-of-the-kyiv-caves), kept on 3 May, and he followed his spiritual father in the angelic life and in all the virtues, gaining humility and obedience. He was meek, hard-working and obedient, to the joy of all the brethren, and spent his nights in prayer and the reading of Scripture; he fasted strictly, eating only bread and water, and God made him worthy of the gift of working wonders and healing the sick.

When he fell ill near the end of his life, he prayed to God with tears to be made a sharer in the glory of the saints and not to be parted from his spiritual father: “Jesus Christ, do not part me from my teacher, Saint Theodosius, in thy Kingdom!” As he prayed, an angel in the likeness of Theodosius appeared by his bed, bent over him and told him that the Lord had heard his prayer and sent him to assure him of it: he would not be parted from him in the life to come. And Damian gave up his soul in peace into the Lord’s hands. doxologia.ro gives no year.

The Serbian church's Ohrid Prologue keeps him on this day not alone but at the head of a company of three, under one heading with [Jeremiah](/saints/jeremiah-of-the-caves) and [Matthew](/saints/matthew-of-the-caves): «Печерски прозорљивци и чудотворци», seers and wonderworkers of the Caves, all three of the eleventh century. The Romanian life this folder was built from keeps him by himself, and neither of the other two was a folder here until this day was read.

The Russian church calendar keeps the same three together and is the one source that puts a year to him: «Прпп. Дамиана, пресвитера, целебника (1071), Иеремии (ок. 1070) и Матфея (ок. 1085) прозорливых, Печерских». It gives him 1071, calls him a presbyter and a healer — which is the epithet this folder's name already carried out of the Romanian — and calls all three of them prescient. The folder held no year at all before these two readings; it holds 1071 now, inside the century the Prologue gives.

*After doxologia.ro — [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-cuvios-damian-tamaduitorul-de-la-lavra-pecerska); read 17 September 2026; the Russian church calendar's [5 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261005.html), read 4 October 2026; and the Serbian church's [Ohrid Prologue for the Julian 5 October](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-10-18&prolog=1), read 4 October 2026.*
