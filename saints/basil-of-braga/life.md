# Basil of Braga

saint.gr says that the holy Epitacius and the holy Basil lived in the first century, and gives each of them a clause. Of him it says: «ο δε Άγιος Βασίλειος αναφέρεται πιο επισκοπικούς καταλόγους ως δεύτερος Επίσκοπος της πόλεως Μπράγα της Πορτογαλίας το 60 μ.Χ.» — he is named in the episcopal lists as second bishop of the city of Braga in Portugal, in the year 60. Of his fellow it says that he is reckoned the first bishop of Tui in Spanish Galicia.

The page adds one sentence for the two of them together: «Οι Άγιοι κοιμήθηκαν με ειρήνη» — the saints fell asleep in peace. It gives him no birth, no death year, no companions beyond [Epitacius](/saints/epitacius-of-tui), who is named with him in the day's line, and no hymn. Nothing further about him is recorded here.
