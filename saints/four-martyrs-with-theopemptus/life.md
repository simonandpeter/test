# The Four Martyrs with Theopemptus

saint.gr's calendar for 11 Ιουνίου names them and gives no life, and it does not name them at all: they are «Άγιοι Τέσσερις Μάρτυρες που μαρτύρησαν μαζί με τον Άγιο Θεόπεμπτο», the four holy martyrs who were martyred together with [Theopemptus](/saints/theopemptus-11-june), and the page's whole notice for them and for him is one line, «Όλοι μαρτύρισαν δια ξίφους» — they were all martyred by the sword.

Above it stand the two verses of the synaxarion, which count them the same way: that Theopemptus, finding four fellow-contestants by the sword, was himself found the fifth. Because the page names none of the four, they are kept here as one entry and not as four folders. No place, no emperor, no judge and no year is given for them, and the page prints no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/11/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/569/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
