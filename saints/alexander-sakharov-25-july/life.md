# Alexander (Sakharov), 25 July

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Alexander among the hieromartyrs on 25 July and gives no life. Its line is «Сщмч. Александра пресвитера (1927).» — the hieromartyr Alexander the presbyter, 1927 — and the calendar's own name page for him adds the surname: «Александр (Сахаров), протоиерей, сщмч.», Alexander Sakharov, archpriest.

This calendar keeps two priests of that one name and that one surname, and they are not the same man. Each has his own numbered name page — 9262 for this one, 9261 for the other — and each has his own day and his own year: this one on 25 July, dead in 1927, the other on 20 November, dead in 1937. The day lines themselves give only the forename, so the two name pages are the whole of what distinguishes them, and this folder carries the day in its name for that reason.

The line and the name page do not agree about his grade either: the line calls him a presbyter and the name page an archpriest. Both readings are kept here, the one in what kind of saint he is and the other in his office, and nothing read resolves them. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 25 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260725.html); read 13 October 2026.*
