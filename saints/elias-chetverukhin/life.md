# Elias (Chetverukhin)

The calendar’s own line is the whole of what this folder records of him. The Сретенский monastery’s calendar for 5 декабря ст. ст. names him in the day’s last clause, with the two other saints of the twentieth century it keeps on this day: «Сщмч. Илии пресвитера (1932).» The rank is «сщмч.», a hieromartyr; the grade is «пресвитера», a presbyter; and 1932 is the only year the calendar gives him.

The name page behind his place in the line gives the surname the line leaves out, and a higher grade with it — «Илия (Четверухин), протоиерей, сщмч.», an archpriest — and that heading is the whole of what it says of him. It lists one other day, «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church; one row per church is this corpus’s schema, so that day is recorded on his feast and he does not render on it.

Behind that page there is no life. Nothing is said here of where he was born, where he served, when or by whom he was arrested, or how and where he died, because no page read says any of it. Two articles on the parent site stand linked under his name, both of them about a synodicon kept by the writer’s grandfather, who is the priest of this line; neither was read for this folder, and the calendar is what is cited.

The day page carries six hymns, and all six stand under the names of the ancient saints of the same day.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 5 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261205.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13893.html); read 13 October 2026.*
