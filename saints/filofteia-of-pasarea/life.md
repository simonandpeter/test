# Filofteia of Pasărea, mother of Calinic of Cernica

Filofteia of Pasărea was from Bucharest, the daughter of believing parents, and received the name Floarea at her baptism. She married young — Antonie, a believing young man — and with him made a fine Christian household, settling in the Saint Bessarion quarter of Bucharest, where the four sons God gave them were brought up in the love of God and of their country. That is the whole of the life doxologia.ro carries for her: a single paragraph, with no year in it and no account of her death.

What the rest of the calendar's own words add is little more. The day's line names her as the mother of [Calinic of Cernica](/saints/calinic-of-cernica), and his life, on the same site, says of her that when her children were grown she withdrew to the monastery of Pasărea and received the great and angelic habit as the schemanun Filoteia — the name under which the calendar now keeps her. Her troparion says she was gentle and hard-working, raised her household in the fear of the Lord, became a schemanun and was given grace for the working of wonders.

The hymn was sung, the page notes, by the Tronos psaltic group on the day of the general proclamation of the canonisation of sixteen Romanian women saints, 6 February 2026. Nothing read here gives her dates.
