# Tatiana (Egorova)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names her last of thirteen in one line on 10 декабря, and second of the two women in it. The line is «Сщмчч. Анатолия, Александра, Евгения, Константина, Николая пресвитеров, и с ними мчч. Петра, Евсевия, Михаила, Дорофея, Лаврентия, Григория и мцц. Александры и Татианы (1937).» — five hieromartyr presbyters, and with them six men martyrs and two women martyrs, all of 1937 — and the calendar’s own name page adds the surname the line leaves out: «Татиана (Егорова), мц.»

That is the whole of the record for her. The line sets the two women apart from the eleven men with a plural of its own, «мцц.», and gives them no grade and no office; she died in 1937; and of the thirteen only one, the second presbyter, has a life page. From that page it can be read that twenty-three people were shot on this day in the Ryazan region, so the thirteen of this line were part of a larger company; of this woman it says nothing.

Two women of her forename stand on this one day of the calendar, eleven years apart, and only the surnames on the name pages tell them apart. The day page carries eleven hymns and not one is under any of the thirteen names. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 10 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261210.html); read 13 October 2026.*
