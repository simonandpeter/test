# Eulogius of Palestine

Eulogius was born of unbelieving parents, and after their death he shared out the whole of his inheritance among the poor. Then, having made himself poor for Christ, he went about the land of Palestine as a beggar, teaching the unbelievers and turning them to Christ God. He was slandered to the lord of that country, and suffered many torments at his hands, because he would not sacrifice to the idols; and at the last his head was cut off.

That is the whole of what doxologia's page for him says. It gives no year and no emperor, names no city, prints no hymn, and tells nothing of his burial.
