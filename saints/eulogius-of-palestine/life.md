# Eulogius of Palestine

Eulogius was born of unbelieving parents, and after their death he shared out the whole of his inheritance among the poor. Then, having made himself poor for Christ, he went about the land of Palestine as a beggar, teaching the unbelievers and turning them to Christ God. He was slandered to the lord of that country, and suffered many torments at his hands, because he would not sacrifice to the idols; and at the last his head was cut off.

That is the whole of what doxologia's page for him says. It gives no year and no emperor, names no city, prints no hymn, and tells nothing of his burial.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and gives him a life where doxologia gave a line. saint.gr says the holy martyr Eulogius came of Palestine and was the son of rich idolatrous parents; that he himself became a Christian and was baptised, and after his parents' death shared out his wealth among the poor and went about the whole city and country teaching the idolaters the word of Christ and baptising many of them in the name of the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit. He was denounced by the idolaters to the archon of the country, who gave the order to seize him and bring him before him; and the saint confessed his faith in Christ with courage and openness and refused to sacrifice to the idols. The tortures began at once: they stripped him, beat him violently with hard cords made of oxen's sinews, and then cut off his honoured head. The page gives no year, no emperor, no name for the archon and no relic, and prints no hymn; the distich over him plays on his name, that all creation blesses Eulogius, whose head was cut off for the Creator's sake.
