# Vyacheslav (Zakedsky)

Vyacheslav Ivanovich Zakedsky was born in 1879, a priest’s son. In 1900 he
finished the course of the Yaroslavl seminary and was made teacher of the
parish school at Tolgobol in the Yaroslavl district; at this time he
married Nadezhda Mitrofanovna Solontsova, daughter of the deacon of the
Transfiguration cathedral of Rybinsk, Mitrofan Solontsov, of an old
priestly line. On 21 January 1901 he was ordained deacon to the
Transfiguration cathedral of Rybinsk and made teacher of the Law of God in
the cathedral parish school, in 1909 also in the eighth parish primary
college, and for the twenty-fifth anniversary of the parish schools he was
awarded a medal. After the February revolution, in May 1917, he was moved
to the prison church of Rybinsk and ordained priest. In that church, it
seems, during the suppression of the Yaroslavl rising, in the night of
10/23 August 1918, he was savagely wounded, and soon died of it — 10
August of the old style, the day the Russian calendar keeps him — and was
buried in the town cemetery by the church of Tikhon of Zadonsk. On 26
December 2001, on the representation of the Yaroslavl diocese, the Holy
Synod resolved on his canonisation; the calendar keeps him also in the
synaxis of the saints of Rostov and Yaroslavl.
