# John (Pavlovsky)

John Petrovich Pavlovsky was born on 28 January 1876 in the village of
Popovonavolotskoye in the Shenkursk district of Archangel province, a
priest’s son. In 1892 he finished the Shenkursk church school and in 1893
left the first class of the Archangel seminary; on 14 August 1893 he was
made psalmist of the church of Peter and Paul at Ust-Kozhva, and on 13
September 1894 moved to the church of the Saviour at Bakurino; in 1900 he
passed the deacon’s examination before the examining commission of the
Archangel seminary, was ordained deacon on 19 March 1900 and for his
diligence kept as psalmist at Bakurino. In July 1901 he was removed from
his post, but that winter, on 19 December 1901, was made psalmist of the
church of Zosima and Sabbatius at Zamezhnoye; on 9 August 1908 he was
admitted to the diaconate again, and on 15 February 1910 confirmed as
deacon of the St Nicholas cathedral of Ust-Tsilma. On 23 July 1912, before
the same commission, he passed the examination for the priesthood, a
serious one, and on 25 August 1912 was ordained priest for the church of
the Synaxis of the Mother of God at Moshyuga. From 1894 to 1901 he had
been catechist of the Bakurino parish school, and from 1912 to 1918 he
taught the Law of God at the one-class village school of Moshyuga; after
1918 he presumably went on serving as priest of the Moshyuga church. In
1933 he was under investigation by the NKVD on suspicion of concealing
valuables, but not brought to trial; from 1935 he lived in the village of
Bolshoye Galovo in the Izhma district of the Komi region and served as
priest, but not for long. On 30 June 1937 he was arrested and held in the
Izhma prison, charged among other things with carrying on
counter-revolutionary agitation among the collective farmers, speaking of
an imminent change of the existing order and the fall of the Soviet
power, and praising Hitler. On 16 September 1937 the troika of the Komi
NKVD sentenced him under article 58-10 to the highest penalty, and he
received a martyr’s death by shooting in the village of Izhma on 19
December 1937 — 6 December of the old style; the Russian calendar keeps
him on 6 September (Julian). By a decision of 6 October 2001 the Holy
Synod numbered him among the saints, in the synaxis of the new martyrs
and confessors of Russia; the Russian calendar keeps him also in the
synaxes of the saints of Komi and Arkhangelsk.
