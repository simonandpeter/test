# John (Pavlovsky), Presbyter, Hieromartyr (1937)

John Petrovich Pavlovsky was born on 28 January 1876 in the village of Popovonavolotskoye in the Shenkursk district of Archangel province, a priest’s son. In 1892 he finished the Shenkursk church school and in 1893 left the first class of the Archangel seminary; on 14 August 1893 he was made psalmist of the church of Peter and Paul at Ust-Kozhva, and on 13 September 1894 moved to the church of the Saviour at Bakurino; in 1900 he passed the deacon’s examination before the examining commission of the Archangel seminary, was ordained deacon on 19 March 1900 and for his diligence kept as psalmist at Bakurino — the calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. From 1912 he was a priest in the churches of the Komi country; in 1933 he was investigated on suspicion of concealing valuables, and in 1937 arrested for counter-revolutionary agitation — charged with talk of the fall of the Soviet power and praise of Hitler. The troika of the NKVD sentenced him to be shot, and he was executed on 19 December 1937; he was numbered among the saints in 2001, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 6 September (Julian).
