# The Two Martyrs of Thrace

saint.gr's calendar for 25 Οκτωβρίου names two martyrs from Thrace and does not name either of them. One sentence is the whole of the narrative: «Οι Άγιοι Δύο Μάρτυρες από τη Θράκη μαρτύρησαν, αφού συμπιέστηκαν μεταξύ δύο μεγάλων πετρών» — they were martyred by being crushed between two great stones. The couplet above says that wicked men found even a stone press, in which two fellow-contestants are squeezed together.

Because the page gives no names, this folder is the pair and not a person, as the corpus keeps `thirty-three-martyrs-of-heraclea` and `fathers-slain-at-raithu`. Thrace is the only place named; there is no year, no emperor, no city and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 25 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/25/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2814/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
