# Martin of Tarakine

saint.gr's calendar for 12 Νοεμβρίου keeps Martin, bishop of Tarakine, and its notice of him is short. He was a distinguished Illyrian from Sabaria of Pannonia, devout and virtuous; and because he defended Orthodoxy the Arians maltreated him publicly and drove him out of the city. He took refuge at Milan in Italy, but suffered the same there from Auxentius, the Arian bishop of that city, and was forced to withdraw to the island of Galaria in the Tyrrhenian sea, which was wholly deserted, and lived on wild greens.

Afterwards he became bishop of Tarakine and distinguished himself by his evangelical works: he fed the poor, defended the wronged and shepherded in a godly way the flock entrusted to him. He died in peace. The page gives no year for any of it, and prints no hymn; its distich says Martin received the mightiest glory from God, as one clothed in mercy.

A note under the entry warns that he is not the same as the venerable Martin the bishop kept on 10 November. The other Martin whom this same day keeps, the wonderworking bishop of Frankia, the page does not mention at all.
