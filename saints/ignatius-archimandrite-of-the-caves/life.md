# Ignatius of the Caves

saint.gr's calendar for 20 December names the venerable Ignatius, archimandrite of the Lavra of the Caves, and gives no life: his page carries the one sentence «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Οσίου» — we have no details of the venerable one's life. What the calendar prints is the whole of what the source holds, the name, the rank of archimandrite, and the house, the Lavra of the Caves, which the page does not locate and does not name further.

The only other matter on the page is the lexicon note it sets at the head of every Ignatius of the calendar, that the name comes from γηγενής and means the earth-born, the native, and that an Ignatius was a Byzantine writer. The feast is marked fixed — «Τύπος εορτής: Σταθερή. Εορτάζει στις 20 Δεκεμβρίου εκάστου έτους» — and no hymn is printed with it.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 20 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/20/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2613/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
