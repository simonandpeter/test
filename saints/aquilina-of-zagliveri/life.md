# Aquilina of Zagliveri

Aquilina was born in the village of Zagliveri near Thessalonica, to devout parents - until her father, after a brawl in which a Turk was killed, accepted Islam to escape the death penalty. Her mother stood unmoved in the faith of Christ and led her daughter along the way of salvation day by day; and against her father's persistent efforts and the threats of the Turks that she renounce her faith, Aquilina would not deny Christ. She was martyred by the Turks after several days of torture on 27 September 1764, at the age of nineteen - the very day the Romanian calendar keeps her.
