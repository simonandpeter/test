# Gennadius of Kostroma

Gennadius was born, saint.gr says, in the city of Kostroma of Lithuania, the son of a devout and noble family. His name was Gregory, and from childhood he showed a great love for the liturgical life of the Church. Because the Roman Catholic element was in the majority in the region where he grew up, he wished to leave his birthplace and go to live in neighbouring Russia; so he came to Moscow, where he met another young man, Theodore, with the same burning love for the monastic life.

Together they went to take counsel of [Alexander of Svir](/saints/alexander-of-svir), whom the same calendar keeps on 30 August, and Alexander sent Gregory to the monastery of Komel and Theodore back into the world. At Komel Gregory became the most devoted disciple of Saint Cornelius, whom the calendar keeps on 19 May, and was tonsured a monk with the name Gennadius; the gifts of the Holy Spirit, the page says, adorned the new monk's being.

Towards the end of his life Cornelius took Gennadius with him and they founded a new monastery at Kostroma, the monastery of Lyubimov. There Gennadius lived out the rest of his life in peace and ascetic labour, and God counted him worthy to work wonders and to have the gift of foresight. The Tsar Ivan held him in great reverence and made him tutor to his daughter. He fell asleep in peace in the year 1565. No year of his birth is printed, and no hymn.
