# Zenobius (Sutormin)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him on 10 января ст. ст. and gives no life. The whole of its account is one line, «Сщмч. Зиновия пресвитера (1920)» — the hieromartyr Zenobius, a presbyter, 1920 — and behind that line the calendar's own name link carries the surname the line leaves out, «Зиновий (Сутормин), иерей, сщмч.», the priest Zenobius Sutormin.

The calendar names no birthplace, no diocese, no parish, no place of death and no manner of it. The year 1920 in its bracket is the whole of what it dates him by, and it is the year of his death, in the first wave of killings of Russian clergy after the revolution. He stands on this day among the new martyrs and confessors of Russia.

The day page carries troparia for four of the saints it heads, and none under his name. That is everything the source says of him.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 10 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260110.html); read 3 October 2026. The calendar prints no life page for him.*
