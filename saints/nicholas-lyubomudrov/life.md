# Nicholas (Lyubomudrov)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 20 октября ст. ст. in a line of four words, «Сщмч. Николая пресвитера (1918).» — a hieromartyr, a presbyter, and the year 1918. The line does not even print his name in full: it gives the genitive «Николая пресвитера» and leaves the rest to the name link behind it, whose title reads «Николай (Любомудров), иерей, сщмч.» and adds the surname and the grade, a priest rather than an archpriest.

1918 is the first year of the killings of clergy that followed the revolution, and it puts him nineteen years before the company of eleven the same day keeps on its own line. Nothing on the page connects him to them, and the calendar carries no life page under his name; the four hymns the day page prints are for two other saints it heads.

Nothing further is written here — not a birth, not a diocese, not a parish, not a charge, not a place of death. The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line. The corpus already holds a priest of this surname under another forename, kept on another day; nothing on the page connects the two and nothing is inferred.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 20 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261020.html); read 4 October 2026.*
