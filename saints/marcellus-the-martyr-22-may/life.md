# Marcellus (22 May)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 22 mai reads «Sfântul Mucenic Marcel», and the page that line links to carries no Viață tab and no Tropar tab — only the name, the day, and the epistle and gospel appointed for it, which are the readings of a martyr. What the calendar says of him is the one word Mucenic, martyr. It gives no country, no century, no manner of death and no companions.

The name is a common one and this corpus holds other men who bear it, kept on other days. Nothing on this page says whether its Marcellus is any of them, so the day is carried in his name here, and the question of whether he is one of the others is left open rather than answered.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 22 mai — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/22-mai) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-marcel-0), which prints no life; read 20 September 2026.*
