# Michael of Cyprus, of the five of Samothrace

saint.gr keeps five new martyrs together on this day, and refers their life to another day than this one: «Βλέπε βιογραφικό τους σημείωμα την Κυριακή του Θωμά» — see their biographical note on Thomas Sunday. What the 6 April page itself gives is the names, the year and the place: [George](/saints/george-of-samothrace-a), [Manuel](/saints/manuel-of-samothrace), [Theodore](/saints/theodore-of-samothrace), [a second George](/saints/george-of-samothrace-b) and Michael, each headed «(; - 1835)».

The day's line calls all five from Samothrace, and the page's own list heads this one «Αγιος Μιχαηλ Απο Την Κυπρο», Michael from Cyprus. Both readings stand on the one page and neither is corrected there. Its couplet praises the island rather than telling the story: «Χορὸν τὸν πεντάριθμον νέων Μαρτύρων, / Σαμοθράκη γεραίρε νῦν ἐπαξίως» — Samothrace, honour worthily now the choir of five new martyrs.

The hymns say a little more. The apolytikion calls them lamps of Samothrace and the adornment of Makri, who contended steadfastly for Christ and undid the error of the enemy, and names Manuel with Theodore and Michael and the two Georges. The kontakion says they preached Christ our God with a bright voice to most savage barbarians, and the megalynarion calls the five bright sons of the island of Samothrace and of the country of Makri, its great glory and its guards, and physicians and saviours of the sick.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/06/index.aspx) and [the page of the five](https://www.saint.gr/192/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
