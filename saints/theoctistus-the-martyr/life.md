# Theoctistus

The Greek synaxarion says only that Theoctistus was martyred by the sword, and prints a verse: giving worship to the Creator and not to the creature, Theoctistus bears the taking off of his head with joy. doxologia.ro, for the Romanian calendar, lists the martyr Theoctistus on the same day and prints no life. Both calendars keep him on 3 October; that the two are one man rests on the day, the name and the rank, since neither page says more, and no year is given.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 3 Οκτωβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2581/saint.aspx); doxologia.ro — [the saint’s page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-teoctist); read 17 September 2026.*
