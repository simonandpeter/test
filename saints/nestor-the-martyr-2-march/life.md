# Nestor

doxologia.ro's calendar for 2 martie names Nestor — «Sfântul Mucenic Nestor» — and gives no life for him. His page on the site carries no Viață tab and no Tropar tab, and not even the summary sentence such pages usually have: what stands under his name is only the readings appointed for the day, the Apostle from the second letter to Timothy and the Gospel from John. Nothing is said of his country, of his time, or of the manner of his death.

So this folder records a name and a day, and nothing further. What the calendar does not print is not supplied here.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, pairs him with [Trivimios](/saints/trivimios) on one page, and gives the city and the reign that doxologia left out. saint.gr says the holy martyrs Nestor and Trivimios — or Trivíminos — came of the city of Perge in Pamphylia, the country of the Kibyrrhaiotai, and lived in the years of the impious emperor Decius. Being Christians and preaching Christ with a spiritual courage, they were denounced to the ruler of Perge; he sent a crowd of soldiers at once, had them seized and brought bound into court, and set all the instruments of torture out in front of them. The martyrs despised the threats and confessed Christ with a noble bearing, and for that they were stripped and beaten with hard ropes made of oxen's sinews, then hung up and their flesh torn away, and at the last beheaded. The page closes with a guess of its own — that this Nestor is very probably the same as the one it keeps on the twenty-eighth of February, because their biographies coincide — and that guess is recorded on the attestation and not acted on here.

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar keeps him with the man who suffered beside him, and keeps the two of them a day earlier than the Greek and the Romanian do: its line for 1 марта ст. ст. is «Мчч. Нестора и Тривимия (III).», and the life page behind it is headed for the pair and repeats that day. What it says agrees with saint.gr on every point this folder already records. The two were of the region of Pamphylia in Asia Minor; during the persecution of the impious emperor Decius, whose reign it gives as 249 to 251, they preached Christ without fear.

When they stood before the pagan court the governor had every kind of instrument of torture laid out in front of them, to frighten them into denying the faith, and to all the threats they answered that nobody would be able to part them from Christ. The judge in his anger had them racked: they were beaten with dry ox sinews, hung on a tree and had their bodies scraped, and they did not stop glorifying the Lord, and were beheaded. The day prints no hymn under either name, and the century on its line, «(III)», is the same third century the reign it names falls in, so the folder’s dates are left as they stood. He suffered with [Trivimius](/saints/trivimios).

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 2 martie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/2-martie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-nestor-0); read 19 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 2 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/02/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3882/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery, which keeps the two of them on 1 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260301.html) and [the life they share](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life539.htm), read 5 October 2026.*
