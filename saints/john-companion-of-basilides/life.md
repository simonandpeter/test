# John, companion of Basilides

saint.gr's calendar for 7 Ιουνίου names two men on one line and on one page — «Άγιοι Βασιλείδης μάρτυρας Στρατιώτης και Ιωάννης ο οσιομάρτυρας», the holy Basilides, martyr and soldier, and John the venerable-martyr — and then says, in its only sentence, that it has nothing to tell: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο των Αγίων».

So the Greek calendar keeps the two of them together on this day and gives each of them a rank and nothing else. There is no century, no country, no emperor, no manner of death, no relics and no hymn, and not even a couplet; the ranks in the day's line are all there is. The other of the two is [Basilides](/saints/basilides-the-soldier).
