# Philaret of Chernigov

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him on 9 August and gives no life. Its line is «Свт. Филарета, архиеп. Черниговского (1866).» — the hierarch Philaret, archbishop of Chernigov, 1866 — and the calendar's own name page for him adds the surname and the second half of the see: «Святитель Филарет (Гумилевский), архиепископ Черниговский и Нежинский», Philaret Gumilevsky, archbishop of Chernigov and Nizhyn.

That is the whole of the record. He was an archbishop of Chernigov and Nizhyn; he died in 1866; the calendar keeps him on this day, within the afterfeast of the Transfiguration. The day page carries a troparion and a kontakion for the apostle it heads, and nothing under his name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 9 августа ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260809.html); read 13 October 2026.*
