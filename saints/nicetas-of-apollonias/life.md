# Nicetas of Apollonias

Doxologia's life of Nicetas is two sentences long, and this is all of it. He was bishop of Apollonias in the days of those who fought against the holy icons, and he was not only faithful, devout and wholly upright in his belief, but merciful and full of love towards men, and renowned for his learning and for his word.

When he was pressed to renounce the veneration of the holy icons of our Lord Jesus Christ and of His most pure Mother, of the holy angels and of all the saints, he would not bend. He was condemned to exile and subjected to unbearable torments, and falling gravely ill from them he gave his holy soul into the hands of God.

The calendar calls him a confessor, and the page gives him no year, no account of his exile beyond that clause, and no hymn; none has been supplied here from anywhere else.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and places his see. saint.gr says Apollonias was a city of Bithynia which came under the metropolis of Nicomedia, and that this athlete of the Christian faith lived in the years of the iconoclasts; it was his piety and his other virtues that brought him to its episcopal throne. He was an exact knower of the holy Scriptures, a teacher and an eloquent and most practical preacher, and was marked out as well for his complete freedom from love of gain and for his works of charity. The storm of the iconoclasts sought to carry him along in its movement against the holy icons; for his refusal he was hunted, exiled and put to many hardships and ill-usages, and he fell gravely ill and gave up his soul to God, having been counted worthy of the honourable crown of the confessor and of perpetual memory from the Church. The couplet over him says that Nicetas, bearing dreadful things for the sake of Christ's image, now beholds Christ's own face in heaven. The page gives him no year and prints no hymn.
