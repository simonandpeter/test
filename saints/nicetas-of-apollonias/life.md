# Nicetas of Apollonias

Doxologia's life of Nicetas is two sentences long, and this is all of it. He was bishop of Apollonias in the days of those who fought against the holy icons, and he was not only faithful, devout and wholly upright in his belief, but merciful and full of love towards men, and renowned for his learning and for his word.

When he was pressed to renounce the veneration of the holy icons of our Lord Jesus Christ and of His most pure Mother, of the holy angels and of all the saints, he would not bend. He was condemned to exile and subjected to unbearable torments, and falling gravely ill from them he gave his holy soul into the hands of God.

The calendar calls him a confessor, and the page gives him no year, no account of his exile beyond that clause, and no hymn; none has been supplied here from anywhere else.
