# Asterios (7 August)

saint.gr's calendar for 7 August names Saint Asterios the venerable martyr and wonderworker, and then says what the synaxaria do not know: when and where he was martyred they do not report. What it has instead are two readings of him. In most of the codices, the page says, he is written down as «μάρτυς και συγκλητικός, δια ξίφους τελειωθείς» — a martyr and a senator, brought to his end by the sword. In the Synaxaristes of Saint Nikodemos he is given as «Oσιομάρτυς και θαυματουργός», a venerable martyr and a wonderworker, which is the reading the day's listing follows.

The page sets a couplet over him: Asterios, his neck cut through by the sword, shines among the choirs of the contestants as a star does. It glosses his name from the word for a star: the one who shines like a star. It prints no hymn to him, and it offers no year, no city and no judge to put with either of its two readings.
