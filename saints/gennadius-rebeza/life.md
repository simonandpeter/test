# Gennadius (Rebeza)

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him seventeenth of twenty-four and gives no life; the life here is the one azbyka.ru prints for the civil 2 декабря, out of pstgu.ru. It opens with both of his names at once — «архимандрит Григорий (в миру Геннадий Матвеевич Ребеза)» — so Gennadius is the name he was given in the world and Gregory the name he was given in religion, and the Sretensky calendar lists him under the first of the two. He was born in 1872 in the province of Poltava, was tonsured in 1895 and ordained, and in 1936 was arrested at Odessa and sentenced to exile in Kazakhstan. He was sent to the settlement of Mayskoye in the Pavlodar region, and arrested there again the next year.

The case papers quote his one answer about the counter-revolutionary work he was said to have carried on with other servers of the cult: «Контрреволюционной работой при нахождении в ссылке я не занимался, но всех ссыльных служителей культа, проживающих в поселке Майское я знаю всех по личности, но фамилии не припоминаю». The group indictment names him as one of the two the rest were supposed to have followed, «под руководством Мишутина и Ребеза», and charges them with saying that the constitution of the USSR existed only on paper and that the Soviet power was a passing thing which must soon fall. At two in the morning on 2 December 1937 he was shot. Where he is buried is not known.
