# Eugenius the Confessor

Doxologia's calendar names Eugenius for 19 February and gives no life. His page carries the line «Sfântul Cuvios Mărturisitor Evghenie», the date, and the same readings it appoints for Macarius, whom it keeps on the same day — the Apostle from Galatians on the fruit of the Spirit, and the Gospel from Matthew, Come unto me, all ye that labour. There is no life tab, no troparion tab and no notice.

The line says that he is kept as a venerable one and as a confessor: a monk who suffered for the faith and did not die of it. The calendar prints the two names together and says nothing more of either, and this entry adds nothing to the line.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 19 februarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/19-februarie) and [the page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-marturisitor-evghenie); read 19 September 2026.*
