# Modwena

saint.gr's calendar for 5 Ιουλίου names her and gives no life. The whole of its account is one sentence, «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέριες για τον βίο της Ιρλανδής Αγίας Modwena» — we have no details of the life of the Irish saint Modwena. That one word, Ιρλανδής, is the only thing the source says about her beyond her name and her day: she was Irish.

The page gives no century, no year, no place in Ireland, no monastery, no parentage, no rank beyond Αγία and no manner of death, and it prints no hymn and no synaxarion couplet under her name. It prints the name itself in Latin letters, Modwena, and nowhere gives a Greek form of it. That is everything the source says of her.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2011/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
