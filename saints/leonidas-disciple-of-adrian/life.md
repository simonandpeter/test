# Leonidas, disciple of Adrian

saint.gr keeps Leonidas on this day beside his elder, [Adrian of Poshekhonye](/saints/adrian-of-poshekhonye), and tells his story only as part of Adrian's. The two of them were monks in Russia in the sixteenth century; when Adrian, in the year 1540, saw in a vision an aged monk named Bestouz calling him to found a new house, it was Leonidas, his disciple, who left the monastic community with him, carrying the icon of the Dormition of the Theotokos their abbot had given them as a blessing.

The page tells how the place was settled on: robbers came on the icon in a tree and meant to take it, but a hidden power pushed them back, and in their terror they left bread and fish below the tree. The monks gave thanks for the God-sent gift and understood that this was the place granted them, and they built there the monastery of the Dormition. Of Leonidas himself the page says only that he lived as an ascetic in a manner pleasing to God and fell asleep in peace, in the year 1549 — before the robbers came back, ten years after the founding, and killed his elder.

The day's line and the page's headings call him a martyr, but its body has him die in peace; the calendar's word is recorded on the attestation and the rank is not written into this folder. There is no place or year of birth, no family, no office in the house, no relic, no distich and no hymn.
