# Sergius (12 August)

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for the day is «Όσιοι Σέργιος και Στέφανος», the venerable Sergius and [Stephen](/saints/stephen-12-august), and the whole of its notice is one sentence: «Οι Όσιοι Σέργιος και Στέφανος απεβίωσαν ειρηνικά.» The venerable Sergius and Stephen departed in peace. The page gives no year, no country, no monastery and no hymn, and it says nothing of what joined the two men beyond the one line that keeps them together.

What it does print is the distich the Greek books give the pair, and that distich is about their equality: «Ἴσων ἱδρώτων, Σεργίῳ καὶ Στεφάνῳ, / Ἴσοι στέφανοι· καὶ γὰρ οὕτω τὸ πρέπον» — for equal sweat, equal crowns to Sergius and Stephen; for so it is fitting. The pun is on the second name, στέφανος, a crown, and it is the only characterisation of either man the source supplies.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/12/index.aspx) and [the life of the pair](https://www.saint.gr/786/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
