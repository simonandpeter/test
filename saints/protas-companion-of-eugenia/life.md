# Protas, companion of Eugenia

saint.gr's calendar for 24 December keeps Protas and Hyacinth together, on one page which gives each of them a head line of his own. They were, it says, servants and later fellow ascetics of the holy [Eugenia](/saints/eugenia), whom the same calendar keeps on this day, and they were martyred by the sword at Rome. The distich above the sentence speaks to the two of them at once: cut down by the sword, Hyacinth and Protas, you are allotted the first honours of the martyrs of God.

Because the page names both men rather than counting them, each of them keeps a folder here, and each relates the other: this one's companion is [Hyacinth](/saints/hyacinth-companion-of-eugenia). The calendar gives them no year, no household but Eugenia's, no place but Rome and no hymn, and marks the feast fixed. The corpus's other Protas-shaped folder, `protasius-of-milan`, carries another name and another day. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence by which saint.gr marks an entry it will not vouch for.

The Охридски пролог keeps him on 24. децембар ст. ст., inside the entry it heads „Преподобна мученица Евгенија и други с њом.“, where he is one of the two faithful eunuchs with whom [Eugenia](/saints/eugenia) fled from her parents to the Christians driven out of Alexandria. «Тада бише посечени и оба она евнуха, Прот и Јакинт» — then both those eunuchs were beheaded, Protas and [Hyacinth](/saints/hyacinth-companion-of-eugenia), at Rome, after the beautiful virgin Basilla and before Eugenia’s own end.

That is the whole of what this book says of him: he is named twice, once as one of the two who went with her and once as one of the two who were beheaded. It gives him no year, no parentage and no word of his questioning. It is the heading’s own «и други с њом», and others with her, that holds him on this day; the year 262 the entry prints belongs to Eugenia’s own beheading and is not written here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/24/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3225/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026; and the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 24. децембар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-01-06&prolog=1), read 8 October 2026.*
