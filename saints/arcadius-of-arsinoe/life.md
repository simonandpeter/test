# Arcadius the Wonderworker, Bishop of Arsinoe

Arcadius was born at Arsinoe in Cyprus, the son of devout and wealthy parents, Michael and Anna. He was very well educated in Constantinople and came back to Arsinoe, his own country, where he became widely known for his ascetic struggles and his great virtues; so when the holy Nikon died, Arcadius was chosen to succeed him on the throne of Arsinoe. Having tended his flock in a way pleasing to God, he died in peace on 29 August. His brother was St Theosebios of Arsinoe.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 29 August — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2325/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
