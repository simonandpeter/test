# Philip, father of Eugenia

saint.gr's calendar for 24 December names Philip, and tells who he was in one sentence: he was the father of the holy [Eugenia](/saints/eugenia), whom the same calendar keeps on this same day, and he was martyred, put to death with a knife. The distich set above the sentence plays on the weapon — that Philip, who was himself a truly two-edged sword against error, is killed by the mouth of the knife.

That is the whole of his own entry. Eugenia's page says more of him, and says it as part of her story: that her father was called Philip and her mother Claudia, that he was appointed prefect at Alexandria and went there with his whole household, and that when everyone in the house had received Christianity the idolaters out of hatred wounded her father mortally. The calendar gives him no year of his own, no hymn, and no office in the Church, and marks the feast fixed. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence by which saint.gr marks an entry it will not vouch for.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/24/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3224/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
