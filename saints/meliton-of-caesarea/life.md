# Meliton of Caesarea

Doxologia.ro's page for Meliton carries no life and no troparion, only a single notice, and it says this: that the holy martyr Meliton suffered a martyr's death in the days of the emperor Trajan, at Caesarea in Cappadocia, together with the holy martyrs Theodotus and Theodota, Diomedes, Eulampius, Peter, Asclepiodotus and Golinduch.

That is the whole of what the page gives. It names no year, no charge and no manner of death, and none of those is recorded here. The seven named beside him are not printed as entries of their own on the calendar's page for 3 iulie, so they have no folders here either.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 3 iulie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/3-iulie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-meliton); read 24 September 2026.*
