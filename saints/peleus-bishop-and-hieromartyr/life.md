# Peleus

Peleus and [Nilus](/saints/nilus-bishop-and-hieromartyr) came from Egypt. They presented themselves boldly as Christians, with Patermuthius, Elias and a hundred other Christians, before the pagan king; they suffered horrible torments, their right eyes were put out, and at last they were burned alive — what great epic, the verse asks, for Peleus, who went in with Nilus into the midst of the fire? The Greek calendar keeps the bishops Peleus and Nilus on 17 September, and in separate entries the glorious Patermuthius and Elias, martyred by fire with them, and the hundred Egyptian martyrs beheaded with one eagerness.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 17 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2458/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
