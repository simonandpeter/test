# Tatiana (Chekmazova)

The Russian calendar gives her a line of her own on 28 September and no life: «Прмц. Татианы (1942)» — Tatiana the venerable-martyr, 1942. Its index of names adds the surname and the one detail that distinguishes her, «Татиана (Чекмазова), послушница, прмц.»: a novice, not yet tonsured, and counted among the monastic martyrs all the same.

1942 is the latest year on this day's page, and later than the great wave — a woman who died in a camp during the war rather than in the shootings of 1937. The calendar does not say that; it prints the name, the rank, the novitiate and the year. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which refused this reading on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line.

*After the Russian church calendar's [28 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260928.html) and its [index entry for her](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14334.html); read 4 October 2026.*
