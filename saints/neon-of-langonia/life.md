# Neon of Langonia

The Russian calendar keeps him on 16 января ст. ст. at the end of a company of seven: «Мчч. Спевсиппа, Елевсиппа, Мелевсиппа, бабки их Леониллы и с ними Неона, Турвона и Иовиллы (161-180)» — the martyrs [Speusippus](/saints/speusippus), [Eleusippus](/saints/eleusippus) and [Meleusippus](/saints/meleusippus), their grandmother [Leonilla](/saints/neonilla), and with them Neon, [Turbo](/saints/turbo-of-cappadocia) and [Jovilla](/saints/junilla-of-cappadocia). Its name link for him is «Неон Лангонийский, мч.».

They suffered in Gaul, the life says — by another account in Cappadocia — in the second century, in the persecution of Marcus Aurelius. Leonilla was baptised in old age by a disciple of the bishop of Smyrna and brought her three grandsons to Christ; the brothers broke the idols and reproached the pagans for their madness, and were thrown into a fire that did not harm them; their grandmother was beheaded after their deaths.

Of Neon it says exactly this: «Святой Неон видел подвиг святых братьев и, записав их страдания, передал свою рукопись Турвону, сам же открыто исповедал Христа, за что был жестоко избит и скончался от побоев» — he saw the brothers' contest, wrote their sufferings down, handed his manuscript to Turbo, and then confessed Christ openly himself, for which he was cruelly beaten and died of the beating. So the written account of this company came through him.

The martyrs are especially honoured in Spain, the page ends, where many churches are dedicated to them, and the Greek emperor Zeno gave their relics to France, to the city of Langres — which is the «Лангонийский» the calendar's own name link gives him. The day page prints no hymn under their names.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 16 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260116.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life229.htm); read 3 October 2026.*
