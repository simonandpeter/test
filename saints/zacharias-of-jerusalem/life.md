# Zacharias of Jerusalem

saint.gr says Zacharias was born in the second half of the sixth century and was elected patriarch of Jerusalem in 609. When the Persians took Jerusalem in 614 he was made captive and carried off into Persia, and from there he wrote an important letter to the Church of Jerusalem urging repentance and compassion on it.

When the emperor Heraclius defeated the Persians in 628, Zacharias returned to his see, bringing the Precious Cross back with him. He fell asleep in peace in the year 632. The page gives him no birthplace and names no relics, and it prints a couplet — blessed are you even in dying, Zacharias, Zacharias the president of a blessed land — and no hymn. It closes with a note that the Jerusalem Kanonarion puts his memory on 31 January and on 28 October, which is recorded in this folder's Greek attestation.
