# Evagrius, companion of Theodoula

saint.gr's calendar for 18 Ιανουαρίου keeps Evagrius among those who came to believe through Theodoula — with Macarius, Helladius the commentaresios, and Boethus. Theodoula lived in the years of Diocletian, whom the page dates 284 to 305, and she was from Diocaesarea of Cilicia. When the prefect Pelagius pressed her with violence to deny Christ, she set against him that she was a Christian and would remain one.

Pelagius then put her to tortures, over which her woman's nature rose by the power of divine grace; and such was the impression made by her most modest and heroic bearing that the prefect's own men, who had been among her tormentors before, declared to him that they acknowledged Jesus Christ as their God. So he threw her into the furnace, together with Evagrius and Macarius. Two others, Helladius and Boethus, were beheaded. The page's headings give the year of all five as 298.

Of Evagrius himself the day says nothing beyond this: that he believed through [Theodoula](/saints/theodoula-of-diocaesarea) and was cast into the fire with her and with [Macarius](/saints/macarius-companion-of-theodoula). The epigram the page sets over the two men reads «Βληθέντες εις πυρ οσφράδια Κυρίω, Ώφθητε Μακάριε Ευάγριέ τε». Neither his city nor his trade nor his age is printed.
