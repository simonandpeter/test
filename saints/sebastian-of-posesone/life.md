# Sebastian of Posesone

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Φεβρουαρίου names him and gives no life. Its line reads «Όσιος Σεβαστιανός του Ποσεσόνε», and the whole of the page standing under it is a referral: «Για τον Όσιο Σεβαστιανό του Ποσεσόνε βλέπε στις 18 Δεκεμβρίου» — for the venerable Sebastian of Posesone, see 18 December. Above the referral stands only saint.gr's standing gloss on the name Sebastian, that it comes from σεβαστός, the reverend.

So the Greek calendar keeps him twice, and the fuller account stands on its December day, which is not this day's reading. The place the calendar calls «Ποσεσόνε» is not explained here, and the form of it in this folder's name is no more than a transliteration of what the page prints; the entry gives no century, no year, no office, no relics, no couplet and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3663/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
