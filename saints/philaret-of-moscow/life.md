# Philaret of Moscow

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The entry for 19 noiembrie reads «Sfântul Ierarh Filaret, Mitropolitul Moscovei și Kolomnei» — Philaret, hierarch, metropolitan of Moscow and Kolomna — and the page behind that line carries no Viață tab and no Tropar tab, only the day's Epistle and Gospel. A name, a rank, a see and a day is the whole of what this source has.

The calendar gives him no century, no year of birth or death, and nothing of what he did; his see is the one thing that tells him apart from the other Philarets. This folder records what the Romanian calendar keeps on 19 noiembrie and stops there, and the Russian calendar, which is where the rest of him would be, has not been read for this row.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 19 noiembrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/19-noiembrie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-ierarh-filaret-mitropolitul-moscovei-kolomnei); read 25 September 2026.*
