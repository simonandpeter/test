# Philaret of Moscow

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The entry for 19 noiembrie reads «Sfântul Ierarh Filaret, Mitropolitul Moscovei și Kolomnei» — Philaret, hierarch, metropolitan of Moscow and Kolomna — and the page behind that line carries no Viață tab and no Tropar tab, only the day's Epistle and Gospel. A name, a rank, a see and a day is the whole of what this source has.

The calendar gives him no century, no year of birth or death, and nothing of what he did; his see is the one thing that tells him apart from the other Philarets. This folder records what the Romanian calendar keeps on 19 noiembrie and stops there, and the Russian calendar, which is where the rest of him would be, has not been read for this row.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 19 November, and has as little to tell as the Romanian one. saint.gr prints his name as «Άγιος Φιλάρετος Πατριάρχης Μόσχας» — Patriarch, where doxologia calls him Metropolitan — sets his years as 1782 to 1867, and then says outright that it has no details of his life. It adds one thing the Romanian line does not: that his relics lie in the Lavra of the Holy Trinity and Saint Sergius at Moscow.

**The Russian church calendar keeps him on the Julian 5 October with the synaxis of the hierarchs of Moscow**, seventeen names in one line under one polyeleos service, marked «(Моск. Свт.)» and not expanded: Peter, Theognostus, Alexis, Cyprian, Photius, Jonah, Gerontius, Joasaph, Macarius, Philip, Job, Hermogenes, Tikhon, Peter, Philaret, Innocent and Macarius — two and a half centuries of metropolitans, then the patriarchs, then the metropolitans of Moscow and Kolomna, ending with a man shot in 1937. He is the fifteenth of them. The Russian index agrees with this folder about his see and gives the surname in brackets as the calendar does for the modern hierarchs, «Святитель Филарет (Дроздов)». One further day is listed, 19 November.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 19 noiembrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/19-noiembrie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-ierarh-filaret-mitropolitul-moscovei-kolomnei); read 25 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 19 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/19/index.aspx) and [his page](https://www.saint.gr/2683/saint.aspx), read 2 October 2026; the Russian church calendar keeps him on the Julian 5 October with the synaxis of the hierarchs of Moscow — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261005.html); read 4 October 2026.*
