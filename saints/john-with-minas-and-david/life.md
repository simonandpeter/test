# John, with Minas and David

saint.gr's whole account of the three is one sentence: «Οι Άγιοι Οσιομάρτυρες Μηνάς, Δαβίδ και Ιωάννης ήταν μοναχοί και τελειώθηκαν τοξευόμενοι» — the holy venerable-martyrs [Minas](/saints/minas-with-david-and-john), [David](/saints/david-with-minas-and-john) and John were monks and were made perfect by being shot with arrows. The day's line adds nothing but the rank word, οι Οσιομάρτυρες.

Above it the page prints a couplet which is the only other thing said of them: three servants of God, abbas, slayers of the passions, are killed with arrows by slayers of men. Under John's name it sets a gloss on the name — a Hebrew word, it says, meaning the grace and favour of God towards men — followed by its standing list of the famous Johns, the Forerunner, the Theologian, Chrysostom and many other saints, kings and hierarchs. None of that belongs to this man; it belongs to the name.

Nothing else is told. The page gives no century, no country, no monastery, no persecutor and no year; it does not say whether the three were of one house or only of one death, and it lists no relic and prints no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/239/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
