# Anatoly the Younger of Optina

doxologia.ro's calendar names him on 30 iulie as «Sfântul Anatolie „cel Tânăr” de la Optina» and gives no life. His own page on that site carries no Viață tab at all: what it offers instead is the akathist of the elders of Optina, a daily prayer of those elders, a prayer of his own for deliverance from Antichrist, a prayer to the elders of the monastery, icons — among them one titled «Soborul Sfinţilor Stareţi de la Optina», the synaxis of the Optina elders — and photographs of Optina in Russia under places of pilgrimage.

So the only things recorded here are the ones the source actually prints: the day, the name, the epithet „cel Tânăr”, the monastery he is named from, and the company of the elders of Optina among whom that site keeps him. Nothing is said of his years, his parents, his tonsure or his repose, because nothing of that is printed there. The corpus already holds three of the Optina elders — [Macarius](/saints/macarius-of-optina), [Hilarion](/saints/hilarion-of-optina) and [Isaac](/saints/isaac-i-of-optina) — and this entry stands beside them with its life still to be read from a source that gives one.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 30 iulie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/30-iulie) and [the saint's page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-anatolie-cel-tanar-de-la-optina), which prints no life; read 24 September 2026.*
