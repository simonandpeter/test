# Venustus of Africa

saint.gr's calendar names two men and counts the rest. The holy martyrs [Heliodorus](/saints/heliodorus-of-africa) and Venustus were martyred, the page says, together with seventy-five other Christians in Africa, in the reign of the emperor Diocletian, who held the empire from 284 to 305. That is the one sentence the page has for them, and each of the two is given a head line of his own above it.

None of the seventy-five is named, and the page gives no place in Africa, no governor, no manner of death, no relics and no hymn. A Venustus also stands among the martyrs of Cibalae in Pannonia on another day and in another company; nothing on this page joins the two, and nothing is claimed here beyond this name, the rank, the number, the province, the reign and the day.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/06/index.aspx) and [the life of the company](https://www.saint.gr/1526/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
