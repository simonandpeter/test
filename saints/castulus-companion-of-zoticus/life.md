# Castulus, companion of Zoticus

Castulus is the last of the four martyrs the Greek calendar names in one line on 12 Ιανουαρίου: «Άγιοι Ζωτικός, Ρογάτος, Μοδεστός και Καστούλιος οι Μάρτυρες». The page behind the line gives all four one sentence — [Zoticus](/saints/zoticus-companion-of-rogatus), [Rogatus](/saints/rogatus-companion-of-zoticus), [Modestus](/saints/modestus-companion-of-zoticus) and Castulus were soldiers, and they were martyred in Africa — and adds that it has no further details of their lives.

The Greek writes his Latin name Καστούλιος. Beyond that, the day, the trade and the continent, the calendar keeps nothing of him: no emperor, no city, no year, no manner of death, and no hymn.
