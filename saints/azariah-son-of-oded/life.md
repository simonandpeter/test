# Azariah, son of Oded

Azariah was son of Oded — Addo, saint.gr adds as an alternative — and lived in the years of Asa, son and successor of Abijah in the kingdom of Judah, whose reign the page puts at 910 to 870 before Christ. He came from the land the page calls Sembatha, and he preached godliness to the people.

Asa upheld the worship of the true God, fortified Judaea well, and armed and trained five hundred thousand fighting men. According to the account in the second book of Chronicles, chapters fourteen and fifteen, Zerah the Ethiopian came out against Judaea — said, the page notes, to be Pharaoh Osorkon I, successor of Shishak, who had beaten Rehoboam king of Judah — with an army in the millions and three hundred chariots. Before the battle Asa prayed to God most fervently.

When he had won, Azariah, inspired by the Holy Spirit, went to king Asa and to the people and said: hear me, you king Asa and all the people of the tribes of Judah and Benjamin. The Lord will be with you when you take care to be with him, worshipping his name and keeping his commandments; when you seek him you will find him, but if you forsake him he too will forsake you. Words, the page comments, most precious for every state and every people.

Azariah died peacefully and was buried in his own field. saint.gr prints no hymn for him; its couplet sets the oracles he gave before his death against the Pythia, who falls silent when she has prophesied crookedly.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/03/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1195/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
