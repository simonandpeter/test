# Alexander of Svir

Alexander of Svir was born on 15 June 1448, the day of the prophet Amos, and named Amos at baptism, to Stephen and Vassa, peasants of the village of Mandera by Lake Ladoga on the river Oyat, a tributary of the Svir. They had two grown children and prayed for another son, and heard a voice from above: rejoice, good couple, you will bear a son in whose birth God will give comfort to his Churches. Amos grew up obedient and meek, avoiding games and laughter, wearing poor clothes and wearing himself down with fasting; and the life says that, far from the events of history, in the depths of the forests of the Russian North, he made another, spiritual history, and was granted extraordinary gifts of the Holy Spirit. The Romanian life adds that he gave himself wholly to the service of God and the brethren, passing his days in the monastery’s obediences and his nights in vigil, prayer and psalmody, with such watchfulness, humility and obedience that one might have thought him an angel in the flesh. He died in 1533. The Greek synaxarion keeps him on the same day but has no details; the Romanian calendar keeps him with a full life.
