# Etheldreda

saint.gr's calendar for 23 Ιουνίου names her and says it has no life to give. The whole of its account is one sentence: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο της Βρεττανίδας Αγίας Etheldreda (ή Audrey, AEthelthryth)» — we have no details of the life of the British saint Etheldreda, or Audrey, or AEthelthryth. The page prints her name in Latin letters, gives the two further forms, and offers no Greek form of it at all.

Beyond that sentence the page carries only her years, in the heading over the entry: «(636 - 679)». It gives no place in Britain, no rank, no monastery, no manner of death and no hymn, and it prints no verses for her as it does for most of the day's other saints. Four icons of her are shown, captioned with her name alone.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 23 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/23/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/617/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
