# Severus (8 September)

Severus was martyred by the sword. That is the whole of the Greek synaxarion’s notice for 8 September; its verse has him say, I am ready to bear every suffering — and the sword too for me? The Greek calendar keeps him on the feast of the Nativity of the Theotokos.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 8 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/953/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
