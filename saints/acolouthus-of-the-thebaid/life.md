# Acolouthus of the Thebaid

saint.gr gives the martyr Acolouthus a single paragraph and no more. He was from the Thebaid in Egypt, and he was martyred in 303, in the years of the emperor Galerius Maximian, whose reign the page sets at 286–305. A fervent Christian, he preached the word of God, and for that he was arrested; and when he refused to sacrifice to the idols he was tortured without mercy and then thrown onto the pyre, where he received the unfading crown of martyrdom.

What stands beside that paragraph is the couplet the synaxarion sets over his memory, which turns the fire into a bridal chamber and plays on his name, the follower who followed: «O Mάρτυς Aκόλουθος ως προς παστάδα, Eπηκολούθει τοις άγουσι προς φλόγα» — the martyr Acolouthus followed those who led him to the flame as though to a marriage bed. saint.gr prints no hymn to him and names no place where his body was laid.
