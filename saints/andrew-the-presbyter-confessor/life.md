# Andrew the Presbyter

Andrew was the schoolfellow and lifelong companion of [Hypatius, Bishop of
Lydia](/saints/hypatius-bishop-of-lydia), raised with him in the faith. He was made deacon and
then priest, and lived under Leo the Isaurian. Both men contended for Orthodoxy against the
iconoclast heresy and were arrested for resisting the imperial decrees; they were dragged bound
through the streets and then killed. The Greek calendar keeps them together on 20 September.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 20 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/984/saint.aspx); read 26 August 2026.*
