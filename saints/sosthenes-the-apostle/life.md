# Sosthenes of Colophon

saint.gr's calendar for 8 December keeps six apostles of the Seventy in one entry and names them all: Sosthenes, [Cephas](/saints/cephas-the-apostle), [Apollos](/saints/apollos-the-apostle), [Tychicus](/saints/tychicus-the-apostle), [Caesar](/saints/caesar-the-apostle) and [Epaphroditus](/saints/epaphroditus-the-apostle). Of Sosthenes it says that he was shown forth as bishop of the city of Colophon in Ionia, and that he was marked out for his gift of teaching and for his self-denial.

He was a fellow worker of the apostle Paul, the page says, and a mark of the love Paul bore him is that in the first letter to the Corinthians he calls him his fellow soldier. It then quotes Chrysostom to the effect that the apostle Sosthenes and Crispus, whom the calendar keeps on 4 December, are one and the same person — I think, he says, that this man is also called Sosthenes; a man so faithful that he was beaten, and was always beside Paul. The page reports that reading and does not settle it, and nothing here is settled from it either.

The couplet set over his name turns on his own: Sosthenes, bearing your strength, my Christ, saved many by clothing them in strength. The page adds that Epaphroditus took the see of Colophon after him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3203/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
