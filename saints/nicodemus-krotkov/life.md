# Nicodemus (Krotkov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 8 August as «Сщмч. Никодима, архиеп. Костромского (1938).», and the reading behind his name titles him more fully: «Священномученик Никодим, архиепископ Костромской и Галичский». The calendar's own name page gives the surname it keeps in parentheses — «Святитель Никодим (Кротков), архиепископ Костромской, сщмч.»

He was born in 1868. From 1911 to 1917 he governed the diocese of Chyhyryn, then the diocese of Petrovsk, and then Chyhyryn again until 1921. From 1921 to 1924 he held the see of Simferopol, and from 1924 to 1932 he was held in the camp on Solovki.

From 10 June 1932 until 20 December 1936 he governed the diocese of Kostroma. In 1936 he was arrested once more and sent to Kandalaksha, and there he could not bear the conditions of living in a yurt and died on 8 August, which the reading gives as 21 August in the new style, in 1938.

He was canonised in the diocese of Kostroma in 1995 as a locally venerated saint, and numbered among the New Martyrs and Confessors of Russia for the whole Church at the Jubilee Council of Bishops in August 2000. The reading keeps two further memories of him that this day page does not repeat: the synaxis of the new martyrs on 26 January, and the synaxis of the saints of Kostroma on 23 January.

One folder already in this corpus names him, and it is the reason the two are set beside each other here: [Demetrius (Ignatenko)](/saints/demetrius-ignatenko), archpriest of the Tauric diocese, stood before the proletarian court in December 1922 beside his own bishop, charged with refusing to give up the liturgical vessels, and that bishop was he. The reading used for this folder does not mention it; the other folder's does.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 8 августа ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260808.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4859.htm); read 13 October 2026.*
