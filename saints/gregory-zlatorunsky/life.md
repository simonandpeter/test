# Gregory (Zlatorunsky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names a Gregory on 8 июня and gives no life of him. Its line for him is the whole of what it says: «Сщмч. Григория Златорунского пресвитера (1918).» — the hieromartyr Gregory Zlatorunsky, a presbyter, and the year 1918. So what the calendar tells is that he was a priest who was counted among the martyrs and that he died in 1918.

His own name page on that site, 15071, carries nothing but the heading «Григорий (Златорунский), сщмч.», the two days «26 января (Новомуч.) , 8 июня», and one icon. There is no life page for him on the site, no article, and no page names a parish, a diocese or a place. The day page prints hymns for several of the entries it heads and none under his name.

The surname is on the day's line itself as well as on the name link, which is not the usual way of this calendar with the new martyrs; the first of the two days the name page gives is the January commemoration of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which was not read, and nothing of it is in this folder.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 8 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260608.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/15071.html); read 8 October 2026.*
