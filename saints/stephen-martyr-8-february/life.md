# Stephen (8 February)

saint.gr names him with one companion and gives the two of them a single sentence between them. Its calendar for 8 February prints «Άγιοι Νικηφόρος και Στέφανος οι Μάρτυρες», and the page behind that line says this much: that the holy martyrs [Nicephorus](/saints/nicephorus-martyr-8-february) and Stephen were martyred after their flesh had been torn to pieces. There is no city here, no emperor, no century, no relics and no hymn.

What the page adds is the reading of the name and a couplet, and the couplet plays on both names at once: for Nicephorus and Stephen, who were scraped, a victory-bearer's crown was woven at the end. Στέφανος, from στέφανος, a crown, it glosses as the prize-winner, the man worthy of the crown of victory.

The same page records that their memory is repeated on 25 October. That day's page was not read, and the day kept here is the day page that was.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/533/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
