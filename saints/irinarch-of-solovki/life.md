# Irinarch of Solovki

Irinarch was tonsured in the Solovki monastery, and in his monastic life, the reading says, he zealously imitated the venerable [Zosimas of Solovki](/saints/zosimas-of-solovki), whose memory it places on 17 April, and [Sabbatius of Solovki](/saints/sabbatius-of-solovki), whose it places on 27 September — the two days on which this calendar keeps those two founders. In 1614, after the death of abbot Antony, Irinarch became his successor.

The Solovki house at that time mattered greatly in the defence of northern Russia against the Swedes and the Danes, and the new abbot did much to strengthen it: under him a stone wall with towers was raised and deep ditches dug and lined with stone. Caring for the outward safety of the house, the reading says, he gave as much attention to its inward and spiritual strengthening. Humble and meek himself and constantly absorbed in the thought of God, he was zealous to keep a truly monastic spirit in the monks, and many worthy ascetics grew up under his guidance. With his blessing and under his protection the venerable [Eleazar of Anzersky](/saints/eleazar-of-anzersky), whom the reading calls his friend and fellow-struggler and whose memory it places on 13 January, founded the skete on the island of Anzer.

A charter of the tsar to the Solovki monastery in 1621 commanded the monks to live by the rules of the holy fathers and in full obedience to the abbot — the reading names him inside the quotation — and to the elders.

The last two years of his life he spent in the labour of silent prayer. He reposed on 17 July 1628.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 17 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260717.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4251.htm); read 9 October 2026.*
