# Maximus (Rumyantsev)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and gives no life. Its line for 31 июля ст. ст. reads «Мч. Максима (1928).» — the martyr Maximus, who died in 1928 — and behind that line the calendar's own name page gives the name in full as «Максим (Румянцев), мч.», with the surname the day line leaves out.

Nothing else is printed. The calendar names no place, no parish, no diocese and no manner of death; it makes him a martyr and not a hieromartyr, so he is one of the laymen of those years, and it carries no life page and no hymn for him. The forename, the surname and the year 1928 are the whole of what it keeps.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 31 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260731.html); read 13 October 2026.*
