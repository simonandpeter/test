# Thea

saint.gr names the holy Thea on this day and gives her one sentence, and the life says exactly that: «Η Αγία Θεή τελειώθηκε διά ξίφους. Δεν έχουμε περισσότερες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο της Αγίας» — the holy Thea was perfected by the sword, and we have no further details of the saint's life. The page gives no year, no country, no emperor, no judge and no relic, and it prints no hymn.

The distich over her is the only other thing there, and it says what she was killed for rather than who she was: Thea mocking at the gods that lead men astray, the patrons of that error kill her with the sword. The site does not name the place or the reign in which that happened.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 23 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/23/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3689/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
