# Secundus of Spain

saint.gr's calendar for 28 December keeps Secundus, who lived in the first century. According to the tradition, the page says, he was sent by the apostles into Spain to spread the word of the Lord. He went first to the city of Spain which is today called Cadiz, and there, because of his virtue and of the word he spoke that was pleasing to God, many idolaters came over into the bosom of the Church.

Afterwards he went to other cities of Spain as well and preached the divine Gospel, after the example of the Apostles. His last labours and contests, the page says, were crowned by a martyr's death. It gives no year, no judge and no manner of death; the couplet set over the entry says the sword — «Γίνου Σεκούνδε και συ Mάρτυς εκ ξίφους, / Kαι Mαρτύρων κάλλιστα λάμβανε γέρα», be you too a martyr by the sword, Secundus, and receive the fairest rewards of the martyrs.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/28/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/550/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
