# Finnian of Clonard

saint.gr's calendar for 12 December keeps the holy Finnian of Clonard, the wonderworker, and gives him one sentence of life: he was the founder, the abbot and the head of the school of the glorious monastery-school of Clonard in Ireland. The page's own head gives his years as 472 to 549, and its body gives no year at all.

Nothing else is printed of him — no parentage, no teacher, no disciple, no wonder, no relic, no hymn and no synaxaristic couplet — and the calendar writes both his name and the name of his house in Latin letters beside the Greek, which is why the English form here is copied and not made up. What is recorded is that sentence, the two years, and the by-name the day's line gives him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3240/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
