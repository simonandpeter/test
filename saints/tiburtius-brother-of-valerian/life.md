# Tiburtius, brother of Valerian

saint.gr's calendar for 22 Νοεμβρίου names three together: «Άγιοι Κικιλία, Βαλεριανός και Τιβούρτιος». Cecilia lived in the third century, the page says, and her parents were idolaters and noble people besides; but Cecilia heard the teaching of Christ and asked to be baptised. A little while afterwards her parents married her to a young nobleman, Valerian, who under Cecilia's influence embraced the Christian faith himself; and with Valerian his brother Tiburtius came into the bosom of the Church too.

In the persecution under Diocletian the three of them went on declaring their faith in Jesus Christ and went on with their work of charity, and for that they were arrested and suffered the last penalty, death by beheading. The couplet the page sets over Cecilia has her bearing the burning of the bath and then washing in a bath of blood by the sword; the one it sets over the men has the sword killing Valerian and his fellow contestant together, as they pelt error with reproaches.

The page adds that the relics of Valerian and of Tiburtius both lie in the Roman Catholic basilica of Saint Cecilia at Rome. It gives none of the three a year of birth, and names the prefect only in the kontakion it prints for Cecilia, as Almachius.

This folder is Tiburtius, who came into the Church after his brother [Valerian](/saints/valerian-husband-of-cecilia) and died by the sword with him and with [Cecilia](/saints/cecilia-of-rome).

**The Russian calendar keeps the four of them on one clause and one life page.** The Сретенский monastery’s calendar prints for the Julian 22 November «Мц. Кикилии (Цецилии) и мчч. Валериана, Тивуртия и Максима (ок. 230).» and hangs a single life behind all four names. That life tells the story this folder already carries, with the same bishop hiding from the persecutors in a cave by the Appian Way, the same four hundred converted in Cecilia’s house, the same three days of heat and smoke and the same executioner who struck and did not kill; it calls the governor «Аммах» where the Western reading calls him Almachius.

What it adds is nothing about him that this folder did not already have, and it leaves out the fragrance of roses and lilies and the seven days of teaching the Western reading gives him. The Russian page has his brother [Valerian](/saints/valerian-husband-of-cecilia) turn him to Christ, has the two of them give part of their estate to the poor, nurse the sick and bury the tortured, and has them refuse together to sacrifice and be beaten with lashes together. They were beheaded on the morning after the night both of them spent teaching [Maximus](/saints/maximus-the-capitularius) in his house.
