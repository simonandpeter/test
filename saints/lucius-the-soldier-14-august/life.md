# Lucius (14 August)

saint.gr's calendar for 14 Αυγούστου names him and says one thing about him. Its whole notice is the sentence «Ο Άγιος Λούκιος ο στρατιώτης μαρτύρησε δια πυρός» — Saint Lucius the soldier was martyred by fire. There is no country, no city, no century, no emperor, no year and no hymn. That he was a soldier is in the line itself and is all the station the calendar gives him.

The couplet of the synaxarion printed above it turns the fire into baking: «Φυγών παλαιάν Λούκιε ζύμην πλάνης, / Kαυθείς επώφθης ένζυμον Θεώ νέον» — having fled the old leaven of error, Lucius, you were burned and shown forth as new leavened bread for God. This folder stands for the Greek line of 14 August alone, and is marked by its day because the calendar keeps another Lucius, of Africa, on 23 May.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 14 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/14/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2269/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
