# Audatus

saint.gr names him and gives no life. Its calendar for 7 February prints «Άγιος Αύδατος ο Μάρτυρας», and the page behind that line holds a single sentence: that the holy martyr Audatus was martyred in Phrygia in the fourth century after Christ. There is no emperor here, no manner of death, no city within Phrygia, no relics, no reading of the name, and no hymn.

That is the whole of the entry. The page prints no couplet over him, which is rarer on this site than a short life is, and it names no companion. What the Greek calendar keeps of him is the day, the province, the century, and the rank it gives him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3627/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
