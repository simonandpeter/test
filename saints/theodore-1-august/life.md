# Theodore (1 August)

saint.gr's calendar for 1 Αυγούστου names him and gives no life. Its whole notice of him is one sentence: «Ο Άγιος Θεόδωρος μαρτύρησε δια ξίφους» — Saint Theodore was martyred by the sword. There is no country, no century, no emperor, no judge and no hymn. What the Greek calendar keeps of him is therefore a name, a day and a manner of death, and nothing else at all.

Besides that the page prints the gloss on the name — ΘΕΟΔΩΡΟΣ, the gift of God — and the couplet of the synaxarion, which is the only other thing it says of him: «Τῷ Θεοδώρῳ θεῖος ἐνσκήπτει πόθος, / Τυχεῖν ποθεινῶν δωρεῶν διὰ ξίφους» — a divine longing falls upon Theodore, to win the longed-for gifts by the sword.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2185/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
