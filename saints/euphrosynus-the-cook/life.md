# Euphrosynus the Cook

Euphrosynus, an unlettered and rustic villager, left the world in manhood and entered a coenobium, where he was tonsured. Despised by his fellow-monks for his lack of education and his simplicity, he embraced the humility of Christ and served them in the kitchen — serving, the Romanian Lives say, not as to men but as to God, with great humility and submission, labouring in obedience day and night; and as he was always blackened with the charcoal and the ashes all laughed at him and mocked him, and he took beatings too from the more careless, who found occasion in his silence and forbearance. But the blessed man bore the insults and humiliations with greatness of heart, and now bathed in sweat, now panting and cheerful, ran the course of the virtues in secret, escaping men’s notice. In that coenobium was a virtuous priest who for three years with fasting and prayer begged God to show him the good things which God has prepared for those who love him (1 Corinthians 2:9); one night as he slept his mind was caught up into paradise, into a most delightful and fragrant garden full of every kind of tree, sweet flowers and the clearest running waters, which no human tongue can describe, and as he wondered whose this marvellous — the synaxarion’s life runs on past the point read here. The Greek and Romanian calendars keep him on 11 September.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 11 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/1000/saint.aspx); The Romanian life is at doxologia.ro — [the page](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-cuvios-eufrosin-bucatarul); read 23 August 2026.*
