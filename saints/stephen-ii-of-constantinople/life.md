# Stephen II of Constantinople

saint.gr's calendar for 18 July keeps two bishops together, Stephen the Second, from Amasea, archbishop of Constantinople, and [John](/saints/john-of-chalcedon), metropolitan of Chalcedon. Of Stephen it says that before he became patriarch of Constantinople he was metropolitan of Amasea, which was also his homeland, and that he succeeded [Nicholas the First](/saints/nicholas-the-mystikos) on the patriarchal throne after that man's second patriarchate.

The rest is dates and the authorities for them. His enthronement took place in the year 925. He died on 18 July 928 according to Leo the Grammarian, or on the 15th of the same month according to Kedrenos — the page gives both and settles neither, and the calendar itself keeps him on the 18th. It prints no account of his birth, his character, his years in office or his end, and no hymn.

Above the two notices the calendar sets one couplet for the pair: «Λυθέντες εκ γης οι δύω Eπίσκοποι, / Πάρεισιν ήδη τω νόων Eπισκόπω» — loosed from the earth, the two bishops stand now before the Bishop of minds.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2154/saint.aspx), which he shares with John of Chalcedon; read 1 October 2026.*
