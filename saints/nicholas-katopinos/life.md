# Nicholas Katopinos

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Φεβρουαρίου names him and gives no life at all. Its line reads «Άγιος Νικόλαος ο Κατοπινός», and the page standing under it says that his memory is mentioned only in Delehaye's synaxarion, with no other information whatever, in these words: «μνήμη τοῦ ἁγίου Πατρὸς ἡμῶν Νικολάου τοῦ Κατοπινοῦ» — the memory of our holy father Nicholas Katopinos.

That formula, our holy father, is the only thing the page says about what he was, and it is the whole ground of the one type recorded here. It gives him no century, no place, no office, no relics, no couplet and no hymn; and what stands above the sentence is only saint.gr's standing gloss on the name Nicholas, the people's conqueror, which belongs to the name and not to this man.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3647/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
