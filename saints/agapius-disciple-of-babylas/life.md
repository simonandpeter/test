# Agapius, disciple of Babylas

Agapius has his own line on doxologia.ro's calendar for 24 ianuarie, and the life behind that line is his teacher's: «Sfântul Mucenic Vavila din Sicilia, este pomenit împreună cu ucenicii săi, Timotei şi Agapie» — Babylas is remembered together with his disciples Timothy and Agapius. The page says nothing of Agapius apart from the three of them, and what follows is what it says of all three.

[Babylas](/saints/babylas-of-sicily) was born of good and God-loving parents at Antioch, gave his inheritance to the poor, the widows and the orphans when his parents died, and withdrew to the mountain; Agapius and [Timothy](/saints/timothy-disciple-of-babylas) kept the stillness there with him. After Babylas was made a priest, and the pagans sought to give him up to the magistrates, the three crossed to Sicily and brought many there to the knowledge of God.

They could not stay hidden. The magistrate took all three, and when they confessed Christ to be the true God they were beaten with staves until they bled, then carried through the city and struck in various cruel ways, so as to frighten the cities of Sicily and to satisfy the tormentors. The saints held firm, looking to the everlasting good things. The next day they were cut down with the swords and thrown into a fire; the fire did not harm their bodies, and Christians took them and buried them worthily on the island of Sicily.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, with [Babylas](/saints/babylas-of-sicily) and [Timothy](/saints/timothy-disciple-of-babylas), on one page. saint.gr has Babylas withdraw to a hesychastērion with his two disciples after his parents' death, and it is on a missionary journey of his to Sicily that the governor there, having tried in vain to pull the saint and his disciples from the faith, put them to fearful tortures which proved unable to bend their fighting spirit or their faith, and at last slaughtered them with knives and threw their bodies into the fire. The distich the page prints for the two disciples together says of them only that they were plainly imitators of their Teacher: «Τον Aγάπιον συνάμα Τιμοθέω, Σαφώς μιμητάς οίδα του Διδασκάλου».

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 24 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/24-ianuarie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-mucenic-agapie-0), which is the life of Babylas and his two disciples; read 19 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 24 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/24/index.aspx) and [the life of the three of them](https://www.saint.gr/3516/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026.*
