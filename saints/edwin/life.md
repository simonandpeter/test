# Edwin

Edwin was baptised at Easter 627 by Bishop Paulinus at York, the Greek synaxarion says. After his baptism the people of his kingdom came to Christianity in crowds, and since there were no churches or baptisteries yet they were baptised in lakes and rivers. Edwin became a true Christian and gave the Church much help. He was killed in battle in 633, at the age of forty-eight.

That is the whole of the Greek notice, which does not name his kingdom. The Greek calendar keeps him on 4 October, and its heading gives his life as 585–633.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 4 Οκτωβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2594/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
