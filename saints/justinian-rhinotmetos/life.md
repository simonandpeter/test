# Justinian Rhinotmetos

saint.gr's notice for 2 Αυγούστου is one sentence of prose and no more: this, it says, is Justinian II the New, the Slit-nosed, who reigned from the year 685 to the year 695 and was buried at the Holy Apostles. There is no account of his life, no parentage, no deed, no manner of death, no year of birth and no hymn. The couplet of the synaxarion is addressed to him as a king: «Οὐκ ἐκποδών σοι σκῆπτρον ὤφθη ὧ ἄναξ, / Σὺ γὰρ κατοικεῖς Βασιλείαν τὴν ἄνω» — the sceptre was not seen to be out of your way, O king, for you dwell in the Kingdom above.

He is not Justinian the Great, whom the Romanian calendar keeps on this same civil day. The page's own gloss on the name mentions that emperor separately, as the great emperor of Byzantium who raised the church of Hagia Sophia; but the saint of this entry is the later Justinian, of the reign of 685 to 695, and the two are two men.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 2 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/02/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2195/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
