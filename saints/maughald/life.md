# Maughald

Maughald came from Ireland, and in his earlier life he was a robber. It was [Patrick](/saints/patrick-of-ireland) who led him to the Orthodox faith; and through baptism he became a new man in Christ, and in order to escape the dangers of bad company and of temptation he left the things of the world and settled on the island of Man.

To that island Patrick had sent Germanus, when he consecrated him bishop; and Maughald reached it after Germanus had fallen asleep. In the year 498 he was elected by the Christians bishop of the local church there, and he wore himself out in the missionary work of spreading the faith. Maughald fell asleep in peace.

That is the whole of the page. It gives him no year of birth or of death, no parentage, no companion besides Patrick and Germanus, and no relic, and it prints neither couplet nor hymn. The calendar's line for the day spells his name in Latin letters and marks him only «(Ιρλανδός)», the Irishman.
