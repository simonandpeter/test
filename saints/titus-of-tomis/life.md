# Titus of Tomis

Doxologia.ro's calendar for 3 ianuarie names him and gives no life. The line is «Sfântul Mucenic Tit, episcopul Tomisului» — the holy martyr Titus, bishop of Tomis — and that is the whole of what the calendar says: a name, the rank of martyr, and the see of Tomis on the Pontic shore. The page the calendar links carries no life, no year, no emperor and no account of his death.

What it offers in place of a life is a single article, under both „Sfinte moaște” and „Articole despre sfânt”, headed «Sfinții Mucenici Tit și Gordian, propovăduitori și apărători ai credinței ortodoxe în Dacia Pontică». That article was not read for this entry, and nothing in it has been used here.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 3 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/3-ianuarie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-tit-episcopul-tomisului); read 19 September 2026.*
