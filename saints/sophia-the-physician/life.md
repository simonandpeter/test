# Sophia the Physician

doxologia.ro's calendar names her «Sfânta Muceniță Sofia Doctorița» for 22 mai, and her page carries no Viață tab. What it prints of her is three sentences, and they are about her icons: that she is shown with a cross in her hand and with medical instruments; that she was a woman physician and died beheaded, as a martyr; and that she is also shown in the icons of the extended synaxis of the unmercenary physician saints.

That is the whole of what the calendar says. It gives her no country, no century, no emperor and no companions, and it prints neither troparion nor condac for her. Her epithet here, Doctorița, is the calendar's own word and is the reason she is kept apart in this corpus from the other women of her name whom it holds on other days.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 22 mai — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/22-mai) and [her page](https://doxologia.ro/sfanta-mucenita-sofia-doctorita), which prints no life; read 20 September 2026.*
