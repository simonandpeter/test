# Nicephorus of the Cave

saint.gr's calendar for 9 Νοεμβρίου names Nicephorus «ὁ ἐν τῷ Σπηλαίῳ», Nicephorus in the Cave, and says plainly that it has nothing of his life: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ρώσου Οσίου» — we have no details of the Russian venerable one's life. So what is recorded here is the name, the epithet of the cave, the day, and that the page calls him Russian.

There is no century on the page, no monastery named beyond the cave of the epithet, no year of birth or death, no couplet and no hymn. The same calendar keeps a Luke under the same epithet, also called Russian and also without a life, three days earlier on 6 November; nothing on either page joins the two.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 9 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/09/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2961/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
