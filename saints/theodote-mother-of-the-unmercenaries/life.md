# Theodote, mother of the Unmercenaries

The Greek calendar keeps Theodote on 2 January and says of her only who her sons were and how she died. saint.gr's whole account is one sentence: the holy Theodote was the mother of the Unmercenaries [Cosmas](/saints/cosmas-of-asia) and [Damian](/saints/damian-of-asia), whom the same calendar keeps on 1 November, and she departed this life in peace.

The page's epigram turns on the same contrast: Theodote passed out of this life by sickness, she who bore into the world the looseners of sickness. No birthplace, no year and no other detail is printed there, and none is supplied here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 2 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/02/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/10/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
