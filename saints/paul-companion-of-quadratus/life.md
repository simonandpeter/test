# Paul, companion of Quadratus of Corinth

Paul was one of the six friends whom saint.gr keeps together on 10 Μαρτίου and names in its line, «Άγιοι Κοδράτος, Ανεκτός, Παύλος, Διονύσιος, Κυπριανός και Κρήσκης». The page puts their martyrdom at Corinth in the persecution of the emperors Decius or Valerian, when the governor of Greece was Jason, and leaves the choice between the two reigns open. It gives no year.

Their guide was [Quadratus](/saints/quadratus-of-corinth), whom the page has born in the mountains above Corinth while his mother hid from the persecution, orphaned as a babe and fed by the clouds that gathered over him; when he was grown he taught the Christian faith to those who had taken refuge with him — Paul among them, with [Cyprian](/saints/cyprian-companion-of-quadratus) and [Dionysius](/saints/dionysius-companion-of-quadratus) and the rest.

The page says that they were arrested because they were Christians, that the governor Jason tried by various means to entice them and to persuade them to renounce their faith and sacrifice to the idols, and that they confessed Christ with spiritual manhood, were tortured, and were at last beheaded; and so, it says, the holy martyrs entered into the joy of the Kingdom of God. Of Paul by himself the page says nothing further.

Paul is named in the page's distich for the three taken together — «Γνωστοῖς Ἄνεκτον σὺν δυσὶ κτείνει ξίφος», the sword kills Anectus with two of his acquaintance — and in the apolytikion, «Παῦλος καὶ Διονύσιος». The page prints an apolytikion that names all six of them and a kontakion that calls them a lamp of six lights at Corinth, and it gives the six of them one commemoration.

The Serbian church keeps him on this day with his companions, and the Ohrid Prologue of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović names the five of them in one clause. When a new persecution arose under Decius, it says, [Quadratus](/saints/quadratus-of-corinth) was brought to trial and thrown into prison, and five of his companions joined themselves to him there and confessed the name of Christ; and it then prints the five names.

All of them were dragged through the streets and beaten with staves and with stones by the heathen, and by their children above all, until they were dragged to the place of execution. There the martyrs prayed to God and were beheaded with the sword. In that place a spring of water burst out of the earth, which to this day carries Quadratus's name and is a reminder of the heroic death for Christ of the six of them. They suffered honourably for the truth, the entry ends, in the year 250 at Corinth, in the time of the emperor Decius and of his governor Jason.

The Russian church keeps the company on this same day, and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery tells the arrest and the trial at more length than the other calendars read here. By order of the persecutor Decius, whose reign it gives as 249 to 251, a commander named Jason came to Corinth; the saints were seized together and thrown into prison, and at the questionings Jason turned most often to the eldest of them, who defended the faith and, under torture, found strength to hold the rest up and tell them not to be afraid. Getting no denial from any of them, Jason had them thrown to the beasts, and the beasts would not touch them; they were tied by the feet to chariots and dragged through the city while the crowd threw stones; and at the last they were sentenced to the sword, asked a little time to pray at the place of execution, and went up to the executioner one after another, bowing the head under the raised blade.

The page names him fourth of the five friends and followers who came out to the mountains for the hermit physician's teaching, and adds «и многие другие».

Two things on that page are worth saying because they do not agree with each other. Its line for the day closes with the year 258 and its life opens in the reign of Decius, 249 to 251; the interval this folder already holds is 249 to 259, which contains both readings, so nothing was narrowed. And the same line runs on past the company into fifteen more names and «и иных многих», among them seven men this very calendar also keeps, with the same name pages, on 31 января ст. ст., and a group of women it also keeps on 16 апреля ст. ст.; the day page carries no hymn at all.
