# Firmilian of Caesarea in Cappadocia

doxologia.ro's notice of Firmilian is four sentences, and this is the whole of it. He was a learned and active bishop in Caesarea of Cappadocia from the year 230. He took part in the theological disputes of his time: he condemned the schism of Novatian, and the position of Pope Stephen of Rome, who accepted the baptism of heretics; and he presided at the council of Antioch called against the heretic Paul of Samosata.

Of his writings one letter survives, written in the year 256 and addressed to [Cyprian of Carthage](/saints/cyprian-of-carthage). He died in the year 268, and the Romanian calendar keeps his memory on 28 October. The page gives no account of his birth, his ordination or his death, and none is added here.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 28 October, and joins a second name to his: saint.gr's line is «Όσιοι Φιρμιλιανός Αρχιεπίσκοπος Καισαρείας και Μελχίων Πρεσβύτερος Αντιοχείας», and one notice serves them both. Both of them contended against the heretics, it says; [Basil the Great](/saints/basil-the-great) mentions Firmilian with affection; and at a synod which this archbishop convoked at Iconium it was decided that the Novatians, and heretics generally who wished to come over to the true Church, were to be baptised. The epigram above the notice has the two of them die in peace — «Εἰρηνικῶς θνῄσκουσιν εἰρήνης τέκνα, Φιρμιλιανὸς καὶ σὺν αὐτῷ Μελχίων» — and that companion is the corpus's [Melchion of Antioch](/saints/melchion-of-antioch). The page gives no year and prints no hymn.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 28 octombrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/28-octombrie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-firmilian-episcopul-cezareei-capadociei); read 24 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 28 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/28/index.aspx) and [the notice he shares with Melchion](https://www.saint.gr/2822/saint.aspx), read 1 October 2026.*
