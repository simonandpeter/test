# Theodosius (Boldyrev)

He was born on 8 February 1868 in the village of Ekoleno in the Voronezh province, the son of a peasant, and was brought up and schooled in a monastery before he was ordained priest. During the persecution of the thirties he was arrested and exiled; the reading has his own account of it, that in 1931 he was sentenced by a collegium of the OGPU at Leningrad to three years for counter-revolutionary agitation and served it at Arkhangelsk. He came back from exile in December 1935, settled in the Tver country on the invitation of an old friend, and served in the church of the village of Afimyino in the Vyshny Volochek district.

He was arrested again on 21 December 1937, in the new wave of persecution, and questioned the next day by the assistant of the district officer of state security. Asked why he had come to Afimyino he gave the friend’s letter and the promise of a place in the choir; asked what he had been tried for in 1931 he gave the article and the sentence plainly; and asked about the agitation the investigation said it had evidence of, he answered that he had never held counter-revolutionary conversations with anybody and had never spoken a thought against the Soviet power or the order of things.

The indictment was drawn up on 24 December and a troika of the NKVD sentenced him to be shot on 27 December. He was sixty-nine years old. He was shot on 29 December 1937, and was numbered among the New Martyrs and Confessors of Russia at the Jubilee Council of Bishops in August 2000.
