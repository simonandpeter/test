# Plotinus (20 February)

saint.gr's calendar for 20 Φεβρουαρίου names him and gives no life. Its line reads «Όσιος Πλωτίνος», and the page standing under that line says only that the venerable Plotinus fell asleep in peace and that we have no further details of his life. Its couplet has the Lord speak and the angels obey: «Tου Kυρίου φήσαντος, ήχθω Πλωτίνος, Άγοντες ήκον Άγγελοι τον Πλωτίνον» — at the Lord's word, let Plotinus be brought; and the angels came, bringing Plotinus.

Above the couplet the page prints its standing gloss on the name — that it comes from πλέω, to float, and that Plotinus was a Neoplatonic philosopher of Lycopolis in Egypt who lived from 204 to 269. That belongs to the name and to its most famous bearer, not to this saint: the entry says nothing of when or where the venerable Plotinus lived, so no year is recorded here. It names no relics and prints no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 20 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/20/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3765/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
