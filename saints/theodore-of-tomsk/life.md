# Theodore of Tomsk

The Greek calendar names him on 20 Ιανουαρίου as Theodore of Tomsk, and the page behind that line says in one sentence that there is nothing more: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ρώσου Αγίου» — we have no details of the Russian saint's life. Under it the page sets a single link out, to a page of feasts and saints which has not been read here.

So the day claims a name and a Siberian city and no rank beyond *Άγιος*, which the site gives to martyrs, hierarchs and laymen alike; this folder therefore records no rank for him. No century, no office, no manner of life and no hymn is printed. A fuller account would have to come from a Russian source that has not been read.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 20 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/20/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4259/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
