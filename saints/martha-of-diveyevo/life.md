# Martha (Milyukova) of Diveyevo

Martha — Maria Semyonovna Milyukova in the world — was born on 10 (23) February 1810 to a peasant family of the village of Pogiblovo, today Malinovka, in the Ardatov district of Nizhny Novgorod province; the Milyukovs, a family of righteous and God-pleasing life, were close to the elder Seraphim of Sarov, and besides Maria there were two elder children, a sister Praskovya and a brother John. The village and those round it were crown villages, belonging not to a landowner but to the treasury, their land cut separately and poor, the black earth having been seized by the neighbouring landowners; the peasants lived very poorly, and the children worked hard — the calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. At thirteen, with the blessing of Seraphim, she entered the Diveyevo community, where she led a strict ascetic life — a chosen vessel of grace, Seraphim called her; she took part in the founding of the Mill community and carried stones for the building of the church, and died at nineteen on 21 August 1829. Seraphim himself tonsured her into the schema before her death and foretold the incorruption of her relics, which rest in the church of the Nativity of the Theotokos at the Seraphim-Diveyevo monastery; the Russian calendar keeps her on 21 August (Julian).
