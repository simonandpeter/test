# Hyginus of Rome

Hyginus — or Hygienus, as saint.gr allows the name to be spelt, «ο Άγιος Υγίνος (ή Υγίεινος)» — was born at Athens and was a philosopher. Afterwards he was ordained bishop of Rome, and the page dates his episcopate 136 to 140. He was martyred in the year 140, under the emperor Antoninus Pius, whose reign the page gives as 138 to 161.

That is the whole of the entry: three sentences, and nothing about how he was killed, or what he taught, or where he was buried. The calendar keeps him on 11 January and prints no hymn for him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/11/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1094/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
