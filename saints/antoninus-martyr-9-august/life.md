# Antoninus

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 9 august reads «Sfântul Mucenic Antonin», and the page behind that line carries the same name and the same day, the epistle and gospel appointed for a martyr — 2 Timothy 2 and John 15 — and nothing else: no narrative, no troparion, no year, no country, no account of how he died.

What the line does assert is that he was a martyr, which is what «Mucenic» says. Nothing further is set down here because nothing further was read; the Greek, Russian and Serbian calendars were not consulted for him, and their rows say so.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 9 August, and names his city. saint.gr says the country of Antoninus was Alexandria, where he preached the truth of the Gospel of the Lord Jesus Christ publicly and with zeal; that he was arrested by the governor of Alexandria and cruelly tortured; that they first hung him up and tore his flesh; and that they then threw him into a lighted furnace, where he gave up his holy soul to God. His holy relic, the page ends, was found inside the furnace untouched, the fire having harmed not one hair of the head. The page gives no year and no emperor, and prints no hymn.

The Ohrid Prologue of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović gives him four sentences on the Julian 9 August, and they are the first life this folder has had: the Romanian and the Greek each gave it a line. It heads him Antony rather than Antoninus and says of him only that he was a citizen of Alexandria, which is the one thing the Greek line says too. Brought before a heathen prince, he declared his faith freely, and was tortured, beaten and raked, and would not deny Christ.

At last he was thrown into the fire, and out of the middle of it he spoke to the people: my beloved brethren, do not be slaves to the body, but care more for the soul, which is given you by God and is akin to God and to the divine powers. And so, teaching the people and burning in the fire, he gave up his holy soul to God. The entry gives no year, names no emperor and does not say who the prince was.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 9 august — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/9-august) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-antonin); read 24 September 2026; saint.gr's calendar for 9 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/09/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2292/saint.aspx), read 1 October 2026; and the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 9. август ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-08-22&prolog=1), read 6 October 2026.*
