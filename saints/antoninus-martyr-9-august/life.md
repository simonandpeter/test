# Antoninus

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 9 august reads «Sfântul Mucenic Antonin», and the page behind that line carries the same name and the same day, the epistle and gospel appointed for a martyr — 2 Timothy 2 and John 15 — and nothing else: no narrative, no troparion, no year, no country, no account of how he died.

What the line does assert is that he was a martyr, which is what «Mucenic» says. Nothing further is set down here because nothing further was read; the Greek, Russian and Serbian calendars were not consulted for him, and their rows say so.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 9 august — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/9-august) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-antonin); read 24 September 2026.*
