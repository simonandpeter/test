# Vladimir (Pankin)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and carries no life. Its line for the twenty-fourth of March, old style, puts two priests of two different years in one printed sentence and leaves out the full stop between them: «Сщмч. Александра пресвитера (1918) Сщмч. Владимира пресвитера (1920).». He is the second of the two, and the name page behind «Владимира» is headed «Владимир (Панькин), иерей, сщмч.», giving both the surname and the grade the line leaves out.

That page lists the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church on «26 января» and this twenty-fourth of March. Nothing else is said of him on either page: no place, no diocese, no parish, no year of birth and no account of how he died. The 1920 the second half of the line closes with is the whole of his chronology.

This calendar keeps a good many priests of this forename whom it tells apart by the surname on the name page and by nothing else, and the corpus now holds three of them from three separate pages and three separate days. The second source for the Russian new martyrs, azbyka.ru, was not read for him.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 24 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260324.html); read 8 October 2026.*
