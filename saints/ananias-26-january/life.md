# Ananias (26 January)

These saints lived, saint.gr says, in the years of the emperor Diocletian and of Maximus, governor of Phoenicia, which the page dates 295. When the priest Ananias was arrested, and because he confessed Christ and threw down the idols by his prayer, Maximus gave orders and they scourged him without mercy and burned his sides with heated spits; then upon his cut limbs they threw vinegar and salt.

After that they put him in the prison, where by a wonder he was fed by God for whole days together. The wonder drew to faith in Christ the gaoler [Peter](/saints/peter-the-gaoler) and seven other [soldiers](/saints/seven-soldiers-26-january), whom the governor drowned in the sea together with Ananias. So all of them together received the crowns of the contest from the Lord who gives them.

The page gives no birthplace for Ananias and no year of birth, and prints no troparion and no kontakion. Above the account it sets a couplet: «Πέτρος σὺν ἑπτὰ τὴν θάλασσαν εἰσέδυ, / Οἷς Ἀνανίας ἡδέως συνεισέδυ» — Peter with the seven went down into the sea, and Ananias gladly went down with them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1172/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
