# Parthagape

Parthagape was martyred by drowning in the sea, finding above, the verse says, a sea of gifts. That is the whole of the synaxarion’s notice for the martyr kept on 2 September, with its verse; the Synaxaristes have nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 2 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/916/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
