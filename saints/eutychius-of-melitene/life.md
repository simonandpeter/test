# Eutychius of Melitene

Doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 28 mai reads «Sfântul Sfințit Mucenic Eutihie, Episcopul Melitinei», the holy hieromartyr Eutychius, bishop of Melitene, and the page the calendar links for him carries no Viață tab — only a canon of prayer, a troparion in Romanian and a recording of it in Greek, icons, and the readings appointed for the day. Where he suffered, and when, and under whom, the source does not say.

The rank and the see are the whole of what is stated: a bishop of Melitene who died a martyr. The troparion printed under his name is the common of a martyr with his name set into it, calling him one who in his contest received the crown of incorruption and, having God's strength, overcame the tormentors. No year and no place is entered for him, because the page gives none.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and is franker than the Romanian about how little is known. saint.gr opens by saying outright that where he came from and when he contended are unknown — «Είναι άγνωστο από που καταγόταν και πότε άθλησε» — and then gives what there is: he was raised to be bishop of Melitene, was arrested for his Christian activity, refused to sacrifice to the idols, and after many tortures was thrown into the water, where he found a martyr's death. The page adds a correction against its own heading: Sophronios Eustratiades, in his hagiology, held that the form Eutyches and the see of Melitene are both wrongly written, and that the right reading is Eutychius, martyr and not bishop. The correction is recorded here and nothing is changed on it.

The Russian church calendar keeps him on 28 мая ст. ст. and gives him a life of one sentence: that he was bishop of Melitene, a fellow-worker of the holy apostles, and that he suffered for Christ in the city of Melitene in the first century. It names no persecutor, no company, no manner of death and no year. The phrase «сотрудник святых апостолов» is the only thing it adds to this folder’s own reading of him, and the century is the whole of its chronology.
