# Ananias, one of the Three Youths

doxologia.ro prints one long life for the three youths and [Daniel](/saints/daniel-the-prophet) together, and Ananias is named in it first of the three. He was of the sons of Judah and of royal family, one of the children Nebuchadnezzar had brought to the palace from among the captives — young men without blemish, fair of face, wise and learned — to be taught letters and the Chaldean tongue and fed for three years from the king's own table. The chief of the eunuchs renamed the four: Ananias he called Shadrach, [Misael](/saints/misael-of-the-three-youths) Meshach, [Azarias](/saints/azarias-of-the-three-youths) Abednego, and Daniel Belteshazzar.

With the other three he refused the king's meat and wine so as not to be defiled, and asked for the seeds of the earth and water instead; and after the ten days' trial their faces were fairer than those of the children fed at the king's table, and God gave the four sharpness and wisdom, so that the king found them ten times wiser than all the enchanters of his kingdom.

In the eighteenth year Nebuchadnezzar made an image of gold sixty cubits high and six cubits broad and set it in the plain of Dura in the land of Babylon, and summoned all the governors and lords of his dominions to its unveiling; and a furnace was built there in the field for the destruction of any who should go against the royal order, while the herald cried that at the sound of the trumpet, the flute, the lute and every kind of music all peoples should worship the golden image, and whoever did not fall down should be thrown into the furnace of perpetual fire.

All the peoples fell down and worshipped, but certain Chaldean men came to the king and accused Ananias, Azarias and Misael of not worshipping his gods or the image; and when the king called them and asked whether it was true, they answered: our God whom we serve is in the heavens, and is able to bring us out of the burning furnace and to deliver us out of your hands, O king; be it known to you that we will not serve your gods and will not worship the golden image you have set up. Nebuchadnezzar was filled with rage and the look of his face changed against Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego, and he ordered the furnace heated seven times over and strong men to bind them and throw them in.

As they were thrown in the fire came out of the furnace and burnt those who had accused them; and the three righteous men fell bound into the middle of the great fire and walked in the flame, praising God and blessing him. The king's servants did not stop feeding the furnace with brushwood, brimstone, pitch, tow and vine-shoots, and the flame rose some forty cubits and burnt all the Chaldeans standing round it; but the angel of the Lord came down to those who were with Azarias in the furnace and blew the flame away from them like a breath of dew, so that the fire did not touch them at all, nor grieve them, nor trouble them.

Then the three praised and blessed and glorified God: blessed are you, O Lord, God of our fathers, most praised and most exalted for ever — and the rest of the songs that are written in the book of Daniel. Nebuchadnezzar heard them singing and wondered, and rose in haste and said to his lords: did we not throw three bound men into the middle of the fire? They answered, truly, O king. And he said: but I see four men loosed, walking in the middle of the fire, and they are unhurt, and the form of the fourth is like the Son of God. Then he came to the mouth of the burning furnace and said: Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego, servants of the Most High God, come out and come here.

The three came out, and all who had been called gathered to see them, and how the fire had not overcome their bodies, and the hair of their heads was not burnt, and there was no smell of fire on them. Then the king bowed to God before them and said: blessed is the God of Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego, who sent his angel and brought his servants out of the evil fire; and I command that every people and tongue that speaks blasphemy against their God be destroyed and their houses plundered, since there is no other God who can deliver like this. And he honoured the three youths greatly, magnifying them before all and counting them worthy to be set over all the Jews in his whole kingdom.

They came with Daniel to a great old age. The life says, citing Cyril of Alexandria and others, that after the death of the kings who had honoured them a king named Attic questioned them about their faith, and being reproached for his ungodliness ordered their heads struck off; and that when Ananias was beheaded Azarias spread out his garment and received his head, Misael received the head of Azarias, Daniel the head of Misael, and last of all Daniel's own head was cut off. The heads are said to have joined themselves to their bodies again, and the angel of the Lord to have carried the bodies to Mount Gebal and laid them under a stone; and four hundred years later, at the Resurrection of Christ, these also rose and appeared to many and fell asleep again. doxologia.ro gives no year for him, and this folder gives none.

The Greek calendar keeps the four of them on the same day, and saint.gr tells the furnace with the youths' own words in it. The three young Hebrews, it says, studied at Nebuchadnezzar's imperial court together with [Daniel](/saints/daniel-the-prophet), whom the emperor had renamed Belteshazzar, and were given great place in the state when they came of age. Then Nebuchadnezzar made a golden image of his own and required all his officials and the people to worship it; Daniel was away on a mission, and it was the three youths who did not worship it.

They were denounced to the king at once, and he told them that if they truly had not worshipped it the furnace of fire awaited them. Then the three answered: hear, O king, the God of heaven whom we worship is so strong that he can bring us out of the furnace of fire whole and unharmed and save us from your hands; and even if he does not do it, know that we do not worship your gods and do not bow to your image. And indeed, when they threw them into the fire, the three youths came out whole and unharmed. [Azarias](/saints/azarias-of-the-three-youths) and [Misael](/saints/misael-of-the-three-youths) are the other two.
