# Claudianus, companion of Pappias

saint.gr's calendar for 25 Οκτωβρίου names three martyrs in one line and tells a single story for them. [Pappias](/saints/pappias-of-attaleia), [Diodorus](/saints/diodorus-companion-of-pappias) and Claudianus suffered in the years of Decius, the page says, whose reign it dates 249 to 251. They came from Attaleia in Pamphylia and were herdsmen by trade; but they worshipped the one true God, and for that Publius, the governor of Pamphylia, arrested them.

Because he could not change their mind, the page says, he beheaded them in the end. That is the whole of the entry. The couplet above it says that one sword cuts the heads of three contestants whose mind was one and the same in three heads. There is no year inside the reign, no further place, and no hymn.
