# Paul (Dobromyslov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him on 20 января ст. ст. and gives no life. The whole of its account is one line, «Сщмч. Павла пресвитера (1940)» — the hieromartyr Paul, a presbyter, 1940 — and behind that line the calendar's own name link carries both the surname the line omits and a higher rank than the line gives him, «Павел (Добромыслов), протоиерей, сщмч.»: the archpriest Paul Dobromyslov.

The calendar names no birthplace, no diocese, no parish, no place of death and no manner of it. The 1940 in its bracket is the whole of what it dates him by, and it is the year of his death. He stands on this day among the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and he is the only name on it from his own century besides a Georgian confessor.

The day page carries troparia for three of the saints it heads, and none under his name. That is everything the source says of him.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 20 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260120.html); read 3 October 2026. The calendar prints no life page for him.*
