# Marcian of Syracuse

saint.gr's calendar for 30 Οκτωβρίου names «Άγιος Μαρκιανός ο Ιερομάρτυρας», and its notice begins with his commission: he was ordained bishop by the Apostle [Peter](/saints/peter-the-apostle) and sent to Syracuse in Sicily. The page says nothing of where he came from, who his parents were or how old he was, and gives him no year.

There, it says, the saint destroyed the temples of the idols by his prayer; and he did not only that, but worked many wonders, so that many of the heathen came to believe. But the envious could not bear the saint's boldness, and they put him to death by violence — the notice and the epigram over it both lay the killing to the Jews of the city, «οἱ Χριστοκτόνοι», and the epigram says the death was by a noose.

That is the whole of the entry: no relics, no place of burial and no hymn. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands.

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on the Julian 30 October and its life adds three things to the Greek notice. He was a disciple of the holy Apostle [Peter](/saints/peter-the-apostle) and was sent by him to Sicily; there he settled in a cave near the city of Syracuse and spread the faith of Christ from it successfully; and his relics are now in the Italian city of Gaeta. On the death the two calendars agree: he ended his life as a martyr, killed, and the killing is laid to the Jews of the place.

The page then closes with a bracket that neither of the other calendars has, and it is a claim about who he is rather than about what he did: that this hieromartyr is one person with a bishop of Sicily whom the same calendar keeps on 9 февраля, and whom this corpus keeps as a folder of his own, read from the Romanian and Greek calendars for that day. The bracket is recorded in the Russian attestation and nothing here acts on it. The day's line dates him to the second century where its own life makes him a disciple of Peter in the first; both readings are held in the interval and neither is preferred.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 30 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/30/index.aspx) and [his notice](https://www.saint.gr/2855/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 30 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261030.html) and [his life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4616.htm), read 4 October 2026.*
