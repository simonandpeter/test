# Theophanes the Faster of the Caves

The Russian calendar's 11 October prints «Прп. Феофана , постника Печерского (XII)» — Theophanes, the Faster, of the Caves, in the twelfth century — and the site's index of names repeats it as «Феофан Печерский, Постник» and gives him one other day, 28 September, with the venerable fathers of the Near Caves. There is no life on either page.

So the epithet is the record. «Постник», the Faster, is a name the Caves gave to monks whose abstinence was the thing remembered of them, and it is the only thing this calendar says about how he lived; the century is the only thing it says about when. The long accounts of the fathers of the Kyiv Caves are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026 even with a browser user-agent, so the line stands as the line, and padding it out of a general knowledge of the Caves would be inventing a man the source only names.
