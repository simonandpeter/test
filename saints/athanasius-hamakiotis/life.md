# Athanasius Hamakiotis

Athanasius was born in 1891 in a mountain village of Kalavryta, in Greece, and was given the name George at his baptism. He left for the monastery at the age of fifteen, and seven years later was tonsured to the monastic life with the name Athanasius. He was ordained deacon at twenty-five and priest at thirty, in 1921. Doxologia's calendar gives no more of his early years than this.

From 1931 he served in various churches in Athens, and in 1936 he was appointed vicar of the church of the Panagia Neratziotissa at Maroussi, which became known throughout Attica for the charitable work he did there. He is remembered for the beauty with which he served the Divine Liturgy, and for the almsgiving and the love he showed to everyone who came to him.

The last four years of his life he lived in the small monastery of the Panagia Phaneromeni at Rodopoli in Attica, which he built himself. He fell asleep in the Lord on 17 August 1967 and was buried in the monastery courtyard. The faithful may still visit his cell in the monastery and venerate his head.
