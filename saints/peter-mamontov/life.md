# Peter (Mamontov)

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him eighteenth of twenty-four and gives no life; the life here is the one azbyka.ru prints for the civil 2 декабря, out of fond.ru. Pavel Ivanovich Mamontov was born on 7 June 1880 in the village of Dedinovo in the Zaraysk district of the province of Ryazan, in a peasant family. He finished the parish school, went to Moscow in 1893 to work as a boy in a shop, and in the same year left it for the Transfiguration Guslitsky monastery, where he lived a year before his call-up came due. He waited for it working at the porcelain works at Dulevo, served a single month in 1901 as a militiaman, went back to the works for half a year, and in 1902 returned to the monastery for good.

That house had been founded in the middle of the nineteenth century in a country of Old Believers, as a missionary monastery meant to serve the overcoming of the schism. He was enrolled among the brotherhood on 13 October 1908, tonsured with the name Peter on 29 June 1912 and ordained hierodeacon on 18 April 1914, and he stayed there until the closure of 1929; in 1919 and 1920 he was mobilised into the rear militia and served at the Moscow veterinary garrison infirmary. In 1930 he was sent to the Nikitskaya church in the village of Byvalino, where the abbot Joasaph (Krymzin) was the senior priest, and in 1933 he was ordained hieromonk to that same church.

On 27 September 1937, the feast of the Exaltation of the Cross, he asked the parishioners of the Nikitskaya church to give what each of them could towards the keep of the hierarchy. They answered the call and a collection was made, and when he was arrested that collection was charged against him. At the beginning of November false witnesses were questioned, and on their testimony he was charged with systematically carrying on anti-Soviet agitation aimed at undermining the measures of the party and the government.

He was arrested on 18 November 1937, put in the prison of Noginsk and questioned on the 22nd. Four times the investigator put the charge to him in a different shape — anti-Soviet agitation, counter-revolutionary agitation among the faithful and the collective farmers under cover of religion, the spreading of provocative rumours that the Soviet power was persecuting religion — and four times he said he had done nothing of the kind and had nothing to say on the matter, and at the last he said he stood by the evidence he had already given. On 25 November 1937 the troika sentenced him to be shot, and he was shot on 2 December 1937 and buried in a common unmarked grave at Butovo.
