# Antonina (Bryanskikh)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names the martyr Antonina on 27 December and gives no life. Its line is «Мц. Антонины (1937).» — the martyr Antonina, 1937 — and the calendar’s own name page for her carries the surname the line leaves out: «Антонина (Брянских), мц.»

That is the whole of the record. The calendar gives her no grade and no office, only the rank of martyr; the year of death the line gives is 1937; the calendar keeps her on this day among the memories of the Nativity afterfeast. The day page carries four hymns — three for the protomartyr Stephen and one for the venerable Theodore — and none under this name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 27 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261227.html); read 13 October 2026.*
