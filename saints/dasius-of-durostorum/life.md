# Dasius of Durostorum

saint.gr's calendar for 20 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιος Δάσιος που μαρτύρησε στο Δορύστολο» — Dasius, who was martyred at Dorostolon. He lived towards the end of the third century, the page says, in the time of Maximian, and received the crown of martyrdom in that city, which was built near the river Istros, the Danube. The couplet set over the notice plays on the sword: Dasius, contending by the sword, was to find an athlete's crown out of the sword.

He belonged to the military ranks, and a great faith in Christ held him; and for that reason he was denounced to his commanding officer. The officer tried first to tempt him with promises, and when he saw that he had not managed it he threatened him with torture. The saint stayed faithful to the Lord all the same. So his commander gave the order for his torture, and at the end of it for his beheading.

The page adds where his relics are: they lie in the Roman Catholic church of Saint Cyriacus at Ancona in Italy. It gives him no rank beyond the soldiering, no year of birth and no hymn.
