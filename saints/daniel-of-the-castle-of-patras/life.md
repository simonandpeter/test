# Daniel of the Castle of Patras

saint.gr's whole notice of him is four sentences, and the first of them says where the source comes from: in the life of the venerable [Nicetas of Thebes](/saints/nicetas-of-thebes) it is recorded that he came to Patras and met the venerable Daniel, the one in the Castle. The page says he lived in the eleventh century, that he came from Patras itself, and that he lived with great ascesis and much prayer.

It adds one thing more about him, and it is the thing the calendar remembers him for: he received from God the gift of hospitality, as Abraham had it — that is, to receive people, to give them lodging and to give them rest, guiding them spiritually. The page gives no year of birth or death, no monastery, no account of his end and no hymn.
