# Gerasim (Mochalov)

**Twenty years in a hermitage, and when it was closed he served three parishes in fourteen years.** The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him eleventh of the sixteen it closes with 1937, first of the five it calls «прмчч.», and carries no life for him; the life here is what azbyka.ru prints for the civil 5 декабря out of fond.ru, from the hegumen Дамаскин (Орловский)’s volume of lives of the new martyrs of the Moscow diocese for November. Григорий Игнатьевич Мочалов was born in 1870 in the village of Маринино in the Дмитров district of the province of Moscow, of a peasant family, and was schooled in the village school. On 6 September 1903 he was received among the novices of the Смоленская Зосимова пустынь in the province of Владимир and put to obedience in the locksmith’s shop; on 13 December 1906 he was enrolled in the brotherhood, and on 2 April 1908 tonsured to the mantia with the name Герасим. In 1910 he was ordained hierodeacon and in 1920 hieromonk.

**Three churches, each until it was shut.** In 1923 the godless power closed the Зосимова пустынь, and he went to serve in the Николаевский Песношский monastery in the Дмитров district, where he served until that too was closed; he was then appointed to the church of the village of Гари, once part of the same district, and from 1936 served in the church of the Protection in the village of Кикино in the Дмитров region.

**He was arrested, questioned and sentenced in four days.** On 27 November 1937 he was arrested, shut in the Таганская prison in Moscow, and questioned the same day. The investigator demanded testimony on the active anti-Soviet activity he was said to be carrying on among the population; he answered «Никакой антисоветской деятельности я не вел и это отрицаю» — he had carried on none and denied it. The investigator went on to ask about the woman in whose house he lodged and to insist that he had gathered anti-Soviet meetings of believers there and agitated among them; he rejected the inventions flatly, confirming only that he had lived there and that if anybody came to him it was to arrange the celebration of the church mysteries. Pressed again to admit counter-revolutionary activity, he rejected all of it.

**False witnesses signed what the investigation needed.** On 1 December the troika of the NKVD condemned him to be shot, and on 4 December he was shot and buried in an unmarked common grave at the Бутово range outside Moscow. That day was the Julian 21 ноября, the day before the one the calendars keep him on; of the five men this line calls «прмчч.» he is the only one not of Оптина, and the only one the sources put to death on the 4th rather than the 5th.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 22 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261122.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9207.html), which carries no life; and the article azbyka.ru prints for the civil 5 декабря — [the entry](https://azbyka.ru/days/sv-gerasim-mochalov); read 6 October 2026.*
