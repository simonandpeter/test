# Januarius the Presbyter

The Greek synaxarion keeps four martyrs together on 30 August — Felix the bishop, Januarius the presbyter, Fortunatus and Septiminus — with a verse for each and a note that their memory is repeated on 16 April, where their life stands. Januarius, the presbyter, lost his head to the iron of a man who breathed murder.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 30 Αυγούστου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/284/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
