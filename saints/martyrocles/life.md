# Martyrocles

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life. Its whole notice of him is one sentence: the holy Martyrocles was martyred, having been put to death with shot arrows — «Ο Άγιος Μαρτυροκλής μαρτύρησε, αφού τον θανάτωσαν με εκτοξευμένα βέλη». The day's line is as bare as that: «Άγιος Μαρτυροκλής», with no country, no century and no companion.

Above the notice the page sets a distich, which plays on the name and adds nothing to the facts: «O Mαρτυροκλής Mαρτύρων εύρε κλέος, Mάρτυς φανείς άριστος εκ τοξευμάτων» — Martyrocles found the glory of the martyrs, shown a most excellent martyr by the arrows. There is no year, no emperor, no place and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/11/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2123/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
