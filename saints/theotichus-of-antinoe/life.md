# Theotichus of Antinoë

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him last of four martyrs of Antinoë in Egypt on 14 декабря — «Мчч. Филимона, Аполлония, Ариана и Феотиха» — and the reading behind the line tells how he came into their company. It was not as a Christian. He was one of the bodyguards of the governor who had been persecuting them, and the reading calls him the senior of those men.

The governor was [Arianus of the Thebaid](/saints/arianus-of-the-thebaid), who had [Apollonius the Reader](/saints/apollonius-the-reader) and [Philemon the Flute-Player](/saints/philemon-the-flute-player) put to death and was then healed of a wounded eye by dust taken from Philemon’s grave. He repented, confessed Christ, and was baptised with his whole household and with his bodyguard; and those men, the reading says, went willingly to the torments for the love of Christ and were put to death.

The calendar gives him no life of his own and nothing else about him: not where he was born, not how he died, not a word he said. The reading dates his death and Arianus’s to 4 March 287, a year after the two they had killed. The day page carries one kontakion, and it is for the martyrs of the other company the day heads, not for his.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 14 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261214.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life3117.htm); read 13 October 2026.*
