# Nicholas (Amassiysky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him among the hieromartyrs on 13 декабря and gives no life. Its line is «Сщмч. Николая пресвитера (1938).» — the hieromartyr Nicholas the presbyter, 1938 — and the calendar’s own name page adds the surname the line leaves out: «Николай (Амассийский), иерей, сщмч.»

That is the whole of the record. He was a priest; he died in 1938, in the years the Russian church counts its new martyrs; and the calendar keeps him on this day alone, his line joined to no one else’s. The day page carries troparia and kontakia for five of the memories it heads, and none under his name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 13 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261213.html); read 13 October 2026.*
