# Auctus

Auctus was martyred at Amphipolis of Macedonia, near Kavala, with Taurion and with Thessalonike, whose passion comes first in saint.gr's notice for the three. She was the daughter of a priest of the idols, scourged, broken in her ribs, stripped of her property and exiled for refusing to deny Christ, and she died in that exile confessing him.

Auctus and Taurion, the page says, accused the brutal torturer and murderer of Thessalonike to his face, and for that they were denounced to the consular Thorybios. He ordered them stoned, and then a series of horrible tortures; but because the saints came through all of it whole and unharmed in a wonderful way, their beheading was ordered, and so they received the glorious crowns of martyrdom.

The couplet the page prints for the two of them turns on Taurion's name: «Ἐφεῦρον Αὖκτος, Ταυρίων εὐκτὸν τέλος, / Ξίφει τμηθέντες, οἷα ταῦροι Κυρίου» — Auctus and Taurion found the end they prayed for, cut down by the sword like bulls of the Lord. There is no year on the page and no hymn. Kept with him are [Taurion](/saints/taurion) and [Thessalonike](/saints/thessalonike).

days.pravoslavie.ru keeps the three of them together on the same day the Greek calendar does. Its line for 7 ноября ст. ст. is «Мчч. Авкта , Тавриона и Фессалоникии (739).», and the name link behind «Авкта» gives the calendar's own nominative, «Авкт Амфипольский (Македонский), мч.» — of Amphipolis and of Macedonia, which is where the life buries all three.

The life behind the three name links is one paragraph and is the same for all three. Thessalonike was the daughter of a pagan priest. When her impious father learned that his daughter had become a Christian he beat her without pity and drove her out of the house, leaving her no means of living at all. Auctus and Taurion tried to stand up for the Christian woman and to bring the hardened father to his senses; the priest denounced them and they were seized. Having confessed their faith in Christ before their torturers and borne cruel torments, they were beheaded. Soon after their martyrdom Thessalonike died too, and her body was buried with honour in the city of Amphipolis in Macedonia together with the martyrs Auctus and Taurion.

**The line prints a year nothing else gives these three.** It closes «(739)», and that figure is now the only date this folder carries; the life gives no year, names no emperor and no persecutor but the father, and saint.gr's line «Άγιοι Αύκτος, Ταυρίων και Θεσσαλονίκη» gave them no year either. It is recorded because the calendar prints it, and the date's note says that it stands alone.

The day page prints a troparion of tone 4 under the names of two of the three, «Тропарь мучеников Авкта и Тавриона», and it names Taurion and Auctus and not Thessalonike. It is kept in this folder with the day's common of martyrs.
