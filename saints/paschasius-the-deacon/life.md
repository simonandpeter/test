# Paschasius

saint.gr's calendar for 31 Μαΐου names him and gives two sentences. Paschasius lived in the fifth and sixth centuries, it says, and was a deacon of the Church of Rome. He wrote many theological works, which have not come down to us.

He fell asleep in peace in the year 512, and he is mentioned by [Gregory the Dialogist](/saints/gregory-the-dialogist), bishop of Rome, whom the same calendar keeps on 12 March. That is the whole of the source: it gives him no birthplace, no year of birth, no titles of the lost works, and no hymn.
