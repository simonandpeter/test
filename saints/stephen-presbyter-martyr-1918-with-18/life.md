# Stephen (Nemkov), with 18 martyrs

Stephen Nemkov (1882–1918) finished the Simbirsk seminary and served as deacon and then priest in the village of Deyanovo. In 1918, in the summer rising of the district against the Bolsheviks, a punitive detachment of Chekists arrested him with eighteen of his parishioners; refusing to flee, he was savagely beaten, and on 27 August he was shot with the others — but survived the shot to the head, and they finished him with a bayonet. The calendar at azbyka.ru tells his story with that of [Michael Voskresensky](/saints/michael-presbyter-martyr-1918-24-august) of Bortsurmany, arrested by the same Latvian Red Army detachment with twenty-eight of his people; he was numbered among the saints in 2000, and the Russian calendar keeps him with his eighteen on 27 August (Julian).
