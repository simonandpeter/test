# Nicholas (Zalessky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Nicholas among the hieromartyrs on 26 December and gives no life. Its line is «Сщмчч. Николая , Николая , Михаила пресвитеров и Михаила диакона (1930).» — the hieromartyrs Nicholas, Nicholas and Michael, presbyters, and Michael the deacon, 1930 — and the calendar’s own name page for this memory carries the fuller form the line leaves out: «Николай (Залесский), иерей, сщмч.»

That is the whole of the record. He was a priest; the year of death the line gives is 1930; the calendar keeps the memory on this day, the second day of the Nativity. The line names four men of two forenames and the four name pages behind it give the surnames the line omits, which is what tells them apart. The day page carries no troparion and no kontakion under this name; the three it prints belong to the day’s own feasts and to one of its icons. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 26 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261226.html); read 13 October 2026.*
