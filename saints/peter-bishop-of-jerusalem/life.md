# Peter of Jerusalem (26 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιος Πέτρος επίσκοπος Ιεροσολύμων» and then says two things about him, both of which are about the sources and not about the man. He is unknown to the synaxaristae. And his memory is recorded in the Jerusalem Kanonarion, page 119, in the edition of the Archimandrite Kallistos.

That is the whole of the entry. The page gives him no country, no century, no year, no predecessor or successor in the see, no manner of death, no relics and no hymn, and it sets no couplet over him. The see is the one thing it says of him, and this folder records the line and the two sentences and nothing else, because there is nothing else on the page to record.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3091/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
