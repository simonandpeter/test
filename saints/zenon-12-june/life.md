# Zenon (12 June)

saint.gr's calendar for 12 Ιουνίου names him «Όσιος Ζήνων», and what stands behind that line is less a life than a question the page leaves open. It says he is placed by Hippolyte Delehaye and by Machairas among the saints of the Church of Cyprus, and is indeed held to have been bishop of Kyrenia; Machairas is quoted for it — «Ζήνωνος του Αγιωτάτου επισκόπου Κιρυναίων», of Zenon the most holy bishop of the men of Kyrenia.

Then it sets against that the Synaxarion of Constantinople, which on 12 June makes mention, without any memoir, «των Οσίων Ζήνωνος και Τριφυλλίου επισκόπου» — of the venerable Zenon and Triphyllios the bishop. Triphyllios, the page says, is the well-known fourth-century bishop of Nicosia, whom it sends the reader to find on 13 Ιουνίου; but it is not certain, it goes on, how far the venerable Zenon kept with him can be identified with the Zenon of Kyrenia.

The page adds that there is another Cypriot saint of the name: Zenon bishop of Kourion, who with Reginos bishop of Constantia and Evagrios bishop of Soloi upheld the autocephaly of the Church of Cyprus at the Third Ecumenical Council at Ephesus in 431, and who signed its acts. So three men of one name stand behind this one line, and the page declines to say which of them it is keeping. No see and no century is recorded here for that reason, and the page prints no hymn.
