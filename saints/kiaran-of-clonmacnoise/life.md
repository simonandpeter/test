# Kiaran (Ciarán) of Ireland

The Greek synaxarion keeps Kiaran — Ciarán — on 9 September and says it has no details of his life except that he was born in 512 in Connacht in Ireland. This entry records the line and nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 9 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/988/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
