# Basil Sungurov

Basil was born on 20 March 1876 in the village of Spasskoye in the Ruza
district of Moscow province, the son of the psalmist Arseny Sungurov. After
the Volokolamsk church school he was made psalmist in 1898 of St Nicholas at
Amelfino in the Volokolamsk district, in 1910 psalmist of St Nicholas at
Zhegalovo in the Bogorodsk district, in 1915 deacon of the same church, and
in 1919 priest of the church of Mary Magdalene at Ulitkino in the same
district; in 1935 he was appointed to the Theophany church at Brykovo in the
Istra district, and in 1936 given the pectoral cross. He was arrested on 28
August 1937 and put in the Taganka prison in Moscow; two duty witnesses gave
the required testimony, and he, hearing the false witness read, did not
confirm it, and to the interrogator’s last question — do you admit that you
carried on counter-revolutionary activity to the last? — answered, no, I do
not. On 19 September 1937 the troika sentenced him to death; he was shot the
next day, 20 September 1937, and buried in an unknown common grave at
Butovo. The life is by the igumen Damascene (Orlovsky); the Church keeps him
on 7 September of the old style, 20 September, and in the synaxes of the new
martyrs of Butovo and of Moscow.
