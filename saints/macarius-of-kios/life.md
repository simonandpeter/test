# Macarius of Kios

Macarius was born at Kios in Bithynia and was first called Manuel; his father was Peter and his mother Anthousa. As a boy his parents gave him to a pious tailor to learn the trade. Unexpectedly his father denied Christ and went to Prousa, and when Manuel too came to Prousa his father seized him and took him before the judge with the false charge that he had promised to become a Turk; for all his protests the Turks beat him and circumcised him by force. A few days later he escaped to the Holy Mountain and was tonsured a monk at the Skete of Saint Anne with the name Macarius.

After twelve years on the Holy Mountain he took the blessing to bear witness for the faith, and came by way of Constantinople to Prousa, where he was arrested and brought to the court. There he confessed Christ and rebuked the religion of Muhammad, saying that he would never leave the light to go into darkness. He was cruelly tortured, savagely stoned and beheaded on 6 October 1590, and his head was later taken to the Skete of Saint Anne.
