# Andropelagia of Alexandria

Andropelagia and Thecla were virgin sisters, seized at Alexandria by the governor Valerian, who put them to harsh tortures and at last beheaded them; the verse says Andropelagia was as manly as Thecla towards the cutting off of her head. The synaxarion adds a note — the entry runs on past the point read here. The Greek synaxarion keeps the martyrs of Alexandria under Decius in separate entries on 6 September; the Russian calendar counts them as the eleven with the presbyter Faustus and the deacon Abibus.
