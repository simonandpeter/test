# Agapitus of Auxerre

Agapitus came of Gaul and lived between the fourth and the fifth centuries, saint.gr says. His parents pressed him to marry, and he married Martha; but after the wedding the two of them agreed to live in chastity and virginity, and Martha became a nun and Agapitus a monk. In 388 he was elected bishop of Auxerre, and he shepherded his diocese in a way pleasing to God; and for the holiness of his life God endowed him with the gift of working wonders. He fell asleep in peace in the year 418.

That is the whole of the notice. It names no wonder, no work of his, no place in the diocese, and no relic; it gives no year for his birth, and it prints no hymn.
