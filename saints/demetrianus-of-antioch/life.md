# Demetrianus of Antioch

saint.gr's calendar for 10 Νοεμβρίου keeps Demetrianus, bishop of Antioch and hieromartyr, and the whole of its notice is three sentences. He was bishop of Antioch from 253 to 256 — or, by other accounts, from 256 to 262. He was led away captive into Persia in 256, and died there. His successor on the throne of Antioch, Domnus, who held it from 270 to 273, was his son.

The page gives him no account of his captivity, no manner of death, no burial and no hymn, and prints no couplet for him. Its head line puts the year of his death at 262, which is the later of the two readings its own text sets side by side, so both stand in the dates recorded here. Two other bishops of this name are kept by the same calendar on other days, at Kythrea in Cyprus and at Tamassos.
