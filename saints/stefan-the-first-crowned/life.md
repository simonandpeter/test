# Stefan the First-Crowned

Stefan was the first crowned king of Serbia, anointed at his own royal foundation of Žiča by St Sava - his brother in the flesh and his father in the spirit - and together, the life says, the two of them raised Serbian Orthodoxy to great heights of glory. He was a true and zealous Christian, a wise ruler and a lover of peace. Of his four sons, three ruled Serbia one after another, and the fourth, Predislav, at the urging of his uncle Sava took monastic vows and became in time Sava II, third archbishop of the Serbs and a saint himself.

Before his death, at his own desire, Stefan was tonsured a monk by his brother with the name Simon, and he departed to the Lord on the twenty-fourth day of September in the year 1224. His wonder-working relics rest at the monastery of Studenica.
