# Theophylact of Bulgaria

saint.gr's calendar for 31 December keeps the venerable Theophylact of Bulgaria, whom it also calls ο Χαλκιδεύς, the man of Chalkis. Its whole account of him is three sentences. He was born at Chalkis, it says, and lived in the eleventh century; he was made archbishop of Ohrid and of Bulgaria; and he was an important ecclesiastical writer, who worked particularly on the interpretation of Holy Scripture.

That is all. The page names not one of the works it credits him with, gives no year of his birth or of his death, tells nothing of his rule of the see, names no relic, prints no couplet and prints no hymn. Where it keeps him beyond this day is in two companies it links at the end: the synaxis of all the saints of Euboea, which is the island Chalkis belongs to, and the Fifteen Hieromartyrs.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 31 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/31/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3733/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
