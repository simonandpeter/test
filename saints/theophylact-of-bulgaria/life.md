# Theophylact of Bulgaria

saint.gr's calendar for 31 December keeps the venerable Theophylact of Bulgaria, whom it also calls ο Χαλκιδεύς, the man of Chalkis. Its whole account of him is three sentences. He was born at Chalkis, it says, and lived in the eleventh century; he was made archbishop of Ohrid and of Bulgaria; and he was an important ecclesiastical writer, who worked particularly on the interpretation of Holy Scripture.

That is all. The page names not one of the works it credits him with, gives no year of his birth or of his death, tells nothing of his rule of the see, names no relic, prints no couplet and prints no hymn. Where it keeps him beyond this day is in two companies it links at the end: the synaxis of all the saints of Euboea, which is the island Chalkis belongs to, and the Fifteen Hieromartyrs.

The Охридски пролог for the Julian 31 December is nearly the whole of what this folder knows of him. He was born on the island of Euripos and brought up at Constantinople under the most famous teachers of the day. As a cleric of the Great Church he was chosen bishop and sent, against his own will, to Ohrid, where he spent about twenty-five years — the book puts them approximately at 1082 to 1108, hedging the figures itself, and they are the span of his episcopate and not of his life, so nothing in the folder's dates was altered to fit them.

Chomatianos of Ohrid called him „најмудријим архиепископом“, the wisest of archbishops. He was a man of enormous learning, worldly and theological, of refined Byzantine taste, melancholy and sensitive; and among the Slavs at Ohrid he felt himself an exile among barbarians. He wrote Commentaries on all four Gospels and on other books of the New Testament, which this book calls the best work of its kind after светог Златоуста and says is read to this day with great profit; of his other works it names the Letters and the Живот светог Климента Охридског, the life of the Ohrid saint whose name this folder leaves in the book's own Cyrillic because writing another saint's life four centuries after him is not being his companion. In old age he withdrew from Ohrid to Thessalonica, where, it is thought, he ended his earthly life and passed over into blessed eternity.
