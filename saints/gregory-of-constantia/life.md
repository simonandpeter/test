# Gregory of Constantia

saint.gr's entry for this Gregory is not a life but a citation, and the life says no more than the citation does. His memory, the page reports, is mentioned in the Patmos Codex 266 in these words: «Τῶν Ὁσίων Πατέρων ἠμῶν Γρηγορίου Κωνσταντίας τῆς Κύπρου [καί] Ἀδριανοῦ» — of our venerable fathers Gregory of Constantia in Cyprus and [Adrianus](/saints/adrianus-of-cyprus). The see in the day's line and in the manuscript is Constantia, the old Salamis on the east coast of Cyprus.

Nothing else stands on the page. There is no century, no year of birth or of repose, no predecessor and no successor in the see, no deed, no manner of death, no relic and no hymn; and the site offers nothing at all about the man beyond the words the codex uses of him. It adds a guess about his companion — «Ίσως ο Άγιος Αδριανός να ήταν και αυτός Επίσκοπος στην Κύπρο», perhaps St Adrianus too was a bishop in Cyprus — and that guess is recorded and not acted on.

The one further thing the site prints is a distich, and it is over Gregory alone: like a stag delivered out of the snare of this life, Gregory goes up to where the living water is. The corpus keeps many men of this name and none of them stands on this day or is named on this page.

The Russian church keeps him on this same day, and its line is as bare as the Greek citation: «Свт. Григория , еп. Констанции Кипрской.» — the rank, the forename, the see, and nothing else. It is the only one of that day's ten lines that closes without a year, where the other nine all carry one, and the only one of its nine people with no life page behind it; azbyka.ru's day for the same 17 марта prints the line equally bare and equally undated. So this calendar adds no fact to the folder. What it adds is a second witness, independent of the Patmos codex, that the Church keeps a bishop of Constantia in Cyprus of this name on 4 March, and a rank word, Свт., which is the hierarch the folder already records.

Two things it does not do, and both are worth saying because the Greek page did them. It does not name the companion: the Greek line of this day pairs him with Adrianus out of the codex's «[καί] Ἀδριανοῦ», and the Russian line names nobody, so that folder's russian row stays undocumented here and no company is claimed off this calendar. And the name link behind the line calls him Кипрский, of Cyprus, where the line itself and this folder's display name have the city: the stored Russian form is the index's, as the brief requires, and so this folder now holds the island in one place and the city in another, which is the calendar's own difference and not a reading made here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 4 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/04/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3793/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 4 марта ст. ст. — [the Russian day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260304.html), which prints his line and no life, read 6 October 2026.*
