# Ephrem of Kiev

Ephrem was born at Kiev and was steward to Iziaslav, prince of Kiev and son of Yaroslav, whose years saint.gr gives as 1054 to 1068. He became a monk and practised his asceticism in the Great Lavra of the Caves of Kiev. But the devil, the page says, who hates what is good, raged at seeing the family of monks increase, and so made the ruler Iziaslav angry and brought many afflictions on the fathers of the Caves.

When that trial had passed, Ephrem gave himself with holy zeal to asceticism and to prayer. His wish to visit holy places took him to Constantinople and to Palestine, for he wanted to venerate the places where the holy Apostles and the great fathers and ascetics of the desert had contended and lived. Returning to the Lavra of Kiev, he brought back with him the monastic typikon and the church order of the monks of the monastery of Studios at Constantinople.

About the year 1090, the page says, Ephrem was elected bishop of the city of Pereyaslavl, and was ordained by John, metropolitan of Kiev. So, having lived in a way pleasing to God, he fell asleep in peace in the year 1098, and was buried in the Lavra of Saint Anthony of Kiev. The page gives no year of birth and prints no troparion and no kontakion; the same calendar keeps two other Ephrems on this day, and this is neither of them.

The Russian calendar keeps him on the same menologion day as «Прп. Ефрема Печерского, еп. Переяславского (ок. 1098)», and its two hymns say what the Greek page did not: that he travelled into the land of the Greeks, copied out the rule of the monastery of Studion and brought it back «аки некий многочестен дар», as a gift of great price, by which the Russian Church is governed to this day.
