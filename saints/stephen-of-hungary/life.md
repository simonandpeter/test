# Stephen of Hungary

saint.gr's whole account of Stephen is two sentences. He lived at the beginning of the eleventh century, it says, and he is the founder of the state of Hungary; and the declaration of his sanctity was made in the summer of the year 2000 by the Ecumenical Patriarchate. The page gives no year of birth, no year of death, and nothing of his reign beyond that founding.

What it does carry is his apolytikion, which says what kind of king the Church remembers. He is there as one who laid up the sceptre of his kingdom with God and was known for a gentle king, devout and loving what is good; who brought the people of Hungary into subjection to God with the weapon of the Cross; and who now triumphs gloriously in the heavens with the angels — «θριαμβεύεις νῦν ἐνδόξος ἐν οὐρανοῖς, ὦ Στέφανε σύν Ἀγγέλοις».

The page keeps him beside [Hierotheus](/saints/hierotheus-of-hungary), the first Orthodox bishop of Hungary, sent from Constantinople in the same years and glorified in the same summer, and it links both of them to the Exarchate of Hungary of the Ecumenical Patriarchate.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 20 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/20/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2309/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
