# Dracon

Dracon is unknown to the synaxarion of Nikodemos of the Holy Mountain, saint.gr says, and is recorded instead in Codex 76 of Messina, where it is said that he lived under Diocletian — whose reign the page puts at 284 to 304 — and under Decius, governor of Nicaea, and that he came from the village of Arauraka.

Seeing the unjust slaughter of so many Christians, the saint was indignant and presented himself of his own accord before the governor Decius and confessed Christ, having first, out of holy indignation, reviled the gods of the idols. He was arrested for it, hard tortured and thrown into prison; and because he went on confessing Christ, he was beheaded. The page gives no year of his own, no burial and no hymn, and the gloss it puts on his name reads it as the keen-sighted.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/11/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2982/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
