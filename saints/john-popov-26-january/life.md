# John (Popov) (26 January)

The Russian calendar's line is all there is of him. It names him alone on this day, «Мч. Иоанна (1938)» — the martyr John, 1938 — and the name behind that line reads «Иоанн (Попов), иерей, мч.», John Popov, priest, martyr. That is his surname, his rank and the year of his death, and the calendar gives nothing else.

There is no life page behind his name and no hymn for him on the day. The day's own hymns are those of the Synaxis of the New Martyrs and Confessors of the Russian Church, which this calendar heads the day with and which names nobody, so none of them is taken for him. Nothing here says where he served, where he was arrested or where he died; the year 1938 is the calendar's own and the only date it prints.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 26 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260126.html); read 3 October 2026.*
