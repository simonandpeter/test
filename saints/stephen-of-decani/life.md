# Stephen of Dečani

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 11 noiembrie reads «Sfântul Ștefan de la Decani, regele Serbiei», and the page it points to has no Viață tab and no Tropar tab: it carries the name, the day and the readings appointed for the day, and nothing else.

What the calendar's own line does say is that he was king of Serbia and is named from Dečani. It gives no century, no year, no parentage, no manner of death, and no hymn. He is not the Stefan the First-Crowned the corpus already keeps; nothing on this page joins him to that king.

The Serbian calendar would be the natural place to find him documented, and this reading did not open it; that row is left `undocumented` with the omission named.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 11 noiembrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/11-noiembrie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-stefan-de-la-decani-regele-serbiei); read 25 September 2026.*
