# Plantinos

saint.gr's calendar for 21 Οκτωβρίου names Plantinos, [Irene](/saints/irene-companion-of-plantinos) and [Marina](/saints/marina-of-raithou) together, and for a life it gives a citation rather than a story. The memory of Plantinos, the page says, is recorded in the Jerusalem Kanonarion at page 115, accompanied there by that of Saint Irene and of Saint Marina of Raithou. That is the whole of the page's own authority for the three of them.

The page quotes a sticheron for Marina out of the office of [Philotheus the Athonite](/saints/philotheus-of-dionysiou), sung on this same day, and adds nothing further for the other two. It gives no year, no reign and no hymn of their own, and it prints no relics for them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 21 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/21/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2554/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
