# The Two Hundred Martyrs of Nicomedia

saint.gr's calendar for 10 Οκτωβρίου keeps one entry for two hundred martyrs, «Άγιοι Διακόσιοι Μάρτυρες», and names none of them. Its distich counts them: «Ἀνδρῶν τετραπλῆν οἶδα πεντηκοντάδα, / Ξίφει τελειωθεῖσαν, ὦ θείου τέλους!» — I know of a fourfold fifty of men, perfected by the sword; what a divine end. The page sets them in the persecution of Maximian, which it dates 286 to 305.

When the Christians of Nicomedia fled to the mountains in that persecution to escape death, the page says, these were arrested after the arrest of [Eulampius](/saints/eulampius-of-nicomedia) — it refers the reader to the entry of Eulampius and [Eulampia](/saints/eulampia-sister-of-eulampius), who are honoured the same day — and the result was that they were all slaughtered together with Saint Eulampius. The page of the brother and sister adds that it was the miracle of the cauldron of boiling water that made two hundred pagans believe in Christ, and that they were beheaded with the two.

The entry names nobody, so it is one folder and not two hundred, and nothing is recorded here of where any of them came from beyond Nicomedia. saint.gr prints no hymn under this entry; the megalynarion on the page of Eulampius and Eulampia greets the two hundred with them. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/10/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2706/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
