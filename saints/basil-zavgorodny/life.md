# Basil (Zavgorodny)

The calendar's own line is the whole of what is recorded here. The Сретенский monastery's calendar for 28 ноября ст. ст. names two priests, a deacon, two monastics and a laywoman in one clause closing on one year: «Сщмчч. Алексия , Алексия пресвитеров, Василия диакона, прмч. Рафаила , Викентия и мц. Анисии (1937).»

He stands third in it. The name page behind that place gives the surname the line leaves out together with the grade: «Василий (Завгородний), диакон, сщмч.» — a deacon and a hieromartyr. The calendar carries no life page behind that name page and none on the day itself, and the day page's troparia all stand under other names.

So nothing is said here of where he was born, what he did, when or by whom he was arrested, or where he died: no page read says any of it. The year 1937 is the year the line closes on, and it is given for all six at once.

His name page lists one other day, «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church. One row per church is the schema, so that day is recorded here and he does not render on it.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 28 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261128.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9489.html); read 7 October 2026.*
