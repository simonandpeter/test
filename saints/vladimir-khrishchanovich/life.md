# Vladimir (Khrishchanovich)

The Russian calendar keeps him on this day as «Сщмч. Владимира пресвитера (1933)», and the name behind that line reads «Владимир (Хрищенович), иерей, сщмч.» The page it links to is not a life of him but a collective account of the twenty-three hieromartyrs of Minsk, where he is one entry among many and where his surname is spelled Хрищанович; both spellings are the source's own and both are recorded here.

What that entry says is this. He was born in 1875 in the village of Gezgaly in the Lida district of the Vilna province, in a plain peasant family. He finished the Slutsk church school and served as psalm-reader in the church of St Nicholas in the village of Gorki in the Bobruisk district of the Minsk province, and in 1930 he was ordained priest. In December 1932 he was arrested, and at the interrogations he said plainly that he was not content with the policy of the Soviet power.

In his last letter he asked his wife «не забывать Бога, от Которого всё зависит», not to forget God, on whom everything depends. On 12 February 1933 he was sentenced to be shot, and the sentence was carried out soon after. That is the whole of the entry; the day prints no hymn for him.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 30 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260130.html) and [the collective account](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4766.htm); read 3 October 2026.*
