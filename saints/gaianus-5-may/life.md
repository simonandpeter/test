# Gaianus (5 May)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 5 mai reads «Sfântul Mucenic Gaian» — Gaianus the martyr — and his page opens no Viață tab, carrying only the day and the readings appointed for a martyr.

He is a man's name, not the Armenian nun Gaiane whom other calendars keep; the Romanian line gives him the masculine form and the masculine rank, and says nothing else. Gaius and Neophytus stand on the same day with pages as bare.

The Greek calendar keeps the masculine form and joins the three. saint.gr prints one line and one page for «Άγιοι Νεόφυτος, Γάιος και Γαϊανός οι Μάρτυρες», heads him within it «Αγιος Γαϊανος ο Μαρτυρας», and sets over the three a couplet that counts them: three martyrs take death most manfully for the sake of the Holy Trinity. The one sentence of story is for all of them: the holy martyrs [Neophytus](/saints/neophytus-5-may), [Gaius](/saints/gaius-5-may) and Gaianus were perfected by the sword, and their synaxis used to be kept at the church of the Holy Unmercenaries Cosmas and Damian, in the quarter of Dareios. So the Romanian pages' silence about what joined them is answered here, and nothing else is: no year, no emperor, no place of martyrdom and no hymn.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 5 mai — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/5-mai) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-gaian); read 20 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 5 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/05/index.aspx) and [the life of the three](https://www.saint.gr/389/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026.*
