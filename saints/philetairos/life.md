# Philetairos of Nicomedia

saint.gr's calendar for 30 December keeps Philetairos, who was of Nicomedia. He was distinguished, the page says, for the depth of his faith and also for a boundless outward beauty, which he never reckoned on, knowing that it is the beauty of the soul that counts and not the outward kind. He never relied on it, although it would have secured him a comfortable life; and the idolaters, wanting to make use of his natural beauty, kept trying to draw him over to idolatry.

Once, when the emperor Diocletian came to Nicomedia, he heard about Philetairos and asked to be introduced to the person with so much beauty. As soon as he saw him he was charmed, and asked him to enter his retinue, on the condition that he would deny Christianity. The saint answered that he would serve the emperor, but that he would not stop hymning and worshipping Jesus Christ. For that confession he was imprisoned, though he was let go again after a little while.

When Maximian came to the imperial throne, Philetairos was hunted and tortured. He got away, however, and went to a mountain in the parts of Sigriane; and in those parts he met the holy Eubiotos, whom this calendar keeps on 18 December, and lived with him in brotherly love, shared study and shared prayer. The page tells no death of his and gives no year. Through him, it says on another of this day's pages, [the seven martyrs](/saints/seven-martyrs-30-december) came to believe in Christ.
