# Eudocia (Martishkina)

The Russian church calendar names her and gives no life. Its line for 12 мая ст. ст. reads «Мц. Евдокии (после 1937)» — the martyr Eudocia, after 1937 — and behind that line stands a name page, and nothing more: «Евдокия (Мартишкина), мц.», with three days of commemoration, 26 января among the new martyrs, this day, and 10 июня among the saints of Ryazan. So the calendar says that her surname was Мартишкина, that she is numbered among the new martyrs of Russia and among the saints of Ryazan, and that she died after 1937.

The clause «после 1937» is worth reading as it stands. It is not a year: it is the calendar saying that it does not know when she died, only that it was not before that, which is why no upper bound is recorded for her. Nothing else is said — not where she was born, not where she lived, not how she died, and her grade is simply martyr, with no monastic rank. The day page prints hymns for four of the saints it heads and none under her name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 12 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260512.html) and [her name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/10067.html); read 8 October 2026.*
