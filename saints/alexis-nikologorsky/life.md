# Alexis (Nikologorsky)

Alexis was born in 1869 in the village of Nagornoye in the Dmitrov district of the province of Moscow, the son of a psalmist. He finished the seminary in 1889 and was ordained priest to the church at Volochanovo in the Volokolamsk district. Over twenty-seven years there he was decorated many times for blameless service: he was inspector of the church schools of the district, a dean, and an investigator in spiritual causes at the consistory. It was at Volochanovo that the persecution found him.

In 1930 the authorities took everything he had and demanded a large tax; unable to pay it, he was sentenced to two years in a labour camp. He petitioned from prison for his case to be reviewed, the sentence was quashed, and he was released after three months. He had probably met [Thaddeus](/saints/thaddeus-of-tver) while serving near Volokolamsk, where the archbishop lived in one of the villages for a time after his own exile; so on release he went to the diocese of Tver, which Thaddeus then governed, and was given the church of Cosmas and Damian at Plotnikovo in the Rzhev district. His wife had died by then and his six children had grown and scattered; one daughter lived with him.

In 1937, in that church, he kept the forty-seventh anniversary of his priesthood. On 24 October the authorities arrested him and put him in the Rzhev prison. The investigator asked about his property before and after the revolution and made a point of his long service, his church decorations and the responsible offices he had held, found nothing in the answers he could call a crime, and fell back on threats. «Антисоветской деятельности среди населения я не проводил», he answered, and the next day that he had preached only of the true Orthodox faith and had allowed nothing anti-Soviet into his sermons. The troika sentenced him on 25 November and he was shot on 27 November 1937. He was canonised for the whole church in August 2000.

The day's line names twenty-seven new martyrs of 1937 together — twenty-three presbyters, a deacon, a hieromonk, a lay martyr and a woman — and the calendar carries a life page for ten of them, of whom he is one. The men of that line were tried separately, in different districts and different prisons, and no one of these lives names another of them, so no relation is recorded between them here.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 14 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261114.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4740.htm); read 5 October 2026.*
