# Timon of Nadeyev

The line the Greek calendar prints for him on 21 Ιανουαρίου is «Όσιος Τίμων εκ Ρωσίας», the venerable Timon, out of Russia, and the whole of the page behind it is one sentence: «Ο Όσιος Τίμων, ο ένοικος της ερήμου, ασκήτεψε στο Ναδέγεβ της Ρωσίας και κοιμήθηκε με ειρήνη το έτος 1840 μ.Χ.» — the venerable Timon, dweller of the desert, practised the ascetic life at Nadeyev in Russia and fell asleep in peace in the year 1840.

So the day claims a place, a manner of life and a year of repose. No year of birth is printed, no monastery beyond that name, no teacher, no miracle and no hymn. The English form used here keeps the place the page names; the Greek spells it «Ναδέγεβ», and no Russian spelling is set down in this folder because none was read.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 21 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/21/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1296/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
