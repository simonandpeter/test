# Parthenius of Chios

saint.gr's calendar for 8 December keeps the venerable Parthenius of Chios, who came from the village of Daphnonas. He was betrothed young, the page says, and traded between Chios, Smyrna and Constantinople; and while he was preparing for the wedding he was told that his betrothed had fallen gravely ill, and before he could get back from his journey she had died.

Wanting to see her dead with his own eyes, he went to the grave at night, dug her up and took her in his arms. Then, turning over the troparion of the Beatitudes of the funeral service — let us go out and see in the graves that man is bare bones, food of worms and a stench — he said nothing to anybody, left the cares of the vain world, became a monk at the New Monastery of Chios and gave himself to ascetic labours.

With his elder's blessing he used to climb to the summit of the mountain called Penthodos, light the lamp, and generally care for the ruined little church of the holy apostle and evangelist Mark that stood there. At some point, by a divine will, he found a cave on the south-eastern side of the mountain and decided to live as an ascetic in it; and there he lived fasting, keeping vigil and praying night and day.

The Lord, seeing his spiritual labours, rewarded him with many spiritual gifts, among them that of discernment: he foretold the great and terrible earthquake of Chios of 1881. His fame spread quickly and the ruined little church of Saint Mark grew into a skete, where Parthenius and his disciples kept their monastic duties; and people came streaming from all the parts of the island to hear his honey-flowing words and to ask his prayer and his blessing. He fell asleep in peace on 8 December 1883.
