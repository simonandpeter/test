# Serapion the New

saint.gr's whole notice of him is three sentences, and this is all of them. The holy Serapion lived in the years of the king Severus and was a God-fearing man, good in his purpose. He was arrested by the ruler Aquila, and when he was asked what religion he was of, he confessed with courage that he believed in and reverenced Christ. They then threw him into the fire and burned him alive, and so he received the unfading crown of martyrdom.

Above the notice the page sets a distich, and that is the only other thing it prints of him: «Πίον τι Xριστώ θύμα και Σεραπίων, Xριστού παρ’ εχθρών εις το πυρ βεβλημένος» — Serapion too was a rich sacrifice to Christ, thrown into the fire by the enemies of Christ. The day's line calls him «ο νέος», the new; the page gives no country, no city, no year — only the emperor's name — and no hymn.
