# John the Hairy of Rostov

John of Rostov, the Merciful, called the Hairy, lived the feat of folly for
Christ at Rostov, bearing want and sorrow; he had no fixed dwelling and
rested only now and then in the house of his confessor, the priest of the
church of All Saints, or with an aged widow. Living in humility, patience
and unceasing prayer he guided many spiritually, among them Irinarch the
recluse of Rostov. After long labours he died on 3 September 1580 and was
buried, by his will, beside the church of St Blaise, behind the altar. He
had great hair on his head, whence his name; and he was called the
Merciful for the many healings worked at his tomb, and in memory of the
patriarch of Alexandria John the Merciful, of the seventh century, whose
name he bore and with whom the Russian calendar also keeps him on
12 November. The Greek synaxarion keeps him on the same day, 3 September, a
Russian, a fool for Christ.

The Russian calendar keeps him on two days and prints two lives behind the second of them, and between them they say this. His origin and his homeland are unknown. He took up the folly for Christ's sake in the city of Rostov in the reign of Ivan the Terrible, 1547 to 1584, enduring want and sorrow and having no settled lodging; only now and then he rested in the house of his confessor, a priest of the church of All Saints, or with one aged widow. Living in humility, patience and unceasing prayer he was the spiritual father of many, [Irenarchus of Rostov](/saints/irenarchus-the-recluse) among them. He always carried a Psalter with him and read it.

He died in great old age on 3 September 1580, and was buried by his own wish beside the church the lives name «церковь святого Власия», behind its altar. He was called Власатый because he had «власы на главе велики», great hair on his head. The name Милостивый, the merciful, he was given for the many healings worked at his tomb, and also in memory of [John the Merciful](/saints/john-the-merciful-of-alexandria), the patriarch of Alexandria whose name he bore and whose day, 12 ноября ст. ст., is the second day this calendar keeps him on.
