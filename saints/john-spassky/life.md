# John (Spassky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names John Spassky on 27 апреля and gives no life of him. Its line for the day is the whole of what it says: «Сщмч. Иоанна пресвитера (1941).» — the hieromartyr John the presbyter, and the year 1941 — with a link to his name and none to any reading. So what the calendar tells is that he was a priest, that he was counted among the hieromartyrs, and that he died in 1941.

His own name page on that site, 9642, carries nothing but the heading «Иоанн (Спасский), иерей, сщмч.», which gives the surname the day's line leaves out and writes the grade «иерей», priest, beside «сщмч.», and the two days «26 января (Новомуч.) , 27 апреля». There is no life page for him anywhere on the site, no icon and no article, and no page names a parish, a diocese or a place of any kind. The day page prints nine hymns, three for the kinsman of the Lord and six for the abbot of the Caves, and none under his name. The 26 января day page was not read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 27 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260427.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9642.html); read 8 October 2026.*
