# Sabbas of Daphnousia

saint.gr says first where Sabbas is not to be found. His memory, it tells us, is passed over in the synaxarion of Saint Nikodemos and in the printed Menaia; Delehaye's synaxarion has him, but with no biographical note. What the page then prints is a memorandum out of the Lavriotic Codex I 70, folio 2026, and that one paragraph is the whole of what is recorded of the man.

This man, it runs, for his virtuous manner of life according to God, which he showed from infancy in all reverence and modesty and fear of God, and for the virtue that was in him, was made bishop of Daphnousia by synodal vote; and taking up the helm of the throne he trained everyone to abide in the true and reverent faith, and turned many of the idolaters and baptised them. And so, having lived well and in a way pleasing to God, he departed to the Lord, whom he had longed for from infancy.

That is all. The page gives no century, no year of his consecration or of his death, no city of Daphnousia beyond the name of the see, no wonder, no relic and no hymn.
