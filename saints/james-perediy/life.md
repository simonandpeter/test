# James (Perediy)

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him fourteenth of twenty-four and gives no life; the life here is the one azbyka.ru prints for the civil 2 декабря, out of pstgu.ru and the Orthodox Encyclopaedia. Yakov Andreyevich Perediy, whose surname azbyka gives in both forms at once — «Передий (Передерий)» — was born on 23 October 1871 in the village of Mliny in the Gadyach district of the province of Poltava. He had only an elementary education, but he sat the examinations as a candidate for the priesthood and was ordained priest; in 1934 he was raised archpriest. He served in the church of the village of Velbovka in the Gadyach district of the Kharkov region.

On 28 August 1936 he was arrested on a charge of belonging to a «контрреволюционной группе церковников», and on 27 March 1937 a special conference of the NKVD sentenced him to five years of exile in Kazakhstan. In exile he worked as a watchman of the road-building works in the village of Borodulikha in the Bel-Agach district of the East Kazakhstan region. On 21 November he was arrested on a charge of anti-Soviet agitation against the collective-farm order, held first in the lock-up of the Bel-Agach district branch of the NKVD and then moved to the Semipalatinsk prison. He did not admit the charge, and on 27 November the special troika of the East Kazakhstan region sentenced him to be shot. He was buried in an unmarked grave. His name was put into the synaxis of the new martyrs by a resolution of the Holy Synod of 27 December 2000.
