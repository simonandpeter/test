# Diomedes (3 July)

The six were taken together, saint.gr says, by the emperor Trajan, whose first year it gives as 98, and he pressed them to deny Christ and sacrifice to the idols. They would not be persuaded by the emperor's orders, and so, after they had been tortured, they were shut up in the prison where [Hyacinth the Chamberlain](/saints/hyacinth-the-chamberlain) already lay; and by him they were strengthened in the faith of Christ. The company this folder belongs to is [Theodotus](/saints/theodotus-3-july), [Theodote](/saints/theodote-companion-of-theodotus), [Dolindouch](/saints/dolindouch), [Eulampius](/saints/eulampius-martyr-3-july) and [Asclepiades](/saints/asclepiades-the-martyr).

When Hyacinth had died a martyr they were brought out of the prison and led to the temple to eat of what had been offered to the idols. They not only did not eat but reproved the religion of the idols with boldness. So they were hung upon wood and their flesh torn; and then, having been taken down from the wood in a barbarous way, they were beheaded, and all of them received the crown of martyrdom. Christians buried their honourable relics with honours in the place where they were martyred.

The page gives the company no city, no judge but the emperor, and no year but 98. It prints a separate distich for each, and the second name it gives in two forms: «Δολινδούχ (ή Γολινδούχ)», Dolindouch or Golindouch, and «Ευλάμπιος (ή Ευλαμπίδης)», Eulampius or Eulampides. Both variants are the source's, and neither is resolved here.

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps four of the company on 3 июля ст. ст. — «Мчч. Диомида , Евлампия , Асклипиодота и мц. Голиндухи (II).» — the same menologion day as the Greek page this folder was made from, and marks them the second century where the Greek gives the year 98. The Russian line names four of the six and not all of them, it carries a name link for each of the four and no life page at all, and the day page prints no hymn under any of these names.
