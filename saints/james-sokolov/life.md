# James (Sokolov)

**Twenty-one years a psalm-reader in the church he was afterwards deacon and priest of.** The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him fifth of the sixteen it closes with 1937, among the «пресвитеров», and carries no life for him; the life here is what azbyka.ru prints for the civil 5 декабря out of fond.ru, from the hegumen Дамаскин (Орловский)’s volume of lives of the new martyrs of the Moscow diocese for November. He was born in 1874 in the village of Тимоново in the Дмитров district of the province of Moscow, son of a psalm-reader, Иван Соколов. He finished a two-class school and in 1893 was appointed psalm-reader to the church of the Entry at the churchyard of Чёрная Грязь. In 1914 the metropolitan of Moscow ordained him deacon, and in 1921 the bishop of Дмитров ordained him priest to the same church.

**The first sentence was for grain, and he got out of it.** In 1930, for not fulfilling the grain-collection quota, the authorities arrested him and sentenced him to five years in a corrective labour camp; he managed to prove his innocence, and after eleven months in prison was released by decision of the Moscow regional court.

**The second arrest was built on neighbours and on shutters.** On 27 November 1937 he was arrested again and shut in the Таганская prison in Moscow. False witnesses testified that believers from other parishes came to his house, and that the shutters of the house were closed while they were there, and that he had spoken with hostility of the Soviet power. He was questioned at once, did not admit himself guilty of anti-Soviet activity, and explained that the singers came to his house for rehearsals and that he did not know their surnames.

**Three days from the sentence to the pit.** On 1 December 1937 the troika of the NKVD condemned him to be shot, and on 4 December he was shot and buried in an unmarked common grave at the Бутово range outside Moscow. That day was in 1937 the Julian 21 ноября, the feast of the Entry of the Theotokos, and the day before the one the calendars keep him on.
