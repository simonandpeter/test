# James of Bathys Ryax

saint.gr's calendar for 21 Οκτωβρίου names a venerable James and says at once how little is known of him. He is unknown, the page says, to the Synaxaristes of Saint Nicodemus; his memory is carried in Delehaye's Synaxaristes, with no biographical detail at all beyond a single phrase, which the page quotes: the memory of the venerable James who became steward of the monastery of Christ the Saviour of Bathys Ryax, and who came from the second province of the Cappadocians.

That is the whole of the entry. The page gives him no century, no abbot, no companions, no relics and no hymn, and it prints no couplet for him. His office is the one word the citation supplies, that he was the steward of his house.
