# Gamaliel the Teacher of the Law

Gamaliel was a renowned teacher of the Jewish law in the first century after Christ, the head of one of the principal rabbinic schools of his time, of the party of the Pharisees, and a leading member of the Sanhedrin, the supreme court of the Jewish people. When the apostles were brought to judgement for preaching the Gospel, Gamaliel spoke before the Sanhedrin and asked that they be let go without punishment. Its members took his advice, but before releasing them they ordered the apostles beaten with rods, as the Acts record at 5:40.

By tradition, doxologia.ro says, Gamaliel came to believe in Christ, and was baptised together with his son by the Apostle Paul, possibly after the two of them had been witnesses of the martyrdom of the Archdeacon Stephen. That paragraph is the whole of what the site prints of him: his page carries no Viață tab and no hymn, only the day's epistle and gospel. The Romanian calendar keeps him on 2 august, the same day on which it keeps the bringing of Stephen's relics.
