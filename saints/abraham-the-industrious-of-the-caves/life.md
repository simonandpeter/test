# Abraham the Industrious of the Kyiv Caves

The Russian calendar keeps on 21 August (Julian) the venerable Abraham the Industrious of the Kyiv Caves, of the twelfth or thirteenth century, whose relics rest in the Near Caves, and prints no life under the line; this entry records the line and nothing more.

*After the day’s line of the Sretensky calendar (days.pravoslavie.ru), 21 August (Julian) — [the page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260821.html), which prints no life; read 23 August 2026.*
