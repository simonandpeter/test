# Sylvester (1 March)

saint.gr's entry for this Sylvester is not a life but a citation, and the life says no more than the citation does. His memory, the page reports, is mentioned in the Jerusalem Kanonarion at page 36 in these words: «Ἐν τὴ Ἀναστάσει, τοῦ ἁγίου πατριάρχου Σιλβέστρου» — at the Anastasis, of the holy Patriarch Sylvester. His festival used to be held in the church of the Anastasis in Jerusalem, and, the site says plainly, of which city Sylvester was Patriarch, whose memory the church of Jerusalem keeps, is unknown.

So the rank in this folder is a line in a manuscript and the see is a blank the source itself leaves blank. There is no century here, no country, no name of a predecessor or a successor, no deed, no manner of repose, no relic, no distich and no hymn.

The same Greek day keeps a martyr [Sylvester](/saints/sylvester-companion-of-sophronius), killed by the sword with Sophronius, on a page of its own. Two entries on one calendar are two commemorations, and nothing on either page connects the two men; the corpus's other Sylvesters, of Rome and of the Kyiv Caves, stand on days of their own and neither is named here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3780/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
