# John (Vasilyev) (4 May)

The Russian church calendar names him and gives no life. Its list for 4 мая ст. ст. reads «Сщмч. Иоанна пресвитера (1942)» — the hieromartyr John the presbyter, with the year of his death — and the name page behind that line gives the surname the line itself leaves out: «Иоанн (Васильев), иерей, сщмч.» It lists his days as 4 мая and 26 января, the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church, and carries nothing else at all.

So what the source says of him is this: that he was a priest, that he is honoured as a hieromartyr, and that he died in 1942. It gives no diocese, no parish, no place, no date of birth, no account of his arrest and no manner of death, and the day page prints no troparion or kontakion under his name. None of it has been supplied here.

The calendar keeps two other men under the same name and surname, each on a day of his own: an archpriest shot in 1937, and a layman who was the warden of his church and died in exile in 1932. The three are told apart by their days, their years and their grade, and nothing read puts any two of them in one sentence.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 4 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260504.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14253.htm), which gives no life; read 8 October 2026.*
