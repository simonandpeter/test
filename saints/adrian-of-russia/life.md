# Adrian of Russia

Andrei Zavalishin was his name in the world. When he discovered Alexander of Svir in the frozen wilderness he became his disciple; he was tonsured at the famous monastery of Valaam and lived the ascetic life on a peninsula of Lake Ladoga, where he founded a monastery. He was the spiritual father of Princess Anna, daughter of Tsar Ivan IV the Terrible.

He met a martyr’s end in 1549, attacked by robbers on his way back from Moscow, and was buried at his monastery, in the church of St Nicholas. His relics were found incorrupt in 1551; after the revolution of 1917 there is no news of their fate. The Orthodox Church in America keeps him the same day as Adrian of Ondrusov.

The Russian calendar keeps him on 26 August (Julian) as the venerable Adrian of Ondrusov, who died in 1549; the Sretensky calendar’s note adds that the finding of his relics, in 1551, is kept on 17 May.
