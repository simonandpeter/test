# Asaph of Wales

Asaph lived in the second half of the sixth century and was a bishop in Wales, saint.gr says, and that is the whole of what it fixes about him. He was an ascetic in a monastery that stood near the banks of the river Elwy in north Wales, a house founded by [Kentigern, bishop of Glasgow](/saints/kentigern-of-glasgow), whose memory the page gives as 13 January. God granted him the gift of working wonders.

He wrote monastic rules, typical prescriptions for his church, and other works, and he fell asleep in peace. The page names none of the writings, gives no year of birth or of death, prints no hymn and names no relic, and it calls his see simply Wales without naming a city.
