# Jonah of Kyiv

saint.gr's calendar for 9 Ιανουαρίου names «Όσιος Ιωνάς ο Γέροντας», Jonah the Elder, and the page behind the line gives him two sentences and no more. He came out of Russia, and he lived the ascetic life at the monastery of the Holy Trinity at Kiev, where he fell asleep in peace in the year 1902. The page adds, under its heading for relics, that a part of his relics is kept in that same monastery of the Holy Trinity at Kiev.

Nothing else is printed for him here: no parents, no tonsure, no elder of his own, no account of the years between Russia and Kiev, and no hymn. The calendar keeps him on 9 January.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 9 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/09/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1104/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
