# John (Pommer)

The Russian calendar gives him one line on 29 September and no life: «Сщмч. Иоанна, архиеп. Рижского (1934)» — John the hieromartyr, archbishop of Riga, 1934. Its index of names adds the surname and keeps the see, «Святитель Иоанн (Поммер), архиепископ Рижский, сщмч.», and lists him also on 26 January with the whole company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia.

That is everything the source holds, and for an archbishop of a capital it is very little: no birth year, no account of the killing, no place. 1934 also sets him apart from almost every other new martyr on these days — he died not in the Soviet Union but in independent Latvia, which the calendar does not say either. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which refused this reading on 4 October 2026 — a 403 to the fetcher's user-agent and to a full browser one alike — so the line stands as the line.

*After the Russian church calendar's [29 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260929.html) and its [index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/12489.html); read 4 October 2026.*
