# Quadratus of the East

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαρτίου names him at the head of a company: «Άγιοι Κοδράτος, Θεοδόσιος, Μανουήλ και άλλοι Τεσσαράκοντα μάρτυρες από την Ανατολή» — the holy Quadratus, Theodosius, Manuel and forty other martyrs from the East. The page behind the line tells their passion in one paragraph and gives no year, no city, no emperor's name and no judge's name; it says only that they lived in the years of the Church's persecutions, under the idolatrous emperors.

When they had been seized they were pressed to deny Christ, and instead they repeated their confession many times over and declared that they would never desert the standard of the Gospel. The judge, at his wits' end, ordered their sides to be laid open; and when that torture too proved powerless to change their devotion to Christ, he ordered them beheaded. Nothing distinguishes one of them from another in the narrative, which speaks throughout in the plural.

What the page gives him of his own is a couplet, printed «Eις τον Kοδράτον»: «Κοδρᾶτε, θαυμάζω σὲ τῆς εὐανδρίας! Πῶς ὑπτιάζων ἀνδρικῶς σφάττῃ μάκαρ» — Quadratus, I marvel at your courage: how, thrown on your back, you are manfully slain, blessed one. His fellows are [Theodosius](/saints/theodosius-of-the-east), [Manuel](/saints/manuel-of-the-east) and [the forty](/saints/forty-martyrs-of-the-east), each of whom the page gives a couplet of his own. No relic, no house and no hymn are recorded.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3861/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
