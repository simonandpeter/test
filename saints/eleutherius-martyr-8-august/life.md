# Eleutherius (8 August)

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives almost no life. The one page behind the day's line carries three parties — «Άγιοι Ελευθέριος, Λεωνίδης και τα Άγια Νήπια», Eleutherius, [Leonides](/saints/leonides-companion-of-eleutherius) and [the Holy Infants](/saints/holy-infants-with-eleutherius-and-leonides) — and all that it says of their story is two sentences: «Μαρτύρησαν όλοι δια πυρός. Η σύναξη τους γίνεται στην Αγία Ειρήνη στις Ιουστινιαναίς.» They all suffered martyrdom by fire, and their synaxis is kept at the church of Saint Irene in Justinianae.

Above those sentences the page sets the distich the Greek books give the two men, «Ἀμφοῖν ἀθλητῶν, οὐ θυόντων τῇ πλάνῃ, / Ποινὴν κατακρίνουσιν οἱ πλάνοι φλόγα» — of both contenders, who would not sacrifice to the error, the deceivers decreed the flame as punishment. The page glosses the name Eleutherius as the lover, the herald and the champion of freedom, and it gives no year, no country and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2236/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
