# Michael (Razhkin)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him third in a line it gives to four men — «Сщмчч. Алексия, Николая, Михаила пресвитеров и мч. Сергия (1938).» — three hieromartyr presbyters and a martyr, all of 1938. The name link behind him carries the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line leaves out: «Михаил (Ражкин), иерей, сщмч.», a priest of that name.

Four entries on one line are four men, and the calendar prints a life page for only two of them; this is not one of the two. It gives him no parish, no diocese, no place and no manner of death, and no hymn stands under his name. Nothing further is written here, and nothing is reasoned out from the surname or from the year he shares with the other three.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 23 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260223.html); read 3 October 2026.*
