# Dometius (8 March)

saint.gr's whole notice for him is two sentences, and the second is an admission: the venerable Dometius fell asleep in peace in the year 363, and we have no further details of his life. There is no birthplace, no monastery, no teacher, no office, no relic and no hymn — a year, a manner of death, and the rank of a venerable monastic. The page prints over him one distich, which says that Dometius paid the debt of his death by the hands of angels sent to collect it.

The corpus keeps several men of this name, and none of them is this one. The closest by year is Dometius the Persian, a venerable martyr kept by the Romanian calendar on 7 August, whose death is placed in the same reign; but that man was killed and this one died in peace, and they are kept on separate days, which by this corpus's settled rule makes them two commemorations and two men. What tells this Dometius apart is only his day and his peaceful end, and that is little enough to be worth saying out loud.

The Russian calendar is the only one of the four to put a by-name on him, and it gives him nothing else. Its line for the day reads «Прмч. Дометия перса (363)» — the venerable-martyr Dometius the Persian, 363 — and behind the name there is an index page carrying this one day of memory and no life, no hymn and no further word. The year agrees with the one the Greek calendar prints. The by-name does not stand alone in that index: the same calendar keeps a second Dometius the Persian on 7 августа, a hierodeacon and hieromartyr of Persia and Syria, martyred with two disciples, and that man has a life and a service and his own page. Two pages of one calendar, one name, two days and two ranks; and the day is what joins this line to this folder.
