# Brithwald of Canterbury

Brithwald succeeded [Theodore of Tarsus](/saints/theodore-of-tarsus-archbishop-of-canterbury) on the archiepiscopal throne of Canterbury, coming to it in the year 693. saint.gr calls him an excellent knower of the Scriptures and of the affairs of the Church, and says that he was a good shepherd and a rule of faith and of virtue. The page sets the name in Latin letters beside the Greek, as Brithwald.

He fell asleep in peace, in a manner the page calls venerable, in the year 731. That is the whole of what saint.gr gives for him: no birthplace, no monastery, no account of his years in the see, and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 9 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/09/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1095/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
