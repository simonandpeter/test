# Blasius (22 February)

saint.gr's entry for the holy Blasius is two sentences, and the life says exactly what they say: he was a bishop, and he fell asleep in peace. The page then adds the one thing it knows about him, which is a doubt rather than a fact: some reckon him bishop of Rome — as Nikodemos the Hagiorite does — but there is no mention of such an episcopate of his in the bishops' lists. So the site prints the received identification and refuses it in the same breath.

It gives no year, no country, no see and no hymn. The distich over him says that the hierarch Blasius was seen to be great, having himself appeared rather as an ornament to the throne. Nothing else about him is written here, because nothing else is printed there.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 22 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/22/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3674/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
