# John Dermokaites

saint.gr's calendar for 17 November keeps «Όσιος Ιωάννης ο Δερμοκαΐτης», and its whole notice of him is two sentences. His memory, the page says, is recorded in Delehaye's synaxarion, with the note that he practised asceticism on Olympus of Bithynia. And this is the monk John, known for the holiness of his life in the days of Romanos Lekapenos, whose reign the page gives as 919 to 944 — the emperor who, after his fall, sent a sealed letter to this John and confessed his sins to him.

That is the whole of the source: a surname, a mountain, a synaxarion that preserved the memory, and one fallen emperor's letter. The calendar gives him no monastery of his own, no year of birth or death, no companion and no hymn, and the years it prints belong to the reign rather than to the man, so no date is recorded here beyond the page's own words.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 17 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/17/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3032/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
