# Nicetas of Thebes

saint.gr's notice of him is three sentences. It says the venerable Nicetas of Thebes was an offspring of the seven-gated city of the Thebans; that at the age of sixteen he became a monk, drawing other ascetics to himself, who were drawn as by a magnet by his spiritual person and the purity of his character; and that he was found worthy of the gift of working wonders, and reposed in the Lord full of days.

His own apolytikion, printed on the same page, adds that he followed the Lord from childhood by prayer, vigil and ascesis of every kind, that having received the monastic habit he was visibly bathed in light, and that one Theodore saw him so — a name the notice itself does not explain. The page gives no year of birth or death and no century, and the calendar keeps him beside [Daniel of the Castle of Patras](/saints/daniel-of-the-castle-of-patras), whom his life has him meet when he came to Patras.
