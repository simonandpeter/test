# Maximus of Marcianopolis

Maximus was one of the martyrs Maximus, Theodotus and Asclepiodote, who came from Marcianopolis in Thrace and were martyred cruelly for confessing Christ: first they were scourged, then their hands and feet were cut off, then they were imprisoned and at last beheaded — in one woman and two young men, the verse says, there was one fervour of heart towards the cutting. The Greek calendar keeps them on 17 September.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 17 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2454/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
