# John (Skadovsky)

John Georgievich Skadovsky was born at Kherson in 1875 into a noble family, took a higher education, and before the revolution was a zemstvo chief. After 1917 he was ordained priest and served in a church of his own city. In January 1931 he was arrested in the case of the Kherson group of the Odessa branch of what the authorities called the True Orthodox Church, and was given five years in the camps.

In February 1933 he was released early, forbidden to live in twelve cities, and settled at Kamyshin. In 1935 he was arrested again and sentenced to five years' exile in Karakalpakia. There he was with the exiled archbishop [Procopius (Titov)](/saints/procopius-titov), and the indictment drawn against the two of them at Turtkul charged them with having organised an unlawful house of prayer. Arrested once more in 1937, he was sentenced to be shot by an NKVD troika on 28 October, and on 23 November 1937 the archbishop and the priest were shot together and buried in a common unmarked grave.
