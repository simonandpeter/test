# Yuri (Novitsky)

Yuri Petrovich Novitsky was born in 1882 in the town of Uman in the province of Kiev. He finished the First Gymnasium and the university of Kiev, and from 1914 he was a lecturer and then professor in the chair of criminal law at the university of Petrograd. He was chairman of the board of the Society of United Orthodox Parishes of Petrograd, and a layman: the calendar gives him no rank in the Church at all.

He was tried with [Benjamin of Petrograd](/saints/benjamin-of-petrograd) in the case opened on 10 June 1922, after the metropolitan's arrest on 29 May over the seizure of church valuables, with eighty-six others in the dock. The tribunal gave sentence on 5 July, and in the night of 12 to 13 August 1922 he was shot on the edge of Petrograd with the metropolitan, with [Sergius (Shein)](/saints/sergius-shein) and with the other layman of the parish society, [John (Kovsharov)](/saints/john-kovsharov).

That is all the calendar's lives say of him: a professor of criminal law who chaired the parishes of his city and was shot for it. A cross stands over the symbolic grave of the Russian new martyrs in the brotherhood cemetery of the Lavra in Petrograd, and the day page prints no hymn under his name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 31 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260731.html) and [the life the four share](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life356.htm); read 13 October 2026.*
