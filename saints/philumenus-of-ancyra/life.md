# Philumenus of Ancyra

He sold bread in Ancyra. The Сретенский monastery's calendar for 29 ноября ст. ст. prints «Мч. Филумена (ок. 274).» and the name page behind it gives him the city the line omits, «Филумен Анкирский»; and its life, five sentences long, is the whole of what is recorded: he suffered for Christ in 274, in the persecution of the emperor Aurelian, whose reign the page dates 270 to 275, and his trade was the grain and bread trade in that city.

Men who envied him informed the governor Felix that he confessed the Christian faith, and he was brought before the court. He did not deny Christ. For that they drove nails into his hands, his feet and his head and made him walk. He bore the torture manfully and died of the loss of blood, giving up his soul to God.

Nothing is said of where he came from, of his family, of his age, or of what became of his body; the calendar carries no hymn under his name on that day, and no other calendar read keeps him at all.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 29 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261129.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life3247.htm); read 7 October 2026.*
