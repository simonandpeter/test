# Nicetas (Pribytkov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him in one line for 21 декабря ст. ст.: «Сщмч. Никиты, еп. Белевского (1938).» The line declines the forename to «Никиты», gives his see as Belev and no surname, no place of birth, no year of birth and no word of narrative. The rank word «Сщмч.», the see and the year it closes with, 1938, are the whole of what it says of him.

Behind that «Никиты» stands the calendar's own name page 13370, which carries the fuller form «Святитель Никита (Прибытков), епископ Белевский, сщмч.» — the surname the day's line leaves out. That page lists two days: this 21 декабря and «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. It carries no life, and neither does the day page. So this folder says what the calendar says and no more: a bishop of Belev of that name, who died a martyr in 1938 and whom this calendar keeps on this day.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 21 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261221.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13370.html), which carries no life; read 13 October 2026.*
