# The Hundred and Twenty Martyrs of Persia

saint.gr's calendar for 19 October keeps «Άγιος Σαδώθ ο επίσκοπος και οι Εκατόν είκοσι Μάρτυρες από την Περσία» — the bishop [Sadoth](/saints/sadoth-of-persia) and the hundred and twenty martyrs of Persia — and of the hundred and twenty it names not one. Its verse for them turns the number over and says only that a tenfold ten of martyrs, fellow witnesses, and twice ten besides, die struck by the sword.

What story they have is their bishop's. Sadoth — or Sadok, or Sadoch — lived about the year 330, the page says, and believing in Christ became bishop of the small Christian community of Persia. For his charitable work he was loved not only by the Christians but by the idolaters too, which galled the Persian priests; so they accused him before Shapur, who believed them and summoned the saint to defend himself and to deny Christ. Sadoth refused, and for that he was led out to the executioner together with a hundred and twenty Christians who belonged to his small but choice flock.

The calendar gives the company no names, no year of their own and no hymn, and the page adds that the memory of these saints is kept again on 20 February, where it counts them as a hundred and twenty-eight. The two numbers are the source's and are left as they stand: this folder is the hundred and twenty of the October entry, because the October entry is the page it is read from.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 19 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/19/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2770/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
