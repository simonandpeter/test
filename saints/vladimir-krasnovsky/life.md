# Vladimir (Krasnovsky)

days.pravoslavie.ru names him second of seven on one line for 12 ноября ст. ст. and prints no life for him; the Russian church calendar at azbyka.ru prints one. He was born on 5 July 1889 in the village of Izmailovo near Moscow, the son of a psalmist, Platon Krasnovsky; his mother baked the prosphora at the church. His father died before the persecutions began and his mother in 1918. He finished the Pererva theological school and served for a time as a psalmist, as his father had done.

In 1923 he was ordained deacon by the bishop of Klin, a vicar of the Moscow diocese whom azbyka's page names «Гавриилом (Красновским)» — the same surname as his own — and began serving in the church of the icon of the Mother of God «Всех скорбящих Радость» at Assaurovo in the Dmitrov district. In 1925 the priest's place at the church of the Holy Spirit in the village of Dubrovki fell vacant, and the parish meeting resolved unanimously to ask the bishop of Dmitrov to ordain the deacon Vladimir priest for their own church. The request was granted at once.

In 1930 that church was closed by the authorities, and in 1931 he was appointed to serve in the church of the village of Legchishchevo in the Lopasnya district of the Moscow region. On 11 November 1937 he was arrested and imprisoned in the town of Serpukhov. The ground of the arrest was the evidence of «двух свидетелей на негласном допросе» — two witnesses at an unminuted interrogation — as the investigation's own document puts it, and he was questioned the same day along the lines of what they had said.

Asked whether he admitted carrying on counter-revolutionary agitation among the collective farmers against the existing order, he answered that he had carried on no agitation against the Soviet power, since he could make nothing of that politics. Asked whether he admitted doing it systematically in the course of his duties, at funerals and the like, he said he had never engaged in counter-revolutionary agitation. Charged with slandering the leadership at a funeral dinner in the hamlet of Pikalovo, he said that it had never happened; and as the interrogator read out the evidence of those who had informed on him, he rejected it as false — «Этого я никогда и нигде не говорил, все это на меня показано ложно».

Pressed once more and told he had given enough lying evidence, he said: «Честное слово, я нигде никому не говорил о том, что конституция вызывает много вопросов; я ее читал и с ней был согласен.» Asked at the last whether he admitted the charge, he said he did not, because he had nowhere and never said any of it. On 17 November the troika sentenced him to be shot. He was shot on 25 November 1937 and buried in an unmarked common grave on the Butovo range near Moscow.

The day page carries no hymn under his name or any of the other six of 1937, and no relation is written to them: his arrest, his prison and his troika are all his own.
