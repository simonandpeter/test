# Stephen Xylinites

saint.gr's calendar for 24 Μαρτίου names a venerable Stephen, surnamed Xylinites, and its page for him is one sentence long: the venerable Stephen Xylinites lived as an ascetic and was counted worthy by God of the gift of foresight. That is the whole of the account, and this life says no more than the page does.

The page gives him no country, no century, no monastery, no instance of the foresight it credits him with, and no relic; it prints neither a couplet nor a hymn under his name, and it does not explain the epithet. Nothing has been taken from any other source and nothing supplied from memory. Many other men of the name are kept on other days and in other calendars, and nothing on this page identifies him with any of them; if a fuller life of his is printed elsewhere, it has not been read.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/24/index.aspx) and [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/88/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
