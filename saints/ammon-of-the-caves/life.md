# Ammon of the Caves

Ammon visited the Holy Mountain and Jerusalem and then settled in the Lavra of the Caves, where he became known for his holy life. That is the whole of the Greek notice; it gives no century, and does not say more of the Lavra. The Greek calendar keeps him on 4 October; whether the Russian calendar keeps him is for its own pages.

The Russian church calendar keeps him on the Julian 4 October and gives this folder its first date, which the Greek notice explicitly lacked: «Прп. Аммона, затворника Печерского (XIII)», the thirteenth century. It also gives the word the Greek has not, «затворник», recluse. The caves are named on the line because the same page keeps a second Ammon, the Egyptian of about 350. Unlike the day's two other men of the Caves, Helladius and Onesimus, his index entry lists no second day with the synaxis of the Near Caves.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 4 Οκτωβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2603/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026; the Russian church calendar keeps him on the Julian 4 October — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261004.html); read 4 October 2026.*
