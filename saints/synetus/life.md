# Synetus

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 12 decembrie reads «Sfântul Mucenic Sinet», and his own page on that site carries the name and the date and nothing else about him: no Viață tab, no troparion, no kontakion, no city, no century, no emperor, no account of what he was accused of or what he answered or how he died. One thing the line does assert, and it is all this entry rests on: that he was «Mucenic», the Romanian word for a man who died for the faith.

Nothing further is set down here because nothing further was read, and nothing is inferred from the name or from the company of the day. Another calendar may well carry his passion; none was consulted for this page, and the three other churches' rows say which.
