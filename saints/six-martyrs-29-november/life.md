# The Six Martyrs (29 November)

saint.gr keeps six martyrs together on 29 November and names none of them. Where and when they were martyred is unknown, it says — «Οι Άγιοι Έξι Μάρτυρες είναι άγνωστο που και πότε μαρτύρησαν» — and what it gives instead of a history is the tradition about their end: that when they were being pursued, a rock opened and they went inside it.

That is all, and the couplet over the entry turns the same image: Christ, who in his death found a rock for his own burial, opened a rock for the burial of six men. No country, no century, no persecutor, no names; no relics and no hymn. Because the page names nobody, this folder is one entry for the company, and no folder is made for any member of it.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 29 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/29/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3117/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
