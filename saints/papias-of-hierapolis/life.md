# Papias of Hierapolis

The Ohrid Prologue of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović gives him the shortest entry of the day — two sentences — and they are about his writing rather than his life. He was a disciple of the holy apostles, it says, and a patrological writer; and from him we have the testimony about the Gospel according to Matthew and the Gospel according to Mark, about the four Marys and about the brethren of the Lord, as well as one work preserved incompletely, the Exposition of the Lord's Sayings.

That is the whole of the record read for him here. The Prologue gives him no year, no century, no rank and no account of how he died, and nothing of the kind is supplied: it names him for the city of Hierapolis and for what later readers owe to him. The corpus keeps two other folders for men of this name — a martyr under Diocletian on 28 June, and one of a company on 20 November — and neither is this man.
