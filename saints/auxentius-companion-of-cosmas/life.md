# Auxentius, companion of Cosmas

The calendar keeps him beside the bishop he served, and the single life page it offers for the entry is a life of the two of them together. He and [Cosmas of Chalcedon](/saints/cosmas-of-chalcedon) lived in the ninth century, the page says, at the time when the iconoclasts were persecuting those who held to Orthodoxy; and the word it gives him is сподвижник, a fellow-struggler, the bishop’s helper in that fight. Everything else it says of him it says through the bishop.

The bishop was a native of Constantinople who had gone into a monastery while still young and taken the monastic tonsure, and who was afterwards ordained to the see of Chalcedon, where he defended the Orthodox faith zealously against the iconoclast heretics. The heretics tried many times to draw him over to their side and he stayed faithful to the end. When the emperor Лев Армянин, whose reign the page dates 813 to 820, decreed that the holy icons be taken out of the churches, the bishop would not submit, and for that he was driven from his cathedra and sent into confinement.

When he was brought back from exile this man was beside him again, and the two went on together defending the veneration of the holy icons. The unceasing persecutions weakened the bishop in body, the page says, but he remained as firm in spirit as before; and the account closes by saying of the pair of them that to their very repose they kept the Orthodox faith unshaken.

Of this man alone the page gives nothing: no country, no family, no see, no year of birth or of death, and no hymn. His name page lists the one day and the one joint life, and the epithet «Халкидский» it attaches to his name is not explained anywhere on either page.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 18 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260418.html), [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life882.htm) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/28.html); read 8 October 2026.*
