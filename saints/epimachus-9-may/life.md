# Epimachus

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 9 mai reads «Sfântul Mucenic Epimah» — the holy martyr Epimachus — and the page it links for him carries neither a Viață tab nor a Tropar tab, only the feast line "9 Mai" and the readings appointed for the day, the epistle from the second letter to Timothy and the gospel from John.

Nothing else about him is printed there. His country is not named, no century is given, and the manner of his martyrdom is not told. The word «Mucenic» in the calendar's line is the whole of what the source says about how he died, and no other calendar has been read for him.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 9 mai — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/9-mai) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-epimah-0), which prints no life; read 24 September 2026.*
