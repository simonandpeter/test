# Epimachus

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 9 mai reads «Sfântul Mucenic Epimah» — the holy martyr Epimachus — and the page it links for him carries neither a Viață tab nor a Tropar tab, only the feast line "9 Mai" and the readings appointed for the day, the epistle from the second letter to Timothy and the gospel from John.

Nothing else about him is printed there. His country is not named, no century is given, and the manner of his martyrdom is not told. The word «Mucenic» in the calendar's line is the whole of what the source says about how he died, and no other calendar has been read for him.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and names his companion. The holy martyrs Epimachus and [Gordian](/saints/gordian-9-may) came of Rome, saint.gr says, and contended under the emperor Decius, who reigned from 249 to 251. They were arrested, and the magistrate pressed them to deny Christ and to sacrifice to the idols; and because they stood unshaken in their faith they were cruelly tortured and then beheaded, and so received the crowns of the contest. Their holy relics were laid in one and the same grave, and a considerable part of them is kept in the Benedictine abbey at Kempten in Bavaria, in Germany; their synaxis is held in the church of the holy martyr Stratonikos, and their memory, the page adds, is repeated on the thirty-first of October. The couplet over Epimachus says that the sword took off a contender, a mighty fighter of God against error: «Aνείλεν Eπίμαχον αθλητήν ξίφος, / Θείον μαχητήν ευσθενή κατά πλάνης».
