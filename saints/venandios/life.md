# Venandios

saint.gr's calendar for 30 Μαΐου names him and gives two sentences. Venandios was born in Gaul, it says, and lived in the fourth and fifth centuries. He was joined in spirit to the venerable Karpasios, whom the same calendar keeps on 1 June, and to [Honoratus](/saints/honoratus-of-arles), whom it keeps on 16 January and whose own page names him archbishop of Arles; and together the three of them set out on a pilgrimage to the monasteries of the East.

The venerable Venandios fell asleep in peace in Greece. That is the whole of it: the page names no monastery of his, no place in Gaul and no place in Greece, gives no year, records no wonder, and prints no hymn. The English form of his name here is a transcription of the Greek the page prints and not the Latin Venantius, which it does not.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 30 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/30/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2319/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
