# Abbanus

saint.gr's calendar for 13 May names him and gives him three sentences, which are the whole of what is known here. Saint Abbanus came from Ireland and lived in England in the second century. He was baptised in the year 165, and he preached the word of God in the region of Abingdon in England. He fell asleep in holiness and in peace.

The page prints no hymn, no relics, no parents and no office; it does not say who baptised him, how long he preached, or where he died. The Greek calendar keeps an Irishman of this name in England on 13 May, and that, with the year of his baptism, is the whole of the entry.
