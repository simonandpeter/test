# Nicephorus Phocas

saint.gr's calendar for 11 December keeps the holy Nicephorus Phocas, emperor of Byzantium, and says at once where it has him from: his memory is recorded only in the Lavriote Codex B 4, folio 133, where there is also a full service to him with canons, published by L. Petit in the Byzantinische Zeitschrift in 1904 and by Dmitrievsky at Kyiv in 1911. The page's own head gives the year of his death, 969, and no year of birth.

Nicephorus Phocas occupies an important place in the firmament of the Byzantine emperors, the page says, chiefly because of his military policy before and after his crowning; but his offering to the Church was great too. He helped the venerable [Athanasius](/saints/athanasius-the-athonite) the Athonite to build the first monastery of the Holy Mountain, today's Great Lavra, setting an official frame for the working of the monastery and of the whole district.

It is said, the page adds, that under the imperial robe he wore a monastic habit, and that he had made plain his wish to leave the throne and become a monk. It is worth noting, it ends, his attempt to honour as martyrs all the soldiers who fell during his contests with the unbelievers; but that attempt found the patriarchs and the bishops against it, and so the emperor was obliged to abandon his plan.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/11/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3171/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
