# Abimelech

saint.gr's calendar for 4 Νοεμβρίου names «Δίκαιος Αβιμέλεχ» — the righteous Abimelech — and tells his story out of Genesis and nowhere else. The name, the page says, was borne by kings of Gerar. He gave hospitality to Abraham, who presented his wife Sarah to him as his sister; but Abimelech did not take her for himself, because he learned the truth from a dream he had. That incident, the page adds, is in the book of Genesis of the Old Testament, in chapters 20 and 21.

That is the whole of the entry: no year, no account of his reign, no death, no relics and no hymn. The rank this folder prints is the calendar's own word for him, Δίκαιος, the righteous — which is how the Church names the holy ones of the Old Covenant.

The same day's listing carries one more thing under his name, which is not a person and has no folder here: a narrative reading, «Διήγησις εις τον θρήνον του Προφήτου Ιερεμίου περί της Ιερουσαλήμ, και εις την άλωσιν ταύτης, και περί της εκστάσεως Αβιμέλεχ» — the lament of the prophet Jeremiah over Jerusalem, the city's fall, and the trance of Abimelech. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου».

*After saint.gr's calendar for 4 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/04/index.aspx) and [his notice](https://www.saint.gr/2886/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
