# Christopher of the monastery of Saint Sabbas

saint.gr's whole account of him is one sentence, and half of that sentence is about what it does not know: «Ο Άγιος Οσιομάρτυρας Χριστόφορος, άσκησε στη μονή του Αγίου Σάββα. Δεν έχουμε περισσότερες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο και το Μαρτύριο του Αγίου» — the holy venerable-martyr Christopher lived the ascetic life in the monastery of Saint Sabbas; we have no further details of the saint's life and martyrdom.

That is all. The page gives no century, does not say at whose hands he suffered or by what death, names no abbot and no cell, lists no relic, and prints neither couplet nor hymn. It glosses his name — Χριστόφορος, from Χριστός and φέρω, the one who reveals Christ and his teaching by his works and his words — and that gloss belongs to the name and not to the man.

Two days earlier the same calendar keeps another monk of the same Lavra of Saint Sabbas, Bacchus the venerable-martyr, on the same kind of one-line notice; nothing on either page joins them, and neither is any of the other Christophers or Bacchuses the corpus holds.
