# Elijah of Murom

saint.gr's calendar for 19 December keeps the venerable Elijah of Murom, the wonderworker, whom it also calls the one in the Cave; and it says of him only that it has nothing of his life: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ρώσου Οσίου» — we have no details of the life of the Russian venerable one. That one sentence is the whole of the entry.

So the Greek line gives four things and no more: the fixed day, the place Murom, the word that he is a Russian venerable, and the two by-names, the wonderworker and the one in the Cave. Unlike several other Russian saints the same calendar keeps in these days, this page carries no year even in its heading, so no year of birth and no year of death is recorded here. It lists no relic and prints no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 19 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/19/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3300/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
