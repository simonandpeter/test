# Elijah of Murom

saint.gr's calendar for 19 December keeps the venerable Elijah of Murom, the wonderworker, whom it also calls the one in the Cave; and it says of him only that it has nothing of his life: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ρώσου Οσίου» — we have no details of the life of the Russian venerable one. That one sentence is the whole of the entry.

So the Greek line gives four things and no more: the fixed day, the place Murom, the word that he is a Russian venerable, and the two by-names, the wonderworker and the one in the Cave. Unlike several other Russian saints the same calendar keeps in these days, this page carries no year even in its heading, so no year of birth and no year of death is recorded here. It lists no relic and prints no hymn.

The Охридски пролог has what saint.gr says it has not. Its fourth entry for 19. децембар ст. ст. is five short sentences, and they make him a monk of the Kiev Caves — which is the Greek page’s own by-name for him, the one in the Cave, said the other way round. It gives the year of his end as 1188, the first year this folder has held. It calls his incorrupt relics wonderworking, and then adds the detail the whole entry turns on: on his right hand the first three fingers still stand joined for prayer, from which it is seen that he died at prayer, and Bishop Nikolai takes that for a reproof of those who do not cross themselves with three fingers.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 19 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/19/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3300/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026; and the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 19. децембар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-01-01&prolog=1), read 8 October 2026.*
