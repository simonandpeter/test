# Luke (Voino-Yasenetsky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 29 мая ст. ст. and gives him a life of its own. It begins by saying what kind of saint he is: a confessor who showed in himself the image of a good shepherd, healing sicknesses of the soul and of the body alike, and gave an example of the office of an archpastor joined to that of a physician; and that his theological treatises wake faith and persuade those who doubt that God is, refuting various pseudo-scientific theories. By his labour, it says, he showed what the bearing of the cross of Christ is.

He was Валентин Феликсович Войно-Ясенецкий in the world, and was born at Kerch on 27 April 1877. Having finished the gymnasium and thought over the choice of a way of life, he decided that he was bound to do only what was «полезно для страдающих людей», of use to people who suffer, and chose medicine. After the university he practised and did scientific research; in the 1920s he was working as a surgeon at Tashkent and taking an active part in church life, going to the meetings of the church brotherhood. The words of Иннокентий, bishop of Tashkent — «Доктор, вам надо быть священником», doctor, you must be a priest — he took as a call from God.

After three years in the priesthood he was tonsured a monk with the name of the apostle, evangelist and physician Luke, and on 30 May 1923 he was secretly consecrated bishop. From that time, the calendar says, his way of the cross as a confessor begins: many arrests, torture and exiles, and none of them weakened his zeal in the archpastor’s duty or in serving people as a doctor. From 1946 to 1961 he was the ruling bishop of the Crimean diocese.

He died on 11 June 1961 — which is this feast’s own day in the new style — on the day of All the Saints who have shone forth in the Russian land. But the shepherd did not leave his flock: many wonderful healings were worked at his prayers. In 1996 his holy remains were found, and they now rest in the Holy Trinity cathedral of Simferopol. The calendar closes with a prayer that God, by his prayers, may give us also strength to bear our own cross and to confess the holy Orthodox faith well.
