# Vladimir (Vinogradov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him among the hieromartyrs on 9 декабря and gives no life. Its line is «Сщмч. Владимира пресвитера (1919).» — the hieromartyr Vladimir the presbyter, 1919 — and the calendar’s own name page adds the surname the line leaves out: «Владимир (Виноградов), иерей, сщмч.»

That is the whole of the record. He was a priest; he died in 1919, in the years in which the Russian church counts its new martyrs; and the calendar keeps him on this day alone, his line joined to no one else’s. The day page carries seven hymns, for the day’s own feast and for others whom it heads, and not one of them is under his name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 9 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261209.html); read 13 October 2026.*
