# Matthew of Yaransk

saint.gr calls him Matthew out of Russia on the day's line and Matthew of Yaransk on his own page. He was Mitrophan Kuzmich Shvekov in the world, born in 1861 in the town of Vyatka. His father was a shoemaker, and in his early years, the page says, he went into trade himself; nothing is told of his schooling or of what turned him from the market to the monastery.

In 1891 Mitrophan was tonsured a monk in the monastery of Saint Alexander and took the name Matthew. There, the page says, he was exercised in obedience and taught unceasing prayer; God gave him the gift of working wonders, and his face became a spiritual refuge and a consolation for the people of God. Having lived so in the love of God, he reposed in peace in 1927. The page says nothing of his last years, gives no monastery after the first and prints no hymn.
