# Medoule, with her company

saint.gr's calendar for 25 January names Medoule with her company and gives no life. The whole of what her page prints about what happened to them is a single sentence: that they were martyred after being thrown into the fire. Nothing else is recorded there — not her homeland, not the reign under which they suffered, not the number of the women who suffered with her, and not one of their names. The page prints no troparion and no kontakion.

Above that sentence stands a couplet of verse which plays on her name: «Σεπτὴ Μεδούλη, τοῦ Θεοῦ Λόγου δούλη, / Δούλοις Θεοῦ σύναθλος εἰς πῦρ ὡράθη» — venerable Medoule, handmaid of the Word of God, was seen in the fire as the fellow-contestant of the servants of God. That is a reading of the name and not a fact about her life, and it is set down here as what the page prints and nothing more.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 25 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/25/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1157/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
