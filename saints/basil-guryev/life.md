# Basil (Guryev)

He is the fifth of the six presbyters the Russian calendar keeps in one line on 30 September, eleven people under the one year 1937. The day line prints only «Василия»; the surname is the site's index of names, which heads his page «Василий (Гурьев), иерей, сщмч.» — Basil the hieromartyr, priest. One thing on that page is his alone among the eleven: it lists three days of memory rather than two.

The other ten carry this day and «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. His page carries a third, «26 апреля (Бут.)», an abbreviation the calendar uses and does not expand, so it is written down here as it was read and not guessed at. Nothing else is printed: no birth, no diocese, no parish, no charge, no place of death. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line.

*After the Russian church calendar's [30 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260930.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14336.html); read 4 October 2026.*
