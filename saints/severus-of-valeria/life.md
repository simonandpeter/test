# Severus of Valeria

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 27 июня ст. ст. names him «Прп. Севира пресвитера (VI).», a venerable presbyter of the sixth century, and the name link behind the forename gives the epithets the line leaves out, «Севир Валерийский (Италийский), пресвитер» — of Valeria, and of Italy. His life page puts him in the church of the Most Holy Mother of God in the village of Interoclea in central Italy, and says he was marked out by a virtuous life pleasing to God. The page gives no year at all, so the century of the line is the whole of what is recorded here.

One story is the whole of the life. He was at work in the vineyard cutting the clusters when he was called to bring the Holy Mysteries to a dying man. «Идите обратно, я сейчас вас догоню» — go back, he said, I shall follow you at once; and a little was left to cut, so he stayed a short while in the garden to finish the work. When he reached the sick man he was told that he had already died.

Holding himself guilty of a man’s death without repentance he was seized with trembling and began to weep bitterly. He went into the house where the body lay, and calling himself a murderer he fell in tears before the dead man. Suddenly the dead man came to life, and told everyone standing there that the demons had wanted to take possession of his soul, but that one of the angels had said: «Возвратите его, потому что о нем плачет пресвитер Севир, и ради его слез Господь даровал ему этого человека» — give him back, for the presbyter Severus is weeping over him, and for the sake of his tears the Lord has granted him this man.

He gave thanks to the Lord, heard the risen man’s confession and gave him the Holy Mysteries; and the man, remaining in unceasing prayer, lived seven days more and then departed to the Lord with joy. The day page prints no hymn under his name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 27 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260627.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1406.htm); read 9 October 2026.*
