# Arethas (Nasonov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names the hieromartyr Arethas among the entries for 28 December on 28 December and gives no life. Its line is «Сщмчч. Арефы и Александра пресвитеров (1938).» — the hieromartyrs Arethas and Alexander, presbyters, 1938 — and the calendar’s own name page for this memory carries the fuller form the line leaves out: «Арефа (Насонов), иерей, сщмч.»

That is the whole of the record. He was a priest; the year of death the line gives is 1938; the calendar keeps the memory on this day, inside the afterfeast of the Nativity.  The day page prints two hymns and both belong to the twenty thousand martyrs of Nicomedia; nothing stands under this name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 28 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261228.html); read 13 October 2026.*
