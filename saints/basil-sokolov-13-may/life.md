# Basil (Sokolov, 13 May)

He was born in 1868 in the village of Старая слобода in the Александровский district of the province of Vladimir. He finished the Вифанская seminary and from 1888 served in the village of Пустое in the same province; after his wife died in 1906 he entered the Moscow Theological Academy and finished it in 1910, and from 26 December 1910 he served at the church of Николы Явленного on the Arbat. The Russian calendar's name page 9286 calls him an archpriest and its shared life page a priest, and the earlier of the two words is recorded here as his office.

In April 1922 he was arrested in the case of the confiscation of church valuables, and under questioning he did not admit himself guilty of agitating against it. The indictment of 8 May charged him with a sermon preached on 7 April in that church after the valuables had been taken, in which, it said, he had used religious prejudice to call the people to turn to God and ask Him to sweep from the face of the earth those who had carried the confiscation out — as God had once swept away ancient Babylon — and had put it in the words of the psalm, «Дщи Вавилоня окаянная, блажен, кто имет и разбиет младенцы твоя о камень».

The public trial of fifty of the arrested had opened on 26 April in the hall of the Polytechnic Museum under the chairmanship of the tribunal's president Бек. On 8 May the Moscow revolutionary tribunal sentenced eleven men to be shot; on 18 May the Politburo struck six names from that list, and five were left: this man, [Christopher (Nadezhdin)](/saints/christopher-nadezhdin), [Alexander (Zaozersky)](/saints/alexander-zaozersky), the hieromonk [Macarius (Telegin)](/saints/macarius-telegin) and the layman [Sergius (Tikhomirov)](/saints/sergius-tikhomirov).

They were shot at the beginning of June 1922 in what the page calls the ship of the Cheka — the premises of the archive of a former insurance company, where the condemned were shot in the windowless rooms built for its safes in the half-basement — and buried at the Калитниковское cemetery in Moscow. They were canonised in 1998 as locally venerated saints of the Moscow diocese, and numbered among the New Martyrs and Confessors of Russia at the Jubilee Council of Bishops in August 2000.

Three other folders of this corpus store the same Russian name, read off three other name pages of the same calendar: a priest of the province of Tver, born in 1880 and shot in 1937, kept on 16 ноября; an archpriest of 1937 with no life at all, kept on 27 ноября; and a protodeacon of 1938, kept on 20 марта. Four pages, four days, three grades and three years, and nothing anybody has read puts two of the four men in one sentence. This folder's day stands in its name for that reason, and the reading is set out on its Russian row.
