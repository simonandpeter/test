# Theodosius (Belenky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Theodosius on 29 December and gives no life. Its line is «Сщмч. Феодосия пресвитера (1938).» — the hieromartyr Theodosius the presbyter, 1938 — and the calendar’s own numbered name page for this memory carries the surname the line leaves out: «Феодосий (Беленький), иерей, сщмч.»

That is the whole of the record. He was a priest; the year of death the line gives is 1938; the calendar keeps the memory on this day, inside the afterfeast of the Nativity. The line names one man and he is in this draft. The day page prints seven hymns — two for the gravedigger of the Kyiv Caves, two for the abbot of the Unsleeping and three for the infants of Bethlehem — and none under this name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 29 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261229.html); read 13 October 2026.*
