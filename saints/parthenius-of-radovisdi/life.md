# Parthenius of Radovisdi

saint.gr calls him a newly manifested saint of the Church. He came from the village of Vatsounia in Thessaly, and from childhood he had the priestly calling; worthily, and by divine grace, he became bishop of Radovisdi, in the prefecture of Arta. The page heads him with one year only, 1777, the year of his birth left as a question mark.

He was an excellent shepherd of his people, and by his warm love and by his own holy example he supported his flock in its every need. He used regularly to go out into the fields and bless the herdsmen's animals. So, having lived in holiness and in justice, he gave up his blessed soul to God on 21 July 1777.

On the day of the translation of his holy relics they gave off a heavenly fragrance. Part of the bones of his hands is at the Monastery of Dousiko, near Trikala. It is said, the page adds, that Saint Parthenius was especially a wonderworker in the healing of the diseases of animals.
