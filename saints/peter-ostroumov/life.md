# Peter (Ostroumov)

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 27 июня ст. ст. names him in four words, «Сщмч. Петра пресвитера (1939).» — a hieromartyr, a presbyter, the forename in the genitive and a year. It gives no surname, no place of service, no diocese and no account of his death. The name link behind the forename supplies the calendar’s own nominative with the surname and the grade, «Петр (Остроумов), иерей, сщмч.», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from; the parentheses are the calendar’s own.

His name page carries nothing beyond that. It heads «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 27 июня» and stops, with no life and no narrative of any kind, and nothing is supplied for it here. The year in the line, 1939, is the year of death recorded here, and the calendar gives no year of birth. The line’s «Сщмч.» makes him a hieromartyr and «пресвитера» a presbyter, and the name link repeats the rank as «иерей», a priest.

The second day his name page lists is «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which is a synaxis and not a second feast, so he renders on 27 июня ст. ст. only. The surname stands in the display name because the calendar gives him no epithet and no see to tell him by. The day page prints five hymns and not one of them is under his name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 27 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260627.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14279.html); read 9 October 2026.*
