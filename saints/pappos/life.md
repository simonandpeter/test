# Pappos

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 3 June reads «Όσιος Πάππος», the venerable Pappos, and the page behind it says only this: the venerable Pappos died peacefully; we have no other details of the venerable one's life. So what is recorded here is a name, the rank of a venerable — that is, a monastic — and a death that was not a martyrdom.

The page's couplet is the only other thing it gives him, and it plays on his name, which is the Greek word for a grandfather: Pappos has died, not the grandfather of grandchildren but rather the father of virtues as his daughters. There is no century here, no country, no monastery and no wonder; there is no troparion. A reader who wants more will have to want it from another source than this one.
