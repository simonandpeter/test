# Christea the Gardener

doxologia.ro's life of him is two sentences long, and this is the whole of it: the holy martyr Christea was a gardener, and he lived in Constantinople; in the year 1748, confessing the right faith, he died by the sword. The page names no birthplace, no parents, no judge and no place of execution, and says nothing of how he came to be tried. What it gives is his trade, his city, the year, and the manner of his death.

The one other thing it prints for him is a troparion, and that is worth setting beside the life, because it says rather more of him than the life does. It asks the All-merciful, who planted flowers for eternity in his garden — the martyrs who poured out their blood — and who counted the faithful Christea the gardener among them and granted him many gifts, to remember us for the love of him. The page prints the hymn without a tone and with its own spelling and punctuation, and it is copied here as it stands there.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 12 februarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/12-februarie), [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-mucenic-hristea) and [the troparion](https://doxologia.ro/troparul-sfantului-mucenic-hristea); read 19 September 2026.*
