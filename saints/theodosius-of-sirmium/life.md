# Theodosius of Sirmium

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar for 27 марта ст. ст. carries one life for the two men it names together, and this is the whole of it. They suffered for the faith in Christ in the year 304 at Sirmium. Seeing how the pagans put Christians to death day after day, they came themselves to believe in Christ and resolved to suffer for the faith they had taken; and standing before the governor they confessed boldly that they were Christians, so that he and those about him wondered at their courage.

The governor had them shut in prison and the guard charged to keep them strictly. After some days he had them brought out again and set to persuading them to deny Christ and sacrifice to the idols, and they held firm in their confession. Then he ordered them hung upon a tree and their bodies raked with sharp iron hooks; after that the two were laid upon sharpened tridents, and at the last they were beheaded.

This folder is the second of the two the line names. The calendar’s name page 2212 calls him «Феодосий Сирмийский», of Sirmium, and gives him this day and no other; it says nothing of his birth, his trade or his age. [Manuel of Sirmium](/saints/manuel-of-sirmium) suffered beside him and has a folder of his own, with the same life in it.

One caution belongs here. The Greek calendar keeps on the day before this one a company whose line runs «Άγιοι Κοδράτος, Θεοδόσιος, Μανουήλ και άλλοι Τεσσαράκοντα μάρτυρες από την Ανατολή» — three named men and forty more from the East — and the corpus holds those named men as folders of their own. Nothing was read across: that entry gives no year and no city, and this one gives the year and the city and names two men only. Whether the two pairs are one pair is left open here. The day page prints no hymn for anybody.
