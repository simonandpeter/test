# Ananias of Crete

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 22 April reads «Sfântul Cuvios Anania din Creta» — the venerable Ananias of Crete — and his own page carries no Viață tab, no troparion and no notice of any kind: icons, and the readings appointed for a venerable father, and nothing else.

Those readings are the Epistle to the Galatians on the fruit of the Spirit — love, joy, peace, long-suffering, kindness — and the Gospel of Matthew in which the Lord calls to himself all who labour and are heavy laden. They are the common of a monastic saint and say nothing of this man in particular.

So what stands here is what the calendar keeps and no more: a venerable man named Ananias, of Crete, commemorated on 22 April. His years, his monastery, his teachers and the manner of his life are not given, and nothing has been supplied for them. He is not the apostle Ananias of the Seventy who baptized Paul at Damascus, whom the same calendar keeps on 1 October and this corpus already holds.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 22 aprilie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/22-aprilie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-anania-din-creta), which prints no life; read 20 September 2026.*
