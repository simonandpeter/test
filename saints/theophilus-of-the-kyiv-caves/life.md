# Theophilus of the Kyiv Caves

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 24 октября ст. ст. as the third of three recluses of the caves of Kyiv, and his own page is three sentences long: he laboured in the monastery of the Kyiv caves in the twelfth and thirteenth centuries, and the relics of the saint rest in the Near caves, which the page also calls the caves of the first of the monastery's founders.

The only thing said of him beyond that is a phrase out of the service. The life the three of them share reports that in the common service to the venerable of those caves he is named «в чудесех изящный» — excellent, or exquisite, in miracles. Which miracles, and whose, the page does not say; he has no story, no place of origin, no elder and no year, and the epithet is the whole of his memory besides his name.

The day's line and both of his pages agree on his century where they disagree for the second of the three, so this folder's reading is theirs: the twelfth and thirteenth, with no year of birth and none of death, which is why it is written down as a span of life and not as a death. The day page carries troparia and kontakia for four of the saints it heads, and none under his name. [Arethas](/saints/arethas-of-the-kyiv-caves) is the one of the three with a story, and [Sisoes](/saints/sisoes-of-the-kyiv-caves) is as bare as he is.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 24 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261024.html), [his own page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life6895.htm) and [the life the three share](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4583.htm); read 4 October 2026.*
