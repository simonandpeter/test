# Turbo of Cappadocia

saint.gr's calendar keeps him on 17 Ιανουαρίου together with [Junilla](/saints/junilla-of-cappadocia), and its page for the two of them is four sentences long. It begins by saying what the sources are not: «Άγνωστοι στους Συναξαριστές και τα Μηναία. Μνημονεύονται στον Κώδικα της Κρυπτοφέρης Βγ IV» — unknown to the synaxaristae and the menaia, remembered in the Grottaferrata codex Βγ IV. They suffered a martyr's death in Cappadocia, where they came from, together with Neonilla and her three grandchildren, whom the same calendar keeps on 16 January.

Of Turbo the page says that he destroyed the idols and proclaimed that he was a Christian, that he was arrested, and that he was beheaded. It gives no year and no place beyond Cappadocia. The page's own heading for him reads «Αγια Τουρβων», with a feminine article, while the sentence about him is masculine throughout — «Ο δε Τουρβών κατέστρεψε τα είδωλα» — and this folder follows the sentence rather than the heading.

The Russian calendar keeps him on 16 января ст. ст., a day earlier than the Greek, and in the same line as the grandmother and her grandsons rather than apart from them: «Мчч. Спевсиппа, Елевсиппа, Мелевсиппа, бабки их Леониллы и с ними Неона, Турвона и Иовиллы (161-180)». Its name for him is «Турвон Лангонийский», of Langonia, where the Greek calls him Cappadocian — the Russian life says the company suffered in Gaul, «по другому сказанию, в Каппадокии», by another account in Cappadocia, and that the emperor Zeno later gave their relics to Langres.

Of Turbo himself it says only that [Neon](/saints/neon-of-langonia) handed him the manuscript in which he had written down the brothers' sufferings, and that Turbo, imitating the passion-bearers, also ended his life a martyr. It gives him no place and no manner of death, and dates the whole company by the reign of Marcus Aurelius.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 17 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/17/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1117/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 16 января ст. ст. — [the Russian day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260116.html) and [the Russian life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life229.htm), read 3 October 2026.*
