# Alexius (Nikatov)

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him first of the fifteen and gives no life; the life here is the one azbyka.ru prints for the civil 3 декабря, out of fond.ru and the Moscow diocese’s volumes. He was born in 1875 in Poland, in the village of Дофнары in the Brest-Litovsk district of the province of Grodno, to a village schoolmaster, Гавриил Никатов; soon after his birth the family moved to Russia, and there he grew up, was schooled, and finished one class of the seminary. From 1899 to 1901 he served in the army as a private, and after the army he married a girl called Елизавета.

In 1905 he became psalm-reader at the church of the village of Княжево in the Mozhaysk district of the province of Moscow; in 1906 he moved to the village of Петровское in the Vereya district and served there until 1920 — first as psalm-reader, then as deacon, and in the last year as priest. He and his wife had five children. From August 1920 to July 1921 he was a schoolmaster in the village of Суверня in the Serdobsk district of the province of Saratov, and in the same year got a priest’s place at the church of the village of Воскресенки in the Podolsk district. In 1923 he was moved to the church of the Tikhvin icon of the Mother of God in the village of Игнатово in the Dmitrov district of the Moscow region, and for his long pastoral labours he was given many church awards, among them the rank of archpriest. The witnesses’ statements show that he had a numerous flock and that people often came to him from other towns and villages.

From the twenties he was deprived of the vote as a clergyman and his household was requisitioned, and it was not easy in those hungry years to feed a family of that size. He was arrested on 19 November 1937 on a charge of active counter-revolutionary activity and of agitation aimed at breaking up the collective farms, of «гнусной клевете по адресу советской власти» and of terrorist leanings; from that day he was held in the Taganka prison in Moscow. He denied every charge and admitted nothing. By the decision of the judicial troika of the NKVD administration for the Moscow region of 27 November 1937 he was sentenced to be shot, and the execution took place on 3 December 1937 at the Butovo range, where he was then buried in a common grave.
