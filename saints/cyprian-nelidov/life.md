# Cyprian (Nelidov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names a Cyprian on 3 июня and gives no life of him. Its line for the day is the whole of what it says: «Прмч. Киприана (1934).» — the venerable-martyr Cyprian, and the year 1934. So what the calendar tells is that he was a monk who was counted among the martyrs and that he died in 1934.

His own name page on that site, 13721, carries nothing but the heading «Киприан (Нелидов), иеромонах, прмч.», which gives the surname the day's line leaves out and grades him иеромонах, a priest-monk, and the two days «26 января (Новомуч.) , 3 июня». There is no life page for him on the site, no icon and no article, and no page names a monastery, a diocese or a place of any kind.

The day page prints four hymns, two for a prince whose relics were carried to Moscow and two for an icon, and none under his name. The 26 января commemoration of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia was not read, and nothing of it is in this folder.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 3 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260603.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13721.html); read 8 October 2026.*
