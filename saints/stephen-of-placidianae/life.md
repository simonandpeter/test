# Stephen of Placidianae

The Greek calendar keeps three men together on 11 Ιανουαρίου, and Stephen is the first of them: «Άγιοι Στέφανος ο εν Πλακιδιαναίς, Θεόδωρος και Αγάπιος ο Αρχιμανδρίτης» — Stephen who was at Placidianae, [Theodore](/saints/theodore-companion-of-stephen), and [Agapius](/saints/agapius-of-apamea) the archimandrite. The place-name is all that the calendar attaches to Stephen, and saint.gr's page says plainly that it has nothing further.

What that page does say is this: «Η μόνη πληροφορία που έχουμε είναι ότι ο Όσιος Αγάπιος ασκήτεψε στην Απαμεία της Συρίας. Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τους βίους των Αγίων» — the only information we have is that the venerable Agapius lived the ascetic life at Apamea in Syria; we have no other details of the lives of these saints.

So for Stephen there is a name, a place, a day, and the couplet the page prints over the three of them: that they now stand before the throne of the Trinity, having left the earth and taken up their dwelling in heaven. There is no year, and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/11/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1088/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
