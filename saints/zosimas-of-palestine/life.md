# Zosimas of Palestine

Zosimas was a monk of the monastery by the Jordan in the reign of Theodosius the Younger. He is the Zosimas who found [Mary of Egypt](/saints/mary-of-egypt) struggling beyond the Jordan, who carried the holy Communion to her once and who buried her the following year; and he departed to the Lord at the age of a hundred.

That is the whole of what doxologia prints for him, and the page names its own source for it: Saint Nicholas Velimirović's Ohrid Prologue, in the Romanian translation of Mihaela Grosu, volume one, page 406. It gives no year of birth and no year of death, and no account of his own monastery beyond its standing by the Jordan; the long account of him belongs to her life, told there by Sophronius, and is printed on this same site under her name for 1 aprilie.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 4 aprilie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/4-aprilie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-cuvios-zosima-din-palestina); read 19 September 2026.*
