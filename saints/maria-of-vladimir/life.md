# Maria of Vladimir

saint.gr's calendar for 19 Μαρτίου keeps a Russian princess who ended her life a nun, and gives her three lines. She is called Maria, or Martha, with the surname Svarnova, and she was princess of Vladimir in the time of the grand prince Vsevolod III Georgievich. She became a nun in the monastery of the Dormition of the Theotokos at Vladimir, and fell asleep in peace in the year 1206. The page heads her «(; - 1206)», so the year of her birth is a question mark.

That is the whole of what has been read. saint.gr gives her no parentage, no account of her years as princess, no reason for her taking the habit, no relics and no hymn; it prints no couplet over her either. Nothing has been added here from any other source or from memory, and the two names the page gives are both recorded as it prints them rather than one being chosen over the other.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 19 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/19/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3923/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
