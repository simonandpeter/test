# Theognostus (29 July)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Theognostus second in a pair of hieromonks on 29 July. Its line is «Прмчч. Серафима и Феогноста (1921).» — the venerable martyrs Seraphim and Theognostus, 1921 — and the calendar's own name page for him says only «Феогност, иеромонах, сщмч.»

That is unusually little. His companion [Seraphim (Bogoslovsky)](/saints/seraphim-bogoslovsky) gets a surname from the calendar and he gets none, and no place either, so this folder carries the day in its name to tell him from the two other men of the name the corpus already keeps. Nothing was invented to fill the gap.

The line and the name page do not agree about what kind of saint he is: the line calls him a venerable martyr and the name page a hieromartyr. This folder writes the line's reading, keeps the name page's rank, and records the disagreement rather than settling it.

A reading stands behind the entry and was not opened. The day's two hymns for the pair, which are in this folder, call them the God-given protectors of Alma-Ata and place their struggle in the mountains of the Tian Shan; nothing has been written into this life or into a `location` from them. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 29 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260729.html); read 13 October 2026.*
