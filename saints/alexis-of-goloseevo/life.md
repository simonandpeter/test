# Alexis of Goloseevo

In 1840 a ninth child was born to a family in Kyiv, and his parents named him Vladimir; he was to become the venerable Alexis. The boy said nothing at all until he was twelve, when at the service of the Resurrection the metropolitan of that time, Philaret — himself since numbered among the saints — said to him, “Vladimir, Christ is risen!” At the third time the child answered, “He is risen indeed!”

The years passed, and the young Vladimir, driven by a longing for God, went to the Lavra of the Caves, wishing to enter the ranks of the monks. At the tonsure the elder meant to name him Nicholas, but in that moment forgot the name he had meant to give him, and an inward voice said to him, “Alexis”; and that stayed his name in the monastic life.

Alexis was a man of grace and a great spiritual father. He heard the confessions of the monks and of the people who sought him out, both at the Lavra of the Caves and at the monastery of Goloseevo, which the holy metropolitan Peter Mohyla had founded in the seventeenth century. He went to the Lord in 1917, and at once wonders flowed at his grave, and many came there to ask help and healing.

When the monastery of Goloseevo was opened again in 1993 his relics were taken up and set in the church for veneration, and on 4 October of that same year his canonisation was made. A portion of his relics is kept also in the reliquary set out for veneration in the great church of the Frumoasa monastery at Iași. doxologia prints no hymn for him.
