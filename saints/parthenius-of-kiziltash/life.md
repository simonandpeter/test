# Parthenius, Abbot of Kiziltash, Monk-martyr

Parthenius was born in 1815 at Elisavetgrad. He was tonsured on 23 December 1845 and ordained hieromonk on 8 April 1846; from July 1848 he was dean of the Korsun monastery at Kherson and later steward of the Kherson bishop’s house, and in 1852, at the Tenginsk fortification of the Black Sea coastal guard, he distinguished himself by an engineer’s talent, proposing the easiest way to raise sunken cargo — a special commission approved the invention and ordered such machines built for the ports. The Sretensky calendar’s life runs on past the point read here, to the monastery of Kiziltash in the Crimea and his death in 1867.
