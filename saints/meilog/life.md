# Meilog

saint.gr's calendar for 31 December keeps the holy Meilog, and its whole account of him is one sentence of offices: «Ο Άγιος Μέιλογκ ή Μελέκ ήταν ο πρώτος Ηγούμενος Χλούες, Επίσκοπος Πλουμέλεκ Γαλλίας και Αρχιεπίσκοπος Κέλτικα ή Γαλικίας βόρειας Ισπανίας» — the holy Meilog, or Melek, was the first abbot of Chloues, bishop of Ploumelek in France, and archbishop of Celtica, or Galicia, of northern Spain.

The head of the entry gives his years, 500 to 596, and that is the only other thing the page has. There is no account of any of the three offices, no deed, no manner of death, no relic, no couplet and no hymn. Every name in the sentence is the site's own transcription into Greek letters, and the site is itself unsure what the last of them is, offering Celtica or Galicia for the same see; so the names stand here as it writes them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 31 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/31/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2480/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
