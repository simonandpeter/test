# Alexander (Medvedev), Deacon, Hieromartyr (1918)

Alexander Medvedev (1889–1918) served as deacon in the village of Bolshe-Trifonskoye in the Irbit district; in the years of persecution he went on serving despite the danger, and was killed without trial by Red Guards on 10 September 1918 at the station of Yegorshino — they cut him down with sabres. He was born in 1889, studied at the Ekaterinburg school of psalmists without finishing it, and began early to serve in church, first as a sacristan, and from 1 July 1910 was allowed to act as psalmist at the church of John the Forerunner at Bolshe-Trifonskoye; just before the revolution Alexander Nilovich was ordained deacon, and served there until his martyr’s death. Most of the villagers had strong farms and were well-off — kulaks, as the Bolsheviks called them — and the October revolution found no sympathy with them; the committee of the poor set up by the Bolsheviks to carry out requisitions of food and property met resistance from the mass of the villagers — the calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. He was numbered among the saints in 2002, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 28 August (Julian).
