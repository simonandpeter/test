# Sozomenus of Cyprus

saint.gr's calendar for 20 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιος Σωζόμενος ο θαυματουργός» and gives him a long notice. A codex from the Holy Trinity at Rizokarpaso says that he came from Karpasia in Cyprus, the page reports, while from his own service and from other sources we learn that he lived before the Frankish occupation of the island, about 1100. His parents were God-fearing people, and they led him from childhood into the study and the knowledge of the word of God, so that in time he came by a learning rare for his age.

When he came of age he decided to leave his house too and to make for the wilderness, where, free of every earthly care, he would think of one thing only and care for one thing only: how to please God. So, after wandering a good while, he came to a cave in the face of a cliff, rocky and high. There he stopped; he went in, made the sign of the cross, and in a voice shaking with feeling and fear began to chant softly a verse of the third antiphon of the fourth tone, that his heart might be lifted up to the Word and nothing of the world's delights draw it down.

As time went on his fame spread. He refused his love and his help to nobody, and his lodging stood open to all; and the gift of healing with which God had graced him he offered gladly to everyone who took refuge in his grace with faith and a sincere repentance and asked it of him.

So, having lived a life pleasing to God, he gave up his soul to the Lord. Christians took his body with reverence and buried it at the back of the cave, wetting it with the tears of their affection, and his tomb became, in the page's words, a clinic for diseases. The sick still come to it from different parts to be healed, and on his feast thousands of pilgrims gather at the cave where he practised his asceticism.
