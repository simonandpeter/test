# Vladimir (Vyatsky)

The Russian calendar gives him a line of his own on 26 September and no life: «Сщмч. Владимира пресвитера (1939)» — Vladimir the hieromartyr, presbyter, 1939. Its index of names adds the surname, «Владимир (Вятский), иерей, сщмч.», a priest, and keeps him here and on 26 January with the whole company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia.

The year sets him slightly apart from the five named in the line above his, who died in 1937: 1939 is late for the great wave, and the calendar says nothing about where he served or how he died. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which refused this reading on 4 October 2026 — a 403 to the fetcher's user-agent and to a full browser one alike — so the line stands as the line.

*After the Russian church calendar's [26 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260926.html) and its [index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9528.html); read 4 October 2026.*
