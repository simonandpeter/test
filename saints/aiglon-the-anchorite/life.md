# Aiglon the Anchorite

saint.gr's calendar for 17 Αυγούστου names him and says one sentence about him, and the sentence is the whole of what the Greek calendar knows: «Ο Όσιος Αίγλων ήταν αναχωρητής που έζησε ασκητικά και απεβίωσε ειρηνικά» — the venerable Aiglon was an anchorite who lived ascetically and died in peace. There is no country, no desert named, no monastery, no century, no year, no manner of death beyond the peace of it, and no hymn.

The page prints no couplet of the synaxarion for him either, which on this site is unusual and is itself a measure of how little there is. This folder stands for that one line of the Greek 17 August, and is marked by nothing but his name and his calling, because the calendar gives him nothing else to be known by.
