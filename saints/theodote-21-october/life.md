# Theodote (21 October)

saint.gr's calendar for 21 Οκτωβρίου names Theodote together with Socrates the presbyter, and tells one story for the two of them. They lived in the third century, the page says, when Alexander Severus was emperor, and they were martyred at Ancyra. Theodote came of a noble family and was bold in the faith: wherever she found herself she spoke of the Gospel and tried to increase the number of those devoted to the worship of Christ.

The presbyter [Socrates](/saints/socrates-companion-of-theodote) was a priest of the kind who do not only serve the liturgy, the page says, but enlighten and build up, and are ready at need to be sacrificed for the faith and for their flock; and in that work he had the pious Theodote for a precious helper, who by her teaching prepared pagan women for the knowledge of the truths of the faith and for holy baptism.

For this the two of them were denounced and arrested, and with threats and tortures they were pressed to sacrifice to the idols. The woman and the priest rejected the impious proposal with indignation, and sealed the confession of their faith with their blood, having been put to death by beheading. Her own couplet calls her a pure sacrifice given to God, as her name says. The page prints no hymn for them.
