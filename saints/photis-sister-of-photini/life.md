# Photis, sister of Photini

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Φεβρουαρίου prints «Αγία Φωτίς» as a line of its own, and the page standing under it gives one sentence: «Η Αγία Φωτίς ήταν η τρίτη αδελφή της Αγίας Φωτεινής και μαρτύρησε αφού την έδεσαν σε δύο δένδρα και την ξέσχισαν» — Photis was the third sister of St Photini, and she was martyred after they tied her to two trees and tore her apart. The fuller story is on the page of [Photini the Samaritan](/saints/photini-the-samaritan), which says that her sons and her five sisters were martyred together with her: she had been baptised among the first women of Samaria after her meeting with Christ at Jacob's well, had preached the Gospel in Africa and at Rome, and was put to death at Rome by the emperor Nero.

The rest of the household the calendar prints beside her on this day is [Anatole](/saints/anatole-sister-of-photini), [Photo](/saints/photo-sister-of-photini), [Paraskeve](/saints/paraskeve-sister-of-photini), [Kyriake](/saints/kyriake-sister-of-photini), [Photinos](/saints/photinos-son-of-photini) and [Joses](/saints/joses-son-of-photini). This page gives Photis no birthplace, no year, no relics and no hymn of her own — the second apolytikion on Photini's page names all eight of them together — and over the one sentence it prints a couplet, «Δώσεις ἔπαθλον δενδροάθλῳ Φωτίδι, Δενδρῶνα τερπνὸν τῆς Ἐδὲμ Θεοῦ Λόγε»: to Photis, who contended on the trees, the Word of God will give the pleasant grove of Eden for her prize.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3655/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
