# Dionysius of Thrace

The Russian church calendar prints four men in one line for 6 мая ст. ст. — «Мчч. Варвара воина, Вакха, Каллимаха и Дионисия (ок. 362)» — and gives the four of them a single life. They lived in the fourth century and served in the army of the emperor «Юлиан Отступник». Of the four, [Barbarus](/saints/barbarus-6-may) was a Christian in secret; the other three came to the faith in one hour, in the middle of his torture, and were put to death before him.

Barbarus had won great honour in that army. In the war against the Franks he fought a single combat against a giant of the enemy's and beat him, for which he was praised by the emperor and given the title of comes, a bodyguard of the sovereign. After the victory the commander wished to offer sacrifice to the pagan gods, and offered the first of it, as an honour, to the victor. Barbarus then confessed himself openly a Christian and refused; and by the emperor's order he was put to many torments, hung up and his flesh cut away until his entrails fell out.

He called on the Lord for help, and an angel of God came and healed his wounds, so that not a trace of them was left. It is at this point that the life brings in the other three. The commander «Вакх» and two soldiers, «Каллимах» and «Дионисий», seeing the wonder, believed in Christ and renounced the pagan gods, and for that they were beheaded at once. Barbarus himself was tortured for seven days more and beheaded in the year 362.

What the calendar says of this man is that his name was «Дионисий», that the name page behind the line gives him the epithet Фракийский, and that he was one of the two soldiers who, seeing an angel heal a man who had been cut open in front of them, believed in Christ, renounced the pagan gods and were beheaded on the spot. The source gives him no birthplace, no family, no age and no rank beyond the soldier's, and the day page prints no hymn under his name. His companions in that hour were [Bacchus of Thrace](/saints/bacchus-of-thrace), the commander who had called for the sacrifice, and [Callimachus of Thrace](/saints/callimachus-of-thrace), the other soldier.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 6 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260506.html) and [the life of the four](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1002.htm); read 8 October 2026.*
