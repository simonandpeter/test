# Lupicinus of Verona

saint.gr's calendar for 31 Μαΐου names him and gives two sentences. Lupicinus lived in the fifth century, it says, and was bishop of Verona; and he fell asleep in peace. Βερόνα is Verona in northern Italy, and the see is the only thing the page tells us about his place.

That is the whole of the source. It gives no year of birth and no year of death, names no predecessor and no successor, tells nothing of his teaching or his household, records no wonder, and prints neither hymn nor epigram for him. What it does state is a bishop of a named see in a named century who died in peace and not by martyrdom.
