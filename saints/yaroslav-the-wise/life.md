# Yaroslav the Wise

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him for 20 февраля ст. ст. in five words — «Блгв. кн. Ярослава Мудрого (1054).» — the right-believing prince Yaroslav the Wise, who died in 1054. «Блгв.», благоверный, is the calendar's word for a ruler it keeps as a saint, and «кн.» is prince; the name link behind the line gives the nominative with the office, «Ярослав Мудрый, князь», and the epithet «Мудрый», the Wise, is part of the name the calendar itself prints.

The calendar sets no life page under his name here and no hymn on this day for him. Nothing of his reign is written in this record, because the line is the whole of what was read on it: no see of government, no city, no kinship and no deed. That is a deliberate silence rather than an omission — what an English reader may know of this prince from elsewhere is not what the source cited here says, and only what it says is set down.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 20 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260220.html); read 3 October 2026.*
