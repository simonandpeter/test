# Sebastian of Cartagena

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Φεβρουαρίου prints «Άγιος Σεβαστιανός ο δούκας» as a line of its own, and the page standing under it gives one sentence: Sebastian was ruler of the city of Cartagena; he embraced the Christian faith through the evangelical word of [Photinos](/saints/photinos-son-of-photini), the son of St [Photini](/saints/photini-the-samaritan) the Samaritan; and he was martyred under the emperor Nero, whose reign the page gives as 54 to 68.

That is the whole of it. The calendar calls him «ο δούκας», the duke, and gives him no year of birth, no account of his death beyond the reign and the manner the couplet implies, no relics and no hymn. The couplet plays on his name, which comes from σεβαστός, the reverend: «Σεβαστιανῷ τοῦτον εἴπω τὸν λόγον. Σεβάζομαί σε, τὴν κάραν τετμημένον» — this word I will say to Sebastian: I reverence you, whose head was struck off.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3650/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
