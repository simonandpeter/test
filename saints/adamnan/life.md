# Adamnan

The Greek synaxarion keeps Adamnan, a saint of Scotland, on 23 September, and its heading gives his death in 704. Of his life it says only that it has no details, and it counts him among the synaxis of the saints who shone in Scotland.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 23 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2336/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
