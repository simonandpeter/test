# Emilian the Penitent

The Ohrid Prologue says Emilian was born at Rome and committed many grave sins in his youth; but when he sobered from his sinning and came to himself, he trembled at the very thought of the Judgment of God. He entered a monastery, and by fasting, by vigil and by obedience he tamed his body and dried it out. In every good struggle he was a model to his brethren, and he often went out of the monastery at night to a cave nearby, to pray.

The abbot of that monastery did not know where he went, and one night followed him secretly. He saw Emilian standing at prayer in fear and weeping. All at once a heavenly light, stronger than the sun, lit up the whole of that mountain, and the cave and Emilian in particular; and a voice was heard from heaven: Emilian, thy sins are forgiven thee. The abbot was struck with awe and fled back to the monastery.

The next day he told the brethren everything he had seen and heard that night, and from then on Emilian was held in great honour among them. He lived long, the entry ends, and fell asleep in the Lord. It gives no year and no century, names no monastery and no mountain, does not give the abbot a name, and prints no hymn.
