# Honoratus of Amiens

saint.gr names him and gives two sentences. It says that Saint Honoratus was born at Port-le-Grand in France, near Amiens, that he was elected bishop of Amiens, and that he fell asleep in peace. Under the entry it cites the Apostoliki Diakonia of the Church of Greece.

Nothing else is printed there: no year, no century, no parents, no monastery, no wonder, no relics and no hymn. He is not Honoratus of Arles, the founder of Lérins, whom the corpus keeps in January; this is a bishop of Amiens, and the page names his birthplace and his see and stops. What it does not say is not set down here.
