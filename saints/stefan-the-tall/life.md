# Stefan the Tall

The Ohrid Prologue keeps him on the Julian 19 July and gives him four sentences and no more, so this folder’s whole account is a commemoration rather than a life. He was the son of the holy prince [Lazar of Serbia](/saints/lazar-of-serbia) and of the princess Milica. He was the defender of Christianity in the Balkans in its hardest days. He was the founder of the fair endowments of Manasija and Kalenić. And after many labours and troubles he died on the nineteenth of July in the year 1427.

The entry gives him no rank word at all — not despot, not prince, not venerable — and the one rank in it belongs to his father, whom the book calls a holy prince. So this folder records none, and takes its one year from the book’s own last sentence. His mother Milica has no folder in this corpus, and the entry names no other person.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 19. јул ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-08-01&prolog=1); read 6 October 2026.*
