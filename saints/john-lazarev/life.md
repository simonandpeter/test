# John (Lazarev)

Archpriest John Mikhailovich Lazarev was born in 1876 in the village of Kurmysh in Nizhny Novgorod province, and served in the church of the Dormition of the Mother of God in Nizhny Novgorod. He was arrested in August 1937. On 21 September the sentence, the highest measure, was read out, and he was shot soon after. The Jubilee Council of Bishops of the Russian Orthodox Church glorified him in 2000.

The Russian calendar keeps him on 21 September old style on one line with the priests [Valens (Nikolsky)](/saints/valens-nikolsky), [Alexander (Belyakov)](/saints/alexander-belyakov), [Andrew (Benediktov)](/saints/andrew-benediktov), [Peter (Sakharovsky)](/saints/peter-sakharovsky) and [John (Nikolsky)](/saints/john-nikolsky), of 1937. azbyka.ru prints a seventh priest, Basil Nosov, on its line for the day, whom the Sretensky day does not name; it keeps him also with the saints of Nizhny Novgorod, and prints no hymns for him.

*After the life printed by the Russian church calendar at azbyka.ru — [the entry](https://azbyka.ru/days/sv-ioann-lazarev); the Sretensky calendar (days.pravoslavie.ru), 21 сентября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260921.html); read 17 September 2026.*
