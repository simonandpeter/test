# Alexis (Stabnikov)

Archpriest Alexis Stabnikov was rector of the cathedral at the Yugovsky works near Perm, now the settlement of Yug in the Perm district. On 4 October 1918 he was killed by Red Army men during the mass repressions in the Perm country. azbyka.ru’s life, drawn from the Orthodox Encyclopedia, says no more.

The Russian calendar keeps him on 21 September old style on one line with the priests [Alexander (Fedoseev)](/saints/alexander-fedoseev), [Constantine (Shirokinsky)](/saints/constantine-shirokinsky) and [John (Flerov)](/saints/john-flerov), of 1918; azbyka.ru keeps him also with the saints of the Perm metropolis, and prints no hymns for him.
