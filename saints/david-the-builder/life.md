# David the Builder

David IV, called David the Builder, was king of Georgia. The life doxologia.ro prints under his name runs to four sentences. It gives his years as 1073–1125 and his reign as 1089 to 1125, notes that he is sometimes counted as David II, and says that from 1089 he helped his father Giorgi II in the governing of the country.

The same page says he defeated the Turks at the battle of Didgori in 1122 and took the city of Tbilisi, and that under his rule Georgia became the strongest state in the Caucasus. It adds that his tomb stands at the old entrance of the monastery of Gelati, which was founded in 1106.

Of his piety, his manner of life or his death the Romanian page says nothing, and nothing beyond what it prints is set down here.
