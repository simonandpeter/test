# John of Karpathos

John of Karpathos lived, most probably, in the seventh century; the churches of Karpathos hold many wall paintings and portable icons of him, and he is very likely the later bishop of Karpathos whose works Photius the Great read.

Two of his works stand in the Philokalia: a hundred chapters of encouragement addressed to the monks of India, and a supplementary ascetic and consolatory discourse. In 1985 Ambrosios, metropolitan of Karpathos and Kasos, asked the Ecumenical Patriarchate to enrol him in the official calendar; a service was composed and his feast fixed on 25 August, and a large icon painted for the church of the Holy Apostles on Karpathos, where his festival is kept each year. The synaxarion quotes him: however many times you happen to fall, so many times get up and run after your Lord until you reach him — and the enemy suffers from us too, though by God’s economy we do not see it, lest we grow slack.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 25 August — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2648/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
