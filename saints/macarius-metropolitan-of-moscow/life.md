# Macarius of Moscow (16 February)

saint.gr names him and then says that it knows nothing else. Its calendar for 16 February prints «Άγιος Μακάριος Μητροπολίτης Μόσχας», Saint Macarius metropolitan of Moscow, and the page behind that line answers with a single sentence: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου» — we have no details of the saint's life. There is no century here, no year, no account of his rule of the see, no relics, no couplet and no hymn.

What the line gives is a rank and a see, and those are recorded here; the name it glosses only as the happy one. The corpus keeps several Macarii of Russian sees from Russian sources and none of them on this day, and nothing on this page is enough to say which man the Greek calendar means — so the day and the see are kept here as the page gives them, and the English form carries the day to keep him apart from them.

**The Russian church calendar keeps him on the Julian 5 October with the synaxis of the hierarchs of Moscow**, seventeen names in one line under one polyeleos service, marked «(Моск. Свт.)» and not expanded: Peter, Theognostus, Alexis, Cyprian, Photius, Jonah, Gerontius, Joasaph, Macarius, Philip, Job, Hermogenes, Tikhon, Peter, Philaret, Innocent and Macarius — two and a half centuries of metropolitans, then the patriarchs, then the metropolitans of Moscow and Kolomna, ending with a man shot in 1937. He is the seventeenth and last of them, and the line's second Macarius: the ninth name is the metropolitan who died in 1563, whom this corpus does not yet hold. For a page that told saint.gr nothing at all about him, this is the first thing the Russian calendar adds — the company he is kept in.
