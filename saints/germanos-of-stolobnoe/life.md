# Germanos of Stolobnoe

saint.gr gives the venerable Germanos two sentences and the life says no more than they do. He was born in Russia; he became a monk and lived as an ascetic in the desert of St Nilus at Stolobnoe — the island hermitage on lake Seliger that took its name from Nilus who settled it — and he fell asleep in peace in the year 1614. The day's line calls him «του Στολομπέν», of Stolobnoe.

The page gives no place of birth in Russia, no year for it, no abbot, no office in the hermitage, no relic and no hymn, and it heads him with a question mark where the birth year would be. The hermitage's dedication is a place name here and nothing more is claimed from it.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 22 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/22/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3683/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
