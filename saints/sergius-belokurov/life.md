# Sergius (Belokurov)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him for 22 февраля ст. ст. in one line that it gives to fifteen — «Сщмчч. Михаила, Иоанна, Виктора, Иоанна, Сергия, Андрея, Павла пресвитеров, прмчч. Сергия и Антипы, прмц. Параскевы, мчч. Стефана и Николая, мцц. Елисаветы, Ирины и Варвары (1938).» — seven hieromartyr presbyters, two venerable-martyr hieromonks, a venerable-martyr novice, two martyred psalm-readers and three women martyrs, all of 1938. The line carries the forenames only, declined, and no surname for any of them.

The name link behind the fifth of those names carries the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line leaves out: «Сергий (Белокуров), иерей, сщмч.», a priest of that name. Fifteen entries on one line are fifteen people, and they are kept apart here by the names and the offices the calendar's own links give them.

It prints no life page for him and no hymn under his name; the only hymns it sets on this day belong to a finding of relics with which it heads the day. It gives him no parish, no diocese, no place and no manner of death. Nothing further is written here, and nothing is reasoned out from the surname or from the year he shares with the other fourteen.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 22 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260222.html); read 3 October 2026.*
