# Hilarius (16 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 16 December keeps two martyrs together, Probus and Hilarius, and the whole of what its page says of them is one sentence: the holy Probus — or Promus — and Hilarius were martyred by the sword. It gives no year and no reign, it names no place and no judge, it tells nothing of their lives before that sentence and lists no relic of either of them.

What else the page carries is an epigram in which the two speak to each other: Probus says that the flame is a kind of green herbage, and Hilarius answers, yes, dearest Probus. Nothing is drafted from it. Because the entry names them both, each has a folder here and each relates the other, [Probus](/saints/probus-16-december) beside this one; and the page prints no hymn for either of them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 16 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/16/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3279/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
