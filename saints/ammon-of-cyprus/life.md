# Ammon of Cyprus

The Ohrid Prologue keeps him on the Julian 9 December and gives him four lines, which are the whole of what this folder knows. He was born and brought up in Cyprus. For his great spiritual learning and his many virtues, and for his mercy above all, he was made archbishop after Saint Damian. Having served the church faithfully and pleased God, he died in peace in the sixth century.

That is all, and two of the things a reader would want are not in it. The book names no see: it says who he followed and not what he held, and the Cyprus in this folder's name is the island it says he was born and brought up in, not a throne it says he sat on. And it does not say which Damian he followed; the corpus keeps thirteen men of that name and not one of them is an archbishop, so none has been named here. Where the source has four lines, this folder says what they say and no more.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 9. децембар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2025-12-22&prolog=1); read 7 October 2026.*
