# Vitalis (23 July)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 23 iulie is «Sfântul Mucenic Vitalie» — the holy martyr Vitalis — and his own page on the site prints the day, the epistle and the gospel read on it, and icons, with no Viață tab and no troparion. No place, no century and no account of his suffering is given there.

He is not the Vitalis this corpus already keeps, whom the same calendar prints on 11 ianuarie as «Sfântul Cuvios Vitalie», a venerable and not a martyr; the two lines give different ranks on different days, and nothing read here makes them one man. This folder is kept for the martyr of 23 July alone, and is marked by his day because the calendar gives him nothing else to be known by.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 23 July, and saint.gr gives both a year and a trade. Saint Vitalis bore his witness in the year 62, it says; he followed the trade of an armourer and belonged to the fervent Christians of those years. Being once present at Ravenna at the torture of a physician called Ursicinus, he was shaken, and in a loud voice encouraged the physician to endure the martyrdom — with the result that the physician bore it bravely. Then the judge Paulinus ordered Vitalis to be seized and tortured; and covered in blood he was taken to a deep pit, where they killed him by throwing great stones down on him. But the divine judgement was not slow in coming. Vitalis had been denounced to Paulinus by a certain priest of the idols, who was there too and threw stones at him; suddenly a demon took hold of that man, and in convulsions he cried out in a wild voice, «Vitalis the martyr of Christ is burning me hard», and in his madness threw himself into the current of the nearby river and drowned.
