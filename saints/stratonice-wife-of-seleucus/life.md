# Stratonice, wife of Seleucus

saint.gr's calendar for 31 Οκτωβρίου names «Άγιοι Σέλευκος και Στρατονίκη οι σύζυγοι», husband and wife, and its notice is about the marriage more than about either of them. The two were joined in the bonds of marriage young, the page says, and they were a genuine Christian couple, of the kind the Apostle Paul sets out so perfectly in his letter to the Ephesians: they were one soul and one heart.

But when they were called to choose between their life and their faith, the notice goes on, they did not hesitate a moment. To stay united they had to accept death for the Gospel; and so they fell as holy victims, to rise again in splendour on the day of the resurrection. The epigram set over them says that the spouses pour out blood by the sword and that, dead, they pour out myrrh for ever.

There is no year on the page, no city, no persecutor and no account of where they were buried, and no hymn; the myrrh of the epigram is the only thing said of their relics. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου». Her husband [Seleucus](/saints/seleucus-husband-of-stratonice) is kept with her on the same day.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 31 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/31/index.aspx) and [the notice of the two](https://www.saint.gr/1041/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
