# Basil of Moldovița

saint.gr gives him two sentences. The venerable Basil the Wonderworker lived between the fourteenth and the fifteenth century in Moldavia in Romania, it says; in the year 1451 he is mentioned as abbot of the monastery of Moldovița, and he was counted worthy also of the gift of working wonders. He fell asleep in peace. That is the whole of the notice, and it is the page and not the day's bare line that places him at all.

It names no wonder of his, no year of his birth or of his death, no teacher and no disciple, and it prints no couplet and no hymn. The two centuries it gives are all this folder has for a date, and the one fixed point inside them is the mention of 1451.
