# Olga (Maslennikova)

Olga was a parishioner, and that is the whole of what she was charged with. She was born on 10 July 1874 at Kaluga, finished the parish school, and belonged to a city church the page names for a great martyr, where she helped with the work of the building and where [Augustine (Belyaev)](/saints/augustine-belyaev), the archbishop whom this same day's long line of 1937 heads, was serving. The church's dedication is in the page and deliberately not in this paragraph: a dedication is not a relation, and the saint it is named for has a folder here.

The authorities arrested her on 29 October 1937. Called to interrogation, she did not admit the charges brought against her and, the calendar says in as many words, named nobody. On 19 November 1937 an NKVD troika sentenced her to eight years' imprisonment, and she died in a labour camp in 1941. No day, no month and no place of death is recorded, and the line that keeps her gives the year alone.

She was numbered among the saints of the New Martyrs and Confessors of Russia at the Jubilee Council of Bishops of the Russian Orthodox Church in August 2000.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 10 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261110.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4878.htm); read 4 October 2026.*
