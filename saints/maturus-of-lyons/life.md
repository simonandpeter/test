# Maturus of Lyons

saint.gr's calendar for 2 June keeps a company of the martyrs of Lyons and names nine of them. Its whole narrative is one sentence: the holy hieromartyrs Photinos, who was a bishop, and Sanctius the deacon, and the holy martyrs Vettius, Epagathus, Ponticus, Biblis, Attalus, Alexander and Maturus were perfected in martyrdom, together with other martyrs, in the year 177, under the emperor Marcus Aurelius, who reigned from 161 to 180, at Lyons in Gaul.

The page sets each of the nine in a head line of its own, with the same date against each of them, «(; - 177)», and it gathers the rest under one further heading which names nobody — the holy martyrs who bore witness with Saints Photinos and Sanctius. So the nine who are named have a folder each, which is what this corpus does with a synaxis that enumerates its members, and the unnamed have none, because there is nothing to count them by. The page gives no manner of death for any of them, no order of suffering, no place of burial and no hymn.

The other eight of the nine are [Photinos of Lyons](/saints/photinos-of-lyons), [Sanctius of Lyons](/saints/sanctius-of-lyons), [Vettius of Lyons](/saints/vettius-of-lyons), [Epagathus of Lyons](/saints/epagathus-of-lyons), [Ponticus of Lyons](/saints/ponticus-of-lyons), [Biblis of Lyons](/saints/biblis-of-lyons), [Attalus of Lyons](/saints/attalus-of-lyons) and [Alexander of Lyons](/saints/alexander-of-lyons). The same calendar keeps on this day a tenth martyr of Lyons of the same year and the same reign, Alcibiades, and gives him a separate entry and a separate page without numbering him among these nine; his own page wonders whether he is the Alcibiades kept on 16 August. That question belongs to his folder and not to this company.

Of Maturus the page says nothing but his name, his rank of martyr and the year 177. He is the last of the nine in the day's line, and the corpus keeps nobody else of the name.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 2 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/02/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4407/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
