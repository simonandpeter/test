# Sergius (Orlov)

The calendar’s own line is the whole of what this folder records of him. The Сретенский monastery’s calendar for 8 декабря ст. ст. closes the day with him, alone in a line of his own and the only saint of the twentieth century on a day of ancient ones: «Сщмч. Сергия пресвитера (1937).» The rank is «сщмч.», a hieromartyr; the grade is «пресвитера», a presbyter; and 1937 is the only year the calendar gives him.

The name page behind his place in the line gives the surname the line leaves out together with his grade in orders: «Сергий (Орлов), иерей, сщмч.», a priest, and that heading is the whole of what it says of him. It lists one other day, «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church; one row per church is this corpus’s schema, so that day is recorded on his feast and he does not render on it.

Behind that page there is no life, and no article stands under his name. Nothing is said here of where he was born, where he served, when or by whom he was arrested, or how and where he died in 1937, because no page read says any of it.

The day page carries four hymns, and all four stand under the names of two of the ancient and medieval saints of the same day.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 8 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261208.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14369.html); read 13 October 2026.*
