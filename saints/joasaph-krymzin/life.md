# Joasaph (Krymzin)

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him sixteenth of twenty-four and first of the five venerable-martyrs, and gives no life; the life here is the one azbyka.ru prints for the civil 2 декабря, out of fond.ru. Ivan Pavlovich Krymzin was born on 7 September 1880 in the village of Rumyantsevo in the Nizhny Novgorod district, in the peasant family of Pavel Ivanovich and Lyudmila Ivanovna Krymzin. He finished the elementary school there and in 1904 entered the Catherine hermitage of the Moscow diocese, where, by his own account, he got his spiritual education from the monastery’s services, from hearing the word of God and from learning to keep the commandments of the Gospel.

The way of monastic life was not easy in that house in those years. Founded in 1658 on the place where the great martyr Catherine appeared to the tsar Alexis Mikhailovich, by the beginning of the twentieth century it had become the place to which monks who had not kept their vows were sent from other monasteries to mend, and men under penance made up half of its inhabitants. He was enrolled among the brotherhood on 6 April 1909, tonsured with the name Joasaph on 9 January 1912, ordained hierodeacon that July and soon made warden of the monastery’s metochion in Moscow, and ordained hieromonk on 19 March 1917.

On 20 February 1918 the hermitage was handed to the Krasnostok convent, evacuated from the Grodno diocese in the first war, and its brotherhood moved to the Vysotsky monastery at Serpukhov; he asked instead to go to the Transfiguration Guslitsky monastery. There he kept the priestly and choir obediences from 1919 and the obedience of steward from 1921, until the authorities closed the house in 1929. From 1931 he served at the Nikitskaya church in the village of Byvalino in the Pavlovo-Posad district, and on 24 September 1935 he was raised abbot. The second priest of that parish was another former Guslitsky monk, the hieromonk Peter (Mamontov), who is the eighteenth name of the same day line.

He was arrested on 18 November 1937, put in the prison of the town of Noginsk and questioned on the 21st. Asked whether he admitted being arrested for systematically carrying on counter-revolutionary agitation against the measures of the party and the government, he answered: «Нет, я никогда никакой антисоветской агитацией не занимался и виновным себя не признаю», and he answered every other question the same way. On 25 November 1937 the troika of the NKVD sentenced him to be shot, and he was shot on 2 December 1937 and buried in a common unmarked grave at the Butovo range near Moscow.
