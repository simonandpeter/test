# Theion, with two children

saint.gr's calendar for 1 February keeps Theion together with two children, and its page says as much about what is unknown as about what is: it is unknown where and when Theion and the two boys bore witness, who were made perfect by the sword. That is the whole sentence, and there is nothing else on the page — no year, no reign, no city, no persecutor, no name for either child, and no apolytikion and no kontakion.

The two are not given a line of their own on the day and are not named anywhere on the page, so no separate folder is made for them here; what is recorded is that they were two, that they were children, and that they died with him. The couplet above the sentence puts the order the other way about: the children die readily by the sword, and Theion followed with all readiness.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/559/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
