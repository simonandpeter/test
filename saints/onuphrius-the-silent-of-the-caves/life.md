# Onuphrius the Silent of the Caves

saint.gr's whole notice for him is two sentences. The Russian ascetic, the venerable Onuphrius the silent one in the Cave, lived in the twelfth century after Christ; and we have no other details of his life. The calendar's line for 21 Ιουλίου says the same and no more, «Όσιος Ονούφριος ο σιωπηλός εν τω Σπηλαίω».

So what the folder holds is a name, an epithet, a house, a nation and a century. The page does not say which Cave it means or in what city, when in the century he lived, or how the silence was kept, and it prints no hymn for him.
