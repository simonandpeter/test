# Vendimianos

Vendimianos was born in the middle of the fifth century, saint.gr says, of noble and wealthy parents, in Great Mysia, the old country of north-western Asia Minor. He lived as a monk on the mountain called Oxeia and was a disciple of the venerable Auxentius «of the Hill», whom the same calendar keeps on 14 February.

After his teacher's death Vendimianos went into the cleft of a great rock, where he built a small cell and stayed forty-two whole years in the strictest asceticism; and the result of it, the page says, was that he won great victories over the demons. When he understood that the end of his life was near, in the year 512, he told the man who had written the life of the venerable Auxentius the things of his own life, and then knelt down and gave up his holy soul.

The page gives no year for his birth, and prints no apolytikion and no kontakion. Its couplet makes him a tree: Vendimianos, a great tree of virtue, planted in earth and carried over into heaven.
