# Paul of Ptolemais

Paul and his sister [Juliana](/saints/juliana-of-ptolemais) were put to death under the emperor Aurelian (270–275) in the Phoenician city of Ptolemais. When the emperor came to the city, Paul, standing among those who met him, made the sign of the cross, and was noticed; he was seized and thrown into prison, and when he was brought to trial the next day he confessed his faith in Christ openly and boldly, and was cruelly tortured. Juliana, seeing her brother suffer, began to accuse the emperor before everyone of injustice and cruelty, and was tortured too. They were beaten, torn with iron hooks and burned on red-hot grills, and their wonderful endurance could not be broken; three of the soldiers who tortured them, struck by the greatness of their spirit, believed in Christ — Quadratus, Acacius and Stratonicus — and died with them.

doxologia.ro keeps the two on 4 martie and prints a longer life of them. It says that Aurelian had given his order against the Christians throughout the world, and that he came through Assyria into Phoenicia and entered Ptolemais himself to force them to sacrifice; that Paul, seeing him come in, signed his face with the cross and said to his sister, «Îndrăznește, sora mea, și nu te teme» — take courage, sister, and do not be afraid, for a great trial is coming upon the Christians. After they were put to the sword their bodies were left unburied outside the city with soldiers watching from a distance, and for seven days and nights the dogs would not touch them, the wolves that came sat beside them as guards, and the carrion birds flew over without settling; when the guard was withdrawn by night the Christians came and buried them with honour.
