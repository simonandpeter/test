# Mary of Tarsus

The Ohrid Prologue, for the Serbian calendar, says that Mary lived a wholly dissolute life at Tarsus. Two monks travelling through the city put up at the inn where she lived in fornication, and when she came to them they rebuked her and pushed her away as unclean. At once she repented and vowed from that hour never to sin again; the monks took her and brought her to a women’s monastery, where she struggled into old age, and she was granted the working of wonders while she still lived. The Prologue gives no date.
