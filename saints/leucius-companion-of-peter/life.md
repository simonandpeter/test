# Leucius, companion of Peter and Severus

saint.gr's calendar for 11 Ιανουαρίου names three martyrs in one line, «Άγιοι Πέτρος, Σεβέριος και Λεύκιος οι Μάρτυρες», and the page behind the line has one sentence for all three: «Οι Άγιοι Πέτρος, Σεβέριος και Λεύκιος, μαρτύρησαν στην Αλεξάνδρεια» — [Peter](/saints/peter-companion-of-leucius), [Severus](/saints/severus-companion-of-leucius) and Leucius were martyred at Alexandria. Leucius is the last of the three names.

The page glosses his name beside the line, from the Latin lux, lucis: the bright one, the shining one. Beyond that it gives no emperor, no governor, no year, no manner of death and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/11/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1093/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
