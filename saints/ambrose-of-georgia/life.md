# Ambrose (Tselaia)

saint.gr says Ambrose, surnamed Tselaia, was born in the year 1861 in the village of Martvili, in the province of Samegrelo in Georgia. He studied at the ecclesiastical seminary of Tiflis and at the ecclesiastical academy of Kazan, and took up the monastic life at the monastery the page names as Seliski; in 1906 he became abbot of the monastery of St John the Baptist.

On the fourteenth of October 1921 he was elected Catholicos-Patriarch of Georgia, and shepherded his flock, the page says, in a manner pleasing to God. He fell asleep in peace on the twenty-seventh of March 1927 — a date which is not the day the Greek calendar keeps his memory on, and the site gives no reason for the difference.

That is the whole of what the page prints. It names no relic, gives no hymn and no distich, and says nothing of what those six years as patriarch cost him; nothing is added here that it does not print.
