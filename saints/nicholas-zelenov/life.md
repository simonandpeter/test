# Nicholas (Zelenov)

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him tenth of the fifteen, the last of the presbyters, and gives no life; the life here is the one azbyka.ru prints under his own name for the civil 3 декабря, out of fond.ru. He was born in 1887 in the hamlet of Бочевино in the Bronnitsy district of the province of Moscow, son of a peasant, Поликарп Зеленов. He sang in the church choir from 1897; out of the Vladimir seminary in 1907 he became a schoolmaster at a parish school in the Murom district; in 1911 he served as deacon in the village of Загарино in the same district, and in 1912 was ordained priest to a parish in another village of it, where he served until June 1917, when he was appointed to Загарино. He served there until 1926, was moved to the village of Марково in the Pokrov volost, and from 1928 until his first arrest served at Сеньго-Озеро in the Orekhovo-Zuyevo district. The villagers loved him and bought him a cow; he and his wife had no children and lived very modestly, owning almost nothing.

**What caused the first arrest was a newspaper article.** On 24 October 1929 the local paper «Колотушка» printed a piece headed «Поп агитирует против колхоза». An anti-religious lecturer who had come to the collective farm could not understand why, in a village of a hundred and fifty households, fewer than ten per cent belonged to it; the paper wrote that «немаловажную роль в борьбе с колхозами играет местный поп», who against the cultural work of the reading-room had organised a church choir in which the young people learned sacred chant to the sound of the priest’s violin. The cutting is filed with the case under a resolution of 26 October: «Завести дело и взять под стражу» — open a case and take him into custody.

He was arrested on 3 November 1929 and questioned the next day. He said that the whole village could see the collective farm’s mismanagement for itself — the harvest not got into store in time, the workers labouring unwillingly and slackly, not as they did on their own land — and that everybody knew it; and that the young people’s choir had not been founded by him at all but earlier, by the wife of the priest before him. «Виновным себя в провокации против колхозного строительства не признаю», he ended. The second charge was that he had refused to baptise an infant whose godmother belonged to the collective farm; the godmother’s own evidence refuted it, and the child’s father said the priest had at first refused because she had not yet taken the prayer of the fortieth day, and had baptised the child on learning that she had taken it in another church.

Of the gatherings in his house he said plainly: «Я понимаю, что, если бы я не устраивал у себя дома религиозных спевок, молодежь пошла бы на посиделки, так как в то время ни клуба, ни народного дома и избы-читальни в деревне не было… Виновным себя в агитации против колхозов я не признаю». During the investigation the parishioners petitioned for his release on bail, because they had no other priest and the other parishes were far off, and there were the newborn, the dead and the sick who needed the Christian rite; about a hundred people signed it, and it had no effect.

On 23 February 1930 the Special Conference of the OGPU Collegium sentenced him to three years in a camp. Having served it he was appointed to the Protection church of the village of Пустоши in the Shatura district, and in April 1933 petitioned the bishop of Kolomna for a vacancy in that vicariate and was appointed to the church of the Nativity of the Mother of God at Старынино in the Zaraysk district, where he served until his arrest. He was arrested on 22 November 1937 by the Zaraysk section of the NKVD and questioned the same day, answering each of four charges «нет, я это отрицаю»; he was held in the prison of the Kolomna district, and on 27 November 1937 the troika of the NKVD sentenced him to be shot. He was shot on 3 December 1937 at the Butovo range near Moscow and buried in a common unmarked grave.
