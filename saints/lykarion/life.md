# Lykarion

The Russian calendar's life for 6 февраля ст. ст. tells one story of three. The virgin martyrs [Martha](/saints/martha-sister-of-lykarion) and [Mary](/saints/mary-sister-of-lykarion) were sisters by blood, lived in Asia — Asia Minor, the life glosses — and burned with the desire to suffer for the Lord Jesus Christ. A pagan commander was passing their house one day, and the sisters came out to him and declared aloud that they were Christians.

He paid them no attention at first, but they went on insistently crying their confession after him; and then both of them were seized together with their brother, the boy Lykarion. All three were crucified, and their mother stood by at the execution and strengthened them in their suffering for Christ. The sisters were run through with spears, and the boy Lykarion was beheaded by the sword. The calendar's line calls him «прмч. Ликариона отрока», and gives no year for any of the three.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 6 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260206.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life404.htm); read 3 October 2026.*
