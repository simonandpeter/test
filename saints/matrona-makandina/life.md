# Matrona (Makandina)

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names her last of the six women it grades прмц. on the one line it gives to fourteen people of 1938 for the Julian 1 March, and prints no life for her. Matrona Alexeyevna Makandina was born in 1889 in the village of Konstantinovo in the Alexandrov district of the province of Vladimir, into a peasant family, and in 1910 she entered the Alexeyevsky monastery in Moscow as a novice. After the house was closed she lived in a lodging with other women of monasteries the godless power had shut, keeping the monastic order of life.

She was arrested on 28 December 1930 and held in the Butyrka prison in Moscow. At the interrogations she said that at first she had been indifferent to the Soviet power and then had come to think ill of it, because that power «закрывает монастыри и не дает служить Богу» — closes the monasteries and does not let one serve God — and that the old government had been better. On 8 February 1931 she was sentenced to three years’ exile in the Archangel region, which she served in the town of Pinega; at the end of the term, in 1934, she came home to Konstantinovo.

On 26 February 1938 she was arrested again, charged with systematic counter-revolutionary agitation and active propaganda, and admitted no guilt. On 8 March 1938 the troika of the НКВД for the Moscow region sentenced her to be shot for spreading counter-revolutionary rumours of the imminent fall of the Soviet power. She met her martyr’s end on 14 March 1938 at the Butovo range outside Moscow, and the page says she met it «вместе со своей сестрой инокиней Анной» — together with her sister, the nun [Anna (Makandina)](/saints/anna-makandina), whose own charge was the same rumour and whose sentence was passed the same day.

She was numbered with the new martyrs and confessors of Russia by a decision of the Holy Synod of the Russian Orthodox Church of 21 August 2007, which is seven years later than her sister’s glorification and is the one date on which the two pages part. Her monastic grade is azbyka.ru’s, «послушница», a novice, and the calendar’s name link gives her no monastic rank at all, only the grade прмц.; no office is written, because the corpus writes one where the hosts agree. azbyka.ru also attributes her page not to Игумен Дамаскин (Орловский), as it does the others of this line, but to hram-ks.ru.

Besides 14 марта her page keeps her on the moveable synaxis of the new martyrs who suffered at Butovo and the moveable synaxis of the saints of Moscow, and neither is a fixed day nor an offset from Pascha this corpus can hold.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 1 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260301.html), which names her on one line with thirteen others and prints no life under her own name; and the Russian church calendar at azbyka.ru for 14 марта — [the life](https://azbyka.ru/days/sv-matrona-makandina); read 5 October 2026.*
