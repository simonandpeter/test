# Eugene (Yakovlev)

The Сретенский monastery's calendar names him first of the two in «Сщмчч. Евгения и Михаила пресвитеров (1937)» and carries no life for him; azbyka.ru prints one out of fond.ru, signed by Игумен Дамаскин (Орловский). Евгений Никанорович Яковлев was born in 1856, and in the nineteen-thirties was serving as a priest in the city of Горький. Of his parents, his schooling and his ordination the page says nothing, and nothing is added here.

He was arrested in November 1937 with a large group of clergy — among them, the page says, the hieromartyr priest Василий Завгородний and the martyr Анисия Масланова, a church warden — on the investigation file of Александр (Похвалинский), archbishop of Богородск and vicar of the Горький diocese. The charge was taking part in a «контрреволюционной церковной повстанческой организации». Under investigation he refused to sign the protocols and refused to admit any guilt.

On 2 December of the same year the special troika of the NKVD administration for the Горький region sentenced him to be shot. He was executed on 7 December 1937 and buried in an unmarked common grave in the Бугровское cemetery of the city of Горький. He was glorified by the jubilee council of bishops of the Russian Church in 2000. The second priest the calendar's line names beside him served in another province and the pages read connect the two men in nothing but the line itself, so nothing is said here of him.
