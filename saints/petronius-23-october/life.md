# Petronius (23 October)

doxologia.ro's calendar names Petronius for 23 octombrie and gives no life. His page has no Viață tab and no Tropar tab; it prints the day, the appointed Epistle and Gospel — Galatians on the fruit of the Spirit, and Matthew, Come unto me, all ye that labour — and one sentence of notice.

That sentence is: «Sfântul Petroniu a fost, probabil, un ascet al deșertului, care s-a mutat la Domnul în pace» — Saint Petronius was, probably, a desert ascetic, who passed to the Lord in peace. The word "probably" is the source's own, and it is left standing here rather than tidied away.

So nothing is recorded of him beyond the name, the day, and a guess about the kind of life he led: no place, no century, no companions, no wonders, and no hymn.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 23 October, and saint.gr says no more than doxologia did, with the same hedge: «Ο Όσιος Πετρώνιος ήταν μάλλον ασκητής της ερήμου, που απεβίωσε ειρηνικά» — Petronius was probably an ascetic of the desert, who died in peace. What the Greek page adds is a couplet, which plays on the stone in his name: Moses, sheltered by a rock, sees God; and Petronius too, standing upon an immaterial rock. It gives no year, no desert, no companions and no hymn.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 23 octombrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/23-octombrie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-petroniu), which prints no life; read 24 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 23 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/23/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2788/saint.aspx), read 1 October 2026.*
