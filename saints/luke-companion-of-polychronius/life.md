# Luke, companion of Polychronius

saint.gr's calendar for 7 Οκτωβρίου prints one line for a company of ten, «Άγιοι Δέκα συναθλητές του Αγίου Πολυχρονίου» — the ten fellow contenders of Saint Polychronius — and the page behind that line names each of them. The Polychronius they are gathered around is [Polychronius](/saints/polychronius-the-martyr) the presbyter, whom Arians cut down upon the holy table while he was serving, in the days of the Arian emperor Constantius.

The ten the page names are [Parmenias](/saints/parmenias-companion-of-polychronius), [Polyteleios](/saints/polyteleios-companion-of-polychronius), [Elymas](/saints/elymas-companion-of-polychronius), [Mokios](/saints/mokios-companion-of-polychronius), [Chrysotelis](/saints/chrysotelis-companion-of-polychronius), [Maximus](/saints/maximus-companion-of-polychronius), Luke, [Abdias](/saints/abdias-companion-of-polychronius), [Semnios](/saints/semnios-companion-of-polychronius) and [Olympiadas](/saints/olympiadas-companion-of-polychronius). Of their lives it says only «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον Βίο των Αγίων» — we have no details of the life of the saints. Luke is the seventh name in that list.

That is the whole of the record. saint.gr gives the company no year, no place and no manner of death, and prints no hymn for them; the fourth century it states for Polychronius is his own and is not extended to his companions here. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/07/index.aspx) and [the life the ten share](https://www.saint.gr/2700/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
