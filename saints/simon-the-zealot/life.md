# Simon the Zealot

This apostle was of Cana of Galilee by descent, known to our Lord Jesus Christ and to his most pure Mother, since Cana is not far from Nazareth. When Simon held his lawful wedding, Jesus was called there with his disciples, and when the wine ran short the Lord turned the water into wine. Seeing such a wonder, the bridegroom at once believed that the Lord was the true God, and leaving his wedding and his house he followed him eagerly; for which he was called "the Zealot", that is, the ardent one.

He was kindled with so great a divine love for Christ, the life says, that he overlooked his bride and all worldly things for the love of God, and made his own soul a bride to the deathless Bridegroom, being numbered among the disciples of Christ and being one of the twelve apostles.

After he had received the Most Holy Spirit with the other apostles, who came down in the form of tongues of fire, he went over into Mauritania and into Africa, preaching Christ and enduring for him with a burning zeal. Afterwards, being in Britain and enlightening many by the word of the Gospel, he was crucified by the unbelievers and ended his life by a suffering of one form with Christ's. The page gives no year.
