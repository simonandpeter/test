# Paulinus of Athens

The Romanian calendar names Paulinus for 18 mai and sends the reader to the life it prints for the martyrs of this day, where he appears in the closing paragraph and nowhere else. The martyrs Heraclius, Paulinus and Benedimus, it says, were citizens of Athens, and there they preached Christ with much boldness, urging all the pagans to leave off the empty service of idols and to be baptised in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit.

For this they were seized and handed over to the ruler of Athens. Under examination they confessed the true God, the Maker of every creature, and called the idols insensible stones and wood and things made by men's hands. They were tormented with many kinds of torment; and at the last they were thrown into a heated furnace, and so died and received the incorruptible crowns from Christ our God.

That is the whole of what the page says about him. It gives no year; the reign of Decius which it names belongs to the martyrs of Lampsacus earlier in the same life, and is not stated of the three Athenians, so no date is recorded here. Heraclius and Benedimus, who died with him, are not named in the Romanian calendar's list for the day and so have no folder of their own here.
