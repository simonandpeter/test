# Jonah (Lazarev)

He stands first on the second of the two company lines the Russian 8 October prints, «Сщмчч. Ионы , еп. Велижского, прмч. Серафима , сщмчч. Петра , Василия , Павла , Петра , Владимира пресвитеров, мчч. Виктора , Иоанна , Николая и мц. Елисаветы (1937)», and he is the one of the eleven the site carries a life for. He was Ivan Ivanovich Lazarev in the world, born in 1869 in the village of Luchino in St Petersburg province, in a priest's family. He finished the Novgorod seminary in 1892 and was made a supervisor at the Zvenigorod church school. His years at Novgorod were the years when its rector was the archimandrite Tikhon (Nikanorov), later archbishop of Voronezh, who was himself put to death by the militant godless in 1919.

He was tonsured on 4 November 1892 with the name Jonah and ordained hierodeacon on 3 December of the same year. On 23 December 1893 he was enrolled in the brotherhood of the Savva-Storozhevsky monastery and on 6 January 1895 ordained hieromonk. At the end of 1899, at the petition of that same Tikhon, by then bishop of Polotsk, he was enrolled in the Polotsk bishop's house, and in January 1900 made its treasurer and sacristan. In November 1900 he was made superior of the Transfiguration monastery at Nevel, and in September 1902 moved to the Skovorodsky monastery at Novgorod, and a year after that raised to archimandrite.

In 1907 bishop Tikhon was living in the stauropegial New Jerusalem monastery of the Resurrection as its superior, and before leaving it for a see of his own he proposed to the Synod that the archimandrite Jonah, whom he had long known and well, should be put in his place; on 22 March 1911 Jonah was appointed to it. In January 1918 he was sent to the disposal of Tikhon, now archbishop of Voronezh and Zadonsk.

On 29 January 1926 he was consecrated bishop of Nevel and vicar of the Vitebsk diocese, and for reasons the page calls independent of him he was never able to take up the office. In May of the same year he was retired, with the blessing of the deputy locum tenens metropolitan Sergius to live in the village of Batyushkovo in the Dmitrov district of the Moscow region; living there he served in the church of St Nicholas in that village. Many honoured him for the gift of discernment and came to him for spiritual direction.

In the thirties a system of corrective labour camps was built across the Dmitrov district, and the prisoners of them dug the canals and the locks. Among those prisoners were a great many clergy, and the bishop sent help in to the priests who were held there, through prisoners who had free exit from the camp. In the persecution of 1937 he was arrested on the charge of carrying on counter-revolutionary agitation under the guise of church sermons, and under interrogation he admitted no guilt. On 17 October the NKVD troika sentenced him to be shot; he was shot on 21 October 1937 and buried in an unmarked grave. He was numbered among the new martyrs and confessors of Russia for church-wide veneration at the Jubilee Council of Bishops in August 2000.

The site does not agree with itself about his see. The day line and his index entry both call him bishop of Velizh; the life calls him bishop of Nevel and vicar of Vitebsk, and names Nevel again inside the text, at the consecration and at the monastery he had led there a quarter of a century before. This folder follows the life and records the day line's reading rather than settling it.
