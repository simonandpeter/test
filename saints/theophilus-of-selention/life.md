# Theophilus of Selention

saint.gr's calendar for 10 Οκτωβρίου keeps Theophilus the Confessor together with [Longinus the Stylite](/saints/longinus-the-stylite), and gives this Theophilus a life of his own. He came, the page says, from a village near Tiberioupolis, and was brought up on the right principles of the faith. While still young he visited the monk Stephen, who widened his spiritual and intellectual horizons; and with the help of his parents, who were devout people, he built a monastery on Mount Selention, where he lived as a monk.

In the time when Leo the Isaurian reigned, who made war on Orthodoxy, Theophilus judged that he had to be at the centre of the battle. So, leaving his skete, he began to preach for Orthodoxy; and for that work he was imprisoned at Nicaea and pressed with beatings and other torments to deny his faith. Keeping the flame of faith unquenched, he submitted to the torment of the rods. Bearing the honourable marks of that witness he returned to his monastery, where he was received with honours; but he withdrew again to his skete, and there gave up his spirit to the Lord.

The same calendar keeps another Theophilus the Confessor, also under Leo the Isaurian, on 2 Οκτωβρίου: the corpus's [Theophilus the Confessor](/saints/theophilus-the-confessor), whose entry has him going about Constantinople, imprisoned, exiled, and dying in exile. The two are separate entries on separate day pages with separate lives, and the settled rule of this corpus is that two entries on one calendar are two commemorations; this folder is the Theophilus of Mount Selention. saint.gr gives him no year and prints no hymn. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/10/index.aspx) and [the life he shares with Longinus](https://www.saint.gr/2708/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
