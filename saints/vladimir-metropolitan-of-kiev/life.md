# Vladimir of Kiev

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its page for him on 25 ianuarie carries the line «Sfântul Vladimir, Mitropolitul Kievului» — Saint Vladimir, Metropolitan of Kiev — together with the readings appointed for a hierarch's feast and a set of short articles drawn from his own writings on the bringing up of children. There is no account of his life on the page, no year of birth or of death, no place beyond the see itself, and neither troparion nor kontakion.

So what is set down here is the whole of what was read: that the Romanian church keeps on the twenty-fifth of January a saint named Vladimir who was metropolitan of Kiev, and that doxologia.ro prints words of his about parents and children and about the years in which a child is formed. The Russian calendar for the corresponding day is where this entry should be finished, and it was not consulted.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same twenty-fifth of January, and saint.gr's page carries the life doxologia.ro's did not. He was born on 1 January 1848 in the village of Malye Moroski in the province of Tambov, and his name in the world was Vasily Nikiforovich Bogoyavlensky; he studied at the theological school of Kiev, taught at the church school of Tambov and married there, and was ordained presbyter in 1882. His wife died in 1886 and his only child soon after, and he was tonsured a monk with the name Vladimir. He was made bishop of Staraya Russa in 1888, archbishop of Samara in 1891 and of Kartli and Kakheti in 1892, metropolitan of Moscow in 1898 and of Saint Petersburg in 1912, and was given Kiev in 1915. Deposed by the Ukrainian council for refusing to declare an ecclesiastical autonomy, he took refuge in the monastery of the Caves, where on 23 January 1918 revolutionaries seized him and shot him; the one thing he asked for was a little time to pray.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 25 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/25-ianuarie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-vladimir-mitropolitul-kievului), which prints no life; read 19 September 2026; and after saint.gr's calendar for 25 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/25/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1162/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026.*
