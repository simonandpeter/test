# Vladimir of Kiev

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its page for him on 25 ianuarie carries the line «Sfântul Vladimir, Mitropolitul Kievului» — Saint Vladimir, Metropolitan of Kiev — together with the readings appointed for a hierarch's feast and a set of short articles drawn from his own writings on the bringing up of children. There is no account of his life on the page, no year of birth or of death, no place beyond the see itself, and neither troparion nor kontakion.

So what is set down here is the whole of what was read: that the Romanian church keeps on the twenty-fifth of January a saint named Vladimir who was metropolitan of Kiev, and that doxologia.ro prints words of his about parents and children and about the years in which a child is formed. The Russian calendar for the corresponding day is where this entry should be finished, and it was not consulted.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 25 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/25-ianuarie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-vladimir-mitropolitul-kievului), which prints no life; read 19 September 2026.*
