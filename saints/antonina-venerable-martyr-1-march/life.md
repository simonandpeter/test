# Antonina (1 March)

saint.gr names the venerable martyr Antonina on this day and gives her one sentence, and the life says exactly that: «Η Αγία Οσιομάρτυς Αντωνίνα μαρτύρησε το έτος 1924 μ.Χ. Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο της Αγίας» — the holy venerable martyr Antonina was martyred in the year 1924, and we have no other details of the saint's life. The rank word Ὁσιομάρτυς is the one thing besides the year that the line carries, and it says she was a monastic.

There is no country, no city, no monastery and no name of a house, no account of the arrest or of the death, no relic, no distich and no hymn, and the page gives no year for her birth. Nothing further is written here than the site prints, and the site prints one sentence.

The same Greek day keeps [Antonina of Nicaea](/saints/antonina-of-nicaea), drowned in the lake of Nicaea under Maximian, on a page of her own. Two entries on one calendar are two commemorations, and sixteen centuries stand between the two years saint.gr gives them, so the two are not the same woman; the corpus's three other Antoninas — of Crodamna, of Nicomedia, of Tismana — stand on days of their own.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3788/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
