# Nicholas (Shumkov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery gives Nicholas an entry of his own on 8 August, and no life. Its line is «Сщмч. Николая пресвитера (1937).» — the hieromartyr Nicholas the presbyter, 1937 — and the calendar's own name page for him adds the surname the line leaves out: «Николай (Шумков), иерей, сщмч.», Nicholas Shumkov, priest.

The day prints two priests of this name and they are a different pair of years apart: the entry before his keeps a Nicholas who died in 1930, and his own line says 1937. The surname on the name page is what tells them apart, and it is why this folder carries it.

That is the whole of the record. He was a priest; he died in 1937; the calendar keeps him on this day, within the afterfeast of the Transfiguration, among the new martyrs of Russia. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 8 августа ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260808.html); read 13 October 2026.*
