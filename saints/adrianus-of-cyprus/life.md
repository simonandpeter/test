# Adrianus, companion of Gregory of Constantia

saint.gr names Adrianus on this day only beside [Gregory of Constantia](/saints/gregory-of-constantia), and the whole of what it has for either of them is one manuscript entry. The memory of Gregory, the page says, is mentioned in the Patmos Codex 266 in these words: «Τῶν Ὁσίων Πατέρων ἠμῶν Γρηγορίου Κωνσταντίας τῆς Κύπρου [καί] Ἀδριανοῦ» — of our venerable fathers Gregory of Constantia in Cyprus and Adrianus. That is his whole attestation: a name in a list of two, under the word venerable.

The page then adds a guess of its own: «Ίσως ο Άγιος Αδριανός να ήταν και αυτός Επίσκοπος στην Κύπρο» — perhaps St Adrianus too was a bishop in Cyprus. Because that is the site's perhaps and not its statement, no see is written into this folder and the rank of bishop is left out of it; the word the codex uses, venerable father, is the one kept.

There is no century here, no country of his own, no monastery, no deed, no manner of repose, no relic and no hymn, and the distich the page prints is over his companion and not over him. The etymology block on the name lists a Roman emperor, a Jewish sophist of Phoenicia and a Byzantine admiral; all three are notes on the word and none is this man.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 4 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/04/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3793/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
