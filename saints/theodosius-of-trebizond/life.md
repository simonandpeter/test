# Theodosius of Trebizond

Theodosius came from the village of Korissos near Kastoria, and was born of devout parents. He was the brother of [Dionysius of Athos](/saints/dionysius-of-athos), whom the calendar keeps on 25 June and who founded the skete of the Honourable Forerunner on the Holy Mountain. Theodosius himself became a monk at the monastery of Philotheou on Athos, and when the abbot there fell asleep he was made abbot in his place.

One day Turkish pirates fell upon the monastery's shore and carried him off as a captive into Bithynia. He contrived to get free, and came to Constantinople; and at the urging of the patriarch Callistus, to whom he was bound in the things of the spirit, he became abbot of the famous monastery of Saint George of the Manganae.

For his virtue and for the venerable manner of his life he was elected bishop of Trebizond in the year 1370. Theodosius fell asleep in peace about the year 1392. The page prints for him the couplet that calls his a double crown, as hierarch and as a venerable man of the Lord, and no hymn.
