# Anastasius (25 October)

saint.gr's calendar for 25 Οκτωβρίου names Anastasius and gives him a short notice without a name of place or a year. This martyr presented himself of his own will before the tyrants, the page says, and confessed Christ with courage; and because he stayed firm in that confession they beheaded him and threw him into the sea. The couplet above asks what it could write for him beyond the grace of Christ, for whose sake he hurried to die by the sword.

Then a pious woman, when the sea gave the body up, took the honoured relic and buried it with honours, and built a church there in the saint's name; and his relic, by the grace of God, worked many wonders. The page names neither the tyrants nor the woman nor the place of the church, gives no year, and prints no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 25 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/25/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2801/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
