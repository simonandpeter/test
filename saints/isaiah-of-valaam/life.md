# Isaiah of Valaam

saint.gr's calendar names him in one sentence and gives no more: the venerable Isaiah lived his ascetic life in the monastery of Valaam in Finland and fell asleep in peace in the year 1914. The page adds only a note on the name, that Isaiah is a Hebrew word meaning the saviour who comes from God. It gives him no birthplace, no other detail and no hymn, and nothing is supplied here. He is the second of the ascetics of Valaam the Greek calendar keeps in these days: it has another, Nicholas, on 6 January.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/126/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
