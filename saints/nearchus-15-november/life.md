# Nearchus (15 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 15 November keeps three martyrs together, [Eupsychius](/saints/eupsychius-15-november), Nearchus and [Carterius](/saints/carterius-15-november), and says of them only where it found them. The memory of these three martyrs, the page says, is recorded in the Paris codex 1578 and in the Patmos codex 266, together with their company; and we have no further information about their life.

That is the whole of the source. It gives the three no country, no reign, no year, no manner of death and no rank beyond martyr, and it prints no hymn for them. Of the company the two codices keep with them it names not one person, so the company has no folder here and only these three, whom the line does name, do.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 15 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/15/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2919/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
