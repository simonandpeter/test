# Cornutus, ascetic of Acheras in Cyprus

Cornutus — Kournoutas or Cornutius — is one of the three hundred saints who came from Palestine to Cyprus, one of the Heliophotoi; he was much honoured in Cyprus, as is gathered from the many ruined churches, especially in Paphos, in villages such as Geroskipou, Salamiou, Souskiou and Polemi. An old icon inscribed Saint Cornutus shows him as a bishop, but what is surer is that he lived as a monk and ascetic in various places. The Greek calendar keeps him on 12 September.
