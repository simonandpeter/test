# Andrew (Kosovsky)

The calendar’s own line is the whole of what is recorded of him here. The Сретенский monastery’s calendar for 3 декабря ст. ст. commemorates him alone, in a few words and a year: «Сщмч. Андрея пресвитера (1920).» It is the first of the day’s three twentieth-century clauses and the only saint on this day dated 1920.

The name page behind his place gives him the surname the line leaves out together with his grade: «Андрей (Косовский), протоиерей, сщмч.». Behind that page there is no life, and the day page carries no hymn under his name; its six hymns all stand under the names of the ancient saints of the same day.

So nothing is said here of where he was born, where he served, when or by whom he was arrested, or how and where he died. No page read says any of it, and the year 1920 is the year the line closes with.

His name page lists one other day, «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church. One row per church is the schema, so that day is recorded here and he does not render on it.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 3 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261203.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9281.html); read 7 October 2026.*
