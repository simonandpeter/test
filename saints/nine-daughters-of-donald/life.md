# The Nine Daughters of Donald

saint.gr's calendar for 15 July keeps nine sisters whose names it does not give: the nine daughters of [Donald](/saints/donald-of-scotland), who stand on the day's line beside their father and have a heading of their own on his page. What it says of them is short. After their mother's death they and their father took up an ascetic life, working the land with their own hands, eating very little bread and drinking water once a day.

After his repose the nine went to live in a monastery at Abernethy, and there they became known as the Nine Maidens, or the Nine Holy Virgins. That is the whole of the notice. The page gives no year for any of them, no name for any one of the nine, no account of how they died and no hymn, and nothing has been added here from anywhere else.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 15 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/15/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4234/saint.aspx), which they share with their father; read 1 October 2026.*
