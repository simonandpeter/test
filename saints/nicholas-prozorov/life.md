# Nicholas (Prozorov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Nicholas first of a pair of priests on 8 August and gives no life for either of them. Its line is «Сщмчч. Николая и Сергия пресвитеров (1930).» — the hieromartyrs Nicholas and Sergius, presbyters, 1930 — and the calendar's own name page for him adds the surname the line leaves out: «Николай (Прозоров), иерей, сщмч.», Nicholas Prozorov, priest.

That is the whole of the record. He was a priest; he died in 1930; the calendar keeps him on this day, within the afterfeast of the Transfiguration, among the new martyrs of Russia. The day page carries a kontakion for the hierarch it heads, a service for a translation of relics and three hymns before an icon, and nothing under his name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 8 августа ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260808.html); read 13 October 2026.*
