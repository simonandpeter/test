# Andronicus of Atroa

Andronicus “in Atroa” is mentioned in Delehaye’s synaxarion with a notice like that of Peter “in Atroa”, kept on 13 September. He lived in the reigns of Nicephorus and Stauracius and the patriarchate of Tarasius, the son of Cosmas and Anna, from Elaea in Asia; he lived in strict ascesis at Atroa and died in peace. The Greek calendar keeps him on 12 September.
