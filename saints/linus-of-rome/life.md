# Linus of Rome

Linus is kept on 5 Νοεμβρίου with four other apostles of the Seventy, and he is the one of the five whom saint.gr names out of the second letter to Timothy rather than out of the letter to the Romans. The page calls all five of them true shepherds of the rational flock of the Church. Of Linus it says that he was shown forth as the first bishop of Rome, that he shepherded the church there with foresight and daring, and that he was martyred by beheading.

His episcopate, the page says, lasted eleven years and three months. That is the whole of what is told of him: no year, no emperor, no place of burial, and no hymn. The couplet the Greek books give him plays on his name, which they read as a song and a net: «Καθείς, Ἰησοῦς γνώσεως θείας λίνον, / Ζωγρεῖ σε, Λῖνε, καὶ μεθιστᾷ τοῦ βίου» — letting down the net of divine knowledge, Jesus takes you alive, Linus, and removes you from this life.

Those kept with him are [Hermas](/saints/hermas-5-november), [Patrobas](/saints/patrobas), [Gaius](/saints/gaius-of-ephesus) and [Philologus](/saints/philologus-of-sinope).

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2889/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
