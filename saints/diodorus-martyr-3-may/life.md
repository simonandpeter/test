# Diodorus (3 May)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 3 mai reads «Sfântul Mucenic Diodor», and the page that line points to carries the day, the epistle appointed for a martyr and the gospel read with it, and nothing else — no country, no century, no manner of death, no hymn.

The same calendar prints «Sfântul Mucenic Rodopian, diaconul» on the same day, and his page is as bare. The two names are set down here as the calendar sets them down, without the claim that they suffered together, which the source does not make.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and pairs him with the same deacon, and saint.gr adds the city and the reign. These saints lived in the years of the emperor Diocletian, it says, giving the year as 302, and for their faith in Christ they endured a great deal of abuse and scourging from the soldiers of the city of Aphrodisias in Caria — a country of Asia Minor, the page explains, which occupied its south-western corner, opposite the stretch of sea between the islands of Samos and Rhodes. In the end they were stoned by those same soldiers, and so gave up their souls to God who gives the crown. The one man the page names beside him is [Rodopianus the deacon](/saints/rodopianus), and its couplet makes the stones the gift: to Rodopianus and Diodorus the striking stones were roses, or rather gifts.

The Russian calendar keeps the two of them on 29 апреля where the Greek and the Romanian keep them on 3 May, and its account is four lines: «Святые мученики Диодор и Родопиан диакон пострадали при императоре Диоклетиане (284–305) в Афродисии Карийской. За распространение христианской веры между язычниками они были побиты камнями» — they suffered under Diocletian at Aphrodisias in Caria, and were stoned for spreading the Christian faith among the heathen. It names the province where the Greek page named only the city, and it gives the reign rather than a year, so the year this folder already carries stands.
