# Joseph the Wonderworker (of Volok)

The Greek synaxarion keeps on 9 September the venerable Joseph the wonderworker “in Velon” — the Russian Joseph whom its spelling points at, of Volokolamsk, kept the same day of the Russian reckoning outside the weeks read here — and says it has no details of his life. This entry records the line and nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 9 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/989/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
