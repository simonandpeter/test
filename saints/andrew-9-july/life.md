# Andrew (9 July)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 9 iulie reads «Sfântul Mucenic Andrei», and the page the calendar links under that name carries the name again and nothing more: no life, no troparion, no year, no place, no companions. What the Romanian calendar prints of him on this day is that he is a martyr and that he is called Andrei, and this folder says no more than that.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 9 July, and names the two together as the Romanian one does: «Άγιοι Ανδρέας και Πρόβος». saint.gr's whole notice of them is one sentence — that the holy Andrew and [Probus](/saints/probus-9-july) were martyred by fire. Above it the page prints a distich, «Εστώτες εις το σκάμμα του πυρός Λόγε, Aύραις ανεψύχοντο σαις άνδρες δύω»: standing in the pit of the fire, O Word, the two men were cooled by your breezes. The page gives no country, no century and no year, tells nothing of their trial, and prints no hymn.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 9 iulie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/9-iulie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-andrei-0), which prints the name only; read 24 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 9 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/09/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2014/saint.aspx), read 1 October 2026.*
