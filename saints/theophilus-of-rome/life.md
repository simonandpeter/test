# Theophilus of Rome

saint.gr's calendar for 28 Φεβρουαρίου names four martyrs in one line — «Άγιοι Μακάριος, Ρουφίνος, Ιούστος και Θεόφιλος οι Μάρτυρες» — and the page standing under it gives one sentence: «Οι Άγιοι αυτοί μαρτύρησαν στη Ρώμη το έτος 250 μ.Χ. επί αυτοκράτορα Δεκίου (249 - 251 μ.Χ)» — these saints were martyred at Rome in the year 250, under the emperor Decius, whose reign the page gives as 249 to 251. Because the line names each of the four, each is kept here: Theophilus, with [Macarius](/saints/macarius-of-rome), [Rufinus](/saints/rufinus-of-rome) and [Justus](/saints/justus-of-rome).

That is the whole of the entry. The page prints the four names again above the sentence, each with its own rank word, and gives them no account of their suffering, no manner of death, no governor, no relics, no couplet and no hymn; Rome is the only thing in it besides the names and the year. The epithet in this folder's name is that place, because the calendar gives these men nothing else to tell them apart from the others of their names.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/28/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1936/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
