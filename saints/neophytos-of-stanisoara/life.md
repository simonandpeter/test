# Neophytos of Stânișoara

Neophytos mortified his body with every kind of hermit’s labour and adorned himself with many virtues — unfeigned love of God, joy in a blameless conscience, peace after the victory over every passion, long patience in temptations and troubles, and a meekness that knew no anger: a man truly honourable and wonderful, the Romanian life says, whose goodness cannot be shown in few words, who drew others to the same life. The Romanian calendar keeps him on 3 September with [Meletius](/saints/meletius-of-stanisoara), the two hermits of the monastery of Stânișoara, with a common troparion and kontakion.

*After the Viețile Sfinților as printed by doxologia.ro — [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-cuvios-neofit-de-la-manastirea-stanisoara); read 23 August 2026.*
