# Celerinus of Carthage

Celerinus came from Africa. During the persecution of the Christians under the emperor Decius, whose reign saint.gr puts at 249 to 251, he was at Rome, where he was arrested and thrown into a dark prison; but he was released from it and came back to Carthage. There St Cyprian ordained him deacon.

Cornelius, Pope of Rome — 251 to 253, the page says — and the blessed Augustine both praise him. When he fell asleep is not known. He is honoured as a martyr, the page explains, for what he suffered at Rome, and not for a death by violence; and saint.gr prints no hymn for him and no verses.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/03/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1204/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
