# Dositheus the Obedient

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives one sentence: the venerable Dositheus the obedient lived the ascetic life in the community of Abba [Seridos](/saints/seridos-of-gaza), which was under the guidance of Saint [Dorotheus](/saints/dorotheus-of-gaza), and he ended his days in peace. The Romanian calendar keeps the two of them together on 13 august, the disciple beside his teacher, and keeps Seridos's own house in the same line.

That is the whole of what the page prints: no year, no country beyond the Gaza of Dorotheus's own entry, no account of the obedience the epithet remembers him for. The Greek, Russian and Serbian calendars were not consulted for him, and their rows say so.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and says the same small thing. saint.gr's whole notice is that the venerable Dositheos was the disciple of abba [Dorotheos](/saints/dorotheus-of-gaza) — «βλέπε ίδια ημέρα», see the same day, the page adds, for the Greek calendar keeps the two of them together on 13 August — that he too proved himself in the ascetic struggle, and that having lived after his elder's example he died in peace. The couplet printed above it calls him a copy-line of submission and the ornament of the obedient: «Tης υποταγής υπογραμμός ωράθης, / Δοσίθεε αγλάισμα των υπηκόων». No year, no century and no hymn are given.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 13 august — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/13-august) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-cuvios-dositei); read 24 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 13 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/13/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/972/saint.aspx), read 1 October 2026.*
