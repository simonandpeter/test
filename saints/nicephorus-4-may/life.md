# Nicephorus (4 May)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 4 mai reads «Sfântul Cuvios Nichifor» — Nicephorus the venerable — and his page on that site opens no Viață tab: it carries icons, a list of articles about him, and the epistle and gospel appointed for the day, and no text of its own.

So the rank is all that is recorded: a monk, kept by the Romanian calendar on this day. No century, no country, no monastery and no hymn is printed, and none is set down here.
