# Romanus of Tarnovo

saint.gr names him and gives him one sentence. Its calendar for 17 February prints «Όσιος Ρωμανός ο εκ Τυρνόβου», the venerable Romanus who was from Tarnovo, and the page behind that line says that the venerable Romanus the hesychast came from Tarnovo in Bulgaria, lived in the fourteenth century after Christ, and fell asleep in peace about the year 1370.

That, with the reading of the name, is the whole of the entry: the page glosses Ρωμανός either from ῥώμη, strength, as the robust and brave man, or as the Byzantine word for a Roman, the name of many emperors. It gives him no monastery, no teacher, no disciple, no relics, no couplet and no hymn; what it calls him is a hesychast, and where it puts him is Tarnovo.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 17 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/17/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2389/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
