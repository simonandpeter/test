# Michael (Berezin)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names the hieromartyr Michael on 31 December and gives no life. Its line is «Сщмч. Михаила пресвитера (1937).» — the hieromartyr Michael the presbyter, 1937 — and the calendar’s own name page for him carries the surname the line leaves out: «Михаил (Березин), иерей, сщмч.»

That is the whole of the record. He was a priest; the year of death the line gives is 1937; the calendar keeps him on this day, the giving-back of the feast of the Nativity and the last day of the church year’s December. The day page prints one hymn only, a kontakion of the venerable Melania, and nothing under this name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 31 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261231.html); read 13 October 2026.*
