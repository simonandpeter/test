# Caluphus the Egyptian

The Russian calendar's notice of him is a single paragraph and it is all ordeal. He lived in the third century and came from the city of Thebes in Egypt, which is why the calendar calls him the Egyptian; and for confessing the faith of Christ he was seized and brought before the governor of the city. At the governor's order he was hung head downwards with a heavy stone about his neck and cruelly beaten, and the page records what he said under it, over and over: I bear it all for the hope of blessedness in the life to come.

Then he was untied and pressed to sacrifice to the idols, and would not agree. At the last he was thrown into a fire and met his martyr's end in it. The calendar puts this in the year 303, in the persecution of the emperor Maximian Herculius, the co-ruler of Diocletian. It gives him no age, no family, no companions and nothing of his relics.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 19 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260519.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1105.htm); read 8 October 2026.*
