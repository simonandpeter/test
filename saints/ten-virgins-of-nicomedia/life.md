# The Ten Virgins of Nicomedia

saint.gr's whole account of them is one sentence: «Οι Άγιες Δέκα Παρθένες μαρτύρησαν στη Νικομήδεια (ίσως επί Μαξιμιανού (286 - 305 μ.Χ.), τότε που πλήθος χριστιανών μαρτύρησε σ' αυτή την πόλη)» — the holy Ten Virgins were martyred at Nicomedia, perhaps under Maximian, whose years it gives as 286 to 305, at the time when a multitude of Christians was martyred in that city. It names not one of them, and this folder therefore stands for the company and not for ten women.

It tells their death in the same breath: they had their eyes put out, and then their bodies were flayed, and so they gave up their spirits to God. The couplet set over the entry counts them by that double torment: «Διπλούν τον άθλον τρήσιν είτα και ξέσιν, / Xορού γινώσκω διπλοπενταπαρθένου» — I know a double contest, a piercing and then a scraping, of a choir of twice five virgins. The page gives no year that it will stand behind, no judge, and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 31 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/31/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1508/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
