# Basil of Poiana Mărului

Basil was born of believing parents in 1692, in the Russian lands. Loving the virtuous life from his youth, he left the world and spent a long while as a hermit, first in the sketes of Russia and then in the Moșenschi hills near Kiev, living there with a divine zeal and going often, for his soul's profit, to the monks given to prayer and to other hermits tried in the inward work.

When a persecution rose in those parts against the Orthodox faith and against monks above all, he left his homeland together with his disciple Michael the hieroschemamonk and came into Wallachia. He settled at the monastery of Dălhăuți and was before long chosen its superior, guiding that house with wisdom and gentleness for nearly twenty years.

Wishing afterwards for a quieter place, he went with twelve of his disciples to the monastery of Poiana Mărului in the mountains of Buzău, and became the guide of the monastic life of that whole countryside; under his direction came the monks of the sketes of Trăișteni, Cârnu, Rătești, Ciolanu, Bontești, Pometul, Rogozu, Cotești, Găvanu and Valea Neagră, set in the mountains of Buzău and Vrancea.

The life says he surpassed beyond compare all the fathers of his time in the understanding of Scripture, in the teaching of the God-bearing Fathers, in spiritual wisdom, and in a full knowledge of the dogmas and canons of the Eastern Church and their right interpretation. He was the confessor and guide of Saint Paisius of Neamț, whom he tonsured a monk in 1750, while the two of them were on the Holy Mountain of Athos.

He wrote several works for those who wish to read the Fathers on the prayer of the heart and on watchfulness and to understand them rightly, gathering testimonies from the lives and teachings of the holy monks, from the monastic rules and from the church typikon to show that monks are never to be released to eat meat; and he wrote a word on repentance. The patriarchs of Alexandria, Antioch and Jerusalem, then at Bucharest, examined him, blessed him, and judged his teaching altogether Orthodox and profitable to the soul.

Toward the end of his earthly life he fell ill, bore it with thanksgiving and remained in the unceasing prayer of the heart. In that blessed state he received the Holy Mysteries and departed in peace to the Lord on 25 April, in the year of salvation 1767, honoured by all as a tried teacher of the ascetic labours.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, under the name Basil the Hesychast, and its whole notice is three sentences: «Ο Όσιος Βασίλειος, ο Ησυχαστής, γεννήθηκε το έτος 1692 στη Ρουμανία. Ασκήτεψε θεοφιλώς στο Άγιον Όρος και στην περιοχή Ποϊάνα Μαρουλούι της Ρουμανίας. Υπήρξε διδάσκαλος του Οσίου Παϊσίου (Βελιτσκόφσκιυ) και κοιμήθηκε με ειρήνη το έτος 1767.» He was born in 1692, practised the ascetic life in a God-pleasing way on the Holy Mountain and in the country of Poiana Mărului in Romania, was the teacher of Paisius Velichkovsky, and fell asleep in peace in 1767. The one thing the Greek adds is Athos; the one thing it puts differently is his birthplace, which it gives as Romania where the Romanian life gives the parts of Russia, and that disagreement is recorded and not resolved here.
