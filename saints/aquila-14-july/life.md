# Aquila (14 July)

saint.gr's calendar for 14 July prints a line of two names, «Άγιοι Ακύλας και Ιλάριος», and the page behind it gives one sentence of fact and then stops: Aquila and [Hilarius](/saints/hilarius-companion-of-aquila) were martyred by stoning. «Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τους βίους τους» — we have no other details of their lives — is how the notice ends. There is no year, no place, no emperor, no judge and no hymn.

What the page does print beside the notice is the couplet the Synaxarion sets over the pair: «Tον Iλάριον και τον Aκύλαν λίθοις, / Άνδρες πλάνοι κτείνουσιν οις θεοί λίθοι» — deceived men kill Hilarius and Aquila with stones, men whose own gods are stones. That is the whole of the source, and this folder keeps to that length rather than borrowing a life from one of the other men who carry the name.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 14 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/14/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/704/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
