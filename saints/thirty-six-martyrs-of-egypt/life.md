# The Thirty-six Egyptians

saint.gr keeps a company of forty-one on the Greek 2 Ιουλίου, and prints them in one line: Anthimus the elder, Paul, Bilon, Theon, Heron, and another thirty-six Egyptians. Its whole account of them is three sentences. «Όλοι οι Άγιοι αυτοί μαρτύρηαν κατά τα έτη 305 με 310 μ.Χ.» — all these saints were martyred in the years 305 to 310. Anthimus, it says, was an old man and most probably of Greek descent, and he was martyred at Thessalonica in the years of Maximian; and the rest were martyred at Thessalonica in the years of Maximian too.

That is the end of what the calendar gives. No trial, no judge, no manner of death and no single name among the thirty-six is printed, and nothing of that kind is supplied here from elsewhere. The six entries this company is kept in — [Anthimus the Elder](/saints/anthimus-the-elder), [Paul](/saints/paul-companion-of-anthimus), [Bilon](/saints/bilon-the-martyr), [Theon](/saints/theon-companion-of-anthimus) and [Heron](/saints/heron-companion-of-anthimus) — stand on that one line and those three sentences, and the only thing that distinguishes the five named men from one another on the page is the order in which it names them.
