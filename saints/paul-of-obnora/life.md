# Paul of Obnora

The Greek calendar prints him on 10 Ιανουαρίου as «Όσιος Παύλος ο εκ Ρωσίας», Paul from Russia, and the page behind the line names him Paul of Obnora. He was born at Moscow about the year 1317. His love for the monastic life carried his steps to a monastery in the region of Priluki in Russia, and in his ascetic labours he took [Sergius of Radonezh](/saints/sergius-of-radonezh) for his pattern.

He became, saint.gr says, one of the most renowned and best loved of the elders of Russia — «στάρετς», the page writes, glossing the Russian word with the Greek for elder. He fell asleep in peace in 1429, at the age of a hundred and twelve.

The page gives nothing further: no tonsure, no founding, no relics, and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/10/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1114/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
