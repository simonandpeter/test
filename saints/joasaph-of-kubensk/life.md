# Joasaph of Kubensk

The Russian calendar keeps him under both of his names in one line - "the venerable prince Andrew, in monasticism Joasaph, of Spaso-Kubensk" - because his life is exactly that turn. He was born Prince Andrew, son of the Zaozersk prince Demetrius Vasilievich and the princess Mary, a house the calendar's life remembers for its piety. At twenty he laid the principality down and took the tonsure at the Spaso-Kamenny monastery on Lake Kubenskoye, receiving the name Joasaph after the prince of India who had made the same exchange.

The life remembers him for full obedience, fasting, love of books, and a gentle simplicity that surprised the older monks; he lived enclosed in his cell under the direction of the elder Gregory, later bishop of Rostov, and was granted, it says, a vision of the Lord Himself. He spent five years in the monastery in all. In his last year he ate once a week and received the Holy Mysteries every Sunday, and having taken leave of the brethren - urging them not to grieve at his going - he died quietly with prayer on his lips on 10 September 1453, and was buried in the monastery. At his tomb, the life records, many miracles and healings were worked.

*After the Православный церковный календарь (days.pravoslavie.ru), [Преподобный Иоасаф Каменский](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/id1033.htm); read 30 August 2026.*
