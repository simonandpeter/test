# Ephraim of Atskuri

saint.gr gives him two sentences. Saint Ephraim was the son of the prince of the city of Kartli, it says, and a disciple of the venerable [Gregory of Khandzta](/saints/gregory-khandzteli), whom the same calendar honours on 6 October. He served as archbishop of Atskuri in eastern Georgia in the years 845 to 885, and he fell asleep in peace.

That is the whole of the notice, and the day's own line calls him nothing but Ephraim the Great. The page gives no year for his birth or for his death, no name for his princely father, no account of what made him great, and neither couplet nor hymn; the interval of the see is the only date on the page, and it puts him alive in 885 and says nothing about how long after.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 17 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/17/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1951/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
