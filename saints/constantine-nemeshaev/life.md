# Constantine (Nemeshaev)

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar for 9 ноября ст. ст. carries eleven names on one line and he is the second of them: «Сщмчч. Парфения , еп. Ананьевского, Константина , Димитрия , Нестора , Феодора , Константина , Виктора , Илии , Павла пресвитеров, Иосифа диакона и прмч. Алексия (1937).» One year stands for all eleven, «Сщмчч.» heads the ten clergy and «прмч.» the monk who closes the line, and the grades it prints sort them into a bishop, eight presbyters, a deacon and a hieromonk. The name link behind his place supplies the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line leaves out, «Константин (Немешаев), иерей, сщмч.», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from.

**days.pravoslavie.ru gives this company nothing but that line.** Each of the eleven name pages carries a title, a list of days and no text at all, and not one of them has a `/Life/` link of any kind; the day page's hymns are the two martyrs' of 284–305, the venerable Matrona's, the Mother of God's before the Скоропослушница icon and the wonderworker of Aegina's, and nothing is sung under any of these eleven names. The life below is azbyka.ru's, read in the in-app browser because that host answers no fetcher, and the paragraphs are this corpus's own.

**The two hosts swap the two Constantines of this line.** days.pravoslavie.ru puts Константин (Немешаев) second, next to the bishop, and Константин (Черепанов) sixth, between Феодор Чичканов and Виктор Климов; azbyka.ru's line for the same day reverses them, and nothing on either line says which reading is which man. Neither host is preferred here on its own authority, and the question is settled on the hosts' own life and name pages.

**What settles it is azbyka's own life for Черепанов.** It says he «проходил по коллективному делу вместе с протоиереем Нестором Паниным, протоиереем Феодором Чичкановым и иереем Виктором Климовым» — he went through a collective case together with those three — and was shot with them at two in the morning. His place on a line must therefore be the one among those three, which is the sixth place and is where days.pravoslavie.ru puts him. azbyka's own life for Немешаев has him arrested in the Moscow country, sentenced by a troika there and shot at Butovo, and names none of the three. Two of azbyka's pages contradict azbyka's line; the Сретенский order stands, and the name pages 9659 and 9662 keep the two men apart whichever way the line is read.

He was born on 23 November 1879 in the village of Pesochnoye in the Zhizdra country of the Kaluga province, the son of a priest. He followed his father and entered the Kaluga theological seminary; on finishing it he married, and four children were born to them. Until 1930 his place of service was most likely his own village, and it was there that he and his family met the revolution and lived through the deprivations of the years after it.

He was arrested more than once, in 1921 and again in 1930, and the second arrest ended in a trial and the seizure of his property. The family had to move to the Moscow region, to the village of Vedeny in the Ruza district, where the OGPU went on pursuing him: his lodgings were searched and nothing was found. In 1930 the family moved to Dedinovo in the Lukhovitsy district, and there he served until his arrest in 1937.

The wave of 1937 could not pass over such a pastor: even in those years the church he served was full of people. He was arrested on 6 November 1937 for active counter-revolutionary activity and held in the prison at Kolomna. Asked whether he admitted having carried on slanderous counter-revolutionary agitation among the people, he answered «Среди населения контрреволюционную агитацию я никогда не вел». A troika of the NKVD sentenced him to be shot on 17 November 1937.

He was shot on 21 November 1937 and buried in an unmarked common grave at the Butovo range outside Moscow, two days short of his fifty-eighth birthday. **The calendar keeps him on 9 ноября ст. ст., which is the civil 22 November, and his life has him shot on 21 November 1937** — the day before, 8 ноября old style. The difference is one day and not the thirteen of the style conversion, so it is neither a second feast nor an arithmetic slip that could be read away: five of the eleven died on the 22nd and six on the 21st, and the calendar holds all eleven on the one day. The attestation's day is the day page that listed him, as the brief requires, and the death above is the date his own life gives.
