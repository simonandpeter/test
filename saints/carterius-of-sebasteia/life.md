# Carterius of Sebasteia

saint.gr's calendar for 2 Νοεμβρίου keeps ten soldiers in one line: «Άγιοι Εύδοξιος, Αγάπιος και άλλοι οκτώ μάρτυρες, Αττικός, Μαρίνος, Ωκεανός, Ευστράτιος, Καρτέριος, Νικοπολιτιανός, Στύραξ και Τωβίας». The notice behind it gives each of the ten a line of his own under «Άγιοι που εορτάζουν», and each line is dated «(; - 315)». They were all soldiers in the city of Sebasteia, the notice says, in the years of the emperor Licinius.

In the year 315 they were examined by Auxanios, the governor of Sebasteia, by the duke Marcellus, and by Marcus Agricolaus. They confessed their Christian faith with courage and were cruelly tortured in various ways; and at the end they were thrown into the fire and burned alive, so taking the unfading crown of martyrdom together. The epigram over them counts them: ten champions who endured the fire, and the ten came to the best of ends.

The page gives no further circumstance — no trade before the army, no city of origin, no relics and no hymn — and the ten are told apart only by their names. The corpus keeps a presbyter of this name at Caesarea, with a Greek feast on 8 January, who is a different man by rank, city and day. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου».

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar keeps the same company one day later than the Greek, on 3 ноября ст. ст., in a single line of seven names and a phrase: «Мчч. Аттика, Агапия, Евдоксия, Катерия, Истукария, Пактовия, Никтополиона и дружины их (ок. 320).» His is the fourth of the seven, and the name link behind it does something none of the others does: it prints two spellings, «Катерий (Картерий) Севастийский», and the bracketed one is the word the Greek line uses, so the calendar itself joins the two readings of his name. Four of the seven are men this corpus already held from the Greek line of ten; five of that ten the Russian line does not print, and they are the «дружина», the company, it leaves unnamed. The calendar gives the company no life page and no hymn.

Two of the three names the corpus did not hold were made folders on this day, [Istucarius](/saints/istucarius-of-sebasteia) and [Pactobius](/saints/pactobius-of-sebasteia); the third was left unseated, as a question about whether it is a word the Greek list already prints under another spelling. The Russian year is «ок. 320» against the Greek's 315, so his death is recorded here as 315 or 320, with neither chosen.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 2 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/02/index.aspx) and [the notice of the ten](https://www.saint.gr/1051/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 3 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261103.html), read 4 October 2026.*
