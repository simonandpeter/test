# Sava II

saint.gr names him and gives him two sentences. Its calendar for 8 February prints «Άγιος Σάββας Αρχιεπίσκοπος Σερβίας», and the page behind that line numbers him: Saint Sava the Second, archbishop of Serbia, fell asleep in peace in the year 1271. Of his birth it gives nothing, not even a year — its own heading leaves that date a question mark — and it gives no account of his rule of the Serbian church, no hymn and no couplet.

The one thing it adds is where he lies: his body, it says, is at the Patriarchate of Peć in Kosovo, and incorrupt. That, the number and the day are the whole of the entry.

He is not [Sava of Serbia](/saints/sava-of-serbia), the first archbishop and the son of Stephen Nemanja, whom the Greek calendar keeps on 14 January and this corpus keeps with him; and he is not [Sava III](/saints/sava-the-third) either, whom the Romanian calendar keeps on 26 July. The number on this page and the year 1271 are what set him apart from both, and nothing on the page joins him to either.
