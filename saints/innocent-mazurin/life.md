# Innocent (Mazurin)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery closes its list for 31 октября ст. ст. with him, in four words — «Прмч. Иннокентия (1938)» — and the name link behind the line adds the surname the line leaves out and the grade it does not: «Иннокентий (Мазурин), иеродиакон, прмч.», a hierodeacon of that name, one of the new martyrs of the Russian church. The abbreviation on the line is the one the calendar uses for a monastic put to death.

So the year is the whole of what is recorded, and the year is 1938. There is no life page behind the line, and the name page carries no text, no icon and no hymn — only his name and his three days. The other two are 26 января, the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and 26 апреля, the synaxis of those shot at the Butovo range outside Moscow; so where he died is something the calendar says only by the company it numbers him with, and this folder records it as that and no more.

The second Russian calendar, azbyka.ru, which has given this corpus a full life and its archival references for others of these men, refused the request with a 403 on the day he was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 31 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261031.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14344.html); read 4 October 2026.*
