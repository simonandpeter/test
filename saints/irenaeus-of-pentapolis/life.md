# Irenaeus of Pentapolis

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαρτίου names him second of four on one line — «Άγιοι Θεόδωρος ο Ιερομάρτυρας, Ειρηναίος ο Διάκονος, Σεραπίων και Αμμώνιος οι αναγνώστες» — and the page behind the line carries a single sentence about all of them: the holy hieromartyr Theodore was martyred in the year 304 in the Pentapolis of Libya, «μετά του Διακόνου του Ειρηναίου και των αναγνωστών Σεραπίωνος και Αμμωνίου», together with his deacon Irenaeus and the readers Serapion and Ammonius.

So the source gives him a rank, a bishop and a year, and nothing else: he was deacon to [Theodore](/saints/theodore-of-pentapolis), he suffered with him and with the two readers [Serapion](/saints/serapion-of-pentapolis) and [Ammonius](/saints/ammonius-of-pentapolis), and the place was the Pentapolis of Libya, the five cities of Cyrenaica. The page names no city among the five.

It heads him «(; - 304)» and so gives him no year of birth. It records no emperor, no judge, no manner of death, no relic and no hymn, and of his name it says only «ΕΙΡΗΝΑΙΟΣ (από την λέξη ειρήνη) = ο ειρηνικός, ο ειρηνοποιός» — the peaceable, the peacemaker.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3868/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
