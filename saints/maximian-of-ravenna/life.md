# Maximian of Ravenna

saint.gr says Maximian was born in the year 498 in the region of Istria in Italy, and settled from there in Constantinople. On 14 October 546 he was ordained at Patras as bishop of Ravenna, by Vigilius of Rome, whose years the page gives as 538 to 555.

In the city of Ravenna, it goes on, the person and the holiness of Maximian's life are alive both in church history and in the Christian monuments. To him are owed the dedications of Ravenna's famous basilicas of St Vitalis and St Apollinaris, which he adorned with splendid mosaics; and at Ravenna, on his own initiative, the church of the protomartyr Stephen was raised as well, in which he laid twenty-one relics of saints. He fell asleep in peace in the year 556. The page prints no couplet and no hymn under his name, and what stands above it is only its standing gloss on the name Maximian and on a sixth-century Byzantine philosopher and astronomer who bore it, which is not about this saint.
