# The Hundred and Fifty Martyrs of Palestine

Of the hundred and fifty martyrs, the Greek synaxarion says, we know only that they were martyred in Palestine. Its verse counts them as ten tens and five tens contending and carrying off their crowns. It names none of them and gives no date.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 29 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2399/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
