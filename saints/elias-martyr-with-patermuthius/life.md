# Elias the Glorious, martyr with Patermuthius

Elias and [Patermuthius](/saints/patermuthius-the-martyr) the glorious were martyred by fire with the bishops [Peleus](/saints/peleus-bishop-and-hieromartyr) and Nilus — falling into the furnace, the verse says, Patermuthius urges Elias to equal zeal. That is the whole of the Greek synaxarion’s notice for 17 September; this entry records it and nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 17 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2459/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
