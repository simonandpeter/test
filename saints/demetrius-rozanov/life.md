# Demetrius (Rozanov)

The Russian calendar names him third of five in one line for 26 September: «Сщмч. Афанасия, Александра и Димитрия пресвитеров, мчч. Иоанна и Николая (1937)». Its index of names gives him as «Димитрий (Розанов), протоиерей, сщмч.», an archpriest, and keeps him here and on 26 January with the new martyrs and confessors of Russia.

That is everything the source holds — no place, no parish, no account of his death. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which refused this reading on 4 October 2026. The four he is named with are [Athanasius (Dokukin)](/saints/athanasius-dokukin), [Alexander (Levitsky)](/saints/alexander-levitsky), [John (Zolotov)](/saints/john-zolotov) and [Nicholas (Gusev)](/saints/nicholas-gusev).

*After the Russian church calendar's [26 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260926.html) and its [index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14333.html); read 4 October 2026.*
