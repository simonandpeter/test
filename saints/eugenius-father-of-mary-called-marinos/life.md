# Eugenius, father of Mary called Marinos

saint.gr's calendar for 12 Φεβρουαρίου keeps the venerable Eugenius, and the whole of what its page for him says is two sentences: «Ο Όσιος Ευγένιος ήταν πατέρας της Οσίας Μαρίας της μετονομασθείσης Μαρίνος. Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίου του Οσίου» — Eugenius was the father of the venerable [Mary](/saints/mary-called-marinos), the one renamed Marinos; we have no details of the venerable one's life.

So he is kept for his daughter's sake and for nothing else the calendar records. Her page adds the one fact about him that is anywhere on the day: that he had been left a widower, and that she took refuge with him in a monastery, disguised as a man, because she wanted the monastic life. No country, no century, no year, no hymn, no relics and no distich are printed for him, and the page gives him only its gloss on the name, that Ευγένιος is ευ with γένος, the man of good birth and good feelings.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/12/index.aspx) and [his page](https://www.saint.gr/1238/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
