# Liviu Galaction Munteanu

Liviu Galaction was a teacher of theology and one of the confessors of the communist years in Romania. He was born on 16 May 1898 at Cristian, in the county of Brașov, into a family of schoolteachers, and was formed as a theologian at the "Andrei Șaguna" lyceum in Brașov and at the Theological-Pedagogical Institute in Sibiu. He took his doctorate in theology at the university of Cernăuți.

On 17 February 1927 he was ordained priest by Bishop Nicolae Ivan, who made him a professor at the Theological Academy in Cluj; later he was rector of it as well. Through the Horthyist occupation he stayed in Cluj, keeping up with Bishop Nicolae Colan a core of Romanian and Orthodox resistance.

After 1948, against every obstruction from the communist authorities, Bishop Nicolae Colan kept him at the head of the new theological institute until it was closed down in 1952. His attempts to form the theology students to answer the pressures the state was imposing drew the notice of the Securitate, which watched him constantly, arrested him in 1952 and pressed him to give up his work. He was posted to a parish at Bistrița, and there turned to catechising children.

In 1958 Bishop Teofil Herineanu made him vicar administrator, having a plan to revive catechesis in the eparchy of Cluj. The syllabus he drew up for children and for adults brought him to the attention of the Securitate once more. He was investigated, and in 1959 sentenced to eight years in prison for "plotting against the social order". He was held in the prisons of Aiud and Gherla, and died at Aiud on 8 March 1961, of the inhuman treatment the regime put him to.
