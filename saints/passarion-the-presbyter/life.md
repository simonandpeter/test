# Passarion the Presbyter

He was one of the most famous ascetics of Palestine, saint.gr says. He was a contemporary of [Juvenal](/saints/juvenal-of-jerusalem), Patriarch of Jerusalem, who held that see from 420 to 458, and he served as teacher to [Euthymius the Great](/saints/euthymius-the-great), whose own day the page gives as the twentieth of January. He became the founder of an almshouse for the old at Jerusalem, in which there was also an oratory dedicated to his name, and in that oratory his memory used to be kept on the twenty-first of November.

The two venerable men who are carried with him, [Agapius](/saints/agapius-disciple-of-passarion) and [Philemon](/saints/philemon-disciple-of-passarion), were his disciples and fellow-ascetics. The memory of all three, the page says, is recorded in the Jerusalem Kanonarion, at page ninety of Archimandrite Kallistos's edition. It adds one thing more, and adds it as a question: Passarion is perhaps the same man as the one who is commemorated on the eleventh of August. The page does not settle that, and nothing here settles it either.

Beyond this there is nothing: no year of birth or death, no birthplace, no account of his asceticism, no relics and no burial place, and the page prints neither apolytikion nor kontakion for him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/10/index.aspx) and [the life of the three](https://www.saint.gr/1544/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
