# Didymus of Laodicea

Didymus was one of the three martyrs Diodorus, Diomedes and Didymus, all of Laodicea in Syria, who were seized by the ruler there and, because they confessed bravely that they were Christians, were scourged to death; giving their flesh to the whips, the verse says, they scourged error. The Greek calendar keeps them on 11 September.

The Ohrid Prologue, for the Serbian calendar, keeps [Diodorus](/saints/diodorus-of-laodicea), Didymus and [Diomedes](/saints/diomedes-of-laodicea) together, and says only that they were beaten for Christ’s sake in Laodicea and gave up their souls to their Lord.
