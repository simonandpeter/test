# Laodicius the Jailer

saint.gr's calendar for 13 May names him and gives him two sentences. The holy martyr Laodicius was a jailer at Traianopolis in Thrace, at the time when the martyr [Glyceria](/saints/glyceria-of-traianopolis) was shut up in the prison there. Having been catechised by her into Christianity and having confessed Christ as true God, he was arrested and beheaded. The page's couplet sets him among the peoples calling on the Word as God and brought to the sword's judgment.

Glyceria's own page, read the same day, tells the same thing from her side: after she had been stoned without a stone touching her and had endured various tortures, she was thrown into prison, where she catechised and brought to the Christian faith her jailer Laodicius, who then confessed his faith with courage and was martyred for Christ. Neither page gives the year of his death, and neither prints a hymn for him.

The Ohrid Prologue, read for the Julian 13 May, has him in the body of its entry for [Glyceria of Traianopolis](/saints/glyceria-of-traianopolis), and gives him one sentence. The emperor's deputy had had her beaten and thrown into prison, and had sealed the prison doors meaning to starve her; an angel of God brought her heavenly food instead, and when the deputy opened the prison expecting to find her dead he found her healthy and bright and cheerful. The jailer Laodicius, seeing that wonder, confessed Christ the Lord himself, and was beheaded at once.

The book gives him no year. The year 177 that closes the entry is said of her suffering, not of his, and nothing here reckons it onto him.
