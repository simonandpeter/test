# Dometius, brother of Maximus

Dometius and his brother [Maximus](/saints/maximus-brother-of-dometius) were, saint.gr says, shoots of a lordly root, brought up with every ease and honour that their father's imperial station gave them; the page does not name the father. Their striking looks and the comfort written on their faces bore no witness to the humble and struggling mind of their hearts. Out of love for Christ they came one day to Abba [Macarius](/saints/macarius-the-egyptian) asking to be received as his disciples and fellow ascetics, and the practised eye of the teacher of the desert was deceived: he thought that young men so well reared would not bear the desert's hard trial, yet he did not send them away, and showed them a place to build a small cell of stone.

For three whole years they struggled beyond what is human and came to a very high measure of virtue, giving Abba Macarius no sign of life; they went out only to church, to communicate, and were silent there. At last Macarius came to visit them. They greeted him in silence, and the younger, at a nod from the elder, went on weaving his baskets. At the morning prayer Macarius saw, at every verse of the psalter they said, a lamp of fire come out of their mouths and reach to heaven.

The elder brother had come to perfection, the page says, while the younger was still under assault from the evil one, and was guarded from it by an angel because of his ascetic labour beyond nature and his longing for spiritual completion. A few days later the elder fell asleep, and three days after him the younger. The two are much honoured in the desert of Nitria, and the monastery raised where they laboured is called by the Egyptians today «Μονή Baramous», the monastery of the Romans. Which of the two brothers was the elder the page does not say, and it is not decided here.
