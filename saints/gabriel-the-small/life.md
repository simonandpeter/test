# Gabriel the Small

saint.gr's calendar for 17 Μαρτίου names a venerable-martyr Gabriel, surnamed the Small, and gives him three lines and no more. It says he lived in Georgia through the second half of the eighteenth century and the beginning of the nineteenth; that he was an ecclesiastical secretary and a calligrapher; and that he was martyred in the year 1802 by the Muslims. The page heads him «Αγιος Γαβριηλ ο Μικρος ο Οσιομαρτυρας (; - 1802)», leaving the year of his birth a question mark.

That is the whole of what has been read. saint.gr gives him no birthplace within Georgia, no account of his arrest or of the manner of his death, no persecutor by name, and names no relics; it prints neither a couplet nor a hymn under his name. No year of birth, no place and no narrative has been taken from anywhere else, and none has been supplied from memory. Other Gabriels are kept on other days and in other calendars, and nothing on this page identifies him with any of them; if a fuller life of his is printed elsewhere, it has not been read.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 17 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/17/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3915/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
