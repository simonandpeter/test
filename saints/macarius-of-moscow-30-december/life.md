# Macarius of Moscow (30 December)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps the hierarch Macarius, metropolitan of Moscow, on 30 December, and its line is «Свт. Макария , митр. Московского (1563).» The calendar’s own name page for him is «Святитель Макарий, митрополит Московский и всея Руси» — metropolitan of Moscow and of all Rus — and the year it gives for his death is 1563.

What the day page says of him beyond the line is said in the hymns it prints under his name, and they say what he is remembered for: that by God-wise teaching and by the copying of books he took pains to enlighten the Russian people and to glorify the saints of this land, and that he is reckoned a successor of the first hierarchs of Rus. The troparion sets him beside the great shepherds and the teachers of the whole world and calls him a servant of the Wisdom of God.

He is not the Macarius of Moscow this corpus already held: that one is Macarius (Nevsky, Parvitsky), who died in 1926 and whom this same calendar keeps on 16 февраля. The reading behind this line, life 6711, was opened; nothing is written here from it beyond what the line and the hymns carry, because this folder is the calendar’s record and not a biography. 

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 30 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261230.html); read 13 October 2026.*
