# Nicholas of Novgorod

saint.gr's whole notice of him is one sentence: we have no details of the life of this Russian saint except that he was a fool for Christ. The day's line names him «Άγιος Νικόλαος του Νόβγκοροντ», Nicholas of Novgorod, and the page glosses the name Nicholas as the victor out of the people. Beyond that it prints no century, no parents, no account of his folly or of his repose, no relics, no distich and no hymn.

Its heading gives the feast as fixed, kept on 27 July of every year, and the entry carries neither an asterisk nor the sentence by which saint.gr marks an entry it will not vouch for. The Greek calendar keeps a number of Russian saints in just this way, with a name, a town and one clause of description, and this folder says no more than the page does.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 27 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/27/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2080/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
