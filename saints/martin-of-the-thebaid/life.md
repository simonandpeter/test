# Martin of the Thebaid

saint.gr's calendar for 24 Μαρτίου names a venerable Martin whom it calls the Theban, and its page for him is one sentence long: the venerable Martin lived and struggled as an ascetic in the Thebaid of Egypt, and departed in peace. That is the whole of the account, and this life says no more than the page does.

Over him it prints a couplet — brought up well, O Martin, thou didst die most excellently in old age, and dying wast added to the fathers who had died. It gives him no year, no monastery, no wonder and no relic, and prints no hymn; nothing has been taken from any other source and nothing supplied from memory. Two other men of the name are kept on other days in this corpus and nothing on this page identifies him with either.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/24/index.aspx) and [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/86/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
