# Aldhelm of Sherborne

saint.gr's whole entry for him is one sentence: «Ο Άγιος Aldhelm έζησε τον 6ο αιώνα μ.Χ. και ήταν επίσκοπος της πόλης Sherborne» — saint Aldhelm lived in the sixth century and was bishop of the city of Sherborne. The page prints both his name and his see in Latin letters and gives him no Greek form at all, which is unusual enough on this site to be worth saying.

There is nothing else. No birthplace, no parents, no monastery, no writings, no account of his episcopate, no year of death and no relics; no synaxarion couplet and no hymn. A century, a see, and a name in the Latin alphabet is the whole of what the Greek calendar records of him here.
