# Sebastiana (7 June)

doxologia.ro's calendar names her and gives no life. The line for 7 iunie reads «Sfânta Cuvioasă Sebastiana», the venerable Sebastiana, and her page on the site has neither a Viață tab nor a Tropar tab. Under its headings for icons, articles and photographs stands one entry only, and it belongs to the three saints of the day together: «Sfântul Ierarh Mucenic Teodot, Sfânta Cuvioasă Sebastiana și Sfânta Muceniță Zenaida».

That is how the site keeps her, beside [Theodotus](/saints/theodotus-of-ancyra) and [Zenaida](/saints/zenaida-martyr-7-june) on this day, and it is a title and not a life. The corpus holds another Sebastiana, a martyr and disciple of the apostle Paul whom the Russian calendar keeps on 16 September; she is called a martyr and this one is called venerable, they stand on different days, and nothing read here makes them one woman. No year, no country and no hymn is given for this Sebastiana.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 7 iunie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/7-iunie) and [her page](https://doxologia.ro/sfanta-cuvioasa-sebastiana); read 24 September 2026.*
