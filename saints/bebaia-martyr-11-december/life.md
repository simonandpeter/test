# Bebaia (11 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 11 December keeps four martyrs in one entry and names all four: [Terentius](/saints/terentius-martyr-11-december), [Vincentius](/saints/vincentius-martyr-11-december), [Emilian](/saints/emilian-martyr-11-december) and Bebaia. The whole of the life it gives them is one sentence — that the holy Terentius, Vincentius, Emilianus and Bebaia were martyred by the sword. No country, no reign, no persecutor and no year is printed for any of them.

She is the one woman among the four, and the couplet set over the entry counts her as such: cut down, three and one, they show forth the three persons of God and the one nature. Nothing else is said of her. The Greek calendar keeps another Bebaia on 4 September, whom the corpus already holds as the companion of Thathuel, and nothing here joins this one to her.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/11/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3169/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
