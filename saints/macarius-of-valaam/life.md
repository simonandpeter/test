# Macarius of Valaam

saint.gr's calendar for 20 Φεβρουαρίου names him and gives one sentence. Its line reads «Άγιος Μακάριος ο Οσιομάρτυρας και ο συν αυτώ αναιρεθέντες» — the holy venerable-martyr Macarius and those slain together with him — and the page standing under that line says that Macarius was abbot at the monastery of Valaam and was killed, together with thirty-four novice monks of the house, by the Latins in the year 1578.

That is the whole of it. The thirty-four are counted and not named, so they are not kept here one by one. The page gives Macarius no birthplace and no year of birth — its heading leaves the first of his two dates a question mark — and it names no relics and prints neither couplet nor hymn under his name.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 20 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/20/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3771/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
