# Galina (10 February)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery keeps her in three words for 10 февраля ст. ст. — «Св. Галины (III).» — a saint of the third century, and that is all. The line calls her «Св.», holy, and gives her no rank beyond it: not a martyr, not a monastic, not a princess. The name link behind it carries the bare nominative «Галина», with no surname and no epithet to set her beside or apart from anyone.

The calendar prints no life page for her and no hymn under her name. Nothing further is written here, and in particular she is not identified with any other Galina the calendar keeps on another day; the line and the century are the whole of what was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 10 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260210.html); read 3 October 2026.*
