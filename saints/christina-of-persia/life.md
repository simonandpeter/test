# Christina of Persia

doxologia.ro's calendar names Christina of Persia and prints almost nothing of her life. What its page says is the whole of it: that for the unshaken confession of her faith in Christ she was cruelly tortured in Persia, in the fourth century; that from those torments and from the unceasing scourging she grew so weak that she died; and that her soul, leaving behind her tortured body, entered into the joy of Christ, the King and Master.

Nothing else is given — no city, no persecutor, no year, and no hymn. This folder records what the Romanian calendar keeps for 13 March and no more.

The Greek calendar keeps her on the same day and says the same one thing. saint.gr's page for her holds a single sentence — that the holy Christina in Persia was martyred after they had whipped her to death — and two lines of verse which turn on the same fact: «Μάστιξ τὸ τύπτον· σάρξ τὸ πάσχον Χριστίνης. / Χριστοῦ χάριν χέουσα κρουνοὺς αἱμάτων» — the whip is what strikes, the flesh of Christina what suffers, as she pours out streams of blood for Christ's sake. It adds no year, no place in Persia, no persecutor and no hymn.

The Ohrid Prologue keeps her on the same menologion day and tells the same one thing in four sentences. For the unshaken confession of the faith of Christ she was cruelly tortured in Persia in the fourth century; her torturers scourged her with whips until she grew weak and died; and her soul parted from her tortured body and went to dwell in the eternal joy of Christ the King and Lord. It adds no year, no city in Persia, no persecutor and no hymn.

The Russian church keeps her on the same menologion day and tells the same single thing in one sentence. The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery closes her line with the fourth century, and the reading behind it says only that for the confession of the faith of Christ she was beaten with whips until she died, in that century. It adds no city in Persia, no persecutor, no year and no hymn.
