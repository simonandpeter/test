# Bianor of Pisidia

doxologia.ro prints four lines about him and no more. Bianor was from Pisidia, in Asia Minor. Brought before Severian, governor of Euphratensis, he confessed his faith in the Lord Jesus Christ with great courage; and for that he was put to many torments, and in the end received a martyr's death together with the martyr [Silvanus](/saints/silvanus-companion-of-bianor).

The page gives no year, no place of death, no manner of it, and nothing of his life before the trial; the calendar prints him and Silvanus on two lines of its own and sends both to this one short notice. What is here is what the Romanian calendar has of him.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 10 July, and names the two together as the Romanian one does. saint.gr says Bianor came from the province of Pisidia and was arrested for confessing Christ by the governor of Euphratesia, which it says is now called Atzar and lies in Syria. They hung him up and beat him without pity with wooden swords, and then burned him with red-hot iron pellets, so that he lost his teeth and his ears. [Silvanus](/saints/silvanus-companion-of-bianor), who was standing by and watching his torture and his courage, believed and confessed Christ in front of all the idolaters, and had his tongue cut out on the spot and then his head. Bianor's right eye was put out, the skin of his head was flayed, and at the last he was beheaded; and so the two received the crown of the contest together. The page gives no year and no emperor, and prints no hymn.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 10 iulie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/10-iulie) and [the notice of the two martyrs](https://doxologia.ro/sfintii-mucenici-vianor-siluan); read 24 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 10 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/10/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/699/saint.aspx), read 1 October 2026.*
