# Procopius the Persian

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιος Προκόπιος ο Πέρσης» — Procopius the Persian — and begins by saying what does not have him: he is unknown to the synaxaristae and to the Menaia. What it knows it has from his own service in the Paris Codex 259, folio 229a: that he reproved the error of the idols, and suffered a martyr's death after many and various tortures.

That is the whole of the entry. The page gives him no city, no century, no year, no emperor, no companions, no relics and no hymn, and sets no couplet over him; the one thing it places him by is the epithet in his line, the Persian. Besides that it prints only its usual gloss on the name, from προκοπή, the capable one, which says nothing about him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3087/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
