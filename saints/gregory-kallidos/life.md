# Gregory (Kallidos) of Heraclea

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 29 июня ст. ст. names him «Свт. Григория, митр. Ираклийского (1925) (Конст.).» — a hierarch, a metropolitan of Heraclea, the year 1925, and a parenthesis marking him a saint of the Church of Constantinople rather than of the Russian church. The name link behind the forename gives the calendar’s own nominative with the surname and the second see the line leaves out, «Святитель Григорий (Каллидос), митрополит Ираклийский и Редестосский» — of Heraclea and of Rhaedestus.

His name page carries nothing else. It heads «День памяти: 29 июня», lists a tab of icons, and prints no life, no year of birth, no place of birth and no account of his death. Nothing is supplied for any of it here; the year in the line, 1925, is the year of death recorded, and the calendar gives this as his only day.

The stored Russian form is the name with the surname the link supplies and without either see. The link joins the two sees with «и», and a name form is not a sentence: putting «Ираклийский и Редестосский» into a name form would store a clause rather than a name, so the sees are in the display name and in this paragraph instead, and the whole string the calendar prints is in the form’s own note. The day page carries two hymns, both of the chief apostles whose feast this day is, and none under his name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 29 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260629.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14281.html); read 9 October 2026.*
