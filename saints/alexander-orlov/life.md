# Alexander (Orlov)

He is the last of the six presbyters the Russian calendar keeps in one line on 30 September, and the sixth name before the line turns to a deacon, a schemanun and three lay martyrs, all eleven of them under the year 1937. The day line prints «Александра» and leaves the presbyters' rank to the plural that follows them; the site's index of names heads his page «Александр (Орлов), протоиерей, сщмч.», which makes him an archpriest.

The corpus already holds a great many Alexanders and more than a dozen of them are new martyrs, so this folder is keyed on the day and the surname rather than the name. The index keeps him here and on 26 January with the company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and prints no birth, no diocese, no parish, no charge and no place of death. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line.

*After the Russian church calendar's [30 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260930.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14482.html); read 4 October 2026.*
