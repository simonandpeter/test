# Adalgius of Novara

saint.gr says that the venerable Adalgius came from Ireland and lived in the seventh century after Christ. He was a disciple of the venerable Fursa, whom the same calendar keeps on 16 January and whom it calls the Apostle of Picardy. Having lived as an ascetic and in a manner pleasing to God, the venerable one fell asleep in peace, in the year 686.

That is the whole of the account: a country, a century, a master, a way of life named in one word and a year of death. The epithet the day's line gives him — «της Νοβάρα», of Novara, in Lombardy — is all that ties him to the place he is remembered in, and the page does not say when or why he went there, nor whether he founded anything. It prints no hymn for him and no verse, and it gives no form of his name but the Greek one.
