# Sarapabon the Senator

Of Sarapabon little is known: he lived at Alexandria under Decius, the raging persecutor of Christians, in 250, and the governor Valerius, unable to frighten him into sacrificing to the idols, killed him in anger with his own sword; his body was buried with great reverence by devout Christians who honoured his zeal and wisdom and were grateful for his frequent kindnesses. The Greek synaxarion keeps the martyrs of Alexandria under Decius in separate entries on 6 September; the Russian calendar counts them as the eleven with the presbyter Faustus and the deacon Abibus.
