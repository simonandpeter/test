# Asclepia

saint.gr's calendar for 4 Ιουλίου names her and gives no life. The whole of its account is one sentence, «Η Αγία Ασκληπία η θαυματουργή απεβίωσε ειρηνικά» — the holy Asclepia the wonderworker reposed in peace. The page gives no country, no city, no century, no year, no parentage and no manner of life, and it prints no hymn under her line.

Besides that sentence it carries only the couplet the synaxaria print for her: «Aσκληπιάς άμισθος ασκληπιάδης, / Παύουσα προίκα και τετμημένη νόσους» — Asclepias, a healer who took no fee, putting an end to sicknesses for nothing and cutting them away. The page's heading explains her name from αίγλη and ήπιος and notes the Asclepius of mythology and a Neoplatonist philosopher of the same name; neither is a statement about her. That is everything the source says.
