# Nicholas (Popov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him in a single line for 13 марта ст. ст.: «Сщмч. Николая пресвитера (1919).» The line declines the forename to «Николая», gives no surname, no place, no year of birth and no word of narrative; the year it closes with, 1919, is the only year it gives, and the rank word «Сщмч.» that governs the name is the only rank.

Behind that «Николая» stands the calendar's own name page 14415, which carries the fuller form «Николай (Попов), иерей, сщмч.» — the surname the day's line leaves out, and a priest's grade. That page lists two days: this 13 марта and «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. It carries no life either, but it does link a parish church that bears his name, the church of the Hieromartyr Nicholas of Verkhnegnutovo at the village of Staroselye in the diocese of Uryupinsk and Novoanninsky; so the place his veneration is attached to is Verkhnegnutovo, though the calendar does not put that epithet in his name.

azbyka.ru, which has lives for some of the martyrs of these years, was not read for him. What the calendar prints is therefore the whole of what this folder says, and it says that rather than fill the silence: a priest of the Russian church of that name, who died a martyr in 1919 and whom this calendar keeps on this day.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 13 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260313.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14415.html), which carries no life; read 7 October 2026.*
