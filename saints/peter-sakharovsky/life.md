# Peter (Sakharovsky)

Archpriest Peter Ivanovich Sakharovsky was born in 1876 and served in the Holy Trinity and Saviour churches of Nizhny Novgorod. In August 1937 he was arrested, charged with counter-revolutionary activity and sentenced to the highest measure, and he received the martyr’s crown by shooting soon after the sentence. The Jubilee Council of Bishops of the Russian Orthodox Church glorified him in 2000.

The Russian calendar keeps him on 21 September old style on one line with the priests [Valens (Nikolsky)](/saints/valens-nikolsky), [Alexander (Belyakov)](/saints/alexander-belyakov), [John (Lazarev)](/saints/john-lazarev), [Andrew (Benediktov)](/saints/andrew-benediktov) and [John (Nikolsky)](/saints/john-nikolsky), of 1937. azbyka.ru prints a seventh priest, Basil Nosov, on its line for the day, whom the Sretensky day does not name; it keeps him also with the saints of Nizhny Novgorod, and prints no hymns for him.

*After the life printed by the Russian church calendar at azbyka.ru — [the entry](https://azbyka.ru/days/sv-petr-saharovskij); the Sretensky calendar (days.pravoslavie.ru), 21 сентября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260921.html); read 17 September 2026.*
