# Nicholas (Rozov) (20 February)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him for 20 февраля ст. ст. in four words — «Сщмч. Николая пресвитера.» — a hieromartyr, a presbyter. The name link behind the line carries the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line leaves out and a rank the line does not give: «Николай (Розов), протоиерей, сщмч.», an archpriest of that name, one of the new martyrs of the Russian church.

The line gives no year at all, which is unusual among this calendar's twentieth-century entries and is why no date is recorded here. It prints no life page for him and no hymn under his name; it names no parish, no diocese, no place and no manner of death. Nothing is reasoned out from the surname, and the corpus's other folder for a man of that surname has a different forename and is not connected to him here.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 20 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260220.html); read 3 October 2026.*
