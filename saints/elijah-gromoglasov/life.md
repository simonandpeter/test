# Elijah (Gromoglasov)

**A professor of church law at two universities before he was a deacon, and one of the fathers of the Council of 1917.** The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him seventh of the sixteen it closes with 1937, among the «пресвитеров», and hangs a full life behind his name page. Илия Михайлович Громогласов was born on 19 July 1869 in the village of Ермиши in the Темников district of the province of Тамбов, son of a deacon, Михаил Громогласов. He was schooled at the Шацк church school, finished the Tambov seminary, and in 1893 the Moscow Theological Academy, leaving it a candidate of theology and kept on as a professorial stipendiary. A year later he was acting lecturer in the history of the Russian schism, and in 1900 was appointed lecturer in English.

**He was dismissed from the Academy in 1911 for what he had printed in the newspapers.** In 1902 he married a daughter of the prince Николай Фёдорович Дулов; in 1908 he defended a dissertation on the definitions of marriage in the Кормчая and was made a master of theology; in 1910 he was appointed extraordinary professor of the Academy in the history of the Russian schism. The next year he was dismissed «за прогрессивные взгляды», for progressive views expressed in the press. He took a post as inspector of classes at the Мариинское girls’ school, teaching Russian letters and general history at the same time in a Moscow girls’ gymnasium of which his wife was the head. In 1914 he passed the examinations of the law faculty of Moscow University with a first-class diploma; a year later he passed the examinations in church and state law, was given the degree of master-candidate in church law, and was taken among the privat-docents of the law faculty in the chair of church law. In 1917 he was restored to his professorship at the Academy, in the chair of church law, while remaining a professor of the University.

**He was an active member of the Local Council of 1917–18 and was elected to the Supreme Church Council.** He stood for the part of the laity in the affairs of the Church and for the conciliar principle in her government. At the beginning of 1922 he left the secular service, having resolved to become a clergyman: on 18 February 1922 the patriarch ordained him deacon to the church of the hieromartyr Антипа in Moscow, and on the 20th priest of the same church.

**Four arrests in fifteen years.** A month after his ordination, on 22 March 1922, he was arrested; at the trial over the seizure of church valuables he was sentenced to a year and a half’s imprisonment, commuted to a year of exile. Returning, he was made rector of the church of the Resurrection in Кадаши. On 8 March 1924 came the second arrest and a sentence of three years’ exile to the Urals, which he got deferred by obtaining a hearing with the procurator of the Supreme Court. After the death of the patriarch in 1925 he was arrested again and the same sentence applied, three years from 19 May 1925, far to the north in the village of Сургут in the Тобольск district. When it was over he was forbidden to live in Moscow or in a number of other large towns, and so came to Tver, where he served in the church of the icon of the Unburnt Bush.

**The fourth arrest was in the case made against the archbishop of Tver.** On the night of 2–3 November 1937 he was arrested for the fourth time and accused of belonging to a counter-revolutionary fascist-monarchist organisation headed by [Thaddeus of Tver](/saints/thaddeus-of-tver), the archbishop; [Alexis (Benemansky)](/saints/alexis-benemansky), an archpriest of the same town, was accused in the same words. He did not admit himself guilty. On 2 December the troika of the NKVD sentenced him to be shot, and the life closes: «21 ноября (4 декабря н. ст.) 1937 года протоиерей Илья Громогласов был расстрелян. Погребён священномученик Илья в общей могиле на Волынском кладбище». **He was canonised twice**: as a locally venerated saint of the diocese of Tver on 6 (19) September 1999, and among the New Martyrs and Confessors of Russia at the Jubilee Council of Bishops in August 2000.
