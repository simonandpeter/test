# Zoilus the Roman

saint.gr's whole account of him is one sentence: «Ο Άγιος Ζωΐλος μαρτύρησε αφού τον κρέμασαν επάνω σε ξύλο» — Zoilus was martyred after they hung him upon a piece of wood. The day's line adds one word to it, ο Ρωμαίος, the Roman, which the body of the page does not repeat.

The couplet above the entry turns on that wood: struck by the arrow of a bow upon the tree, Zoilus, thou strikest the one who brought in corruption by a tree. That is everything the calendar has of him.

The page gives no century, no emperor and no judge, no city — not even Rome, outside the day's own epithet — no relic, and no hymn. The corpus holds no other man of this name, so nothing here had to be told apart from anybody.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 13 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/13/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/257/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
