# Antony (Bystrov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and gives no life. Its line for 3 июля ст. ст. reads «Сщмч. Антония , архиепископа Архангельского (1931).» — the hieromartyr Antony, archbishop of Arkhangelsk, of the year 1931 — and the line carries a link to his name and none to a reading. The name page behind it adds his family name, Быстров, and widens the see to «архиепископ Архангельский и Холмогорский», archbishop of Arkhangelsk and Kholmogory. Beyond the title it prints one line of days, «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 3 июля», and the address of a parish church in Arkhangelsk dedicated to him.

So the calendar sets down an archbishop of the Russian church who died in 1931, and no more than that. The first of those two days is not a feast of his own: it is the general commemoration of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which this calendar prints beside the proper day of each of them, and because both his pages call him a martyr that commemoration is the ground on which the rank of new martyr stands in this folder.

Nothing else about him was found. The day page prints a great many hymns for the saints it heads and none under his name, and azbyka.ru, which is where an account of a bishop who died in 1931 would be, was not read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 3 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260703.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14420.html), which name him and give no life; read 9 October 2026.*
