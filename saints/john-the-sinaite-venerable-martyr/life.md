# John the Sinaite

saint.gr's calendar for 12 Φεβρουαρίου names «Άγιος Ιωάννης ο Σιναΐτης ο Οσιομάρτυρας», John the Sinaite, venerable-martyr, and heads his page «(; - 1091)». The whole of what it prints is one line: «Ο Όσιος Ιωάννης μαρτύρησε το έτος 1091 μ.Χ. Δεν έχουμε περισσότερες λεπτομέρειες για τον Βίο του Αγίου» — the venerable John was martyred in the year 1091; we have no further details of the saint's life.

So the mountain, the double rank and the year are the whole of what the Greek calendar keeps of him. He is not John of the Ladder, the other and far better known John of Sinai, who is kept in March and did not die a martyr's death; nothing on this page connects the two. No parentage, no monastery, no account of the martyrdom, no hymn, no relics and no distich are printed, and the page gives only its usual gloss on the name Ιωάννης and its list of the great Johns it recalls.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/12/index.aspx) and [his page](https://www.saint.gr/1236/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
