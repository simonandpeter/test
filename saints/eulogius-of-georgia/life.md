# Eulogius of Georgia

saint.gr says Eulogius was born in Georgia and lived in the years of queen [Tamara](/saints/tamara-of-georgia). Because he loved God and the monastic life he became a monk; and God blessed his spiritual labour and his folly for Christ's sake, and gave him the gift of foresight. Together with the venerable [John the Philosopher](/saints/john-philosopher-of-georgia) he followed the queen to the war against the sultan Rukn ed-Din at Basiani, in the year 1203.

The Georgian army, led by king David Soslan, came first to the monastery of Vardzia, where Eulogius and John prayed with the queen for the victory of the Christians. Then the Georgian forces moved on to Basiani, where the sultan lay with an army the page puts at four hundred thousand men. The queen, with the two ascetics in her train, halted near the village of Kozrkhe and stayed there, and her prayer, the page says, was unceasing.

One day, while Eulogius and John were with her, Eulogius looked up, moved from where he sat and went out of the tent crying: behold the grace of the Lord! — and climbed towards the top of a hill. John, who had stayed with the queen, said: the fool has had a vision, and I think it was a good one. Calling that day and that hour to mind afterwards, it emerged that at exactly that moment the Georgian fighters had routed the sultan's army, which was ten times their number. Eulogius fell asleep in peace; the page gives him no year of his own, and nothing about his death but that.
