# Matrona (Vlasova)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names her on 25 октября ст. ст. and carries no life for her. Its whole entry is «Прп. Матроны исп (1963).» — a rank word, a second rank word shortened, a forename in the genitive and a year — and the title on the name link behind it supplies the surname, «Матрона (Власова), исповедница». She is one of the latest-dated saints this calendar keeps on any day of the Julian October: a confessor who died in 1963, within living memory.

What the two words of rank say is that she was a monastic and that she was a confessor rather than a martyr — that is, that she suffered for the faith and did not die of it. The calendar gives her no monastery, no office, no name in religion beside the one it prints, and no day in 1963; no `Life/` href follows her name on the day page, and her name page carries the two days it keeps her on and nothing of her own.

It does carry a link to an article on Православие.Ru, «Дивеевская Голгофа» by Elena Balashova, whose printed teaser names the people the article is about — a metropolitan, a bishop, village priests, sisters of Diveyevo, a blessed woman and her cell-attendants — and says that their ways of the cross were different but their standing in the faith firm, and that the monastery of Diveyevo was what bound them together. **Which of those she is the teaser does not say, and this folder does not guess**; the article itself was not followed, because it is not the calendar.

The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian confessors are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line. The day page's six troparia and kontakia belong to the three older entries it heads, and none of them to her.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 25 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261025.html) and [her name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/12725.html); read 4 October 2026.*
