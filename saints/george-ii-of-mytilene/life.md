# George II of Mytilene

The Russian calendar's notice of him is three sentences long, and they are just enough to tell him from the other bishop of his see whom the same calendar keeps on 7 апреля. He was made bishop of Mytilene within the years 820 to 829, in the time of the iconoclast troubles, and he died in 842 at Mytilene. The calendar numbers him the second of his name in that see, and the numeral is the only thing in the line that does the separating.

What the page adds belongs to the history of his relics rather than to his life: in the twelfth century a Russian abbot named Даниил, who travelled in the East and left a description of the journey behind him, saw them. The calendar gives no year of birth, nothing of his family, and nothing of what he did in the see; the folder accordingly says no more than this, and the ninth century its day line prints is the century the death year falls in.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 16 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260516.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1081.htm); read 8 October 2026.*
