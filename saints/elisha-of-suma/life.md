# Elisha of Suma

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar names him on 14 июня ст. ст. as «Прп. Елисея Сумского (XV-XVI).», and behind the line it keeps two short readings of him. Both agree on the little there is: he was a monk of the Соловецкая house, tonsured there, who occupied himself with the weaving of fishing nets. His life, the fuller of the two readings says in so many words, is unknown; what is remembered of him is one circumstance of his dying.

Fishing with other brethren sixty versts from the monastery, he was told that his end was near. He put himself in God’s hands, but grieved greatly that he had not been tonsured into the great angelic habit, the schema. The brethren resolved to carry the sick elder to Сума, where the monastery kept a подворье, and the devil raised a crowd of dangers on the road; but by the intercession of the venerable Зосима Соловецкий they came safely to the place. There, to the brethren’s horror, he died. After earnest prayer to Зосима the dead man came back to life, was tonsured into the schema, communicated of the Holy Mysteries, and fell asleep again in the Lord.

More than a hundred years later his coffin came to the surface of the ground, and God glorified him with healings. The shorter reading puts the beginning of those wonders in 1688, at his grave under the floor of the Никольская church of the town of Сума in the Архангельская diocese. The day line dates him «XV-XVI» and the calendar gives him no year of his own; nothing further is written here.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 14 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260614.html) and [his lives](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/id765.htm); read 9 October 2026.*
