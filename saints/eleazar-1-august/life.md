# Eleazar (1 August)

saint.gr's calendar for 1 Αυγούστου names him and gives no life. Its whole notice of him is one sentence: «Ο Άγιος Ελεαζάρος μαρτύρησε, αφού του έκαψαν το κεφάλι» — Saint Eleazar was martyred after they had burned his head. There is no country, no century, no emperor, no judge and no hymn. What the Greek calendar keeps of him is therefore a name, a day and a manner of death, and nothing else at all.

Besides that the page prints the gloss on the name — ΕΛΕΑΖΑΡ, a Hebrew word, the help of God — and the couplet of the synaxarion, which is the only other thing it says of him: «Καυτηριασθεὶς Ἐλεάζαρος κάραν, / Ψυχοβλαβῶν ἀπῆλθε κρείττων ῥευμάτων» — Eleazar, cauterised in the head, went away stronger than the streams that harm the soul. The Maccabees' teacher Eleazar is kept in his own entry on this same day and is a different man; nothing here is claimed about the two.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2183/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
