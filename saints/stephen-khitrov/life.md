# Stephen (Khitrov)

The calendar's line is all there is of him. For 5 августа ст. ст. it prints «Сщмч. Стефана пресвитера (1920).» — the hieromartyr Stephen the presbyter, 1920 — and behind the forename the name link gives his surname and his order, so that the calendar's own fullest form of him is Стефан (Хитров), иерей. There is no life page, no diocese, no parish, no place and no manner of death.

The day line gives him «пресвитера» and the name link «иерей», which are the same order under two words, the first Greek and the second Russian. What the calendar records of him is a name, a priesthood, a day and the year 1920, which is early: of the men this day keeps from the persecution he is the first.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 5 августа ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260805.html); read 13 October 2026.*
