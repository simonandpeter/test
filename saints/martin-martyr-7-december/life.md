# Martin (7 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 7 December keeps three martyrs in one entry and names all three: [Priscus](/saints/priscus-martyr-7-december), Martin and [Nicholas](/saints/nicholas-martyr-7-december). Of Martin it says only that he was martyred after being cut to pieces with an axe. No country, no reign, no persecutor and no year is printed for him or for the other two.

The page gives each man his own couplet, and his is about the axes: with axes they chop up the flesh of Martin, the impious men, in the shambles of human flesh. The festival of all three, the page adds, is kept near the wall of the Blachernae. The Greek calendar keeps other men of this name on other days, which the corpus already holds, and nothing here joins this one to any of them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3196/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
