# Simeon Ștefan of Transylvania

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 24 aprilie reads «Sfântul Ierarh Simion Ștefan, Mitropolitul Transilvaniei», with the cross that marks the calendar's higher rank of service, and his own page on the site carries no Viață tab and no Tropar tab — only icons, photographs, headlines of articles about the feast, and the readings appointed for the day, the epistle from Hebrews and the gospel from Matthew 5.

What the site does print about him is in another saint's life. The life of [Sava Brancovici](/saints/sava-brancovici-of-transylvania) says that Sava was set on the metropolitan throne at Alba Iulia in 1656 «în locul răposatului ierarh Simeon Ștefan» — in the place of the reposed hierarch Simeon Ștefan. So he held the metropolia of Transylvania before Sava and was already dead by 1656; the page prints no year for his birth or his death, and none is set down here.

The headlines the site keeps on his page record that the Holy Synod decided the canonisation of the metropolitans of Transylvania Andrei Șaguna and Simion Ștefan, and that the solemn proclamation of his canonisation was held on a Sunday; the pages themselves were not opened, and no date is taken from them.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 24 aprilie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/24-aprilie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-ierarh-simion-stefan-mitropolitul-transilvaniei), which prints no life, together with [the life of Sava Brancovici](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-ierarh-sava-brancovici); read 24 September 2026.*
