# Simeon Ștefan of Transylvania

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 24 aprilie reads «Sfântul Ierarh Simion Ștefan, Mitropolitul Transilvaniei», with the cross that marks the calendar's higher rank of service, and his own page on the site carries no Viață tab and no Tropar tab — only icons, photographs, headlines of articles about the feast, and the readings appointed for the day, the epistle from Hebrews and the gospel from Matthew 5.

What the site does print about him is in another saint's life. The life of [Sava Brancovici](/saints/sava-brancovici-of-transylvania) says that Sava was set on the metropolitan throne at Alba Iulia in 1656 «în locul răposatului ierarh Simeon Ștefan» — in the place of the reposed hierarch Simeon Ștefan. So he held the metropolia of Transylvania before Sava and was already dead by 1656; the page prints no year for his birth or his death, and none is set down here.

The headlines the site keeps on his page record that the Holy Synod decided the canonisation of the metropolitans of Transylvania Andrei Șaguna and Simion Ștefan, and that the solemn proclamation of his canonisation was held on a Sunday; the pages themselves were not opened, and no date is taken from them.

The Russian calendar keeps him too, and marks the fact that it is keeping somebody else’s saint: the line reads «Свт. Симеона Трансильванского (1656) (Рум.).», and «(Рум.)» is its own mark for a Romanian commemoration, as «(Серб.)» is for a Serbian one. It gives no life. The line carries a link to his name and none to a reading, and the name page behind it prints his title in full — «Святитель Симеон, митрополит Трансильванский», the hierarch Simeon, metropolitan of Transylvania — the single day 24 апреля, and nothing more.

Two small things come of it. The calendar writes the see out where the line abbreviates it, and it prints neither a family name nor the Ștefan this folder’s own display name carries. And it gives a year, 1656, which is the first year of any kind this folder has had from any calendar; it is recorded as the year of his repose, which is what the shape of such a line means in this calendar, and its basis is set as traditional because a year off a bare line is weaker than a year out of a life.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 24 aprilie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/24-aprilie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-ierarh-simion-stefan-mitropolitul-transilvaniei), which prints no life, together with [the life of Sava Brancovici](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-ierarh-sava-brancovici); read 24 September 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 24 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260424.html), which names him and gives no life, read 8 October 2026.*
