# Alexander (Parusnikov) (14 June)

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 14 июня ст. ст. carries five names on one line and his is the third of them: «Сщмч. Иосифа и Димитрия пресвитеров (1918) Сщмчч. Александра , Павла пресвитеров и Николая диакона (1938).» The line gives the forenames in the genitive, two grade-class words for the five together, and two years — 1918 for the first two names and 1938 for the last three — and it gives no surname to any of them. The name link behind his place in it supplies the calendar’s own nominative with the surname, «Александр (Парусников), протоиерей, сщмч.», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from.

That is everything this calendar records of him. He has no life page, no icon, no hymn, no place, no age and no parish anywhere on it, and the name page behind his place on the line carries two days and no text at all. The second of those days is «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia; that is a synaxis and not a second feast, and he does not render on it. Nothing is reasoned here from the surname.

The line’s «Сщмчч.» and «пресвитеров» make him a hieromartyr and a presbyter, and the link’s «протоиерей» is recorded as his office, Archpriest. He is given no relation to the four others on the line: they share a line, a grade-class and a year, and the calendar prints nothing at all that says they died in one place or knew one another.

The calendar prints this same name string on a second name page, 9259, whose days are «26 января (Новомуч.) , 3 ноября» and whose line, on that other day, dates its archpriest 1937. Neither page carries a life, a place or a parish, so there is nothing by which the two entries could be compared beyond the day and the year, and they are kept apart because the source keeps them apart. This folder is the 14 июня entry and nothing is said here about the other.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 14 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260614.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/11398.html); read 9 October 2026.*
