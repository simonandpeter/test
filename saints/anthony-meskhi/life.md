# Anthony Meskhi

saint.gr's calendar for 16 Μαρτίου names him «Άγιος Αντώνιος εκ Γεωργίας», the holy Anthony out of Georgia, and the page behind that line holds one sentence about him: that the holy Anthony, whose surname it gives in brackets as Meskhi, lived in the thirteenth century in Georgia, in the years of the ruler [Demetrius II](/saints/demetrius-of-georgia) — whom the same site keeps on 12 Μαρτίου and whom it calls the Self-sacrificed — that he worked as a missionary, and that he fell asleep in peace.

Nothing else is given: no birthplace, no monastery, no field of that mission, no year of his own, no relic and no hymn, and the rest of the page is the calendar's note on the name Anthony. The icon it prints shows him together with [Poimen](/saints/poimen-of-georgia), the fool for Christ whom the calendar keeps on this same day and in the same century; the page says nothing of any tie between them beyond that, and the corpus records none.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 16 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/16/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3904/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
