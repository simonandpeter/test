# John (Nikolsky, 24 November)

The calendar's own line is the whole of what is recorded of him here. The Сретенский monastery's calendar for 24 ноября ст. ст. names him third of five priests in «Сщмчч. Александра , Алексия , Иоанна , Корнилия и Митрофана пресвитеров (1937)», and the name page behind that third name gives him his surname and his grade: «Иоанн (Никольский), иерей, сщмч.» — a priest and a hieromartyr. The calendar carries no life page for him, and azbyka.ru's own line for the civil 7 декабря names him and stops.

So nothing is said here of where he was born, where he served, when or by whom he was arrested, or where he died: no page read says any of it. The year 1937 is the year the day line closes the clause with, and it is given for all five men at once.

The name is not a rare one in these years, and this corpus already keeps two priests who answer to it with other surnames. It also keeps another Никольский of the same forename, on 21 сентября ст. ст., whose life is written out of azbyka.ru and who was born in 1868 at Кладбищи in the province of Нижний Новгород and shot at Горький on 4 October 1937: a different name page, a different day and a different dossier.
