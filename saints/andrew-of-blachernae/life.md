# Andrew of Blachernae

saint.gr keeps a martyr Andrew on 28 November and gives him two sentences. He was an ascetic, it says, and lived in a cell near Blachernae; and he was a fellow-contestant of the venerable Stephen the Confessor, [Stephen the New](/saints/stephen-the-new), whose own page it keeps on the same day. Andrew was martyred for the holy icons, dragged along the ground by the iconoclasts until he died.

That is the whole of the page's account. It gives him no country, no monastery, no year and no emperor by name, and prints no hymn for him; what it prints besides is his couplet, which turns his death into his confession — «Ἐκ γῆς ἐπλάσθην, γῆ με δὴ καὶ κτεινάτω. Πλάστου γὰρ αὐτῆς Ἀνδρέας τιμῶ τύπους»: out of earth I was moulded, let earth then kill me, for I, Andrew, honour the images of its Moulder.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 28 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/28/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3099/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
