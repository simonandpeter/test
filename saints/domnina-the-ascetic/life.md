# Domnina the Ascetic

Domnina was born of well-believing and wealthy parents, and gave herself to God from infancy, taking up the labours of the desert and the restraint of every bodily impulse. She made herself a hut beside her mother's garden and passed day and night in it, weeping without ceasing, so that her tears wet not only her cheeks but her clothing as well, for the clothing she used was made of hair.

At cockcrow she would go into the church and offer God a hymn of praise together with the whole crowd of the faithful. Her food was lentils softened in water, and she bore the whole labour of that fasting although her body was frail and she was, the life says, all but dead while yet alive.

She covered her face carefully with a cloth, so that neither could she see anyone's face nor anyone hers. Her hair garment covered her to the knees, and she spoke easily and gently with all who came to her. Living so, day and night, she was raised up at the last, with the works that had pleased God, to Christ her beloved Bridegroom. doxologia gives her no country, no year and no hymn.
