# Artemidorus (20 September)

Artemidorus was martyred by the sword together with [Thalus](/saints/thalus-the-martyr).
That is the whole of the Greek notice for 20 September. He is not the Artemidorus the same
calendar keeps on 8 September.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 20 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/981/saint.aspx); read 26 August 2026.*
