# Moses (2 December)

The Greek calendar keeps a confessor Moses on the second of December, and the whole of saint.gr's entry for him is an account of where his memory is and is not. He is unknown, it says, to the Synaxarist of Saint Nikodemos — «Ο Άγιος Μωυσής (ή Μωσής) ο Ομολογητής είναι άγνωστος στον Συναξαριστή του Αγίου Νικόδημου» — and he is commemorated as Confessor on the second of December in the Synaxarist of Delehaye.

Then it offers a guess and marks it as one: perhaps he is the same as the Moses the Steward whom the Parisian Codex 1578 mentions on the third of December. Perhaps; the page does not decide it, and nothing is decided here. He has no country, no century, no office beyond the rank word Confessor, no account of what he confessed or suffered, no relic, no couplet and no hymn. What the calendar attests is the day and the rank.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 2 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/02/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3128/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
