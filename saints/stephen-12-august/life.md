# Stephen (12 August)

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for the day is «Όσιοι Σέργιος και Στέφανος», the venerable [Sergius](/saints/sergius-12-august) and Stephen, and the whole of its notice is one sentence: «Οι Όσιοι Σέργιος και Στέφανος απεβίωσαν ειρηνικά.» The venerable Sergius and Stephen departed in peace. The page gives no year, no country, no monastery and no hymn, and it says nothing of what joined the two men beyond the one line that keeps them together.

What it does print is the distich the Greek books give the pair, and the pun of it is on this name: «Ἴσων ἱδρώτων, Σεργίῳ καὶ Στεφάνῳ, / Ἴσοι στέφανοι· καὶ γὰρ οὕτω τὸ πρέπον» — for equal sweat, equal crowns to Sergius and Stephen; for so it is fitting. The page's gloss reads the name the same way, as the victor worthy of the crown of victory, and that is the only characterisation of him the source supplies.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/12/index.aspx) and [the life of the pair](https://www.saint.gr/786/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
