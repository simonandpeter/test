# Vladimir (Ryasensky)

**The one name on this day the calendar dates to 1932, and the only one of the eighteen of the Soviet years here who was not shot.** The Сретенский monastery’s calendar gives him a sentence to himself — «Сщмч. Владимира пресвитера (1932).» — before the company of sixteen it closes with 1937, and days.pravoslavie.ru hangs a full life behind his name page. He was born in 1891 in the town of Осташков in the province of Tver, son of a priest, Фёдор Рясенский. Out of the Tver seminary he was ordained priest to the church of the village of Ясеновичи in the Вышний Волочёк district, moved at his own petition on 2 September 1916 to the church of the churchyard of Волго in the Осташков district, and in the twenties served in the Знаменский church in Осташков itself. The Cheka called him in many times and demanded with threats that he stop preaching, and every time he answered: «Произносил и произносить буду» — I have preached and I shall go on preaching.

**The case against him was built on a funeral.** In November 1929 one of the oldest priests of Осташков died, an archpriest who had been dean there for many years, and the respect for him was such that the burial brought almost the whole town into the church; the procession from the cathedral to the cemetery stretched across half the town. Many priests preached in his memory, and this one said that the dead man had suffered much persecution from the authorities, had been among the first they went after, and had been sentenced in 1918 to thirty years’ imprisonment, which had ruined his health and hastened his end. That sermon, with others preached over four days of memorial services, went into the file.

**The informant invented the conspiracy to cover his own absences.** A harness-maker of Осташков, wanting a respectable excuse for trips to Moscow away from a strict wife, told her and his brother-in-law that he was going to secret counter-revolutionary religious meetings in the Житный monastery, that there was an organisation calling itself «Красный якорь», the Red Anchor, with a seal bearing an anchor, that the meetings were held in the monastery cellar and that sentries were posted for safety. He did not trouble to invent what was said at them or who belonged. The brother-in-law wrote it all to the Осташков GPU, was called in at the end of December 1929, and confirmed it.

**Under interrogation he gave the investigators nothing.** In the summer of 1930 the GPU arrested the chairman of the church council and several others active in church life, and him with them. In prison he was cruelly used — beaten, and the hair of his head pulled out a hair at a time — and the life says his courage was astonishing: he did not deny God and did not admit the invented crimes. Asked about the clergy he knew he said he knew them but had never talked politics with them, that he was himself apolitical; asked about the council chairman he said he knew him as the cathedral warden and went to him only on parish business. The charge as drawn was that he had said the breaking of religion by the organs of power led to the fall of the people’s culture and the ruin of Russia. None of the accused admitted the state crimes laid on them, the file would not bear the least examination, and the OGPU officer decided it was «нецелесообразно» to send such a case to open court.

**He died in a camp, on the feast whose afterfeast is the day he is kept on.** On 26 December 1930 the troika of the OGPU gave the doctor of the case ten years in a camp, him and the cathedral precentor five years each, the harness-maker three, and the warden five years of exile to the Northern Country. On 30 January 1931 the priest, the precentor and two laymen went under escort to the camps of Мариинск. The hard conditions of the investigation prison at Великие Луки, the journey through the transit prisons and work beyond his strength told, and a year and a half later he was dead: the life closes «Священник умер в день праздника Введения во храм Пресвятой Богородицы, 4 декабря 1932 года» — on the feast of the Entry of the Theotokos, 4 December 1932, which in that year was the Julian 21 ноября, the day before the one he is commemorated on. He was numbered among the saints at the Jubilee Council of Bishops in August 2000.
