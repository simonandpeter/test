# Aristokles the Athonite

Alexis Ambrosiev was born in 1846 in Orenburg, in the Urals. After his wife’s death in 1876 he left for the Holy Mountain and entered the monastery of St Panteleimon, where he was tonsured on 12 March 1880 with the name Aristokles; in 1884 he was ordained deacon and priest, and in 1886 he received the great schema.

In 1887 he was sent to the monastery’s metochion in Moscow, where he became its new builder and the spiritual father of many; he was there from 1891 to 1894, back at the monastery from 1895 to 1909, and in Moscow again from 1909 to 1918. He was adorned with the gifts of foresight and of healing, and his self-giving love softened the hardest hearts; a crowd of people in need found their salvation beside him, and he was justly called the Elder of Moscow.

He died on 24 August 1918 in his cell at the metochion, having crossed himself three times before the icon of the Mother of God Gorgoypekoos, to which he had built a church. He was buried beneath that church; in 1923, to keep his body from desecration, it was moved to the Danilov monastery, as he had foretold. The Moscow Patriarchate enrolled him among the saints in 2001, and in 2004 his relics were brought back to the metochion’s church.
