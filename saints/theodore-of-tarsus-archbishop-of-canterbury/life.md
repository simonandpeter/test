# Theodore of Tarsus, Archbishop of Canterbury

Theodore came from Tarsus and became archbishop of Canterbury in England in 668; he fell asleep in 690. That is the whole of the Greek synaxarion’s notice for 19 September, which links him with Cuthbert, Mildred, Adrian abbot of Canterbury and Brithwald archbishop of Canterbury; this entry records it and nothing more.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 19 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/986/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
