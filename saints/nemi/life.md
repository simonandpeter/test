# Nemi

saint.gr's calendar for 31 December keeps the holy Nemi, and its whole account is one sentence with a parenthesis after it: «Η Αγία Νέμη μαρτύρησε δια ξίφους. (Κατ' άλλους, πρόκειται για Άγιο, τον Άγιο Νέμη)» — the holy Nemi was martyred by the sword; by others, this is a man, Saint Nemi. The page's own line and its own sentence make her a woman and it reports the other reading without taking it, and that is how this folder keeps her.

There is nothing else: no century, no country, no city, no judge and no hymn. The couplet set over the entry gives the sword again and nothing more: «Ανήρ ξιφήρης ου δεδίττεται Nέμην, / Kαν το ξίφος θήξειεν εισέτι πλέον» — the man with the sword does not frighten Nemi, even if he should sharpen the sword still further.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 31 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/31/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1511/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
