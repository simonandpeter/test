# Anastasia (Andreyevna)

saint.gr's whole entry for this Anastasia is one sentence, and it is mostly a statement of what the site does not know: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο και το Μαρτύριο της Ρωσίδας Αγίας Αναστασίας (Αντρέγιεβνα) παρά μόνο ότι ήταν διά Χριστόν σαλή» — we have no details of the life and martyrdom of the Russian saint Anastasia (Andreyevna) except that she was a fool for Christ. The day's own line adds the one word «η Νεομάρτυς», the new martyr.

So three things are recorded here and no more: that she was Russian, that she bore a patronymic the site renders in Greek letters as Αντρέγιεβνα, and that her way of life was the folly for Christ's sake. There is no year of birth and none of death, no city, no monastery, no account of her martyrdom, no relic, no distich and no hymn. The site does not say in which persecution or century she suffered, and nothing is supplied here that it does not print.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3783/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
