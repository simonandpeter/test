# Sophia of Slutsk

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names her in one line for 19 марта ст. ст.: «Прав. Софии, кн. Слуцкой (1612).» The line gives the rank «Прав.», righteous, the title «кн.» for a princess, the place Slutsk, and the year 1612, and nothing else — no day of birth, no parentage, no narrative. Behind it the calendar's own name page 2678 carries the fuller form «София Слуцкая, княжна» and carries no life either.

What the calendar does give her besides the line is a troparion, and the hymn is the only thing in this entry that says anything about her beyond her rank and her town. It calls her blessed mother, says that God honoured her with the incorruption of her relics and with the shining of wonders, and asks her, in remembrance of her righteous life, to obtain health for the body and salvation for the soul. So the Church of this calendar keeps her as a princess whose body was found whole and at whose tomb wonders were counted.

The name page lists two days besides this one, «8 июня» and «8 июня (Белорус.)», the second marked as the Belarusian church's own; her row stands on the 19 марта this day page printed her on, and the June days are recorded on it. azbyka.ru, which has lives for many of the saints of these centuries, was not read for her, and no other calendar in this corpus was consulted. So the line, the name page and the troparion are the whole of what this folder knows.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 19 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260319.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/2678.html), which carries no life; read 8 October 2026.*
