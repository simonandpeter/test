# Achaemenides

saint.gr's calendar for 3 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιος Αχαιμενίδης», and sets him between two thrones: he lived in the years of Theodosius the Younger, emperor of Byzantium, and of Yazdegerd, king of the Persians, about the year 410. He was the son of a distinguished Persian family and had wealth and offices of his own.

It was not long, the page says, before he learned of the Christian faith and came into its bosom, leaving behind the religion of his fathers and the family property with it. The king heard of it and made every effort to persuade him back to his first religion; and when he saw that he could not tempt him, he ordered him tortured and publicly humiliated.

Seeing that after all the torments he had borne Achaemenides would still not renounce Christianity, the king set him free on the condition that he leave Persia; and the saint died in peace. That is why this folder calls him a confessor and not a martyr. The epigram over him says that, longing for God the Word, he was careless both of his father's glory and of the torments. No hymn is printed, and the page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου».

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/03/index.aspx) and [his notice](https://www.saint.gr/2872/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
