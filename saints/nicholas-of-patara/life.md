# Nicholas of Patara

The Ohrid Prologue gives him his own heading on the Julian 6 December, directly after his nephew's, and the whole of his entry is one sentence: the uncle of the great saint Nicholas, who both set him on the spiritual life and ordained him priest. The rest of what this book knows about him stands in that nephew's entry above. There [Nicholas of Myra](/saints/nicholas-of-myra) is said to have learnt the spiritual life from his uncle Nicholas, bishop of Patara, and to have been tonsured in the monastery of New Sion, which that same uncle had founded.

So the book makes him three things and no more — a bishop of Patara in Lycia, the founder of a monastery, and the teacher and ordainer of the most widely honoured bishop in the Church. It gives him no year, no century and no reign, and nothing of his own episcopate; the 343 it prints a few lines above belongs to his nephew. Where the source has one line, this folder says so and says what the line says.

*After the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 6. децембар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2025-12-19&prolog=1); read 7 October 2026.*
