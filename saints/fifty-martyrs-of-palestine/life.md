# The Fifty Martyrs of Palestine

The fifty martyrs came from a country called Zoora. They were denounced to the duke of the place as Christians; at first they were sent to the mines as labourers, but since they persisted in their faith they were at last burned alive in the fire — God-minded men out of good counsel, the verse says, despise the fire out of good daring. The Greek calendar keeps them on 17 September.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 17 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2461/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
