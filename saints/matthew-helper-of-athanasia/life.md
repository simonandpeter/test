# Matthew, helper of Athanasia of Aegina

saint.gr says the venerable Matthew, or Matthias as it also spells him, lived in the ninth century and bound his life to that of [Athanasia of Aegina](/saints/athanasia-of-aegina). When she decided to live as a nun in a quiet place, the page says, she used Matthew as her helper: it was he who carried to the women ascetics what they needed for their keep, bought with the money from the handwork that Athanasia sold.

To this blessed Matthew there came a man all of whose joints were paralysed. The venerable one had pity on him, took off the cloak he was wearing and laid it on the paralytic's shoulders; whereupon his bones cracked terribly and the man was healed at once. Another man, whose face had been disfigured by a devilish working, he crossed with his own hand and granted him healing.

So Matthew shone with wonders and signs in the place of his stillness and his asceticism, and fell asleep in peace. The page gives him no parentage, no year of his own, no monastery and no burial place, and it prints neither couplet nor hymn.
