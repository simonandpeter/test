# Aed of Ferns

Aed was born at Inisbrefny, an island in Lough Templeport, about the year 550, saint.gr says; his father was called Setna and his mother Eithne. He was one of the first bishops of the town of Ferns in Ireland, and he founded monasteries at Drumlane, near Milltown, at Ferns, at Disert-Nairbre and at Rossinver. He fell asleep on 31 January 632.

That is the whole of what the Greek calendar prints of him, and it prints his name and every one of those places in Latin letters rather than in Greek — so no Greek form of his name is recorded in this folder, because the source gives none. There is no apolytikion and no kontakion, nothing about his episcopate beyond its being early, and nothing about his repose beyond its date.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 31 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/31/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2686/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
