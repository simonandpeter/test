# The Six Fathers (15 January)

saint.gr's calendar for 15 Ιανουαρίου keeps six fathers together, «Άγιοι Έξι Πατέρες», and names not one of them. The page behind the line is three sentences long and two of them are refusals: «Είναι άγνωστο, που και πότε οι Άγιοι έξι Πατέρες έζησαν. Κοιμήθηκαν με ειρήνη» — it is unknown where and when the six holy Fathers lived; they fell asleep in peace.

The third sentence sends the reader on: their memory is repeated on 4 January, and there they are spoken of as six Martyrs rather than as fathers. The page does not reconcile the two ranks, and neither is corrected here.

Over them it prints a couplet: six translucent souls, having flown away out of this life, stand together with the six-winged Minds. There is no place, no century, no name and no hymn; and because the calendar enumerates nobody, this is one entry and not six.
