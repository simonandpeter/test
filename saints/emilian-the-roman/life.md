# Emilian the Roman

saint.gr keeps three martyrs together on this day and gives them a single sentence: the holy Emilian the Roman, [James](/saints/james-companion-of-emilian) and [Marianus](/saints/marianus-companion-of-emilian) were martyred at Rome in the year 259. That is the whole notice. The page's head repeats the year over each of the three as «(; - 259)», and says nothing else at all — no manner of death, no persecutor, no rank, no relic, no hymn, not even the distich the site usually prints.

So four things stand recorded here and no more: his name, his epithet, the city, and the year. The epithet and the city say the same thing twice, and the year is in the persecution of Valerian, which the page does not name. Nothing distinguishes him further, and nothing is added here from anywhere else.

The corpus keeps three other saints of this name, none of whom this page's man is: a confessor kept by the Romanian calendar in January, a hieromartyr bishop of Trebia kept by the Russian and Serbian calendars in August, and Emilian of Durostorum. This Emilian is the one with Rome, the year 259, and two companions beside him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3876/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
