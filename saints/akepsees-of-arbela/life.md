# Akepsees of Arbela

saint.gr's calendar for 11 December keeps two martyrs in one entry and names both: [Aeithalas](/saints/aeithalas-of-arbela) and Akepsees. They came from the city of Arbela in Persia, the page says, and Aeithalas was a bishop and Akepsees a deacon. Their warm and living faith drove them to preach the Gospel daily and to draw souls to Christ the Saviour.

They were denounced to the Persian king; and having stayed unshaken in the Christian faith, after dreadful tortures they were beheaded. The couplet set over the entry gives Akepsees the words, and he speaks them to his bishop: seeing me suffering by the sword, be jealous, Aeithalas — I was jealous of you.

Nothing else is printed of him: no parentage, no age, no relic and no hymn. What the page adds of Aeithalas out of Saint Nicodemus's Synaxaristes, that he had first been a priest of the idols, it does not say of this deacon.

days.pravoslavie.ru keeps the two of them on the same menologion day and prints one short reading for the pair, which is the first account either folder has had. They were Persians, it says. Akepsees was a pagan priest in the city of Arbela; having been healed of a sickness by the prayers of a Christian bishop, he turned to the faith of Christ and confessed it boldly, and for that he was thrown into prison. Soon a deacon of the church of Arbela, the holy Aeithalas, was shut in the same prison. The martyrs were brought before the governor, confessed their faith again in front of him, and were beheaded. The Russian line is the only one of the two calendars that gives a year: «(354)».

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/11/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3166/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 11 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261211.html) and [the life of the pair](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life3103.htm), read 13 October 2026.*
