# Akepsees of Arbela

saint.gr's calendar for 11 December keeps two martyrs in one entry and names both: [Aeithalas](/saints/aeithalas-of-arbela) and Akepsees. They came from the city of Arbela in Persia, the page says, and Aeithalas was a bishop and Akepsees a deacon. Their warm and living faith drove them to preach the Gospel daily and to draw souls to Christ the Saviour.

They were denounced to the Persian king; and having stayed unshaken in the Christian faith, after dreadful tortures they were beheaded. The couplet set over the entry gives Akepsees the words, and he speaks them to his bishop: seeing me suffering by the sword, be jealous, Aeithalas — I was jealous of you.

Nothing else is printed of him: no parentage, no age, no relic and no hymn. What the page adds of Aeithalas out of Saint Nicodemus's Synaxaristes, that he had first been a priest of the idols, it does not say of this deacon.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/11/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3166/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
