# Germanus of Auxerre

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him and gives no life. Its line for 31 июля ст. ст. reads «Свт. Германа, епископа Осерского (448).» — the hierarch Germanus, bishop of Auxerre, who died in 448 — and behind that line the calendar's own name page is titled «Святитель Герман епископ Осерский, епископ Осерский, свт.», which prints the see twice over. That doubling is the page's own and is not a second office.

Nothing else is printed. There is no life page, no year of birth, no place and no manner of death, and the day page carries no hymn under his name: its troparia are for the metropolitan the calendar heads the day with, for the righteous man of Cappadocia, and for the Cross of the forefeast. The see, the rank and the year are the whole of what the calendar keeps of him.

He is not new to this corpus, though the folder is. Five lives already written here from Western sources name him in their prose — those of [Alban of Britain](/saints/alban-of-britain), [Genevieve of Paris](/saints/genevieve-of-paris), [Ninnoca of Brittany](/saints/ninnoca-of-brittany), [Palladius of Scotland](/saints/palladius-of-scotland) and [Priscus, martyred at Besançon](/saints/priscus-martyr-at-besancon) — and each of them had him in its story with no folder to point at. What those five say of him stands in them, read from their own sources; none of it is repeated or added to here.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 31 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260731.html); read 13 October 2026.*
