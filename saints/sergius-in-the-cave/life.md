# Sergius in the Cave

saint.gr's calendar for 7 Οκτωβρίου names Σέργιος «ὁ ἐν τῷ Σπηλαίῳ», Sergius in the Cave, and gives him two sentences and nothing more. It says that he was of Greek descent; that before he took up the ascetic life in the Lavra of the Caves he had lived as an ascetic on the Holy Mountain; and that he fell asleep in 1412. The head of the page writes his dates as «(; - 1412)», with the year of his birth unknown.

That is the whole of the record. The page names no office and no abbacy, gives no place in either house, tells nothing of how he came from the Holy Mountain to the Caves, and prints no hymn for him. It carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2699/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
