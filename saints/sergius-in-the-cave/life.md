# Sergius in the Cave

saint.gr's calendar for 7 Οκτωβρίου names Σέργιος «ὁ ἐν τῷ Σπηλαίῳ», Sergius in the Cave, and gives him two sentences and nothing more. It says that he was of Greek descent; that before he took up the ascetic life in the Lavra of the Caves he had lived as an ascetic on the Holy Mountain; and that he fell asleep in 1412. The head of the page writes his dates as «(; - 1412)», with the year of his birth unknown.

That is the whole of the record. The page names no office and no abbacy, gives no place in either house, tells nothing of how he came from the Holy Mountain to the Caves, and prints no hymn for him. It carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands.

The Russian calendar keeps him on its own 7 October, and what it says does not fit the entry this folder was written from. Its line is «Прп. Сергия Послушливого, Печерского (ок. XIII)» — Sergius the Obedient, of the Caves, about the thirteenth century — and its index heads him «Сергий Печерский, Послушливый» and gives him two days, this one and 28 September with the fathers of the Near Caves. The life behind the line is three sentences: that he is known to have laboured in the twelfth and thirteenth centuries; that his name shows the distinguishing labours of his life, the renunciation of his own will and perfect obedience to his superior; and that his incorrupt relics rest in the Near, or Antoniev, caves.

The epithet and the day are the Greek entry's own — «ὁ ἐν τῷ Σπηλαίῳ», in the Cave, kept on 7 Οκτωβρίου — but the two details saint.gr adds to them are not. Its Athos tonsure and its year 1412 are what the Russian calendar prints two lines below, on the same day, for another man: [Sergius of Nurma](/saints/sergius-of-nurma), who was tonsured on the Holy Mountain and died in the forests of Vologda. Which of the two the Greek page meant cannot be settled from either page, and nothing here settles it: the folder keeps the Greek entry's name and day, the Russian row records the centuries and the caves the Russian prints, and the death interval was widened to carry both.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2699/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026; and the Russian church calendar's [7 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261007.html) with [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/1973.html) and [the life it reaches](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life6832.htm), read 4 October 2026.*
