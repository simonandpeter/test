# Polyeuctus (1 August)

saint.gr's calendar for 1 Αυγούστου names him and gives no life. Its whole notice of him is one sentence: «Ο Άγιος Πολύευκτος μαρτύρησε, αφού τον έχωσαν μέσα σε κοπριά» — Saint Polyeuctus was martyred after they had buried him in dung. There is no country, no century, no emperor, no judge and no hymn. What the Greek calendar keeps of him is therefore a name, a day and a manner of death, and nothing else at all.

Besides that the page prints the gloss on the name — ΠΟΛΥΕΥΚΤΟΣ, from πολύ and εύχομαι, the much longed-for — and the couplet of the synaxarion, which is the only other thing it says of him: «Ἰὼβ καθέδραν, φημὶ δὴ τὴν κοπρίαν, / Ὁ Πολύευκτος εἶχεν εἰς τιμωρίαν» — Job's seat, I mean the dunghill, Polyeuctus had for his punishment.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2186/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
