# Athenodorus (7 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 7 December keeps the holy Athenodorus, who came from Mesopotamia and lived in the time of the emperor Diocletian, which the page puts at 290. Wonderful, it says, was the strength of soul with which he bore the dreadful torments he was put to for his faith in Jesus Christ; and it names them in order.

First they burned his flesh with torches lit from resin. Then they put red-hot iron balls under his arms. And after that they laid him out on a heated bronze carpet. The patience and endurance he showed drew fifty idolaters, who were watching the torments, to Christianity; and so he gave up his spirit to the Lord and was crowned with the crown of martyrdom. The couplet over his name says that Athenodorus, rejoicing, had his spirit taken from him before ever the sword came near.
