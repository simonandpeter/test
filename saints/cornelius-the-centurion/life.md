# Cornelius the Centurion

Of Cornelius the Acts of the Apostles tell (10:1–13): a God-fearing Roman centurion, to whom an angel of the Lord pointed out that he should meet the apostle Peter; converted to Christianity — the first of the pagans to believe in our Lord Jesus Christ, the Romanian Lives say, who after his baptism left all pagan things and followed Peter — he taught the teaching of Christ in Phoenicia, Cyprus, Antioch and Ephesus, and was chosen bishop of Skepsis in Mysia. His missionary zeal bore rich fruit and many pagans believed the Gospel; some denounced him to the governor Demetrius, who seized him and led him into a pagan temple before a crowd to sacrifice to the idols, and Cornelius not only stayed immovable in his faith but by his confession brought the governor’s wife and son to believe the teaching of the Gospel — he told Demetrius his family would come to no harm, and the governor understood a little later — the synaxarion’s life runs on past the point read here; the [Demetrius, Evanthia and Demetrianus](/saints/demetrius-of-skepsis) kept on 11 September are that family. You lead Cornelius out of the faithless life, the verse says, my Christ, first-fruit of the faithful from the nations. The Greek and Romanian calendars keep him on 13 September, the Romanian with a troparion.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 13 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2420/saint.aspx); The Romanian life is at doxologia.ro — [the page](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-sfintit-mucenic-corneliu-sutasul); read 23 August 2026.*
