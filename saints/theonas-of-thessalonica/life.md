# Theonas of Thessalonica

Theonas was born in the second half of the fifteenth century; by some accounts his birthplace was the island of Lesbos, by others Veria. He began the monastic life on Athos at the monastery of the Pantocrator, at the kathisma of Saint Onuphrius, and was ordained priest there. At that time the venerable Jacob was struggling at the skete of Iviron, and the fame of that father brought Theonas to him to become his disciple; and when Jacob went out into the world to preach the word of God, his disciple followed him.

After his teacher's martyric end, on 1 November 1519, Theonas became the guide of the remaining disciples, and for a short while in 1520 the superior of the monastery of the Honourable Forerunner at Dervekista in Aetolia. Then he returned with Jacob's disciples to the Holy Mountain, to the monastery of Simonos Petras; and after about three years, in 1522, he left the Mountain again and moved to the monastery of Saint Anastasia the Deliverer from Potions, near Thessalonica, which he restored.

As superior there he built a school and a hospital, and several chapels around the monastery — of the Holy Trinity, of the Holy Apostles, of Saints Cyricus and Julitta, of Saint Anthony, of Saint George, of Saint Demetrius and of Saint Elias — and founded a small skete to which he would withdraw for stillness. In that time about a hundred and fifty disciples gathered around him.

For the holiness of his life he was enthroned over Thessalonica around the year 1537, and for about seven years he shepherded the flock entrusted to him with holy wisdom and led many towards salvation. The calendar line for the day calls him archbishop and the life page calls him metropolitan; both words stand in the source and neither is corrected here.

He departed to the Lord on 4 April 1542. His relics are kept whole at the monastery of Saint Anastasia the Deliverer from Potions, where the page says they give off an indescribable fragrance and pour out miracles and healings on those who come to his reliquary in faith. The page adds that his memory is kept on 4 April and also on the fourth Sunday of Great Lent, which moves with Pascha and is not held here.
