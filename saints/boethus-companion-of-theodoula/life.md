# Boethus, companion of Theodoula

saint.gr's calendar for 18 Ιανουαρίου keeps Boethus among those who came to believe through Theodoula — with Evagrius, Macarius, and Helladius the commentaresios. Theodoula lived in the years of Diocletian, whom the page dates 284 to 305, and she was from Diocaesarea of Cilicia. When the prefect Pelagius pressed her with violence to deny Christ, she set against him that she was a Christian and would remain one.

Pelagius then put her to tortures, over which her woman's nature rose by the power of divine grace; and such was the impression made by her most modest and heroic bearing that the prefect's own men, who had been among her tormentors before, declared to him that they acknowledged Jesus Christ as their God. So he threw her into the furnace, together with Evagrius and Macarius. Two others, Helladius and Boethus, were beheaded. The page's headings give the year of all five as 298.

Of Boethus the day gives the name alone and the manner of his death. He believed through [Theodoula](/saints/theodoula-of-diocaesarea) and was beheaded with [Helladius](/saints/helladius-the-commentaresios), and the epigram the page sets over the two of them reads «Θείον Βοηθόν συν Κομενταρησίω, Δόξης κατηξίωσε Χριστού, το ξίφος». No city, no office and no age is printed for him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1126/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
