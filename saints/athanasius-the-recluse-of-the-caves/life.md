# Athanasius the Recluse of the Caves

saint.gr keeps on the second of December a venerable Athanasius whom it calls the Recluse, «ἐν τῷ Σπηλαίῳ» — in the Cave. The whole of its entry for him is one sentence: the venerable Athanasius lived in the Lavra of the Caves and healed those who visited him with faith — «Ο Όσιος Αθανάσιος έζησε στην Λαύρα των Σπηλαίων και θεράπευε όσους τον επισκέπτονταν με πίστη».

Nothing else is there. No century, no name in religion, no abbot, no account of the enclosure he is named for, no single healing told, no relic, no couplet and no hymn. The page does not say which Lavra of the Caves it means, and this folder does not supply one. Three things are attested and no more: the day, the enclosure, and the healings.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 2 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/02/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2604/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
