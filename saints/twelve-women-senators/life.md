# The Twelve Women Senators

saint.gr's calendar for 8 Ιουλίου keeps a company of twelve women of senatorial rank and names none of them. The whole of its account is one sentence: «Οι Αγίες αυτές, προσήλθαν στον Χριστιανισμό από τα θαύματα του Αγίου Προκοπίου (βλέπε 8 Ιουλίου) και μαρτύρησαν δια ξίφους» — these holy women came to Christianity through the wonders of [Procopius](/saints/procopius-of-jerusalem), whom the same day keeps, and were martyred by the sword.

The page gives them no names, no city, no age and no separate deaths, and no year in its text, though it heads them «(; - 303)». It prints no hymn under their line, only the couplet the synaxaria set over them: «Δις έξ ανείλεν ευκλεείς κόρας ξίφος, / Hγουμένας σκύβαλα της γης τα κλέη» — the sword took off twice six noble girls, who reckoned the glories of the earth as refuse. That is everything the source says of them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2048/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
