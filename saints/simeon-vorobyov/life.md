# Simeon (Vorobyov)

He and his brother [Demetrius (Vorobyov)](/saints/demetrius-vorobyov) were born in the village of Klyuchevoye in the Maksatikha district of the province of Tver, to a devout peasant family; their father was called Mikhail. He was born in 1871, his brother in 1873, so that in the persecution of 1937 both were past sixty. Neither was educated to any purpose, the reading says, but both could read and were very well read in the books of the Church, and both had been brought to prayer and to the services from childhood. Both served in the army as private soldiers, he from 1903 to 1907.

Before the revolution he had a small peasant holding — a horse, a cow and a sheep. The holdings were too slight to be worth taking at first, but in the early thirties the brothers were arrested, everything they had was confiscated, and they were sent with their families to the Urals. He had two sons, Nikolai of eleven years and Mikhail of five. A year later they were allowed home, and nothing was given back.

He had been the church warden at Klyuchevoye before the exile, and the priest who came to the village gave him work at the church as its watchman and taught his son Nikolai to sing and read at the kliros, so that the boy soon served as psalm-reader. What the reading says was chief in the two brothers was their faith and their great love of the church, and it was in their two households that the priest lodged and the parish found its support.

On 23 July 1937 he, his brother and his son Nikolai were arrested and shut in the prison at Bezhetsk. The interrogation records are printed in full, and his answers are short: he had been church warden, the priest had lodged with him, his son was psalm-reader and he the watchman; he had carried on no counter-revolutionary activity among the people; he did not admit himself guilty of what he was charged with. On 13 August the NKVD troika sentenced the two brothers to be shot. Nikolai escaped the sentence only because he was seventeen, and was given eight years in the camps. The brothers were shot on 17 August 1937. The reading closes with their glorification among the New Martyrs and Confessors of Russia at the Jubilee Council of August 2000.
