# Alexius of Bithynia

saint.gr's calendar for 10 Ιουνίου names him and gives no life. Its notice is one sentence — «Ο Άγιος Αλέξιος απεβίωσε ειρηνικά. Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του» — that Alexius died peacefully, and that we have no further details of his life. The day's own line is what supplies his see: «Άγιος Αλέξιος επίσκοπος Βιθυνίας», Alexius bishop of Bithynia. The page gives no century, no year and no town, and nothing about how he came to that see.

Above the notice it prints the two verses of the synaxarion for him, «Πόνων ο Aλέξιος αλέξημά τι, / Eύρες τελευτήν και τρυφάς νυν εν πόλω» — a play on his name, which the page glosses from ἀλέξω, to ward off, as the champion or defender: Alexius, some warding-off of pains, you found your end and now your delight in heaven.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/10/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/539/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
