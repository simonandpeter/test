# Victor of Marseille

Victor suffered at Marseille in the third century. After cruel torments he was thrown into prison, where he converted the guards to the faith of Christ, and he died crucified on a cross. That is the whole of the Prologue’s notice, which the Serbian calendar keeps on 23 August (Julian).
