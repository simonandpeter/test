# John (Smirnov) (24 October)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him first of two priests of 1937 on 24 октября ст. ст.: «Сщмчч. Иоанна и Николая пресвитеров (1937).» The line declines his name and gives it without a surname; the title on the name link behind his place supplies both the surname and the grade, «Иоанн (Смирнов), иерей, сщмч.», and that link is the whole of what the calendar has for him, because no life page follows his name.

The surname is one of the commonest in this calendar's companies, and the corpus already holds eight other men who bear it. One of them matters: a priest of this same forename and surname, kept by this same calendar on 27 августа ст. ст. and dead in this same year. The two are separated by grade, by day and by name page — that one an archpriest of Zaraysk, this one a priest, two commemorations on one calendar — so by the rule this wave has settled they are two men, and this folder's slug and display name carry the day to say so.

Nothing on either page says where he was born or served, what he was charged with, or whether he died with [Nicholas](/saints/nicholas-nikolsky), the second of the two names on his line.

The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026, so the line stands as the line. The day page's thirteen troparia and kontakia belong to the four older entries it heads and to the icon it keeps, and none of them to this line.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 24 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261024.html); read 4 October 2026.*
