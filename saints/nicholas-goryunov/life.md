# Nicholas (Goryunov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him in a single line for 9 марта ст. ст., one line for nine persons: «Сщмчч. Михаила, Алексия, Димитрия, Сергия, Сергия пресвитеров и Николая диакона, прмч. Иоасафа и прмцц. Наталии и Александры (1938).» The line declines the forename to «Николая» and gives no surname, no place, no year of birth and no word of narrative; the year it closes with, 1938, is the only year it gives, and the rank word «Сщмчч.» that governs his name is the only rank.

Behind that «Николая» stands the calendar's own name page 9836, which carries the fuller form «Николай (Горюнов), протодиакон, сщмч.» — the surname the day's line leaves out, and a protodeacon's grade. That page lists two days: this 9 марта and «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. It carries no life, and neither does the day page; azbyka.ru, which has lives for some of the martyrs of these years, was not read for him. So what the calendar prints is the whole of what this folder says, and it says it rather than fill the silence: a protodeacon of the Russian church of that name, who died a martyr in 1938 and whom this calendar keeps on this day.

*After the Православный церковный календарь, Сретенский монастырь of the Сретенский monastery for 9 марта ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260309.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9836.html), which carries no life; read 7 October 2026.*
