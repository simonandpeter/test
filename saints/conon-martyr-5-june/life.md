# Conon (5 June)

saint.gr's calendar names him Conon from Rome and gives him two sentences: «Ο Άγιος Κόνων μαρτύρησε δια πνιγμού μέσα στη θάλασσα. Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου.» — the holy Conon was martyred by drowning in the sea; we have no other details of the saint's life. The page admits the gap itself, and nothing here fills it: no year, no persecutor, no city but Rome and no relics.

The couplet above the sentence turns his drowning into an arrival: «Εἰσδὺς Κόνων θάλασσαν εὗρε λιμένα, / Ἄριστα χερσὶν οἰακισθεὶς Κυρίου» — Conon, going down into the sea, found a harbour, steered most excellently by the hands of the Lord. The page also glosses his name, from κονέω, as the impetuous one, and prints no hymn.
