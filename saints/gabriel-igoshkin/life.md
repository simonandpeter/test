# Gabriel (Igoshkin)

He is the last line of the Russian 5 October and the latest death in this corpus's Russian October so far: «Прп. Гавриила исп (1959)» — Gabriel, venerable, confessor, 1959. «Исп.» is the mark of a confessor and not of a martyr, which here means something plain: he was imprisoned and he came out, and he died six years after Stalin rather than in 1937. The site's index of names gives the surname and the office, heading his page «Гавриил (Игошкин), архимандрит, исп.», an archimandrite.

That index keeps him here and on 26 January with the whole company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and prints nothing else: no birth, no monastery, no sentence, no camp, no place of death. The long lives of the twentieth-century Russian saints are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026 even with a browser user-agent, so the line stands as the line, and for a man who lived into the nineteen-fifties that is a conspicuous silence rather than an old one.

*After the Russian church calendar's [5 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261005.html) and [its index entry for him](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9167.html); read 4 October 2026.*
