# Jeremiah of Rome

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names the two of them together on 6 апреля ст. ст. in one short line, «Мчч. Иеремия и Архилия иерея (III).» — the martyrs Jeremiah and the priest Archilius, third century — and the two name links behind it give each of them the city the line leaves out: «Иеремий Римский, мч.» and «Архилий Римский, иерей, сщмч.», Jeremiah a martyr and Archilius a priest and a hieromartyr, both of Rome. No other calendar read for this corpus keeps either of them.

There is a life page behind the line and it is one sentence long. «Святые мученики Иеремий и иерей Архилий (Алхимий) приняли мученическую кончину в III веке» — they received a martyr’s end in the third century; «о них имеется свидетельство святителя Григория Двоеслова», there is a testimony about them from the hierarch whom the Russian church calls the Dialogist, who died in 604 and whose own day the page gives as 12 марта. That is the whole of it. There is no emperor, no judge, no city beside the Roman name on the links, no manner of death, no relic and no hymn.

The one thing the page adds to the day’s line is in its own title, which reads «Святые мученики Иеремий и иерей Архилий (Алхимий)»: the priest’s name is given a second form, «Алхимий», in brackets, and both forms are stored on his folder because both are printed. The witness is not made a companion: a testimony about two martyrs is not a martyrdom with them, and the hierarch who records them takes no `related` row here.

[Archilius of Rome](/saints/archilius-of-rome) is the priest of the pair, and the calendar gives him the rank of a hieromartyr where it gives Jeremiah of Rome the rank of a martyr; that difference of one word between two men on one line is the only thing in the whole entry that tells them apart.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 6 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260406.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life799.htm); read 6 October 2026.*
