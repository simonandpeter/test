# The Soldiers (8 May)

saint.gr's calendar names them and gives no life. Its whole account is one sentence: these holy martyrs — a σπείρα, which the page glosses as a τάγμα, a cohort or company of soldiers — were perfected by beheading. It does not say how many they were, nor under which emperor, nor before what judge, nor in what city; it names not one of them, and it prints no relics and no hymn.

The couplet set over them says only that a cohort minded towards Christ has its heads cut off, and that in dying so it does not imitate the cohort that killed Christ: «Xριστοφρονούσα σπείρα τέμνεται κάρας, / Xριστοκτονούσαν σπείραν ου μιμουμένη». Because the page enumerates nobody, they are kept here as the one company the calendar names and not as folders one by one.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1530/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
