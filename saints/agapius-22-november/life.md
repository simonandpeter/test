# Agapius (22 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 22 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιος Αγάπιος» and gives him two short sentences. He was martyred by the sword. And in the Paris Codex 1578, the page adds, it is noted that he was from Cappadocia; that codex is the whole of its authority for the one fact it has about him beyond the manner of his death.

The couplet set over the notice turns on the word for a skull: for the sake of God, who was stretched out on the Place of the Skull, you stretch out your own skull to the sword, Agapius. The page gives him no city, no year, no emperor, no companions and no hymn, and prints no relics for him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 22 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/22/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3071/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
