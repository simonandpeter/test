# Murin of Corfu

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him among twelve martyrs on 28 апреля, and the life it serves for that whole company gives him one paragraph. The apostles Jason and Sosipater had come to the island of Corfu about the year 63 and had been imprisoned there by its ruler, whose daughter [Kerkyra](/saints/kerkyra) declared herself a Christian when she learned what the martyrs were suffering and gave away all her jewellery to the poor. Her father could not argue her out of it, and the punishment he devised was to shut her up alone in a prison and send in to her a robber and fornicator named Murin, to dishonour the bride of Christ.

What happened instead is the whole of what the calendar tells of him. As the robber came up to the prison door a bear fell upon him. Kerkyra heard the noise, drove the beast off in the Name of Christ, and then healed the man's wounds by her prayer; and after that she taught him the faith of Christ, so that he declared himself a Christian there and then and was put to death on the spot. The calendar names no place he came from, no family and no year of his own, and sets him down in the single line it gives the company as the eighth of twelve.

The name is the Slavonic word for an Ethiopian, and this calendar uses it as his name: the day's line declines it among eleven personal names, the page under his name heads «Мурин Керкирский, мч.» with the rank of martyr, and the title of the life page lists «Мурин» eighth in the same series of twelve. Nothing on either page says whether it began as a description of the man or was the name he was called by, and nothing of the kind is claimed here. Four of the eleven who suffered with him, «Зинон, Евсевий, Неон и Виталий», were burned after Kerkyra's death, when her father set out to kill every Christian on the island; the other seven had died in the cauldron before her, and the calendar prints all twelve in one line.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 28 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260428.html), [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/1583.html) and [the life of the company](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life946.htm); read 8 October 2026.*
