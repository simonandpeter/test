# Sositheus

saint.gr's calendar for 9 December names Sositheus and gives him one line of life: the holy Sositheus was martyred by the sword. The page prints no country, no reign, no persecutor and no year, and it joins him to no other name on the day. What it adds is the couplet set over his name, which plays on the name itself.

The word writes Sositheus among those who were saved, it says, he who was cut down for the God who saves. That is the whole of the entry. The gloss the site sets beneath the name belongs to a tragic poet of Alexandria of the third century before Christ, and nothing of it touches this martyr.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 9 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/09/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3210/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
