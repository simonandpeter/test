# Theophanes (17 May)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 17 mai reads «Sfântul Cuvios Teofan» — the holy venerable Theophanes — with no epithet, no monastery, no country and no century, and his own page on the site carries only that line, the date, and the day's scripture readings. There is no Viață tab and no Tropar tab, and nothing that would tell him from the other men of the name whom the calendars keep.

The display name here carries the day for that reason. The rank word «Cuvios» is the whole of what is said about his manner of life: that he was a monastic. This folder asserts only that the Romanian church keeps a venerable Theophanes on 17 May; whether he is the same as any Theophanes kept elsewhere is an open question that this page cannot settle, and no other calendar was checked.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 17 mai — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/17-mai) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-teofan), which carries no life and no troparion; read 24 September 2026.*
