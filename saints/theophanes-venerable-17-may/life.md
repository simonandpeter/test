# Theophanes (17 May)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 17 mai reads «Sfântul Cuvios Teofan» — the holy venerable Theophanes — with no epithet, no monastery, no country and no century, and his own page on the site carries only that line, the date, and the day's scripture readings. There is no Viață tab and no Tropar tab, and nothing that would tell him from the other men of the name whom the calendars keep.

The display name here carries the day for that reason. The rank word «Cuvios» is the whole of what is said about his manner of life: that he was a monastic. This folder asserts only that the Romanian church keeps a venerable Theophanes on 17 May; whether he is the same as any Theophanes kept elsewhere is an open question that this page cannot settle, and no other calendar was checked.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same civil day, and it is the Greek that gives this bare Romanian line a life. saint.gr has Theophanes and [Nektarios](/saints/nectarius-venerable-17-may) as brothers by the same parents and hieromonks, born and bred of the noble house of the Apsarades at Ioannina late in the fifteenth century. Their own autobiography, it says, begins at the moment they gave themselves to God and makes no mention of their noble descent, their upbringing, their education or their property; their parents and their three sisters took the monastic habit and lived in a cell near the village of the island. The two were schooled at the famous Philanthropinon monastery under the abbot Makarios Philanthropinos, and neither riches nor glory nor learning could overcome their love of God.

Very young, about 1495, they took the holy habit from a venerable elder named Savvas and stayed with him ten years in the hermitage of the Forerunner on the island of Ioannina, until he fell asleep on 9 April 1505. Then they went to the Holy Mountain and were received by the former Ecumenical Patriarch Niphon II, living as a monk at Dionysiou, from whom they asked and received a rule and canon of the monastic state, and who told them to go back to the cell of their repentance. Finding it held by lay lords who claimed founders' rights over it, they withdrew further into the island to a half-ruined hermitage of Saint Panteleimon in a cave above the lake, where a hermit named Antony had been enclosed eighteen years. With the written leave of the metropolitan of Ioannina and of the patriarch Pachomios I they cut away a great piece of rock, filled the hollow with earth and stones, and at their own expense raised the church of the Forerunner in 1506 or 1507, with cells beside it; afterwards they built the hermitage of Saint Nicholas at Lepeno for their parents and sisters. The Greek calendar names the two of them founders of the Varlaam monastery at Meteora, and its head line dates Theophanes's death to 1544; his left palm is kept incorrupt at Varlaam.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 17 mai — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/17-mai) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-teofan), which carries no life and no troparion; read 24 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 17 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/17/index.aspx) and [the Greek life](https://www.saint.gr/1619/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026.*
