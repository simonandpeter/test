# Alexander (Fedoseev)

Alexander Fedoseev served as priest of the church at the Maykor works in the Solikamsk district of Perm province. During the mass repressions in the Perm country in 1918 he was shot by Red Army men, on 4 October 1918 — or, by other sources, on 21 December. azbyka.ru’s life, drawn from the Orthodox Encyclopedia, says no more.

The Russian calendar keeps him on 21 September old style on one line with the priests [Alexis (Stabnikov)](/saints/alexis-stabnikov), [Constantine (Shirokinsky)](/saints/constantine-shirokinsky) and [John (Flerov)](/saints/john-flerov), of 1918; azbyka.ru keeps him also with the saints of the Perm metropolis, and prints no hymns for him.

*After the life printed by the Russian church calendar at azbyka.ru — [the entry](https://azbyka.ru/days/sv-aleksandr-fedoseev); the Sretensky calendar (days.pravoslavie.ru), 21 сентября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260921.html); read 17 September 2026.*
