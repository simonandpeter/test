# Theophilus of Brescia

saint.gr's calendar for 27 Απριλίου names him and gives almost no life. Its whole notice of him is one sentence — «Ο Άγιος Θεόφιλος έζησε τον 5ο αιώνα μ.Χ. και χειροτονήθηκε Επίσκοπος της πόλεως Βρεσκίας της Λομβαρδίας» — that Theophilus lived in the fifth century and was ordained bishop of the city of Brescia in Lombardy. Nothing else is printed: no year of birth, no year of death, no kindred, no act of his episcopate and no manner of his end.

The page adds only its usual gloss on the name, that Θεόφιλος is from Θεός and φίλος and means the God-beloved. The feast type it gives is the plain fixed one, «Σταθερή. Εορτάζει στις 27 Απριλίου εκάστου έτους», and the century and the see are the whole of what the source has to give.
