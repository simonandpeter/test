# Satorninus (7 May)

saint.gr's whole account of the two of them is one sentence: the holy [Rufinus](/saints/rufinus-7-may) and Satorninus were martyred by the sword at Caesarea, of Cappadocia, at the time when the proconsul of Nicomedia Perinios was bringing the martyr [Codratus](/saints/codratus-of-nicomedia) there — and it sends the reader to that man's page on the same day.

The couplet set over them speaks of the pair and not of either by name: the sword dared the cutting of two fellow-contenders, for they had been tempered in the faith of the Lord. The page gives them no year, no judge of their own, no country, no relics and no hymn, and it does not say whether they belonged to Codratus's company or whether his passing through only dated their death. Nothing is claimed here beyond the name in the form the page prints it, the rank, the sword, the city and the day.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/07/index.aspx) and [the life of the pair](https://www.saint.gr/4004/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
