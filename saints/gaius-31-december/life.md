# Gaius (31 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 31 December keeps the venerable Gaius, and gives no life of him. Its whole account is two sentences: «Ο Όσιος Γάιος απεβίωσε ειρηνικά. Δεν έχουμε άλλες πληροφορίες για τον βίο του Αγίου» — the venerable Gaius died in peace; we have no other information about the saint's life. There is no century here, no country, no monastery, no deed and no hymn.

The one other thing the page keeps of him is the couplet set over the entry: «Πολλούς ανέτλη Γάιος θείος πόνους, / Kαι νυν τα λαμπρά των πόνων έχει γέρα» — the divine Gaius bore many labours, and now he has the bright rewards of those labours. That is the ascetic's end stated in verse, and it adds nothing to the two sentences above it.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 31 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/31/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1507/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
