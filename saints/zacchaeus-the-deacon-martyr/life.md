# Zacchaeus the Deacon

The whole of the life doxologia prints for him is two short paragraphs, and this is what they say. The blessed Zacchaeus was brought before the judgement seat of the idolaters with a heavy iron chain on his neck, and when the judge questioned him he confessed Christ before the crowd. For that he was tormented with heavy and cruel torments; then they bound him, threw him into prison and put his feet in the stocks, and stretched so he bore it manfully four days and four nights.

After him the holy Alphaeus, who was full of the Holy Spirit, was brought to the judgement seat too; and when they had torn his whole body with blows and burned his ribs, they put him in prison to suffer the same things there together with his fellow in the contest, Zacchaeus. On the next day both of them were beheaded with the sword, and so the blessed ones received the crown of the contest.

The page gives no year, no place and no see for either of them, and says nothing of Zacchaeus before his arrest beyond the rank the calendar's line gives him, that of deacon.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 18 November, with [Alphaeus](/saints/alphaeus-companion-of-zacchaeus) beside him, and dates the two 307. saint.gr says Zacchaeus was deacon of the Church of Gadeira — a small island of Spain, the page explains, lying in the Atlantic very close to the Spanish mainland. He was dragged in chains to the tribunal of the idolaters and confessed Christ with courage. For that he was tortured hard and thrown into prison, set for four days and nights upon a special instrument of torture.

Afterwards they brought Alphaeus to the tribunal, who was full of the Holy Spirit, and tore his sides; then they burnt his wounds and threw him into the prison, as they had his fellow contestant Zacchaeus. The next day they beheaded them both, in 307, and so their holy souls flew to the throne of God. The verse over Zacchaeus's name plays on his namesake in the Gospel: the Zacchaeus of old poured out half his wealth, but this one, Saviour, pours out all his blood under the sword.

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar keeps the two of them on one line for the Julian 18 November, «Мчч. Закхея, диакона Гадаринской Церкви, и Алфея, чтеца в Кесарии Палестинской (303).», and gives them one life page. They suffered, it says, under the emperor Diocletian, whose reign it puts at 284 to 305. In the cruellest of the persecutions the deacon of the church of Gadara was seized among many others, confessed his faith openly, and under torture did not deny Christ.

That persecution was so cruel, the page goes on, that many could not hold out and, in terror of the tortures, agreed to sacrifice to the gods; and it was in going after them that [Alphaeus](/saints/alphaeus-companion-of-zacchaeus) was taken and locked into the same stocks with him. For the night the martyrs were thrown into prison, where they prayed without ceasing and held each other to the resolution to bear every suffering for the name of Christ and so to find eternal life. In the morning the two were beheaded.
