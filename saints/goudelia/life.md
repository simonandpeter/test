# Goudelia

Goudelia, or Gobdelia, was a Persian Christian and an apostle to her people, the Greek synaxarion says, who brought many unbelievers to salvation in Christ. She lived in the fourth century, when Shapur was king of Persia, about 340. Because she carried on her missionary work so boldly she was arrested and shut up in prison for many years, and suffered terribly there; only divine grace kept her through the damp, the dark and the frequent want even of water, and none of those long torments dimmed her faith. Then they flayed her head, and her endurance still did not give way, and at last she died on a cross.

The synaxarion itself doubts the story. Perhaps, it says, the sources were confused over time, and [Gobdelaas](/saints/gobdelaas-son-of-sapor), who is kept on the same day, became Goudelia or Gobdelia through copyists’ errors, with an identical life; but other sources say that there really was a martyr Goudelia, who died by the sword with no other details of her life, and it calls that the more likely and the more accepted. Its verse has her head brought by the sword to Christ, the head of all.

The Romanian calendar keeps her on the same day. Its life says that she turned many Persians to the Christian faith and was taken by Sapor, king of the Persians; refusing to sacrifice to fire, she was put in prison and tormented with hunger for many years; brought out and still unwilling to deny Christ, she was first flayed of the skin of her head and then nailed fast to a post, and gave up her soul to the Lord.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 29 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2402/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026; the Romanian life is at doxologia.ro — [the page](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfintei-mucenite-gudelia).*
