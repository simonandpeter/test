# Uar (Shmarin)

The Russian calendar names the hieromartyr Uar (Shmarin), bishop of Lipetsk on 10 September of the old style, in his own line after the seventeen of 1937. The calendar links no written life, so this entry records the line and nothing more.

*After the Православный церковный календарь (days.pravoslavie.ru), [10 сентября ст. ст.](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260910.html); read 30 August 2026.*
