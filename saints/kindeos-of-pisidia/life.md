# Kindeos of Pisidia

saint.gr's calendar for 20 Φεβρουαρίου names him and gives almost no life. Its line reads «Όσιος Κινδέος Επίσκοπος Πισιδίας», and the page standing under that line says only this much: that the venerable Kindeos — or Kindaios, for it allows both spellings in the one sentence — was bishop of Pisidia and fell asleep in peace, and that we have no further details of his life.

What it prints beside the line is its couplet, which plays on his being taken out of the earth and turned back towards it again at the bidding of the earth's own maker: «Eκ γης ελήφθης και προς αυτήν εστράφης, Γης δημιουργού τη κελεύσει Kινδέα». It gives him no years, no city, no see beyond the province, no relics and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 20 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/20/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3764/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
