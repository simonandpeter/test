# Constantine of Cornwall

saint.gr's calendar for 9 Μαρτίου names him «Άγιος Κωνσταντίνος της Κορνουάλλης ο Μάρτυρας», Constantine of Cornwall the martyr, and heads him «(520 - 576)». What the page says of him is two sentences: that the holy martyr Constantine was born about the year 520 and was king of Dumnonia in England, and that he was martyred in the year 576. It gives no account of the martyrdom, no place for it and no persecutor.

The rest of that page is a note on the name itself, which it derives from the Latin consto, to stand firm, as the name of one who has a firm and strong character, and a list of its better-known imperial bearers, who have nothing to do with the king in the British west. Of him it adds nothing further: no relic, no church, no hymn. What is recorded here is the commemoration, a kingdom, and the two years the page prints.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 9 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/09/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1252/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
