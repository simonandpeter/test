# Swithn

saint.gr's calendar for 15 July names him and gives no life. The line reads «Άγιος Swithn», in Latin letters inside the Greek listing, and the page behind it says one sentence and stops: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγγλοσάξωνα Αγίου» — we have no details of the life of this Anglo-Saxon saint. There is no year, no place, no rank, no office, no hymn and no account of anything he did.

So what this folder records is exactly that: a saint the Greek calendar keeps on 15 July, whom its own source calls Anglo-Saxon and about whom it declines to say anything further. The spelling is the page's own and has been kept as printed rather than corrected towards any English name it might be short for, because the page gives nothing that would identify him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 15 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/15/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2173/saint.aspx), which prints only that one sentence; read 1 October 2026.*
