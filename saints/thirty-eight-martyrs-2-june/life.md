# The Thirty-eight Martyrs (2 June)

saint.gr's calendar for 2 June keeps thirty-eight martyrs and names none of them. Its one sentence about them is the whole of what is recorded: the thirty-eight holy martyrs bore witness after they had been thrown inside a bath-house and the door was sealed. There is no year here, no city, no emperor and no persecutor — and no name, which is the first thing a reader looks for.

What the page sets above that sentence is a couplet, and the couplet is the only other thing it gives them: in vain is the door of the bath barred against the contestants, for Christ is near, crying out, I am the door. The company is not the thirty-eight martyrs of Thrace whom the same calendar keeps on 23 August; those died by the sword, and the synaxarion there wonders whether they are the martyrs of 20 August. Here there is nothing to wonder about, because there is nothing to compare: a number, a manner of death, and a verse about a door.
