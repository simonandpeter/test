# Maximus (6 March)

saint.gr names the venerable martyr Maximus on this day and gives him one sentence, and the life says exactly that: «Ο Όσιος Μάξιμος μαρτύρησε δια λιθοβολισμού» — the venerable Maximus was martyred by stoning. The rank word Ὁσιομάρτυς on the day's line says he was a monastic, and the manner of the killing is the only other fact the page holds.

There is no year, no country, no city, no emperor, no judge, no monastery, no relic and no hymn; and the etymology block the site sets over the name lists a philosopher of Tyre of the second century and another of the fourth century before Christ, both of them notes on the word rather than statements about him.

The one thing beside the sentence is the distich, and it is a play on the stones: Maximus, looking with his whole mind towards the crowns, was himself a stone against the throwing of the stones. The corpus keeps a good many men of this name — the Confessor, the Greek, of Kapsokalyvia, of Moscow, and the rest — and none of them stands on this day or is named on this page.
