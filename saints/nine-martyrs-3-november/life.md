# The Nine Martyrs (3 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 3 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιοι Εννέα Μάρτυρες», and the page behind that line says one thing only: the holy Nine Martyrs were martyred by the sword. There is no city, no persecutor, no year, and not one of the nine names. The calendar keeps them as a company, and that is the whole of what it tells.

The epigram set over them says a little more than the notice and tells nothing new: with souls unturned, cut down by the hands of men in error, nine men turn the error back on itself. It is from that epigram, «ἄνδρες ἐννέα», that this folder knows the nine were men.

Because the nine are named nowhere, this is one folder and not nine, in the shape the corpus already uses for a company it cannot name. The display name takes the day because the same calendar keeps another company of nine martyrs on 30 October, which saint.gr itself does not vouch for and which this reading therefore refuses. No hymn is printed, and this page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου».

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/03/index.aspx) and [their notice](https://www.saint.gr/2876/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
