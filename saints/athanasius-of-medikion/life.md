# Athanasius of Medikion

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him for 26 октября ст. ст. as «Прп. Афанасия Мидикийского (ок. 814)», and the name link behind that line adds the office the line itself leaves out — «Афанасий Мидикийский, эконом, прп.», the steward of his house. Its short life for him begins with a boy's flight: for the love of the monastic life he left his parents' home in secret, and his father fetched him back by force.

After some time he entered the monastery of Medikion, in Bithynia, with his father's leave this time, and there he stayed. The calendar says one thing more about those years, and it is the thing it thought worth saying: he was a fellow-ascetic of [Nicetas of Medikion](/saints/nicetas-of-medikion), whom it keeps on 3 апреля. He died about the year 814.

On his grave a cypress tree grew up, and by the grace of God healings came from it. That is the whole of what the page gives. It records no birthplace, no parents' names, no year of birth, no manner of death and no hymn of his own — the troparia and kontakia the day page sets belong to the great martyr it heads, to an archbishop of Novgorod, and to the earthquake the day commemorates. Nothing is added here beyond what the page says.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 26 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261026.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4596.htm); read 4 October 2026.*
