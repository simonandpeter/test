# Agathonike of Carthage

saint.gr's calendar names her and gives no life. Its line for the day is «Αγία Αγαθονίκη η Παρθενομάρτυς η εν Καρχηδόνι» — the holy Agathonike, virgin martyr, at Carthage — and the whole of the page behind that line is a single sentence: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο της Αγίας Αγαθονίκης.» We have no details of the life of Saint Agathonike.

So three words are the record: that she was a virgin, that she was a martyr, and that her place was Carthage. The page gives no year, no emperor, no manner of death, no relic and no hymn; it does not even print for her the distich of the Greek verse books that it prints for most of the others of this day, and the gloss it offers on the name is the gloss of the masculine Agathonikos, the glorious victor.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 10 Αυγούστου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/08/10/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/773/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
