# Nicetas the New

saint.gr's calendar for 17 December keeps Saint Nicetas the New, and what its page says is that there is nothing to say: no biographical details of his survive, only a certain discourse of the grand logothete Theodore Mouzalon which refers to him and which is kept at the Great Lavra on the Holy Mountain. That one sentence is the whole of the entry.

So the page gives him no year of birth and none of death, no homeland, no office, no manner of death and no relic, and it prints no hymn for him. Its epigram calls him an athlete new in time but equal to the old in his contests, which is what the by-name the New answers to; and that, with the reference to Mouzalon's discourse, is everything the Greek calendar offers about him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 17 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/17/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3286/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
