# Chionia of Caesarea

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps on 16 July three martyrs of Caesarea in Palestine, and its reading of them is four sentences long. Chionia, her sister Alevtina — whom the calendar also names Valentina — and Paul came, it says, out of Egypt, and in the persecution of the emperor Maximinus, whose reign it gives as 305 to 313, they were brought to Caesarea in Palestine.

There, without the least fear before the governor, they confessed themselves followers of Christ. In the year 308, after merciless torments, the two sisters were burned, and Paul was beheaded.

That is the whole of what this calendar says of her. It gives her no age, no trade, no words of her own and no place of burial, and the epithet its name page adds — «Кесарийская (Палестинская)», of Caesarea in Palestine — is the city she was brought to rather than the country she came from.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 16 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260716.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4244.htm); read 9 October 2026.*
