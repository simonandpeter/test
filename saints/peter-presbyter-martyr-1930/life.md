# Peter (Uspensky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him on 10 января ст. ст. and gives no life. The whole of its account is one line, «Сщмч. Петра пресвитера (1930)» — the hieromartyr Peter, a presbyter, 1930 — and behind that line the calendar's own name link carries both the surname the line omits and a higher rank than the line gives him, «Петр (Успенский), протоиерей, сщмч.»: the archpriest Peter Uspensky.

The calendar names no birthplace, no diocese, no parish, no place of death and no manner of it. The year 1930 in its bracket is the whole of what it dates him by, and it is the year of his death, in the years of the collectivisation arrests. He stands on this day among the new martyrs and confessors of Russia.

The day page carries troparia for four of the saints it heads, and none under his name. That is everything the source says of him.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 10 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260110.html); read 3 October 2026. The calendar prints no life page for him.*
