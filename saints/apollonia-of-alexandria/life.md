# Apollonia of Alexandria

saint.gr's calendar for 30 Οκτωβρίου names «Αγία Απολλωνία, η Παρθένος και Πρεσβύτης, και οι συν αυτή εν Αλεξάνδρεια Μάρτυρες», and it begins by saying why she is on this day at all. Her memory is kept in the Church of Greece on 30 October by a decision of that Church taken in the year 2000, while the Western Church keeps her on 9 February and counts her the patron of dentists — as the dentists of Greece did too, until in 1967 they chose a different patron.

She suffered at Alexandria, the page says, a little before the persecution under Decius broke out, which it dates 248 to 249. Eusebius of Caesarea, reporting a letter of [Dionysius of Alexandria](/saints/dionysius-of-alexandria) about the events that went before that persecution, remembers «τήν θαυμασιωτάτην πρεσβῦτιν παρθένον» — the most wonderful aged virgin — who fell into the fire of her own will.

The passage the page quotes is plain: they took hold of her, broke out all her teeth and struck her jaws, and piled a pyre before the city, threatening to burn her alive unless she would cry out their impious proclamations along with them; and she, having begged a moment and been let go, leapt into the fire and was burned up.

The page adds that «παρθένος πρεσβῦτις» plainly means she was a deaconess, and regrets that the synaxaria carry nothing more about her. The martyrs who suffered with her at Alexandria have a heading of their own in the entry and are named nowhere, so they are one entry here and not folders. The apolytikion the page prints asks of her the grace of driving away the pains of teeth, and is copied in full. The page carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου».

*After saint.gr's calendar for 30 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/30/index.aspx) and [her life](https://www.saint.gr/2851/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
