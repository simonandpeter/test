# Theodore, companion of George

saint.gr's entry for the two of them is one line: we have no details of the life of the saints. Above it stands the synaxarion's distich, which says that Theodore and [George](/saints/george-companion-of-theodore) raised a great trophy against error out of their contests. The calendar's line is «Άγιοι Θεόδωρος και Γεώργιος», and that is all it says.

The page gives neither of them a byname, a place, a century or a hymn, and it does not say what the contests were or whether they ended in blood. What stands above the notice is the site's gloss on the two names, with its list of a Samian architect, a Neoplatonist philosopher and two ancient physicians, which belongs to the names and not to these men.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 21 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/21/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2023/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
