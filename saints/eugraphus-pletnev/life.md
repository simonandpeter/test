# Eugraphus (Pletnev)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him third of four in one line on 10 декабря and gives none of the four a life. The line is «Сщмчч. Иакова и Александра пресвитеров, сщмч. Евграфа пресвитера и сына его мч. Михаила (1918).» — the hieromartyrs James and Alexander, presbyters, the hieromartyr Eugraphus the presbyter and his son the martyr Michael, 1918 — and the calendar’s own name pages add the surnames the line leaves out, his being «Евграф (Плетнев), иерей, сщмч.»

That is the whole of the record, and one thing in it is more than a name: the fourth of the four is «сына его», his son, and the son’s name page carries the same surname. He was a priest; he died in 1918, the first year of the persecution in which the Russian church counts its new martyrs; and the calendar says nothing of where the two of them served or how they died. Nothing is supplied here.

The day page carries eleven hymns, for a company of martyrs of the fourth century and for three of the saints it heads, and not one of them is under any of these four names. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 10 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261210.html); read 13 October 2026.*
