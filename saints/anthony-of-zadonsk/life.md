# Anthony of Zadonsk

The Greek synaxarion keeps Anthony of Zadonsk on 29 September, and says that it has no details of the life of this Russian saint except that he was a fool for Christ. That is all it prints, and it gives no date.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 29 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2407/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
