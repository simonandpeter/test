# Anastasia (27 April)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names this martyr on 27 апреля and gives no life of her. Its line for the day reads «Сщмчч. Павла и Иоанна пресвитеров, мчч. Петра , Николая , Авксентия , Сергия и мц. Анастасии (1922).» — two hieromartyr presbyters, four martyrs and a woman martyr, seven people under one year, each with a name link of her own. She is the seventh and last of the seven, and the only one the line grades «мц.», a woman martyr.

That name page, 10048, carries nothing but the heading «Анастасия, мц.» and the two days «26 января (Новомуч.) , 27 апреля». What it adds to the line is nothing at all: the line already prints the bare forename «Анастасия», the grade «мц.» and no surname, and the page repeats just that. There is no life page for her anywhere on the site, no icon and no article. The day page prints nine hymns — three under the name of the kinsman of the Lord and six under the name of the abbot of the Caves — and none under any of the seven names of this line. The 26 января day page was not read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 27 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260427.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/10048.html); read 8 October 2026.*
