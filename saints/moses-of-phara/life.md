# Moses of Phara

The Greek calendar keeps a venerable Moses on 27 November, and saint.gr begins by saying how little stands behind him: he is unknown to the Synaxarist of Saint Nikodemos and to the printed Menaia, «ο Όσιος Μωυσής είναι άγνωστος στον Συναξαριστή του Αγίου Νικόδημου και τα έντυπα Μηναία». His memory, with a short notice, is found in the Parisian Codex 1621, and that notice is the whole of what is known of him.

It says that he came of the city of Phara; that he became a monk while still young; and that he lived eighty-five years in ascetic labour on a mountain, inside a cave, with severe fasting and prayer. He walked the ascetic road in a manner befitting God, and died in peace.

The page gives no century and no year, names no emperor and no abbot, and prints neither hymn nor distich for him, so no date is recorded here. His distichless, bookless memory is exactly what saint.gr says it is: one entry in one manuscript.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 27 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/27/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2732/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
