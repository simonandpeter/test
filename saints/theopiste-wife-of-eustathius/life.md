# Theopiste, wife of Eustathius

Theopiste was called Tatiana before her baptism and was the wife of [Eustathius
the Great Martyr](/saints/eustathius-the-great-martyr), baptised with him and with their two
sons. Exiled with the family by Trajan, she was separated from her husband on the road and found
again years later. Under Hadrian she was tortured with them for refusing the sacrifices. The
Greek calendar keeps her with her husband and sons on 20 September.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 20 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/985/saint.aspx); read 26 August 2026.*
