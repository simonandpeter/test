# Barbarus, companion of Alexander and Acoluthus

saint.gr's calendar for 14 May keeps three martyrs on one page with one notice between them: [Alexander](/saints/alexander-companion-of-barbarus), Barbarus and [Acoluthus](/saints/acoluthus-the-martyr). These saints, it says, having lived a holy life and brought many idolaters to the Christian faith, were martyred by beheading; and their synaxis and feast is held in the church of Saint Irene, which stands near the sea.

That is the whole of the account, and the couplet above it counts the three — for the severed heads of three fellow contenders the Trinity sets three worthy crowns. The page's note on the name says only what it means: Βάρβαρος, the untamed and the hard. No century, no country, no emperor, no judge, no relics and no hymn is printed, and none is set down here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 14 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/14/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4020/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
