# Anthimos the New, the blind ascetic of Cephalonia

Anthimos the new ascetic, the blind missionary of the Aegean, was born at Lixouri in Cephalonia in 1727 — a contemporary, the synaxarion says, of the other great equal-to-the-apostles and martyr of the nation, Kosmas of Aetolia, whose work and offering his resembled. Athanasius at baptism, he lost his sight at seven, struck by smallpox, and found it again by God’s intervention and the unsleeping prayers of his faithful and virtuous mother Atzouleta and his own; they had taken part in a forty-liturgy — the synaxarion’s life runs on past the point read here.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 4 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2349/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
