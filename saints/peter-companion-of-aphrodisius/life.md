# Peter, companion of Aphrodisius

saint.gr's calendar for 14 Μαρτίου names him with his companion, «Άγιοι Πέτρος και Αφροδίσιος οι Μάρτυρες», and the page behind that line holds one sentence about the two of them: that the holy martyrs Peter and Aphrodisius were martyred in Africa by the Vandals. There is no year, no city, no judge, no manner of death, no relic and no hymn, and the rest of the page is a note on the names.

So what is recorded here is the commemoration, the continent and the persecutors — which is more than many of this calendar's bare lines give, since the Vandals put the Arian persecution in Africa in the fifth century, though the page itself does not say so and no year is taken from it here. [Aphrodisius](/saints/aphrodisius-companion-of-peter) is kept with him in the same line. The page glosses his name from πέτρα, a rock: the one who is steady as a rock.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 14 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/14/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1387/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
