# Pancharius (25 May)

saint.gr names him and says outright that it knows nothing about him. Its whole entry is two sentences: «Η Εκκλησία μας, μαζί με τον μάρτυρα Άγιο Κελεστίνο μνημονεύει και τον Άγιο Παγχάριο. Δεν έχουμε όμως πληροφορίες για τον βίο του» — our Church commemorates saint Pancharius together with the martyr saint Celestine; we have, however, no information about his life.

So what is kept on 25 Μαΐου under his name is a name and a companion. The page gives him no country, no century, no rank beyond the bare «Άγιος», no manner of death and no relics; it prints no synaxarion couplet of his own and no hymn; and it does not say whether he suffered with Celestine or is merely remembered beside him. Nothing further about him is recorded here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 25 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/25/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1718/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
