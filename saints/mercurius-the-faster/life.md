# Mercurius the Faster

saint.gr's calendar for 4 Νοεμβρίου names «Όσιος Μερκούριος ο νηστευτής», and the page behind that line carries no life at all: its whole content is a cross-reference — «Για τον Όσιο Μερκούριο τον νηστευτή βλέπε στις 24 Νοεμβρίου», for the venerable Mercurius the Faster see 24 November. So the Greek calendar keeps him on this day and sends the reader to another day for everything about him.

What this folder therefore records is exactly that: the day, the epithet, and the rank word Όσιος. There is no century, no monastery, no country, no account of his fasting or his death, no relics and no hymn. The page's own heading is firm that the memory falls on 4 November every year, and the 24 November page is where it says the life stands.

The entry carries no asterisk and not the sentence «Δεν είναι διασταυρωμένη η ύπαρξη του Αγίου», so the site vouches for the entry as it stands, empty as it is.

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar for 4 ноября ст. ст. keeps him as «Прп. Меркурия Печерского (XIV).», and behind the line stands an index of two short lives. Together they give what the Greek page would not: he was a monk of the Kyiv Caves monastery who struggled in the Far, Theodosian caves in the fourteenth century and was a strict faster, which is the epithet the Greek calendar gave him and the word the day's own troparion uses of him, «постника Печерского, в Дальних пещерах».

What both lives record of him beyond that is one friendship, and they say it is all that is known. He was bound by one mind and brotherly love to the venerable [Paisius](/saints/paisius-of-the-caves-lavra), whom this calendar keeps on 19 July and places in the same century; the two were inseparable and lived in one cell, and after their deaths they were laid in one coffin. Their relics now rest in the Far, Theodosian caves.

That Paisius is taken to be the one this corpus already held from saint.gr's 19 Ιουλίου, whose own page says only that no details of his life have come down. The identification rests on the day, on the house and on the century, and on nothing else, and it is recorded here as a reading. Of this man's birth, his family, his tonsure and the year of his death the calendar says nothing at all.

The day page carries a troparion and a kontakion under his name and both are now in this folder. His life pages list «Дни памяти: 28 августа (Печер.(Д)) , 4 ноября , 24 ноября», and the second of them says why: 24 ноября is kept for his name-day with the great-martyr of the same name, and 28 августа with the Synaxis of the Far caves. Neither day page was read, so both are recorded here as notes and not as feasts, and this folder renders on 4 ноября ст. ст. only.
