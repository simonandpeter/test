# Willibald

saint.gr's calendar names him and then says, in its only sentence, that it has nothing to tell: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου» — we have no details of the saint's life. The name is printed in Latin letters, «Άγιος Willibald», on the day page and on the life page alike, and the page offers no Greek rendering of it.

That is the whole of what the Greek calendar keeps of him. No century, no country, no see, no monastery, no manner of death and no relics are given; no rank beyond the word holy; no couplet and no hymn. Nothing is supplied here that the page does not print.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/492/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
