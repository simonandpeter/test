# George Bozic

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives him two sentences. The line for 4 June reads «Άγιος Γεώργιος ο Ιερομάρτυρας ο εκ Κροατίας», Saint George the hieromartyr, of Croatia, and the page behind it says this: the holy hieromartyr George, whose surname it gives in a parenthesis as Bozic, was a priest in the town of Našice in Croatia; he underwent dreadful tortures and bore witness in the course of the Second World War at the hands of fanatical Roman Catholics.

So what is recorded of him is a forename, a surname, a town, a priesthood, a war and the hands the page says he died at. There is no year here — not of birth, not of ordination and not of death — no account of what the tortures were, no place of burial and no relic; and there is no hymn and no verse. The Serbian calendar, which is the one that would be likeliest to keep a priest of Slavonia, was not opened for this draft.
