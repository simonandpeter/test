# The Twenty-three Martyrs

Twenty-three martyrs who died by the sword together with [Adrian of Nicomedia](/saints/adrian-of-nicomedia), kept on the same day. The synaxarion gives their names: Anatolius, Anthimus, Antiochus, Gentilius, Eleutherius, Hermogenes, Euethius, Euretus, Eutychius, Theagones, Theodore, Thyrsus, John, Carteras, Claudius, Cyriacus, Marinus, Mardonius, Menodius, Plato, Synetus, Troadius and Pharetrius — in the fourth century.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 26 August — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/904/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
