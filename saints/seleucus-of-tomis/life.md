# Seleucus, martyr with Gordian

Seleucus and Gordian took the crown of martyr’s glory in the fourth century
under Licinius; Gordian, Seleucus and Macrobius were martyred in Galatia,
the Greek synaxarion says — having confessed Christ they were thrown by the
unbelievers as food to the wild beasts. The Romanian calendar keeps the six
martyrs of Dobrogea the same day — Macrobius, Gordian, Helias, Zoticus,
Lucian and Valerian — and names Seleucus in their life, among those who
suffered at Tomis about 323–324. The Russian calendar keeps a Seleucus of
Galatia on 13 September of the old style, 26 September, with Stratonicus of
Nicomedia and the Egyptians Cronides, Leontius and Serapion, martyrs of the
third century, and its life says that after many torments he was thrown to
the beasts with his wife; whether that Seleucus and this one are the same
man neither calendar says.
