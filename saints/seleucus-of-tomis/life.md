# Seleucus, martyr with Gordian

Seleucus and Gordian took the crown of martyr’s glory in the fourth century under Licinius; Gordian, Seleucus and Macrobius were martyred in Galatia, the Greek synaxarion says — having confessed Christ they were thrown by the unbelievers as food to the wild beasts. The Romanian calendar keeps the six martyrs of Dobrogea the same day — Macrobius, Gordian, Helias, Zoticus, Lucian and Valerian — and names Seleucus in their life, among those who suffered at Tomis about 323–324.
