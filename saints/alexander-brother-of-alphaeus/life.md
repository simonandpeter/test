# Alexander, brother of Alphaeus and Zosimas

[Mark the Shepherd](/saints/mark-the-shepherd), the brothers Alexander, [Alphaeus](/saints/alphaeus-brother-of-alexander) and [Zosimas](/saints/zosimas-brother-of-alexander), [Nikon](/saints/nikon-companion-of-mark-the-shepherd), [Neon](/saints/neon-companion-of-mark-the-shepherd), [Heliodorus](/saints/heliodorus-companion-of-mark-the-shepherd), and [the virgins and children](/saints/virgins-and-children-with-mark-the-shepherd) with them lived in the years of Diocletian, when Magnus was governor of Pisidia, about 290, the Greek synaxarion says.

Mark was an old man who kept sheep. Because he confessed that he was a Christian he was horribly tortured and sent to Claudiopolis, and there three brothers of the village of Katalyton, Alexander, Alphaeus and Zosimas, were called in to make bronze fetters for him; but as they began the work they felt their hands go numb, and marvelling at it they at once confessed Christ. They were martyred horribly, molten lead poured into their mouths, and then nailed to a rock; and Mark, after still more torture, was at last beheaded.

The other martyrs, Heliodorus, Nikon and Neon, died the same death with many virgins and children, all of them beheaded at a place called Moromilion. The synaxarion names none of the virgins and children.

The Russian calendar keeps the whole company on the same 28 September, in one line that runs the two groups together — «Мчч. Александра, Алфея, Зосимы, Марка пастыря, Никона, Неона, Илиодора и прочих (IV)» — and dates them to the fourth century. Its index of names is what separates them: he and his brothers are of Caluta, «Мученик Александр Калутинский», and the shepherd's company is of Claudiopolis.

The Serbian church's Ohrid Prologue keeps the three brothers inside its entry for [Mark the Shepherd](/saints/mark-the-shepherd), third of the four it enumerates on this day, and says outright the one thing the Greek entry leaves to be inferred: that they were «три брата ковача», three brother smiths, called in by the governor and ordered to make the instruments of Mark's torture. All three, having talked with Mark, received the faith of Christ and refused the order — where the Greek has their hands go numb over the work. The governor condemned them, and molten lead was poured into their mouths, which the Greek says too.

The Prologue does not add the nailing to the rock, and it names no village for them where the Greek gives Katalyton and the Russian index Caluta. It prints their three names only in the accusative, inside its own clause, so no Serbian form of the name is recorded on this folder.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 28 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2468/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026; the Russian church calendar's [28 September](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260928.html), read 4 October 2026; and the Serbian church's [Ohrid Prologue for the Julian 28 September](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-10-11&prolog=1), read 4 October 2026.*
