# Andrew of the Raphael monastery

saint.gr's calendar for 14 May names him and gives no life. Its page for him says one thing only: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Ρώσου Αγίου» — we have no details of the life of the Russian saint. Everything else this folder holds comes from the two lines around that sentence: the calendar's own «Άγιος Ανδρέας ηγούμενος της μονής Ραφαήλ (Τομπόλσκ)», and the head line's «(; - 1820)».

So what is recorded is an abbot of a monastery called Raphael, placed by the page at Tobolsk in Siberia, who died in 1820. No parents, no birthplace, no tonsure, no act of his rule, no relics and no hymn is printed. The page's one cross-reference is to the synaxis of all the saints who shone in Siberia, and its cited source is the feasts and saints listing of the Orthodox Church in America.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 14 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/14/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4241/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
