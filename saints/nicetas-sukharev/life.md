# Nicetas (Sukharev)

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 21 июня ст. ст. prints him in the line «Мч. Никиты (1942).», and the name link behind his place in it supplies the calendar’s own nominative with the surname, «Никита (Сухарев), мч.» (name page 14464). That is where the name form in this folder is read from; the line gives the forename in the genitive and no surname at all.

His name page carries no life. It heads «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 21 июня», and under «Сайт прихода» it names a parish — «Храм вмч. Никиты на Швивой (Вшивой) горке, за Яузой г. Москва» in the city diocese of Moscow — and stops there. That is a church of the great-martyr of his own name and not a statement about him, and no place of service, no age and no manner of death is supplied for him here. He is graded «мч.», a layman’s grade, and is given no clerical or monastic rank.

The second day his name page lists is the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which is a synaxis and not a second feast, so he renders on 21 июня ст. ст. only. His is the latest year of the five twentieth-century names this day carries, 1942, and the only one the calendar grades as a layman.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 21 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260621.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14464.html); read 9 October 2026.*
