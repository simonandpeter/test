# Pamphilus (5 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 5 Νοεμβρίου names Pamphilus among the martyrs it places in the years of the emperor Maximian, when the governor of Palestine was Urbanus; the page sets the year 298 beside the governor's name. Of Pamphilus himself it says that he was adorned with many virtues, and that he died after many tortures inside the prison, together with other confessors of the Christian faith. It names none of those confessors and gives no number for them.

There is no city, no rank, no burial, no year of his own and no hymn on the page. What stands beside the notice is the couplet of the synaxarion, which counts his prison and the scourging that came before it as two crowns: «Φρουρᾷ δαμασθείς, καὶ πρὸ τῆς φρουρᾶς ξέσει, / Διπλοῦν λάβοι Πάμφιλος εἰκότως στέφος» — overcome by prison, and before the prison by the scraping, Pamphilus would fittingly receive a double crown.

The same day's calendar keeps several other martyrs under the same governor of Palestine, each in an entry of its own, and prints no hymn for any of them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2895/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
