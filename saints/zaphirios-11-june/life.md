# Zaphirios (11 June)

saint.gr keeps him on 11 June and has one source for him, which it quotes entire: a note in codex 743 (6250) of the Russian monastery of Saint Panteleimon on the Holy Mountain. It says that on the Holy Mountain of Athos in 1821, 1822 and 1823, when the revolution broke out in Greece, Abobut Pasha of Thessalonica came and subdued the Mountain, and many hard things happened. Then, sending men of his own to the Mountain, they gathered the boys they found there, seventy in number, and brought them to Thessalonica and made them Turks. Only one stood firm in the faith and was martyred, by name Zaphirios.

That is the whole of what the page knows of him: a boy among seventy taken off the Holy Mountain, the one of them who would not deny Christ. The head of the page brackets him «(; - 1821)», and its verses say that he proclaimed the divinity of Christ bravely and so found the glory of the martyrs, and that on the eleventh Zaphirios stood before Christ. The site glosses his name from σάπφειρος: precious as the sapphire.

The page adds that the service for him was composed by the hieromonk Athanasios Simonopetritis, hymnographer of the Great Church of Christ; and it prints his apolytikion, his kontakion, two megalynaria and the oikos, all of which name him. The kontakion locates him on the Mountain and says he refuted the religion of Mohammed and was beheaded.
