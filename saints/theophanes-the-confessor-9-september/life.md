# Theophanes the Confessor (9 September)

Theophanes was born in 283, under the emperors Carus and Carinus, to
fanatical pagan parents; but their son heard the preaching of Christians and
was drawn to the life of truth and love. One day, still young and
unbaptised, he met in freezing weather a child so poor that it was in danger
of dying of cold; the sight tore his heart, and without losing time he
dressed the poor child in his own coat. When he came home his parents were
alarmed, and asked what had become of his garment; he answered that he had
given it to Christ. The fanatical pagan parents soon understood that their
son was drawn to Christianity and forced him to leave the house; then
Theophanes was baptised and went through various cities preaching, and
afterwards withdrew to Mount Diabenos to an old ascetic, from whose
experience and wisdom he learned much. When the elder died he stayed alone
on the mountain in prayer and unceasing study and striving for higher moral
and spiritual perfection; then he came down again into the world and took up
his preaching once more, wiser now, more spiritual and more edifying. In
that time he was arrested, threatened and tortured, but stayed unmoved in
his confession, and he died at seventy-five in his hermitage. Appearing like
a very great star of God, the verse says, you dissolve the night of error,
Theophanes. The Russian church calendar tells the same life with other
dates: baptised in youth, he fled his pagan parents to an elder on Mount
Davis who had laboured there seventy-five years and learned from him the
reading of the holy books and the rule of monastic life; the elder died
after five years and Theophanes lived alone in his cave fifty-eight years,
then came down to preach to the pagans and converted many, was seized and
tortured by order of the emperors Carus (282–283) and his sons Numerian and
Carinus (283–284), bore it bravely and was let go alive, went back to the
mountain, lived seventeen years more and died in peace about 300 — which
cannot sit with the Greek life’s birth in 283, one of the two having read
the persecution as a birth year. The Russian calendar keeps him on 9
September of the old style, 22 September.
