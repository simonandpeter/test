# Theophanes the Confessor (9 September)

Theophanes was born in 283, under the emperors Carus and Carinus, to fanatical pagan parents; but their son heard the preaching of Christians and was drawn to the life of truth and love. One day, still young and unbaptised, he met in freezing weather a child so poor that it was in danger of dying of cold; the sight tore his heart, and without losing time he dressed the poor child in his own coat. When he came home his parents were alarmed, and asked what had become of his garment; he answered that he had given it to Christ. The fanatical pagan parents soon understood that their son was drawn to Christianity and forced him to leave the house; then Theophanes was baptised and went through various cities preaching, and afterwards withdrew to Mount Diabenos to an old ascetic, from whose experience and wisdom he learned much. When the elder died he stayed alone on the mountain in prayer and unceasing study and striving for higher moral and spiritual perfection; then he came down — the synaxarion’s life runs on past the point read here. Appearing like a very great star of God, the verse says, you dissolve the night of error, Theophanes.
