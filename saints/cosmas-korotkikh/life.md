# Cosmas (Korotkikh)

The Сретенский monastery's calendar names him fifth in its clause of fifteen for 25 ноября ст. ст. and carries no life for him; azbyka.ru prints one out of fond.ru. He was born on 25 October 1886 — the day of the memory of [Cosmas of Maiuma](/saints/cosmas-of-maiuma), for whom he was named — at Большая Уса in the province of Perm, the son of a peasant, Родион Коротких. He chose the service of God as a child, left his father's house and went to Moscow to study, finished the pastoral school there, married Прасковья Ивановна and had a daughter and a son.

From the age of eighteen he was a psalm-reader, and in 1912 was ordained deacon; his own papers mention that in 1917 he was deacon of the Annunciation cathedral of Воткинск, and from 1916 to 1919 he taught the Law of God in a Воткинск elementary school. In 1921 he was ordained priest to that cathedral. The persecution of the nineteen-twenties and thirties reached him in full: he was twice held in the Сарапул house of correction in 1927 and had to change his place of service many times. On 30 March 1927 the bishop of Воткинск, [Onesimus (Pylaev)](/saints/onesimus-pylaev), blessed him to go on serving as rector of a cemetery church; afterwards he served at Нечкино, and from September 1928, by the blessing of a bishop of the Old-Rite communion, at the единоверческий church of Izhevsk.

Of the family's later wanderings no papers survive, but by the middle of 1937 he and his wife were living at Лукино in the province of Moscow, where a convent of the Exaltation of the Cross had stood before the revolution. They lived poor and owned nothing. Having gained no earthly wealth he preached the true one, and said: «Кто верит в Бога до конца, тот останется верным сыном Христа» — whoever believes in God to the end will remain a faithful son of Christ. He cared for the people of Лукино and the villages round it, and witnesses said afterwards that nuns came out from Moscow at the feasts to sing in his choir.

His zeal did not go unnoticed. He was arrested on 27 November 1937 and held in the Серпухов prison on a charge of systematic counter-revolutionary agitation. The record of his one questioning, on 28 November, is three short questions and three short answers; he did not admit guilt. The judicial troika sentenced him to be shot on 1 December, and the sentence was carried out on 8 December 1937 on the Butovo range outside Moscow, where he was buried in an unmarked common grave.
