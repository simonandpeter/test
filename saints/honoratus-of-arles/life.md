# Honoratus of Arles

Honoratus was born in Lorraine, saint.gr says, and his own countrymen called him «Ληρώνη» or «Πλανασία»; his parents were heathen. Young, he embraced the Orthodox faith and came to the East, to be taught the things of the monastic commonwealth. Afterwards he came to the islands of Lérins, off Gaul, and there in the year 375 he built the monastery of Lérins.

That house, the page says, contributed most greatly to the christening of Provence and of other parts of Gaul. He was ordained bishop of the city of Arles — Arelate — which lies near Marseilles and had been an episcopal see from the middle of the third century, and an archbishopric from the year 400 until 1801.

He fell asleep in peace in the year 429, the year in which Aetius beat back the Visigoths at Arles. That is the whole of what saint.gr prints of him: no verses, and no hymn.
