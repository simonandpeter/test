# Gregory Gravanos of Nisyros

Gregory was a member of the Kollyvades, saint.gr says, and his wanderings are the movement's. When the Kollyvades went beyond the bounds of the Holy Mountain the monks were scattered among the islands of the Aegean; and Gregory with others came to Patmos, and later to Lipsi, where he built a hermitage in honour of the Most Holy Mother of God. Pirate raids forced him to leave that island.

He returned later to Patmos, and there he met [Macarius Notaras](/saints/macarius-notaras), whom the page cross-references to 17 April, and who was living at the Kathisma of All Saints that he had himself made on the hill of Koumana. Gregory stayed with Macarius for a short while, and then went off to another part of the island called Grava — and it is from that place, the page explains, that his surname Gravanos comes.

He became widely known as a spiritual father, and there is a tradition, it says, that even the patriarch of Constantinople visited him. At some point he was obliged to leave Patmos for Ikaria, and there he fell asleep, in the year 1812. Of his birth, his parentage, his tonsure and his relics the page says nothing, and it prints no hymn.
