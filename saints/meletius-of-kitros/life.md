# Meletius of Kitros

saint.gr's whole notice for him is a single sentence: Saint Meletius was bishop of Kitros, in Thessaloniki, a hieromartyr and an ethnomartyr, and he was martyred in 1821. Its heading gives him that one year, «Άγιος Μελέτιος (; - 1821)», with the year of his birth left as a question mark, and the calendar's line for the day gives him no byname at all.

So the folder holds a see, a rank, a year and the word the Greek church uses for a bishop killed in the national rising. The page does not say where or how he was killed, what he had done, or how old he was, and it prints no hymn for him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 21 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/21/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2032/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
