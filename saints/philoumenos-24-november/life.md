# Philoumenos (24 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 24 Νοεμβρίου names two men in one line, «Άγιοι Φιλούμενος και Χριστόφορος», and gives them a single sentence: they were martyred by the sword. That is the whole of the notice: no country, no city, no emperor, no year, and no word about who they were to each other. The page adds nothing to it but the couplet set over the line, and a gloss on the name Christophoros.

The couplet plays on the first name, which means the loved one: they cut down Philoumenos and Christophoros, who were shown by the deed itself to be Christ's own friends. The page gives them no country, no city, no century, no year, no emperor, no relics and no hymn, and it does not say whether they were kin or companions — only that they died together by the same death.

This folder is Philoumenos, named first in the line and in the couplet, who died by the sword with [Christophoros](/saints/christopher-companion-of-philoumenos).

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/24/index.aspx) and [the life the two share](https://www.saint.gr/2994/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
