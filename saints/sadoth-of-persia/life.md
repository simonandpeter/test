# Sadoth of Persia

saint.gr's calendar for 20 Φεβρουαρίου names him and gives no life. Its line reads «Άγιος Σαδώκ ο Ιερομάρτυρας και οι μαζί μ' αυτόν Εκατόν εικοσιοκτώ μάρτυρες» — the holy hieromartyr Sadok and the hundred and twenty-eight martyrs with him — and the page standing under that line prints a couplet and a referral, and nothing else: for Sadok and the hundred and twenty-eight martyrs, see 19 Οκτωβρίου, where their memory is repeated as Sadoth the bishop and the hundred and twenty martyrs from Persia.

So the Greek calendar keeps him twice, and its two entries do not agree on how many suffered with him. The couplet keeps to the larger figure: «Φέρει σὺν ὀκτὼ καὶ Σαδὼκ Ἐπισκόπῳ, Ἡ δωδεκαπλῆ Μαρτύρων δεκὰς ξίφος» — the twelvefold decade of martyrs bears the sword, together with eight and with Sadok the bishop, which is a hundred and twenty and eight. His companions are nowhere named or enumerated on this page, so they are not kept here one by one; the fuller account stands on saint.gr's October day, which is not this day's reading, and this page gives him no year, no see beyond Persia, no relics and no hymn.

The Serbian church keeps him on the same day, and the Ohrid Prologue of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović begins with the dream. He was bishop in Persia after the holy Simeon, and once in his sleep Simeon appeared to him and said: yesterday I, today you. He explained the words to his flock as meaning that last year Simeon had suffered and this year he would — and that same year, in fact, the king Shapur seized him with many of his clergy and people and brought him to trial.

First they were ordered to bow down to fire and the sun as to a godhead, and he answered that they were ready to die gladly for their God but would not bow to the sun or to fire. Then they were tortured and sentenced to be beheaded with the sword, and before the beheading he raised a prayer to God: wash us, Lord, in our own blood from our sins. So he gave his body to death gloriously, with his priests and his faithful, and his soul to the immortal God, in 342 or 344.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 20 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/20/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2771/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026; and the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 20. фебруар ст. ст. — [the Serbian day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-03-05&prolog=1), read 4 October 2026.*
