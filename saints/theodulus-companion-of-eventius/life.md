# Theodulus, companion of Eventius

saint.gr's calendar for 16 Μαρτίου names him with his companion, «Άγιοι Εβέντιος και Θεόδουλος οι Ιερομάρτυρες», and the page behind that line holds one sentence about the two of them: that the holy hieromartyrs Eventius and Theodulus were presbyters in the Church of Rome and were martyred under the emperor Hadrian, together with [Alexander](/saints/alexander-of-rome-pope), the bishop of Rome, whom the same calendar keeps on the same day.

That is all the page has. There is no year within the reign, no charge, no judge, no manner of death, no relic and no hymn, and of the two priests it distinguishes nothing at all: what is said of him is said of [Eventius](/saints/eventius-companion-of-theodulus) in the same breath. What is recorded here is the commemoration, the Church they served in, the rank of presbyter, the reign, and the bishop they died with.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 16 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/16/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3903/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
