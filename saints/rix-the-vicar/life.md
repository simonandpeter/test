# Rix the Vicar

saint.gr's calendar for 6 Ιουλίου keeps him with the woman he had been set to judge: «Άγιοι Λουκία η παρθενομάρτυς, Ρήξος βικάριος και πολλοί άλλοι που μαρτύρησαν στην Κομπανία» — Lucia the virgin-martyr, Rix the vicar, and many others who were martyred in Campania. The page says that [Lucia](/saints/lucia-of-rome-companion-of-rix) was seized by Rix the vicar, who pressed her to sacrifice to the idols and to deny Christ; but she was not persuaded, and instead she led Rix himself to the faith of Christ.

From him she was then counted worthy of great honours and settled in a quiet house, where she gave herself to prayer and fasting. She asked Rix to go to Campania in Italy and to be martyred there for Christ; and he, leaving behind wife, children, wealth and all his worldly glory, went away with her. In that city both of them were arrested. Before the governor they confessed Christ with courage, and for that they were beheaded, and with them many other holy martyrs were beheaded as well — the company the same day's listing calls the Twenty-four Martyrs.

The page gives no year for him in its text, though it heads both their names «(; - 301)», and it gives him no country of his own, no age and no parentage. It prints no hymn under their line, only the synaxarion couplet: «Ιδούσα Pήξου την τομήν η Λουκία, / Oυ προς τομήν έρρηξε φωνήν δειλίας» — Lucia, seeing the beheading of Rix, broke out in no voice of cowardice at her own.
