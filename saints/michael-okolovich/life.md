# Michael (Okolovich)

The Russian church calendar names him and gives no life. Its list for 7 мая ст. ст. reads «Сщмч. Михаила пресвитера (1938)» — the hieromartyr Michael, a presbyter, with the year of his death — and the name page behind that line carries one thing more than the day gives: his surname. It heads him «Михаил (Околович), протоиерей, сщмч.», and then prints his days, «26 января (Новомуч.), 7 мая», and nothing else at all.

So what the source says of him is this: that he was a priest, that his family name was Околович, that he died in 1938, and that he is numbered among the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, whose synaxis is the January day. The two pages differ about his grade — the day's line calls him a presbyter and the name page an archpriest — and both readings are recorded. Nothing else has been supplied: there is no birth year, no place, no diocese, no parish, no account of his arrest or of how he died, and the day page prints no troparion or kontakion under his name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 7 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260507.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13703.htm), which gives no life; read 8 October 2026.*
