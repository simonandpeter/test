# Apollinaris (Mosalitinov)

Apollinaris — Athanasius Semyonovich Mosalitinov in the world — was born in 1873 to a peasant family of the Kryukovo district of the Graivoron county of Kursk province, and had a home education. In August 1901 he entered the Nikolaev monastery at Verkhoturye and in 1903 was received into the brotherhood at his petition; in 1904 he was called up for active service and after two years, discharged, was taken back among the brethren. On 27 March 1908 he was tonsured Apollinaris by the abbot, the hieromonk Xenophon (Medvedev), and in September 1910 ordained hierodeacon; he bore very diligently the obedience of a joiner on the building of the cathedral of the Exaltation of the Cross, for which he received the bishop’s blessing with a certificate, and all the reports say of him: of very good conduct, capable of obediences and very diligent. In 1918, at the authorities’ demand, he was sent to serve in the village of Krasnogorskoye; he was arrested by Red Army men and savagely killed with bayonets on 30 August 1918 for refusing to give up his pastoral service, and was buried at the monastery. The Russian calendar keeps him on 30 August (Julian).
