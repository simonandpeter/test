# Agathon of Alexandria

saint.gr's entry for the hieromartyr Agathon is a single sentence, and the life says exactly that and no more: he was a presbyter, and he was martyred together with other Christians at Alexandria. The page gives no year, no emperor, no manner of death, and no names for the Christians who suffered with him. It prints no hymn and records no relic.

What else the page carries is not about him. Under the name it gives the etymology — Agathon, from ἀγαθός, the man very good in soul and in character — and a note on the Athenian tragic poet who bore the name, the friend of Socrates, Plato and Euripides whose victory in 416 BC gave Plato the occasion to write the Symposium. That is the site's habit with Greek names and says nothing of the martyr.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 14 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/14/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3709/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
