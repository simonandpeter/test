# Joseph of Thessalonica

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its page for him carries the feast line 15 Iulie and nothing else — no Viață tab and no Tropar tab — so all that is recorded here is what the calendar's own line for the day says: «Sfântul Ierarh Iosif, Arhiepiscopul Tesalonicului», the holy hierarch Joseph, Archbishop of Thessalonica.

No year, no place beyond the see, and no account of his life were found on the Romanian pages read for this day, and nothing has been supplied from elsewhere. The other calendars have not been checked for him.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 15 iulie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/15-iulie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-ierarh-iosif-arhiepiscopul-tesalonicului), which prints only the day; read 24 September 2026.*
