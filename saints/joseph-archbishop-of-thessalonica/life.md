# Joseph of Thessalonica

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its page for him carries the feast line 15 Iulie and nothing else — no Viață tab and no Tropar tab — so all that is recorded here is what the calendar's own line for the day says: «Sfântul Ierarh Iosif, Arhiepiscopul Tesalonicului», the holy hierarch Joseph, Archbishop of Thessalonica.

No year, no place beyond the see, and no account of his life were found on the Romanian pages read for this day, and nothing has been supplied from elsewhere. The Russian and Serbian calendars have not been checked for him.

The Greek calendar keeps him a day earlier, on 14 July, and saint.gr tells the life the Romanian page withheld. It says he lived in the years of Leo V the Iconoclast (813-820), that his parents were the devout Photinos and Theoktiste, and that his brother was [Theodore the Studite](/saints/theodore-the-studite); because of his praiseworthy life he was chosen archbishop of Thessalonica. After some time in the see he was recalled to Constantinople by imperial order, presented himself to the emperor, and confessed with courage that he reverenced and honoured the holy icons. For that he was ill-used, imprisoned, and exiled to an island; afterwards the impious emperor Theophilos made him taste harder sufferings and darker prisons, and in hunger and thirst and many afflictions he departed for eternal life in 832.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 15 iulie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/15-iulie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-ierarh-iosif-arhiepiscopul-tesalonicului), which prints only the day; read 24 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 14 Ιουλίου — [the Greek day](https://www.saint.gr/07/14/index.aspx) and [the Greek life](https://www.saint.gr/705/saint.aspx), read 1 October 2026.*
