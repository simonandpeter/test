# Romanus (30 May)

saint.gr's calendar for 30 Μαΐου names him and gives one sentence. The holy martyrs Romanus, Teletius — «Τελέτιος (ή Μελέτιος)», the page allows the second spelling — and Christina, it says, were martyred at Nicomedia by the sword. Nothing else about Romanus is on the page: no birthplace, no age, no trade, no judge, no emperor, no year, and no hymn.

The page adds two things which this folder does not carry. It notes of the Christina of the line that she «δεν αναφέρεται σε όλα τα συναξάρια», is not mentioned in all the synaxaria, so no folder is made for her; and the day's line joins to the three «το πλήθος των εν Νικομήδεια καέντων αγίων», the multitude of the saints burned at Nicomedia, whom the page neither names nor numbers.

What is left is Romanus and [Teletius](/saints/teletius), each with his own heading and his own rank on the page, and each with his own folder here, carrying this same account. The two-line epigram the page prints over them — «Pώμη Pωμανός συμπνέων Tελετίω, Ήκει μετ' αυτού συντελεσθήναι ξίφει» — puts them together and is a distich rather than a service, so no hymn is taken from it.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 30 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/30/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/428/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
