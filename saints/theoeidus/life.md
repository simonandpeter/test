# Theoeidus

saint.gr's calendar names the holy martyr Theoeidus and gives him one sentence. He ended his life in martyrdom, the page says, after the executioners trampled him underfoot. Its two-line verse says the same in its own way: the feet that tread on the little flesh of Theoeidus press out his soul into the winepress of heaven. No year, no place and no further notice of him is printed there.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/578/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
