# Zoe (18 December)

doxologia.ro's calendar names her and gives no life. Its line for 18 decembrie reads «Sfânta Muceniță Zoe», and the page the calendar links under that name carries no Viață tab: no life, no year, no place and no companions. What the line says is that she was a martyr, that she is called Zoe, and that the Romanian church keeps her on this day.

Her page's Tropar tab is a recording made for the day's two martyrs together, whose title names them both — «Troparul Sfântului Mucenic Sebastian și al Sfintei Muceniţe Zoe (18 decembrie)» — with the sung petition that they pray to God for us; there is no hymn text on it. The long life doxologia prints for Sebastian on the same day was read here only in its opening and its close, and she is named in neither, so nothing has been carried over from it to her. No year, no country and no account of her martyrdom has been taken from anywhere else, and none has been supplied from memory.

The Greek calendar keeps her on the same day and gives her the company the Romanian line left out. saint.gr's entry names ten people together — Nikostratos and his wife Zoe, [Tranquillinus](/saints/tranquillinus) and his wife [Marcia](/saints/marcia-wife-of-tranquillinus), [Claudius](/saints/claudius-18-december), [Tiburtius](/saints/tiburtius-18-december), [Castor](/saints/castor-18-december), [Castulus](/saints/castulus-18-december), [Marcus](/saints/marcus-son-of-tranquillinus) and [Marcellinus](/saints/marcellinus-son-of-tranquillinus) — and says that they all suffered in the persecution Diocletian set in motion against the Christians, whose years it gives as 284 to 304, and that they suffered together with Saint [Sebastian](/saints/sebastian-of-rome), whom the same day's calendar keeps.

Of Zoe herself it says that she was the wife of [Nicostratus](/saints/nicostratus-18-december) and that the two of them died together, and it says how: they died of suffocation, after they had been hung upside down and a special hearth of fire was built beneath them, so that much smoke should come up and go straight into their faces. Its epigram for her turns her name: Zoe, lifted up by the head, receives from a foul-smelling smoke the end of life.

The Ohrid Prologue keeps her on the same menologion day, inside the heading „Свети мученик Севастијан и други с њим.“ — Sebastian and the others with him — and names her in the body. She was the wife of [Nicostratus](/saints/nicostratus-18-december) the jailer, it says, and had been dumb for six years; [Sebastian](/saints/sebastian-of-rome) healed her of it, and brought her and the whole house of Nicostratus to baptism.

She suffered first of them all. They caught her at the tomb of the Apostle Peter, where she was praying to God; and after they had tortured her they threw her into the river Tiber. The book gives no year for it, and this is the only one of the day's deaths it puts in order before the others.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 18 decembrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/18-decembrie) and [her page](https://doxologia.ro/sfanta-mucenita-zoe), which prints the name only; read 28 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 18 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3289/saint.aspx), read 2 October 2026; and the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 18. децембар ст. ст. — [the day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2025-12-31&prolog=1), read 7 October 2026.*
