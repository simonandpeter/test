# Cyril, Nikon and Macarius (Serbian calendar, 30 August)

The Православни подсетник’s calendar line for 30 August (Julian) names “Свети Кирило, Никон и Макарије” after the translation of Alexander Nevsky’s relics, and prints nothing else about them: its Ohrid Prologue for the day has the synaxis of the Serbian hierarchs, the three patriarchs of Constantinople, Christopher and Eulalius, and no Cyril, Nikon or Macarius. Who they are is not said, and this entry does not guess; it records the line.

*After the Православни подсетник’s calendar line for the day (pravoslavno.rs) — [the month](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=kalendar&godina=2026&mesec=09), which prints no life; read 23 August 2026.*
