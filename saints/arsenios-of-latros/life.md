# Arsenios of Latros

saint.gr's calendar for 13 December keeps the venerable Arsenios who practised asceticism on Mount Latros. Exactly when he lived, the page says, is not known to us; from what it has of his life it concludes that he lived between the eighth and the ninth century. He came from Constantinople, of a rich and distinguished family, and was himself a patrician and a general — of the Kibyrrhaiotai, and afterwards in the Byzantine fleet.

Once, in a terrible storm, the fleet went down and he alone was saved. He then gave what was left of his life to God, became a monk, and lived in strict fasting and prayer. Where he was first a monk the page does not know. Later he withdrew to an isolated place opposite the locality called Hieron, and from there to Mount Latros, where he gave himself to the harshest ascetic labour.

From Latros he was called to govern the monastery of Kellibara; but being troubled there and seeking quiet, he left and shut himself inside a hole, wearing out his body. The monks of the monastery found him and brought him back, and there he lived in a cell and saw the brethren only once in the week, on the Sunday, when he ate with them. He had a presentiment of the end of his life, called all the monks of the monastery together, gave them fitting spiritual counsel, and died in peace.
