# Basil of Mirozh

saint.gr keeps two abbots of Pskov together on this day and gives them three sentences. The venerable martyrs Basil and [Joasaph](/saints/joasaph-of-snetogorsk), it says, lived in the thirteenth century in the region of Pskov in Russia; Basil was abbot of the monastery of the Transfiguration of the Saviour of Mirozh, and Joasaph abbot of the monastery of the Nativity of the Theotokos of Mount Snatna; and the saints were martyred in the year 1299, when the Germans attacked Pskov and the monasteries of the region. Because the day's line names both, each is kept here as a folder of his own.

Of this one, beyond that, the page records nothing: no place of birth and no year for it, no family, no elder, no account of his years in the house, no manner of death beyond the raid it happened in, no burial and no relic. It prints no distich over either man and gives no hymn.

The corpus keeps seven other men of this name — of Ancyra, of Parium, of Ostrog, of Chernigov, of Thessalonica, of the Kyiv Caves, of Poiana Mărului — and none of them stands on this day; a prince Basil of Rostov is kept on the same Greek day, on a page of his own, and the two are not the same man.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 4 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/04/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3797/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
