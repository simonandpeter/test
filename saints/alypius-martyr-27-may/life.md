# Alypius (27 May)

saint.gr's calendar names him and gives him a single sentence: «Ο Άγιος Μάρτυς Αλύπιος τελειώθηκε, αφού του συνέτριψαν το κεφάλι του με ογκόλιθο» — the holy martyr Alypius was perfected after they crushed his head with a boulder. The page adds no year, no place, no persecutor and no trial, and it prints no hymn for him.

The couplet it sets above the sentence turns the manner of his death into the one thing said of him: «Τὸν εἰς κεφαλὴν γωνίας τιμῶν λίθον, / λίθῳ κεφαλὴν Ἀλύπιε συντρίβη» — honouring the stone that is the head of the corner, Alypius had his own head broken with a stone. The page also glosses his name, from the alpha of privation and λύπη, as the man who is never grieved.
