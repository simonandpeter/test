# Eudocia (Safronova)

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar for 3 ноября ст. ст. names her alone on a line of three words and a year: «Мц. Евдокии (1938).» — the rank word «Мц.», martyr, the forename in the genitive, and 1938. The name link behind the forename supplies the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line leaves out, «Евдокия (Сафронова), мц.», and repeats the rank and nothing else. That form, with the surname, is what this folder stores.

That is everything the calendar records of her. She has no life page, no icon, no hymn, no place, no age and no parish anywhere on this calendar, and the name page behind her place on the line carries two days and no text at all. The name page behind her name also lists «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. That is a synaxis and not a second feast, and she does not render on it. Nothing further is written here, and in particular nothing is reasoned from the surname.

No office is recorded and no grade is given: the rank word is the whole of what the calendar says of her state, and she is a lay woman martyred in 1938 so far as this page goes. Nothing is written of where, or at whose hands, or at what age.

The Russian church calendar at azbyka.ru prints what days.pravoslavie.ru does not, though not under her name: a life for the three clergy of her parish, headed «СВЯЩЕННОМУЧЕНИКИ ИОАНН КЕСАРИЙСКИЙ, ПЕТР КОСМИНКОВ, ПРЕСВИТЕРЫ И СИМЕОН КРЕЧКОВ, ДИАКОН», and it names her as the warden of their church. In the persecution it happened, the page says, that the godless arrested not only the priest but the whole clergy and the most active lay members of the church council, as at Быково in the Ramenskoye district: the priests [John (Kesariysky)](/saints/john-kesariysky) and [Peter (Kosminkov)](/saints/peter-kosminkov), the deacon [Simeon (Krechkov)](/saints/simeon-krechkov), herself as warden of the church, and the council members «Петр Пискарев-Молоков» and «Александр, Екатерина и Дмитрий Чиликины».

They were taken over three days, from 1 to 3 November 1937, and shut up in the Taganka prison in Moscow. On 15 November 1937 one NKVD troika sentenced the two priests and the deacon to be shot, two of the council members to ten years in a labour camp, and her and two others to eight. The three clergy were shot on 16 November 1937 and buried in one unmarked common grave on the Butovo range near Moscow; she was sent to the Taishet camp, died in prison on 25 August 1938 and was buried in an unmarked grave. The page records no questioning of hers and prints nothing she said.

One arrest, one village and one troika decision are what the relations now written here rest on. The service azbyka prints with the life is headed «Ме́сяца ноября́ в 3-й день» and «Новому́чеников Бы́ковских», its text approved by the Holy Synod of the Russian Orthodox Church on 14 May 2018, журнал № 37, and it names her with the three clergy as «му́ченица Евдоки́я Сафро́нова»; its troparion is now in this folder with the English typed here. The office her folder wanted for this is the church warden's, «староста», and no pack holds a key for it, so none is recorded and she keeps no office.
