# Eudocia (Safronova)

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar for 3 ноября ст. ст. names her alone on a line of three words and a year: «Мц. Евдокии (1938).» — the rank word «Мц.», martyr, the forename in the genitive, and 1938. The name link behind the forename supplies the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line leaves out, «Евдокия (Сафронова), мц.», and repeats the rank and nothing else. That form, with the surname, is what this folder stores.

That is everything the calendar records of her. She has no life page, no icon, no hymn, no place, no age and no parish anywhere on this calendar, and the name page behind her place on the line carries two days and no text at all. The name page behind her name also lists «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia. That is a synaxis and not a second feast, and she does not render on it. Nothing further is written here, and in particular nothing is reasoned from the surname.

No office is recorded and no grade is given: the rank word is the whole of what the calendar says of her state, and she is a lay woman martyred in 1938 so far as this page goes. Nothing is written of where, or at whose hands, or at what age.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 3 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261103.html) and [her name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/10069.html); read 4 October 2026.*
