# Nicetas (Delektorsky)

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar for 6 ноября ст. ст. heads a line of eleven names with his, and he is the only one of the eleven with a life page: «Сщмч. Никиты , еп. Орехово-Зуевского, Анатолия , Арсения , Николая , Николая , Константина пресвитеров., прмчч. Варлаама и Гавриила , Гавриила , прмцц. Нины и Серафимы (1937).» He was Фёдор Петрович Делекторский in the world, born in 1876 in the town of Pokrov in the Vladimir province. He finished the Kholm theological seminary in 1908 and then the Moscow Theological Academy with the degree of candidate of theology; he directed the Academy's choir and wrote a jubilee hymn for 1914.

**The page's own chronology will not stand.** It has him finish the seminary in 1908 and the Academy after that, serve a year as the Academy's assistant secretary, and then be ordained priest **«в 1898 году»** — ten years before the seminary he is said to have left in 1908. No other page was read that could break it, so both years are left exactly as the page prints them and neither is read as correcting the other; nothing in this folder's date depends on either.

From 1917 to 1921 he was rector of the Peter and Paul cathedral at Perm, and then of the cathedral of the Nativity of Christ in the town of Alexandrov in the Vladimir province. Between 1921 and 1924 he took the monastic habit, and on 16 (29) April 1924 he was consecrated bishop of Bugulma. In 1926 he was appointed bishop of Orekhovo-Zuyevo, vicar of the Moscow diocese, and from 1927 bishop of Nizhny Tagil, vicar of the Sverdlovsk diocese.

He was arrested four times — in 1924, 1925, 1930 and 1937 — and the charges were different each time: links with the «белочехи», the White Czechs; celebrating without a patent; commemorating the patriarch Tikhon. In 1927, the page says plainly, apparently for not agreeing with the position metropolitan Sergius took in his Declaration, he was retired at the age of fifty-one; and from that point **his wandering and homeless life began, and it lasted until his death**.

The 1930 arrest was in a flat in Moscow belonging to a woman the page names only as «Елизавета», who lived on the Samoteka and gave shelter to wanderers and homeless clergy. He was sentenced to three years of corrective labour and served it until 1933 on the building of the Dnieper dam, where he worked as a groom and a watchman. After his release he served secretly in the churches of Orekhovo-Zuyevo, and from 1935 he was a wanted man. He lived by gathering scrap and handing it in.

In 1936 and 1937, hiding where he came from, he slept in police barracks: one policeman was moved by the homeless man with a pity he could not explain to himself, let him sleep in the harness room and sometimes gave him tea. He himself, the page says, went hungry all the time.

On 18 October 1937 he was tracked down and arrested the fourth time. By the window of the cemetery church where they took him he quietly laid his wallet with his real papers in it, because for the last two years he had lived under the invented surname **Макаров**, on documents he had got from a prisoner; local people found the wallet and brought it to the police station, which made his position very much worse, because those papers said that he was a bishop. Besides the documents the wallet held eight bonds, a needle, thread, scissors and a five-rouble gold piece sewn into a scrap of cloth. **That was the whole of his property.**

He was taken to the Taganka prison in Moscow. The witnesses in his case were two priests, one from Orekhovo-Zuyevo and one from Zagorsk, who described him as «a monarchist and a revolutionary, slandering Soviet power»; the indictment said that he «был нелегальным, бродячим епископом», an illegal and wandering bishop, an activist of the True Orthodox Church, who carried on anti-Soviet agitation. On 4 (17 new style) November 1937 the troika of the NKVD administration for the Moscow region sentenced him to be shot, and two days later, on **6 (19 new style) November, he was shot at the NKVD range at Butovo**.

**19 November 1937 is the Julian 6 November in the civil reckoning, so this calendar keeps him on the day he was shot.** The page adds that the authorities thought him so dangerous that even before his arrest they arrested and soon shot the precentor of the Orekhovo-Zuyevo cathedral, «Андреев», for nothing but keeping up contact with him; that man is left in the page's own Cyrillic, as every bystander here is, because no folder in this corpus is identified with him. He was numbered among the New Martyrs and Confessors of Russia for church-wide veneration at the Jubilee Council of Bishops in 2000.

Two readings of his see stand on these pages and the folder takes the narrower. The day line and his name page both call him «еп. Орехово-Зуевского» and «епископ Орехово-Зуевский», and that is his `office` here; his life page is headed **«епископ Нижнетагильский, Орехово-Зуевский»**, naming both sees, and its own narrative gives him a third, Bugulma, before either. All three are in the prose above. He is given no relation to the ten others the line names: the line sorts them under him, but nothing on his life page says that any of them died with him.
