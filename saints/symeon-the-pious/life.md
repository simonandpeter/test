# Symeon the Pious

saint.gr's calendar for 12 Μαρτίου names him «Όσιος Συμεών ο Ευλαβής», the venerable Symeon the reverent, and the page behind that line begins with what is not there: «Η μνήμη του αποσιωπάται από τους Συναξαριστές», his memory is passed over in silence by the Synaxarists, and is mentioned in the Byzantine Eortologion of Gedeon, page 220. What it then says of him is that he lived in the middle of the tenth century and became the teacher of [Symeon the New Theologian](/saints/symeon-the-new-theologian), who composed an akolouthia for him which has not survived.

That is the whole of the page: no country, no monastery, no year, no relic and no hymn, and the service his own disciple wrote for him is named only to say that it is lost. The Greek calendar keeps the New Theologian himself on 12 October, not on this day, so the man the corpus already holds under that name is not this one; what is kept here is the elder, the teacher, on the day the Greek calendar gives him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1921/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
