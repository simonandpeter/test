# Amphilochius of Pângărați

Amphilochius was born in 1487 somewhere in the north of Moldavia and began the monastic life very young at Moldovița monastery, from which he came to Pângărați in 1508; there he was chosen by all as superior, and led the community for fifty-six years. The hieromonk Anastasius of Moldovița, who knew him, says of him: from childhood he laboured in the monastic life, witnessed by all for his good deeds; he was a very skilled writer and knew the craft of working wood, and was honoured and favoured by all the princes, though in book-learning he showed himself a simple man; and to old age he governed his life well, in hunger and thirst, in patience and labours, in every spiritual striving, and from the demons bore many assaults and temptations until death, and finished his life well, giving dust to dust and going the long road of the fathers, taking the wages of his labours from the just Judge, and buried with honour by his disciples. His labours were not overlooked by the Master of all but drew the care and protection of Demetrius, the monastery’s patron, who by a divine appearance asked the prince Alexander Lăpușneanu to build at Pângărați a church in his name on the site of the old wooden one burned by the Turks: while the voivode was at the market town of Piatra, on 22 October at midnight a fair young man in white robes appeared to him in a dream and said, rise, voivode, and go quickly to Pângărați, where a hermit Amphilochius has lived nearly fifty years — the Romanian life runs on past the point read here. The Romanian calendar keeps him on 7 September with [Simeon](/saints/simeon-of-pangarati), the founder, with a shared troparion.
