# Rodopianus

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 3 mai reads «Sfântul Mucenic Rodopian, diaconul» — Rodopianus the deacon, a martyr — and the page behind it carries the day, the epistle appointed for a martyr and the gospel read with it, and nothing more. The deaconate is the one thing the calendar says about him beyond the name and the crown.

The same day prints «Sfântul Mucenic Diodor», whose page is equally bare. Nothing on either page joins them, and nothing here does either.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and pairs him with the same Diodorus, and saint.gr adds the city and the reign. These saints lived in the years of the emperor Diocletian, it says, giving the year as 302, and for their faith in Christ they endured a great deal of abuse and scourging from the soldiers of the city of Aphrodisias in Caria — a country of Asia Minor, the page explains, which occupied its south-western corner, opposite the stretch of sea between the islands of Samos and Rhodes. In the end they were stoned by those same soldiers, and so gave up their souls to God who gives the crown. The one man the page names beside him is [Diodorus](/saints/diodorus-martyr-3-may), and its couplet puts his name into the stones: to Rodopianus and Diodorus the striking stones were roses, or rather gifts.

The Russian calendar keeps the two of them on 29 апреля where the Greek and the Romanian keep them on 3 May, and its account is four lines: they suffered under Diocletian at Aphrodisias in Caria and were stoned for spreading the Christian faith among the heathen. Two things in it are new to this folder. It names the province, «в Афродисии Карийской», where the Greek page named the city alone; and the page under his name grades him священномученик, a hieromartyr, where the Greek made him a martyr and a deacon. The grade was not changed on the strength of it: a deacon is not a priest, and each source's reading is left standing as that source's own.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 3 mai — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/3-mai) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-rodopian-diaconul); read 20 September 2026; and saint.gr's calendar for 3 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/03/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/879/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 29 апреля ст. ст. — [the Russian day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260429.html) and [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life953.htm), read 8 October 2026.*
