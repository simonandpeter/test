# Manuel of the East

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαρτίου names him third in a company of four entries: «Άγιοι Κοδράτος, Θεοδόσιος, Μανουήλ και άλλοι Τεσσαράκοντα μάρτυρες από την Ανατολή» — the holy Quadratus, Theodosius, Manuel and forty other martyrs from the East. The page tells the passion of all of them together, in the plural, and gives no year, no city, no emperor and no judge by name; it says they lived in the years of the Church's persecutions, under the idolatrous emperors.

When they were seized they were pressed to deny Christ, and they repeated their confession many times over, declaring that they would never desert the standard of the Gospel. The judge, at his wits' end, ordered their sides laid open, and when that torture proved powerless to shift their devotion to Christ he ordered them beheaded.

His own couplet, headed «Eις τον Mανουήλ», reads «Ξίφει χεθήτω, κἂν κοτύλη φησὶ μοι, Ἐμμανουήλ, πέφυκεν αἵματος μία» — let it be poured out by the sword, though it be but one cup, Emmanuel says to me, of blood there is only the one. The Greek does not construe cleanly as the page prints it, and it is copied here unmended. The page glosses the name, «ΜΑΝΟΥΗΛ (από Εμμανουήλ) = εκείνος με τον οποίο είναι ο Θεός», the one with whom God is. His fellows are [Quadratus](/saints/quadratus-of-the-east), [Theodosius](/saints/theodosius-of-the-east) and [the forty](/saints/forty-martyrs-of-the-east).

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3861/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
