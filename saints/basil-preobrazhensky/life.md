# Basil (Preobrazhensky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names a priest Basil on 1 июня and gives no life of him. Its line for the day is the whole of what it says: «Сщмч. Василия пресвитера, мц. Веры (1940).» — the hieromartyr Basil, a presbyter, with the martyr Vera beside him and one year, 1940, given to them both. So what the calendar tells is that he was a priest, that he was counted among the hieromartyrs, and that he died in 1940.

His own name page on that site, 13720, carries nothing but the heading «Василий (Преображенский), иерей, сщмч.», which gives the surname the day's line leaves out and grades him иерей, a priest, and the three days «26 января (Новомуч.) , 23 мая (Ростов.) , 1 июня». There is no life page for him on the site, no icon and no article, and no page names a parish, a diocese or a place of any kind. The second of those three days is the synaxis of the saints of Rostov and Yaroslavl, which is as near as anything printed comes to placing him.

The day page prints eight hymns — for the martyrs of its first line, for two venerable abbots and for the Athonite fathers — and none under his name. Neither the 26 января commemoration of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia nor the 23 мая Rostov synaxis was read, and nothing of either is in this folder.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 1 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260601.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13720.html); read 8 October 2026.*
