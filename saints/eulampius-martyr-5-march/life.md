# Eulampius (5 March)

saint.gr names the holy Eulampius on this day and gives him one sentence, and the life says exactly that: «Ο Άγιος Ευλάμπιος μαρτύρησε στην Παλαιστίνη διά ξίφους» — the holy Eulampius was martyred in Palestine by the sword. The country and the manner of his death are the whole of what is recorded of him; there is no year, no emperor, no judge, no city in Palestine, no relic and no hymn.

The one thing the site adds is the distich over him, and it is a play on words rather than a fact: Eulampius, his neck cut through by the sword, puts to shame a stiff-necked impiety. The site does not say whose impiety, or in what persecution.

The corpus keeps a Eulampius of Nicomedia who was martyred with his sister Eulampia and stands on another day, and he is not this man; nothing on this page connects the two.

The Russian church keeps him on this same day, and its line is the barest of the day's fourteen, «Мч. Евлампия .» — no year, no century, no place and no companion. Its life is two sentences: he lived in Palestine, and he was beheaded by the torturers for his faith in Christ. **The one thing the calendar adds is a place**, and it adds it in the index rather than in the line: the name link heads him Евлампий Палестинский, of Palestine, where this folder had nothing to tell him apart by but the day the corpus keeps him on. No dates are added, because no source read gives him one.

He stands on the day beside the Eulogius above, who is also of Palestine and was also beheaded; nothing on this host makes the two a company, neither line names the other, and the two lives are separate pages that do not cross. The shared province and the shared manner of death are not a relation, and no row is recorded between them.
