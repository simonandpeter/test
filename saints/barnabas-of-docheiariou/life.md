# Barnabas of Docheiariou

As a layman he was called Basil, and he worked at the dependency of the monastery of Docheiariou at Longos in Sithonia. In that time the abbot of the monastery was [Neophytos](/saints/neophytos-of-docheiariou), who was praying that God would help him finish the building of the church, which had stopped for want of means. Then Basil was enlightened, and found in the ground of the dependency a great treasure of gold coins. He went to the monastery and told the abbot, who sent him back with three monks to carry the treasure; and on the way back the monks wanted it for themselves and threw him into the sea tied to a heavy stone. The holy Archangels saved him out of the depth of the sea and he was found safe in the monastery.

He was tonsured a monk in that house by Neophytos and given the name Barnabas. When Neophytos became Protos of the Holy Mountain he appointed Barnabas abbot of the monastery, being, the notice says, held worthy by all to be its head and to better it. He laboured much for his house: he adorned the great church and the chambers with the lives of the saints, and furnished it with doors of gold and silver and with other vessels and things of the kind, which [Euthymius](/saints/euthymius-the-cellarer) and Neophytos had cared for before him; and being its second founder after Neophytos, and its third after Euthymius, he gave up his soul in peace.

The monastery's charters name him as abbot until the middle of the twelfth century, and its codex says that this holy monastery of the Holy Commanders has for its fame and praise its first founder Euthymius, and then Neophytos and Barnabas, who also consecrated it and were well pleasing to the Lord, as the accounts of them witness, and who in their own time worked wonders as the monks of old did. The notice closes by saying that Barnabas could be commemorated with the other two founders of the monastery, on 9 November — and that is how the Romanian calendar prints him. It is taken from Moses the Athonite's *Saints of the Holy Mountain*, and his page carries no Tropar tab, so the folder has no hymn.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 9 noiembrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/9-noiembrie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/cuviosul-varnavas-dohiaritul); read 25 September 2026.*
