# John (12 June)

saint.gr's calendar for 12 Ιουνίου names him and gives no life. Its whole notice is one sentence: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου Ιωάννη του Στρατιώτη» — we have no details of the life of Saint John the Soldier. So what the Greek calendar keeps of him is a name and a trade, and nothing else at all: no century, no place, no emperor, no manner of death.

What the page prints besides is the couplet of the synaxarion, which turns the trade into the whole of the praise: «O στρατιώτης νυν χορεύει εν πόλω, / Όπου στρατιαί Aγγέλων Iωάννης» — the soldier John now dances in heaven, where the armies of the angels are. There is no hymn for him on the page.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/466/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
