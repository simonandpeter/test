# Pergetos

saint.gr names him and then says that it knows nothing else. Its calendar for 8 February prints «Άγιος Περγέτος», bare — no epithet, no see, no city, no rank beyond the word holy — and the page behind that line answers with a single sentence: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Αγίου», we have no details of the saint's life. There is no century here, no manner of death, no relics, no reading of the name and no hymn, and this folder therefore carries no rank and no dates.

What the page does print over him is a couplet, and the couplet is itself about the poverty of the record: how should I pass Pergetos by unhonoured, it asks, when he is counted in among the saints? That question, the name and the day are the whole of the entry.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 8 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/08/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/530/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
