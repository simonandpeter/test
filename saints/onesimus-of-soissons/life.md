# Onesimus of Soissons

saint.gr's calendar for 13 May names him and gives him two sentences. Saint Onesimus lived in the fourth century, and in the year 350 he was elected bishop of the city of Soissons in France. He fell asleep in peace in the year 361; the page's head line gives his dates as an unknown birth and that death, and the calendar line calls him only Onesimus from Gaul.

The page tells nothing else — no birthplace, no parents, no act of his episcopate, no relics and no hymn — and it makes no connection between him and the Onesimus of the Epistle to Philemon, whom the corpus keeps separately. The Greek calendar keeps a bishop of Soissons of this name on 13 May, and that is the entry.
