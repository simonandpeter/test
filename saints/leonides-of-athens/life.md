# Leonides of Athens

saint.gr gives him two sentences and no more. In the Synaxaristai, it says, it is recorded that the holy Leonides, bishop of Athens, fell asleep in peace in the year 250; and some of the codices call him a martyr, a thing which is not proved. The page keeps him on 15 April as a fixed feast, and it says nothing of his birth, his family, his election to the see of Athens, or the manner of his going beyond that one word, peace.

Its couplet is the only picture it offers: «Σκότος συνέσχε τὰς Ἀθήνας ἀθρόον, Δύναντος αὐταῖς ἡλίου Λεωνίδου» — a darkness took hold of Athens all at once, when Leonides their sun went down. The apolytikion the page prints calls him a most holy shepherd and a wise hierarch of the renowned city of the Athenians, who served the Saviour in holiness, contended beyond nature and pulled down error, and it asks him now to intercede for those who honour him.
