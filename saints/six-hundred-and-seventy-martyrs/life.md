# The Six Hundred and Seventy Martyrs

saint.gr's calendar for 25 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιοι Εξακόσιοι Εβδομήκοντα Mάρτυρες» — six hundred and seventy martyrs — and its whole notice is one sentence: they were martyred by the sword. The page gives them no country, no city, no year, no emperor and not one name out of the six hundred and seventy, and so this is one entry and not six hundred and seventy.

What it does print is the couplet set over the line, and the couplet does nothing but count them twice over: they cut down thirty score of men, and with them seventy men more. That arithmetic is the whole of the source's interest in them, and it is the whole of what this entry can say. The page prints no relics for them and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 25 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/25/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1054/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
