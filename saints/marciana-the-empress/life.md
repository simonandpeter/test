# Marciana the Empress

doxologia.ro's calendar for 27 ianuarie names her and gives no life. The page it links under her name, *Viaţa Sfintei Marciana, împărăteasa*, carries a single sentence: «Sfânta Marciana împărăteasa este socotită cea întocmai cu Sfinţii Apostoli» — Saint Marciana the empress is reckoned equal to the holy Apostles. That is the whole of what the Romanian source says of her.

It gives no century, no city, no family and no account of her life or her death, and nothing has been added here from elsewhere. What the day keeps of her is the title of empress and the rank of one equal to the Apostles.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 27 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/27-ianuarie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfintei-marciana-imparateasa); read 19 September 2026.*
