# Theophilus (5 November)

Theophilus is the second of three martyrs whom saint.gr's calendar keeps in one entry on 5 Νοεμβρίου, and everything the page says of him it says of the three together. They lived in the years of the emperor Maximian, when the governor of Palestine was Urbanus, beside whose name the page sets the year 298; they were young in years and of good presence; and they were martyred by being beaten with fists until they died. The page gives no country, no trial, no burial, no year of their own and no hymn.

The synaxarion's couplet for the entry names only the first of them: «Τὸν Τιμόθεον, καὶ συναθλητὰς δύω / Κτείνουσι πυγμαί, καὶ Θεὸς τιμᾷ Λόγος» — fists kill Timothy and his two fellow-contenders, and God the Word honours him. Theophilus is one of those two; the others are [Timothy](/saints/timothy-5-november) and [Theotimus](/saints/theotimus-5-november).

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2891/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
