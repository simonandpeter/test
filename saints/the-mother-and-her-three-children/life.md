# The Mother and her Three Children

saint.gr's calendar for 2 June keeps a mother and her three children and gives no name to any of the four. The one sentence it prints about them says that the holy mother and her three holy children bore witness by the sword; and that is all. There is no year, no reign, no city, no persecutor and no account of how they were taken — only that the four died together and that the sword did it.

The couplet the page sets above the sentence is the only other thing it gives them, and what it gives is a voice: cut down together with her children, O mother blessed in her children, see, you cry out, here am I and the children. The corpus keeps a second such company from the same calendar, two martyrs who are a mother and her daughter and are likewise unnamed, on 28 January. A reader who wants a name will not find one here, and the page does not pretend to have one.
