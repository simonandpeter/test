# Gaianus (7 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 7 December keeps two martyrs in one entry and names both: [Gaius](/saints/gaius-martyr-7-december) and Gaianus. The whole of the life it gives them is one sentence — that the holy Gaius and Gaianus were martyred by fire. No country, no reign, no persecutor and no year is printed for either of them.

The couplet set over the entry gives Gaianus the only words either of them speaks: though you be thrown into the midst of the furnace, Gaius, I will not leave you. The Greek calendar keeps other men of this name on other days, which the corpus already holds, and nothing here joins this one to any of them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3188/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
