# Leo (Ershov)

Leo Ershov was born on 12 February 1867 in the town of Krasnoufimsk in Perm province, the son of an Old Believer merchant. In 1881 he finished the two-class town school and took up trade himself; he had great knowledge of the schismatics’ doctrine, was respected among them and was a reader in the Fedoseyev sect, but serious spiritual searching and the striving to find the truth led him gradually to the conviction that it is the Russian Orthodox Church that keeps the apostolic teaching pure, and at twenty-seven, on the feast of the Meeting of the Lord, he by personal — the calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. Ordained priest in 1896, he served at the Holy Trinity cathedral, became a well-known missionary among the Old Believers and a teacher of the Law of God; in 1918, for openly condemning the red terror, he was arrested during the liturgy itself, and after cruel tortures shot on 2 September 1918 with the priest [Alexander Malinovsky](/saints/alexander-presbyter-martyr-1918-20-august). In 2002 his relics were found, with the marks of torture on the body; he was canonised in 2000, and the Russian calendar keeps him on 20 August (Julian).
