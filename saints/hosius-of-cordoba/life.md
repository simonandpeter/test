# Hosius of Córdoba

Hosius lived in the days of Constantine the Great, distinguished for his virtue and for his brave part in the struggles for Orthodoxy. He took part in the First Ecumenical Council at Nicaea, and in the council of Sardica in 347, and helped to clear Athanasius the Great of the Arians’ charges against him.

When the emperor Constantius pursued Athanasius relentlessly he did the same to the bishop of Córdoba, subjecting him to many exiles and hardships; but Hosius kept his Orthodox mind upright to the end. Theodoret praises him for it, and Athanasius the Great calls him the father of bishops. The Romanian calendar keeps him on the same day.

The Russian and Serbian calendars keep him the same day, 27 August (Julian): bishop of Córdoba in Spain for more than sixty years in the fourth century, deeply honoured by Constantine the Great, who made him a close counsellor; he pressed Constantine to summon the First Ecumenical Council at Nicaea in 325 and was the first to sign its decrees, and in 347 presided at the council of Sardica. After Constantine’s death he firmly defended Athanasius of Alexandria against the Arian emperor Constantius, and for it was exiled to Sirmium; returning to Córdoba he died in 359 — so zealous for Orthodoxy, the Prologue says, that before his death he anathematised the Arian heresy once more.
