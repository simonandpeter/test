# Expeditus

Expeditus is one of five martyrs saint.gr prints together on 19 April, with [Hermogenes](/saints/hermogenes-19-april), [Aristonicus](/saints/aristonicus-19-april), [Rufus](/saints/rufus-19-april) and [Galatas](/saints/galatas), and of all five the page says the same thing and nothing else: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο και το μαρτύριο των Αγίων» — we have no details of the life and the martyrdom of the saints. The calendar's line for the day names them «Άγιοι Ερμογένης, Εξπέδιτος, Αριστόνικος, Ρούφος και Γαλατάς οι Μάρτυρες».

The page heads each of the five separately, each as ο Μάρτυρας, the martyr, and that title is the whole of what it asserts about them. It gives no century and no emperor, no city, no judge, no manner of death, no relic, no couplet and no hymn. The glosses it sets under some of the names are notes on the words themselves and on classical bearers of them, and they are not about these men.

The corpus holds nobody else of this name. A folder this short is what the source has, and this one does not supply what the calendar does not say.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 19 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/19/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1969/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
