# Archelaus, with a hundred and fifty-two martyrs

saint.gr keeps the holy Archelaus on this day together with the hundred and fifty-two martyrs who suffered with him, and gives the whole company one sentence: the saints were martyred by the sword. Then it adds a parenthesis which unsettles its own count — in the Koutloumousian Menaion, it says, the martyrs are numbered a hundred and forty-two. The site prints both numbers and chooses neither, and both are recorded here.

None of the company is named on the page, so they are kept in this one entry and not as folders of their own: a number is what the calendar prints, and a number is what this folder holds. Of Archelaus himself nothing at all is told beyond the name and the sword — no country, no city, no year, no emperor, no judge, no office, no relic and no hymn. The distich the site prints gives him only his place in the order of the killing: Archelaus, laying his neck first under the sword, begins the cutting for you, divine people of the Lord.

The same Greek day carries a second page, for [Photius](/saints/photius-companion-of-cyril) and [Cyril](/saints/cyril-companion-of-photius), of whom it says only that they probably belonged to these hundred and fifty-two. That «μάλλον», probably, is the site's own, and it is recorded on those two folders without being acted on here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3839/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
