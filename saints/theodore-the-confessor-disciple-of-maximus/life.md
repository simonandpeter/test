# Theodore, disciple of Maximus

Theodore was one of the disciples of [Maximus the
Confessor](/saints/maximus-the-confessor), and suffered what his teacher suffered: his tongue and
his right hand were cut off. After twenty years of exile he gave up his soul to the Lord. The
Greek calendar keeps him on 20 September with Maximus and the others.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 20 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/982/saint.aspx); read 26 August 2026.*
