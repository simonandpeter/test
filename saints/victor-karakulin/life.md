# Victor (Karakulin)

The Russian calendar names him and nothing more, and its line is the barest on the day: «Сщмч. Виктора (1937)» — the hieromartyr Victor, with the year and no epithet, no see and no surname. The surname is in the name link behind it, «Виктор (Каракулин), иерей, сщмч.», which also gives the grade the line omits, and that page carries a heading and two days and not one sentence of narrative. The first of its two days, 26 января, is the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which is a company's feast and not a day of his own.

This folder therefore asserts only what the calendar asserts: that the Russian church keeps a priest of this name and surname as a hieromartyr on this day, and that he died in 1937. The corpus already keeps another Victor on this menologion day, of a different surname, and nothing read links the two. What a second source would add is everything, and azbyka.ru was not read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 19 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260519.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9516.html), which carries no life; read 8 October 2026.*
