# John of Verkhoturye

saint.gr gives him two sentences. Saint John of Verkhoturye lived in Russia between the seventeenth and the eighteenth century, it says, and practised asceticism in a way pleasing to God in the monastery of Saint Nicholas in the district of Verkhoturye, and fell asleep in peace. The day's line calls him a fool for Christ; the prose calls him an ascetic and does not use the word again.

Its heading gives 1701 for the year of his death and none for his birth. The page names no parents, no elder, no wonder and no act of folly, and it prints neither couplet nor hymn; the only other thing on it is a link to the synaxis of all the saints of Siberia.
