# Ammonius of Pentapolis

saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαρτίου names him last of four on one line — «Άγιοι Θεόδωρος ο Ιερομάρτυρας, Ειρηναίος ο Διάκονος, Σεραπίων και Αμμώνιος οι αναγνώστες» — and the page behind it has a single sentence for the company: the holy hieromartyr Theodore was martyred in the year 304 in the Pentapolis of Libya, together with his deacon Irenaeus and the readers Serapion and Ammonius. Ammonius was therefore a reader, the second of the two the sentence names in that order.

He suffered with [Theodore](/saints/theodore-of-pentapolis), with the deacon [Irenaeus](/saints/irenaeus-of-pentapolis) and with his fellow reader [Serapion](/saints/serapion-of-pentapolis), in the Pentapolis of Libya, the five cities of Cyrenaica. The page names no city among the five, gives their bishop no see, and says nothing of how any of the four died.

It heads him «(; - 304)», so there is no year of birth, and it records no emperor, no judge, no relic and no hymn. The list of philosophers and sculptors the page attaches to the name Αμμώνιος is a note on the word and has nothing to do with the martyr. The corpus has no rank slug for a reader, so that office stands in the prose only.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 26 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/26/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3868/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
