# Aristides of Athens

Aristides was an Athenian patrician, a philosopher of a glorious Athenian family, who lived in the second century under Hadrian. He became a Christian through Dionysius and Hierotheus and wrote to Hadrian an apology for the persecuted Christians; then he was driven out, and as Hadrian was absent went to Rome, where he made his defence, and was then brought back to Athens, where he was martyred, hanged in the agora of Athens on 13 September 120. Jerome wrote an encomium on him and calls him equal-to-the-apostles.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 13 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2426/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
