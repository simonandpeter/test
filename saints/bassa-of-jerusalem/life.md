# Bassa of Jerusalem

saint.gr's calendar for 9 December keeps the holy Bassa, and what it gives her is an office and a foundation rather than a story. She was a patrician, the page says, and the abbess of a women's monastery at Jerusalem, where she also founded the monastery of Saint Menas, whose abbot was Stephen, bishop of Jamnia.

It then names its own witness: the memory of Saint Bassa is recorded in the Jerusalem Kanonarion, at page 120. That is the whole of the entry — no century, no country of birth, no manner of death, no relic, no hymn and no synaxaristic couplet. The Greek calendar keeps other women of this name on other days, and nothing here joins this one to any of them.
