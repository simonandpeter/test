# Platon Aivazidis

Platon Aivazidis was born on Patmos in 1852 and brought up in the Christian ideals. At fifteen he became a novice in the monastery of the Theologian there, and studied at the Patmias School. In 1892 he became deacon to the Metropolitan of Lemnos, and later went to study in Constantinople, where he met Germanos Karavangelis, Metropolitan of Kastoria, and became his assistant.

When Germanos became Metropolitan of Amaseia in Pontus, Platon followed him. There the Turks accused him of having stirred up a revolt of the Greeks, and in the end he was hanged, on 21 September 1921.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 21 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/3425/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
