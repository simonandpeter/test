# Vincentius (11 December)

saint.gr's calendar for 11 December keeps four martyrs in one entry and names all four: [Terentius](/saints/terentius-martyr-11-december), Vincentius, [Emilian](/saints/emilian-martyr-11-december) and [Bebaia](/saints/bebaia-martyr-11-december). The whole of the life it gives them is one sentence — that the holy Terentius, Vincentius, Emilianus and Bebaia were martyred by the sword. No country, no reign, no persecutor and no year is printed for any of them.

The couplet set over the entry counts them and reads the count: cut down, three and one, they show forth the three persons of God and the one nature. The gloss the site sets under his name — from the Latin for conquering, the victor — is its standing note on the name itself. Nothing else is said of him, and nothing here joins him to the Vincent of Spain the corpus keeps on another day.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/11/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3169/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
