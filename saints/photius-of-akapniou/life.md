# Photius, founder of the Akapniou monastery

saint.gr's whole notice of him is two sentences, and neither of them is a life. The memory of the venerable Photius, it says, is mentioned only in the Lavriotic codex Γ 86, folio 142a, where there stands as well an idiomelon poem by Demetrius Beaskos, great steward of the metropolis of Thessalonica; and his memory is mentioned also by the Jerusalem codex 309, folio 91. The page prints those two manuscript references and stops.

What the calendar remembers him for is in the day's line and nowhere else: «Όσιος Φώτιος κτήτορας της Μονής Ακαπνίου στη Θεσσαλονίκη» — the venerable Photius, founder of the monastery of Akapniou at Thessalonica. The page gives no century and no year, no country, no account of his life or of his end, and no hymn of its own; the poem it reports in the codex it does not quote.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 9 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/09/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2016/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
