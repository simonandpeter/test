# George, companion of Theodore

saint.gr's entry for the two of them is one line: we have no details of the life of the saints. Above it stands the synaxarion's distich, which says that [Theodore](/saints/theodore-companion-of-george) and George raised a great trophy against error out of their contests. The calendar's line is «Άγιοι Θεόδωρος και Γεώργιος», and that is all it says.

The page gives neither of them a byname, a place, a century or a hymn, and it does not say what the contests were. What stands above the notice is the site's gloss on the name George, the tiller of the earth, which belongs to the name and not to this man.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 21 Ιουλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/07/21/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2023/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
