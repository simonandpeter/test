# Paul, companion of Reverianus

saint.gr's calendar for 1 Ιουνίου names two men and a company, and the life behind the line is two sentences. The holy hieromartyrs [Reverianus](/saints/reverianus), who was a bishop, and Paul the presbyter came from Italy, it says, and lived in the third century; and they were sent to preach the Gospel in Gaul.

They were arrested in the region of Autun and were martyred in the year 272, together with other Christians, under the emperor Aurelian, whose reign the page dates 270 to 275. That is all: the page gives Paul no birthplace in Italy, no church of his own, no age, no manner of death, and it prints no hymn.

The other Christians who died with them have a heading of their own on the page and no names and no number in it, so this corpus makes them no folder; the two who are named have one each, and both carry this same account.
