# Sabinus of Cyprus (12 May)

The Russian church calendar keeps him on 12 мая ст. ст. and gives him a notice of eight sentences. He was born, it says, in the Phoenician city of Lycia. Hearing of a renowned ascetic, [Epiphanius of Cyprus](/saints/epiphanius-of-cyprus), he went to him and received the monastic tonsure, and for five years he laboured with him in the desert. Afterwards he wrote down his teacher's life and his labours, which is the one thing the calendar says he made.

When his teacher was raised to the see of Cyprus he ordained Sabinus presbyter; and after the death of the man who had guided him he became his successor on that see. A wise archpastor, the notice says, he fulfilled his new obedience zealously, defending the Church from heretics. He reposed in advanced years in the middle of the fifth century, and the calendar gives him no year and no account of his death.

The day line and his life page do not agree on his rank. The line prints «Свтт. Савина , архиеп. Кипрского (V) и прочих свтт. Кипрских», which makes him archbishop; the page is headed «Святитель Савин, епископ Кипрский», which makes him bishop. Both are set down here and neither is chosen. The rest of that line — the other hierarchs of Cyprus — names nobody, so nobody else is written from it. The day page carries a troparion and a kontakion for two of the hierarchs it heads, and none under his name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 12 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260512.html), [the life](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1045.htm) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/1912.html); read 8 October 2026.*
