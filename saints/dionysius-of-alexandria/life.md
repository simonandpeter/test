# Dionysius of Alexandria

Dionysius was bishop of Alexandria, the Greek synaxarion says, and was martyred in the reign of Valerian, in 254. The prefect Aemilian tried in vain to draw him away from the Christian faith with promises, threats and punishments, for Dionysius was an athlete of Christ already proven in the persecution under Decius. With him were gathered eight of his disciples, in every way worthy of their teacher in the fervour of their piety and the firmness of their faith; all of them were bound and shut in a narrow place, harshly tortured there, and so gave up their holy souls to God.

The entry names six of the eight: [Faustus](/saints/faustus-disciple-of-dionysius-of-alexandria), [Gaius](/saints/gaius-disciple-of-dionysius-of-alexandria), [Peter](/saints/peter-disciple-of-dionysius-of-alexandria), [Paul](/saints/paul-disciple-of-dionysius-of-alexandria), [Eusebius](/saints/eusebius-disciple-of-dionysius-of-alexandria) and [Chaeremon](/saints/chaeremon-disciple-of-dionysius-of-alexandria).

Its verse has Dionysius, with an eightfold company of fellow athletes, pass from the darkness and dwell in the place of light. The Greek calendar keeps them on 3 October, and the entry says that the memory of Eusebius and Chaeremon, with Faustus and Gaius, is repeated on 4 October. The two disciples it does not name have no folder.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 3 Οκτωβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2582/saint.aspx); read 17 September 2026.*
