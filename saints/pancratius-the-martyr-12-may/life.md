# Pancratius (12 May)

saint.gr's calendar for 12 May names him and gives him one sentence. The holy martyr Pancratius came from Syria or from Phrygia — the page offers the two and does not choose between them — and was martyred at Rome in the year 304, in the time of the emperor Diocletian, who reigned from 284 to 305. Its head line gives his dates as «; - 304», an unknown birth and that death.

Nothing else is printed: no parents, no office, no judge, no manner of death, no relics and no hymn. The Greek church keeps a martyr of this name on 12 May, distinct from the bishop of Tauromenium and from the recluse of the Kyiv Caves whom the corpus already holds, and this folder carries the calendar's line and the one sentence behind it.
