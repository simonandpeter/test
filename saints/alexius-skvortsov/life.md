# Alexius (Skvortsov)

days.pravoslavie.ru’s calendar for 21 июня ст. ст. prints him in the line «Сщмч. Алексия , Павла и Николая пресвитеров (1938).», and the name link behind his place in it supplies the calendar’s own nominative with the surname, «Алексий (Скворцов), иерей, сщмч.» (name page 14278). That is where the name form in this folder is read from; the line gives the forename in the genitive and no surname at all.

His name page carries nothing else: «Дни памяти: 26 января (Новомуч.) , 21 июня» and then no life, no place and no account of his death in 1938. Nothing is supplied for that here. The line’s «Сщмчч.» makes him a hieromartyr and «пресвитеров» a presbyter, and the name link repeats the rank as «иерей». The corpus holds two other folders whose stored forms carry «Скворцов», a Nicholas and a Sergius, and neither is an Alexius.

The second day his name page lists is the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which is a synaxis and not a second feast, so he renders on 21 июня ст. ст. only. He is given no relation to the two others on his line: they share a line, a grade word and a year, and the calendar prints nothing that says they died in one place or knew one another.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 21 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260621.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14278.html); read 9 October 2026.*
