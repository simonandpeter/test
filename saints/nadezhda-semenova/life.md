# Nadezhda Semenova

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names Nadezhda Semenova on 25 апреля and gives no life of her. Its line for the day reads «Сщмч. Сергия пресвитера, св. Надежды , исп (1938).» — the hieromartyr Sergius the presbyter, and the holy Nadezhda, a confessor — and the year that closes it stands at the end of an entry naming two people, so it is not certainly hers alone.

Her own name page on that site carries nothing but the heading «Надежда Семенова, исп.», that rank, and two days: 26 января, which is the general commemoration of the New Martyrs and Confessors of Russia, and 25 апреля, the day read here. There is no life page for her anywhere on the site, no icon and no article, and the day page prints no hymn under her name. The 26 января day page was not read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 25 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260425.html) and [her name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/15091.html); read 8 October 2026.*
