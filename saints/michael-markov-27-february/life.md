# Michael (Markov)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names him for 27 февраля ст. ст. in four words — «Мч. Михаила (1938).» — a martyr who died in 1938. The name link behind the line carries the calendar's own nominative, with the surname the line leaves out and with the word for his service: «Михаил (Марков), псаломщик, мч.». «Псаломщик» is the reader who sings and reads at the services, and it is the only thing the calendar says about his place in the church; he is one of the new martyrs of the Russian church in the Soviet years.

The calendar prints no life page for him and no hymn under his name. Nothing further is written here: no parish, no place and no manner of death, because the calendar gives none of them, and nothing is reasoned out from the surname or from the year.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 27 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260227.html); read 3 October 2026.*
