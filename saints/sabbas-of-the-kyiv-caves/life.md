# Sabbas of the Kyiv Caves

saint.gr's whole account of him is three sentences. Sabbas of the Caves of Kyiv lived in the near caves of the Lavra of Kyiv in the thirteenth century; and in the manuscripts and in the canon of the service to the Fathers of the Caves of Kyiv he is called Wonderworker. He fell asleep in peace. The calendar's line for the day calls him only «Όσιος Σάββας ο εν τω Σπηλαίω», the venerable Sabbas in the cave.

The third sentence explains why the Greek calendar keeps him here rather than anywhere else: «η μνήμη του εορτάζεται στις 24 Απριλίου, εξαιτίας του Αγίου Μάρτυρα Σάββα του Στρατηλάτου» — his memory is kept on 24 April because of the holy martyr [Sabbas Stratelates](/saints/sabbas-stratelates), whose day this is. The day is his namesake's, in other words, and not a date of his own.

Nothing else is printed of him: no year of birth or death, no parentage, no elder, no wonder in particular although he is called Wonderworker, no relic and no hymn, and no couplet.

The Russian calendar is the first of this folder’s sources to say where he struggled, when, and why his day is his day. He struggled in the Near Caves of the Kyiv Caves monastery in the thirteenth century, and in the manuscript calendars, in the «Книга о святых» and in the canon of the service to the venerable fathers of the Caves he is called a wonderworker. His memory is kept on 24 April, the page says plainly, «ради тезоименитства со святым мучеником Саввой Стратилатом» — for the sake of his bearing the same name as the martyr Савва Стратилат, whose own line heads this same day of the calendar. So the day this folder now renders on is a day of a shared name and not the day of his repose, which the calendar nowhere gives.

He is kept besides with the synaxis of the venerable fathers of the Near Caves on 28 September and with the synaxis of all the Kyiv Caves wonderworkers on the second Sunday of Great Lent. The first of those is a synaxis and not a second feast of his own; the second is moveable and could not be held by this corpus at all. Beside him in the same caves, and in the two hymns the day prints, lies [Alexios the Recluse of Kyiv](/saints/alexios-the-recluse-of-kyiv), whose own page says that his day is this day because his relics rest next to these.
