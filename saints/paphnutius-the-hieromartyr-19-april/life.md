# Paphnutius (19 April)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The page for 19 April prints his line — «Sfântul Sfințit Mucenic Pafnutie» — and his own page carries no Viață tab at all: only the hymns, a canon of prayer to him, icons and the appointed readings. It tells nothing of his country, of the see where he served, of the persecution in which he suffered or of the year of his death, and this folder does not supply what the calendar does not say.

What the hymns say of him is this. The troparion appointed for him is the common of a hieromartyr: that he was a sharer in the apostles' ways and a successor of their thrones, who taught the word of truth rightly and endured in the faith unto blood. The kontakion is more nearly his own — that he served first as a priest with a pure soul, and suffered afterwards with a most steadfast mind, and so was well-pleasing to God; and that, dwelling now in the heavens, he dances with the martyrs and rejoices together with the hierarchs.

Three other men of the name stand in this corpus — Paphnutius the Egyptian and Paphnutius the father of Euphrosyne, both kept on 25 September, and Paphnutius of the Thebaid, whom the Serbian calendar keeps on 11 September — and nothing on this page identifies him with any of them.
