# John, son of Theodore the Varangian

John was the son of [Theodore the Varangian](/saints/theodore-the-varangian), and with him the first martyr of the Russian land. The reading calls him a handsome and devout youth who confessed Christianity as his father did, Theodore having taught him the faith after returning from his own long service in the Byzantine armies.

In 983 the prince of Kyiv, then still a pagan, resolved on a human sacrifice after a victory in the field, and his elders and boyars said: *«Бросим жребий на отроков и девиц, на кого падет он, того и зарежем в жертву богам»* — let us cast lots on the youths and the maidens. The lot fell on John, and the reading remarks that it did so, probably, not without design.

His father refused the demand and faced the armed crowd with him on the gallery of their house. The posts were cut away beneath them and both were killed. Their grave is unknown. The calendar grades John «отрок», a child, and gives him no other title; the lower timbers of the burnt house, the reading closes, were uncovered in 1908 in the excavations beside the altar of the Tithe church at Kyiv.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 12 июля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260712.html) and the readings [life6877](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life6877.htm) and [life4222](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4222.htm); read 9 October 2026.*
