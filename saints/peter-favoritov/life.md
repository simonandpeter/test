# Peter (Favoritov)

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar for 7 ноября ст. ст. gives him a line of his own, and it is four words and a year: «Св. Петра диакона, исп (1930).» — a deacon, a confessor, 1930. The name link behind «Петра» supplies the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line leaves out, «Петр (Фаворитов), диакон, исповедник», and that is where this folder's name form is read from.

**«Св.» and «исп», not «Сщмч.»** — the calendar does not call him a martyr. It calls him a saint and a confessor, which is what this folder's types record: a confessor and a deacon, with Deacon as his office. The year 1930 is the year on the line and nothing on the page says how he died, or whether he died in prison, in exile or at liberty.

That is everything the calendar records of him. He has no life page, no icon, no hymn, no place, no age and no parish anywhere on this calendar, and the name page behind his place on the line carries its title, three days and no text at all. That page also lists «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which is a synaxis and not a second feast; this folder renders on 7 ноября ст. ст. only. His name page lists a third day, «23 мая (Ростов.)», the synaxis of the saints of Rostov, which is likewise a synaxis and not a feast; that day page was not read, and no place is taken from it.

He is given no relation to anybody. His line names nobody else, and the fifteen names of 1937 that the same day page carries on another line are another entry and another decade; sharing a day is not a relation here.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 7 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261107.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/9860.html); read 4 October 2026.*
