# Anthousa, with twelve servants

saint.gr keeps Anthousa and her twelve servants together on this day and gives them one sentence: «Η Αγία Ανθούσα και οι δώδεκα υπηρέτες της μαρτύρησαν διά ξίφους. Δεν έχουμε περισσότερες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο τους» — the holy Anthousa and her twelve servants were martyred by the sword, and we have no further details of their life. The page carries no year, no place, no emperor, no judge and no hymn.

The twelve are named nowhere. The day's line calls them «οι δώδεκα υπηρέτες της», her twelve servants; the life page heads them «Αγιοι Δωδεκα Υπηρετες Της Αγιας Ανθουσας» and then prints her own name below — and that is as near to a list as the site comes. So they are kept here in her name and not in twelve of their own.

The distich over her is the one thing the page adds, and it is about the twelve: «Εὔνους ἐφεῦρες, Ἀνθοῦσα, τοὺς οἰκέτας, Ξίφει θανούσῃ, συνθανόντας σοι ξίφει» — you found your household servants well disposed, Anthousa, dying with you by the sword when you died by the sword.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 22 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/22/index.aspx) and [the page for her and the twelve](https://www.saint.gr/3679/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
