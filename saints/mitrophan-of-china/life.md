# Mitrophan of China

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him on 10 июня and gives no life of him. Its line is «Сщмч. Митрофана пресвитера и иже с ним мучеников многих (1900).» — the hieromartyr Mitrophan, a presbyter, and many martyrs with him, and the year 1900. So what the calendar tells is that he was a priest counted among the martyrs, that he did not die alone, and that he died in 1900.

His own name page on that site, 14812, carries nothing but the heading «Митрофан Китайский, иерей, сщмч.», which gives the epithet the day's line leaves out and grades him иерей, a priest, and the one day «10 июня». There is no life page for him on the site, no icon and no article, and no page names a parish or a place beyond the word Китайский, Chinese.

The «мучеников многих» of his line has a name link of its own, to «222 китайских новомучеников убиенных при Боксерском восстании в 1900 г.» — two hundred and twenty-two Chinese new martyrs killed in the Boxer rising of 1900 — and that page keeps the company not on this day but on «11 июня», the day after. The company enumerates nobody by name on either page, so no folder was made for it here, and the next day's reader will meet it on its own day.
