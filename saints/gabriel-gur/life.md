# Gabriel (Gur)

days.pravoslavie.ru's calendar for 6 ноября ст. ст. carries eleven names on one line and his is the ninth of them: «Сщмч. Никиты , еп. Орехово-Зуевского, Анатолия , Арсения , Николая , Николая , Константина пресвитеров., прмчч. Варлаама и Гавриила , Гавриила , прмцц. Нины и Серафимы (1937).» The line sorts the eleven into four classes and gives each class one rank word: «Сщмч.» for the bishop and the five presbyters, «прмчч.» for the abbot and the two hieromonks, «прмцц.» for the two nuns, and the single year 1937 for all of them. The name link behind his place in it supplies the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line leaves out, «Гавриил (Гур), иеромонах, прмч.», and that is where the name form in this folder is read from.

That is everything the calendar records of him. He has no life page, no icon, no hymn, no place, no age and no parish anywhere on this calendar, and the name page behind his place on the line carries its title, two days and no text at all. That page also lists «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, which is a synaxis and not a second feast; this folder renders on 6 ноября ст. ст. only. Nothing further is written here, and in particular nothing is reasoned from the surname.

Here the line and the name page agree: the line's «прмчч.» and the page's «прмч.» both make him a venerable martyr, and the page's «иеромонах» makes him a hieromonk. No office is written beside it, the grade word being the type itself. Of the three the line classes together he is the only one whose two readings match.

He is given no relation to the ten others the line names. They share a line, a year and the classes the line sorts them into, and **the one life page this entry has does not say that any of the other ten died with its own subject** or knew him; a shared day is not a relation here, and a line of men sharing a line, a grade and a year is not a company unless a page says they died together.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 6 ноября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261106.html) and [his name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/13846.html); read 4 October 2026.*
