# Juliana of Moscow

The Russian church calendar names her and gives no life. Its list for 3 мая ст. ст. reads «Прпп. Иулиании (1393) и Евпраксии (1394) Московских» — the venerable Juliana and Eupraxia of Moscow, with a year for each — and the name page behind the first of those names carries the heading «Иулиания Московская», the two days on which she is kept, and nothing further except a link to the Conception convent in Moscow, «Зачатьевский ставропигиальный женский монастырь». The day page prints no troparion or kontakion under her name.

So what the source says of her is this: that she was a woman under monastic vows, that she is kept together with [Eupraxia of Moscow](/saints/eupraxia-of-moscow), that she died in 1393, that her memory falls on 3 мая and again on 24 августа as Moscow keeps it, and that the house her name is attached to is that convent. It gives her no birthplace, no family, no office and no account of her labours, and none has been supplied here.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 3 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260503.html) and [her name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14456.htm), which gives no life; read 8 October 2026.*
