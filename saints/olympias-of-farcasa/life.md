# Olympias of Fărcașa

Olympias was born on 8 September 1880 and grew up and lived in piety, keeping the fasts, the feasts and the customs of her forefathers strictly. Though she could not read, she knew prayers, troparia and psalms by heart, and her faith was simple and strong. She was an unmatched housekeeper who raised eight children, giving them not only food and clothing but a moral upbringing founded on the fear of God and respect for other people. Doxologia's page names her as the mother of the venerable Petroniu of Prodromu.

Almsgiving was one of her chief virtues: she received with love anyone who needed help and gave generously to the poor. The remembrance of the dead was a sacred duty to her and prayer a daily practice. Even in old age, and suffering, she did not miss church, and spent every coin she was given on candles lit before the Mother of God.

Feeling her end near, she prepared with fasting, confession and communion, and in her last days sang church hymns and prayed without ceasing. On 4 July 1967, at the age of eighty-seven, she died quietly with a lit candle in her hand and a smile on her lips. She was numbered among the saints by the Holy Synod of the Romanian Orthodox Church in its session of 1 July 2025, under the title Saint Olympias of Fărcașa, with her commemoration on 4 July. Doxologia's troparion page for her is a recording of the hymn and prints no text, so no hymn is recorded here.
