# Lot (22 October)

saint.gr's calendar for 22 Οκτωβρίου names a venerable Lot and describes him in one sentence: a wise ascetic of the desert who died in peace, whose sayings are in the «Παράδεισος των Πατέρων», the Paradise of the Fathers. The couplet over the entry turns on the two men of the name, saying that this new Lot of the new covenant, new in his manner of life as well, dwells worthily with the Lot of the old.

For a life the page quotes Nicodemus the Hagiorite. Lot said this to Abba Joseph: father, according to my strength I make my little prayer, and my little fast, and my study, and my stillness; and according to my strength I keep my mind clean of evil thoughts; what else is there for me to do? Then Joseph rose up and stretched out his hands to heaven — and, the page says, O the wonder! the ten fingers of his hands became like ten burning lamps — and he said to Lot: if you will, strive to become all of you like fire, in prayer.

That is the whole of the entry. It gives him no century, no desert, no year and no hymn.

The Ohrid Prologue keeps him on the Julian 22 October and is the second book this folder has; its heading calls him the great Egyptian ascetic. He was the contemporary of [Arsenius the Great](/saints/arsenius-the-great) and of [Agathon of the Desert](/saints/agathon-of-the-desert). He struggled in his own monastery beside a lake near the city of Arsinoe, and set many of the brethren on the way of salvation.

His close friend and counsellor was Abba Joseph. Lot said to him once: father, I fast as much as I can, I keep prayer and silence and meditation, and I guard myself from unclean thoughts as far as my strength allows — what, then, am I still to do? Then the elder stood up, raised his hands to heaven, and his ten fingers shone like ten flaming candles; and he answered Lot: if you will, then become all fire.

Having pleased God and set many on the way of salvation, the entry ends, Saint Lot fell asleep in peace in the fifth century — which is the only date this folder has, and comes from this book.
