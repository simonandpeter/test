# Alexandra (Bulgakova)

The Russian calendar's last line for 2 October is hers and is three words long: «Мц. Александры (1938)» — Alexandra, martyr, 1938. «Мц.» is the feminine of martyr and marks a laywoman with no rank in the Church and no office, so even her state in life is given only by that abbreviation. The site's index of names supplies the surname, heading her page «Александра (Булгакова), мц.», and keeps her here and on 26 January with the whole company of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia.

1938 puts her a year after the worst of the Terror and makes her the latest death on a day whose other people died in 304, 740, 936, 1338, 1504 and 1817. The index prints no birth, no trade, no parish she belonged to, no charge and no place of death. The long lives of the new martyrs are on azbyka.ru, which answered 403 to this desk's fetcher on 4 October 2026 even with a browser user-agent, so the line stands as the line.

*After the Russian church calendar's [2 October](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261002.html) and [its index entry for her](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14536.html); read 4 October 2026.*
