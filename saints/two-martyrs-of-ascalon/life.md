# The Two Martyrs of Ascalon

saint.gr's calendar names them by their number and their city and gives no life. Its whole account is one clause — «Οι Άγιοι αυτοί μάρτυρες, μαρτύρησαν αφού τους έχωσαν στη γη μέχρι τη μέση», these holy martyrs were martyred after being buried in the earth up to the waist. The page gives them no names, no century, no year, no emperor and no judge; it names no relics and prints no hymn.

What it prints beside the line is a couplet on the same image: «Ἀνηρότως σοι γῆ φύει καρπόν, Λόγε. / Ὁ καρπὸς ἄνδρες, ἄχρις ὀσφύος, δύο» — unploughed, the earth brings forth fruit for thee, O Word; and the fruit is two men, as far as the loins. Because the source names neither of the two, they are kept here as one entry and not as two folders.

The same day's entry for the venerable Platonis ends with an alternative account in which she was martyred with two other Christians at Ascalon in Palestine. saint.gr does not say that those two Christians are these two martyrs, and this folder does not say it either.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 6 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/06/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/195/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
