# Anthimos Vagianos of Chios

The Greek synaxarion keeps on 3 September the translation of the relics of Anthimos Vagianos of Chios, whose feast is 15 February, and has no details of the translation; nothing more is recorded here.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 3 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/4081/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
