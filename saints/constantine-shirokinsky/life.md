# Constantine (Shirokinsky)

Constantine Grigoryevich Shirokinsky was born in Perm in 1876. He served in the Holy Trinity church at Sludka in Perm, and was married with four children. On 9 September 1918 he was arrested as a “monarchist and counter-revolutionary”. His wife and children petitioned the provincial Cheka for him on 11 September, saying that he had made no counter-revolutionary move against the Soviet power, and petitioned again on the 12th, the 13th and the 21st; on 24 September more than thirty of the Perm clergy signed a request to be allowed to stand surety for him. None of it helped. On 4 October 1918 he was sentenced to the highest measure, to be outlawed and shot as a monarchist and counter-revolutionary, and the sentence was carried out the same day.

The Russian calendar keeps him on 21 September old style on one line with the priests [Alexander (Fedoseev)](/saints/alexander-fedoseev), [Alexis (Stabnikov)](/saints/alexis-stabnikov) and [John (Flerov)](/saints/john-flerov), of 1918; azbyka.ru keeps him also with the saints of the Perm metropolis, and prints no hymns for him.
