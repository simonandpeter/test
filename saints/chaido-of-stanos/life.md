# Chaido of Stanos

Chaido came from the village of Stanos in Chalkidiki and lived in the nineteenth century. After the rising of 1821, to escape the immoral harassment of the Turkish commander, she had to leave the village with her mother and settle on Thasos, where she entered a metochion of the Pantokrator monastery at Kallirachi, served in the church and lived a virgin life given to God; after her mother’s death she gave herself wholly to prayer and fasting and came to spiritual contemplation. By the local tradition, when the Turks raided the metochion angels snatched the saint away and saved her from the fury of the unbelievers; two days later she returned to the metochion — the synaxarion’s life runs on past the point read here.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 1 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/2498/saint.aspx); read 23 August 2026.*
