# Leo of Patara

saint.gr's calendar for 18 February prints them on one line, «Άγιοι Λέων και Παρηγόριος οι Μάρτυρες οι εν Πατάροις της Λυκίας Αθλήσαντες» — the martyrs Leo and Paregorius who did their contending at Patara in Lycia — and the page behind it says the synaxaristes record that they were seized there. Paregorius was seized first, and was martyred through many and various torments; and Leo used to pray at the place where they had thrown Paregorius's body, weeping for the death of his beloved friend and martyr, and longing to follow the same road of martyrdom himself. And the occasion was found.

At some feast of the heathen Leo came up to the temple of the idols and broke the candlesticks and the lamps of the idols in pieces, singing hymns to the Lord meanwhile. He was seized at once and taken before the governor of the place, and before him confessed his faith in Christ; and the torments began at once. They beat him with bull's sinews and dragged him to a precipice, and threw him over it; and so the contender of Christ Leo received the crown of martyrdom.

The page gives neither of them a homeland, a year, an emperor or an office, and prints no hymn for either. Its couplet for Leo turns on the name: drawing strength from God above, Leo, you endured violent things strongly, as a lion does. His companion is [Paregorius](/saints/paregorius-of-patara), and what they share is the day, the city and the friendship the page makes the hinge of the story.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 18 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/18/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3735/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
