# Tatiana, wife of Metrophanes Ji-Sung

saint.gr names her among the Chinese martyrs of 1900 as the presbytera — the priest's wife — of father [Metrophanes Ji-Sung](/saints/metrophanes-of-peking), the first Chinese priest of the Russian mission at Peking, and gives her no life apart from her death. In the year 1900 the secret society of the «Boxers», backed by the dowager empress, let loose a persecution on the Christians of China; on 10 June proclamations were posted in the streets of Peking calling on the idolaters to slaughter them, and the following night the Boxers went through the city with torches, seizing the Orthodox in their houses.

When the buildings of the Russian mission had been burnt, some seventy Christians, women and children, took refuge in the house of father Metrophanes, and the greater part of them were slaughtered there. The Boxers found the priest sitting in his courtyard and stabbed him in the breast; and Tatiana his wife they beheaded, as they beheaded their son [Isaiah](/saints/isaiah-of-peking), who was twenty-three years old. Their other son [John](/saints/john-of-peking) they mutilated at the very hour of his father's martyrdom.

The page says nothing more of her than that. It counts the whole number lost from the mission — three hundred souls out of a thousand, of whom [two hundred and twenty-two](/saints/two-hundred-twenty-two-chinese-martyrs) received the unfading crown of martyrdom — and sends the reader for more to the «Συναξάριον Ορθοδόξων Κινέζων Μαρτύρων» of Georgios E. Piperakis, Athens 1997.
