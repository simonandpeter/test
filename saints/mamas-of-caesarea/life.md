# Mamas of Caesarea

Mamas was born in Paphlagonia — at Gangra, in 260, the Greek synaxarion says — to devout and noble Christian parents, [Theodotus](/saints/theodotus-father-of-mamas) and [Rufina](/saints/rufina-mother-of-mamas), who for openly confessing their faith were seized by the pagans and imprisoned at Caesarea in Cappadocia. Knowing his bodily weakness Theodotus prayed that the Lord would take him before the tortures, and died in prison; Rufina followed him, dying in giving birth prematurely to a son whom she commended to God in prayer, asking him to be the orphan’s keeper and defender — and God heard her: a rich Christian widow, Ammia, buried the parents with honour and took the child home and raised him with a mother’s love. He did not speak until he was five, the Prologue says, and his first word was “mama”, whence his name. At school he was unusually quick, and raised at home in the Christian spirit he did not hide his faith but confessed it before his fellows and mocked the idols. At fifteen, under the persecution — of Aurelian, the Prologue says — he was brought before the emperor, who asked him only to deny Christ with his lips; neither with heart nor with lips, he answered, will I deny my God and King Jesus Christ. He was beaten, burned with candles and at last thrown into the sea, and an angel of God saved him and led him to a high mountain near Caesarea, where he lived in solitude and prayer and the wild beasts grew tame at his holiness — the Romanian life says they gathered to him in the desert like sheep to a shepherd. Found there and tortured again, he overcame fire and beasts, and was run through with a trident by a pagan priest, and so gave his soul to God; many healings came from his relics.
