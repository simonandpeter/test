# Alexis Toth

Alexis was born in the Austro-Hungarian Empire in 1854. His father was a Greek Catholic priest and his uncle a bishop, and after a hard preparation he was himself ordained a Greek Catholic priest in 1878. He taught Church History and Canon Law at the Catholic seminary in Prešov. In 1889 he came to the United States and served as pastor of the Greek Catholic church of Saint Mary in Minneapolis; and in 1891 he and the faithful of that church were received into the Orthodox Church by Bishop Vladimir, metropolitan of the Aleutian Islands.

He gave his time to the sick and the infirm and helped the needy without ceasing, and published many articles to catechise his people. In 1893 he moved from Minneapolis to Wilkes-Barre in Pennsylvania, where he brought many Greek Catholics back to the Orthodox faith. He passed to the Lord at the age of fifty-six, on 7 May 1909; seven years later his relics were moved to Saint Tikhon's monastery, where they remain. He was canonised and numbered among the saints of the Orthodox Church in May 1994. This, in full, is what doxologia.ro prints about him.
