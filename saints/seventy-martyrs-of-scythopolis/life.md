# The Seventy Martyrs of Scythopolis

saint.gr's calendar for 28 Ιουνίου keeps a company of seventy and names none of them in its line. That line reads «Άγιοι Εβδομήντα Μάρτυρες οι εν Σκυθοπόλει», the holy seventy martyrs at Scythopolis, and the page behind it says only that all these saints were martyred by the sword. No century is named, no persecutor, no year and no relics, and the page prints no hymn.

Its couplet counts them against the Lord's own seventy: «Eίχες μαθητάς επτάκις Σώτερ δέκα, Έχεις αθλητάς και τοσούτους εκ ξίφους» — you had seven times ten disciples, Saviour, and you have as many contenders by the sword. One name is given, in a line of its own and outside the account: one of them, the page says, Saint Basil, is honoured on 6 July as well. That day has not been read here, and because the source enumerates nobody the seventy are kept as one entry.
