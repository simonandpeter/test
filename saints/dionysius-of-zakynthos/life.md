# Dionysius of Zakynthos

The Greek calendar keeps 24 August for the translation of his relics; his life stands under 17 December, the day of his death.

He was born in 1547 at Aigialos on Zakynthos, Draganigos Sigouros in the world, of a wealthy family with a Venetian title. He became a monk at the monastery of the Strofades with the name Daniel, was ordained priest by Theophilos, bishop of Kefalonia and Zakynthos, and in 1577 went to Athens to find a ship for Jerusalem; there the archbishop, Nikanor, heard him preach and made him bishop of Aegina, with the name Dionysius. Worn down by the labour he resigned, served as temporary bishop of Zakynthos until 1579, and withdrew to the monastery of the Theotokos Anafonitria.

The Sigouros and Mondinos families were at deadly feud, and in one clash his brother Constantine was killed. The killer, fleeing, sought refuge at the monastery without knowing whose brother he had killed; Dionysius, its abbot, drew the confession from him, hid him, and helped him escape — for which the island calls him the Saint of Forgiveness. He died in great old age on 17 December 1622 and was buried at the Strofades, where his body was found incorrupt and fragrant. The Ecumenical Patriarchate recognised his sanctity in 1703; on 24 August 1717 his relics were brought to Zakynthos to protect them from pirates.
