# Onesiphorus (9 November)

Onesiphorus, doxologia.ro's life says, suffered many torments for Christ in the reign of Diocletian, taking beatings and wounds over his whole body, and was burnt in cauldrons. At the last he was dragged by wild horses and broken on sharp stones, and gave up his soul to God. His body was taken away secretly by the faithful and laid in the village called Panghianete. That is the whole of the notice, one paragraph long.

The same paragraph is printed, word for word and with only the name changed, under [Porphyrius](/saints/porphyrius-martyr-9-november), who is kept the same day; and the two are set together in the heading of the day's hymn, «Troparul Sfinţilor Mucenici Claudiu, Castor, Sempronian şi Nicostrat; al Sfinţilor Mucenici Onisifor şi Porfirie», which marks them off as a company of two beside the company of four. The hymn itself is a common of martyrs and names neither of them.

The page gives no year: it places him by the reign of Diocletian and by nothing else. He is not the Onesiphorus of the Seventy the corpus already keeps, the bishop of Colophon held on 7 September; nothing on this page joins him to that man.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 9 noiembrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/9-noiembrie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-onisifor); read 25 September 2026.*
