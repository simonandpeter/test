# James of Samosata

doxologia.ro keeps James on 29 ianuarie as one of seven, and the page under his name is the page of all seven: «Sfinţii şapte mucenici, care s-au săvârşit în Samosata au fost Filotei, Iperehie, Aviv, Iulian, Romano, Iacob şi Parigorie» — the seven holy martyrs who were made perfect at Samosata. With him the calendar names [Philotheus](/saints/philotheus-of-samosata), [Hyperechius](/saints/hyperechius-of-samosata), [Abibus](/saints/abibus-of-samosata), [Julian](/saints/julian-of-samosata), [Romanus](/saints/romanus-of-samosata) and [Paregorius](/saints/paregorius-of-samosata).

The page says of them together that, being soldiers of Christ and standing against the error of the idols, their arms and their thighs were broken with thick staves; that afterwards their bodies were torn, heavy chains were laid on their necks, and they were put in prison.

They were brought out again and hung up, and iron nails were driven through their heads; and in that manner, the page says, they gave up their souls to God. It gives no year, no emperor and no governor's name, and nothing of James apart from his name and this common death, and nothing more is set down here.

The Greek calendar keeps the seven together on the same twenty-ninth of January, and saint.gr's page for them names them in its own order — Philotheus, Hyperechius, Abibus, Julian, Romanus, James and Paregorius — and gives their end in one paragraph. As soon as they had become soldiers of Christ, it says, they denounced the error of idolatry with boldness and courage; for that the idolaters seized them and put them to horrifying tortures; and at the last they hung them up and pierced their honoured heads through with iron nails, while at every fresh blow the saints confessed, «είμαι Χριστιανός» — I am a Christian. So the seven gave up their souls to God and received the crown of martyrdom. The page gives no year, no emperor and no governor, and prints no apolytikion and no kontakion; above the paragraph it sets a couplet counting them: for the Word who was nailed upon a Cross, seven heads of contestants were nailed through.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 29 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/29-ianuarie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-mucenic-iacob); read 19 September 2026; and after saint.gr's calendar for 29 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/29/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3547/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026.*
