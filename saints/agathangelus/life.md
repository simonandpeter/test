# Agathangelus

Agathangelus comes into the passion of [Clement of Ancyra](/saints/clement-of-ancyra), which doxologia.ro prints under both their names, as a young man in a crowd. The faithful had been gathering at the prison where Clement was held, until the prison, the page says, had become a church; and when the guards reported it the soldiers came by night, took the whole multitude out of the city like sheep to the slaughter, and asked each one before the stroke whether he would deny Christ to stay alive. Not one was afraid. Men, women and children were killed there, and one young man alone got out of the executioners' hands — not because he feared death, the life insists, but because he wanted to suffer greater torments for the true God. His name was Agathangelus.

He went afterwards to Clement, who received his coming as the coming of an angel of God, gave thanks, and asked God to give the young man the courage and the strength to endure to the end and be counted worthy of the martyr's crown. From then the two are one company in the page's account, passing together from tormentor to tormentor: under two emperors, Diocletian and Maximian, and under the governors Agrippinus, Curichius, Dometius, Sacerdon, Maximus, Aphrodisius and Lucius.

It was Lucius who killed him. He coaxed Agathangelus first, then, finding his mind unbending, began to torture him: iron nails heated in the fire were driven into his ears and through his brain, so that a great darkness came over him and smoke came out of his mouth and his nose; his ribs were burned with torches; and then his head was cut off with the sword. The page dates his end to the fifth day of November, although the Romanian calendar keeps him with Clement on 23 January. When Clement heard of it he was filled with joy and gave thanks for his fellow sufferer; and when Clement himself was killed at the altar, he was buried near Agathangelus's grave in the cave church.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 23 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/23-ianuarie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-mucenic-agatanghel); read 19 September 2026.*
