# Marinus (17 March)

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. The line for 17 martie reads «Sfântul Mucenic Marin», and the page it links to carries neither a Viață tab nor a Tropar tab: there is the heading, the day, and nothing further. What the line says is that he was a martyr and that the Romanian church keeps him on this day, and that is the whole of what has been read here.

No year, no country, no account of his suffering and no hymn has been taken from anywhere else, and none has been supplied from memory. Martyrs of this name are kept on other days and in other calendars, and nothing on this page identifies him with any of them. If a life for him is printed elsewhere, it has not been read.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 17 martie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/17-martie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-mucenic-marin-0); read 19 September 2026.*
