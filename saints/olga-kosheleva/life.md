# Olga (Kosheleva)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names her for 21 февраля ст. ст. in three words — «Мц. Ольги (1939).» — a woman martyr of 1939. The line carries the forename only, declined, and no surname. The name link behind it carries the calendar's own nominative with the surname the line leaves out: «Ольга (Кошелева), мц.», a laywoman of that name, to whom the calendar gives no office.

It prints no life page for her and no hymn under her name; the hymns it sets on this day belong to two of the older saints it heads and to an icon of the Mother of God. It gives her no parish, no diocese, no place and no manner of death. Nothing further is written here, and nothing is reasoned out from the surname or from the year.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 21 февраля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260221.html); read 3 October 2026.*
