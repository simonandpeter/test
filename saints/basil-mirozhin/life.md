# Basil (Mirozhin)

The calendar’s own line is the whole of what this folder records of him. The Сретенский monastery’s calendar for 7 декабря ст. ст. keeps five saints of the twentieth century among the older ones, in four lines and under four years, and his is «Сщмчч. Петра и Василия пресвитеров (1941).» He is the second of the two men his line names, and his line closes the day, under the rank «сщмч.», a hieromartyr, with the grade «пресвитеров», presbyters, and 1941 is the only year the calendar gives him.

The name page behind his place in the line gives the surname the line leaves out together with his grade in orders, an archpriest. Its heading is «Василий (Мирожин), протоиерей, сщмч.» and that heading is the whole of what it says of him. His name page lists one other day, «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church. One row per church is the schema, so that day is recorded here and he does not render on it.

Behind that page there is no life, and no article stands under his name. Nothing is said here of where he was born, where he served, when or by whom he was arrested, or how and where he died in 1941, because no page read says any of it.

The other man of his line is recorded in this folder’s `related`, in both directions: the calendar commemorates the two together under one year, which is more than a shared day. He is not named in this life, because nothing read says what the two had to do with each other beyond the line and the year. The day page carries nine hymns, and all nine stand under the names of the older saints of the same day.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 7 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261207.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14495.html); read 13 October 2026.*
