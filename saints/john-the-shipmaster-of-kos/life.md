# John the Shipmaster of Kos

saint.gr calls him «Άγιος Ιωάννης ο Ναύκληρος», John the shipmaster, and heads him «(; - 1669)». He was a shipmaster of Kos, and the Turks on the island forced him into Islam. He threw off the Turkish clothes, however, and went on living as a Christian; and when the Turks understood it they seized him, whipped him without mercy, and then shut him in prison.

In the prison they kept trying to turn him to Islam, and the new-martyr answered them, in the words the page prints: «Εγώ στον Κύριο μου Ιησού Χριστό πιστεύω και αυτόν ομολογώ για Θεό αληθινό, με όλη μου την ψυχή και την καρδιά. Τη δε δική σας θρησκεία αποστρέφομαι και είμαι έτοιμος να υπομείνω όσα βασανιστήρια και αν μου κάνετε για την αγάπη του Χριστού μου» — I believe in my Lord Jesus Christ and confess him true God with my whole soul and heart; your religion I turn away from, and I am ready to bear whatever torments you do to me for the love of my Christ.

Seeing that his mind would not be moved, his tormentors brought him under torture before the judge, who ordered him beaten without pity and condemned him to be burnt alive. So, the page says, on 8 April 1669 this new-martyr of Kos was martyred. The service for him was composed by [Gerasimos Mikragiannanitis](/saints/gerasimos-of-little-st-annes); the historic chapel of John the Forerunner on the island was renamed for the new-martyr, and the street that leads up to it bears his name. The couplet says that he sailed the sea of this life in fire and so reached calm harbours.
