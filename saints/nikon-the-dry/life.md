# Nikon the Dry

saint.gr's calendar for 11 December names the venerable Nikon the Dry, he who is in the Cave, and then says in one sentence that it has no details of the Russian venerable's life. The one other thing it prints is where his body is: the relic of the saint lies incorrupt in the Lavra of the Caves at Kyiv. No century, no parentage, no manner of death, no hymn and no synaxaristic couplet is given.

What the entry says is in the line itself and in that note, and it is set down here as they have it: a Russian ascetic whom the Greek calendar calls the Dry, places in the Cave, and whose incorrupt relic it puts at Kyiv. The same calendar keeps another Nikon of those caves on 23 March, the abbot, whom the corpus already holds; that is another entry and nothing here is claimed from it.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 11 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/11/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3173/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
