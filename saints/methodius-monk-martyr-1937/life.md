# Methodius (Ivanov), Hieromonk, Monk-martyr (1937)

Methodius — Nicholas Mikhailovich Ivanov in the world — was born in 1899 in the city of Simbirsk, a priest’s son. He finished the parish school and the church school at Alatyr and entered the Simbirsk seminary, which he had no time to finish because of the coup in Russia, completing his education in a Soviet school; from 1920 he taught at Saransk, from 1921 at the art and technical school at Penza, and the same year moved to Moscow to teach in one of the higher schools. But teaching in a Soviet school on a programme founded on godless ideology was incompatible with the religious convictions of a deeply believing — the calendar’s life runs on past the point read here. In 1923 he was tonsured at the Moscow Protection monastery and ordained hieromonk; arrested in 1929 for criticism of the Soviet power he was sentenced to three years’ exile, and on his return served at the church of the village of Sukovo, where a monastic community formed about him. Arrested again in 1937 on a charge of counter-revolutionary activity, he was shot on 9 September 1937 at the Butovo range; the Russian calendar keeps him on 27 August (Julian).
