# The Two Holy Children

saint.gr's calendar for 23 Οκτωβρίου names two holy children and does not name either of them. One sentence is the whole of the narrative: «Τα Άγια Δύο Παιδιά μαρτύρησαν διά πυρός, που άναψαν στο έδαφος και τα εξανάγκασαν να τρέχουν μέσα σ' αυτή» — they were martyred by fire, which was lit on the ground, and they were forced to run through it. The couplet over the entry asks the Word to give the crowns to two child runners who have run an Olympic race of fire.

Because the page gives no names, this folder is the company and not a person, as the corpus keeps `thirty-three-martyrs-of-heraclea` and `fathers-slain-at-raithu`. There is no country, no city, no year, no emperor, no parents and no hymn: the sentence above and the couplet are the entire entry.
