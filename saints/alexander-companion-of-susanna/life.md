# Alexander, companion of Susanna

Alexander was a son of [Claudius](/saints/claudius-companion-of-susanna) and [Praepedigna](/saints/praepedigna-of-rome), and brother of [Cuphius](/saints/cuphius-of-rome) — a family of the emperor Diocletian’s kin, baptised after talking with the household of the virgin [Susanna](/saints/susanna-of-rome), exiled when the emperor learned of it, and soon burned at Ostia near Rome, their ashes thrown into the sea. The life names him and says no more; the Prologue names only the men, “with their households”.
