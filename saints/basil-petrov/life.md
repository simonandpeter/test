# Basil (Petrov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him on 2 января old style and prints nothing beyond the one line «Мч. Василия (1942)» — Basil, martyr, 1942. The name link behind that line carries the calendar's own nominative together with the surname the line itself leaves out, «Василий Петров, мч.», and there the entry ends: the calendar holds no life page for him at all.

So what can honestly be said is what the calendar says. A martyr named Basil Petrov is kept on this day, and the year given for his death is 1942. The calendar ranks him simply Мученик, a martyr, and not a priest, a monk or a bishop. No birthplace, no account of his life and no account of his end is printed there, and none has been supplied here from anywhere else.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 2 января ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260102.html); read 3 October 2026.*
