# The Eight Anchorites

saint.gr's whole account of them is one sentence: «Οι Άγιοι αυτοί Οκτώ Οσιομάρτυρες ήταν μοναχοί και τελειώθηκαν διά ξίφους» — these eight holy venerable-martyrs were monks, and were perfected by the sword. The calendar's line for the day adds one word to that, calling them anchorites as well as venerable-martyrs, and this folder stands for the company because the page names not one of the eight.

Its couplet is the only other thing it has of them, and it says no more than the sentence does: they cut off together the necks of eight men, subdued under Thy yoke, O Word. There is no year, no country, no desert or monastery, no persecutor and no judge, no relic and no hymn, and this folder does not supply what the calendar does not say.
