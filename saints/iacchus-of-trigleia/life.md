# Iacchus of Trigleia

saint.gr's calendar for 17 December keeps Saint Iacchus, and the whole of what its page says of him is one sentence: Saint Iacchus was from Trigleia, and was martyred by the sword. There is nothing before that sentence and nothing after it — no year, no emperor, no judge, no place of death, no relic and no hymn.

What the page does carry besides is an epigram addressed to him: be strong, Iacchus; and having had your head cut off, be enrolled among those who were cut down, and be crowned upon the head. Nothing is drafted from it. Trigleia, on the Bithynian shore of the Propontis, is the one place the entry names, and no coordinate is proposed for it here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 17 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/17/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3285/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
