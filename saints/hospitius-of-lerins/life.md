# Hospitius of Lérins

saint.gr's Hospitius lived in Gaul and practised his ascesis on the island of Lérins. For a greater ascesis he had bound his body with chains, and he ate only a little bread. The God of the Trinity gave him the gift of prophecy and of wonderworking. That is the whole of what the page records of his life, and it adds no monastery, no teacher, no disciple, no miracle in particular and no relics.

For the end of it the page offers two dates and declines to choose: he fell asleep in 580, «ενώ κατ' άλλους το 681 μ.Χ.» — though according to others in 681. The century between them is not resolved here, and no year is recorded for him on that account. The page prints no synaxarion couplet and no hymn to him.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 21 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/21/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1701/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
