# Gregory of Argos

Gregory Kalamaras was Metropolitan of Argos and Nafplio from 1810 to 1821, and the nephew of his predecessor there, another Gregory (1800–1810). He was born in 1769 at Alagonia near Kalamata. He had been metropolitan of Erythrai and afterwards of Patras — the entry dates Patras 1780–1799, which sits badly with its own year of his birth, and it is left as printed — and it was as a former metropolitan that he was elected to Nafplio and Argos in 1810.

In 1819 he was initiated into the Filiki Etaireia by Daniel Pampoukis, abbot of the monastery of Vrachos at Nemea, and he in turn brought into it the leading men of his province, at Nafplio, at Argos and in the villages round about, and his own archdeacon. The Turks came to know of his work for the nation before the rising, and by order of the kaymakam of Tripolis he was shut in the prisons of that town with other hierarchs. There he suffered terribly, until the ill-treatment, the hunger and months of torment killed him, on 18 or 21 September 1821.
