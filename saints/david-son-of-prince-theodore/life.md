# David, son of Prince Theodore of Yaroslavl

David was born at Sarai, at the court of the Golden Horde, to the holy prince
[Theodore](/saints/theodore-prince-of-yaroslavl) of Smolensk and Yaroslavl
and to Anna, the khan’s daughter baptised for the marriage — the Orthodox
Church in America’s life of his father tells it. He ruled Yaroslavl after
his father’s death in 1299 and died in 1321; his brother
[Constantine](/saints/constantine-son-of-prince-theodore) had, it seems,
died before him. On 5 March 1463 the three were found in one grave at the
Saviour monastery of Yaroslavl, the sons laid beside their father, and they
have been kept together since. The Greek synaxarion keeps them on
19 September and says it has no details of the lives of these Russian
saints; the Russian calendar keeps them the same day of its own reckoning.

*After the life printed by the Orthodox Church in America for 19 September —
[the day’s lives](https://www.oca.org/saints/all-lives/2026/09/19); the Greek
entry is the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 19 Σεπτεμβρίου —
[the entry](https://www.saint.gr/3846/saint.aspx); read 5 September 2026.*
