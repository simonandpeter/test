# Jonah of Moscow

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 31 March is «Sfântul Ierarh Iona, Mitropolitul Moscovei și al Întregii Rusii» — the hierarch Jonah, Metropolitan of Moscow and of All Russia — and the page the calendar links for him carries no Viață and no troparion: only icons, places of pilgrimage, articles, texts by the saint, photographs and the day's Gospel.

So the one thing the Romanian source states of him is his see and his day, and this entry records that and nothing more. The Russian calendar, which is the place his life would be read, has not been checked here.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and says plainly that it has no life of him. «Γνωστός στη ρωσική Εκκλησία, άγνωστος στους Συναξαριστές», saint.gr writes — known to the Russian Church, unknown to the synaxaristae — and in place of a narrative it offers a comparison: he is for the Russians what St Mark Eugenicus, 1375 to 1461, is for us, which sets him among those who held the Orthodox side against the union of Florence. It heads him «(; - 1461)», the same year in which it puts Mark's death. What it does record is how he came to be sung: his service was composed by the hierodeacon Theophilus Paschalides and published at St Petersburg in 1897. It adds that his memory is repeated on 15 June, and prints no hymn of its own.

**The Russian church calendar keeps him on the Julian 5 October with the synaxis of the hierarchs of Moscow**, seventeen names in one line under one polyeleos service, marked «(Моск. Свт.)» and not expanded: Peter, Theognostus, Alexis, Cyprian, Photius, Jonah, Gerontius, Joasaph, Macarius, Philip, Job, Hermogenes, Tikhon, Peter, Philaret, Innocent and Macarius — two and a half centuries of metropolitans, then the patriarchs, then the metropolitans of Moscow and Kolomna, ending with a man shot in 1937. He is the sixth of them, and his index entry carries five days in all — 31 March for his repose, 27 May for the translation of his relics, 15 June, this synaxis, and «15 декабря (интронизация)», the day of his enthronement. That is as many days as any name in this corpus holds, and one of them is neither a death nor a translation. No year is printed anywhere, so this folder still stands Undated.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 31 martie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/31-martie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-ierarh-iona-mitropolitul-moscovei-al-intregii-rusii); read 19 September 2026; and after saint.gr's calendar for 31 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/31/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/147/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026; the Russian church calendar keeps him on the Julian 5 October with the synaxis of the hierarchs of Moscow — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261005.html); read 4 October 2026.*
