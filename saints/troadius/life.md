# Troadius

saint.gr gives the holy Troadius one sentence, and the life says exactly what it says: he was perfected by the sword together with other Christians, under the emperor Decius, whose reign the page puts at 249 to 251 — or, according to Nicodemus the Hagiorite, under Akakios the emperor in the year 253. The site sets the two readings side by side and does not choose between them, so both are kept here and the date stands as the span they make.

The day's own line couples him with «οι συν αυτώ», those with him, and the page names none of them; a company nothing enumerates is not folders, so they are recorded in this life and nowhere else. There is no country, no city, no judge, no relic and no hymn, and no year for his birth.

The one thing the site adds is the distich, which is an exhortation and not a fact: do not delay, Troadius, to die by the sword — do not delay, for delay will have no crown. Nothing else is written here.

The Serbian church keeps him on this day, and the Ohrid Prologue adds the one circumstance saint.gr leaves out, which is a witness. It says that he suffered for Christ as a youth, and that [Gregory the Wonderworker of Neocaesarea](/saints/gregory-the-wonderworker-of-neocaesarea) saw him in a vision bearing the torments bravely for Christ until he was killed, and saw his soul, as it was parted from the body, hastening joyfully towards heaven. The Prologue puts his suffering and his glorification in the third century and gives no year, no city and no judge.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 2 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/02/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3879/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026; and the Охридски пролог of Bishop Nikolai Velimirović, as the Православни подсетник serves it for 2. март ст. ст. — [the Serbian day](https://www.pravoslavno.rs/index.php?q=citanja&datum=2026-03-15&prolog=1), read 4 October 2026.*
