# Peter, son of John of Syracuse

Peter was one of the two sons of [John](/saints/john-of-syracuse) of Syracuse in Sicily, with his brother [Antony](/saints/antony-son-of-john-of-syracuse), in the years of the emperor Basil I (867). When the Agarenes took Sicily they carried off John and his sons as captives and brought the boys up in their own religion. Grown, the sons did not forget the faith their father had taught them, and worshipped the one true God in secret. When the Agarenes’ savage chief Abrahim learned of it he was enraged, the more because he had given them high offices, and he seized them and tortured them in the most barbarous and horrible way. At last, when he had cut off every limb of their bodies, the two sons gave up their holy souls to God; and the father too gave up his soul gloriously, when Abrahim drove the sword into his throat. The entry’s verses have John, slain, slaying the enemy and its error with it, and Antony and Peter enduring the cutting limb from limb like firm rocks.

The Russian calendar keeps the four on one line on 23 September old style, in the ninth century, calling John’s younger son Antoninus. Its life says they suffered under Ibrahim, the cruel ruler of Africa, who after taking and destroying the Sicilian city of Syracuse carried [John](/saints/john-of-syracuse) and his two children, Peter and [Antony](/saints/antony-son-of-john-of-syracuse), captive to Africa and had the boys taught Arabic and the sciences. As they grew up he came to love them so for their wisdom and virtue that he called Antoninus his kinsman and made Peter his chief steward; but learning that the young men secretly confessed Christ, he flew into a rage and had them put in iron stocks and beaten with knotted sticks. After a long beating Antoninus was tied with straps onto an ass and led round the city, beaten and reviled, and bore it all giving thanks to God; Peter was beaten and thrown into prison. Their father was brought, and Ibrahim seized him by the neck with his left hand and drove a knife into his throat with his right; the father’s body was thrown down by his children’s, and at Ibrahim’s order a great fire was lit and the martyrs’ bodies thrown into it. The old man [Andrew](/saints/andrew-the-martyr-23-september) the tormentor starved for a long time and then ran through the breast with a spear, and when the martyr began to give thanks to God he ran him through again, and as he bled they cut off his head with a sword.
