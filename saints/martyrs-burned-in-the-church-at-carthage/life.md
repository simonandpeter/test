# The Martyrs Burned in the Church at Carthage

saint.gr's calendar for 7 December keeps the Orthodox martyrs who were burned inside a church. These holy martyrs, the page says, were burned alive by the Arians at Carthage at the moment when they were gathered together in the church and were praying. It gives no number for them and names not one of them, and it names neither emperor nor magistrate.

The couplet set over the entry is the only other thing it has, and it turns on the building: truly a temple appeared in the midst of the temple, this multitude burned for right worship. The neighbouring entries of the same day put the Arian persecution at Carthage in the reign of Zeno, but this page does not say so and no year is recorded here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 7 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/07/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3190/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
