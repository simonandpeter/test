# Olympias, companion of Maximus

saint.gr gives her one sentence and no life. The holy martyrs [Maximus](/saints/maximus-companion-of-olympias) and Olympias, it says, were martyred in Persia in the years of the emperor Decius, which it dates 249 to 251. That is the whole of the notice: it names no city, no judge and no manner of death, it says nothing of her family or her age, and it gives her no year of her own, so this folder carries no dates. The page prints no couplet and no hymn for the two of them.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 15 Απριλίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/04/15/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1323/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
