# Raphael (Sheichenko)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names a Raphael on 6 июня and gives no life of him. Its line for the day is the whole of what it says: «Прп. Рафаила исп (1957).» — the venerable Raphael, a confessor, and the year 1957. So what the calendar tells is that he was a monk, that he was counted among the confessors rather than among the martyrs, and that he died in 1957.

His own name page on that site, 14271, heads him «Рафаил (Шейченко), иеромонах, исповедник», which gives the surname the day's line leaves out and makes him a priest-monk, and it lists three days: «26 января (Новомуч.) , 6 июня , 9 июня (обретение мощеи)» — the first the general commemoration of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, the second this day, and the third, three days later, the finding of his relics. No page names a monastery, a diocese or a place of any kind.

That page also links articles about him on another site, and none of them was read for this folder, so nothing of his life is here: no year of birth, no monastery, no account of his imprisonment and no account of his death. The day page prints eleven hymns, for two ascetics of Egypt and Dalmatou, a bishop of Great Perm and two Russian abbots, and none under his name.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 6 июня ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260606.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14271.html); read 8 October 2026.*
