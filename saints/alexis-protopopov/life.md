# Alexis (Protopopov)

His line in the calendar is three words and a year. The Сретенский monastery’s calendar for 21 апреля ст. ст. prints «Сщмч. Алексия пресвитера (1938)» — a hieromartyr of that name, a presbyter, 1938 — and the name page behind the forename gives the surname the line leaves out entirely: «Алексий (Протопопов), иерей, сщмч.»

That is the whole of it. There is no life page for him on the day page and none on his name page, and 1938 is the only year either of them gives. Nothing is said of where he was born, what parish he served, when or by whom he was arrested, or where and how he died. The surname is the one thing the name page knows that the line does not, and it is what distinguishes this folder from the many others the same calendar gives the same forename and the same grade.

His name page lists one other day, «26 января (Новомуч.)», the synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of the Russian Church, and that is what the rank of new martyr here rests on; that day page was not read. One row per church is the schema, so the day is recorded on his Russian row and he does not render on it.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 21 апреля ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260421.html) and [the name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/14248.html); read 8 October 2026.*
