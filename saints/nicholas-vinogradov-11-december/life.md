# Nicholas (Vinogradov, 11 December)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names him among the hieromartyrs on 11 декабря and gives no life. Its line is «Сщмч. Николая пресвитера (1937).» — the hieromartyr Nicholas the presbyter, 1937 — and the calendar’s own name page adds the surname the line leaves out, and something more: «Николай (Виноградов) (другой), протоиерей, сщмч.» The «(другой)» is the calendar’s own word, *another*, and it is there because the calendar keeps more than one archpriest of this name.

That is the whole of the record. He was an archpriest, which is what the name page says where the line says only presbyter; he died in 1937, the worst year of the persecution in which the Russian church counts its new martyrs; and the calendar keeps him on this day alone, his line joined to no one else’s.

Three other men of this same name and surname are kept by this same calendar on three other days, with the years 1931, 1937 and 1948, and this folder takes the day into its slug and its name for that reason. The calendar’s «(другой)» says that it too distinguishes him from one of them; which one it does not say, and nothing is supplied here. The day page carries four hymns and not one is under his name. Nothing else is written here, because nothing else was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 11 декабря ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261211.html); read 13 October 2026.*
