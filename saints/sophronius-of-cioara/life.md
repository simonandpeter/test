# Sophronius of Cioara

Sophronius came from the village of Cioara-Sebeș in the county of Alba. A lover of God from childhood, he received the monastic habit in one of the hermitages of Wallachia and became a noted hermit and a doer of the Gospel of Christ.

In 1756 he returned to the village he was born in and founded a little hermitage in the forests round about, known as the Skete of Cioara, gathering a few disciples there. Then, seeing the oppression and injustice the Orthodox Romanians of Transylvania were under, and burning with zeal for Christ, he went for years through the villages of Ardeal urging the faithful to keep the Orthodox faith undefiled.

For this he was thrown into prison by order of the ruler of Ardeal and beaten cruelly. Brought out, he began again to confess the faith in the villages of the Apuseni mountains, and was imprisoned and tormented a second time. Freed at the prayers of the faithful, on 14 February 1761 he gathered a great assembly at Alba Iulia, asking equal rights for the Romanian people and an Orthodox bishop for the faithful of Ardeal.

That same year, having seen his wish fulfilled, he withdrew to the monastery of Curtea de Argeș, where he lived a little longer and then died in peace. The Romanian Orthodox Church canonised him in 1955 and keeps his memory on 21 octombrie.
