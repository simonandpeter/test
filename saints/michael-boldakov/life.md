# Michael (Boldakov)

The Сретенский monastery’s calendar names him on the Julian 16 November as the second of the two martyrs killed with a presbyter: «Сщмч. Феодора пресвитера и с ним мчч. Анании и Михаила (1929).» Michael Orestovich Boldakov was a layman of the town of Kimry in the province of Tver. He was arrested in May 1929 over the closing of the church of the Transfiguration there, with its rector [Theodore (Kolerov)](/saints/theodore-kolerov) and its warden [Ananias (Boikov)](/saints/ananias-boikov), after the parishioners had kept watch about the church for three days rather than give it up.

Twenty were charged. The trial opened at Kimry on 20 October 1929, the Moscow papers were invited and admission was by ticket, and none of the accused admitted guilt; on 27 October he was sentenced to be shot with the priest, the warden and two members of the church council, whose sentences were afterwards cancelled. The three were shot at eleven at night on the Julian 16 November, the 29th by the civil reckoning, and the papers of that morning had already printed that it was done. Their funeral was held in the cathedral of Kimry before a great gathering.

What azbyka.ru says of him it says in a footnote, and it is worth saying here. He was a resident of Kimry whose brother served as psalmist in the church of the Transfiguration; he himself was not a parishioner of that church and was seldom in Kimry at all, being occupied with market gardening in the Kaluga region — and he was not in Kimry in those days either. Neither host gives him a year of birth, an age or a place of birth, and neither gives him a page of its own: he is told inside the priest’s life, and the newspaper notice of the execution called him simply «мирянин», a layman.
