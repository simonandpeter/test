# Wulfhilda of Barking

saint.gr's calendar for 9 December keeps the holy Wulfhilda, whose name it also prints in western letters. She was born after the year 986, the page says, was dedicated to God from a young age, and kept her promises and her monastic vows in spite of the difficulties and temptations she met. While she was still a novice she refused the offers of marriage of King Edgar, who had fallen desperately in love with her.

In the end the king was persuaded to leave her in peace, since she was wholly given to the monastic life; indeed he himself made her abbess of the monastery of Barking and gave the house considerable lands. To that same monastery Wulfhilda gave her own paternal inheritance. She lived with great austerity and became a pattern of virtue, and she fell asleep about the year 1000, leaving a good memory behind her.

The page's two dates do not fit each other: Edgar of the English died in 975, eleven years before the year it gives for her birth. Only the repose it dates is written down here, and the difficulty is left as the page's own.
