# Wilfrid of York

saint.gr's calendar for 12 October keeps «Άγιος Wilfrid της Υόρκης», and its page says in two sentences everything it has: that Saint Wilfrid was born in 634 in Northumberland and fell asleep in 709, and that we have no other details of his life. The calendar gives him no rank — not venerable, not bishop — names no monastery and no see beyond the York in his epithet, tells nothing of what he did, and prints no hymn and no verse for him.

So this folder holds what the Greek calendar holds: the name as saint.gr prints it, in Latin letters with a Greek epithet; two years, which it states outright; the region he was born in; and the day it keeps him on. What the wider English tradition says of Wilfrid is not here, because this source does not say it.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Οκτωβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/10/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2725/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
