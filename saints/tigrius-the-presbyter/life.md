# Tigrius

saint.gr's calendar for 12 Ιανουαρίου keeps two men in one line, «Άγιοι Τίγριος ο Πρεσβύτερος και Ευτρόπιος» — Tigrius the presbyter and [Eutropius](/saints/eutropius-companion-of-tigrius) — and the page behind the line has one sentence for both of them: «Οι Άγιοι Τίγριος και Ευτρόπιος έζησαν κατά τους χρόνους του βασιλέως Αρκαδίου (395 - 408 μ.Χ.). Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο των Αγίων.»

That is: they lived in the years of the emperor Arcadius, whom the page dates 395 to 408, and we have no details of the lives of these saints. So what the calendar holds of Tigrius is a name, the rank of presbyter, a reign, a companion and a day. It prints no city, no manner of death, and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1410/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
