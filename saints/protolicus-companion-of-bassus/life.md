# Protolicus, companion of Bassus

saint.gr keeps three martyrs together on this day — Bassus, Antony and Protolicus — and gives them one page and one sentence. The holy martyrs Bassus, Antony and Protolicus were martyred at Alexandria, the page says, being thrown into the sea. That is the whole of what the site prints of them: no year, no emperor, no judge, no order of their deaths, no relic and no hymn.

The page's headings for the three are uneven, as they often are on this site. Over Antony it prints the etymology of the name and nothing else; over Bassus and over Protolicus it prints the name alone. None of that is about the martyrs, and the sentence above is the entry.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 14 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/14/index.aspx) and [the page for the three of them](https://www.saint.gr/3711/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
