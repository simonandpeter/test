# Laurence the Recluse of the Kyiv Caves

The Greek calendar keeps him on 20 Ιανουαρίου with [Euthymius the Hesychast](/saints/euthymius-the-hesychast), and the page behind the line says of both that they lived in a manner pleasing to God, in ascetic labour and prayer, in the Lavra of Kyiv. Laurence, it says, lived between the thirteenth and the fourteenth century in the monastery of Saint Demetrius, as a recluse, and his relic lies incorrupt in the Lavra of the Caves of Kyiv.

One story is told of him. A man possessed was once brought to him from Kyiv, and Laurence out of humility maintained that he could not heal him; so that the grace of God resting on the monastery of the Caves of Kyiv might be revealed to all, he said they should take the man there — who cried out in terror at the mere naming of the monastery and of its venerable fathers. So it was done, and the poor man was made well and finally freed from the tyranny of the demon.

He is not the Laurence of Chernigov whom the Romanian calendar keeps on this same day; that elder is another man of a much later age, and nothing is carried between the two folders. No year of birth or of repose is printed here, and no hymn.
