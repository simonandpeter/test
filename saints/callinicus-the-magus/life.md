# Callinicus the Magus

saint.gr says they were martyred in the years of the emperor Elagabalus, who reigned from 218 to 222. [Meletius](/saints/meletius-the-stratelates), the leader, as the page puts it, of this whole line of martyrs, was serving as a soldier together with two other saints, [Stephen](/saints/stephen-companion-of-meletius) and [John](/saints/john-companion-of-meletius). They were denounced for believing in Christ and ordered to deny him; and because they did not do it they were put to dreadful torments. In the end Meletius they hanged, and Stephen and John they beheaded.

But [Serapion the Egyptian](/saints/serapion-martyr-24-may) the idolater, when he saw the holy Meletius break the idols of the temple by his prayer, believed; and Callinicus the magus, when he too saw that Meletius had suffered nothing from the deadly poison they had given him to drink, believed as well. Both of them, having confessed Christ steadfastly, were beheaded.

At the sight of their martyrdom others believed and gave their lives for Christ: twelve komites and tribunes, whom the day's line names as [Festus](/saints/festus-of-the-twelve-tribunes), [Faustus](/saints/faustus-of-the-twelve-tribunes), [Marcellus](/saints/marcellus-of-the-twelve-tribunes), [Theodore](/saints/theodore-of-the-twelve-tribunes), [Meletius](/saints/meletius-of-the-twelve-tribunes), [Sergius](/saints/sergius-of-the-twelve-tribunes), [Marcellinus](/saints/marcellinus-of-the-twelve-tribunes), [Felix](/saints/felix-of-the-twelve-tribunes), [Photinus](/saints/photinus-of-the-twelve-tribunes), [Theodoriscus](/saints/theodoriscus-of-the-twelve-tribunes), [Mercurius](/saints/mercurius-of-the-twelve-tribunes) and [Didymus](/saints/didymus-of-the-twelve-tribunes); three women, [Marciane](/saints/marciana-martyr-24-may), [Palladia](/saints/palladia-companion-of-meletius) and [Susanna](/saints/susanna-companion-of-meletius); and two infants, [Cyriacus](/saints/cyriacus-the-infant) and [Christian](/saints/christian-the-infant). After them followed a multitude of martyrs whose number, the page says, reaches eleven thousand two hundred and eight. Not one of that multitude is named, so not one of them is a folder here.

The manner of each death comes from the couplets the synaxarion sets over them, which the page prints one by one. Of Meletius: they call the pine bitter, but to him who was hung upon it it was a kind of Hymettian honey. Of Callinicus: beheaded, you exchange the magus for a contender, and you were not called Fair-Victor for nothing. Of Stephen and John: with John, Stephen bows his head to the sword for longing after the unfading crowns. Of Serapion: the martyr, not sacrificing to carved things, has his neck cut through by a worshipper of carved things. Of the twelve tribunes: perishable were the ranks of tribune and count, and out of the fire they found the rank that does not perish. Of the two infants: the sword took away Cyriacus and Christian, Christian servants of the Lord. Of the three women: Marciane, Susanna and Palladia are killed with wood for not sacrificing to Pallas.

The Russian church calendar keeps him on 24 мая ст. ст. with the company of Meletius, naming him fifth of the seven: «Каллиника волхва», Callinicus the magus. **It tells nothing of him.** Its life page for the company, which that very line links to, names the commander, the two tribunes Стефан and Иоанн, Феодор and Фавст, the women and children, the twelve tribunes and Серапион, and does not mention him once; and his name page gives the title and nothing more.

What the calendar does give him is the company and its circumstances. Their commander was the military governor of the province of Galatia under the emperor it dates 218 to 222; he prayed that the pagan error might end, and the demons of the shrines fled into dogs whose howling terrified the province; he destroyed the dogs and the shrines with his soldiers and died under torture confessing Christ; and the rest of the regiment, who declared themselves Christians, were put to the sword with their wives and children, 1218 of them, or by some sources eleven thousand.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 24 Μαΐου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/05/24/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1712/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026; and after the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 24 мая ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260524.html) and [the life of the company](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life1135.htm), which names him nowhere, read 8 October 2026.*
