# Diodorus of Corinth

doxologia.ro keeps Diodorus on 31 ianuarie as one of seven martyrs of Corinth, and the page under his name is the page of all seven. With him it names [Victorinus](/saints/victorinus-of-corinth), [Victor](/saints/victor-of-corinth), [Nicephorus](/saints/nicephorus-of-corinth), [Claudius](/saints/claudius-of-corinth), [Serapion](/saints/serapion-of-corinth) and [Papias](/saints/papias-of-corinth). They were all of them men of Corinth, the life says, and were seized for the confession of Christ in the time of the emperor Decius, in the year 250.

They were brought before Tertius the proconsul, who governed the land of Hellas from Corinth. After many torments hard to bear, the page says, they came to the end of their lives in various ways: Victorinus, Victor and Nicephorus were put in a mortar and pounded until they gave up their souls; Claudius died after his hands and his feet had been cut off; Diodorus was burnt; Serapion was beheaded; and Papias was drowned in the sea.

So Diodorus was burnt. The Romanian page gives no more of him than his name, his city, that year and that death, and nothing further is set down here.

The Greek calendar keeps the seven together on the same thirty-first of January, and saint.gr's page gives each man's death separately. They came from Corinth, it says, and were arrested in the reign of Decius, which it dates 249 to 251, because they confessed their faith in Christ with boldness; they were brought before the proconsul Tertius, who was governor of Greece, and he put these contestants of the faith to horrifying tortures, and all of them received the crown of martyrdom. Diodorus was thrown into a white-hot furnace, and the couplet the page gives him calls him into it: the crown of your labour is in the furnace too — enter, martyr Diodorus, and be crowned. The page adds a remark of its own about the day: «Πιθανώς να είναι οι ίδιοι με αυτούς της 5ης Απριλίου» — they are probably the same as those of 5 April, which is the second day doxologia.ro keeps them on as well. It prints no apolytikion and no kontakion for them.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 31 ianuarie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/31-ianuarie) and [the life](https://doxologia.ro/viata-sfantului-mucenic-diodor); read 19 September 2026; and after saint.gr's calendar for 31 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/31/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3575/saint.aspx), read 30 September 2026.*
