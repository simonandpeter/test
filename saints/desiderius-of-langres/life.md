# Desiderius of Langres

saint.gr says he came from the city of Genoa in Italy and lived in the fourth and fifth centuries, and gives him three sentences and nothing more. He preached the Gospel in the city of Langres in Gaul and was elected its bishop; and he fell asleep in peace in the year 407.

The page gives him no birth year, no family, no account of his preaching beyond the naming of the place, and no hymn. The same day carries a second Desiderius, the hieromartyr bishop of Vienne who was killed in 608, and the two pages have nothing in common but the name; nothing further about this one is recorded here.
