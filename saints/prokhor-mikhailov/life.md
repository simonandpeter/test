# Prokhor (Mikhailov)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names the two of them together on 6 апреля ст. ст. in five words, «Мчч. Петра и Прохора (1918).», and the two name links behind the line, pages 10021 and 10027, give the surnames the line withholds: «Петр (Жуков), мч.» and «Прохор (Михайлов), мч.» Both are laymen, both are martyrs, and the calendar lists for each of them this day and the moveable synaxis of the new martyrs on 26 января. Behind the line is a life, and it is headed for the two of them «и иже с ними» — and with those who were with them.

On 27 March 1918 the commandant of the Никулинская volost came to the priest of the village Гнездово in the Вышневолоцкий uyezd of the province of Tver with two armed Red Guards, a clerk and two members of the volost committee, and produced the order of the chairman of the volost soviet, «Журавлева», to make an inventory of the church property. In the presence of the clergy and the churchwarden they began it, and put into it everything of silver and of copper and the vestry besides. The wooden church was lately built and poor, and they took all the money in it: twenty-three roubles and thirteen kopecks, and two deposit books for two hundred.

On the fourth day after that the chairman was seized by a crowd of the people and sent under escort to the neighbouring committee at Козлово; but on the road, crossing the river, he threw himself into the water and drowned. The people took it for a plain punishment of God for the spoiling of the church.

In April the volost assembly met, and the parishioners of Гнездово began to reproach the Red Guards with seizing church property unlawfully. **Two of them were conspicuous above the rest in their zeal for the church**, and they are the two this day keeps. The Red Guards arrested about thirty people there and then at the assembly, beat them in confinement, and after a while set them walking to the uyezd town of Вышний Волочек. On the road the prisoners were cruelly beaten, and ten of them were killed before the town was reached.

Peter (Zhukov) had already been beaten in the volost committee until his whole head was in wounds and his fingers were broken; at the seventh verst his tormentors made an end of him — they cut his cheeks open, cut out his tongue, and shot him. Prokhor (Mikhailov) bore no less: he was beaten for two days, and on the road was given eight bayonet wounds and then shot at the ninth verst, when he had lost consciousness and had stopped feeling the torture.

The bodies of the two, whom the page calls the new confessors of Tver, were solemnly buried in their own parish; and the archbishop of Tver, «Серафим (Александров)», gave his blessing that what had happened should be made known through the whole diocese and that public memorial services should be sung in every church of town and country. They were numbered among the New Martyrs and Confessors of Russia for church-wide veneration at the Jubilee Council of Bishops in August 2000.

**Of the ten killed on that road the calendar names these two and no more**, and the thirty arrested it does not name at all, so no folder is made for any of them: the line enumerates two men and this batch holds two. The drowned chairman is written in Cyrillic and is nobody’s companion; so is the archbishop who ordered the panikhidas, an order for a memorial service being not a martyrdom shared.

[Peter (Zhukov)](/saints/peter-zhukov) is the other of the two, and the page tells their deaths in one breath and two verst-marks.
