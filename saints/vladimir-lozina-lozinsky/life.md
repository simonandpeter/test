# Vladimir (Lozina-Lozinsky)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery keeps him on 13 декабря among five presbyters of 1937, and for him alone of the five it carries a reading. He was born on 26 May 1885 at Dukhovshchina in the province of Smolensk, the son of district physicians; his mother caught typhus nursing patients in the district hospital and died in 1888, and his widowed father took the boys back to St Petersburg.

He finished the gymnasium of the Imperial Philanthropic Society in 1904 and went straight to the law faculty of the university; in 1910 he entered the service of the Governing Senate, and two years later finished the archaeological institute as well. The war found him unfit for the front, and he spent it moving the wounded from the Petersburg stations to the hospitals. When the Bolsheviks closed the Senate in 1917 he took work as a statistician on the railway.

The wish to be a priest ripened slowly, the reading says, under the pressure of the Russian catastrophe, and he declared it when the open persecution of the Church began. He entered the theological institute in Petrograd in 1920 and asked for ordination that November, and he served the university church of All Saints, of which he was rector by 1923.

He was arrested again and again: in 1924 over the Spasskoe Brotherhood, and in February 1925 sentenced to ten years in the camps for serving memorial services with the imperial family remembered in them. He was at Solovki first, where his fellow prisoners remembered that the aristocracy of his bearing did not leave him even when he was weighing out stinking fish at the food stall; in 1928 the camp was commuted to five years of exile in Siberia.

From 1934 he served at Novgorod, and in 1935 became rector of the cathedral of the Archangel Michael there. He was arrested once more in May 1936, sent for examination to the regional hospital for the insane and found sane, arrested with a group of his parishioners in November 1937, and shot at Novgorod on 13 December of that year by decision of a special troika. The place of his burial, the reading ends, the Lord has left hidden. He was numbered among the new martyrs and confessors of Russia at the jubilee council of August 2000.

The day page carries troparia and kontakia for five of the memories it heads, and none under his name.
