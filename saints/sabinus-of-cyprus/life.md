# Sabinus of Cyprus

Doxologia.ro’s calendar for 15 octombrie names him «Sfântul Ierarh Savin, Episcopul Ciprului», and the life on his page is two sentences long. It says that our most venerable father Savin — Sabin — the bishop was made bishop for his unspeakable goodness; and that, hating quarrels, he fled and withdrew, and laboured so much that he became also a worker of wonders.

He healed sicknesses, it says, drove out demons and foretold what was to come. Helping many and winning them over, serving Christ, he left the world and his parents, and then reposed in peace. The page gives no year, no see beyond Cyprus itself and no account of his death, and it prints no troparion; nothing further is added here.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day, 15 October, and tells why he left his see. saint.gr says Sabinus was held by many virtues, and that for that reason he was persistently asked to accept the episcopal throne, which he refused because he sought the monastic life; and that at a moment of great psychological pressure they carried him along and he accepted the office. As a bishop he did great things in the work of charity, but he could not bear the wear and the pressures that come with administration, from laymen and from others; and so he resigned the episcopal office and returned to the solitary life of his cell. There he undertook many ascetic labours and came to great heights of virtue; God counted him worthy to work wonders, and he healed many of his fellow men of bodily and of spiritual sicknesses; and having profited many by his wise teaching as well, he died in peace. The page adds a note of its own: the Paris Codex 1578 refers to him as bishop of Cyprus.
