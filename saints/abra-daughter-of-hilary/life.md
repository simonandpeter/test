# Abra, daughter of Hilary of Poitiers

saint.gr's calendar for 12 December keeps the holy Abra, daughter of Saint [Hilary](/saints/hilary-of-poitiers), bishop of Poitiers, whom the same calendar keeps on 13 January. She lived in the fourth century, the page says, about the years 343 to 360, and she was born before her father was baptised a Christian and became a bishop.

Afterwards, on his own counsel, she became a nun, and she fell asleep in a manner befitting a saint. That is the whole of the entry: no place of birth, no monastery, no abbess, no manner of death, no relic, no hymn and no synaxaristic couplet. The calendar writes her name in Latin letters and gives no Greek form of it, and it is copied here as the page has it.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 12 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/12/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/4334/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
