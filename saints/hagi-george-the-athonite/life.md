# Hagi-George the Athonite

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and gives no life. Its line for 17 decembrie reads «Sfântul Cuvios Hagi-Gheorghe Athonitul», and the page the calendar links under that name carries neither a Viață tab nor a Tropar tab: the heading, the day, and nothing further. What the line says is that he was a venerable father, that he is called Hagi-Gheorghe, that he belonged to the Holy Mountain, and that the Romanian church keeps him on this day.

No year, no monastery, no country and no account of his life has been taken from anywhere else, and none has been supplied from memory. The English form of the name here keeps the Romanian spelling of the first element, because no other form of it was read. If a life for him is printed elsewhere, it has not been read.

*After doxologia.ro's calendar for 17 decembrie — [the day](https://doxologia.ro/17-decembrie) and [his page](https://doxologia.ro/sfantul-cuvios-hagi-gheorghe-athonitul), which prints the name only; read 28 September 2026.*
