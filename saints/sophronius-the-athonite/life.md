# Sophronius the Athonite

saint.gr says that Sophronius the Athonite was born in Epirus of pious parents in the eighteenth century, and that from a child he was marked out by the modesty of his life and his reverence for the things of God. When he came to the age of marriage his parents married him, against his will, to a modest woman.

On the night of the wedding he left for the Holy Mountain. There, at the skete of Saint Anna, he was tested and tonsured a monk, and afterwards he was ordained priest and reached great heights of holiness. His life, the page says, was virtuous and angelic, and his spiritual perfection was an example not only to the brethren of that skete but to the whole of the Holy Mountain.

Having lived in that manner, he reposed in peace. The page gives no year of birth beyond the century, no year of death at all, and no other person in his story. It prints one apolytikion for him, in the first tone, to the model «Τῆς ἐρήμου πολίτης».
