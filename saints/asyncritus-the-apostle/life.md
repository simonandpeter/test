# Asyncritus

According to the church writings, the page says, Asyncritus was ordained bishop in Heraclea of Pontus, in Asia Minor. He is commemorated together with the apostles [Hermes](/saints/hermes-apostle-of-dalmatia), [Phlegon](/saints/phlegon-of-marathon), [Herodion](/saints/herodion-the-apostle), [Agabus](/saints/agabus-the-apostle) and [Rufus](/saints/rufus-apostle-of-thebes), and named with them in the apostle Paul's letter to the Romans.

That is the whole of what doxologia prints for him. It gives no birthplace, no year, no account of his preaching and no manner of death of his own; the page for Agabus says of all six of them that they ended on the same day as a result of the torments they were put to, and that is the only thing said here about how his life ended. His page carries no painter's rule and no separate troparion beyond the one the six share, in the third tone.
