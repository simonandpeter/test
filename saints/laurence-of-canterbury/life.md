# Laurence of Canterbury

Laurence was an Anglo-Saxon — saint.gr prints the English form of the name beside the Greek, «Ο Άγιος Λαυρέντιος (Laurence)» — and lived, the page says, about the middle of the sixth century and the beginning of the seventh. He accompanied St Augustine, whom the Greek calendar honours on 26 May, and worked for the spreading of the Orthodox faith in the south of England.

He succeeded Augustine in the place of Archbishop of Canterbury and remained on the throne until his repose in the year 619. That is the whole of what saint.gr gives of him: no monastery, no account of his dealings with the kings, no hymn and no verses.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 3 Φεβρουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/02/03/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1190/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
