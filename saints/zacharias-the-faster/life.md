# Zacharias the Faster of the Kyiv Caves

Zacharias struggled in the Far Caves of the Great Lavra of Kyiv in the thirteenth and fourteenth centuries. In his lifetime he was honoured for his great abstinence: he ate nothing boiled or baked, but only after the sun had set tasted a little of the green herbs, and that with measure. For the holiness of his life he received a gift from God, that at the mere speaking of his name the demons were driven out.

doxologia.ro's calendar names him and prints no more than these few lines; it gives him no century but the thirteenth and fourteenth, and no troparion.

The Greek calendar keeps him on the same day and says one thing about him, which is where he struggled: the venerable Zacharias lived at the end of the eleventh century and the beginning of the twelfth, and was an ascetic in the Lavra of the Caves at Kyiv. That is the whole of saint.gr's entry — no fast, no wonder, no relic, no couplet and no hymn. Its century is not the one the Russian sources give this folder, which is the thirteenth and fourteenth; the day and the Lavra are what identify the line, and the page's own dating is recorded on its attestation rather than written over what was read before.
