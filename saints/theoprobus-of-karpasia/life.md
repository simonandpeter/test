# Theoprobus of Karpasia

saint.gr's whole notice for him is two sentences, and the second is a disclaimer: «Ο Όσιος Θεοπρόβος έζησε κατά τον 4ο μ.Χ. αιώνα. Δεν έχουμε περισσότερες λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο του Οσίου.» — the venerable Theoprobus lived in the fourth century after Christ, and we have no further details of his life. Everything else the calendar gives is in the day's own line, which makes him bishop of Karpasia in Cyprus.

There is no hymn, no birthplace, no account of his see and no year beyond the century, and nothing is supplied here.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 4 Ιανουαρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/01/04/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/474/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
