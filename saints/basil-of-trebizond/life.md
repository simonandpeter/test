# Basil of Trebizond

saint.gr's calendar for 20 Οκτωβρίου names Basil, bishop of Trebizond, and says at once that the Synaxaristes do not know him. He was known, the page says, to the church of Trebizond itself, which kept his memory on this day every year with a sung office; and that office was found in manuscript at Trebizond by Papadopoulos Kerameus, who reported it in the Byzantine Chronicles, volume 12, page 143.

The page places him under the reigns of Leo the Wise and Constantine Porphyrogennetos, and says that for the holiness of his life he was numbered among the saints of the church of Trebizond. It gives no year, so this folder carries no dates; it prints no hymn for him and names no relics.
