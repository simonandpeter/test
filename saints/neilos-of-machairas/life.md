# Neilos of Machairas

saint.gr's calendar for 13 December keeps four founders of the monastery of the Panagia of Machairas in Cyprus together, and Neilos is the fourth of them. [Ignatios](/saints/ignatios-of-machairas) left him his successor by a written ordinance, and he is the first enthroned abbot of the coenobium that Ignatios and [Prokopios](/saints/prokopios-of-machairas) had raised with the help of the emperor Manuel Komnenos, on the mountainside where Ignatios and [Neophytos](/saints/neophytos-of-machairas) had found the icon of the Panagia.

In 1201 Neilos wrote a Typical Ordinance — the rules that govern the monastic life and the orderly running of a coenobium — which survives to this day, and from which, the page says, all the information there is about the founding of the monastery is drawn. In 1209 he was elected bishop of Tamasos, leaving Ioakeim his successor as the new abbot; and it is very likely, the page adds, that he also became archbishop of Cyprus between the years 1215 and 1221.

As bishop of Tamasos he confirmed the Ordinance he had written as abbot, and founded a women's monastery dedicated to the Theotokos, which he named Blachernitissa. The monastery of Machairas itself stands at the eastern end of the Troodos range near the peak of Kionia, at a height of 870 metres; it is called royal because it was built with royal help, and stavropegial because it became self-governing in church order, and it keeps its own feast at the Entry of the Theotokos, on 21 November.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 13 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/13/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3252/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
