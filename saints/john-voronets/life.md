# John (Voronets)

The Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery names John fourth in a company of eight on 7 August, and his is the second of the two names in that company with a reading behind it. The calendar's own name page gives him as «Иоанн (Воронец), протоиерей, сщмч.», John Voronets, archpriest, where the day line prints the bare forename in the genitive.

The reading is the calendar's notice of the twenty-three hieromartyrs of Minsk. He was born on 20 June 1864 into a priest's family in the village of Kholopenichi in the Borisov district, finished the Minsk theological seminary, and was ordained priest in 1888. He served in the townlet of Smilovichi in the Minsk province.

In 1930 he preached against the policy of robbing the peasantry, and that was the cause of his arrest in April of that year. He served his exile at Chernihiv, returned to Smilovichi and served again in its Георгиевская church, and was called in for night interrogations by the NKVD, as a rule on the eve of some church feast.

In 1935 the church at Smilovichi was closed and he began to serve in secret. When the All-Union census came he urged people to write themselves down as believers, confessing Christ in that way. He was arrested in July 1937, named nobody under interrogation and admitted no guilt, and was shot on the feast of the Transfiguration. The calendar keeps him on the day after it.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 7 августа ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20260807.html) and [the notice of the Minsk hieromartyrs](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life4766.htm); read 13 October 2026.*
