# Nicodemus the Hagiorite

Nicodemus was born on Naxos in 1749 to Antony and Anastasia Kallivoursi; his
name in the world was Nicholas. He learned his first letters on Naxos and went on to the school
of the same town under Chrysanthos, the brother of Kosmas of Aetolia, and at sixteen to the Greek
school at Smyrna. In 1775 he came to the Holy Mountain and was tonsured at the monastery of Saint
Dionysius with the name Nicodemus, serving as its reader and secretary before withdrawing to a
cell to write. His books include the Synaxaristes, the Eortodromion, the New Ladder and the
Unseen Warfare. He died of a stroke at sixty, in the early hours of 14 July 1809, in the cell of
the Skourtaioi at Karyes. The Greek calendar also keeps him on 20 September.

*After the Ορθόδοξος Συναξαριστής (saint.gr), 20 Σεπτεμβρίου — [the entry](https://www.saint.gr/701/saint.aspx); read 26 August 2026.*
