# Castor (5 November)

saint.gr's calendar for 5 Νοεμβρίου keeps Castor and Agathangelus in one entry, and the whole of its account of them is a single sentence: Castor — who, according to uncorroborated information, was a bishop — was martyred by fire, and Agathangelus by the sword. The page gives neither of them a country, a century, an emperor, a see or a year, and prints no hymn for the entry. The bishopric it reports of Castor it reports as uncorroborated, and nothing more is recorded of it here.

What the page does print for each of them is his couplet out of the synaxarion. Castor's reads his end as near and his crown as nearer: «Oυκ εις μακράν σου του πυρός Kάστορ λύσις. / Tι του λόγου δηλούντος; Eγγύς το στέφος» — your release from the fire is not far off, Castor; and what does the saying mean? The crown is near. The man kept with him is [Agathangelus](/saints/agathangelus-5-november).

*After saint.gr's calendar for 5 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/05/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/2897/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
