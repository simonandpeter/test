# Ethelbert

saint.gr says Ethelbert lived in the eighth century, that he was a most devout king, and that he was brought up in the nurture and admonition of the Lord. Very young he succeeded his father on the throne, and reigned forty-four years. He used to declare that the higher a man stands the humbler he ought to be, and he made that conviction the rule and the manner of his own life.

Wishing to secure the succession, he asked for the hand of Ælfthryth, daughter of Offa, king of Mercia. He was received as a guest for a few days at the court of the man who was to be his father-in-law, and there, before the marriage, he was murdered in the year 794 by the queen's men; she was ambitious to annex his kingdom to their own.

He was buried in a place unknown, the page says, but his grave was revealed by a light from heaven. That is all it prints of him: no kingdom of his own is named, no wonder beyond the light, and no hymn.
