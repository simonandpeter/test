# Porphyrius the Stratelates

saint.gr's calendar for 25 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιος Πορφύριος ο Στρατηλάτης» — Porphyrius the commander — gives «Πορφυρίων» as another form of the name, and then says the whole of what it has in a single sentence: he was martyred by the sword together with his two hundred soldiers. The page sets no city, no year and no emperor beside it, and it names not one of the two hundred.

The couplet over the line makes his rank the point of him: with your army, Porphyrius, you come willingly to the sword, as befits a commander. Besides that there is the page's gloss on the name, the royal one, the magnificent, and the note that a Tyrian philosopher of that name, a pupil of Plotinus, lived from 233 to 304 — which is the site's habit with any name and says nothing about him. The page prints him no relics and no hymn.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 25 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/25/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1057/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
