# Porphyrius the Stratelates

saint.gr's calendar for 25 Νοεμβρίου names «Άγιος Πορφύριος ο Στρατηλάτης» — Porphyrius the commander — gives «Πορφυρίων» as another form of the name, and then says the whole of what it has in a single sentence: he was martyred by the sword together with his two hundred soldiers. The page sets no city, no year and no emperor beside it, and it names not one of the two hundred.

The couplet over the line makes his rank the point of him: with your army, Porphyrius, you come willingly to the sword, as befits a commander. Besides that there is the page's gloss on the name, the royal one, the magnificent, and the note that a Tyrian philosopher of that name, a pupil of Plotinus, lived from 233 to 304 — which is the site's habit with any name and says nothing about him. The page prints him no relics and no hymn.

**The Russian calendar keeps him on 24 ноября ст. ст. and gives him the story saint.gr left out.** Its life page for the day is a joint one — of the great-martyr Catherine, the martyr Augusta and the martyr Porphyrius with his two hundred soldiers — and in it he is a воевода, a commander at the court of the emperor Максимин, whose reign the page dates 305 to 313. The empress, who had heard much of [Catherine](/saints/catherine-of-alexandria) and wished to see her, talked him into escorting her to the prison with a detachment of soldiers.

The two of them were struck by the martyr's strength of spirit and by the face that shone with grace; she opened the Christian teaching to those who had come, and they believed and turned to Christ. The next day, when Catherine was brought back to the tribunal and threatened with the wheels, an angel broke the instruments of execution to pieces and the flying fragments killed many of the pagans. Seeing that, the empress [Augusta](/saints/basilissa-wife-of-maxentius) and the courtier Porphyrius confessed their faith in Christ before them all, with the two hundred soldiers, and were beheaded.

The page names not one of the two hundred, so they are no folders here either, and they stand in this life as they stand in the Greek one. It gives him no city of his own beyond the Alexandria in the heading of his name page, no family and no age, and no hymn is printed under his name on the day page, whose troparia and kontakia are for the two Mercuriuses and for Catherine.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 25 Νοεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/11/25/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/1057/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026; and the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 24 ноября ст. ст. — [that day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261124.html) and [the joint life behind his name](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Life/life3218.htm), read 7 October 2026.*
