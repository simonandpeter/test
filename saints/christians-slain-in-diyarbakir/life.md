# The Orthodox Christians slain in Diyarbakır

On 30 Ιουνίου the Greek calendar keeps a company with no names in it. saint.gr's entry is a commemoration rather than a life: «Μνήμη των αγρίως σφαγιασθέντων υπό Κουρδικών ορδών εν τω νομώ Διαρβεκίρ της Μικράς Ασίας Ορθοδόξων Χριστιανών υπαγομένων εις το Πατριαρχείον Αλεξανδρείας που έλαβε χώρα το έτος 1896 μ.Χ.» — the memory of the Orthodox Christians, under the Patriarchate of Alexandria, savagely slaughtered by Kurdish bands in the province of Diyarbakır in Asia Minor, which took place in the year 1896.

The page then says «Δεν έχουμε άλλες λεπτομέρειες για το γεγονός», we have no further details of the event. So there is no number, no name, no place within the province and no account of the killing — only the year, which the page states, and the church these people belonged to. Nothing further is recorded here, and nothing is reconstructed from the history of that year.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 30 Ιουνίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/06/30/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/643/saint.aspx); read 1 October 2026.*
