# Hypatius of the Thebaid

saint.gr's calendar for 14 December keeps Saint Hypatius together with the thirty-six martyrs who suffered with him, and its page is two sentences long and mostly about where his memory is recorded. It says that Hypatius is unknown to the Synaxaristes of [Nicodemus the Hagiorite](/saints/nicodemus-the-hagiorite) and to the printed Menaia, and that his memory is found in the Parisian Codex 1590 together with that of [Philemon](/saints/philemon-the-flute-player) and [Apollonius](/saints/apollonius-the-reader), whom the same day's calendar keeps.

It says further that he was one of the thirty-six martyrs from the Thebaid of Egypt under the governor [Arrianus](/saints/arianus-of-the-thebaid), whom that same day's calendar keeps too — the man who, as the entry for him tells it, had Philemon and Apollonius tortured and beheaded, was struck blind, was healed by earth from Philemon's grave, and then believed in Christ himself and was martyred with four of his bodyguards.

That is the whole of what saint.gr prints of Hypatius. The thirty-six it names with him in the calendar line it does not name one by one, and no folder is made for them. It gives him no year of birth and none of death, it prints no hymn of his, and the apolytikion it prints for this day names a company of six among whom he is not counted.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 14 Δεκεμβρίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/12/14/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3260/saint.aspx); read 2 October 2026.*
