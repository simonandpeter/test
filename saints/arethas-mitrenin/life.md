# Arethas (Mitrenin)

He was born on 21 November 1879 at Kronstadt, and was called Alexander Fyodorovich Mitrenin in the world. The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery, which keeps him on 24 октября ст. ст., gives his monastic life as a list of dates: he entered the monastery of Valaam on 26 May 1902, was enrolled among the novices on 20 March 1906, was tonsured a monk with the name Arethas on 22 May 1910, was ordained hierodeacon on 30 July 1915 and hieromonk on 28 April 1921. From February 1917 he kept the choir obedience at the Moscow metochion and took his turn of the services.

In 1925 he went out to Finland, to the monastery of Valaam, and on 22 October of that year he was expelled from it «за верность Русской Церкви и Юлианскому календарю» — for his faithfulness to the Russian Church and to the Julian calendar. From 1927 he lived in the chapel of the Valaam monastery in Leningrad, on Vasilyevsky Island. He was arrested on 18 February 1932.

The page prints a sentence of his interrogation and the sentence of the court. He said: «...Против Сов. власти никогда активно не выступал, но советским человеком быть не могу, так как нас, монахов, лишили всего: монастыри и церкви закрывают, священнослужителей высылают; вообще сов. властью везде гонение на религию» — he had never taken any active part against the Soviet power, but could not be a Soviet man, because they, the monks, had been stripped of everything. The resolution was: «Выслать в Казахстан сроком на 3 года с 17/02.1932 г.» He died in exile in the November of 1932 and was buried at Tashkent, in his fifty-third year, and was numbered among the New Martyrs and Confessors of Russia at the jubilee council of bishops in August 2000.

**The three pages that name him give him three different ranks**, and this folder keeps all three readings: the day's line calls him venerable and a confessor, the title on his name link a hieromonk and a confessor, and his own life page is headed «Священномученик Арефа Валаамский», a hieromartyr — which neither of the others says, and which does not sit easily with a page that has him die in exile of nothing it states. The line's words are in his types, the link's grade is his office, and the life page's title is recorded here.
