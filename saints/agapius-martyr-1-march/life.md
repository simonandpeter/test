# Agapius, with Charisius and Nicephorus

saint.gr names three martyrs together on this day — [Charisius](/saints/charisius-martyr-1-march), [Nicephorus](/saints/nicephorus-martyr-1-march) and Agapius — and then says in one sentence that it knows nothing about them: «Δεν έχουμε λεπτομέρειες για τον βίο των Αγίων», we have no details of the saints' lives. The line enumerates all three by name, so each is kept here as a folder of his own rather than as a company; but the whole of what is recorded of any of them is the name and the day.

The etymology block the page sets over the name says Αγάπιος is the much-loved one, the one full of love, and then adds «Αθηναίος πολυμαθής φιλόσοφος» — an Athenian polymath philosopher. That is the site's note on the word and on other men who have borne it, and nothing on the page attaches it to this martyr, so it is not taken as a fact about him. The distich over the three counts rather than describes them: a triad of contenders now stands in heaven before the Trinity that created all things.

There is no year, no country, no emperor, no judge, no manner of death, no relic and no hymn. The same Greek day keeps [Agapius of Colciu](/saints/agapius-of-colciu), a venerable of the Holy Mountain with a page and a story of his own, and he is not this man; the corpus keeps eight further bearers of the name, and none of them is on this day.

*After saint.gr's calendar for 1 Μαρτίου — [the day](https://www.saint.gr/03/01/index.aspx) and [the life](https://www.saint.gr/3779/saint.aspx); read 30 September 2026.*
