# Anastasia (Lebedeva)

The Russian church calendar of the Сретенский monastery names her for 29 октября ст. ст. in four words — «Мц. Анастасии (после 1937)» — and the name link behind the line adds the surname the line leaves out: «Анастасия (Лебедева), мц.», a martyress of that name, one of the new martyrs of the Russian church. It gives her no grade and no state of life, so this folder records none.

**What the calendar says about when she died is that it does not know.** «После 1937» is after 1937, and it is a finding rather than a year: her death is recorded here with an open upper bound for that reason, which is how this corpus keeps a date a source has deliberately left unfinished. There is no life page behind the line and her name page carries no text, no icon and no hymn.

What it does carry is days, and she has one more of them than anybody else on this page: besides this one, the 26 января synaxis of the new martyrs and confessors of Russia, and the 23 мая synaxis of the saints of Rostov, which is the only thing on any page read here that places her anywhere. The second Russian calendar, azbyka.ru, which has given this corpus a full life and its archival references for others of these years, refused the request on the day she was read.

*After the Православный церковный календарь of the Сретенский monastery for 29 октября ст. ст. — [the day](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/Days/20261029.html) and [her name page](https://days.pravoslavie.ru/name/10047.html); read 4 October 2026.*
