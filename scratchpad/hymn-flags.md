
## Round 2 (slices emitted after 8a6e080)

Raised by the rendering pass, each one a question about the *data* rather
than the English. None blocks a write; all want the author's eye.

- **kosmas-of-aetolia · troparion · 2 [ro]** — "ai primit lumânarea Duhului",
  "thou didst receive the candle of the Spirit". Almost certainly a scrape
  corruption of *luminarea* ("the illumination"), which is the ordinary phrase.
  Rendered as written rather than as guessed; flip it if the source page reads
  *luminarea*.
- **lawrence-of-kaluga · kontakion · 8 [cu]** — «инее» is not a word, and the
  dative chain wants a relative. Rendered without it. The hymn also turns from
  the third person to direct address mid-way; kept, not smoothed.
- **mamas-of-caesarea · troparion · 4 [cu]** — «имели» for «имеяй»; read as the
  singular the rest of the hymn is in.
- **macarius-the-protopsaltes · troparion · 5 [ro]** — "tălmăciundu-le" for
  *tălmăcindu-le*.
- **john-v-patriarch-of-constantinople · kontakion · 8** — the text is plural
  and names Alexander, John and Paul, the three patriarchs kept together. It is
  John's hymn and was filled, but if it belongs to the other two folders as
  well, the corpus does not yet say so.
- **phoebe-the-deaconess · both hymns [el]** — «ενδιαφέροντα» ("interesting")
  stands in two places where the syntax wants a short word, most likely «ἐν».
  A scraper substitution, not a Greek word in this slot. Rendered around it.
- **samuel · troparion · 5 [cu]** — «сподобися» where the sense wants
  «сподобился еси»; read as the second person the hymn addresses.
- **kosmas-of-aetolia**, checked: «lumânarea» appears nowhere else in the
  corpus, and «luminarea Duhului» appears nowhere either, so the corpus itself
  cannot settle it. The source page can.
- **three-virgins (Menodora, Metrodora, Nymphodora) · both hymns [el]** — the
  longer text is OCR-corrupt throughout («τᾶς» for «τὰς», «συνδεδεμένος» for
  «-μένας»), and the shorter has «ὅθεν προϊστάντι ἡμῶν», read as
  «προΐστανται ἡμῶν». The one place in the slice where a reading was chosen
  rather than transcribed.
- **mamas-of-caesarea · troparion · 1 [el]** — «Μόρφου τὴ πάλει» for «τῇ πόλει».
- **nicetas · [ro]** — the third member of the Glory doxology is not the standard
  one; the source was followed rather than the canned formula.
- **mitrophan · troparion** — closes by asking peace for "our land and thy city",
  not «спаси души наша»; rendered as it stands.
